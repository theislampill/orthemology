## Late cooperation: what was investigated and what was not

### B5: co-bearability, de re eligibility and constitutive perfection

`SOURCE_ARGUMENT.md` §6a, `MODEL_AUDIT.md` §2, and the independent review §5 recognise a new explicit argument in Majmu 20:178–181. They distinguish:

1. A combined power profile is realisable in one bearer.
2. Each already identified partner can possess that profile.
3. The profile is an eligible improvement of that very partner.
4. The partner cannot supply its own missing completion.
5. This is dependence incompatible with its intrinsic necessity.

The source's concentration examples and the Minhaj pure-perfection argument are assessed positively rather than ignored. The independent review explains why unification does not alone establish de re transfer, why a new collective bearer is insufficient, and why the self-completion inference needs a relevant anti-self-origination rationale. It also observes that independently warranted unrestricted eligible power could make part of B5 redundant.

**Credit:** direct source reading, substantial premise decomposition and adversarial philosophical judgement. **Not earned:** a concrete interpretation preserving the independently accepted antecedents, a bridge-specific search, or a proof that existing pure-perfection commitments do or do not discharge the exact de re eligibility obligation. The late review's source bindings do not include a fresh direct reconciliation of T17 P65–68 with a typed B5 eligibility interpretation; it discusses the issue primarily through the new primary passages and existing model summaries.

### B6: complete determination to exclusive original completion

The late packet expressly distinguishes D(A,e) implying occurrence of e from D(A,e) implying entire original provision, and then from exclusive provision. It grants the valid exclusion when fully exercised unborrowed original completion and same-respect CND are supplied. It recognises that the unchanged-agent/source argument fixes will, exercise, instruments, target and relevant state; ordinary half-effort/full-effort comparisons do not preserve that background.

**Credit:** a sound written conditional decomposition and a materially stronger source-informed test specification. **Not earned:** a new attempt to interpret complete determination without building the disputed entire-provision conclusion into it, then test that interpretation against the fixed-background source constraints. A saturated ordinary mechanism would not automatically be a full counterexample. Equally, demanding B6 itself as an antecedent would be circular.

### M1/P2b: effect completion to intrinsic perfection

This is not merely a generic claim that agents need powers. Minhaj 2:180–183 expressly treats realised efficacy as perfection; Majmu 20:180–181 connects external production to acts and qualities within the producer. Earlier worked cases genuinely explore an exercise norm and competing goods. The late review's four-level distinction between capacity, genus, token act and effect is analytically useful.

The remaining untested step is the exact transfer from a shared undertaking's completion to a completion of the independently original agent under a rich fixed interpretation. The current packet neither refutes that transfer nor derives it from weaker antecedents. It is not fair to claim the source ignored cooperation; it is equally unfair to infer the transfer's truth from the inadequacy of earlier models.

### B2 and the third-cause argument

Necessary concurrence, efficient conditioning, dependence of the joint result and a common reason are kept apart. The source's actual response to foreknowledge, common benefit and a third efficient cause is read and appraised. There is no certified interpretation in which intrinsic knowledge/reasons determine matching original wills while preserving the full relevant power and perfection commitments. Nor is there a derivation that all such concurrence is efficient receipt.

This is a substantive narrowed question. It belongs as a stress control in the same bounded B5/B6 interpretation attempt, not as a separate open-ended programme in the metaphysics of reasons.
