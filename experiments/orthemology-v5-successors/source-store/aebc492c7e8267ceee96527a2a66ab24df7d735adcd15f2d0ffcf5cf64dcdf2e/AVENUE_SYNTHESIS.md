# Twelve-avenue relevance synthesis

All numbered results below are unadopted claims in the pinned catalogue. The full family map records 287 orthogonal families as well as every candidate and weak analogy. No catalogue claim has yet been transferred to Orthemology by this review.

## 1. Orthability and necessary being

No direct transfer demonstrated. Algebraic reconstruction, model categoricity, invariant rigidity and connectedness theorems do not supply actual original completion, a resource's intrinsic bearer, broad receipt representation, contingency-as-need or essential nonreceipt. The current source-bound A/B/C bridge keeps these roles explicit. In particular, 240's categoricity is uniqueness up to isomorphism in specified cardinalities, not numerical or metaphysical uniqueness.

## 2. Source and attribute ascent

No direct transfer demonstrated. Recovering a string (122), root state (229) or smooth coefficient field (365/372) from observations is not an inference to source-owned assertions, wisdom, perfect control or truthfulness. The actual bearer, original productive source, speaker and owner of an assertion must remain distinct. The corpus adds no warrant for D1/D2, whole-history source-mode coverage, or the all-context defect/conformance premise.

## 3. Objective norm fixing

No direct transfer demonstrated. Optimal control, approximation ratios and transport minima optimize already specified objectives; they do not identify which standard has practical jurisdiction or which source grounds it. A mathematical optimum cannot supply the disputed normative premise.

## 4. Unknown structure restoration

Priority: 104, 122, 124, 129, 140, 229, 365 and 376. These give potential exact services for **declared** finite games, deletion channels, known precedence graphs, automaton models, Gaussian measurements, tree broadcasts, boundary inverse problems and effectively forced dynamics.

Useful positive target: construct a fully specified finite restoration game or unit-job scheduling contract, then prove that its winning/certificate semantics matches capacity restoration with all continuing resources counted. Useful negative target: translate an exact all-time reachability query into the forced-fluid compiler's halting detector. Neither establishes unknown hidden dynamics, arbitrary support recovery, robust physical sensing or unrestricted deadlines. Family 149 offers a separate fixed-rate mass-action case, not a general continuation theorem.

## 5. Provenance resilient restoration

Priority: 119, 122 and 229. Noisy-summary information, exact word reconstruction and root-state inference can be compared with existing grounded support and archive retraction. The immediate research question is what additional source, time, force and ownership labels are required beyond recoverable content.

Secondary graph/cut methods (089, 102, 117, 174), edit comparison (099, 121) and superstring assembly (128) cannot preserve provenance merely by preserving distances, cuts or strings. The existing four-label cheapest-cut failure is not automatically an NP-hardness reduction. Any new cut result needs the exact labelled-support and alias-transport semantics.

## 6. Warranted collective diagnosis

Priority: 119, 133, 140 and 229, with 161/190 as possible observation-insufficiency controls. Specify the observed law, latent model and decision target first. Full answer-law agreement, finite-message information and graph-invariant agreement are different measurements. None identifies an actual group's mechanism by itself.

Potential study: compare a noisy binary summary, finite-memory Gaussian learner and a graph/community reconstruction test under their stated assumptions. Retain independent input/noise assumptions, prior distributions, observation access and averaging conventions. Factor-of-IID representations (231, 236) illustrate alternate same-law mechanisms, not causal-history identification. This would still require actual empirical testing for a real group.

## 7. Identity through copying and synchronisation

Priority: 122, 133, 140, 229, 365 and 373. Their exact distinctions are content reconstruction, equivalence under a selected invariant, information retained after compression, root-state recovery, gauge/diffeomorphism uniqueness and nonattainment of a deterministic realization.

A promising written comparison fixes a declared continuation contract and asks which equivalence relation each observation can identify. Reconstructing the same content or producing an isomorphic state does not establish same historical bearer, preserved authority or numerical personal identity. Banach/operator-algebra examples in the map are only structural analogies unless such carriers are actually introduced.

## 8. Specification and semantic fidelity

Priority: 104, 116, 129, 235, 243, 245, 266 and 376. Each offers a precise place to audit prose theorem → encoded statement → certificate → runtime semantics. Family 266's binary64/compiler-conditioned exclusion is especially useful beside its integer/rational certificate companion; the two verification contracts must not be conflated. Family 189 is another possible exact-computation/readback example.

For 116, changing scalar/natural arithmetic to noncommutative matrices changes semantics. An identity checker for a new matrix-valued fragment needs its own grammar, typing and F/G interpretation bridge. For 376, a finite force description and an exact all-time detector must retain clocks, precision and history resources. No present Python/Lean/runtime equivalence follows from external proof artifacts.

## 9. Type theoretic completion

Priority: 116, 242, 243, 244 and 245. Family 245 is separately owned; universal weak normalization across all legal expressions/contexts is its hypothesis, not a result one may assume for a selected HasE term. No normalization theorem alone constructs the current literal x/x+0 witness.

116 is a potential new restricted semantic-checker domain, conditional on a fresh typed noncommutative encoding. 242 separates a unique auxiliary witness from a decision procedure. 243 separates ordinary polynomial-time solvability from choiceless definability. 244 separates specific selection principles in relative set-theoretic models. None supplies a whole-rule HasE separator or closes scalar fusion. Higher-category semantics (312, 317) remain a possible future modeling direction, not an existing translation.

## 10. Measure and computability

Priority: 104, 116, 129, 140, 229, 235, 242, 243, 244, 373 and 376. Preserve the input representation, quantifiers, oracle access, randomness, cost model and uniformity in each case.

Three particularly useful distinctions are: computable threshold via opposing certificates without a finite-size convergence modulus (235); unique witness without decidability (242); equal optimal infima without an attained deterministic optimum (373). The target-dependent oracle in 283 is an additional caution against hiding synthesis resources. Almost-sure existence, Borel selection, efficient computation and finite operational guarantees remain different services.

## 11. Occurrence sensitive language

Potential interfaces: 119, 122 and 229, with 128/134 as restricted assembly or finite-language tools. A reconstructed string or root value does not preserve an occurrence's source, assertion force, time or warrant. The grounded-attestation baseline therefore remains essential. A useful extension would explicitly compare a content-only decoder with an occurrence-labelled archive and prove exactly which composed queries survive the decoding. No actual source authentication or new ownership fact follows from the catalogue.

## 12. Empirical and specialist validation

Priority for methodology: 266 and, secondarily, 189. The full catalogue read and per-manuscript trust crosswalk improve triage and evidence visibility. They do not constitute participant experiments, external human endorsement, independent empirical replication, proof-body review or a fresh Lean/Comparator replay.

The next acceptance gate for any selected result is narrow: read the exact manuscript body and dependencies, inspect the stated formal endpoint and its assumptions, establish prose/formal correspondence, and separately prove the Orthemology interpretation. Run only the specifically authorized verification. Successful mathematics verification still does not validate an empirical or metaphysical application.
