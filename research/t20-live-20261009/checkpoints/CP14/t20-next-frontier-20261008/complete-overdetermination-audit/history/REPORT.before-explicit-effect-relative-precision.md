# Duplicate sufficient production: positive process-level warrant, unresolved original-resource completion

9 October 2026 UTC. Bounded T20 research. No protected-repository mutation, final integration, closure, or historical-duration claim.

## Result

The strongest newly tested alternative is genuinely different from complementary joint manifestation: A and B each have an actually operative, individually sufficient productive route to the same effect. Neither needs the other's route to complete its own. An objection based only on **indispensable foreign supplementation** therefore does not automatically exclude this alternative. Defining `entire` as a singleton total provenance set would likewise decide the disputed issue by convention.

Two consequential primary treatments give substantive reasons for actual causal activity under redundancy, beyond alternative capacity or an occurrence certificate. In particular, Schaffer's noncompositional cases and complete-process argument give **prima facie warrant for two intact individually sufficient actual processes terminating in one effect**. This can properly be called duplicate whole-effect production at that process/outcome scope. The unestablished stronger claim is that each producer supplies the **entire unreceived original resource basis** and satisfies the corresponding perfection standard. The failed promotion is to that stronger claim, not a denial of all senses of whole-effect production.

The source's exact nonduplication argument consequently still needs its stronger whole-production interpretation. The new reading **weakens any inference from causal independence or complete-process actuality alone to exclusive production**. My independent local judgment is that this inference has not been established against the each-complete rival, but neither has the rival's original-agent extension. This is a material weakening of an unrestricted self-evidence claim, not a re-ranking of complete original-agent theories. A cumulative judgment must weigh the remaining independent arguments; it cannot use the old verdict about indispensable joint production as a refutation of this different rival.

## 2. Fixed target and strongest proposal

Keep five claims apart:

1. A has enough standing capacity to produce e in an appropriate circumstance.
2. A's activity really is a cause of actual e.
3. An A-only sufficient productive route is actually operating, rather than merely available if B were absent.
4. A's actual route provides the whole effect-relative original productive basis, without a missing part or independently supplied requisite.
5. A is the only actual productive original of e.

The strongest rival asserts 3 and 4 for both A and B, while rejecting 5. It is not adequately described as each willing its part conditional on the other completing the job. Whether 4 entails 5 is the disputed nonduplication question; it must not be settled by inserting 5 into a stipulated definition of 4. Conversely, neither 2 nor 3 automatically establishes 4.

The effect must remain fixed. A death, shattering, or motion under an ordinary description may be a legitimate causal explanandum. But sameness of that explanandum does not imply equality of every created occurrence in the causal history. A complete-history comparison must count independently real created intermediates, recipient alterations, and other products. It must not count the originals' uncreated intrinsic acts as created additions. Nor must a causal relation be treated as another created entity merely because it is real.

A local counterexample need not reproduce the cosmos: an independently justified instance of two complete producers of one effect would challenge a universal nonduplication rule. The stronger claim that such producers explain the actual connected created order has additional coverage obligations. These are different tests.

## 4. What the strongest account earns

### Actuality survives redundancy

S1–S2 and H1–H2 give a real rationale for moving beyond claim1. A completed operative connection can matter even where removing its initiator leaves another adequate route. Merely pointing out that the endpoint no longer counterfactually depends on A does not establish that A was idle. Nor does replacing physical transmission with a nonphysical primitive automatically make the account meaningless.

For the intended noncompositional case, the positive warrant reaches claim3: two intact individually sufficient processes are actually active toward one effect. Calling this only “causal membership” would understate the evidence. The p.38 component argument is one argument for causal individualism; it is not a ceiling on the stronger support available from the other cases and arguments.

An appropriate transport of the intrinsic-pattern rationale would say: if the A-to-e route is genuinely complete and actual, and its causal character is unchanged when a parallel B-to-e route is present, B's presence does not itself remove A's production. Repeat symmetrically. This is stronger than writing two arrows whose intended causal interpretation is unexplained. It supplies a defeasible reason for taking the arrows seriously **when the relevant causal structure and invariance are independently established**.

This reasoning is not yet a result about original agency. Its key antecedent concerns a complete route and its causal character; sameness of A's intrinsic act alone does not certify it. Equally, an opponent must not demand that a primitive original route reduce to ordinary mechanics while allowing its own original causation to remain primitive.

### No quantifier jump from process completion to original-resource completion

A path can run all the way to its destination while failing to include everything needed for the destination's occurrence. An actually adequate initiating route may presuppose a recipient, its powers, sustaining conditions, and other resources. Finishing that route does not by itself provide these resources originally.

There is a second, independent issue. Even a route that is sufficient in context may actually supply only one component of the target's fine-grained realization. Claim2 then holds, and a defensible claim3 may hold at a chosen target, without claim4. S4 is decisive against attributing claim4 to the first paper simply because its title and argument defend individual overdeterminers.

This is not a denial that an ordinary partial contributor causes the whole event. Nor does it retract the positive process-level warrant just recognized. It denies that either finding automatically answers the stronger original-resource question. H1 makes the same semantic caution indispensable for the second treatment. In particular, H2's uniqueness condition belongs to its blueprint construction; it is neither a universal axiom excluding redundant actual causes nor a proof that every cause supplies the whole original basis.

### The genuinely noncompositional option remains substantive but conditional

The most promising extension for the rival is a direct noncompositional effect: each original's activity has a complete direct productive relation to precisely e, with no differing created routes or component effects concealed by e's description. Here the physical component objection would be inapplicable by hypothesis. S3 prevents rejecting that hypothesis merely because its causal mode is nonphysical.

The two-spells case should receive a stronger evidential description than “a clear represented scenario.” A coherent, intelligible causal possibility, assessed with general arguments about causal status, supplies prima facie modal evidence. Its force is against treating duplicate actual causation as a conceptual contradiction. The present audit does not defeat that evidence by demanding actual physical laws, maximally fragile event identity, or additional external traces. Nor does the component account in S4 show that every case in S3 secretly works by component addition. The conditional noncompositional case likewise cannot be dismissed by an argument whose premise is precisely composition of separate forces.

But this extension adds exactly the claims whose warrant is required. It needs a contentful interpretation under which each relation constitutes entire actual production, not just an independently sufficient cause among others; an exact effect or complete-history inventory; and an original-resource account. The two treatments do not supply that package. The shortfall is not lack of a mechanical reduction or a complete account of every wise reason. It is that their independently defended causal relation is compatible with cases that lack the package.

Accordingly, there are three distinct evidential levels: a formally represented case; defeasible modal warrant for intact individually sufficient actual processes producing one effect; and a warranted metaphysical instance of two entire unreceived original resource bases. The reading moves beyond the first to the second. It does not establish the third. The single actual effect at the second level should also not be confused with strict numerical identity across alternative worlds; the latter would be an additional origin/individuation thesis. This audit finds neither an independently established whole-original instance nor a contradiction in every possible extension.

## 5. Confronting the actual nonduplication argument

The direct source return here was to the supplied Oneness extraction, printed23–25. The wider primary interpretation remains bound to the complete-efficacy and cooperation-reassessment records, especially Majmu20:175–177. No fresh Arabic scan collation is claimed.

At printed23 the source moves from each equal agent's intact power to the impossibility of each entirely causing the same effect, explicating independence in terms of sole entire production. Printed24 then turns the other's nonproduction into a condition of one's exercise and therefore a restriction. The house-building illustration gives intuitive force to a production interpretation in which another real worker's contribution displaces part of one's entire work. The stronger inherited Majmu argument adds unchanged efficacy and nonabsorptive augmentation. The argument is therefore substantive; it is not merely an accidental English use of “exclusive.”

Three appraisals follow.

**First, the explicit exclusive conclusion is secure at its own meaning.** Two distinct agents cannot each be the sole actual original of the same effect in the same respect. Nothing in the new sources contests that trivial incompatibility.

**Second, the each-complete rival targets the promotion to that meaning.** Given two genuinely redundant whole routes, B is not an indispensable supplement to A. Calling B an actual cause does not show that A waits on B's leave, receives B's power, or leaves a part of e to B. An argument whose reason is that A lacks an indispensable resource must first demonstrate that lack in this case. It cannot infer it from the mere union of actual contributors. This is why the consent-branch verdict for complementary joint manifestation is not automatically transferable.

**Third, the affirmative account has a stronger available answer.** Its claim is that genuine whole original production cannot be duplicated in a numerically single effect: unchanged operative addition must either add real created work or qualify the first agent's supposed entire efficacy. If warranted at that scope, this answers the redundant rival without requiring any disagreement of wills. But the exact nonduplication or nonabsorption principle is doing the work. It cannot be replaced by an occurrence entailment, a provenance singleton definition, or the mere presence of two uncreated acts.

The new primary arguments do not defeat this stronger answer at original-resource scope. Their process-level warrant does not certify entire unreceived original provision. Conversely, the noncompositional and complete-process arguments supply reason to reject a broader principle excluding two intact individually sufficient actual productions of one effect. A fair assessment must hold those two claims apart.

## 6. Resulting judgment and bounded stopping point

- **Fresh positive warrant:** two intact individually sufficient actual processes can produce one effect, with prima facie modal warrant beyond endpoint sufficiency. This is whole-effect production at the specified process/outcome scope.
- **Failed promotion:** neither examined account establishes each of two unreceived originals actually providing the entire same original productive resource basis. The predicates also cover partial contribution, but that does not cancel their stronger noncompositional support; the outstanding issue is the original-resource extension.
- **Distinct residual:** indispensability-based foreign-completion reasoning does not by itself refute an actually redundant whole-route rival. The source's whole-production nonduplication/augmentation rationale must be assessed directly.
- **Independent evidential balance:** no warranted plural-original countermodel or new universal exclusion theorem has been obtained. The source retains an affirmative rationale for comprehensive unborrowed adequacy. But the present rival proposes that each actual route already has that adequacy, so the rationale does not yield a differential verdict against it without the disputed nonduplication or exclusivity bridge. The new modal evidence materially weakens an unrestricted self-evidence claim for that bridge. Whether the cumulative common-source judgment remains stronger depends on the remaining arguments and their scope; this narrow audit neither preserves it by default nor defeats it by announcing a complete rival.

Further examples of firing squads, threshold devices, or completed ordinary causal chains would repeat the established causal-membership credit. Useful continuation would have to warrant or refute the specific extension from actual redundant productive routes to entire original provision, while preserving the chosen effect, full created history, and the distinction between intrinsic act and effect. No new response table, code model, or Lean exercise was needed.
