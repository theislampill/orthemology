# The practical perfection warrant against an intrinsically malicious source

## Result

The strongest malicious-source challenge survives more of the source ascent than an image of a needy, ignorant or purely evil being suggests. A source may be intrinsically actual, fully informed, efficacious, uncoerced and intentionally organized while pursuing a bad end. It need not be evil in every metaphysical respect. Its Knowledge, Power and purposive coherence are positive resources. The vice lies in how it orders them. The claim that evil is a privation therefore does not, by itself, exclude this candidate.

The positive Taymiyyan answer is a respect-specific practical-perfection argument. It first identifies intelligent willing of genuinely fitting good as an excellence independently of source attribution; separates that excellence from creaturely need or subjection; and argues that a complete original giver of such an eligible excellence cannot be wholly inferior to its derivative recipients in that respect. This is a substantive qiyās al-awlā rationale. It is not simply the definition of a good source, and its premises can receive rational warrant without already accepting a revelation. But its epistemic force, the objective status of its norm, and the metaphysical impossibility of every malicious alternative are three distinct questions.

A further result sharpens the burden. Even if an original-giver argument establishes some source beneficence, a mixed source can possess that virtue in some exercises and act maliciously in others. Universal impeccable Will needs a stronger intrinsic-completeness and modal-scope warrant, just as some source Knowledge is weaker than omniscience. The continuation therefore improves the positive argument and specifies where it stops. The frozen attribute-v1 and its accepted review remain unchanged.

## 1 The rival at full strength

Call the strongest relevant candidate M. Grant M all upstream conclusions presently available at their stated scope:

- M is one necessary, unreceived, intrinsically determinate original source of the declared productive field
- Its actual complete exercises supply the field's real efficacy and actual cognitive subjects
- M apprehends every relevant fact, including the character and consequences of the acts under examination
- M's efficacy is genuinely guided by represented ends; cognition is not merely an idle accompaniment
- No external agent coerces M, supplies its perfection, or creates a need which its acts must repair
- Its standing nature includes an original orientation toward certain ends, including suffering or the frustration of other subjects' good
- Particular exercises are appropriately distinguished from the source's standing nature, using the same kind/particular distinction available to the affirmative account

M need not deceive constantly. It may supply real perception, valid reasoning, benevolent human subjects and effective correction. Those goods can figure as prerequisites, instruments or contrasts within a malicious purpose. Alternatively M may genuinely value some goods while also favoring intrinsically bad outcomes. These descriptions are bridge tests, not demonstrations that M is metaphysically possible or that it actually exists.

Distinguish three rival versions:

1. **Metaphysically pure evil:** no positive actuality, cognition, power or good in any respect. This is easily incoherent under a privation account and is not the strong target
2. **Morally exclusive malevolence:** positive existence and cognitive/agential capacities, but every ultimate practical end is malicious; derivative goods are only instrumental
3. **Mixed original agency:** some genuine benevolence and truthfulness, alongside at least one knowingly malicious or knowingly false authoritative exercise

An argument against the first does not establish an argument against the second. An argument establishing that a source has some genuine practical excellence may exclude the second while leaving the third. The third is particularly important for trust: even an often beneficent source need not be impeccably truthful in the particular communication on which a repair depends.

Stephen Law's published challenge asks for a reason why an omnipotent, omniscient good deity is epistemically preferable to an evil counterpart; the observed goods and evils create a proposed symmetry. That is a challenge to justification, not an assertion that an evil deity exists. Our mixed-source control is narrower: it need not defend perfect symmetry between every theodicy and reverse theodicy. [Law, “The evil-god challenge,” Religious Studies 46 (2010), 353–373, verified publisher abstract](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/religious-studies/article/abs/evilgod-challenge/E925DF20AF17F3C87B9449B25F649F27).

## 2 Why three tempting short arguments fail

### 2.1 Full Knowledge is not already practical integrity

M need not mistake pain for pleasure, falsehood for truth, or the victim's harm for the victim's benefit. Its choice can track the facts accurately because the bad outcome is what it intends. Calling M irrational in the instrumental sense would miss the challenge. It can select highly efficient means to its end.

There is a stronger practical-rationality claim: a fully informed and nondefective agent is properly moved by the objective reasons it recognizes. That is a plausible positive premise to defend. It is not a logical consequence of a set containing every true proposition. The source can know that an act is contrary to an objective practical reason and, under a motivational-externalist rival, still choose it. Our evaluation of that choice as defective does not itself prove that an unreceived source cannot have the defect.

Qāʿida fī al-maḥabba explicitly distinguishes theoretical cognition from cognition involved in action, then asserts that God's Knowledge of good and bad acts entails the corresponding love and hate. The source is therefore not reducing all cognition to efficient production. Its asserted linkage already includes the perfected relation between evaluative cognition and practical orientation. It is evidence for the intended theological account, not an independent formal proof that every conceivable knower must have that linkage. [Qāʿida fī al-maḥabba, final sections on active/receptive Knowledge and Will, hosted lines1429–1445](https://ar.wikisource.org/wiki/جامع_الرسائل/قاعدة_في_المحبة).

### 2.2 Nonneed is not absence of an intrinsic end

M's delight in suffering need not be a bodily appetite or a borrowed existential condition. It may be stipulated as a standing practical orientation whose particular satisfactions occur through M's own power. The affirmative source likewise allows renewed satisfaction in fitting acts without receiving its being from an external completer. One cannot admit the latter and reject the former solely because the satisfaction has an actual object or occurs through an act.

A contemporary argument against an evil deity says that such a being must create victims in order to achieve its goal and is therefore incomplete. That argument needs a distinction between a goal's involving a created object and the source's receiving its intrinsic perfection from an independent supplier. Without it, the same objection would threaten any good source that intends actual creaturely flourishing instead of merely contemplating its possibility. This is a local criticism of that need-based argument, not a verdict on every argument in its author's paper. [Alvaro, “The Evil God Challenge: Two Significant Asymmetries,” author-provided text, exposition of premises V–VI](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/346642969_ARTICLE_THE_EVIL_GOD_CHALLENGE_TWO_SIGNIFICANT_ASYMMETRIES).

Ibn Taymiyya's MF 8:145–147 expressly distinguishes externally supplied completion from actual satisfaction in one's own originated acts. That distinction does not morally vindicate M. It shows why a further evaluative premise is required. [MF 8:145–147, web lines1434–1444](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/22/806/مسألة-حسن-إرادة-الله-لخلق-الخلق-وهل-يخلق-لعلة-أو-لغير-علة).

### 2.3 Privation does not erase positive malicious agency

An actual harmful exercise uses real existence, understanding and power. A privation analysis may locate its badness in a due good's loss, an action's disordered relation to its target, or the lack of a fitting practical orientation. None makes the exercise nonexistent. A source of such an exercise need not be an entity composed entirely of evil-as-nonbeing.

Page and Baker-Hytch defend an intrinsic asymmetry through a privation/functional account of goodness. They carefully distinguish a thesis's metaphysical necessity from our fallible epistemic confidence in it. Their argument gives a serious reason to reject a wholly evil ultimate being. For the present mixed-source challenge, the further question is whether productive and cognitive adequacy exclude a specifically practical privation, rather than merely excluding total absence of positive actuality. [Page and Baker-Hytch, “Meeting the Evil God Challenge,” §§1.2–1.4](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/papq.12304).

MF 14:18–22 itself makes distinctions which block a crude shortcut. It discusses privative evils, then treats actual pain and its causes as real and bad in specified relations, while affirming a wise good within the divine account of creation. That affirmation is not a freestanding derivation of all-purpose benevolence from the proposition that pain is relationally bad. Its claims about how goods predominate in creation are not adopted here as independently measured empirical conclusions. [MF 14:18–22, web lines1091–1102](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/22/869/?idfrom=1433&idto=1435&start=0).

## 3 The strongest positive qiyās al-awlā argument

### 3.1 Recognize the practical excellence before assigning it to the source

Begin with a deliberately controlled comparison. Two agents have equally accurate understanding, equal available power and equal freedom from external coercion. Each understands that a certain avoidable injury would destroy another subject's knowledge or welfare, and no competing relevant reason justifies that injury. One respects the recognized reason against the injury; the other inflicts it simply because that very frustration pleases it. The first possesses a practical excellence which the second lacks: its agency is appropriately integrated with the value it correctly apprehends.

The comparison does not rank mere pleasant emotion above unpleasant emotion. It does not call every refusal to cause pain virtuous: surgery, protection, punishment and conflicts of goods require their own relevant reasons. Nor does it assume that all finite-agent duties apply unchanged to an original source. It isolates a proposed excellence in the relation between accurate evaluative understanding and exercise, after role differences and possible competing reasons are made explicit.

Why regard this as more than taste? The target loss is real for the affected subject; the agent understands that loss; the source of its choice has been separated from ignorance, coercion and a compensating good; and the preference's being effective does not reverse the target's condition. We can recognize the difference between intelligently respecting that reality and subordinating it to an arbitrary relish for its destruction. That gives a prima facie rational warrant for the practical comparison.

The controlled description is not evidence that we have identified an actual divine act lacking every possible justifying reason. It is a way of identifying the proposed practical norm and the shape of its violation. Application to an actual history would require evidence about the relevant facts and reasons.

This warrant is defeasible in application. A mistaken description of the recipient, role, consequences or omitted reason can overturn the particular judgment. A stable judgment across clarified cases supports a general practical principle; it does not turn our psychological confidence into an automatic proof of metaphysical necessity. The pure-excellence claim is what the next stages must assess.

### 3.2 Establish eligibility rather than copy a creaturely passion

A source need not suffer alongside a creature, depend on reciprocity, need social protection or obey an external superior in order to apprehend another's good and respond fittingly. Those are some finite conditions under which humans exercise concern; they are not obviously identical with concern's positive content. Recognition of another subject's condition and intelligent respect for genuine reasons is intelligible without each limitation.

This is the affirmative eligibility argument. It identifies the content retained when limitations are removed. It is stronger than “nothing has been shown to make it impossible,” but remains open to a positive incoherence objection. A rival can argue that the relevant norm is constitutive only of mutually vulnerable animals or members of a cooperative community. The proponent must then explain why knowingly originating and governing another cognitive subject does not eliminate every reason relating to that subject's good.

Eligibility must also concern this very source. Showing that some possible independent agent could exercise beneficence does not show that the actual g can do so. The strongest rival may propose that its malicious orientation belongs essentially to its identity. Extracting the value-relevant content from creaturely limitations makes a positive source-level eligibility case, but does not alone settle that de re dispute. The proponent must either defend the compatibility of the excellence with this source's actual nature, or independently argue that complete original sourcehood excludes an essential privation in that respect. Conversely, the rival's stipulation that malice is essential is not proof that such a source is metaphysically possible. Original giving and the eligibility argument assess that proposed essential nature; neither side may obtain the answer just by defining its preferred bearer.

Here an important bridge is explicit: a real good or harm to a subject provides a relevant practical reason to an informed agent whose action affects that subject, although its strength and implications depend on role and competing reasons. Mere epistemic access to the fact does not prove this cross-agent normative relevance. The positive rationale is that the subject's good is an actual feature of what the agent is choosing to produce or destroy, rather than an irrelevant external name. Accepting this reason does not entail that the source must maximize every creature's preference or satisfy incompatible goods together.

MF 6:137–140 insists that relative creaturely excellences cannot simply be projected unchanged. The text proposes contrasting two bearers and distinguishing an absolute excellence from a quality which is better in one respect and worse in another. Its role-specific examples show that the method itself requires this filtering. Thus the eligibility burden is Taymiyyan, not an external objection imposed on an unqualified copying argument. [MF 6:137–140, web lines1604–1619](https://islamweb.net/ar/library/content/22/591/مسألة-تفصيل-الإجمال-فيما-يجب-لله-من-صفات-الكمال).

### 3.3 Connect the eligible excellence to original giving

Now suppose an actual dependent subject genuinely exhibits this practical excellence, and g is its independently complete original giver. The recipient's achievement is not self-originated. Its real understanding, power and fitting integration are all within the productive target. The source-sensitive claim is that a complete original account of this pure excellence should not place its entire actual possession in a derivative recipient while leaving the eligible original giver wholly inferior in that same respect.

This is the substantive a fortiori warrant. Its rationale is not “g is bigger,” “g came first,” or “g is defined as morally good.” It concerns the origin of an independently recognized excellence. It also differs from saying that an efficient producer must share every effect property. Bodily dependency, recovery from ignorance and susceptibility to pain are not the pure positive respect being compared. MF 6:76–79 supplies the original-giver structure; MF 6:129–130 explicitly treats wise discriminating Will and mercy toward others as superior to purposeless, unprincipled efficacy. [MF 6, the two loci](https://quranislam.github.io/fatwa/ibn-taymiyyah/fatwa81.html).

An opponent can still grant the ranking and deny this origin principle. It can hold that an original source produces moral excellence without itself possessing it, just as the intrinsic impersonal rival contests cognitive original-giver priority. That denial is a real metaphysical alternative, not an inconsistency with efficient production alone. Conversely, the proponent has now supplied more than a label: the excellence, its eligibility, complete reception and the claimed respect-specific inversion have each been explained.

### 3.4 Extend possession to intrinsic practical completeness with care

If the preceding reasoning establishes that g possesses genuine benevolence, it defeats a source wholly devoid of such concern. It does not yet defeat a source that acts benevolently on some occasions and maliciously on others. The principle “the giver is not wholly without the given excellence” is weaker than “every eligible practical act is impeccable.”

The stronger Taymiyyan completion route says that eligible pure perfection belongs to the self-sufficient source intrinsically, without a condition that leaves it absent or requires another to confer it. Applied to practical agency, complete fitting responsiveness would then be a standing perfection governing the source's exercises, not one successful episode amid contrary defects. An exercise opposed to a decisive fitting reason would be incompatible with that perfection, even if it displayed immense cognitive and productive skill.

This is a genuine positive argument if its intrinsic-completeness premise is independently warranted. But the scope cannot be supplied by changing “some practical excellence” into “maximal practical adequacy.” The proponent must establish that the unreceived source is eligible for the whole practical excellence, that its determinations are internally sufficient for that standing perfection, and that no contrary exercise can occur without contradicting it. “Maximal” as merely comparative among the abilities currently described does not entail the complete absence of every moral defect.

There is a further modal step. Actual impeccable orientation, if established, is not automatically necessary impeccable orientation across every possible exercise. Essential intrinsic determination is needed at that scope. The same discipline used in the prior Knowledge argument therefore applies here: possession, comprehensive scope, intrinsic standing status and all-world preservation must not be merged.

## 4 Three levels of conclusion

| Level | What can be warranted | What it does not automatically establish |
|---|---|---|
| Natural evaluative recognition | In a clarified case, intelligent respect for a genuine undefeated good is better than gratuitous frustration of it | Every initial intuition is correct; all role differences are irrelevant; universal modal certainty |
| Objective normative status | The act's value or defect is grounded in the subject, action and circumstances, rather than made true by the source's preference or an observer's label | Every knower is motivated accordingly; the original source must instantiate every virtue |
| Metaphysical source exclusion | Under independently defended eligible-perfection, original-giver and intrinsic-completeness warrants, a contrary source exercise is impossible | Bare necessary existence, Knowledge or efficient sufficiency alone entail benevolence |

The levels can form a rational argument. The fact that the last is not delivered by logic alone does not make the first two arbitrary. Equally, calling the first “natural” does not settle the third. A claim of certainty about an intrinsically necessary truth and a justified but revisable belief about it are epistemically different.

This does not require treating moral norms as separate efficient creators. Three accounts must remain distinct. On an independent normative-realism account, true reasons constrain correct evaluation without producing the source's being. On a relational account, a subject's real nature and circumstances make an outcome beneficial or harmful; originating that subject does not make every injury beneficial simply by preference. On a source-nature account, normative authority is grounded in the source's own practical character. The third cannot authenticate the character by first assuming it is good. The first two do not by themselves establish the source's practical motivation. The report's comparison leaves that ultimate grounding issue open while asking whether a recognized norm is genuinely relevant to the source's exercise.

MF 8:434–436 offers especially helpful distinctions. An act may already possess a beneficial or harmful character knowable independently of a new command; a command may also establish a further normative status; and a command can have a testing purpose whose point is the response rather than completion of the commanded object. The text also separates an act's badness from liability to punishment without communicated law. Thus value, authority, motivation and accountability are not one undifferentiated property even within the source tradition. [MF 8:434–436](https://quranislam.github.io/fatwa/ibn-taymiyyah/fatwa129.html).

## 5 Reasons choice and contingency remain consistently typed

The world-bridge work establishes no premise that an original source's motivating reasons must be objectively good. It also distinguishes actual efficacy, sufficiency of source-plus-actual-exercise, and an invariant complete determinant. The present continuation keeps those distinctions.

M can have a sufficient determining exercise and a motive, such as delight in a particular harm. To reject that motive as unfitting is a normative assessment. To show that it cannot belong to an original source requires the practical-perfection argument. The source's failure to act for a good reason is not the same as its lacking any explanatory differentiator. Consequently Ṣafadiyya's rejection of arbitrary undifferentiated selection cannot by itself exclude M: a bad but determinate preference can still differentiate alternatives.

Nor does establishing practical impeccability immediately establish a uniquely necessitated world. Several acts may be permitted or comparably fitting. Conversely, if a full theory implies that one uniquely best act is required in every complete circumstance, the modal consequences must be addressed rather than hidden under “choice.” The present argument excludes an unfitting exercise only under the specified practical perfection; it does not settle every tie, ordering or eternal-act question.

## 6 False attestation is a particularly sharp practical test

The strongest local test does not ask whether the source maximizes everyone's happiness. It asks whether a source knowingly authorizes a false proposition as reliable truth-directed testimony in the very communicative role it undertakes.

There is an independently identifiable epistemic defect here. The utterance purports to provide a truth-linked reason for p while the source knows not-p. Its effectiveness at manipulation does not make it correct as testimony. A completely informed source can nevertheless choose that defective act under the malicious or mixed rival. Excluding that choice requires role-specific practical integrity; it does not follow from source omniscience or from a recipient's reasonable but fallible trust.

A command, question, test, withheld truth and assertion are different acts. MF 8:436's test-command category illustrates why an instruction need not assert that its surface object will actually be completed. This can explain some apparent mismatch without making the source a false testifier. But the category cannot be imposed merely to rescue an inconvenient assertion. The actual content and contextual evidence must warrant the interpretation; an unconditional false attestation cannot become a harmless test solely because the preferred source theory demands it.

The separately reviewed action-disclosure interface makes the distinction operational. Its fixed-view theorem requires an integrity-informed sender, an actual authenticated truthful context-bound message and a correct common root/menu/action contract. Under that contract the number of distinguishable messages constrains guaranteed safe action. A malicious sender can possess the same integrity information and expressive capacity while selecting the harmful recommendation. A mixed source may speak truth about the target but lie about which action channel is safe. The same distinction prevents an epistemic self-defeat shortcut. A malicious source need not make every derivative belief false or every correction unwarranted. It may sustain genuine knowledge for a bad larger end. To argue that M predictably destroys our warrant requires a further account of its actual goals and the resulting cognitive environment. It cannot be inferred from the word “malicious” alone, and actual local Knowledge is not already evidence of universal source truthfulness.

## 7 What would genuinely defeat the remaining rival

The strongest remaining rival grants objective norms, genuine derivative virtues, source cognition and purposeful efficacy. It denies that the origin of those virtues requires a necessarily impeccable original practical orientation. Its denial has two separable forms: it rejects original-giver priority for practical excellence, or accepts some source excellence while denying unrestricted intrinsic completion.

The affirmative route can rationally press it through the controlled comparison and the source-sensitive inversion: why should the wholly received agent genuinely respect a good which its independent original giver is eligible to apprehend and honor, while the giver is devoid of or defective in that same pure achievement? A reply invoking different overall power or unlimited manipulative success changes the respect of comparison. A reply invoking creaturely limitations must show that those limitations constitute the virtue, not merely accompany it. A reply invoking intrinsic malevolent essence contests the original-perfection doctrine rather than explaining why its values are themselves more fitting.

These are substantial argumentative pressures. They do not yet force every rival into contradiction with the accepted sourcehood premises. The precise remaining premise is:

**An independently complete original source of genuine reason-responsive practical excellence possesses that eligible excellence intrinsically and comprehensively, so that no actual or possible exercise can oppose a decisive objectively fitting reason within its scope.**

This premise contains distinguishable requirements of normative relevance, eligibility, original possession, comprehensive scope and essential governance. It is not a harmless synonym for efficient sufficiency. The continuation provides a positive rationale for each part and concrete rivals for the transitions. A source-neutral proof of the entire principle, or an independent demonstration that the malicious/mixed source is metaphysically realizable, remains unavailable.

## 8 Research consequence and stopping point

The continuation has reached a useful stopping point. The malicious-source challenge is no longer answered with need, ignorance, sheer absence or an equivocation on “pure evil.” The positive qiyās al-awlā argument is reconstructed through independently recognizable practical excellence and filtered source eligibility. Its possession-to-impeccability and actual-to-necessary scope obligations are now explicit. The actual source/choice account stays consistent with the world-bridge's distinction between standing nature and completed acts, and false authoritative attestation provides a particularly precise practical test.

Further progress would require a substantive defense of unrestricted original practical completeness, a stronger account of cross-agent normative reasons, or independent evidence of truthful source communication. More finite assignments to unconstrained virtue predicates would only repeat the same independence result. No canonical or Track T promotion is warranted by this continuation, and the accepted attribute-v1 remains immutable.
