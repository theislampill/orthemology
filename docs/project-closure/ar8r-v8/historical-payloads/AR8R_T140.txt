AR8R-T140 — Asymmetric orientation is not grounding identity
Theorem

An independently supplied agent/patient, producer/recipient, or manifestation-role asymmetry may define an oriented relation on a powers network. The existence of such an orientation does not by itself establish that the relation is metaphysical grounding.
Proof by profile twins

Take two target models with the same bearers, reciprocal powers, role labels, oriented causal relation, interventions, and outcomes. In model M_R, interpret the oriented relation literally as grounding. In model M_I, treat grounding discourse as an explanatory representation without a worldly grounding relation. Every declared powers and causal coordinate agrees; the grounding-realism target differs. Therefore grounding identity does not factor through the oriented powers profile alone. ∎
Result

reciprocity alone -> no intrinsic orientation;
independent asymmetry -> orientation possible;
orientation alone -> grounding identity not established.

A positive powers-to-grounding theorem still needs an independently motivated role identity, truth-linked discriminator, or correspondence theorem.
