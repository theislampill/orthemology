The positive argument explains the benefit and requirements of a successful ultimate answer. The direct rival accepts the local explanation while leaving ultimate existence primitive. The further rival constructions are developed proposals with unverified ultimate commitments. No consistency check is a metaphysical possibility proof, and no finite formal model supplies world warrant.

Whole actual origination is stronger than sufficient endpoint power. Genuine local intentional production retains the corresponding knowledge, will and power requirements. Neither point establishes a common ultimate bearer or closes the admitted R5 interface.
