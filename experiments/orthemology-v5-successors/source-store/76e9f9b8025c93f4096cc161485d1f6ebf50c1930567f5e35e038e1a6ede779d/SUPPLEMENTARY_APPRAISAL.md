# Supplementary rereading of printed pages 231 to 233

Reader-facing study record, 7 October 2026. The interpretations and reading claims below belong to the recorded study and its bounded rereads. This edition of the notes adds no source-reading or proof credit.

Direct image inspection confirms the positive argument's exact subject: a genuinely divine claimant must create, act, benefit and protect. The argument proposes partition, dominance or common subjection as the alternatives for a putative peer. The order and interrelation of the world then count against separated domains. Printed 232 expressly distinguishes conflict in action and bringing into existence from conflict in end and divinity. The scan reads الغاية والإلهية, as the independent reviewer reported; it should not be silently quoted as the different electronic العبادة والإلهية.

Printed 233 explicitly says complete overcoming entails unity because partnership is incompatible with that completeness. This is a substantive premise about full supremacy. It cannot be obtained merely from the weak existential statement that some necessary bearer exists. Nor should a model that omits full supremacy be presented as a countermodel to this full argument. Conversely, inferring complete supremacy for an already identified bearer requires its own warrant and cannot be credited as proved simply by adopting the source's divine predicate.

The following fly analogy attacks powerless alleged gods and their inability even collectively to create or recover what is taken. It directly addresses those alleged objects of worship. Extending the analogy into a universal impossibility claim about any pair of intrinsically capable originals depends on the preceding sovereignty and coordination argument, not merely the empirical incapacity of the example.

This three-page supplementary pass supplements the complete received-body readers. It is not a full second reading or an independent collation of every manuscript note. At the time of this record, the supplementary reader retained the position of an earlier appraisal dated 08:40, pending the larger philosophical assessment. That earlier appraisal is outside this finite selection; no view is reconstructed from the reference alone.
