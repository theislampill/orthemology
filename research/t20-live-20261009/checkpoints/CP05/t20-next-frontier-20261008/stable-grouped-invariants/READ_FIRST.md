# Read first

This sibling adds a finite-sample stability theorem to the earlier population count-invariant result.

- RESULT.md: quantitative support geometry, moment separation, finite rational-grid decoder and the O(M^(8C) log(1/delta)) group bound at fixed C,w.
- WEIGHT_ESTIMATION.md: separately budgeted accuracy-alpha guarantee for matched weights.
- NEGATIVE_CONTROLS.md: rare components, calibration collapse, missing common calibration, failed persistence, absolute-count ambiguity and computational scope.
- SOURCE_AUDIT.md and SOURCE_BINDINGS.json: inspected predecessors, unchanged source identities and evidence limits.
- exact_controls.py, EXACT_CONTROLS.md and results/: thirteen exact control families and a complete synthetic finite-grid witness. No sampled data or exhaustive large-grid search.
- REVIEW_STATUS.md: independent mathematical review and its final bindings.
- MANIFEST.json: author freeze.

The stronger promises are known C,M,w, a common static actual rate in [1/(2M),3/(2M)], held conditionally independent repeats, and independent groups. Absolute counts remain outside the target. Polynomial sampling is not practical tractability, polynomial bit-complexity, or physical/source validation.
