## 3. Three noninterchangeable source-relative levels

For a mechanism `m`, state `w`, and independently specified truth target `T`, use:

```text
Src(w): the relevant source premise is authentic in the declared Track-N theory;
Des(w): a personal source assigned the mechanism its function;
Aim_T(w): the assigned function is truth-directed toward T;
Fit(w): the fiṭrah is healthy in the relevant respect;
Op(w): the faculties operate according to that constitution;
Env(w): the environment is suitable for the declared design plan;
Acc_T(w): this episode is accurate relative to T;
Und(w): the episode's source-relative warrant remains undefeated.
```

Define three levels rather than one:

\[
NFunc_T(w):=Src\land Des\land Aim_T\land Fit\land Op\land Env,
\]

\[
NAcc_T(w):=NFunc_T(w)\land Acc_T(w),
\]

\[
NWarr_T(w):=NAcc_T(w)\land Und(w).
\]

`NFunc_T` is proper functional operation under the declared Track-N contract. `NAcc_T` is episode success. `NWarr_T` adds undefeated warrant custody. None migrates to neutral Track T without independent support for its source and bridge predicates.

## 4. FPF-1A — conditional Track-N closure

Within a theory that independently accepts the relevant predicates and these contracts:

\[
Src\land Des\land Aim_T\land Fit\land Op\land Env\models NFunc_T,
\]

\[
NFunc_T\land Acc_T\models NAcc_T,
\]

\[
NAcc_T\land Und\models NWarr_T.
\]

These are transparent contract consequences, not new general mathematics. Their value is to prevent three authority levels from being merged and to expose exact deletion burdens.

### Appropriate deletion controls

For `NFunc_T`, deleting any of `Src`, `Des`, `Aim_T`, `Fit`, `Op`, or `Env` defeats the declared conjunction. `Acc_T` is not a proper-function guard: a properly functioning mechanism can err in a difficult episode. `Und` is not an episode-accuracy guard: an accurate, properly functioning episode can still face a live defeater.

For `NAcc_T`, `Acc_T` is additionally required. For `NWarr_T`, `Und` is additionally required.

The minimality claim is contract-relative. It does not assert that every proper-function theory has this exact vocabulary.

## 5. FPF-1B — neutral profile cannot identify the Track-N realization

Let

\[
q(w)=(accuracy,reliability,corrigibility,coherence)
\]

omit source history, authorial intention, fiṭrah health, proper-operation provenance, environment classification, and defeaters. Then each of `NFunc_T`, `NAcc_T`, and `NWarr_T` can vary inside a fibre of `q`.

An explicit pair shares `q=(1,1,1,1)`:

- a Track-N-positive world satisfying every source predicate;
- an impersonal selected-effect or powers-based truth tracker with the same neutral performance profile but no personal designer or Track-N source relation.

Therefore none of the three source-relative predicates factors through `q`. No deterministic neutral-profile classifier can recover them exactly.

This is an application of the established profile/fibre factorization theorem family and receives zero general mathematical novelty credit.

## 6. FPF-1C — proper-function realization parity

A selected-effect theory may assign proper function by actual selection history. A dispositional theory may assign proper function by an intrinsic truth-disclosing power. Either can, under its own independently justified premises, match the truth-tracking neutral profile while withholding a personal designer.

Conversely, design by itself is insufficient: a malicious designer can assign deception as the installed function. Thus:

```text
selected-effect/dispositional proper function
DOES NOT ENTAIL
personal designer
```

and:

```text
design simpliciter
DOES NOT ENTAIL
truth-directed or praiseworthy proper function.
```

The source-relative fiṭrah account can supply a richer package **inside Track N** because its source predicates include Creator, intended truth-directed disposition, and restoration. That richer explanatory role remains conditional on source truth and does not break neutral realization parity by operational-profile evidence alone.
