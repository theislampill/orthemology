## 1. The representation restriction is warranted, with narrow novelty

Majmūʿ 16:305 expressly includes the cognitive-volitional representation itself among occurrences through the original's willing and power. The surrounding 303–305 sequence distinguishes prospective resolve, executive intent, apprehended particular content, and its intrinsic differentiation. Consequently a q-specific representation cannot be left unexplained and then credited with an ultimate explanation of that same particular determination.

The general anti-choice-encoding principle was already explicit in the prior particular-determination report, including its warning about a future plan naming act and occasion. It is not newly discovered here. The actual increment is application of the adjacent representation-origination clause to the intermediate representation's own explanatory status and to a specified circularity objection.

The reciprocal wording does not establish the alleged same-token productive cycle. The source does not identify the willing involved in the representation's occurrence with the executive willing being guided, or identify practical guidance with the requisite efficient-production edge. The small countervaluation defeats that displayed entailment only. It is neither a complete model of the source nor proof that all richer circularity objections fail. Successive willing, an adequate particular account, and a first temporal stage are also different questions. No perpetual succession or intrinsic-location label alone answers the content question.

## 3. The wall improves original-scope attribution without conflating agents

Qurʾān 18:82 expressly attributes to the Lord the willing that the two beneficiaries reach maturity and recover their treasure. The closing denial of independent initiative supports directed action without identifying the derivative deed with a divine intrinsic act-token.

## 4. The strongest alternative-means objection remains properly scoped

A genuinely available, equally effective, less costly intervention under the same relevant conditions would challenge an all-things-considered justification, even without changing the whole history. The revised §7 explicitly admits that this possibility has not been excluded. The displayed D-over-U reason and the attributed intentional relation nonetheless survive. A reason favoring D over U is not an exhaustive ranking of every local option.

Similarly, changing the king, treasure arrangement, or larger history does not by itself refute the narrower means relation. Such alternatives matter to stronger explanatory claims which these reports do not establish. Requiring their exclusion before allowing any particular local explanation would erase the warranted positive scope. Conversely, local intelligibility alone does not prove complete wisdom or ultimate selection.
