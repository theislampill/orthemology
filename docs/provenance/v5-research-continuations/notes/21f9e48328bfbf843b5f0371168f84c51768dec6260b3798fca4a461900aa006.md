# What the new repair theorems contribute to the source/intentionality frontier

Status: isolated research synthesis, 2026-09-30. Current canonical B0–B16, R5, meniscus and source statuses are unchanged. The mathematical results have ordinary proofs and exact finite checks, with selected general-geometric and score/precision results now kernel-checked as specified in FORMALIZATION_SCOPE.md; the philosophical interpretation below does not inherit that proof credit or any metaphysical-possibility certificate.

## 1. Result first: real normative restriction, still no root-local thought

The new work strengthens the previous accuracy bridge. It no longer merely says that distinct scores can recommend incompatible repairs. It characterizes which scoring geometries can share arbitrarily fine corrective access:

- For positive-definite quadratic scores on a finite truth polytope, common coherent repair near an interior point requires and is locally guaranteed by agreement of the metrics' tangent projection on the tested perturbation direction
- For positive diagonal scores on the categorical simplex, one transverse direction already forces proportional weight vectors
- For smooth coordinate-additive proper Bregman scores on three or more categorical outcomes, common local repair at every interior point forces positive-scale/affine equivalence of the generators
- In a general finite truth hull, constrained affine-dependency components of tangent rank at least two fix one positive affine generator scale per component on its attained coordinate ranges; free singleton coordinates remain unrestricted and rank-one blocks require separate analysis. This classification has an independently reviewed ordinary proof and a kernel-checked general finite-hull necessity bridge
- In the same fixed additive categorical regime, even universal access to strict common infinitesimal improvement suffices for that equivalence; a coherent endpoint need not be available as an initial premise. See NUDGE_COMPARISON.md
- Under an independently justified transitive, fixed-problem coordinate symmetry, the quadratic specialization fixes equal weights
- An accepted binary repair contains norm-parameter information whose required precision increases as the incoherence shrinks. Statistical/control interfaces can therefore turn successful correction into quantitative evidence-resource constraints

These are substantive restrictions on jointly admissible normative models. Their premises do not explicitly assert the resulting metric equivalence. They can therefore supply a genuine missing operational premise to M10/P15 and to controlled restoration. The universal standard-blind correctability demand is stronger than ordinary coherence, objective correctness, or the claim that every actual norm permits some repair. It must be defended rather than silently read into those notions.

The source conclusion is more limited. None of these theorems identifies the selected norm as an occurrent original thought, determines its constitutive host, or identifies that host with the fundamental ground. Norm compatibility, norm access, norm authority, original representation and ultimate bearer identity are different relations.

## 2. What changed relative to the prior return

The predecessor proved that each positive weighted quadratic score has the same coherent undominated region, while a particular two-score pair lacks a common coherent repair. The new local theorem says exactly when such obstructions are unavoidable near every interior coherent point, gives a finite-epsilon separation bound, and extends the classification beyond the quadratic family.

There is a noteworthy sharp exception: on two categorical outcomes, quadratic and binary-entropy/log scores share arithmetic coherification for every incoherent interior forecast, although their generators are not proportional. Three outcomes are sufficient for the additive global rigidity theorem. For the same two scores on three outcomes, an explicit rational forecast has no common repair, certified by polynomial inequalities rather than numerical optimization.

The separate ANCHORED_FAMILY_COMMON_REPAIR.md corollary also closes a genuine multi-standard quantifier gap: if every member of an arbitrary family has universal joint correctability with one fixed anchor, the block theorem forces exact structural compatibility with that anchor, and one anchor projection repairs the whole family simultaneously. This is not an inference from arbitrary pairwise set intersections, and it requires the rank/range/global-context hypotheses.

The theorem does not by itself select the actual score. Each singleton generator class satisfies the common-repair requirement. In a general hull, even the compatible class can leave different positive scales between dependency blocks and arbitrary free-coordinate scores. Those differences can change rankings of coherent alternatives while preserving the same local corrective access. Thus compatibility need not identify all normative comparisons. What it excludes is treating an incompatible plurality of unresolved standards as though one standard-blind correction were always guaranteed. A further independently warranted symmetry or substantive score axiom can narrow the remaining class. This distinction keeps the result from overstating its norm-fixing force.

## 3. Norm access is a causal requirement, not yet a mental realization

For the binary family, let the underlying standard be alpha and the input forecast b=(x,y). Its weighted-quadratic coherent projection is

    t=alpha x+(1−alpha)(1−y), c=(t,1−t).

This is a complete constructive repair law. At b=(1/2+e,1/2), observing a valid repair t identifies alpha within the local binary norm-readout bound. A valid common architecture must consequently carry enough alpha-dependence, whether in observations, memory, a configured parameter, or direct causal coupling. If its complete available input law and private randomness are identical under two disjoint repair standards, no fixed controller can meet both.

An explicit algebraic implementation uses two weighted input channels and a summing node. One channel carries x with gain alpha; the other carries 1−y with gain 1−alpha. The output t is their sum. A passive-network idealization could encode these gains as conductance ratios. This is an implementation model, not a claim that real hardware has exact infinite precision or that physical computation resolves consciousness. It demonstrates what the mathematics actually requires: the parameter enters the mechanism. It does not require a stored sentence saying which norm is correct, a deliberate act of norm selection, or the fundamental source's original thought about alpha.

There are two distinct failures to avoid:

1. If alpha is merely set to whichever value makes the desired output successful after the fact, the target contract has changed. That is not correction under the earlier unresolved standard class
2. If alpha is a fixed causally effective parameter, its presence explains the output but does not by itself confer normative authority on alpha. Causal implementation of a norm and grounding of its authority are separate questions

The strongest R5 already grants one carrier with causally aligned truth/target/norm/selection/efficacy roles. Giving that carrier the exact compatible repair law strengthens its functional credentials. It does not contradict the rival unless an independently defended constitutive theory rules out an impersonal realization of the newly added structure.

## 4. Endogenous norm-fixing is a real research avenue, not a synonym for self-installation

A more serious impersonal rival need not rest entirely on an unexplained truth register. Organizational theories try to ground biological functions in differentiated, mutually maintained constraints: a trait contributes to a system's maintenance while the organization produces or maintains the trait. That is a substantive proposal for non-arbitrary local functional standards. The authors also distinguish preserving a particular regime from the absolute indispensability of every functional trait. Whether it supplies epistemic or moral authority is a further issue. [Mossio, Saborido and Moreno 2009, §§4–6](https://www.ias-research.net/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/Mossio_25592036.pdf)

A quantitative semantic-information framework measures the causal contribution of system–environment information to a stipulated viability function under correlation-scrambling interventions. Its authors explicitly retain the analyst's choice of viability function, alongside system boundary, timescale and initial law; they discuss optimization of some choices rather than claim these inputs have vanished. Their negative-entropy proposal also does not uniquely track a particular organism's identity. This supports intervention-defined information relevance, not an automatic theorem of original intentionality or ultimate norm authority. [Kolchinsky and Wolpert 2018, §§1.2, 4 and 6](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6227811/)

These proposals change the evidential burden. A positive intellectual-source argument should show why such internally maintained, nonaccidental corrective organization remains inadequate under the intended notion of representation or authority. Merely observing that the models contain mathematics is insufficient; so is calling every selected viability function arbitrary without examining how biological identity constrains it.

Conversely, an endogenous account cannot settle every normative question by choosing a unique attractor. For any proposed target r, the contraction alpha_(t+1)=(alpha_t+r)/2 has one globally attracting fixed point r. Stability and uniqueness therefore do not determine which r is warranted. If the updating law is itself justified by another criterion, that criterion is part of the explanation and must remain visible.

Time-indexed mutual maintenance also differs from complete same-token self-grounding. The dynamics x_(t+1)=y_t and y_(t+1)=x_t, supported by external conditions, can produce cross-time closure without an entity wholly producing or authorizing itself in the same token and respect. A B0-style prohibition must not exclude ordinary causal feedback by changing its temporal or explanatory typing.

## 5. A conditional limit on exporting viability to a necessary root

Suppose an alleged norm ranks actions solely by the probability that its bearer continues to exist, and every admitted intervention preserves that bearer's existence with probability one. Then all actions have the same maximal value. Such a criterion cannot distinguish truthful from false representation, fitting from unfitting selection, or wisdom from unwisdom at that bearer.

This elementary result becomes relevant if a proposed ascent identifies a necessarily existing ground and then tries to derive its substantive norms solely from maintaining its own existence. Assuming the relevant intervention possibilities lie within the necessity claim's modal scope, bare survival is constant. A nontrivial criterion must instead concern a richer organization, flourishing, fitting relation, or effects on dependent beings. That extra content is the new load-bearing premise.

This is NOT a refutation of the cited semantic-information theory: its negative-entropy viability proxy is not bare modal existence, and the authors state its scope. Nor is it a criticism of theological accounts that independently ground goodness in a necessary nature. It prevents an equivocation between survival-based functional normativity, low entropy, essential identity and unrestricted Wisdom.

## 6. Original intentionality and ultimate source remain different origins

An original intentional state can be semantically nonderivative while its bearer is causally or metaphysically dependent. Here 'original' means that the state does not get all of its aboutness by another subject interpreting it. It does not automatically mean that the state or its subject is uncaused, ungrounded, or necessary. Searle's account explicitly combines intrinsic intentionality with a biological realization picture, so a causal-to-semantic transfer principle cannot simply be treated as a shared premise. [Searle 1984, opening argument and biological realization discussion](https://www.newdualism.org/papers/J.Searle/Searle-Synthese-1984.pdf)

Consequently the enriched R5 can grant actual local thinkers, genuine understanding and original content while denying that the fundamental powers bearer is the subject of those thoughts. The rival owes a defensible realization/grounding theory; the repair theorems do not discharge that debt. The positive source account likewise owes a relation making the relevant content a state of the ground itself rather than merely a state causally supported by it.

The strongest inherited positive hosting conditional remains valid:

- One and the same representational particular exists throughout the intended modal range
- It essentially has one constitutive intellectual host
- Hosting entails the host's existence

Then that host exists throughout the range. The new norm-fixing results do not produce the first premise or identify this host with the already proposed ground. A necessary norm, a proposition type, an everywhere available repair law, a universally valid theorem, and one necessary representational particular are not interchangeable objects.

Minimal local subjecthood should still be credited when genuinely established. If original understanding is constitutively a subject's understanding, one cannot concede that very understanding while denying every subject by a detached Boolean flag. But minimal subjecthood does not automatically confer practical agency, unrestricted knowledge, stable Wisdom, source authenticity or revealed identity.

## 8. The exact remaining bridge choices

A source argument aiming to move beyond the new mathematics needs one of the following, with its own warrant:

1. A constitutive-semantic argument: the selected objective normative/content structure is intrinsically representational at the proposed ground, and that kind of representation requires an intellectual host
2. A bearer-identity argument: the independently established original representational host is the same entity as the necessary or ultimate ground, rather than a dependent subject
3. A genuinely discriminating intervention: a common target-blind bearer-/episode-local manipulation of uptake or ownership, with independently warranted implementation maps for the personal and impersonal candidates
4. An explicit abductive comparison: an account showing why intellectual hosting, normative integration and efficacious selection explain the actual facts better than essentialist impersonal structure plus local minds. This must compare explanatory laws and costs rather than count predicates
5. A source-conditional argument with authenticated content, referent identity and recipient warrant, explicitly retaining its source-relative scope

The current CT owner already demands the third kind of complete common experiment. Norm-precision interventions manipulate available information about a score; they are not automatically interventions on original understanding or because-of-fittingness. Confusing these interventions would recreate the same bridge under a more sophisticated measurement protocol.

## 9. Recommended research disposition

The immediate positive contribution is a rigorous compatibility/precision interface that the dynamic restoration lane can use. It genuinely relates mathematical correctness, admissible standards, achievable correction, information access and path constraints. It narrows M10 and provides contentful premise tests rather than another unconstrained label twin.

The main philosophical research target remains the constitutive locus of original representation and normative authority. The new mathematics makes the demanded normative structure less arbitrary and its access requirements sharper. It does not establish that this structure must be originally represented by a necessary intellect. The fair next comparison is therefore between explicitly developed source architectures, not between a proven mathematical law and a bare `Personal=false` assignment.
