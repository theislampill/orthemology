# Complete-profile local dominance: an exclusion route

## Scope and interpretation

This is an elementary conditional lemma, not a newly established metaphysical principle. Its value is the dependency structure: excluding a defect need not first construct a single globally perfected alternative character. The essential work lies in independently warranting the lemma's substantive premises. The new organisation does not show that those premises are logically weaker or independently easier to warrant than the earlier comprehensive-priority claim: R_G retains complete-profile eligibility, and N quantifies over every eligible complete alternative.

Fix a bearer s and an assurance context, including the original source, recipient, root, interval, version, actual safety facts and the nonfactive communicative role. A complete profile specifies the source's relevant choices and all the other pure-perfection respects affected by those choices, including effects on other contexts. Let K be the profiles genuinely eligible for this same bearer with its full essential profile preserved. The current actual profile a is in K.

Membership of K is a metaphysical eligibility claim. The mere ability to form an accurate sentence does not establish it. Neither does a profile for a different, better source belong to K merely because it has similar productive powers.

Let x ≺ y mean: y is strictly better than x in the relevant pure-perfection comparison of complete profiles. The order cannot overlook a loss elsewhere and call a local change nonspoiling. One sufficient interpretation is a Pareto order over independently justified pure-perfection respects: none is reduced, and at least one improves. A satisfaction in betrayal is not automatically such a perfection merely because the source wants it. Conversely, a property cannot be omitted from the comparison just because it makes a preferred argument difficult; its status must be argued.

Let Lie(x,c) mean that in context c the source deliberately asserts content it takes to be false, presenting that content in the genuine assurance role. In the fixed test the source knows the underlying facts, so this lie is also false. Nonlying alone does not imply accurate assertion when relevant knowledge is absent.

A repaired profile y need not assert the same proposition as x. If x falsely asserts that the fixed unsafe root is safe, y may accurately assert that it is unsafe. The response changes; the safety fact and the assertoric role do not. Holding the false proposition fixed while making that very assertion true is not a permissible repair.

## Generic and stronger repair premises

**Eligible strict repair R_G.** For every eligible profile x and every in-scope context c, if Lie(x,c), there is an eligible complete profile y in K which repairs that particular lie while preserving the assurance role and satisfies x ≺ y in the independently justified complete-profile comparison.

**Complete-profile nonspoiling repair R_P.** In addition, y repairs fidelity without reducing any other relevant pure-perfection respect anywhere in the complete profile. If ≺ recognises this Pareto improvement, R_P entails R_G. The generic lemma only requires R_G; the preferred nonspoiling application requires the stronger R_P. A strict overall normative improvement with an outweighed loss could support R_G without supporting R_P.

**Actual practical nondomination N.** There is no eligible complete profile y in K with a ≺ y.

N is a proposed consequence of original practical priority. It is not a definition of 'original giver', and it is not established merely by having an actual original giver. A direct source-priority account may defend a stronger exclusion without relying on a same-bearer counterpart: it can challenge the proposed defective essence itself rather than grant that essence and search its alternatives. This lemma is an alternative comparison route; it does not make the counterpart route mandatory.

## Exclusion lemma

If a ∈ K, R_G and N hold, a contains no in-scope lie.

Proof. Suppose Lie(a,c) at an in-scope c. R_G supplies y ∈ K with a ≺ y. N excludes just such a y. Therefore there is no such c.

The proof does not require a total order, a uniquely best profile, a finite context set, a greatest world, an algorithm reaching a maximum, or the prior construction of one profile repairing every context simultaneously. It uses one improved complete profile for a hypothetical offending context. It does not establish that a nondominated profile exists; here a and N are premises.

R_G may be weakened for this argument to repair only the actual profile a, provided the resulting premise is independently warranted and does not simply omit its potentially bad contexts. The general formulation is useful for the essential scope below.

## Essential scope

Let W_s be the admissible same-bearer possibilities and a_w the source's actual exercise/profile at w. If, for every w ∈ W_s, the appropriately fixed K_w contains a_w and satisfies R_G,w and N_w over the intended assurance domain, then no a_w contains an in-scope lie. Thus the source is essentially nonlying on that domain.

Nothing requires the same y to witness repairs at different w or c. But actual-world nondomination cannot be promoted to all w, and local unoverriddenness cannot be promoted to every authoritative assertion. Essential T0 over a larger domain needs R_G and N, or another source-priority warrant, over that larger domain. The fixed no-overriding-reason case does not establish that all imaginable false assertions fall in its domain.

Relevant source knowledge remains a separate condition for moving from nonlying to the truth of an asserted proposition. Actual token attribution, content and context remain separate from the metaphysical exclusion. Recipient warrant D2 is not created by the truth of the conditional lemma or by source authenticity alone.

## Countermodel 1: local eligibility plus nondomination is insufficient

Use two independently fixed assurance contexts, each with a known unsafe root and the same nonfactive role. Let 1 denote the accurate response 'unsafe' and 0 the deliberate false response 'safe'. Every profile retains genuine intentional beneficence in a third, fixed episode.

Let K = {(1,0),(0,1)}. Give the two fidelity coordinates their ordinary componentwise pure-perfection order: y strictly improves x only if no coordinate falls and at least one rises. Each context has a same-bearer eligible truthful response. Each profile has genuine fidelity in one context and lies in the other. Neither profile dominates the other, so either may satisfy N. But neither lie has an eligible nonspoiling repair: the truthful alternative at that coordinate loses fidelity at the other.

The imagined coupling 'exactly one of the two must be false' is a formal constraint, not an independently established possible essence. The interpretation therefore disproves a proposed inference from local eligibility and nondomination to nonlying; it does not prove an essentially malicious original source metaphysically possible. A full original-priority principle might rule out that coupling. If so, that additional result must supply the repair or direct-defect-exclusion warrant.

The weaker grant of productive capacity plainly also fails: a machine or agent can physically form both responses even if its complete eligible profiles have the displayed coupling. No formational ability identifies K.

## Countermodel 2: repair without nondomination is insufficient

Let K contain all four binary profiles. Every lying coordinate can be repaired without spoiling another. Choose actual a=(1,0). The agent knowingly gives one true and one false assurance. It has source ownership, role knowledge, actual benefit elsewhere and the relevant communicative capacity, but a is dominated by (1,1). The failure is exactly N.

Source-origin, productive capacity, institutional authorship and knowledge of the normative balance can all be added as true predicates to this mathematical interpretation. No declared structural axiom conflicts with them. This is an inference-independence result for those interpretations, not a model of a fully proved metaphysics. In particular, the full affirmative source-priority law is not among the satisfied predicates.

## Countermodel 3: repair does not establish a realised maximum

For countably many contexts let K contain precisely the profiles which are truthful at finitely many contexts, and false at all others. Any single lying coordinate can be changed to truthful while remaining in K and preserving every already truthful coordinate. Therefore the nonspoiling repair condition R_P holds, and it supplies R_G for this componentwise order. Every profile is still dominated by such a repair. There is no nondominated profile and no all-truthful profile.

This mathematical example shows why the exclusion lemma is not an existence theorem. A demand for one joint character can require a closure/compactness condition if it is approached constructively. The exclusion proof instead consumes actual nondomination. The infinite example supplies no reason to believe that a source's real possible-character space has this structure.

## No unique world follows

Take two distinct styles of faithful response or two distinct permissible histories, neither a pure-perfection loss relative to the other. Allow repair of false assertions within either style/history and compare only profiles whose additional respects permit the comparison. Both fully truthful profiles can be nondominated and incomparable. Nonlying can therefore constrain source character without selecting a unique truthful sentence, mandating communication, or choosing one entire world.

## What original-giver priority still owes

The source argument is not defeated by calling it normative or by demanding a measurement of every eligible profile. Direct rational recognition could warrant N or a stronger direct principle. But its content must be source-wide practical superiority in the relevant respect, not merely that the source is productive, underived or knowledgeable. To invoke R_G one must recognise an eligible improvement of the complete exercise, not just a locally better description that neglects the rest of its effects or this bearer's essence. To use R_P specifically, no other relevant pure respect may be reduced.

These are substantive alternative burdens. An advocate need not prove the whole character space, supply an algorithm, or construct a superior source. The defender must warrant enough of the exact premise being used. The lemma isolates the repair and nondomination claims for assessment; it does not establish them from the facts already admitted by Eighth or demonstrate a weaker metaphysical burden.

## A sharper residual fork

Given a candidate lie, the conjunction of N and R_G is impossible. Therefore a defender of the candidate must reject actual nondomination or identify a failure of the proposed complete-profile repair. The latter can be a same-bearer incompatibility or a lost pure perfection outside the local scene. Merely pointing out that truthful communication is relational does not identify either failure. Conversely, merely ruling out an undefeated justification for the lie does not establish that every other pure respect can be preserved.

For example, an abstract choice can trade fidelity against a genuine but lesser benefit. If the reason for fidelity decisively outweighs the benefit, the lie still lacks an undefeated justification; nonetheless the accurate option need not Pareto-improve every listed respect. An independently warranted strict overall comparison could support the generic exclusion lemma here. The stricter nonspoiling version R_P requires its stronger premise and cannot claim it from the no-overriding-reason stipulation alone.

The comparative relation must concern the source's practical perfection, not simply the amount of benefit present in a world. Otherwise every omitted optional creation could be labelled a source defect, silently importing an obligation to create a uniquely best or ever larger world. Neither the lemma nor the source text establishes that obligation.
