# Original efficacy, nonabsorption, and the strongest coalescent rival

Independent philosophical appraisal, 7 October 2026 UTC. This is a new source-and-argument assessment. It neither certifies research closure nor constructs another model. Earlier reports, sources and archives are unchanged.

## 1. Judgement first

The affirmative argument has substantial positive warrant. Complete original productive agency is plausibly more than an intrinsically possessed command whose desired result happens to occur: it is an agent's actually adequate, unborrowed exercise of efficacy. Ibn Taymiyyah explicitly argues for that stronger conception. He treats a real partner as contributing something, rejects the absorption of unchanged productive work, and connects externally completed efficacy to a lack of the independence appropriate to an original lord. This deserves assessment as an argument, not dismissal as an unexplained “source-mode” label.

The strongest current rival nevertheless reaches the disputed inference. Its two agents have the represented full-Q constructors, primitive unreceived cognition, peer-independent reasons for their own full-Q activity, and one genuinely joint production of the same source-neutral Q. Neither agent claims to be the sole entire original provider of that joint occurrence. It does **not** concede that one agent receives its intrinsic power, cognition, willing or own-activity end from the other. It concedes actual relational co-production. A further argument is needed to identify that relation as an externally supplied intrinsic or constitutive perfection.

My principal conclusions are:

1. **Nonabsorption is a serious causal principle, not a logical consequence of counting agents.** Majmu 20:175 asserts it for unchanged agency and supplies an intelligible difference-making rationale. The trace reduction usefully separates its sufficient conditions. The retained sources do not independently establish every one of those conditions for primitive original co-production, especially co-preservation.
2. **Actual efficacy is a perfection, but this does not by itself establish that exclusive actual authorship of every chosen effect is an intrinsic perfection.** The source argues for the transfer; the refined rival disputes its application without denying all actual agency or taking a peer-valued end.
3. **The source's original-explanation requirements exclude several inadequate rivals without circularity.** Mere correlation, nonproductive foreknowledge, an unexplained contingent mixing, donated intrinsic powers, and an external mechanism passed off as its user's own original efficacy do not suffice. But an explicitly productive joint ground is not excluded by those requirements alone. Making individual whole-provider coverage an antecedent would decide the target question by assumption.
4. **The full metaphysical plural-original alternative remains unestablished.** A source-neutral event calculus and typed nonreceipt do not establish that two necessary, maximally perfect bearers could have this productive nature. Conversely, their incomplete metaphysical warrant is not a demonstration of impossibility.
5. **A defeasible preference for one actual common original source remains reasonable, but the refined rival narrows its comparative advantage.** I retain that preference on the positive conception of complete original provision, not on a failed-model count. I withhold deductive exclusion of the strongest rival. Confidence in ruling it out should decrease relative to an assessment based on its former missing intention or cognition semantics; confidence in what the source actually argues remains high.

The decisive question can now be put without a slogan: **Why must a primitive original agent's actual contribution either be wholly and locally manifested in a distinct created effect, or be a deficiency in the agent itself, when its intrinsic act, standing power and independently valued end do not require the peer?** The inspected texts strongly endorse an answer; they do not yet remove every substantive premise from that answer.

## 3. The strongest affirmative argument

### 3.1 Original agency must do explanatory work

An original agent cannot be merely an uncaused occupant of a causal diagram. Its life, understanding, power and willing must belong intrinsically to it; its activity must genuinely explain the effect. A maker who supplies only one enabling condition while borrowing the rest has not thereby explained the whole concrete product. The Dar maker-preserver discussion is especially useful: constantly moving something is not equivalent to producing its substance and all its attributes, and merely being a desired exemplar is not efficient production at all. [S3, S7]

This establishes a positive standard of explanatory adequacy. It prevents the rival from answering a question about original production with bare co-occurrence, a correct forecast, or a condition on an otherwise independently existing world. It also shows why preserving the effect belongs to the scope of the inquiry. An enduring thing's continued actuality is not simply free after its first appearance.

### 3.2 A genuinely productive addition should have an effect

Majmu's reasoning is that an unchanged productive agent already has its effect. Add another genuinely acting equal contributor, without changing the first, and the combined product should be more or greater. Otherwise the additional contributor has been treated as equivalent to its absence. The examples allow a like result from one human agent only with a corresponding change in that person's willing, movement, instruments or other operative state. [S1, L908]

The strongest generalization is not conservation of a quantity of divine effort. It is **realization of genuine productive work**: if B's actual activity really contributes while A's productive exercise remains intact, something about complete created actuality should manifest B's contribution. A hidden cancellation would itself require real causal conditions and occurrences; a coarse visible endpoint must not erase them. Minhaj's distinction between each intrinsic act and each agent's effect *in* the one external product supports this contribution-sensitive ontology. [S4, V03L0268, 0272]

This is a substantial reason to distrust a model whose only reason for declaring B productive is membership in a rule's support set. Programming an “actual cause” label does not make a contribution real. The rival owes an account of what B contributes, not just why the evaluator prints B.

### 3.3 Complete original exercise cannot borrow the completion that makes it complete

If A's exercised power and decisive willing are fully adequate to produce the entire q, then q is originally supplied through A. If an independent B supplies an indispensable original requisite of that very production, A's exercise was not complete in that original respect. Describing the arrangement as voluntary or harmonious does not supply the missing efficacy. Majmu makes this transition explicit; it is not merely arguing that q exists whenever A wills q. [S1, L912–915]

The source then presses beyond outcome dependence. An original agent's very actual efficacy is a perfection; if that efficacy is completed from outside, its original adequacy is not intrinsic. A being whose constitutive perfection depends on another is not self-sufficient in the intended sense. The anti-circle argument is powerful where the capacities or active completion needed to give anything are themselves supplied reciprocally. [S3, 170–183]

Optional acts do not escape this concern simply by being optional. If an undertaken exercise is defective of original agency, choosing it does not transform deficiency into perfection. A creator may choose different effects without choosing a borrowed source of its ability to create them.

### 3.4 Intact standing powers alone do not answer the objection

Minhaj 1 and Dar 9 expressly address agents each capable when alone but whose operation is conditional on the other's allowing it. The ability to act while another refrains is weaker than unimpeded original sovereignty. This is a substantive, plausible distinction: retaining a dormant tool is not the same as possessing independent efficacy here and now. [S2; S8, L720–750]

For the strengthened rival, the proper question is therefore whether A's original efficacy is genuinely operative in the joint episode, or whether A has only an intrinsic full-Q command while the joint relation supplies the efficacy of that command. Mere command identity cannot settle that question.

### 3.5 The remaining affirmative steps

Where partners divide a genuine pure power, concentration of the power in one bearer plausibly improves independent productive adequacy. The necessary original should not possess an arbitrary privation of a realizable pure perfection. This gives the Majmu concentration argument a real rationale, although the move from a possible concentrated profile to each existing bearer's eligibility remains additional. [S1, L916–921; S3, 182–183]

Where independently completed effects supposedly coalesce, the actual coalescence is contingent and needs an efficient explanation. If one source modifies the other's completely determined product without its will, the other's efficacy is defeated; if with its will, the arrangement returns to the cooperation question. The resulting exclusion of joint original ownership, if warranted, permits actual connectedness to propagate a common original source across the field. [S5; S3, 177–181]

This is the full affirmative structure. The early steps concerning genuine agency, intrinsic resources and complete explanation support the later classifications. The latter are not all analytic restatements of the former.

## 4. The strongest rival and what it actually concedes

### 4.1 Its fair formulation

Take the current proposal in its strongest stated form. A and B are proposed primitive original bearers. Each has a full-Q constructor and intrinsically selects its own fitting full-Q activity. Neither takes the other's participation as a valued constituent of its own end. Each has standing unreceived cognition, including knowledge of the other's intrinsic policy. One direct law realizes q through both actual roots; no emitted created input, hidden saturator or law-making third original is posited. When B is suppressed in the diagnostic comparison, A's same command, source-neutral Q and own end remain satisfied. The rule can be uniformly named across cases.

These are meaningful interpreted features at the model's stated scope. The actual support changes from A-and-B to A. Whole-effect causal nonduplication holds because neither is attributed sole entire provision in the joint case. The candidate does not meet whole-provider coverage there, and does not claim to.

### 4.2 Three dependencies must not be collapsed

1. **Receipt:** B efficiently supplies an intrinsic capacity, cognition or act that A needs to be the relevant agent.
2. **Dependence of the actual productive configuration:** the actual joint relation includes B, so that very joint configuration would not be present without B.
3. **Dependence of A's source-neutral success:** A cannot realize Q or its own end without B.

The strengthened candidate denies 1 and 3 while affirming 2. It also denies that affirming 2 automatically creates 1. Its own intrinsic act can remain real and fulfilled under a different external realization. “A needs B to be co-producing with B” is true but does not yet show a deficient power over Q.

Thus it is inaccurate to report that the rival has conceded received intrinsic perfection. What it concedes is that A is not the exhaustive actual original provider of joint q. To infer deficiency from that concession requires the actual-efficacy norm under examination.

### 4.3 Why the source's noninterference argument does not immediately finish this case

The source's strongest objection is that A cannot exercise *sole entire* production while B genuinely contributes. That is correct. The rival replies that Q and A's own activity are not prevented: only exclusive provenance is absent. If the requested target is “q solely from A while B is also an irreducible original producer of q,” the target is contradictory by description. Failure to realize that description is not independently a defect in power over a source-neutral q.

The reply is substantial, but not conclusive. A source defender can say that sole complete original provision is precisely the relevant pure perfection, not an optional competitive relation. That claim must be defended as a perfection of the agent's exercise, rather than read into every use of “complete.” The revised candidate has placed that normative and explanatory premise at the center; it has not refuted it.

### 4.4 Why necessary concord is not itself efficient donation

The retained source explicitly turns necessary concord into partial efficient conditioning of each will by the other. It also explicitly permits harmless concomitance in other contexts. The rival's independent intrinsic policies and nonreceived primitive cognition target the transition between these categories. [S3, 168–172; S4, V03L0272–0273]

A forecast calculated from those policies is not a new efficient cause of their fulfillment. There is no justification for demanding a created cognitive mechanism from this rival while accepting primitive original cognition in the single-source case. Nor does a mathematical definition sharing parameters create efficient receipt.

The interpretation remains conditional. The fitting common Q and the practical goodness of each full-Q exercise have not been established for real necessary original bearers. But those are now particular warrant obligations; “knowledge is missing” is no longer the correct general objection.

### 4.5 The suppression comparison is relevant but bounded

Suppressing a known peer act changes a truth that the omniscient bearer knows. This is not an ordinary possible world preserving every fixed intrinsic truth and policy premise. It is a controlled comparison of relevant productive resources. The source's own distinction between intrinsically possible power and nonoccurrence fixed by knowledge makes it inappropriate to infer incapacity simply because such a comparison changes a known fact. [S9, V02L0181–0185]

Conversely, the comparison cannot certify the metaphysical possibility of removing an intrinsic necessary policy while holding everything else fixed. Its result is narrower: in the declared production interpretation, the surviving act and its independently valued end do not obtain their success from the peer. That is enough to locate an inferential burden, not enough to establish the complete rival ontology.

### 4.6 Process goods remove one objection, not all objections

Asbahani 422–423 directly rebuts “the endpoint could be obtained directly, therefore the means are pointless.” A process may realize other goods. The rival may accordingly value each bearer's own wise productive activity, rather than invent a need for the peer as an end. [S6]

But the passage concerns possible wisdom in means and benefits to creatures; it does not certify this particular original coalescent exercise as a pure perfection. A proposed good must really occur and must be appropriate to the bearer. Saying “own fitting activity” does not prove that full-Q coalescent participation is fitting.

There is also a crucial accounting point: B's additional intrinsic act is a real difference in total reality, but, on this source ontology, it is not thereby a created output. Counting it as Majmu's additional created trace would collapse intrinsic creating into the created product. [S9, V02L0243–0246]

## 5. How much of the positive trace reduction is warranted?

The reduction is logically informative. It replaces an undifferentiated additive principle with smaller causal-ontological assumptions. Its proof was inspected; this appraisal makes no new kernel-replay claim.

- **Real manifestation:** every genuinely nonidle productive act has a real created contribution. The source's “two effects in it” strongly supports an effect-level distinction. But the direct joint q may itself be the manifestation of both acts. Showing that each must have a separately individuated manifestation is stronger than showing that neither intrinsic act is idle.
- **Locality under a fixed exercise:** A's own contribution remains the same when the peer is added or removed. This is strongly suggested by Majmu's unchanged-agent comparison. It is not a consequence of fixed command syntax. It requires that the relevant productive state determines its own manifestation independently of the peer's co-instantiation.
- **Disjointness at the original respect:** the two contribution sets do not contain the same entirely provided token. Causal nonduplication yields this only after each token is warranted as that source's *entire original* contribution. It does not create those tokens or supply their whole-provider attribution. Using this step to establish individual whole ownership of every joint manifestation would assume the bridge under review.
- **Retention in complete history:** real contribution, interaction and cancellation occurrences must not be erased by an endpoint projection. This is persuasive once their reality and individuation are fixed. It does not turn proof records, support labels or causal relations into additional created entities by itself.

Under those assumptions, the extra nonempty contribution makes the complete joint history differ. That is an earned conditional result. The strongest rival can deny per-root separate created manifestations or locality while retaining distinct intrinsic acts, joint efficient support and nonduplication. Those denials are genuine metaphysical costs, but they are not formal contradictions with the weaker premises.

### Preservation is the hard test

The Dar maker-preserver passage explicitly endorses dependence in continued existence and the superiority of providing as well as preserving the effect. It also insists that making a condition such as motion does not amount to making the substance and every attribute. This is excellent warrant against an impoverished “initial trigger” creator. [S7]

It does **not** say that every preserving original act creates an additional external item distinct from the enduring thing's present actuality. Moving from one enduring bearer to one existence-stage per time still supplies only one stage jointly sustained. It does not supply one fresh stage per root. The same issue returns at the stage level.

The passage's causal criterion is continuing existential dependence. With plural supports, “remove all sustaining activity and the bearer fails” is weaker than “remove either particular root and the bearer fails.” The former does not entail the latter. The source's fuller maker-preserver norm is substantive evidence that actual dependence matters, but cannot be used to infer particular-root indispensability without addressing redundancy/coalescence.

Consequently, positive trace reduction is **not yet warranted as a universal exclusion of co-preservers**. It is warranted for a preserving account that independently identifies distinct fresh source-neutral effects, localizes their manifestations and retains them in complete history. The inspected source does not itself deliver that account. This is a positive limitation discovered by the targeted reading, not merely a demand for more paperwork.

## 6. Can original-explanation qualifications exclude coalescence without circularity?

Some can; the decisive proposed one remains substantive.

An adequate original explanation must distinguish efficient production from truth, purpose, formal specification and mere conditions. It must account for actual contingent coalescence rather than say “they are harmoniously related” and stop. It cannot have either agent create the other's capacity needed for that creating. It must keep the intrinsic acts, the created product and the explanatory relation distinct. These are independent requirements with strong source warrant.

The direct rival meets their **declared structural** versions: A and B's acts are the productive grounds of q; there is no efficient edge into either original's power or cognition; the law describes realization rather than functioning as a third object. Asbahani 145 demands a doer of contingent mixing and says the doer is within the stipulated pair. Its rejection of joint doers then uses the earlier exclusion of cooperation. The bare contingent-cause demand therefore cannot independently prove that earlier exclusion. [S5]

Nor can one insist that every joint event have an earlier separately produced mixing mechanism. Original action already needs some basic power-to-effect connection. A nonentity law is not automatically an uncaused concrete machine. If a real coupling entity is proposed, it requires its own explanation; if a primitive productive relation is proposed, the question is whether that relation can characterize original powers. It is not settled by demanding a producer for the mathematical notation.

The stronger qualification is this: **all that makes A's chosen full production actually adequate must be attributable to A's intrinsic resources; an irreducibly original peer cannot constitute any of that completion.** This is noncircular when independently warranted as a constitutive condition of original perfected agency. It is circular when “original” is simply defined by individual entire-provider coverage and that definition is used as proof that every unreceived powerful agent has such coverage.

The positive warrant is that an original explanation ought to terminate the receipt of efficacy, rather than stop at an uncaused partial contributor whose actual sufficiency is still elsewhere. The rival's answer is that no intrinsic efficacy is received: the two intrinsically complete activities jointly manifest without either making the other active. The difference between an *unreceived source* and an *individually exhaustive explanation of a chosen product* is precisely what needs a bridge.

A symmetry check is important. The single-source account also has primitive agency and a power-to-effect relation. The rival should not be charged merely for using primitives. Its extra burden is the specific interaction: why two whole-target original activities count as real contributions while leaving every source-neutral created manifestation as it would be with one. The affirmative account has a simpler and more unified productive interpretation here; that is comparative warrant, not a hidden deduction.

## 7. Changed and unchanged judgements

### Changed

- **The former missing-intention objection no longer applies generally.** The new candidate interprets full-Q content and a non-peer-valued own end on the same production semantics. The remaining question is the worth and original adequacy of that exercise.
- **The former missing-cognition objection must be withdrawn at the declared structural scope.** Primitive standing knowledge and nonreceipt are now represented. No created cognitive machine is required.
- **Rule-label switching has no independent philosophical force.** Equivalent encoding keeps the label fixed. Actual support, not a branch name, is the invariant issue.
- **Confidence that current rivals fail for independent upstream omissions is lower for this candidate.** The old review correctly described older models. Its missing-will and missing-knowledge reasons must not be inherited unchanged as the assessment of this one.
- **The trace theorem supplies a sharper conditional warrant, while preservation exposes its limit.** “Distinct created traces” is an argued premise needing a sustaining-specific interpretation, not an automatic consequence of continued existential dependence.
- **The easy futility objection is weaker.** Source-authorized process goods mean that endpoint sufficiency does not settle whether the mode of activity has value.

### Unchanged

- Two exclusive entire producers of one effect in one respect are incompatible, and vicious reciprocal efficient provision remains excluded under its stated dependency assumptions.
- The source explicitly addresses harmonious cooperation, intact powers, necessary concord, common benefit and foreknowledge. It has not overlooked the general family of objections.
- The deduction from full individually exhaustive original determination plus causal nonduplication remains sound. Its application to the rival is not supplied by ordinary output sufficiency.
- The de re concentration step is not repaired merely by showing a jointly realizable power profile; in this full-Q rival it is largely bypassed because both already have the proposed solo constructors.
- Neither the model nor this appraisal establishes metaphysical possibility of plural perfected originals. Neither establishes a universal impossibility theorem from the reduced premises.
- I retain a defeasible preference for one actual common original source. Connectedness discriminates strongly against separated domains, less strongly against this designed coalescent rival. The preference's positive basis is comprehensive, unborrowed productive explanation; it must not be reported as new certainty about the disputed bridge.

### Epistemic confidence versus deductive validity

There is high textual confidence about the intended affirmative argument and high deductive confidence about its explicit conditional core. There is substantial philosophical warrant for requiring actual unborrowed efficacy. There is weaker warrant for the universal claim that any nonexclusive original undertaking therefore receives an intrinsic perfection, especially for a co-preserver with unchanged own power, act and end.

A coherent reduced interpretation is evidence that some additional premise is doing work. It is not a metaphysical possibility certificate. An explicit source assertion and an intuitively powerful rationale are evidence for that premise. They are not equivalent to deriving it from weaker premises. These two limits should be kept equally visible.

The earlier separate affirmative assessment of at least one actual Necessary Being is outside this appraisal. Nothing here newly proves or overturns it.

## 8. Further discrimination that could genuinely help

The targeted work here was an actual source test: direct comparison of the maker-preserver context, the two-effects-in-one-product claim, the unchanged-agent principle and the process-goods rebuttal; then a bounded keyword search in the retained Dar and Minhaj files. The search yielded the existing explicit preservation and two-effects loci, not a new statement that each sustaining root creates its own fresh external token. That is a bounded negative result, not corpus-wide exhaustion.

Three further lines would be useful if pursued as arguments rather than repeat model scans:

1. **Individuation of an effect in a bearer.** Find and analyze a source passage where the author distinguishes a real productive contribution from a merely relational attribution when a bearer or state is preserved unchanged. The desired discrimination is whether “two effects” means two independently identifiable created actualities, two causal relations, or two manifestations whose individuation itself includes their producers. Only the first, plus locality and suitable attribution, directly supplies the trace route without target source-indexing.
2. **Pure perfection of actual independence.** Derive, from independently defended necessary-being and agency premises, why every chosen full-Q productive exercise must be individually exhaustive. The argument must explain why shared actual realization is a privation of the agent, distinguish optional particular efficacy from the essential genus, and survive the strongest case where no peer is valued or needed for the agent's own end. A general praise of activity or an appeal to the word “original” would not finish it.
3. **A source-grounded norm for choosing the particular exercise.** Establish whether a wise original bearer may value its own genuine productive activity when the same created endpoint would occur anyway. The Asbahani process-goods passage licenses the question, not the answer. Any exclusion should identify a violated perfection or explanatory requirement rather than assume cost, fair labor division, or an arbitrary maximization rule.

The immediate obstruction is conceptual, not lack of execution access. The retained Arabic supplies the substantive principles and their intended applications, but no inspected passage independently identifies the needed per-root preserving actuality or derives the universally exhaustive exercise norm without returning to the cooperation argument. Another finite table, owner-label convention, proof replay or archive rebuild would not remove that obstruction. A stronger source passage or a successful noncircular philosophical derivation could.

No unqualified closure is claimed. The new accomplishment is an independently checked, source-specific location of both the strongest affirmative warrant and the strongest rival's remaining challenge.

## 9. Additional source discrimination completed before handoff

A parallel independent reviewer pointed to the preceding Asbahani pages. I then directly inspected the retained scans at printed **414–416** (files p-514 through p-516), including their visible editorial apparatus. This is a further actual reading, not credit for the reviewer's inspection.

These pages strengthen a specific control beyond the 422–423 process-goods point. The seventh reply, running from the bottom of 414 into 415, positively argues that acting without wisdom is more deserving of deficiency than acting for a wisdom that comes to exist at the time at which the agent loves its occurrence. The tenth reply at 416 positively identifies acting for wisdom as more entitled to count as a perfection. This is direct source support for evaluating wise actual activity positively without inferring an antecedent intrinsic defect merely from the newly realized intended end.

The dialectical layers matter. The eighth reply exploits the opponent's permissive account of willing. The ninth begins by conceding completion through a new event **for argument's sake** and draws a consequence against that opponent. It would be wrong to quote that concession as Ibn Taymiyyah's unqualified endorsement of externally acquired intrinsic perfection. The affirmative seventh and tenth replies supply the stronger source control without that mistake.

The result slightly strengthens the rival's **own-wise-activity motivation** and sharpens the affirmative proof obligation. One may not say that a newly realized loved end, simply as such, reveals deficient original agency. One must show either that this *coalescent* act is not genuine wise efficacy, or that its relevant completion is received from an independent peer in a way that this permitted realization of wise activity is not. These pages do not establish the goodness of the coalescent act, independently necessary plural bearers, or the direct-joint production law. My qualified overall judgement is unchanged; the source-specific reason for rejecting a blanket actualization-to-deficiency inference is stronger.
