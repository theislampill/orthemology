# Complete self knowledge and Knowledge of created effects

## Positive result and exact rival

There is a defensible positive route from a sufficiently characterized knowing source to Knowledge of its effects. If the source knows its actual complete creative determination, knows that this determination is efficacious for a particular effect, and its apprehension is closed under that known consequence, it knows the effect. The route excludes a source which understands its own complete efficacious creative act yet is wholly ignorant of what that very act produces.

This is more than the assertion that every cause knows its effect. It applies only after initial cognition and the relevant completeness of self-knowledge have been established. A source which knows merely that it exists, or knows its standing capacity but not its actual exercise, does not satisfy the premise. A noncognitive source does not satisfy it either. The argument therefore develops the positive account beyond initial Knowledge without pretending to close the initial original-giver bridge.

## Primary source structure

In Darʾ, hosted section 017, Ibn Taymiyya first distinguishes self-knowledge from cognition causally relevant to creation. The source's cognition does not cause the source's own existence. Knowledge of what it intends to produce is a condition of creation, together with Power and Will; bare cognition is not the whole creative act. He then reconstructs complete self-knowledge through the source's intrinsic attributes, volition and efficacy, and distinguishes this from limited human awareness. The context again states that initial self-knowledge is a separate argument. [Primary text, lines 519–546 in the captured reading](https://www.islamicbook.ws/amma/dr-taardh-alaql-walnql-017.html)

The neighbouring objection reported from al-Rāzī asks whether knowing the source as this particular being already gives knowledge of its causal relation, or whether knowing that relation presupposes knowledge of its effect. Ibn Taymiyya answers by appealing to full comprehension of the source's actual character and its intrinsic implications. This exposes the question rather than erasing it: “complete self-knowledge” must be stronger than awareness of a thin self-description, but must not be defined simply as already knowing every effect.

The source's discussion of completed Will matters. A standing power to create either x or y does not settle which effect occurs. An actual efficacious willing of x does. The necessity is conditional upon the completed productive determination, not the necessity of that same volition or effect across every possible history. No Thomistic pure-act premise is needed for this distinction.

## The argument without a hidden target substitution

Let A identify the source's particular actual creative determination. Let X identify the resulting dependent actuality. There are four separate premises:

1. A actually obtains as an intrinsic exercise of this source
2. The source genuinely knows A, including the respect which differentiates it from incompatible possible exercises
3. A is sufficient for X, and the source knows that efficacy relation
4. The source's knowledge is not deficient in the relevant consequence: knowing A and its implication to X includes knowing X

Then the source knows X. Factivity ensures that the attributed Knowledge concerns what really obtains. If the source's exercise is genuinely unborrowed and the independently complete productive source accounts for it, the retained identity route identifies the knowing bearer with that source. Ownership of an ordinary derivative thought still does not establish that premise.

The principle in step 4 need not say that every human knower knows every logical consequence of anything she knows. It is the ideal completeness condition relevant to the source's alleged exhaustive self-understanding. A finite agent can know a premise and fail to recognize its consequence; the primary text explicitly recognizes that difference. Thus a source attributed only limited self-knowledge remains a live weaker rival, while a source attributed complete comprehension cannot use that finite ignorance to escape the conclusion.

The argument also need not make the effect the cause of the source's Knowledge. Knowing an efficacious act and its consequence can explain knowledge of the effect even if the effect is not an independent supplier of cognition. Epistemic consequence, productive causation and the object's being what it is are different relations. The source's self-knowledge is not self-creation.

## Why the efficacy premise is substantive

A probabilistic disposition is not yet an actual complete determination of its realized outcome. Knowing a chance law and the initial state may yield knowledge of a distribution without yielding knowledge of the realized event. If the impersonal or personal rival regards the source as only a nonnecessitating initiator, the above inference cannot silently treat initiation as a sufficient completed act.

The affirmative creative account instead says that the source's completed Will and Power originate this determinate actual effect. Its relevant content includes an efficacious determination, not merely a disposition waiting for an independent realized selector. This is why the argument fits the complete intentional-source role rather than generic causation. If a rival proposes genuinely undetermined outcomes under the same intrinsic source exercise, that rival contests this efficacy premise and must be assessed there.

The intended scope also matters. If A suffices only for a broad result while contingent details depend on something outside A, knowledge of A establishes only that broad result. Universal productive coverage and complete comprehension of all relevant determinations are needed for Knowledge of all produced particulars. The fact that the source knows one target does not itself establish that coverage.

## The thin and thick self knowledge tests

Consider three alternatives containing the same source. Its standing nature and capacity are the same. In two it creates a recipient of one type; in the third it creates a different effect. A description containing only the source's generic standing nature does not distinguish these alternatives. Knowing that description alone does not determine which particular effect exists.

Now include the relevant actual productive determination in what the source knows of itself. If the effect is fixed by that determination, alternatives which agree on it also agree on the effect. The epistemic uncertainty about the effect is then removed by that known determination and the known efficacy relation. This is a structural illustration of the valid implication, not a computational test for consciousness.

The accompanying Lean `CreativeKnowledge.lean` states the consequence generically. Its finite interpretation separates the source's invariant standing capacity from its variable actual exercise. It verifies that effect knowledge follows from the latter informational partition but not from the former. The relational model is not itself a conscious knower; genuine Knowledge in the philosophical application remains a granted semantic premise.

## Why no modal collapse follows

At one alternative the source knows A and therefore X. At another it knows a different determination B and therefore a different effect Y. The source's ability and standing cognition can remain intrinsic throughout. Nothing in the inference makes A or X true in every alternative.

This is the same distinction preserved in the Ṣafadiyya and Darʾ source material: intrinsic standing attributes are not identical with the claim that each particular completed act is always occurring. The implication A → X may be stable while A is contingent. Even factive Knowledge of X at the actual world does not transform X into an absolutely necessary truth.

The positive gain is precise. A complete intentionally efficacious source which exhaustively apprehends its own actual creative determination cannot simultaneously be wholly ignorant of the resulting effect. A source that knows only a thin invariant essence, a source whose cognition is incomplete in its consequences, or an initiator of outcomes not fixed by its known determination does not yet fall under that conclusion.

## Integration with the main argument

The original-giver argument provides a proposed warrant for the source's actual cognitive perfection. Intrinsic determination provides the proposed warrant for its essential standing character. The present argument then shows how a genuinely intellectual creative architecture can connect that Knowledge to its actual productive scope. These are cumulative steps, not independent repetitions of the same conclusion.

This gives the personal account a concrete explanatory form: a source with its own understanding, an actual determinate intention, and adequate efficacy can account for a truth-directed created order without being the created subject or copying its mistakes. It must still justify its cognition, fullness of self-understanding, purposes and efficacy. The result is a constructive defended conditional, not a claim that the whole divine-attribute ascent has been completed.
