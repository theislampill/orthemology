# Arabic and formal research feedback cases

These cases describe what actually changed in the research. Source reading, formal validity and substantive warrant retain separate credit. Boundaries and overlaps are in archive-root `t20/reading/READING_INDEX.json`; exact input hashes and result endpoints are in the JSON companions. No new primary reading or proof replay was performed for this supplement.

A documented source-guided design can be distinguished from a later contextual check of an existing result. A retrospective alignment remains useful, but it does not establish that the source caused the result. Where no completed specialised transfer exists, that limit is explicit.

## AR-X06 Repeated traces do not identify their source or occurrence

**Return and chronology:** The result returns to the interpretation of transmission: recovered content cannot alone certify source attribution or occurrence history.
