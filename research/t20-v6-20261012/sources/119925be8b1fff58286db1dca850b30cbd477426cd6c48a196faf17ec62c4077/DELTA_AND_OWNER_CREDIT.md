## Important distinctions for the mathematical handoff

1. **Static vs evolving sufficiency.** A minimal audit-profile label suffices for a *declared static* likelihood family, not necessarily for future transitions, revisions of likelihood tables, timed hybrid branches or value-of-information calculations.
2. **Audits and correction can commute while a numerical step does not.** Covariance of a continuous vector field does not imply covariance of every approximation. Positivity/descent of an update is weaker than preservation of the permitted audit square.
3. **An audit-invariant law may lose a certificate.** Tail/exponential-moment assumptions and Fisher path-length properties may need a new proof even after successful Bayesian reweighting; neither is implied by total-variation control alone.
4. **Original and extended formal calculi remain distinct.** A proof in an added dependent-induction/pair system does not retroactively turn nonderivability in the source calculus into a contradiction or repair raw-code inequality.
5. **Metaphysical bridge premises are substantive.** A connected graph of locally uniquely sourced effects glues only after the source-overlap, domain-coverage and individuation premises have been justified in the actual intended sense.
