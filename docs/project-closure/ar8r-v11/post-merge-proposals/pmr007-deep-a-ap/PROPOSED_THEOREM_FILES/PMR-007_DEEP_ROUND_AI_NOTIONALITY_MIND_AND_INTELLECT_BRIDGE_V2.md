```text

  an external, modal, or formal order object;

NOTIONAL_REPRESENTATION(n,o):
  a universal/concept/meaning representing o;

MENTAL_HOST(m,n):
  n subsists in or is tokened by a mental/intellectual faculty/entity m;

CONSTITUTIVELY_GROUNDS(m,o):
  m is the constitutive ground of o, not merely a downstream representer;

  o is underived at the declared scope;

  m is underived and constitutively grounds the selected order;
```

A mental host is not automatically an ultimate intellect-ground, subject,
person, agent, or wise chooser.

## 4. NMIB-1A — hosted-notion conditional

`NM-HOST` and `NOTIONAL_REPRESENTATION(n,o)` entail the existence of some mental
host for `n`.

This establishes only a mental token/host relation.  It does not establish that
`m` grounds `o`.

## 5. NMIB-1B — exact H7 bridge decomposition

The move from an underived structural order to an underived intellectual ground
requires at least the following independent hard premises:

```text
H7a STRUCTURE_TO_INTRINSIC_NOTION:
  the relevant order is intrinsically semantic/notional, not merely a
  structure later represented by minds;

H7b CONSTITUTIVE_HOSTING:
  at least one mental host of the intrinsic notion constitutively grounds the
  order rather than merely representing it downstream;

H7c UNDERIVABILITY_TRANSFER:
  if an underived order is constitutively grounded by m, m is underived at the
  relevant scope;

H7d UNIQUE_CONSTITUTIVE_HOST:
  at most one mental host constitutively grounds the selected order.
```

Under `NM-HOST + H7a + H7b + H7c + H7d`, an underived structural order has
exactly one underived constitutive intellectual host in the declared model.

This is a dependency-minimal **conditional specification**.  It does not prove
H7a–H7d.

## 7. Strongest surviving H7 rival

The strongest current rival compatible with the source-reconstructed
mental/external distinction is:

```text
necessary or underived impersonal external/modal order
+
particular external realizers
+
derived local minds that abstract universal/notional representations from it
+
no ultimate mental ground.
```

This rival does not deny that universals, as mental abstractions, exist in
minds.  It denies H7a and H7b: the order itself is not intrinsically a notion,
and its mental representations are downstream.
