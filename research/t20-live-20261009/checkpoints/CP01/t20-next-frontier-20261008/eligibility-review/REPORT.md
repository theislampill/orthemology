# Independent eligibility review: the correction holds, with a distinct application burden

8 October 2026 UTC. Independent review of the bearer-eligibility finding and its source/model interfaces. This is bounded research, not protected integration, final acceptance, or T20 closure.

## Result first

**The central correction is warranted.** The source explicitly argues that essential nonreceptivity is worse than receptive lack. A proposed bearer cannot answer a justified pure-perfection comparison merely by saying that its essence forbids acquiring the perfection. Comparative disqualification does not require an identity-preserving acquisition, merger, or transformation. The objection that demanded such an acquisition as a necessary condition of *every* eligibility argument should be withdrawn.

The correction does not yet certify the strongest coactive rival's defeat. Three questions remain logically distinct:

1. Why must each putative underived original satisfy the source's unrestricted pure-perfection requirement?
2. Why is the particular excellence, at its specified scope, a coherent pure perfection?
3. Does the actual rival lack that excellence or receive the relevant foreign completion?

The third question is not reducible to denying the hierarchy or denying that independent adequate agency is good. Both represented bearers already have standing solo work-capacity. They can contest the classification of their *actual joint undertaking* as incomplete without pleading essential incapacity to acquire solo power. The finding recognizes this in its later sections; its opening restriction of the remaining disagreement to hierarchy or purity should be broadened to include this application question.

A second result strengthens the affirmative research direction: Akmaliyya 6:125–126 contains a **universal-dependence comparison**, distinct from unaided capacity and successful willing. If that comparison independently warrants universal dependence on every eligible original over all actual distinct external bearers in the relevant completion relation, uniqueness follows without donor distribution, consolidation, or nonabsorption. If the domain includes only created effects, joint dependence on two sources is not excluded by that fact alone. This is a consequential difference in premises, not another numerical-identity objection.

## 2. What the nonreceptivity reply establishes

Akmaliyya L082–090 does more than expand a technical privation predicate. Its first reply orders three conditions: possessing the relevant perfection, lacking it in a receptive bearer, and being unable to receive it. The last condition is not made immune to comparative deficiency by the terminology of privation. Darʾ707 L004 and L008–009 make the same point, with the separate animate-the-inanimate answer only at L010–011.

This answers the following objection:

> Unless A can literally become the consolidated or more excellent individual while retaining its numerical identity, that individual's superiority cannot count against A's qualification.

That demand is too strong. An immutable or essentially restricted candidate can fail a standard. Its inability to change into a satisfactory candidate does not show that it satisfies the standard. The source's treatment of necessary attributes likewise does not presuppose a previously bare subject acquiring them: Akmaliyya L110–134 explicitly rejects that picture.

The comparison is substantive. It has considerable rational force for life, cognition, and agency: total incapacity is not an improvement over the same pure excellence's remediable absence. Nothing in this judgment is rendered worthless because it is not first proved from an unrelated formal calculus. The live philosophical dispute concerns the entitlement and scope of that judgment, not whether it can count as evidence at all.

There is nevertheless a condition. The property has to be a genuine coherent pure perfection. Inability to produce a created being that is nevertheless self-necessary is not a defect, because the proposed task is contradictory; the source states that example at L073. A type-relative remedy for need is not promoted into an absolute excellence merely by being desirable in its original context; L078–080 and L258–273 supply that control. The nonreceptivity reply cannot transform any arbitrary description into a pure perfection.

**Disposition:** withdraw an unrestricted identity-acquisition requirement and the claim that the source overlooks essential nonreceptivity. Retain the narrower claim that a consolidation construction *by itself* does not establish this particular bearer's eligible perfection. The finding's new comparative route supplies a different warrant, subject to its own premises.

## 3. Necessary existence, unrestricted pure perfection, and the donor route

### 3.1 The source claims more than a maximal element

Akmaliyya L016 requires the maximum possible perfection, with every deficiency-free perfection possessed. L037–038 reasons from necessary, ancient, self-subsisting existence and invokes superiority from every aspect. This is a claim of unrestricted pure-perfection completeness. It is not merely the claim that no *already admitted actual bearer* happens to be better under a partial order.

Here pure-perfection completeness concerns eligible attributes; it does not mean production of every possible external good. Accordingly, two incomparable maxima are no counterexample to the argument as stated. Such a response would replace the source's stronger premise with a weaker one. Nor does a qualitative tie collapse numerical individuals: Darʾ791 L006–016 and792 L002–014 specifically distinguish shared concepts from individually possessed existence and properties.

The real promotion requiring appraisal is from underived existential standing to unrestricted priority in pure excellence. L037 begins with the necessary ancient creator already established in the preceding existential argument; L038 invokes superiority in every respect while advancing the a-fortiori comparison. It is therefore not an independently demonstrated theorem of merely having no efficient producer. The finding correctly admits this.

This does not make the source's reasoning empty or secretly circular. The affirmative rationale is that unqualified basic excellence belongs more fittingly to an underived, self-subsisting explanatory ground than to a more dependent being. Its purity qualification is intended to remove the usual counterexamples involving creaturely needs or kind-specific benefits. The nonreceptivity comparison then prevents a restricted essence from serving as an unexplained exemption. These are intelligible metaphysical reasons. Their breadth must be assessed on those grounds.

The strongest dissent must deny something substantive: for example, that superiority in existential independence entails unrestricted entitlement to every pure excellence, or that the disputed *relational scope* adds a pure excellence beyond already complete unborrowed agency. Merely saying “different essences,” “both necessary,” or “not formalized” does not answer the source.

### 3.2 The donor audit is valid but local

The source-of-perfection argument at L039–046 supports the superior entitlement of an actual giver to the perfection it gives. It cannot distribute every perfection to every candidate original without an additional premise about each one's scope of giving. Every perfection having some appropriately perfected source is weaker than every source possessing every perfection.

The executed donor audit retains even the extra condition that each of two source variables gives something. Of 17 satisfying giver/possession assignments, 10 do not make every source possess every perfection. The assignment is a quantifier test only. It does not certify real original sources or a plural-original metaphysics.

Nor does this invalidate the separate a-fortiori comparison. The donor flaw becomes a criticism of the whole proof only if the original-to-unrestricted-perfection bridge has no remaining independent warrant. The source actually offers that additional warrant. The proper assessment is to test it, not suppress it.

## 4. The stronger universal-dependence route and its exact domain

Akmaliyya L217–223 distinguishes several comparisons:

- everything depends on a bearer that needs nothing, rather than only some things depending on it;
- being capable by oneself, rather than needing an assistant;
- accomplishing the whole oneself, rather than accomplishing part with a participant or helper;
- comprehensive creation and power, rather than restricted creation and power;
- actual willing being fulfilled and no unwilled event escaping it.

These statements are closely related but are not interchangeable. Absence of dependence by A does not reverse the dependence relation and establish that everything depends on A. The source calls this a unity argument in L217; it is not confined to the standing-capacity comparison. L235–240 then requires wise discrimination together with efficacious power, and explicitly retains the no-helper/no-opponent standard. Reading the whole passage as mere causal domination or as mere capacity would understate it.

### 4.1 A donor-free conditional uniqueness theorem

Fix a single relevant dependence relation D(x,y): x receives from y the kind of subsistence or constitutive completion that original independence excludes. Let the domain consist of actual distinct external concrete bearers.

Assume:

1. An original o does not depend on any distinct external bearer in relation D.
2. Every eligible original o satisfies universal dependence: every actual distinct external bearer x depends on o in relation D.

If a and b were distinct originals, premise 2 for a would give D(b,a), while premise 1 for b would deny D(b,a). Therefore there is at most one original. An existence argument remains separately necessary.

The proof needs no identity-preserving acquisition, merger, donor distribution, augmented-output theorem, or numerical identity principle. The accompanying exhaustive finite checks confirm the implication for domains of one through four bearers; the two-line argument proves it generally.

The theorem does **not** independently justify premise 2. That is the source-level work of classifying universal dependent scope as a coherent pure excellence and extending the unrestricted pure-perfection requirement to every proposed original. It would be illegitimate to replace that work with “an original means the sole creator.” Conversely, an independently defended perfection premise that entails uniqueness is not circular merely because it entails uniqueness.

### 4.2 Which things, and which dependence?

“All” cannot mean every abstract type or include the bearer as its own efficient product. Necessary intrinsic attributes also cannot be treated as externally donated objects: Akmaliyya L120–134 and L163–165 expressly distinguish them from things separate from the bearer. Darʾ792–793 reinforces the distinction between a property's necessary subject and an efficient producer. The appropriate unrestricted candidate domain is **all actual numerically distinct external bearers**, not all terms in a property inventory.

But the application to a putative peer has to be stated. If universal dependence ranges only over created effects, both sources can be necessary to every such effect without either receiving its own subsistence from the other. That relation-level assignment satisfies the created-only premise and does not establish uniqueness. It is not a countermodel to the unrestricted source principle: it denies that broader domain, and it authenticates neither genuine joint production nor complete original efficacy.

This norm concerns actual external bearers, not every possible creature or good. It is neither ordinary power over all coherent tasks nor a requirement to actualize every possibility. In particular, it cannot demand that A create the proposed necessary B while B remains self-necessary: L073 explicitly excludes that contradiction. The proposed inference instead disqualifies the hypothesis of an underived peer by the stronger comparative standard, if that standard is justified. Failure to perform a contradictory task supplies no deficiency premise.

The respect of dependence matters as much as its domain. Need for another's actual contribution to this effect cannot silently become efficient derivation of one's existence. The contradiction requires an independence premise excluding the *same* completion relation as the universal-dependence premise asserts. The broader source claim about unborrowed actual efficacy may supply this, but it must do so explicitly.

L217's extension even outside the supposed realm and its explicit unity language support a genuinely unrestricted comparison; the text does not merely celebrate a large portfolio of created effects. Still, the local polemic against restricted divine creation does not mechanically settle the hypothetical peer's domain or prove the universal comparison from thin efficient nonreceipt. The source gives a substantive comparison that can ground that extension. The review should present the comparison as the defended norm it is, with its domain and respect exposed.

The strongest affirmative rationale is broader explanatory sufficiency: the independently sufficient source of the complete order lacks a causal-scope restriction suffered by a merely partial source. The strongest rival response is not that A cannot turn into the sole source. It is that universal dependence by *every external bearer* adds no pure excellence beyond genuinely complete unborrowed wise agency, or that the actual joint relation does not restrict that agency. Both responses challenge substantive source norms or applications and owe a positive account. This review supplies no authenticated world that settles them.

## 5. Actual joint activity remains a separate application test

The current full-Q rival is stronger than an essentially role-limited source. Both participants already have represented solo constructors. It therefore passes the standing-receptivity issue at the model's declared scope. The comparative reply cannot refute it by attributing to it a lack it does not claim.

The source's further challenge is actual: if a peer supplies some of this undertaking's productive adequacy, then the first bearer is not actually accomplishing the whole unaided. L218 and L238–239 provide direct positive support for ranking that difference. This is not reducible to demand for exclusive credit or for an extra intrinsic entity to be transferred.

The rival's serious answer is correspondingly about completion: could an irreducibly joint manifestation express two already adequate intrinsic activities, rather than complete either one's deficient activity? Accepting the pure value of adequate agency does not by itself answer whether that description is coherent or instantiated. Neither calling it “joint” nor calling the intended effect “fulfilled” settles the classification.

The nonabsorption report correctly localizes a related burden. Local response sensitivity does not prove strict finite augmentation. The source's unchanged-effective-activity and actual-contribution principles do substantive work. The new eligibility argument strengthens why original efficacy should not be externally limited; it does not turn response-law variation into authenticated causal loss or contribution. Conversely, an algebraic absorption example does not refute the source's full perfection argument.

Optional acts prevent another shortcut. Akmaliyya L075, L103–109 and L157–160 make particular execution depend on fitting time, willing and wisdom. Necessary standing perfection does not force maximum actual production of every external good. This also means that the optionality of cooperation cannot by itself refute the no-foreign-completion requirement. The dispute is which complete undertaking is willed and whether its adequacy is genuinely unborrowed, not whether every available solo output must occur.

The wise-determination report's conditional diagonal-means argument can answer a selection problem given its stated reasons. It supplies no independent result about the production relation. This review does not infer absence of adequate wisdom from the adapter's failure to exhibit all of it, nor infer adequate wisdom simply from a selected-plan field.

## 6. Exact executed operation

`src/dependency_audit.py` executes three limited audits:

1. **Comparative-disqualification premise removal.** Across 512 proposition valuations, the source-shaped assumptions entail possession of the eligible pure perfection. The inference remains valid with all eight combinations of acquisition, donor and consolidation flags, including all false. Removing each substantive premise exposes its own countervaluation. These include different failures for the original-to-unrestricted-perfection bridge and for the nonreceptivity comparison. This is a dependency proof, not a proof of the premises' metaphysical truth.
2. **Donor quantifiers.** Exhaustive two-source/two-perfection assignments validate the per-perfection perfected-giver conclusion while refuting unrestricted distribution to every source. This confirms the finding's local negative result without supplying a new metaphysical rival.
3. **Universal-dependence domain.** Exhaustive relations through four bearers validate at-most-one under the explicitly shared relation and unrestricted external domain. The created-effects-only assignment demonstrates precisely which stronger premise is absent. Removing universal scope or independence also removes the implication.

## Disposition

**Affirm:** the source-grounded correction about nonreceptivity; the independence of comparative disqualification from literal acquisition; the donor quantifier warning; the need to preserve optional wise activity and numerical individuation.

**Withdraw:** any claim that essential nonreceptivity is an unanswered escape, that lack of an identity-preserving merger alone defeats every eligibility route, or that donor distribution failure eliminates the independent perfection comparison.

**Tighten:** distinguish the original-to-unrestricted-perfection warrant, the purity/scope of the perfection, and whether this actual rival lacks it. Read unrestricted pure-perfection completeness rather than weak order-theoretic maximality. Keep universal dependence, unaided capacity, actual whole provision, and successful wise willing separate.

**New affirmative frontier:** test the universal-dependence comparison itself as an independently warranted pure perfection over distinct external bearers. If established in the requisite respect for every original, it removes dependence on nonabsorption for uniqueness. If restricted to created effects, it does not. No protected integration, global refutation, or T20 closure follows from this review alone.
