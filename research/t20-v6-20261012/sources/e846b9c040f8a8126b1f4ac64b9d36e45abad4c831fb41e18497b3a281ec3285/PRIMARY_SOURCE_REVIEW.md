## Abadi Burrows Lampson and Plotkin

*A Calculus for Access Control in Distributed Systems*, author-hosted full PDF, especially §§1 and 5. The paper distinguishes trustworthy computation, authentication and authorization, and explains how a principal's powers depend on roles and delegation policy. This is direct prior art for avoiding inference from an authentic copied statement to permission for a different actor. Its declared treatment assumes relevant timestamp/lifetime checks and other infrastructure. The present byte-repair model does not improve that calculus or inherit its assumptions for free. [Author-hosted PDF](https://homepages.inf.ed.ac.uk/gdp/publications/Calculus_for_Access_Control.pdf).

## W3C PROV O

The 30 April 2013 Recommendation, §§3.1–3.2 and related property definitions. It distinguishes derivation, quotation, revision, specialization and alternate entities. It also allows provenance descriptions to have provenance. These distinctions support keeping source identity, a derived copy and an amended assertion separate. A PROV graph is a representation of these claims; adopting its vocabulary does not prove the represented history, independent source roots, or current recipient authority. [PROV-O Recommendation](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/).

## RFC 9110

HTTP Semantics, §§8.8 and 13.1.1. Strong validators support representation-sensitive conditional operations; If-Match can stop a stale state-changing request from overwriting a newer representation. This is established operational precedent for the expected-revision guard. Entity-tag checks do not replace actor authorization, prove the target's normative fittingness, or supply a non-bypassable executor. The reference implementation is not an HTTP server and makes no RFC conformance claim. [RFC 9110](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9110.html#section-13.1.1).

## RFC 9421

HTTP Message Signatures, verification/application requirements and §7.2.2, with the examples of modified unsigned components in Appendix B.4. The specification makes signature coverage and replay defenses application-specific. A valid signature on some components does not protect omitted ones. This supports the present separation between a past authenticated disclosure and the complete action/recipient/time binding needed for intervention. No cryptographic signature system is implemented by this model. [RFC 9421](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9421.html#section-7.2.2).

## Anna Sofia Maurin

*Infinite Regress — Virtue or Vice?*, full university-hosted PDF, especially the dependency discussion on pp. 18–21. Maurin analyzes how the direction and explanatory context of dependence affect whether a regress is vicious. This supports reading Candidate E's recursive condition as a dependence claim rather than an inference from infinity itself. It does not settle the existence or legitimacy of a particular holistic normative structure. No claim is made that the elementary recurrence examples here establish a metaphysical possibility. [University-hosted PDF](https://www.fil.lu.se/hommageawlodek/site/papper/MaurinAnnaSofia.pdf).

## Linda Radzik

*A Coherentist Theory of Normative Authority*, *The Journal of Ethics* 6 (2002), 21–42, publisher abstract only. The abstract explicitly proposes that normative justification by coherence can avoid regress. This warrants treating the coherentist reply as an actual developed research position rather than a newly invented escape hatch. The full article was not accessible through the opened publisher page; its detailed argument is not reconstructed or adjudicated from secondary snippets. [Publisher abstract](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1023/A:1015887107235).

## Peter Railton

*Normative Guidance, Evaluative Guidance, and Skill*, *Analyse & Kritik* 43 (2021), 235–252, full journal-hosted PDF, especially pp. 244–246. Railton distinguishes a causal model's descriptive content from performance goals supplied to its use. The distinction warns against claiming that a successful causal checker itself establishes which standard should govern. His broader account concerns evaluative guidance and learning; it is not reduced here to a universal rejection of natural normativity. [Journal-hosted PDF](https://www.analyse-und-kritik.net/Dateien/62c5a36907876_railton-normative_guidance.pdf).
