# What the new comparison establishes

The useful connection is now more exact than “several branches preserve information.” Different research fronts can impose requirements that become strictly stronger when used together. What survives the combination depends on how those requirements compose and which additional mathematical structure is justified.

## Common refinement has three different mechanisms

In the new gradient classification, two source partitions specify two audit families. Each family separately allows interactions within its groups. Requiring the same gradient to commute with both forces the energy to separate over their common refinement. The proof uses the actual Fisher–transport metric and a homogeneous extension of the energy. The generated audit group can remain smaller than the refinement's full audit group. The extra conclusion comes from being a gradient of one sufficiently regular energy.

In the cone corollary, complete identity-summing instruments can be combined into one tagged instrument. Their full outcome columns distinguish exactly the common-refinement groups. The inherited cone theorem then classifies closed convex stable families. This is a direct application of existing mathematics. Its conclusion concerns stable sets, and can contain states that no finite observed sequence reaches. Convexity and closure do real work.

In the controlled Fisher continuation result, several freely composable linear control families generate the sum of their control subspaces. The resulting fitness signatures refine the individual signature classes. Exact output histories then distinguish the grouped barycentres for that refined class structure. Here actual composition of allowed controls supplies the stronger observational contract.

These are partitions of source labels, cone summands or control signatures. They are not automatically the minimal equivalence partitions of entire inquiry states. Joining the available state summaries may still require further transition-stable refinement, as the next example shows.

The same finite partition operation appears in all three arguments, but the inferences are not interchangeable. Gradient integrability, tagged complete instruments, and reachable control composition are different warrants for using it. None can be removed merely because the resulting diagrams resemble one another.

## Separately sufficient records need not form a jointly sufficient record

The nine-state correction example provides an explicit warning for the inherited sidecar programme. A record can fully preserve every x-only correction, and another can fully preserve every y-only correction, while their combined stored values still fail to preserve an x correction followed by a y correction. The missing distinction is a single rank-one product direction v⊗v. It becomes consequential under composition.

This is the actual finite linear-closure principle of H-Continuation operating within controlled nonlinear normalisation, rather than an analogy to it. One must close the required record under the combined operation family. Merely concatenating independently sufficient profiles can omit the interaction that mixed histories expose. ET-05 had already demonstrated the related phenomenon for historical source revisions; the new application supplies the exact current-law dynamics and missing dimension.

Conversely, requiring every possible correction can overstate the memory needed by a restricted application. The six-state example has a smaller exact quotient when only x-fitness corrections are allowed. The need to retain a distinction follows from the declared contract, not from the mere existence of different underlying laws.

## Where this helps the foundational programme

These results sharpen a representation discipline already used by the foundational work. A source correction, change in evidential role, new permitted inquiry, or combination of individually adequate grounds can change which distinctions matter. It is therefore unsafe to infer a sufficient cumulative record from separately adequate route summaries without checking their joint dependencies and operations.

The actual FND-033 source already implements a substantial part of that discipline. Its selective-revalidation argument, actual-basing guard and retained-report comparison are retained as achieved work. The new mathematics does not assign likelihoods to fiṭrah, turn a formal support bit into human knowledge, or replace the affirmative arguments for necessity and common sourcehood. It tells a proposed application exactly what additional semantic and operational obligations it must meet.

The result is a more informative crosswalk: an established foundational application, a precise remaining interpretation boundary, and additional mathematical classifications that can test future applications. This improves the research state without pretending that every available front has been exhausted or that a new theorem is needed wherever two subjects meet.
