# Proposed bounded continuation: contextual testimony and enacted service

Status: design recommendation only. No new research result, formal implementation, new primary reading or actual-world institutional validation is claimed here.

## Objective and exact inherited interfaces

Investigate the missing transition from a received, possibly translated utterance and retained evidence to a successor's justified conclusion, then separately to an actually re-realised bounded service. Keep a positive case in each lane; do not replace the organisation question with archive integrity.

Reuse:

1. T20 `T20Grounded.Derivable base rule available c`, `derivable_sound`, `derivable_after_retraction_iff` and `MinimalFor`. Their semantic soundness premises remain external; availability cannot establish a root's authenticity.
2. T20 `QuotientBase`/`quotient_derivable_iff`: carrier aliases of one actual acquisition can be identified when the map and saturated availability are supplied. A successor's **new** act is not an alias of a predecessor's earlier act merely because the office continues.
3. T20 `OccurrenceControls`: historical duty, current prospective standing, present applicability and implementation are separate. Its current `.ownsDuty0` and `.ready` primitives are the precise abstraction points to open for this experiment.
4. T9 continuation synthesis: joint occurrence/realiser/authority binding, retained or lawful reacquired calibration, exclusive/shareable grants, and next-service access. Do not claim these distinctions anew.
5. T16 finite-renewal contract: a stateful semantic relation, model-level predecessor/successor history, independent permission and removal of predecessor operative support. Its effective infinite-path theorem is inherited, not required for the finite experiment.

The prior T9/T16 sources establish restricted operational contracts but leave their actual-world semantic/bearer adequacy outside the theorem. This design cannot silently fill that gap with a predicate named GenuineContinuation.

## Lane A: nontrivial occurrence/translation test

Fix a tiny message language with two distinct surface encodings, a contextual proposition semantics, and force constructors `assert`, `quote`, `directive`. Context explicitly contains speaker, addressee, original time and evaluation time. Include a proposition with an indexical: for example, the original message by P to office R at t0 asserts that R has a specified duty during interval I. Encoding 2 may replace the words but must preserve P/R/t0/I. A byte-identical copy interpreted with speaker B/time t1 is a different reading.

Distinguish:

- the proposition denoted by the original occurrence;
- what a new carrier reports about that occurrence;
- what the carrier currently asserts;
- the recipient's grounds for attribution and semantic fidelity;
- the current instance of a bounded prospective attestation rule;
- current actionable applicability, including whether B occupies R and the present falls in I.

### Positive contrasts

A1. Different encoding, preserved original context and assertion force: the recipient can derive the same historical duty from source attribution, relevant veracity and the proved encoding correspondence. No independent duplicate jurisdiction proof is required.

A2. A genuinely established rule prospectively covers new reports issued by the current occupant of R within domain D and conditions K. A new report by successor B qualifies through evidence for the actual succession and membership. A merely general employee's report does not. Do not identify this new occurrence with P's old assertion.

A3. The original source's old obligation remains warranted after B loses permission to issue future rules, provided the old evidential route remains sound and available.

### Failure controls

- Exact bytes with `now` rebound to t1 fail original-content preservation.
- A translated quotation of “do a” does not become a fresh directive from P to B.
- Preserved historical duty with current applicability missing does not yield present action.
- A valid institutional appointment outside the attestation domain does not transfer source ownership of this new content.
- Self-asserted provenance plus a mutually endorsing cycle provides no independent anchor.
- Loss of this exceptional-source route must not be recast as a proof that T17's separate ordinary entitlement route is always unavailable.

The primary sources motivate distinctions: Asbahani 389–391 on original speech/carrier act; 725–728 on report scope; Nubuwwat 767–772 and 777–781 on custody, certification purpose, mission scope and rival explanations. The finite interpretation is the researcher's declared model. Do not claim it authenticates an actual medieval episode or that machine acceptance establishes its Arabic interpretation.

### Feasible result

A compositional correctness statement for the selected encoding/occurrence transformation, plus a finite interpreted decision matrix for the cases above. The statement should preserve a fixed contextual proposition when the original binding is transported, and demonstrate failure under specified rebinding/force coercions. Recipient warrant then follows only from explicit grounded evidential and epistemic premises. A semantic transformation theorem is different from the present formal fixture's assumption that `.ownsDuty0` is already true.

## Lane B: distinguish evidence support from actual re-realisation

### Concrete model class

Use a finite two-state service with at least a read operation and a state-changing operation, for a fixed nonzero two-request horizon. Give the predecessor and two differently encoded successor realisers explicit transitions and readouts. A construction/migration transition must initialise the successor from the predecessor's actual state, after which predecessor storage and actuation are removed from the operative support. Test all source states and request sequences in the declared finite domain.

A successor's capability must be computed from its available transition/actuation resources and the actual migrated state, not supplied as a `.ready` Boolean. One negative realiser should retain the same current readout and an authentic copy of a verification receipt but lose a resource needed after the first state-changing request. Another should preserve the abstract code and data but never undergo the required actual model-level installation/transfer. These are model transitions, not evidence of real physical deployments.

Permission and constitutive institution/role are separate: an authentic copy of a source grant must not allocate an exclusive office to two simultaneous successors. Use T9's already earned allocation distinction without counting it as novel.

### Exact cross-layer question

Over a finite common fault universe U, let E be the complete inclusion-minimal root supports for a selected grounded evidential conclusion. Separately derive C from the concrete service semantics: C contains the inclusion-minimal available resource sets under which an admitted successor policy executes the stated service contract through the chosen horizon. Deriving C means exhaustive finite transition/policy checking or a proved refinement; it is not a list of assumed continuation predicates.

Investigate the exact condition:

For every retraction Δ ⊆ U, if some E-support survives, then the stated service still has an admitted realisation.

The candidate characterisation is that each minimal E-support contains at least one C-support. The research must prove the statement under the precisely stated monotone damage model and test it against the directly evaluated service. General monotone support/hypergraph mathematics is established; the programme-specific contribution is a checked bridge between retained testimony and a declared actual model-level re-realisation, including the failure profiles that expose the semantic gap.

### Discriminators

B1. A complete causal handoff preserves the service under each declared relevant fault profile; recipient evidence alone is insufficient unless the resource-support refinement holds.

B2. An authentic old receipt remains available while power, successor state or current execution permission is removed. The historical assertion remains warranted; current service fails.

B3. Two systems have the same evidence-support family and present output, but different C families due to different causal construction/next-step resources. Therefore no function of the evidence family alone decides their bounded operational continuation.

B4. Equal minimum cut cardinalities do not establish the implication: the identities and bundles of the failed roots matter. This connects the actual source-root accounting to a decision about future service, beyond a scalar resilience score.

B5. A replacement may implement the same service and be lawfully appointed without being numerically the same physical bearer. Conversely, same bytes or lineage alone does not prove current capacity. These last conceptual distinctions are inherited; the new credit belongs only to the exact computed/proved cross-layer relation and controls.

## What this can say about an organisation

Name the intended narrow institutional type before claiming application: a constituted service/reporting office is one candidate. State separately which founding/succession rule individuates that institution, why it is legitimate/constitutive in that type, and what actual causal transition instantiates it. A new attestation or operational theorem cannot establish this merely by an uninterpreted identifier.

A successful result would show: information/knowledge can continue while operative service does not; a constructed successor can continue the declared organisation-level service despite changed material/encoding; and the role or institution's constitutive identity requires additional, identified source evidence. If that last premise cannot be earned, the appropriate result is a precisely narrowed underdetermination claim with matched histories, not a declaration that institutional identity has been solved.

## Stopping boundary

Stop when the finite occurrence/translation cases and two-handoff service class have explicit semantics, all positive/negative outcomes are independently checked, the candidate all-retractions relation has a proof or counterexample, and the residual institution-constitution premise is precisely located. A failed general characterisation with a discriminating countermodel is acceptable. Do not pursue universal natural-language semantics, every actual institution, personal identity, indefinite physical survival or global revelation authentication to finish this bounded work.
