disposition: REPAIR_REQUIRED

The audit was conducted against the frozen V1 candidate and primary finite semantics, not against a desired personal-architecture conclusion.

## Blocking findings

### AQ-F01 — source compatibility was compressed into one Boolean

The candidate must preserve authentication, translation, source reconstruction, version, scope applicability, and world-link admissibility as separate coordinates. Equal source-role compatibility alone cannot select an actual-world referent.

### AQ-F02 — evidence update and final preference were conflated

Equal likelihood entails no **evidential update**. It does not entail equal posterior probability under unequal priors. The theorem must distinguish likelihood parity from final preference.

### AQ-F03 — zero-probability events were untreated

Conditional odds are undefined when both candidate probabilities vanish. The repaired statement must restrict Bayes-factor claims to positive-probability events or use extended likelihood-ratio conventions explicitly.

### AQ-F04 — the representation must be candidate-independent

A channel selected differently for A and R, or after observing which candidate wins, defines a changed experiment and can encode the verdict. The data-processing statement requires one frozen common Markov channel.

### AQ-F05 — source exclusion and predictive surplus were not typed separately

A source-conditioned admissibility filter is not a predictive likelihood ratio. The repaired packet must preserve distinct source, evidence, and prior/coding gates.

### AQ-F06 — version custody was under-specified

Same source bytes do not imply current applicability. The source gate must be version- and scope-indexed.

### AQ-F07 — experiment completeness was overread

Equality on the registered experiment does not establish equality on every possible experiment. The conclusion must remain relative to the frozen experiment family.

### AQ-F08 — ancestry and novelty ceilings were incomplete

The mathematical components are standard Bayes-odds, data-processing, automorphism invariance, and interpretation-fibre facts already represented by Deep AN, AO, AP, AF, and AL. General mathematical novelty is zero.

## Required repair

Create V2 with the six-coordinate source contract, three explicit gates, positive-event guard, common-channel guard, frozen-experiment scope, swap-invariant score theorem, hidden-anchor and version-drift controls, and exact ancestry/nonclaim ceilings.
