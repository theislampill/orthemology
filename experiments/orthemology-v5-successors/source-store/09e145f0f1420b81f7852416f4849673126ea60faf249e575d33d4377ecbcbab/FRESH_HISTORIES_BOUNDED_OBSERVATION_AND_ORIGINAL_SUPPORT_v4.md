# Fresh histories, bounded observation, and original support

Twelfth Orthemology research • 4 October 2026

Research candidate. Written mathematical proofs and philosophical assessment; no formal implementation or canonical change. Independent mathematical and source-accounting review is in progress.

## Result

A proposed causal history must supply more than a trajectory satisfying its local equations. It must also support the chance law those equations are alleged to describe. This investigation makes that distinction precise for an endlessly upstream additive process.

The mathematical contribution is an explicit finite-additive concentration bound. If a real-valued state is independent of an ordinary Gaussian innovation of variance v, the probability that their sum lies in an interval of length L is at most L/√(2πv). The proof needs only real-valued finite additivity, the stated independence, and the Gaussian marginal's tight tail. It does not require finite propagation or a countably additive joint distribution.

Consequently, a fixed present state cannot have positive probability in even one bounded interval while admitting independent Gaussian innovation blocks of arbitrarily large variance from earlier states. Full tightness and joint countable additivity are stronger than needed for that contradiction. A quantitative consequence measures how much state–future-noise dependence bounded capture requires. A coherent finitely additive control shows why the positive bounded-capture premise matters: every sample state is a finite real number, yet every bounded interval has probability zero.

The foundational consequence is more discriminating than a general rejection of infinite ancestry. An independently warranted fresh forward mechanism can support the independence assumption; a justified finite-range observation model can supply positive bounded capture. Both must belong to the same probability state and interpretation. This identifies an eligible warrant route, without supplying new evidence from an identified actual mechanism or calibration. What remains to be warranted for this original-source inference is that the particular wholly received productive support under consideration could realise that precise mechanism and observation contract. Neither the actual correction nor arbitrary causal arrows supply that modal bridge.

## 3. The exact probabilistic commitment

Work on one probability space with an ordinary real-valued, nonnegative, normalised, finitely additive probability P. All interval, tail, rectangle, and sum events used below are measurable. Let Y and Z be real-valued states, with:

- interval-rectangle independence: P(Y∈A, Z∈B)=P(Y∈A)P(Z∈B);
- Z's interval and tail probabilities given by an ordinary centred Gaussian of variance v>0.

This fixes the relevant independence. Mutual independence of a sequence of increments is weaker: an earlier state might encode those future increments. Nor is independence inferred merely from writing Y+Z as a next state.

For a historical application, let X_0 be one fixed terminal state. At horizon n, write

X_0 = X_(-n) + Z_n,

where Z_n has law N(0,n) and is independent of X_(-n) in the specified sense. All horizons concern the same law and terminal state. A suitable forward filtration with genuinely fresh Gaussian innovations would supply these finite-block assumptions. Their causal interpretation remains a substantive premise.

No property of an infinitely distant initial event is assumed. The proof compares arbitrarily long finite blocks within the proposed complete history.

## 4. Finite-additive Gaussian concentration

Let I be a bounded interval of length L, contained in [−R,R]. Fix δ>0 and M>R. Partition [−M,M] into finitely many disjoint half-open interval cells C_j of length at most δ, including the final right endpoint in the last cell.

If Y∈C_j and Y+Z∈I, then Z lies in an interval D_j of length at most L+δ. The Gaussian density is at most c_v=1/√(2πv). Independence and positivity therefore give

P(Y∈C_j, Y+Z∈I) ≤ P(Y∈C_j)c_v(L+δ).

Sum over the finite partition. The contribution from |Y|≤M is at most c_v(L+δ). On the remaining sum event, |Y|>M and Y+Z∈[−R,R] force |Z|>M−R. Hence

P(Y+Z∈I) ≤ c_v(L+δ) + P(|Z|>M−R).

The Gaussian tail becomes arbitrarily small as M grows. Because the probability value is an ordinary real number, the bound holds without the tail term. Letting δ become arbitrarily small yields

P(Y+Z∈I) ≤ L/√(2πv).

Only finite sums were used. The limiting step is an order argument about ordinary real error bounds; it is not countable additivity of P. Tightness of Y was never assumed. The tail was controlled by Z because, on the event of interest, a very large Y would require a very large compensating Z.

Infinite support of the noise prevents an exact finite list of possible starting values, but its tight marginal permits sufficiently accurate finite partitions.

## 5. The sharp bounded-capture consequence

Apply the bound at every horizon to the same terminal event. For every bounded I,

P(X_0∈I) ≤ |I|/√(2πn) for every n,

so P(X_0∈I)=0.

Thus one independently justified assertion P(X_0∈I)>0 contradicts the full history package. Tightness of X_0 would imply such an assertion, and countable additivity of a real-valued terminal marginal would imply tightness. Neither stronger condition is logically needed once positive mass for one bounded interval is available.

There is a prospective observation interpretation. If a capture event C has positive probability and valid capture certifies X_0∈I for a fixed bounded operating range I, then P(X_0∈I)≥P(C)>0. A warranted operating model can therefore supply exactly the probability premise the obstruction needs.

A realised reading alone does not supply it. An event of probability zero need not be logically impossible. Moreover, conditioning on a successful present observation can alter the relation between an earlier state and its future innovation block. A posterior bound on X_0 must not be combined with an unconditional freshness law that the conditioning destroys. The observation and innovation claims must belong to the same law, conditioning state, and explanatory respect.

This is a substantive warrant requirement. A calibrated prospective chance model can give positive reason for bounded capture. Pointwise finiteness of a state, merely seeing a reading, or the possibility of eventually searching for it cannot replace that model.

## 6. Quantifying the dependence required by bounded capture

The bound can diagnose more than exact independence. Let P_n be an actual joint law of (X_(-n),Z_n) satisfying the block recurrence and the Gaussian Z_n marginal. Let Q_n be an independent benchmark law with the same two marginals and the required measurable sum events. Under finite additivity, no unique canonical product extension is assumed; the conclusion applies to any benchmark meeting that contract.

Use the inherited convention d_TV(P_n,Q_n)=sup_A |P_n(A)−Q_n(A)|, which lies between zero and one. For E_I={(y,z):y+z∈I}, the actual law gives P_n(E_I)=ρ_I, where ρ_I=P(X_0∈I), while the independent law gives Q_n(E_I)≤|I|/√(2πn). Therefore

d_TV(P_n,Q_n) ≥ max(0, ρ_I−|I|/√(2πn)).

A genuinely warranted bounded-capture probability thus gives a finite-horizon lower bound on the failure of the independent state–block account. It does not require an infinite-history conclusion to be useful. One positive ρ_I gives an asymptotic lower bound of at least ρ_I, rather than automatically forcing maximal distance.

If the one fixed terminal law is tight, choose a bounded interval I_ε with probability greater than 1−ε, and only then let n grow. The inequality gives liminf d_TV≥1−ε. As ε was arbitrary, d_TV tends to one. This is an elementary application of the concentration estimate and the inherited event-distance method, not a new general theory of statistical dependence.

The measured separation would still not identify an original supplier. It could reflect global preparation, anticipatory state selection, dependence among operative resources, or failure of the offered stochastic interpretation. Those explanations require further evidence. The useful gain is an exact quantitative burden for the independence claim at the same law and target, not a source conclusion from correlation.

## 7. A coherent finite-additive history

The absence of bounded capture does not itself make the remaining mathematical specification inconsistent. A concrete control distinguishes the roles of finite additivity and observational regularity.

Use the fixed Borel sample space Ω=R×R^Z, with coordinates (x,ξ). The noise coordinates have their ordinary countably additive iid N(0,1) law Q. Put X_0=x and define X_t=x+S_t, where S_t is the appropriate finite signed sum of innovations between zero and t. Every state is a finite real number and every recurrence X_(t+1)=X_t+ξ_t holds pointwise for all integer times.

For positive integer R, let P_R make x uniform on [−R,R] and independent of all noise. Fix one nonprincipal ultrafilter and take the ultralimit of P_R(A) for every Borel event A. This defines one ordinary real-valued, normalised, finitely additive probability μ. Its entire noise marginal is exactly Q.

Full past–future independence requires proof because the ordinary anchored laws do not automatically have it at negative times. Fix t. Compare P_R with the law under which X_t, rather than X_0, is uniform on [−R,R] independently of all noise. Their total-variation distance is at most

E_Q[min(1, |S_t|/(2R))],

which tends to zero. This follows from the exact distance between a uniform interval law and its translate, followed by ordinary dominated convergence under Q. The bound is uniform over all Borel events, not just finite-coordinate cylinders.

Under the reanchored comparison law, all past states are determined by X_t and earlier noise, and are independent of future noise. The vanishing comparison error passes that factorisation to μ. This proves rectangle independence of the full past state/noise sigma-field from the future innovation sigma-field at every fixed time. Reanchoring also gives a stationary law and translation-invariant position marginals. No countable intersection of separate probability-one claims is used; the recurrences already hold pointwise.

Every bounded position interval has μ-probability zero, although each sample position is finite. Thus the position marginal is not countably additive: countably many bounded unit intervals cover R but each is null. The noise marginal remains countably additive. A nonprincipal ultrafilter is an explicit nonconstructive choice assumption; no sampler or effective construction is claimed.

The control establishes consistency of one law with zero bounded capture. It does not classify all non-tight marginals or supply an entrance law for every such marginal.

This is a coherent probability specification, not a metaphysical-possibility certificate. Statistical past–future independence, even when exact, does not itself establish intervention-level causal autonomy. The law and recurrence alone do not decide actual productive direction. The anchored parametrisation nevertheless permits explicit finite forward-intervention semantics; expressing earlier states in terms of later coordinates is no barrier to that construction.

Fix an integer j and a real replacement value a. Put δ=a−ξ_j, replace ξ_j by a, leave the other innovations unchanged, and set x′=x+δ when j<0 and x′=x otherwise. This is a Borel map on Ω. Direct substitution gives X′_t=X_t for t≤j and X′_t=X_t+δ for t>j. Thus the whole prehistory through X_j is fixed, the selected innovation changes, and its effect propagates forward. All recurrences remain valid. For a finite set J of prescribed replacements, the anchor adjustment is Σ_(j∈J,j<0)(a_j−ξ_j), and X′_t−X_t=Σ_(j∈J,j<t)(a_j−ξ_j). Replacements at distinct times commute.

The pushforward is again a real-valued finitely additive probability. Its unchanged innovations retain their joint Gaussian law; selected coordinates are fixed at their prescribed values. Full past–future factorisation survives because the transformed past uses only original past information and the transformed future noise uses only original future information. This is an intervention pushforward, not conditioning on a null Gaussian event. The construction supplies positive abstract forward semantics, while leaving actual manipulability and physical or metaphysical realisability to further warrant. It licenses finitely many prescribed replacements. An infinite-past family could require a divergent anchor-displacement sum, so no arbitrary infinitary recombination follows.

The control sharpens an observation boundary. Let a search expand its inspected range to [−m,m] at budget m. It terminates on every finite-real sample, yet every fixed-budget success event has probability zero under μ. Pointwise termination is not positive capture at any fixed finite budget. Earlier finite-observation work owns the general distinction; this is its specific application to the stochastic control.

## 8. Positive controls against overgeneralisation

### Paths do not settle the chance law

Fix X_0=0 and take mutually independent ordinary Gaussian increments. Define earlier states by backward finite sums from zero. All step equations hold and the increments remain mutually independent. But X_(-n) is defined using its future block, so it is not independent of that block. This gives a perfectly ordinary probability law for paths while failing the precise fresh-history premise.

The construction establishes an inferential distinction. The recurrence alone does not identify the actual productive direction of the represented dependence.

### Infinite ancestry need not spread probability without bound

For 0<|a|<1 and an integer-indexed family of independent N(0,1) innovations ξ_t, define

X_t = Σ_(j≥0) a^j ξ_(t−1−j).

The Gaussian series converges in L² and almost surely, with squared coefficients summing to 1/(1−a²). Countability of the time indices gives one common probability-one set on which all recurrences X_(t+1)=aX_t+ξ_t hold. The states are measurable with respect to earlier innovations and have a stationary tight Gaussian law. Future innovations are statistically independent of the past state information under this law. Every earlier innovation has a nonzero contribution coefficient, but accumulated innovation variance over a block stays bounded.

This familiar stable-process control is not a metaphysical validation of infinite ancestry. Its exogenous noise coordinates do not automatically denote uncaused concrete resources. It shows why the obstruction's accumulating-variance dynamics, rather than the bare number of contributing stages, matters. It changes a substantive law and therefore does not refute the proved bound.

### Lack of a stationary probability is insufficient

The deterministic integer transition x↦x+1 has a coherent nonstationary history X_t=t, with marginal point mass at t. It has no countably additive invariant probability on the integers, but it has perfectly well-defined countably additive marginals at every time. A finitely additive invariant position mean is a different contract. Thus the relevant obstruction must address a whole family of compatible marginals; absence of a stationary distribution alone is too weak.

These controls retain positive mathematical content rather than treating every infinite representation as equally suspect. They do not decide the actual world's ultimate constitution.

## 9. What could warrant the causal premises?

The strongest affirmative identifies a real or rationally defensible spontaneous mechanism. Its new input has the stated chance law under each relevant earlier state; the preparation does not encode the outcome; and no relevant common preparation or global constraint defeats the claimed independence. Freshness then belongs to the defended operating account. It is more than a stipulation attached to an arrow.

A forward productive interpretation supplies a related reason. A permitted change of the newly supplied input should not, solely to preserve a chosen final outcome, retrospectively change the state from which the mechanism operates. An anticipatively selected family of paths may fail that interpretation even when the numerical equations look right.

Forward causal direction by itself is still insufficient for independence. Two forward processes can be correlated by a common preparation. Likewise, a finite recursive system often derives freshness from earlier independent inputs, whereas an endlessly upstream collection does not inherit that derivation merely by repeating the same local equation. The positive judgement must concern the actual chance relation, including its appropriate information domain.

An original source could make a genuine difference here. It might supply all the relevant powers and coordinate their states globally, changing exclusive availability or state–future-noise dependence while preserving real subordinate contributions. It would then be doing substantive work. But if it leaves every premise of the incompatible fresh-history law unchanged, supplying additional power does not remove the incompatibility.

A nonoriginal account can also propose joint restrictions on its powers. Those restrictions are not vindicated merely by being proposed, but neither does their existence automatically amount to a further productive donation. To infer an original coordinator, an argument must identify the actual coordination resource and justify its original productive role. Constitutive organisation and productive provision must retain their inherited distinction.

## 10. The separate realisability bridge

The strongest cosmological use is a modal reductio. If every actual entirely received support architecture could realise the prohibited fresh process while retaining positive bounded capture, the mathematical obstruction would exclude such a complete support architecture. A general theory independently warranting that realisability claim could provide genuine evidence for original participation. Its strength or disagreement about it would not disqualify it as rational warrant.

A general principle would suffice, but the reductio needs only a warranted realisability claim for the particular actual support under consideration. An independently justified structural class containing that support could also suffice. Neither approach must cover every conceivable received architecture. The bridge must preserve the complete relevant supply respect; classifying temporal transitions alone would not establish exhaustive existential reception.

There are positive reasons to investigate the claim. Actual modular powers can often vary their manifestations independently. A well-characterised randomisation can be insulated from local preparation. A finite-range observation device can have a justified positive capture probability. These are more substantial than saying that every logically described history is possible.

Their joint extension is nevertheless the disputed step. Compatibility of each finite operating segment does not itself establish one law for all segments together. Nor does an arbitrary received ancestry necessarily supply clocked additive dynamics, indefinitely growing independent block variance, or the same observational chance model. The impossibility of an entrance law combining these fresh additive Gaussian blocks with positive bounded capture identifies what the modal extension would have to provide.

A direct basic recognition of the stronger extension remains eligible. The test is whether its content actually concerns this joint realisability, rather than only local powers or an appetite for explanation. An abductive defence likewise must explain why the independent-modules account is appropriate to the actual support, rather than naming whatever would yield an original source.

The admitted correction supplies no new classification of its entire productive ancestry as that architecture. This is why the present result sharpens an existence-entailing strategy without establishing an actual original participant, a common bearer, or N2/N3.

## 11. Exact increment and current status

The investigation has moved from a generic paradox proposal to a precise interface between causal interpretation, probability, and observation. Its mathematical increment is the finite-additive Gaussian bound, the weak positive bounded-capture contradiction, its quantitative state–block dependence consequence, and a coherent finitely additive history showing the regularity boundary. Its philosophical increment is a precisely scoped route by which mechanism and observation evidence could warrant the stochastic premises, separated from the remaining actual-support realisability claim. The route is eligible and well specified; no new actual mechanism, calibration, or classification of the correction's support has been established here.

The proof does not identify dependence with causation, infer a productive source from a statistical correlation, or derive intervention-level causal autonomy from probability-law independence. It does not refute general causal finitism, validate a rootless cosmos, or assume that every infinite ancestry shares one probability law. Inherited positive original-possession and rational-recognition credit remain intact.

Along this stochastic route, the next substantive source-warrant advance would be an independently supported realisability claim for the selected actual support at this exact scope, or an actual productive coordination account. Another causal diagram, an uninspected author's label, or an observed finite endpoint without a same-law chance account would not supply it. Independent original-participation arguments need not pass through this stochastic route.
