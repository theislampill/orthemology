## The accepted findings in simple terms

### An explanation for a young reader

A group can really do something together. Two people may lift a table that neither could lift alone. Calling them a team helps describe their joint action, but the new name does not show where all their strength originally came from. Likewise, showing that a whole has a power is different from showing that it originally supplies everything needed for that power. The research makes that distinction sharper. It does not show that complex things cannot be genuine agents. \[ASM; ACC\]

There is a second lesson about careful replacement. Imagine a recipe containing two sorts of blanks: blanks for ingredients and blanks for kinds of ingredient. Replacing one sort must not accidentally capture a blank that belongs to a smaller recipe inside it. The formal result proves that two exact replacement operations preserve proofs in the existing language. It is a proof about that language, with its particular rules. It does not prove that every possible recipe language has the same property. \[F1–F3\]

The new typed language can also build a pair containing something and a small certificate that it equals itself in its type. Its rules prove that this construction behaves properly when related inputs are used. Matching something on one side with something on another side does not automatically make two things within either side equal. Those are separate checks. \[N1–N4\]

The successor makes the pair recipe work for every type allowed by the language, including a type that itself describes such a recipe. It also proves that replacing both kinds of blank together keeps the recipe valid, even when an inserted type depends on an earlier ingredient. This is the new combination; the earlier pieces supplied only parts of it. \[ALL1–ALL3\]

These examples explain distinctions; they are not observations establishing an original source, experiments about agency, or a model of the full metaphysics.

### An explanation for an older student

The philosophical question separates causal contribution, possession of a power, and original provision of its productive basis. A person can genuinely understand evidence and correct a mistake while using capacities that the person did not originally supply. A composite may likewise have real powers through its organisation. The disputed inference is from those facts to one particular bearer originally accounting for the correction's complete actual productive origin. The accepted articles show where an attempted inference changes its accounting relation or requires an additional warrant. \[ASM; ACC\]

The formal question also contains two kinds of variable. A source index is a variable inside the polynomial representing a source term. A type parameter is a variable in the type expression. Pi and Sigma bind source indices; All binds a type parameter. Substitution must respect each binding discipline, and a type inserted for a parameter can itself contain source indices. The two proved admissibility theorems construct evidence in the original judgement after either kind of substitution. Their interaction therefore transforms the source indices inside the inserted type images as well. \[F1–F3\]

The new nucleus goes further than raw-index replacement. A variable may have an arbitrary declared type, and later types can depend on earlier variables. Its theorem says that a term built by the finite typing rules preserves the declared relation between two valid assignments. Identity is checked inside each assignment’s type; a relation connecting the assignments is not automatically an equality rule inside either one. \[N1–N3\]

The successor adds genuine All, which binds a type parameter, to the syntax-generated typed contexts. Uniformity across type choices is proved from its rules rather than supplied as an assumption. Its mixed-substitution theorem handles term and type replacement together, including open replacement types. A concrete witness packages a value with a typed equality certificate for every type, then instantiates that polymorphic package at its own type. This is a bounded impredicative result, not a universe or a normalisation theorem. \[ALL1–ALL3\]

Neither kind of progress settles the other. A kernel proof can establish that a syntactic operation preserves a judgement without establishing that a real bearer satisfies the judgement's intended metaphysical interpretation.

## Composition and the original productive bearer

### The target and the inherited positive case

The fixed philosophical target is the admitted reason-responsive correction concerning a displayed Minhāj bibliographic header. It is a correction made because the subject understands the displayed evidence. It is not a claim about a complete edition, a coincidentally correct output, or a new empirical finding about consciousness. \[ASM §1\]

P1 concerns original productive completion for that correction's whole actual origin. P2 concerns the particular bearer of that original actuality. The inherited argument grants real recipient agency and prima-facie explanatory force to original possession. Those positive commitments remain in place. They neither require the rejection of internal complexity nor make every describable alternative a defeater. A suitably directed basic recognition or abduction remains eligible as warrant. The present inquiry does not establish a new actual bearer with the required complete scope. \[ASM §§1–2, 7–9\]

The new contribution is a focused application to an assembly inference. Several contributors might jointly account for a target, and they might compose a concrete whole. It does not follow merely from those two claims that the whole originally owns every contribution in the same productive respect. The issue survives the admission that the whole is real: reality of the composite does not itself reconcile incompatible original-source attributions. \[ASM §4\]

### The exact conditional conflict

The application can be stated precisely without presenting its inherited principle as a new theorem. Fix a target T, a whole w, a member b and a proper source exercise m. Suppose m belongs to the relevant actual productive origin of T; b owns m unborrowedly in the stated ultimate productive respect; w is history-complete for that same target and respect; and w and b are distinct bearers. Under the inherited accounting interpretation, history-completeness puts m within w's ultimate productive account, while the unborrowed-exercise exclusion yields $\mathrm{w}\ =\ \mathrm{b}$. Together with $\mathrm{w}\ \ne{}\ \mathrm{b}$, these premises are inconsistent. \[ASM §4; ASM-R §2\]

One member exercise suffices for this application. No finite number of members or first temporal member is assumed. Its force nevertheless depends on retaining the same exercise, target, productive respect and accounting interpretation. Independence only from outsiders is weaker than proper unborrowed possession: members can receive from one another or the whole. An intrinsic feature is not automatically a distinct source-bearer. The conditional conflict cannot be applied before those interpretative questions are settled. \[ASM §§4–5\]

The possible responses are substantive. A whole could be originally productive while its members genuinely act derivatively. It could instead possess a narrower joint capacity. Apparent members could be intrinsic aspects of one bearer. Alternatively, a theory could defend joint original possession under a different accounting relation. These are ways to clarify the disputed inference, not an exhaustive catalogue of possible worlds or certification of a preferred ontology. Changing the relation also removes any automatic entitlement to the old uniqueness consequence. \[ASM §5; ASM-R §4\]

### What the result does and does not earn

The application identifies a specific conflict in promoting a complete plural explanation into a complete original bearer while retaining incompatible member attributions. It does not newly prove the inherited ownership condition, supply a general criterion of organisational ownership, refute composite sources, or establish an adequate plural ultimate ontology. No actual P1/P2 or necessary participant follows. Its value is the more exact burden it places on the proposed route to that conclusion. \[ASM §9; ASM-R §§5–6\]

## Original provision and constitutive possession

The second article examines the accounting relation itself. An actual receiving mode can depend on a particular source exercise without being numerically identical to that exercise. If the target is reception through that source, a purported complete original account cannot leave the indispensable efficacy wholly supplied outside its account. This is the inherited positive inclusion warrant, retained rather than newly discovered here. An endpoint-only claim would have a narrower target; silently adding the actual receiving mode would enlarge it. \[ACC §1; ACC-R §1\]

A simple case helps locate the distinction. A lamp's receiving electricity through a particular cable requires that cable's relevant conducting activity. A different cable might light the same lamp, but it would not thereby be the same actual reception through the first cable. This illustrates why endpoint sufficiency and completeness for an actual receiving mode differ. It does not identify an ultimate original supplier or resolve a theory of constitution.

The new clarification concerns a further step. If b receives the efficacy of an allegedly unborrowed exercise from another independent source, it is borrowed in that respect. But if a second bearer claims constitutive possession without claiming to donate that efficacy to b, mere distinctness does not establish the donation premise. Conversely, a constitutive label does not establish that the two bearers originally possess the same proper exercise. \[ACC §§2–3; ACC-R §§2–3\]

The positive subject-indexed rationale must also be preserved. A proper exercise is the actual agency of its subject, rather than an event with an interchangeable actor label. Two subject-indexed acts can be realised in one occurrence without being one proper act. The question is whether this rationale warrants the asserted exclusion in the particular constitutive interpretation. It cannot be settled simply by stipulating an exclusive predicate, but neither is it defeated by describing a different predicate. \[ACC §2; ACC-R §2\]

A purported original co-ownership case therefore needs distinct actual source-bearers, one token proper exercise, the same absolute productive respect, the required full-origin accounting, and the same bearer-identity relation throughout. One connected rational presentation could support these claims; they are not demands for five independent experiments. The assessed case does not newly warrant their conjunction. \[ACC §§3–4; ACC-R §3\]

The two articles consequently have different but compatible roles. The assembly article applies an inherited conditional while retaining its interpretation. The accounting article clarifies what must be warranted before that interpretation covers constitutive co-possession. Neither settles actual original completion, particular original sourcehood or necessity. Neither converts an unestablished rival into a refutation of the inherited affirmative. \[ASM-R; ACC-R\]
