# Full face-threshold forward-KL asymptotic

This new scratch packet proves, for the inherited Joe(n+1,n/(n+1)) versus product-n hard pair, that the full pair of coordinate-minimum thresholds has

    KL(alternative || reference) ~ 1/(2n^4).

The predecessor proves infinite chi-square for this same channel at each n. The present proof resolves the resulting KL/sample-exponent question with a uniform C2 expansion on growing logarithmic boxes and a dependence-safe exponential-margin entropy tail estimate.

Read RESULT.md for the theorem and proof. SOURCE_AUDIT.md distinguishes directly checked calculations from inherited inputs. NEGATIVE_CONTROLS.md states failed shortcuts and observation-class exclusions. controls.py and CONTROL_RESULTS.json contain bounded symbolic and deterministic numerical diagnostics. SOURCE_BINDINGS.json identifies read-only predecessors. REVIEW_STATUS.json and the separate full-face-threshold-kl-review packet record the fresh independent PASS bound to the final result hash. The review itself is full-face-threshold-kl-review/RESULT.md.
