# Warranted continuation after fork, translation and merge

Status: Ninth reorientation research synthesis, 3 October 2026. Ordinary mathematical/interpretive research; no new formal implementation, canonical edit, publication, or general novelty claim. This memo preserves the accepted v5 work and separates inherited results from the candidate synthesis. Direct source recovery subsequently showed that the main export/next-state mechanism is already in H-MOAT-E3. The restricted application in `RESTRICTED_REPAIR_AND_NEXT_HANDOFF_MODEL.md` must therefore be credited as a source-sensitive application/control, not a new theorem. `SYNTHESIS_RESULT.md` gives the final disposition.

## 1. Strongest question and provisional answer

The useful question is not whether a successor matches a predecessor's present output. It is whether the recipient can still use the predecessor's reasons and organisation for the declared future tasks, under the actual meanings, source events, permissions, realisers and available interventions of this transition.

The missing composition issue is **joint binding**. Local packets can each carry a possible interpretation, a valid conditional authority proof, and an adequate solo continuation. They do not thereby describe one simultaneously realisable continuation. The same source occurrence cannot receive one interpretation in one branch and an incompatible interpretation in another merely because the corresponding proofs are separately convenient. A source's one-use grant cannot be counted as two grants after copying. Nor may a successor's future reacquisition ability be supplied by a source that the very migration has destroyed.

This is not a claim that genuinely applicable factive warrants in one actual world can have a false conjunction. The weak local premise is a separately existing admissibility witness, often relative to a different completion of missing information or a different solo execution. Calling those witnesses already actual, jointly applicable warrant would beg the question. The quantifier change from separately available witnesses to one compatible actual episode is the burden to investigate.

No numerical/metaphysical identity theorem follows. A task-relative continuation relation can branch: two successors can retain the same reasons and capability, where the source permits this. A merge can join previously distinct contributions. Numerical identity cannot simply be read off that relation.

## 3. Proposed relational interface, before formal implementation

Start with the declared organisation/task contract, not a universal definition of identity. Its variables arise from the actual task. Examples include operative organisational state, source-assertion occurrence, reading, version, role bearer, issuer, grant, allocated successor, physical realiser, and available future observations or interventions.

A local packet over variable set V_i represents a relation R_i of the alternatives still compatible with its evidence. An alternative is a joint assignment, not an independently selectable value for each column. Its provenance records which source event and which transformation justify the constraint. In particular:

* a new quotation or translation occurrence has its own token identity while referring to an earlier source occurrence;
* a quotation can preserve content and source attribution while changing the current speech act;
* an authority condition is attached to the issuer's actual grant and its scope, not to the mere presence of a syntactically valid certificate;
* copied evidence can occur at several recipients while its acquisition origin remains one event;
* a resource-use or reservation record is shared on the relevant source key rather than reset by each representation change.

The mathematical join of the local relations describes simultaneous assignments compatible with all recorded constraints. This join is an analysis object, not an authentication procedure and not a definition of warranted continuation. Three separate questions remain:

1. **Compatibility:** Is there a common assignment? Can each surviving local alternative actually extend to one?
2. **Decision adequacy:** Do all alternatives still warranted by the receiver's evidence support the same proposed adoption/action, or does the receiver need more evidence or a contingent policy?
3. **Realised continuation:** Is that policy currently authorised and causally executable, and does its actual successor retain the information, permissions and support needed for the declared downstream tasks?

A nonempty join answers only a possibility question. It is not the receiver's selection of the true tuple, a licence to choose a favourable reading, a mechanism for bringing the tuple about, or continuing competence.

The intended extra result is a precise composition obstruction and a bounded repair interface for this relation. It is not `Continuation iff field1 and ... and field5`.

## 4. Discriminating original-programme instances

### A. Ambiguity survives each branch but not their binding

One source occurrence has two admissible readings. Two descendants each faithfully report their two local readings and the same source occurrence and version. There are at least two possible source-bound alignments:

* parallel: {(0,0),(1,1)};
* crossed: {(0,1),(1,0)}.

Both preserve each descendant's complete unary ambiguity set {0,1}. A downstream operation that must align the two realisers needs action 0 for the parallel relation and action 1 for the crossed relation. Neither operation requires resolving whether the actual reading is 0 or 1. It requires retaining how the ambiguities are related.

If copying/translation keeps only the two sets and the source label, the receiver cannot choose that operation correctly in both cases. An exact alignment relation repairs the loss without inventing a preferred interpretation. MLT covers a supplied bijection's coherent use; it does not supply an erased map to a receiver or retain the future query interface for it. General lossy translation permits a relation, not only a bijection.

For three binary descendants, even complete pairwise marginals need not settle a joint reading constraint: the even-parity and odd-parity triples have identical projections onto every pair. This is a standard higher-order-correlation control, not a new parity theorem. It prevents a repair that merely adds more pairwise labels.

### B. Locally available source authority fails after simultaneous forking

An authentic source grants one of two successor roles the right to perform one exclusive operation. Each successor's solo projection has a valid execution in which it is selected. Copying the grant packet to both does not exhibit one history selecting both. The local possible-authorisation judgements survive; their simultaneous use does not.

If instead the source explicitly grants a shareable right to both, both uses can be legitimate. The obstruction concerns the actual grant's allocation semantics. It is not a claim that branching is intrinsically unlawful.

This also exposes the difference between a new occurrence identity and a grant-use identity. A translated or reserialised instruction is a new occurrence; it does not, by that fact, reset the earlier grant's use count or expiry.

### C. An exact source-history isomorphism does not supply a fork–merge

Let one complete source command k be mapped to k_A by a whole-history transport T_A and to a different k_B by T_B. Each separately translated history may be legal under the Ninth theorem. No single function on their shared source-command occurrence can have both values. One cannot combine the two conclusions into a common transformed state without declaring what duplication, origin tracking and shared-store semantics now mean.

The accepted negative controls are directly relevant: unchanged commitments, tombstones and receipt keys do not automatically apply to a transformed command. Taking a union of translated records can change progress; dropping one branch's records can change safety. A real merge needs a new applicable joint transition, not only two isomorphism proofs.

### D. Present state and output continue, future calibration does not

A replacement realiser can preserve the declared current operative state and output but erase a source-bound calibration/alignment relation required for a later translation or further transfer. If it retains a genuine authorised reacquisition interface, the later capacity can remain available. If migration destroys that source or the sole credential for reaching it, promising to reread later supplies no capacity.

This instance follows the historical distinction between first and recursive apoautosis. A finite successful handoff cannot be promoted to indefinite repeatability. Required downstream task families, budgets and support must be explicit.

### E. Same outward organisation, different origin

A causally produced successor and an independently produced lookalike may have the same present state, outputs, stored proof text and stated lineage identifier. A snapshot-only check cannot discriminate them without independent evidence tying this concrete occurrence to the transfer event. The independent lookalike may acquire its own valid warrant or rights; behavioural sameness alone does not give it the predecessor's causal history or grant.

This is an applicability obstruction. It does not prove that one object is or is not the numerically same subject, nor does it identify an ultimate metaphysical source.

### F. Quotation, assertion and faithful translation

A translated quotation can faithfully retain the original assertion, its ambiguity and who made it while being only a report in its present context. Treating the quote as a fresh grant or a second independent testimony is invalid. Conversely, changing speech act is not necessarily a defective translation: a pedagogical report can legitimately quote a directive. The receiver must retain which force belongs to the source occurrence and which belongs to the present one.

## 5. A sharp bounded ambiguity-retention result

This is a supporting upper-scope counting illustration, not the main continuation-competence result, a claimed new general theorem, or a kernel result. Requiring every membership query forces preservation of essentially the entire arbitrary relation by choice of the task family. The restricted next-repair problem in the companion note needs only one task-relevant alignment bit, rather than this large representation.

Fix k >= 2 descendants, each with d >= 2 possible local readings. Let R be an arbitrary nonempty subset of [d]^k whose projection onto every coordinate is all of [d]. Thus all local ambiguity packets are exactly the same, regardless of R. The future task family consists of the finite compatibility queries Q_t(R): is tuple t in R? No later source query is available. A retained b-bit annotation must let one decoder answer every Q_t exactly for every allowed R.

**Lower bound.** There are at least 2^(d^k-d) allowed relations: include the d diagonal tuples (i,...,i), which already make every marginal full, and independently choose the remaining d^k-d tuples. Distinct relations disagree on at least one Q_t. Therefore the retained annotation must distinguish at least that many cases, so b >= d^k-d.

**Positive bound.** The d^k-bit characteristic table of R answers all queries, so b <= d^k is attainable without choosing a reading. With unrestricted finite computation, indexing the allowed relations gives the exact minimum ceiling(log2 N_{d,k}), where N_{d,k} counts full-marginal relations.

For two descendants, inclusion-exclusion gives

N_{d,2} = sum_{i=0}^d (-1)^i binom(d,i) (2^(d-i)-1)^d.

First require every row to be nonempty, then exclude empty columns. For d=2 there are seven relations and three bits are necessary and sufficient in a fixed-length coding model. Restricting to bijective alignments gives d! relations and the corresponding exact lower bound ceiling(log2(d!)).

**What the result adds here.** Keeping every branch's complete ambiguity set and source identifier can still require almost d^k additional bits of joint compatibility for unrestricted later occurrence tasks. The required interface can grow exponentially in branch count even though no branch loses its own readings. This makes the future-competence burden explicit and discriminates full relational transport from the already studied inverse-bijection setting.

**What it does not add.** This is elementary counting/separation applied to the specified occurrence task family. It does not replace V14 fibre recoverability, assert general completeness, supply real-world semantics, show that all applications need exponential memory, or claim that one yes/no query needs the entire table. It fixes simultaneous preservation of the whole future query family. Per-query access to an external source has a different budget. A compact genuine generative model may encode a restricted relation class much more cheaply.

## 6. Constructive bounded composition without resolving ambiguity

A useful positive protocol is available when the application's independently justified semantic/resource model factors over a join tree.

Each bag retains its exact relation; adjacent bags retain their exact common variables and agree on the separator relation. The running-intersection property says that every variable's bags form a connected subtree. Starting with any permitted tuple in one bag, choose a matching tuple in each adjacent bag and recurse. Separator agreement supplies each choice; running intersection ensures the choices agree wherever they overlap. The resulting joint assignment extends the starting tuple. Hence no permitted local tuple needs to be silently discarded, and compatible assignments can be constructed. This is the established acyclic database/CSP result, not new general mathematics.

For the programme, the separator must include the actual shared dependencies: a source-occurrence reading, version, shared entitlement allocation, or a future reacquisition dependency when those affect the task. Replacing a common dependency by two unlinked local names invalidates the premise. A higher-order capacity or compatibility factor cannot be omitted because its pairwise projections look harmless.

The receiver must then compute or justify an action/policy adequate across the still-live joint alternatives. If no such action is available, the valid result is a conditional claim, a request for discriminating evidence, or a refusal of that operation. Preserving ambiguity can preserve warrant for withholding a conclusion.

Execution must use the actual shared authority/reservation mechanism, and the realised successor must retain the required interface for the next declared tasks. The join proof cannot instantiate this mechanism. Thus the constructive result is a finite relation-and-query repair, to be attached to an independently supported realiser/authority history, not an apoautosis demonstration.

## 7. Application evidence and irreducible world binding

The receiver needs evidence appropriate to the live task, rather than a universally fixed checklist. The following are examples of load-bearing questions:

* Which actual source event, utterance, measurement, grant or acquisition does this packet report? Is the source identifier anchored independently of the copied packet?
* Does the interpretation retain the actual context, version, ambiguity and source force required by the query? Which correlations among alternatives survive?
* Which evidence is a new acquisition, and which is a repeated presentation of one source? Which dependence/fault model is actually warranted?
* Does an authorisation apply to this bearer, action, time, version and shared allocation state? Has a relevant cancellation or competing use occurred?
* Did the actual transfer occur through the claimed physical mechanism? Does the observed successor realise the operative state and required future response repertoire?
* Are promised rereads, maintenance, reconstruction, further migrations and necessary human/harness permissions actually available at the future decision point?

Authenticated bytes may establish integrity relative to an issuer or key. They do not establish the issuer's competence or normative authority, the truth of its statement, an unobserved physical causal bridge, or indefinitely available support. The mathematical relation cannot authenticate its own ground interpretation. This residual burden is not a defect removable by adding another self-issued certificate.
