# P15 research seed — propositions to test, not accepted results

This file distils the proposed research avenues that motivated P15. Every item below is a hypothesis, modelling proposal or target for proof/countermodel analysis.

## 1. Extra-structure thesis
A descriptive evidential state `e in E` does not by itself encode how distinct dimensions ought to trade off. Scalarisation weights, an improvement order, utility, a common objective, hard constraints or another normative comparison rule are candidate **bridge structures**. P15 must determine when this is a mathematically substantive statement rather than a definitional restatement.

## 2. Relative underdetermination
Let `D` be a fixed descriptive structure and `Ext(D)` a nontrivial, independently motivated class of admissible normative extensions. If two extensions agree on all descriptive facts but disagree on `phi`, then `phi` is not invariant across the fibre over `D`. P15 should formalise the exact entailment notion and prove the minimal result.

## 3. Scalarisation witnesses
For crossing/incomparable tuples, different admissible scalarisations may reverse a ranking. This can exhibit the location of an extra ordering premise. P15 must state the exact assumptions on weights/order and not generalise beyond them.

## 4. Game-theoretic repetition of the same boundary
A feasible mixed-policy space does not by itself define “profitable deviation.” Ordinary Nash best response requires a payoff/preference representation. P15 should expose this as an evaluation-entry boundary without claiming that utility is the only possible normative bridge.

## 5. Stability is not normativity
A descriptive fixed point of dynamics `G` and a normative optimum of `W` are different mathematical objects. P15 should give countermodels to automatic identification and identify conditions under which a valid bridge theorem could relate them.

## 6. Reduct/fibre/section picture
A normative structure can be viewed, where formally appropriate, as an enrichment whose reduct is `D`. The fibre over `D` collects admissible enrichments. A proposed is-to-ought rule behaves like a choice of enrichment/section. P15 must define “canonical” precisely before claiming non-canonicity.

## 7. Gap detector rather than gap bridge
The prospective Orthemology contribution is not a universal rule deriving ought from is. It is a disclosure mechanism: hold the source fixed, vary admissible bridge structure, and test whether the target judgement is invariant.

## 8. Generalisation
The same schema may apply to fact->value, evidence->ranking, observation->ontology, data->classification, model->decision, and descriptive-equilibrium->normative-optimum transitions. P15 must determine whether these are instances of one typed schema or merely analogies.

## 9. Machine visibility
A useful implementation target would attach bridge provenance to target-level judgements: exact descriptive input identity, bridge identity/version, target proposition, assumptions, invariance status, and witness extensions/countermodels. This is a proposed audit/certificate surface, not normative authority.

## 10. Scope ceiling
Even a successful relative-underdetermination theorem does not by itself settle Humean metaethics, normative supervenience, reductive naturalism, Moore's open-question argument, or global novelty. P15 must state the exact philosophical ceiling.
