## 1. What the original charter actually fixes

The original MEN-8 charter asks for ONE common interpretation satisfying the strongest lane-premise union while withholding its integrated conclusion, or a nonexistence proof under minimal independently warranted premises. Separate countermodels do not discharge that task.

The retained common-model theorem owner, AR8R-RP-001-v1, specifies a class K, signature, union Γ and conclusion C parametrically. It does not populate a final Γ or C. Its own provenance says that it is a post-campaign role-preserving replacement, not the unavailable historical T150 payload. Its logical result is ordinary semantic nonentailment and claims no general novelty.

The R5 current owner requires target-faithful identity, world truth, warrant and a changed consequence, with the original target relation, sufficiently complete representation, non-conclusion-encoding bridge, independently true premises, and survival against plural/external resources and representation incompleteness. R5 is a fixed checked-architecture control, not permission to invent stronger separated interfaces after a successful bridge.

P15/source-main retains the integration guide, disposition and reconciliation owners, but this selective local snapshot lacks its linked theory/ tree and several named program owners. This ledger uses the retained exact owner copies in Sixth selected-source-union and Seventh pinned-source. These are historical snapshot evidence, not a statement about current repository HEAD. A full all-lane frozen model signature is not recovered merely by reading the guide.

## 2. Three premise sets must not be silently merged

- ΓE: admitted explanandum and independently supported local facts, plus correctly scoped conditional theorems. A theorem A⇒B is included without asserting A.
- ΓG: ΓE plus explicitly granted strong source hypotheses used in the source and integrity comparisons. These are serious conditional concessions, not newly warranted actuality claims.
- ΓB: disputed bridging principles needed for necessity, ultimate cognition, intrinsic norm authority, essential perfection and recipient use. These may be examined or conditionally granted, but cannot be relabelled independently warranted because they occur in an accepted paper.

A strongest argument deserves all its actual premises at their actual strength. It does not follow that every defended premise is established, or that every premise employed by an alternative proof is jointly admitted. In particular the object-level and exact-origin-fact modal routes are alternatives; N9 integrity's direct-priority and counterpart-dominance routes are alternatives.

## 4. Claimed conclusions must be named, not bundled under one C

C-origin: one actual original bearer completely covers the whole productive target. P1/P2 already grant this at the fixed scope; their joint denial is not a rival preserving ΓG.

C-unity: numerical identity of independently established roles. UC licenses only identity of complete sources of the same productive modes. Co-location, relation identity, global-field completeness and derivational unification are different claims.

C-necessary: this actual bearer necessarily exists, rather than a source role being occupied everywhere. Neither actual P1/P2 nor D supplies N2/N3.

C-cognitive: this original source has its own apprehension. OG-K challenges an impersonal source. The integrity lane grants S-A, so an impersonal rival cannot preserve that strongest hypothetical lane unchanged.

C-norm-ground: this source's nature constitutes the relevant objective authority, rather than merely apprehending it or institutionally fixing a particular designed function. A separate identifying/grounding relation is needed.

C-integrity: the same source has essential appropriate practical governance/T0. Some genuinely fitting source acts, even whole productive provision of derivative virtue, do not state this conclusion.

C-disclosure: this actual message is a warranted true address that the recipient can use for this action. Generic capacity, source nature, truth and warrant do not coincide.

C-MEN8: the exact integrated world-directed consequence specified by the original fixed architecture. Neither C-origin alone nor failure of C-integrity alone can be called an R5/MEN-8 outcome without registering its relation to that exact consequence and the full premise signature.

## 5. Immediate strong-grant exclusions

1. Rootless active reception cannot preserve single-bearer P1/P2 for this same whole productive cone. That failure uses the explicitly granted completion hypothesis; it is not an independently warranted refutation of every infinite active order.
2. Two genuinely independent complementary sources cannot each be declared separately complete for a shared actual productive mode while preserving UC. A jointly sufficient plurality instead denies single-source completion.
3. A wholly impersonal original source cannot preserve an explicitly source-level intentional S-A act. It can preserve AG as a universal conditional and admit recipient intentionality, but that weaker package is different.
4. A knowing liar cannot preserve PT for the very lying token, or R_G+N on a domain containing that token. The present record has not independently established those extra premises in the disputed source case.
5. No rule licenses turning the failure of one of these grant-preservation tests into independent evidence that the corresponding bridge is actually true.
