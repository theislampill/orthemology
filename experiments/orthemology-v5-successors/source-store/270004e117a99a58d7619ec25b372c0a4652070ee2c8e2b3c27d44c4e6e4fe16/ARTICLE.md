# Composition and the original productive bearer

Eleventh Orthemology research • 3 October 2026

## Result

A plurality's complete productive contribution cannot be promoted into the original contribution of its composite merely by changing the grammatical subject. Under the programme's inherited treatment of proper unborrowed exercises, that promotion can conflict with the very source attributions from which it began. A genuine whole-source account must locate the ultimate productive possession: in a whole whose members act derivatively, in intrinsic aspects that are not separate source-bearers, or in a defended alternative theory of joint original ownership. This is a specific test of a proposed assembly inference. It is neither an impossibility proof for composite agents nor a new proof that an original whole actually exists.

The strongest affirmative nevertheless has real content. A concrete whole can possess and exercise a power through differentiated internal contributions. Its complexity does not make it an arbitrary aggregate, and its parts need not be inert. What remains to be warranted for P1/P2 is that this particular whole originally accounts for the admitted correction's complete actual productive origin. A comparison with a whole-action argument and its direct critic does not supply that warrant. It clarifies why a proposed whole's natural unity and its original productive scope need separate assessment.

## 1. Fixed target and inherited credit

The explanandum is the admitted reason-responsive correction of the false claim about a displayed Minhāj bibliographic header. The subject understands the displayed evidence and changes the claim for that reason. The finding concerns that witness. It is not an inference about a complete edition, a reward score, or a merely correlated output.

The subject and the relevant exercise are actual under the admitted cognitive interpretation. A genuinely received productive resource requires an actual supplier; a description alone cannot supply it. Genuine recipient agency is retained. The subject can truly apprehend, choose and exercise a capacity without having originally supplied its own existential basis. These are inherited starting points, not achievements of this comparison. [H1 §§1–3; H2 §§1–3; H5 §1]

P1 concerns original productive completion at the whole actual-origin scope. P2 identifies a particular bearer of that original actuality. The target includes the actual productive contributions and receiving modes relevant to this correction, not merely the ability to reproduce a similar endpoint. N2/N3 concern the further existential explanation and necessity of that same bearer. Their open status must not be used to withhold credit from a genuinely warranted original source or necessary participant, but neither conclusion is established merely by identifying a local agent. [H1 §§4–7; H3 §§4–9; H5 §§2–3; N1 §3]

The inherited original-possession argument has prima facie explanatory force. An original productive nature would answer the supply question in a way that another received contribution, considered by itself, does not. The genuinely active non-well-founded proposal has not been certified as an adequate explanation or a metaphysical possibility. Its mere description does not defeat a properly targeted rational recognition. The local-to-collective extension remains a substantive question already located in earlier work. This article does not repeat that dispute as a new result. [N2 §§2–4; N3 §§2–3; T1, “Explaining existential availability”]

## 2. A stronger affirmative than a named totality

The candidate whole-source argument should be developed in two stages.

First, the proposed whole is a real bearer with a positive integrated capacity. The account identifies how its differentiated resources belong to one subject and how their joint exercise is genuinely its act. This can be supported by a constitutive account, a causal account of organisation, basic recognition, or a suitable abduction. There is no requirement that every case be settled by a mechanical measurement. Nor must every power be intentional; the present cognitive target simply makes intentional integration particularly important.

Second, the bearer possesses originally the efficacy by which the target's whole actual origin is supplied. Its complete productive account leaves no indispensable exercised contribution wholly furnished by another independent source. This does not require it to create its own existence by a prior act. Original possession is the proposed standing manner in which its efficacy is available. It also does not make every contingent act necessary.

This is stronger than putting all known contributors inside one boundary. The source would explain why the positive powers and their coordination belong to one productive reality. If independently warranted, this would address the common-bearer gap while preserving genuine subordinate agency and internal complexity. Its potential advantage over an unexplained collection is real: coordination and common possession would themselves receive an account.

The advantage must be earned at the same target. A singular noun does not reduce the number of unexplained powers. Conversely, an explanation that genuinely derives their common possession should not be denied credit merely because a plural rival can be described. The proper comparison asks what each account explains, which actual facts support it, and whether the new claim contributes explanatory content beyond the desired conclusion.

## 4. The assembly inference under test

Consider this proposed route to a bearer:

1. Several actual contributors jointly furnish the full productive origin of the target.
2. They compose a particular concrete whole.
3. Therefore that whole is the original complete productive bearer of the target.

The first two statements can be significant. The inference is not secured by their meanings. In particular, the whole may have a genuine joint capacity while the original actuality of each contributing power remains attributed to distinct members. The problem is not merely that the joint explanation is plural. The problem is whether the inference retains the original productive attributions needed to support its conclusion.

The inherited sourcehood account supplies an exact conditional test. Take an actual mode m contributed by member b. Suppose:

- m is one of the target's relevant actual productive modes;
- m is b's proper unborrowed exercise, in the inherited ultimate productive respect;
- the proposed whole w is complete for that same actual target;
- w and b are distinct source-bearers in the same interpretation.

History-inclusive completeness assigns m to w's ultimate productive account. The inherited unborrowed-act exclusion then identifies w with b. That conflicts with the stated distinctness. One member's actual mode suffices; no assumption about the number of members or a temporal first member is required. [H3 §§4–7; H4 §§3–5]

Here m is the source-intrinsic productive exercise admitted to the target's whole actual history. If the starting item is instead an effect-side receiving mode, the argument needs a separate step: that mode requires b's actual exercise, and independently complete production cannot leave that indispensable efficacy wholly outside its account. This is the inherited prerequisite bridge. The receiving mode and the source's exercise are not assumed identical, and a merely logical relation does not manufacture an efficient supply relation. [H3 §6; H4 §§2–4]

The test requires absolutely proper unborrowed member agency in the declared productive domain. Independence only from entities outside the composite is weaker. Members may receive from one another, or from the whole, while no outsider supplies them. Such a proposal lacks the test's proper-unborrowed-member premise; it returns to the separate adequacy question about an internally received field. Likewise, being constitutively dependent on a whole is not by itself efficient reception. The productive interpretation must be warranted before the exclusion is applied.

The underlying theorem is inherited. The present increment is its application to the composition-to-source inference: adding a concrete whole to a complete plural explanation does not preserve every original-member attribution while also giving the whole the inherited complete-source role. Mereological inclusion cannot be substituted for ultimate productive provision without checking that incompatibility.

This is a conditional attribution conflict, not a refutation of plural production. It does not assume that the plurality is a metaphysically possible complete cosmos. It also does not establish that the inherited exclusion applies to every theory of constitution or collective agency. It tests the proposed promotion while its terms retain the programme's accepted conditional interpretation.

## 5. Four coherent ways to respond

### A. The whole is originally productive; the members are genuinely derivative

On this account, the whole's nature supplies the relevant actuality and efficacy. Its members really act, but their productive possession is furnished within that source's account. The contradiction disappears because the member exercise is not unborrowed in the same ultimate respect.

This is a serious positive route. The recipient's activity is not reduced to a passive relay. A member can own its intentional act while receiving the capacities and actuality used in it. What must be established is the source-level supply relation, rather than inferred from composition. If this relation genuinely covers all relevant actual receiving modes, an internally differentiated whole is eligible for P1/P2.

### B. The plurality remains originally productive; the whole has a narrower joint role

The composite can be real and exercise a joint capacity. It may organise the members, realise a common plan or produce a joint result. Those roles can earn ordinary whole-level causal credit. They do not give it ultimate productive accounting for members' own independently original exercises merely because these occur inside it.

On this interpretation the whole is a genuine bearer, not necessarily an arbitrary aggregate. Its role is nevertheless narrower than P1. That is a substantive distinction between what it actually does and the entire original source claim, rather than a denial of its existence or efficacy.

### C. Alleged members are intrinsic powers or aspects, not separate source-bearers

A source's real intrinsic features do not become additional creators merely because they are distinguishable. The test above requires distinct bearers each owning a proper unborrowed exercise. If the account instead contains one bearer with several intrinsic powers, the premise about distinct source-bearers is absent.

This response cannot be ruled out by a general ban on composition. It requires a warranted account of the bearer and its features. Reclassifying genuinely independent contributors as attributes solely to obtain unity would beg the question; correctly identifying intrinsic aspects would not. The actual ontological classification, rather than a count of descriptive parts, decides whether the test applies.

### D. Joint original ownership requires a different accounting relation

A theory may defend the view that a whole originally possesses an exercise through constitution while a distinct member also originally owns it. If the whole does not furnish the member's efficacy in the inherited productive sense, it cannot simply claim HistoryComplete at that sense. If it does furnish that efficacy while retaining both ultimate ownership claims, it disputes the applicability or content of UnborrowedActs.

That is a substantive proposal to assess. The inherited theorem must not be used to reject it before its interpretation is given; the proposal must not use the theorem's uniqueness conclusion while silently replacing its accounting relation. A shared word such as “possession” does not identify constitutive inheritance, instrumental deployment and ultimate productive provision.

In particular, an internal member is not automatically an external giver, and dependence on an intrinsic feature is not automatically dependence on another independent source. The present test applies only when a proposal retains distinct member-bearers with genuinely proper unborrowed exercises and also gives the whole ultimate productive accounting for those very exercises. If the new ontology denies that interpretation, its case must be examined on its own terms.

## 6. Why dynamical closure does not repair assembly by itself

An adequate total state-description can contain every input needed to predict a system's behaviour. It can thereby identify a principled explanatory boundary. It does not by itself assign all productive power to one owner. The description may include many different actual subjects and relations, and a law may govern their joint evolution without making each subject's power the exercise of another subject.

For the admitted correction, a full account can include the displayed source, access channel, received capacities and the subject's understanding. This does not make the source display a knower, or make every causal contributor the subject that understands the evidence. A correct description of their conjunction leaves those distinctions intact.

The whole-source advocate can strengthen the proposal: the whole does not merely match a description but actually governs or produces the local exercises. That would address power ownership if independently warranted. The actual-origin question still asks whether the powers and receiving modes used in this production are supplied by the proposed whole at the original scope. Whole-level governance cannot be silently identified with the original availability of its governor.

A basic presentation could have that stronger content. An abduction could support it through a concrete account of the whole's powers and their explanatory priority. The comparison does not demand a second proof merely because the first reason is basic. It tests whether the reason actually concerns original provision, or only an already actual system's coordinated operation.

## 7. The actual correction and the three candidate readings

The admitted cognitive interpretation identifies a real subject who understands and corrects for reasons; an account consisting only in coincidentally correct output would change that target. This adds no empirical subjecthood finding and supplies no new global bearer merely by redescribing the correction as an integrated event.

An integrated derivative agent preserves those data and the admitted reality of recipient agency. A coordinated upstream plurality could also preserve them if it genuinely supplies one reason-responsive recipient; no unified original subject is contained in that local description. An original whole could preserve and explain them if its standing powers actually supply the recipient and relevant modes. These are distinctions in the offered explanatory content, not certified alternative worlds or an assertion of equal plausibility.

The useful discriminant is whether the offered account independently explains the original possession and actual coordination, at the complete-origin scope. Recipient integration cannot alone provide that evidence, because its very admission allows real powers whose possession is received. A described plural rival does not erase the inherited reason for original possession, but the absence of a completed rival does not establish the particular whole either.

The cross-organisational question of maintaining, recruiting and repairing a system's own powers can be useful evidence of genuine ownership at a derivative scope. It is not a new result here. Earlier continuation work already examines causal endogeneity, externally supplied support and nonopportunistic boundaries. This article does not claim a new ownership criterion by repeating those tests. Nor does such a criterion automatically identify the original ground of the powers it finds.

## 8. Particular reference without an artificial extra burden

A genuine singular source, if actually warranted and uniquely identified at the target's full scope, need not additionally be known by a personal name or sensory acquaintance. An actuality-anchored description can identify a particular. That allowance is already explicit in the inherited warrant review. [N3 §2.4]

It cannot turn a plural contribution into one bearer, identify an arbitrary preselected entity with the source, or establish that the same bearer exists at every alternative. The present composition problem therefore concerns ontological and productive attribution, not an artificial demand for another naming ceremony.

## 9. Disposition and what would change it

The investigation finds no new actual P1/P2 closure. The original-possession rationale retains its inherited prima facie credit. The whole-action comparison supplies a serious candidate route for assessment but no independently earned bridge from joint evolution or composition to original provision of the correction's whole productive origin.

The bounded increment is a sharper assembly test. A complete plural origin account cannot simply be repackaged as one complete original bearer while retaining distinct members' proper unborrowed contributions under the same inherited accounting conditions. The whole-source advocate must positively establish derivative member agency, a genuine one-bearer/aspect ontology, or a different defensible joint-possession relation. The test identifies what must change; it does not prescribe a preferred metaphysics by definition.

New evidence capable of changing the judgement would therefore include one of the following:

1. A source-level account that warrants this particular whole's actual ownership and original supply of the relevant powers and receiving modes, rather than only its existence or dynamical completeness.
2. A basic or abductive reason identifying one concrete bearer whose coordinated powers have that complete original scope, with its content surviving the distinction between recipient integration and source-level provision.
3. A defended constitutive-possession account that establishes the required original productive role without covertly retaining incompatible independent-member attributions or borrowing the inherited uniqueness theorem after changing its meanings.

A necessary concrete participant would receive immediate bounded credit if such evidence established it. No such participant is newly warranted in this inquiry. No conclusion about essential integrity, actual disclosure, recipient authority, R5, canonical adoption or a complete metaphysical countermodel follows.
