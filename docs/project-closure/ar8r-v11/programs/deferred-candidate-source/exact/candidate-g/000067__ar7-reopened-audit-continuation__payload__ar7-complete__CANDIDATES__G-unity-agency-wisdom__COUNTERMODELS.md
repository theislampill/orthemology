# Countermodels — Candidate G

## CM-G1 — One bearer, four brute features

One necessarily existing entity has primitive modal, actualizing, semantic, and normative features. The features are freely recombinable and connected to explananda by independent bridge laws.

**Result:** defeats bearer-count unification. It is derivationally equivalent to plural primitives under carrier-boxing invariance.

## CM-G2 — Unified impersonal control field

One necessarily existing impersonal field represents state variables, ranks them by a primitive objective functional, selects transitions, and realizes them lawfully.

**Result:** matches the functional rational-integration graph without subjectivity or personality. It preserves a possible abductive case for unity/purpose but defeats the direct intellect/person bridge.

## CM-G3 — Holistic dispositional monism

A necessary concrete whole has relationally complete powers whose joint nature fixes modality, actuality, content-bearing causal roles, and teleological tendencies.

**Result:** offers one-factor actual modal ground and cross-domain constraints without distinct intellect or Will. Candidate G must compare rather than dismiss it.

## CM-G4 — Thick brute totality

A primitive total fact contains all modal, actual, semantic, and normative relations, including their coordination.

**Result:** maximally covers the explanandum while declining explanation. Coherent unless brute totality is independently penalized.

## CM-G5 — Distributed rational architecture

A network has no single subject but jointly realizes representation, evaluation, selection, and efficacy.

**Result:** defeats the move from integrated rational roles to numerical unity or personality.

## CM-G6 — False unifier

A compact single-principle theory derives every explanandum but reverses explanatory direction or fits only through gerrymandered definitions.

**Result:** shows derivational economy is not sufficient; direction, naturalness, fit, and independent motivation remain necessary.
