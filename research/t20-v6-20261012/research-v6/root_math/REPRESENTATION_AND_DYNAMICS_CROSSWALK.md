## Foundational use and ceiling

These constructions can sharpen representations of cumulative grounds, revision history, source dependence, preserved authority and future inquiry. They do not establish that a particular cognitive system has the intended proper function, that a source is reliable, or that a declared target is true. Equally, their general incompleteness does not withdraw independently earned warrant for a particular affirmative argument. The mathematical task is to preserve the distinctions that that argument actually uses, under the corrections being considered.
