# Determination transport: a conditional means result and a rejected obstruction

8 October 2026. Bounded source-neutral mathematical review. No source-level or metaphysical conclusion.

## Useful result

The three coactive ways to realize a fixed target do **not** obstruct a faithful deterministic selector in the retained reactive model. Conditional on a fixed intended target q, equivalent practical roles, and an exchange-invariant joint-plan requirement, **(q,q) is the unique exchange-invariant goal-achieving profile**. Each bearer can implement it locally by the inherited q-policy, without receiving the other's actual exercise. This needs no assumption that one outcome uniquely maximizes value.

That is a positive conditional means result, not a proof that exchange invariance is obligatory, the target is wise, the intended relationship is eligible for originals, or the resulting exercises instantiate source-level complete willing. Equal standing power alone supplies none of those extra premises. The general symmetry criterion is useful, but the tempting symmetry obstruction for this particular model is inapplicable: independent opposite shifts of the two settings preserve the coactive table while breaking its solo laws.

## 1. Exact selector criterion

Let a group G act on reason records R and on a family of nonempty candidate sets C(r), with gC(r) = C(gr). The family must preserve every feature counted as a reason or as fixed operative structure. A deterministic selector s satisfies s(r) in C(r). Call it equivariant when s(gr) = g s(r).

For finite R, an equivariant selector exists **if and only if** each stabilizer G_r = {g : gr = r} has a common fixed candidate in C(r). The construction also works for an infinite context set when one orbit representative and one corresponding fixed candidate have been chosen per orbit; that extension is not needed here.

Proof. If s is equivariant, then g s(r) = s(gr) = s(r) for each g in G_r. Conversely, choose a representative r_i and a candidate a_i fixed by G_(r_i) for each orbit. Define s(g r_i) = g a_i. If g r_i = h r_i, then h^(-1)g stabilizes r_i and fixes a_i, so the definition is independent of the transporter. It is a selector and is equivariant. No value ranking appears in the proof.

It suffices that the stabilizer has **one common fixed candidate**. It need not fix all candidates or act trivially. A transitive nontrivial action on a fibre has none; a fibre containing several choices can still have exactly one fixed candidate. Confusing multiplicity with obstruction would invalidate the proposed discriminator.

This theorem is elementary structural mathematics, not a new general choice or agency theorem. Its contribution here is a check on which transports the retained rival actually licenses.

## 2. Positive result in the retained model

For an odd number n of settings, the retained law is

    F(a,b) = 2^(-1)(a+b) mod n;
    F(a,idle) = a;
    F(idle,b) = b.

The source file uses n = 2^k - 1 for a catalogue of k events. Its three-setting instance has F(a,b) = 2(a+b) mod 3. For fixed q the goal-achieving coactive profiles are

    C(q) = {(q+t, q-t) : t in Z/nZ}.

Agent exchange sends (a,b) to (b,a), preserves the complete law including solo cases, and preserves the intended target. A fixed profile must satisfy a=b. Requiring also F(a,b)=q gives a=q by idempotence F(a,a)=a. Thus the fixed candidate set is exactly {(q,q)}.

For n=3:

| q | Goal-achieving profiles | Fixed by agent exchange |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | (0,0), (1,2), (2,1) | (0,0) |
| 1 | (0,2), (1,1), (2,0) | (1,1) |
| 2 | (0,1), (1,0), (2,2) | (2,2) |

The general argument actually needs only that the joint operation be commutative and idempotent. Oddness supplies the retained modular construction; it is not an additional moral or modal principle.

### Practical-role premise

Exchange is a within-context automorphism only if the reason record is unpointed between the two bearers: same intended q, compatible goal content, no distinguished practical role, and all further relevant reason data transported faithfully. If one bearer has a distinct assignment, a de re reason, or a distinguishing intrinsic feature, exchange may move to a different context or fail to preserve reasons.

Mere renaming of agents in two presentations does not establish this stronger symmetry. In a signature with rigid role constants, a relabeling transports those constants too; it need not be an automorphism fixing the original pointed context. If the target names and bearer roles are both fixed rigidly, the identity group imposes no preference among the three profiles. Equal standing powers do not eliminate distinct practical reasons.

Accordingly, the result can vindicate the **form** of an exchange-invariant joint means plan under the stated premises. It cannot establish those premises merely from structural nonreceipt or equal ability. Nor does it show that every rational or wise plan must be invariant: the appropriateness of equivariance for the declared reasons is a separate assumption.

### Legitimate instrumental search and local execution

C(q) is an inverse planning query under a known law: which settings would achieve this intended target? Using anticipated outcomes to select instrumental means is legitimate here. It does not define the space of metaphysically admissible worlds, define CompleteWill, or erase the six countergoal active profiles from the nine-profile operational table.

The surviving plan factors into the two inherited local policies:

    s_A(q) = q;    s_B(q) = q.

Each policy takes its own intended q and uses the solo-output identification of its available setting. Neither receives the other bearer's actual exercise or a post-execution success observation. Their independently issued exercises realize (q,q), hence q, under the retained law. This proves local executability and conditional goal achievement. It does not derive that the bearers know the law, know they share q, know their practical reasons are equivalent, or know the other will use this policy. Those are epistemic premises. Existing model forecasts remain conditional on the supplied policies.

The achieved events still have both active originals as contributors and **no individual entire provider**. Selection symmetry does not change that source-accounting result. Solo sufficiency of each selected setting is not actual individual whole production while both contribute.

## 3. Faithful versus unfaithful setting transports

In the three-setting algebra, allow a setting permutation alpha for A, beta for B, and an output permutation gamma, keeping idle fixed. A transport of the complete law must satisfy

    F(alpha(a),beta(b)) = gamma(F(a,b))

also when either bearer is idle. The A-solo cases force alpha(a)=gamma(a); the B-solo cases force beta(b)=gamma(b). Hence alpha=beta=gamma. All six common permutations preserve the three-setting law: equal inputs return that input, and two different inputs return the third code.

Exhaustive enumeration checks all 216 triples of permutations. Eighteen preserve only the coactive table; exactly six preserve the complete idle-including law. If outputs are held de re fixed, three preserve the coactive table but only the identity setting transport preserves the full law. Optional agent exchange adds the faithful slot symmetry separately.

The proposed obstruction uses, for nonzero c,

    tau_c(a,b) = (a+c,b-c), with q unchanged.

It rotates the three candidates in C(q) freely, so it would obstruct a tau-invariant deterministic selector. But it breaks F(a,idle)=a: the transported A exercise produces a+c while the fixed output is still a. It likewise breaks B's solo law. Both nontrivial shifts fail all six solo cases. There is no common output relabeling that repairs both shifts, as the forced equalities alpha=beta=gamma show.

Thus this free-cycle group is valid only for a deliberately **reduced** coactive-only signature. It cannot be imported into the actual setting/goal distinction, standing solo repertoire, and full operation law. An impossibility proved after discarding those features has no established application to the retained model.

Common translations of all settings and outputs are different: they move q to q+c, so nontrivial translations are not in a fixed-q stabilizer. Common reflections that do fix q leave (q,q) fixed. The larger algebraic group, including exchange, therefore also has an invariant selector.

The six permutations are algebraic code transports only. The actual Catalogue interprets codes as nonempty event sets; extra event identity or membership structure may forbid some code permutations. The affirmative result already holds with target codes fixed and exchange alone, so it does not depend on granting those stronger relabelings. Every application must specify which event and reason structure is retained.

## 4. What a genuine obstruction would establish

For comparison, a single abstract reason record with three candidates cyclically permuted by a faithful C3 symmetry has no deterministic equivariant selector: no candidate is fixed by all rotations. The whole candidate set remains equivariant; its uniform probability distribution is also invariant. Thus the obstruction concerns deterministic particular selection, not the coherence of a reason-conditioned set of fitting alternatives, permission of all members, or representation of uncertainty.

For such an obstruction to apply, one would have to establish all of the following independently:

1. The reason record includes the operative, intrinsic, historical, and practical data relevant to this comparison.
2. The alleged symmetry preserves that data and the full intended operation, rather than only the successful output.
3. Selection is deterministic as a function of this record and is required to respect those transports.
4. The resulting reason stabilizer has no common fixed candidate.

That package establishes an incompatibility among the assumptions. It does not by itself decide whether the record omitted a genuine particular reason, whether equivariance was unwarranted, or whether a weaker set-valued account of fittingness was intended. It says nothing about metaphysical admissibility unless separate source evidence supplies the application.

## 5. Complete willing and particularizing data

Suppose one augments a reason record by the already selected action token w, forming (r,w). The projection s(r,w)=w is automatically equivariant, since g(r,w)=(gr,gw). The new stabilizer must fix w. There is therefore no obstruction. This correctly represents fully determinate actual willing, but offers no independent explanation of why w rather than another fitting act was selected. Calling w a sufficient particular reason without additional grounds merely builds the selection into its alleged explanation.

Genuine particularizing data can also reduce a stabilizer and permit a selector. The theorem does not require that such data be a unique value maximizer; different fitting outcomes may remain equally valuable while another relevant consideration specifies this action. Conversely, algebra cannot certify that an added marker is a substantive reason rather than brute preference renamed. Its provenance, explanatory role, and source warrant require independent assessment. Breaking every symmetry is sufficient but not necessary: a stabilizer may remain nontrivial while fixing an eligible action.

Finally, holding (r,w) fixed holds its selected token fixed by construction. That does not exclude optional or successive acts represented by other contexts or other complete particular willings. The formalism supplies no reason to demand alternative actions under an unchanged complete decree, and no reason to infer necessity of the whole act history from single-context determinacy.
