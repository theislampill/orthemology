## Asfahani translation

**Authority:** translated-primary-text access based on two Arabic editions identified by the translator; not this project’s Arabic-primary verification.

Relevant locators in the supplied Markdown snapshot:

- lines 68–99: the creed’s necessary-creator and attribute bundle and Ibn Taymiyyah’s initial affirmation of the selected predicates;
- lines 108–124: scriptural Names, the distinction between Names and attributes, and praiseworthy versus blameworthy speech/volition;
- lines 128–171: speech subsisting in the speaker, uncreated Speech in the source-relative account, and the attribute-locus argument that an attribute predicates the locus in which it subsists.

The source supplies a Track-N premise that functional production in a downstream locus is not sufficient for speaker predication. It does not itself supply actual-world referent selection, Arabic-primary verification, a neutral personality theorem, or the complete Wisdom guard package.

## El-Tobgui

**Authority:** secondary scholarly reconstruction.

It supplies historical and conceptual context for the Taymiyyan reconstitution of language, ontology, epistemology, the mental/external distinction, fiṭrah, khabar, and rational knowledge. It does not become direct primary predication or neutral formal proof.

## al-Ujayri

**Authority:** contemporary Atharī synthesis.

The supplied text argues from mastery, order, purpose, and repeated construction toward an omniscient and wise doer. Those claims are source-relative philosophical argument, not independently proved neutral bridge premises in Deep CK. The text is used to identify the target argument and its assumptions, not to grant the conclusion.
