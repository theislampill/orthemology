## 1. Countable Bayes layer inspected independently

The new layer does not assume the Bayes-risk identity it seeks to establish.
Its relevant premises are the support/weight/loss conditions, not an oracle
mode, a risk bound, or a desired summability statement.

Power-weight summability is derived by geometric domination. Every Bernoulli
score sequence is positive and dominated by those weights, hence summable.
Summability makes the scores tend to zero; a positive first score therefore
forces an attained maximum within a finite head. No countable supremum is
silently treated as attained.

The source proves concavity of the actual log score on (0,1), derives interval
quasiconcavity along the ordered support, and obtains monotone sides around an
attained maximum. It then passes the finite adjacent-minimum identity to the
countable sum using proved convergence. Thus the reduction to adjacent binary
overlaps is established for the specified weights, not supplied as a premise.

Exact D0/D1 geometry proves that no arbitrary real report accepts two supported
parameters. The affine report at a selected support point is accepted there.
The conditional Bayes theorem consequently covers reports accepted at one
point and reports accepted nowhere. Real-valued report-risk sums are justified
by domination; the zero-default convention for nonsummable real `tsum` is not
being used to erase a divergent risk.

Policies have type `Nat -> Finset Nat -> Real`. The finite set records positions
containing a one, so it retains the entire ordered word. Only the likelihood
score depends on its cardinality. Arbitrary policies are not restricted to
count-only statistics. Summing over the complete powerset of positions gives
the exact binomial multiplicity. A single classical choice of a maximizing
support point for each n,k gives a common deterministic policy attaining the
whole overlap cost.

The normalizer is proved strictly positive and its defining real series is
proved summable. Normalized weights sum to one, and all joint full-word masses
sum to one at each fixed n. A seed value selects an entire policy; report
randomizations may therefore be correlated across time. The lower bound holds
pointwise in that seed, and integration over a probability measure preserves
it. The witness can be constant in the seed, with measurable report coordinates.

## 2. Actual infinite probability law

The process namespace constructs, rather than assumes, the required law:

    latentLaw = sum_i ofReal(w_i) *
      [dirac(i) product infinitePi(Bernoulli(a_i))],
    experimentLaw = eta product latentLaw.

This order matters. It mixes once over a fixed index and then supplies that
index's entire iid stream. It is not the infinite product of fresh per-receipt
mixtures. The sample's same latent coordinate is used to score every stage.
The seed is outside this whole experiment in a product law, making it
independent of both the index and all receipt-generating randomness.

The Boolean coin law is normalized directly. `Measure.infinitePi` is the actual
countable product measure. The source proves its full-word cylinder mass by
identifying the observed prefix event with a finite product cylinder and
evaluating that product. Positions outside the prefix are unconstrained; the
decoder's values there do not fix or reveal a future receipt.

The latent mixture's mass is proved one from the normalized weights. With the
probability-space seed, the full experiment is a probability measure. These
facts are not assumptions appended to the terminal iff.

## 3. Causality, literal loss, and expectation identity

The report in a sample `(z,(i,y))` is

    pi z n (the one positions among y(0),...,y(n-1)).

The reporting function does not receive i. The fixed latent index appears only
in the loss evaluator. There is no correctness feedback, future-tail argument,
stopping rule, abstention option, or alternative sample clock in the policy
interface. Earlier reports can be reconstructed from the same seed and receipt
history, so excluding a separate earlier-report argument does not restrict the
intended causal class.

`literalFailure` is exactly zero when both inherited excesses are nonpositive
and one otherwise. Equality is accepted. `totalFailures` is an extended
nonnegative sum of these indicators at n=1,2,..., so it counts actual literal
failures and cannot cancel them or replace them by excess-loss magnitudes.

Coordinate-measurable policies give measurable stage loss by finite word
partitions and countable latent-index decomposition. The total loss is
measurable by the countable nonnegative sum. This suffices for all required
Tonelli/Fubini steps even when the resulting expectation is infinite.

The expectation bridge is then derived in the following order:

1. Integrate a function of a finite prefix using the proved infinite-product
   cylinder masses
2. Integrate through the actual latent-index mixture by its countable measure
   sum and Dirac/product decomposition
3. Identify each stage's normalized failure mass with the full-word report-risk
   sum, using proved score summability
4. Apply nonnegative Tonelli over all positive report indices
5. Apply product-measure Fubini over the independent seed

The resulting `actual_expectation_eq_randomizedCost` is proved from these
objects. It is not an input assumption. The terminal
`exists_finite_actual_expectation_iff` then invokes the already proved analytic
criterion, and `every_policy_infinite_of_not_summable` quantifies the same
coordinate-measurable full-history policies under this actual law.
