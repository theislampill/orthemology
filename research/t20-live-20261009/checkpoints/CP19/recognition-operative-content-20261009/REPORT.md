# Recognition content and selective truth directed functioning

9 October 2026 UTC. Additive T20 research. No integration, closure, confidence revision, or historical active-time certification.

## Result

Two previously uncompleted primary investigations sharpen the question without independently certifying a human Creator-recognition episode.

First, an immediate recognition can deploy acquired content richer than its present sensory trigger. A critic cannot infer that a thought is merely about danger or personal need just by describing that trigger. An affirmative cannot infer the richer content and its warrant from the trigger either. The missing work concerns the actual acquired or native capacity and its current use, not an inevitable requirement for a presently articulated argument.

Second, a precise source criticism survives a coherence test: systematic deception across a report can coexist with strict truth-tracking for one fixed component. This defeats an express incoherence claim in the inspected 2017 rival. It does not establish that the recipient knows the component, that the whole report is true, or that an unqualifiedly unreliable source is generally trustworthy.

Direct transport of the finite truth-tracking check to a proposition held necessarily true fails. That negative result is retained explicitly. None of these results changes T17's retained positive recognition appraisal; this operation supplies neither new independent verification nor an identified defeater requiring its withdrawal.

## A concrete recognition episode

Take the storm case at Ameri 175 on its strongest intended reading. The person immediately apprehends the actual Creator as the one to whom the appeal is directed. Do not weaken this at the outset to a wish, generic agency, an unnamed possible helper, or an inference from fear. Likewise, do not make it an eyewitness scientific record: the page gives neither authenticated subject-level acquisition history nor an independently observed episode.

We can now distinguish three explanatory possibilities without deciding among them by labels.

1. The crisis makes previously possessed recognitional competence operative. The current act may be psychologically immediate even if that competence developed through earlier experience, reflection or instruction. The previous warrant might be preserved rather than newly supplied by danger.
2. The crisis is an occasion for a new, truth-directed presentation of the intended object. Its claimed cognitive content is richer than the sensory presentation; that alone does not invalidate it. What warrants the step is the capacity's actual operation, not the intensity or efficacy of the appeal.
3. The crisis elicits a practical appeal under uncertainty. Such an appeal does not by itself establish an assertoric belief, still less the specific content in the strongest intended reading. A later interlocutor can supply a new identification that was not already operative.

These are different acquisition/basing histories, not three numerical values of a variable named warranted. The third is a legitimate control for an inference from observable action, but it is not a rebuttal to a genuinely granted case of the first or second sort. Conversely, describing the actual episode as recognition does not settle which history produced it.

The useful discrimination concerns what was cognized when. Did the person already recognize the intended individual under the same role before the crisis? Did that knowledge become salient? Did a new recognition occur, or did the later conversation supply its identification? A subject need not possess a complete causal theory to know; ordinary recollection and testimony can be evidence about this sequence. Still, a reported rescue appeal and retrospective identification do not automatically establish their own acquisition chronology.

The source's broader developmental position permits external assistance. Therefore a learned recognitional route is not automatically an objection to qualified fiṭrah. Nor does one specimen of learned classification prove that every basic concept must be learned. The substantive dispute is over this capacity's origin, content and appropriate operation. Neither an innate-versus-learned slogan nor absence of conscious premise recital adjudicates it.

### The countermodel

Fix six independent ordinary propositions P0,…,P5. Think of six separately enacted provisions, whose contents remain fixed across the model. The speaker accurately observes each truth value. He has a stable plan to report the correct answer about P0 and to report the opposite of the truth about P1,…,P5. The plan can serve a deceptive purpose: one accurate statement helps disguise five lies. Earlier reputation-building is compatible with this plan; it adds no contradiction.

The recipient understands the ordinary assertions and accepts each answer using the same general procedure. He has no private marker identifying the truthful component and no independent knowledge of the speaker's selective policy. Granting ordinary comprehension does not grant source reliability, justification or knowledge. No claim is made that this simple acceptance rule is a complete account of human basing.

Let E be the fixed event that the speaker asserts P0. When P0 is true the speaker asserts it; when P0 is false he asserts its negation. Therefore, throughout the 64 valuations, E holds if and only if P0. Each complete report has exactly one true component and five false ones. Its conjunction is false in every valuation.

The companion script checks all 64 valuations. There are 32 false-P0 alternatives, and E is absent in all 32. Thus any selection of closest false-P0 alternatives inside this stipulated domain preserves the relevant absence of E. The result does not depend on silently shifting the meaning of P0 or calling whatever happens to be true “p.” The proposition is fixed; only its truth value varies.

This is a coherent realization of target-specific truth-tracking with global deceitfulness. The authors can deny that the recipient knows, dispute whether this is the appropriate modal domain for an actual human case, or require a broader method-level or rational-basing condition. Each is a substantive move. They cannot reject the mere coexistence of the specified local policy and global deception as a logical impossibility.

There are two senses of a whole report here. Its communicated conjunction is false. The fact that the six particular assertions occurred can nevertheless be observed truly. Fixing the entire actual assertion sequence, rather than only its first component, does not defeat this coherence result: every false-P0 alternative changes the first assertion, so the entire observed sequence is absent there too. The script checks that stronger control. This does not make the communicated conjunction true or establish the recipient’s epistemic access to the policy.

The result is deliberately narrow. The 2017 authors already work with content-relative sensitive reasons. The model does not show they overlook that distinction everywhere; it shows that their particular character-based rejection does not follow without an additional premise. Stronger readings on which “untrustworthy about P0” already means failure of P0-tracking would exclude the model by definition, but would change the stipulated case rather than refute its coherence.

### What remains undecided

The recipient's acceptance of every statement has a poor global truth ratio. Is the selected true belief nevertheless knowledge? One may answer yes on a strongly content-local externalist view, or no because the actual acceptance depends on an inadequate source appraisal or an epistemically defective broad method. Our table contains no knowledge predicate and selects neither verdict. It leaves explicit the very basing/proper-function issue under investigation.

The same discipline applies to defeat. Evidence of systematic error elsewhere can be a rational undercutter, but it does not logically entail that every component lacks an actual truth connection. A successful component likewise cannot authenticate the entire worldview or source. Defeat, local connection and universal reliability need different grounds.

## A failed direct transport that matters

The component model presupposes genuine false-target alternatives. Now hold a target N true throughout all admitted metaphysical possibilities, as one might do for an explicitly necessary existence claim. A constant assent mechanism and a content-responsive one both pass the bare condition that no false-N alternative contains the same evidence, because there are no false-N alternatives.

The companion control records zero false-target rows. This proves no defect in recognizing necessary truths, and no defect in an account that imposes additional relevant, hyperintensional, competence or basing conditions. It only shows that this possible-world truth-tracking screen cannot identify the appropriate route in that application. The developed 2017 rival explicitly tries to address this issue using relevant conditionals; the simple vacuity objection therefore does not refute its stated position.

Blocking a vacuous inference schema is not by itself a proof that a selected subject's actual counterfactual or intellectual basis is adequate. That application needs an independently motivated account of what is held fixed, the operative content, and the relevant dependence. Conversely, absence of a completed theory in this packet is no evidence that the subject lacks knowledge.

For fiṭrah, replacing necessary Creator-existence with “I will receive this particular rescue” would create a testable contingent target, but it would change the proposition. A failed prediction of a particular rescue cannot automatically defeat recognition of an existent Creator; a successful rescue cannot automatically establish the stronger identification. This is a failed direct transport, not a new test of whether God exists.
