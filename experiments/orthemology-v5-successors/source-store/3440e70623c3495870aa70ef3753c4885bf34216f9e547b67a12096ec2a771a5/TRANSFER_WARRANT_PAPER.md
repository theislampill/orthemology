# Warrant for explaining existential availability

Tenth Orthemology research candidate, 3 October 2026. This paper continues the accepted kind-priority investigation without changing its frozen findings. The present conclusions await a fresh independent review.

## The question and the present assessment

The most promising affirmative defence is not that a possible answer magically becomes an actual one. It is that the actual availability of a kind for instantiation presents a genuine explanatory demand, and that this demand does not disappear merely because every displayed production starts from a bearer already instantiating the target class. Where an independent possible answer shows that the demand is not inherently unsatisfiable, there is a defeasible rational reason against treating actual existential availability as an unexplained exception. That reason can be appreciated basically. It does not require a neutral decision procedure or proof that every alternative cosmos is impossible.

This gives the transfer to an explicitly existential-supply relation a more specific positive rationale than an appeal to a generic appetite for explanations. But the force is prima facie. A serious challenge contests whether the availability respect remains unanswered when a complete actual history contains genuine production at every stage, rather than merely questioning whether an extra explanation would be welcome. The distinction matters both to the basic judgement and to attempts to derive transfer from causal counterfactuals.

The sharpest new negative result concerns the latter attempt. Removing every cause of a token effect is different from removing every account adequate to the existential-supply question. Under the second description, a proposed received-efficacy history can retain its actual productive causes. The ordinary causal-dependence rationale therefore does not automatically justify the supply-specific counterfactual needed by that adaptation. This is an argument about a changed explanatory relation, not a finite modal countermodel or a metaphysical certification of the rival.

The resulting position is differentiated: a newly targeted prima-facie reason for existential-supply answerability; an independently warranted local adequacy norm inherited from the accepted kind paper; a precise limitation on one attempted derivation of the stronger transfer; and no new actual necessary-participant conclusion. The application still needs an independently warranted modal witness, the exact scope of the transfer, and a justified assessment of joint explanation.

## 1 The exact target and inherited boundary

Let Q be a fixed family of classifications covering the actual contingent candidate bearers, under the substantive kind conditions in the accepted kind paper. Let p_Q say that at least one classification in Q has a contingently existing instance. Q is fixed from the actual situation; it is not replaced by the kinds present in a comparison world. The existence of at least one such instance is the actual target assumed for this investigation. A contingent exercise alone does not establish that its bearer is contingent.

Write E_O for an ordinary adequate explanation of the proposition at the declared source standard. Write E_A for an adequate answer specifically concerning its existential availability. These are working distinctions for assessment. E_A is not defined as explanation by a necessary being, a first member, a unique source, or a fully explained source.

The target transfer T_A says: if p_Q actually holds and an E_A answer to that same p_Q is possible, an actual E_A answer exists. The investigation assesses its warrant rather than taking that conditional as an additional established premise.

The accepted local norm is this: an activity cannot, solely through its output, supply the antecedent existential availability on which its productive channel depends. It applies to an explicit supply question, and is rationally warranted at that bounded scope. It does not by itself exclude an entire joint history, nor establish that E_A is the very relation to which a previously warranted transfer principle applies.

The two accepted components already established the smaller modal-witness burden, the common-basis limitation, the difference between witness explanation and exhaustive inventory explanation, and the narrow null-preservation result for a specified law-only adaptation. Ninth already established the importance of preserving the same explanatory standard and the prima-facie appeal of original possession. None of those is claimed as a discovery here. The new work assesses the reasons for the transfer itself after the target and its supply respect have been separated.

Primary inspection is centred on Pruss2009 §3.2.2 and connected passages, with source locators and custody in SOURCE_LOCATORS.md. The direct 2004 antecedent was also reread at the relevant passages; no new synopsis of that work is added.

## 2 Why the supply respect is a real candidate for explanation

### 2.1 Availability can be the issue without a demand for a first event

Consider two admitted sorts of causal answer in a schematic workshop. A copying activity makes a numerically new patterned object by using an antecedent patterned object. A different activity first assembles a patterned object using resources that do not already instantiate that pattern. Both can be genuine production. The first answer explains the new recipient; the second can also explain the availability of the pattern for that productive lineage. Nothing about the distinction makes copying unreal or passive.

The example is a control on the explanatory distinction, not an analogy proving that the cosmos has a first manufacturer. In the cosmological question, the target is timeless, and there need not be an earliest member. The relevant issue is the source of the availability relied on by the offered account, rather than the time at which availability began. A plural account or one containing infinitely many subordinate producers can address that issue if its relevant availability is supplied within a wider account that does not merely assume the very availability being investigated.

The question is independently intelligible. It is not generated by first deciding that a necessary source exists and then defining a question only that source can answer. One can ask it about a restricted class that has contingent outsiders. An outsider's existence can remain unexplained at that restricted target. A nonproductive metaphysical account also remains eligible if it really accounts for the target's availability. The target fixes what needs explanation; it does not predetermine a bearer type.

### 2.2 A constraint on relevance differs from a preferred cause category

It would be unreasonable to argue that because a light could have been switched on intentionally, its actual illumination must have an intentional explanation. The same illumination may in fact be caused accidentally. Restricting explanations to a preferred agent, mechanism or discipline is not automatically licensed by the possibility of an explanation of that sort.

The affirmative should therefore not defend transfer for every freely chosen subrelation of explanation. Its better proposal is that E_A marks a condition of adequacy for this particular question, not a preference for one explanatory mechanism. An answer that uses antecedent instantiation to explain an additional instance has not thereby answered a request for the availability of instantiation on which its channel operates. This criticism concerns the direction and target of the offered answer. It does not require deterministic production, contrastive selection of one world, or an explanation of every background truth.

This gives E_A a principled claim to attention. It also identifies its burden accurately. Keeping the proposition p_Q unchanged does not alone keep every explanatory question about it unchanged. The bare existential proposition does not syntactically demand an account of every witness or of the origin of all witnesses. The affirmative must defend the availability respect as an appropriate demand at this target. Its independent intelligibility and the warranted local norm give real reasons to do so; they do not analytically settle universal adequacy.

### 2.3 Explanation of obtaining cannot be replaced by a proof of satisfaction

Showing that an actual object instantiates a classification establishes p_Q. It need not explain why the instantiation obtains. Conversely, giving a genuine causal explanation of a particular instance goes beyond merely proving satisfaction. The active opponent is entitled to insist on this distinction: its local answers include real actual producers and productions.

Accordingly, the affirmative cannot reject every witness-mediated account as a logical trick. Its specific objection is to the account's use of target-class availability as an antecedent resource. The issue survives even when the recipient is genuinely produced. Whether that objection applies to a complete joint account must be examined at joint scope; it cannot be won by substituting the weaker claim that a list of witnesses is not yet an explanation.

## 3 The strongest positive case for target-preserving transfer

### 3.1 The defeasible anti-exemption argument

Here is the strongest affirmative argument at its proper epistemic strength.

First, the question concerns an actual feature of reality: the target kinds are contingently instantiated. It is not a request to explain a falsehood, an arbitrary linguistic decoration, or an already known impossibility.

Second, the local channel-dependence judgement identifies an explanatory respect that additional received production can leave untouched. The respect is not added because the ordinary account is emotionally unsatisfying. It concerns the existential availability that the ordinary account uses in doing its explanatory work.

Third, if an independently warranted possible account genuinely supplies that availability, the demand has an intelligible attainable standard. This removes one principled reason to exempt the demand, namely that no answer of that respect could exist. The possible account need not be actual, unique, necessary, or the best conceivable explanation.

Fourth, the move from a familiar local case to the timeless class question should not automatically remove the demand solely because the proposed actual history has no first member. A change in number, temporal extent or distribution of productive roles is not by itself an explanation of why the availability respect ceases to need an answer. The explanatory request is not a demand for the earliest event.

These considerations support a defeasible presumption that the actual availability question has an answer in E_A. They supply more than a permission to ask: they identify a real respect of actuality, a reason that the offered answer may leave it untreated, and a reason against exempting that respect merely because of the account's global shape. This is an ampliative rational reason. It is not the deduction that whatever question is legitimate must have an actual answer.

The reason is appropriately modest. Removing a particular obstacle does not by itself establish the conclusion. The support depends on the antecedent rational force of taking eligible actuality to be intelligible, and on the claim that this availability respect is still genuinely at issue. A thinker who directly appreciates those contents can have basic defeasible warrant without first deriving them from a different principle. Merely calling them basic, however, does not turn a judgement about one channel into a judgement about all possible joint accounts.

### 3.2 What the possible witness contributes

The reduced target helps in a definite way. One suitable instance produced through a target-independent channel can demonstrate possible availability supply; reproducing the complete actual inventory is unnecessary. That can make the third step easier to defend without making the other steps automatic.

The possible witness is a test of the demand's eligibility, not a deposit of actual productive resources. It neither transfers the identity of its producer to the actual world nor tells us that the same mechanism operates here. The actual answer, if warranted, can differ. The possible situation must nevertheless answer the same availability question rather than merely exhibit another produced witness whose availability again depends on a Q-instance.

A defender can therefore improve the case through independent modal warrant for that witness. But a richly described outsider does not create that warrant. The accepted origin-dependence condition and fixed-Q discipline remain separate; the smaller target does not discharge them.

### 3.3 Why this does not demand the strongest explanation

The proposed E_A answer need not settle why its source chose this outcome, prove that the effect was inevitable, identify all causes, explain its producer's own existence, or explain all other products. It can be plural and indeterministic. A necessary bearer with a contingent productive exercise is not excluded merely because the exercise is contingent.

The phrase adequate answer concerns this specified respect. It must not be converted into complete explanation in every respect. This matters because an ordinary account can be entirely adequate to its own question while leaving other questions unanswered. The affirmative is entitled to defend the availability question; it is not entitled to infer that every improvement over an adequate answer must actually be realised.

## 4 The serious challenge to that positive case

### 4.1 The received-efficacy reply concerns explanatory coverage

The strongest opponent does not posit dormant powers waiting for a first activation. It offers an actual nonempty history in which each source genuinely has the power it exercises and each relevant recipient is genuinely produced. It then contends that these actual productions account for the existential actuality of their recipients, and that their joint coverage leaves no further existential fact untreated.

This reply is not yet a certified adequate explanation. Its account of joint coverage needs defence, just as the affirmative's joint nonpresupposition does. But it directly challenges the second and fourth steps above. If the total account really addresses all the obtaining that makes p_Q true, the absence of an additional original supplier is not a randomly selected unexplained exception. It is a substantive view of what explanatory adequacy consists in.

That challenge has more force than saying that another cosmos can be described. It identifies a place where the affirmative's reason may change its content: from a channel's dependence on one antecedent witness to an allegedly unaccounted-for availability of the entire class. The accepted common-basis result explains why the change is not automatic. The new transfer defence cannot treat the opponent as granting that very global lack of explanation.

### 4.2 Why the affirmative reason nevertheless survives

The opponent's assertion of joint coverage does not itself answer the availability criticism. A collection of answers can each employ the very existential resource under investigation even though each answer makes a genuine contribution. The affirmative may rationally judge that receiving an available resource and accounting for its availability remain different respects when the roles are distributed indefinitely. Nothing in ordinary logic forces that judgement to wait for a proof that every alternative metaphysics is impossible.

The present record therefore supports neither a flat equivalence of the two accounts nor an all-things-considered victory for one. The affirmative has a target-directed prima-facie reason; the rival has a specific challenge to the scope of that reason. The missing step is not a neutral algorithm. It is a successful account of what the whole actual explanatory history provides in the specified respect.

The original-source proposal also carries its own burden. Intrinsic possession of power does not, by itself, explain the bearer's actuality. For a restricted class question, an outside bearer's existence can legitimately remain background. For the actual coverage used in the necessary-participant inference, the outside bearer's status is a further consequence to be earned, not a label that makes its actuality self-explanatory.

### 4.3 Local epistemic success does not settle the disputed respect

Reasoning from reliable perceptions, successful causal explanations or well-supported inquiry can provide strong motives to resist wholly unconnected brute occurrences. But denial of an E_A answer need not amount to denial of all those local causes and explanations. The active rival preserves them.

Consequently, the claim that inquiry needs actual causes does not immediately show that inquiry needs every global availability demand answered by a target-independent account. Nor does admitting one disputed explanatory limit automatically license arbitrary causeless failures in otherwise well-supported local explanations. A further generalisation would be needed in either direction. This is a limitation on that support route, not a denial that broad rational intelligibility can be basically warranted.

## 5 The causal-counterfactual adaptation

### 5.1 The exact new candidate

The causal route is attractive because it tries to turn a possibility premise into a necessity of actual causal explanation rather than merely announcing a rational preference. The relevant source loci are T-CAUSAL and T-RETURN. We need not repeat that argument to test a proposed supply-specific adaptation.

Let A mean that there are obtaining facts, activities or other real explanantia that objectively provide an E_A-adequate account of p_Q. No A means their absence, not our ignorance of them or the deletion of a written description. The adaptation would need a warrant for the following claim in a situation where A holds: if no such adequate explanantia obtained, p_Q would not obtain. It would also need a suitable return judgement linking a supposed actual situation with p_Q and no A to the relevant comparison situations where A holds.

The first is a substantive dependence claim. It is not the accepted local norm. The local norm tells us that a particular activity's output does not supply its own antecedent availability. The new conditional says that the target would fail if no adequate availability account existed. One is a restriction on what answers a question; the other concerns what reality would be like without answers of that class.

### 5.2 Why the deletion rationale changes

In an ordinary token-causal test, removing every cause of an effect is a supposition about all its productive causes. In the proposed E_A test, removing every adequate availability account can leave the internal productive causes untouched. An opponent's genuine received efficacy is not removed merely because it has not been credited as adequate to the supply question.

This is especially clear in a mixed control. Add an independent outside production of one F-instance to a proposed internally productive F-history. Removing the outside production need not, on the opponent's own account, remove the internal history. If the outside production still counts as one adequate nonpresupposing supply of the existential target, its removal does not automatically eliminate the target. If it does not count as an adequate supply unless it also excludes such independent persistence, the adaptation has strengthened adequacy to include precisely the disputed dependence.

The control is not an assertion that the internal history is metaphysically possible. It asks what the proposed reason has established. To infer the supply-deletion conditional, one must independently show that the internally productive residue cannot sustain the target, or that no account with such a residue could count as E_A. Ordinary facts about productive action alone have not yet shown either.

Ordinary overdetermination does not erase this issue. Deleting all independently sufficient token causes may remove the familiar redundancy. Deleting only the accounts admitted as E_A can leave the rival's productive resources in place. The quantifier's domain has changed.

### 5.3 The best repair and its price

The best affirmative repair is to insist that we are discussing causes of the availability itself, not causes of individual recipients. On that individuation, every genuine cause of this very explanandum qua availability would already be an E_A supplier. The causal principle could then target the relevant respect rather than an arbitrarily preferred causal subtype.

That repair has real content and deserves consideration. It locates the issue in the object and relation of explanation rather than merely requesting a stronger slogan. But it requires an independent reason to identify the availability explanandum in this manner and to apply the relevant causal counterfactual to it. A possible outside production can show how such a cause might operate; it does not by itself establish that every adequate actual answer must have the same target-independent scope.

The return judgement also remains separate. Metaphysical possibility of a comparison situation does not itself show that the actual situation must remain relevant under every nested counterfactual return. A defender may reasonably use an intuitive rather than a complete algorithmic account of relevance. What is needed is a reason for this specific comparison, not a demand for a universal formal semantics.

The narrow conclusion is thus important: the causal adaptation cannot be credited as independent evidence for E_A transfer merely because an ordinary causal argument supports a transfer at its own relation. Replacing the relation requires checking the premise that does the explanatory work.

### 5.4 A contingency check peculiar to the reduced target

That every actual witness is contingent does not imply that the existential proposition p_Q is contingent. Different contingent witnesses might occupy a role in different worlds. This observation does not establish such a modal arrangement or any necessary occupant.

It matters when an adaptation invokes a principle whose domain is contingent states or propositions. The reduced kind target must then independently satisfy that domain, or the principle must have a justified broader domain. The modal reduction to one possible witness does not decide this. A restricted transfer formulated for true explainable propositions need not inherit the contingency restriction; the point concerns a proposed causal replacement of it, not a refutation of the original kind route.

## 6 The actual-powers connection as a stronger control

The connected source locus T-POWERS suggests testing a stronger theory in which possibilities require actual powers capable of their realisation. Such a theory can potentially explain why merely drawing a possible donor is insufficient: genuine modal warrant would itself answer to actual reality.

For the present investigation, however, two distinctions determine what follows. A power to bring about another F-witness is not automatically a power to supply the antecedent availability required by the entire F-directed channel. The latter is the relevant achievement if the modal premise is possible E_A rather than possible ordinary production. A modal account must preserve that distinction in what it says can be realised.

Nor is the actual ground of a possible producer's availability automatically that very producer. An actual bearer g might have a mediated power to produce a source s with a different classification. From s being outside Q in a comparison situation, it does not follow that g is outside Q actually. If the proposed achievement requires s's supply to be independent of Q, a Q-dependent chain producing s may fail that requirement; but that failure needs the independence and achievement-preservation argument. It cannot be inferred merely by identifying the ground of a possibility with its possible realiser.

Further, an actual capacity need not be actually exercised. If an independently warranted capacity bearer lay outside Q, actual coverage would already warrant its noncontingent and therefore necessary existence; that intermediate gain should be credited without waiting for actual exercise. But an actual bearer of a relevant capacity would not, for that reason alone, be an actual contributor to the history explaining p_Q. A route that adds a principle of actual exercise or derives it by restricted explanation has a further step to defend. Neither the actual outside capacity bearer nor its exercise is established here.

The strongest affirmative version can try to exclude actual unsupplied availability itself. Begin with actual p_Q and assume for reductio that no E_A answer obtains. Then the composite claim q_A, that p_Q obtains and no E_A answer obtains, is actually true. Mere actuality does not yet activate a theory specifically requiring powers for nonactual possibilities.

The independently warranted modal premise M supplies an important next step. A possible E_A answer is a possible situation where q_A is false. Thus, under the reductio assumption, q_A is contingent, even if p_Q itself is necessary. This does not derive q_A's contingency from the contingency of its witnesses; it derives it from actual q_A and the possible answer that the transfer argument already needs.

Now add the modal commitment that an actually contingent truth can be both false and possible in some comparison situation. At such a situation, q_A is nonactual but possible. If the proposed powers theory covers this composite state, including its explanatory-absence component, it requires an eligible power or chain capable of realising q_A. To reach a contradiction, add the achievement-preservation claim that any such realisation would supply an E_A answer to p_Q. The realised target would then both have and lack an E_A answer.

This is a substantive conditional route from the actual target. Its ingredients are M, the specified modal commitment, the powers theory's exact domain and realisability conditions, and achievement preservation. The capacity/exercise distinction alone does not defeat it: the argument is about the impossibility of a capacity's proposed target, not an assumption that every actual power must be exercised.

The precise remaining question is whether bringing about the composite target necessarily supplies its existential availability, or can amount to genuine token production whose availability still depends on antecedent F-instantiation. Only the former supports the required contradiction. Treating production of any adequate witness as supplying E_A would settle the disputed respect by assumption; treating a merely enabling channel as an actual realisation would also miss the powers theory's required achievement.

The exact-qualified-fact contradiction technique is already owned by Ninth F1, especially The exact origin fact bridge and The premise relocation result. There, fact-level productive coverage preserves an actual bearer's unreceived whole existence, while causal-content fidelity makes its productive realisation received. The contradiction tests the cost of that stronger coverage; it is not newly discovered here.

The present adaptation changes the target and the operative fidelity requirement. It starts without an identified actual original bearer and uses existential availability without an adequate supply answer. Productively realising a recipient is recognisably different from its whole existence being unreceived; by contrast, productively realising a witness does not by itself constitute E_A adequacy. That is exactly why achievement preservation needs fresh warrant here. The modal premise does yield the useful contingency of q_A, avoiding the need to infer contingency of p_Q from its witnesses. The credited advance is this target-specific check and diagnosis, not a new contradiction method or a successful productive-coverage theorem. Ninth's warning remains: an independently supported general theory could do real work, but a target-specific completeness demand may merely relocate the desired conclusion into its coverage premise.

Likewise, an alleged possibility of supply independent of Q raises the useful question whether it could be grounded wholly in Q-dependent powers. Answering negatively requires a substantive claim about what those powers can realise at the existential-supply respect. These are good target-specific routes if independently supported. Neither is settled just by the general existence of actual powers, nor dismissed because some powers can remain unexercised.

## 7 Two shortcuts that would lose the result

First, the supply respect cannot simply be inserted into the proposition whose actual truth is assumed. The proposition that the target kinds are instantiated is known under the granted target premise. The proposition that their availability has been independently supplied adds the disputed explanatory achievement. A principle applying only to actual truths cannot establish that added actuality by taking it as its input.

Second, E_A should not be defined by absence of every contingent contributor. A necessary source can produce a contingent agent that genuinely contributes to later effects. The existence of that internal agent need not be an unexplained antecedent of the whole account if it is supplied within it. The relevant exclusion concerns an account wholly unable to address its target availability, not a demand to purify every explanatory chain of contingent members.

Both restrictions preserve the desired intermediate conclusion. If there is an actual concrete contributor outside the actual covering class, it deserves credit as a noncontingent, hence necessary, particular participant. There is no need first to prove unity, complete sourcehood, P1/P2, N2/N3, categorical intrinsic explanation or essential integrity. The present investigation simply has not yet established that antecedent.

## 8 What has been earned

The positive advance is a defence of why target-sensitive existential answerability has prima-facie rational support. Its force comes from actual explanatory content, the independently intelligible channel/availability distinction, and resistance to an exemption based solely on global shape. It is not reducible to the mere possibility of some explanation. This supports provisional rational preference for an account that addresses the supply respect, while leaving the actual source inference open.

The negative advance is a precise diagnosis of a tempting independent derivation. The supply-deletion counterfactual is stronger than the local relevance norm, and ordinary cause deletion does not establish it after the domain of qualifying accounts has changed. The appropriate repair would independently defend the causal individuation of availability and the relevant counterfactual application. That repair may be basically warranted; it cannot be counted twice by using the desired supply dependence as both the premise and the new conclusion. The powers adaptation sharpens the target-specific contingency and fidelity checks while retaining Ninth F1's ownership of the exact-fact contradiction technique.

The received-efficacy rival remains active and genuinely productive in the test. It is neither defeated by a missing first node nor certified adequate by a diagram. Its serious challenge concerns whether joint actual production already covers the obtaining that makes p_Q true. The affirmative's serious response concerns whether that coverage still uses the availability under investigation. These are precise competing assessments, with genuine reason on the affirmative side and an identified scope challenge on the other.

The current status is therefore prima-facie support for E_A answerability, rationally warranted local nonpresupposition, and a justified limitation on the attempted causal derivation. All-things-considered actual application of E_A transfer, joint exclusion and the necessary-participant consequence remain unestablished in this paper. This is a bounded research judgement, not a claim that no rational person can possess stronger basic warrant.
