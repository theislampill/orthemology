## 2. Typed latent-state model

For person/system `i`, times `t0<t1`, target proposition/domain `q`, and
intervention `j`, distinguish:

```text
D(i,t,q)       latent disposition state;
OCCL(i,t)      impairment/occlusion set;
REC(i,t,q)     occurrent recognition;
ASSENT(i,t,q)  assent;
REPORT(i,t,q)  verbal/self-report output;
BEH(i,t,q)     behaviour;
REM(j)         reminder/corrective intervention class;
LAND(j,i)      intervention causally lands on the target mechanism;
TRANS(i,j,t0,t1) latent transition attributable to j;
SRCOMP(D,q)    source-compatibility under an explicit source interpretation;
WARRANT(i,q)   recipient/subject warrant;
```

A source-relative restoration predicate might be:

\[
Restore(i,j,q,t_0,t_1)
:= Impaired(D_{t_0})\land Healthier(D_{t_1})\land LAND(j,i)\land
CausalTransition(j,D_{t_0},D_{t_1})\land SRCOMP(D_{t_1},q).
\]

This is a model specification. It is not directly observed merely because
`REPORT` or `BEH` changes.

## 3. Output-fibre theorem application

Let a model `m` include latent states and causal history. Let the registered
observation profile be:

\[
E(m)=(REPORT_{pre},REPORT_{post},BEH_{pre},BEH_{post},\ldots)
\]

and the target be:

\[
Q(m)=Restore(m).
\]

If there exist `m1,m2` such that:

\[
E(m_1)=E(m_2)\quad\text{and}\quad Q(m_1)\neq Q(m_2),
\]

then restoration is not identifiable from that profile over the declared model
class. This is an application of the repository’s fibre criterion, not a new
theorem.

The checker supplies two explicit collisions:

```text
corrected output after true latent restoration
  = same two-timepoint output as memorization/compliance without restoration;

no immediate output change after latent restoration
  = same two-timepoint output as no change and no restoration.
```

Therefore both false positives and false negatives are possible under an
output-only profile.

## 4. Proper-function firewall

No transition from output success to proper function is licensed without the
account-specific function-fixing relation.

### Causal-role function

A module may contribute to correct output under the current system
decomposition. This says nothing about historical selection, a truth aim,
source-relative fitrah purpose, or warrant.

### Selected-effect function

A response may have been selected because it produced conformity, social
approval, or deception. Selected effect does not entail truth-connected
normativity.

### Design function

A designed module needs an independently identified designer, specification,
and authorization. The existence of a useful role does not establish those
facts.

### Plantingian proper function

Plantinga’s account, at its own scope, requires proper operation under a design
plan, a suitable cognitive environment, a truth aim, and high objective
reliability. None is established by one correct output or runtime closure.

### Fitrah-oriented function

The source-relative route additionally needs authenticated source, target
applicability, latent-state interpretation, impairment model, appropriate
environment, operation, causal landing, and a truth/world bridge. It cannot be
made neutral by relabelling a questionnaire score “fitrah.”

## 6. Countermodels

### SB-CM28 — memorization

The subject memorizes the expected answer; post-output is correct; latent
orientation and defeaters are unchanged.

### SB-CM29 — social compliance

The subject reports assent under social pressure while privately rejecting the
content. Report changes; recognition and warrant do not.

### SB-CM30 — expectancy/demand response

The subject infers the experimenter’s desired answer. The intervention changes
behaviour through demand characteristics, not restoration.

### SB-CM31 — latent change without report

Occlusion decreases and recognition becomes available, but fear, language
limits, or response policy blocks report. Restoration can occur without the
registered output.

### SB-CM32 — defeater suppression

A temporary intervention suppresses an objection or competing response without
repairing the target disposition. Output improves and then decays.

### SB-CM33 — alternative strategy

A generic reasoning heuristic yields the correct answer without activating the
source-relative disposition posited by the model.

### SB-CM34 — proxy noninvariance

The same score has different meanings across languages, prior-learning groups,
or source traditions. Measurement noninvariance defeats cross-group latent
comparison.

### SB-CM35 — source-relative construct mismatch

A neutral psychological construct such as confidence or coherence is mapped to
fiṭrah without a source interpretation and bearer-applicability bridge. The
proxy exists; the theological identification does not.

### SB-CM36 — truth/warrant split

A proposition is true and the subject reports it, but the process is accidental
or defeated. Truth, assent, proper function, and warrant remain distinct.

should preregister at least:

```text

B. Rival mechanisms

     generic reasoning, prior knowledge, temporary defeater suppression,
     and language effects.

C. Design
   - comparator intervention;
   - randomization/blinding where feasible;
   - pre, immediate post, and delayed follow-up;
   - negative-control outcomes and interventions;
   - mediation variables tied to the claimed causal pathway;
   - preregistered exclusion and missing-data rules.

D. Measurement
   - multi-method indicators rather than one scalar;
   - measurement invariance across groups/languages;
   - reliability and calibration;
   - separation of classifier/probe accuracy from represented content;
   - adversarial tests against scripted and impersonal response generators.

E. Causal/identification ceiling
   - exact model class;
   - which fibre collisions are excluded and by what evidence;
   - no promotion from output change to latent restoration, proper function,
     recipient warrant, or theological truth.
```

This is a preregistration request, not a claim that such a study has been run.

## 8. Ontology and personal/impersonal effects

A latent-state model can be realized personally, impersonally, distributively,
or as a conceptualist classification. A causal change in a representation does
not by itself establish a subject who apprehends it de se or intentionally takes
it up as a reason. Property dualism is likewise not inferred from a distinct
latent variable; the variable may be multiply realized under physicalist,
property-dualist, hylomorphic, functional, or other architectures.
