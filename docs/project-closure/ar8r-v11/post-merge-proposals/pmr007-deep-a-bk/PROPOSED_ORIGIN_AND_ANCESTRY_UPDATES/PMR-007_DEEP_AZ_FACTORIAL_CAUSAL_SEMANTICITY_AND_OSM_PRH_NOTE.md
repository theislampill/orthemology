## OSM evidence use

Sun et al. report that hippocampal activity progressively decorrelates into
latent task-state-like representations and that CSCG most closely reproduced
both selected endpoint organization and the reported trajectory among the
tested models.  This supports distinguishing sensory form from latent task
state as an empirical question.  The study does not independently cross every
sensory form with every task-state content, nor does it establish intrinsic
semanticity, proper function, or personality.

## PRH evidence use

PRH supplies evidence and a hypothesis about cross-model and cross-modal kernel
alignment.  It can motivate candidate form/modalities and shared-structure
comparisons.  It does not supply a frozen literal content variable, a crossed
causal intervention, or an A/R architecture likelihood.

## Language and source custody

A paraphrase, translation, recitation, or protocol variant counts as a new form
with the same content only after lexical, syntactic, contextual, referential,
source, version, and applicability guards are satisfied.  Otherwise, the
factorial design crosses analyst labels rather than preserved meanings.
