## 1. Question

Does the union of the strongest currently admitted **neutral** structures force one personal, wise, speaking ground, or can one finite typed model satisfy them while withholding those conclusions?

The tested union `U` includes:

```text

U3 an externally actual impersonal originator/realizer, added as a rival premise rather than derived;
```

`U` excludes source-conditioned divine predicates and does not treat the role labels in U8 as already established Divine attributes.

## 3. R5CU-1A — common-model satisfiability

There exists an allocation of W/P/K role predicates among `{a,b,c,d}` such that:


Therefore `U` does not entail one common personal, wise, speaking bearer in the declared formal semantics.
