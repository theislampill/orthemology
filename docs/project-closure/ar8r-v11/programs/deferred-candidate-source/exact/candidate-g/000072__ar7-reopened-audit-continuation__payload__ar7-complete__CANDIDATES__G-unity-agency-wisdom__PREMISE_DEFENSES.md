# Premise defenses — Candidate G

## G-P1 — Orthability is a joint explanandum

**Premise.** A genuine correction episode jointly requires modal alternatives, diachronic identity, content, an objective assessment ordering, actual transition, and a relation making assessment relevant to realization.

**Defense.** Removing any component changes the phenomenon:

- without identity, there is no persisting target or claim being corrected;
- without alternatives, there is no contrast between retained error and correction;
- without content, there is no determinate error-content;
- without assessment, there is change but no correction;
- without actual transition, correction remains merely possible;
- without assessment-to-realization linkage, improvement is accidental rather than guided.

**Limit.** This is a constitutive analysis of guided correction, not a proof that every component shares one ontological ground.

## G-P2 — Carrier unity is insufficient

**Premise.** Housing independent primitive features in one bearer does not yield explanatory integration.

**Defense.** Carrier-boxing invariance gives a structure-preserving bijection between the plural and boxed theories. It is supported by established distinctions among ontological, ideological, and brute-fact parsimony and by derivational accounts of unification.

**Status.** PROVED WITH SOURCE-CRITICAL REDUCTION.

## G-P3 — Derivational integration is a real theoretical virtue

**Premise.** Deriving many explananda with fewer independent primitives and reusable patterns is a defeasible theoretical virtue.

**Defense.** It increases understanding, exposes counterfactual dependence, and rules out joint profiles rather than merely redescribing them. Kitcher and Kovacs develop derivational unification; Cowling and McIntyre show why reducing entity count while retaining primitive ideology/brute facts is not an automatic gain.

**Countermodel.** A maximally unifying but false or asymmetric derivation. Therefore unification requires fit, direction, and non-ad-hocness and is not sufficient by itself.

## G-P4 — Rational architecture can integrate Ω

**Premise.** Rational-purposive architecture can make representation, evaluation, selection, and realization systematically interdependent.

**Defense.** Practical reasoning is not merely a container for independent features: represented alternatives delimit choice; reasons affect ranking; ranking affects selection; selection and efficacy affect outcome. An integrated architecture predicts failures when links are severed.

**Countermodel.** A nonconscious control system can realize the same functional graph. Thus the premise supports functional rationality, not intellect in the rich personal sense.

## G-P5 — Impersonal rivals are inferior

**Premise.** No impersonal architecture matches the explanatory profile at equal cost.

**Defense.** NOT YET ESTABLISHED. Candidate rivals include immanent teleology, holistic powers, impersonal rational order, evolutionary/natural functional organization, and distributed control.

**Status.** PRIMARY OPEN BURDEN.

## G-P6 — Abductive virtues are truth-conducive here

**Premise.** Cross-domain unification and brute-fact reduction warrant comparative belief.

**Defense.** At most defeasible. The argument needs either a calibrated meta-abductive principle or a narrower claim about understanding rather than truth.

**Status.** OPEN; cannot be smuggled in as a PSR.
