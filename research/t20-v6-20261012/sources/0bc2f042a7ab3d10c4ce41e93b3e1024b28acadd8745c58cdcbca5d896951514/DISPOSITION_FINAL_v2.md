## Why both designs fail the novelty gate

The first design proposed separating causal carriage, competence, warrant, authority and identity. The second proposed distinguishing a maintained organisation from a successor whose renewal is externally orchestrated. Both are useful distinctions, but their operative content is already explicit in the recovered companion and earlier user argument. Adding recruitment or repair to the function label does not supply a new bearer criterion. A symmetry-selector counterexample would also largely formalise an existing challenge.

A copy can remain an eligible continuation. The inherited distinction concerns what was actually causally continued, whose re-realisation capacities are genuinely exercised, and which future faculty survives. It does not exclude copying by terminology or require substrate-free self-sufficiency.

Montévil and Mossio's *Biological organisation as closure of constraints* (2015), DOI 10.1016/j.jtbi.2015.02.029, is already a substantively used companion comparator. A new repository witness was inspected privately; it receives no fresh source synopsis or new-work allowance here. Exact identities and inspection limits are recorded separately.

## The actual question still open

When nested or overlapping candidate organisations each contribute effectively to maintaining and renewing one productive episode, what independently warranted fact makes a particular boundary the appropriate bearer of the claimed exercise, and does that fact survive the proposed re-realisation?

The current material does not generally derive that boundary. An intervention can test a specified causal attribution; naming its target does not itself justify the bearer boundary. Enlarging the organisation to include an external coordinator may establish a claim about the enlarged collective, but it changes the original referent. Organisation-level powers can be genuine and derivative; attributing them neither establishes original existential possession nor determines numerical identity or legitimate authority.

This is an unresolved research burden, not a theorem that bearer attribution is impossible. A further advance needs an independently argued discriminator or a precise new obstruction. Repeating the inherited capacity, provenance and endogeneity conditions does not meet that burden. The present candidate therefore stops at source-grounded non-admission.
