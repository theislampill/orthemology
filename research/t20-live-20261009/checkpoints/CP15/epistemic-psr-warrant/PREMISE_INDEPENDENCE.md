# Local provenance without a global explanatory closure

## The claim being tested

Let G say that every actual contingent fact has an explanation. Let R be the 2020/2021 Koons–Pruss principle requiring an external explanation of each plurality of basic natural facts. Let L describe an actual causal/provenance condition for an empirical belief and S its safety over a specified epistemically relevant neighborhood.

The target is the entailment from L and S to G, or to R. It is not the conditional from the authors' **entire** epistemological/metaphysical package to R. In particular, the interpretation below does not grant their epistemic symmetry and unrestricted proximity claims and then somehow evade their consequences.

## Interpretation

Use a family of world-descriptions with the following fixed interpretation:

- B is a contingent, wholly particular background fact about a separate natural object. At the actual world B obtains without an explanation. A permitted alternative lacks B. There is no explanatory or causal arrow into B, and its existence is not stipulated necessary or self-explanatory.
- X is the binary condition of a local observed target: a lamp is lit or unlit.
- C is the physically operative signal arriving from that target; E is the subject's resulting visual evidence; Y is the subject's belief formed through that visual route.
- In the intact regime, X causes C, C causes E, and the informative aspect of E causes the corresponding Y. The target and the subject's ability to discriminate it vary together across the intact cases. C and E are not merely idle true descriptions of events that affect Y for unrelated reasons.
- The fixed mechanism has C = X, E = C, and Y = E, with one interpreted truth-conditional content: Y = 1 is acceptance that X = 1. The arrows are explicitly granted causal/operative relations, not inferred merely from these algebraic equalities.

Meaning, genuine belief, perceptual competence, and appropriate operative use are interpreted premises of the thought experiment. They are not generated by writing variables into a file. The interpretation grants the familiar local epistemic condition under test without purporting to reduce it.

The actual description has B true, X = C = E = Y = 1. The intact family also permits X = C = E = Y = 0, both with B and without B. This supplies substantive target variation rather than making the safety of the lamp belief vacuous by retaining only worlds in which the lamp is lit. The local episode's causal regime is insensitive to the separate B variation.

First consider the modal structure whose admissible worlds are just intact descriptions of this family. G is false at the actual world because of B. R is also false if B is among the basic natural facts: its singleton has no explanation. Yet local perceptions are caused in every world of this structure. Accordingly, the inference from one brute fact to the *metaphysical possibility* of an uncaused perception is not valid merely by the logical form of “some fact lacks an explanation.” The interpretation's restricted modal space is not certified as the real metaphysical space. Deciding whether it excludes possibilities illicitly is precisely where a further symmetry/recombination argument is required.

Now enlarge the modal space to include a deceptive world D: the lamp is unlit, but an appearance E = 1 occurs without an appropriate causal connection to the lamp and produces Y = 1 through the same uptake procedure. D can represent an uncaused E or an uncaused intermediary C. The phenomenological evidence type is nonfactive and can be matched in D. We do not reject D as “not really a belief” or “not the identical token.” It is a candidate counterpart mistake in the sense relevant to the authors' reply to origins essentialism.

For a local neighborhood N comprising intact descriptions, every member in which Y = 1 also has X = 1. Thus the displayed safety conditional holds. A nearby intact not-X world has E = Y = 0, so the obvious corresponding sensitivity check also passes if that is selected as the closest not-X alternative. Neither check is a full analysis of knowledge.

The membership of N is an explicit modal assumption about the intact causal regime. It is not justified simply by our desire to preserve knowledge. The model identifies the hypothesis a localist has to defend: disruption of this genuinely operative route is not an epistemically close alternative merely because some other background fact is brute. Koons–Pruss challenge exactly that hypothesis. Their argument cannot be said to have been answered by drawing N.

## Adversarial controls

1. **Bring D into N.** Safety fails immediately: Y = 1 with X = 0 on the matched nonfactive evidence. Thus the test has not protected the verdict by a factive-evidence definition or by excluding errors from what counts as believing.
2. **Remove operative use.** If E is present but does not cause Y through its informative aspect, the intended provenance condition fails even when accidental correlations survive. The inherited Dretske correction is respected.
3. **Make B a universal law instead.** G may still fail, but R need not. This weaker example does not challenge the exact BNF-restricted thesis. The particular B in the main interpretation was chosen to expose this difference rather than hide it.
4. **Grant a priori-access symmetry.** If every scenario compatible with a priori information must remain live for second-order knowledge, and the authors' symmetry admits bad counterparts nearby in one such scenario, the interpretation does not establish second-order knowledge. A localist needs a competing account of epistemic access or of how its local modal condition is warranted.
5. **Demand ultimate explanation as part of L.** If local warrant is defined to require an explained entire causal ancestry, the main interpretation no longer satisfies that stronger L. This is an additional epistemic premise, not something silently contained in an ordinary X-to-E-to-Y route.
6. **Add probabilities.** None are assigned. The absence of a global chance measure neither establishes nor falsifies the explicitly qualitative S. A probability-based theory might demand more. In particular, a numerical reliability conditional on “no intrusion” cannot establish unconditional reliability when the intrusion is relevantly live.

## What follows and what does not

This is an ordinary premise-independence construction: formal/local causal and modal conditions can coexist with an unexplained contingent background. It is not evidence that the actual world is of this kind. It does not settle how worlds ought to be ordered, whether B can really be brute, how human beliefs acquire content, or whether the subject knows that it knows. No Lean wrapper, world-count statistic, or satisfaction label would improve those substantive judgments.

The authors' stronger conclusion is therefore challenged only at named bridges, not refuted with every premise preserved. Their challenge remains useful: the localist must defend the causal/modal insulation rather than treat the word “local” as a warrant generator.
