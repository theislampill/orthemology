# Warranted correction, original actuality, and the strongest R5 challenge

## Result and exact subject

An actual warranted empirical correction requires more than an occurrence, a true output, or a complete inventory of its productive history. It requires the right connection between the target, the subject's evidence, and the correction. This gives an adequacy constraint stronger than extensional success, applicable equally to personal and impersonal source proposals. Applying it to a concrete rival is world-directed only after its epistemically damaging alternatives or defeaters have been independently established. The constraint alone is not a new defeat artifact.

It does not yet give a necessary being. The inference from an unexplained fundamental basis to uncontrolled counterfeit evidence requires a further principle connecting kinds of facts and a further account of epistemic closeness. An intrinsically determinate basis can deny that transfer without identifying truth with its outputs. Essential powers alone, however, do not exclude unrelated intrusions: the rival must supply an additional completeness or closure claim, and must justify it rather than disguise it as a consequence of having powers.

The strongest surviving challenge is consequently an **intrinsic-source proposal with a principled, epistemically adequate scope of production**, genuine dependent thinkers, independently target-fixed correctness, and explicit essential connections. It is a serious philosophical challenge, not a demonstrated metaphysically possible universe. Its claims to actuality, explanatory sufficiency, and normative adequacy remain burdens. This inquiry does not establish them by assigning predicates in a model.

The affirmative result is a repaired, source-sensitive argument: begin with actual rational activity; defend its real productive dependence and target-sensitive epistemic support; demand an original adequate account of the received actuality; then argue separately for complete scope, existential necessity, one bearer, and source-level Knowledge. The substantive increment is the diagnosis of the anti-skeptical bridge's independent generalization burdens and a fresh stress test of independent overdetermination at the bearer/act interface. The recovered third-tranche work already contains the principal mode-based reply; that reply is retained, not claimed as new. No new actual-world defeat of the strongest rival is claimed.

No canonical file is changed, no archive is promoted, and no deductive Necessary-Being, integrated-champion, meniscus, or natural-closure claim is made.

## 2. The actual target has three different kinds of dependence

Fix a genuinely warranted empirical correction: a subject changes a false judgment about a particular target after obtaining and understanding appropriate evidence. This is an admitted case of knowledge, not a claim that an arbitrary successful machine output is knowledge. The argument may start from a more modest actual mental exercise, but then it earns a correspondingly weaker conclusion.

Three questions have different answers.

1. **What makes the corrected proposition true?** The target must be as represented, under the content actually at issue. The correcting practice's endorsement is not sufficient. A correction of a measurement concerns the measured object; a correction of a quotation concerns the source text. Different questions may have different appropriate targets.
2. **Why is the subject warranted?** The subject's evidence and exercise must bear the right relation to that truth. A fortuitously correct guess and a well-grounded correction can share their final sentence. The relevant reason must be doing the epistemic work claimed for it.
3. **What supplies the actual exercise?** There must be actual powers and an actual subject. An uninstantiated equation cannot itself perform the exercise. A description of the truth condition is not yet its concrete productive source.

The first is not automatically efficient production; the second is not reducible to an output frequency; the third does not automatically make its ultimate supplier the subject who understands. A full explanation may connect them. It may not substitute one for the others.

This preserves the adopted distinction between orthability-L, objective orthability-O, and a created representation/application R. Objective O is a family of conditions, not another object waiting to be manufactured. An actually true correction gives some of those conditions a world-directed application. It does not, merely by occurring, prove every intended evaluation true or every practice authoritative.

There is an important positive consequence. An alleged source that accounts for the target, the evidence, and the subject only as three independent actualities has not yet explained *knowledge*. Their apt relation is part of the actual explanandum. For the empirical case, the route from the target to the evidential basis must be appropriately maintained. For an inferential case, content and the inferential ground must be appropriately related. The same constraint applies to an alleged personal creator: making a knower, an object, and a belief is compatible with making a misleading evidential situation.

Call this **target-sensitive joint adequacy**. This is not a new name for a deity or a common bearer. It is a requirement on the explanation of this actual epistemic success. Its content is independently supplied by what distinguishes the admitted correction from a lucky true response. It can exclude a bad account without first proving a global source ontology.

## 3. The strongest positive anti-skeptical move

A defender of the world bridge can argue that received epistemic success cannot float free of reliable causal order. If appearances or their intermediaries can arise in epistemically relevant circumstances without the target that would warrant them, then the same apparent method can yield error. If such a possibility is undefeated and sufficiently close, calling the actual output knowledge is unjustified. This is a genuine objection to a rival that leaves its epistemic connections wholly ungoverned.

Koons and Pruss defend a restricted PSR using an anti-skeptical argument about empirical knowledge. Their conclusion is not already a personal source. [Koons–Pruss, §§2–5, 7–10, author PDF](https://robkoons.net/uploads/1/3/5/2/135276253/koons-pruss2020_article_skepticismandtheprincipleofsuf.pdf).

Our selective reconstruction for the present task follows; it is not the paper's full argument:

- **K1. Actual epistemic success:** at least one relevant empirical correction is knowledge, and in the stronger version we can know that some such knowledge occurs
- **K2. Epistemic integrity:** its epistemic standing meets the relevant theory's actual safety, reliability, or no-defeater requirement
- **K3. Cross-kind generalization:** allowing a primitive natural fact without the demanded explanation also permits the epistemically damaging fact-patterns
- **K4. Closeness or exposure:** those patterns count as epistemically close or cannot be assigned a sufficiently low objective risk
- **K5. Certification scope:** ruling them out requires an appropriately general, independently warranted explanation principle rather than a merely case-local entitlement
- **K6. Collective explanatory passage:** the warranted principle reaches the relevant plurality of actual natural facts, with the noncircularity, explanation typing, and explanatory witness needed for a terminal conclusion

K1–K2 exert real pressure. K3–K6 do not follow just by rewriting K1. They are where a repair would have to earn a world-directed source conclusion.

Three epistemic notions must remain distinct throughout. An **actual nearby safety failure** can matter without the subject knowing it. A **possessed or accessible undercutting defeater** concerns reasons the subject has, under the relevant defeater theory. An **epistemically possible scenario in second-order inquiry** concerns what can be certified about knowledge. They are not interchangeable. Mere inability to rule out a metaphysical description is not, on every epistemology, an actual safety failure or a possessed defeater. The argument below asks whether a rival implies the appropriate stronger condition; it does not stipulate that all unexplained possibilities are defeaters.

In particular, failure to explain why a fundamental reality exists is not yet failure to explain why a photon, memory, or judgment has its actual origin. Nor does a question about the former automatically make an alternative origin for the latter a nearby possibility. The defender must show the relevant symmetry, rather than treating all unexplained propositions as interchangeable.

This is not a cheap appeal to our inability to disprove skepticism. If the rival admits pervasive, unbounded, salient counterfeits and offers no reason their effects respect the supposed knowledge channel, it fails the admitted target. The open question is whether rejecting the demanded ultimate explanation *commits* it to that epistemically permissive picture.

## 4. A serious intrinsic-power reply, and the extra premise it actually needs

The strongest reply does not say that laws, considered as abstract sentences, produce evidence. It posits concrete reality with intrinsic determinate powers. Some properties have essential patterns of manifestation; particular cognitive subjects arise with actual capacities for understanding and correction; the target and the cognitive route are coupled in the required respects.

Bird's powers account is a serious philosophical resource for this reply: laws are consequences of essential relations among properties, not external rules pasted onto an otherwise lawless world. His monism concerns the dispositional character of fundamental properties, not one concrete cosmic bearer. He also recognizes individuation and structural challenges. This supplies a reason to take essential powers seriously, not a proof that one actual necessary producer or an epistemically adequate cognitive nature exists. [Bird, “Monistic Dispositional Essentialism,” §§2–4, PDF pp. 2–5](https://www.alexanderbird.org/Research/Papers/Monistic_Dispositional_Essentialism.pdf).

The reply must be stated more strongly than ordinary dispositional essentialism:

**Local essential powers fix how existing things act; a separate origin-completeness claim excludes new unreceived contributors to the relevant field.**

The first claim alone does not prevent an unrelated object from appearing and mimicking a signal. This is the strongest part of the source advocate's objection to a merely local powers theory. An electron's essential charge does not, just by being charge, account for every possible new electron. A disposition to respond reliably cannot by itself guarantee that its environment is not filled with counterfeit inputs.

The strengthened rival therefore has to say something like this: all members and changes in the relevant productive field arise from the intrinsic basis and its admissible exercises; the basis cannot generate an epistemically indistinguishable error in the relevant circumstances unless a real malfunction or altered target relation occurs. The latter restriction must follow from an account of the method, its target, and its normal conditions. It cannot be a ceremonial label saying that the actual belief is safe.

This is a real explanatory commitment. It is neither a consequence of a two-world table nor merely the absence of a recorded incoming edge. But it is not transparently inconsistent. A rival can seek an essential connection between the full basis and its dependent field, while declining an explanation of the basis's own existence. Alternatively it can grant a necessary unreceived basis and contend that its actuality is intrinsic. In the latter case the rival has conceded an important upstream endpoint; the remaining question is what kind of necessary source it is.

The critic can reasonably ask: why this closure principle rather than a wider possibility space? The rival owes a principled answer. But a demand for that answer is not itself a demonstration that uncaused counterfeit evidence must be possible or close. Conversely, the rival cannot claim victory merely by adding closure and safety clauses. Their truth and warrant remain to be established in the actual world.

### The adequacy constraint and what would make its application world-directed

The following combination is unacceptable for the admitted empirical case:

1. The actual correction is claimed to be warranted through method M about target T
2. The rival permits an undefeated, epistemically relevant counterfeit that preserves the subject's operative basis but disconnects it from T in a way that defeats M's warrant
3. No further target-sensitive explanation, entitlement, or independently supported risk restriction is supplied

Once premise 2 includes an actual warrant-defeating condition, the resulting exclusion is an analytic application of the admitted knowledge target. Its substantive use therefore depends on independently showing that a concrete rival really has that condition. No such general demonstration against every primitive basis is obtained here. Neither formal conceivability nor lack of a known prohibition automatically establishes relevant closeness, defective reliability, or a possessed defeater.

This local constraint improves the positive argument because it states what real order must do for the correction. The nontrivial research gain is the identification and assessment of K3–K6, including the missing field-closure claim in a powers-only reply. It cannot be credited as R5's defeat: a separated-track realization can preserve exactly this local target-sensitive interface without identifying all relations or bearers.

## 5. Three controls that expose the disputed inference

These controls are argument tests, not computer-generated worlds and not metaphysical possibility certificates. Their point is to force the two sides to identify which premise they reject.

### 5.1 The disappearance alternative

Suppose a primitive concrete basis exists actually, with a genuinely truth-sensitive cognitive process among its dependent effects. Suppose the basis could have been absent, and in that alternative neither the subject nor its evidence exists. The alternative is a challenge to necessary existence. It is not yet a case in which the subject believes falsely by the same method.

Thus a bare possibility of the basis's absence does not by itself defeat the method's safety or truth connection. The source advocate needs a bridge from contingent unreceived existence to an epistemically relevant false-belief alternative, or a separate existential-explanation principle. The rival has not established that its primitive contingent basis is metaphysically possible; the point is that epistemic loss and existential absence are different conclusions.

### 5.2 The caused counterfeit

Suppose all the relevant events do have causes, but an ordinary deceptive mechanism generates the same apparent evidence when the target is absent. The causal chain can be complete while the belief is unwarranted. Therefore causal explanation, even extended far upstream, is not sufficient for knowledge. The explanation must preserve the right target relation.

A personal-source theory also needs this discrimination. Merely adding an intelligent cause to the top of the chain does not prove that every downstream belief is well grounded. Any appeal to divine Wisdom, truthful revelation, or nondeception requires its own warranted scope. It cannot be used to establish the upstream source and then be derived solely from that same epistemic success.

### 5.3 Essential response without field closure

Suppose a receptor's genuine intrinsic power makes it respond appropriately to a particular input kind. That does not settle whether inputs of that kind can be supplied by a misleading route or whether unrelated intrusions can occur. Fixing the receptor's nature is weaker than fixing the complete field of possible origins.

This prevents the rival from concealing K3–K4 inside the words “essential powers.” The strengthened rival must explicitly defend modal scope and target linkage. Equally, it prevents the source advocate from saying that because ordinary powers do not furnish closure, only a personal source can do so. A necessary impersonal basis with essential origin constraints is precisely the alternative still requiring assessment.

## 6. Does the rival borrow the structure it is meant to explain?

“Borrowing” must identify an actual explanatory dependence, not merely detect a term in our description. A theory of intrinsic powers must be expressible and intelligible; that does not make an independently existing language the producer of those powers. A real attribute's inherence in a source is not an external efficient supplier. The same distinction that protects real divine attributes must be applied fairly when assessing rival intrinsic structure.

The relevant questions are these:

**Actuality.** Is the productive basis an actual existent, or only a rule saying what would happen if instantiated? Only the former can satisfy a productive explanation of this actual exercise. The strongest rival grants the former; its actuality is a substantive proposal.

**Power.** Is a determinate productive capacity grounded in what the basis is, or merely named after the observed outcome? A claim to a “power to produce exactly what happens” with no independent counterfactual constraints is explanatorily idle. A genuine nature can earn credit by constraining outcomes, error conditions, and interactions. Those constraints are not themselves proof of source-level understanding.

**Truth and norm.** Does the theory preserve a distinction between the target's being as represented and the system's producing its preferred output? Does it identify which relation fixes correctness, rather than let a score or intended task do so by stipulation? A source supplies productive actuality; it need not efficiently produce a logical implication or create the truth of a necessary relation. If independent norm-fixing reality is indispensable to a claimed *all-ground* account, it must be acknowledged. It does not thereby become another efficient creator.

**Warrant.** Does the account connect the subject's reason-sensitive exercise to the target nonaccidentally in the relevant circumstances? Coexistence of truths and true beliefs is insufficient. This is the specific pressure applied in §§2–5.

**Explanatory finality.** Is the proposed basis intrinsically sufficient, or does its power await an independent real contribution? A complete productive source cannot leave an indispensable productive contribution wholly outside its account. But a plural account may deny that any one member is individually exhaustive; defeating that reply requires a common-bearer argument, not an accusation that it changed its view by being plural.

There is a serious contemporary distinction relevant to the norm and cognitive cases. Dasgupta distinguishes substantive facts that call for grounding from autonomous facts that are not apt for that form of explanation; he considers essential mind–body connections as candidates for the latter. This is a developed way to resist the demand that every explanatory connection be a separately produced fact. It does not establish that the relevant connections really are autonomous, or that physical processes account for epistemic authority. Nor is his grounding relation identical to the retained productive-supply relation. [Dasgupta, “The Possibility of Physicalism,” §§VII–IX, printed pp. 575–586](https://mail.shamik.net/papers/dasgupta%20the%20possibility%20of%20physicalism.pdf).

The rival's strongest answer is therefore not “nothing needs explaining.” It is: the basis really exists and acts; some connections belong to its nature or to the essence of the dependent activity, rather than being imported from another efficient source; the epistemic link must be grounded through those connections. The unresolved burden is whether this really explains *genuine cognition and its authority*, or only restates them as unexplained features of an unspecified nature.

The positive source argument has a corresponding burden. Calling the basis “intellect” can locate actual cognition at the source. It does not by itself explain why a distinct created subject has this content or why its evidence is apt. The two proposals must face the same particular epistemic explanandum and the same demand for informative intrinsic adequacy.

## 7. The affirmative source argument, repaired rather than abandoned

The strongest affirmative route is not an inference from a mathematical necessity to an extra abstract object, followed by an unexplained conversion into a concrete person. It starts with something actual: the correcting subject and its exercise. It then asks how the actuality of that exercise is possessed.

The source-sensitive reasoning is as follows.

1. The admitted correction is an actual exercise with objective truth conditions and a real epistemic basis
2. Its efficacy is received in identified respects; the subject's existence and exercise are not made actual solely by its endorsing the correction
3. An actual received exercise requires actual productive provision in those respects; an uninstantiated possibility of provision will not do
4. A purported complete ultimate account must include all indispensable actual productive contributions within the stated target, and must preserve the target-sensitive epistemic relation rather than merely the final output
5. An account composed wholly of received efficacy faces a substantive completion demand: what makes the efficacy actual rather than only available on another condition of receipt? Original intrinsic actuality is a positive way to answer that question
6. If a complete original source is obtained, necessity still requires the existential claim that merely contingent actuality cannot be intrinsically sufficient without reception
7. A common bearer still requires a reason why the ultimate account is individually exhaustive rather than irreducibly plural
8. Knowledge at that bearer still requires a cognitive-adequacy or intentional-institution warrant, not merely the presence of objective structure and knowledge in its effects

Steps 2–3 have a plausible local warrant that need not wait upon a finished cosmology. Ibn Taymiyya expressly distinguishes a person's knowledge of an individual effect's dependence from prior possession of a universal causal proposition; the displayed examples are writing and building. Our application to the correction episode is a reconstruction. This supports a route from particular real dependence, without making every apparent causal judgment infallible. [Darʾ 3:119–121](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/413/501/فصل).

Step 5 is stronger than the principle that every dependent thing has another dependent cause. The original-possession rationale has explanatory force: passing a supply requirement along does not explain why anything has the relevant actuality originally. But an infinite realist may insist that every receiver really has its power, and a holistic realist may claim the entire concrete order has intrinsic efficacy. These are the proper opponents. Describing them all as empty relays would evade the issue. The accepted upstream review leaves precisely this ultimate-adequacy dispute open.

Step 6 also has a positive rationale. Intrinsic existential sufficiency differs from merely failing to observe a cause. If the thing's being itself is genuinely sufficient for its actuality, absence is excluded in the intended sense. The rival replies that conditional essence may fix what an existent is without explaining that it exists. The affirmative side must therefore defend the existential strength of its sufficiency principle. Merely changing “brute” to “necessary” is not an explanation on either side.

The Aṣfahāniyya passage gives a particularly useful source check. It first warns that a bare necessary-existent conclusion can be admitted by opponents who deny the intended distinct creator; contingent dependents and their originating source must be established. It then develops intrinsic sufficiency rather than mere lack of an incoming cause. The source itself thus blocks an indiscriminate necessity-to-creator promotion. [Aṣfahāniyya, hosted section 001, lines 61–75](https://www.islamicbook.ws/ageda/alaqidt-alasfhanit-001.html).

Step 7 is not an aggregate objection in disguise. One can ask about all actual dependent contributions without positing another concrete entity called the whole. But even a complete joint answer need not be one bearer. Hume's Cleanthes presses the member/whole issue and separately raises a necessary material-reality alternative. Those objections should be separated; neither is disposed of by pointing out that the observed world is integrated. [Dialogues IX.7–9](https://davidhume.org/texts/d/9).

For step 8, the strongest retained Taymiyyan route is the original-giver/perfection argument: the original source of an eligible cognitive perfection is not wholly without that perfection in its own appropriate manner. Its distributive adequacy principle must do real work. A rival that grants genuine cognition in dependent subjects but denies source-level cognition disputes that principle. It does not refute the source by relocating the recipient's thought, and the source advocate cannot refute it simply by conflating productive supply with cognitive ownership.

This repaired chain is a constructive result. It identifies an actual starting point, specifies what its explanation must retain, motivates original possession, and gives a source-faithful interpretation of intrinsic necessity. It remains an argued metaphysical case with unresolved premises, rather than a disguised deduction from the accepted same-token no-go.

## 8. Complete sourcehood and contingency: the same test for both sides

“Complete” can mean at least three things:

- Every actual productive contribution to a declared target is supplied by the source
- The source plus its actual exercise is sufficient for this outcome
- The source's invariant necessary nature, without any contingent exercise or condition, necessitates this very outcome

The third is stronger than the first two. If the complete determinant of an outcome is necessary and necessitates that outcome, the outcome is necessary. A rival cannot combine that claim with the genuine alternative possibility of the outcome's absence. This is an exclusion of an inconsistent package, not an independent demonstration that all impersonal explanations have that package.

An impersonal power may instead have nonnecessitating manifestations or a contingent intrinsic exercise. It then owes an account of that exercise and of any contrastive explanatory demand. A personal source can appeal to contingent willing; it also owes an account of why that exercise explains the selection and whether the relevant reasons are necessitating, favoring, or merely compatible. Absence of a necessitating contrastive reason is not automatically absence of every contrastive explanation. Nor is nondeterministic manifestation automatically intentional choice.

The Athari source boundary matters. Ṣafadiyya distinguishes standing activity-type from a particular act or effect, and insists that the complete determinate condition of acting differs from the condition of not acting. This supplies a real source-faithful response to invariant-state collapse without a blanket ban on renewed acts. It does not by itself settle the contrastive explanation of every determination. [Ṣafadiyya, hosted /002, lines 630–635 and 697–700](https://www.islamicbook.ws/amma/alsfdit-002.html).

Likewise, real attributes need not be external parts productively furnished to an otherwise bare source. Darʾ distinguishes efficient production from inherence and intrinsic concomitance, and warns against importing every meaning of “necessary in itself” after establishing only a narrower meaning. This inquiry uses no pure-act assumption and no indiscriminate composition principle. [Darʾ 3:110–112](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/413/496/تعليق-ابن-تيمية).

## 9. Independent overdetermination and the act-domain question

The strongest plural challenge must not be replaced by two complementary partial causes. Suppose A and B each have genuinely unborrowed productive efficacy; each is sufficient to produce the very same dependent endpoint x; both actually exercise that efficacy; and neither supplies the other's act. This is stronger than a necessary A supplying material while a necessary B supplies a missing form. It tests whether **sufficient production of x** entails **complete accounting for x's actual origination**.

Three scope claims differ:

- **Endpoint completeness:** A can fully produce x, without needing B's exercise for that endpoint
- **Dependent-field completeness:** A fully produces every member of the declared dependent field D
- **Actual-mode completeness:** A ultimately supplies all actual productive modes by which the relevant field receives its actuality

The retained uniqueness theorem applies to the third role together with unborrowed-act exclusion and a productive witness. If B has an actual unborrowed exercise among those modes, A's asserted mode completeness includes it, and the two independent-source claims cannot both stand. This is the strong intended claim; it should not be weakened to a dispute about labels of necessary existence.

The issue is whether the first or second role independently entails the third in the actual world. Counterfactual endpoint sufficiency alone does not. A redundant actual contribution may be unnecessary for x considered under a narrow description while still belonging to x's actual origination. Calling it redundant does not make it unreal. Calling A sufficient does not make A its supplier.

The recovered third-tranche `WHOLE_TOKEN_ORIGINATION_AND_ACTS.md` and `foundation/DEPENDENT_MODE_PREREQUISITE_BRIDGE.md` already supply the strongest positive reply. If the rival admits a real dependent effect-side reception mode m_B, then B's actual exercise is indispensable to **that mode**, even if dispensable to the endpoint x. Universal dependent-field coverage includes m_B. Complete productive independence then cannot leave m_B's indispensable actual efficacy externally furnished by an unborrowed B. This avoids equating the effect-side mode with an uncreated source-intrinsic act. It remains a substantive mode/prerequisite interpretation, not a result from the word “sufficient.”

The present stress test adds a distinction the reply must preserve: **unborrowed from another source is not the same as independent of one's own bearer, power, or will**. B's act may be nonautonomous relative to B while still unborrowed relative to A. Whether that places it directly in D depends on the ontology and the selected relation. There are three cases:

1. If an act is a separately produced event, it may lie in the efficiently received field. But a blanket adoption of this ontology for divine acts would need to be defended; it cannot classify every uncreated intrinsic exercise as a created event by fiat
2. If an act is an intrinsic exercise or mode, its dependence on B may be bearer-inherence, constitutive realization, or actualizing support. Merely requiring B's intrinsic power is not yet proof of efficient reception from another. An actual-support interpretation can be proposed, but its scope and transitivity must be justified rather than imported from the earlier productive-ancestry theorem
3. If producing is a primitive enacted relation, no extra action-object need be posited. B must still really act; a narrative cannot replace its efficacy. The complete target may legitimately include that enacted reality, but doing so is an explicit actual-origination scope claim, not a theorem that every true relational description is another created entity

The affirmative route is strongest in the effect-side mode case already defended in the third tranche. It includes a real dependent item on the rival's own ontology, identifies an indispensable **productive** prerequisite, and tests independent complete provision of that item. The source-intrinsic shortcut is weaker unless its actual-support interpretation is independently earned. See `ACT_ONTOLOGY_AND_COMPLETENESS.md` for the full positive reconstruction and its controls.

There is a serious historical comparison. Suárez distinguishes source-specific actions from their endpoints and distinguishes receiving being from a cause from that cause's counterfactual indispensability. He permits an absolute-possibility treatment of distinct total actions with one numerical endpoint, while denying that co-applied natural agents left to their natures work that way. His discussion invokes divine concurrence, not two ultimate independent creators. The Latin continuation must therefore accompany the famous permissive paragraphs. [DM XXVI.4.9, 13–20, digital edition pp. 17–20](https://homepage.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/Michael.Renemann/suarez/d26.pdf); [Adriaenssen, §§III–VII](https://academic.oup.com/pq/article/75/2/351/7606908).

This comparison does not refute the strong actual-mode-complete source thesis. It prevents a weaker endpoint thesis from being silently renamed as that thesis. Nor does ordinary warrant itself select maximal history-inclusive scope. Two truth-sensitive evidential routes can support a correction without either being the sole source of all its actual evidence. Likewise, a hypothetical pair of ultimate productive routes would not be epistemically defective merely because they were redundant. Their metaphysical adequacy must be judged by the source-completeness argument, not assumed to fail through an unrelated truth/error test.

## 10. What the strongest rival can and cannot presently claim

The strongest candidate can jointly *propose* the following without the demonstrated target shifts of the weaker controls:

- An actual, intrinsically determinate productive reality, rather than a free-standing sentence or empty law
- A principled field of dependent reality whose powers and origin constraints preserve the relevant epistemic connection
- Genuine local subjects who understand truths and reasons, including their own correction episodes
- Correctness fixed by the actual content and target, allowing error independently of endorsement
- Complete productive provision in the declared respect, while distinguishing it from exhaustive necessitating explanation of every possible contrast
- Intrinsic modal and constitutive connections, rather than an independent efficient supplier smuggled in as a law

No known contradiction has been derived here from that conjunction alone. But its philosophical debts are substantial and exact: establish its original actuality; vindicate the claimed modal field closure; explain genuine cognition without replacing it by behavioral reliability; fix epistemic normativity without arbitrary parameter choice; show that its intrinsic connections are informative and legitimate rather than outcome-named primitives; and, if it claims a single necessary source, independently warrant that necessity and bearer unity.

These are not all the same debt. An original source argument can earn a necessary concrete basis while leaving intellect open. A norm-fixing argument can defeat an arbitrary target convention while leaving source unity open. A successful local epistemic explanation can block a skeptical counterfeit without explaining why the whole productive field exists. A restoration account can recover warranted cognition without proving the metaphysics of its ultimate giver.

The comparative evidence therefore has to be matched at each joint. Kleinschmidt gives a serious rival to the claim that explanatory reasoning already commits us to an unrestricted PSR: explanatory power can be a weighty but defeasible theoretical virtue. Her argument is not a direct refutation of every later anti-skeptical argument, and the fact that explanation is valuable does not itself prove her alternative. It shows why an appeal to explanatory practice needs further work before becoming a universal existence principle. [Kleinschmidt, “Reasoning Without the Principle of Sufficient Reason,” §§1 and 3, printed pp. 65–67, 77–78](https://phil971-2022.colinmclear.net/materials/readings/kleinschmidt-psr-reasoning.pdf).

## 11. Exact disposition

The local adequacy condition is **epistemic-source inadequacy under a genuinely warrant-defeating counterfeit condition**. Its application is analytic once that condition is supplied; the missing world-directed work is to establish that a concrete rival really permits the appropriate damaging alternative. The substantive investigation isolates cross-kind generalization, closeness, certification scope, and collective explanation as separate burdens. It is not a universal theorem that every brute fact permits every other brute fact.

The new rival specification is an **intrinsic-power source with explicit epistemic field obligations**. It is stronger than an arbitrary response law and more candid than a modal table. It remains unestablished as actual or metaphysically possible. Its survival against this argument means that the argument has not excluded it, not that the rival is true or explanatorily equal in every respect.

The repaired positive route is **actual correction → real target-sensitive dependence → original intrinsic adequacy → separately warranted complete scope, necessity, unity, and cognitive perfection**. The anti-skeptical investigation supplies a reason to demand real epistemic order, and isolates the generalization needed to reach ultimate sourcehood. It does not turn the complete-coverage theorem into proof that coverage exists.

Accordingly, the frozen public statuses remain: same-token same-respect theory-adequacy no-go established; world-directed extension conditional on the reflection bridge; global underived modal order not established; Necessary Being not deductively established; R5 undefeated. The strongest next discriminant is not another finite role-coinstantiation test. It is an independently defended account of why the admitted target-sensitive epistemic order requires an original *cognitive* source, or why the strengthened intrinsic impersonal alternative cannot supply that order on its own stated terms.
