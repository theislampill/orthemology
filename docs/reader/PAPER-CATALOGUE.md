## How to use this catalogue

Choose a work, not a file extension. A work can have several reading forms; a reference volume can contain more than one source document; a checkpoint can contain many works and supporting records. A PDF, its generated TeX and its Markdown input are not three independently authored papers.

This preview maps the representative works and all governed publication-profile relationships. It is **not** a list of every substantive work in the repository. Entries based only on an introduction or edition record do not claim a complete substantive review. The [source-reading record][pilot] makes the actual examination scope explicit.

## Core exposition and formal reference

### Main exposition

**Orthemma–Ortheme Systems: An Analysis-Relative Architecture for Auditable Classification and Handling.** Read the [Markdown manuscript][main] or its [governed PDF][render-orthemma-ortheme-systems-draft].

This is the principal exposition of cases, state-types, bound standards and rule-governed handling. It separates correctness of a result from adequacy of its pathway. Its current-draft role does not imply that it incorporates every later tranche or mathematical development. The Markdown is the substantive editing source; the PDF is a reading output.

### Formal reference

**Orthemic core reference.** Read the [composite PDF][render-orthemic-core-reference-draft], the [formal core][core], or the [multi-actor conflict note][multi].

The [publication profile][profile] binds **both** source documents to this one rendering. They remain independently discoverable components: the core formalisation and a derived multi-actor extension. Edit the appropriate Markdown component, not generated LaTeX. The composite PDF is not a third independent manuscript, and a catalogue route that loses the multi-actor component is incomplete.

## Philosophical and theological manuscripts

### Ground of intelligibility

**Orthability and the Ground of Intelligibility: A Transcendental Argument from Rule-Governed Resolution.** [Source][ground] · [Governed PDF][render-orthability-ground-of-intelligibility-draft].

The source presents its argument and premise qualifications as a cross-framework dialectical analysis. Its philosophical claims are not validated by the engineering examples. This preview identifies the manuscript and edition relationship; the broader comparison with later foundational research remains to be completed.

### Atharī companion

**Orthability, the Divine Attributes, and the Uncreated Speech of Allah: An Atharī Creed-Internal Development.** [Source][athari] · [Governed PDF][render-orthability-divine-speech-athari-draft].

This is an explicitly Atharī, creed-internal development, not a conclusion attributed to every Islamic school. Preserve that stated route and the source's reasoning/revelation distinctions when reading it beside other foundational works. Do not treat a build receipt as support for its metaphysical premises.

### Dynamic orthability

**Dynamic orthing, noetic learning, and dynamic orthability.** [Source][dynamic] · [Governed PDF][render-dynamic-orthing-noetic-learning-orthability-draft].

The source relates represented standards, learning, cognition and orthability while distinguishing primary-text verification, secondary reconstruction, cross-source synthesis, Orthemological extension and creed-internal inference. It is a separate manuscript, not merely an appendix hidden behind the older companion README. Its source-labelled and empirical boundaries remain part of the reading.

## Mathematical manuscripts and preserved editions

### Information and composition

**Information, Composition, and Self-Revision: Local Descriptions and Operative Access.** [Preserved PDF][information-pdf] · [Tracked TeX][information] · [Edition relationship note][lineage].

The lineage note establishes matching title/abstract and the companion citation. It does **not** establish a byte-identical rebuild of the supplied PDF. The PDF is a preserved reading edition; the tracked TeX is not silently promoted to verified exact build input.

### Decisive and reflexive revision

**The Cost of a Decisive Revision: Evidence, Reuse, and Self-Application.** [Preserved PDF][decisive-pdf].

**Reflexive Revision under an Evidence Budget.** [Related TeX manuscript][reflexive].

Keep both titles visible. The [lineage note][lineage] identifies the relationship and shared abstract but explicitly leaves exact source-to-PDF provenance unverified. These are not known to be independent unrelated discoveries, nor established interchangeable editions. Retaining that unresolved relationship is more informative than either merging or separating them without qualification.

### Finite coordination information

**A bounded information consequence for coordination explanations.** [Ordinary mathematical text][coordination] · [Twelfth collection guide][twelfth].

The paper analyses the information needed by a complete coordination mediator. Its finite-alphabet capture result supplies a sharp fixed-horizon Gaussian bound and a matching construction. Its exact role and limitations appear in the [result entry](RESULT-INDEX.md#finite-mediator): a finite program description need not be a complete mediator, and single-horizon attainment does not establish one forward-causal history across all horizons.

This substantive work is readable in Markdown. Lack of a PDF is not a reason to omit it.

### Tensor moments and coercive dynamics

**Full tensor moment dynamics under polynomial damping.** [Written predecessor][tensor].

**Square positivity forces exact dynamics for every coercive polynomial.** [Written successor][coercive].

These are separate mathematical works. The successor explicitly strengthens the square-positive-to-representable bridge under its stated conditions, while relying on the earlier recovery/exhaustion result. Preserve the predecessor, including its historical frontier, and explain the stronger successor rather than rewriting either original.

The [tensor result entry](RESULT-INDEX.md#tensor-moments) distinguishes all-grade moment dynamics, complete-path identification and initial-record ambiguity. Neither paper is labelled a new kernel proof by this catalogue.

### Fisher geometry and retained information

**Finite-audit Fisher descent: exact criterion and sharp cardinality threshold.** [Source][fisher].

**When the finite sidecar theorem meets a continuous audit fibre.** [Corollary][sidecar] · [Existing ordinary review][sidecar-review].

The first work defines a full-source Fisher metric and gradient-descent problem on finite positive joint laws. The corollary states what preserving selected ambient norms requires on a fixed weight fibre. A finite-valued supplement cannot distinguish that entire fibre when it has positive dimension; the sharp continuous-coordinate count is fixed-fibre, not a general global chart theorem.

The [geometry result entry](RESULT-INDEX.md#fisher-geometry) specifies the stronger preservation contract. These are actual conditional constructions; identifying their variables with a real person's cognition requires additional modelling and evidence.

## Recognition and correction

**The Epistemological Architecture of Sound Reason.** [Preserved source substrate][sound].

**Concrete and Sound Reason: Bearers, Axes, and the Taymiyyan Comparison.** [Project distinction note][concrete].

**Forty retained primary passages: complete bounded qualification.** [Later qualification report][retained].

These are different kinds of source, not three editions of the same paper. The broader substrate argues for foundational recognition, cognitive vindication through tawātur and correction of acquired obstructions. The distinction note supplies claim-relative precision and its own source/warrant boundaries. The later report retains affirmative foundational appraisals with bounded source qualification.

Read the [atlas discussion](RESEARCH-ATLAS.md#recognition-sound-cognition-and-correction) for their intellectual connection. The report's title is the source's title; this refactor does not claim to have reread or authenticated all forty primary passages.
