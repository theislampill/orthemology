## Prospective review tests

### Positive warrant

- Identify the exact explanandum and the offered positive explanatory relation
- Credit reasons that make a bridge more compelling without demanding that all philosophy be formal entailment
- Separate deductive validity, source-specific warrant, abductive ranking, explanation, and empirical/world-directed truth
- Compare specified intrinsic natures on both sides; neither an impersonal Q nor a mental conjunction earns explanation merely by naming one bearer
- Charge source cognition, purpose, efficacy, coverage, goodness, and nondeception separately unless an argued relation connects them

### Strong rivals

- Intrinsic concrete powers: real productive actuality, genuine derivative cognition, objective correctness, lawfulness expressive of nature, and no external lawgiver
- Plural original contributions: individually unreceived resources jointly sufficient for the actual history, without each being individually source-complete
- Intrinsic teleology/normativity: real success and failure conditions not equated with popular acceptance or bare regularity
- Grounding pluralism/non-well-founded reception: actual powers throughout, not an infinite series of merely hypothetical transfers

These are objection specifications. Their logical descriptions do not certify metaphysical realizability. Equally, failure to provide such a certificate is not itself a proof of the positive argument.
