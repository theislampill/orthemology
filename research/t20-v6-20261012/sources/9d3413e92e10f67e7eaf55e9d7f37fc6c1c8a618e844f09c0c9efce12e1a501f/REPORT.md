## 3. Direct primary-language check

A bounded electronic recheck confirms the inherited reading. No new print-edition or manuscript collation is claimed.

### Darʾ1:293–294

The continuous passage first says that a particular existent's external existence is its concrete essence/reality, rather than a second external item. It then rejects identifying differentiated attributes or an attribute with its bearer:

“فيجعلون هذه الصفة هي هذه الصفة، ويجعلون الصفة هي الموصوف”

It gives the knowledge/power/will identity claim as the rejected example, then affirms:

“ومعلوم أنه جوهر واحد له صفات متعددة”

Close translation: it is known to be one substance possessing multiple attributes.

The paragraph criticizes both collapsing distinct attributes into one and multiplying one qualified individual into independent substances. Its lesson is therefore neither bare identity nor separable-component ontology. [Primary electronic witness](https://www.islamicbook.ws/amma/dr-taardh-alaql-walnql-002.html), section “وجود الله هل هو ماهيته أو زائد على ماهيته؟,” authorial reply, corresponding to browser lines248–255. The mapping to printed1:293–294 is inherited from CP16's edition-linked electronic inspection, not freshly authenticated from this unpaginated mirror.

### Majmūʿ6:98–101, with the explicit differentiation control at6:109

At6:100, the author distinguishes productive dependence from necessary concomitance. A necessary divine attribute is not an effect manufactured by its bearer; co-entailment is accepted. At6:101 he positively identifies the inseparability of essence and perfections as perfection. This is the same positive move as daee §6.3, and remains intact.

The same epistle later makes the nonidentity qualification explicit at6:109:

“فإن الواحدة من هذه الصفات ليست هي الأخرى، بل كل صفة ممتازة بنفسها عن الأخرى، وإن كانتا متلازمتين يوصف بهما موصوف واحد”

Close translation: one of these attributes is not the other; each is distinguished in itself from the other, although they are concomitant and qualify one subject.

This sentence directly rules out deriving identity from nonseparability. It also expressly locates differentiated attributes in one bearer, so it cannot be used to invent independently subsisting attribute-agents. [Primary electronic witness](https://quranislam.github.io/fatwa/ibn-taymiyyah/fatwa81.html), printed markers6:100–101 and6:109. The6:109 control was already used in the CP16 semantic audit; its present electronic recheck is not a new discovery.

## 4. Exact proposition under test

Retain:

- The intellect can isolate a real attribute or consider a bearer without attending to every qualification.
- This conceptual operation does not establish that the attribute or an attribute-free bearer can exist separately.
- Necessary intrinsic attributes do not thereby cause their bearer, get produced by it, or require foreign completion.
- Real knowledge and real power are differentiated qualifications of the same concrete bearer.

Reject only the additional, unsupported inference:

**Because essence and attributes cannot exist separately, knowledge and power have no real differentiation outside thought, or are numerically identical with each other or with the bearer.**

That rejected proposition is not shown to be an actual premise of §6.3 on its strongest contextual reading. No inspected downstream code or argument was found and tested as committing that promotion. The present result therefore warrants clarification at most, not a defect label for the source argument.

## 5. Optional narrow clarification, not an applied edit

If the wording is later edited under separate authorization, the two potentially overbroad formulations can be clarified without changing the argument:

For Step2:

“The mind can consider the essence and its attributes separately for analysis; this does not make them separable in external reality. What exists is the one concrete essence, genuinely qualified by its real, necessary attributes.”

For Step3:

“These attributes are genuinely differentiated from one another and subsist inseparably in the one divine essence. Their conceptual isolation does not make them independently subsisting things: knowledge is not power, while neither is a detachable component or an external supplier of divine perfection.”

This makes explicit the inherited reading. It should not be presented as newly repairing a demonstrated identity doctrine.
