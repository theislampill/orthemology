# PMR-007 Deep V V1 cold audit

Disposition: `REPAIR_REQUIRED`.

The primary finite checker is internally consistent at its declared Boolean
scope, but the V1 prose overstates what that finite product establishes.

## Blocking findings

### V-F01 — class-level and item-level bearers are not yet coupled

Core A's fibre condition is a property of a declared classification system.
Core B's warrant condition is a property of a particular judgment/derivation
and its transport. V1 writes both as predicates on an unexplained `e`. Without
a typed paired bearer, the product can combine unrelated verdicts.

**Required repair:** define an evaluation episode containing one declared target,
one comparison class/profile system, and one frozen warrant object that purports
to warrant that same target in the same recipient scope.

### V-F02 — V1 overreads Core B's authority

The merged Fable packet marks the Core B calculus and all of its derived
properties `DERIVED_BUT_UNVERIFIED`. V1's phrase “the declared
certificate/warrant chain is valid” sounds like the full calculus has been
verified.

**Required repair:** use only a minimal finite root-completeness predicate:
all roots required by one frozen warrant object are present in the declared
transport domain and the explicitly listed version/authority/invalidator guards
pass. Do not claim the full calculus, cut rule, radius, or composition theory.

### V-F03 — product wrapper is not reductive or causal unification

`J=(A,B)` trivially answers two predicates jointly. It does not derive either
predicate from the other, explain why they coordinate, or furnish one calculus
whose rules generate both. Calling it “the common core” is too strong.

**Required repair:** call it the coarsest exact **joint diagnostic product**.
Classify `FABLE-R1-B06` as narrowed at the wrapper/diagnostic level while the
common integrated calculus remains open.

### V-F04 — Core A's false-closure vulnerability is inherited

The product does not prevent a declared profile from being coarsened or a label
from being packed into the profile. A constant target on a constant profile can
make the profile-certification coordinate pass without an independently sound
target or admissible profile.

**Required repair:** state that profile integrity, target independence, and
anti-label-leakage remain separate guards. The product does not solve them.

### V-F05 — “semantic” is too strong for the first coordinate

Fibre constancy establishes certifiability of a declared target from a declared
profile. It does not establish semantic truth, reference, world adequacy, or
source truth.

**Required repair:** rename the coordinate `ProfileCert`, keep `WorldAdeq`
separate, and preserve source-formal-world nontransfer.

### V-F06 — the world axis needs a noncircular definition

V1 says `declared target = independently fixed world target` but does not type
how the world target is fixed. The finite witness can test equality, not justify
that target's authority.

**Required repair:** treat the world target as an independently supplied
parameter of the finite model. The theorem establishes coordinate independence,
not world truth or target selection.

### V-F07 — scalarization claim needs narrowing

Monotone scalar functions do exist. Equal positive weights tie the mixed states;
unequal weights rank them. What fails is a canonical strict ranking that both
extends the product order and remains invariant under swapping the two axes.

**Required repair:** state noncanonicity and policy dependence, not scalar
impossibility.

### V-F08 — coding claim is fixed-length/worst-case only

Four attained diagnostic states require four codewords and therefore at least
two fixed-length bits. This does not establish an expected-length lower bound
under an arbitrary nonuniform distribution, an interactive lower bound, or a
channel-robust code bound.

**Required repair:** preserve the fixed-length/worst-case scope.

### V-F09 — theorem-family and prior-art ceiling

The joint map is the universal product of two predicates; its coarsest-quotient
property is a standard factorization fact. The four-state structure has close
formal relatives in Belnap/Ginsberg bilattice work, although their axes and
orders are not the same as profile certifiability and warrant completeness.

**Required repair:** classify the relation as `SHARED_PRODUCT_MECHANISM_ONLY`
and assign zero general novelty.

### V-F10 — no adoption or meniscus transition follows

A finite product diagnostic neither adopts either Fable core nor resolves the
program's target, source, implementation, world, or external-review burdens.

**Required repair:** keep repository status proposal-only, owner adoption
pending, integrated champion `NONE`, and meniscus `MENISCUS_NOT_REACHED`.
