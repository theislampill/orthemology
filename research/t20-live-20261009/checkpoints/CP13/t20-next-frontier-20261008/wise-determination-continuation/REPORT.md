## Result

The strongest retained rival still has its earned conditional explanation of the means `(q,q)`. The surviving disagreement is not whether a chosen plan has enough coordinates, nor whether only one outcome maximizes a score. It concerns whether a knowingly chosen original-peer undertaking can be a wise and fully appropriate exercise when its actual productive adequacy is irreducibly joint.

A source-critical comparison now makes one boundary unusually explicit. **Ibn Taymiyya distinguishes an external object being needed for knowledge *of it* from that object giving the knower a perfection. He also counts actual knowing, not merely standing capacity, as perfection.** His conditional comparison even says that, were the object existent through another, knowing it would be better than not knowing it. This blocks attributing to him an indiscriminate inference from object-conditioned actual perfection to foreign donation. It is a conditional source-appraisal, not an independent metaphysical counterexample. It does **not** authenticate an independently original peer, establish the metaphysical possibility of that supposition, or justify transporting the epistemic relation to joint original production.

The attempted transport breaks at an exact place: an object that determines what knowledge is about has not thereby supplied original productive resources for the knowing act; the retained joint-production rival expressly assigns the peer such a role in producing the intended effect. Whether that latter role is a foreign completion of the subject's appropriate actual efficacy remains the narrow dispute. The source has a serious affirmative argument for its classification; the epistemic distinction does not refute it.

This is a new bounded **comparison of retained evidence**, not a newly discovered passage. The earlier complete-volume ledger already reads and summarizes it. The current operation recovers the continuous Arabic, exact voice boundary, and limited hypothetical force, then tests the proposed application. No new finite model or selection table was warranted.

## 1. Strongest positions preserved

### Affirmative

Under the adopted original-explanation and pure-perfection account, an original's actually completed undertaking must obtain its appropriate productive adequacy through its own original resources. This is not the claim that every possible effect must always exist or that every act-token is eternally necessary. It is a claim about the original adequacy of an undertaking once actually complete. Created means can be genuine causes while their relevant actuality, capacity and adequacy come from that same original source.

Majmu20 item2032 L003–008 treats complete power and decisive willing as entailing the product through the bearer alone; L013 transfers relevant dependence in the product to the bearer’s act and attribute. Minhaj2 item156 L040–041 identifies conditioned actual efficacy as a perfection needing another. These arguments are stronger than a ban on newly implanted intrinsic objects. They address a relationally specified perfection of actual productive agency.

The source's appraisal is substantively motivated: complete original explanation should reach accomplished agency rather than stop at internal readiness while independent resources supply its achievement. The present test does not demand that this basic comparison first be deduced from source-neutral thin premises before it may have defeasible warrant.

### Actual surviving rival

The rival retains two intrinsically unreceived capable/willing bearers, genuinely exercised acts, truthful cognition, a known primitive co-manifestation law, and conditional target-directed policies. It denies that the peer gives either actor its standing power or intrinsic willing. It accepts irreducibly joint original production and denies that this makes either actor’s fulfilled appropriate activity intrinsically lesser or externally completed in the relevant perfection sense.

The richer variant values actual goal-directed participation by a designated peer. The inherited symmetry rationale can intelligibly select the diagonal means given the target, equivalent practical roles and the exchange-invariant planning norm. It requires neither a unique numerical optimum nor efficient receipt of the peer’s chosen setting. These are real conditional credits.

They do not establish a fully wise original-peer end, full original sourcehood, or actual sole provision by either actor. Conversely, neither side has supplied every detailed wise reason for actual creation. Requiring an exhaustive hidden-reason inventory only from the rival would change the common evidential standard.

## 3. Executed operation: test the epistemic-to-productive analogy

### Continuous source and voice

Primary: [Darʾ taʿāruḍ, electronic subsection016](https://www.islamicbook.ws/amma/dr-taardh-alaql-walnql-016.html). Local current extraction: `sources/dar016-extracted.txt`, L1012–1054 continuously, supplemented by L0465–0545 and L0705–0758. The acquired HTML is retained. The relevant authorial response is identifiable from the text, not just an editorial heading:

1. **L1013–1017:** “قال أبو البركات” introduces Abū al-Barakāt's account. It places intrinsic perfection in the capacity to know rather than every achieved cognition, and says action expresses perfection. This is quoted, not adopted wholesale.
2. **L1018–1026:** “قلت” introduces Ibn Taymiyya's assessment and criticism. The bare-capacity reading is not simply his conclusion.
3. **L1028–1036:** he proposes additional responses. Actual knowledge is an intrinsic excellence; relation to an object is not itself discrediting. A separate response says all actual external objects are his created dependents.
4. **L1038 and L1040:** alternative accounts of knowing are expressly introduced as the replies of those holding them: timeless knowledge with new relations, or knowing a thing as existent after knowing it as nonexistent. These alternatives must not all be flattened into a single asserted account of token identity.
5. **L1041–1047:** the author criticizes Abū al-Barakāt's exclusion of actual cognition from perfection. An excellence whose individual realization cannot be eternal can be perfect at its fitting time. Intrinsic acts and attributes belong to the agent's own reality; their novelty is not external donation.
6. **L1049:** he distinguishes “another gave the perfection” from “without that other there would not be knowledge of it,” rejecting the former reading and granting the latter. This is an authorial explanatory distinction.
7. **L1051:** the first answer invokes his origination of the object and its concomitants. This is an actual theological ground, not a second independent original.
8. **L1052:** “لو قدر موجودا بغيره” is a supposition: if the object were existent through another, knowing it would be more perfect than not knowing it. The conditional comparison does not assert that such an object actually exists, establish its modal possibility, or identify its other source as an independently original peer.
9. **L1053:** the third answer explicitly holds the object’s existence fixed across “knows / does not know,” and argues that losing possible knowledge in those circumstances is the deficiency. It does not compare the world with the object to a world without it. Nor does it prove that a knower can originate that object while preserving the object's alleged independent origin.
10. **L1054–1055:** the text returns to “قال أبو البركات.” The grant at L1049–1053 therefore belongs to Ibn Taymiyya's response, not to the ensuing quotation.

The electronic text contains awkward strings, including L1031 and the central L1049 wording ‘إن ذلك بغير أعطاه الكمال’. No repair is silently made or certified. The giver/object distinction is read from the two complete contrasted clauses and the continuing three answers at L1051–1053, rather than resting on an emended token. The explicit conditional at L1052 is retained as conditional even though its surrounding argument is authorial.

### Strongest attempted shared principle

A serious pro-rival analogy is:

- Actual knowing and actual wise productive agency are both excellences, not mere latent capacities.
- Each can be described relationally toward a distinct actual object.
- A distinction in what must obtain for that relational excellence to be realized does not by itself show that another efficiently gives the subject a new intrinsic perfection.
- Therefore the fact that a peer participates in a wisely intended relation need not *merely by its relational form* disqualify the subject.

The first three premises receive real support from the source and prior type controls. The limited conclusion is fair. It prevents a reply that dismisses the cognition comparison merely because cognition is standing capacity while productive efficacy is actual achievement. The inspected response explicitly counts actual cognition as perfection.

The stronger conclusion would be: **therefore irreducibly foreign original resources may partly supply the actual productive adequacy of an original’s undertaking without any perfection-relevant foreign completion.** That does not follow from the shared principle.

### Exact failed map

In the epistemic case, the external object is what an act/state is about and what makes the corresponding object-relative description applicable. The author does not infer that the object supplies the knowing power or produces the knowing act. Indeed his distinction is directed against treating those relations as interchangeable. The conditional alternative at L1052 likewise compares better and worse knowing given the object; it does not positively furnish the causal origins of that knowledge.

In the retained production case, the peer's power and actual exercise are stipulated to belong to the original productive basis of Q. Calling that peer merely the intentional object of A's wisdom would drop precisely the productive role under examination. Conversely, calling the epistemic object an original co-producer of the subject's cognition would add a role absent from the source.

A content-preserving transport consequently needs a further premise: the peer's contribution to the undertaking's actual original adequacy is perfection-relevant in exactly the same way as the object's specifying knowledge-of-it. Neither the source passage nor the retained model supplies that equivalence. The affirmative denies it because the effect’s productive basis is part of the undertaking’s achieved efficacy. The rival can challenge that classification, but cannot establish the challenge by replacing productive contribution with intentional aboutness.

This is not proof that the narrow efficacy norm is universally true. A relationally specified property can fail to be constitutive foreign receipt; an actual joint productive basis can fail to donate a new intrinsic object. Those observations remain available to the rival. The missing point is a warranted account of *whose original resources constitute the appropriate realized agency*, not a graph edge named donation.
