## Positive foundations: what can be used now

### Intrinsic sufficiency to necessity

S2 report 01 §§1.1–1.4 supplies a useful concise arrangement of the already-established source case:

- The actuality to be explained is concrete; no externally suspended possible essence is needed.
- An entirely received productive order cannot acquire original efficacy merely by collecting or indefinitely extending received members.
- The same actual original witness has no external existential supplier.
- Positive intrinsic existential sufficiency, rather than a vacant predecessor list, is the basis for necessity through itself.
- Darʾ directly considers the neither-through-self-nor-another proposal and gives a foundational rational response. Absence of a proof from weaker conceded premises does not by itself undercut its warrant.

The retained direct return is stronger evidence than the companion for these claims. Its two formulations locate the disagreement honestly: thin nonreceipt needs a substantive sufficiency/necessity bridge; thick through-self already includes positive sufficiency, so excluding the third combination is a substantive principle. Neither makes the argument mere wordplay. The next principal work should develop and weigh that positive premise rather than generate another weaker modal description.

### Complete original adequacy to exclusivity

Report 02 §§2.1–2.3 and §2.7 preserve the strongest positive argument: original resources must supply accomplished production, not merely an available route; needed assistance or forbearance can reveal foreign completion; a necessary pure perfection cannot be lost just through coexistence. The current source record adds nonabsorption, proper-part, augmentation and unrestricted-supremacy arguments. These are substantive comparative reasons, not a demand for vanity or sole credit.

The exact bridge is already recorded in `reassessment-semantic-warrant/ROOT_ADJUDICATION.md`: why must complete unreceived actual adequacy entail exclusive actual attribution, rather than freedom from indispensable foreign supplementation? The source's reasons count at a defeasible philosophical standard. The strongest nonexclusive-original proposal has not independently supplied the full original resources needed to become a meaning-preserving counterinstance. Ordinary redundant processes may bear indirectly on a genuinely universal intermediate premise, but they neither certify the stronger proposal nor force a further downgrade.

S4 helps keep the positive bearer intrinsically rich without importing outside production. S5 helps defend informed purposeful acts against a static inventory of perfection. Neither adds exclusivity by definition. Self-knowledge can make an independently established defect transparent; it cannot supply the missing defect classification through factivity.
