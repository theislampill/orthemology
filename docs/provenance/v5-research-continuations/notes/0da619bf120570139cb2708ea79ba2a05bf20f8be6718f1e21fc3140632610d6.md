## The composition is substantive

1. For fixed epsilon in (0,1/4], the two hidden norms are alpha=false = 1/2-epsilon and alpha=true = 1/2+epsilon. The two repair outputs are the corresponding exact centers 1/2+alpha(a)epsilon
2. `good_iff_same` proves from the literal two-state `BinaryGood` inequalities that action a is target-good exactly when a equals the hidden Boolean. The diagonal target is derived, not supplied as an unrelated label table. Both output forecasts are coherent
3. The declared sensor law is Bernoulli(alpha(theta)), independent of the chosen action. Each matching singleton support is self-verifying because even that target-preserving action distinguishes the two norm models
4. The final experiment is the explicitly normalized independent product over action/sample-index pairs. Coordinate marginals and joint independence are proved from finite cylinders. The final theorem does not accept them as external premises
5. The shared selector maximizes the literal finite product likelihood through finite-list greedy selection. Its tie rule and initial choice are fixed independently of the true theta. `selectedRepair` has no hidden true-theta argument
6. The recursive history uses exactly the already acquired prefix of each selected support's stack. The independent review additionally proves full-history prefix locality and next-choice prefix locality, deriving the history length and count bounds. Changing unacquired coordinates cannot change the decision
7. Positive finite laws give KL drift and the strong law. On one probability-one event, all prefix clocks are handled pathwise. If a bad candidate were selected infinitely often, an informative action in its singleton support would be sampled infinitely often. The false/true likelihood ratio would tend to zero, contradicting its maximizing selection
8. Singleton supports mean one block is exactly one physical repair action. Thus the conclusion is almost-sure eventual **literal** score repair, rather than merely eventual validity of an abstract support label

The final event is measurable. This was independently proved from measurability of `selectedRepair`, the actual score predicate and its countable eventual-event union/intersection structure.

## What the theorem does and does not say

The displayed endpoint `generated_experiment_eventually_repairs` takes epsilon with its interval hypotheses, true theta and a fixed initial choice. It supplies no maximizer oracle, assumed sensor independence, exact-readout endpoint or hand-labelled target premise. The model still **stipulates** that the observation distribution is coupled to the norm parameter. It does not establish that empirical frequencies generally determine normative truth.

This is a mathematical measurable policy over exact real likelihoods. The Lean definitions are noncomputable, including finite-set list extraction and arbitrary real comparison. No runnable controller, effective comparison routine, uniform convergence rate, stopping-time bound or numerical robustness implementation is certified. The rational finite tests are a separate specialization, tested for both fixed Boolean list orders, not code extraction from the Lean selector.

The disturbance is fixed, and there are two known candidate norms. Informative feedback continues on target-good actions. This is therefore a positive contrast to the earlier continuum/no-more-probes theorem and does not contradict it. The proof gives almost-sure eventual success, not correctness at every finite time or on every infinite tape. The fresh control closure proves this sufficient full-support controller theorem; a complete general necessity/zero-support classification is a separate research lane.

## Sharp controls and negative tests

- At epsilon=0 both action labels give the same accepted repair, so the exact singleton-target claim would fail. A compiled control records that degeneracy
- At admitted epsilon, the wrong action fails `BinaryGood` and there is no action good for both norms. Separate controls prove this from the literal predicate
- Target-preserving actions remain informative, which is the exact information-access premise separating this result from the finite-query obstruction
- Under the **flipped** sensor-to-norm coupling, the same selector eventually fails the intended norm almost surely. This is a compiled theorem, not a simulation, obtained by composing the already proved result for the opposite model with the wrong-label exclusion
- Replacing the sensor relation by total indistinguishability makes the matching singleton support fail self-verification; full support alone is insufficient
- Equal one-coordinate marginals do not imply the required independence. A persistent random bit shared by every coordinate has the correct Bernoulli marginals but locks the likelihood rule onto that bit. At epsilon=1/4 it has eventual error probability 1/4. The finite control file verifies the rational masses and repeated choices; the infinite constant-tape calculation is ordinary mathematics, not a new Lean probability theorem
- Exact finite-time error probabilities remain strictly between zero and one in the tested full-support two-model cases. They are not substituted for the strong-law argument
- Source mutations that peek at the first unused stack entry, flip the generated sensor's hidden-model index, or collapse both repair labels to one fixed output are rejected
