# From actual evaluable activity to original existential actuality

## The positive result and its exact reach

The best next step is to begin with an actual exercise, rather than try to turn a necessary condition of evaluation into an independently existing object. A genuine correction gives us something real whose occurrence and productive conditions require explanation. Its truth also supplies the objective distinctions needed for that particular truth. These are related starting points, but they are not the same explanandum.

An affirmative source argument can then proceed through the nature of productive reception. A recipient cannot supply the actuality on which its receiving and acting depend; adding further recipients does not by itself change the kind of explanatory contribution being supplied. A complete productive account must eventually identify actuality possessed originally, or explain why an irreducibly non-well-founded structure itself discharges that role. The strongest Taymiyyan argument favors original possession because it completes the productive explanation rather than indefinitely transferring the condition under which another receiver acts.

This extension strengthens that argument in four concrete ways. It identifies a primary-text-supported local route to real actuality and an actual producer, avoiding an abstract-to-concrete inference. It derives independence from a precise complete-coverage condition without requiring the whole ancestry relation to be well-founded. It separates a plurality of actual dependent things from a reified aggregate when assessing totality arguments. Finally, it identifies the positive intrinsic-sufficiency reading needed for the move from absence of a producer to necessity in itself.

The argument remains conditional at its most contested points: whether every relevant actual dependence has a complete original account; whether that account is borne by one source rather than irreducibly plural reality; and whether actual sourcehood has the required essential modal status. The countably infinite controls below make those disputes exact. The extension does not repeat or alter the sealed Knowledge argument, prove that every formal control is metaphysically possible, or change Track T's accepted status.

## 1  Start from a real exercise with a specified truth condition

Choose a genuinely occurring correction, e: someone recognizes that an earlier answer is false and replaces it for a stated reason. Three claims have different warrants.

First, e really occurs. This is an actual-world premise about an exercise, not a claim that every possible world contains a thinker. It gives actual reality at the outset. One need not infer a concrete existent from the necessity of a logical proposition. The particular bearer can initially be characterized modestly as the real subject or concrete system whose activity occurs; its substantial ontology, persistence and ultimate source have not thereby been settled.

Second, the corrected evaluation is true. That supports the distinctions and conditions required for this truth. It does not follow merely because a practice produces the label “correct.” A mistaken judgment is still an actual exercise, so the two claims must remain distinct. The reality of an error itself supports a distinction between endorsement and correctness; it does not establish that the entire practice's preferred standards are objectively authoritative.

Third, if e is a newly occurring exercise whose efficacy is received, e has a real productive explanation. This is an additional causal judgment. Its warrant is the actuality and received character of the exercise, not the fact that a written proof contains a dependency arrow. In particular, owning a correction does not establish that the corrector's efficacy is independently unborrowed.

Ibn Taymiyya explicitly argues that knowledge of a particular effect's dependence need not await possession of a universal causal proposition. His examples include a particular writing and building; he distinguishes knowing the individual dependence from first deriving it through a universal syllogism. See Darʾ, displayed 3:119–121, [the paragraph beginning “واعلم أن علم الإنسان”](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/413/501/فصل), with the wider continuation in [hosted /005, lines 75–89](https://www.islamicbook.ws/amma/dr-taardh-alaql-walnql-005.html). The application to an actual correction is this extension's reconstruction, not a quotation of his example.

The methodological benefit is substantial. An objector cannot require that every justified particular causal judgment first be proved from a universally accepted theory of every possible cause. Conversely, the passage does not make the causal judgment infallible simply because it seems immediate. The positive case must identify the relevant dependence in this episode. The familiar fact that a reasoner's changing state requires actual powers is stronger evidence than treating every logical presupposition as a separate efficient cause.

### A more precise reflection requirement

The accepted formal no-go becomes world-directed only with its reflection package. We preserve that requirement. The present route does something narrower: it starts from a real case and analyzes the meanings of its admitted claims. If a rival says that this very exercise completely generates every condition of its own determinacy, the rival must preserve the same exercise, the same respect, and genuine productive dependence. That local target matching is more defensible than assuming that every edge in an arbitrary formal system corresponds to ontological generation.

Where the rival instead says that concrete reality is intrinsically determinate and never generated all determinacy, the same-token contradiction does not apply. We can still ask for the productive explanation of e, but we have changed from the transcendental no-go to a cosmological source inquiry. Stating this transition is progress: it prevents a defective total-generation story from becoming the only alternative to a necessary creator.

## 2  An actual producer is real without being inferred from an abstract norm

A real productive act cannot be performed by a merely absent producer. This gives a straightforward implication once production is established: the producer is actual. It is not yet an argument that there must be a producer for every fact, or that the producer is the final source.

Darʾ 3:110–112 makes a particularly useful distinction. Ibn Taymiyya separates the dependence of an effect on a producer from an attribute's dependence on its bearer and the reciprocal concomitance of inseparable features. He also warns against importing all possible meanings of “necessary in itself” into the argument after only a particular meaning has been established. [Primary passage](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/413/496/تعليق-ابن-تيمية).

This supports an Athari reconstruction with real intrinsic attributes. A real attribute and its bearer can be inseparable without either being an external efficient maker of the other. Consequently, a source argument need not assume that every distinguishable feature is a separately supplied component or that a necessary bearer must lack real positive attributes. The producer's priority in the present argument is productive priority in one respect; we do not generalize the passage into a new temporal theory of every divine act.

Now consider an abstract correctness condition. Its obtaining can be indispensable to the truth of an evaluation without supplying the energy, existence or agency of the evaluator. The fact that a result follows from an equation is not the act of producing its concrete instance. This excludes a specific inadequate answer: offering only an uninstantiated rule while claiming to have supplied the actual productive account.

A stronger impersonal rival can posit an actual intrinsically structured reality with genuine powers. That has supplied more than an abstract rule, and the source argument should recognize it. If this reality exists unreceived, it is already a candidate for original actuality. If it exists necessarily and supplies the entire dependent field, it occupies much of the upstream role being sought. Its impersonal character is a later dispute, not a failure to posit anything real.

A Platonist might instead posit a noncausal essential ground of concrete realization. That is a different explanatory relation. It cannot be dismissed just by repeating that abstract objects are not efficient agents. The case against it must show why the concrete productive target requires an actual exercising power rather than only an existential entailment. This extension defends that demand for actual exercises; it does not announce a general refutation of every noncausal account of existence.

## 3  The original-possession argument against a wholly derivative account

The affirmative argument is strongest when “received” describes the source of actuality, rather than merely a correlation between existing things.

1. The exercise e and the powers exercised in it are actual.
2. An explanation that attributes all the relevant efficacy to reception must identify what actually supplies that efficacy. A merely possible supplier or an uninstantiated response law cannot perform that work.
3. If a proposed supplier receives the efficacy required for this very supply, its contribution does not eliminate that requirement. The requirement is carried into the account of e.
4. An account containing nothing but such received contributions has not identified any originally possessed efficacy. Adding a new intrinsic or holistic power can answer the challenge, but that power must be counted among the positive resources of the account.
5. A complete ultimate productive explanation must settle the source of the actual efficacy, rather than leave all of it conditional upon its further reception in the same respect.
6. Therefore a complete ultimate productive explanation requires original actuality, unless a non-well-founded theory can justify the claim that the whole pattern of real receptions itself supplies complete productive sufficiency without original possession.

Steps 3 and 4 are not an argument that an infinite quantity is too large to traverse. They track what each proposed contributor contributes. They also do not say that a dependent agent is causally idle. A received power may really produce an effect. Its real efficacy and its inability to supply its own complete existential basis can both be true.

The important warrant for step 5 is the difference between specifying a received exercise and supplying the reality exercised. If the explanandum is the actual efficacy of the whole relevant productive account, indefinitely identifying another receiver never changes the mode in which that efficacy is possessed. The source advocate has a positive explanation of why a terminus is appropriate: an intrinsically actual source can exercise what it has without requiring another source to supply that very possession. This is a substantive account of productive completion, not a preference for short proofs or temporally first events.

The source text treats complete agency and freedom from further productive need as central. Darʾ, hosted /005, lines 59–64, reasons from the need of an originated effect to an agent complete in the relevant action; an intermediate that still needs another is not by itself that complete agent. This is an authorial argument, not the al-Rāzī quotation in the preceding section. Its appeal to rational immediacy is evidence of how the author warrants the premise, not a modern consensus certificate. [Primary text](https://www.islamicbook.ws/amma/dr-taardh-alaql-walnql-005.html).

### Why the infinite rival must be granted its strongest starting point

The serious rival says that every member of an infinite chain is actual and receives from another actually efficacious member. There is no powerless first member. It proposes genuine production at each link, not an empty chain waiting to begin. The familiar demonstration that removing a hand leaves a stick unable to move a stone does not by itself address that proposal. Oberle's critique makes this structural objection explicit in his examination of regress defenses. [Oberle, sections 2–3](https://thomasoberle.weebly.com/uploads/3/8/1/8/38187281/thomist_paper.pdf).

This rival can satisfy local causality, producer actuality, irreflexivity, transitivity and the principle that every contingent actual member receives. It can also preserve objective correctness in a real episode. Thus those properties alone cannot establish source foundation.

The positive reply is not to deny the assigned actuality. It is to ask what the theory means by complete productive reception. If the relations are only conditional routing rules, the actual active configuration is additional. If a real global capacity gives them efficacy, that is an original resource needing its own bearer and modal account. If the rival insists that genuine reception is primitively non-well-founded and needs neither resource, it contests step 5. The source argument can reject that as an inadequate complete ultimate account, but the rejection is metaphysical and explanatory. A diagram with an infinite descending order is not formally contradictory.

This is not a concession that the two accounts have equal explanatory merit. The rooted account identifies a positive mode of possession that closes the question it opened. The infinitely derivative account owes a reason why explanatory discharge occurs although every contributor remains a recipient in the very respect under explanation. Still, that pressure is not a proof obtained from local causal premises alone.

## 4  Independence can follow from complete coverage without global well-foundedness

A useful refinement removes an unnecessarily strong demand. We do not need to show that every productive ancestry relation everywhere is well-founded merely to infer that a complete covering source is independent.

Let R(y,x) mean strict actual productive ancestry in the relevant existential respect. Assume it is transitive and irreflexive. A candidate g covers x's productive ancestry when:

- g is a productive ancestor of x
- every productive ancestor y of x is either g or receives productively from g in that respect

Suppose g had a productive predecessor h. Since g is upstream of x, transitivity puts h upstream of x. Coverage says that h is g or is downstream of g. The first case gives self-production immediately; the second gives a finite productive cycle and hence, by transitivity, self-production. Both contradict irreflexivity. So g has no productive predecessor.

This deduction is positive and exact. It does not assume independence in the definition of coverage. Coverage concerns the scope of g's contribution to a specified effect and all its productive prerequisites. Independence follows when that scope is combined with strict productive priority. The condition is stronger than merely having a root: an independent source of one branch need not cover another branch that also contributes to the effect.

The philosophical bridge is equally exact. Here the target includes its declared actual productive ancestry, not merely a qualitatively specified endpoint. If R includes actual but endpoint-redundant contributors, Coverage expresses history-inclusive completeness; it is stronger than the ability to produce the endpoint without them. For an indispensable-prerequisite reading, the target must instead include the actual receiving mode whose realization requires that contributor. Neither reading follows from bare endpoint sufficiency. With that scope fixed, a complete ultimate producer cannot leave an indispensable efficient contributor to the very target supplied wholly outside its productive account. This is not the claim that g must produce every object mentioned in a true description of x. A distant mountain's being shorter than x does not make the mountain a productive prerequisite of x's height. The requirement applies to real exercised efficacy needed for the production. That is why the relation must be typed as productive ancestry, not generic implication.

The theorem does not establish that such a g exists. Calling any explanation “complete” does not manufacture a single bearer with this coverage. A joint plurality of actual contributors, or a non-well-founded productive field, may be offered instead. The existential completeness claim remains the burden. But if a complete covering bearer is independently defended, independence is now a consequence of a more informative scope condition; global well-foundedness is unnecessary.

The formal model includes a covering root above a countably infinite subordinate chain. The ancestry relation is not well-founded, since the subordinate chain has no minimal member, yet the root productively covers it all. This shows why “there is an original source” should not be identified with “no infinite descending sequence exists anywhere.” The model is a logical scope test, not an assertion that every member-to-member edge captures an actual causal mechanism.

## 5  The totality argument need not posit an extra aggregate

A related positive route asks for the complete productive account of all actual dependent reality. It is often attacked as though it must first invent one additional object called “the totality.” It need not.

Fix the actual contingent bearers themselves. The proposition that these particular bearers exist together is true actually. If at least one is contingent, the fixed conjunction is false at some alternative where that member is absent. We do not need the much stronger premise that all the members can be absent together. Nor does writing the conjunction add a concrete object to the universe.

This clarifies the argument's target. A complete productive origin of those bearers must actually account for each bearer within that scope. A cause of their arrangement alone is not yet their complete existential source. Conversely, if an arrangement is a further real dependent feature, it should be included in the target instead of being invoked as a source with no actuality account.

Ibn Taymiyya explicitly distinguishes the individuals, their arrangement, and the combination of both when discussing the totality objection. In Darʾ, hosted /005, lines 277–283, he allows that there may be no extra whole beyond the individuals. Lines 363–371 distinguish producing all the individuals from producing only a relational arrangement, and deny that the necessary source produces itself as one of “all existents.” The text does not authorize a source to be its own product. [Primary text](https://www.islamicbook.ws/amma/dr-taardh-alaql-walnql-005.html). The paginated discussion at [3:179–180](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/413/526/الرد-على-ذلك-من-وجوه) separately explains why an arrangement dependent on the members is not made intrinsically necessary merely by calling it a whole.

This answers one important objection: no additional aggregate is needed for the question of the concrete members' complete productive explanation. It does not yet answer the strongest distributive objection.

### The distributive objection remains precise

In Hume's Dialogues IX.9, Cleanthes says that explaining the individuals suffices and that collecting them into a whole can be an act of the mind. IX.7 additionally asks why a material reality could not possess necessary existence. These are distinct objections. [Hume, Dialogues IX.7–9](https://davidhume.org/texts/d/9).

The source advocate can accept that an inventory does not create a new being. The point is instead that a list of explanations may leave every explanator's efficacy received from elsewhere within the same list. In the infinite case, this is exactly the original-possession dispute. It cannot be answered by repeating that each member has an immediate cause, but it also cannot be refuted solely by the fact that no single immediate cause explains them all.

There is a quantifier issue as well as a metaphysical one. “For each dependent member there is a producer” does not imply “there is one producer for all dependent members.” Even a complete joint explanation might be irreducibly plural. To obtain one common bearer, the argument needs either a defended common-source existence premise or a separately justified integration of the actual productive roles. The retained sourcehood uniqueness result constrains candidates already admitted to be independently complete. It does not conjure one from local explanations, and still requires an actual productive witness.

The strongest reply therefore has two stages: first defend original productive actuality against a wholly derivative explanation; then defend the coverage and bearer unity required for the global role. Refuting reification objections makes the first stage cleaner. It does not skip the second.

## 6  From original actuality to a uniform necessary source

Actual independence and intrinsic necessity should be distinguished before they are connected. A graph can simply have an actual root that fails to exist elsewhere. No contradiction appears until a substantive existential premise is added.

The positive Taymiyyan rationale is stronger than “no incoming arrow.” In Sharḥ al-ʿaqīda al-Aṣfahāniyya, the author explains existence through oneself through the sufficiency of the being itself, not merely the absence of a recorded cause. The decisive short phrase is «إذ نفسه كافية في وجوده». The surrounding discussion contrasts this with existence received wholly or partly from another, and connects intrinsic self-sufficiency with inability to fail to exist. [Primary text, hosted lines 70–76](https://www.islamicbook.ws/ageda/alaqidt-alasfhanit-001.html).

That is a positive proposed account: an ultimately self-sufficient existent has no still-unfulfilled existential condition, and its intrinsic actuality is not conferred by a further source. A nature genuinely open to being absent does not, solely as such, supply the actuality of its instantiation. If its actuality is not intrinsic in the relevant sufficient sense, then another actual source must supply it. The argument favors an intrinsic explanation over a brute contingent existent because the latter leaves the very difference between actual presence and possible absence unexplained.

Two cautions make this defense more exact. First, compatibility with absence is not itself a formal causal relation. A brutalist can deny that a contingent root needs any explanation. The source argument contests that denial through a restricted principle of existential explanation, not through the bare law of noncontradiction. Second, “if g exists, g has its nature” does not entail that g exists. Conditional essence must not silently become unconditional existential sufficiency. The positive self-sufficiency claim must have the latter intended force; merely repeating the object's identity is not enough.

The retained formal route is consequently appropriate. Once an actual complete-cone root g has been obtained, suppose every actually existing contingent bearer in the declared complete field receives its existence. If g could fail to exist, it would receive actually, contradicting its roothood. Therefore this same actual g exists throughout the modal scope. This is de re necessity of one witness, not a conversion of worldwise witness existence into a uniform witness.

Uniform underivability requires another step. Generic essential reception says that if this very individual can be existentially received anywhere, it is received wherever it exists. Under that doctrine, an actual necessary root cannot be received at an alternative; the alleged alternative reception would carry back to the actual world and contradict roothood. The doctrine concerns the constitutive manner of the same individual's actuality, not a fixed parent or a fixed sequence of intermediaries.

The source-based rationale is that existential reception is not an accidental accompaniment of an independently complete bearer: it is how that bearer has its actuality. It is therefore reasonable to regard being wholly received rather than self-sufficient as an essential distinction. This is stronger than saying that a necessary source happens not to receive actually. The source passage supports the intrinsic distinction, while the exact all-world identity transport remains our explicit reconstruction. A rival who allows the same individual to alternate between received and autonomous existence directly contests it.

No general prohibition on real attributes, intrinsic concomitance, renewed acts or contingent effects is used. Standing source-independence is not the claim that every exercise or object of the source is necessary. Nor does the modal proof alone show that necessity is explained by the bearer’s own nature; that essential interpretation is part of the defended metaphysics.

## 7  Steelman of brute intrinsic order

The strongest brute-order proposal should be allowed actual intrinsic structure, genuine causal powers, real truth and error, and real cognitive subjects. It need not pretend that meaningless syntax manufactures meaning or that laws instantiate themselves. It can say that structured concrete reality simply exists and that its internal natures determine which transitions, contents and evaluations are correct.

Three versions differ materially.

A contingent primitive base disputes existential explanation. It can explain many changes within the world while declining to explain why this base exists. That is a coherent stopping proposal at the formal level. It falls short of the complete ultimate explanation sought here because it leaves the actualization of its base primitive. Calling it complete on that larger target is the point contested; it need not be called self-contradictory.

A necessary primitive concrete base grants a necessary unreceived actuality, provided “necessary” is genuinely existential and applies to one actual bearer. It may then be a real rival to the theological characterization of that bearer, rather than a rival to every upstream conclusion. One cannot rule it out by treating matter as unknowable, inert stuff if the rival has expressly posited intrinsic powers. Conversely, saying that unknown intrinsic properties might necessitate the base does not itself give a developed explanation of their content or necessity.

An irreducibly plural necessary order may sustain the actual field without one bearer being complete for it. Shared constraints, mutual fittingness or modal nonseparability do not alone show that one source supplies the other sources' efficacy. The split-source formal control exposes this scope gap. Explanatory unification earns credit only when it derives the integration, not when many primitive features are grouped under one label.

The source argument's positive advantage is strongest where it identifies a common original productive basis, relates its attributes to its nature, and explains how contingent dependent acts arise without leaving an external supplier. It is weakest when it merely replaces “primitive order” with “source” and leaves the same number of independent explanatory demands untouched. The sealed Knowledge packet addresses some of those further demands; the present upstream argument does not borrow its disputed conclusions.

## 8  What has genuinely advanced

The real-episode route makes a real actuality premise explicit and avoids deriving a concrete entity from abstract evaluability. Its local causal rationale has a precise Taymiyyan methodological ancestor. The source/attribute distinction prevents an unwanted simplicity premise from entering the argument under the word “dependence.” Complete productive coverage yields independence without global well-foundedness, and a checked infinite model shows that the distinction matters. The plurality analysis blocks a crude aggregate objection while exposing the still-substantive common-bearer quantifier. Finally, intrinsic sufficiency makes the actual-root-to-necessity warrant more informative than an unexplained prohibition of brute termini.

These are positive structural and textual gains. They do not establish all their metaphysical premises by formalizing them. A complete deductive Necessary-Being conclusion remains unavailable from the accepted Track T no-go alone. The strongest research direction is now sharper: defend original productive possession and the existence of genuinely complete common coverage, then defend the essential modal character of that same bearer. The surviving rivals contest those exact claims, rather than escaping through a merely syntactic account of an actual exercise.
