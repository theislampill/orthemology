## Candidate

Compare a unified-personal architecture `A` and a source-compatible impersonal or powers architecture `R` on one finite typed comparison surface. The candidate claims that the registered evidence can prefer `A` only through at least one of three routes:

1. an independently warranted source-conditioned exclusion of `R`;
2. predictive or interventional surplus;
3. an independently defended prior or coding policy.

When source compatibility, priors, and represented evidence distributions are equal, any candidate-swap-invariant score ties the two architectures.

## Provisional proof idea

Bayes odds remain unchanged under equal likelihood. A common representation channel cannot increase total variation. A source-role bundle determines one world referent only relative to an admissible interpretation family. Therefore source compatibility, common representations, and equal likelihood do not by themselves distinguish the two architectures.

## Provisional controls

Matched rival, candidate relabeling, hidden source-world anchor, prior reversal, version drift, target leakage, and post-outcome representation selection.

## V1 authority ceiling

Candidate only. Source compatibility, experiment completeness, zero-probability events, common-channel status, and the distinction between evidence update and final preference still require audit.
