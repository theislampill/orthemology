# Semantic warrant tests: what would actually discriminate?

These tests operationalize the candidate norm account without claiming that a successful test creates normative authority. They are intended to expose a false application of the argument, not to certify all cognition from outside cognition.

## 1. Independence is relative to the disputed claim

“Independent evidence” does not mean evidence with no common ultimate cause, no common language or no common source object. It means evidence whose relevant warrant is not entirely inherited from the very route or premise challenged in this assessment.

For an OCR error, direct inspection of the same source image can be independent of the OCR rule while sharing the source. For source authenticity, two copies of that image do not provide two independent historical witnesses. For an author's own words, the source itself is the appropriate target even when its claim about the world is false. For the truth of that world claim, faithful attribution is insufficient.

This gives four distinct assessments:

| Question | Appropriate anchor | What a duplicate does not add |
|---|---|---|
| Did OCR reproduce these marks? | The particular inspected image and numeral convention | New authenticity evidence |
| Is this image the identified edition? | Edition/custody evidence beyond the disputed filename | A second historical source |
| Did the author assert p? | Text, speaker and dialectical context | Truth of p |
| Is p true? | Its domain-specific reasons and evidence | Independent world confirmation merely from faithful quotation |

A shared root can therefore be decisive for one question and insufficient for another. Counting roots without the respect of assessment can reject good local corrections or award false global corroboration. The norm-fixing argument's requirement is claim-relative route independence, not metaphysical independence from a common Creator or a prohibition on comparing a copy with its original.

## 2. Target-preserving intervention rather than reward improvement

Suppose a transcription system changes from output 71 to 17. The same trace fits at least five hypotheses:

- H1: it corrected a misreading of an identified source whose marks are 17
- H2: it changed the reference from a source marked 71 to a different source marked 17
- H3: it redefined the output code so that the token 17 denotes the old value
- H4: it changed the acceptance metric without changing its source relation
- H5: it obtained one lucky output while the general error mechanism remains

A meaningful correction test retains the original source identity, the output semantics and the relevant application context. It checks the actual source marks through an appropriately independent route and tests whether the diagnosed mechanism changes as predicted on further suitable cases. If reference or code must change, the change is reported as such. A correct repair can include that change, but it cannot silently claim to have corrected the old assertion under unchanged semantics.

The aim is not to require a universal randomized trial for every error. One clearly legible original can settle one transcription. The stronger claim of a reliable repaired process needs stronger evidence. Warrant is claim-scaled.

## 3. Function-fixing evidence changes the permissible inference

Equal output histories under a finite test do not make two function hypotheses equivalent. Suppose an artifact consistently emits accurate temperature readings.

- A history of intentional instrument design can support measurement as its intended function
- A record of selection because accurate detection supported a reproducing lineage can support a selected-effect claim
- Its role in an identified self-maintaining organization can support an organizational account
- A reward log can show a trained objective, including the possibility of rewards that diverge from accuracy

The first three are not refuted because the fourth has the same output. Conversely, no output score proves any one history. A broken instrument with an independently documented design can retain its function; an accurate decorative device need not acquire that design history merely by behaving similarly.

The test must therefore compare claims at the correct level. To distinguish two historical explanations, investigate history. To test accuracy, compare with the target. To assess moral legitimacy, examine the reasons and authority for the end. If the ultimate hypotheses entail the same local facts, the local dataset does not discriminate them merely because the researcher prefers one vocabulary.

## 4. Plural norms: diagnosis before aggregation

When two standards conflict, identify which row actually applies:

1. Same referent, different harmless representation: establish the conversion and preserve both outputs where useful
2. Different referents accidentally conflated: split the claims and correct their bindings
3. Different legitimate respects: compose the repairs and prove preservation
4. Different subjects or permissions: transport warrant only where its conditions hold
5. Live hypotheses about a hidden fact: collect discriminating evidence or use a genuinely safe uniform policy
6. Real pro-tanto reasons in conflict: justify priority without calling sacrifice full satisfaction
7. Absolute incompatible demands under exactly the same scope: record infeasibility and revisit scope, authority or the assumption of joint satisfiability

An optimizer that assigns weights to the competing demands immediately jumps to one particular treatment of row 6. It has not established that the case belongs there or that its weights are justified. A repair system should diagnose the semantic kind of conflict before choosing an optimization formalism.

## 5. Intrinsic impersonal source versus intellectual source

A score, benchmark or formal model cannot by itself decide between these ultimate accounts when both grant the same accurate derivative cognition. A serious discriminating argument must attack a premise they do not share.

The candidate intellectual argument appeals to original cognitive and practical adequacy: an independently complete source of genuine cognition and fitting action is not wholly devoid of the corresponding eligible perfection. The rival denies that respect-specific priority, proposing an intrinsic noncognitive nature capable of producing the derivative achievement. Neither side is helped by replacing genuine cognition with a lookup-table behavior.

Three possible contributions should be separated:

- **Conceptual exclusion:** show that the rival's intrinsic nature and actual explanatory role contradict one another, without putting mentality into the role definition
- **Explanatory discrimination:** develop both positive accounts and show one leaves an independently motivated demand unmet while the other answers it
- **Empirical discrimination:** derive different observable consequences from developed accounts under the same experiment and coding contract

The present local norm argument supplies none of these as a complete defeat of the strong impersonal source. It does identify the right target: genuine content, function, apprehension and fitting reasons, rather than an arbitrary reward. The sixth upstream and attribute lanes supply further premise defenses; their force must be assessed independently.

## 6. When to stop an actual common-repair claim

A common repair has been established only for the announced respect, scope and route. It should be withheld or narrowed if:

- source identity or the represented target remains unresolved in a way that changes the action
- the relevant norms have been accepted only because the chosen output satisfies them
- a repair deletes counterevidence or silently demotes another live obligation
- two apparently independent confirmations inherit the same disputed premise
- evidence reaches an actor but the action's authority or recipient-specific warrant does not
- the intervention is only correct in a hidden state the actor cannot identify and no safe uniform route exists
- the target can be reached once but no claimed persistence condition has been checked

The positive counterpart is equally important. Complete ultimate metaphysical agreement, full causal diagnosis and a single shared representation are not always required. A well-supported target relation, a sound intervention and sufficient preservation/coverage can establish a real local repair. That is a substantive outcome worth retaining while more ambitious source questions remain open.
