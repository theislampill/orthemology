## Declared contract

Fix strictly positive conditional columns P=(p_1,...,p_k), each with r>=2 outcomes, and a current normalised probability vector q (sum_i q_i=1) with at least one strictly positive weight representation. Let

    W={w>0 : Qw=q},   d=k-rank(Q).

Column normalisation implies sum(w)=1. Thus W is a nonempty relatively open convex subset of an affine space of dimension d. The retained signature is constant on W.

Require preservation of the full-source Fisher squared gradient norms of the conditional coordinate p_(j,i), for one fixed target coordinate i and j=1,...,k-1. These are observables of the ambient joint-law geometry, even though this fibre holds the numerical conditionals fixed. They are not gradients computed inside the fixed-P fibre itself.

## Corollary

1. Any retained supplement satisfying this norm contract must distinguish every w in W.
2. If d>0, no finite-valued sidecar can satisfy the contract on all of W.
3. Among continuous real-coordinate supplements on this fixed fibre, d coordinates are necessary and sufficient. This is not a claim of a globally continuous minimal chart across changing-rank P strata.

Proof. For positive p_(j,i)<1 the known numerator c_j=p_(j,i)(1-p_(j,i)) is strictly positive. The preserved norm n_j=c_j/w_j determines w_j=c_j/n_j for j<k; normalisation recovers the last weight. Thus equal supplemented records force equal weights. If d>0, W is uncountable, so a finite alphabet cannot be injective.

For the continuous lower bound, parameterise W by an open subset of R^d. If a continuous injective supplement took values in R^m with m<d, compose it with the standard embedding R^m into R^d. Invariance of domain would make its image open in R^d, whereas that image lies in a lower-dimensional subspace with empty interior. Contradiction. For sufficiency choose a basis of ker(Q) and retain the d affine coordinates of w relative to a fixed solution. Together with fixed P,q these recover w exactly. Equivalently choose d coordinate rows independent on ker(Q). When d=0 the existing signature already fixes w and no supplement is needed.

## What this changes in the crosswalk

The finite sidecar result remains correct. It supplies a minimum alphabet for a finite declared contract. It cannot be applied to an unrestricted continuous audit-weight fibre by counting only the discrete provenance labels. A finite discretisation changes exact preservation to an approximate or restricted-family question.

The stronger norm contract is also optional. For the posterior-answer-only contract F already suffices by the inherited result. Some fixed objectives descend even where the complete cometric does not. An independently chosen reduced metric likewise changes the contract. Therefore this corollary does not demand full source-law retention for every noetic model, nor does it turn mathematical weights into actual epistemic warrant.
