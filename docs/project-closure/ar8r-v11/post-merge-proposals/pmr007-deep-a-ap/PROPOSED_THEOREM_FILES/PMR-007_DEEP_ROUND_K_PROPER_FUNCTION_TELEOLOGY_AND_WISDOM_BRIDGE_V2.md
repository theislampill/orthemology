## 1. Required coordinate separation

Use the following typed coordinates:

```text
PF_T:  the bearer has proper function relative to target T;
REP:   the system carries a truth-correlated representation or discriminator;
REL:   the mechanism is reliable in the declared environment;
AG:    one bearer is an agent/intentional subject;
K:     that bearer understands the relevant end, means, and circumstances;
F:     the end is objectively fitting or praiseworthy;
W_F:   the bearer selects the end because of its fittingness;
M:     the selected means are appropriately proportioned to the end;
C:     the bearer has practical capacity to employ the means;
X:     the bearer nonaccidentally executes the means toward the end;
OWN:   the deliberative and executive roles belong to the same bearer in the same episode;
STAB:  the reason-responsive pattern is stable over the declared counterfactual episode class.
```

`REP` is not `K`; reliable representation may be impersonal. `PF_T` is not `F`; a proper function may be etiologically or organizationally fixed without being praiseworthy.

## 2. Three-level role contract

This round defines three nonidentical levels for audit and comparison:

### Wise-directed choice

\[
WD(g,a,e,m)
:=
AG\land K\land F\land W_F\land M\land OWN.
\]

### Effectively executed wise-directed episode

\[
EW(g,a,e,m)
:=
WD(g,a,e,m)\land C\land X.
\]

### Stable bearer-level Wisdom eligibility

\[
SW(g,\mathcal E)
:=
STAB(g,\mathcal E)\land
\forall a\in\mathcal E\,[Relevant(a)\rightarrow WD(g,a)].
\]

These are typed role contracts, not proofs that any actual bearer satisfies them and not a reduction of every ordinary or theological use of “wisdom.” `EW` is not required for every wise choice: external prevention can block execution while `WD` remains.

## 4. Countermodel architecture

### K-CM1 — harmful corrigibility

A personal optimizer understands and reliably realizes a destructive installed end. `AG,K,C,X,REL` hold; `F` fails. Computational and practical success do not yield wise direction.

### K-CM2 — impersonal truth tracker

A selected-effect or powers-based mechanism has `PF_T,REP,REL` but no agentive understanding or fitting-end selection. `AG=K=W_F=false`.

### K-CM3 — accidental beneficence

An impersonal event produces a fitting outcome. `F` and the outcome hold, but `AG,K,W_F,OWN` fail.

### K-CM4 — knowledgeable vicious agent

One bearer has knowledge, will, and power but selects a vicious end. `F` fails; Wisdom does not follow from K/W/P.

### K-CM5 — wise direction, blocked execution

The agent understands and selects a fitting end with fitting means, but an external obstacle blocks execution. `WD=true`, `EW=false`. This repairs V1's success conflation.

### K-CM6 — benevolent but incompetent selection

The end is fitting and intentionally selected, but relevant beliefs are false or means disproportionate. `K` or `M` fails.

### K-CM7 — selected deception

A trait's selected effect is successful deception. Etiological proper function and current success coexist with failure of truth-directed fittingness.

### K-CM8 — distributed roles

Representation, evaluation, selection, and efficacy are distributed across bearers. Every guard is globally instantiated but the common-bearer intersection is empty.

### K-CM9 — one successful episode, unstable disposition

An agent performs one effectively wise-directed episode but counterfactually chooses vicious ends in nearby relevant cases. `EW=true`, `STAB=false`.

### K-CM10 — unique impersonal root

Deep J's one lineage root is a nonmental powers field. Efficacy unity does not yield Wisdom.

### K-CM11 — direct source predication

Track N predicates the Wise of a bearer. This supplies a school-internal source premise if accepted; it is not a neutral derivation from `PF_T`, `REP`, or the graph results.

## 5. Proper-function and Candidate E adjudication

The proper-function baseline distinguishes causal-role, organizational, selected-effect, designed, learned-objective, teleological, epistemic, and fiṭrī functions. None supplies all `WD` coordinates merely by being a proper-function account.

Candidate E contributes a negative constraint: objective radical correction cannot derive all authority from the defeated current token basis. It does not select among type-level, fixed-point, primitive-norm, naturalized, fiṭrī, or personal norm sources. Therefore:

```text

not entailed.
```

## 6. Candidate G and common-bearer effect

The role contract is bearer-indexed. Separate sets of bearers eligible for `AG`, `K`, `W_F`, `M`, `C`, and `X` do not yield one wise bearer unless their total intersection is nonempty or one bearer is independently fixed. This is an application of Deep G's common-bearer result, not a new theorem origin.

Even a common bearer can carrier-box independent guards. Genuine Wisdom-oriented unification would require reason-sensitive cross-dependencies, such as knowledge of fittingness constraining selection and means choice, rather than a conjunction of idle predicates.
