# An authoritative direction can disclose a good and add a good

9 October 2026 UTC. Additive, source-grounded philosophical work within active T20. The existing affirmative appraisals of actuality, intrinsic necessity, common sourcehood, Knowledge, Will, Wisdom, and fitting practical acknowledgment remain in place. This report does not reopen those arguments, create a new mathematical theorem, or authenticate a particular present directive.

## Result

**A genuinely authoritative direction can add a real relational excellence to an act whose earlier goodness it also discloses.** Ibn Taymiyya states this combination explicitly at *Majmūʿ* 14:144 and 146: justice, beneficence, and maintaining family relations can benefit their agents without a command; being commanded adds a further good. The threefold taxonomy at 8:434–436 therefore describes sources of qualification which can combine, rather than three mutually exclusive classes of acts.

The constructive explanation is that an actual direction makes a particular performance available as a fitting response to its rightful source. That response was not already an actual relation to an unissued direction. The positive basis is the inherited appraisal of due acknowledgment of genuine excellence and benefaction, together with the actual, appropriately scoped direction. The new good is not created merely by assigning a number or calling compliance good. Nor must command-dependent goodness replace the independently intelligible good of the performance.

This earns a substantive account of **added reason and qualified performance**. An all-things-considered obligation additionally requires warranted obligatory force, jurisdiction, applicability, and the relevant absence of defeating conditions. The source's own distinction between imposing an obligation and the act's resulting obligatory qualification supports that further branch; the assertion that an act becomes good does not alone establish it.

The same primary division supplies a third possibility beyond real source-law change and inert mistaken belief. A jurist's mistaken representation need not alter the original ruling, while an independently governing duty to report the result of qualified inquiry can still guide present action. The separate commissioned-content-access report owns the detailed application of this latter finding. It is one shared source, not another independent witness.

The complete electronic division starts at 142 with a question about prayer after a promised answer. The author's answer at 143 preserves genuine causal means, relevant conditions, and impediments. ## 3. The positive reconstruction

Consider the source's example of beneficence. Fix an eligible act of helping, its recipient's real benefit, and the relevant capacities. Do not erase the benefit in order to manufacture room for authority.

1. **There is already an intelligible good.** The person's benefit and the fittingness of helping do not await our discovery of an instruction.
2. **A rightfully directing source actually addresses the agent about that performance.** This occurrence and its content must be warranted, with the source's title and domain established through the independently retained argument. Mere causal production of a sentence is insufficient.
3. **That event changes the practical relation.** Performing the act can now answer this actual direction. An earlier performance, or one with no such direction, cannot be an intentional response to this very address merely by producing the same physical effect.
4. **Fitting responsiveness has positive worth.** On the inherited due-response appraisal, acknowledging genuine excellence and benefaction through appropriately directed conduct is an intelligible good. It is not exhausted by fear, reward, or the producer's desire for advantage.
5. **The two grounds can be jointly realized.** The performance can help the recipient and realize fitting responsiveness. The latter need not cancel, replace, or manufacture the former.

The normative work is in steps 2 and 4, not in the grammatical fact that a command has occurred. The relation in step 3 identifies what has changed without yet declaring every such relation good. Step 4 supplies the previously defended evaluative reason for this relation to count. Consequently the explanation is not the circular inference “obedience is owed because it is obedience.” It is the application of a standing due-response judgment to an independently identifiable new address.

This reconstruction is an affirmative application of the source's account, not a verbatim proof found in one paragraph. It gives positive reason to accept real added goodness when the stated relation is warranted. It does not promise a deduction of all normative significance from norm-free facts, or defer all appraisal until every possible critic agrees.

### The controlled contrast

An informative report that helping benefits someone can improve the agent's access to an existing good without itself directing the agent. A rightful direction to help can do that and also specify a fitting response to its speaker. A counterfeit direction can produce an impression of that relation without actually establishing it. These differences persist even when the physical assistance and its benefit are the same.

This is a comparison of relevant descriptions and stages. It is not a metaphysical possibility certificate obtained by deleting a divine act while holding its complete factive foreknowledge and determining wisdom unchanged. No fully specified alternate divine history is claimed.

The comparison must also distinguish **the normative standing of the prospective act** from **the agent's accomplished excellence in performing it for the relevant reason**. An actual applicable direction can bear on what should be done even where a subject refuses it. Conversely, a physically matching performance does not automatically display deliberate acknowledgment. The present passage does not decide every technical question about intention, validity, reward, or excusing ignorance.

## 4. Why this is neither arbitrary fiat nor merely discovery

**Not merely discovery:** the actual address is part of the circumstance under which the performance can be responsive to this source. Learning an unchanged beneficial feature and receiving a new direction are different events. The author explicitly combines disclosure with acquired qualification, so an exclusively evidential interpretation misses part of his position.

**Not arbitrary fiat:** the source's actual account constrains both the act and the directing by wisdom and intelligible good. The addition is tied to a warranted relationship and fitting response, rather than to the claim that any powerful speaker's desire gives itself rightful force. The inherited pure-perfection appraisal gives positive source-specific warrant; it is not a permission to treat all created speakers as similarly entitled.

At 146 the author also mentions commands among human beings. That analogy concerns how a direction or trial can make a practical difference; it does not establish that every causally effective human order has rightful force. Causal enforcement and intentional adoption of an utterance are themselves different, and neither alone confers jurisdiction. The inherited attestation and practical-authority guards therefore apply rather than being rediscovered here.

**Not numerical addition:** two respects of goodness do not entail twice the value, an additive utility function, stronger obligation in every circumstance, or a unique optimum. They may interact. The relevant conclusion is that an exclusively physical or independently beneficial description can omit a normatively relevant relation.

**Not a general authority proof:** the direction does not confer its own title upon its speaker. Nor does deserved gratitude imply unrestricted control over every otherwise open choice. The prior title/domain appraisal remains load-bearing. If it is warranted, requiring it again as though no philosophical work had supplied it would be an unwarranted reset; if it is absent in a particular application, the new passage does not create it by stipulation.

The obligation branch is similarly exact. Goodness may be compatible with recommendation. To infer an obligatory performance, one needs the source's actual obligating direction, with its intended force and conditions. *Majmūʿ* 8:434 expressly distinguishes the obligating address from the resulting obligation. A truthful assertion that an obligation already obtains is yet another route: it can warrant the proposition without creating the obligation.

## 5. Three changes with different owners

The last part of the shared division requires more than a binary taxonomy of norm change and belief change.

1. **Actual source-side direction or authorised replacement:** the content of a governing address, or its applicability, can make a real difference to the directed performance. This includes the added-good case; it need not erase the independent good. Whether it changes a standing type or instantiates a conditional rule is a modeling choice requiring declared semantics.
2. **Changed representation of unchanged source law:** at 152–155, ignorance of a concession can lead people to attribute a prohibition or requirement that the source did not impose. The author explicitly denies that this amounts to abrogation of the law. Mere confidence or institutional repetition does not repair the source attribution.
3. **A genuine practical duty under the person's epistemic situation:** at 153–154 the author says a qualified inquirer must state what the inquiry yields. Thus an erroneous view of the source ruling can coexist with a distinct, genuinely governing practical requirement. This does not make the erroneous source-attribution true. Nor does it establish that every negligent, coerced, or unqualified judgment is authorised.

The separate commissioned-content-access analysis develops the exact third case and its consequences for access. This report uses it only to prevent collapsing distinct normative owners. The source's punitive theological explanation is not adopted as a diagnosis of any contemporary person's ignorance or suffering.

The coordinated primary reader found at 14:159–160 that hidden knowledge and disagreement may sometimes be a mercy, while maintaining one actual truth; removal of doubt can also have different practical consequences. That is relevant against an unrestricted assumption that more explicit information is always better. It supplies no general permission for wilful ignorance or concealment.
