## Blocking findings

### AZ-F01 — the content variable was treated as already semantic

`C` is meaningful only relative to an independently warranted semantic,
translation, referent, and version contract.  Without that contract the design
crosses experimental labels, not literal contents.

### AZ-F02 — intervention feasibility was hidden

The experiment requires candidate-independent interventions that can vary form
while holding content fixed and vary content while using controlled forms.
Natural languages and physical systems may not permit every cell.  Feasibility,
positivity, consistency, and no-interference are explicit guards.

### AZ-F03 — observation was conflated with intervention

Diagonal observational support does not identify the potential-response table.
The positive theorem must be stated in `do(F=f,C=c)` or otherwise randomized
interventional terms, with confounding separated.

### AZ-F04 — necessity of every cell lacked a model-class qualifier

Every cell is necessary only for exact classification over the unrestricted
finite response-table class.  Restricted parametric, monotone, compositional,
or source-constrained classes may require fewer cells.

### AZ-F05 — deterministic and stochastic claims were not separated

V1 proves an exact deterministic finite-table result.  A stochastic extension
must use complete interventional response distributions and distributional
form-invariance/content-sensitivity, not silently reuse deterministic equality.

### AZ-F06 — mediation was overclose to intrinsic semanticity

A system can respond invariantly to a declared content variable while that
variable is externally assigned, represented by an impersonal law, or supplied
by a downstream interpreter.  The result does not close Deep AY H7a2.

### AZ-F07 — semantic response was overclose to intentional uptake and proper function

An impersonal semantic processor can satisfy the entire table.  Content
sensitivity does not establish first-person ownership, selection because of
fittingness, personality, proper function, or Wisdom.

### AZ-F08 — temporal, history, and version stability were omitted

The finite table is one frozen episode and contract.  History-dependent
policies, learning, version drift, dynamic membership, and stale semantic
bindings require separate temporal models.

### AZ-F09 — common-experiment and representation guards were incomplete

The same forms, contents, interventions, and response coding must be used for
all compared architectures.  Candidate-dependent labels or representations can
manufacture apparent semantic mediation.

### AZ-F10 — ancestry and novelty were incomplete

The result is a finite factorial causal-design/positivity identification fact,
combined with AR8R semantic-custody controls.  It receives zero general
mathematical novelty.

## Required repair

1. State the deterministic finite `do(F,C)` theorem with semantic and feasibility guards.
2. Prove the missing-cell impossibility only for the unrestricted table class.
3. Add a distributional stochastic extension.
4. Separate semantic mediation from H7a2, intentionality, proper function, and Wisdom.
5. Add version, source, candidate-dependent-coding, and impersonal-realizer controls.
6. Run a distinct three-form/three-content rereview and stochastic-table checks.
