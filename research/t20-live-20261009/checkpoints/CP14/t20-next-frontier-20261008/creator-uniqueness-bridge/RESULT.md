# Causal explanation and creator uniqueness

9 October 2026 UTC. Bounded T20 audit of Pruss's §5.5 variant. Existing reports and repository files are unchanged. No integration, T20 closure, metaphysical countermodel certification, or historical-time credit is claimed.

## Result

**Pruss's argument identifies a real burden for plural total explainers, but the printed reasoning does not independently establish uniqueness at the retained created-order interface.** Its first explanatory target includes contingent powers, acts, nonexercises, and explanation-status facts about necessary agents. That is substantially stronger than the complete created field. Its further steps need both an adequately typed anti-bootstrap principle for powers and a bridge from explanation of nonexercise to a kind of preventative control whose reciprocal possession is impossible.

The strongest critical result is a distinction within that last bridge. An agent's activity can causally explain another's nonexercise through reasons, circumstances, or an incompatible achieved outcome. That can warrant a broad preventative description. It does not by itself warrant a strategy that controls the peer against every admissible exercise. Conversely, if prevention has the latter meaning, its derivation from causal explanation needs defense. Reciprocal standing preventative powers and an indefinitely repeatable description of them are not already a contradictory joint exercise or a vicious causal descent.

This does **not** establish a pair of perfect original creators satisfying every source premise. The retained joint-production account does not automatically satisfy individual explanation of all contingent truths, and a common-reason concordance account is not yet an account of each subject's complete particular act. The audit locates unsupported transitions without treating either omission as a full rival metaphysics. It also does not reverse or preserve the separate common-source warrant appraisal by authority: this argument supplies no new deductive closure of that dispute.

## 2 Exact logical dependencies

Use one fixed global domain W of admissible worlds and rigidly identified individuals. N(x) means that this very x exists at every world in W. Let E_w(x,s) mean that x's causal activity in w explains the fixed proposition s. Factivity requires E_w(x,s) to imply truth of s at w.

Pruss's creator condition is, schematically:

 C_w(x) iff N(x) and E_w(x,s) for every globally contingent s true at w.

The input is a global claim, for every w there is some x satisfying C_w(x). It does not already provide one individual occupying the role throughout W. The argument attempts to derive that stronger de re conclusion. No S5 quantifier exchange supplies it.

The proof has the following dependencies:

1. **Individual total coverage.** Each relevant world has a necessary individual whose activity explains each contingent truth there. Collective coverage alone does not give this individual premise.
2. **Power closure.** IP permits a power attainable through one's own available activity to count among one's indirect powers.
3. **Power essentialisation.** Contingent possession of a power allegedly requires self-explanation through an antecedently presupposed instance of that same power. Ruling this out needs more than IP's closure rule.
4. **Explanatory activity to successful exercise.** E_w(x,s) is treated as x exercising a power to make s true. This is strongest when the causal activity actually brings about the relevant s, and less immediate for derivative negative or explanation-status truths.
5. **Explanation of omission to prevention.** Explaining a peer's failure to exercise a power is treated as exercising a preventative power over that exercise.
6. **Exclusion of reciprocal prevention.** The resulting hierarchy is declared vicious, or its compressed mutual-prevention form absurd. A substantive account of priority, robustness, or jointly admissible successful exercises is needed here.
7. **Nondegenerate possibilities.** Distinct comparison worlds are separated by a contingent proposition. A second world is also used to derive uniqueness within one world. The intended contingent-creation setting provides the relevant background; the proof is not a theorem of an arbitrary one-world structure.

Items 1–3 are invoked at lines 4118–4141; items 4–6 occur at 4152–4197. Some are expressed as inferences rather than separately named assumptions. Identifying them does not show that they are false or demand derivation from metaphysically neutral premises. It identifies what their warrant must cover.

### What the modal construction gets right

Take C_w1(A), C_w2(B), and a fixed p true at w1 and false at w2. Put q = ¬E(B,¬p), with its world evaluation understood. At w1, q follows from factivity. At w2, ¬q follows from B's total coverage. Therefore q is genuinely contingent.

If A's explanation of q is a successful exercise P1, and P1 belongs to A necessarily, then A has P1 at w2. It cannot successfully exercise P1 there because q is false. A's nonexercise is contingent: the same subject successfully exercises the same power at w1. Necessary agreement between agents does not make every particular exercise necessary, so concordance alone cannot defeat this step.

The same reasoning can be iterated if the successive prevention bridges and power transfers hold. This is genuine content. The defect is not a silent move from necessary existence to necessary action, nor a requirement that perfect agents will contradictory outcomes.

## 3 What IP does and does not establish

### Direct and indirect power

Distinguish D_x(A), an immediately exercisable capacity, from I_x(A), ability to achieve A through an available sequence of one's activities. If x can obtain D_x(A) and then use it, IP supports I_x(A) already. It does not show that D_x(A) was already possessed.

For example, making a tool can explain an agent's later direct ability to perform a task even when its prior tool-making ability already made the task indirectly achievable. No explanatory loop is needed. The premise and conclusion concern different power types. This example is only a typing control; it is not a metaphysical account of a creator.

A charitable reconstruction therefore applies IP to the same **complete standing ability**, including available indirect means, on both sides. Under that reading, the direct/indirect objection alone does not refute the argument. But an additional principle is then visible:

 **No ultimate power bootstrap:** an exercise that already presupposes x's complete standing ability to achieve A cannot provide the complete explanation of x's possession of that very ability in the same respect.

This has independent motivation. Explaining the manifestation of a power is not necessarily explaining why its productive basis exists. An explanation that must import its whole explanandum as an already available resource has not supplied that basis. Applied to the correctly identified complete power, this is a serious explanatory constraint, not merely numerical uniqueness restated.

### Possession and maintenance

Pruss uses **explanatory** priority at lines 4137–4141, not an initial temporal event. No first act or first moment should be inserted. Still, explanatory priority must relate the same explanandum at the same level of completeness.

An earlier or explanatorily prior capacity can enable activity that sustains its later instantiation. The latter instantiation's explanation need not explain the entire standing capacity across the history. Similarly, an activity can preserve access to a resource without first originating the resource. These are acyclic dependencies when the indexed relata differ. They do not refute no-bootstrap, but they block treating every explanation of possession as first acquisition or whole-origin explanation.

To derive modal necessity from the reductio, the source must warrant all of the following: contingent possession really is the complete same-respect power fact; its required causal explanation concerns that complete possession; the explanatory activity presupposes an available route of the appropriate power type; and IP identifies that route with the same ultimate ability whose complete explanation is demanded. Only then does no-bootstrap rule out the contingent case.

IP by itself is not a theorem that every necessary being's power is essential. Nor is a fact's contingency identical to acquisition at a first time. A contingent power can be possessed throughout a history; its explanatory question remains modal and ontological. Conversely, a necessarily available overall repertoire can have contingent direct manifestations. The two distinctions must not be collapsed.

The strongest interpretation thus makes the essential-power result conditional on a credible but stronger explanatory adequacy principle. It cannot be imported merely from the printed words “causal activity explains.” There is no need to admit vicious self-production in order to withhold that import.

### What remains when essential powers are granted

For the retained original-agent question, stable essential powers are already proposed independently. The present audit therefore grants them for the rest of the challenge. A failure to derive them from IP does not defeat that stronger candidate or establish its defeat by the source. With the power-transfer step supplied, the nonexercise-to-control bridge, alleged viciousness, and coverage of peer contingencies remain to be assessed. The prevention analysis below is not conditional on winning the IP criticism.

## 4 Explanation of nonexercise and power over another agent

Three claims should be kept separate:

- x's causal activity adequately explains y's nonexercise of R.
- x has an available way of bringing about or securing that nonexercise in appropriate circumstances.
- x has a successful control strategy over y's exercise of R, robust against all independently admissible peer responses in the relevant respect.

The first can often support the second. Neither automatically supplies the third.

### An independently intelligible local counterinterpretation

A presents B with a good reason to refrain from an optional act. B appreciates it and freely refrains. A's activity can be causally relevant to explaining B's nonexercise while leaving B the agent of its own response. The explanation need not be an efficient manufacture of B's will or a capacity to override it. Nothing in this example requires immoral willing, contradictory joint intentions, or a vicious causal loop.

This is a local counterinterpretation to an unrestricted explanation-to-coercive-control rule, not a counterexample satisfying the complete creator premise. If B's independent contingent response is an indispensable additional unexplained input, A has not yet explained all contingent truths by itself. The defender can press precisely that further question.

To isolate the remaining issue, grant rather than challenge total explanatory coverage. It supplies E_w(A,¬Exercise(B,R)). The additional conclusion that A has robust control over B still requires a rule connecting that explanatory relation to such control. Quantification over every contingent explanandum does not itself change what the explanatory relation means. A defender may argue that complete causal explanation entails the stronger control, but that is exactly the substantive bridge to assess.

The source's own nonnecessitating reasons framework makes the distinction especially relevant. An explanation need not entail the chosen response; the necessary agency and reason-appreciation background need not remove the agent's authorship. Thus the defender cannot dismiss every reason-mediated explanation merely because it falls short of coercion. It must explain why **the totality explanation required here** demands the stronger form of control, or show that the weaker form already suffices for the contradiction. Neither follows just from the word “causal.”

There is an additional distinction at the first q step. q denies B's successful explanatory production of ¬p. It does not say that B never considers, attempts, or wills ¬p. A's production of p can account for q through factivity and incompatibility without establishing control of B's intrinsic will. Treating these explananda as interchangeable would enlarge the conclusion.

### Prevention need not mean coercion

It would be unfair to refute Pruss simply by assigning a coercive meaning that he does not explicitly state. Successful persuasion, dissuasion, or removal of an occasion can count as prevention. On that broad reading, explanation of nonexercise may indeed warrant a preventative power. The remaining problem moves to the alleged impossibility of reciprocal prevention. On a stronger sovereign-control reading, that impossibility may become more plausible, but the earlier bridge becomes more demanding.

Necessary voluntary concordance can preserve separate owned acts without foreign efficient donation. That observation alone does not make each subject a total explainer, establish full particular wisdom, or prove unrestricted perfection. It does show why absence of a rival exercise must not silently be reclassified as defeated hostile agency.

## 5 The regress and its compressed alternative

### Standing powers do not jointly exercise themselves

After the transfer steps, the argument has A's standing power to prevent B's prevention and B's standing power to prevent A's prevention. These capacities may be present in both worlds while their successful exercises alternate.

At the level of success propositions X and Y, the relevant pattern can be:

 □(X → ¬Y), □(Y → ¬X), ◇X, and ◇Y.

This is consistent. It contains no world where X and Y both obtain. Necessary possession of the two corresponding standing abilities does not supply that missing joint exercise. Ordinary preemption and inhibition illustrate this logical point, but ordinary agents are not hereby certified as perfect originals.

One possible strengthening is that each agent has an independently selectable strategy guaranteed to succeed against every eligible peer exercise, together with common admissibility of those strategies. That gives a real incompatibility. Another is an independently warranted strict hierarchy of ultimate control which reciprocal control would violate. Neither is merely IP, essential power possession, or factive causal explanation. Perfect wisdom may constrain common admissibility, so disagreement cannot simply be stipulated to obtain the desired contradiction.

### An infinite description is not yet an infinite causal descent

The successive P1, P2, P3, and so on name powers by increasingly nested targets. No argument in the passage shows that each requires a numerically new actual exercise, a new donated capacity, or an independently prior act of disabling a peer. At w1 the odd-level successes belong to A; at w2 the even-level successes belong to B. The opposite peer's relevant successes are absent in each case.

One actual activity can have indefinitely many true achievement descriptions. It may explain multiple nonexercise facts at once. This does not establish that the activities in question really have such a common basis; it blocks the inference from infinitely repeatable descriptions to a vicious chain without inspecting the dependence relation.

To invoke the strong, independently plausible ban on vicious efficient circles, the defender must show an actual same-respect productive dependence: A's relevant adequacy is waiting on a contribution whose adequacy is in turn waiting on A's. To invoke explanatory well-foundedness instead, it must identify a genuinely descending sequence in that order. Alternating which world contains an exercise does not itself produce either relation. The critique therefore does not rely on licensing an unexplained simultaneous causal loop.

### Reduced alternating-preemption control

Consider two fixed necessary subjects A and B, with their standing conditional capacities held stable across w1 and w2. At w1, A's successful activity a produces p and preempts B's opposing successful activity b. At w2, B's activity b produces ¬p and preempts a. This specifies actual achievements and causal inhibition; it does not posit simultaneously contradictory wise choices.

At w1, a therefore both produces p and prevents B from preventing a. At w2, b both produces ¬p and prevents A from preventing b. Repeating the description adds further prevention targets. Even if powers are individuated finely enough that these are different power descriptions, their realizations can remain the same a and b, supported by the same two standing capacities. There is no requirement in this reduced signature for a new prerequisite or a prior donation at each level.

The capacities here are conditional: each succeeds in a suitable preemption context, not against an already fixed successful opposing exercise. IP can be respected by counting available preparatory sequences among indirect abilities; there is no acquisition event in the displayed control. This neither establishes nor tests the full metaphysical IP thesis. Its limited point is that stable capacities, actual inhibition, and repeated prevention descriptions do not alone yield a vicious regress.

**What this control does not preserve:** it supplies no complete explanation of which preemptive activity occurs, every peer choice, the opportunity/timing facts, or all explanation-status facts. It therefore does not authenticate individual creator coverage. Nor does it establish perfected original efficacy, full necessary voluntary concordance, or unrestricted power robustness. Adding those labels would not repair the omissions. This is a reduced control for the regress inference, not a premise-preserving two-creator countermodel.

### The compressed argument retains the same demand

Pruss also considers A's explanation of the total fact that B exercises none of its preventative powers. It is reasonable, under an inclusive power taxonomy, to package A's complete relevant activity as one composite power P; there is no need to insist on finitely many primitive powers. But achieving that complete nonexercise pattern in one context is not yet a uniform control strategy against every changed peer determination.

If a counterpower Q would prevent P, and P would prevent Q, the resulting successful exercises exclude one another. Without an additional robustness/common-admissibility or strict-priority premise, mutual exclusion is not absurdity. Quantifying over all preventative powers in the actual explanation does not supply independent co-exercisability of them.

The origin HTML contains awkward indexing and polarity wording in the compressed and same-agent parenthetical passages. The audit grants the natural intended repairs. No criticism depends on a typo.

## 6 Total contingency and the created-order target

The argument's totality is modal, not a taxonomy of created substances. A contingent truth about an uncreated necessary agent's act, power possession, or explanation relation falls within it. Inclusion does not imply that the act is externally received, that its bearer begins, or that contingency entails creation.

Accordingly, restriction to the created field removes the immediate right to invoke creator coverage for:

1. the necessary agent's own contingent possession of a power;
2. q, a contingent fact about another agent's explanatory activity;
3. each contingent nonexercise of the iterated powers; and
4. the total nonexercise fact in the compressed argument.

This is related to the closure issue in `modal-psr-bridge/ACTUALISATION_AUDIT.md`, but it is not the same proof. Actualisation needs truth-inclusion of an explanation-status negative. The present proof needs **explanatory coverage and power consequences** for the generated facts. Its failed transport should not be charged to the earlier actualisation inference.

### The strongest restricted-target repair

Full coverage of every contingent truth is stronger than the local uniqueness reasoning strictly needs. A restricted argument could separately establish explanatory coverage of the particular power-possession and nonexercise facts used in its construction. For instance, explanation of a created p might account for q through factivity and incompatibility. A suitably warranted causal/explanatory closure rule could therefore recover some omitted facts.

That is a possible repair, not an automatic entailment of the restricted target. Explaining a proposition, accounting for its logical consequences, causing a negative meta-fact, and controlling another agent's act are different operations. Restoring the first q by such a route would not, by itself, restore essential-power possession or the strong prevention contradiction. The local repair must specify and defend each required closure.

Pruss himself proposes exempting contingent creatures' free choices while retaining their prerequisites and facts independent of those choices, at lines 4230–4239. This does not automatically exempt the free acts of a putative necessary peer. It also does not prove that every selected p and every generated omission is independent of creaturely choices; a necessary agent might act in response to them. The suggested repair needs an eligible distinguishing proposition and closure for the facts actually used. Pruss presents it as a proposed adjustment, not a completed derivation.

## 7 Collective explanation and the exact endpoint

The text explicitly recognizes that a cosmological argument may yield only collective explanation. Allowing a collective as the creator may at most recover the same collective across worlds, conditional on the preceding inferences. Numerical unity of its members does not follow from numerical unity of that collective.

The interaction and simplicity considerations merit consideration, but familiar committee limitations do not establish limitations in every necessarily concordant perfected plurality. Conversely, stipulating a harmonious collective does not establish its independent explanatory adequacy. The retained candidate must still explain why the proposed original-peer mode is eligible and sufficiently grounded. This branch returns to that substantive issue rather than resolving it by changing the noun for its bearer.

Even a valid proof of one creator would not show that only one necessary being exists. A necessary noncreator whose existence and relevant features are necessary falls outside the contingent-truth demand. A contingent act might be covered without the agent's necessary existence being produced. “Unique total creator,” “unique necessary existent,” and “unique unreceived original bearer” are therefore different conclusions.

The final omnipotence claim also needs its stated interpretation. If one and the same creator operates in every world and its powers are transported globally, it has the corresponding power for every eligible contingent outcome instantiated somewhere in W. This is a substantive range result conditional on the earlier steps. It does not automatically give control while arbitrarily holding incompatible peer determinations fixed, identify every modal invariant property with a constitutive essence, or establish the original-bearer reception predicates.

## 8 What a counterinterpretation would have to preserve

There are three different standards:

**Local inference counterinterpretation.** Typed acquisition, acyclic maintenance, reason-mediated voluntary nonexercise, and mutually exclusive successful preventative capacities show why the displayed transports are not rules of mere logic or ordinary causal vocabulary. These are non-question-begging discriminators because their modest antecedents do not stipulate multiple perfect creators.

**Full weak-signature interpretation.** One could assign two necessary subjects world-alternating activity, broad explanatory coverage, and essential closed power sets. Merely declaring every true proposition explained, however, would not warrant the intended causal and personal explanation relation. No such assignment is promoted here into a philosophical result, and no trivial formal wrapper was made.

**Full metaphysical rival.** To preserve the substantive source premise, a plural interpretation would have to explain all contingent truths in each world through an eligible individual's activity, including the other subject's actual choices and all relevant explanation-status truths, while independently vindicating the powers and explanatory standards it uses. The retained collective original-support model and bare necessary covariance have not done this. Denying individual full coverage instead is a challenge to applicability, not a countermodel to that premise.

Thus the honest verdict is not that a perfect pair satisfying all source commitments has been exhibited. It is that the argument has not established the additional power/explanation and anti-regress principles needed to exclude every such pair. One cannot repair that gap by citing the absence of a fully developed rival; nor can the rival evade the real total-coverage demand by silently replacing it with created-order sufficiency.

## 9 Positive credit and the T20 boundary

The argument earns four positive credits:

1. It exposes a genuine consequence of individual total explanation: a necessary peer's contingent nonexercise is not exempt merely because the peer itself exists necessarily.
2. Its discriminating q construction correctly makes explanatory activity itself part of the modal comparison, and its cross-world identities are explicitly de re.
3. IP motivates closure of genuine indirect ability; no ultimate power bootstrap is independently intelligible when the explanandum really is the complete same-respect power.
4. Conditional on successful uniqueness and power transfer, the common creator has a broad global range of causal ability. This is more than a creator of one local outcome.

The warranted limitations are equally specific. IP does not erase power types or possession indices. Explanation of abstention does not alone establish sovereign inhibition. Alternating preventative capacities do not prove common successful exercise, strict dominance, or a vicious actual dependence chain. The collective qualification leaves membership unity open. Restriction to the received created field requires new local coverage warrants.

The inspected `OriginalBearerBridge.lean` supplies no shortcuts here. Its Completion concerns an actual unreceived intrinsic resource relevant to a received target; Realisation concerns that resource's bearer; SupportTransport and ContingencyNeed concern receipt. None identifies causal explanation with robust prevention, derives essential powers from IP, or turns a collective into one individual. Conversely, the present criticisms do not refute those conditional theorems or the independently argued actual-efficacy/perfection considerations.

**Stopping result:** Pruss's variant is a serious, directly relevant total-explanation challenge. It does not yet provide a separately warranted uniqueness proof applicable to the retained created-order and necessarily concordant rival. The open work is now precisely an explanatory-control and same-respect power argument, rather than another generic assertion that two agents might disagree. No new campaign or formalization is needed to establish this bounded finding.
