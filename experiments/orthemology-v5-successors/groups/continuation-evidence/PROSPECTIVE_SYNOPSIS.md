# Bounded synopsis of the unrun handoff study

Status: **NOT_RUN**. This is an authored synopsis of the supplied five-page `PROSPECTIVE_VALIDATION_SPECIFICATION_v5.pdf`, original SHA-256 `32c402281880ea6d8e5d8bde79fe76591fd69f713eef13e66fc7dcbf9f8bb4e9`. All five original pages were inspected. The PDF remains private custody; this synopsis is not a full literal conversion, preregistration, participant experiment, or specialist validation. The original specialist questions remain preparation only, with no invitation or response.

The proposed service is a three-person diagnostic cell with named roles, work queue, ledger, record store and authorised consultation. Two people remain while an incoming interpreter replaces an actually departing member. An experimenter arranges the handoff; the design does not test self-initiated renewal or numerical personal identity. Baseline-qualified cells would be randomly assigned in concealed blocks to a 2-by-2 contrast of immediate record access and interpretive support. The primary comparison is both supports versus neither. An unchanged-membership arm is absent, so the design does not isolate the cost of handoff itself.

The primary endpoint is timely, warranted completion by the whole continuing cell: a real personnel transition, a warranted determination for a newly resolvable case, and warranted uncertainty for an unresolved case. Incoming-role performance is scored separately. A retained colleague may compensate well enough for the whole cell to succeed while the new interpreter fails. The evidence supporting a judgement and the discharge of the service obligation are also separate: deferring a resolvable case can merit caution without counting as completed service.

The fictional task uses $q=L-d$ with threshold 80. For example, $L=100$ and $d\in[23,25]$ warrants below-threshold, $d\in[17,19]$ warrants above-threshold, and $d\in[18,22]$ warrants uncertainty. The same $[17,19]$ range with $L=90$ instead warrants below-threshold. The task thereby tests whether the relevant frame and record are applied, rather than whether a label is repeated. Warranted substitutes and memory may count; producing an administrative document is not itself a constitutive success requirement. New counterbalanced cases change consequential inputs, not just names.

The original deadline is 20 minutes. Only a ten-minute extension authorised before expiry for an access fault, without seeing outcomes, may count as a revised contract. Original-deadline performance, revised-contract performance and later recovery remain different observations. Later reacquisition occurs only after sealing the initial endpoint and is tested on a matched fresh case. It cannot retroactively establish uninterrupted service.

The access manipulation distinguishes immediate direct reading of the versioned $L$ record from institutional retention without the incoming role's initial direct permission. Authorised requests can later restore access in every arm; compensation through another legitimate route is an outcome, not automatically contamination. Interpretive support is item-free frame training compared with matched navigation, with the same ordinary manual available. Staffing and $L$ are held fixed, and new cases are released after the logged departure. There is no pre-outcome teach-back cue. Whether the incoming interpreter performs a consequential step or is bypassed is recorded separately.

Each episode would bind pre-transfer records, roles and authority, actual departure, contract versions, a recipient/content/time matrix, request traces, deliberation certificates, new-case decisions and times, and later repair tests. A late explanation needs corroboration before it supports earlier understanding. Missing traces can leave an endpoint unknown rather than demonstrate incapacity. The proposed outcome vector records original and revised completion, role transfer, compensation, appropriate uncertainty, unsupported decisions, wrong frame/version, pending work, timeout, technical failure and recovery. Two adjudicators would be masked where possible; agreement would not establish construct validity. Certificates may themselves alter behaviour, so reactivity is part of the design's limitation.

All assigned, baseline-qualified cells remain in the denominator. The design leaves sample size, power model and minimum meaningful difference unspecified; it is not an executable preregistration. Proposed group-level uncertainty and worst-case missing-outcome bounds do not authorise receipt-conditioned causal mediation analysis. A precise null would limit the incremental support packages in this toy service; a wide interval would be inconclusive. Neither would resolve all organisational continuity or identity questions. The source expressly stops before recruitment, an experiment, external contact, a new proof, or novelty endorsement.
