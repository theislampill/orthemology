## Electronic pages 37–38 (bounded-reflective-reread)

Reread fixes three distinctions. First, a bestowed cause can possess efficacy while its causal character is removable, opposed or condition-dependent. Second, being the true original giver includes bestowing the causal capacity and opportunity, rather than competing with the secondary giver for one exclusive deed. Third, original giving does not license inferring moral approval from material success. This model makes full self-sufficiency a claim about the origin of all required efficacy, not just unused standing power. Yet applying it to every possible original demands an independent reason why the framework is exhaustive, rather than merely the fact that observed humans are dependent. The source's argument is preserved positively without treating an integrated description as deductive uniqueness.
