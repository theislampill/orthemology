## Main constructive test

The prior particular-determination report requires an independently described relevant feature and its actual determining role at a stated target. It should be tested at that stated target, rather than silently escalated to an explanation of the entire divine history.

The ship narrative can supply a local, contrastive means explanation: a threat to sound ships, preserving the owners' use, and the deliberate defect as a means. Its actual intentional basing is supplied by the narrated explanatory attribution under the retained authenticated-disclosure premise; an invented model in which a defect merely happens to help would not establish that basing. It does not disclose why this world, this king, every exact microparameter, or no alternative history was selected.

The wall narrative has the further explicit attribution of the Lord's willing that the children reach maturity and recover their treasure. The derivative wall repair, the purpose, and original willing must remain distinct. This can support a local original-scope purposive claim, without converting a human deed into a divine act-token or identifying a complete intrinsic volition inventory.

An alternative that avoids the king, reveals the treasure otherwise, or changes the whole background does not negate the narrower claim that the narrated act served the stated purpose in the narrated circumstances. It would matter to a unique-optimality or ultimate-history claim. Those stronger claims are not obtained here. Conversely, acknowledging a suitable local means does not establish that it was the sole possible fitting means.

## Source controls discovered in the continuous context

10:478 distinguishes correctness as compliant, qualified inquiry from knowing the particular truth. At479 the Khidr case is explicitly placed under particular circumstances known through specific evidence, rather than an exception to the standing divine norm. This provides a source-level basis for revising an application when previously unknown relevant facts are disclosed without redefining the norm. It does not authorise any present claimant's private intuition or harmful act.

10:473–477 rejects both blanket denial and blanket acceptance of inspiration, and distinguishes inaccessible articulation from absence of a ground. The source conditions its evidential appraisal; the existence or sincerity of a feeling is not itself its validation.

10:531–534 distinguishes what is intended by a command from prerequisites forced by its realisation. The source refuses automatically adding sanctions for every omitted prerequisite. This is a potentially relevant guard on transporting logical dependency counts into normative burden; no actual repository defect or new transport theorem is asserted here.

10:536–547 distinguishes unknown requirements, qualified mistaken inquiry, actual truth, good purpose and bad purpose. At546–547, the actor and critic can each be sincerely mistaken; either or both can also be culpable. These alternatives need evidence in application. The report must not infer hidden inner states from mere disagreement or behavioural outcome.

10:500 explicitly assesses extraordinary results through their causes and their ends. It supplies source support for preserving pathway as well as output, not independent empirical validation of a noetic implementation.
