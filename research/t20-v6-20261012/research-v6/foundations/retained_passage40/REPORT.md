## 2. Necessity and productive completeness are stronger than a generic regress ban

Dar3 items487–489 (queue17/5/0; printed100–101) distinguish intrinsic possible existence from existence actually received through a sufficient provider. A collection does not become an unreceived provider merely by being infinite or by having no designated first temporal member. This is consistent with FND-004/FND-006's positive productive-adequacy route and does not substitute a finite-time cosmology for it.

The author's qualification at163–166 (0a987cf8, queue1) is important: a route can be sound as a route to a Necessary Being while its bare conclusion does not yet distinguish the actual Creator of the world. That narrower historical target is not the later whole-field actual-adequacy argument. Likewise,159–162 (3f007997, queue11) retains a valid anti-regress proof while denying that every person's knowledge of a Creator must wait for that particular proof. Formal completion and actual basing remain distinct.

Dar3:145–147 (db82c3ca, queue37) expressly grants that cooperation can make independently supplied participants more effective than each alone. Thus the stronger argument is not the invalid transfer from weak parts to a weak whole. It targets an entire field whose existence and productive actuality are all received and therefore supplies no unreceived basis itself. That distinction directly supports the current source-owned completeness premise and explains why an endpoint-sufficient cooperative toy does not automatically defeat it.

Dar3:144 and421–424 (c390dedc/258f814e/89212ebc) permit intrinsic concomitance and distinguish it from prior productive circularity. Essence and attributes need not be an essence plus an external donor. The composition label alone proves no external composer. ## 3. Cooperation, concordance and one numerical production

The received Dar9 chain337–367 (queue29,39,2,8,26,28,4,19,36,38) and Minhaj3:304–336 (queue31,20,27) contain actual positive arguments, not merely later summaries. They repeatedly grant real creaturely efficacy and distinguish a participant's own act, its particular contribution, the external joint product, receptive conditions, and removal of impediments. This directly excludes a causal-nullity reconstruction.

They also retain the stronger adversary. Dar016 L715–717 expressly considers each alleged source having intrinsic, unborrowed ability rather than simply deriving its ability from the other. The subsequent argument appeals to complete original efficacy, actual numerical production and the connected received field. Reading only the rejection of reciprocal ability-donation would therefore miss the later branch.

The nonduplication claim is about coactive original whole production of the same numerical effect in the same productive respect. A nonacting source's capacity, two different contributions, a counterfactual alternative production, and collective support of distinct effects are different cases. The same texts distinguish choosing not to exercise an available contrary from being unable to exercise because another producer prevents or supplies the act. This is the source context for FND-009/FND-010 and the later eligibility appraisal, not a newly established universal theorem about every formal cooperation relation.

The historical argument sometimes uses rulers, cooks, jurists or ordinary partners to illustrate a common ground. Those examples cannot alone establish that every ordinary coordinated system requires a single commanding person, or that every common normative requirement is itself an additional efficient producer. The substantive transport remains the stated complete-original-resource scope. The current affirmative actual-field appraisal supplies its separately examined application; the examples do not replace it or reset it.

Attribution changes matter. Dar9:337 begins a quotation of Ibn Rushd. The agreement/alternation criticism at353–355 is first reported and then expressly answered. The author explicitly rejects the alleged conclusion that unity is known only from revelation. Removing the quotation boundaries would invert the source's verdict. Historical claims about all human cultures and several physical examples are preserved as source assertions, without fresh universal historiographical or scientific validation.
