# Causation, content and rational warrant

8 October 2026. A bounded successor assessment. The complete argument-from-reason chapter has been read; this is not whole-book credit, repository integration or T20 closure. The public chapter summary is separately limited to 200 words. The reasoning below is an independent assessment rather than a substitute digest.

## Judgement

The chapter raises a genuine requirement: a sound transition between symbols is not yet a person's justified acceptance of a conclusion because they understand its grounds. The computer reply must therefore be taken seriously. A proof checker is not an independent demonstration that intrinsic intentionality, conscious understanding, rational basing and proper function arise within a wholly nonmental reality.

A narrower incompatibility is false. Local causal determination does not prevent a mechanism from implementing form-sensitive, truth-preserving transitions under a fixed interpretation. The new finite model establishes that restricted point and exhibits a faulty alternative that fails under the same interpretation. It does not satisfy all the antecedents of the strongest anti-naturalist target.

The stronger exclusion remains **not independently established by this checked argument**. This is a substantive judgement, not merely a complaint that the position lacks a formal theorem. The crucial case against realized content relies on a disputed division between purely structural and merely conventional significance, and on a slide from a contingent token's production to the necessity of the logical relation represented. The evolutionary route separately needs a grounded reliability and defeat argument. None is supplied by calling the basic causes non-rational, by the possibility of useful false belief, or by the absence of a complete currently available scientific explanation.

This result does not vindicate metaphysical naturalism or defeat theism. It does not withdraw the programme's retained Creator, source-owned veracity, common-source or recognition appraisals. It identifies the precise work that an additional argument from reason would have to perform before receiving independent evidential credit.

## Six distinct questions

1. **Causal production:** What conditions actually produced this token assent or output? A causal contribution may be only an occasion, such as drawing attention to a problem.
2. **Logical entailment:** Does the conclusion follow from the premises? This relation can hold even when no one reasons, or when someone reaches the conclusion badly.
3. **Reason responsiveness:** Does the actual route respond appropriately to the relevant grounds or logical form? Mere dependence on two input flags is insufficient; a form-sensitive implementation is still weaker than understanding their significance.
4. **Intentional content:** What fixes what the states are about? A supplied interpretation is not an account of original intentionality.
5. **Semantic correctness:** Given those contents and an actual situation, are the premises and conclusion true? A valid inference can have false premises.
6. **Warrant and rational basing:** Is this the subject's competent, undefeated, appropriately truth-connected basis for belief? A theorem, a true output or a beneficial effect does not answer that alone.

The fitrah warranting-basis clarification already separates a defective argument's causal occasion from an adequate warranting proof. The distinction works here too. Neither a natural causal account nor a divine common origin automatically classifies every token transition as warranted or unwarranted. Specific truth-connected operation matters.

## The strongest exclusion route

A charitable reconstruction has this shape:

- Rational inference requires states with genuine propositional content and a suitable causal role for apprehended grounds.
- The envisaged naturalism places all causal work in a nonpurposive physical basis and its organization.
- Such a basis cannot realize original content or causally efficacious apprehension of a rational relation; any added semantic description is only an interpreter's assignment.
- Therefore that naturalism cannot contain the rational inference its reasoned defence presupposes.

The third premise is the important bridge. If established, the argument is more than the genetic fallacy of dismissing a belief because it has a physical history. A genuinely demonstrated account on which content does no relevant work could undercut a claim to believe on those grounds.

But causal closure alone does not establish this premise. If a mental property is realized by an organized physical/relational property, its contribution need not be a second independent push competing with the first. That is a candidate explanation requiring defence, not a completed naturalization. Conversely, merely uttering “realization” or “emergence” does not make the candidate adequate.

The targeted Reppert paper expressly sees the danger of the simplistic argument and distinguishes compatible explanatory levels. Its stronger objection therefore deserves a stronger answer than an AND gate. The useful objection to its proposed dichotomy is precise: independence from one particular material is not independence from all causal organization. A state playing a role in an implemented mechanism is constrained by transitions, connections and relations, even when different materials can implement it. That point does not establish intrinsic content; it defeats the inference that multiple realizability itself makes the role arbitrary convention. A history- and environment-sensitive content account would need examination on its actual commitments rather than exclusion by the word “physical.”

Convention itself also needs careful scope. An isolated object's honorific history may make no difference to one collision, while a convention embedded in agents and causal practices can explain other transactions. Holding every fact about the vehicle and its surrounding practice fixed while changing only an observer's label is not an intervention on such a relational property. This does not prove that social convention explains original thought. It explains why a narrow irrelevance example cannot by itself exclude all relational realization.

## Necessity belongs to the entailment

The condition that true A and true A→C cannot coexist with false C is different from the claim that a particular brain or computer must always execute the corresponding transition. A contingent, fallible mechanism can implement a sound rule when functioning appropriately. Failure on another occasion does not make the entailment contingent, and the necessity of the entailment does not make the token mechanism infallible.

The new model is useful exactly here. Under a fixed interpretation it distinguishes a matching-rule mechanism from an acceptance-only mutant. The former passes every declared truth-preservation case; the latter accepts not-p from true p and true q→not-p when q is false. The contrast concerns a fixed inferential relation, not arbitrary labels moved around to make one implementation look successful.

What remains unanswered is how an actual subject grasps the relation as necessary. The model neither supplies nor eliminates that grasp. The move from “a physical description does not itself state a law's necessary validity” to “a physical subject cannot apprehend it” needs an additional account of knowledge and realization. It does not follow merely from the grammar of a lower-level description.

Logical laws and formula types are not automatically additional extramental particulars, much less independently necessary bearers. A subject may reason about a universal relation without that relation being another independent producer. Nothing in this assessment transports the mathematical type's generality into an ontology of competing originals.

## What the implementation actually contributes

MODEL_AND_TRANSPORT.md gives the exact equations, source-error control and claim ceiling. Its independent pre-construction review rejected the weaker proposed acceptance-bit test. The final implementation matches encoded antecedents, varies formula registers under a fixed codebook, and checks compositional truth preservation for all 512 packets and four valuations. The reported 2,048 cases are finite checks, not probability estimates or evidence that all human faculties are reliable.

Operational closure is restricted to one update after initial inputs are fixed. Comparing alternative initial packets does not inject an external cause midway through that run. Historical dependence on a programmer and interpretive dependence on a semantic map remain distinct, explicit dependencies. Nothing here establishes the metaphysical self-sufficiency of the whole natural world.

Thus the computer objection survives against the ambitious use of the example. The test does not refute the strongest chapter claim. It does block one possible shortcut: formal appropriateness and local physical determination are not contradictories simply because one description uses logical vocabulary and the other does not.

## What the inherited kernel check does and does not add

The inherited `RouteProbability.endpoint_law_identification` theorem proves an actual mathematical conditional about its declared finite probability model: equality of its generated endpoint laws at the specified profiles implies equality of its anonymous route histograms. It is not fairly described as a test of meaningless marks. The model's definitions, normalized laws, algebra and proof dependencies have genuine mathematical content under their specified interpretation.

A fresh non-mutating Lean 4.19.0 `--trust=0` check of the imported verification file succeeded. Both source manifests also passed. This task did not rebuild the entire dependency tree or rerun the inherited mutation suite; its prior receipts remain prior evidence.

The fresh result still does not establish that a physical target instantiates the model's productive occurrences, independence, calibration, admissible interventions or complete catalogue. It does not prove that the human reading the result understands it, is warranted in a metaphysical application, or has reliable faculties under every proposed genealogy. Those are different bridges. A successful kernel run can assist a competent person's knowledge without being an independently original knower.

The external boundary therefore cuts both ways: the checker is stronger than accidental true output, but weaker than a complete account of semantics and warrant. Its existence cannot alone settle the strong argument from reason.

## Evolution, useful error and defeat

The possibility of adaptive false content is important. It refutes an automatic inference from usefulness to truth. It does not establish that most beliefs are false, that reliable faculties are improbable given a particular evolutionary account, or that an accepted naturalism supplies an undefeated global defeater.

No proposed probability should be manufactured by enumerating freely assigned semantic labels. A reliability claim needs its content-fixing rules, relevant domain, environment distribution, dependency assumptions and conditioning information specified. Counting interpretations in this model supplies none of those. A system can be reliable in practical discriminations yet unreliable in an abstract domain; neither direction of transfer is automatic.

The targeted Balfour reading contains both a probability step and a challenge to extending practical selection into comprehensive knowledge. The targeted Plantinga texts make the epistemic step explicit: the intended conclusion concerns rational acceptance and defeaters, not simply the falsity of naturalism and evolution. The exact book pages cited by Ameri were not all directly verified, and the receipt says so. Shared lineage adds no independent confirmation.

There is a separate consciousness issue. Selection of an already available useful capacity does not by itself explain that capacity's first emergence. The chapter is right to reject that as a complete explanatory answer. But failure of that answer is not a proof that no nonmental organization could realize consciousness. Establishing impossibility requires the stronger exclusion argument, while establishing an actual origin requires more than an abstract compatibility model. This assessment supplies neither.

## Circularity and the common standard

The chapter's distinction between epistemic order and ontological dependence prevents an easy objection: it need not demand a completed proof of God before any reasoning may be rational. An explanation of why a faculty is reliable is different from the order in which a person can justifiably rely on it.

Nor should every use of reason to examine reason count as a special defeater for one worldview. Some basic reliance is unavoidable. A particular origin account can still undercut confidence if it supplies a substantive reason to expect error or content-insensitivity; global circularity slogans cannot replace that demonstration. Equally, a sophisticated origin story does not remove already identified local errors.

Consistent with warrant-standard-review, lack of a neutral formal derivation or universal exclusion is not automatically a reason to withhold all positive philosophical judgement. Here the reason to withhold the *additional no-go conclusion* is specific: the proposed structural/conventional exclusion and token/necessity inference do not close the offered alternatives, and the reliability argument's measure and defeat bridge remain unsettled. This does not make naturalism equally credible, nor erase existing positive reasons for a rational Creator under the programme's adopted background.

A rational, competent and truth-directed source could intelligibly explain truth-capable creatures. Moving from that intelligibility to the unique or actual source, or from common origin to the warrant of this token belief, requires the appropriate independent commitments. The fitrah common-origin control is preserved: even a true conclusion and one good original source do not automatically turn every route to it into knowledge.

## Net contribution

Established here:

- Correct full-chapter scope and source locator, with stronger primary bridges identified.
- A checked difference between merely input-sensitive and form-sensitive local implementation under a fixed semantics.
- A fixed-content bad-source control showing why valid execution does not authenticate premises.
- An exact obstruction to importing the implementation as a proof of autonomous intentionality or warrant.
- A failed direct transport from common-original nonduplication to mind/brain explanatory exclusion, detailed separately.

Not established: a naturalistic theory of mind, an anti-naturalist impossibility theorem, a new independent proof of theism, actual human factive recognition, whole-book coverage or programme closure. The next discriminating philosophical step is a defended account of original content and rational basing, assessed against the strongest realization/exclusion argument. Another successful arithmetic run would not answer that question.
