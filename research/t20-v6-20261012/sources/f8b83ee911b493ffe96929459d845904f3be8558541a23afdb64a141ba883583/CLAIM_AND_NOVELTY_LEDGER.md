# Necessary source probe and exact increment

## Research question

Can the primary argument against incomplete determination supply actual warrant for the move from a particular original actual bearer to that bearer's unconditional existence? The starting point is the accepted Seventh conclusion, not an earlier unrepaired version.

## What the test strengthens

The test grants, conditionally, original productive completion and identification of one particular bearer. It also grants complete determinate production throughout the specified whole actual origin. It does not substitute endpoint sufficiency. The remaining critic may therefore accept the primary's stronger determination claim in its received-production domain while disputing the transfer to whole original existence.

## What the new evidence changes

Newly probed direct primary passages supply a more exact anti-arbitrariness rationale and controls on what an unreceived or possible existent means. The article tests their intended existential force rather than reducing them to an objection against self-production. It also tests the neighbouring argument which deliberately weakens the necessitation assumption. That weaker argument returns to productive realisation and cannot carry the discarded modal force by nomenclature alone.

## What was already established

The difference between actual independence and intrinsic existential sufficiency; the permission for direct particular recognition; the no-self-origination objection; the possibility of active derivative powers; the strong same-target uniqueness result; the separation of complete actual determination from bare source existence; and the symmetric burden on positive modal assertions are all inherited. This lane does not claim them as Eighth advances.

## Exact finding

No independently warranted intrinsic nature or universal realisation principle closes N2/N3 in this probe. The new result is a source-supported boundary after a stronger affirmative test. The proposed brute or noncausal rival is not thereby established as actual or metaphysically possible. A critic's narrower objection to the inference remains separate from that positive ontology.

## Reader facing scope

Use the article as a developed philosophical assessment of one attempted bridge. Do not cite it as proof of a necessary being, as a general disproof of theism, as certification of a rival cosmos, as a temporal first-event argument, or as an operational source of truthful revelation or repair authority. Productive completion, particular identification, existential adequacy, and unconditional sufficiency remain separate.
