# Instantiation, obtaining, and the exact productive-bearer premise

8 October 2026. Bounded successor to the completed qualitative-ontology and Arabic-source reports. T20 remains ACTIVE. This adjudication adds no primary acquisition, countermodel, formal theorem, integration, or closure. Earlier checkpoints are preserved.

## Result

The completed comparison supplies **no source-backed counterexample to restricted Realisation**. The T20 interface explicitly interprets its resource tokens as concrete intrinsic instantiations in the same existential respect, while its bearer type is not restricted to primitive material individuals. Dasgupta's opposition to primitive material individuality therefore does not contradict that premise. Derived concrete bearers can satisfy it, and his retained universals and states of affairs cannot be excluded from every possible bearer interpretation merely because his ontology is called generalist.

The substantive pressure is on the **exhaustive application of productive Completion**, or on extending the resource domain to qualitative obtaining while retaining Realisation and SupportTransport without a new argument. The published qualitative proposal has independent ontological motivation. Neither it nor the held Arabic arguments establish that every adequate existential explanation must furnish the interface's actual unreceived concrete resource. Nor do these sources establish that an autonomous qualitative explanation of nonempty actuality actually succeeds.

The remaining disagreement is principally about **classification and direction of dependence**, not simply whether concrete instances exist. Generalism can preserve ordinary existential truths while denying that the instances are fundamental constituents or prior grounds of the obtaining whole. The Arabic nonpriority argument works when a proposed antecedent ground is already the same bearer's inhering quality. Extending it to the generalist whole requires the contested premise that the whole has just that instance-dependent ontology. Dasgupta's conceptual-versus-metaphysical priority reply blocks an immediate inference from object-based teaching or model semantics to that conclusion; it does not complete a proof of metaphysical independence.

The autonomy of obtaining remains a separate unresolved question. The 2009 ontology does not supply the existence-grounding essence contemplated in the 2016 footnote. A primitive obtaining predicate supplies no such warrant merely by being primitive.

## The exact distinction the comparison resolves

Three propositions must be distinguished:

1. An obtaining qualitative assertion entails, or can preserve as true, some ordinary assertion that concrete instances exist.
2. Those instances are fundamental constituents or ontologically prior grounds of the qualitative obtaining.
3. Every adequate explanation of received actuality includes an actual unreceived concrete intrinsic resource relevant to it.

The first does not establish the second. Neither establishes the third. Conversely, rejecting primitive individuality does not by itself reject the first or deny that actual powers have bearers.

This distinction is source-supported. Dasgupta explicitly recognizes the alternative redundancy objection at printed p. 41: an individualist may claim that nontrivial general facts entail individuals even though changing primitive identities makes no further dynamical difference. At pp. 54–55 he leaves several treatments of ordinary individual claims open, including reduction and equivalent-content realism. At p. 66 he distinguishes the conceptual use of an individual-domain presentation from the metaphysical priority he proposes. It would misstate his argument to infer either that every individual assertion is false or that his use of ordinary model elements concedes fundamental individuals.

It would likewise overstate the Arabic argument to treat every denial of antecedent property-instance constitution as a proof that no qualitative-first account exists. The relevant question is what the purported qualitative fact is, and why its dependence runs in the direction being asserted.

## What the strongest held Arabic argument establishes

Ṣafadiyya S001198–215 and S003132–139 provide a substantive dilemma for treating a particular human as constituted by antecedent animality and rationality. If these are already inhering qualities of that human, their role requires the subject whose constitution they are supposed to explain. If they are independently existing substances, the account falsely multiplies the subjects in the ordinary case under discussion. S003645–658 connects this to the distinction between a middle term in inference and an external constitutive or causal intermediary. This is a reasoned challenge to projecting the composition of a definition into the composition of an actual thing.

The argument supports a conditional nonpriority result: **a quality-instance already individuated as inhering in this bearer cannot simply be promoted into an antecedent autonomous ground of that same bearer's whole existence**. It does not rest solely on the observation that people can choose different words for a definition. The dependency of the already-inhering quality is doing substantive work.

Generalism does not offer that specific object. It proposes fundamental obtaining states composed of universals, without primitive material individuals among their constituents. Calling these states inhering qualities of particular bearers would supply the disputed classification rather than derive it. Likewise, the Arabic rejection of one numerically common external humanity does not automatically refute every highly structured qualitative fact. The completed source report correctly distinguishes mental universals, particular instances, and the modern fact proposal.

Akmaliyya120–134 supplies a bearer principle for attributes and insists that actual independently existing subjects are not devoid of qualities. It also expressly distinguishes necessary concomitance from efficient making. Thus even an accepted subject–attribute interpretation would not, without further premises, turn the bearer into an efficient producer of the entire dependent field. Neither a purely mental law-token nor the act of entertaining it should be confused with the truth or alleged noncognitive ground that the strongest rival proposes.

These arguments retain their positive force within their stated categories. No new independent exhaustion argument emerged from the completed return establishing that every obtaining qualitative fact belongs to those categories with the relevant dependence direction. Repeating the source's general ontological commitment does not add another independent reason for that particular extension.

## What Dasgupta's answer establishes

The 2009 case is more substantial than eliminating variables. It combines physical symmetry and redundancy considerations, a defeasible parsimony preference, an account preserving general content and implication, and replies to objections concerning causation, complexity, and ordinary thought. A rival need not provide an ontology-neutral demonstration to earn serious comparative standing. The same evidential standard applies to the affirmative subject–attribute ontology.

Its target remains primitive **material** individuals. The restriction is explicit at p. 37; nonmaterial reality is deferred. Its fundamental ontology retains universals and obtaining states, and its local-causation reply preserves causal relevance among ordinary general facts. This is not an argument that nothing determinate exists, that every actual power is bearerless, or that no immaterial original could exist.

The appendix's reply is also real but bounded. At p. 66 the author argues that learning qualitative primitives through individual-based semantics need not make the individuals metaphysically prior, using the analogy of learning relative velocity through an absolute-velocity conception. The reply defeats a quick semantic-presupposition objection. The author explicitly leaves a full defense of the strategy outside that discussion. Neither the analogy nor the bidirectional translation alone proves that the proposed qualitative base can obtain independently of every form of determinate realization.

The material ontology and the Arabic ontology consequently meet at a disputed metaphysical premise. Both provide positive arguments; neither read argument decides the other's strongest formulation simply by its own terminology. This is an evidential obstruction to the specific inference under examination, not a declaration that all considerations on the two sides have equal weight.

## Where the T20 interface actually engages

The unchanged `OriginalBearerBridge.lean` declares the interpretation and separates the premises. Its logical consequences remain valid at that interpretation.

**Restricted Realisation.** Every actual resource has an actual bearer in which it is intrinsic. When resources retain their declared interpretation as concrete intrinsic instantiations, generalism's denial of primitive material individuals supplies no counterexample. A derived bearer need not be a fundamental individual. This does not mechanically prove the premise's truth for all intended cases; it identifies why this source does not defeat it.

**Completion.** Every received target has an actual relevant unreceived resource. The controversy is whether a genuinely adequate qualitative explanation, if available, would have to satisfy this productive requirement. Completion does not quantify over explanations in the code; the disputed step is the philosophical inference from the existence of a received target, or the demand for its adequate explanation, to the existence of such a resource. Dasgupta does not prove Completion false. His positive qualitative category makes its claim to cover every adequate existential explanation a substantive additional commitment rather than a consequence of explanation as such.

Even a successful qualitative ground would not by itself prove that no relevant unreceived resource also exists. It could undermine the inference from explanatory adequacy to that resource requirement while coexisting with productive resources. A claim that Completion is actually false would additionally need the relevant resource absence or incompatibility, which these sources do not establish.

**An enlarged resource domain.** If an obtaining qualitative whole or an essential fact is included among resources, one has changed the interpretation. The requirements of actuality, relevance, an intrinsic bearer, and reception transport must then be defended for that new category. One cannot retain the old concrete-instantiation warrant by carrying over the same predicate names.

**A qualitative entity as bearer.** Conversely, nothing in the abstract type `B` excludes a retained universal or state by name. But naming one as the bearer does not establish that it has a concrete intrinsic unreceived resource in the same existential respect. Classifying a state as its own resource would require the same substantive support. This is neither an automatic escape for generalism nor an automatic absorption of it into the productive-source argument.

**SupportTransport.** The source's derivation of smaller facts from a holistic fact is not definitionally whole-existence reception. No read source establishes that every derivative bearer is received in that sense, or that an obtaining whole is unreceived productively. Hence the same-bearer conclusion remains conditional on the distinct actuality, intrinsic-resource, and reception premises.

No new modal conclusion follows from this adjudication. Necessary qualitative actuality need not identify one rigid necessarily existent particular, and one comprehensive fact need not be one efficient agent. The inherited one-original beginningless case remains untouched: neither least generation of every token nor a first temporal token is imposed here.

## Why obtaining and autonomy remain open

At p. 53 the 2009 paper explicitly distinguishes a term denoting a zero-place state from asserting that it obtains. This marks the actuality claim; it does not explain that actuality. Syntactic primitiveness is compatible with several metaphysical interpretations and establishes neither brute facthood nor autonomy.

The complete 2016 reading supplies additional philosophical motivation for autonomous essential facts and separates autonomy from unexplained-but-explanation-apt bruteness. Its domain-specification account of essence is a serious proposal. Section9 nevertheless needs an extra existence-grounding assumption to derive an essentially existing individual. Note44 sketches a qualitative alternative to that assumption. The 2009 paper supplies a motivated ontological framework for that sketch, but does not supply the particular essence whose actual truth explains qualitative obtaining, establish its autonomy, or show the proposed explanatory direction in an actual case.

The exact application residual is therefore smaller than a demand for a complete alternative cosmos: identify an actual qualitative essential basis, state which existence claim it grounds, and give the warrant for its truth, grounding direction, and autonomy. A concrete successful case could undermine an exhaustive productive-bearer inference without reconstructing every fact in the universe. Mere translation, a nonempty model, or a label declaring autonomy would not establish that case. Equally, failure to supply the case in these papers is not a proof that it is impossible.

Billon's law-relative completeness criterion does not remove this residual. Explanatory links are already granted, and completeness leaves the laws themselves outside the requested explanation. Whether a law has existential force, actually holds, and needs or admits further explanation must be retained as separate questions. A law that explicitly entails existence cannot be refuted by deleting that clause and then exhibiting absence.
