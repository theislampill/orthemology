## Blocking findings

### DM-F01 — abstract transducer presentation was promoted to concrete one-carrier realization

A transition table is an abstract presentation. Calling it a one-carrier realization risks importing physical implementability, causal efficacy, and bearer unity. The repaired theorem must claim canonical **formal table presentation** and boundary-trace equivalence only. Concrete realization is an additional world/implementation premise.

### DM-F02 — token responsiveness was called genuine reason responsiveness

The frozen `Z` inputs are registered reason tokens. Sensitivity to token changes does not establish truth-linked semantic content, apprehension as a reason, objective fittingness, or action because of fittingness. Separate:

```text
ZRESP: token-sensitive transition/output behavior;
SEM: registered tokens carry the relevant reason content;
FIT_O: the governing end is objectively fitting;
UPTAKE: a bearer apprehends the content as a reason;
BECAUSE_F: selection occurs because of fittingness.
```

### DM-F03 — distributed compilation overclaimed causal/intervention preservation

A composed table preserves the declared boundary traces. It need not preserve internal module interventions, causal decomposition, latency, resource use, provenance, or counterfactuals outside the frozen boundary vocabulary. Narrow the compilation claim to a declared boundary profile.

### DM-F04 — reduct nonentailment omitted model-class closure

Two arbitrary expansions of one reduct witness nonentailment only when both are admitted by the intended model class and no independent bridge axiom forbids the impersonal expansion. State this closure/eligibility assumption explicitly.

### DM-F05 — the finite trace checker was presented too close to an all-length proof

The checker tests lengths at most seven. All-length equality follows from identity of `delta` and `alpha` by induction, not from the bounded execution. Separate proof from regression evidence.

### DM-F06 — neutral underdetermination was allowed to sound like metaphysical or probabilistic parity

The same neutral reduct proves only logical/profile underdetermination at the declared signature. It does not establish metaphysical possibility, equal prior probability, equal likelihood under richer evidence, or causal idleness of mentality. Deep H's parity conditions remain separate.

### DM-F07 — `SAME_CARRIER` remained vulnerable to carrier-boxing

A formal container or common physical bearer can box independently primitive modules. It does not by itself establish one subject, intentional ownership, or derivational unity. Rename the weak condition and preserve Candidate G's parity theorem.
