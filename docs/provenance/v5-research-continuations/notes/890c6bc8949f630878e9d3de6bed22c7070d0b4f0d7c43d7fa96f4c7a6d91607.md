## Exact premise scope

The two terminal theorems take finite action and feedback types, a measurable shared history policy pi, one shared private seed probability rho, action-specific time-independent observation laws P, strict positivity of every model/action/symbol mass, normalization, and modelwise almost-sure eventual target membership. The hidden-model type Theta itself is arbitrary. Targets are finite action sets. The default action and an inhabited feedback type are explicit.

The source is a necessity theorem for the **defined shared-seed, fresh controlled-iid policy model**. It does not establish a universal representation theorem for arbitrary abstract behavioral kernels, hidden-state sensors, history-dependent observation laws, parameter-coded private seed distributions, seed/feedback correlation, or stack-peeking policies. Its law-equality part permits zeros; the final rival-transfer/necessity wrapper deliberately imposes full support. The greatest-kernel characterization is classical and noncomputable for arbitrary Theta; effective finite decision requires additional effective finite representations and decidable law equality.

This packet does not prove zero-support necessity or a single literal Lean iff joining this action-history policy with the earlier recorded-block sufficiency construction. Such a composition requires its own precise policy-realization binding. No historical priority, normative authority or canonical adoption follows.
