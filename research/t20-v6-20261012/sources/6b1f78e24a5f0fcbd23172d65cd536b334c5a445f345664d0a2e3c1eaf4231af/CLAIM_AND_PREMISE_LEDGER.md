## Relations kept separate

An independently caused interruption is independent of the particular interference or withdrawal under assessment. In the strong original-source comparison, its secondary productive process remains within G's whole-origin coverage. No independent absolute supplier is added to the premise set.

Within P3 also separate R's epistemic reason to expect provision, the practical permissibility of its reliance, and a relational claim on G. The first two do not define the third. That claim is the object of the argument.

- Normativity of source governance: why G has a reason concerning R.
- Authority of a source directive: why R has a reason to obey or believe G.
- Function-fixing: what F is for under the relevant actual account.
- Source norm-grounding: what ultimately constitutes the relevant normative truth.
- Actual fidelity: what G actually does.
- Essential integrity: what this same G cannot do in the specified possibilities.
- D1: this actual occurrence's source, content, target and context.
- D2: this recipient's truth-linked epistemic standing.

C1 does not collapse these relations. It is a positive bridge from intentional source activity plus an independently defended normative principle to a particular source responsibility. It neither assumes nor concludes that the source is the ultimate ground of every reason.

## Direct comparisons with inherited theorems

1. Seventh PT uses a current token-governing knowledge purpose. C3 is a reason persisting after the source abandons that purpose, so it is not an instance of PT. C3 does not conclude the source complies.
2. Ninth R_G+N excludes an actual in-scope lie. C1–C6 can apply without any lie and do not compare all complete profiles. They do not establish R_G or N and are not advertised as easier substitutes for them.
3. Sixth actual normative relevance already applies recipient goods to source choices. C1–C6 specify one distinct historical relation and its positive continuity/release implication. This is an application-level inferential extension, not discovery that recipient goods matter at all.
4. Ninth late ASSESSMENT already supplies the generic prima-facie expectation point. No new probabilistic preference or global trust verdict is claimed.
5. The mixed personal architecture remains a conditional challenge with unresolved compossibility. C3 means a contrary motive cannot cancel an antecedent responsibility merely by replacing the end. It does not exclude knowing breach or certify its possibility.
