# Candidate G — derivationally unified rational-ground abduction

## Neutral statement

A unitary ground is explanatorily preferable to plural primitive modal, actual, semantic, and normative facts only insofar as its independently motivated internal architecture derives cross-domain constraints rather than merely housing one primitive feature per explanandum.

A rational-purposive source is a live unification candidate because an integrated practical-reason architecture links representation of alternatives, truth-sensitive assessment, evaluative ordering, selection, and effective realization. The argument is abductive and does not proceed from bearer-count alone.

## Exact intended conclusion

```text
A_UNIFIED_RATIONAL_PURPOSIVE_GROUND_HAS_ABDUCTIVE_FORCE_ONLY_IF_IT_PASSES_DERIVATIONAL_UNIFICATION_PARITY
```

No claim of entailment to personality, divinity, or a Necessary Being is included.

## Formal architecture

Let the orthability complex be:

```text
Ω = <M,A,S,N,R>
```

where:

- `M`: identity and modal alternatives;
- `A`: concrete obtaining and efficacy;
- `S`: content and truth conditions;
- `N`: fittingness/correctness ordering;
- `R`: a realization relation connecting represented alternatives, evaluation, selection, and actual transition.

Compare:

```text
H_P: plural primitives <M,A,S,N,R>
H_B: one bearer with freely recombinable primitive features <m,a,s,n,r>
H_U: an integrated architecture K from which multiple Ω-relations are derived
```

By carrier-boxing invariance, `H_B` has no derivational advantage over `H_P` unless its feature space is constrained or its relations share non-conjunctive derivations.

`H_U` earns unification credit only if it yields at least one cross-domain law such as:

```text
represented alternatives constrain eligible selections;
truth-sensitive assessment constrains belief revision;
objective reasons constrain evaluative ranking;
selection plus efficacy constrains realized transition;
wisdom constrains ends and means jointly.
```

These relations must not be restated as independent primitive dispositions.

## Rational-source proposal

A source is **functionally rational-purposive** relative to Ω only if it instantiates all of:

1. **discrimination:** represents or otherwise differentiates relevant alternatives;
2. **assessment:** orders them under truth/fittingness/reason relations;
3. **integration:** makes assessment difference-making for selection;
4. **selection:** resolves among eligible alternatives where more than one is live;
5. **efficacy:** makes selected alternatives difference-making for actuality;
6. **diachronic unity:** preserves ownership or integration across assessment and realization.

This is a functional characterization. It does not yet establish phenomenal consciousness, first-person subjectivity, moral agency, or theological personality.

## Strongest current inference

If:

1. objective orthability is real rather than contract-relative;
2. Ω is a genuine cross-domain explanandum;
3. a complete account is required rather than declined;
4. plural/thick bruteness is penalized under a defended theoretical-virtue principle;
5. an integrated rational-purposive architecture derives Ω with fewer independent primitive degrees of freedom than its rivals;
6. the architecture is not merely defined by the explananda it was built to fit;

then a unified rational-purposive ground is abductively supported.

Every premise is defeasible. The conclusion is comparative, not deductive.
