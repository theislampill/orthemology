## 6. Use geometry to do discriminating work

After recovering existing results, identify the smallest genuinely useful bridge to the live research. Prefer reuse and conditional theorem statements over an expansive new infrastructure project.

Candidate questions include:

**Representation fidelity.** Does the proposed representation keep cases apart whenever a required result, warrant or continuation observable differs? Use the existing fibre criterion and continuation results. Smooth proximity must not substitute for bearer identity, authority or evidential independence.

**Coordinate invariance.** When two descriptions represent the same geometry, do the stated operations transform correctly? For a diffeomorphism F with g1 = F* g2 and J1 = J2 composed with F, the usual gradient-transport identity is a candidate reusable lemma. A semantic cross-domain map needs its own preservation contract; a mathematical isomorphism does not automatically transport source authority or meaning.

**Metric sensitivity.** Separate a harmless coordinate change from an actual change of metric. Which conclusions are invariant across an admissible metric family, and which merely depend on a selected metric? Do not choose a metric because it makes the preferred philosophical answer look like a minimum.

**Admissible correction.** Show where a proposed step preserves hard constraints and recorded obligations. Decreasing a smooth proxy does not compensate for a wrong bearer, fabricated evidence or missing authority. Newly revealed obligations can enlarge the ledger while improving the inquiry.

**Continuation after compression.** Can tensor contraction, a quotient or a reduced model still answer the future questions that remain authorised? Test it against the stored higher-order and predictive-memory counterexamples, not just the terminal score.

**Dynamics.** Recover existing energy/stability results before proving substitutes. A decreasing J establishes decrease of J under the declared flow; it does not by itself establish truth, global convergence, indefinite continuation or successful restoration. Extend the conclusion only where additional hypotheses support it.

Standard identities retain standard ancestry. A formalisation or application can be useful without being a new theorem in differential geometry. Label inherited mathematics, a new model, a new conditional theorem, a formalisation, a counterexample and an application separately.
