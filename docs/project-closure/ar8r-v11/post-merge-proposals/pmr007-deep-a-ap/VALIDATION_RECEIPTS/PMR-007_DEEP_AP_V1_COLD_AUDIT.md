## Blocking findings

### AP-F01 — source object and world object were insufficiently sorted

The discourse/source bearer `g` and a world object `w` belong to different
semantic roles. `h(g)=w` is a denotation/interpretation relation, not numerical
identity across sorts.

### AP-F02 — nonempty interpretation family was doing world-existence work

`H != empty` is an independent bridge assumption. A coherent source role bundle
without an admissible world interpretation does not establish a world bearer.

### AP-F03 — interpretation adequacy was bundled into map membership

Source bytes, locus, translation, attribution, proposition reconstruction,
Track-N acceptance, role preservation, referent eligibility, and world adequacy
must remain separate guards.

### AP-F04 — role preservation was overread as complete-profile transfer

A positive-role-preserving map permits the image to have additional or
contrary unregistered features. Reflection or a fuller semantic contract is
needed for complete-profile transfer.

### AP-F05 — role completeness is a strong comparison-class assumption

The claim that every translated role-bearer is the image of some admissible map
must be declared. Without it, a singleton image can be produced by a hidden
anchor or by arbitrarily restricting `H`.

### AP-F06 — singleton image was overread as source-derived uniqueness

`|F_H(g)|=1` is relative to the declared map family. It is evidence of source-
relative referent determination only if `H` was independently warranted and not
constructed to select the desired object.

### AP-F07 — uniqueness of role realizer is sufficient, not always necessary

Under a non-role-complete family, other structural or anchoring constraints may
force a singleton image even when several objects satisfy the translated role
bundle. The unique-role-realizer equivalence requires role completeness.

### AP-F08 — version and translation drift were not formal guards

A new source version, translation, or role reconstruction changes `H`, `U_W`,
and the image fibre. Prior denotation does not silently transport.

### AP-F09 — source compatibility was overread toward personality and Wisdom

A world object may satisfy the selected functional role bundle while remaining
impersonal under the neutral vocabulary. Source filtering does not prove the
neutral H7/H8 or Wisdom bridges.

### AP-F10 — theorem-family and novelty ceiling were incomplete

The formal core is an image-fibre/interpretation application of established
factorization mechanisms. General mathematical novelty is zero.
