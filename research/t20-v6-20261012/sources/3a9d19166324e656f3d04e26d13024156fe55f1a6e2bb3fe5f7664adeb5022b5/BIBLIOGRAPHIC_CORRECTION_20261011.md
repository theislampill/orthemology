## Correction

In `REVIEW.md`, the title-page secondary publisher mark described as **“Dar al-Ulum”** should read **«دار جودة للنشر والتوزيع» (Dar Jawda for Publishing and Distribution)**.

I independently inspected the supplied enlarged title-page logo image `private-locator:dc3d79e5a1fa6f8b5b14`. The visible mark reads «دار جودة للنشر والتوزيع». The earlier lower-resolution reading was mistaken.

The principal plain imprint remains **Maktabat Dar al-Minhaj**, first edition **1430 AH**, with catalogue/copyright material dated **1428 AH**. The source identity, argument assessment, primary page locators, textual-variant account, inherited-credit controls, and application limits are unchanged. No broader bibliographic investigation is claimed.
