## Claims

### KP10-1 Smaller target reduces the modal production burden

**Claim:** A positive existential target requiring one suitable instance does not require production of the entire actual qualitative inventory. The latter's exact multiplicity, all-instance exhaustion and joint creator coordination burdens do not transfer automatically.

**Status:** Warranted target comparison. It does not establish a possible outsider, actual explanation transfer, actual causal participation or a necessary contributor.

**New scope:** The source argument is prior art. Its comparison with the accepted Ninth inventory diagnostics and its application to this programme are new. No first-discovery claim is made.

**Owner:** Paper §2; source locator K-TARGET; prior Ninth F3 §4.

### KP10-2 A common basis is not target priority

**Claim:** From the prior kind-instantiation fact m_s supporting both activity a_s and existential truth p_Q, p_Q's priority to a_s does not follow. A distinct recipient m_t offers another potential witness route whose adequacy must be assessed separately.

**Status:** Justified inference limitation. It is not a metaphysically certified internal cosmos or proof that the proposed witness-mediated answer satisfies E.

**New scope:** The exact single-source projection test is new in the inspected programme; Ninth's collective-lifting diagnosis remains inherited. This result identifies an earlier point of dispute in the newly examined route.

**Owner:** Paper §3.

### KP10-3 Antecedent-witness nonpresupposition has independent rational force

**Claim:** Activity which uses an antecedently instantiated member of the target class can explain a new recipient without, for that reason alone, explaining the class's existential availability. This motivates a target-relative norm distinct from merely banning any explanans which entails its effect.

**Status:** A bounded local relevance judgement is rationally warranted at the explicitly existence-supply question: an activity cannot, solely through its output, supply the antecedent existential availability on which its productive channel depends. Direct basic warrant is legitimate; a further derivation is not mandatory. Same-E identification and application to the whole joint account remain unestablished. The broader original-possession comparison retains prima-facie support without becoming a new actual-world source-existence conclusion.

**Positive scope:** The norm need not require whole-origin E*, a temporal beginning, all-source unity, necessitation or explanation of the outside producer's own existence.

**Owner:** Paper §4.

### KP10-4 A single-source norm does not automatically exclude joint activity

**Claim:** A collection can succeed without any member independently supplying its whole explanation. The participant inference therefore needs a no-wholly-internal-account norm at joint scope, not merely a ban on each cause explaining its own membership.

**Status:** Necessary scope restriction; no new metaphysical counterexample or impossibility result.

**Owner:** Paper §5. This preserves, rather than supersedes, Ninth's joint-scope discipline.

### KP10-5 Four dimensions of explanation remain independent

**Claim:** Adequacy, explanatory depth, modal necessitation and contrastivity must not be identified. In particular, accepting an adequate nonnecessitating account neither warrants all witness-mediated existential answers nor requires them to be rejected.

**Status:** Explanatory-kind control for the present route. The general distinctions are inherited; their application here is new.

**Owner:** Paper §6; K-E and K-CAUSE locate the source boundaries.
