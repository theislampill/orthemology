# Coverage, credit and remaining verification

## What this delivery is

A whole-corpus-oriented field guide, a source register for seven explicitly supplied input bundles, a set of unchanged selected entry reports, written cross-front mathematical deductions, a philosophical relevance crosswalk, and a new finite exact-arithmetic diagnostic implementation.

The previous unfinished attempt had already built an inventory and reported 118 distinct recursive ZIP objects. Its scripts and recoverable metadata are preserved under `predecessor_work/`. The new inventory is independently computed by the packaging program and records its actual count in `inventory/CORPUS_SUMMARY.json`; disagreement must be investigated rather than silently normalised.

## Important operational limitation

Tool calls in the resumed execution stopped returning visible content to the assistant. The assistant therefore did not observe the new directory listings, expanded source reports, test outputs or final integrity receipt. The files and receipts are generated by commands that perform their own checks, but they are not described as independently observed successes by the assistant.

This limitation particularly affects the user's request for exhaustive familiarisation with every T20 report. The narrative is grounded in the supplied detailed research history and earlier context, and the new ordinary arguments are written explicitly. It is **not** a claim to have freshly read every line of every archive or completed a new proof review of every T20 theorem.

No missing proof is reconstructed from its title and assigned verification credit. No automatic source extraction is labelled a scholarly reading. No old internal review is relabelled an external review.

## Corpus scope

The seven top-level inputs are listed with hashes in `inventory/INPUTS.json`. Nested archives are deduplicated by exact bytes, not filename. All duplicate locations remain recorded. The member inventory preserves content hashes, sizes, directories, nested locations and path warnings.

A separate reconciliation lists every standalone ZIP mounted in the runtime and whether its bytes occur in the seven-input recursive corpus. A ZIP not found there is not silently declared missing from T20; it may be an unrelated input, an older review packet, or a newly generated derivative. Read its identity and purpose.

This does not certify that every T20 archive ever produced has been delivered. That stronger claim requires Dot's canonical list or equivalent author-side evidence. It also does not include active, undelivered research merely because a progress message mentions it.

## What is fresh in the mathematical text

D1–D8 are written ordinary deductions developed here. Their constituent ancestry is identified by mathematical subject, and some are deliberately standard results used in a new combined specification. No exhaustive prior-art search or proof of global originality is claimed.

The strongest candidate contribution is the operation-relative minimum finite sidecar combined with the geometric projectability and hybrid-interface conditions. Its novelty relative to every source file in the large corpus remains to be checked by a visible source review.

The implementation is new and self-contained. Its exact controls are distinct from the original research replays. The test oracle is separately implemented code within this same assistant-produced package, not an independent researcher.

## What is inherited

All original T20 Lean verification, written reviews, mathematical examples and Arabic-source appraisals retain their original scope. This delivery has not rebuilt the original Lean corpus, validated the physical/historical premises, newly collated the Arabic editions, or demonstrated empirical fidelity of a noetic model.

The guide preserves positive philosophical conclusions at the scope reported in their sources. It does not treat a local caveat as a global withdrawal, but neither does it promote a retained philosophical appraisal to an unconditional kernel theorem.

## What the portable verifier proves

The verifier checks the exact packaged file inventory, SHA-256 values, sizes and safe paths. Its adversarial tests check rejection of tampering, missing and extra files, symlinks and unsafe manifests. This is byte custody and contract conformance, not authenticity without an independently pinned archive hash and not mathematical truth.

Research tests separately exercise the new finite code. Passing them is finite executable corroboration, not a proof of the analytic results or a validation of actual DAEE semantics.

## Repository and programme authority

No protected repository was edited, no PR was merged or approved, no new calculus rule was adopted, and no T20 completion or research-duration credit is supplied by this package. Its handoff is an additive research input.
