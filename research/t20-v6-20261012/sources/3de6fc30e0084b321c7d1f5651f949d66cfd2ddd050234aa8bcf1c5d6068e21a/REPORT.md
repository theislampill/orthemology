# Why actual warning matters to punitive accountability

11 October 2026 UTC. Constructive own-act application within active T20. Earlier positive appraisals and completed packets remain unchanged.

## Result

**The retained source supplies a positive practical reason for an actual communicative provision which is not exhausted by the prior existence or recognition of a moral norm.** In Ibn al-Qayyim's application of Qurʾān 28:47, messenger-sending removes the specific plea that no messenger and book had been supplied. He simultaneously maintains that the relevant actions were already wrong and that divine punishment does not occur for them before the message establishes the case. The communication has a distinct operative role without first manufacturing the actions' wrongness.

This gives a narrowly specified wise own-act explanation: **why supply warning rather than treat antecedent wrongdoing alone as sufficient for punitive treatment?** The act answers a further condition governing that treatment. Neither God's already knowing the wrong, the bare existence of a correct standard, nor a declaration that the condition has been met supplies the communicative event it concerns.

The result does not conclude that communication makes punishment obligatory, that warning always produces assent, or that every particular recipient's case is thereby established. It identifies the source-owned rationale and tests substitutions at its actual scope. Mercy, guidance and an opportunity to respond remain part of the source context; the sending is not reduced to a device for obtaining punishment.

## 2 The source argument and its limits

### Wrongness and message establishment are distinct

The retained edited *Miftāḥ dār al-saʿāda*, 2:872–878, was reread continuously, with its apparatus kept distinct. At 874–876 the author argues that commands and prohibitions can address independently intelligible good and bad features; explaining prohibition merely by prohibition would empty its offered reason. At 877 he then invokes 28:47 and distinguishes the wrong character of the prior acts from punishment after messenger-sending. His decisive formulation joins “أن القبح ثابت للفعل في نفسه” with “إلا بعد إقامة الحجة بالرسالة”. The surrounding argument at 878 expressly refuses both inference from absent punishment to absent wrongness and inference from rational wrongness alone to pre-message punishment.

The word translated as desert or deservingness also occurs before the message. It must not be silently redefined away to make the passage easier. The author's conjunction is that those actions have a culpable/penal basis, yet the punishment is withheld pending the further message condition. Accordingly this report uses **condition governing punitive treatment**, not an assertion that there was no morally relevant fault before communication or that the message alone creates all desert.

This is the author's own endorsed reconciliation. The rival positions he reports and the modern editor's references are not additional premises of our conclusion. The transmitted account remains at the already inherited source/disclosure warrant; no new historical authentication or individual case adjudication has occurred. [Retained witness's official source locator](https://ibnelqayem.com/ar/media/download/86).

### The original's stated purpose rather than inferred benefit alone

The complete Arabic 28:44–50 sequence was read through the connected text service. Verses 45–47 connect sending, mercy, warning, possible remembrance, and the no-messenger plea. Verses 48–50 then distinguish the coming of the truth from the recipients' response. This context does not say that successful provision guarantees willing acceptance. Nor is the no-message plea the exhaustive purpose of sending.

It does not infer a particular communicative intention merely from general original causation or foreknowledge of some eventual uptake. The two books are a same-author contextual combination, not two independent historical witnesses.

## 3 Executed own-act inference

Fix the source's described relation: actual prior wrongdoing, a prospective punitive treatment, and the message-established case relevant to that treatment. The explanation does not begin by stipulating that every treatment the source selects is fitting.

1. The wrongness of the relevant conduct has an intelligible basis independently of whether this communication occurs.
2. Under the source's affirmed account, that fact alone does not satisfy every condition governing punishment. Establishment through the message has a further role.
3. Actual sending and explanatory warning address the specifically identified absence of such provision. The source text itself assigns that purpose, rather than leaving us to invent a benefit after the outcome.
4. Therefore this own communicative action has an independently characterizable practical rationale. Its performance changes the relevant communicative relation while leaving the prior wrongness intelligible.

This discharges the own-act practical-content requirement at a limited respect. It explains a positive provision in relation to its stated end; it does not derive the entire occasion, exact wording, chosen messenger, timing, or complete history from that end. The previously warranted Knowledge, Will, Wisdom and disclosure framework supplies the application's foundation. The text supplies the local purpose and condition; the argument above states their consequence. Those are distinct evidential roles.

No external moral object is made an efficient donor of the original's knowledge, actuality or essential perfection. Conversely, the practical reason is not rendered idle: it identifies what the actual provision accomplishes and why substituting an unrelated achievement fails.

## 4 The precise substitutions tested

**Correctness without provision.** Retain objective wrongness, an articulated norm, and the source's full knowledge, but remove the message-establishment condition. The retained goods do not satisfy this source's further condition. Thus saying that the standard was already true is not an answer to why actual warning matters. This does not deny ordinary rational moral knowledge.

**Appointment without explanatory discharge.** Retain a title or a message existing somewhere, without establishing the relevant communicative relation. The inherited commissioned-content result prevents calling that successful explanation by definition. The current passage gives no detailed universal test of when the case has been established for each person, so this report does not substitute a bare dispatch, audible syllables, or institutional label for that accomplishment.

**A verdict instead of its ground.** Merely asserting that no excuse remains does not itself supply the omitted message condition. This is an inference about the proposed substitution, not an accusation that the original makes empty declarations. If the assertion truthfully reports that an adequate communicative provision already occurred, that occurrence, rather than the assertion alone, does the relevant work.

**Provision without universal uptake.** Replace establishment with the stronger requirement that every recipient actually assents. That changes the target. The source's own sequence permits rejection after truth arrives. It supports distinguishing provision from acceptance; it does not settle every contested instance of ignorance, inability or culpable refusal.

These substitutions are tests of the named source relation. No generic rival system or Boolean “warranted” coordinate is introduced.

## 5 What follows and what does not

The source's condition is necessary for the punitive treatment under discussion. Satisfying it alone does not establish that punishment occurs, must occur, or is otherwise justified in every respect. Other conditions, responses and grounds may matter. Nor does withholding punishment imply that the underlying conduct was objectively permissible. Both invalid converses are ruled out by keeping the source's two levels distinct.

The account also gives no permission for a human agent to punish anyone. It concerns a theological own-act explanation, not contemporary legal or culpability guidance. It creates no claim about the fate of a named person or group.

The constructive increment is therefore exact: **an actual original communicative provision can have a normative practical role beyond the mere abstract availability of a true norm or the intensifying of belief.** Its role is stated independently of occurrence and survives the distinction between moral reality and actual guidance. The application remains bounded by the inherited source warrant, without demanding that every foundation be derived from weaker premises.
