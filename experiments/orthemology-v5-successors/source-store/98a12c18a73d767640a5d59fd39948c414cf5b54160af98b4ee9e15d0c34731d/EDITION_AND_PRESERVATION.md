# Original al-Sawaʿiq: identity and boundary review

Reader-facing study record, 7 October 2026. The interpretations and reading claims below belong to the recorded study and its bounded rereads. This edition of the notes adds no source-reading or proof credit.

Reviewed 2026-10-07. Scope: identity, preservation and natural boundaries only, not a continuous study of the body. The selected boundary evidence records the source coordinates, image hashes and dated corrections; the separate split-volume source identities record their exact URLs and hashes.

## Identity and strength of boundary evidence

The retained Saaid combined PDF is the original al-Sawaʿiq al-mursala ʿalā al-Jahmiyya wa-l-muʿaṭṭila by Ibn al-Qayyim, edited by Ḥusayn b. ʿUkāsha b. Ramaḍān, with takhrīj by Ḥusayn b. Ḥasan Bāqir and Karīm Muḥammad ʿĪd. PDF 1 identifies volume 1; PDF 2 explicitly gives Dar ʿAtaʾat al-ʿIlm / Dar Ibn Hazm, first printing 1442/2020, ISBN 9789959858085. This is not al-Mawṣilī's abridgment.

The combined PDF lacks a separate volume 2 title at its physical seam. A separately acquired, independently permitted two-PDF witness provides direct physical-volume evidence: https://www.darulkutub.co/pages/bookdetails.php?id=226. Both downloaded PDFs returned HTTP 200. Volume 1 contains 682 pages and ends at printed 604. Volume 2 contains 716 pages, has its own volume 2 cover, and begins body text at printed 605 (its PDF 5); it ends at printed 1316. IMPORTANT: this split witness is the 1441/2020 Dar ʿAlam al-Fawaʾid printing, whose imprint is visible on volume 2 PDF 3. It is not the 1442 imprint. Its matching layout, page text and continuous numbering corroborate the 604/605 seam for the Saaid printing. Therefore distinguish direct proof for the 1441 split witness from strong same-layout corroboration for the 1442 combined witness; do not claim to have seen a 1442 volume 2 cover.

## Inherited four-part divisions: separate from current two volumes

The Islamicbook body explicitly retains these natural divisions:

1. source001: 731 says انتهى الجزء الأول. Preceding body ends source001: 730; source001: 732 starts chapter 17. In the 1442 scan, the former is printed 183/PDF 261 and chapter 17 begins printed 184/PDF 262. Thus this is definitely not the 604/605 physical two-volume seam.
2. source002: 465 ends the body of inherited part 2 and then says انتهى الجزء الثاني ويليه الجزء الثالث وأوله فصل فهذا الطاغوت الأول. The preceding Qurʾanic anchor is فبأي حديث بعد الله وآياته يؤمنون. source002: 466 begins فصل فهذا الطاغوت الأول. Both sides are on ONE page of the 1442 scan: printed 471/PDF 549. This is a legacy volume break occurring within the current volume 1, before the remaining first-taghut discussion on 471–472. It is not the start of the second taghut; the latter begins printed 473/PDF 551.
3. source003: 360 ends inherited part 3 and says part 4 follows, beginning الوجه السابع والعشرون بعد المائة. This is again an inherited four-part division, not one of Islamicbook's four web-section edges.

These are securely explicit electronic part divisions. Their pattern is consistent with the older four-volume ʿAli al-Dakhil Allah / Dar al-ʿAsima 1408 edition, independently catalogued at https://www.islamarchive.cc/ketab_content/5597/p-26, but no printed original of that older edition was acquired or directly checked here. Do not claim definitive edition attribution for the inherited markers on this evidence alone.

## Preservation limits from the local editor introduction

- Intro printed 29/PDF 29 (corrected after high-resolution reinspection): the received discussion of the second taghut nominally reaches 242 faces (مائتين واثنين وأربعين). At manuscript folio 54 b, numbering jumps from 47 to 50: faces 48–49 may have been omitted, OR their numbering may be erroneous. At folio 69 b, numbering jumps from 72 to 79: faces 73–78 are not given; the copyist notes هكذا في الأصل. The editor therefore counts 234 present faces (مائتين وأربعة وثلاثين). Do not present both gaps as equally certain textual losses. The portions against the third and fourth taghuts were not found.
- Intro 30/PDF 30: the author promises another concluding chapter after the four taghuts. It is absent even from al-Mawṣilī's abridgment, so the editor does not know whether it was written.
- Intro 55–56: al-Mawṣilī's abridgment is a distinct selective rewriting and includes material from the missing part of the original; it cannot be substituted silently for the received original.
- Intro 60–63: no complete original manuscript was found. Aleppo is the fullest principal witness; Berlin contains about one-third of the received original and supplies some missing text. A Baghdad copy descends from Aleppo. The abridgment is an auxiliary textual control.
- Intro 15–16: historical accounts vary on book size, and the Aleppo manuscript records a historical part 2 beginning. This is not evidence of the current printed 604/605 seam.
- Final printed 1143/PDF 1221 note 4 gives the received-text limit and quotes the 1110 AH copyist's completion note. This cannot establish that the full historical work survives.
