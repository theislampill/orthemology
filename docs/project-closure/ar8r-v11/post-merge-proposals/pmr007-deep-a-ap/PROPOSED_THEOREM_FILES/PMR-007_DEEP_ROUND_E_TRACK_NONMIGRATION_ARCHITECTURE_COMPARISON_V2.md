## 2. TNM-1 — source-premise nonmigration theorem

Let `T0` be a neutral premise set, `S` a set of source-relative premises, and
`C` a target conclusion.  If there is a model of `T0` in which `C` is false,
then `T0` does not entail `C`.  If `T0 union S` entails `C`, the result remains
source-relative unless every load-bearing member of `S` is independently
established at neutral authority.

### Proof

A model of `T0` with `not C` is by definition a countermodel to `T0 |= C`.
Adding `S` may exclude that model, but the exclusion then depends on `S`.  Until
`S` is independently available in the neutral premise class, the derivation
cannot be reclassified as neutral. QED.

This is elementary consequence bookkeeping.  Its program role is to prevent
both source laundering and source erasure.

## 3. Exact source eligibility matrix

### N-S1 — necessary existence versus Creator

The translated Asfahani commentary explicitly states that a proof yielding only
“a necessary existence” does not thereby affirm the Maker who originated the
heavens and earth; a contingent/originated effect and originator bridge are
needed.  This is **translated-primary access to a school-internal source
argument**, not Arabic-primary verification and not a neutral formal proof.

### N-S2 — external actuality

The same translation criticizes an “absolute” necessary existence existing only
as a mental construct and argues for an extramental necessary existent.  This is
a source argument relevant to the external-actuality bridge.  Its premises and
inference have not been independently admitted as a neutral theorem.

### N-S3 — qiyās al-awlā and attributes

The translation states an a-fortiori rule: an already established Creator has a
better claim to intrinsic perfections free of defect, and a better claim to
transcend defects.  It does not allow the rule to establish the Creator, does
not erase the praiseworthiness/defect/nonassimilation guards, and does not turn
direct source predication into neutral theorem credit.

### N-S4 — unity proof restriction

The commentary rejects the particular composition/indistinguishability proof
used in the creed for oneness.  Source compatibility therefore cannot be
claimed for every generic necessary-being-to-unity argument merely because its
conclusion is source-accepted.

### N-S5 — uncreated Speech

The translation presents the Salaf/imam source claim that divine Speech
subsists in Allah and that the Qur’an is uncreated Speech.  This is a direct
school-internal/revelational premise.  It supplies N-track content for UG-T9 and
attribute ascent; it is not a neutral consequence of articulability.

### N-S6 — fiṭrah and proper functioning

El-Tobgui’s dissertation supplies a secondary scholarly reconstruction in which
a healthy fiṭrah undergirds cognitive and moral faculties, can be corrupted,
and conditions reliable intuitive judgment.  Al-ʿUjayrī supplies a contemporary
Atharī synthesis of innate recognition of Allah and its possible occlusion.
These materials support a source-relative proper-function architecture.  They
are neither Arabic-primary verification nor a neutral proof that fiṭrah is
world-true or divinely designed.

### N-S7 — Candidate N architecture

The preserved A-to-N map classifies Candidate N as a provisional internal
architecture with source and specialist burdens open.  Its six-role integration
must therefore remain a modern reconstruction, not an explicit single-source
theorem or established neutral ontology.

No source item in this round is classified as independently Arabic-primary
verified.
