## What most blocks a foundational-breakthrough claim?
The missing compiler is the largest immediate assurance gap. Even after it is
fixed, the foundational claim still lacks an independently validated theorem
that is not an instance/integration of existing realisability and dependency
techniques. CV-C may support a technical computability contribution, not by itself
a new foundation. This distinction survives any increase in test count.
