# Authoring-stage review and limits

This is a same-assistant preparation/review record, not an independent review.

## Substantive checks

1. The paper is explicitly a new companion, not a revision or replacement of LM3.
2. Event-level continuation, faculty possession, faculty-preserving transition,
   and recursive capability are separated rather than silently identified.
3. The user's endogenous-capacity emphasis is preserved. Physical support and
   legitimate external permission are not redefined as defects to eliminate.
4. The T/C/G/A working interpretation is identified as such; the source naming
   those coordinates is an integration instruction, not a completed theorem.
5. Exact continuation retains domain, order, operative-readout and effect premises.
6. Future task preservation is indexed to a declared family, not every possible task.
7. The covering bound is necessary for the stated information model and is not
   presented as sufficiency for available physical observations.
8. Per-request information is not confused with one simultaneous answer vector.
9. Destructive inquiry retains initial labels and current executability separately.
10. The finite-request theorem includes successor capability as a postcondition;
    finite and indefinite physical claims are distinguished.
11. Approximate recurrence bounds do not become failure proofs when inconclusive.
12. Organisational continuation, generation, lineage and numerical identity are
    not conflated.
13. Prior-art discussion is bounded and identifies inherited mathematics; no
    historical-priority finding or exhaustive literature review is claimed.
14. The final PDF has no unresolved-reference or replacement-character markers;
    final LaTeX compilation has no overfull-box or undefined-reference warning.
15. All final PDF pages were rendered. Layout was inspected using contact sheets
    and selected full-page views, including the title and core finite-request theorem.

## Executed checks

The new finite checker passed all 15 groups normally and under Python -O; the
result JSON files match byte-for-byte. This is not a rerun of LM3 or the
consolidation reports' original verifiers and does not imply general proof,
physical validation, causal attribution or independent review.

  empirical faculty or comparative usefulness claims.

  stipulated causal role.

  for any stronger historical or program-wide claims.
- Noise, continuous state, stochastic control, unbounded request streams and
  maintained resources require additional models and proofs.
