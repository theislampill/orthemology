Proceed into a connected passage only if it adds to the existing specification, pure-perfection or original-giver arguments. Stop if it repeats those arguments without advancing their disputed premise. Do not initiate a new general attributes campaign.

These are priorities, not assertions that the candidate pages contain the proposed arguments. If only one operation is wanted now, choose the first.
