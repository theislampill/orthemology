# A local route to an original participant

## Source argument

Pruss’s research notes give a conditional first-cause argument using acyclic causal paths, founded reverse-well-ordered internal chains, and no reverse omega1 ancestry. Applied to a target’s complete ancestor cone, it yields a relevant root while allowing supported infinite ancestry. The inspected argument does not establish its crucial common-bound premise for the actual correction. Countability or a strict real-valued rank can address the size restriction without supplying that bound. Necessity, uniqueness and complete sourcehood remain further questions. The bodies were inspected on the author’s homepage; these are research notes, not a verified peer-reviewed result.

## The relevant ancestor application

Choose one actual ancestor v of the correction e. The premises need hold only on v’s complete incoming-closed cone. Its root is then an ancestor of e. No condition need cover e’s other branches. This is a local application of the cited argument, not a new general theorem.

Incoming closure is essential: a selected mechanism cannot be declared independent merely because its earlier suppliers were omitted. Calling its root originally possessed existential actuality also requires that the relation exhaust the relevant sources of its availability. A root in a narrower trigger history does not establish that stronger role.

## Positive warrant and the remaining question

The inherited positive reason concerns real received efficacy. A productive exercise cannot furnish, solely through its output, the antecedent availability it already uses. Where a route’s retained input is independently identified, tracing that input has genuine warrant. One need not first establish source uniqueness or necessity to credit an actual original participant.

But ordered receiving acts do not by themselves identify one common supplying input. Extending a history upstream can supply what a shorter account treated as its boundary resource. The active infinitist therefore contests applying a fixed-input judgement to the enlarged route. This reply does not certify the rival’s adequacy or cancel original possession’s prima-facie explanatory advantage.

The missing actual application is narrower than a claim about all the correction’s ancestry: identify one relevant ancestor whose complete incoming cone independently meets the stated conditions. No such witness has been established here. The result is a reduced warrant obligation and a valid conditional route, with no new actual original participant, necessary bearer or complete source claimed.
