## Narrow primary-source extension

The full relevant report in Ibn Abi Ya‘la's Tabaqat al-Hanabilah names this path:

Ibn Abi Ya‘la → al-Mubarak → Muhammad ibn ‘Ali ibn al-Fath [al-‘Ishari] → ‘Ali ibn Mardak → ‘Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Hatim → Yunus ibn ‘Abd al-A‘la → al-Shafi‘i.

It contains the extended attributes list and, near the end, conditions obligation on a report attaining the relevant observation-like evidentiary standing. The condition begins: “فإن كان الوارد بذلك خبرا”. This is not the claim that every report attains that standing. The electronic transcription contains visible textual defects and misplaced headings, so its exact orthography is not a manuscript collation. [Tabaqat, al-Shafi‘i entry, received lines 1223–1225](https://www.arabic-keyboard.info/books/ar/read-book/طبقات-الحنابلة).

Al-Dhahabi's Siyar 10:79–80 quotes a shorter formulation through a different reported route:

al-Hakkari → Abu Ya‘la al-Khalil ibn ‘Abd Allah → Abu al-Qasim ibn ‘Alqamah al-Abhari → ‘Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Hatim → Yunus ibn ‘Abd al-A‘la → al-Shafi‘i.

It retains the reason/revelation, ignorance and affirmation/nonresemblance material. It omits the detailed attributes list and the report-as-witnessing clause. Therefore even acceptance of the shorter wording would not automatically authenticate every sentence of the longer version. [Siyar 10:79–80](https://islamweb.net/ar/library/content/60/1674/الإمام-الشافعي).

The common tail begins at Ibn Abi Hatim. **A shared tail does not establish literary copying or eliminate the possibility of independently transmitted support.** Conversely, two printed occurrences with different upper chains are not authenticated independence by themselves. In Siyar 19:67–69, al-Dhahabi combines praise of al-Hakkari's piety with Ibn ‘Asakir's reservation about his reliability in transmission. This additional source issue means the apparent alternate route cannot be treated as a clean rescue without its own assessment. It does not establish fabrication. [Complete al-Hakkari entry](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/60/4654/الهكاري).
