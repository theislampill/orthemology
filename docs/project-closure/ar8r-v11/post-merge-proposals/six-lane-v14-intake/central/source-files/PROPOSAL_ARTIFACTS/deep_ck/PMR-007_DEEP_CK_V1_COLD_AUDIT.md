# Deep CK V1 cold audit

**Disposition:** `REPAIR_REQUIRED`

The audit was conducted over the frozen V1 theorem/model hashes. It did not use the intended V2 repair as authority.

## Blocking findings

1. **CK-F01 — candidate interpretation versus actual-world selection.** `interpretation supplied` does not identify the actual world or make the source referent unique across admissible interpretations.
2. **CK-F02 — untyped predicate preservation.** V1 does not distinguish functional-role preservation from intensional predicate preservation, nor preservation direction and strength.
3. **CK-F03 — bearer relation under-typed.** `common bearer` does not state that the source referent is that bearer; it also risks conflating existence, uniqueness, and transworld numerical identity.
4. **CK-F04 — fittingness signal leakage.** The theorem text does not sufficiently distinguish response to a declared fittingness signal from independently objective fittingness.
5. **CK-F05 — Wisdom packed into the guard list.** The positive clause is a source-relative role specification unless an independent account of Wisdom is supplied. It must not be presented as a neutral metaphysical theorem.
6. **CK-F06 — applicability and world adequacy collapsed.** Recipient/target applicability, world adequacy, and actual-world source-to-bearer co-reference require separate coordinates.
7. **CK-F07 — finite-profile scope not exact enough.** The checker exhausts all open-loop words through horizon three and verifies kernel commutation. V1 must not imply exhaustive architecture-family, infinite-horizon, empirical, or physical completeness.
8. **CK-F08 — checker acceptance predicate defect.** The V1 checker incorrectly required 64 target-leak failures even though the target-leak controls correctly produced zero failures. The failed V1 result must be preserved and the acceptance predicate repaired.
9. **CK-F09 — plural control source status.** The plural model is a neutral/source-compatible control only after recording that strong intensional source-predicate preservation fails; it is not a fully source-preserving Track-N model.
10. **CK-F10 — source authority roles incomplete.** The source bundle combines translated-primary access, secondary reconstruction, contemporary synthesis, and project formalization. These must be individually typed.

## Nonblocking notes

- The neutral architecture collision is useful only as a fresh bounded reconstruction, not exact recovery of Deep CG or an exhaustive implementation of Challenger A's factorial.
- The mathematical mechanism is a conservative-expansion/profile-fibre application, with zero general novelty and substantial ancestry in AR8R-T294/Deep BV.
- The source-locus argument has source-relative force but remains conditional on source and interpretation custody.

## Required repair

Create V2 with an explicit source-to-world guard ladder, typed predicate strength, actual-world and co-reference coordinates, a source-relative role-package conclusion, exact finite/common-kernel scope, preserved V1 checker failure, explicit U/I/P map, and separate neutral versus Track-N dispositions.
