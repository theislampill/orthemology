# Original provision and numerical-effect identity: new discriminating research

7 October 2026. This lane continues the reopened T20 investigation. It preserves all earlier artifacts. The findings below are conditional mathematics, direct source interpretation, and identified remaining philosophical obligations. They are not a declaration that this research frontier or T20 is closed.

## Findings first

The new work yields four useful discriminations beyond the earlier emitted-input/addition/OR packet.

1. The strongest relevant Arabic argument is not merely an appeal to numerical origin-essentiality. Majmu 20:175 says that an unchanged agent plus a genuinely contributing equal partner cannot produce even an equal or like complete result: the additional productive work must make the result greater or more. This is a substantive nonabsorption principle. Positive complete-output accounting proves it conditionally; ordinary outcome sufficiency, distinct intrinsic act tokens, and nonduplication do not by themselves supply that accounting.
2. Direct joint-event production can be represented without created emissions, a hidden created saturator, or an additional efficient root. In the declared operational semantics, the contingent occurrence has the two intrinsic acts as its productive grounds. This answers the bare demand for a cause of that occurrence. It does not automatically answer the source's stronger independence-of-efficacy argument, establish that the rule realizes full original agency, or justify interpreting a guarded joint production as unchanged complete individual production.
3. The same complete **bearer-event history in the declared small ontology**, and every root-input intervention on it, can come from different actual productive derivations. A new proof shows there is no decoder of entire original provision from that extensional interface alone. Adding all causal contribution relations or counting each firing as a separate created occurrence changes the interface and can break the equality. Which additions are extramental created effects rather than explanatory records requires source and ontological argument.
4. Requiring a separate earlier jointly produced mechanism for every jointly produced event leads to an impossible well-founded regress. A finite DAG scan and general Lean proof establish that conditional result. The source's contingent-coalescence premise does not by itself state this stronger requirement. Every finite joint-production graph must bottom out in a first primitive combination of previously separate root contributions; merely inserting more created mechanisms relocates that point.

No full metaphysical plural-original countermodel is established. Nor is the primitive relational rival refuted merely because it denies nonabsorption, whole-original-provision, or an efficacy/perfection bridge when one of those is the inference being tested. The exact reduced-signature independence result is earned; full original-bearer, perfected-agency, intentional admissibility and actual-cosmos interpretation remain unestablished.

## 1. Direct source return and stronger argument

### 1.1 Independence and unchanged actual agency

Minhaj 1:438–441 distinguishes having alternatives oneself from needing another agent to permit or enable a performance. It addresses capacities existing while the other acts, not only two already-completed creations. The argument is therefore stronger than saying that two numerically identical **exclusive** acts cannot coexist. It moves from the denial of duplicate sole production to the inadequacy of a power exercisable only with the peer's noninterference. The source explicitly classifies that required noninterference as external enabling.

This gives a real objection to an interpretation that preserves only a list of standing commands. Nevertheless, the classification from an extrinsic condition of a result to received power is an argued step. It is not to be assumed as an antecedent of its own test. In the new direct-event programs, suppression preserves the exact same intrinsic act object but changes which productive rule is active. This is a negative control against promoting command identity to unchanged complete operative production.

### 1.2 Nonabsorption is stronger than provenance-based numerical difference

Majmu retained L906–916 was reread directly after the first models. L906 distinguishes whole independence from participation and L907 treats strengthening by multiple proofs. L908 then generalizes: where the original agent is unchanged, joint output cannot be like the separate output; otherwise the additional would be equivalent to the absent. The examples explicitly mention changes in will, movement and instruments when a person performs alone what people jointly perform.

The argument invokes additional productive efficacy, not merely different ancestry. A model that keeps the same numerical bearer while adding a real effect in it has not yet answered this claim. Conversely, a claim that different causal histories must make different numerical objects is stronger than the passage requires. The whole product might differ in a genuine state or degree while the created bearer remains numerically the same.

L912–913 supplies a second step: complete power plus decisive will requires production through the agent alone, which excludes another producer in the same entire original respect. That is a source-explicit whole-provision claim, not just the proposition that the intended output exists. The tests below do not replace it with the weaker output proposition. They ask what additional semantic principle validates that transition.

### 1.3 Distinct intrinsic acts, distinct effects in a bearer, distinct bearers

Minhaj V03L0272, electronically page321, first says that the act occurring in one subject cannot be the same act occurring in the other. It then says that, in a detached created thing, one agent's effect **in it** cannot be the other's very effect **in it**. The text need not be read as asserting two detached created substances. It allows a single cooperative product containing distinguishable contributions. V03L0268 also explicitly recognizes the collaborators' own distinct acts and the mixing of their effects in one outside product.

The correct type separation is therefore:

- Intrinsic act token, with its own bearer
- Created bearer or product e
- The particular productive contribution or effect in e
- Complete actual productive basis of e
- The cross-context counterpart or numerical-identity relation, if one is asserted

A noninjective map from two act tokens to one created bearer refutes no source claim by itself. The live issue is whether the two actual contributions are genuine distinguishable created effects, what joint completion they supply, and why that completion constitutes externally received intrinsic/source efficacy. A generic injectivity theorem is retained only as a type-control negative result; it is not advertised as a decisive countermodel to Minhaj.

### 1.4 Bare occurrence explanation is not a demand for a law-producing third entity

The retained Asbahani scan was newly viewed at printed144–147, not credited through an earlier receipt. At145 the argument says contingent mixing requires a doer and that the doer is not outside the stipulated pair. It then considers one of the pair mixing the other's product, using the earlier cooperation exclusion and the bridge from changed product to changed determining power/will. Page146 explicitly classifies consent as cooperation and nonconsent as opposition.

This wording concerns the cause of actual contingent mixing. It does not separately posit an abstract law as a concrete efficient entity needing a third maker. The prior exclusion of original cooperation does important work in ruling out both roots jointly explaining that mixing. A direct joint productive relation is therefore a challenge to those additional source-mode steps, not an automatic violation of the bare no-unexplained-occurrence demand. It must not be dismissed merely because the model has a two-input function.

The source nonetheless asserts a much stronger total case: original powers are intrinsically possessed; efficient receipt cannot circle; their perfect efficacy cannot come from a peer; full determination should be wholly productive; independent domains cannot account for the connected actual creation. The direct relation does not certify all those claims by drawing an acyclic graph.

### 1.5 Perfection and action controls remain in force

Minhaj 2:180–183 calls realized efficacy a perfection and argues that what is necessary by itself cannot acquire its intrinsic perfection externally. Majmu L916–921 connects inability to act independently to fuller possible power, then to self-completion and dependence. The exact disputed application remains whether differentiated joint outcome-completion is external receipt of such a perfection rather than an extrinsic relational role of already possessed activity.

Safadiyya retained L618–627 and Salim2:141–146 separate necessary genus of activity, particular intrinsic acts, and particular created effects. The particular-act anchor remains Salim2:145, while the global-inactivity argument is at2:142. No generic rule here says that any newly described relational participation state is a newly acquired constitutive perfection. No model here establishes the full temporal divine-action profile or permits global inactivity by deleting its agent from existence.

Dar9's continuously reread L600–794 supplies the broader context. L627–650 rejects two whole-alone producers while affirming genuinely differentiated cooperation among created causes. L640–644 distinguishes a subject's own act from a separate result requiring other created conditions. L710–750 then argues from independent original power to independent productive efficacy and distinguishes self-chosen alternatives from another agent's enabling. These controls make the argument stronger than a universal prohibition of causation by several factors. They also locate the actual issue in the transfer to original-source independence.

## 2. New operational interpretations

### 2.1 Production rules, not arbitrary ownership assignments

The Python engine executes guarded rules whose positive prerequisites are intrinsic acts or earlier created events. Actual proof records retain the rule, positive root support, and absence guards. The created event key has no source/owner component. Attribution is calculated from the executed derivations.

For actual A and B acts directed toward e:

- Direct coalescence fires one rule with both roots: {A,B} produces e. When only A acts, an A-solo rule produces e, and conversely.
- Redundant production fires A's and B's independently enabled rules. The event-key ontology merges their e destinations.
- A-priority produces e through A when A is active, otherwise through B. B-priority reverses this.

All four programs have the same created event-set history at every root-input profile. They differ operationally in guards, enabled rules and actual proof support. They are not one evaluator with arbitrary ownership labels appended. Nor is every difference in proof records thereby certified as a difference in extramental causation.

The derived exhaustive-provider relation says all actual positive productive support for e comes from the same root. In the joint coalescence model neither is the exhaustive provider. In A-priority A is. A distinct derivation-provider relation asks whether an actually enabled derivation uses only that root. Redundant production assigns both roots this latter status while assigning neither exhaustive provision. This explicitly exposes the attribution distinction.

Whole-effect CND holds for the exhaustive relation; it is inhabited in solo and priority cases, not merely at an emitted-input level. Its joint-coalescence case is vacuous because no root wholly supplies e. This is not by itself a countermodel defect: CND excludes duplicate whole provision rather than asserting an entire owner exists in every cooperative case. But source P6-to-entire-provision cannot be claimed from that vacuous case. Its applicability is the disputed bridge.

### 2.2 Intentional content is now interpreted, with its exact limit

The first bare rule engine included an uninterpreted intention annotation. It receives no credit for complete determining will. The current field explicitly says it is uninterpreted, and a separate `IntendedAct` interpreter supplies actual content semantics.

Each interpreted intrinsic command carries an exact set of created event keys. The command's contents generate the corresponding event rules. Exact goal satisfaction means the entire generated created event set equals that goal. Changing the goal changes the output. Matching goals q,q yield exactly q, and suppressing B while keeping A's act object and goal q yields exactly q. Different goals can fail each agent's exact whole-output intention.

All16 subsets of a four-event catalogue were executed. A generic Lean theorem proves the event-set identity for arbitrary event-index types and target predicates, including beyond any numeric bound. This is not a claim that all metaphysically eligible effects are sets of such events. It is a concrete constructor family with equal target capacities, no hidden target ceiling, and no claim of complete will merely from its label.

The matching-goal admissibility condition is explicit. The interpreter does not derive it from wisdom, knowledge or intrinsic necessity. It also does not guarantee each exact global target against every independently combinable rival command. Those remain substantive issues addressed in the complementary willing lane.

### 2.3 The active productive mode is not fixed

In direct coalescence the remaining intrinsic act really is unchanged, but the actual rule switches from AB-joint to A-solo after suppression. Holding only the command object fixed therefore does not establish that the **entire operative production mode** remains fixed. The code tests and report preserve this as a negative control.

This does not establish that every source-authorized comparison must freeze the whole external relation to the peer, which would make deleting the peer incoherent by definition. It identifies the choice that needs warrant: is complete individual determination a property of the intrinsic act/disposition, or of the entire actually instantiated productive basis? In the latter reading the model fails the antecedent; in the former it demonstrates why output determination and entire actual provision are different. Source L908–913 argues for the stronger productive reading, but the interpretation is not earned by merely relabelling the guard.

## 3. Numerical identity and complete-product controls

### 3.1 Four identity questions stay separate

1. In the actual model there is one created bearer e, with two contributing roots in the coalescence program. This is within-model identity, not an inference across worlds.
2. Its ordinary state and all created bearer-event states in the small ontology can be the same across input interventions.
3. Its complete causal derivation can differ even when those states agree.
4. Whether those occurrences are the same numerical metaphysical effect across counterfactual worlds requires an independently justified identity/counterpart interpretation. Equal keys or states do not prove it.

The source's CND is about actual entire production in a fixed respect; it does not require a universal origin-essentiality thesis. Neither endorsing nor denying transworld origin-essentiality settles the within-world attribution problem.

### 3.2 Non-idempotent firing and real-effect-in-bearer controls

The redundant program merges two e-producing firings into one event key. A non-idempotent occurrence interpretation counts both firings, yielding two occurrences. This is a substantive alternative ontology, not a discovery that the same existing target has two elements. The direct coalescence program has one joint firing, so it does not rely on this duplicate-firing merge. It instead needs a defensible primitive joint productive relation.

A separate contribution relation records each active root's actual incidence in a production. It is distinct from both intrinsic act tokens and created bearer count. A single bearer can have two such incidences. Adding these relations to a causally enriched history distinguishes the joint and solo cases while preserving e's bearer key. That is enough to display the source's two-effects-in-one-product possibility.

There are then two serious readings:

- If the incidences are only explanation/proof records, the output equality survives, but the records cannot be counted as additional extramental created product to prove Majmu's greater/more claim.
- If they are genuine created effects in e, the complete created product is enriched and the joint/solo total differs. But their reality, individuation and role in the intended effect require argument. Counting a root label does not independently provide it.

There is an additional eligibility trap. If a purported created requisite is individuated only as 'B's contribution', requiring A alone to produce that very B-indexed relation can make the target impossible by construction. This cannot demonstrate a missing pure standing perfection without an independent argument that the same numerical created requisite is an eligible source-neutral target for A. Real source-independent states, work, phases or powers in e would be better discriminants than ownership labels.

## 4. Conservation, absorption and interaction

A generic Lean theorem proves: for any effect type, if a measure on the **complete created output** is amountA for the solo case and amountA+amountB jointly, with amountB positive, then joint and solo outputs differ. No injective provenance map is required. Cancellation in a production algebra independently gives a sufficient condition: x⋆y=x forces y to be the identity.

These are independently meaningful conditional results. They do not establish that original creative acts have conserved positive created-output weights. Treating an uncreated intrinsic act as an emitted created quantum would violate the action/effect distinction. Counting distinct productive derivations as positive output mass would assume the disputed trace realism.

A saturator with explicit waste illustrates the valid physical-style use: the visible state can remain1 while total output changes from (1,0) to (1,1). All side effects are counted. A bilinear direct merger a+b−ab illustrates the different possibility: the interaction correction cancels the duplicated quantitative count. It is an exact identity, not an independent account of the original causal reality of that correction.

The finite algebra scan covers all commutative binary operations with identity0 on sizes2,3,4 and filters associativity:

- Size2:2 candidate tables,2 monoids;1 absorbing and1 cancellative
- Size3:27 candidate tables,9 monoids;8 absorbing and1 cancellative
- Size4:4,096 candidate tables,94 monoids;90 absorbing and4 cancellative

This explores reversible group-like and noncancellative interaction behavior as well as familiar additive/saturated examples. It is exhaustive only for that finite algebraic signature. Finite closed monoids do not faithfully represent unbounded positive additive production: a nontrivial finite system cannot carry an indefinitely increasing natural-number productive amount under closed iteration. Therefore these counts cannot estimate the prevalence, plausibility or exhaustion of metaphysical production laws.

## 5. P7 and the coalescence resource account

Three primary interpretations and a mechanism refinement were examined.

### A. Nonentity production law

A direct hyperedge has A and B's intrinsic acts as joint productive grounds of the contingent event. Its rule is a mathematical specification, not a third efficient entity. In the reduced graph no new original root exists, each intrinsic act is unreceived, and no path efficiently produces the peer's act or capacity. The effect is efficiently explained by the acts at this interface.

This is a formally consistent challenge to deriving exclusive provision merely from those structural premises and exact output occurrence. It is not full source-fidelity: whether such primitive joint efficacy belongs to two independently perfect original agents is exactly the source-mode question. Demanding another agent to make a nonentity law simply assumes away the rival. Treating the declared function as its own metaphysical explanation is equally inadequate.

### B. Created mechanism

An actual jointly created m followed by e has complete history {m,e}, while an unmediated solo route can have {e}. The complete-product comparison then fails, although the selected e state survives. If A makes m and B acts through it, the graph is acyclic but B's realized efficacy depends on A's created resource. This preserves the intrinsic-act/capacity sort at the graph level; the transfer of that dependence to received constitutive perfection is still M1/P2b, not a graph theorem.

A stronger created-mechanism variant produces the same m and e in solo and joint profiles. It preserves the bearer-event history and merely moves primitive joint production from e to m. Thus 'there is a created mechanism' is not by itself an exhaustive objection or solution.

### C. Intrinsic jointly instantiated relation

A merely relational truth about two independently issued acts is not automatically an efficiently received intrinsic state. It can be described without a third concrete entity. If instead the proposal treats a real shared token as the very same intrinsic act of both subjects, it violates the act-bearer distinction. If two own states are efficiently made by the other, it violates intrinsic nonreceipt or creates a cycle. If an external real coupling is produced, it returns to the created-mechanism case.

A primitive jointly grounded relation can remain without those efficient donations if its role is external realization, but then the original-source norm must decide whether that realization is an illicit external completion. These cases are semantic alternatives to be appraised, not an assertion that every conceivable relational metaphysics has been exhausted. No fresh kernel proof of intrinsic originality is assigned to a relation merely because it is named 'intrinsic'.

### D. Where the mechanism regress actually begins

The finite DAG scan tries10,104 acyclic two-root programs with1–4 created nodes and nonempty earlier-parent sets. Of these7,932 have a jointly supported terminal event. Every such graph has a first jointly supported node with no earlier jointly supported parent. This is the precise primitive combination point.

The general Lean theorem proves that if every jointly grounded node at finite derivational depth requires an earlier jointly grounded node, no such node exists. This does not assume a fixed finite graph size. Its interpretation burden is substantial: the 'every joint event needs an earlier joint mechanism' premise is stronger than the source's demand for an efficient explanation of each contingent occurrence. A direct A/B productive basis is already such an explanation in the reduced semantics.

## 6. Exact premise-preservation judgment

The new interpretations retain or test these commitments at expressly limited scope:

- Actual agency uses declared executable act/command constructors. The intended event-set contents are genuinely interpreted in the separate command interface.
- Two rigid bearer identifiers and their own act tokens remain distinct. No peer supplies their intrinsic acts or standing constructor in the operational graph.
- Productive rule graphs are acyclic and contain no third original root. This is efficient-graph nonreceipt, not a complete theory of necessary-by-itself existence.
- CND is applied to the whole created event's exhaustive actual support, not silently transferred from emissions. Cooperative instances can have no entire owner; duplicate derivation sufficiency is distinguished from entire provision.
- Equal commands construct every target in the declared event-set family. This is not all possible metaphysical powers or pure perfections.
- Complete output intentions are met in matching-goal profiles; the source-worthy admissibility of those profiles is not derived.
- Remaining intrinsic commands are fixed under diagnostic suppression; complete operative-rule identity is explicitly not preserved in the coalescence program.
- The contingent occurrence has efficient root grounds. A separate law-maker is neither inserted nor excluded by metaphysical fiat.

Nonabsorption, P6-to-whole-provision, M1/P2b's external-efficacy application, and the source's original-concordance classification remain the claims tested or awaiting warrant. Their denial is not by itself a failure to preserve independent upstream assumptions. Full original necessity, complete intrinsic wisdom/knowledge, source-worthy exact-will admissibility, and empirical coverage of the whole actual connected created order remain unrepresented or only partially represented. Those are the reasons this is not a full-source countermodel.
