## The discriminating application to DAEE

DAEE's [sound-reason account, §2](https://github.com/theislampill/daee-epistemics/blob/c86b3c6673147b8802fe222373a165a37d4d24a8/atomics/skill/references/sound-reason-epistemology.md#L124-L155), lists properly functioning faculties, truth aim, suitable environment, and high objective probability. Its externalist explanation correctly denies that the subject must understand the producing mechanism. The question tested here is what its environmental condition must mean.

On a **broad reading**, it asks whether the person has the right general kind of faculties and inhabits their normal sort of world. That interpretation leaves a local broken evidential dependency unrepresented. A successful correction of distraction, motivation, or conceptual confusion can leave that dependency unchanged. Thus showing that the person is now attentive and reaches the correct answer does not establish that the particular route has become adequate.

On a **local reading**, suitability also concerns the operation actually responsible for this acceptance and the circumstances that bear on its informativeness. This can preserve the required distinction. The intervention may repair the relevant dependency, provide a new adequate ground, or reveal that the current route should be suspended. It need not first certify the health of the person's entire cognitive constitution.

The difference is substantive. A reason to discount a particular indication can be obtained without a reason to reject the indicated proposition. Equally, a separate reason to retain the proposition cannot make the discounted indication reliable. It is this two-way constraint that distinguishes epistemic correction from a procedure whose sole success condition is securing the preferred answer.

This does not require an observer to eliminate every imaginable misleading circumstance. The relevant adverse circumstance must be warranted in the case, or explicitly stipulated when testing an account. A merely possible unreliable route does not defeat an actual competent recognition. No such adverse circumstance has been established for the retained Creator-recognition appraisal by this investigation.

## The necessary-target transport test

The new primary qualification cannot automatically settle initial recognition of a necessary truth. This is where the inherited CP19 positive result controls the application.

Suppose an attempted formalization uses an ordinary probability measure over a state space on which the target Q is true everywhere. For every positive-probability conditioning event A, the conditional probability of Q remains one. This follows because A and A intersected with Q are the same event. Adding more local circumstances to A therefore cannot make that output-truth statistic distinguish competent use from irrelevant assent.

This is an elementary semantic observation, not a new theorem or a refutation of the primary account. It applies only to the stated possible-state probability implementation. It does not establish that every account of epistemic or objective probability must use that implementation. Nor does it require importing metaphysically impossible Creator-free states or treating a contingent rescue prediction as the necessary target.

The positive factive-ground route remains available. CP19 verifies a relevant inference when independently known contents genuinely operate as grounds. The competent addition-and-equality route to an arithmetic conclusion is richer than the claim that its conclusion is necessary. Its warrant depends on what is known and actually used; an investigator cannot transfer it to another route by writing the proof beside the same answer.

One could instead assign probabilities to a contingent event such as whether the proposed ground was actually operative. That is a different target. The numerical value would then require evidence and a justified model of uptake; it would not derive basing from the necessity of Q. This identifies exactly why a probability field or another posterior cannot replace the missing application evidence.

The conclusion is affirmative and bounded: local adequacy is a meaningful anti-luck constraint, and existing content-using inference can satisfy a stronger positive appraisal. Neither claim supplies an unperformed identification of initial Creator recognition.
