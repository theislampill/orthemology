## Strong affirmative arguments actually present

### Necessary bearer and complete perfection

V02L0071–0083 supplies an explicit necessary/contingent and created/creator argument, while V02L0100–0120 gives a sustained bridge toward intrinsic complete perfection. A necessary whole cannot receive an intrinsic constituent of its completion from an independent other without becoming derivative in that respect. A mere external companion is not thereby the subject's own perfection. The argument is given under both a partless conception and a conception allowing differentiated intrinsic qualities. It therefore cannot fairly be dismissed as merely defining 'lord' to contain the desired conclusion.

The bridge includes positive principles: the necessary exceeds the merely possible, a producer/giver has prior title to pure perfection, and a genuinely possible perfection without defect is not barred from the ultimate creator while found in its derivative. Those principles need assessment on their merits; the source does not treat them as mere labels.

### Realized efficacy and common sourcehood

V02L0114–0119 and V03L0236–0276 concern complete productive efficacy, not merely possession of an unbestowed partial ability. Efficacy is expressly called perfection at V02L0117. The source reasons from interconnected products, recipient conditions, materials and cooperating motions to the nonindependence of purportedly separate makers. Its examples include humans and their parental matter, air, food and water, so the argument is not wholly tied to its obsolete celestial model.

Ordinary collaboration is treated in detail: each collaborator retains a distinct intrinsic act and power, while external effects may combine. Two independently complete whole-alone producers of one numerically identical effect are rejected; this is not the same claim as denying complementary contributions to a composite outcome. The source then argues that purported ultimate creators cannot obtain enabling power or completion from a superior third source or reciprocally from one another.

### Harmony and foreknowledge are directly addressed

V03L0269–0276 considers necessary agreement, necessary opposition and contingent alternatives. The central objection is a circle in completed productive determination: each will or action would be part of what makes the other agent able and willing to act. Noncausal concomitance is explicitly distinguished elsewhere, notably V02L0109. It would therefore be wrong to say that the author simply overlooks necessary harmony or bans every relation.

V03L0272 directly considers coordination through a shared good or foreknowledge. A proposed good within the created order is downstream from the ultimate makers, while knowledge of a new occurrence answers to the occurrence and the occurrence answers to its producer's will. Foreknowledge is consequently not automatically a productive explanation of the very willing whose outcome is known. This is an explanatory-direction argument, not a claim that divine knowledge is temporally learned from an independently existing creature.

### Intrinsic action is not an external product

V02L0236–0246, especially L0245, distinguishes an intrinsic creating from the created object: the creating may obtain through power and will without a second creating manufacturing that creating. V03L0239 and L0246 deny an outside giver of divine will or intrinsic acts. V02L0188 and V03L0099, L0122–0127 and L0213–0214 distinguish a creature's actual action, God's producing it, and the bearer of the quality. A causal description of the whole does not erase genuine creaturely agency or make the same numerical act the intrinsic action of both bearers.

This is affirmative material for original accounting of intrinsic exercise, with a strong warning against representing every account by one undifferentiated efficient-manufacture edge. Whole-bearer existence, intrinsic necessary quality, intrinsic voluntary act, external product and actual enabling condition must remain distinguishable.

### Truthfulness and attestation

V03L0069–0080 explicitly stages the two attestation premises: the event functions as divine certification, and divine certification is truthful. Several proposed school defenses are surveyed; V03L0078 explicitly says a no-wisdom theory being defended dialectically is not the author's own preferred position. V03L0079 and L0194–0200 argue positively from truth as perfection, false certification as intrinsic falsehood, and exclusion of intrinsic divine defect. These are substantive normative/perfective premises, not consequences of omnipotence alone.

The same source expressly allows extraordinary events accompanying false claimants when counterindications expose them (V03L0201–0202). A genuinely truth-entailing sign cannot certify falsehood, but identifying an encountered occurrence as such a sign requires evidence. Miracle-only exclusivity is rejected; character, conduct, content and other routes can matter. V02L0270–0272's Khadija example presents rationally grounded expectation before an independent revealed assurance. V03L0410 distinguishes the authority's reliability from accurate access to what that authority actually said.

## Necessary distinctions for the modern interpretation

1. An intrinsic attribute can be uncaused and beginningless without being an independently subsisting source. The source explicitly distinguishes these senses at V02L0082–0083, L0106–0109, L0331–0337 and L0365–0368. The essence's necessitating a quality can mean inseparable implication, not efficient production. A formal predicate 'exists at every supplied world' alone does not identify whole-existence original self-sufficiency.

2. Original productive accounting for a source's own exercise need not mean efficient creation of its own existence or manufacture of its own necessary attribute. The source expressly blocks that collapse. The modern source/contribution/occurrence separation can support this distinction; adequacy depends on its actual semantic map, not the names alone.

3. Actual efficacy is stronger than standing partial ability. A reconstruction must neither weaken the source's premise to avoid its challenge nor treat the stronger premise as independently warranted merely because the theorem assumes it.

4. Foreknown nonoccurrence differs from intrinsic impossibility. V02L0181–0185 and the volume 3 agency discussion preserve capacity alongside known nonperformance. Full fixed-background inconsistency is not equivalent to absence of every relevant causal ability.

5. Generic perfection and its particular classification are separate. V02L0383–0385 expressly recognizes disputes over whether affirming or denying a particular predicate is the defect. General agreement cannot settle the contested instance. V02L0341 denies a human exhaustive numbered attribute list.

6. Nonenumerability is not a refutation of universal theorem interfaces. The current anchored-field theorem is reported to allow arbitrary, potentially infinite occurrence and contribution types; only its controls are finite, and it does not claim to enumerate all divine attributes or actual-world requisites. The source cautions constrain an unwarranted exhaustive finite inventory claim, not universal quantification as such. No repository theorem was modified or newly verified in this study.

7. A mental option is not automatically externally realizable, but a failed alternative is not automatically proof of the preferred account. V02L0138 and L0163–0168 state both controls. V02L0202–0215 distinguishes ranking imperfect accounts from establishing truth, and forbids rebutting falsehood with another falsehood. These apply symmetrically to source arguments and proposed countermodels.

## What remains genuinely live

The source provides a serious affirmative explanation of why an ultimate source must have full intrinsic efficacy and why product-interdependence threatens divided ultimate sourcehood. The live question is not whether such an argument exists. It is whether the intended mapping from product coordination to a contributor's intrinsic productive completion is independently warranted for each proposed role and countermodel.

In particular, a coordination proposal must be assessed as either actual reciprocal productive dependence, lawful concomitance, dependence on a third standard/source, or some other fully specified relation. Calling it harmony does not prove its adequacy; calling it dependence does not prove the source's required causal priority. The text's own precise distinctions make that assessment sharper.

The truthfulness argument likewise supplies real premises beyond effective power. Their rational warrant and the empirical recognition of certification cannot be hidden in the term 'sign'. Accurate transmission remains a separate layer. Acceptance of a verified conditional at any one layer does not by itself discharge the metaphysical, normative and evidential premises at the others.

Accordingly, these readings support continued serious appraisal of the full-source case. They do not by themselves settle every required semantic assignment or claim source-mode closure. The necessary-being starting point remains provisionally usable with its meanings kept separate; the stronger conclusion remains an open appraisal rather than a predetermined rejection or automatic promotion.
