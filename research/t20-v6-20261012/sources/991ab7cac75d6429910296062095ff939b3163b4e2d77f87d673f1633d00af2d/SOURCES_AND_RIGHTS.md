# Source use and distribution

The analytical reports, diagnostic implementations and tests are authored for this delivery. They distinguish original analysis from inherited theorem statements and source reconstructions. Existing user artifacts are identified by hash but are not recursively repackaged.

Classical Arabic locator cues are brief public-domain authorial passages as encountered in specified electronic witnesses; modern editorial matter and whole scans are not reproduced. Contemporary publications are linked and analysed, not redistributed in full. The Koons–Pruss article is published under CC BY 4.0, but this package does not include its full body. Its authors, title, DOI and publication details are retained in the ledger.

No endorsement by the cited authors, publishers, source hosts or Orthemology owner is implied. An electronic witness, a publisher abstract and a critical-edition scan are separate evidence objects. Known access limits, uncertain editions and unmatched quotations remain explicit. No font, toolchain, library dependency, private conversation or repository snapshot is bundled.
