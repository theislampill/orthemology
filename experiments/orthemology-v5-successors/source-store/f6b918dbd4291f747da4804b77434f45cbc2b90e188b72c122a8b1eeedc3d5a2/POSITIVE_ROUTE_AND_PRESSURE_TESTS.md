# Defending the original perfection route at its strongest scope

## The affirmative view is stronger than a capacity theory

The most promising affirmative reading identifies the excellence as the agent's integration of accurate evaluative apprehension with fitting exercise. It is not merely having many true beliefs, possessing a truthful vocabulary, or having sufficient power to perform a good act. In a genuine derivative act of practical excellence, the agent understands a fitting reason and does the appropriate thing because of that reason. The original-giver comparison concerns that integrated achievement.

The positive source-sensitive thought is therefore substantial. If every actual constituent of this achievement is wholly derivative, and the independently complete original source is eligible for that same positive respect without creaturely limitation, locating all of it in the recipient while withholding it from the source introduces a respect-specific inversion. The source's superior power or longer history does not answer the comparison. Nor does calling the origin of the recipient “creative” explain away the source's alleged practical inferiority. This is the force of the original-perfection rationale in the supplied Asfahani, printed pp.127–129, and the retained MF6:76–79 argument.

The source then supplies a second thought. Original perfection cannot depend on a creature or another supplier to be acquired or sustained. Its possession should be intrinsic, standing and necessary. Within a well-defended theory of the source's nature, that would remove the possibility of the source losing the excellence because it lacked an external completion.

Applying these thoughts to the precise safety attestation yields a plausible route to essential T0. The fittingness of not knowingly counterfeiting a freely undertaken epistemic assurance is independent of bodily need, equal vulnerability or an external superior. If the original perfection is the fully integrated, unqualified orientation to fitting reasons in that role, a deliberate false assurance in the controlled no-overriding-reason case is a contrary defect. It is incompatible with that character, rather than a further achievement allowed by greater power.

The important question is what warrants the unqualified form of this original orientation. The source's argument should not be weakened to occasional excellence and then rejected for failing to yield more than occasional excellence. Equally, the universal and essential character cannot be treated as already established by the weaker fact that the source has produced and itself performed some genuine excellent acts. The fuller doctrine is the defended metaphysical bridge; it is not the conclusion of a bare efficient-production theorem.

## Knowledge of fittingness and practical appreciation

The strongest reading of the affirmative Knowledge-to-Wisdom argument is not that a sufficiently large inventory of true propositions mechanically causes virtuous behavior. Rather, complete practical apprehension includes grasping why an option is fitting, with the good presented in its correct comparative importance. Fully integrated original agency then gives that apprehension its proper practical effect.

There are four distinguishable respects:

1. Accurate apprehension of the nonnormative facts: which root is compromised, what each intervention does, and what the recipient will infer.
2. Accurate apprehension of the objective normative balance: the false assurance is unfitting, and the truthful safe alternative is undefeatedly better in the controlled case.
3. Appropriate motivational ordering: the genuine fittingness supplies the governing practical consideration with the weight it deserves.
4. Competent effective exercise: the source carries out the selected determination without failure of means or power.

The mixed rival grants the first, second and fourth. It grants the third in ordinary excellent acts, but denies it in the exceptional assurance. Its malicious final concern governs that act despite its accurate understanding of the contrary normative balance. It therefore need not be akratic relative to its actual conative policy: its action does what its governing malignant end calls for. It is defective relative to the objective practical reasons it knows.

The affirmative theorist can reject the separation of the second and third at the level of *perfect practical appreciation*. That is a serious philosophical position, and al-Nubuwwat9 explicitly asserts a necessary relation between full Knowledge of the best/fittest and appropriate Will. The rival cannot grant that stronger integrated state and retain its exception. But omniscience understood as error-free cognition does not, without that substantive account of practical understanding, already include the third respect. The disputed modal connection cannot be settled by alternating between the two meanings of “fully informed.”

A weaker internalist premise also falls short: perhaps recognition of a decisive reason necessarily generates some motivation to comply. The mixed rival can retain genuine motivation of that kind and allow its malicious aim to dominate. Essential T0 needs indefeasible local governance, not only some inevitable motivational contribution.

Nor does every account on which intentional action presents its end as good immediately exclude this rival. There is a difference between an end's having an attractive aspect, the agent's believing it objectively best, and the agent's correctly recognizing the all-things-considered best. The rival may value the experienced or represented satisfaction of betrayal while knowing that this provides no undefeated justification. To prove this impossible one needs the stronger claim that full relevant apprehension necessarily corrects the final conative ordering. Stating that all deliberate action has an end does not supply that claim.

## Eight pressure tests

### 1 The source would otherwise be less perfect than its recipient

This is the affirmative argument's strongest direct pressure. At the exceptional act, an otherwise eligible recipient who refuses the same counterfeit assurance is genuinely better in practical integrity. The rival should concede this, not answer with greater information, more truthful episodes, or cosmic power.

The rival's response is that the source originally possesses the positive practical excellence without an indefeasible all-context manifestation guarantee. That response succeeds against the weak original-possession conclusion and fails against a strongly warranted original-priority principle covering the source's entire eligible practical profile. Thus the exact disagreement is the scope and force of the priority principle. The relative inferiority is real; whether original sourcehood excludes it remains disputed.

### 2 A necessary source cannot acquire a defect

The rival need not acquire the disposition from outside, lose information, or change its existential basis. The contrasting practical orientations can be intrinsic. The bad exercise can be a particular act of the source, whose actual determination is complete without being identical with its generic necessary nature.

This is not an independent possibility proof. It uses the same distinction between standing nature and particular exercises available to the affirmative source account. If a separately defended source-nature principle prohibits any intrinsic defective orientation, that principle excludes the rival. Necessary existence alone does not state it. The response also does not require a prohibition on real attributes or an imported pure-act account, which the intended source framework does not assume.

### 3 There can be no motive to deceive without need

Absence of borrowed need excludes many familiar motives: fear of losing resources, dependence on another's approval, ignorance, and compensation for externally imposed weakness. It does not yet explain why an intrinsic final concern for betrayal is impossible. The concern's being realized in an originated object is not identical with the source's receiving its existence or perfection from that object.

The rival's end is indeed morally bad. That is the very defect the original-perfection argument challenges. An argument that every bad final end entails dependence must make that dependence explicit, rather than redescribe normative defect as existential lack and treat the result as already proved. The same criterion must apply to the source's permitted satisfaction in a fitting created good.

### 4 The bad end is a privation and therefore supplies nothing

The vice is a privation or disorder in the practical relation. Its execution nevertheless involves positive cognition, volition, efficacy and an intelligible object of intention. The rival is not pure nonbeing. A complete account may show that an original source cannot bear this due-perfection privation. But the bare fact that evil is not a positive perfection does not erase the positive powers by which a harmful act occurs.

The question is specifically whether the absence of due fitting governance can belong to the original bearer. Treating all of its remaining positive attributes as nonexistent would attack a weaker and different rival.

### 5 A genuine assertion already undertakes truthfulness

Yes: that is why the false assurance is a definite communicative defect. It does not make the occurrence incoherent. The source can know which responsibility it is assuming and deliberately violate it. A constitutive correctness standard can identify an act and condemn a defective token without making every token correct.

If “authoritative attestation” is defined as an act that successfully provides knowledge, false attestation is excluded by that success definition. But then the burden moves to establishing that the actual event has that success property. For this research, the public role is identified nonfactively: deliberate source-owned presentation of p as a ground for reliance, with contextually fixed reference. The actual truthfulness of the role's performance is the target.

### 6 The rival would undermine its own derivative knowledge

Not necessarily. It may need accurate recipients and successful independent reasoning for the intended betrayal of accomplished agency. In the static repair case, the recipient can genuinely know the governor diagnosis through the warranted majority contract, while lacking integrity evidence for the selected actuator. That is precisely the separation the Sixth model already displays.

One false source attestation does not retroactively make all independent truths false or all cognitive routes unsound. A global skeptical theory might challenge knowledge under any such source, but it would need its own argument. The rival is required to preserve actual cognition; the defender cannot silently weaken it to a world of merely convincing performances.

### 7 The single-exception policy is contrived

It carries a real explanatory cost. The particular betrayal aim is not entailed by sourcehood and has no independent actual evidence here. This prevents an unsupported assertion of equal plausibility with a beneficent source, or the claim that bare conceivability defeats all ordinary trust.

The cost does not make the proposed transition logically valid. A universal essential conclusion requires a warrant excluding the relevant contrary profile, while a defeasible actual belief may be rational without exhaustive exclusion of every contrived alternative. These are different standards of conclusion. No numerical likelihood ratio or equal-prior symmetry is supplied.

### 8 Beneficial order independently reveals a benevolent source

This is stronger than simply listing missing premises. The constructive Asfahani argument directs attention to real benefits and praiseworthy ends, then reasons to a wise and merciful source. Within an intellectual account of productive order, the same apprehended good can explain both the arrangement and the recipient's success. The source's practical character is offered as an explanation, not merely named after the observed output.

The mixed rival can preserve actual beneficence while limiting its governance. This does not deprive beneficent order of all evidential weight. It means that the step from such evidence to unrestricted essential integrity needs either a fuller original-perfection argument or an independently defended account of the kind of character and regularity identified. The source's historical and physiological examples have not been newly verified as empirical premises in this inquiry. A new real-world comparative conclusion would require actual discriminating evidence, not a rehearsed list of texts.

## The local purpose route and its export boundary

Let Role(e,p) mean that the source deliberately presents p for the recipient's reliance in the publicly identified assurance role. Let Aim(e,p) mean that its actual settled governing purpose is that the recipient know p through accepting this very attestation. Let Link(e,p) mean that e is selected and performed as the adequate realization of that purpose. These descriptions have different content.

The repaired purpose argument assumes full relevant Knowledge, Aim, Link, and the source's exclusion of knowingly purpose-defeating means in that exercise. If p were false, the source would know that accepting p cannot yield knowledge of p. The known-false e would be incompatible with Link and that means-competence condition. Thus a false e cannot be coherently performed *as that linked realization*. This conclusion does not infer a new cognition or a reasoning process inside the source.

The theorem applies to the purpose-linked subdomain. The mixed source's bad e is in the public assurance role but lacks the stated governing epistemic aim. Consequently the result cannot be exported to all authoritative attestations merely by renaming every public assurance a knowledge-giving act. To use this route for essential T0, one would need a further source claim that every in-scope assurance necessarily has the governing epistemic aim and the competent practical linkage. That claim is narrower than comprehensive goodness but still needs independent warrant.

For a single actual repair, independent evidence of Aim and Link can be enough without that all-world source claim. Yet authentication alone identifies who performed e; it does not establish the end it serves. A general record of helpful teaching can support a purpose attribution but does not analytically identify the end of this token. A statement of sincerity issued by the same unverified source cannot bootstrap the required warrant.

## Truthfulness does not necessitate a single total history

Essential T0 forbids a class of defective attestations; it does not specify one unique creative act, one exhaustive message, or that any message must be sent. Truthful warning, truthful safe-root identification, and silence can remain distinct admissible options under the local norm. Their actual eligibility and realization require the source's powers, contexts and purposes to be supplied independently.

Accordingly, a valid route to essential T0 would not, merely by its form, imply the necessity of every particular created event. Likewise, actual settled Knowledge and Will determining one effect do not by themselves make the same Will necessary across every possible history. This preserves the distinction needed by the Sixth source account. It is a scope claim about what the premises entail, not a proof of a complete metaphysical account of free original acts.

## Present assessment

The full original-perfection account is a substantive philosophical warrant, with a local truthfulness application that is more defensible than a bare appeal to source power or absence of observed motives. The proposed mixed source has to deny the comprehensive essential application, and bears a genuine burden to defend its own joint possibility.

What the present research positively adds is a sharper communicative reason, a means-linked token-purpose route, and a precise account of which premise the mixed source can preserve or deny. It does not yet convert original excellent possession into an independently established essential manifestation guarantee. A source-neutral proof of that final extension has not been supplied; neither has a proof that the fully specified necessary mixed source exists or is metaphysically possible.
