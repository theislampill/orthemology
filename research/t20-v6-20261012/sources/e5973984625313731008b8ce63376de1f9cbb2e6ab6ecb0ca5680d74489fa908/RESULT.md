# Stronger rivals for testimony and institutional continuation

7 October 2026. AI-authored bounded research continuation. Existing T20 results and both prior archives remain credited and unchanged. This report supplies useful additional experiments and exact limits. It does not issue a closure verdict; the owner must approve the eventual obligation-by-obligation case.

## Results

1. Actual voluntary transfer and source causation do not alone determine communicative purpose. The same physical token transfer occurs when the sender commissions a report and when the sender sends a ring for repair. An independently retained, correctly interpreted source instruction and the witnessed transfer discriminate the chosen bounded commission. Neither establishes the truth of the report or the sender's private sincerity.
2. Stronger appropriation rivals survive substantially more evidence than a copied identifier. A fresh challenge response can be fabricated adaptively, obtained through authorized maintenance or stolen access, or appropriated after the source dispatched it elsewhere. Actual source emission and authentic prior commissioning still fail to show that this particular response was dispatched as this bearer's attestation.
3. A constitution can lawfully change what counts as an authorized successor. In the executed model, a valid amendment makes a freshly appointed independent replacement the office-holder. The same service, same authentic vote packet and same appointment fail when the founding rule is entrenched, a voter has lost membership, publication is absent or the appointment precedes effectiveness. Causal descent does not cure those defects. Temporary service outage does not annul a valid appointment.
4. Identical received translated words can come from an original assertion, a quotation or a true report of an embedded false assertion. Independently retained discourse frame and original context jointly determine the tested source-assertion judgement. Correct translation of the embedded words does not recover omitted speech-act structure.

These are conditional finite models with stated observation assumptions. They do not authenticate revelation, a real historical token, a constitution's external legitimacy, or a person's numerical identity.

## 1. What is stronger than the credited earlier cases

The accepted resumption supplied correct results once original force, source context, full-record anchors and a fixed governing office were supplied. Its counterexamples included a copied lineage label, independent twin and false owned report. Those remain useful. Here the rivals can share genuine source access, real source emission, authentic prior statements, the same appointment text, and the same complete received fragment. The disputed conclusion can still differ because the source intended a different task, a particular sign reached a different bearer, the authority-generating history changed, or discourse structure was lost.

The test is not whether adding a field called intended or legitimate makes a predicate true. Histories execute actions; observation menus project a witnessed action, an independently retained original instruction, a founding text, a dated institutional event or a full discourse prefix. The accuracy and independence of those observations remain application premises. A fabricated record saying that an event occurred is never equivalent to observing that event.

## 2. Custody, purpose, scope and sincerity

Eight token histories are generated: attestation, repair, sealing, theft, finding, coercive taking, arrival-only credential and a conditional instruction followed by theft before its voluntary-handover condition is satisfied. Each has the identical received ring and the identical carrier claim: the sender sent the carrier and inventory is one. The interpreter tracks token custody and actual voluntary sending. It activates the stated inventory commission only when the separately uttered conditional instruction is followed by its voluntary handover.

The repair and attestation cases have the same real giver, recipient, token and transfer event. Their different purposes appear in the original source instruction. General employment is constant and does no discriminating work. The conditional-instruction/theft pair prevents the original instruction alone from being sufficient. Over the declared world set the unique minimal sufficient observation family is {prior instruction, transfer}.

Inventory is separately realised as a state containing zero or one item. The carrier always reports one. Every token history is crossed with both states and with a private-sincerity variable, giving 32 worlds. A commissioned false report remains commissioned; its falsehood makes the finite scoped-veracity property fail. There is no observation family in this menu that determines report truth or private sincerity. Publicly undertaking a communicative act and privately believing its content are different questions. The model does not purport to settle every philosophical account of illocutionary intention.

Nubuwwat 771 is especially important here: it distinguishes voluntary sending, certification-purpose and later envoy falsehood. The model's explicit source instruction is one possible discriminator. It is not a universal requirement for attestation. The source also permits nonconventional intentional signs understood through known circumstances and character. No newly authenticated historical observation is available here to independently establish those circumstances for a particular real claimant.

## 3. A source-produced sign can still be appropriated

Seven challenge histories run at each of four challenges, for 28 worlds:

- Envoy: the source gives the reporting instruction, emits the response and dispatches it to E.
- Maintenance: the same source emits and dispatches a response to E for a terminal test, explicitly excluding the inventory mission.
- Fixed replay: E reuses an old response of zero.
- Adaptive counterfeit: E reads the challenge and fabricates the same apparent response.
- Stolen access: E gets a real source-terminal response using stolen access.
- Aborted envoy: the authentic prior instruction exists, but delivery does not occur; E fabricates a response.
- Diverted to another: the original instruction exists and the source emits the real response, but dispatches it to X; E appropriates it.

At challenge 2, the identical fresh challenge/response leaves six candidates. Observing actual source emission reduces this to envoy, maintenance, stolen access and diverted-to-another. Reading the authentic original instruction leaves envoy and diverted-to-another. Inspecting actual dispatch to this bearer selects the envoy. This is an executed surviving-rival calculation, not a statement that every extra certificate helps.

The menu's minimal sufficient family for the narrowly specified sign-mission judgement is {original instruction, dispatch record}. In this selected history class, source dispatch of a response is already causally downstream of source emission, so a separate emission observation is redundant once a faithful dispatch record is available. This is model-specific sufficiency, not a universal independent-evidence checklist. Likewise the computed singleton access-history discriminator for origin is a correlation in this restricted class, not a claim that an access log generally authenticates an output. If actual source logs are compromised, even the proposed positive observation route loses its warrant.

The response is an ordinary toy token. It has no stipulated cryptographic unforgeability. A fresh challenge removes only the fixed replay rival at nonzero challenges; it is not a proof that a historical miracle occurred, a declaration of divine intent, or a test of all the premises of the source's prophecy argument. The source's fully qualified notion of a genuine sign entails truth; the substantive application question is how one independently establishes that this event is such a sign. The program never answers that question by naming an event genuine.

## 4. Amendment, authority and service

The initial declared conventional office uses predecessor transfer. Its adopted constitution names A, B and C as board members, requires two current votes for amendment, provides a publication delay of one time unit, and either permits this type of amendment or entrenches the initial rule. The founding authority is also stipulated to retain a pre-vote member-removal power in this finite example. Whether this is a legitimate real founding settlement is outside the model.

The actual sequence proposes board appointment, receives authentic votes from A and B, optionally publishes it at time 2, and receives an appointment of N by A and C at time 2 or 4. In one rival the founding authority removed B before voting. The interpreter checks votes against the membership at the vote, checks permission to amend against the prior constitution, and applies the amended rule only after publication and the delay. It does not infer a new rule from the fact that a board acted as if it had one. A premature appointment is not retroactively validated when a later amendment becomes effective.

Crossing amendment permission, removal, publication, appointment time, technical descent and service availability gives 64 worlds. For the fixed authentic vote packet, N holds the office exactly when amendment was permitted, B remained a voter, publication occurred, and the appointment time was at least 3. A separately calculated formula checks all 64 outcomes against the event interpreter.

The smallest sufficient observation family for office occupancy in this class is {founding text, membership history, publication record, appointment record}. The vote packet is not in that family because it was held fixed across the entire experiment; it is still an indispensable background input to the substantive argument. A non-vote or different motion would require a different world family. Minimality is always relative to what is already held fixed.

The valid amended regime licenses an independent installation. Conversely, a technically descended endpoint can provide exactly the same read/toggle/denial service yet lack the appointed office. The service trace is directly executed but intentionally smaller than the already credited two-migration resumption; this is not another finite-renewal result. An appointed endpoint can be unavailable and retain its office. Nothing here proves ethical legitimacy from a constitution, or numerical identity from conventional occupancy. If rival founding settlements are both genuinely live and no authoritative independent evidence selects one, the constitutional conclusion remains conditional on the settlement.

## 5. Semantic ambiguity with identical evidence

The controlled discourse has three constructions: an assertion of a contextual location proposition, non-endorsing mention of those words, and a narrator's assertion that the embedded speaker uttered them. It varies the embedded speaker P/Q and time 0/1, giving 12 cases. The original fragment is “I am here now.” Structural translation rearranges it into the same controlled target-language tuple in every case. A lossy exporter drops framing and context.

A fixed valuation makes P-at-lab-at-0 true and the other location atoms false. The outer report that Q uttered the words at time 1 is true because that utterance event occurred; the embedded location claim is false. The narrator has not thereby asserted the location claim. A quotation similarly has no asserted truth value for its embedded content. These controls distinguish semantic force and nested content, not just unequal metadata strings.

The independently retained full discourse prefix discriminates assertion, mention and report. The original context discriminates speaker and time. Neither alone settles whether the source asserted P-at-lab-at-0; together they do over the declared family. A carrier's later quotation record and the exact translated fragment alone do not. The translation remains correct at the embedded-word level throughout. This is a limited grammar, not evidence that an Arabic phrase has been translated correctly or that every natural-language interpretation can be decided.

## 6. Evidence burden and exact obstructions

There is useful further discrimination here, so a claim that only packaging remained would have been too strong. The tests now identify concrete additional evidence and what each piece rules out. The remaining obstructions are specific:

- No independent historical acquisition establishes actual custody, communicative purpose, mission scope or the veracity premise for a real revelation claim. The primary texts articulate the inference and its conditions; they are not a newly conducted historical authentication of all those conditions.
- Perfectly matching public testimony and behavioral records can coexist with distinct private belief in these models. No available observation in the declared menu reveals that private difference. That is not a claim that intention or truthfulness is never knowable through other evidence.
- A faithful witnessed dispatch/instruction route is possible in the toy example. If all such channels are themselves equally compromised or merely copied from the claimant, they provide no new discrimination. The negative control deliberately makes every observation equal while preserving different commission outcomes, and correctly returns no sufficient menu.
- The authority interpreter can evaluate an independently selected conventional constitution. It cannot settle external moral legitimacy, disputed ultimate founding authority or numerical identity by running that constitution's own rules. Further actual institutional work requires a named institution and independent founding/amendment history, neither supplied by these models.
- The semantics has a declared discourse grammar and observation menu. Full natural-language source reading may have live pragmatic or textual alternatives beyond it. Here no additional uniquely identifying historical discourse record has been produced.

These obstructions delimit the results rather than erasing the existing positive finite cases. They also do not establish that no further research could ever help. No closure verdict follows from them.

## 8. Independent challenge and enlarged-history results

The first family above was preserved byte-for-byte in history-v1 before further work. An independent reviewer supplied natural history extensions that defeat generalising its menus. These have now been executed in extended_histories.py; they do not invalidate the original fixed-family results.

### Missing proposals and lost cross-record order

Take the actual base constitutional event sequence. Remove the proposal while retaining all votes, publication and appointment events. Alternatively, keep every event but place publication before the votes at their shared timestamp. The existing interpreter gives office-holder N for the base history and P for both variants. The entire old observation menu is identical across those three cases: service, vote packet, founding text, membership history, publication record, appointment record and technical history. Their differences lie in the omitted proposal and in cross-record order, which separate per-type projections erase.

Thus the first family's four-item minimal menu is not sufficient over this enlarged family. This is stronger than needing a new identity guard or another signature: every displayed original record can be authentic while their joint chronology remains inadequately represented.

A further explicit experiment uses a separately disclosed zero-delay constitution and all five proposal/vote/vote/publication/appointment events at one timestamp. All 120 event orders are executed. Two orders appoint N and 118 retain P; the old menu still has collisions. A witnessed chronological record retaining the proposal and ordering, together with the independently known founding rule, discriminates these histories. “Witnessed” is essential: a claimant writing a sequence number on a packet does not establish that ordering in the world.

This is an application of T9's already credited joint-history discipline. It is not a new general chronology theorem. The new result is an actual authority-changing counterhistory for this conventional office and a concrete additional observation route.

### Lawful amended remit versus the sender's own adoption scope

A second interpreted charter permits two-current-vote amendments to the appointment method and to the office remit, with publication and a one-unit delay. The executed sequence validly changes to board appointment, appoints N, changes the remit from inventory to safety, then has N issue a safety report.

Two independently acquired source statements precede that sequence. One adopts inventory reports only and explicitly excludes enlargement by later office amendments. The other adopts reports within the office's duly amended remit. A coarse received summary of the two statements is identical. The full source statements are different. All institutional events and the valid current holder/remit are the same.

Under the fixed clause, N's safety report is outside the source's adoption even though N lawfully holds the amended office. Under the dynamic clause, it is source-owned through the standing rule. The report concerns whether a room has any hazards; both empty-room and hazard-present states are executed. The latter supplies a false source-owned report under the dynamic human clause and falsifies its finite scoped-veracity premise. Ownership does not manufacture truth.

This preserves the important positive direction of the original T20 argument: genuinely established prospective adoption can own novel content without a fresh token-level endorsement. A later private change of mood does not silently cancel a still-valid public undertaking. The source's actual rule, revocation conditions and meaning must determine the result. Conversely, legitimate office expansion alone cannot rewrite an independently established narrower source statement.
