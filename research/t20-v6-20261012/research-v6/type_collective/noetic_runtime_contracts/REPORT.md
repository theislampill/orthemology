## 1. The actual procedural structure

The IR is required before dispatch, with initial burden discovery distinguished from burdens newly exposed after a landed operation. It tracks current claim type and level, reason-category, concealment/orientation, confidence and alternatives, restoration target, source status, active owners, held routes and post-operation decisions. Reconstruction is a separate pre-dispatch obligation: a partial reconstruction permits bounded HOLD/PARTIAL, and failed reconstruction blocks ordinary dispatch. This is a specification of evidence requirements, not a proof that a natural-language parser obtains the right state.

Routing prioritizes register/concealment, deformation, reason and imported-criterion gates, semantic and higher-order burdens before ordinary content. A topical label or affiliation does not activate an owner. Held and never-triggered owners differ from active owners. The body must actually be available and its specific operation must be applied; a catalogue row, module name, trace label or syntactic Target/Operation/Result triple is insufficient. An operation must change the bounded claim-state and support the claimed landing.

The recursive owner distinguishes submoves inside one burden from a new burden after landing and reread. Multiple submoves remain distinct when their functions are active; more than three triggers a cohesion check rather than a numerical cap. Conversely, distinct target families, claim levels, source frames or restoration functions cannot be collapsed merely to shorten output. A next burden must be input-, commitment-, graph- or dependency-bound, and its eligibility must be freshly checked. A held initial burden becoming available is not new MRP discovery. Newly generated burdens require their own node, provenance, operation and landing/hold.

STOP, HOLD, RECURSE and PARTIAL are differentiated by live pressure, release permission and limits. Current active modules, next move and released response are recomputed; prior diagnoses/progress and explicitly held material persist until a supported change. This is a real requirement to preserve history-relevant control state. It supplies no numerical descent measure or finite closure bound. The held-set equations summarize carry discipline; prose also permits explicit resolution/discharge, so the displayed subtraction-by-release formula alone is not a complete executable update semantics.

The control relations are explicitly narrow. Plain gradient notation denotes a ranking/preorder over eligible routes, not a literal vector derivative or truth/warrant metric. Divergence/curl are target-explicit diagnostics on a transition-produced relational state; a directed acyclic residual dependency is not automatically a cycle. LoopBreak is partial and requires a named loop, licensed grounding, local change and reread. Language coupling is a partial relation from the agent's execution field to an uncertainty-bearing diagnosed interlocutor field. It is neither an isomorphism nor a surjection nor a guaranteed update. Execution-field closure never establishes persuasion, conversion, soul access or uptake.

## 3. M1 and M8: what the larger contracts qualify

### Valid retained core

M1's activation floor (49–55) requires the exact self-applicable rule and an actual self-undermining result; when absent it expressly says to clear M1 rather than force it. M8's floor (95–105) requires a selected premise and valid or locally accepted consequence steps. Its formal schema (109) is valid classical reductio. Its informal schema is a bounded dialectical consistency test when the consequence is really entailed and rejected by the interlocutor; rejection is not independently a proof of objective falsity.

The surrounding contract reinforces those conditions: recursive owner 484–552 demands a concrete premise/criterion/warrant operation, exact result and changed state; 1137–1138 concedes only a representative subset is structurally checked. IR 71–75, 116–129 and 215–221 keeps affiliation apart from operative warrant and requires independent grounds for motive, sincerity, culpability and interior state. Case-state 158–166 expressly distinguishes sincere clarification/doubt from refusal and blocks diagnosis leaking from a quoted claim onto the requester. The proper contextual reading is conditional owner use, not automatic verdict production.

### Physical ontology and conceivability

M1 119–137 asserts that exhaustive physical ontology predicts inability coherently to conceive explanatory frameworks beyond the physical, and hence is falsified by theistic conception. The needed implication is absent. A finite counterinterpretation has only physical objects, including a physical token encoding `there exists something nonphysical`, and a physical agent able to entertain that token. Entertaining the proposition does not make its existential content true. Even granting the source's phenomenological datum, representation of a purported nonphysical explanation does not contradict the physical constitution of all actual representational events. No broader gate or source-status rule adds the missing semantic bridge. The external quotations need not be verified to establish this entailment failure; their wording and historical provenance receive no fresh verification credit here.

A narrower valid conditional remains: a theory that explicitly entails inability to form the relevant conception conflicts with a verified counterinstance. That extra premise is stronger than exhaustive physical ontology and cannot be silently substituted for it.

### Selective debunking and response interpretation

M1 58 and 136–137 turn debunking into global unreliability. A model can declare one belief-forming mechanism unreliable while retaining independently assessed reliable perception or reasoning used to diagnose it. A selective debunking argument is therefore not automatically self-refuting. A genuinely global rule defeating every premise or inferential faculty on which its own assertion depends may support the conditional M1 operation; the scope premise must be established.

M1 103–107 infers from lack of movement, qualification, a new objection or a subject change that the barrier is protective rather than sincere doubt. The same observable response can arise from misunderstanding, a valid counterargument, partial disagreement, or a concealed motive. The observation alone does not distinguish those cases. The broader uncertainty and target-locus rules constrain a faithful deployment to a provisional reread, with a missing differentiator, rather than the categorical interior/barrier conclusion. They do not retroactively make that categorical sentence valid.

### M8 example entailments

- Guidance (111): absence of one theological or normative notion of righteous guidance does not by itself deny every reliable distinction in inquiry. The reductio works only if the asserted premise actually quantifies over the very guidance/reliability the assertion uses.
- Induction (118): the claim that only a nonphysical ground could support reliable induction is an additional philosophical premise, not a consequence of the stated physical ontology. A physical probabilistic process can exhibit stable prediction; whether this suffices for a specified kind of justification requires a separate argument. The example does not establish the advertised exhaustive dilemma without that argument.
- Moral criticism (119): rejecting objective moral facts can coexist with criticism expressing an endorsed preference, mutual commitment, contractual norm or conditional practical standard. Objective categorical authority cannot be inferred from criticism as such. A contradiction requires the stronger authority claim to be independently present.
- Relativist recommendation (120): recommending adoption within or on shared practical grounds does not necessarily assert framework-independent truth. The self-application objection applies to an explicitly unrestricted truth claim, not every recommendation.
- Fitrah (122): criticism of a specified deliverance does not entail rejection of every cognitive vantage. The universal unreliable-system premise and claimed tawatur exclusion must be independently warranted and matched to the target. They cannot be supplied by the conclusion of the reductio.

These are local failures or missing premises in these examples. They neither refute the conditional methods nor withdraw separately sourced affirmative philosophical arguments. L_check metadata and coherent dispatch structure provide no independent logical repair.

## 4. Faithful cross-front use

The mathematical joint-hull example just reviewed shows that equal separate projections and equal ambient scores can conceal opposite joint repair feasibility. It is a useful countermodel to a proposed representation that discards joint constraints. The runtime bodies independently require preservation of alternative frames, held burdens, dependencies, register state and operation provenance. That supports testing a proposed compression against a stated downstream decision. It does not identify the runtime preorder with the smooth score gradient or prove a noetic convergence theorem.

Likewise, finite-history transport requires exact one-step joint action/receipt/state erasure and aligned initial information; a prose assertion that state is reread does not supply such a kernel law. Native certificate erasure preserves its proved proposition while losing the examined cost information; module identity or source association does not preserve all provenance. These comparisons are interface obligations and bounded countermodels. No unproved end-to-end runtime correctness or general cognitive implementation is imported.
