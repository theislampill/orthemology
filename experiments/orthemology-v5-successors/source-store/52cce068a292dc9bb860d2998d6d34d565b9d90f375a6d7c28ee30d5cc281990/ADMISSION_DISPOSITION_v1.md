## Concrete candidate and its positive reason

Grant the original argument its actual particular g and full unreceived productive status conditionally, rather than attempting to derive them from the admitted correction. The candidate proceeds as follows:

1. Actual proper efficacy must belong to an actual bearer. An uninstantiated rule cannot exercise it.
2. The bearer's original efficacy cannot be furnished by its dependent product, or manufactured by an exercise already requiring that efficacy. Its power is genuinely intrinsic, rather than a passive relay.
3. A merely conditional description of what g does when present does not account for g's whole existential actuality. If complete absence is genuinely open, the actuality/absence contrast remains an appropriate question.
4. The proposed extension says that any intrinsic nature whose complete noninstantiation is possible needs a further actual existential condition to be instantiated. An adequate nonbrute account must therefore locate existence-entailing sufficiency either intrinsically in this g or in a further supplier.
5. Further supply conflicts with g's granted whole-existence originality in the same respect. Intrinsic existential sufficiency would exclude complete absence of this very g.

The reason is substantive: an explanation advertised as accounting for a bearer's whole existence cannot use that same existence merely as an operative prerequisite; conditional power-possession does not perform the promised existential work. A positive intrinsic account would address a respect that an unexplained primitive occurrence leaves unanswered. That is more than preference for a short explanation, lack of an incoming arrow, or a ban on all intrinsic explanation.

The disputed step is the extension in step 4. The legitimate constraint on what counts as a complete existential account does not establish that every actual original bearer has such an account. In particular, failure of self-production does not itself establish existential answerability, the actuality of a satisfactory answer, or the exhaustive type of that answer. The candidate can defend that extension basically or abductively; its non-deductive form is not an objection. But the inherited reasons do not newly supply its full existential content merely by redescribing proper unborrowed power.
