## Strongest rival dispositions

### Unrestricted epistemic intrusion

If the proposal really yields the relevant safety failure, defective reliability, or an undefeated accessible defeater, it cannot explain the admitted epistemic success. That is an analytic incompatibility once the damaging condition is supplied. This paper does not establish that every primitive fundamental basis has that condition.

### Primitive contingent intrinsic basis

It can distinguish its own unreceived existence from the received actuality of dependent cognitive links. It owes an account of intrinsic powers, field closure, content, and warrant. Its possible absence is not by itself a false-belief alternative. It contests N1 rather than directly contradicting actual correction.

### Necessary intrinsic impersonal source

It can grant a positive concrete, necessary, unreceived basis and may grant a common productive bearer. This concedes rather than refutes those upstream endpoints. It contests source-level cognitive adequacy and intentional institution. Its claims to genuine cognition production and normative grounding must be developed, not encoded as flattering predicate labels.

### Irreducibly plural complementary original powers

No one member is individually exhaustive. The proposal denies the relevant universal local completion. It is not a counterexample after individual completeness has been granted; it is a rival to the existence of such a complete bearer.

### Independent endpoint overdetermination

A and B are each individually sufficient for the same numerical endpoint, both actually act, and neither supplies the other's exercise. This is stronger than complementary partial causes. Endpoint sufficiency does not entail full actual-mode accounting. A realist effect-side reception ontology activates the retained M1–M2 reply; an intrinsic-act or primitive-relation ontology requires its own source-support interpretation.

### Two independent actual-mode-complete sources

This is excluded by the retained complete-source argument with a real productive witness and proper unborrowed efficacy. No separate claim that every causal overdetermination is impossible is needed. The important open task is earning this strong role in the actual world, not weakening the user's intended claim into mere modal labels.

### Architectural R5

Still undefeated. A target-sensitive local epistemic interface is compatible with preserving separate track realizations. Neither new graph satisfiability nor a stronger philosophical description supplies the owner-required independent world warrant and extra world-directed consequence. This is not a judgment that R5 is true or permanently unbeatable.
