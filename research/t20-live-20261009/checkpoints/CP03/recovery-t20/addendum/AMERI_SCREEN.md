# Sami Ameri: targeted source screen

8 October 2026. Catalogue-first screening, followed by selected continuous arguments, notes and reflective return. No whole-book reading or T20 closure is claimed. Downloaded source books, chapter extracts and page images are excluded from the public deliverable.

## Findings by work

### براهين وجود الله في النفس والعقل والعلم

The selected contingency argument raises a substantive whole-explanation question; local donor explanations do not automatically settle it. Its anti-regress conclusion also uses stronger commitments than T20’s capacity-specific premise. The temporal argument expressly rejects beginningless successive events, including by an independent successive-addition argument. That changes the disputed premise; finite model consistency does not refute it.

The unity appendix supplements cooperation with an attribute-difference/composition argument, cosmic order and parsimony. These are distinct routes needing distinct assessments. Its Ibn Taymiyyah cooperation quotation was traced to the already-held primary context, so it adds no independent witness. The book’s visible 20:97 reference and the primary electronic 20:176 locator remain an unresolved edition/pagination correspondence.

The short epistemic unit distinguishes personal fitri recognition from public proof for another person. This supports a useful boundary in the daee comparison without establishing exact doctrinal equivalence or empirical proper function.

### مشكلة الشر ووجود الله

Ameri supplies positive system-relative reasons, beyond invoking unknown wisdom: stable order enables deliberation, responsibility and genuine interpersonal consequences. He separately limits certainty about each particular reason. He rejects a uniquely mandated numerical minimum of evil without establishing a fully specified range of equally wise alternatives.

This sharpens the joint-original rival’s burden. If actual coalescence is its valued end, removing the peer removes that end even when the output survives. If its stated end survives unchanged, the additional joint relation still needs a grounded reason. This is our application, not Ameri’s endorsement of plural originals. Product imperfection and incomplete original provision remain different questions. His general perfection commitments cannot simply be imported to settle the disputed bridge.

### براهين النبوة

Ameri’s sign argument uses event-kind, claimant, message and setting; it does not license inferring prophecy from every prodigy. The checked Nubuwwat passage permits signs without the prophet’s explicit challenge or evidential presentation. That does not remove possible divine indicating intention.

The primary Safadiyya context behind another quotation makes a stronger contextual claim: this event, in this manner and setting, indicates a choosing source’s purpose to attest Moses. The specific older-edition quotation remains only substantively supported, not verbatim-collated. Majmu 11:301–302 remains an unchecked attribution lead.

The reading improves the positive target for comparison but supplies no new intention-recovery experiment or independent historical authentication.

### فمن خلق الله؟

Structural triage confirms a focused answer to the creator-of-creator objection, with temporal-origin, contingency, complexity and creator-attribute branches. The author distinguishes this project from a comprehensive proof-of-God book. Further continuous argument reading was deferred in favor of the fuller selected treatment above; no negative whole-book finding follows.

### لماذا يطلب الله من البشر عبادته؟

Catalogue-only screening, supplemented by a publisher article citing the book, identifies the request-versus-need issue. The current detail page provided no PDF link. No book contents or complete book argument were inspected; the article is not substituted for them. This is an access/coverage boundary, not a finding against the argument.
