# Foundational crosswalk: what the synthesis contributes, and what it does not

This document connects the mathematical synthesis to the affirmative Orthemology programme without treating mathematical models as automatic proofs of metaphysical premises. It also prevents the reverse error: treating every premise requiring philosophical warrant as if no positive argument had already been supplied.

**Evidence status:** the philosophical appraisals below are inherited from the supplied T20 research record. This execution does not claim fresh collation of the Arabic volumes. The new contribution is a set of explicit relevance, preservation and application arguments, together with the finite reference construction in `CROSS_FRONT_SYNTHESIS.md`.

## 1. Five relations that must not be compressed into “supports”

A source can support a judgement in several different senses:

1. It supplies actual productive actuality.
2. It supplies information bearing on a proposition.
3. It supplies an admissible premise in a proof.
4. It supplies authority for an action.
5. It supplies a disclosed practical reason for a particular act.

The types and directions of these relations differ. In particular, an informational condition of recognising an act is not thereby an existential ground of the agent. A proof rule that licenses a conclusion is not thereby its actual-world warrant. A rightful command's source is not identical with the evidence by which a recipient recognises that command.

The first constructive gain from the whole-corpus view is to make these relation types part of the retained contract. T20's guarded proof supports show how ignoring role-sensitive dependencies can break later inference. Its geometric and moment results show how apparently sufficient numerical summaries can do the same. Its source investigations explain why the semantic distinctions matter.

This is a bridge to actual mathematical work, not a proposal to call every relation a tensor. A tensor or graph is appropriate only after the represented roles and allowed operations have been fixed.

## 2. Preserving a positive conclusion after a local defeat

### The local preservation proposition

Let pi be an admitted derivation of q under a fixed logic, source-admission contract and ordered guard semantics. Let Support(pi) include every premise, source permission and metadata dependency material to pi's validity. Suppose a revision changes a set D of such dependencies.

If Support(pi) is disjoint from D, and the interpretations of its rules and support identities remain unchanged, then the same derivation is still admitted after the revision.

The proof is structural: each leaf retains its admitted premise, and each internal rule retains the guard inputs that licensed it. Induction on the finite proof gives the result.

This is deliberately stronger in its assumptions than “the changed source name is not printed in the proof”. Hidden common provenance or a changed rule meaning can be a material dependency. A supposedly complete support record must earn that adequacy.

### Why this matters philosophically

A failed argument does not erase all grounds of a conclusion. The Arabic research on surviving cognition and on local versus source-wide defeats supplies the corresponding substantive distinctions. The proposition above provides a restricted formal implementation of one part of that account.

This is relevant to the recovered T17 original-power-to-bearer argument, the later Knowledge appraisal and the responsive-receptivity addendum. A limitation in one small application should not silently demote an unchanged broader argument to an unsupported conjecture.

Conversely, a genuinely source-wide reliability defeat or a corrected interpretation can invalidate more than one derivation. Preservation is conditional on the actual support structure; it is not a rule for protecting preferred conclusions.

### A role change may preserve an observation while defeating its old warrant

The source-audit example is stronger than ordinary “independent support survives”. The same observed report can lose testimonial credibility yet remain relevant to an audited comparison with another verifier.

Thus revision is sometimes a change of evidential role rather than simple inclusion or deletion. A useful formal record must distinguish:

- observation of the report;
- reliance on its source as truthful;
- relation of that report to a separately warranted verifier;
- the current inference that uses the relation.

The three-way parity regression in the new reference implementation makes that distinction executable. It does not establish the credibility of any actual report.

## 3. Particular conditions do not automatically imply received essential perfection

The repentance and responsive-receptivity packets concern an important attempted inference:

> A particular realisation depends on a condition involving a creature; therefore the creature supplies the source's essential perfection.

That inference is not valid solely from the dependence statement. The kinds of dependence must be distinguished.

### A minimal non-entailment construction

Let P represent a standing capacity, W the agent's exercise of it, E the relevant occasion or response condition, and A the particular realised activity. A simple formal structure can have

\[
P=1,\qquad A=P\land W\land E.
\]

Changing E can change whether A occurs while leaving P unchanged. The model therefore refutes an unrestricted implication from dependence of a particular occurrence on E to acquisition of P from E.

This is a deliberately thin logical discriminator, not a model of Allah's intrinsic activity and not a new theological proof. It leaves entirely separate the actual explanation of P, the source's ownership of W, the nature of the condition, and whether the proposed interpretation faithfully represents the original doctrine.

### The positive source argument supplies what the toy model does not

The inherited Majmūʿ discussion distinguishes realisation through Allah's own activity from completion received from another, and explains why absence before the fitting occasion is not automatically deficiency. That is substantive positive argumentative material.

The correct cross-front use is therefore twofold: retain the source-based argument's warranted content, and block a formal encoding from collapsing its distinct relations. Do not demand a new hidden mechanism merely because the finite discriminator does not encode one. Equally, do not claim that the discriminator alone establishes all metaphysical details.

### A connection with type identity

The HasE programme shows why different equality relations cannot be silently exchanged. The analogous semantic caution here is about different dependence relations. The proof-theoretic result does not prove the theological account. It provides an exact example of why a translation must preserve the relation an argument actually uses.

## 4. A benefit is not yet an operative reason—but a local explanation need not explain the whole world

Two episodes can have the same beneficial outcome while differing in whether the benefit was known and intended. Compressing them to an outcome score loses a distinction required by an attribution of wise action.

Let r(e) record the outcome alone and q(e) record whether a specified reason was operative. An accidental-benefit episode and an intended-benefit episode can satisfy r(e)=r(e') while q(e) differs. The fibre criterion then shows that the outcome record alone is insufficient for that reason attribution.

The positive remedy is not to abandon practical explanation. Retain independently warranted evidence of the agent's intention, the means–end relation, relevant circumstances and the source's actual adoption of the explanation.

The ship, wall, mediation and self-praise applications illustrate the source side of this remedy. They can provide a reason for a particular intervention, means or content without individuating every word, occasion or possible world-history.

### Scope-preserving explanation

Suppose a retained record determines q_local, a specified local purpose, but not q_global, the reason for selecting the entire history. Failure to determine q_global does not entail failure to determine q_local. They are different target functions.

This is elementary formally, but consequential in the research. Requiring a local example to settle the global selection question can repeatedly reopen an already supported local argument. Conversely, presenting the local example as a complete global explanation is equally invalid.

### Why numerical burden cannot be the general purpose function

The source research explicitly permits beneficial non-disclosure and goods secured through mediation, effort or a recipient's actual response. A correction that reveals a hidden error can also increase the number of known obligations while improving the inquiry.

Consequently, “minimise recorded burden” is not an independently warranted universal objective. In the finite construction, costs are declared quantities for a specified decision problem. They do not define Wisdom, right authority or the proper purpose of cognition.

This is a substantive constraint on a prospective noetic optimiser: the objective must be justified, and it must not silently replace the target philosophical standard.

## 5. Permission, undertaking and the authority of a particular utterance

The source investigations distinguish default permission, a positively enacted rule, genuine grants, source authority and the force of particular speech.

A permission at time t and an obligation at time t' are not contradictory merely because they concern the same physical action. The indices and governing grounds may differ. Default nonprohibition does not entail an irrevocable undertaking never to direct otherwise.

A genuine undertaking is different. If the source has committed to treatment under condition E, an actual recipient satisfying E may acquire a claim under that undertaking. This need not turn the recipient into a superior lawgiver or a supplier of the source's essential perfection.

### A useful inference architecture

A particular practical conclusion requires separately specified inputs:

\[
\text{rightful source authority}
+\text{actual source adoption of this content}
+\text{the content's force in context}
+\text{recipient/situation conditions}
\Longrightarrow\text{this scoped practical obligation}.
\]

This is an inference template, not a claim that every instance is true. Its value is that it prevents the authority of the source from being substituted for evidence that a particular utterance is an obligatory command rather than intercession, advice or a conditional promise.

The Barīra and recipient-right applications are important precisely because they concern these distinctions in concrete cases.

### Consequence for the new finite contract

Enabled actions and obligation labels should be indexed by source adoption, force and recipient conditions where those are material. A compression that keeps only “the source is authoritative” can erase what is needed for the next legitimate response.

A source-sensitive sidecar is therefore not merely record-keeping. It can be necessary to preserve the action semantics. Its mathematical preservation does not establish the actual authority premise; that remains part of the philosophical and evidential analysis.

## 6. Original productive sourcehood and the same-bearer argument

The corpus's affirmative route must not be rewritten as a generic unsolved question each time a local formalisation leaves something unrepresented.

The recovered T17 argument relates an originally possessed concrete productive resource to its bearer: if the bearer borrowed its actuality in the relevant same respect, the concrete power's actuality could not remain existentially original in that respect. The later Darʾ return corroborates the difference between selective mental consideration of an attribute and independently subsisting actuality.

The subsequent intrinsic-necessity appraisal concerns the **same actual original**, not an unrelated abstract necessary proposition. The common-Creator judgement concerns the relevant productively connected created order and its defended original-efficacy premises.

### What the synthesis adds

It identifies three interfaces that any useful mathematical transport must preserve:

1. Numerical bearer identity across the steps of the argument.
2. The distinction between original productive contribution and created or occasion-dependent conditions.
3. The difference between actual instantiation and an abstract possibility or descriptive predicate.

A model preserving only output values can lose all three. The HasE and moment work supply precise examples of analogous losses in their own domains, but not proofs that every metaphysical alternative is impossible.

A faithful transport must state the relation-preserving map. Without that map, resemblance is a lead for research, not a transported theorem.

### Actuality cannot be supplied by a normative label

Objective standards alone do not establish that a particular received reality exists. The programme's accepted route uses independently warranted actual received reality. An actual correction occurrence, the correctness of that correction, its productive dependence and the warrant of recognising it remain different assertions.

The positive argument must preserve its actual witness rather than replace it with the existence of a formal record.

## 7. Warrant, exact recovery and revelation

The scriptural-recovery work is an especially clear stress test for the separation of targets.

Recovering words does not establish the speaker's authority. Failing to recover a unique autograph does not automatically mean that no doctrine or proposition has warrant. A binary minimax defect of one half is not automatically a posterior probability of one half. A deterministic observation map and a vector of retained expected moments are different information channels.

These corrections do not make the availability question unimportant. They make its premises exact.

A positive or negative comparison between traditions requires matched targets and independently justified transmission models. Surviving oral-transmission records, manuscripts, citations, source relations and historical evidence all belong to the relevant panel where warranted. Calling a transmission mass-concurrent does not itself prove every admissible history agrees on the target; calling a tradition variant does not itself establish a witness to the precise non-identification claim.

The further philosophical question is whether a purported truthful and wise communication supplied adequate access to its intended content for its intended recipients. That is not identical with requiring universal disclosure of every detail. It also differs from the independent problem of later preservation.

### The constructive contribution from the whole corpus

The finite contract provides a way to keep content, source authority, actual adoption, recipient evidence, subsequent audits and inquiry costs separate while investigating them together. It supports exact model-relative tests and explicit missing distinctions.

That can advance the philosophy by locating the real bridge obligation. It does not pre-decide the comparison or replace historical research with stipulated probabilities.

## 8. A relevance test for new objections that does not shield the theory

Let an accepted conditional argument establish q from Gamma under an interpretation I. A new model satisfying only a subset of Gamma while falsifying q does not refute the entailment from Gamma. A model using a changed meaning of a load-bearing relation does not refute the claim under I without a justified transport.

However, either model can still expose an important problem: a premise in Gamma may lack warrant, or I may fail to represent the intended subject. Those are substantive criticisms and should remain live when supported.

A useful re-entry rule is therefore:

> Identify the exact premise, inference, interpretation or evidence condition challenged; state what the proposed objection preserves; and supply the reason it threatens the currently supported argument rather than a weaker or differently interpreted substitute.

This is not a demand that every objection prove a whole rival ontology. A local invalid inference or unsupported bridge can be enough. Nor is it permission to ignore an adverse result because the programme is pursuing affirmative foundations.

Its purpose is to avoid two symmetrical errors: endless diversion into models with no established relevance, and immunity from criticism through moving the target.

## 10. What should go back to Dot

The highest-value new item is the **minimum finite sidecar construction for a joint future contract**, together with its geometric counterpart. It offers a positive bridge between exact provenance and smooth noetic coordinates without demanding that either replace the other.

The implementation also supplies adverse controls joining report/audit synergy, shared-proof cost, source withdrawal, rare conditioning, almost-sure stopping and finite moment monitors. They can test a future interface rather than simply add another commentary layer.

The foundational contribution is not a claim to have settled the Creator or Wisdom arguments by computation. It is a sharper account of what their formal representations must preserve, how local corrections can retain earned positive warrant, and why outcome, actuality, authority, intention and typed identity must remain distinct.

Dot should compare these constructions with its latest work before assigning discovery credit. A result that is already present should be marked recovered or integrated, not rediscovered. A genuine defect in these arguments should be retained. The programme remains open.
