# Response to the warrant-threshold audit

7 October 2026. Bounded reassessment by the author of the earlier actual-common-source assessment. Both earlier records are preserved.

## Revised judgement

**I now provisionally accept one complete original source of the actual connected created order, with less confidence than the Necessary Being conclusion. I continue to withhold sufficient warrant for universal SourceMode.** The threshold audit answers enough of my actual comparative objection to justify this revision. It does not eliminate that objection deductively or establish every premise merely by declaring its cost paid. Its achievement is a better weighting of the positive background and the remaining explanatory difference. No new quotation compels the change. ([Audit, §§2–5](../AUDIT.md); [earlier assessment, §§3.2–5](../../actual-common-source-assessment/ASSESSMENT.md).)

## What is accepted, and what still has to be earned

Three levels must remain separate.

1. **Accepted original-completion background:** the complete actual received field requires original existential provision; an original productive resource has a concrete actual instantiation; a bearer whose whole actuality is received cannot supply that resource's originality merely through genuine local agency. With the separately accepted contingency-as-need judgement, some actual original bearer is necessary. These are substantive adequacy commitments, not the bare predicate “necessary.” I retain their positive rationale: adding received suppliers can explain local production without changing the received mode of the exhaustive account. (T17, P40–54 (historical Seventeenth report, 5 October 2026; retained extraction identity in READING_RECEIPTS.json, body excluded); [Safadiyya, SF-S001-L0031–0034](https://www.islamicbook.ws/amma/alsfdit-001.html).)
2. **Additional positive agency background:** relevant knowledge, power and intelligible purposive determination have their own grounds. I had already credited this background and described a sufficiently capable original as a genuine explanatory resource. The order arguments concern coordinated means and conditions, not mere repetition. Their force does not depend on validating every historical physiological example. ([Earlier assessment, §3.2](../../actual-common-source-assessment/ASSESSMENT.md); [Nubuwwat, NB-S003-L0014–0018](https://www.islamicbook.ws/amma/alnbwat-003.html); [Miftah, M0583–M0590](https://ibnelqayem.com/ar/media/download/86).)
3. **Still additional comprehensive scope:** neither point establishes that one bearer actually supplies every material, productive and preserving requisite. T17 explicitly separates original completion, one actual witness, broad standing power and complete common coverage. Adequacy for the actual differentiated field must therefore be supported as part of the common-source hypothesis; it cannot be smuggled in as unlimited power over every possible object or as already accomplished exhaustive production. (T17, P45–54, P63–68, P70–74 (historical Seventeenth report, 5 October 2026; retained extraction identity in READING_RECEIPTS.json, body excluded).)

The audit respects this separation. I accept its bounded scope enlargement defeasibly because it unifies original provision already required for the field with the coordinated employment of those resources. It is an explanatory proposal about their origin as well as their arrangement, not an inference that every organiser must own its materials. Its required capacities remain a cost. The common account earns that cost through the combined target and fit; necessity alone contributes no such entitlement.

## Does “paying the cost” actually answer the objection?

Acknowledging a cost does not pay it. If §2 merely assumed a comprehensive creator and §3 then counted extra creators as redundant, the argument would beg the question. I do not read the completed audit that way: it appraises scope and unity together, identifies the organising-only failure condition, and admits that a demonstrated failure of its proposed agency would defeat the conclusion.

The principal comparison is this: both accounts owe the differentiated powers, materials, acts and relations needed for the same field. Their shared unexplained detail must be charged to both. On the retained positive agency account, putting those relevant capacities within one original bearer need not invent an entirely new kind of efficacy or a separate controller for each fit. A plural-original account additionally makes some of their ultimate ownership irreducibly separate. The current evidence identifies neither a constraint requiring that separation nor a gain from original plurality. Moving a capacity between original owners can preserve the described outcome without explaining why its original ownership is divided.

That difference is modest but real. “No identified job for an additional original” means no job requiring *independently original* ownership, not that secondary agents or differentiated operations are unnecessary. Nor does it mean two agents must quarrel or select their ends independently. The saving disappears if one agency is just a name placed over the same independent owners; the audit expressly excludes that interpretation.

My earlier assessment already allowed that one sufficiently capable source is the least expansive adequate hypothesis and that its unity has positive explanatory content. I then withheld assent because necessary harmony preserves much of the same fit. That established a limit on the strength of the evidence, but I gave it more defeating force than I justified. Shared fit does not erase a residual advantage; a rival's ability to reproduce the explanandum is not itself a compensating reason for its additional original bearers. Under those retained commitments, I now judge the advantage sufficient for provisional belief. This is an abductive judgement, not a claim that scope has first been proved independently and unity then follows for free.

## What survives of the strongest plural objection

The plural proposal remains a substantive challenge, not nonsense. Each original could be described as having its own power, knowledge and intention; joint attainment need not mean borrowed intrinsic agency. Jami's cooperator discussion supplies precisely that distinction. Common objective reasons could coordinate ends without constituting a further efficient agent. The current proposal has not shown why such reasons require independently original owners or why that distribution explains the actual field better. ([Jami VI, E183–E186, especially E185](https://www.islamarchive.cc/ketab_content/28157).)

I do **not** now require a complete rival cosmology or a proof of its metaphysical possibility before an objection can defeat the common-source inference. A sufficiently clear demonstration that the alleged single bearer hides the same unexplained coordination, or that comprehensive competence costs more than the supposedly eliminated plurality, would suffice. A principled partition of original roles could also reverse the economy comparison. My previous discussion identified where these challenges apply; it did not establish that one of them actually neutralises the remaining advantage after granting the positive agency background.

The audit's restraint about world-specific detail matters. One creator's wisdom has not derived every detail of this history. It would be unfair to require plural harmony to do so while allowing an unspecified single determination. Once this shared burden is removed, however, an additional commitment to original ownership still remains. That is the audit's answer, not simply a repeated assertion that order proves unity.

## Calibration and the universal claim

Accepting Necessary Being while withholding common creatorhood was never formally inconsistent. The former has a more direct adequacy rationale; the latter adds a comparative claim about scope and ownership. Acceptance of one disputed principle cannot oblige acceptance of another. The audit's calibration criticism is narrower and persuasive: I needed a reason for the plural proposal to block provisional assent beyond showing that it survives the failed universal shortcuts. I no longer find that reason sufficiently strong in my earlier comparison.

Those shortcuts remain unavailable. The dependence of a joint product does not automatically show receipt of either participant's constitutive power. Actually providing a whole is more comprehensive than providing a part, but it has not yet been sufficiently established that every fitting partial original undertaking is necessarily a defect of its agent. T19 candidly identifies that extra source-mode judgement. The compressed Minhaj transition must still be assessed against the intrinsic-agency and joint-attainment distinction. (T19, P60–78 (historical Nineteenth report, 6 October 2026; retained extraction identity in READING_RECEIPTS.json, body excluded); [Minhaj II, V02L0111–0120](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/mnhaj-003.html).)

Thus my revision concerns the actual explanatory verdict only. It neither proves universal SourceMode nor excludes unrelated necessary realities. It also does not rest on unexamined revelation or on the complete later Safadiyya study. The new position is defeasible assent to common original authorship of this field, exposed especially to a stronger comparative account of comprehensive agency versus independently original plurality.
