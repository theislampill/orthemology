## The positive source bridge deserves its strongest reading

L0100–0120 does substantially more than observe that two rulers might disagree. The author argues that the necessary bearer must have intrinsic complete perfection: externally supplying a genuinely intrinsic constituent would make the whole itself derivative; a merely external companion is not the bearer's own intrinsic perfection. This analysis is offered on both a simplicity theory and a theory allowing multiple intrinsic attributes. It is not exhausted by naming the desired bearer 'perfect'.

The force of the mutual-completion objection is causal priority. Each participant is supposed to provide the other's enabling completion, yet cannot contribute until already enabled. This is explicitly distinguished from permissible concomitance at L0109, including uncaused intrinsic attributes. A reply claiming that the author simply overlooked every noncausal co-presence or logical dependence would misread him.

The world-maker argument then extends from integrated products to their makers. At L0117 actual efficacy counts as perfection; merely possessing some unused capacity is too weak a substitute for the intended premise. The human-material/air/water examples at L0118 carry the argument beyond the obsolete celestial mechanics of the preceding example. At L0118–0119 the author argues that a genuinely cooperative pair lacks intrinsically adequate power and cannot acquire it reciprocally without the excluded priority circle.

These are genuine arguments for the strong source conception. Their full-role application still requires evaluating whether interdependence of the proposed products entails dependence in each maker's intrinsic completed efficacy, rather than only a condition on a joint product. It also requires specifying what counts as a pure perfection, what its bearer is, and the modal strength of 'adequate power'. The argument may not be reduced to the weaker claim that unbestowed capacities automatically suffice; nor may its contested transfers be hidden inside a convenient definition of source.

## Necessary attribute and independently subsisting source are different

The clearest repeated control occurs at L0106–0109 and L0329–0337. Lack of an efficient producer, lack of an independently supplying other, independent subsistence, intrinsic implication and absence of every relation are different descriptions. A divine attribute may be uncaused and beginningless while requiring its bearer. Life may be a condition of knowledge without a separately existing donor supplying either. The source explicitly interprets intrinsic 'necessitation' here as inseparable implication, not the essence's efficient production of the attribute.

Consequently a formal predicate such as 'exists at every supplied world' does not by itself establish whole-existence original self-sufficiency, independent subsistence, productive adequacy or absence of all relations. A model can faithfully distinguish these predicates; it must not identify them merely because each might informally be called necessary. The source is especially useful for retaining this separation while assessing a modern conditional theorem.

## Nonenumerability is not an objection to universal interfaces

L0100 admits divine names reserved to divine knowledge. L0341 rejects a fixed total of eight attributes and says human beings do not encompass the attributes numerically. L0383–0385 distinguishes agreement on a general perfection formula from warrant for disputed particular classifications.

These observations constrain any claim that a finite human list exhausts every divine attribute or every actual-world requisite. They do not refute a theorem universally quantified over arbitrary, potentially infinite occurrence and contribution types. The current anchored-field interface is reported by its maintainer to allow those arbitrary types; only its controls are finite, and it does not claim a total enumeration of divine predicates or all actual-world requisites. The source caution therefore belongs at the representation/coverage claim, not as a spurious refutation of the general quantified interface. No formal proof has been changed or newly verified by this reading.

## Original accounting is not always efficient manufacture

Additional passages already covered in the continuous first pass sharpen a necessary distinction. L0236–0246 separates the intrinsic act of creating from the external created object. Most directly, L0245 says that the product obtains through creating while creating obtains through the agent's power and will, without needing a further creating of that creating. Different surveyed schools handle succession differently, but the shared contrast shows why explanatory/productive accounting for one's own exercise need not be represented as an efficient-creation edge manufacturing that exercise as a separate object.

L0188 distinguishes a human's genuine act, considered as a divinely produced object, from God's own act of creating it. L0331 says the essence's necessitating knowledge is inseparable implication, not making that knowledge. L0367 adds that self-necessity does not mean the self efficiently produces its own necessity. A semantics faithful to this material must therefore distinguish at least whole-bearer existence, intrinsic necessary quality, intrinsic voluntary exercise, external produced effect, and the way in which an adequate source accounts for each. These need not become new repository types merely because they are listed here; the existing source/contribution/occurrence separation may already represent some or all of them. The question is the justified semantic mapping, not a demand to conflate accounting with a uniform manufacturing relation or to infer a defect in the verified conditional from vocabulary alone.

## Foreknowledge and agency need typed modalities

L0181–0185 insists on a substantive power domain instead of the empty formula 'able over what one is able to do'. It also distinguishes impossibility in itself from nonoccurrence fixed by true knowledge or decree. An act can remain intrinsically possible and within capacity while not occurring. A consistency test that freezes true foreknowledge and then permits its contradiction would not express the source's account; neither would an inference from foreknown nonperformance to lack of every relevant capacity.

L0186–0188 further separates an originating creator from the creature who bears and genuinely performs an act. Creating speech in another does not make the creator the liar; creating a human action does not make that action numerically identical with the creator's intrinsic creating. These distinctions must be preserved when translating the argument into productive roles, attribution or responsibility.

## The attestation route is wider than miracle-only reasoning

L0270–0272 connects prior knowledge of divine wisdom with observed character and conduct, using Khadija's recognition before an independent revealed premise as its central example. This is an affirmative rational-evidential path, not merely a later appeal to authority. It depends on the wisdom premise and the actual evidence of character; the present study has not independently authenticated every narrative used to exemplify it.

The broader first pass distinguishes no being left in erroneous conveyance from a sinless entire biography (L0253–0264), excludes deliberate prophetic falsehood (L0277), and shows why admitting and correcting an error can increase warranted trust. It also warns that vivid inner experiences and striking purported supernatural effects can be misidentified (L0296, L0420–0421). Therefore unusual efficacy, intentional production, truthful content, sender identity, trustworthy transmission and warranted recognition are not interchangeable achievements. This is an agenda for the source-mode appraisal after volume 3, not a completed verdict on that appraisal.
