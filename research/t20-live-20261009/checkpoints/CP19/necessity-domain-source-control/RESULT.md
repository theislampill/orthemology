## Finding
III:18 distinguishes at least three intended readings of necessary-through-self: the original essence originating all else; that whose existence admits no absence (which includes necessary attributes); and the existent subsisting in itself (which excludes attributes as separate bearers). Consequently, a count of necessary predicates or qualified realities is not a count of original Creators. The author expressly rejects productive creation of intrinsic attributes while accepting their necessary entailment by the essence.

III:17 makes the same scope guard explicit: calling an inseparable attribute a part or other, and calling concomitance dependence, does not establish dependence on an external originator. This does not erase the affirmative principle that what genuinely admits existence and absence requires a source distinct from it. III:18 declares one original source; the paragraph is a semantic clarification, not by itself a fresh universal proof of numerical unity.

III:239–241 distinguishes a ground of the possible domain outside that domain from a member of the all-existents domain providing the rest. Independence from something external is compatible with real intrinsic attributes. III:241 explicitly allows multiple instances of the broad necessary label and distinguishes that from the unique original efficient producer. Its stricter subsisting-in-itself usage would call attributes possible in a terminological sense; the author flags the resulting equivocation with the disputed beginningless-possible category. A formal treatment should not silently treat every usage of mumkin as the same modal predicate.

III:241–242 rejects reifying the collection as an extra independently subsisting entity. A member explaining the collection must not mean producing every member including itself; it can mean producing the other members and the relevant arrangement. Thus the exact argument is compatible with a total-domain explanatory role while denying self-production. Generic fact-calculus self-explanation cannot simply be transported into productive self-causation.

## Consequence for existing work
The already recorded essence/attribute, fact/bearer and explanatory/efficient distinctions survive this check. This strengthens their direct primary-source basis. It does not invalidate the checked explanatory-fact uniqueness theorem: that theorem concerns its declared E relation and target membership. It does prohibit reading its total target and self-explanation as an original producer efficiently producing its own existence. No new Lean theorem is needed to establish this textual distinction.
