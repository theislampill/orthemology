## Attribution and exact scope

Exact probability-normalized Wasserstein→Fisher–Rao splitting, the normalized density-power reaction, and Gaussian updates are prior art in Crucinio–Pathiraja, *An operator splitting analysis of Wasserstein–Fisher–Rao gradient flows*, [arXiv:2511.18060v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.18060v3), §2 and §3. These are not claimed as new algorithms.

The present contribution is a bounded fibrewise application: fixed entire observed marginal, measurable conditional kernels, fixed positive-definite Gaussian target covariance, supplied Borel target mean, and finite integrated target-relative quadratic moment. Singular conditional inputs are permitted. The proofs establish positive-step law-level definition, finite-entropy entrance, per-fibre and integrated entropy contraction, fixed-step convergence, and a weak single-shrinking-step trace. A separate finite Gaussian implementation evaluates the established formulas in floating point.

No general all-law PDE existence/uniqueness, unique iterated Trotter limit, finite-mixture reaction closure, finite representation of arbitrary conditional kernels, formal-kernel certification, full-metric projection optimality, target authentication, or programme closure is claimed. Single shrinking-step weak convergence does not establish total-variation convergence to a singular input or finite initial action. The supplied target and any additional invariants remain external obligations. Strict numerical symmetry/definiteness checks may reject rounded or ill-conditioned inputs; finite tests provide no universal numerical error certificate.
