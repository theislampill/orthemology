# Warranting basis and causal occasion

8 October 2026. This governing clarification qualifies the wording in PRIMARY_VERIFICATION.md, paragraph beginning “The response argues from the conditions of individualizing indication,” without changing that frozen file or its manifest. It also governs any causal reading of the related acquisition language in INDEPENDENT_ASSESSMENT.md.

## Corrected reading

Replace the interpretation of “a defective argument cannot genuinely produce the knowledge” with:

A defective argument, in the respect in which it is defective, does not establish the conclusion as an adequate warranting proof. A believer’s retrospective attribution does not repair that defect. Encountering the argument may nevertheless occasion attention, recollection, investigation or a separately warranted insight.

The first two sentences capture the epistemic restriction relevant here. The third is an independent distinction compatible with the passage, not a claim that the author explicitly gives every such causal example.

## What the exact passage establishes

The [already inspected Dar electronic014 unit](https://www.islamicbook.ws/amma/dr-taardh-alaql-walnql-014.html), lines924–928, separates knowing, knowing that one knows, and knowing how knowledge arose. Line926 uses “فهو لا يوجب العلم” for the defective reasoning. Its immediate examples concern knowledge already present before subsequent reflection and potentially restored or confirmed by it. In those particular prior-knowledge cases, later reasoning cannot have originated the earlier knowledge. That temporal point should be preserved, rather than turning the whole passage into a claim only about abstract logical validity.

It does not follow that exposure to any defective argument is causally incapable of contributing to a later episode of knowledge. Nor does the source’s warning establish that every subject who offers a failed argument already knew its conclusion. Its actual-formation diagnosis needs its own grounds.

## Three distinct roles

1. Warranting basis: the competent, truth-connected ground on which the subject knows the proposition. Defective reasoning does not become adequate merely because its conclusion is true or the subject credits it. Sound components may provide a different, narrower basis.
2. Causal occasion: an event that directs attention, suggests a question or elicits an independent recognition. Its occurrence can help explain why the subject subsequently knows without supplying the epistemic support for what is known.
3. Retrospective attribution: the subject’s later account of acquisition. This may correctly identify an occasion while misidentifying the warranting basis, or wrongly claim that a later proof originated prior knowledge.

These distinctions permit real causal contributions without laundering defective inference into warrant. They also permit implicit cognitive processing without making it an articulated argument or assuming it is epistemically adequate.

## Programme consequence

A failed model or invalid proposed proof may remain a productive research occasion: it can reveal a neglected distinction, motivate a counterexample search, or prompt a later successful argument. Any warranted finding must still be credited to the valid reasoning, sound observation or appropriate recognition that supports it. A failure may itself warrant a narrower negative conclusion when the defect is correctly demonstrated; it does not validate its original positive conclusion.

Therefore retaining failed models for their documented research value is consistent with the checked passage. This clarification changes no T17 recognition appraisal and supplies no new independent verification of factive recognition. It corrects a causally overbroad possible reading of the assessment’s wording, not the source text.
