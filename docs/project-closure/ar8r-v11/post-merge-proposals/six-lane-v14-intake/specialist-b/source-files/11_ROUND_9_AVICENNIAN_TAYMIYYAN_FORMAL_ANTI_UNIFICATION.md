## 5. Countermodels to forced unification

### SB-CM77 — recorded root, metaphysically contingent

A token has no incoming edge in the frozen graph but acquires a ground in a

### SB-CM78 — nonborrowed contingent object

An object has no ground under the declared relation in the actual world but
fails to exist in another accessible world. `NB` does not entail modal
necessity.

### SB-CM79 — necessary abstractum, no Creatorhood

An abstract object exists necessarily under the model but originates or
actualizes nothing. `MI` or a weak `NE` reading does not entail `CR`.

### SB-CM80 — necessary productive cause with restricted domain

A necessary entity originates every member of a proper subdomain `D1` but not
all of `D2`. Domain-relative originator status does not entail unrestricted
Creatorhood.

### SB-CM81 — unique necessary ground, impersonal

Exactly one nonborrowed necessary ground exists and supports the relevant
structure, but intentional uptake, agency, praiseworthy-end selection, and
personality are false. Necessity and uniqueness do not entail the noetic
attributes.

### SB-CM82 — plural coordinated necessary realizers

Several mutually non-grounded or jointly basic powers realize the registered
roles. A formal root criterion using a coarse aggregate relation records one
system root, while the fine ontology remains plural.

### SB-CM83 — source Creator predication, open world bridge

The source predicate and co-reference package are accepted inside the source
structure, but two admissible interpretations differ on actual-world referent
or truth. Source architecture does not automatically select the world model.

### SB-CM84 — world Creator predicate, no revealed identification

A world model contains one bearer satisfying a declared Creator predicate, but
no authenticated source identifies it. World predicate satisfaction does not
entail revelation.

### SB-CM85 — same conclusion, different proof architecture

Two systems both conclude “one necessary being,” but one uses essential/modal
causal premises and the other source-relative act/attribute predicates. Shared
conclusion text does not establish theorem ancestry or proof equivalence.

### SB-CM86 — proper-function vocabulary leakage

phrase is used to import Plantingian truth aim, a design plan, and warrant.
