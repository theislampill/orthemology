## 2. What the non-PDF mathematics contributes

P03 asks what a proposed finite-capacity coordination record must actually explain. For one horizon, an ordinary joint law has Gaussian noise S with variance n and a mediator M taking at most N values. If Y and S are conditionally independent given M, then for an interval I of length L,

```text
P(Y + S in I) <= 2 Phi(N L / (2 sqrt(n))) - 1.
```

The [source's section 5][coordination] proves the bound by conditioning on mediator labels, bounding capture with a union of at most N translated intervals, and comparing its Gaussian mass with the centred interval of the same total length. It supplies a matching fixed-horizon encoder and state assignment. A fixed positive capture probability therefore needs an alphabet growing at least on the order of the square root of n.

The useful result is more than a status label: it quantifies the information-carrying burden of complete coordination. The assumptions also explain when it cannot be applied. A short program, small instantaneous controller state or quantised readout need not be the complete mediator; correlated side information and accumulated transcripts matter. Attainment optimises the state marginal at one horizon, not a prescribed marginal or one consistent history over all horizons. The construction is retrospective, not an established forward-causal physical coordinator. This pilot read the ordinary proof of the finite-alphabet bound; it did not rerun its historical controls or establish empirical applicability.

This is an example of content that must remain prominent even though its main reading form is Markdown and its storage path includes a source hash.

## 3. Recognition, sound cognition, correction and tawatur

P04 must explain an affirmative epistemic account, not merely list warnings about reports.

The preserved [Sound Reason source, sections 1–2][sound], distinguishes foundational cognition from inference. Its account makes soundness depend on truth-directed proper function in a suitable environment rather than on having an indefinitely prior proof that one knows. A correct conclusion by luck is not enough; conversely, knowledge need not wait for a subject to articulate an entire reflective justification. Section 1.1 presents argument as clarification or rational reminder when basic cognition is obscured.

Its section 4 then makes a broader tawatur claim. Beyond mass transmission of a particular report, it argues that widespread, diverse and non-collusive agreement in rational and fitri deliverances can vindicate cognitive functioning itself. The intended contribution is to distinguish the deliverances of sound faculties from locally acquired deformation, not simply to count transmitter links. Section 5 gives disciplined consideration a corrective role: identify and remove an obstruction, then restore access to the recognition the account treats as basic. That is the positive recognition–vindication–correction relationship the reader must be able to follow.

These are the source's philosophical assertions and argument, not conclusions of the census or software tests. Its claims about normal functioning, universality, non-collusion and the identification of an unimpaired deliverance require their own source and premise warrant. This refactor neither verifies those empirical/general claims nor diagnoses a dissenter as impaired. It must preserve the source's stronger asserted scope rather than silently rewrite the source as holding only a weaker thesis; equally, attributing that thesis does not adopt it as a demonstrated scientific result.

The [project concrete/sound note][concrete] distinguishes instantiated reasoning from epistemic soundness, and claim-relative pathway adequacy from truth linkage. Its narrower treatment separates machine source-dependence assessments, subject-relative warrant and school-labelled reconstruction. Its pending primary-locus qualifications are part of that document's scope. They are not a reason to omit the broader cognitive-vindication argument from the other source, nor permission to overwrite the older note.

The later [retained-passage report, especially sections 2, 5 and 7][retained], positively retains basic-recognition and source-bound foundational appraisals. It explicitly distinguishes ordinary knowledge without universally prior reflective proof from infallibility in every exercise. Its productive-adequacy discussion distinguishes received existence from an original productive contribution and rejects substituting a generic regress ban or a weak-parts/weak-whole inference. The report maintains source-bound, defeasible affirmative appraisals rather than withdrawing every foundational claim.

A reader should therefore see both what the account argues positively and what the inference requires: recognition and proper operation; the cognitive corroboration argument; corrective reasoning; and the additional productive/source premises in a foundational application. The editor must not label all of that simply “open” or “proved by Lean”. This pilot read the qualification report, not all forty underlying primary passages. Fresh primary authentication, metaphysical adjudication and human-cognition validation are outside this refactor.

**Pilot question:** How is tawatur doing more here than detecting copied reports?  
**Source-bound answer:** The broader source uses diverse concordance as part of a vindication of basic cognitive deliverances and proper functioning, with rational correction restoring access to them. A transmitter graph alone does not establish those premises or that conclusion.

## 4. Actual tensor mathematics and actual conditional geometry

### 4.1 Full moment dynamics and the stronger successor

P05 starts with an explicitly mathematical state, not an informal drawing of a mind. A finite-dimensional numerical vector has a full commutative polynomial-moment functional L_t, equivalently all symmetric tensor-moment grades. The hierarchy is

```text
L_t(1) = 1
d L_t(p) / dt = -L_t(h p) + L_t(h) L_t(p), for every polynomial p.
```

The [tensor predecessor][tensor] classifies representable histories and obtains square-positive-to-representable results under quadratic/SOS conditions. The [later coercive theorem][coercive] explicitly removes that general-coercive bridge restriction: for a coercive real polynomial h, coordinatewise C1 regularity including the initial right derivative, and positivity L_t(q^2) >= 0 for every polynomial q at every time, Theorem G supplies exactly one initial law for the complete path,

```text
L_t(p) = integral(p exp(-t h) d mu) / integral(exp(-t h) d mu).
```

Positive-time full moment records are determinate, and the path has a unique global continuation. This does **not** make a fixed initial moment record determine a unique path: its distinct representing laws can generate distinct paths. The theorem separates complete-path identification from initial-record indeterminacy.

The successor's proof first obtains central-time factorial bounds, constructs one-dimensional weighted measures and their consistent signed polarizations, and uses compact algebraic fibres to obtain vector representability. Only then does it use the predecessor's recovery/exhaustion theorem. Its Motzkin-polynomial example distinguishes a genuinely broader hypothesis from the older sum-of-squares certificate. The successor explicitly preserves classical theorem ownership and treats its participating proof development separately from independent review.

This is established-as-written **conditional mathematics in the source**, with its recorded ordinary evidence scope, not a new kernel replay in this refactor. All grades and square tests are required. A finite PSD check, finite sample, ordered semantic tensor or arbitrary noncommutative object is not the same hypothesis. The source supplies no identification of these moments with an actual human mind. A noetic application needs an explicit interpretation and warranted observation/transfer model rather than merely renaming the coordinates.

**Currentness consequence:** preserve both works. Do not keep the predecessor's general-coercive bridge as an unqualified current frontier after reading the explicit successor; do not edit the frozen predecessor to erase its historical frontier.

### 4.2 Fisher geometry is not merely a proposed metaphor

P06 provides an actual differential-geometric construction. The [finite-audit source, sections 2–3][fisher], uses a strictly positive joint probability law mu[j,i] = w[j] p[j,i] on a finite labelled audit/target space. Its full-source categorical Fisher metric has the block form

```text
g = sum_j dw[j] d'w[j] / w[j]
  + sum_j w[j] sum_i dp[j,i] d'p[j,i] / p[j,i].
```

The retained signature contains the conditional laws and their current mixture q = Qw, not necessarily the audit weights. The source derives negative-gradient equations and a nonpositive derivative of the stipulated numerical objective. It then distinguishes pointwise projected velocity, actual flow descent and the stronger preservation of the full cometric.

The [sidecar corollary][sidecar] fixes positive conditional columns and q. The admissible weight fibre has dimension d = k - rank(Q). Exact preservation of selected **ambient** Fisher norms recovers the weights through

```text
||grad p[j,i]||^2 = p[j,i] (1 - p[j,i]) / w[j].
```

Consequently, a positive-dimensional fibre cannot be represented by a finite-valued exact supplement under that norm contract; a continuous supplement needs and can use d real coordinates on this fixed fibre. The existing [ordinary review][sidecar-review] is conditional on the norm identity, which this pilot also inspected in the controlling source. No fresh independent review is claimed here.

This is not a statement that every noetic application needs to retain all source weights: the posterior-answer-only contract is weaker, a different reduced metric changes the question, and the minimum continuous-coordinate result is not a global chart across changing-rank strata. Numerical objective decrease is not itself epistemic improvement or warranted belief change. The reader must distinguish the **actual conditional metric/gradient construction** from proposed interpretations and generalisations; saying that all Riemannian/gradient work is merely proposed would understate this source.

## 5. A substantive connection across the avenues

P07 connects occurrence-sensitive language (avenue 11) to empirical/specialist validation (avenue 12), through a shared joint-feasibility issue. The bridge is explicitly retained in [V6-R04's report][join] and is not inferred merely from matching keywords.

The exact [source-relative records][occurrences] contain six complete occurrences yielding only two distinct complete tuples, (M,M,G) and (G,G,G), where the fixture's labels denote Messenger and God. Multiplying the three coordinate marginals invents (M,G,G) and (G,M,G), neither attested by a complete row in that selected record set. The incomplete (M,M,unspecified) occurrence is not a wildcard. Reported attribution, reported preference and direct exposition also remain different speech/source roles. This computation concerns the supplied interpretations, not an authentication of the Arabic sources or an exhaustive truth verdict among interpretations.

The [missing-outcome analysis, section 2][missing], separately bounds an assigned-arm success proportion between S/N and (S+U)/N. Its sharp endpoint claims require that the extreme completions actually be jointly feasible. Two binary coordinates can each range over {0,1} while the only feasible joint states are (0,1) and (1,0); the total is then always one, not anywhere from zero to two.

What the connection contributes is a common instruction for faithful representation and optimisation: **retain the admissible joint relation before taking coordinate projections or claiming attainable extrema**. It prevents both fabricated complete readings and unjustified sharpness claims. A Cartesian relaxation may remain a labelled outer bound; it is not automatically an attainable solution set.

The transfer still needs each side's own joint relation and its justification. The linguistic record does not establish dependence between real missing outcomes; the statistical inequality does not resolve pronoun antecedents or authorial endorsement. The [existing review][join-review] retains those boundaries. This pilot read the original records, checker body, missing-outcome report and crosswalk; it did not run the archived checker or reacquire the external primary texts.

## 8. Design decisions and continuation

The sample supports the agreed six complementary reader surfaces, not another expanded directory system. It also shows why a single PDF inventory or a single frozen result registry cannot supply the catalogue.

Use one work-centred editorial crosswalk, with existing source/result identifiers and exact section locators. Generate repetitive mappings only where that demonstrably reduces duplicate editing. Keep the substantive descriptions authored and reviewed. Do not create a machine status from the age of a filename, a count of checks, or the presence of the word “tensor”.
