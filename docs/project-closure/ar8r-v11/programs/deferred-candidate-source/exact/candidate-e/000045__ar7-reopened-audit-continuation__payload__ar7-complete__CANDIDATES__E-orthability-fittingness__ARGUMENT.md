# Candidate E — Recursive corrigibility and standard-transcendence

## Neutral statement

An objectively corrective transition can revise standards that a current state happens to endorse, instantiate, or optimize. But if the correction is objective **partly because those standards are themselves defective or insufficient**, the complete authority of the correction cannot be constituted solely by that same defeated token-level basis. The authority must instead be located in a retained higher-order rule, a trans-state or type-level standard, a holistic normative fixed point, a primitive normative fact, or else the purported correction is merely relative rather than objective.

## The target is narrower than generic constitutivism

The argument does **not** claim that norms cannot be constitutive of belief, agency, inquiry, organisms, or practices. A constitutive standard may be a type-level standard that criticizes a defective token. That possibility is one horn of the result. The target is only **token-autonomous authority**: the claim that all authority for a correction is constituted by the very current token-level standards whose authority the correction defeats in the relevant respect.

## Core distinction

Let a state `s` include a current token standard basis `B_s`: its endorsed rules, operative goals, accepted inferential policies, reward function, or other standards whose authority is alleged to be wholly constituted by `s` as it presently is.

A transition `s → t` is:

- **contract-relative improvement** when it better satisfies an independently stipulated contract;
- **self-amendment** when a retained higher-order member of `B_s` licenses replacement of lower-order members;
- **radical correction relative to `B_s`** when `t` is objectively better in respect `r` and part of what makes it better is that no member or combination wholly inside `B_s` has sufficient authority to settle `r` correctly;
- **globally objective correction** only if its correctness is not exhausted by arbitrary current endorsement or contract success.

Self-amendment is not radical correction relative to the full basis. A constitution can validly contain an amendment clause that replaces many rules, including itself. That is not a counterexample: the amendment clause or its application condition remains the authorizing meta-standard at the transition. The hard case is one in which the transition is correct partly because **the full alleged token-autonomous authority is inadequate**.

## Structural result 1 — No token-autonomous radical correction

Suppose:

1. `Corr_r(t,s)`: `t` objectively corrects `s` in respect `r`.
2. `Rad_{B_s,r}(t,s)`: the correction is radical relative to `B_s`; that is, the relevant sufficient authority of every wholly `B_s`-constituted standard is defeated or shown insufficient in `r`.
3. `TokenClosure(B_s,r)`: every fact sufficient to confer normative authority on `Corr_r(t,s)` is wholly constituted by `B_s` and the token state `s`.
4. **Authority adequacy:** a basis that is, by the content of the correction, insufficient to settle `r` cannot at the same time be the complete sufficient authority that settles `r` in favor of that correction.

Then (1)–(4) are jointly inconsistent. Therefore:

```text
Corr_r(t,s) ∧ Rad_{B_s,r}(t,s) → ¬TokenClosure(B_s,r).
```

This is a local structural result, not a derivation of robust realism. It establishes only that radical objective correction cannot be completely token-autonomous in the relevant respect.

## Structural result 2 — Corrigibility-source partition

If radical objective correction occurs, its authority must fall into at least one of five classes:

1. **Retained meta-standard.** A higher-order rule in the original basis licenses the revision. The event is self-amendment rather than radical correction relative to the full basis.
2. **Trans-state or type-level standard.** The authority belongs to truth, evidence, agency, organismic function, practice type, reason, or another standard not constituted by the current token state.
3. **Holistic normative fixed point.** No state has unilateral priority; the correction relation is fixed by a globally coherent whole, equilibrium, or non-well-founded constitution.
4. **Primitive normative fact.** The correction relation or its authority is irreducibly brute.
5. **Error theory or relativization.** There is no objective correction in the intended sense; only contract success, preference change, social acceptance, or descriptive transition remains.

The partition does not rank the horns by itself. It prevents a theory from describing radical objective correction while quietly drawing all authority from the very current token basis that the correction declares insufficient.

## Structural result 3 — Recursive-corrigibility closure condition

Consider a sequence of standards `B_0, B_1, …` in which each later stage correctly revises the authority conditions of the preceding stage. If every stage is alleged to derive all of its corrective authority solely from the immediately preceding token basis, and no retained meta-standard, trans-state standard, holistic constitution, or primitive normative fact is admitted, then no stage supplies a complete authority source for the whole sequence.

This is not an objection merely because a regress is infinite. The defect arises only under an **upstream authority-dependence profile**: the authority of each revision is deferred to a later revision whose authority is itself not yet fixed. A globally constituted or foundationally seeded sequence escapes. An infinite sequence can therefore be benign; what is blocked is the claim that a finite current token autonomously furnishes the complete authority for recursively correcting all of its own authority conditions.

## Why this matters to orthability

Orthability is stronger than a system's ability to move from one state to another or to optimize a selected score. An orthable state can be assessed as correctly or incorrectly placed and can be repaired under a standard that is itself answerable to further correction. Candidate E identifies the minimum source architecture required for such recursive objective corrigibility.

The result does **not** yet show:

- that the trans-token standard is agent-independent rather than kind- or practice-relative;
- that it is teleological in a robust metaphysical sense;
- that it has selected-effect or designed proper function;
- that it is unified;
- that it is intellectual or personal;
- that it is grounded in Wisdom.
