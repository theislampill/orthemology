## Blocking findings

### AG-F01 — “complete neutral union” was overbroad

The witness assembles a selected dependency-closed union through Deep AE, not
every neutral statement in the repository or every S01–S88 burden. V2 must name
the union `U_AE*` and list its exact coordinates.

### AG-F02 — several load-bearing coordinates were stipulated

Underived order, external actuality, target authority, semantic truth linkage,
and world adequacy were represented as Boolean facts. The witness checks
compatibility conditional on them; it does not derive or authenticate them.

### AG-F03 — the fittingness table was presentation-circular

V1 defined the fitting action by the same expression used for the policy.
V2 must freeze an independent admissibility table `H(theta,r)` and then test
policy conformity against it.

### AG-F04 — token/reason semantics were imported

The Boolean `r` coordinate is only a registered reason/semantic variable. The
model does not establish genuine semantic content, truth, or motivating reason
uptake.

### AG-F05 — the nonseparable interaction law was coordinate-dependent

The connected interaction graph has meaning only under Deep AE's coordinate,
function-family, resource, table, and scope guards.

### AG-F06 — W/P/K were role proxies

Global role availability and absent common-bearer co-instantiation cannot be
reported as actual Divine Knowledge, Will, or Power.

### AG-F07 — the actualizer and role bearers were not a metaphysical ontology

Finite labels `o,a,b,c` witness formal satisfiability, not necessary existence,
external reality, causal actuality, or plural metaphysical grounds.

### AG-F08 — personal/impersonal twins established only formal nonentailment

They do not establish equal probability, metaphysical possibility, physical
realizability, or explanatory parity.

### AG-F09 — source incompatibility was not tested

The neutral rival may be incompatible with accepted Track-N source predicates.
V2 must keep `source_contract_accepted=false` and preserve source-relative
filtering as a separate expansion.

### AG-F10 — implementation and empirical transfer were absent

A truth table is not a daee runtime, OSM mechanism, PRH representation, human
agent, or fiṭrī restoration process.

### AG-F11 — theorem-family status was compositional

The result is a strengthened common-model integration control descending from
Deep R, not an independent mathematical theorem. General novelty is zero.

### AG-F12 — the conclusion ceiling omitted the Creator and attribute firewalls

V2 must deny any entailment to Necessary Being, Creatorhood, unity,
intellect, personality, Wisdom, Speech, revelation, or meniscus.
