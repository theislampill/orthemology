# Scoped ownership and admissible specification

This addendum qualifies the earlier assessment. The correct test is whether rightful control over a relevant domain supports an admissible, feasible specification within that domain. Unrestricted duty-creation is unnecessary for this result.

## Direct textual check

Page 48 explicitly concerns authorization: “دخل البيت بلا استئذان، واستعمل الكرسي والسرير بلا استحلال”. It also faults failure to acknowledge the owner's authority over his house. Ameri applies this to acknowledging the world's provider. These are his own sentences, not a quotation from a scholar.

Source: Sami Ameri, first Arabic edition, Takween, 1437 AH / 2016 CE, PDF/printed p. 48. The quoted Arabic is 9 words under a whitespace count. Its sense is entering without asking permission and using the furniture without obtaining leave. No translation is presented as verbatim original wording.

## What the example supports

My earlier emphasis on acknowledgement underdescribed this page. The normative permission structure supports a stronger result than gratitude alone.

As a philosophical reconstruction, an owner with rightful control over a particular use can allow, withhold, or condition that use within the admissible scope of that control. Exercising this power can change which otherwise-admissible conduct is permitted. A corresponding directive can specify what respect for the owner's claim requires. That is a genuine, bounded practical-authority result. It does not depend on a capacity to create any duty whatsoever.

The book supplies this bridge informally through an ordinary moral analogy. It does not merely present greater causal power and label it right. But the application to the creator relies on treating being the world's source/provider as morally rightful ownership with the relevant control powers. The analogy does not separately defend the rule establishing that normative title or determine exactly which divine specifications fall within it. Thus, within the ordinary ownership example, the control premise is invoked as familiar; in the cosmic application, the transfer of that premise remains the justificatory hinge.

## The rival must keep its premises fixed

Suppose the stipulated premise is strong: this owner has rightful, continuing control of the relevant uses, including the particular admissible specification, the subject can comply, and no stronger claim defeats it. A rival cannot accept that same entitlement and reject the owner's specified condition simply because several uses were permissible before the selection. Doing so would deny the very control power granted in the premise. Wisdom and subject capacity are retained throughout this test.

A rival can accept benefaction and an obligation to worship while denying control over this particular choice. That is a coherent objection only because owed worship, by itself, is a weaker entitlement than rightful power to select its realization. It challenges the derivation of the stronger entitlement; it is not a counterexample that grants the stronger entitlement unchanged. The earlier report's proposed counterfactual must therefore be read with this distinction, not used to demand a second proof of an already-granted control right.

There is also an important precision about the source example. Its visitor never obtained permission. It does not directly depict two fully licensed alternatives followed by a new command withdrawing one. The extension to admissible specification is reasonable when the owner's continuing control is part of the original permission structure. Mere ownership should not silently override a previously granted unconditional entitlement. That qualification identifies what must be included in the stipulated control premise; it does not defeat the parent's narrower case.

## What remains for the worship application

For a particular worship specification, the needed additional question is bounded: why does the creator's rightful ownership include authority over this form of response or use? The answer might link creaturely use of the world to conditions of rightful recognition. Establishing that link would close the local case without proving unlimited jurisdiction. A permission-to-use example alone does not automatically prove every positive demand, but neither should its actual authorization structure be reduced to mere advice or gratitude.

A second, separate condition concerns the communicative act. An entitled owner may express a preference, offer advice, or exercise the relevant power. Entitlement does not make every utterance a binding exercise. Where directive or permission-setting force is already established, this condition is satisfied; it should not be reopened merely to evade the scoped result.

Revised result: the source contains an informal ownership-to-control argument relevant to a local specification bridge. Once the relevant rightful control is granted, the in-scope specification binds, subject to the stipulated wisdom, feasibility, and absence of defeaters. The unresolved question is the warrant and precise domain of that control in the creator–creature application. Nothing here authenticates an actual directive.
