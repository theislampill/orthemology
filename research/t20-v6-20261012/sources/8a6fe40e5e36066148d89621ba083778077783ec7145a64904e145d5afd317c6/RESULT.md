# Fixed-target cooperation: a new bounded interpretation attempt

Date: 7 October 2026. This is fresh research, not a replay of the nine older controls. The previous delivered archive, philosophical assessments, source files and theorem packages are unchanged.

## Result first

**No full source-faithful countermodel has been established. A precise obstruction has now been proved and computationally checked in the tried fixed-command production families.** The result is not that all possible cooperative-original interpretations are impossible.

The attempt adds explicit executable capacities, whole-target intentions, fixed actual commands, primitive contribution ownership, structural intrinsic-agency graphs, and separate modal/operational control tests. It does not merely call a pair of nodes powerful or perfect.

Three outcomes matter:

1. **Exact additive production:** equally and intrinsically fully capable agents can actually contribute indispensable partial work. But neither unchanged actual command independently suffices for the fixed target. Enlarging its command repairs the outcome only by changing the productive state the source holds fixed. Under the operational reading of complete determination as an independently sufficient unchanged command, this family does not supply a B6 counterexample. Bare P6 outcome-entailment alone is a weaker condition and is not falsified by this test.
2. **Saturation:** unchanged complete commands can each guarantee the specified qualitative outcome, while neither owns every actual emitted input. This is a genuine machine-checked separation at the stated emission-process signature. It succeeds by allowing redundant paths and by compressing different complete processes to the same observed output. It does not establish two genuinely indispensable original contributions to that same complete numerical effect, or source-faithful CND at that whole-effect respect.
3. **Control:** two agents cannot each unilaterally force incompatible outputs if their command choices can be freely combined. Concordant nonrectangular profiles avoid this contradiction, but their admissibility and matching intrinsic reasons are an additional interpretation burden. The experiment supplies an executable reason/choice rule, not an independently warranted account of necessary concord between ultimate agents.

The B5 reconciliation is also sharper: the main additive agents have equal executable capacity profiles for the fixed target. A separate unbounded construction lets either same rigid bearer execute the sum of any two natural-number command vectors. The defect exposed by fixed-command deletion is a defect in **this exercise's independent sufficiency**, not evidence of a missing standing operation. This does not vindicate their exercise as perfect original lordship; it isolates the required transfer instead of quietly placing it in an antecedent.

## 1. Source constraints actually used

The owner asks for conditional verification and adversarial premise-preservation, not computer certification of metaphysics. The current source reconstruction and independent review correctly require unchanged operative state, actual determination and numerical target. They also distinguish outcome attainment, complete original provision and exclusive original authorship.

The key primary passage is the retained Arabic Majmu 20:174–183, especially web L908, L912–913 and L917–921. L908 argues that a genuinely additional contributor changes the complete effect if the first contributor remains unchanged; ordinary solo/joint examples involve a changed productive state. L912–913 explicitly strengthens complete power and decisive willing into production through the agent alone. L917–921 gives the concentration/de re/perfection/dependence sequence, rather than merely saying that cooperation is bad.

Minhaj 2:180–183 supplies efficacy-as-perfection and the intrinsically necessary perfection argument. Its preceding 2:168–172 control distinguishes intrinsic concomitance from efficient origination. The experiment does not treat every matching state as a causal cycle, nor does it assume the disputed effect-to-intrinsic transfer.

The Safadiyya control distinguishes essential capacity, genus, act token and external product. The particular-act anchor remains Salim 2:145, and the additional global-inactivity argument remains 2:142. Neither is reinterpreted or weakened here. The present algebra has no temporal-global-activity model and claims no result about total divine cessation.

Exact files, hashes, ranges, page qualifications and premise bindings are in `SOURCE_BINDINGS.json` and `PREMISE_MATRIX.json`. Retained electronic Arabic was directly read; there was no new browsing, manuscript collation, scan campaign or whole-book reading.

## 2. The stronger semantic interpretation

A and B are rigid, distinct root-bearer identifiers present at every represented command/reason valuation. Constitutive capacity and intrinsic reason are attached to those same roots. Their efficient equation graph has no incoming productive edge into a bearer or its capacity, and no peer-to-will/act path. This interprets unreceived agency in an explicit structural fragment. It does not prove that such roots satisfy the source's full metaphysical necessary-by-itself profile. In particular, finite constant-domain persistence is not offered as a new proof of intrinsic necessity.

The target is one named token e with a source-neutral two-coordinate state. No target is called 'A's result' or 'the product with B's contribution'. The additive actual target is (2,2); each agent emits (1,1), so both contribute to both coordinates. Their capacities include all nine vectors from (0,0) through (2,2). Every vector in the tested target fragment has a constructive solo command with the peer still present but inactive. The finite bound is a search bound, not a claimed metaphysical ineligibility of larger targets. Lean also proves the construction for arbitrary natural-number coordinate vectors, beyond the finite search bound, and explicitly witnesses concentration of any joint additive command a+b in either same rigid bearer. This remains unrestricted **within that additive family**, not within every metaphysically possible effect domain.

A will separately records the entire intended target and issued command. The actual intention is not covertly replaced by a partial target. Nevertheless, recording a fully specified intention is not enough to certify that its actual exercise is a complete independent determinant. The additive interpretation exposes that failure under this operational reading. It does not prove that source P6 already requires deletion-robust sufficiency, much less exclusive original provision.

Intrinsic agency is separated from created emissions and effects. An ownership relation over four fixed emission tokens is genuinely variable: all 256 relations were checked, 81 satisfy CND, 16 also give every token an owner, and 175 duplicate-owner relations fail. Thus CND is not forced by defining an emission as an owner-labelled object. The concrete fixture has different original owners for differentiated tokens. Applying that fragment to the common numerical effect still requires a faithful account of its operative original requisites.

Operational peer suppression preserves the remaining agent's entire actual will, command and capacity. It is a surgical counterfactual comparison, not automatically a metaphysically admissible world in which a necessary peer retains a complete will while inexplicably failing to act. Actual command-profile worlds and diagnostic interventions are different domains. No bearer is annihilated by a power operation.

The rigid identity of e across comparisons is **stipulated by the interpretation**. The machinery does not prove that a metaphysical numerical effect retains its identity when its complete causal process changes. That weakness is explicitly relevant to the saturation candidate; equality of its output vector is not promoted to a source-defended identity theorem.

## 3. Executed searches and proofs

### Exact addition

All 81 ordered pairs of two-coordinate commands in {0,1,2}² were evaluated. Nine realize the fixed target (2,2). Seven have both actual commands indispensable. None has both commands independently sufficient while unchanged.

Lean proves more than the finite observation: if a+b=q and the unchanged a already equals q, then b=0; if a and b each equal q and jointly sum to q, then q=0. The coordinatewise extension rules out nontrivial complete additive double provision. The general `fixed_solo_excludes_indispensable_peer` theorem needs no arithmetic: the same fixed command cannot both suffice after peer suppression and fail to suffice after that suppression.

This last theorem's indispensable-peer premise has real content. It does not prove that every actual causal contributor is indispensable. Majmu L908–909 supplies a substantive contribution-sensitive claim; treating all causal participation as but-for necessity would assume the disputed extension.

### All Boolean two-input maps

All 16 deterministic maps from two binary commands to a binary output were enumerated. Four are monotone, have no spontaneous output, and produce the actual target at (1,1). Only AND and OR make both inputs causally relevant somewhere. AND supports actual indispensability and fails fixed solo sufficiency. OR supports fixed solo sufficiency and fails actual indispensability. No table has both properties.

This is exhaustive for those exact maps, not all multi-agent causation, richer effect ontologies, probabilistic dynamics or metaphysical models. It complements a general definitional incompatibility proof; it does not turn a finite search into a universal impossibility result.

### Saturation and CND respect

The strongest relaxed candidate uses coordinatewise OR. Both actual commands are (1,1), including under the fixed-remaining-state solo comparison. Each guarantees that whole qualitative target against every other Boolean command. They have distinct emitted tokens and no efficient receipt of intrinsic commands.

But attribution branches:

- If 'entire original provision of e' means that the unchanged command suffices for e alone, both agents qualify and whole-target CND fails. Lean checks this explicitly.
- If it means ownership of every actually emitted input to the process, neither qualifies. CND over primitive emissions is nonvacuous, while CND over entire e is then vacuous. The outcome-to-entire-emission-provenance separation is valid only at that chosen interface.

There is no independent proof identifying either attribution rule with the source's complete original productive respect. Nor may primitive-emission CND be substituted for whole-effect CND without saying so. The actual process contains four emissions; suppressing one agent removes two while keeping the Boolean output equal. If the complete numerical product includes the whole productive process or additional physical consequences, that product is not unchanged. A familiar physical saturator could also require apparatus with its own provenance. The algebraic operator is not automatically a third efficient cause, but the absence of a third symbol is not evidence that its physically interpreted mechanism needs no original provider. This interpretation obligation remains open.

### Rectangular control and concord

Lean proves: if A has a command that forces q for every B command, and B has a command that forces a different r for every A command, their mixed profile is contradictory. This theorem uses the product command space. It does not infer jointly possible contrary wills from separately available powers.

For OR, each agent robustly controls only (1,1) among the four Boolean vector targets when all rival commands are admitted. On the diagonal concordant profiles, every target can be attained and each matching full command has a solo witness. The intrinsic choice rule maximizes a declared score over all four executable commands; both copies calculate their choice without taking the other's will or future product as input. This removes an efficient-priority cycle from the equations, but matching score contents and the exclusion of contrary joint profiles are stipulated. No reason has been established that qualifies them as the necessary wisdom of two original lords. The nonrectangular model is a modal/control diagnostic, not a metaphysical possibility certificate or an equally good actual-world explanation.

## 4. B5 joined directly to T17's accepted perfection argument

The retained T17 report P65–68 was reread directly, not reconstructed from memory. It distinguishes giver-priority from a broader pure-perfection judgement. P66 requires the additional ability to be genuinely feasible for this bearer without contradiction, borrowing, need or impairment; P67 rejects an arbitrary declaration of limited nature; P68 expressly distinguishes standing power from exclusive actual exercise. P74–81 and P108–112 preserve exact will-content and distinguish within-power alternatives from jointly wisdom-compatible wills.

The capacity experiment therefore does **not** assign A only a left-hand power and B only a right-hand power. Such a countermodel would evade retained equal eligible-target power. Each existing rigid bearer has the whole bounded constructor profile for the fixed target. Mere set-union of these identical profiles is too weak to settle Majmu’s concentration claim: the finite agents could jointly yield (4,4), which lies outside either bounded command set. The stronger unbounded interpretation therefore supplies an actual concentration witness: for either same rigid g and any vectors a,b, issue(g,a+b)=a+b. This is separately Lean-proved. It removes the artificial finite ceiling within the additive family. The abstract profile is never a third actual source. No appeal to a hypothetical new unified individual supplies either existing individual's capacity; that capacity is witnessed directly by its own operations.

For this family, B5's components have these different answers:

- U, co-bearability of combined additive productive capacity: witnessed by the unbounded sum-command construction; not inferred merely from set-union.
- R, each identified bearer's eligibility: witnessed by each's already available unbounded sum-command constructor, within the family. Its source-level purity and philosophical applicability are interpretation obligations, not automatic consequences of the code.
- F, a missing pure standing perfection for this additive target: **not found** in the unbounded interpretation; each can already issue the combined command. The bounded set-union lemma alone would not establish this.
- I, inability to supply a missing constitutive completion: no such missing capacity event exists here. Inability of the frozen partial command to attain the whole outcome does not supply that event.
- N, incompatibility of externally received constitutive perfection with original necessity: retained as a substantive principle, but its receipt antecedent is not instantiated merely by the split exercise.

This analysis does not certify the exercise as a perfect original exercise. The additive candidate fails deletion-robust fixed-actual sufficiency. This is the explicitly adopted stronger operational reading, not an unnoticed upgrade of bare P6. The source may reject the selected partial exercise through a norm of unborrowed original efficacy; that is the substantive M1/P2b/SourceMode classification still needing warrant, rather than an absence of an executable standing capacity.

A generic Lean interface `every eligible pure perfection is possessed` would merely restate the accepted ascent unless a new result establishes the particular capacity's feasibility, purity and applicability to this very bearer. The current constructive profile test adds that information at a declared algebraic scope, but does not establish that **exclusive actual provision** is itself a standing capacity or constitutive perfection. No global common-source attribution or 'giver of every other's perfection' claim was assumed. A broader perfection-interface theorem was therefore not added ceremonially.

## 5. Premise preservation and what is not earned

`PREMISE_MATRIX.json` gives clause-by-clause statuses. The material failures are:

- Additive candidate: missing deletion-robust fixed-actual sufficiency, despite full standing power and explicit whole-target intention. Actual target occurrence is retained. Whether the deleted-peer context belongs to P6’s admissible contexts is not supplied by the source or the equations. On an explicitly concordant context domain, bare P6 can hold, but the concordance explanation remains unestablished.
- Saturated candidate: no indispensable differentiated contribution at the saturated output; incomplete numerical-effect identity justification; no faithful whole-output CND/provenance bridge; possible additional mechanism/provision burden.
- Concordant candidate: no independently warranted necessity of matching intrinsic reason contents or exclusion of opposed joint determinations; unrestricted rectangular control is unavailable. Rectangular control is not silently substituted for T17 standing eligible power under wisdom-compatible contexts.
- All candidates: bounded structural original-root semantics and bounded/generic algebraic capacities do not settle full metaphysical necessity, wisdom, every eligible pure perfection, or empirical application to the whole actual connected created order.

P2b, M1 transfer, B5's disputed transfer steps, B6 whole/exclusive production and B2 efficient-concurrence remain outside their own antecedents. Their failure is not by itself a disqualification. The missing interpretation/warrant clauses above prevent a full preserving result. In particular, failure of the stronger operational control tests is not advertised as failure of an already independently established stronger P6 or rectangular-control premise. The source's strengthened argument remains stronger than these successful reduced-signature interpretations.

The negative controls detect changed actual commands, source-indexed replacement targets, unequal partial capacities, empty eligibility, duplicate original ownership, borrowed capacity and circular will donation. They establish test sensitivity, not metaphysical truth.

## 6. Evidence levels and bounded endpoint

The Python searches and 15 discriminating tests are `EXECUTABLY_CHECKED`; the concrete finite fixtures are `FINITE_MODEL_VERIFIED` only at their declared signatures. The listed Lean declarations are `KERNEL_PROVED` conditional mathematics. Source arguments are `SOURCE_ATTESTED`; their universal applicability and the unclosed transfers remain `APPLICATION_WARRANT_REQUIRED` or `OPEN`. No axiom asserting a metaphysical conclusion was introduced. The final axiom printout contains only ordinary Lean logical foundations where used, no `sorryAx` or custom metaphysical axiom.

This attempt reaches the justified stopping point: a richer interpretation has actually been built and tested; the exact family obstructions and failed preservation clauses are identified; T17's eligible-power grant has been joined to B5 without assuming the desired conclusion. It would be inaccurate to claim either a full countermodel or proof of universal cooperation-impossibility. It would also be inaccurate now to describe the late frontier as having received only a review of thin older tables.

The remaining research burden is specifically a source-defensible account of complete original contribution and effect identity that both preserves genuine shared work and distinguishes outcome sufficiency from entire original provision, together with a justified nonrectangular control/wisdom semantics if that route is used. Producing such an account would exceed this tried family. No new books, broad prior-science replay, repository integration or archive reconstruction were undertaken.
