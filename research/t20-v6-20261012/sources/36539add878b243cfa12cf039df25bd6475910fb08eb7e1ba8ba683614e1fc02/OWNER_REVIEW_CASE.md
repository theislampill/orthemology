The stopping reason is that the requested bounded audit has covered the registered history, selected plausible high-consequence omissions, inspected the relevant actual sources, added the justified connections, and independently corrected the resulting interpretations. No assertion is made that the wider scientific frontier is exhausted. Owner acceptance can therefore concern this qualified consolidation boundary while later research carries the recorded open scopes forward.
