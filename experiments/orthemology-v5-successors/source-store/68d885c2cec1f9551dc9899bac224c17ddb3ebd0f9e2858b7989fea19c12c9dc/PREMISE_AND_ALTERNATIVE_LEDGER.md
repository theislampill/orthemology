## Adversarial controls

1. **Retain the actual whole-source service.** Add its one-request window to the surviving menu. C is then adequate without a transferred edge. A manufactured exclusion cannot support J2.
2. **Give R a valid existing Q verdict.** The alleged deficit disappears. Reject a proof which demands raw roles despite this observation.
3. **Allow a rich cheap response to reveal Q through another value.** Complete-response locality fails. Recompute the actual decision cost rather than reusing the cover bound.
4. **Carry only b, with perfect provenance.** Correct binding does not make the packet sufficient: both edge conflicts remain. This is the case's discriminating negative control.
5. **Carry a negative witness for any selected origin.** Q is false and later cost is zero. Do not confuse realised negative cost with all-positive worst-case cost.
6. **Raise the legitimate budget to two requests by an actual accepted arrangement.** The unaugmented C service suffices. The moral principle does not require needless retention after the capacity gap is removed.
7. **Select only copies of a for the next task.** The derived L is {a}; one left-card read suffices. Do not retain irrelevant ancestors or count copies as independent acquisitions.
8. **Change the question to individual attribution answers.** A previous conjunction verdict may no longer suffice. The edge-witness-plus-card plan can still obtain all three roles, but that is a different task contract to assess explicitly.
9. **Lose the boundary-origin map.** The role-only arithmetic is inapplicable until a valid identity/path route is supplied and charged. Correct card labels alone do not repair an unknown referent.
10. **Change the snapshot or selected list.** Old evidence must remain applicable to the new task under a separately justified binding; a copied digest or report heading does not establish that fact.
11. **Make G an equally capable outsider who never organised or assumed the provision.** J1–J4 remain true. N2 is absent, so the special relational-duty inference is unavailable. General rescue reasons may separately apply.
12. **Remove legitimate reliance or add an effective informed limitation.** The operational deficit remains, but N1/N5 and the duty's scope require reassessment. G's institution alone does not support every later recipient demand.
13. **Make every adequate precaution overwhelmingly costly or prohibited.** N4 fails. Two-read arithmetic does not itself prohibit proportionate disengagement or establish blame for unavoidable failure.
14. **Let R freely refuse an adequate offered alternative.** G gains no authority to force the task or assistance. Further responsibility depends on the actual informed decision and any independent duties.
15. **A proportionate arrangement later fails unexpectedly.** J5 does not create strict liability. Conversely a lucky guess by R does not show that knowingly omitted feasible care was adequate.
16. **Use an ordinary parser, shared-ID table and cover planner.** Give it the same maps, witnesses, service menu and verdict alternatives. It reproduces all calculations; no programme-exclusive performance claim survives.
17. **Make the retained origin a value-dependent signal.** If an agreed adequately warranted rule selects a exactly when Q is true and b otherwise, identity can convey Q. Include that observation and reassess the cost; this can be a task-verdict protocol. Do not treat it as the fixed middle-only plan or conceal it to preserve a lower bound.
