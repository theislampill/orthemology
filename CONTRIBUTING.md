# Contributing

This is a research-stage repository with strict honesty discipline. Contributions must preserve it.

## Claim levels

Five kinds of claim stay separate everywhere: conceptual framework, formal definition, empirical hypothesis, metaphysical thesis, design vocabulary. A change may not silently promote a claim between levels. Deterministic validators/fixtures show *consistency*, never empirical utility; do not word anything to suggest otherwise.

## Citation requirements

Every load-bearing related-work or scholarly claim needs a row in the current sourcing surface for its claim area (start at `docs/sourcing/CURRENT-SOURCING-LEDGER.md`, Decision 0019) with a source, stable identifier, and honest verification status (`WEB-VERIFIED` / `RECORD-CONFIRMED` / `VIA-COMPILATION` / `UNVERIFIED`). Do not manufacture citations for fields the text merely names; weaken the claim instead. CI checks ledger completeness (field presence), never live URLs.

## No empirical overclaiming

None of the repository's designed public experiment packets has been run. They stand at their canonical [`experiments/experiment-status.yaml`](experiments/experiment-status.yaml) states (FCSP-2/ER-2 `READY_TO_RUN`; terminology Pilot-0 v2 `READY_FOR_HUMAN_MATCHING_REVIEW`; Pilot 1 / confirmatory templates `DRAFT`) — none externally registered and no term adopted. A separately classified historical internal synthetic AR8R pilot may be summarized only with its exact negative disposition, methodological limits, and zero framework-validation credit; it is not a public packet. Do not write a scalar "everything is READY TO RUN" claim when the packet states differ. Words like "validated", "shown", and "demonstrated" are reserved for explicitly bounded conformance or synthetic results and must name that boundary.

## Decision-record policy

Substantive changes to theory, notation, verdict semantics, or program structure require a numbered record in `docs/decisions/` and must never rewrite historical records, archived patches, ledgers, or frozen packets (`archive/`, `docs/provenance/document-history.md`, the terminology v0 spec, `terminology/pilot0/` post-freeze). Supersede; do not edit history. Registries (`docs/verdict-registry.yaml`, `docs/notation-registry.yaml`) drive validation — change them only together with a decision record.

## Terminology status

Coined terms are candidates gated on the unrun benchmark; contributions must not "adopt" a term, and every distinction must remain expressible in ordinary words.

## Theological school flags

Creed-internal material is explicitly school-labeled (currently: Atharī route-specific). Contributions may not present school-internal claims as neutral conclusions, may not collapse the reason/revelation labels, and must represent contrasting schools accurately and neutrally or not at all.

## Privacy and provenance

Never commit: raw transcripts, session identifiers, absolute local paths, private screenshots, credentials, or the private casebook/longitudinal records. The public manifest (`docs/provenance/RELEASE-MANIFEST.sha256`) is regenerated by `scripts/make_manifest.py` with every change; CI enforces it. Model-assistance provenance lives in `docs/provenance/`.

## Mechanics

Run `python scripts/validate_repo.py` plus the validators in `.github/workflows/validate.yml` before pushing. No force-pushes to `main`.

## Reader maintenance

The existing policies above remain unchanged. The six reader pages are navigation and explanation over source owners, not a second scientific registry. Start at the [verification guide](docs/reader/VERIFICATION-GUIDE.md) and the [work/source crosswalk](docs/project-closure/reader-refactor-issue-35/CROSSWALK.json). An editorial work key is not a scientific result identifier or an acceptance decision.

### Edit the actual owner

For a substantive argument, edit the original manuscript or research source under the applicable scientific authorisation, not its catalogue synopsis. For a governed rendering, consult [the publication profile](docs/publication-profile.yaml): Markdown source owners, generated LaTeX, source sidecars and preserved PDFs are different objects. Do not edit generated LaTeX as prose, infer an exact PDF build from a related TeX file, or repin a publication simply because navigation changed.

For reader-only changes, edit the affected explanation and its one crosswalk relationship. Existing scientific result/status owners retain their identifiers, assumptions and evidence levels. Reviews, manifests, duplicate checkpoint copies and software components do not become independent papers. Maintain the original source path, edition and limitation next to the reading route.

### Five ordinary changes and their bounded footprint

**M01 — Edition:** update the actual publication/edition owner and the existing work’s reading form; preserve a distinct edition when its identity or build relation differs. Do not duplicate the work or rewrite unrelated reader pages.

**M02 — Scoped result:** change the original result owner only under scientific authority, then refresh its result route and materially affected explanations. The atlas, catalogue and frontier page are not six independent copies of one status fact.

**M03 — New non-PDF work:** a substantive source outside familiar paper directories still enters the finite corpus. Review the approved source baseline and owner population, assign a work or justified support role, and add its source-bound reading route. Updating a hash alone is not source review.

**M04 — Repeated checkpoint:** reuse established work and source equivalences, retain the checkpoint’s custody/edition identity, and curate genuinely new work. A copied argument is not another independent result or proof receipt.

**M05 — Changed conclusion:** inspect the changed source passage, then review its bound synopsis and dependent routes. Do not silence the source-currentness failure by merely replacing its checksum. Unrelated explanations need no gratuitous rewrite.

The [collection reading and maintenance record](docs/project-closure/reader-refactor-issue-35/COLLECTION-COVERAGE.md) retains actual exercises and qualifications; the instructions here are not a claim that all five final-candidate exercises have already passed. Resolve changed scope at the existing owner rather than admitting external bibliographies recursively.

### Generate and verify through the existing tools

Stage intended additions/removals before generation, because the source digest and manifest use tracked paths. Run `python scripts/generate_current_state.py`, then `python scripts/make_manifest.py`; preserve the authored state block and inspect the generated diff. Neither output may contain a demand to equal its own containing commit hash.

Run `python -B tests/test_reader_census.py`, `python -B tests/test_reader_pilot.py` and `python -B scripts/check_reader_pilot.py` for reader identity, edition and route controls. Also run `python scripts/generate_current_state.py --check`, `python scripts/validate_current_state.py`, `python scripts/validate_internal_references.py` and the unchanged mandatory gates in [the actual CI workflow](.github/workflows/validate.yml). Repeating generation must leave the candidate unchanged. Use the workflow’s pinned environment when claiming native qualification; a different local interpreter is not the same environment.

A historical command in a source archive is evidence of that source’s procedure, not automatically a supported command for this checkout. Respect public/private input boundaries and do not run a broad scientific replay merely to validate a documentation link. Mechanical checks do not establish scholarly comprehension, empirical validity, independent review or owner acceptance.

## Scholarly consolidation and intended destinations

The following owner-directed research discipline governs current and subsequent scholarly contributions. It preserves the earlier scientific claim levels, citation honesty, experiment/status distinctions, school labels, privacy restrictions and frozen-edition protections. Where an older administrative preference would require another permanent report or numbered record for an incremental scholarly change, use the reviewed change at the existing scholarly and scientific-decision owner instead. Preserve any independently necessary decision/evidence record; do not create a duplicate registry. Existing source and consumer controls remain in force until their function is migrated or independently retired under [R-0002](https://github.com/theislampill/orthemology/issues/38).


Every contribution must have an intended scholarly destination. Start with the strongest existing treatment of the subject and its controlling review. Read the scientific content, including corrections and counterexamples; a filename, catalogue entry or mechanical classification is not evidence of substantive reading. The [README](README.md) links directly to public works.

### Improve the coherent body of science

The default outcome of an extension, stronger theorem or repaired proof is an update to its appropriate scholarly work. Write the definitions, assumptions, argument or complete necessary proof, relevant counterexamples, limitations and sources into that work. Explain how the result relates to existing contributions. A genuinely independent new work may deserve a new file; an incremental discovery ordinarily does not require another permanent research collection.

Temporary notes, experiments and review evidence must have a finite disposition: incorporate their substantive content, retain what remains uniquely necessary for evidence or reproducibility, or remove redundant material once its applicable safeguards pass. Do not accumulate dated principal/continuation/follow-up/status reports, repeated inventories, copied proofs or permanent executor narratives. Preserve independent reasoning and significant negative findings without reproducing every development step.

Use substantive subject names. Internal programme acronyms, tranche numbers, checkpoint codes and worker identifiers are not scholarly vocabulary. Retire their organisational role, including code, generated indexes and compatibility references that depend on it. Do not preserve redundant bookkeeping under a new acronym. Necessary scientific notation, attribution and meaningful publication editions remain legitimate.

Judge the net effect: does the change improve, correct or extend a coherent treatment, and does it retire superseded material where permitted? File-count growth is not evidence of research progress. A small index into fragmented sources is not intellectual consolidation.

### Scientific authority and editions

Preserve accepted editions, their necessary proofs and exact acceptance scope. Make a material scholarly revision reviewable as an explicit successor edition or change; never silently rewrite an accepted paper or frozen proof evidence. Preserve the predecessor in Git and satisfy any independent evidence-custody obligation before replacement-dependent removal. Do not rewrite Git history.

Accepted Papers 1–10 remain authoritative at their accepted editions. The original n-person noetic-field objective of Paper 11 remains unfinished; proposed constructive-correction companions are separate works. A newer filename, successful build, source hash or editorial synthesis cannot change those decisions. The same discipline applies to other substantial manuscripts and foundational arguments.

Keep accepted results, qualified proposals, unresolved claims, superseded formulations, negative results, source records, renderings and owner adoption decisions distinguishable. Scientific review must assess the actual arguments and source evidence. A successful finite experiment does not establish a general theorem; a conditional formal proof does not establish the truth of its philosophical premises; an index cannot manufacture acceptance.

Private manuscript bodies require separate publication authority. Recover permitted identities and status without treating a public summary as receipt of a private primary source. Public consolidation must account for the private dependency rather than silently omitting the work or claiming it has been reconciled. This policy applies to contributors and agents, including Dot, working in this repository; it grants no authority to mutate their separate private research branches.

### Direct reading, review and removal

Current scholarship must be complete in ordinary Markdown, LaTeX, PDF or another appropriate conventional format. Its arguments, proofs, conclusions, citations and qualifications must be directly readable. Custom decoding, archive rehydration and reconstruction of historical fragments are optional recovery facilities, never prerequisites for ordinary understanding. Accessibility is assessed through independent completeness, legibility, attribution and access checks; historical retrieval timings are not acceptance criteria.

For a consolidation, independently compare the new treatment with its distinct substantive predecessors. Keep one compact source-to-result verification record, with exact editions, reading scope, corrections and unresolved omissions. Do not turn that record into the scholarly work or another scientific registry. Preserve failed observations and the exact limits of reviews. An author's incorporation account is not independent review or owner adoption.

Remove a redundant source only after its substantive incorporation or independently necessary disposition is established, affected consumers have been migrated or explicitly retired, and applicable preservation, recovery, privacy and rollback requirements pass. Git history preserves committed development records but does not by itself discharge every independent backup or irreplaceable-evidence obligation. Do not conceal an unresolved gate behind a simulated small tree.
