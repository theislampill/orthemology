# Primary ancestry check

Gaubert and Qu, *Dobrushin's ergodicity coefficient for Markov operators on cones*, author-hosted PDF:
https://www.cmap.polytechnique.fr/~gaubert/PAPERS/GaubertQuIEOTD14QuFinal.pdf

Read on 10 October 2026: PDF pp.1–4 (abstract and opening introduction), especially displayed equations (1)–(2), PDF p.2. These explicitly identify the classical contraction coefficient with maximum pairwise row total variation, equivalently one minus minimum pairwise row overlap. A single uniformly positive column is presented as a sufficient condition, not an equivalent global-minorisation requirement. The authors credit Doeblin and Dobrushin.

This supplies ancestry for the Markov contraction step. The cap subset-sum optimisation and its effective tail approximation are derived separately in ENVELOPE_DIAMETER.md. No claim is made that the paper states that cap-specific formula; later cone/quantum sections and proofs were not inspected or transported. No formal source replay.
