# Jāmiʿ al-masāʾil VI, pp. 94–98: source return

Verified 2026-10-09 UTC. Scope: a bounded return to an already studied text, not a new independent historical witness, new manuscript collation, or new first-pass reading credit. No integration or closure claim.

## Finding first

The passage explicitly rejects two complete agents of one numerically single act/effect and explicitly considers cooperative agents who could each act alone. It does not merely ignore the distinction between standing power and actual exercise. Its argumentative route, however, assigns each real cooperator a distinct part of the effect, defines actual independence as doing the act alone, and treats a helper's participation as prevention of the first agent's complete exercise. Within the checked pp. 93–99, I did not find a further derivation excluding simultaneous, nonexclusive whole production by two original causes that does not rely on that partition/nonduplication premise, exclusive actual independence, or an added unrestricted-exercise/prevention perfection premise.

This is a bounded evidential finding, not a claim that the author offers only a dictionary definition or that all arguments elsewhere in his corpus have been exhausted. The juridical discussion, contrast of intrinsic actions and effects, empirical cooperation examples, and perfection/obstruction argument are substantive additional argumentation. Their applicability to the specified simultaneous whole-cause rival remains conditional on the disputed bridge.

## Exact Arabic anchors, authorial voice, and minimal translation

Transcriptions below are visually checked against the saved scan pages. Diacritics are generally omitted; substantive wording is retained. Awkward printed clauses are not silently repaired. These are excerpts, not a full diplomatic transcription.

### Printed 94, bottom paragraph: general nonduplication claim

«وهذا كله مما يبين أن الشيء الواحد لا يجتمع له سببان مستقلان، وكذلك الفعل الواحد والمفعول الواحد، لا يتصور أن يصدر عن فاعلين تامين، فالشخصان المتعاونان على حمل شيء يقوم بذات كل»

The sentence continues at the top of printed 95:

«منهما من الفعل ما لا يقوم بذات الآخر، فليس فعلهما واحدا بل متعدد.»

Translation: One thing cannot have two independent causes; one act/effect cannot issue from two complete agents. The two bearers each have an act inhering in them that does not inhere in the other, so their acts are multiple.

This is the author's asserted conclusion, not an editor's heading or an opponent quotation. Preceding printed 94 argues that apparently overlapping legal causes yield multiple rulings, even where their practical realization overlaps. That is supporting juridical reasoning, not a separately demonstrated general theorem of event nonduplication.

### Printed 95: effect partition and definition of actual independence

«وأما المفعول، وهو أثر فعلهما، وهو ما قام بالمحمول من الحمل، فأثر فعل أحدهما الموجود في المحمول ليس هو أثر فعل الآخر، بل هو غيره، والمحمول لم يكن محمولا بفعل هذا وحده وأثره، ولا بفعل هذا وحده وأثره، بل بالمجموع، فليس كل منهما فاعلا بل جزء فاعل، والفاعل مجموعهما، وليس كل منهما مستقلا بالحمل في مثل هذا، بل المعلوم بالاضطرار أن المفعول بين فاعلين لا يكون كل منهما مستقلا به، فإن المستقل هو الذي يفعل الفعل وحده، فإذا قدر أن له فيه شريكا وقيل مع ذلك: إنه مستقل، كان جمعا بين النقيضين، وكان التقدير أنه ما فعله إلا وحده، وأنه ما فعله إلا هو وغيره، فيكون فيه إثبات فعل الغير، وهذا جمع بين النقيضين.»

Translation: Each bearer produces a different effect in the carried object. The aggregate acts; each is part of an agent. The independent agent is defined as the one who does the act alone, so affirming both actual independence and a real partner is called contradictory.

Textual caution: the printed final explanation says «فيكون فيه إثبات فعل الغير»; it does not print an additional «ونفيه». Do not silently improve this clause into a more explicit contradiction formula.

### Printed 95, last three paragraphs: solo ability allowed, unchanged contribution rejected

«وإذا لم يكن أحدهما مستقلا بالفعل في صورة التعاون، فهل يقال: لو انفرد أحدهما لاستقل به؟

هذا قد لا يمكن في صور كثيرة، وفي صور يمكن، كأن يكون حينئذ الفعل الموجود من المستقل أكمل منه إذا كان له معين وشريك.

وأما أن يقال: فعله إذا كان له شريك معاون، مثل فعله إذا كان مستقلا، فهذا باطل، وفيه جمع بين النقيضين أيضا، لأنه إذا كان له شريك معاون، وقدر أن المفعول الموجود معه هو مثل الموجود في صورة الاستقلال سواء، فإن لم يكن لهذا الشريك تأثير فيه لم يكن شريكا، وإن كان له تأثير فقد فعل بعض المفعول، فلا يكون إلا فاعلا»

Continues on printed 96:

«لبعضه، وفي الاستقلال فعله كله.»

Translation: Some cooperators could not act alone, but some could; in the latter case their solo act is fuller. A putative partner either has no effect and is no partner, or has an effect and has produced part of the effect. Thus the first agent produces only part during cooperation, although it produces the whole in independence.

The inferential hinge is the move from a partner's having an effect to its producing a distinct part. The proposition that the same entire token effect could be wholly produced by each concurrently is excluded by that premise rather than independently tested under a nonexclusive interpretation of whole production.

### Printed 96: obstruction even if the whole lies within one's power

The central unambiguous continuation reads:

«فإنه إذا لم يكن قادرا على الفعل وحده كان عاجزا، وإن كان قادرا على العمل وحده فوجود الآخر معه منعه عن نفاذ قدرته، إذ هو لا يمكن مع معاونة الآخر أن يفعل الفعل كله، بل بعضه، فإن كان الكل مقدورا كان ممنوعا، وإن لم يكن الكل مقدورا كان عاجزا، والممنوع كالعاجز، فالمشاركة في العمل تقتضي عجز كل منهما، وعدم كمال قدرته على ذلك العمل حين الاشتراك. وهذا تمانع بأن المنع من فعل البعض كالمنع من فعل الجميع، فظهر أن الاشتراك»

Printed 97, first line, completes:

«نفسه مع التعاون والتناصر هو تمانع يقتضي عجز كل منهما.»

Translation: If an agent cannot perform the act alone it is unable; if it can, the other's presence prevents the full exercise of its power. The prevented is treated like the unable; even harmonious cooperation therefore counts as mutual obstruction.

This passage expressly distinguishes ability from its unrestricted exercise. It is stronger than a bare definition but needs the partition premise plus the further perfection judgment that another's participation, including a voluntarily accepted participation, counts as a disqualifying prevention. It supplies neither a temporal priority requirement nor a separate argument that two whole concurrent productions must be two numerically distinct effects.

Textual caution: nearby printed prose has syntactically awkward phrases, including «ومن الممتنع أن يكون بأنه يفعل الجميع وحده» and «ولا المقدور من الواحد قادر بينهما» and «وبذلك يتبين أن الاشتراك في الفعل في كل من الشريكين». These are present in the scan, not merely errors created by the IIFA OCR. No emendation or inferred missing words have been smuggled into the quoted decisive continuation.

### Printed 97: contrary wills do not by themselves exclude cooperation

After presenting the theologians' motion/rest dilemma, the author's response includes:

«لا يدل ... على امتناع الاشتراك فيما وجد، وإنما يدل على أن المتمانعين لا يفعلان شيئا ما داما متمانعين ... لكن ليس فيه ما يدل على أنهما إذا لم يتمانعا بل تعاونا أنهما لا يفعلان، فمن أين يدل هذا على أن الفعل الموجود لا يكون عن اثنين؟»

Ellipses here indicate omitted words, not a continuous quotation. The claim being rejected is the theologians' argument, introduced by «وقولهم»; the negative appraisal is Ibn Taymiyya's argumentative voice. The sole page footnote is the editor's syntactical aid linking «قولهم لو قدرنا ... لا يدل», not an independent doctrinal claim.

### Printed 98: necessary accord returns to the previous argument

«فظهر بما ذكرناه أن دليل التمانع إنما يصح إذا كان التمانع لازما لهما أو جائزا عليهما، وأما إذا قدر وجوب اتحاد الإرادتين، كان هو الدليل الأول المذكور في امتناع الفعل الواحد من فاعلين، ولزوم العجز للمتعاونين، لكن ذاك عجز عن الاستقلال بالفعل، وهذا عجز عن منع الآخر منه.»

Translation: If disagreement is necessary or possible, the contrary-will argument applies. If their wills necessarily agree, the case goes back to the earlier argument against one act from two agents and for the cooperators' incapacity. He distinguishes inability to act independently from inability to prevent the other's action.

The rest of printed 98 invokes differentiated effects in the world and the dualists' distinct light/dark productions, and asserts that no rational person said one effect issued from two. These are further analogical/consensus supports, not a worked derivation addressing concurrent whole original causes while allowing nonexclusive attribution.

- PDF/printed 85 is the collection's title leaf «فصل في التوحيد»; 86 is blank; 87 explicitly attributes the piece to Ibn Taymiyya and begins the authorial discourse. Saved context images verify these boundaries.
- Printed 94–96 have no editor's footnote at the central argument. The author argues in the first-person/plural argumentative voice and distinguishes his position from «المتكلمين»/«قولهم» on 97.
- Printed 97's footnote is an editor's syntactic clarification. Printed 98's footnote is a Qurʾanic locus (23:91). These should not be attributed to Ibn Taymiyya as separate argumentative steps.

## What is and is not established for this inquiry

1. Established source fact: simultaneous cooperation is considered explicitly; alternate times or already completed production are not the only case under discussion.
2. Established source fact: «مستقل» in the decisive definition means actual sole doing. It is not simply interchangeable with possession of sufficient standing power.
3. Established source fact: a cooperator can possess solo capacity; its cooperative exercise is nevertheless treated as partial and prevented.
4. Established source fact: mandatory agreement is not overlooked. It is explicitly remitted to the nonduplication/cooperation argument.
5. Not independently established by these pages: that each of two original agents cannot fully produce the same numerically single effect at the same instant when whole production is defined without exclusive attribution. The text asserts the exclusion and supports it by partition/perfection/analogy; the rival denies the application of the partition bridge.
6. «تحصيل الحاصل» does not occur in the visually checked pp. 93–99. The key contradiction here is co-attribution versus actual sole action, not a second later production of an already produced effect. This absence claim is limited to the checked span.
