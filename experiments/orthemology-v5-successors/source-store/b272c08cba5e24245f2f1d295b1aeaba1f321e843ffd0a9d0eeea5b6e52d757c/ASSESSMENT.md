# Foundational warrant and the standard of justification

Supplementary internal model-assisted philosophical review, 3 October 2026 UTC. This is not external expert validation or a new foundational theorem. It does not amend the accepted report or articles, establish a metaphysical rival, or change programme closure. Historical source exposition remains solely in its existing evidence owners.

## Result

**Retain the report's wording and open closure status. No sentence-level correction is required.** The report does not make an empirical test, a neutral decision procedure, universal agreement, or the elimination of every describable rival a condition of rational knowledge. Its explicit allowances for basic rational recognition are substantive, not empty exceptions. The relevant questions ask whether the offered reason has the stronger content attributed to it, not whether that reason is mathematically demonstrable.

There is nevertheless an important positive epistemic verdict to state expressly when discussing this work: **the original-possession argument supplies a genuine prima-facie reason to favour an original productive basis for a complete existential account. The active rival description does not defeat that reason merely by being describable.** The current record does not establish an all-things-considered comparative verdict about the complete competing metaphysics. Nor does this initial reason yet identify an actual particular source, make the restricted explanation transfer cover the full target, or establish N2/N3. “Open” must not be reported as “irrational to believe,” “no positive reason,” or “the alternatives are equally credible.”

The strongest basic-warrant defence can carry a real bridge: collective nonpresupposition at the exact explanatory target. It cannot carry the bridge merely by transferring a local no-self-production judgement to a plural inventory. The detailed test below identifies where basic warrant would genuinely help, what the existing positive rationale already supports, and what the active challenge actually undercuts.

## 2. The standard must have more than one dimension

Entailment, justified belief, necessity and particular reference are not successive rungs of one certainty ladder.

1. An implication can be demonstratively valid while its premises remain unwarranted about the actual world.
2. A person can have defeasible rational justification for a proposition asserting categorical necessity. Fallibility of the person's justification does not weaken the proposition to a claim of merely contingent existence.
3. A reason can establish an actual particular dependence without identifying a whole-origin source, or establish a necessary participant without establishing common-bearer unity.
4. A basic presentation can rationally warrant connected content without a further proof for every component. Descriptive identification of the actual referent can suffice; sensory acquaintance, a personal name, and articulation of a modal taxonomy are not required.
5. A metaphysical explanation need not necessitate every effect or choice to explain it. But a claim whose stated content is unconditional existence-entailing intrinsic sufficiency must actually include that stronger content.
6. A hypothesis can be rationally preferable without being the only possible account. Conversely, a label such as “best explanation” earns no preference unless the proposed account explains more or explains better without silently purchasing that gain through its unproved conclusion.

The accepted articles already preserve these distinctions: N 124–144, 162–182; I 31–35, 54–56, 82–84; F 108–120, 124–136. R 104 explicitly rejects measurement, universal agreement and derivation from more basic premises as necessary conditions. R 134 credits a necessary participant before unity. These are important limits on how R 8, 14, 20 and 336 may be read.

A genuine rebutting defeater supplies reason against the proposition. An undercutting defeater attacks the connection between the offered reason and that proposition. A formal interpretation can expose an invalid inference without being a rebutting metaphysical counterexample. Mere rival describability does neither to an independent, correctly targeted basic presentation. N 172–182 and R 160 correctly distinguish these jobs.

## 3. The strongest collective basic-warrant defence

### 3.1 Exact proposal

Let P be the declared existential inventory. Let Q be the proposed actual productive exercises offered to explain P. Retain real cognition, real powers, and effective reception at every link. Consider this principle:

**Collective nonpresupposition:** If an account is offered as explaining the existential availability of the whole declared inventory, and all of its productive availability is received within that very inventory, the account cannot discharge that existential explanatory demand merely through the mutually covering receptions. At least some relevant efficacy must be available otherwise than through the very received availability being accounted for.

This is a proposed general constraint on existential explanation, not a definition of “explanation” or an assertion that every explanation must terminate in one individual. It allows several independent contributors, an infinite subordinate ancestry, real agency in recipients, and contingent exercises. It does not require every explanatory fact to have a further explanation. It therefore targets an actual open bridge without bundling P1/P2, unity, N2, N3, cognition and integrity into one axiom. The antecedent's target-relative strength must remain visible (C 39–54, 82–92; F 40–46).

### 3.2 Why this has positive rational force

The reason is not that infinity is unintelligible. A received exercise genuinely answers how one recipient gets its power. It leaves the supplier's own existential possession to another part of the account. Repeatedly changing which recipient is under discussion can leave the mode of possession everywhere received. By contrast, intrinsically possessed efficacy addresses that outstanding respect positively: there is something whose exercise does not require another donor of that very possession. This is an intelligible explanatory improvement for a question about complete existential supply, rather than a short-label preference or a first-event demand (U 49–72; B 88–100; F 100–104).

That improvement is a **prima-facie reason to favour original possession**. No actual infinite cosmos must first be disproved before the reason counts. A person's direct rational apprehension of collective nonpresupposition could also be a basic warrant for the principle; demanding a second derivation as a condition of its being basic would be self-defeating. The relevant discipline is examination of the presented content and actual objections, not an infinite regress of certificates.

Original possession is not automatically a complete explanation of its possessor's existence. It can answer why efficacy is not borrowed while leaving the actuality of the intrinsic bearer primitive. Its positive explanatory gain at the possession question should be credited without crediting N2/N3 as well (N 57–65, 162–168; U 121–131).

### 3.3 The active rival's strongest answer

The rival says that every actual loan is fulfilled by an actually powerful supplier. Nothing is merely conditionally available, and no member awaits a first activation. Its Q has more content than P: actual apprehension, ends, means, exercises and received relations. It denies that existence of every supplier is a collectively prior resource that must first be available before these distributive explanations can operate (J 85–103; C 35–54).

That answer matters. It prevents the positive reason from relying on the false picture of uniformly unfulfilled conditions or empty permissions. It also undercuts an attempted *derivation* of collective nonpresupposition from nothing but each exercise's bearer-dependence and local productive irreflexivity. The latter do not by themselves show the prohibited collective dependence.

The answer does **not** establish that Q explains P adequately, that the structure is metaphysically possible, or that original possession has lost its comparative appeal. Its local fulfilments do not settle the full existential question merely by being called fulfilments. Conversely, the affirmative cannot acquire an all-things-considered victory simply because the rival has not completed its ontology. These are symmetrical burdens of explanatory adequacy, not a presumption that the theories have equal merit (C 74–88; A 19–27; J 103–111).

### 3.4 Can basic warrant close this bridge now?

A genuine basic grasp of the collective principle **at the explanation standard E actually promised** could supply the exclusion bridge. It would not need deduction from local causality, empirical measurement, or an independently certified impossible rival. It would be assessed directly: is the subject appreciating a target-level existential presupposition, or only the perfectly sound limitation of one donor considered alone?

The existing affirmative rationale gives initial support for the former at the level of complete existential supply. Its weaker local elements do not establish that scope, and the present record supplies no further presentation that settles the application to E rather than the stronger whole-origin E*. This is a content/application limitation, not a finding that basic reasoning lacks evidential standing. A correctly targeted basic insight would address it; calling the weaker insight “basic” would not (F 40–46; C 45–54, 86; A 19–25).

**Disposition:** prima-facie reason for original possession; no demonstrated defeat of that reason; no certified active counterexample; same-E collective exclusion remains unestablished in this record. This assessment does not designate the complete cosmological inference “undefeated,” because its separate target, joint-possibility and transfer obligations have not thereby been discharged. Nor does it designate the principle false or the original-source belief unjustified.

## 4. Intrinsic necessity: defend an actual reason, not only a modal table

The strongest concrete positive starting point is intrinsic unborrowed productive power: actual efficacy is not supplied by the product or reciprocal empowerment. This supplies an ontologically serious bearer and a mode of possessing power. It is not merely the absence of a recorded incoming arrow (N 57–63).

The strongest extension is that a possibly uninstantiated intrinsic reality requires an existential condition for its actual instantiation; an original bearer cannot receive such a condition, so its intrinsic reality is unconditionally instantiated. This is a metaphysical explanatory proposal, not efficient self-production. It has a real rationale: a conditionally characterising nature leaves the actuality of its instantiation unaccounted for. The opponent's “the bearer must already exist before it acts” objection misses that intrinsic explanation is not a later action (N 61–65; U 121–125).

The proposal can warrant a de re necessity conclusion if its general existential principle and actual bearer application are rationally warranted. Such warrant could be basic or explanatory and defeasible. No universal consensus is required. But two actual limitations remain:

- The offered intrinsic-power reason distinguishes possessing power intrinsically from receiving power. By itself it does not distinguish intrinsically possessing a power **if instantiated** from having an intrinsically existence-entailing nature. Primitive actuality with the same actual powers preserves that weaker reason's content.
- No actual P1/P2 identification is supplied merely by the modal test's grant. Even a good general law cannot turn a hypothetical g into an established actual whole-origin bearer.

The content-replacement test is therefore legitimate here. It does not assert that the primitive account is metaphysically possible or defeat a stronger direct insight by imagining a rival. It shows that the weaker positive reason has not yet stated what the stronger conclusion needs (N 168–182; R 96–104). An actual basic recognition of unconditional intrinsic sufficiency would fail that replacement test in the right way: replacing sufficiency by primitiveness would remove what had been recognised.

The exact-origin equivalence also is not an epistemic veto. A general, independently motivated theory can yield a consequence equivalent to one of its target instances. Equivalence alone does not make the general theory circular. R 96 and N 118 preserve precisely this escape. The remaining assessment concerns the law's origin-qualified reach, not whether its consequence is strong.

**Disposition:** accepted conditional de re entailments; serious positive intrinsic-power and existential-explanation proposals; no new actual particular recognition or categorical-necessity conclusion established by this record. No global judgement that a philosophical believer lacks justified belief follows.

## 5. Integrity: local normative warrant has already been earned

In the controlled case the source knowingly counterfeits an evidential assurance, with understanding of irreversible reliance and no undefeated overriding justification. Its status as original does not make the unsafe target safe or erase the self-undertaken truth-linked responsibility. This is an affirmative rational reason that the specified act is defective. A numerical measure of perfection is unnecessary. Relational exercise also need not make standing fidelity borrowed (I 27–35).

That local judgement has more warrant than the mixed source's speculative betrayal motive has as an actual explanation. The motive is articulated, but has no independent actual evidence (J 33–47). It cannot defeat ordinary prima-facie trust merely because someone can tell the story. Where genuine intelligent concern is independently evidenced and attribution is secure, it can supply defeasible reason to expect fidelity. The packet, however, does not establish such an actual assurance episode from the ultimate source, and that defeasible expectation would not be essential T0.

A direct source-priority defence can be formulated as follows: the same source that originally gives a genuine practical excellence possesses that excellence without an intrinsic defect in the relevant practical respect. To exclude the present lie it must be source-wide practical priority, not only production of some virtuous acts. This could be a basic rational apprehension, rather than a proof constructed from a survey of all eligible profiles (I 35, 56, 82–84; Logical Appendix 71–75).

The strongest challenge is not “a malicious-source table is consistent.” It is that **originally producing excellence and governing every exercise by that excellence are distinct contents**. The mixed-source case preserves real virtuous giving, real understanding and real defective willing together at the conditional level. It thereby identifies why the premise about some original giving does not yet express comprehensive governance. It does not certify that mixed essence as metaphysically eligible (J 29–47, 51–65).

For the repair route, the actual decisive judgement against the lie can support an overall improvement when an eligible alternative has been identified; it need not support a loss-free Pareto improvement. Even that actual strict improvement does not show the source's actual profile is nondominated. The fact that an act should not be done is different from the claim that this bearer cannot do it. These are substantive distinctions in practical reality, not demands for exhaustive computation (I 45–56, 74–84).

**Disposition:** warranted local normative assessment at the controlled case's admitted scope; rational eligibility of fidelity as source excellence; no new warrant for essential source-wide nondomination, actual divine disclosure, or recipient reliance. The mixed story supplies neither a rebutting actual defeater nor a metaphysical counterexample to a genuinely warranted source-priority principle.

## 6. What can be credited now

- **Actual local recognition:** the corrected claim answers to the displayed witness; under the admitted cognitive interpretation the understanding is reason-responsive. This local normative credit is retained, not suspended while global foundations are open (R 72; F 15–23, 136; L 29–35).
- **Actual local productive dependence:** identified received resources require actual suppliers. This is positive actual-world content at its stated scope; it is not a global single-source conclusion (U 15–25; L 31).
- **Original possession:** the complete-existential-supply rationale supplies a prima-facie reason to favour it. The rival is not a defeater by mere description. The same-E collective application and full argument remain unresolved (§3 above).
- **Necessity:** the modal implications are established as entailments. The intrinsic-power rationale supplies real metaphysical motivation, while unconditional de re instantiation and actual bearer identification remain unestablished (§4).
- **Integrity:** the specified deception is rationally assessable as defective without measurement. Essential nonlying of the actual original source is a further proposition, not a stronger degree of confidence in the local assessment (§5).
- **Rival status:** local adequacy and definite explanatory mechanisms earn consideration; absent actual support and joint metaphysical/explanatory adequacy prevent a completed rival verdict. Their incompleteness does not erase the positive case or prove it (R 164–178).

Thus the warranted intermediate result is not a mathematical proof of metaphysics and not a flat suspension of all judgement. It is a differentiated body of actual local warrant, conditional entailments, prima-facie metaphysical reasons, and unresolved stronger applications.

## 7. Reporting disposition and next discriminating work

No corrective replacement sentence is proposed. R 104, 134–136, 160, 166, 174–178 expressly prevents the sceptical interpretation; N 172–182 and I 82–84 make the basic-warrant permission explicit. The report's wording concerns what this investigation establishes, not everything any rational person could be justified in believing. C 86–94 already credits the positive rationale. A 7, 19–27 preserves its affirmative force after direct primary inspection.

The useful next discriminator is **an assessment of the offered positive reason at its own strongest content**:

1. For collective explanation, defend why actual existential bearer-dependence lifts to prohibited collective presupposition under the precise E at issue, or make a direct rational case that the existential target itself requires that nonpresupposing scope. Do not require a mathematical proof of that metaphysical judgement. Do not conceal strengthening E to E*.
2. For necessity, present the actual referent and the intrinsic feature that does more than characterise it if it exists. The issue is whether that feature rationally excludes noninstantiation, not whether the rival can furnish a g-less cosmos.
3. For integrity, identify why original giving warrants comprehensive governance in this practical respect, or identify an eligible complete improvement together with actual nondomination. The issue is not how to measure every profile.

These are tests of actual reasons, not requests for three new formal encodings. A successful basic or explanatory defence can change a bridge's status without achieving final programme closure. A successful necessary-participant conclusion deserves immediate credit even if unity, N2/N3 and integrity remain open. Nothing in this review requires those later claims as the price of accepting that intermediate gain.
