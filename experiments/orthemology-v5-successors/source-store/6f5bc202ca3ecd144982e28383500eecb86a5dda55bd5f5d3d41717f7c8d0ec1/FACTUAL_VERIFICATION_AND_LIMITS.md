## Attribution rules actually applied

- Scriptural quotations and prophetic reports are identified as the text’s evidential materials. Their historical occurrence and isnad reliability have not been independently verified in this task.
- Ghazali, al-Razi, al-Baqillani, other schools and philosophers are often quoted or reconstructed before the author replies. Their attributed positions are not automatically the author’s own assertions, and the polemical reconstructions have not been checked against complete works of each opponent.
- Modern page headings and footnotes belong to the editor. The focused apparatus comments above are explicitly attributed to him; no blanket apparatus-reading claim is made.
- Jinn transport, sorcery, ancient catastrophe, celestial movement, preserved sanctuaries and bodily functions appear within the historical author’s argument. No supernatural report or ancient scientific account is relabeled an independently verified modern empirical fact.
- Harsh polemic and historical punishments are reported as source content, without endorsement as current recommendations.
