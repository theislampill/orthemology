## What changed the evidential balance

The exact supplied Oneness return confirmed that Ibn Taymiyyah directly addresses agreement, cooperation, intact powers, coalescence, and complete efficacy. The dispute is not that he overlooked harmonious agents. The earlier comparative-disqualification argument also remains a substantive answer to the historical claim that concentrating powers must be an identity-preserving upgrade of each existing candidate.

The new overdetermination reading nevertheless supplies a developed rival to a particular inference. Schaffer gives defeasible modal and causal-process reasons for two actually operative, individually sufficient processes to produce the same whole designated effect in a nonexclusive, effect-relative sense. His nonphysical and conditional noncompositional cases cannot be reduced to his separate component-force argument. This is more than an uninterpreted response table or merely available backup capacity.

The established scope is still below a complete original-resource account. An intact route can presuppose a recipient, sustaining conditions, and other resources. Neither Schaffer nor Hall establishes two perfected originals, each actually supplying the entire unreceived basis of the same created effect or order. But that limitation does not vindicate the converse inference that every actual peer contribution necessarily supplies something the first route lacks.

The exact residual is therefore:

> Does complete unreceived original adequacy require sole exhaustive actual production, or can each of two actual routes already possess whole effect-relative original adequacy without needing the other?

This must be settled by a substantive nonduplication, nonabsorption, or original-perfection argument. It cannot be settled by defining an `entire` classifier as singleton provenance, by observing that both causes are real, or by requiring uncreated acts to leave extra created traces.

I have no independently warranted full countermodel preserving all of the stronger original-perfection premises while falsifying their conclusion. I also cannot use the absence of that countermodel as proof that the disputed bridge is sound. The developed local counterevidence is enough to prevent treating the bridge as an unqualified self-evident consequence of causal independence.

## What de se knowledge contributes

The owner's proposal remains correctly understood as a discriminator. If an agent's actual efficacy depends on an indispensable foreign contribution, complete truthful self-knowledge exposes that qualification; it cannot truthfully represent the same efficacy as wholly independent. Perfect practical conformance can matter once the deficiency classification is warranted.

For the redundant case, however, the presence of a second actual route is not itself proof of such need. Knowledge cannot manufacture that classification. An agent could, at the level of the reduced interpretation, know that its own route is sufficient and another sufficient route is also active. Establishing that this cannot be the truthful self-description of a perfected original is precisely the remaining perfection/production issue.

Neither ordinary propositional knowledge of another being nor correct first-person reference entails existential dependence on that being. The earlier centred-knowledge result retains its limited semantic credit and its reviewed lack of mechanically established action-guiding basing.

## The further uniqueness argument did not close this residual

Pruss's §5.5 variant makes an important positive demand: a necessary peer's contingent act or nonexercise is not exempt from a total-contingency explanation merely because the peer exists necessarily. Its cross-world construction preserves de re identity and genuinely generates contingent omission facts.

But its creator premise covers all contingent truths, including facts about uncreated acts and explanation itself. A creator of the created field alone does not automatically meet that premise. Even granting essential powers independently, causal explanation of a peer's nonexercise does not automatically establish robust sovereign control against every admissible peer exercise. Reciprocal standing preventative powers do not alone yield jointly successful incompatible exercises or an infinite descent of distinct productive prerequisites.

Gellman's original 2000 paper assumes within-world uniqueness and explicitly leaves that stage unproved. It cannot be cited as a completed independent proof of the missing premise. Pruss's variant is a serious additional argument, but its explanatory-control and anti-regress bridges require their own warrant.

The reduced alternating-preemption control identifies a logical scope limit. It does not explain every contingent fact or authenticate two perfect creators. No full premise-preserving metaphysical countermodel has been promoted from it.
