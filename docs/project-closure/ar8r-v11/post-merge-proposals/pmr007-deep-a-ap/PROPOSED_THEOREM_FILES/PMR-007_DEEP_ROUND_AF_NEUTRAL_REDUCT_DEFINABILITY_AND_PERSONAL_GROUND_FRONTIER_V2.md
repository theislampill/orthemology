## 1. Typed setting

Let `L0` be the **complete declared neutral vocabulary actually used by a
proposed bridge**.  It may include modal, actuality, semantic-anchor, norm,
articulability, provenance, causal-intervention, nonseparable interaction, and
implementation coordinates, but each coordinate must be independently eligible
rather than a renamed target.

Let

```text
L+ = L0 union {P}
```

where `P` is a personal, intentional, first-person, Wisdom, or Speech predicate.
Let `K` be a declared class of admissible `L+` structures.

Two structures are:

```text
exact-reduct twins:
  same domain and identical L0 interpretations;

reduct-isomorphic twins:
  their L0 reducts are isomorphic, with the isomorphism carried through the
  target comparison.
```

`P` is **implicitly determined by the neutral reduct on K** when every pair of
exact-reduct twins in `K` agrees on `P`.  The isomorphism-invariant version also
requires agreement across reduct-isomorphic twins.

## 2. NRID-1A — exact twin barrier

If `K` contains two exact-reduct twins `M_I` and `M_P` such that

```text
P(M_I) != P(M_P),
```

then no exact classifier, formula, invariant, or proof object whose value
factors only through the complete `L0` reduct can determine `P` on `K`.

### Proof

Every reduct-only object has the same value on `M_I` and `M_P`, because their
`L0` reducts are identical.  An exact `P`-classifier would have to return both
different target values on that one input, which is impossible. ∎

The same argument holds for isomorphism-invariant objects over reduct-isomorphic
twins.

## 3. NRID-1B — finite attained-class characterization

For a finite attained class, let

\[
ho:M\mapsto Red_{L0}(M)
\]

and let `p(M)` encode the complete finite target interpretation being queried.
Then an exact reduct-only lookup classifier exists **iff** `p` is constant on
every attained fibre of `rho`.

This is the existing finite fibre/factorization criterion, now applied at the
architecture/realizer boundary.  It is not a new abstract theorem.

## 4. NRID-1C — guarded first-order frontier

For an ordinary first-order theory `T` in `L+`, over its full class of models,
the exact theory-level question is whether `T` **implicitly defines** `P` from
`L0`: any two models of `T` with the same `L0` reduct must agree on `P`.

Under the hypotheses of the classical first-order Beth definability theorem,
implicit definability yields an explicit `L0` formula defining `P`.  Therefore a
neutral personal-ground proof at that scope must do one of two substantive
things:

```text
1. prove that every admissible expansion of each neutral reduct agrees on P;
   or
2. supply an independently warranted neutral definition of P.
```

This paragraph does not transfer Beth automatically to finite-model-only,
modal, second-order, non-elementary, intensional, probabilistic, or
source-governed classes.

## 6. Eligible parity-breaking routes

```text
NEUTRAL ENRICHMENT:
  independently observed or proved subject-level uptake, de se ownership,
  because-of-fittingness causation, or another target-separating coordinate;

CLASS RESTRICTION:
  a non-question-begging metaphysical or causal principle excluding one
  expansion;

SOURCE-CONDITIONED EXPANSION:
  authenticated source predicates plus referent identification, producing a
  source-relative class rather than a neutral entailment;

ABDUCTIVE COMPARISON:
  an independently justified prior or explanatory metric that need not factor
  through the shared reduct, yielding support rather than exact entailment.
```

A proxy defined as equivalent to `P`, an idle bearer label, or an unsupported
closure rule is not an eligible enrichment.

## 8. Source, implementation, and world firewall

An authenticated Track-N source may co-predicate personal and Speech attributes
of one referent and thereby alter the source-expanded intended class.  That is
not a neutral Track-T definition unless an independent source-to-world and
referent-identification bridge is supplied.

OSM trajectory similarity, PRH kernel alignment, daee runtime behavior, or
agentic protocol traces can enrich `L0` only at their actually measured scope.
They do not become subject-level evidence merely because their vocabulary is
compatible with agency.

Formal impersonal expansions establish nonentailment in the declared model
class.  They do not establish metaphysical actuality, equal probability, or
physical implementability.
