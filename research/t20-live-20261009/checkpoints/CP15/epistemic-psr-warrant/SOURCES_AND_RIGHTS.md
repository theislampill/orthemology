# Sources and reuse boundaries

## Primary argument witnesses

1. Robert C. Koons, *A New Look at the Cosmological Argument*, *American Philosophical Quarterly* 34(2) (1997), 193–211. [Journal issue metadata](https://www.jstor.org/stable/i20009890). The [read manuscript mirror](https://lionandlambapologetics.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/A-New-Look-at-the-Cosmological-Argument.pdf) includes a cover identifying Koons's [ResearchGate upload](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/253291940_A_New_Look_at_the_Cosmological_Argument). It is dated 18 December 1996 and is not silently equated with the journal pagination. Source text captures are research working material. No redistribution license was established. Omit raw captures from any user-facing or public package; provide links and bounded original analysis instead.

2. Robert C. Koons and Alexander R. Pruss, *Skepticism and the principle of sufficient reason*, online 8 June 2020; *Philosophical Studies* 178 (2021), 1079–1099. [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11098-020-01482-3); [author-hosted PDF](https://robkoons.net/uploads/1/3/5/2/135276253/koons-pruss2020_article_skepticismandtheprincipleofsuf.pdf). The article is licensed [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Captured text preserves the public reader's line/page labels and extraction defects; concatenation removes duplicate overlapping displayed lines, not substantive author text. The report is a new reconstruction and criticism. Attribution is to both authors, and the authors are not represented as endorsing the report.

3. Alexander R. Pruss, *Leibnizian Cosmological Arguments*, [author chapter](http://alexanderpruss.com/papers/LCA.html). Existing source file was inspected for the assigned §§2.1–2.2.2. It is inherited, not a newly acquired source. No additional source body is copied into this package.

## Capture versus reading

Acquisition is not reading. The initial targeted-stage studied spans, then-unstudied captures, failed binary downloads, failed screenshot attempts, and HTML corroboration remain recorded in the unchanged READING_LEDGER.json. READING_COMPLETION_LEDGER.json records the subsequent completion of the remaining 1997 manuscript text. Both selected article witnesses are now read in full, with the visual and version limits recorded separately. No conclusion relies on an unverified formula glyph or a claim about an unread cited work.
