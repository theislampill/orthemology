# Orthemology — objects, mathematics, philosophical method

Orthemology is a proposed, research-stage interdisciplinary study of *objective orthability and warranted resolution*: what makes particular cases really distinguishable and appropriately assessable, what governs correct handling, which processes can apprehend the right distinctions, and how an inquiry should retain, revise and independently review its results. It is not merely better meeting minutes, an unquestionable ontology or a completed proof of God.

## Vocabulary (the terms are proposed, not internationally established)

- **Orthemma:** this particular case or occurrence, with an identity and version.
- **Ortheme:** a repeatable state-type or distinction that may actually apply to an orthemma relative to analysis A.
- **Metaortheme:** a general governing norm/criterion; **metaorthemma:** that norm bound to a particular case and version.
- **Orthing:** the complete governed process of examining, classifying, correcting and handling; **orthing episode:** one concrete, dated execution. A genuine informal investigation can be an orthing-like process even if it has not earned audit-ready qualification.
- **Orthability:** the conditions for meaningful correct discrimination/fittingness. Local, objective and representational senses must not be conflated.
- **Result/pathway separation:** a correct outcome can arise from a defective process and an adequate process can reach an erroneous outcome on misleading evidence. `RESULT_CORRECT` is not interchangeable with `REASONING_PATH_ADEQUATE` or `TOKEN_TRUTH_LINKED`.
- **Residual and successor:** remaining obligations and the continued valid research/evidence state. A checkpoint, filing or reported PASS is not automatically programme closure.

## Programme layers

1. **Typed core and governance:** occurrence/analysis/evidence/bearer/source/procedure/inferred result/actual result, version, permission, duty, provenance, obligations and retained histories. This includes schemata, example instances, tests, independent review and protected integration gates.
2. **Mathematics of inquiry:** typed graded tensors and contractions; fibre preservation; information loss and minimal predictive memory; guarded proof compilation and cost; witness preservation; game-theoretic and causal models; ordinary probability, Bayesian correction, Hessian/Bregman geometry, Riemannian flows, moment realisability and computability; dependent type theories and their exact proof limitations.
3. **Affirmative philosophy:** objective orthability, original actuality, intrinsically necessary productive sourcehood, one complete common original Creator in the relevant connected created order, divine Knowledge, Will and Wisdom, recognition through fiṭrah, proper function, reliabilism, testimony, truthful communication and source-to-norm authority.
4. **Applications:** DAEE-epistemics, formal debate, agent handoff, institutional succession, research-evidence revision, possible mechanistic interpretability and epistemic security. These are not all validated domains merely because the abstract mathematics is conditional and sound.

## Methodological inversion

Instead of assuming one speculative ontology and coercing every downstream case into it, prefer actual cases/recognition/primary-source arguments → precise target/bearer/inference → formal proof or countermodel → check interpretation/warrant → preserve results and negative examples → test structure-preserving transports to adjacent questions → update the research frontier. The proposed research ecology itself can become a mathematical object, but no completed universal runtime is presumed.

## What genuine mathematics does—and does not do

- A **tensor** is a specified multilinear/typed algebraic object, not a generic multidimensional record. Orthemology's September formalisation defines finite-support graded representations and actual contractions; a faithful semantic interface must be declared.
- A **Riemannian gradient** is real mathematics under a specified smooth positive-definite metric, objective and domain. The repository already contains strict-descent Hessian/Bregman constructions. It does not follow that the entire DAEE runtime is a continuous differential-geometric field.
- **Proof assistants** check formal inferences from typed hypotheses. They do not thereby certify actual historical source readings, logical interpretation of unformalised premises, human cognition, or metaphysical actuality.
- A **correctness/performance score** only measures the stated objective. Reducing it may distort evidence, lose source permissions or erase an indispensable future distinction.
- **Prior art matters:** PSR/Hankel rank, proper scoring, Shannon/KL, bisimulation/partition refinement, moment problems, weighted deduction, numerical integration, Set Cover hardness, identity and induction all have classical ancestry. The programme's claimed leverage often concerns exact bridges, scope corrections, coherent synthesis and new conditional consequences rather than inventing those existing fields.

# Foundations: objective orthability, Necessary Being, Creator, attributes and cumulative warrant

**Important epistemic discipline:** T20's inherited philosophical findings are *defeasible positive appraisals* under studied actual-source arguments and their premises. They are not neutral axiom-free Lean discoveries about concrete reality; equally, a later formalism's incomplete generality does not reset these source-based reasons to zero.

## A. The load-bearing positive chain

1. **Objective orthability:** Correct/fitting distinctions and governing standards refer to something beyond the preferences of a particular evaluator. Distinguish local rule compliance, actual correctness and adequacy of the evaluation process. Norms do not themselves instantiate an actual object.
2. **Actual actuality-entry:** Begin from independently warranted *actual received existence/production*, not an abstract possibility whose existence is assumed or a synthetic norm that generates an actual bearer by definition. The T17 original-resource-to-bearer argument and CP24's recovery address how original concrete powers require an actual bearing source.
3. **Original existential adequacy → intrinsic necessity:** The substantive question is why something holding complete original actuality cannot be an entirely received being. The CP23 philosophy reported a positive rational warrant that the same actual original is intrinsically necessary; that verdict is not interchangeable with a new universally certified Lean theorem. Countermodels must preserve concrete instantiation/same-respect premises to be serious.
4. **One complete common original Creator:** A defect in an account of two harmonious creators cannot be reduced to the trivial claim that their wills might disagree. The stronger issue is whether two numerically independent complete original producers can each provide the whole *same concrete* effect, maintain original efficacy, and avoid receiving completion from the other. Productive connectedness and local-to-global overlap assumptions matter. Earlier source-based appraisal favours one complete common original source for the relevant connected created order; keep its scope and exact premises.
5. **Knowledge, Will, Wisdom:** Particular effect-production, knowingly intended means/ends, own-act purpose, actual will, a creature's response, particular exercise and created effect are different. Arguments concerning comprehensive Knowledge require additional perfection/source grounds beyond just a finite causal model. Wise action cannot be scored by beneficial outcomes alone; disclosed intention and intrinsic reasons matter. Actual responsive action need not imply acquiring a missing essential perfection.
6. **Epistemic warrant / recognition:** fiṭrah, basic recognition, testimony, proper function, truth-oriented cognition, inference and corroboration may supply partly independent grounds. A modelled decrease in numerical error does not make an actual belief warranted. An independently warranted surviving ground need not vanish when another certificate is defeated.
7. **Source → norm → particular utterance → recipient duty:** rightful authority, source adoption/authentication, communicative intention, command vs counsel, beneficial non-disclosure, recipient qualification and enforceable obligations are separate. A past permission need not imply irrevocable surrender of rightful authority. Divine fidelity to an undertaking is not external completion from another source.

## B. Why the mathematical research contributes *to* these foundations

A categorical statement `P1 & P2 & P3 ⇒ N` has a formal consequence claim, but warranted knowledge of `N` also needs faithful interpretation of each premise, independent grounds, source currentness and the reason the inference applies to actual reality. Let `W_N(s)` be a *justified*, declared warrant assessment of claim `N` under complete epistemic state `s`, and `r(s)` a compact information record. The fibre criterion

`r(s1)=r(s2)  ⇒  W_N(s1)=W_N(s2)`

characterises exactly when the retained representation is sufficient **for that declared warrant target**. The mathematics does not tell us, without further epistemology, which `W_N` correctly models knowledge. But it can expose when a candidate representation loses independent recognition, source provenance or an inferential dependency and therefore cannot implement its own stated warrant contract.

This is the central cross-front point: tensors, proof costs, information preservation, epistemic geometry and type theory can be *foundational research about warranted apprehension itself*, not merely tools tangentially applied to an already completed theological argument.

## D. A suggested serious warrant-revision test

Construct two scoped investigators affirming `N`, one on independent fiṭrī recognitional and causal grounds plus testimony, another on a single copied testimonial certificate. Give them identical **present conclusion and numerical confidence** if desired. Withdraw that copied source. Specify a legitimate proper-functional/inferential warrant standard and test whether the correct result diverges after withdrawal. - Independent specialist philosophical assessment and ordinary empirical testing remain valuable; lack of external validation isn't absence of valid conditional mathematics, but nor do internal AI reviews certify actual-world metaphysics.

**Research priority:** pursue substantive affirmative premises and high-leverage shared mathematics rather than allowing an endless catalogue of weak rival philosophical systems to displace them.
