## 1. Candidate setting

Let `A_e` be a finite neutral episode structure containing candidate intellectual
hosts, represented content, reason-token uptake, control, and episode-transition
relations for one declared episode `e`.  Let `Aut(A_e)` be the automorphisms of
that complete neutral structure fixing the episode coordinate.

A target `SELF_e(s)` says that `s` is the first-person owner or center of `e`.

## 2. AJ-1 — orbit obstruction

If two candidate hosts lie in the same `Aut(A_e)` orbit, no invariant neutral
selector can choose one of them as the unique `SELF_e` while rejecting the
other.  A uniquely selected center must therefore be fixed by every neutral
structure automorphism.

## 3. Initial positive bridge candidate

The initial candidate proposed that a unique fixed intellectual host which
uptakes the registered reason content and controls the episode is sufficient to
classify a personal agentive subject.
