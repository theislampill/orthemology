# Explanatory solution selection: bounded research probe

Question: Does T17's non-presupposing original-completion rationale have an exact interpretation that distinguishes real resource provision from a merely self-supporting assignment, without prohibiting beginningless action histories or infinite prerequisite families?

Use a relation of rules with arbitrary prerequisite sets and an original-resource predicate. Compare locally satisfied availability (a fixed point) with inductively generated availability (the least closed account). Test an acyclic all-received fixed point, an original seed with an unsupported branch, an infinitely branching generated account, and beginningless act tokens supported by a standing original resource. These controls diagnose the scope of a proposed semantics; no least-solution principle is asserted as a metaphysical theorem.

## Executed design correction

Root review exposed the difference between candidate-rule paths and actually used paths, and between designated seeds and actual nonreceipt. These are now separately tested and guarded. Independent review also shows full token-graph generation is overstrong for an originally supplied beginningless history.
