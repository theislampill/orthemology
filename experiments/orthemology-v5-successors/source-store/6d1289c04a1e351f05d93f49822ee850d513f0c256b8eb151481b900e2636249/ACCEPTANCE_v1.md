## 1. Why the positive grade is substantive

Paper sections2–4 do more than repeat that a sufficiently strong RP would make the theorem valid. They identify a reason to demand a contribution specifically to the proposed supplier's availability: a capacity advertised as sufficient for the whole achievement cannot deliver only its recipient while borrowing the independently available supplier as unexplained enabling background. Once a real contribution and a retained entry are fixed, downstream production cannot remove that entry from the very route whose achievement is being assessed.

That is a rationally warranted local adequacy judgement. It is stronger than an assertion that the argument is formally consistent. It also remains narrower than a universal claim that every absolute possibility has the required realization. The paper preserves that distinction and does not use its local positive grade as a substitute for POS-S.

An objection which grants a genuinely sufficient whole-achievement capacity but treats the crucial supplier as a freely supplied merely possible auxiliary has changed the premise. An objection which questions whether any such capacity exists is legitimate but addresses a different point. The paper gives the strongest affirmative its proper premise rather than defeating a weakened substitute.

## 2. The occurrence-retention issue is resolved at the right scope

The review separately checked three potential fixed items: the actual bearer, its actually possessed power-profile, and an actual classified occurrence relevant to the supply. They cannot be interchanged without argument.

The first retention strategy in section4 is sound locally. If the actual F-bearing occurrence is genuinely retained as support for the supplier, later changes do not produce a history independent of that occurrence. The classification need not itself be an additional efficient cause. The occurrence must be relevant to the supplier's availability, and the target must retain its full support scope.

The second strategy is also legitimate: an independently warranted classification consequence of the retained power-profile can preserve the relevant membership without preserving every accidental feature. But explanatory priority in the other direction does not prove that consequence. A classification's being one basis of a capacity does not make it the only basis.

These are serious ways of supporting RP4. The study does not present either as an automatic consequence of rigid naming or actual coverage. Nor does it classify every conceivable alternative prehistory as metaphysically possible. The remaining issue is the existence of a realization with one of the required retention properties.

The quantifier matters. One eligible realization of the selected S is enough. Showing that another realization changes its upstream conditions does not refute the theorem. An earlier actual entry can support a changed later prehistory; the route need not freeze the entire past or use one universal entry for all targets. Conversely, varying the whole relevant basis cannot be called retention merely because an individual or a power-type keeps the same name.

The final warrant grade is therefore precise: positively justified local occurrence-preserving generation; a serious stronger interpretation for the application; and no automatic universal transport result for every qualified absolute possibility. This reconciles the independently formed reviewer assessment with the author's developed argument without hiding their initial difference of emphasis.

## 3. The subsidiary controls pass

Section2 keeps three important obligations separate.

- Actual p does not establish the extra qualifications in S. Actual S and merely possible S therefore enter different branches of the accepted argument.
- A finite sequence of supplying steps does not imply a finite number of contributors or a finite complete past for each entry resource.
- Joint contribution need not be individual sufficiency. Equally, arbitrary membership in a sufficient basis does not make the chosen witness an active bearer of the required power.

Actual power possession and actual exercise also remain distinct throughout the study. Neither a necessary resource nor the stronger capacity bearer has to exercise the relevant power in the actual field for the conditional existence conclusion to hold. The unresolved adequacy of an actual internal history is therefore not silently made a prerequisite of every route to a necessary bearer.

The return discussion in section3 is assessed at its declared scope. A failure to preserve a downstream agent does not settle whether a retained earlier source can regenerate the relevant capacity. But this repair cannot simply be assumed when the proposed variation removes the whole relevant basis. The discussion neither requires physical time reversal nor supplies an unlisted return premise to the separate Tenth extraction theorem.

## 4. The strengthened essential-grounding route receives its full credit

Sections6–7 correctly evaluate the stronger premise package on its own terms. If the relevant actual concrete intrinsic ground is established, it earns a necessary-particular result. It should not be downgraded to a necessarily occupied role or made to wait for proof of unity, actual exercise or identification with the original target's source.

The criticism does not deny this implication. It identifies two additional bridges needed by the proposed application.

First, removal of one selected sufficient witness is not removal of every adequate witness or replacement. The stronger exhaustive-removal inference is accepted when its joint realizability and counterfactual dependence are actually included. The paper does not extract those conditions from individual contingency or from a change to one actual support.

Second, the type of an existential proposition's subject matter does not by itself determine the type of its grounds. Excluding efficient production and excluding noncausal grounding are different claims. The paper gives the positive representational control its proper force: describing an instance does not supply the instance. It does not then pretend that this settles every substantive ground proposal.

The generic-activity control is appropriately narrow. An existential consequence does not by itself reverse the explanatory relation. That criticism is weaker than establishing a member of one rigidly fixed historical inventory. The text expressly preserves that distinction and does not claim a metaphysical counterexample to the exact-history argument.

These checks preserve the stronger theory's explanatory content. They do not prove it false, treat intrinsic explanation as unintelligible, or demand that a basic metaphysical judgement be derived from a more basic premise. They also do not import its richer existential ground into a powers-only argument without acknowledgement.

## 5. Residual status and ownership

The complete joint possibility in POS-S remains unestablished. Improving the account of how genuine possibilities are realized does not itself give a suitable outsider, productive recipient relation and full support qualification together. The broader coverage and witness-type conditions remain those of the accepted Tenth route.

Ninth F1 retains ownership of the qualified-achievement technique and its premise-relocation control. The accepted kind, transfer and joint-coverage studies retain their respective contributions. This study adds a source-level warrant assessment and sharper affirmative repairs; it does not claim first discovery of the primary theory or another proof of actual necessary existence.

Final byte and source-accounting verification support acceptance at that bounded status. Actual necessary-resource, actual supplying-power, intrinsic-ground, whole-origin identification and later-attribute conclusions must remain separate. None is acquired by exchanging those labels.
