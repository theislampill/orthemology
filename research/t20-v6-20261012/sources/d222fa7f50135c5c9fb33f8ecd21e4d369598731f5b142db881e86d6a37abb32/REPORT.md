# Productive origination from material: the received target and the complete account

11 October 2026 UTC. Additive source-and-application research within ACTIVE T20. This is not a closure report or an alteration of the protected argument.

## Result

**The source's positive case for actual productive receipt does not require an observed creation of raw material from absolute nothing.** A newly produced ordinary creature can be a genuinely new, received concrete individual even when its production uses earlier material and a continuing creaturely source. That is the useful application of the now continuously inspected Darʾ 7:219–224 and its differentiated cases at 5:196–203.

The discriminating gain is not another proof of necessity. It is a source-backed answer to a concrete target-selection question: **does production from something already actual reduce the target to a new property of that old bearer, or eliminate an existential explanandum?** Not in the source's defended material-generation cases. Moreover, “from” does not uniformly identify a persisting substrate, an expended material input, and an efficient supplier. The tree/fruit case positively separates those roles.

The complete original account must consequently explain the actual received product and cover whatever received materials and productive suppliers it uses. Explaining a local transition with such things available is genuine explanation; calling that transition explanation complete for their whole actuality changes its scope. CP16 already supplies the affirmative global-adequacy judgment. This operation gives that inherited judgment a more explicit, source-controlled entry case. CP23's affirmative intrinsic necessity of the same original and T17's accepted essential independence remain intact.

## 2. Continuous source control

### Darʾ 7:219–224: what originates, and what “from material” means

The preceding item's conclusion is quoted al-Ashʿarī. Item 1271 opens with Ibn Taymiyya's “قلت”. His argument starts from observed ordinary generation rather than a preliminary proof that all past events are finite. At 220–221 he contests reducing the new animal or plant to an alteration of retained atom-like subjects. At 222–223, common descriptions such as quantity do not establish numerical identity of their instances. He distinguishes a body's shape from the shaped body, and material from which it originates from a substrate in which it inheres. His account includes the disappearance of an earlier qualified material reality. At 224 he distinguishes dispensing with a disputed method from having proved that method false.

Exact short anchors are “وإنما يشتركان في النوع” and “لكن خلق من مادة”. These identify the numerical/type and material-origin distinctions; neither is a formal receipt predicate. ## 3. Executed discriminator: fruit and continuing tree

Fix the source's ordinary fruit-production case. The application assumes a numerically individuated fruit f and tree s, with f distinct from s, and material inputs m that are not identified wholesale with either merely by the word “matter”. These individuation claims concern concrete individuals, not distinct names for one universal. This is a generic source case, not a new field observation of a dated fruit.

The positive reason for selecting a received target is **its described material, developmental and productive derivation through antecedent realities**. Temporal novelty alone is not doing that work. The fruit's being produced through the tree and supplied materials constitutes the relevant dependent history. Its being a distinct concrete product rather than a new name for the tree supplies the target's identity. The prior fruit-less tree is not that fruit already existing under a different description.

Three proposals can now be tested.

1. **“The tree already existed, so no new concrete reality has been supplied.”** This trades on the identity of the supplier or material setting to deny the identity of the new product. The case deliberately contains both the continuing tree and the new fruit. A description that accounts for the former alone has omitted the latter. No destruction of the tree is required to make the fruit a genuine product.
2. **“The fruit comes from material, so the entire fruit is merely an attribute inhering in that original material bearer.”** This needs a further identity and inherence argument. Production from an antecedent is not that argument. It cannot be inferred from the same amount, common kind, or inherited constituents alone. Conversely, the source's shape examples show why actual persistence must be admitted where it is warranted; a change of name does not create a new bearer either.
3. **“The transition account already gives the complete original existential account.”** A developmental account may adequately explain how available ingredients and capacities produce f. The additional assertion that it covers their own received actuality has a larger target. Under CP16's affirmed completion standard, those resources cannot be silently credited as independently available when their existential provision is included in what the account claims to complete.

The first two tests give a positive, determinate target interpretation; the third preserves the separate global inference. They do not deduce CP16 from sensory novelty, and they do not call an ordinary proximate source an independently sufficient original.

### The material-continuity qualification

This result does not depend on denying every persistent constituent. If some concrete component really continues, preserve its numerical identity and its role in the account. Whether it is intrinsically sufficient or received is then its own existential question; the conclusion cannot be obtained from a mental division of essence and existence.

Four levels must remain separate:

1. **Textual attribution:** Ibn Taymiyya positively affirms real substance transformation and the cessation of former qualified material realities. This is stronger than merely saying that ordinary speakers use a new name. His rejection of the retained prime-matter/atomistic accounts must not be erased to make the text easier to use.
2. **Ordinary productive identity:** the fruit is a newly actual product distinct from its continuing tree. Material contribution to f does not make s numerically f. This supplies the bounded case employed here.
3. **Exhaustive substrate ontology:** denying every continuing fundamental constituent, or identifying the author's rejected entities with modern particles, would require additional arguments. Distinctness of whole products does not alone establish either denial. This operation settles neither modern microphysics nor every reductionist ontology.
4. **The CP16 application:** its entry requires a genuinely received productive target, not proof that every constituent begins with it. The stronger historical substrate thesis is therefore not a premise of this particular application. Any received persistent component used in the claimed complete account remains within CP16's explanatory scope; an alleged unreceived component needs its own substantive assessment.

Thus the text is not silently modernized into a conservation-law thesis, and its explicit transformation doctrine is not downgraded to mere redescriptive change. The narrower argumentative use is an openly stated premise-dependency decision.

No assertion in the source that God creates is used as an independent premise proving God's existence. At this entry stage the relevant material is the ordinary productive case and its target identity. Identifying the complete original is the work of the inherited explanatory arguments, not a theological label smuggled into the observation.

## 5. Guards against two unjustified generalizations

**New occurrence does not mean created bearer by definition.** The entry case is a materially generated ordinary creature with a described productive derivation. It is not a universal inference from having a beginning, occurring successively, or being conceptually distinct. In particular, a particular divine act is not relabelled a created effect. The original/act/effect distinctions and beginningless intrinsic succession results [I08] remain unchanged.

**Whole productive explanation does not mean one temporally joint production.** Different effects can have different completed conditions while sharing an original source. Covering derivative suppliers' existential availability does not require all historical effects or conditions to coexist, a first temporal event, or elimination of every prior act. CP16, original-capacity [I10], and the later succession return already secure those distinctions.
