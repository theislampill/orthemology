## 1. Exact formal content of G

Graph qualifications:

- Interpret “connected” as connectedness of the undirected underlying relation, or explicitly allow either orientation at each path step. Overlap is symmetric, so strong directed connectivity is unnecessary. Directed reachability from one anchor also suffices, but is stronger than needed.
- Paths must be finite. Topological connectedness of a space with an unrelated R is not a replacement. Infinite graphs are permitted under finite-path connectivity.
- Loops, simplicity, acyclicity and transitivity of R are unnecessary. For a finite graph a spanning tree suffices; one need not warrant overlap on every conceivable physical edge.
- If R has several components the argument gives one unique common source per component. Distinct components may still have the same source, but that equality is not established by these premises.
- Nonempty E matters: with E empty, all sources are vacuously globally adequate, so unique global adequacy need not follow. A singleton E is handled by premise 1 without requiring an edge.
- “Fully covering” is the semantic choice of E, not an extra conclusion of connectivity. Globality means every member of this E, not every possible reality, idle putative bearer, disconnected domain, alternative world or productive respect.

Under local uniqueness, edge overlap is equivalent to equality of endpoint source assignments. This does not make the lemma viciously circular: independently evidenced sparse overlap can be useful. It does show why simply defining a relation of common-source agreement and declaring it connected supplies no independent unity warrant.

The compactness aside is standard and correct: closed adequacy subsets of compact S with the finite intersection property have nonempty total intersection. ## 2. The actual meanings required by the application

Use A(g,e,r,w) for **actual complete original productive adequacy of this numerical target in this productive respect and state**, including relevant means and receptive conditions at their original level. 1. An actual composite/joint productive target has individually borne complete original adequacy.

3. The inventory and attribution are faithful: no required foreign original contribution is excluded by a coarse endpoint description.

Ordinary causal interaction alone does not give item 1 or 2. A proximate agent's effect can use received materials without that proximate agent originally supplying those materials. Nor does a source's coverage of one component automatically entail entire coverage of another causally related object in every respect. These are precisely the individuation, mediation and scope premises the source arguments distinguish.
