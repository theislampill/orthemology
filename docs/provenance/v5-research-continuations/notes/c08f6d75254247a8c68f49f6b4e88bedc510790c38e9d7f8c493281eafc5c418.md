# Local smooth repair with an existential sublinear tolerance

Status: kernel-checked in lean/FiniteHullApproximateIff.lean, with literal intrinsic-interior wrappers in lean/IntrinsicBarycentric.lean. Independent finite-hull and hull-interface delta reviews passed; see FORMALIZATION_SCOPE.md for their exact seals. This is not an exact nonlinear common-repair theorem.

## The local equivalence

Let V be a finite truth family, C=conv(V), p∈relint(C) have all coordinates in (0,1), and T=span(C−p). Let a NONEMPTY FINITE family of coordinate-additive smooth proper-score generators have continuous strictly positive second derivatives. Fix a perturbation direction h.

The following are equivalent:

1. There exist positive e_n->0, coherent interior repairs c_n∈C, and one nonnegative tolerance eta_n such that eta_n/e_n->0 and every state loss under every generator worsens by at most eta_n
2. There is one z∈T such that h−z is Hessian-orthogonal to T under every generator's Hessian at p

The necessary direction even permits an arbitrary indexed score family; finiteness is needed here for the sufficient construction's uniform tolerance. No convergence of normalized repairs, O(e_n) bound, or common error estimate is smuggled into the antecedent.

## Constructive sufficient direction

Take a common tangent normal-equation solution z. Strictly positive barycentric weights ensure that p+e z remains in C for all sufficiently small positive e. Both that ray and the baseline p+e h stay in the open score domain after restricting to a tail of a positive sequence e_n->0.

For score k and truth v define

    Delta_(k,v)(e)=D_k(v,p+e z)−D_k(v,p+e h).

Actual differentiability gives

    Delta_(k,v)(e)/e -> (p−v)^T H_k(p)(z−h)=0.

Set eta(e)=sum_(k,v) |Delta_(k,v)(e)|. It is nonnegative and bounds every individual allowed worsening. Because there are only finitely many summands, eta(e)/e->0. This constructs the same coherent repair and one common sublinear error budget for the entire finite family.

The necessity is the general finite-hull all-state coercivity/absorption theorem. It derives linear localization from actual approximate loss comparisons, extracts a tangent subsequential limit, and obtains the simultaneous normal equations by positive barycentric zero forcing.

## The tolerance quantifier is essential

The result constructs SOME tolerance eta=o(e). It does not promise success for EVERY prescribed sublinear tolerance, nor for exact tolerance zero. A tolerance o(e²) can fail even when some tolerance O(e²) succeeds, and O(e²) is itself o(e).

The independent rank-one polynomial control makes this distinction explicit. Quadratic and complement-symmetric quartic generators have identical Hessian tangent projections at every point of the binary coherent line, yet common exact repair fails on a full perturbation interval. The control also excludes every allowance eta<=e²/100, hence every prescribed eta=o(e²) eventually. This is consistent with the present theorem, which supplies some larger sublinear allowance. That quartic generator produces nonconvex forecast losses, so it does not contradict Pettigrew's convex-score binary midpoint result.

At rank at least two, the global dependency-block theorem can still upgrade universal local compatibility across every p and transverse direction to exact common repair: the global condition forces blockwise affine score equivalence. The local approximate iff alone cannot make that upgrade on rank-one blocks.

## Formal scope

FiniteHullApproximateIff.finite_hull_local_approximate_iff uses an explicit strictly positive barycentric certificate. IntrinsicBarycentric.smooth_finite_hull_approximate_iff_intrinsicInterior accepts the literal geometric relative-interior hypothesis by deriving that certificate inside Lean. The latter preserves duplicate listed truth entries and does not conflate truth-index count with coordinate count.

The theorem is about additive statewise loss tolerance, not probability of error, a finite stopping certificate, normative authority or original intentionality. A change of score units requires transporting the tolerance accordingly.
