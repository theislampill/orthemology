## Semantic audit

`conditionalContinuationLaw` first restricts the original canonical seed/oracle input law to the actual event that the acquired history at its fixed length equals the full observed prefix, normalizes by that event's probability, and then maps the original run to its post-prefix histories. Its definition is not a new restart law. The separate observable-map theorem identifies this with restricting the original observed path law and deleting the old prefix. Thus the conditioning is on the full observed action/receipt history, not a stronger hidden-seed or unobserved-state event.

Histories are newest-first. The actual continuation at n is `take n` of the acquired history at length(h)+n. The deterministic drop theorem proves that appending h on the right reconstructs the original acquired history on the conditioning event. The prefix-event intersection identity therefore uses `tail ++ h`. The seed compatibility split is proved for this exact append direction and the original common policy; no model index becomes an input to the policy.

For the finite probability formula, the original full-prefix factorization supplies compatible seed mass times the product of recorded row probabilities. The longer event is a subset of the old event. Positive old-prefix mass forces both relevant factors nonzero, and the finite row likelihood is finite. The old-prefix row likelihood cancels, leaving exactly the common posterior seed mass of restarted tail-compatible seeds times the tail likelihood. Prefix likelihoods across models need not agree, and the old prefix may use pairs outside the later retained set. Null events are explicitly outside the probability restart theorem.

A single-coordinate marginal equality would not by itself identify arbitrary sequence laws. Here the proof also derives continuation consistency: every earlier acquired history is reconstructed from the last one. Consequently every finite path-prefix map is reconstructed from that last marginal. The inherited finite-prefix uniqueness theorem then gives equality on the entire infinite observed-path sigma-field. The canonical restarted side is finite because the derived common posterior seed is a probability measure. This is an actual cylinder proof, not an assumed fresh unused-oracle-tail marginal.

The rational Markov wrappers use the same normalized full rows, impose the source coordinate from the latest observed successor, restart at `currentState initial h`, and pass the original action policy its entire observed action/state history with the old prefix right-appended. The private seed posterior is identical across models for a common positive full prefix even when old-prefix probabilities differ. The matching assumptions are full unrenormalized row equality on the retained continuation pair set only.

The killed map erases the first outside-pair receipt at its acquisition time and all later current histories; earlier path coordinates preserve earlier retained receipts. The kill is applied after prefix deletion. Thus an old prefix outside the retained set does not cause immediate tail killing. Equality of complete killed conditional laws gives equality after restriction to never leaving the retained tail set, and then equality of the measurable exact recurrent-pair event intersected with that no-outside-tail event. This is equality of subprobability restrictions, not a claim that staying has probability one.
