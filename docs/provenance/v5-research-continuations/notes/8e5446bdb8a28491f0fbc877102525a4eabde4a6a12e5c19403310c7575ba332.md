# Hard objections and the smallest affirmative replies

This appendix deepens the candidate in `ARGUMENT.md`. It does not change the frozen canonical verdicts. The point is to test the positive account where a label could conceal an extra assumption.

## A. “You have supplied correct description, not normativity”

The objection has two versions. First, that a relation between an assertion and its object is merely descriptive. Second, that no moral obligation follows from it. The second is granted. The first must distinguish **the obtaining relation** from **its role in assessment**. A particular token purports to represent this source correctly. Its relation to the source makes it fulfill or fail that purport; it is not just another similarity an observer could notice. Changing the target to a different source changes which claim is being evaluated. The token's representational kind and determinate content make the relation an internal correctness condition.

That reply does not reduce a categorical ought to nonnormative facts. It fixes the target of a thinner but genuine assessment: accurate or inaccurate **as this assertion**. A skeptic can deny that assertions and beliefs really have those satisfaction conditions, but then the dispute concerns content and cognition, not whether a score improved. A deflationist can accept the ordinary truth distinction while denying a further substantial property called Normativity; that position is not refuted merely by the word “objective.”

The account therefore makes a modest positive claim: determinate representation plus a real object gives verdict-independent correctness in that respect. Whether this constitutes, exemplifies or depends on an irreducibly normative property is a further metanormative question. The repair account works at the shared local relation where rivals genuinely agree; it does not win the metaphysics by capitalizing the term.

## B. “The activity was selected, so its standard is arbitrary”

Choosing to make a map is optional; once a map purports to depict a particular coast, whether it gets that coast right is not fixed by the mapper's preference. Choosing the referent is not choosing the referent's properties. Likewise, the existence of a conventional unit does not make the represented physical magnitude whatever the speaker wishes. The convention partly constitutes what is said, after which the world bears on whether it is true.

There are limits. If the token has no settled referent, if the activity's kind is genuinely indeterminate, or if relevant conventions underdetermine the assertion, a unique verdict may not follow. If a practice merely awards points for a conventional move, its rules may constitute practice-relative correctness without providing a reason to play. The present positive account does not pretend that all practice rules have the same authority as truthful representation.

A good audit therefore asks: which element is chosen, what was actually fixed by that choice, and what remains answerable to something the chooser did not determine? “Conventional” and “arbitrary” are not interchangeable. Neither is “objective application” the same as “uncreated, convention-independent norm source.”

## C. “Semantic adequacy assumes all the hard work”

It carries hard work because the formal theorem cannot do that work. But it is not empty if the certificate gives an independently inspectable relation and exposes failure conditions. In the source-custody example, the certificate can fail because the image is forged, the page is misidentified, the glyph is misread, the relevant numeral convention is wrong, the assertion is about a different edition, or the intervention updates the wrong downstream claim. Those are materially different hypotheses with different evidence and repairs.

Contrast a certificate saying only “r_i is good under objective standard i.” That is empty relabeling. The usable certificate identifies the source, exact claim, inferential route and intervention, so an independent critic can falsify its link. It makes no claim that every truth has a conveniently available empirical certificate. Mathematics, direct acquaintance and transmitted testimony have different warrant structures; their evidence cannot be forced into one experimental template.

The common-repair result consequently has two achievements. The semantic inquiry supplies fitting local operations; the algebra establishes their joint compatibility without hiding a sacrifice in an aggregate reward. Both achievements are necessary for the asserted conclusion. Neither can certify the other by itself.

## D. “Commuting repairs are too special”

They are deliberately sufficient rather than universally characterizing. Many real repairs must be ordered. Updating a transcription before a claim that copies it works even though the reverse order does not. A sequencing proof can replace commutation if each later step preserves achieved correctness and remaining feasibility.

A stronger, useful general pattern is a well-founded defect order together with (i) a semantically valid decrease for every chosen nonterminal step, (ii) preservation of the actual constraints and (iii) no terminal state outside the real joint target. Those premises can prove termination in a joint target. But the order must measure diagnosed defects without deleting or reweighting them for convenience; otherwise the proof merely certifies the scoring policy. This is adjacent to retained restoration/ranking work and is not claimed as a new theorem here.

The commuting criterion is useful precisely where separate warranted corrections can be composed transparently. Its failure diagnoses interference; it does not prove no repair exists. Its success proves neither minimal cost nor universal optimality, and it is not a license to skip review of a consequential intervention.

## E. “A wise source solves norm-fixing automatically”

Only if “wise” already includes the relevant substantive norms. There are three independent questions:

1. What fixes whether p is true?
2. What fixes which end or act is fitting?
3. Why does this source select the fitting act because it is fitting?

An omniscient source answers every true question correctly. That does not by itself answer the second or third questions. A creator can institute an artifact's purpose, which explains what counts as its design success, while a further assessment asks whether that purpose is good. Intrinsic source Wisdom can unify the answers, but only when its content is independently defended.

The positive source proposal should say that creative intention establishes a creaturely order fitted to genuinely good ends, and that the source's practical perfection explains its doing so for the appropriate reasons. It should not say that any end would be good solely because an otherwise normatively unspecified source chose it. Nor should it infer that the source must receive its perfection from an external norm-giver because truth and goodness are distinguishable in our account. Intrinsic nature and standing attributes need not be separately manufactured components.

## F. “An impersonal intrinsic norm is just a table with flattering labels”

Sometimes it is, and the architecture control exposes that. A finite model containing `target=1` and `norm(1)=good` supplies no account of real content, fitting function or normative authority. It is at most a non-entailment witness for a weak signature.

A serious impersonal rival must do more: identify real powers, histories or organizational relations which explain why a derivative token has determinate content, why that content succeeds or fails, and why the relevant faculty has its function even when it malfunctions. Naturalized function accounts are serious partial attempts. Their success for local biological function would not establish an ultimate necessary impersonal source; their incompleteness would not by itself prove a personal one.

The symmetrical test is crucial. A personal model which replaces the table with `wise(g)` but cannot connect source apprehension, fitting reasons and created actuality is no more explanatory. Conversely, a developed personal account may earn integration credit by supplying those connections where the rival has only independent postulates. That is an actual explanatory comparison, not a victory from counting predicates.

A modal conclusion must be stronger still. Showing that the weak premises leave an impersonal interpretation open is not certifying metaphysical possibility. Showing that an intuitive rival description is incomplete is not demonstrating its impossibility. The canonical R5 architecture remains undefeated until its exact world-warrant and extra-consequence requirements are met.

## G. “Reason-responsive behavior implies source-level reasons”

A derivative subject may really recognize a reason and act on it. The source must adequately explain that reality if it is a complete source. But it does not follow simply from causal ancestry that every cause possesses the effect's mode of cognition. The sixth Knowledge route adds original-giver cognitive priority, with eligibility and complete sourcehood. The norm-fixing account neither replaces nor strengthens that argument by a notational shortcut.

Likewise, an operation may be sensitive to a fittingness predicate without understanding why the end is fitting. Structural counterfactual sensitivity and intentional uptake are distinct. If “because fitting” is defined only as output covariance with the predicate, it cannot subsequently be used as a proof of Wisdom in the thick rational sense.

The strongest intellectual argument therefore concerns **adequacy to genuine cognitive and practical achievement**, not the presence of a selector. Its remaining burden is respect-specific: why must a complete original giver possess the cognitive/practical perfection intrinsically rather than a noncognitive nature capable of producing that achievement in another? That bridge needs a defense against the best unlike-cause alternative, not an appeal to score improvement.

## H. Narrow authoritative truthfulness without smuggled benevolence

An assertion that p is truth-correct iff p. When supplied as authoritative evidence, it purports to give the recipient a truth-linked reason to accept p. A knowingly false certification defeats that epistemic function even if it succeeds as a means of manipulation. This identifies a constitutive epistemic defect without assuming that the source must maximize every creature's welfare or disclose every truth.

It does **not** prove a knowledgeable source incapable of the defective act. Deliberate deception is conceptually different from ignorance. Excluding it in a divine-source argument requires independently defended practical perfection, truthfulness or a source-specific no-defect principle. Treating the source as already perfectly trustworthy while deriving trustworthiness would be circular.

“Recipients are entitled to rely” also has two meanings. A recipient may have reasonable, undefeated but fallible grounds and still be deceived. Alternatively, a strong entitlement may be defined to include the source's actual truthfulness in that scope. The second cannot be used as independent evidence for the very truthfulness it presupposes. Authentication should therefore first state which entitlement is claimed and its grounds.

Purposeful withholding is not the same as falsely asserting. Tests, indirect speech and nonliteral contexts can also differ, but the intended content and scope require independent evidence. An interpretive escape which redescribes every apparent false certification as a test would empty truthfulness of discriminating content. This is a particularly important common-repair guard in source interpretation: preserve the actual text and add the warranted context; do not overwrite the inconvenient assertion's meaning solely to protect a preferred theory.

The useful positive conclusion is narrow. If source practical perfection is independently established, and an act is genuinely an authoritative certification of p whose relevant recipient is meant to rely on its truth, then certifying p while knowing not-p is a defect in the very epistemic relation undertaken. A no-defect premise excludes it in that scope. How the source's authority is established, why it undertakes that relation and which actual communication has that content remain separate questions.

## I. What genuine plural warrant allows us to repair now

Disagreement about final metaphysics need not block all progress. Parties can jointly remove a misquotation, an invalid inference, a mislabeled unit, or an unsupported transfer of authority. They may continue to disagree about the ultimate ground of truth or whether an author proved the stronger claim. Preserving that remaining disagreement is sometimes a more accurate repair than forcing a common verdict.

But epistemic compromise must not be confused with truth by vote. If the target is determinate and parties disagree about p, an intermediate numerical confidence or verbal compromise does not make both contrary propositions true. Common repair concerns the defect actually established: perhaps an evidence record, an inferential step, a source attribution or an action policy. The scope of success must be named.

This provides an affirmative discipline for Orthemology: restore a relation that can actually be warranted, keep unresolved relations visible, and let a later source argument earn any stronger integration. It gives substantial repair without pretending that semantic precision itself establishes necessary existence, one common bearer or a final moral authority.
