9 October 2026 UTC. Independent focused appraisal of the historical cooperation reassessment against the actual reactive, valued-cooperation, wise-determination, and accepted de se successors. Source and model inputs were read only. This report makes no protected change, new model, Lean claim, integration, closure, or research-duration claim.

## Judgment first

The current rival has **not established that two necessarily harmonious beings preserve the full original-perfection and independently adequate sovereignty profile at issue**. But it has done appreciably more than attach matching-outcome labels to two nodes. It has a coherent reduced interpretation of distinct owned powers and acts, no foreign efficient donation, primitive factive cognition, executable peer-independent policies, and a defensible conditional reason for the diagonal means. Those gains answer particular weak objections. They do not answer the source's central question: whether an undertaking whose actual original productive basis is irreducibly plural can be a wholly adequate, unborrowed exercise of each perfected original.

The explanatory problem is therefore **partly advanced and centrally unresolved**, rather than either wholly untouched or solved. Necessary concord at the full metaphysical level, the eligibility of the original-peer end, sufficient particular wisdom, and the perfection of nonexclusive original manifestation remain proposed interpretations without comparable independent warrant. Their absence from the checked signature is not a demonstration that they are false.

My positive appraisal is also stronger than “no refutation found.” Within the adopted original-actuality and pure-perfection background, the source's comprehensive maker-preserver account, comparison of assisted and unborrowed actual efficacy, and comparative disqualification of essentially restricted candidates supply substantial affirmative reasons. I judge these sufficient for **defeasible acceptance of one complete common original source of the relevant actually connected created field**. The current rival has not earned equal explanatory warrant. This is an appraisal of those reasons, not an inference from the absence of a countermodel and not a universal impossibility theorem for all plural originals.

The root's final philosophical judgment remains separate. In particular, neither this appraisal nor the de se work may substitute the disputed efficacy classification into its own antecedents and then announce that every rival has failed an independently settled premise.

## 3. Necessary harmony and sufficient particular willing

There are three different successes here.

First, the ordinary policy is constructed without inspecting the peer's actual execution or postselecting successful outcomes. Its two disclosed policies really do cohere under the law. The conditional explanation does not require one uniquely value-maximizing outcome. Nor should one require ability to act unwisely merely to count as powerful. The source's own antecedent-capacity/complete-determination and wisdom controls exclude both shortcuts.

But **common q is not yet an explanation of why these originals have this particular wise determination**. It is the supplied task for the conditional construction. Treating a program parameter as an external efficient controller would be a mistake; treating it as an independently warranted sufficient intrinsic reason would be another. Shared objective reasons can be neither a third creator nor an accidental match, while their particular realization and normative adequacy remain unexplained at this interface.

Similarly, the supplied `role_delta` selects a unique plan only conditionally. For fixed q it encodes the chosen plan. It adds information, not independent wisdom. `Determination(plan)` specifies execution but does not explain the occurrence or appropriateness of the determination. The actual code explicitly denies the stronger claims.

The ordinary domain implements universal agreement **over its disclosed contexts**. A fully necessary concordance thesis further requires that its domain and intrinsic reason structure appropriately represent all relevant admissible determinations. No such metaphysical completeness has been warranted. That is a substantive obligation, not a rule that every model axiom must have a prior formal proof.

There is no discovered contradiction with the source's independently retained particular-determination requirement, because the candidate does not affirm that these complete wills lack adequate grounds. It leaves their adequacy unestablished. If it claimed that the shared parameter, outcome success, or a generic “will selects” primitive *exhausted* the required explanatory basis, the Majmu8/Bayan5 criticism would apply directly. The epistemic standard must be symmetric. Akmaliyya L230–234 says ignorance of particular wisdom need not defeat independently established general wisdom. The common-source account also lacks a complete catalogue of hidden wise reasons. Missing details are not evidence of reasonlessness on either side. The narrower shortfall is that general perfection of this **original-peer mode** has not been independently established, so it cannot be invoked to make the very disputed mode innocent by assumption.

## 4. Complete particular efficacy and sovereignty over the same order

Here the candidate is explicit enough for a firm distinction.

Necessary concord prevents arbitrary opposed diagnostic settings from being silently promoted into metaphysically admissible perfectly wise worlds, but it does not turn contextual means into uniform control or turn joint provenance into whole original provision.

Likewise, F(q,q)=F(q,idle)=q proves a represented endpoint and own-activity preservation. It does not give the coactive history a solo origin. On this defended interpretation, the rival fails. On the rival's alternative interpretation, its already adequate intrinsic acts have an optional nonexclusive external manifestation, with neither receiving a deficiency-remedying completion. **That classification dispute is the substantive issue.** Stating the source interpretation accurately does not yet make it an undisputed upstream premise of a test of that interpretation.

The nonabsorption dispute is similar. The law allows unchanged intrinsic q-exercises to yield the same source-neutral created target singly and jointly while assigning both actual efficacy. This contradicts the source's stronger productive-augmentation claim. It does not violate a theorem of mere input sensitivity, nonduplication of exclusive production, or intrinsic-token identity. The code also supplies no independent causal/material connectivity relation among the created events and no mapping of its catalogue to the complete actually connected created order. ## 5. Full perfection, bearer eligibility, and the historical B5 reservation

The historical `ROOT_REASSESSMENT.md` made a second reservation: possible unification of distributed powers does not by itself establish that each already identified partner can acquire the unified profile. That quantifier distinction remains correct. But it is no longer an adequate standalone reason to withhold the source's eligibility case.

The subsequent Akmaliyya and Dar reading materially answers the objection by a different argument. If an excellence is coherent and genuinely pure, essential inability to have it does not exempt a candidate from comparison. A candidate can fail the standard without being capable of changing into a successful candidate. The source's comparison of possessing an excellence, lacking it while receptive, and being wholly unreceptive does not require a merger, a temporal upgrade, or numerical survival through removal of an essential restriction.

Thus distinguish:

- **De re acquireability:** this stipulated individual can come to possess the combined capacity while remaining this individual.
- **Eligibility of the whole candidate:** a being with this stipulated limitation can qualify as an absolutely perfected original.

The new comparative argument addresses the second without proving the first. Requiring the first before allowing any judgment about the second would preserve arbitrarily limited candidates by their own essential labels. That is not a fair appraisal of the source.

The condition remains substantive: the proposed excellence must really be pure and eligible at the relevant scope. Underived existence alone is not a thin logical theorem of every positive attribute; the source supplies an a-fortiori metaphysical comparison. Its donor argument also must not distribute every bestowed perfection to every proposed original without an additional premise. These qualifications identify the warrants to assess, rather than cancelling them.

For the **actual current rival**, this correction should not be misapplied. Both actors already possess solo constructors for every represented Q. It is not an essentially partial specialist trying to escape concentration. The live question is actual adequate achievement, not acquiring a missing standing constructor. The source's comparative argument strengthens the actual-efficacy norm; it does not prove by itself that every optional joint manifestation is a defective act.

The richer end likewise merits exact treatment. Goal-directed cooperation can be a genuine relative good. That does not establish a good requiring the peer's **independent originality**, or show that such a good vindicates foreign original completion. An ordinary distinct agent can participate without being another original. Conversely, substituting a created agent does not preserve the same de re peer, the same richer end, or the same complete created inventory. No purported identity-preserving replacement is used here.

## 6. What de se adds, and what it cannot replace

The accepted centered interpretation preserves true self-location: A can know that its act is its own and B's is B's; B can know the converse. Knowledge of a correctly shifted first-person truth does not make its knower numerically identical with that subject. Each can truthfully know that this coactive effect has two original roots and that it does not individually supply the entire actual basis.

The code's own-policy uses q, not a centered knowledge state; the richer End is attached to the selected exercise. Thus action on the basis of recognizing this end as *mine* is a further practical interpretation, not mechanically certified by singleton epistemic accessibility. That correction preserves primitive reason-responsive cognition rather than retracting it.

The strongest additional detector is recognition plus conformance: if this undertaking really is an independently established constitutive defect of original agency, complete truthful self-knowledge makes the actor recognize it as its own, and perfected practical conformance excludes knowingly enacting it. This is a valid contribution of de se. It cannot establish the defect classification from factivity alone.

Accordingly, de se makes a warranted deficiency transparent; it neither supplies nor supplants Ibn Taymiyyah's productive-perfection argument. Requiring each subject to know “I alone am sovereign” without first warranting that exclusive content would import the conclusion. Conversely, once the relevant norm has adequate warrant, ignorance or an unexamined self-description cannot rescue a supposedly omniscient perfect agent.

No demonstrated violation of the independently retained no-vicious-efficient-circle principle, own-act distinction, narrow efficient nonreceipt, factivity, or exclusion of two **exclusive entire** actual producers has been found. The current rival is purpose-built to preserve these at its declared interpretation, and its actual joint attribution does not claim two exclusive origins.

Missing authentication is not falsification. The required connection to the complete actual created field is also not supplied by the catalogue alone.

The candidate directly disagrees with the source's strong actual-independent-efficacy, exclusive-completion, and nonabsorption claims. These are serious argued intermediate commitments, not merely the final conclusion restated. But when their derivation or application is under examination, their denial is the disputed bridge, not an independent antecedent failure. The source may be provisionally accepted on positive warrant without pretending this classification has become philosophically uncontested.

A still broader universal-sovereignty route is distinct. If every eligible original has ultimate mastery over every distinct bearer and is immune to another's mastery in the same respect, plurality contradicts those premises. The source offers positive comparative reasons for this conception. The ordinary full-Q power signature neither represents nor warrants it. Restricting mastery to created things changes the argument; extending it to a putative uncreated peer requires the stronger domain warrant. Neither inability to create a peer *while leaving it uncreated* nor failure to exercise every possible good is a genuine deficiency.

## 8. Why affirmative evidential credit is warranted

I assign affirmative weight to the source for three content-sensitive reasons.

First, the explanandum is completed original agency, including actual recipients, powers, and preservation. Unborrowed internal readiness plus an externally completed achievement does not automatically meet that explanatory target. This is an intelligible reason to favor a source whose accomplished efficacy is adequate through its original resources.

Second, the comparison is about a pure excellence of effective agency, not pride in sole credit or maximal production. The source itself preserves wisdom, fitting nonexercise, successive acts, and the difference between knowing an external object and receiving one's knowledge from it. These controls make its narrower productive claim more credible, while preventing an indiscriminate ban on every relation or condition.

Third, with comprehensive eligible standing power already accepted, the plural hypothesis repairs no independently evidenced shortage in a single source's capacities. Real created differentiation and secondary agency can be retained with one original basis. The rival adds irreducibly plural original manifestation without a comparably supported reason why that addition is required or how it preserves the disputed excellence. Its conditional means rationale makes harmony intelligible; it does not supply parity on original adequacy.

There remain unsupplied details on both sides. Neither has a mechanistic reduction of primitive original agency, a verified complete microscopic creation inventory, or every particular wise reason. These common limitations are not evidence uniquely against the rival. The differential issue is the positive warrant of its extra co-manifestation and perfection claim versus the source's account of adequate unborrowed achievement.

A critic need not construct a full rival cosmos to challenge that account. A substantive argument that actual nonexclusive manifestation is equally adequate, that the comparison loses a genuine eligible good, or that the purity classification is mistaken could weaken the appraisal. The current distinctions show that certain shortcuts fail; they have not supplied such a defeating argument at the cumulative actual-field level.

For that reason, I affirm the saved sufficient-defeasible-warrant judgment rather than reducing it to “one proposal is simpler” or “no refutation has appeared.” I do not thereby certify universal SourceMode, all-distinct-bearer supremacy, or impossibility of every idle or disconnected necessary reality. Nor does this report certify the strongest rival as a full metaphysical possibility. The right present verdict is **positive but defeasible common-source warrant, coupled with a precise unresolved universal application dispute**.
