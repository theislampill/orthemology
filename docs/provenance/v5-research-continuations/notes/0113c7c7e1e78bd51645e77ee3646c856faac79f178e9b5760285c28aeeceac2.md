# Why the same score family must survive cross-context comparison

Status: ordinary algebraic countermodel with exact symbolic identities checked by checks/verify_context_dependent_generators.py. This is a quantifier-deletion control, not part of the frozen Lean chain or a normative-authority argument.

S2 and S7 fix two generator families F and G BEFORE requiring common local repair at every interior coherent point p. Replacing that condition by “for every p, some possibly context-dependent G_p shares a repair with F” invalidates the generator-equivalence conclusion.

## Explicit three-outcome construction

Fix the quadratic generator F(x)=sum_i x_i²/2. At each interior p in the three-outcome probability simplex define

    G_p(x)=sum_i [(x_i−p_i)²/2+(x_i−p_i)^4].

Every coordinate generator is finite, globally smooth and strictly convex: its second derivative is 1+12(x_i−p_i)². Its Hessian at p is the identity, just as F's. It is not positive-scale/affine equivalent to F, because its fourth derivative is 24 while F's is zero.

For b=p+e e_1 use the common candidate

    q=p+e(2/3,−1/3,−1/3).

It is coherent. It and b are interior whenever

    0<e<min(1−p_1,3p_2,3p_3).

At every truth vertex v=e_j, the F loss improvement is e²/6. Direct expansion gives the G_p improvement

    D_Gp(v,b)−D_Gp(v,q)
      = e²/6 + 7e⁴/3 − (8/3)e³(v_1−p_1).

Because v_1−p_1<=1, this is at least e²(1/6−8e/3), which is strictly positive for 0<e<1/16. Thus every p has arbitrarily small exact common strict repairs for F and its local G_p, yet F and G_p are not equivalent generators.

## Exact scope consequence

The theorem needs persistence of which two standards are under comparison across the p-contexts. An implementation that silently rewrites its score when the baseline changes has changed the hypothesis, even if every local episode has a perfectly valid common correction. The same issue appears in a proposed source ascent: proving episodewise compatible contents does not establish one cross-context norm or one persisting representational particular. The mathematical countermodel demonstrates the quantifier distinction; it does not decide whether actual content identity is conventional, functional or constitutive.
