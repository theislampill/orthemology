# Productive sufficiency: the actual scope of necessitation

8 October 2026. Bounded T20 continuation. Earlier material is unchanged. No protected integration, research-duration certification, owner acceptance, or closure is claimed.

## Result

**The inherited sources do not equate a necessary standing bearer with a necessitating antecedent for each particular act. They do demand that a genuinely complete particular productive determination have its effect.** Those are different claims. Their difference is already recognized in the controlling records; no repair of that established distinction is warranted.

**The source also rejects a reasonless-specification exception for divine choice.** Unreceived particular willing is permitted; an unchanged generic will that selects this time or alternative without a particular determiner is criticized. “Irreducible voluntary specification” is consequently acceptable here only in the sense of intrinsic, non-borrowed agency not reduced to an externally imposed mechanism. It must not mean absence of adequate reasons or determination. Neither the inspected passages nor the inherited contracts derive a uniquely maximizing numerical objective for every particular choice.

The new imported-interface test preserves all seven original-bearer premises and the exact positive fixture alongside the existing normalized route probability model. A necessary original bearer and the fixed law coexist with two different represented endpoints; fixing a realized assignment fixes its endpoint. This is a **logical interface result**, not a source-faithful stochastic metaphysics or a demonstration of real causal production. The actual full source's perfection and complete-determiner premises are not represented by that conjunction and are not claimed to have been preserved. Endpoint extensionality and the unconstrained separate Act sort are diagnostic controls, not discoveries resolving voluntary specification.

Beyond the general particular-determination demand, the strongest differentiation from a proposed nonpersonal original is the separately argued account of **pure perfection, informed agency, wise discrimination, and unborrowed actual efficacy**. It cannot fairly be replaced with either “physical things are created by definition” or “all causes must have a deterministic physical antecedent.” Under that substantive account, a merely nonpersonal necessary source lacks relevant excellences; calling it necessary does not answer the objection. Conversely, defining the candidate type to include those excellences excludes a nonpersonal candidate only conditionally upon the disputed eligibility argument.

## 2. Five different explanatory claims

Keep the explanandum, ground, and domain fixed when using “sufficient.”

1. **Actual causal production:** this agent/process genuinely brought about this occurrence. A causal relation or its warranted interpretation is needed; a law-shaped equation alone is not that warrant.
2. **Complete productive determination:** all relevant particular productive conditions are present, including the actual exercise. In the source's adopted account, the relevant effect follows. This does not say the standing essence alone already fixes those conditions.
3. **Sufficient explanation:** an account adequately answers a specified explanatory question. Whole existential actuality, a distribution of outcomes, the realized occurrence, why this rather than another, and why a choice is wise are different questions. Calling one explanation complete does not silently answer all of them.
4. **A lawful probability distribution:** a normalized assignment law and endpoint pushforward specify chances and correlations. They need not pick a realized sample or authenticate the productive reading of their indices.
5. **A uniquely selecting reason:** a reason context independently singles out a particular alternative. A unique numerical optimum is one possible formal device, not the inspected source's universal criterion of wisdom. Conversely, an input that merely encodes the selected alternative is not an independently supplied reason.

A realized productive occurrence can be causally explanatory without its earlier coarse description necessitating it. That is an intelligible rival proposal, not automatically an unwarranted description. Whether it is *exhaustively adequate as an original explanation* is a stronger philosophical question. Likewise, a declared voluntary act does not become sufficiently explained merely because its name is “will.”

## 3. Exact source position on necessitation and willing

### Complete actual determination, not bare standing essence

Majmu8 item805 L294–298 distinguishes enabling antecedent capacity from the capacity contemporaneous with and entailing an act. L305–309 distinguishes incomplete created causal conditions from complete efficacy, while affirming genuine created causal powers. The text therefore does not adopt “not alone necessitating, therefore not causally real.”

L311–312 is decisive for the proposed parity challenge. It criticizes granting an unchanged, equally related will unexplained specification while requiring a complete determiner elsewhere. The stated complete-determiner demand is general. It cannot be honestly presented as a permission for divine reasonlessness that is denied only to natural causes.

Bayan5 item961 L001 rejects the inference from completeness in oneself and ability if one wills to eternal occurrence of the particular act. Its L002–004 reports the familiar equal-path/equal-loaf and will-as-its-own-selector replies; these are not the author's endorsed resolution. Item962 L004–007 distinguishes created decisive wills from God's unreceived willing and independent optional action. The unquarantined concluding sentences of L009 relate occurrence and nonoccurrence to divine willing and nonwilling. The source does not make a separate necessary productive antecedent coerce God into willing.

### Does the demand reach the particular willing itself?

**Yes, beyond a bare will-to-effect condition; no, not as a demonstrated unique-optimizer law.** The continuous argument is important:

1. L311 identifies the challenged proposal: an unchanged eternal will equally related to alternatives and times nevertheless specifies this occurrence without a new differentiating cause.
2. L312 is direct authorial appraisal, not merely quotation. It calls the complete-determiner argument sound and criticizes applying it to human action while exempting divine action. Its rejection of that exception is the authorial bridge to the particular-specification question.
3. L313–318 preserves created causal means and rejects inferring their idleness from a fixed decree. This prevents reading “the complete cause entails” as “intermediate conditions need do nothing.”
4. L321–340 turns to effective willing, love and wisdom; L339 explicitly makes the fittingness of existence and absence time-dependent. A new attained end is not automatically foreign completion.
5. L341–343 reports the dispute about eternal will. At L342 the opponents distinguish the genus of selection from this particular preference and require a cause of this alternative's being willed. This is a reported dialectical formulation. Its direction accords with the author's explicit L312 judgment, but its every sentence is not silently promoted to an independent authorial theorem.
6. L344–356 gives the further response space. The merely dialectical reply concerning newly attained wisdom is challenged at L351. L352–355 distinguishes impossible regress of efficient sources from disputed/allowed succession of conditions and effects, including successive intrinsic acts related to will and power. L355 expressly permits concomitant/correlative mutual conditions; reciprocal dependence must not automatically become vicious efficient-source dependence. L356 summarizes competing explanations, rather than announcing one complete reason-to-will calculus. L357–359 then presents a conditional reply on both beginningful and beginningless assumptions; it is not used here as an unconditional proof of either history doctrine. The final electronic reference to “the second” at L359 is locally awkward. No emendation is made or inferred; the scope distinction rests on the explicit L352–355 formulations.

The strongest warranted reconstruction requires a particular differentiating basis for the willing; merely supplying its occurrence as a new unexplained token does not satisfy the critique. Where “complete determiner” is used in the source's full causal sense, this is stronger than probabilistic propensity alone. But the passage does not separately enumerate an antecedent normative reason-set, prove that this set uniquely maximizes one alternative, or place an external necessitator before God's willing. Explanatory sufficiency, normative justification, causal conditions and temporal priority cannot be substituted for each other.

Nor does the passage establish an ultimate libertarian exemption in which every complete determining condition remains identical while an otherwise ungrounded preference changes. The limited interface witness does not preserve or test such a full determining basis. Successive acts/conditions can matter while standing nature remains the same; no unique eternal history follows from that local claim here.

A requirement of determination for an emergent intrinsic act also does not make that act a separately created bearer. Its relevant internal conditions, original source, and whole-existence-receipt status remain different questions. Neither `Emergent → Created` nor `new token → externally received perfection` is licensed.

The fair conjunction is: standing perfection; actual particular willing; effect-relative complete efficacy; particular intelligible determination; no foreign origin of the divine willing. It is not: one eternally fixed act-token, an external necessitator of God, or a unique maximizing score. Nor does it establish the absence of sufficient reasons when an observer has not supplied them.

The exact electronic strings in item962 L002 and the opening of L009, already quarantined by the inherited report, are not used here. Retained electronic Arabic was re-inspected; there is no new manuscript witness, print collation, scan inspection or full-work reading.

## 4. The actual differentiating premise

Akmaliyya's argument is stronger than a label and more disputable than a logical definition:

- Canonical L035–038 argues that genuinely pure possible perfection cannot be barred from the superior self-sufficient necessary reality while available to a dependent reality.
- L039–046 adds the giver's priority concerning bestowed perfection and rejects foreign completion of the original's constitutive perfection. The donor argument must preserve its donor-specific quantifier; it cannot grant every perfection to every proposed source merely because some source gives it.
- L081–090 identifies life, knowledge and power as pure excellences and explicitly answers the unreceptive-subject objection. Essential incapacity for such excellence is judged worse, rather than a neutral exemption from the comparison.
- L232–240 combines effective unimpeded willing with informed wise discrimination. Mere efficacy without wisdom is not the full proposed perfection; mere wise preference without effective power is not either.

These premises give a substantive reason for admitting intrinsic wise agency while refusing to treat an impersonal law as an equally qualified original merely because it is postulated necessary. They do **not** require reducing every proposal to the same thinner causal graph before philosophical comparison is allowed. The source's comparison can be basic and defeasibly warranted without a proof from causal nonreceipt alone.

The objection must nevertheless be heard at its actual point: does superior existential independence entail eligibility for all these pure excellences, and are the attributed mental/practical properties genuinely pure at original scope? A proposed nonpersonal source can challenge that bridge. It does not succeed merely by assigning itself a different essential type. Equally, a defender has not answered it merely by defining `EligibleOriginal` as wise and personal, or by defining `Physical` as created or incapable. No such exclusion theorem occurs in the tested interfaces.

Two different rival proposals must therefore be assessed separately. A proposed complete stochastic original whose *entire* relevant explanatory basis is a propensity, with no sufficient particular specification, contests the source's general complete-determiner principle. An unexplained divine will-token would face the corresponding objection; it has no special exemption. A physical proposal with an adequate particular productive explanation but without mental agency instead primarily contests the pure-perfection/agency bridge. The present files do not settle either contest by definition.

An indeterministic causal theorist can hold that a stochastic power genuinely produces a particular occurrence without necessitating it and can challenge the source's stronger explanatory demand on substantive grounds. The theory should not be dismissed as no causation simply because it declines that demand. Its objection is to the adequacy criterion itself, rather than a countermodel already preserving that criterion. Conversely, a merely stochastic description of created means can remain compatible with the source's full account when it does not claim to exhaust all particular determining conditions.

“Physical” also needs a substantive interpretation. A nonpersonal fundamental process, a spatial material object, and any reality described by physical theory are not interchangeable classes. This pass specifies no independently warranted physical candidate and therefore certifies neither its necessary existence nor its impossibility. The source's statements about encountered created natural causes cannot by themselves classify every proposed nonpersonal necessary reality without the further perfection/agency argument.

My bounded appraisal retains positive weight for that argument, especially the comparison of self-sufficient effective informed agency with an underived basis that lacks such capacities. The narrow logical control does not defeat that philosophical warrant or establish parity for a physical rival. It equally does not strengthen the warrant by treating normalization or underdetermination as evidence against physical causation. The previously recorded defeasible common-source judgement is not downgraded or converted into a universal theorem.

Primary locator: [Akmaliyya, Majmu6:76–90 and 128–130](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/22/591/). Exact selected electronic scopes and hashes are bound separately.

## 6. Preserved and unestablished premises

Preserved exactly:

- Actual received-target occurrence, positive original completion, bearer realization, same-respect support transport, binary receipt representation, contingency-as-need, constitutive reception.
- Their specific inherited two-world witness, same original witness, and nonvacuous contingent received bearer.
- Declared route kind, enabling, nonempty bundle, calibrated success weights, assignment normalization, endpoint definition and endpoint-law normalization.

Not established by this test:

- Completeness of the represented possible-world domain or physical/modal possibility of either realization.
- An independently warranted causal interpretation of the occurrence indices, or actual production in our world.
- A complete inventory of created reality, comprehensive original maker-preserver adequacy, unrestricted original power, pure-perfection eligibility, wisdom, or unreceived omniscience.
- The source's complete particular determiner of the selected exercise, a positive reason for its selection, or an exception to that requirement.
- A claim that a law alone is a complete cause or complete metaphysical explanation.
- A claim that spontaneous physical origination has been excluded because its standing law fails to necessitate the outcome.

Thus a rival claiming to preserve the *full source doctrine* is obstructed here: the probability signature lacks the predicates and justification for those stronger obligations. Calling the sampled occurrence “the complete productive ground” would simply assume the disputed application. A critic may still challenge an inference without furnishing a whole rival cosmos; the scope challenge is valid at the tested weaker target.
