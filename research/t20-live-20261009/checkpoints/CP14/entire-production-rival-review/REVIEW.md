# Independent review: entire production and redundant actual routes

9 October 2026 UTC. Bounded adversarial review of the supplied Oneness printed 23–29, the current strongest-rival audit, the existing effect-production distinctions, and Schaffer (2003), especially §§1–2 and 4–5. No new model, code, Lean result, source-acquisition campaign, protected change, integration, or closure.

## Verdict

**Schaffer supplies substantive, defeasible counterevidence to the unrestricted impossibility of two actually active, individually sufficient productive processes terminating in one effect. His strongest case is not exhausted by multiple partial material contributions.** The spells and conditional noncompositional cases matter independently of the physical component argument. The evidence is stronger than an occurrence table or a pair of merely possible backup routes.

**He does not establish two perfected originals, each possessing and actually supplying a complete unreceived resource basis. Nor does he establish two sole exhaustive actual provenances.** The latter is contradictory by its explicit exclusivity; the former requires a substantive original-adequacy account beyond his subject matter. Neither limitation cancels his positive evidence concerning redundant actual production.

The author's emerging distinction is therefore sound, provided “not established entire original production” does not silently downgrade the positive remainder to generic causal membership. In an effect-relative, nonexclusive sense, there is positive modal evidence for each process producing the whole designated result. In an original-resource/perfection sense, the proposed extension remains unwarranted by these texts alone.

The earlier affirmative common-source judgment neither survives nor falls merely because this shortcut fails. Its additional pure-perfection and original-adequacy premises need their own appraisal. This bounded review does **not** independently certify that cumulative judgment, and does not infer its reversal either.

## 1. Three targets that must stay separate

**(a) Active redundant sufficiency:** An A-only productive route and a B-only productive route are both actually operative; either suffices for the same designated effect without the other route. “A-only” concerns the original/source coordinate at the chosen interface. Ordinary examples can still presuppose a recipient and background conditions. Two complete paths are not automatically two complete original-resource accounts.

**(b) Sole exhaustive provenance:** A is the only actual original contributor to every relevant productive requisite, and B is also the only such contributor, in the same respect. With A distinct from B and each genuinely contributing, these propositions are incompatible. This is not a proof against (a).

**(c) Perfected original sourcehood:** Each agent's necessary power, complete willing, actual efficacy, and sovereignty are wholly adequate and unreceived. Whether (c) requires (b), rather than allowing (a) with wholly owned adequate resources, is a substantive metaphysical issue. It is not answered by a provenance classifier.

The inherited effect-production report §§4–5 already makes the relevant separation. Its redundant model has two active source-only routes; its joint model has one AB route; its priority model has only one actual route. Equal output tables do not distinguish them. It also distinguishes positive support from required noninterference. These are useful representation controls, not independent authentication of any actual causal ontology.

The strongest-rival audit concerns a richer **joint** reactive candidate. Its conclusions about irreducibly plural actual completion cannot simply be transferred to a rival whose routes are separately complete and redundant. Conversely, the simple redundant comparator does not inherit the richer candidate's epistemic, intentional, and perfection profile.

## 2. What Schaffer independently contributes

### Actual causation, rather than alternative capacity

In §1 (pp.23–25), Schaffer distinguishes symmetric redundancy from preemption, then argues that individual overdeterminers actually cause the effect. His §4 (pp.33–37) supplies a positive process rationale: the connection runs to its result without missing intermediaries; the initiating event is essential to that process, even when not essential to the effect's occurrence. This is not simply the claim that removing B would let A work.

The preemption comparison matters. A cause can remain genuinely productive even when a backup makes it unnecessary for the endpoint. Treating completed actual paths as more reliable here than simple endpoint dependence has independent explanatory motivation. Schaffer candidly describes part of this argument as programmatic. It is defeasible warrant, not a finished analysis of all causation.

His §3 (pp.29–33) adds causal-role considerations. These support actual cause status; they do not independently identify original resource ownership. Their ordinary physical examples also do not settle the special nonphysical extension.

### The nonphysical and noncompositional cases cannot be erased

In §2 (pp.26–29), Schaffer concedes that **actual physical laws plus maximal event fragility** make the two-rock case joint causation of the exact fragile effect. He expressly rejects imposing those restrictions on every causal possibility. Two simultaneous spells are his possible overdetermination case; macro/micro causation supplies a conditional noncompositional case. A component-force argument therefore does not exhaust the paper's range.

The spells case gives prima facie modal evidence: two independently sufficient, unpreempted productive connections can intelligibly terminate in a single metamorphosis. Under that description, there need not be one half-transformation assigned to each. This is a substantive challenge to declaring redundant whole-effect production conceptually contradictory. Its warrant combines a considered causal possibility with the general process rationale, rather than consisting solely of written arrows.

But the description is not an observed metaphysical instance or a proof of perfect original agents. Its causal law and process interpretation remain open to principled challenge. Conceivability can mislead; that possibility is a limitation, not an objection that defeats this particular case by itself.

The macro/micro case is especially conditional. Note 2 (p.41) expressly lists distinctness, independent powers/redundancy, and symmetry as disputed requirements. Schaffer considers what follows **if** a strongly nonreductive metaphysics meets them. This is no empirical discovery of independent originals. It nevertheless blocks the unsupported claim that all overdetermination must compose separate material force contributions.

Section 4's generalized processes and note 16 (pp.34, 42) also forbid rejecting the extension merely because it is nonphysical. Conversely, permitting nonphysical primitives does not itself vindicate any particular primitive law or certify original perfection.

### What the component argument does, and does not, establish

In §5 (pp.37–38), Schaffer explicitly allows each rock to produce components of the shattering and thereby to cause the shattering. This argument establishes individual causal membership without requiring that each make every component. It cannot alone prove duplicated entire production.

It also cannot prove that all his other cases are only partial production. Note 20 (p.43) denies a universal metaphysical principle about parts and wholes; its rock-based argument is limited to ordinary actuality. Reading §5 as a ceiling on §§2/4 would erase the very noncompositional possibility he introduces.

## 3. The exact source inference

Oneness printed 23 first says equal powers persist when the agents coexist, then denies each entirely causing the same effect. It explains independence through an agent's being alone in causing the effect entirely and illustrates this with two entire house builders. Printed 24 makes the other's nonproduction a condition of one's exercise. Printed 25 invokes persistence of necessary power. Printed 28 again offers exclusive production or needed assistance as the relevant alternatives; printed 29 excludes dependence on an assistant.

The explicit exclusive-production claim is secure **as (b)**. The contested step is from intact, unreceived power and completed efficacy to that exclusive interpretation. An actual redundant B-route need not supply a missing stage of the A-route, manufacture A's power, or make A await permission. Loss of exclusive provenance is not already loss of productive capacity. Treating those as equivalent requires the further original-efficacy norm.

The house-building illustration gives intuitive evidence for ordinary materially compositional production. It does not independently justify the universal generalization to all noncompositional causation. Schaffer's alternatives directly put pressure on that generalization. The author's stronger original-adequacy and nonabsorption arguments may answer them, but their answer cannot consist solely in restating that another actual source exists.

Printed 27–28's disagreement branch concerns opposite intended results and necessary power. This review neither assumes incompatible perfect wills possible nor settles necessary concordance. That distinct branch does not repair the agreement-branch inference without its own modal premises.

Editorial notes in the supplied translation, especially notes 19 and 21, offer broader references and explanations. They are not treated here as independent verification of those referenced works.

## 4. Unsupported promotions on either side

**For a source defender:**

- From “B actually contributes” to “A needs B to complete A's productive route.” Redundancy is precisely a candidate counterexample.
- From “A no longer has sole total provenance” to “A's necessary intrinsic productive power disappeared.” This requires a defended identification of perfected power with exclusive attribution.
- From ordinary house-building/component effects to all possible entire production. No general composition law has been established here.
- From two uncreated acts or two real causal relations to two extra created traces. Neither inference follows from multiplicity alone.

**For a rival defender:**

- From an OR response table, applicable certificate, or declared route record to two actual productive processes. The causal interpretation needs warrant; Schaffer offers reasons relevant to that warrant, not an automatic validation of every such table.
- From an intact route to provision of every resource, recipient, and enabling condition by its original. Complete route structure and complete original provisioning are different claims.
- From each agent's nonreceipt of power, or the absence of indispensable supplementation, to the entirety of (c). Sovereignty, necessary intrinsic adequacy, and complete particular willing remain additional commitments.
- From modal evidence for a local redundant effect to an explanation of the actual connected created order. A local counterexample would matter to a universal prohibition, but would not by itself establish an actual plural-original cosmology.

A primitive law can be constitutive of an agent's own power rather than an external third donor. It cannot fairly be disqualified just for being primitive while original causation on the other side is allowed primitives. Equally, calling it intrinsic does not independently prove that its entire original-resource interpretation is correct.

## 5. Effect identity and the resulting evidential balance

Schaffer's two processes concern **one actual effect token**. His counterfactual treatment of event fragility uses counterpart conditions (note 5, p.41). The numerical identity of the actual endpoint, sameness under an appropriate counterpart relation, and strict transworld identity across deleted causes are different commitments. None may silently substitute for another. Nor may one demand a maximally detailed total cosmic history merely to defeat a local counterexample about one specified effect.

At the narrow inference under review, the balance is therefore:

1. There is affirmative evidence for genuine redundant actual efficacy, including a noncompositional modal possibility. “Only actual partial material contributions” understates it.
2. The impossibility of two sole exhaustive provenances survives, but supplies no independent exclusion of nonexclusive complete routes.
3. The passage from unborrowed original adequacy to mandatory exclusive completion is left under substantive pressure. The absence of a fully perfected rival is not evidence that this bridge is valid.
4. The move from those routes to two fully perfected originals is likewise not earned. The source's broader comparative perfection case cannot be refuted merely by pointing to spells.

**Recommended bounded conclusion:** retain Schaffer as positive, defeasible evidence against a blanket ban on duplicate nonexclusive entire-effect production; keep the original-resource/perfection extension open; assess the additional common-source reasons separately. Do not report either a completed plural-original countermodel or a newly secured universal nonduplication theorem.
