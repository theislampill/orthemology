# Simultaneous complete production and taḥṣīl al-ḥāṣil

9 October 2026 UTC. Independent bounded argument test. This is an analytical reconstruction and premise audit, not a new formal model, metaphysical-possibility certificate, source edition, or T20 closure judgment.

## Result

**Simultaneity defeats a merely temporal “the effect was there first” objection. It does not itself defeat the source's argument, which expressly addresses simultaneous powers and production. The strongest argument instead requires a principle about the nonduplicability of entire actual productive receipt in one numerical effect and one productive respect.**

The crucial distinction is between an effect's occurrence being completely determined and its entire actual production being exclusively attributable. Complete power plus decisive will establishes the former at the stipulated causal meaning. It establishes the latter only with further productive-individuation/nonabsorption warrant. Calling the effect “already actual” in a logical rather than temporal order does not supply that further warrant.

The examined source does supply substantive considerations: real productive contribution, unchanged effective activity, effects having productive parts, self-sufficient efficacy as perfection, and the claim that genuine addition must increase or multiply the completed work. These deserve assessment. The dispute is not settled merely by pointing to a definition of “alone,” nor by replying with ordinary causal sufficiency. No inspected argument derives the unrestricted exclusion from outcome necessitation and nonreceipt alone; this is a finding about the examined derivations, not a theorem that no independent argument could do so. Conversely, no independently warranted pair of entire unreceived original producers has been established.

The outcome-entailment/exclusive-attribution distinction and the nonabsorption issue were already identified in the inherited reports. The present contribution is the focused test of the simultaneous taḥṣīl objection: temporal, inferential, prerequisite and productive-receipt readings are separated, and the unique-obtaining/unique-source inference is checked explicitly. No new general discovery of causal redundancy is claimed.

## 1. The target held fixed

The proposed case contains distinct agents A and B, the same numerically individual effect e, the same state/time where time applies, and the same productive respect r. Both relevant actual determinations are operative together. Neither is an idle backup, a merely possible alternative, or a contributor whose missing productive stage is supplied by the other.

Four predicates must remain distinct:

1. **Completed causal determination:** A's actual effective power and decisive willing determine e's occurrence.
2. **An intact sufficient productive process:** A actually causes e through its own complete operating route, even though another route also operates.
3. **Entire original productive adequacy:** A actually supplies the whole productive work and requisites of e in r, through unreceived power and without foreign original completion. This is stronger than a completed path through a previously supplied environment.
4. **Exclusive entire attribution:** A alone is the entire actual original producer of e in r; no distinct original also produces it in that same respect.

The serious strongest rival asserts 3 for both agents and denies that 3 entails 4. That is the proposal requiring justification. Schaffer's process arguments and noncompositional case provide prima facie support at 2 and motivate the proposal; they do not independently warrant its entire original-resource interpretation at 3. The source's original-lord standard cannot be replaced with the ordinary path predicate and then claimed to have been refuted.

Conversely, if 3 is defined as “the complete list of actual original producers has exactly one member, A,” then it already is 4. That reading gives a contradiction for the proposed pair, but does not adjudicate the rival's claim that each is an entirely adequate actual producer without exclusivity. “The total actual basis” must not oscillate between all the effect's genuinely supplied productive work and a by-definition singleton roster of its original suppliers.

Same-respect indexing is essential. The Qayyim return establishes that God originally creates the motion which a creature genuinely performs through received power. That is one product under different, hierarchical productive relations. It supplies neither two coequal originals nor a contradiction. The creature's performance must not be counted as another original creation.

## 2. Four readings of “producing what is already produced”

**Attribution control:** the companion source return did not find the exact taḥṣīl al-ḥāṣil expression in its bounded Dar/Minhaj/Jami passages. It remains a label for the objection being tested here, not an exact quotation from those passages. The alleged Tadmuriyya commentary has not been identified. The positively located al-Ṣāwī commentary on Q21:22 places taḥṣīl in its precedence branch and uses producer/effect exclusion for its simultaneous branch; this is a separate later author, not Ibn Taymiyya. The exact witness and voice controls are preserved in the companion commentary report.

### A. Temporally subsequent re-origination

A first produces e; later B tries to originate that very already-existing e again, in the same respect. B cannot newly bring into existence what exists in that very respect. A later alteration, continued preservation, new temporal stage, or distinct instance would be a different target.

This excludes that sequential description. It does not exclude the proposed simultaneous case, where no effect produced by A is posited as temporally present before B's productive exercise.

The source is not confined to this weak argument. The retained Dar text explicitly discusses being agents of one effect in one state (9:365; retained 016, L0753), following its whole-alone exclusion at 9:363/L0747. The companion return confirms the print/electronic correspondence, and Minhaj 1:439 expressly describes the alleged consequence as the effect existing twice in one state. Thus “what if they act simultaneously?” is a test of its rationale, not a case the source simply forgot.

### B. Logical or explanatory fixing

A's complete actual determination necessitates e. Hence e is settled through A. B's complete actual determination also necessitates e. If “already” means only “entailed by the first sufficient determination,” there is no formal contradiction: two sufficient determinations have the same conclusion.

The word “first” here is an order of exposition. It does not establish productive priority of A over B. Nor does causal priority of A to e, and of B to e, establish a cycle, priority between A and B, or a first-completed production that B encounters. Two causes directed toward one effect are not thereby causes of one another.

This is a narrow inference diagnosis, not a constructed possible world. Interpreting those determinations as actual entire unreceived production would add the very semantics still under assessment.

### C. An independently obtaining effect used as a productive input

If B's alleged origination of e takes e's already-obtaining existence as an enabling prerequisite for producing that same existence, the claim is defective. Likewise, if B only explains something about an effect wholly constituted independently of B's alleged productive relation, the word “origination” requires scrutiny.

The companion Minhaj 2:170–172 return supplies a genuine independent anti-bootstrap reason: conferring the power for an exercise already requires effective power to confer it. A condition of that very exercise cannot be produced only by its dependent result. This excludes reciprocal donation of the required powers; it does not by itself exclude two already unreceived effective powers.

But the simultaneous rival does not concede e as a prerequisite of either action. It proposes e as the common terminus of both. Moving from “e is sufficiently accounted for through A” to “B encounters independently completed e as a productive prerequisite” requires an additional argument. Explanation's sufficiency does not itself establish this dependency structure.

### D. Productive saturation or unique entire receipt

This is the strongest version: once an actual determination supplies the entire numerical production of e in r, there is no further actual productive obtaining of that same e in r for another independent producer to supply. Any real second contribution must therefore concern a distinct product, a proper part, another respect, or a qualification of the first contribution's alleged entirety. Otherwise the second is not actual production.

This is a substantive productive-individuation claim. It does not need temporal priority. It can be independently defended as metaphysics rather than inserted into a word's definition. But the single occurrence of e does not, by logic alone, establish the single-valuedness of its original productive attribution. The rival precisely proposes two actual nonexclusive productive relations terminating in that one occurrence. Showing that each relation would need a distinct created obtaining, rather than the common obtaining, is the unresolved bridge.

Even if the ontology recognizes just one token of e's obtaining, that token's number of original producers is a further question. Reifying the obtaining does not settle its sourcehood; no extra created obtaining-token is required by this report.

Representing origin(e, r) as a function with one source-value would assume this bridge. A relation that records both sources would likewise only represent the rival until its causal interpretation is justified. Neither notation settles the question.

## 3. Strongest affirmative reconstruction

The source's position can be reconstructed without making temporal precedence do the work:

1. A complete original undertaking possesses adequate effective power and decisive will for the fully specified product. Its actual adequacy, including the requisite created resources and conditions, is not completed by a foreign original.
2. Such actual self-sufficient efficacy is a perfection. A peer cannot remove part of it, replace it, or make its exercise depend on permission without qualifying the alleged complete original agency.
3. A genuinely operative additional producer is productively efficacious: its contribution is not an idle label or an unreal second citation of the same fact.
4. If the first complete efficacy remains unchanged, real addition cannot be absorbed into precisely the same complete work without either (a) supplying a distinct product or proper productive part, or (b) displacing, limiting, or qualifying the first producer's entire actual manifestation.
5. Distinct products fail the same-effect hypothesis; proper parts or qualifying completion fail each-entire originality; idle attribution fails actual production. Hence no two distinct complete originals actually supply the same entire numerical production in the same respect.

Premise 4 does indispensable work. It is not simply a restatement of the weaker thought that an ineffective agent is less perfect. It extends the no-idle rationale to **nonabsorptive productive addition** and excludes genuinely redundant, nonexclusive complete relations. It also explains the role of the source's comparisons with bodily cooperation: effort, means, movement, intention, manifestation or the completed result changes when a second real worker contributes.

The source has affirmative grounds for 1–4, rather than merely wanting exclusive credit. The historical reconstruction identifies realized efficacy as perfection in Minhaj 2:180–183. Its Majmu sequel at 20:175 argues that unchanged operative addition yields greater or more complete work; 20:176–177 expressly passes from complete power and will to production through the agent alone. Dar's same-state discussion then treats a peer's necessary abstention as restriction rather than a free selection between one's own contrary actions.

Those grounds can support a substantive philosophical judgment. Ordinary cooperative cases positively support their proper-part analysis where the independently observed productive process warrants that analysis. Extending it to all original, possibly noncompositional production is a further scope claim. Their presence does not turn 4 into a theorem of 1–3. In particular, “B genuinely produces” does not yet entail “B supplies an indispensable missing part of A's actual adequacy” when redundancy is exactly the disputed case. Nor does the perfection of being independently effective rank exclusive efficacy above supposedly equally complete nonexclusive efficacy without the relevant account of productive receipt.

This is a **valid conditional exclusion at the strengthened productive meaning**, with a contested general premise. It is not an accusation that the source's whole argument is a verbal tautology. It is also not an unconditional proof from complete determination alone.

## 4. Where the apparent contradictions obtain

### “e needs A and does not need A”

If “needs A” means that e could not occur without A's exercise, a redundant B-complete route contradicts that necessity. But ordinary actual causation is not automatically indispensability, and complete sufficiency does not imply it. Assuming each entire producer is indispensable would already exclude the rival.

If “needs A” instead means that e actually receives its production from A, two actual reception attributions are not yet a logical contradiction. The claim that this entire same-respect receipt is nonduplicable must be argued. Switching between actual productive reception and counterfactual indispensability manufactures a contradiction.

This does not reduce source-dependence to a counterfactual test. The source's actual receipt claim may be stronger and more appropriate. The audit requires precisely that stronger claim to retain a stable meaning.

### “Each agent is independent and not independent”

If A's efficacy actually requires power, conditions or a missing contribution supplied by B, independence in the relevant original sense fails. The Qayyim chapter's examples give real evidence of such received conditions for creatures.

If B is proposed as redundant, its mere co-presence or actual causal status does not establish that A receives a missing completion. The source needs its saturation/nonabsorption account to show why real co-production qualifies A's entire efficacy even when no indispensability is admitted. Conversely, calling B redundant does not prove that all the stronger actual-resource conditions have been preserved.

### “The effect exists twice in one state”

Two numerically different occurrences are not one numerical effect. This is secure. But two alleged productions of one occurrence do not formally become two occurrences without a production-to-effect individuation rule. Distinct intrinsic acts of A and B do not automatically entail two created effects: the established act/product distinction prevents that count.

A source-grounded principle linking distinct entire same-respect original productions to distinct obtainings would be relevant. Declaring it true through an origin-indexed event name, or requiring a one-to-one map between acts and products, would merely encode it. The present test discovers no new independent derivation of that principle.

### “Each is prevented by the other”

This follows once their simultaneous entire productive efficacy is excluded. It is not an independent exclusion if its only reason is that A cannot make e while B makes e. In Dar, the subsequent restriction and circular-enabling arguments add force to the implications of nonduplication; they should not be presented as independent proofs of the initiating same-effect exclusion.

The source itself distinguishes a contradictory joint target from an otherwise possible target blocked by a peer. A critic should preserve that distinction. Equally, the target “A alone produces e while B also produces e in the same respect” is contradictory by construction. Its impossibility does not by itself show a defect in power over the source-neutral product e.

## 5. Strongest rival, at its earned evidential level

The relevant rival is not alternating production, spontaneous power arising from nothing, complementary half-production, or a cooperation that depends on a foreign completed stage. It is parallel, actual, nonexclusive and noncompositional production: each operative relation is adequate for the whole fixed outcome, neither is preempted, and both terminate in the same occurrence.

Schaffer's complete-process account supplies genuine reasons to regard redundant factors as actual causes, rather than idle alternatives. Its process condition involves an intact route, causal priority, distinct actual events and essentiality to the particular process. It does not equate causal priority with being the uniquely prior producer. The noncompositional spell proposal gives prima facie modal weight to a shared outcome without force-component composition. It cannot fairly be dismissed merely by repeating the two-rocks component explanation.

That weight remains defeasible and limited. An intact process can presuppose a received recipient, its powers, a sustaining environment and operative conditions. Supplying an entire process/outcome in that sense does not establish unreceived original supply of everything entering the exact numerical work. The paper's separate component argument expressly allows causing a significant part to count as causing the shattering. Its individualism therefore cannot simply be substituted for the stronger entire-original predicate.

The warranted pressure is this: simple non-idleness, sufficient actual process, occurrence fixing, or lack of marginal necessity cannot alone settle exclusivity. The unearned promotion would be: therefore two perfected originals actually or metaphysically possibly supply the entire original basis of one numerical effect. Neither that promotion nor a purported contradiction in every such extension has been established here.

## 6. Exact analytical test and its limits

The audit performed a premise-substitution test, not a new code exercise:

- Retain the same actual e, productive respect, complete determinations and their occurrence implications. No contradiction follows merely by applying the occurrence implication once for A and once for B.
- Add exclusive entire attribution for either agent. The second same-respect actual attribution is excluded, but exclusivity is now in the premises.
- Instead add productive saturation/nonabsorptive contribution. The exclusion follows conditionally if genuine second entire production must add distinct work or qualify the first. This identifies the substantive premise requiring warrant.
- Retain only intrinsic nonreceipt and no vicious efficient cycles. Parallel productive arrows into e introduce no cycle by themselves. Their genuine original adequacy remains unverified; absence of a graph cycle does not authenticate it.

This is not a formal independence theorem about a fully interpreted original-agent theory. No toy Lean theorem, OR table, source-support label, new finite causal model, or inherited-kernel replay would improve this particular inference diagnosis. The prior mathematical controls already establish weaker signature separations; they do not prove the missing metaphysics.

Only actual same-token identity is used in the simultaneous test. Comparison cases must separately justify relevant target sameness and preservation of complete productive activity. This report does not assume that a necessary original bearer can be absent, that the same event can have a different origin in another possible world, or that removing a represented exercise leaves the entire productive situation unchanged. Bracketing an exercise is a diagnostic comparison, not automatically a metaphysically admissible world.

## 7. Earned conclusion

The useful intermediate result is narrower and stronger than either “simultaneity solves it” or “the effect is already there”:

**For an independently warranted complete productive determination, occurrence follows. If, additionally, entire numerical production is nonabsorptive and nonduplicable in the same respect, two concurrent complete originals are excluded. Taḥṣīl al-ḥāṣil does not independently establish that additional principle merely by describing e as logically settled.**

The source's best reasons bear on the additional principle and must be weighed there. Their generalization to a genuinely redundant original rival is not discharged by the inspected occurrence, independence or cycle arguments. The rival in turn has genuine process-level modal motivation but lacks independently established entire-original semantics. That balanced result leaves the cumulative actual-common-source judgment for the root adjudication; it neither requires a downgrade nor restores certainty by definition.
