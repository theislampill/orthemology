# P14 research brief — Epistemic tuples and intra-agential equilibrium

P14 is a **supplemental applied-research packet**, not one of the 36 pre-existing canonical frontier obligations. Its `P14-ETxx` identifiers are handoff-local navigation keys only. The supplied PDF is not merged Orthemology canon. The task is to determine what survives as an exact applied contribution after definitions, countermodels and scope guards are made explicit.

The source thesis is that heterogeneous evidentiary force may be represented by tuples rather than an unexplained scalar; partial order and incomparability should be preserved until an aggregation rule is justified; evidence is handled by differentiated, mutually conditioning cognitive loci; finite admissible responses permit mixed-policy simplices; scalar Nash requires additional payoff representation; vector/Pareto, team, constrained or pipeline formulations may sometimes be more faithful; and equilibrium is conditional stability, not truth. The PDF's L0-L7 table already separates these assumptions.

The strongest prospective Orthemology contribution is **not Nash existence**. It is the possibility of a worked domain in which structure-preserving observation can be contrasted with lossy or order-manufacturing scalarisation, admissibility into a common coordination problem can be separated from commensurability, endogenous observers can be modelled without flattening their dependencies, and fixed-point stability can be analysed without conflating it with correctness or authority.
