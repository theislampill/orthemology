The intended same-question inconsistency result is valid when radicality really means that every sufficient authority wholly supported by the disputed full token basis is defeated. ## 1 The closure obligation is acknowledged in the source

The formal packet defines token closure existentially: some wholly token-constituted subset X of the full closure C supplies sufficient authority. Its displayed AA axiom only states that Def(X) excludes Auth(X). Its radicality definition contains Def(C). From AA and Def(C) one gets not Auth(C), but not automatically the nonexistence of authoritative subsets.

For the bare symbols, take a two-element basis C={a,b}; let X={a}; set Auth(X)=true, Auth(C)=false, Def(C)=true, Def(Y)=false for every other Y, Corr=true and every Meta=false. All instances of AA hold, the displayed Rad predicate holds, and existential token closure holds. This is a countermodel to the theorem if step 4 is dropped.

The packet itself explicitly calls step 4 a monotonicity/closure convention and says it must be stated. Accordingly this is an **acknowledged formalization obligation**, not a newly discovered substantive defeat. More importantly, the intended prose of Def says that every wholly basis-constituted standard is insufficient. Read literally with that intended scope, it already forbids the authoritative subset in the countermodel. The model is not a countermodel to that stronger meaning.

There are two legitimate formal repairs:

1. State an upward-sufficiency axiom: if X is an eligible subset of C and Auth(X), then Auth(C). AA at C then suffices. This is a substantive convention about the representation of reasons; it is not automatic in nonmonotonic systems where adding defeaters changes the assessed body of reasons.
2. Prefer witness-relative defeat: for every eligible wholly token-supported X, the proposed radical correction defeats X; AA then rules out Auth(X) separately. This states the intended full-basis radicality directly and needs no new empirical or metaphysical premise beyond that intended definition.

The second repair is used here. It permits partial or defeasible reasons to survive; it rules out only a wholly defeated basis being the complete sufficient authority for this same corrective judgment. No global claim that all normative authority is monotone is needed.

## 2 What the valid implication says

Let TokenEligible(X,B) mean that X is a possible authority witness wholly constituted by the current full token basis B. Let Auth(X,q) be sufficient undefeated authority for the exact judgment q. Define DefeatsAll(B,q) as: for every token-eligible X, X is insufficient for q. Authority adequacy says that insufficiency and complete sufficiency cannot both hold for the same X and q. Then DefeatsAll(B,q) implies that no token-eligible X sufficiently authorizes q.

This is a disciplined same-respect consistency result. Its strength comes from applying it without changing q, respect, time, or the full scope of B. Its proof is not an independent source of warrant for DefeatsAll, for the actuality of q, or for the correctness of any particular proposed replacement.

The source's existential formula is more aptly named **token sufficiency** than exclusive token closure: it says that at least one complete sufficient token witness exists, not that every possible sufficient witness is token-constituted. Strong radicality excludes even this existential sufficiency. If an exclusive-source thesis is used instead, it needs a nonvacuity/existence condition so that a world with no authority source does not satisfy it vacuously.

## 3 The five replies are not five disjoint models of one antecedent

The packet openly permits overlap. Its most charitable classification is a family of ways of responding to the objective-radical-correction challenge:

- **Retained meta-standard:** an old higher-order resource already licenses revision. It denies radicality relative to the full basis, even if every first-order rule changes. It is the classification of this task's worked instance.
- **Trans-state or type-level standard:** a truth, fidelity, practice or function standard obtains independently of this current token's endorsements. It may explain correction of the token without implying mind-independent categorical normativity, selected-effect function, divine grounding or one bearer.
- **Holistic normative fixed point:** authority is determined by a whole rather than by an individual stage. A genuine model needs specified coherence/constitution conditions and a warrant for selecting the relevant fixed point. The mere fact that a finite solver finds an equilibrium is not that warrant.
- **Primitive normative fact:** the correction or authority is basic. This is a live logical option left open by the conditional argument. The packet does not deduce that the primitive is teleological, personal, unique or explanatory in the required sense.
- **Relativization or error theory:** the intended robust objective Corr predicate is denied or weakened. This exits the antecedent rather than supplying an authority source for that very robust claim. Local factual byte equality can remain true even if categorical obligation is denied.

Under strict full-basis radicality, the retained-meta reply is unavailable because it denies that condition. Under assumed robust Corr, error theory is unavailable because it denies that condition. H and P can also instantiate T. The five rows are therefore a useful dialectical taxonomy, not an exclusive exhaustive algebraic partition established simply by excluded middle.

There is a further formal scope point: classical logic applied to unconstrained predicates does not prove Corr implies M or T or H or P or E. A model can set Corr true and all five predicates false unless an authority-existence/classification premise connects them. This is not a refutation of the prose taxonomy when its meanings supply those connections. It means the taxonomy is a representation contract rather than an additional metaphysical theorem delivered by the displayed definitions alone.

## 4 Recursive correction is not settled by an infinite chain

The formal packet correctly says that an infinite sequence of deferred authority does not by itself contradict logic. An equation family A_i iff A_(i+1) admits both all-true and all-false interpretations. A seed, a specified holistic condition, or a choice of fixed-point semantics may resolve that underdetermination; none follows merely from drawing arrows.

The packet's intended negative result additionally supposes that authority for the trigger is available only after completion of the whole never-completed dependency chain. That is an explanatory-dependence condition, not a theorem that every infinite or coherentist source is impossible. The prose's use of both preceding-stage derivation and later-stage deferral should be disambiguated when formalized, because they are opposite dependency directions. This report uses the formal section's explicit later-stage Deferred definition and does not manufacture a contradiction from the wording.

Anna-Sofia Maurin's primary discussion treats the direction and context of dependence as central to diagnosing a regress, rather than infinity alone. Candidate E's caution fits that established issue; the present check gives no novelty credit for it. [Maurin, Infinite Regress — Virtue or Vice, pp. 18–21](https://www.fil.lu.se/hommageawlodek/site/papper/MaurinAnnaSofia.pdf).

## 5 The existence and transport burdens remain separate

An actual objectively justified first-order repair does not demonstrate that the actor's entire token authority basis was insufficient. The full basis must be specified before the correction, including retained rules, accessible evidence and legitimate institutional permissions. Shrinking B after the event to contain only the failed rule manufactures radicality by omission.

Conversely, showing that no wholly disputed-token reason is sufficient does not establish which outside reason is legitimate, how the recipient accesses it, or which physical operation executes it safely. Another recipient can inherit a derivation conditionally while lacking warrant for its source identity, premise interpretation, current permission, or mechanism. Those are the main burdens in `SPECIFICATION.md` and the transport report; they are not discharged by Candidate E's anti-bootstrap implication.

## 6 Proper function and ultimate dependence

The worked criterion is appropriate to the declared source-recovery task. That establishes a local success condition and instrumental role. It does not adjudicate the ten different proper-function accounts listed by the owner. A program that successfully compares byte strings need not have biological selected-effect function, Plantingian warrant, intrinsic teleology, or Wisdom.

The primary access-control literature distinguishes the computing base, authentication and authorization, and allows powers to depend on roles and delegation policy. This is a concrete precedent for separating copied statements from recipient authority, not an account of all epistemic warrant. [Abadi, Burrows, Lampson and Plotkin, A Calculus for Access Control in Distributed Systems, §§1 and 5](https://homepages.inf.ed.ac.uk/gdp/publications/Calculus_for_Access_Control.pdf).

The local repair and transport proofs do not consume necessary-source N2, original-possession cognition, essential truthful attestation T0, Wisdom or metaphysical unity. Removing these candidate premises leaves the result intact. Their absence limits any claimed foundational consequence; it does not invalidate the local result.
