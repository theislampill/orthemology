## Blocking findings

### AY-F01 — the eligible evidence language was underspecified

V1 mentions atomic content relations but then speaks broadly about neutral
profiles.  The theorem must quantify over a declared evidence map or a declared
`Λ`-language and require invariance under every content automorphism.  Evidence
outside that language can break the gauge.

### AY-F02 — profile invariance was too close to metaphysical indeterminacy

Equal eligible evidence means nonidentifiability relative to that evidence
class.  It does not establish that literal reference is unreal, conventional,
or metaphysically indeterminate.

### AY-F03 — content-label automorphism and referent permutation were conflated

A formal automorphism preserves the declared content algebra.  Whether it
preserves actual-world reference is exactly the missing source/world anchor.
The theorem cannot assume either answer.

### AY-F04 — structural H7a1 and content gauge need two separate group actions

Deep AM acts on structural positions; Deep AY acts on content labels.  State
explicitly that trivial structural automorphism does not imply trivial content
automorphism, and conversely.

### AY-F05 — intervention evidence was omitted

A neutral causal or behavioral experiment can break a semantic gauge only if
its registered outcome is not invariant under the content recoding.  If the
experiment and output coding are transformed together, it remains gauge
invariant.  Candidate-dependent coding is not one common experiment.

### AY-F06 — the positive anchor condition was too informal

Define the residual automorphism subgroup after all independently warranted
anchors are added.  Literal interpretation is gauge-identified only when its
residual orbit is a singleton.  This is relative to those anchors, not proof of
their authority.

### AY-F07 — rigid semantics was overclose to constitutive semanticity

Even a trivial residual automorphism group establishes only relative literal
identification.  It does not establish that semantics is intrinsic to the
ground rather than imposed by an impersonal law or downstream interpreter.
H7a2 remains distinct.

### AY-F08 — source and Track-N migration were not typed

A source predicate can add a source-relative designated content or referent,
but it is not a neutral gauge-breaking anchor without an independent bridge.
Translation, source co-reference, and actual-world reference remain separate.

### AY-F09 — theorem-family and novelty custody were incomplete

The mechanism is standard group-action/model-theoretic invariance and a fibre
nonidentifiability consequence.  Its value is the content-side H7a2 firewall,
not new general mathematics or a new historical theorem.

## Required repair

1. Define an arbitrary finite eligible evidence map invariant under a residual content-automorphism group.
2. Prove nonidentifiability for every target varying on a residual orbit.
3. Define exact anchor/stabilizer and singleton-orbit conditions.
4. Separate relative literal identification from H7a2 constitutivity and H7a3 mentality.
5. Add intervention/coding, source, and impersonal-rigidity controls.
6. Run a distinct four-content relational-structure and anchor-stabilizer rereview.
