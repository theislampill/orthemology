## Main mathematical contribution

The cross-front work classifies when positive, origin-labelled evidence audits commute with corrective flows. In the stated weighted Fisher/position metric, the universally commuting fixed-energy gradient flows are precisely the separable mixture-energy flows. An exact Euler counterexample shows that positivity and energy descent alone do not preserve that commutation. A rational frozen-potential weight step preserves positivity, normalisation, descent, and the declared audit covariance together. Further results treat historical likelihood transport, minimum audit profiles, sharp likelihood-error bounds, dependency-directed replay, tail-envelope transfer, and a conditional local-to-global source-unity bridge.

These are results developed for this handoff, not assertions of global priority, a diagnosis of an uninspected T20 integrator, or a claim of completed DAEE integration.
