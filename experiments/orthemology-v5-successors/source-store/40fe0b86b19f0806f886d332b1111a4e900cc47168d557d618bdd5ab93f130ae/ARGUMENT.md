# Existential determination and necessary particular sourcehood

## Result and scope

This paper evaluates one attempted inference from the original productive source of an actual correction to the unconditional existence of that same particular bearer. The inference remains incomplete. The new primary-source checks strengthen the affirmative test but provide no new actual warrant for INTR.N2 or INTR.N3. Original productive completion P1 and identification of a particular bearer P2 remain separate burdens. The stronger critic can locate the missing inference without establishing a brute cosmos as actual or possible.

## The admitted target

The case is the evidence-supported correction of a report about the displayed Minḥāj header. Its truth concerns that page. On the admitted cognitive interpretation, the correction occurs because contrary evidence is understood. The causal, constitutive, evidential and truth relations remain distinct. The episode supplies no independent observation of an ultimate bearer's whole nature.

The target is whole actual productive origin, including the relevant exercises and receiving modes. It is not bare capacity to reproduce an endpoint. A critic conditionally granting the complete-source role must retain that stronger target. The inherited same-target uniqueness result remains intact. Genuine intentional effective agency likewise retains its respect-specific knowledge, will and power; neither side may replace it by a selector function.

P1 is original productive completion. P2 identifies the particular bearer. N2 is adequate nonbrute existential explanation. N3 requires an intrinsic account with unconditional existence-entailing force for that same bearer. This test grants P1 and P2 conditionally, without claiming that the correction has established them.

## The primary argument and its affirmative force

The Darʾ passages demand complete determination, including voluntary divine action. They distinguish production from continued absence, consider weaker actuality reasoning and separate existential division from terminology. Their intended scope includes original existence. [D1–D6]

The affirmative asks what settles actual occurrence if the proposed determinants merely favour it. Applied to an original bearer, it asks why that bearer's own actuality may remain primitive while its effects are explained. An independently warranted intrinsic nature excluding absence would answer that further question without an external supplier or efficient self-production. This is a substantive explanatory proposal whose universal scope still needs defence.

The primary locators, inspection limits and inherited controls are recorded in SOURCE_MAP.md and SOURCE_PROVENANCE.json. The argument below tests the proposal; its examples and evaluations are not translations or descriptions of positions expressly considered in those passages.

## A test that grants the stronger productive premise

Conditionally fix g as the particular original bearer. Grant premise D: every relevant received occurrence is necessitated when its complete actual productive determination obtains. Restricting this concession is the critic's move in the test, rather than a restriction attributed to the historical author.

Now ask whether D entails the unconditional existence of g. One conditional answer is straightforward: if g has an independently warranted intrinsic nature whose obtaining is unconditional and whose content excludes g's absence, the desired conclusion follows. Neither the desirability of that answer nor its conditional correctness establishes the antecedent.

Consider a rival which grants g's original productive role at the full agreed scope. It includes g's proper unborrowed exercise and every actual productive contributor and receiving mode relevant to the target. It also grants D. What it withholds is an existential account of g's own unreceived actuality. This does not omit a cause from the stipulated target while calling the cause irrelevant. The original-source grant is the grant that this bearer's existence is unreceived; it does not additionally grant exhaustive explanation of that existence.

The affirmative's response is to apply its stated demand to g itself. This is the crucial additional scope, rather than an allegation that the rival must produce itself, have no real powers or be a passive relay. If the demand is independently warranted at that scope, the rival fails. If it is merely repeated as a requirement that no residual contrast remain, the point in dispute has not been settled.

The critic's narrower objection suspends judgement about g's possible complete absence. A positive brute ontology would need its own actual and modal warrant. Identifying that an inference is unsupported does not establish a rival cosmos, while the rival's lack of proof does not establish the original inference. The test therefore preserves the symmetric warrant discipline inherited from Seventh.

This remains the earlier residual burden, now tested with a more explicit primary rationale. No new logical narrowing beyond Sixth and Seventh is claimed. The test has value because it lets the affirmative use D at the agreed whole-origin scope rather than dismissing determination generally or appealing to randomness without explaining its relevance.

## Intrinsic explanation and the same bearer

Unreceived actuality differs from an independently intelligible nature making absence impossible. The second is not efficient self-production. The subject-presupposition objection to an act producing its whole bearer therefore does not automatically defeat a constitutive explanation. These distinctions were already established in the preceding research.

Two conditional descriptions expose an inference risk. If an explanation includes g's already instantiated actuality, its entailment of g's actuality does not establish that its own antecedent obtains unconditionally. If it instead specifies what a g would be, the specification's instantiation still requires warrant. Neither observation rules out a genuinely existence-entailing nature. They show why a candidate's content and modal force must be independently established.

A noncausal rival can propose an autonomous ground which realises a concrete bearer without efficiently producing it and without necessitating the result. A merely favouring ground does not meet the affirmative's stated contrastive demand. Whether the demand is a justified condition of every adequate explanation remains contested. Neither side can settle the disagreement by using complete to mean its preferred account.

If a separate ground explains the bearer's whole existence, that bearer is received under the broad existential relation. The proposal then contests identification of ultimate explanation with a concrete efficient source, or the transition from efficient originality to broader autonomy. It does not grant broad existential originality while supplying a separate whole-existence ground. If the ground is merely the bearer's already actual nature redescribed, the initial explanatory question returns.

## What a weaker premise can establish

An uninstantiated specification supplies no actual effect. This challenges a supposed account composed only of such specifications. It does not by itself defeat a productive infinitist who posits actual active suppliers throughout. Conversely, listing actual suppliers does not establish the actuality or explanatory adequacy of an entire proposed order. The original productive-completion burden remains substantive.

For this test, P1 and P2 have already been granted. Establishing original actuality again cannot add the missing unconditional modal content. A weaker argument might support an earlier stage if independently defended; it cannot borrow a stronger conclusion from a premise it has set aside.

The inquiry requires no first temporal event, finite chain or traversal of an infinite series. An original covering source is not inconsistent with every non-well-founded subordinate ancestry by definition. The relevant target and relations remain those fixed in the accepted predecessor.

## Direct recognition as an alternative warrant route

An independently warranted recognition of this very bearer as intrinsically existence-entailing could supply the needed content. It need not first be inferred from a separately formulated universal rule. The inquiry does not rule out basic rational knowledge merely because it is disputed, unmeasured or unformalised.

The question is what is apprehended. Awareness of local dependence, recognition of an ultimate bearer as actual and recognition of that bearer's unconditional intrinsic sufficiency are different claims. The correction does not independently establish the last. If the claimed recognition has only weaker content, a later assertion of necessity changes that content. If it has the stronger content, that is the warrant to defend against the developed critic.

## Exact finding and inherited credit

The new contribution is a source-controlled evaluation of one attempted bridge. No candidate intrinsic nature has acquired independent existence-entailing warrant. Necessitation of a received occurrence when its determinant obtains does not establish necessary existence of that determinant. Necessary occupation of a role likewise does not establish necessary existence of its present occupant. The modal-coverage and same-bearer-retention burdens inherited from Seventh are neither assumed nor disproved.

The affirmative remains a serious proposal with an identified explanatory advantage if its warrant succeeds. The developed critic retains the granted productive target and locates the additional existential commitment. Neither assessment warrants a verdict about an actual alternative cosmos. P1, P2, N2 and N3 remain distinct, while conditional uniqueness and genuine local intentional agency retain their accepted scope.

Further progress requires evidence or argument bearing on that exact transfer. Another causal diagram, a repetition that the source is original or an additional passage simply affirming necessity would not by itself change the result.
