\subsection*{Reading and provenance boundaries}

The full parsed text of LM3 was inspected, with selected pages visually checked for the realisation equations, continuation theorem and related mathematical passages. The full stage-two report, the stage-three apoautosis section and concluding status material, and the opening 440 lines of the archived dialogue were inspected for the claims used here. The dialogue later ranges beyond the topic; its complete later contents are not represented as a source review performed for this paper.

ICS5 and CDR were read in full, including the destructive-experiment table in page-image form. GF1's relevant continuation, future-safe compression and reflexivity passages were inspected from extracted text (physical pages 26--29); raw-byte materialisation of that library item was unavailable. Its full contents and rendered pages are not claimed to have been freshly reviewed. The integration directive was used only to identify the named T/C/G/A coordinates and the explicit non-equivalence warnings, not as evidence that the directed reconciliation had been completed.

The accompanying source register supplies hashes for the locally available exact source files. Hashes identify bytes, not factual correctness or independent provenance. The private source corpus itself is not redistributed in the delivery package. References identify it by title, version, section or archived message time.

\section{Finite checks performed for this paper}
\label{app:checks}

A new standard-library Python checker was written for this companion. It does not run a physical system, contact a network, inspect an unfamiliar environment or control a realisation. Its tests use only stipulated finite sets, functions and rational arithmetic. The checks use explicit exceptions rather than removable Python assertions.

All 15 groups passed under Python 3.13.5, both normally and with \texttt{-O}. The two result JSON files were byte-identical. The reported groups are not independent replications or 15 new theorems. These checks do not reproduce the original LM3, S2 or ICS5 verification suites and do not imply that those original suites have been requalified.

\section{Open obligations and falsification conditions}

\begin{longtable}{@{}p{.25\linewidth}p{.32\linewidth}p{.35\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
Claim under test & What would support it & What would defeat or limit it\\
\midrule
\endhead

Future faculty retained & Correct assessment and execution for the declared future family after support change. & A lost consequential distinction, unavailable reference or consumed resource needed by a later request.\\
Endogenous contribution & Controlled evidence that attributed internal functions actually participate. & An external technical service supplying the decisive function omitted from the declared boundary.\\
Cross-case generalisation & Predeclared held-out transfer within justified model restrictions and matched resources. & Failure on the target distribution or apparent gain explained by extra information, leakage or post hoc case selection.\\
Recursive finite capability & One valid strategy for request sequences, not separate per-request witnesses. & A permitted sequence whose intermediate state removes a necessary capability or violates a cumulative budget.\\
Indefinite capability & Additional maintained resource, error, timing and permission assumptions with an appropriate proof. & The finite-state account omits depletion, accumulating error, disturbance drift or event accumulation.\\
Incremental methodological value & Better specified or more reliably assessed decisions against strong matched baselines. & Equivalent performance at greater total cost, or gains attributable only to extra attention or evidence.\\
Historical novelty & Precise result and thorough comparison with relevant prior work. & An equal or stronger established result, or a new label without an additional conclusion.\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

Failure of an implementation premise is not automatically a counterexample to the conditional theorem. Conversely, a correct theorem does not shelter the implementation from failure. This final distinction is what permits the programme to pursue the faculty seriously without treating either skepticism or enthusiasm as evidence.
