# Attribution-extension primary-source comparison

Checked 2026-10-02. This supplement does not alter the frozen theorem or extend
its observation model.

John R. Douceur, *The Sybil Attack*, IPTPS 2002, Microsoft Research author text:
https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2002/01/IPTPS2002.pdf

The introduction distinguishes remote entities from their perceived identities
and explains why redundant identity counts need not give independent entities.
Our root domains are not necessarily persons or computing entities, labels are
fixed rather than adversarially created, and the alias-class family is bounded
in advance. Thus the present result is not a new general Sybil impossibility,
nor an application proving that every practical system requires one particular
identification authority. It prices one declared unresolved attribution map.

Orestis Alpos, Christian Cachin, Björn Tackmann and Luca Zanolini,
*Asymmetric distributed trust*, Distributed Computing (2024), §4:
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00446-024-00469-1

The article reviews fail-prone systems and the classical Q^3 condition: three
possible maximal fault sets must not cover the whole process universe. That is
the established principle behind our arbitrary-family lower bound after the
root-map projection is justified. The present model has one common uncertainty
family over root maps; it does not implement the paper's process-specific
asymmetric trust or consensus protocols.

Orestis Alpos, Christian Cachin and Luca Zanolini, *How to Trust Strangers:
Composition of Byzantine Quorum Systems*, DISC 2021 brief announcement and
linked author paper:
https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.DISC.2021.44
https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.11331

Composition of fault/trust assumptions and preservation of consistency and
availability are prior subjects. Our result does not claim to introduce them.
Its bounded addition is the exact image-intersection and maximal-fault-world
calculation for one unknown alias class, applied to the separately charged
physical repair/cancellation interface. No cited quorum protocol supplies the
physical gate, permission institution, or provenance-warrant premises for free.

The original v2 Malkhi–Reiter, lease, mobile-recovery and covering comparisons
continue to apply. No new 3k+1 theorem, covering number, general trust theory,
or philosophical source conclusion is claimed.
