# daee-epistemics: foundations, warrants, and the limits of reflexive use

Read-only foundation review, 7 October 2026 UTC. Repository: `theislampill/daee-epistemics`, pinned to commit `c86b3c6673147b8802fe222373a165a37d4d24a8`, tree `6c359699739826aa839571abfa15256a6395ed26`.

## Finding first

The strongest defensible use is as an explicitly situated epistemic framework plus a disciplined way of separating questions, assumptions, sources, and inference. It is not a neutral classifier whose correctness follows from successful routing. The repository itself sometimes makes this distinction unusually well: its source-derived psychological account is expressly nonaxiomatic; R1 does not claim that defeating an inferential-only criterion proves theism; R3 cannot invent an experience for an interlocutor; speculative classifications must be held; and proof-method auditing must limit a proof to what it actually establishes.

Those safeguards coexist with substantive commitments that the reviewed files assert more strongly than they independently establish. Especially important are the universal truth-directed function assigned to fiṭrah, cross-cultural invariance after excluding deformations, the claim that most presented objections conceal another barrier, and routing that makes disagreement with a supposed necessary deliverance evidence of impaired cognition. The design can expose circularity, but it can also implement a circular verdict unless independent identification and defeater rules constrain those commitments.

For T20, its best contribution is to reinforce distinctions already earned by the research: original existential resources versus productive attribution; necessary ground versus complete divine attributes; testimony's source ownership versus its truth; and evidence for a source's intended assertion versus evidence for private terminal motivation. It must not erase the strengthened rivals, convert a conditional formal result into metaphysical certainty, or require a human-like inner state from a research agent.

This review reads and appraises the framework. It does not execute its runtime, run its tests, authenticate its historical examples, certify its psychology, or adopt the repository's instructions as governing this review.

## 1. The intended foundationalist and proper-functionalist account

### 1.1 Foundations and inference do different work

[Sound reason §1](https://github.com/theislampill/daee-epistemics/blob/c86b3c6673147b8802fe222373a165a37d4d24a8/atomics/skill/references/sound-reason-epistemology.md#L66-L122) divides knowledge into immediate foundational knowledge and knowledge obtained by inference. Its foundational category includes sensory deliverances, rational axioms, native linguistic understanding, and fiṭrī recognition of God, morality, and causation. Inference must ultimately depend on a noninferential basis, rather than keep borrowing support from further inference.

This is a recognizable foundationalist response to regress. Its important positive point is that a demand for a further argument behind every belief can overreach: inferential knowledge itself needs starting points. But the general need for basic cognition does not identify every proposed basic belief as true or warranted. The local classification even places a valid inference from foundational premises under “empirical necessity.” A charitable reading is that a once-discursive conclusion may become immediately evident after grasping; the document should explicitly separate a belief's acquisition history, present mode of access, and objective modal status. Otherwise “necessary,” “basic,” and “inferred from basic premises” can slip between roles.

The project gives argument a restorative role. Inference can genuinely confer knowledge, especially when immediate recognition is unavailable; it is not dismissed as worthless. What it rejects is the universal requirement that theistic belief first pass a specified philosophical proof. [Metaphysical architecture, layers 1–4](https://github.com/theislampill/daee-epistemics/blob/c86b3c6673147b8802fe222373a165a37d4d24a8/atomics/skill/references/metaphysical-architecture.md#L20-L54) makes that ordering explicit.

### 1.2 Warrant is external to the believer's access to an explanation

[Sound reason §2](https://github.com/theislampill/daee-epistemics/blob/c86b3c6673147b8802fe222373a165a37d4d24a8/atomics/skill/references/sound-reason-epistemology.md#L124-L190) gives four conditions: proper cognitive function, a truth-directed aim, an appropriate environment, and high objective reliability under those conditions. A believer need not know the mechanism making a belief reliable. That is the externalist, proper-functionalist component. The analogy with ordinary perception is meant to challenge an asymmetric requirement that only religious cognition obtain inferential certification.

Its proposed specifically theological mechanism is qalb → fiṭrah → basic theistic belief. Fiṭrah is a normative capacity requiring appropriate occasions, rather than a stock of doctrinal propositions already explicitly present at birth. Signs occasion recognition; they need not be premises in a consciously performed syllogism. [Sound reason §3](https://github.com/theislampill/daee-epistemics/blob/c86b3c6673147b8802fe222373a165a37d4d24a8/atomics/skill/references/sound-reason-epistemology.md#L192-L236).

The central concession to criticism is explicit: the deliverance of basic recognition is assigned foundational status, whereas the particular qalb-chain, potency/activation model, and seven-deformation account are source-derived inferences. Challenges to those mechanisms must be answered with textual grounding, coherence and explanatory warrant, not dismissed as attacks on an axiom. That statement is a genuine internal constraint, even though the file expresses strong confidence in those inferences.

### 1.3 Proper basicality is not unrestricted permission to believe

[R1](https://github.com/theislampill/daee-epistemics/blob/c86b3c6673147b8802fe222373a165a37d4d24a8/atomics/skill/references/tactics/R1-internalist-criterion.md#L27-L60) compares theistic belief with memory, the past, and other minds. Its operator contract limits the result to pressuring or narrowing an inferential-only criterion; it specifically forbids calling the analogy proof of theism. It also prohibits manufacturing that dispute when the other party has not actually imposed the criterion. This is a strong applicability guard.

[R3](https://github.com/theislampill/daee-epistemics/blob/c86b3c6673147b8802fe222373a165a37d4d24a8/atomics/skill/references/tactics/R3-warranted-basic-belief.md#L27-L62) is even more bounded at intake: acknowledged recognition is required, its possible defeaters must be named, and a genuine report of no such experience blocks the tactic. One analytical move must be followed by reassessment, rather than an automatically queued cumulative argument.

However, R3's prose moves too quickly from involuntary recognition to evidence of proper function. Involuntariness distinguishes an occurrence from a deliberately selected preference; by itself it does not establish truth-directed design, a suitable environment, reliability, or absence of a defeater. Its own four-condition warrant account requires those further distinctions. We should preserve the narrower operator contract rather than treating every unchosen seeming as warranted.

### 1.4 Tawātur is extended from testimony to cognition

[Sound reason §4](https://github.com/theislampill/daee-epistemics/blob/c86b3c6673147b8802fe222373a165a37d4d24a8/atomics/skill/references/sound-reason-epistemology.md#L298-L389) starts with numerous, varied, independent transmission channels and extends the rationale to purportedly invariant rational and fiṭrī deliverances. The intended argument is not simply that many people vote for a belief. It is that independently recurring recognition from reliable faculties carries epistemic weight that a localized speculative argument may not defeat.

The extension needs its own warrant. Independence of reporters is not yet independence of error-generating mechanisms: many people can share architecture, environmental regularities, inherited categories, or similar elicitation effects without collusion. The proposition being attested must also remain fixed. Recognition of agency in nature, a general source of being, a necessary ground, monotheism, and acceptance of a specific revelation are not interchangeable data.

The repository recognizes part of this. [The inductive method](https://github.com/theislampill/daee-epistemics/blob/c86b3c6673147b8802fe222373a165a37d4d24a8/atomics/skill/references/tactics/inductive-fitri-method.md#L28-L116) separates invariant foundations from variable religious superstructure and explicitly prohibits saving every counterexample by ad hoc reclassification. Yet its assertions of invariance, controlled deformation, and the explanatory limits of cognitive science are not accompanied in this file by a dataset, sampling protocol, operational measures, or study-level citations. Pre-specifying a distinction prevents one form of post hoc adjustment; it does not establish that the distinction is correct, the sampling independent, or the rival explanation excluded.

There is also a level distinction worth making explicit: a person's noninferential warrant can be primary while cross-person convergence is the analyst's corroborating evidence. If, instead, mass attestation is necessary to warrant every basic deliverance, the theory risks making supposedly immediate warrant depend on inferential judgments about transmission. The phrase “final epistemic guarantor” should not obscure which of those roles is intended.

## 2. Revelation and ontology: what the architecture assumes and what it can derive

[The kernel](https://github.com/theislampill/daee-epistemics/blob/c86b3c6673147b8802fe222373a165a37d4d24a8/atomics/skill/references/kernel-thesis.md#L18-L66) makes nonconflict between sound reason and authentic transmission a governing commitment. It locates apparent conflict in defective reason, weak transmission, or the wrong criterion. [Prophecy/waḥy](https://github.com/theislampill/daee-epistemics/blob/c86b3c6673147b8802fe222373a165a37d4d24a8/atomics/skill/references/prophecy-wahy-supremacy.md#L15-L61) argues that revelation supplies knowledge beyond what inference from created order can establish: full divine attributes, the unseen, and a complete moral and eschatological account.

Taken conditionally, there is a clear argument: genuinely divine, truthful communication would have epistemic resources unavailable to a limited inference from created effects. That does not authenticate a particular report. The repo's own [§4.3](https://github.com/theislampill/daee-epistemics/blob/c86b3c6673147b8802fe222373a165a37d4d24a8/atomics/skill/references/sound-reason-epistemology.md#L359-L389) separates generic theistic recognition, reliable transmission, content, and specific prophetic authority. Our use must keep that condition in place.

The account is situated, including an explicitly Atharī treatment of divine action, time, attributes and speech. It cannot become methodologically neutral merely by describing itself as sound reason rather than a school's doctrine. Conversely, being situated does not refute it. The right appraisal is premise-by-premise and source-by-source.

Its most useful ontological controls are these:

- Semantic content differs from the mode of its instantiation. Meaningful God-talk need not supply a creaturely mechanism, but unknown modality cannot rescue an actual contradiction. [Sound reason §6](https://github.com/theislampill/daee-epistemics/blob/c86b3c6673147b8802fe222373a165a37d4d24a8/atomics/skill/references/sound-reason-epistemology.md#L440-L493).
- Necessary existence is not necessary emanation of every act. Standing capability and particular voluntary acts must be separated.
- A divine act and its created effect are different ontological roles. Temporal occurrence is not automatically external createdness under this account. [§§6–6.2](https://github.com/theislampill/daee-epistemics/blob/c86b3c6673147b8802fe222373a165a37d4d24a8/atomics/skill/references/sound-reason-epistemology.md#L495-L602).
- Conceptual distinction, real separability, causal dependence and necessary co-occurrence are not equivalent. The attribute-composition analysis is strongest as a demand that these relations be distinguished, not as a verdict that every alternative metaphysics has already equivocated. [§6.3](https://github.com/theislampill/daee-epistemics/blob/c86b3c6673147b8802fe222373a165a37d4d24a8/atomics/skill/references/sound-reason-epistemology.md#L604-L708).
- Temporal succession, current sustaining dependence, numerical infinity, causal regress, and definitional circularity demand different arguments. [Causal-series taxonomy](https://github.com/theislampill/daee-epistemics/blob/c86b3c6673147b8802fe222373a165a37d4d24a8/atomics/skill/references/diagnostics/causal-series-taxonomy.md#L50-L108).

The historical claims require a different evidence class. Accounts of Aristotle's God, the development of kalām, representative fiṭrah positions and their prevalence are historical or interpretive assertions, not axioms of cognition. For example, [fitrah-perspectives](https://github.com/theislampill/daee-epistemics/blob/c86b3c6673147b8802fe222373a165a37d4d24a8/atomics/skill/references/diagnostics/fitrah-perspectives.md#L86-L211) assigns particular positions to Ibn ʿAbd al-Barr and Sayyid Qutb and labels one position consensus/orthodox. Those labels do not substitute for the corresponding primary passages, a defined consensus claim, or an account of representativeness. I have verified that the repo makes these claims, not independently verified their intellectual history.

## 4. Fair adversarial assessment

### 4.1 The strongest positive case

The method catches a real class of research errors: silently treating a contested evaluation rule as universal; moving between meanings of necessity; using a theological quotation as if it independently authenticated its speaker; attributing an entire effect from mere participation; and extending an argument past its conclusion. The [proof-method audit](https://github.com/theislampill/daee-epistemics/blob/c86b3c6673147b8802fe222373a165a37d4d24a8/atomics/skill/references/diagnostics/proof-method-audit.md#L118-L163) requires the claim-carrier, premises, inference and conclusion scope to be explicit. It distinguishes a local proof comparison from installing one proof method as the exclusive tribunal. These are reusable research disciplines even when one does not settle the framework's theological foundations.

It also offers a valuable anti-escalation discipline. A thin excerpt need not produce a total worldview classification, and an unanswered question need not summon a larger argument bank. [Mixed-case handling](https://github.com/theislampill/daee-epistemics/blob/c86b3c6673147b8802fe222373a165a37d4d24a8/atomics/skill/references/diagnostics/mixed-case-handling.md#L41-L82) permits stopping with an identified missing differentiator and explicitly forbids settling motive from model intuition. [Symmetric taqlīd](https://github.com/theislampill/daee-epistemics/blob/c86b3c6673147b8802fe222373a165a37d4d24a8/atomics/skill/references/tactics/symmetric-taqlid-check.md#L41-L52) applies scrutiny to inherited religious commitments as well as inherited skepticism.

### 4.2 Circularity has several different forms

Some reliance on basic cognitive capacities while assessing those capacities is unavoidable. That alone is not a cheap refutation of externalism. Nor does a conditional argument fail merely because its premises are theological.

The specific avoidable loop is narrower: define healthy cognition by producing conclusion C; retain only healthy respondents; report invariant C among retained respondents; use that invariance to establish C and diagnose dissent. The reviewed files risk this loop because their sample condition is freedom from deformations whose manifestations include failure to recognize the target. The explicit ban on ad hoc rescue is helpful, but an independently observable classification protocol is still needed.

Another loop is argumentative: a criterion is rejected because it conflicts with revelation, while the authenticity or interpretation of that revelation is precisely what the criterion was being asked to examine. To avoid this, separate (a) a method's unjustified claim to exclusive jurisdiction, (b) its ordinary evidential merits, and (c) the prior authentication premises of the report. A criterion can be contestable without being false, and failing to prove itself by its own standard does not automatically defeat it unless it claims that universal self-applicability.

The mirror error would be to make every religious claim pass an arbitrarily narrow test before admitting any evidence. A fair audit asks both sides what licenses their rules. It does not simply grant the framework's own starting points immunity under a different name. The title “internalist criterion” also needs care: internal access to justification and a demand for inference behind every belief are different commitments. R1's analogy pressures the latter; it cannot refute every internalist account merely by showing that some beliefs are basic.

### 4.3 Diagnostic confirmation bias is a genuine application risk

[Seven-deformations](https://github.com/theislampill/daee-epistemics/blob/c86b3c6673147b8802fe222373a165a37d4d24a8/atomics/skill/references/diagnostics/seven-deformations.md#L13-L30) says most presented barriers are not the intellectual barrier they appear to be. Its [hawā/shubhah discriminator](https://github.com/theislampill/daee-epistemics/blob/c86b3c6673147b8802fe222373a165a37d4d24a8/atomics/skill/references/diagnostics/seven-deformations.md#L329-L339) uses accelerating new objections after a supposedly complete answer. That observation underdetermines its cause. New objections can expose a previously hidden dependency, reveal that an answer failed, reflect a compound problem, or be normal research progress. The researcher's judgment that an objection has been dissolved needs independent checking before failure to agree becomes evidence of volitional resistance.

The safeguard is to keep the target as the public argument or research artifact. Our reflexive audit can identify an omitted premise, repeated unsupported claim, selective source use or shifting success condition. It cannot infer a human's sincerity, culpability, hidden recognition, or spiritual condition. It also cannot ascribe those faculties to this assistant. This restriction is stronger than merely marking a speculative inner-state verdict with a label.

[Noetic checklist, thin-basis rules](https://github.com/theislampill/daee-epistemics/blob/c86b3c6673147b8802fe222373a165a37d4d24a8/atomics/skill/references/diagnostics/noetic-reading-checklist.md#L289-L350) contains substantial protections: independent signals, provisional labels, an explicit missing differentiator, and no identity-based verdict. It is better read as a limit on inference than permission to obtain motives from sufficiently elaborate prose.

### 4.4 Genuine safeguards, without overcrediting them

- R1 releases only the criterion result, not a full theistic conclusion.
- R3 cannot work with an unacknowledged experience and must take debunking or other defeaters seriously.
- The inductive method prohibits ad hoc foundation rescue.
- [V9](https://github.com/theislampill/daee-epistemics/blob/c86b3c6673147b8802fe222373a165a37d4d24a8/atomics/skill/references/techniques/V9-necessary-knowledge-priority.md#L34-L62) requires the actual defective premise to be located and HOLD/PARTIAL otherwise. This blocks claiming a completed refutation merely because a conclusion violates the framework's floor.
- Mixed-case handling requires nonredundant, independent routes before cumulative escalation.
- Theoretical substrate must be loaded only when its specific burden is live, limiting theory-display and overclassification.

These are procedural specifications. Their presence does not show that practitioners follow them, that their categories reliably identify the intended mechanism, or that their interventions improve reasoning. Those are separate validation questions.

### 4.5 Local tensions worth resolving

1. The noetic checklist provides underspecified or potentially conflicting uncertainty-routing guidance. It requires dimensions 1, 4 and 8 for confident single-profile classification, but later restricts `NS-?` to cases where dimensions 1, 4 and 6 are jointly under-signalled. These thresholds might govern different output modes, such as a provisional pair versus an unknown profile, so their difference alone is not an unconditional formal contradiction. A concrete unresolved case has dimensions 1, 4 and 6 adequately signalled, only dimension 8 missing, and no justified profile pair: confidence is blocked, the earlier rule permits `NS-?`, and the later restriction does not license it. The intended fallback and precedence need clarification. This is an uncertainty-routing ambiguity, not a demonstration that the framework forces a diagnosis; this review keeps the case provisional without assigning a profile. [Lines 293–350 and 352–363](https://github.com/theislampill/daee-epistemics/blob/c86b3c6673147b8802fe222373a165a37d4d24a8/atomics/skill/references/diagnostics/noetic-reading-checklist.md#L293-L363).
2. “Being as such requires a source” in the inductive foundation list needs a stated quantifier and domain. If all being is included, the unreceived source is implicated; if dependent/created being is meant, that qualification is doing essential work. This is a formulation requiring clarification, not a refutation of the developed causal account.
3. Sound-reason treats fiṭrah as capacity activated by signs; fitrah-perspectives stresses an internally awakening positive orientation, and says a child has recognition. These can be reconciled as dispositional orientation versus occurrent propositional cognition, but the distinction should be explicit before developmental or isolation cases are used as evidence.
4. The historical prevalence/consensus labels and several unattributed quotations need primary-source traceability. No bibliography was located in this assigned foundational tranche. That is a bounded provenance limitation, not a claim that no relevant source exists elsewhere.

## 5. Concrete application to the current T20 research

The following applies the useful distinctions to the actual current reports, not to an imagined earlier state. The eight read artifacts are bound in `T20_BINDINGS.json`. Their reported experiments and source inspections retain their credit; I have not replayed them or re-inspected their underlying Arabic scans in this tranche.

### 5.1 Original resource, necessary being, and actual productive attribution

The explanatory-warrant report already demonstrates why full least-generation of a combined standing-resource/token-history graph is too strong for the source's admitted beginningless particular acts. A fixed actually original resource can contribute while full token generation fails. The source distinction is between the origin of standing efficacy and the conditions of particular exercises.

That aligns with the repo's distinction between successive acts and current sustaining dependence, and with its separation of standing capability from a particular created effect. It should change our method in one precise way: audit the explanatory relation before allowing a fixed-point or graph criterion to act as a universal test of original completion. A useful mathematical method in institutional attestation does not acquire jurisdiction over metaphysics merely by sharing a graph form.

The T20 correction from candidate paths to actually used positive paths is equally important. The same original resource and bearer must be preserved through the bridge; an unrelated original or a reflexive path cannot substitute. The reviewed framework supports this demand for an explicit claim-carrier, but does not itself supply the productive-attribution premise.

### 5.2 The strengthened coalescent rival must retain its earned scope

The current efficacy appraisal and intrinsic-willing reports already credit the stronger single-model rival with represented intrinsic standing cognition, structural nonreceipt, independently selected full-Q activity and a non-peer-valued own end. They reject the former blanket missing-knowledge/missing-intention objection at that declared signature. They distinguish actual joint configuration from receipt of an intrinsic perfection and from inability to achieve a source-neutral end alone.

The repo's distinction between conceptual relation, necessary co-occurrence and efficient receipt supports that correction. Its ontology also prevents counting an intrinsic divine act as a newly created emission merely to force additive output. The live question remains why genuine joint original exercise entails deficient intrinsic agency, or why every such act needs separately individuated created manifestation. The source provides serious affirmative warrant; the finite interpretation does not establish a metaphysically possible plural-original world. Neither fact should be laundered into the other conclusion.

In particular, naming the rival a contaminated conception of reason would do no research work. A useful operation identifies the exact premise, shows what follows under it, tests the strongest stated alternative, and records the remaining warrant. T20 already does substantial work of this kind. It should not be restarted as though nothing has been investigated.

### 5.3 Testimony and nonconventional signs

The repository's best testimony distinction is provenance → content → authority, together with separate burdens for transmission method, report class, certainty and doctrinal scope. [Hadith authentication](https://github.com/theislampill/daee-epistemics/blob/c86b3c6673147b8802fe222373a165a37d4d24a8/atomics/skill/references/diagnostics/hadith-authentication-epistemology.md#L76-L185) explicitly prevents a question about a particular report's evidential yield being inflated into rejection of all testimony.

T20's current nonconventional-indication result adds an equally important positive constraint: an explicit prior credential pact is not universally required for a contextual sign. Known circumstances and character can support recognition. A remaining logical rival does not automatically defeat ordinary warranted testimony. Conversely, a finite successful planning model does not authenticate the historical observations, the pragmatic class, or the source's unrestricted veracity.

The independent testimony review corrects the courtesy rival at exactly the right boundary: a terminal courtesy goal may use communicative acknowledgment as an intended means. Identical terminal-goal labels or changed hidden motive do not establish different public adoption. That correction should control any reflexive diagnostic use of the repo: do not collapse terminal aim, intended means, foreseen uptake, publicly undertaken assertion, propositional truth, veracity and normative authority.

The prophecy file's divine-truthfulness account remains conditional on identifying the event as genuinely divine attestation. Calling a token “source-owned” or “genuine sign” is not a fresh authentication result. The current testimony lane preserves the positive source-owned-veracity conditional while leaving actual historical authentication unestablished by these toy models. That is calibrated scope, not a general rejection of prophetic knowledge.

### 5.4 Research closure is not a restoration verdict

T20's ongoing obligation remains open under the owner's closure requirement. The repo's STOP/HOLD/PARTIAL vocabulary may improve claim accounting, but cannot grant permission to close the programme, certify a research duration, or turn an unresolved philosophical bridge into a completed “restoration.” A coherent research workflow can finish a bounded comparison while retaining a live broader question.

## 6. What should change our use of the method

### Adopt now for this read-only research

1. Maintain separate columns for source assertion, historical attribution, philosophical argument, formal conditional, executed check and actual-world application.
2. State a proof's exact premise/form/conclusion before comparing its strength.
3. Audit every relation transition: dependence → receipt; receipt → productive agency; participation → entire provision; token custody → indication; indication → truth; truth → authority.
4. Ask what observation or argument would change the current conclusion, and record an unresolved differentiator rather than multiply labels.
5. Apply the same source-fidelity and argument-scope demands to our affirmative and rival reconstructions.
6. Count independent validation by genuinely different evidential access or method, not by multiple artifacts or agents sharing the same premises and code lineage.

### Adapt rather than import literally

Use “diagnosis” for the structure of an expressed argument or research artifact. Replace inner-state language with observable claims, explicit commitments, source dependencies and demonstrated inference patterns. Separate local methodological scrutiny from a demand that an entire worldview first be relinquished. A contested criterion deserves examination, not automatic invalidation.

### Hold pending further evidence

Do not treat the seven-deformation ontology as empirically validated psychology; infer motive or sincerity; certify uncorrupted cognition; count agreement as successful truth-restoration; infer doctrinal truth from runtime compliance; or give the contemplated Computational Epistemic Security programme implementation status. It remains a future candidate, not a task launched by this review.

## 7. Missing validation and the discriminating work it would require

These are proposed standards, not experiments executed or a request to deploy interventions on people.

- **Textual derivation:** attach the primary work, edition, exact passage, speaker/quotation context and interpretive step for each foundational quotation and each historical generalization. Separate source author, translator/editor and framework synthesis.
- **Independent category identification:** define each deformation using observable criteria that do not already assume acceptance of the target conclusion. Permit none/unknown and genuine disagreement.
- **Blinded discriminant tests:** ask whether independent readers identify the same publicly expressed criterion or inference error with theological agreement and group identity masked; measure disagreement and false positives rather than only routing conformity.
- **Counterexample policy:** pre-state what would weaken a claimed cross-cultural invariant, what observations establish a deformation, and what would require revising the foundation/superstructure division.
- **Intervention outcome:** distinguish greater clarity, accurate understanding, explicit premise change and reduced distress from conversion or verbal agreement. A persuasion result is not by itself a truth test.
- **Symmetric adversarial cases:** include a framework-aligned speaker making an invalid inference and a critic making a valid local objection; success means preserving the latter's result without an unsupported motive diagnosis.
- **Metaphysical bridge tests:** continue source-specific argument rather than generic graph growth: identify which source conception of original efficacy warrants created contribution individuation, complete provider coverage or the exclusion of co-preservation.

The best immediate validation question for our use is therefore modest and concrete: does the framework help us expose a previously hidden premise or prevent an actual overclaim, while preserving the strongest warranted result on both sides? That can be evaluated from the research record without claiming privileged access to anyone's interior state.
