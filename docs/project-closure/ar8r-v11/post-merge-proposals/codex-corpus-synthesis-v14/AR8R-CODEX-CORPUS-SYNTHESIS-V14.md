# AR8R Codex corpus synthesis V14

## Result

The corpus supports one genuine but deliberately thin common structure.
For a domain `X`, an observation or transport map `E : X -> O`, and a
target-relative query `Q : X -> T`, an exact decoder on the attained image
exists exactly when `Q` is constant on every fibre of `E`. A refinement
`R : X -> C` restores exact target recovery exactly when `Q` is constant on
the joint fibres of `(E,R)`.

This is existing elementary quotient/fibre mathematics. The generic statement
is broader than the repository's Boolean-target, nonempty-world `AR-T1` record;
they share the same invariant but are not identical under typed renaming.
`AR8R-T294` is a strict causal-profile specialization of `AR-T1`. V14 assigns
no new theorem identity and no general novelty.

## Two guards that prevent overstatement

First, a decoder on all of `O`, rather than only `range E`, needs an extension
value off the attained image. The exact condition is fibre constancy plus
either surjectivity of `E` or inhabitation of `T`. The empty-domain,
nonempty-observation, empty-target countermodel blocks the unguarded converse.

Second, extensional correctness and a second guard coordinate do not collapse.
Fibre constancy of `(Q,G)` is equivalent to separate fibre constancy of `Q`
and `G`. Content may therefore factor while warrant, applicability,
authorization, provenance independence, version eligibility, temporal
stability, identity, proper function, mentality, or world truth does not.

## Cross-lane adjudication

The common theorem genuinely applies to target-relative distinguishability in
proof applicability, translation, intervention profiles, local strategies,
and profile-based deduplication. In each case the instance map must identify
the domain, observation, target query, and exact failure fibre.

Other recurring structures are not thereby identified with quotient
factorization. Provenance equivalence, probabilistic dependence, reason and
authority contracts, temporal fixed points, causal interventions,
function-fixing relations, modal identity, source interpretation, and
intentional hosting have distinct native objects and failure classes. Encoding
their yes/no answers as labels recovers only the thin extensional theorem; it
does not identify those mechanisms.

## Residual-guided synthesis

Repeated near-misses often expose a missing coordinate rather than a missing
direct bridge: version, provenance root, recipient applicability, bearer,
temporal model, or intervention eligibility. V14 records these hypotheses as
discriminative search instruments. A correction earns credit only if it is
intrinsically defined, survives representation changes, closes more than one
burden, or cleanly partitions rivals. Plausibility alone carries no authority.

## Countermodel boundary

Eleven explicit controls preserve the separations most likely to be erased by
an overstrong unification: correct content without warrant; correct content for
the wrong version; copied certificates with one root; synchronized agents
without identity; local closure without future stability; common intervention
without causal identification; reliable success without proper function;
coherent modal role without numerical identity; an underived role without one
personal bearer; source-relative doctrine without world truth; and finite
articulability without mentality or Speech.

## Formal and empirical state

`CodexFibreSynthesis.lean` proves the four bounded mathematical obligations.
The finite checker exhaustively tests the declared small cases and the
off-range counterexample. These receipts do not establish novelty or adoption.

Four empirical items are protocol drafts only. No experiment has run. B3 can
reach `PREREGISTRATION_READY_FOR_REVIEW_DRAFT`; B1, B2, and B4 remain blocked
on target-specific construct or implementation maps. All four retain ethics,
custody, power, measurement-invariance, missingness/leakage, and external-review
burdens.

## Verdict

The corpus establishes an existing common theorem family, several guarded
multi-lane applications, and an anti-unification boundary. It does not yet
establish a new cross-lane invariant with native explanatory force across all
mechanisms. Integrated champion remains `NONE`; meniscus remains
`MENISCUS_NOT_REACHED`; natural closure remains `NOT_REACHED`.
