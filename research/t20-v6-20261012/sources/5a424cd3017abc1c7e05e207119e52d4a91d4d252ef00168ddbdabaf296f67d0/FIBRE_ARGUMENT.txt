**Yes — directly in the existing mathematics.**

The relevant apparatus is already stated in the pinned research (continuation-complete orthings and the graded-representation formalisation). No new primitives are required.

### Model

Let the hidden domain \(D\) be the set of possible production-and-transmission histories for the target book (authorial act + intermediate copies + independent innovations + losses).  
Let the declared representation \(r = \rho_A\) be the finite-support observable panel currently available (surviving manuscripts, citation loci, internal features, coherence relations).  
Let \(q: D \to Z\) be the target observable “exact human authorial identity and exact original wording.”

### Fibre criterion (Theorem 4.2)

A representation \(r\) preserves \(q\) if and only if  
\[
r(d) = r(d') \implies q(d) = q(d') \qquad \forall d,d'\in D.
\]  
Equivalently, there exists a decoder \(\bar q\) with \(q = \bar q \circ r\) precisely when \(q\) is constant on the fibres of \(r\).

If two distinct histories \(d_1, d_2\) induce the same observable panel yet differ on authorship or autograph, the fibre criterion fails for \(q\). Compression to the available observations therefore does not preserve the target.

### Exact defect of a moment summary (Theorem 1)

Let \(\mathscr V\) be the linear span of the retained observables and \(f\) the indicator (or expectation) of the target claim. The exact defect is  
\[
d_{\mathscr V}(f) = \min_{g\in\mathscr V}\|f-g\|_\infty = \tfrac12\max_{\substack{\lambda,\lambda'\\m_{\mathscr V}(\lambda)=m_{\mathscr V}(\lambda')}}|\langle\lambda-\lambda',f\rangle|.
\]  
Exact recovery holds if and only if \(f\in\mathscr V\). When \(d_{\mathscr V}(f)>0\) there exist separating laws that agree on every retained moment yet disagree on \(f\). For a binary authorship or original-wording claim the maximal defect is \(\tfrac12\).

### Continuation completeness (Theorem 2)

Define the continuation span \(\mathscr C_h\) by closing the contract observables under the declared action–observation operators \(K_{a,o}\). Exact evaluation of all horizon-\(h\) inquiries is possible if and only if  
\[
\mathscr C_h \subseteq \mathscr V.
\]  
If the inquiry “recover the original authorial act and wording” lies outside the span generated by the presently available observations, then \(\mathscr C_h \not\subseteq \mathscr V\) and the minimal augmentation cost  
\[
a_{\min} = \dim(\mathscr V + \mathscr C_h) - \dim\mathscr V
\]  
is strictly positive. The representation is not continuation-complete for that inquiry.

### Proper-functionalist translation

A design plan successfully aimed at truth about the concrete authorial act and original wording via the historical observables succeeds only when the process has access to a representation that preserves the target (fibre criterion) and retains the required continuation span (continuation completeness). When both fail, the pathway that outputs high firmness on the basis of the available panel alone violates the success condition for that specific claim. The belief may still be true, and warrant may be available through a different module whose success conditions do not depend on resolving this deficit; the historical-observation pathway itself does not supply it.

### Residual assumptions (explicit)

- Finite or declared observable panel; analysis \(A\) fixed and versioned.  

- Target \(q\) is the specific human authorship + original wording claim.  

- Design plan under consideration aims at truth about that claim via the historical observables.  

- Passive historical evidence is not treated as a controlled probe that expands the span.  

- No claim about future discoveries or about modules that do not rely on the observables.


The underdetermination is therefore the failure of the fibre criterion for \(q\) together with a positive exact defect and a positive continuation-augmentation cost — all expressed in the mathematics already present.
