## IV. What an actual stronger source-authority argument must provide

- The actual bearer and scope of responsibility; an unidentified ultimate cause is not automatically the issuer of a specific instruction.
- Independently supported practical interests and target identity before a source directive selects its terms.
- The real source-side alternative performances both when the restrictive arrangement was selected and at the recipient's current decision, not only the menu offered to R or the present sunk costs.
- Recipient access to the instruction and its role; due care may exist without identifiable G, but a usable directive needs more.
- A noncircular explanation of why this issuer is entitled to settle this decision, including whether competent disagreement is displaced and why.
- A test involving real disagreement or an otherwise permissible alternative, to show whether the claimed power changes anything beyond ordinary coordination.
- Current warrant, fallibility/defeater treatment and proportionality at this domain. Unity, necessary existence and essential T0 are not prerequisites for every bounded finding.

No all-world obligation, general source ownership, compulsory acceptance of help, permission to suppress evidence, or actual divine disclosure is established.
