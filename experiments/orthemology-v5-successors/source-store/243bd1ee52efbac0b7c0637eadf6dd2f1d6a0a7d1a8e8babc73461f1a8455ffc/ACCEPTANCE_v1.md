# Independent review: composition and the original productive bearer

3 October 2026. Bounded philosophical acceptance of the assembly result identified in `REVIEW_RECEIPT_v1.json`. No external specialist validation or canonical status change is implied.

## Disposition

**Accept the article and claim ledger as a focused conditional application and a discriminating limitation on one composition-to-source inference.** They do not establish an actual original complete bearer, a necessary participant, or a new local ownership criterion. The result is narrower than either a refutation of composite sources or a vindication of plural original production.

The design-stage assessment preceded examination of the developed article. The developed result was then checked against the actual inherited arguments, the relevant primary-source loci and the final wording changes. The final article resolves the two substantive interpretation risks: confusing an effect-side receiving mode with its source's exercise, and treating independence from outsiders as absolute original possession.

## 1. Strongest affirmative retained

A real organised whole can genuinely possess and exercise a power through differentiated internal resources. Such ownership is not disqualified by internal complexity, real parts, derivative agency or constitutive explanation. A particular subject's integrated response to understood evidence can be its activity even when its powers are received. The article correctly separates this local ownership from original provision of everything exercised in the target's actual origin.

The strongest whole-source proposal is also preserved: the whole's standing power originally supplies the component exercises, with real subordinate subjects or intrinsic aspects implementing that power. That would explain the unity and availability of the exercised resources rather than merely place them within a boundary. A correctly targeted basic or abductive reason could warrant that proposal. Neither an experiment nor the elimination of every describable rival is a prerequisite for rational credit.

The article does not supply a new actual reason with that full content. Its source comparison is not evidence that this correction's entire origin has the proposed constitution. The inherited prima-facie reason for original possession remains intact, while the precise particular-bearer application remains open. This is not a verdict that the affirmative is irrational or that all alternatives have equal support.

## 2. Why the assembly test is sound at its stated scope

The tested inference moves from collectively complete contributions plus a concrete composite to original completeness borne by that composite. Under the retained interpretation, one member's proper unborrowed exercise suffices to expose the problem. Whole-origin completeness places that exercise within the whole's ultimate productive account. Unborrowed-act exclusion then identifies the whole with the member. A proposal retaining their distinctness cannot also retain both original attributions in that sense.

This is a valid application of the inherited condition, not a newly proved ownership principle. It changes the assessment of a particular proposed inference: adding an ontologically real whole does not remove the source-attribution conflict simply because the earlier objection to an unreal aggregate has been answered.

The accepted conclusion requires all of the following:

- The member is a distinct bearer, not merely an intrinsic feature of the whole.
- Its exercise is properly unborrowed at the relevant ultimate productive scope.
- The whole claims ultimate productive provision of that same exercise.
- The same actual target and productive respect remain fixed.
- The inherited ownership/accounting interpretation applies to that constitution theory.

Without these premises, the contradiction is not obtained. This restriction is especially important when the whole and its parts are not independent sources in the relevant sense.

## 3. Receiving modes and the actual target

The article preserves the admitted reason-responsive correction about the displayed Minhāj header. An output coincidence, a whole-edition claim or a fresh empirical finding about consciousness would change the target and is not substituted.

For a source-intrinsic exercise admitted to the whole-origin target, the attribution test applies directly under its interpretation. For an effect-side reception mode, a further prerequisite argument is needed: that reception requires the particular source exercise, and independently complete productive provision cannot leave its indispensable efficacy wholly supplied outside the account. The reception mode and source exercise are not identified numerically. Constitution or logical implication alone does not create the efficient supply relation.

Endpoint dispensability is therefore not an answer about the actual receiving mode. Conversely, including every contributor in a description does not establish original supply of those contributors. The article keeps these two judgments distinct rather than obtaining completion by expanding a narrative.

## 4. The strongest counterargument remains genuinely open

The most important objection is that a whole may possess an exercise constitutively through a member without being another external giver of that exercise. The article treats this as a substantive challenge to the inherited accounting or ownership relation. It does not dismiss it by calling all constitution efficient reception.

This objection has a concrete possible effect on the inference. If original constitutive possession is warranted and differs from ultimate productive supply, the inherited exclusion may not apply. A new argument would then be needed for any uniqueness conclusion. If the proposal instead preserves the inherited supply relation and the member's distinct proper original ownership, the conditional conflict remains. Merely locating the member internally does not decide which interpretation is correct.

The four dispositions in article §5 are consequently appropriate: original whole with derivative members; narrower genuine whole-level efficacy; one bearer with intrinsic aspects; or a defended alternative of joint original ownership. They are interpretive alternatives, not certified metaphysical worlds. The classification leaves complex original sourcehood eligible without awarding it by composition.

## 5. Novelty and warranted credit

The bounded increment is the explicit assembly application with the preserved member-attribution conflict and the disciplined dispositions of that conflict. The inherited theorem, general need for a bearer, permission for descriptive identification, eligibility of basic warrant and distinction between ordinary agency and original sourcehood receive no new credit.

Likewise, genuine local ownership would be a real bounded achievement if newly established. This lane does not establish a new local owner or organisational criterion: the admitted subject already has its local credit, and the prior continuation work already owns relevant organisation/maintenance distinctions. No P1/P2 conclusion follows from repeating those gains.

Global predictive adequacy and lawful integration can motivate a candidate boundary. They do not, by themselves, identify one owner of every exercised power or establish original supply of the actual receiving modes. The article's programme-specific application is appropriately separated from the cited prior argument and response. The review adds no historical synopsis and no quotation.
