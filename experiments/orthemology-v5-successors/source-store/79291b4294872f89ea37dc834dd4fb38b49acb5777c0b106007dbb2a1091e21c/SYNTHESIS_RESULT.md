# Result: warranted continuation needs a jointly applicable pathway

## Disposition

The source-grounded answer is stronger than a list of preserved attributes but narrower than a general identity theorem: **a receiver must retain or justifiably reacquire the task-relevant relations that bind this occurrence's reasons, source force and permissions to this actual successor's available operations, including the relations needed after the current handoff.** Matching outputs, having every local certificate, or retaining every local ambiguity set can leave those relations absent.

No new general mathematical theorem was established in this pass. Exact source recovery showed that H-MOAT-E3 already owns the joint-realiser contract, export-collision obstruction, adequate augmented-export repair, resource-composition failure and finite-versus-maintained-continuation distinction. Sixth owns the copied-root safe-repair and recipient-warrant distinctions. Ninth's exact history transport supplies a valuable comparison map and unchanged-key controls; it does not physically enact that comparison. The research gain is a precise integration of these with original occurrence-sensitive language, fork allocation, realiser input interpretation and successor evidence access. The restricted model is an application/control, not a breakthrough relabel.

## Answer to the original question

A successor can be warranted as a continuation for a declared task when there is adequate evidence for the actual pathway by which its operative organisation, task-relevant reasons and usable interfaces continue. This can survive extensive material replacement. It can also admit more than one successor where the source and task allow branching. That judgement is distinct from numerical or metaphysical identity.

No content-only or behaviour-only test decides the judgement in general. A new process can reproduce the same proposition and behaviour while lacking the original source event, its legitimate permission, a relevant interpretation, or the next required observation route. Conversely, a successor can use new tokens and physical material while retaining the relevant source-grounded organisation and warranted means of acting. There is no universal list of five invariants whose conjunction becomes a proof merely by defining the word continuation that way.

The target contract must be fixed before assessment. A first-handoff contract is weaker than a two-handoff or ongoing-service contract. Quietly dropping future commitments after transfer can make an inadequate successor look adequate. Preserving every incidental detail is unnecessary; preserving what the declared future tasks actually require is necessary. Some lost information may be reacquired, but only through an available, applicable and permitted interface.

## The three composition questions

1. **One compatible episode?** Local records may fit different readings, versions, sole-user scenarios or realisers. A joint record must retain their shared dependencies rather than freely combine favourable local witnesses.
2. **Enough evidence for this recipient's decision?** Several compatible episodes can require different legitimate actions. A nonempty join establishes possibility, not which action the recipient may warrant. Preserving uncertainty may require withholding a conclusion.
3. **An available causal continuation, including what comes next?** A correct decision is not a constructor, actuator, current grant, live reservation, functioning readout or future access path. These require their own actual evidence.

The relation among these questions is the central synthesis. None silently supplies the next one. Their mathematical implementations have established antecedents; the world-directed applicability is not manufactured by a new notation.

## Main discriminating control

The restricted model fixes an authentic grant for abstract repair action 0. A replacement realiser can encode that operation at concrete port 0 or 1. Two allowed preparation histories preserve the same current organisational state/readout, grant/source/version and local ambiguity sets, while opposite source-to-realiser alignments require opposite legitimate ports. A packet-only receiver cannot guarantee a lawful repair. A currently valid source/realiser-bound calibration observation distinguishes the cases; its variable payload is one bit. The observation interprets an existing grant rather than issuing one.

This does not require preserving an arbitrary complete relation or choosing a single meaning from an unresolved ambiguity. It preserves precisely the relation relevant to the operation. The proof is an instance of inherited observation/continuation separation, not a new completeness theorem.

For the next handoff, a second needed alignment may remain available only at the retiring source. A campaign can perform the current repair correctly and preserve its resulting state/readout while destroying that next observation route. Storing the second relevant bit or preserving a genuinely usable authorised query service repairs the bounded case. The distinction is inherited from H-MOAT's state/export/viability account and sharpened here into an occurrence-and-authority application.

## Additional interaction: reacquisition can itself lack authority

The repaired model must not treat the calibration observation as free. Suppose the only legitimate calibration right is held by the predecessor bearer and is not delegated. Copying its complete evidence and service address to a successor makes the source physically reachable, but does not license that successor's query. There are three different cases:

* The source already grants the successor the relevant query right: reacquisition can be an available route if the service and interpretation also survive.
* The predecessor is permitted to acquire and transmit the binding before retirement: the successor can retain that warranted evidence within its allowed scope.
* Neither route is authorised: the successor needs a new permission decision or must withhold the operation. Issuing that new permission is a new authority event, not a theorem saying the old packet preserved it.

This prevents the proposed positive repair from smuggling in the missing entitlement. A lawful probe must exist in the common live interpretation before it can be used to decide between disputed interpretations. If every informative probe itself presupposes a different disputed grant, more copies or a better decoder do not supply a common lawful first step. This is an application of the original lawful-menu/common-policy discipline, not a fresh general game theorem.

## Fork and merge results

**Copied testimony.** New carrier occurrences can preserve one source's testimony but do not create new acquisition events or conditional independence. This is inherited conservation. Separate roots still require independent support for any statistical independence or competence premise.

**Exclusive grant.** Two individually adequate solo successors can each possess an authentic copy of a one-successor grant. Their simultaneous use needs one actual allocation history. Interpretation and calibration do not allocate the entitlement. An offline snapshot cannot reveal a later competing use or revocation unless the relevant state is preserved, communicated, or governed by an applicable preallocation policy.

**Shareable grant.** Where the actual source permits both, two successors may both continue the task. No numerical-identity conclusion is needed. A blanket ban on forks would be wrong.

**Merge.** The merger must retain shared occurrence dependencies and respect the actual allocation and revocation history. Duplicate records can refer to one event; inconsistent local resolutions cannot both be asserted as a single original occurrence. Ordinary provenance algebra and join theory already offer machinery for this bookkeeping. They do not establish that the records correctly describe the world.

**Exact history transport.** The Ninth isomorphism compares consistently transformed complete histories. If two branch maps disagree on the same source command, they cannot simply be combined into one function. A relational duplication/merge operation would need a separately specified semantics and real execution evidence. The current source already shows why untransported commitments, cancellations and receipts do not automatically apply to a new command representation.

## Identity distinctions that the receiver must keep available

* Same proposition: equality of asserted content at the declared semantic level.
* Same occurrence: the particular situated assertion, action, grant or acquisition event.
* Same source: an identified origin or issuer, with the relevant sense fixed; a printed label alone is not this fact.
* Same quoted token: the original object/span being quoted, distinct from both the new quotation occurrence and a merely matching string.
* Same bearer: the entity to which a role, reason or permission applies; substitution may require delegation.
* Behavioural equivalence: equality under a task/input/readout contract, not automatic causal or numerical identity.
* Lawful authority: the applicable right to do this action in the current joint state.
* Causal lineage: the actual derivational/physical history relating predecessor and successor.
* Continuing capacity: the retained or genuinely reacquirable means of meeting the specified future tasks while authorised, with support and timing included.

These are not nine axioms that define a theorem. They identify distinct questions whose conflation produces the demonstrated false inferences. Different tasks make different relations load-bearing.

## Language and source force

The accepted Ninth integrity v3 article supplies an actual document-level control: its public capsule preserves the historical speaker's occurrence/stance separately from the present reconstruction's warrant status. `PUBLIC_OCCURRENCE_ATTRIBUTION_CASE.md` examines that structure without reproducing the primary exposition or using private review narratives. The source claim and modern reconstruction are related, not silently declared identical. This is a concrete application of the proposed binding discipline, with no new general theorem claim.

A translation can preserve a proposition and an unresolved ambiguity while changing the current speech act. An accurately attributed translated quotation of a directive is a report of an earlier directive; it is not automatically a fresh directive addressed to this bearer. A new assertion may reuse the original content, but its warrant depends on the actual testimonial/argument route. An exact copied proof token may preserve mathematical support without authenticating the currently named physical policy or realiser.

The relational controls apply to source scholarship, agentic reason transport and occurrence-centred organisational continuation. They make no claim about a live Fusha/Qamus implementation without a pinned source snapshot. Nor do they infer creed-specific testimonial sufficiency from machine provenance.

## What a defensible positive result presently looks like

Use the existing H-MOAT state-and-output/input/effect contract and keep its domain and finite-sequence scope. Supply the task-relevant occurrence binding, through retained data or a justified observation. Preserve full source/applicability evidence and use an actually authorised, available execution path. Where resources or rights are shared, establish one applicable joint allocation history rather than parallel solo certificates. Validate that the successor retains the observation, permission and support interface needed for the next stated tasks.

This is a constructive application discipline with explicit ways to falsify it, not a universal iff. A successful first handoff plus a certified next-step interface can support the bounded next handoff. Indefinite repeatability still needs maintained assumptions and an appropriate causal strategy; it is not obtained by repeating the words continuation certificate. Legitimate termination remains part of the governing contract.

## Evidence and novelty ceilings

The actual application still needs evidence for source/issuer identity, semantic and speech-act interpretation, legitimacy and scope of permission, physical realiser correspondence, observation accuracy/currentness, actual transfer, common resource state and future support availability. A certificate can report such evidence; its presence is not its own authority.

The source-bound impossibility controls do not decide which successor is numerically the same subject, demonstrate apoautosis, establish an unrestricted Generation facet, identify an ultimate necessary source, or prove a general theory of collective knowledge. Their contribution is to prevent those claims being inferred from copied appearance or separately adequate components and to identify a concrete repair when the missing task-relevant binding really is obtainable.
