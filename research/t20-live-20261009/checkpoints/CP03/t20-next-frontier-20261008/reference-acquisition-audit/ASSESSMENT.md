# Reference acquisition and the T17 to T20 source interfaces

8 October 2026. Bounded source and theorem-interface assessment.

## Finding

No illicit promotion was found in the inspected arguments. The original reports and formal interfaces preserve the difference between an actual particular necessary bearer, identification of the common provider's role, and a recipient's warranted identification of an assertion as that source's own. The Dar recognition passage sharpens the interpretation of these distinctions; it does not expose a missing premise that the inspected T20 work had silently assumed away.

This is a bounded negative finding, not certification of every sentence in T17–T20. It supplies no new independent verification of actual basic Creator recognition, no new defeat of the retained positive appraisal, and no revision of the current common-source or source-owned-veracity appraisals. No Lean compilation or new identification theorem is claimed.

## What the actual witness conclusion says

T17R P46 and P48 explicitly allow an actual particular whole-existence-original witness without a name, unique identification or individually complete provision of every contribution. P53 fixes that same witness before applying contingency-as-need. P54 and P61 withhold stronger uniqueness and common-Creator conclusions. Thus “actual particular” qualifies the object of the existential claim; it does not report a subject's acquaintance with that individual.

The exact formal counterpart is `OriginalBearerBridge.abc_same_witness`. Its conclusion is an existential over target t, resource r and bearer g, retaining `OriginalWitness`, `Necessary`, `UniformRoot` and `EssentialNonreceipt` for that same g. `Necessary` means existence at every supplied ontic world. Neither its signature nor the retained `SourceIdentity.necessary_of_actual_root` contains a human knowledge, acquaintance, naming or recognition predicate. A proof binder called g is not a publicly acquired name. Substituting the same witness through a deduction solves a logical witness problem, not a cognitive acquisition problem.

This does not make the conclusion merely hypothetical in the historical philosophical report. T17 positively appraises its premises and actual-world application. The narrower point is that accepting that appraisal does not silently add a singular-recognition conclusion of a different type.

## What common-source identification adds

`AnchoredSourceBridge.guarded_common_original_provider` gives field coverage and equality of actual original contributors with its fixed g, under the explicit qualification, source-mode, interpretation, anchor, connectedness, visibility and field-CND premises. These predicates express productive roles and identities. They contain no assertion-ownership or recipient-knowledge condition.

T19R P148 supplies the exact join to the earlier attribute bearer: participation of that bearer in this field is needed before field uniqueness identifies it with the common provider. Two unlinked existentials do not identify their witnesses. P155 carries the earlier source-owned veracity appraisal through that same-bearer mapping.

P150 consequently says that a field occurrence need no longer have an unidentified original provider merely because the original witness might be one among several. Read in its P146–150 context, this is warranted source-role unification. It does not say that the recipient has acquired acquaintance, learned a public name, or authenticated a selected message. P156 and P165–169 expressly retain those separate application and epistemic burdens. Extracting P150 alone could invite a stronger reading, but the connected original argument blocks it. No correction of the original is warranted by this check. A future compressed summary should say “identifies the common provider with the earlier qualified witness, conditional on participation” rather than implying acquisition of a recognitional route.

## The distinct epistemic interfaces

The following distinctions survive even when every relevant ontic premise is accepted.

1. There actually is a particular necessary bearer. This is a claim about reality, including the bearer across ontic alternatives.
2. A subject knows that exactly one object meets a role description. This requires a warranted epistemic route to the existential and uniqueness claim. Truth alone does not supply that route.
3. A uniquely satisfied description denotes its actual satisfier under a description-based semantics. That is a legitimate candidate way to acquire reference. One need not know the referent's name or have previously met it.
4. A subject can connect that referent across another presentation, such as a particular appearance, name or alleged utterance. Such cross-identification requires an appropriate link between those presentations.
5. A subject has acquaintance or a stronger recognitional grasp of the individual. This may exceed descriptive reference; whether it is required for a particular kind of singular knowledge is substantive, not settled by defining all reference that way.
6. A subject knows that a contextual token is intentionally asserted or adopted by the same source. Neither acquaintance nor the source's unique productive role alone makes every event its assertion.

The metatheoretic de re form of an ontic conclusion, such as a single g existing in every represented world, must not be confused with an epistemic de re claim such as a subject's knowing of a particular g that it is the source. Nor does a normal epistemic formalism alone model recognition, practical ability to pick someone out, acquisition history or human reasoning resources.

## A useful positive interface without compulsory acquaintance

Let F express the qualified source role, V(x) express the relevant source-owned veracity property, and O(x,t,p) express intentional ownership of this occurrence with this determinate content. The proposition p and the token correspondence are fixed across the epistemic alternatives. In an ideal factive, deductively closed knowledge model, the following suffice:

- The recipient knows that every F is relevantly veracious.
- The recipient knows that some F intentionally owns this very token with content p.

Then the recipient knows p. At every admitted evidence alternative, take a witness to the second statement at that alternative, apply the first statement to that witness, and obtain p. No arbitrary choice across alternatives, acquaintance predicate, public name or rigid independently known g is needed. Uniqueness is not needed for this narrower truth transmission: all eligible candidates may be veracious. If uniquely descriptive identification is also wanted, knowledge of a unique F is an additional useful condition.

This is ordinary quantifier reasoning and epistemic closure, not new mathematics or a proof that these premises are satisfied by a human. The single witness inside the ownership claim matters. Knowing that some F exists and that somebody asserted p does not supply the missing co-reference. Likewise, a description identifying the unique producer of a token does not alone identify an intentional asserter, because production and assertion ownership remain different relations. If the desired conclusion is instead knowledge that this very rigid g owns the token, a known identification of the F-role holder with g across the relevant alternatives is additionally needed. Unique satisfaction in every alternative can otherwise select different witnesses. The weaker truth-transmission result does not depend on resolving that stronger question.

Identical surface wording does not ensure fixed p. If an owner-indexed expression such as “me” picks different referents, the alternatives may express different p(g), and the schema does not deliver one fixed proposition by suppressing that variation. An imperative such as “obey me” also raises force and rightful-authority questions outside assertoric truth transmission. Even a warranted fixed proposition about an obligation does not itself identify a practical action target, establish present applicability or authorise implementation. The inherited occurrence/context controls already own those distinctions.

For actual human warrant, the source evidence, comprehension, competent uptake and inferential route must do the work represented here by the knowledge premises. T17R P122–127 already allows ordinary warranted testimony from an unfamiliar person and distinguishes entitlement from the route to knowledge. The grounded-attestation signatures likewise impose no acquaintance or naming condition; authenticated ownership and veracity are the load-bearing inputs. Thus the present schema is an elementary dependency refinement already implicit in those interfaces, not a newly discovered acquaintance-neutral route. It supplies none of ownership, qualification, uniform veracity or ideal closure for an actual recipient. D2 does not require exhaustive prior certification, a named source or a universal acquaintance theory.

## Diagnostic control and its inheritance

This is a re-entry check, not a newly discovered obstruction. ## What the Dar passage adds

The connected authorial unit distinguishes a general necessary-existent conclusion, even with uniqueness, from recognition of its individual referent. It also distinguishes knowing, awareness that one knows, and knowledge of the acquisition route; a later proof may confirm earlier cognition. The authorial response begins at electronic line 913; preceding attributed material is not merged into his voice. This was checked directly at lines 913–928 of the [electronic witness](https://www.islamicbook.ws/amma/dr-taardh-alaql-walnql-014.html). No print pagination or manuscript collation is newly certified.

Its useful interface here is an acquisition question: what grounds this subject's referential grip, and what grounds the later identification across presentations? It does not require replacing T17's existential witness with acquaintance, and it does not remove description-based or trace-based reference by definition. The restorative examples and the temporal correction about later argument remain sound within their stated antecedents. Whether uniquely descriptive or trace-based first reference meets the passage's stronger target of individual recognition remains an open semantic and epistemological question. That question is not settled by T20's ontic proof signatures.
