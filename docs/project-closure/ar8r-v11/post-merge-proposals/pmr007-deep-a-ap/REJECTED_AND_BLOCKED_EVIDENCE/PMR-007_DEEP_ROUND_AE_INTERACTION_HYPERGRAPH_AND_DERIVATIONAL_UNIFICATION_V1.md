
\[

## 2. IHDU-1A — exact additive block-separability criterion

For a partition

\[
\Pi=\{B_1,\dots,B_k\}
\]

of `V`, the following are equivalent:

```text
1. f(x)=c+sum_j f_j(x_{B_j}) for some block-local functions f_j;
2. every nonzero Möbius coefficient support S is contained in one block B_j.
```

### Proof

If the decomposition exists, expand each block-local function in its unique
multilinear basis.  No monomial can involve variables from two blocks, so every
nonzero support is block-contained.

Conversely, group every nonconstant term of the unique expansion by the block
that contains its support and absorb the constant once.  This yields the
block-local sum. ∎
