## New result

The source continuation identifies a genuine published alternative to one bridge in the argument: a substantive existent might be grounded in autonomous qualitative facts without that explanatory base being the essence of an independently existing particular. This is more than a primitive bearer flag or an undeveloped graph. There is a positive account of why some truths might terminate grounding demands, coupled with an explicit distinction between the nature of a thing and a condition classifying its instances.

It is not yet a warranted explanation of the entire received productive-capacity field. The source does not supply an actual qualitative essence fixing that field, identify all its productive resources, or establish that every concrete bearer is wholly received. Crucially, this alternative is **autonomous-base grounding**, not a defence of an endlessly received grounding chain. Combining it with the all-received productive rival is a further philosophical proposal whose compatibility and actual warrant must be shown.

## 2. The new primary argument, at its actual strength

Dasgupta distinguishes brute facts from facts unfit for grounding, motivating autonomy through the analogy between real definitions and unprovable-by-design nominal definitions. Essential autonomy remains a working hypothesis. His PSR requires autonomous grounds, not endless grounding. In §9, an essentially existing particular follows only with an added existence-grounding premise. Note 44 permits qualitative predicate-functor grounds instead. Section 13 distinguishes existence-entailing essences from mere classificatory conditions; note 55 says its conditional chair example falls short of the required existence-grounding essence. The paper defends taking rationalism seriously, without proving it true. [D, especially §§2–5, 9, 13, 16–17; nn.44,55]

The following assessment is our application analysis. It should not be read as additional doctrine attributed to the source.

### A substantive stopping account

The strongest autonomy proposal is not: “L is a law, so stop asking questions.” It differentiates kinds of questions. A causal why-question may be inappropriate to arithmetic without arithmetic thereby being a causally brute physical occurrence. Likewise, a real-definition claim might be of a kind to which a grounding why-question does not apply. If that classification is justified, ending there need not amount to abandoning an otherwise appropriate explanatory demand.

This is a positive reason to investigate the category, rather than to require an agent behind every explanation by definition. But there are two distinct epistemic obligations:

1. Establish that a category capable of playing the autonomous role is intelligible and possibly instantiated.
2. Establish which actual facts occupy that category and that they fully explain the particular target.

The first obligation is not the second. Examples illustrating a kind of ground do not reveal a complete ground of actual productive capacity. Conversely, a failure to identify the actual ground in this inquiry is not evidence that the category cannot exist.

The warrant standard must be symmetrical. The rival need not provide an ontology-neutral demonstration merely to receive serious consideration; the affirmative original-source judgement does not possess such a demonstration either. What matters is the strength of the respective positive reasons and their fit to the actual target. The analogy motivates a real explanatory distinction, and answers the objection that every stopping point is simply brute. It does not settle the further dispute over whether real definitions have the proposed objective autonomy or whether a particular existentially potent essence is actual. Conversely, the accepted source ontology cannot be inserted as an uncontested premise against the very alternative now being tested.

### Why the qualitative option matters

There are three different propositions in play:

- Something substantive is actual.
- Its actuality has an adequate autonomous explanation.
- That autonomous explanation includes the existence or existence-grounding essence of an individual.

The first two do not reach the third merely through the word “explanation.” If an adequate qualitative account can support truths about individuals at a derivative level, ordinary existential discourse may remain true while its ultimate explanation is not another individual. That alternative directly contests the need for the additional individual-existence bridge. It does not erase the observed target or simply relabel it necessary.

The alternative does not require identifying a mental universal with a productive person, nor claiming that an abstract pattern is an efficient cause. Its claim is an ontological/explanatory one about what actually makes the derivative individual facts true. A reply confined to showing that our abstract descriptions are mentally entertained therefore does not yet reach it.

At the same time, a merely syntactic elimination of individual variables proves no ontological conclusion. A successful qualitative proposal needs both adequate expressive content and a supported account of why that content describes the fundamental explanatory structure. Otherwise the notational change may simply conceal the existential work previously done by individuals.

## 3. Comparison with the actual T20 interface

The definitions and theorems in the inherited `OriginalBearerBridge.lean` were directly reread. They do not claim a theorem from arbitrary facts to bearers. Their resource type is interpreted as concrete intrinsic instantiation in the same existential respect. This declared scope prevents a false report of a newly discovered formal gap.

**Occurrence.** A qualitative-first proposal can accept that the observed receiving target exists. Its metaphysical explanation may be revisionary while ordinary existential claims remain true. The source does not itself give the complete receiving-target interpretation needed here.

**Completion.** T20 Completion requires an actual unreceived resource relevant to each received target. An autonomous grounding explanation is not automatically such a resource: it might be nonobjectual, and its explanatory relevance need not be productive contribution. The source's general PSR therefore does not instantiate Completion without additional interpretation. It also does not establish the negation of Completion for actuality.

**Realisation.** For genuinely concrete intrinsic productive resources, the requirement of an actual bearer is unchanged. The qualitative option can allow derived resources and derived bearers to satisfy that restricted requirement. If an autonomous qualitative fact is added to the resource domain in order to obtain an original witness, however, the obligation to realize that fact as intrinsic in a bearer becomes newly substantive. One cannot widen the resource type to capture the rival's base and then apply the original restricted realization principle without argument.

**SupportTransport.** The inherited condition transports whole-existence reception of a bearer to its intrinsic resource. Qualitative ground by itself says nothing equivalent to this receipt relation. A proposal that accepts the restricted resource/bearer relation need not reject this transport principle, while one that identifies every ground with whole-existence receipt owes a justification for that identification. This source does not settle it.

**ReceiptRepresentation, ContingencyNeed, and ConstitutiveReception.** None is supplied merely by the term “autonomous.” In particular, grounding necessity is not identical to essential nonreceipt. Nor does a necessary existential claim identify one rigid particular that is necessary in its own existential mode. The source's distinctions between relative necessity, general necessity and essence make those promotions especially inappropriate.

**Productive attribution and unity.** No pure-perfection profile, actual efficient participation, exhaustive common-provider coverage, or uniqueness of that provider is derived here. The source furnishes a rival explanation category, not the full metaphysical inventory required by those conclusions.

Thus the qualitative option is a challenge to the warrant for extending the resource/bearer ontology to all adequate existential explanation. It is not a countermodel preserving every premise of the typed bearer theorem. The theorem remains valid at its declared scope.

## 4. What a synthesis with Billon would require

A charitable combined rival could say:

1. An actual autonomous qualitative essence underwrites laws with genuine existential force.
2. The laws fix a complete dependent history in the relevant law-admissible domain.
3. Every concrete capacity and bearer appearing within that history is locally received.
4. The autonomous qualitative explanation is not an additional concrete producer whose own productive stock is unreceived.

This is a stronger and more informative hypothesis than a nonempty-solution axiom. But it is a **proposed synthesis**, not a position established jointly by the two papers. A full instance requires a specified essence and law set, an argument for their truth and autonomy, a coherent account of local productive receipt, and a check that the qualitative explanation does not already instantiate the relevant original-provision commitment under another description.

The distinction between grounding and productive receipt makes the synthesis logically discussable: a causal or productive history could be beginningless while its explanatory ground is autonomous. But mere difference of relations does not demonstrate that this exact combination is metaphysically possible or actually realized.

The inherited one-original beginningless control must still be admitted at its proper scope. Its original G can be an adequate actual provider even if the detailed history is not fixed by the weak token recurrence alone. A combined rival that claims to improve global history explanation is therefore answering a further question; it does not remove G just by providing a more determinate history description.

No modal-collapse inference is exported without its premises. If necessary laws determine a unique entire history across all metaphysical alternatives, necessity of that represented history follows only at that scope and under the adopted identity/representation conditions. If only a near-world domain, fixed contingent laws, or a qualitative structure is held constant, the conclusion is correspondingly restricted. Neither route automatically gives a de re necessary original bearer.

This also limits a specific possible objection from our operational counterfactuals. Being able to vary inputs in an explanatory or identification test is not already evidence of a metaphysically possible alternative exercise. A necessitarian may reason about logical, epistemic, relative or counterpossible alternatives. Conversely, making such reasoning intelligible does not certify those alternatives as metaphysically admissible. A uniquely necessitated temporal history could still contain genuinely successive acts; modal fixity does not turn it into one eternally unchanging token. These distinctions affect only attempted transport from counterfactual tests to metaphysical source conclusions.

## 5. Changed assessment and remaining operation

The full reading supports one important positive change: the autonomy/qualitative-base rival now has a primary philosophical motivation and an exact premise at which it resists an individual-existence conclusion. It can no longer fairly be represented as merely a globally coherent all-received graph or a bare law label.

This is reason to withdraw or reduce confidence in an overbroad informal inference from adequate explanation alone to an essentially existing individual, or in the claim that every law-only rival merely declines to explain. The inherited typed theorem already discloses the extra resource/bearer premises and therefore requires no correction on that score. The new evidence increases the pressure to justify their exhaustive application; it does not make their rejection true by default.

It does not by itself warrant reversing the adopted actual-world judgement. No actual autonomous qualitative essence with complete productive-capacity coverage has been identified. But that absence does not prove zero effect on confidence: a critic can undercut a supposedly exhaustive explanation category without constructing an entire alternative cosmos. The source's qualification that it argues against dismissal rather than establishing the PSR's truth matters. So does the difference between showing that an existence-entailing essence is intelligible and showing that a particular such essence is actual.

The actual historical appraisal was checked rather than assumed. T17's retained “Actual supply and original existential completion” and “The actual witness” sections already distinguish broad autonomous noncausal originality from the additional productive/bearer ontology. T18's “The foundational result and the closed extension” expressly withholds an exhaustive bridge from every objective existential fact to the requisite bearer. The independent ground-extension review preserves that limit. The current `warrant-standard-review/REPORT.md` accepts common-source judgement under the adopted F1/perfection background, and expressly says defects in that background can undercut the comparison.

Accordingly the warranted directional revision is differentiated:

- **Conditional theorem:** no reduced confidence. Its realisation and support premises are explicit, and no counterexample preserving them has appeared.
- **Exhaustive ontological applicability:** reduced confidence in an unqualified application of productive-bearer ontology to every adequate existential explanation. The new source gives a positive reason to take the qualitative alternative seriously, rather than only noting that its exclusion was unproved.
- **Actual Necessary Being/common-source judgement:** downward pressure at that upstream ontology-to-actuality step, without a forced reversal or automatic parity. The provisional affirmative judgement under the adopted ontology survives this packet; its unqualified actual-world assurance should be weakened to reflect the strengthened alternative. The downstream comparison among already eligible original agents is not independently defeated, but it cannot repair the upstream premise by its own conditional force.

This is not a retrospective correction of T17 or T18 into a claim they never made, and it assigns no numerical confidence. It is a new, bounded comparative appraisal responsive to a newly articulated positive rival, not preservation of a favoured conclusion as an overriding premise.

The source-consequential next question is now narrower: **must every adequate extramental qualitative explanation of actual productive resources itself be realized by individual subject/property reality, and must that explanation inherit the relevant whole-existence dependence?** An affirmative answer needs its own ontological argument; a negative answer needs an actual qualitative explanatory account. The parallel Arabic-source return is examining exactly this extension. Its answer should not be anticipated here.
