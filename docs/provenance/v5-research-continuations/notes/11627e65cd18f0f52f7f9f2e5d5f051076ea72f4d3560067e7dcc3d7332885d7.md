# Objective norm-fixing and warranted common repair

Sixth v5 tranche • avenue 3 • candidate research • 2 October 2026 UTC

## Result first

Objective correction has a defensible positive starting point that is weaker than a complete theory of objective obligation: a determinate representation or inference can answer to an actual object or truth-preserving relation independently of the evaluator's present endorsement. The most promising account fixes the **object, respect, bearer and achievement first**, then asks which standard correctly articulates that relation. It does not manufacture correctness by choosing a score. Correctness, proper function, epistemic warrant and an obligation to act are nevertheless different relations, requiring different further facts.

This yields a constructive answer to the common-repair question. Several independently warranted standards can admit a common repair when they concern correctly identified, transportable respects and their certified local corrections preserve each other's achievements and the real obligations. Commuting idempotent repairs provide one checkable sufficient condition. The semantic certificates, rather than the algebra, explain why the result counts as repair. A second useful case is an apparent conflict caused by giving two norms the wrong common bearer: preserving an original document and correcting an assertion can be jointly satisfied by a faithful source layer and a distinct corrected-claim layer. A real conflict on the same bearer cannot be dissolved that way.

An intrinsic impersonal source challenger, distinct from the canonical R5 architecture control, can preserve objective truth, target-relative defect and genuine cognitive achievement in derivative subjects. Those facts alone do not entail that the ultimate bearer understands the target. A purposive intellectual source gives a positively intelligible integration of apprehension, fitting end, means and production, but its superiority requires independent arguments for original cognitive adequacy and reasons-responsive Wisdom. The present norm-fixing argument neither supplies those by definition nor reduces all norms to instrumental success.

## 2. Five questions that a single word, “correct,” can hide

### 2.1 Truth

Does the proposition say what is the case? A proposition's being believed, authorized, useful or produced by a properly operating mechanism does not by itself make it true. Nor must a truth be someone's obligation to utter. Mathematical validity and inferential validity are related but different: a true conclusion can be reached by an invalid inference.

### 2.2 Constitutive correctness

Does a token achieve the standard belonging to the activity or representational kind it actually instantiates? A belief that p is truth-correct exactly when p; a deductive inference is truth-preserving when its premises cannot all be true with its conclusion false. Calling an inaccurate belief “imagination” may change the kind of act, but does not vindicate the original belief. Similarly, choosing a different task does not repair the original task while preserving its identity.

Constitutive correctness need not be an all-things-considered demand to perform the activity, attain all its possible successes or preserve it at every cost. Rosen's account, discussed by Shah at pp. 458–459, separates correctness from value, permission and obligation. Shah does not accept Rosen's further universal denial of deliberative force: at p. 472 he argues that belief's truth-standard does guide doxastic deliberation. Shah also distinguishes evidence's rational authority from its exclusive causal influence. His defended conceptual/prescriptive norm addresses deliberate belief formation, not a universal disposition which would make irrational belief impossible; he does not claim a complete meta-normative interpretation [S2].

### 2.3 Instrumental success

Does the process obtain a specified end, given the real causal circumstances? This can be objective as a means–end relation even when the end is chosen or bad. A deliberately misleading indicator can serve its maker's deceptive purpose perfectly. Its success at deception is not measurement accuracy, epistemic warrant or moral justification. Declared utility and hard constraints can formalize this relation; they cannot supply the missing worth or authority of the end.

### 2.4 Epistemic warrant

What makes this subject entitled or nonaccidentally positioned to accept this conclusion, and how strong is that standing? A lucky true answer need not be knowledge. Warrant can differ between subjects with the same true answer, between histories with the same present performance, and between a source witness and someone who merely copies its certificate. In Plantinga's account the relevant faculties, suitable environment, truth-directed design plan and successful truth aim all matter; the publisher's primary abstract confirms that this is an account of warrant rather than the claim that correctness alone demonstrates a Designer [S3].

Here “warranted standard” must be typed. It may mean an evaluator has undefeated but fallible grounds for accepting it; that its content is actually fitting; or that it has legitimate authority in a practical domain. Those meanings cannot silently replace one another.

### 2.5 Moral or otherwise binding practical obligation

Must an agent do this, for reasons not exhausted by an optional goal or institutional role? Neither factual accuracy nor successful functioning settles this. A correct map can aid a wrongful journey; a well-functioning deceptive device can serve a bad aim. Enoch's own summary of the shmagency challenge argues that an activity's constitutive features do not automatically provide an independent reason to engage in it [S5]. The present account accepts that challenge as an open bridge for categorical practical authority, while resisting the stronger and unwarranted conclusion that there is therefore no correct answer about the map.

## 3. Positive account: target-first, respect-specific objective fittingness

### 3.1 The minimal affirmative argument

Consider an actual correction of a numerical transcription. A source page contains “17”; a later report attributes “71” to that page. The source's marks, the relevant numeral practice, the attribution's reference and the report's intended claim jointly make the attribution inaccurate. A correction to “17” is better in that respect because it answers to the same source claim. It is not better because the editor prefers 17, receives a reward for 17, or has the power to alter the archive.

The argument has four explicit premises:

- **N1: determinate target.** There is a particular source, proposition, object, inference or functioning bearer under discussion. Its identity is not reselected after inspecting which answer wins.
- **N2: real relation.** There is an obtaining satisfaction, truth-preservation, fidelity or causal-functional relation between that target and the token evaluated. This relation is not constituted solely by the current evaluator's verdict.
- **N3: kind and respect.** The token purports to do something for which that relation supplies the relevant success condition. The report is an attribution to this page, rather than a free fictional numeral.
- **N4: target-preserving change.** The transition removes the particular mismatch while retaining the target, respect and unaffected obligations. It does not simply reinterpret the old output, suppress the counterevidence or exchange the goal.

Given N1–N4, this is objective correction in a local but substantive sense. The example uses a retained fidelity standard; it does not independently establish Candidate E’s stronger existence premise of radical correction relative to an entire token authority basis. Reversing the editor's preference would not reverse which report faithfully represents the marks. The claim outruns present endorsement but does not assert that conventions have no role in making numerals refer, that all reality is mind-independent, or that one final authority has been derived.

The explanation is not just “the standard is correct because it is correct.” The truthmaker for the attribution can be inspected; its causal and referential connection can be traced; the semantic alternatives can be distinguished by further source evidence. The standard is answerable to those facts. The point is to give an explanatory task to the target relation, rather than treating a label on a loss function as its own justification.

### 3.2 What this account does and does not fix

This account distinguishes **constituting facts**, **evidence for those facts**, and **authorized decision procedures**. Source marks and a real attribution relation help constitute fidelity. A scan or independent transcription supplies evidence. A review policy determines who may amend a published claim. None of the three can be substituted for either other.

For a belief about a nonlinguistic object, the account still needs a successful reference/content story. Naming a target or adding a “world-grounded” field does not provide it. Causal contact, discriminative competence, inferential use and historical reference may jointly constrain the interpretation. The exact relative contribution is a live philosophy-of-content question. Deep AY rightly prevents a formally rigid data structure from resolving it merely by fixing its labels.

The positive account is locally realist about the obtaining target relation. Its achieved objectivity is independence from the present evaluator's verdict in the specified respect, not a completed account of every constituting fact's ultimate ground. It does not require a separately existing universal called Correctness. It is compatible with differing ontologies of the relation's ultimate ground. This is a strength at the common-repair stage: repair can be justified without first settling every metaphysical disagreement about truth.

### 3.3 Why this is not merely an optional truth preference

A person may prefer an inaccurate report, refuse to inquire or pursue a different end. None of those changes makes the report accurate. The truth or fidelity question is not settled by the value of asking it. This supplies an objective correctness relation even if the skeptic rejects an obligation to care about it.

For a subject engaged in sincere doxastic deliberation, evidence about p bears directly on whether to believe p. Seeking a bonus for believing p does not thereby provide evidence that p. The local norm's connection to the kind of cognitive act is therefore tighter than the connection between an arbitrary externally assigned utility and a machine output. That provides a serious constitutive account of epistemic correctness, without claiming it settles all reasons for inquiry or explains cognition's ultimate origin.

### 3.4 How an actual correction warrants this account

A **thick** premise, “this was an actually successful objective correction,” already includes the existence of a correctness relation. It cannot then serve as an independent deduction of the same existence. A **thin** trace, “the system changed output and a score increased,” does not include it and cannot entail it.

A defensible middle is a defeasible case: identify the same target before and after; obtain evidence not generated solely by the disputed rule; show the original mismatch; intervene in its diagnosed cause; retain the target on fresh cases; and test serious alternative explanations such as relabeling, compensation, data leakage and reward hacking. These considerations can make an objective-correction interpretation better warranted than a mere-score interpretation. They remain dependent on some basic perceptual, inferential and testimonial capacities. A finite audit cannot supply an entirely faculty-free certification of all those faculties.

That dependence is not automatically vicious. Using perception to read a source is not the same as allowing a disputed transcription rule to certify every error it makes. Nor is using valid inference to expose a particular invalid form a derivation of all logical authority from that token. What is rejected is complete self-authorization of the disputed basis, not every use of cognition when evaluating cognition. The ultimate epistemic starting points remain philosophically assessable.

## 4. Proper function needs additional fixing facts

The target-first account explains an accurate report more readily than it explains why a device or faculty is **supposed** to perform a certain operation. A broken pressure gauge and a decorative dial can share an output while having different functions. Conversely, a broken gauge can retain its measuring function even when it never succeeds.

Three serious positive accounts must be kept live:

1. **Historical selection.** A reproduced structure's effect can help explain why structures of that lineage exist. A malfunctioning token can therefore retain a proper function absent from its present disposition. Millikan explicitly treats historical function as a theoretical account and uses accidental duplicates to distinguish history from current structure [S1]. The “success is only survival” objection cannot dismiss this account without addressing its producer–consumer and content explanations; it is not a proposal that every current beneficial outcome is a function.
2. **Organizational contribution.** A differentiated self-maintaining organization can fix roles by the mutual dependence of maintaining constraints and their production. Mossio, Saborido and Moreno offer this as a nonarbitrary account of biological teleology and normativity, distinguish function from usefulness, and address malfunction [S4]. Its hardest task here is extending organism-relative viability to truth-directed cognitive assessment, especially where useful misrepresentation and true but costly cognition separate.
3. **Purposive design.** An actual intention can fix an artifact's function independently of the artifact's present behavior. But intention fixes a design function even when its end is morally defective. “Legitimate designer specification” in the canonical matrix must therefore be unpacked: creator-intent, authority to direct this use, and goodness of the end are different claims. We should not bake legitimate authority into the definition and then announce that design establishes it.

A cognitive proper-function claim further needs to specify what is ordered to truth or sound resolution, which environments and defeaters matter, and why the process type is the relevant one. More observed reliability under a selectively chosen reference class cannot settle these questions. An independently supported function history or constitutive cognitive-kind account can restrict the class without making the evaluator infallible.

A fiṭrah-oriented account is a fourth, source-sensitive avenue, not a relabeling of the first three. It needs authenticated textual warrant, correct attribution, interpretation of the disposition and its impairments, and an applicability argument for the actual person and case. Disagreement does not diagnose corruption. Plantingian proper function remains a modern comparison unless a primary source itself warrants a specific claimed equivalence.

## 5. Several genuinely warranted standards: clarify the quantifiers first

Three cases should not share one unqualified “plural standards” label:

- **Epistemic alternatives:** each standard is a live, reasonably supported candidate; only one may be fully correct. Repair should be robust across the unresolved evidence class, or more evidence should be obtained. Deep CQ and FRLA supply relevant conditional controls.
- **Jointly valid partial standards:** each captures a different genuine respect of the same case, such as accurate attribution, preservation of the original evidence and authorized publication. These obligations must be composed, rather than interpreted as rival guesses at one target.
- **Defeasible practical considerations:** each provides a real reason, but neither is yet an absolute all-things-considered requirement. Conflict calls for a warranted priority or balancing account. Requiring every pro-tanto reason to be maximized can manufacture an inconsistency none of the standards asserts.

If two standards truly require incompatible outcomes of the same bearer, in the same respect and circumstances, with no defeasibility or scope difference, calling both “warranted” does not produce a repair. At least the assumed joint completeness, applicability, satisfiability or authority must be reconsidered. Mere confidence of both parties is not joint truth.

## 6. A semantic common-repair criterion

### 6.1 Semantic certificate before set intersection

For each standard i, supply:

- the actual bearer and target respect it governs;
- evidence for its fittingness or legitimate authority, including outstanding defeaters;
- an interpretation from operative representations to that target, with the scope of approximation;
- a defect predicate D_i that tracks the diagnosed mismatch rather than a desired label;
- a repair operation r_i with a causal/semantic account of why it removes that defect;
- the obligations K that must remain invariant, plus action authority and executability;
- the evidence and recipient conditions under which the repair's warrant transports.

These items are a **proof obligation**, not a magical data schema. A fabricated certificate passes no semantic burden. On the other hand, disagreement about an ultimate metaphysical ground does not invalidate a certificate supported by shared actual reference and independently warranted local evidence.

Let K be the domain of states satisfying the unaffected real constraints, not merely a convenient software predicate. Let S_i within K contain those states actually satisfactory in respect i. The representation-to-world map must make the chosen computational r_i correspond to the intervention whose effect is asserted. Preservation may involve source custody, others' interests or continuing intervention access; it is not exhausted by preserving visible output values.

### 6.2 Constructive sufficient result: commuting semantic retractions

Suppose there are finitely many repairs r_1,…,r_n: K→K such that:

1. **Semantic adequacy:** every fixed point of r_i is genuinely satisfactory in respect i: Fix(r_i) ⊆ S_i. The link is independently defended.
2. **Idempotence:** r_i(r_i(x)) = r_i(x) throughout the relevant reachable domain.
3. **Noninterference by commutation:** r_i(r_j(x)) = r_j(r_i(x)) for every pair throughout that domain.
4. **Lawful realization:** every step is executable and authorized, and the certificate's evidence/recipient bindings survive the sequence.

Then R = r_n∘…∘r_1 maps every x in K to a state in every S_i while preserving K. To prove this, commute r_i through the composition until it meets its own occurrence, collapse the duplicate by idempotence, and obtain r_i(Rx)=Rx. Semantic adequacy gives Rx∈S_i. Since each r_i maps K into K, composition preserves the unaffected constraints. If x failed respect i, the endpoint removes that defect rather than merely improving an arbitrary aggregate score.

This is an elementary retraction argument, with no general mathematical novelty claim. It is informative because it derives joint feasibility from separately testable local behavior rather than defining common repair as a nonempty intersection. The substantive achievement is identifying real defect-removing retractions and explaining why their algebra transports to the world. In particular, an idempotent operation “replace all evidence by APPROVED” has no semantic adequacy certificate.

Commutation is sufficient, not necessary. A weaker sequential condition is that each chosen repair preserves all previously achieved satisfactory predicates. In applications this may be easier to warrant. If one operation repairs a fault only under a precondition later destroyed by another, neither condition holds. Common repair can still exist but needs a different plan.

### 6.3 Uncertainty qualification

The four conditions must hold throughout the **evidence-compatible actual cases** to justify an evidence-based policy, or further investigation is required. Checking them only in the evaluator's favorite world silently uses a hidden chooser. The elementary fact that each world has a repair does not give one evidentially available policy across those worlds. FRLA already establishes this class of local-view obstruction. The present contribution is to require that semantic reference, applicability and action warrant, not merely observations, enter the case partition.

Probabilistic confidence may justify a risk-bounded intervention under a separately warranted risk rule. It is not a proof of universal common repair. An “abstain and gather evidence” action may be lawful and prudent without completing the repair. Its availability and preservation costs must also be checked.

### 6.4 Corruption-tolerant semantic coverage, without complete diagnosis

A concrete realization supplied by the parallel restoration lane strengthens the criterion's relevance. Suppose R is an independently justified, idempotent repair for the actual target. Each of n source-labelled intervention roots realizes either R or the identity operation; at most f roots can be the inert alternative. The fail-silent restriction is substantive: a bad root cannot undo a completed repair or create another defect. Execute any f+1 distinct roots. At least one realizes R, and every composition containing R and otherwise only R or identity equals R. It is unnecessary to identify the corrupt roots, receive trusted success receipts or solve the entire hidden structure.

This is a **coverage variant**, not a careless application of the previous fixed-point condition: the identity operation has every state as a fixed point and therefore does not itself certify repair. Joint coverage supplies the missing successful action. Unknown identity/R actions commute and preserve the target once reached. The separate restoration lane owns the root-count proof, adverse active-corruption comparison and exact controls. Here the import is semantic: objective target warrant can suffice for successful repair even when complete causal diagnosis is absent, if interventions are independently known to be harmless and the coverage bound is warranted.

The fail-silent premise is not free infrastructure. If one common trusted gate enforces the restriction, that gate is part of every actual intervention support. Admitting its corruption can collapse the support cut to one; omitting it from the fault budget is an explicit trust assumption, not root independence. The positive result therefore requires a justified omission-only fault model or independently warranted containment with its full support declared.

If a corrupt root can produce an absorbing harmful state, information sufficient to identify the target need not identify a safe intervention. No change in the objective target's status cures that missing action-integrity evidence. Thus knowing which state is correct and knowing how one is warranted to reach it are different achievements.

### 6.5 Three limitations the criterion exposes

Pairwise overlap of satisfactory sets need not yield a joint state: {a,b}, {b,c}, {a,c} have pairwise intersections but empty triple intersection. Shared agreement that each pair could compromise therefore does not prove all standards can be met.

A common endpoint need not have a lawful reachable route. An operation may erase the only admissible evidence or exceed the actor's authority en route. Endpoint scoring cannot certify the path.

A one-time common repair need not remain repaired. Stability under disturbances requires the preserved viability and dynamics arguments, such as the retained CQ conditions, after semantic interpretation is earned. Commuting finite corrections alone do not establish lasting restoration.

## 7. Worked disputes and repairs

### 7.1 Accurate transcription and original-source custody

A research packet contains a retained source image with “17,” a transcription with “71,” and a claim citing the transcription. One reviewer requires the original evidence to remain intact; another requires the attributed numeral to be corrected. These are jointly warranted different respects.

If both are mistakenly encoded as instructions on one undifferentiated byte string, preservation and amendment appear contradictory. The repair is a typed artifact: retain the immutable source bytes and their identity; amend the derived transcription; record the error and the source-grounded justification; update downstream claims and links. The source layer remains exactly what was inspected, and the assertion layer becomes accurate. No change in original evidence is passed off as historical fidelity.

Let x=(o,t,c,a), where o is the source, t the transcription, c the attributed claim and a its provenance annotation (separate fields or an extensional set of correction facts). Fix o throughout. The operation r_t corrects t and its source anchor; r_c corrects c against the fixed source and records its own anchor. When both consult o directly and use independent fields, including disjoint annotation fields or order-insensitive correction-fact sets, they commute and are idempotent. An append-only chronological log need not commute byte-for-byte; the claim is then at the justified semantic quotient which forgets irrelevant log order, with custody retained separately. If r_c instead merely copies whatever t currently says, the naive operations need not commute: claim-first may preserve the old error. Repair the dependency order or make the source-based certificates explicit. This is a concrete test of the theorem's premise, not an automatic application.

If a user instead genuinely demands that the **same published string** remain byte-identical and have a changed numeral, there is no solution under that literal scope. Separate-layer accommodation is valid only if it preserves what the actual obligations meant, not if it replaces them with convenient new obligations.

### 7.2 Equivalent units, a real calibration defect and a fake label repair

Suppose a stipulated reference object has temperature 20°C, equivalently 68°F. Two standards require correct readings in those units. Their numeric targets differ but their physical target does not. The conversion f=9c/5+32 transports the target relation; “make the numbers identical” is a spurious consensus standard.

A Celsius display at 21 and a Fahrenheit display at 69.8 may share a +1°C calibration defect. Recalibrating the common measurement so it corresponds to the same independently established reference repairs both. Merely subtracting one from both displayed numbers fails: Fahrenheit remains wrong. Relabeling “21” as “the correct temperature” does not repair either relation. The real warrant consists in reference identity, the unit conversion and a justified intervention on the calibration mechanism. The numbers are an explicit illustrative stipulation, not a report of an experiment.

If the available evidence cannot distinguish +1 from −1 bias, each actual case has a correction but opposite adjustments are required. An independently calibrated comparison can resolve it; duplication of the same faulty sensor cannot. This is a concrete application of the retained indistinguishability obstruction, not a new information theorem.

### 7.3 True answer, invalid inference and correct epistemic repair

Premises “all D are M” and “a is M” do not establish “a is D.” Suppose independent observation happens to make the conclusion true. The output is truth-correct but the inference is invalid. Awarding a point for the true answer misses the defect.

A common repair under truth and inferential standards can retain the true conclusion **with the independent observation as its reason**, and withdraw the claim that it followed from the two original premises. If no independent evidence is available, a warranted repair may suspend the conclusion while stating that the premises leave it open. Suspension is not a claim that the conclusion is false. This separates world truth, pathway adequacy and subject-relative warrant.

A third practical standard, “never revise a public claim,” does not acquire authority because it makes review easy. If it is instead a genuine evidentiary retention rule, preserve the old version with a correction notice. If it is a binding prohibition on amendment, its authority and scope must be addressed rather than ignored by an accuracy optimizer.

### 7.4 Historical function and current use

A mechanism selected to detect a recurrent environmental signal is repurposed as a toy that rewards false alarms. Successful toy performance does not erase its historical selected effect; restoring its historical detector behavior need not improve the toy designer's present objective. The dispute is not settled by the word “proper.” It asks whether the current task concerns historical biological function, actual present design function, accurate representation or a justified practical purpose. More trials under the toy reward cannot decide which question should govern.

The common repair, where appropriate, is to make the roles explicit and use distinct modes whose claims and outputs are not confused. If the one device must simultaneously emit opposite signals in the same context and channel, no role annotation by itself makes that physically possible. This is a limit of accommodation, not evidence that function is wholly subjective.

### 7.5 Source interpretation without coerced convergence

Two readers agree that a primary author distinguishes standing Knowledge from particular acts but disagree whether a given passage establishes essential Knowledge or only rebuts an objection. A lawful common repair can retain the exact text, identify the speaker and dialectical role, state the weaker claim both accept, and mark the stronger inference as disputed with its actual premises. Such a repair improves attribution and inferential clarity without declaring one reader cognitively impaired.

It does not settle the metaphysical truth by compromise. Some disagreements remain precisely represented. A repair of the shared evidential basis can be common even when final beliefs continue to differ; conflating these outcomes would turn “restoration” into compelled assent.

## 8. Intrinsic impersonal source challenge and the distinct R5 control

### 8.1 Give the rival what objective correction actually requires

The canonical `AR8R-R5-MINIMAL-NONINTEGRATION` control is an architecture comparison: separate tracks retain their results through typed interfaces unless a warranted bridge yields an additional world-directed consequence. It is not itself a doctrine about one impersonal being. A successful account of local correction does not defeat it. The historical integrated R5 finite model, a distinct bridge challenger, carries a primitive target authority and structural roles on g, but explicitly disclaims proper-function or teleology and metaphysical possibility. The stronger philosophical rival U should be allowed:

- intrinsic concrete efficacy rather than an uninstantiated formula;
- genuine derivative agents with determinate reference and true or false belief;
- objective target-relative correctness, real malfunctions and learning from error;
- a nonarbitrary history, organization or intrinsic teleology fixing creature functions;
- an intrinsic relation between its productive nature and those capacities;
- no source-level apprehension or intentional selection.

This proposal need not define correctness as endorsement. It can affirm that a creature's belief misrepresents the actual object despite every creature's approval. The correctness relation is between content and reality, not between the source's beliefs and reality. Saying “a norm must be understood somewhere” is already satisfied by derivative subjects in cases of conscious correction. Saying it must be understood by the **ultimate ground** is an additional bridge.

The proposal must still explain, rather than rename, content, truth-directed function and cognition. But a personal-source account is under the same obligation: uttering “Knowledge and Wisdom” does not explain how the intended target, means and particular cognitive capacity are connected. Explanatory comparisons must charge both accounts for their actual commitments.

### 8.2 What the intellectual account positively contributes

The intelligible personal architecture is this: the source apprehends the object and the relevant target; understands why an end is fitting; understands how a faculty and environment can place the creature in a truth-sensitive relation to that target; and produces that arrangement for the understood fitting reason. The end is not a label attached after success. It guides the specification through actual comprehension and purpose.

This unifies **knowing what**, **understanding why fitting**, **selecting means for that reason**, and **effective institution**. It is a genuine candidate explanation, not merely a list of attributes. The sixth Knowledge work's original-giver argument attempts to establish source-level cognitive actuality; a Wisdom argument must additionally establish proper end and reasons-responsive selection. These are precisely the explanatory resources missing from a merely efficient source description.

Nevertheless, missing from a description is not disproved in reality. An impersonal source with an intrinsic, irreducible norm–power relation contests the claim that those relations require source-level uptake. To defeat it, the personal account needs either a defended impossibility of noncognitive intrinsic fittingness or a comparative argument showing that its positive norm–power story fails while the intellectual story genuinely explains it. No theorem about eligible actions, constraints or utility decides that issue.

### 8.3 Three different ways an intellectual source could fix norms

An intellectual-source account must choose or carefully combine three explanations:

1. **Apprehension:** the source knows an already obtaining truth or fitting relation. This explains perfect epistemic access but, without more, does not explain what constitutes that relation.
2. **Constitution through intellect or intention:** the source's intelligible intending makes an artifact or creature for an end. This can explain a designed function. It does not by itself show that the end is morally good or that every creature is obligated to obey every possible intention.
3. **Grounding in the source's intrinsic nature:** truth, eligible excellence or wise willing is rooted in what the source essentially is. This need not posit an externally coercive standard. It still needs an account of which features are normatively authoritative and how created facts and functions depend on them.

A single account can use all three for different respects: know truths about effects, institute a faculty's design function, and ground the goodness of willing in intrinsic Wisdom. It must not move between them at the point an objection arises. In particular, perfect knowledge of a norm is not equivalent to creating its authority, and being ultimate is not by itself a normative explanation.

### 8.4 Knowledge does not itself yield Wisdom or binding authority

Even complete knowledge of facts and causal means leaves the question of why a particular end is pursued. To obtain Wisdom, add fittingness of the end and the agent's selecting because of that fittingness. To obtain a creature's binding duty to comply, add the relevant authority or independently binding reason. To obtain trustworthy revelation, add actual communicative intention, truthfulness and authenticated disclosure. None is just a synonym for source cognition.

One possible source-sensitive position locates goodness and wise willing in the source's own nature. This avoids both arbitrary choice unrelated to wisdom and an externally coercive superior. It still owes an account of why the nature is a genuinely fitting normative basis rather than merely a necessarily instantiated preference. That is an objection to be answered by the perfection and Wisdom arguments, not evaded by definitional fiat.

No pure-act premise, denial of all intrinsic attributes, or inference from every distinction to externally assembled composition is used here. Real standing attributes and fittingly timed particular acts remain compatible with the intended Athari framework.

## 9. Primary Athari source discipline and the remaining normative bridge

The initial primary locus supplied by the attribute work is Ibn Taymiyya's *al-Nubuwwāt*, hosted chapter 9 [A1]. It argues from purposive arrangement to knowledge and will, links informed choice to preference, treats wrongdoing through ignorance and appetite, and connects wisdom to the reliability of prophetic signs. This is substantial authorial argument for the integration sought here. It is not by itself an independently proved theorem that knowledge entails morally fitting choice. Its key philosophical pressure point is whether every knowingly defective choice must be explained by the specified forms of deficiency, rather than by an irreducibly malevolent orientation.

Accordingly, use the passage as a positive premise-defense and source-attribution resource. Do not flatten the author's multi-stage argument into “all knowing is wise.” Do not treat every purpose as a good purpose or every creative act as a communicated command. The fresh Arabic reading at hosted chapter 9, web lines 105–106, confirms the connected argument in the author's voice. Some historical physiological illustrations there are not used as present empirical evidence. Hosted chapter numbering is not printed-volume pagination.

The source-sensitive route can strengthen the target-first account by grounding creature ends and faculties in an actually wise creative intention. Its applicability to a particular norm needs the intention's content, the relevant source's identity, and warranted transmission or rational access. Authentication of an Arabic passage establishes what it says; it does not alone establish its author's metaphysical premises, the truth of a revealed claim, or its application to the present subject.

## 10. Smallest remaining bridges and stopping boundaries

| Bridge | What is needed | What does not supply it |
|---|---|---|
| Token output → actual content | Determinate reference and cognitive-kind facts | Rigid labels or successful score |
| Accurate content → epistemic warrant | Appropriate truth-linked route, subject conditions and defeater treatment | Truth alone |
| Performance → proper function | Account-specific history, organization, design or source facts | Reliability alone |
| Constitutive correctness → categorical practical authority | A reason/authority account for the applicable domain | Defining an activity by a standard |
| Local standards → common repair | Semantic scope, noninterfering operations, lawful path and transportable warrant | Pairwise agreement or endpoint intersection alone |
| Corrective episode → intrinsic ultimate norm | A grounding/constitution argument beyond the episode's token | The fact it was scored as improvement |
| Intrinsic norm → source Knowledge | Original cognitive adequacy or another independently defended intellectual-host bridge | Objective truth and structural responsiveness |
| Source Knowledge → Wisdom | Fitting ends and selection because fitting, with defect exclusions defended | Information plus efficacy |
| Wise creator → this obligation/disclosure | Scope, authority, content and authentication bridges | Generic creatorhood or a source label |

The positive local result is already useful: objective correction can be anchored in what the episode is about, and common repairs can be justified across serious disagreements without pretending that all ultimate ontology is settled. The more ambitious source route remains connected and substantive. Its smallest unresolved issue is not a missing optimization theorem; it is the metaphysical adequacy of impersonal truth-directed nature against a rational, reasons-responsive source.
