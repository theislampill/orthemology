# Exact controls and scope

The author script passes 16,598 assertions in 29 families, using Python Fraction arithmetic only. Its evidence is in results/exact_controls.json and results/exact_controls.log.

Controls include:

- Exact second-order coefficient algebra for 256 n,m pairs, using only the derived sum-of-joint-success expansion rather than derivatives of unknown copulas.
- 2,187 per-route probability bounds and 729 exact product-to-sum remainder inequalities.
- 9,914 exhaustive pointwise valid-Bernoulli-table candidates checking the two-product rigidity conclusion, plus ten unique-solution checks.
- A genuine polynomial copula perturbation with a symbolic coefficient-norm certificate that its density is at least 1/2 everywhere; finite density checks are additional diagnostics, not the global justification.
- All sixteen threshold-grid cell masses and full sixteen-command held-threshold endpoint transcript laws for n=1,...,4, preserved despite a certified off-grid copula difference.
- The high-command n=1 versus m=2 alias, including its entire three-bit held-threshold replay law.
- Exact radical bounds showing the frozen midpoint Joe alternative separates under the new paired replay statistic, while fresh redraws and same-command replay retain the corresponding ambiguities.
- Valid baseline paired endpoint distributions across 245 count/rate combinations.

The script does not establish the universal theorem by enumeration, differentiate any arbitrary copula, claim empirical sampling, or certify a physical replay operation. Its finite-grid global-factorization countermodel does not claim universal finite-count impossibility. The all-command proof and continuous positive-density construction are written independently in RESULT.md.
