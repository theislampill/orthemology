## Interface decisions

1. Distinct source S, concrete occurrence E and actual contribution/respect C types. Req and EntireOrig are arbitrary relations. EntireOrig is nonexclusive; there is no owner index hidden in C.
2. Separate Field, ActualOccurrence, actually operative contribution, represented participation, actual original contribution and norm-governed ModeRole. Field admissibility, requisite soundness/exhaustiveness, use soundness, role applicability and contributor visibility are independently stated obligations. The structural proof can use only some of these; interpretation still requires all of them.
3. Qualified and ModeDefect remain primitive independently interpreted predicates. Classification says a qualified source's incomplete actual role is defective. Conformance excludes that defect in its qualified actual exercise. Their conjunction yields actual complete coverage by an explicit classical contradiction step. Neither primitive is defined using coverage.
4. A finite path is restricted to admitted occurrences and uses shared actual contribution/respect witnesses. Such witnesses can be retained historical requisites or supplied by genuine mixed occurrences. A path is not a directed causal relation. No reverse causation is inferred.
5. The anchor is an actual original contribution by fixed qualified g. Role applicability gives its norm-governed exercise. Coverage propagates without CND. CND excludes represented rivals; visibility additionally excludes actual rivals. Complete-provider existence and uniqueness have separate declarations.
6. Prove the scoped equivalence of actual mode-satisfaction and global coverage, under the fixed anchor/connected setup. Also expose the represented-participation SourceMode variant and its extra anchor-visibility dependence. Do not infer philosophical warrant from either equivalence.
7. This is a contribution-incidence route, not a proof of Sixth LocalCover for an independent strict ancestry relation. Reused idea: induction across a finite connected path. Old Sixth source ownership stays unchanged; no old theorem is rebranded.
