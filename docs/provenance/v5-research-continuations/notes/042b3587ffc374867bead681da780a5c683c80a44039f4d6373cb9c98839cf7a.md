The attainment and measurability arguments are valid. The product of M finite probability simplexes is compact. For each C,m the corruption mass is linear in the codebook; finite minima and maxima preserve continuity. The minimum defining F is consequently attained. Enumerating finitely many C and selecting the first maximizer is Borel measurable: each selected region is described by finitely many strict or weak comparisons of continuous payoff functions. Composing with the declared measurable codebook map gives an admissible strategy even for a nonfinite seed space.

In schedule 3, the full public transcript is fixed before C is selected, and no later sender response or observation can refine the receiver's conditional action law. That law is a single known probability row. Its b largest coordinates sum to at least b/u; choosing their roots realizes that loss. A uniform row attains b/u against every C. Randomizing the earlier message cannot evade a bound that holds separately for every realized transcript. Hidden correlated sender/receiver resources, advance access to the later draw, or constraints excluding some b-sets would change this game.
