## What this adds

One set of finite constants simultaneously instantiates the reviewed A/B/C,
anchored-field, and veracity interfaces. This is stronger consistency evidence
than three unrelated positive fixtures: the bearer/source sort is literally the
same type, and the same constructor g occurs everywhere.

- Three worlds: g and h exist everywhere; received x and four effect bearers
  exist in two worlds and are absent in the third.
- Canonical target x has exactly the original resource rg and intrinsic bearer
  g as its original witness. The existing A theorem is applied at x, its returned
  witnesses are identified, and the inherited B/C declarations use that g.
- A nonempty field a–k–b contains real mixed production, received x's actual
  productive agency, all its incoming finite support branches and a new mixed
  contribution. g completely provides it. h genuinely provides an outside
  effect, with no hidden incoming contribution to the selected field.
- g owns a true assertion and knows that another content is false without
  asserting it. x owns a knowingly false assertion productively supported by
  g. Its ownership and shortcoming do not transfer to g.
- The fixed tables satisfy fourteen explicit cross-interface compatibility
  checks. Positive reachability is proved equal to the separately enumerated
  support-ancestor relation, using an inductive reachability definition.

There is no new general all-in-one metaphysical theorem. The new declarations
are small certificates about this concrete interpretation, plus direct calls to
the unchanged component results. The model does not derive qualification,
source-mode classification, conformance, knowledge or assertion coverage from
A/B/C. Their finite primitive interpretations are explicit independent inputs.

## Nonvacuity and its exact limit

The model has live existence, received contingent creatures, instantiated
received resources, actual support transport, cross-world reception, nontrivial
productive paths, genuinely mixed incidence, nonspeech source exercises and
true/false owned speech. It contains two distinct necessary original bearers.

The complete good-g package necessarily excludes an incomplete qualified
g-role, false g-owned assertion and g-owned counterfeit. This interpretation
also gives g no shortcoming. Those excluded antecedents are not manufactured
merely to advertise every implication as triggered. The component removal
controls carry their premise-discrimination evidence; x's real counterfeit,
shortcoming and unfitting exercise give D/N live interpretation without a source
identity shift. This packet does not repeat the component minimality campaign.

## Scope retained

A successful replay establishes relative consistency/nonvacuity of the selected
formal signature in the ordinary Lean foundations. It does not establish
metaphysical possibility, actual existence, a complete source-profile model,
correctness of the finite contribution decomposition of reality, full
omniscience, numerical uniqueness of all necessary reality, or revelation.

The optional strict-ancestry theorem verifies this fixed support relation is
irreflexive/transitive, its chosen domain predecessor-closed, and g a complete
local cover there. The same relation has positive-length direct-support paths.
This is only a model-specific compatibility certificate. The general
incidence-to-Sixth refinement remains open; no historical Sixth theorem is
relabelled or newly replayed.

The existential-reception relation and direct productive support are distinct.
For example g has existential dep to m but no direct-support edge to m; x has
the direct-support edge to m but no existential dep edge to m in this chosen
representation. A named theorem checks both distinctions.
