# A restricted explanation route and the actual-source question

Research candidate v3, 3 October 2026. No canonical changes, publication, outreach, or external specialist validation. This continues the same Orthemology v5 programme.

## Finding first

The strongest new result is a scope constraint on an actual-explanation route: the explanation relation promised by a transfer principle must be the same relation ruled out by its anti-regress step. Excluding only a complete explanation does not eliminate an ordinary or partial explanation that the principle might deliver. Our first draft crossed those scopes; this revision corrects it.

A second programme-specific result concerns infinite inventories. Replacing finite qualitative multiplicities by infinite cardinalities loses the guarantee that a witness exhausts all actual instances. Restricting to every finite subfamily does not restore a uniform original cause. Relational occurrence labels do not automatically preserve the origin-neutral possibility warrant. These are precise inference limits; they certify no infinitist cosmos.

The resulting investigation leaves an affirmative route worth pursuing, with its decisive actual-warrant questions located earlier than Ninth's fixed-bearer modal ascent. It has not established P1/P2, N2/N3, essential integrity or an R5 defeat. The exemption-symmetry test below also asks which real explanatory difference permits a preferred source's contingent choice while excluding a rival's active configuration, without pretending that the two proposals are automatically equivalent.

## 1. Exact inherited target and novelty boundary

The retained episode corrects a false claim that the displayed Minhaj header did not identify editor or edition. The correction concerns what the displayed witness says. It does not establish the truth of all bibliographic assertions on the host or collate a printed edition. Under the admitted cognitive interpretation, the subject revises because the contrary evidence is understood; the record is not a demonstration of consciousness in every mechanism involved.

Sixth already gives the target-first account of objective fittingness and distinguishes a thick successful-correction premise from a thin improved-score trace. Seventh already gives the real, reason-responsive cognitive anchor, permits active received powers, and develops genuine infinitary and holistic rivals. Eighth and accepted Ninth separate actual whole-origin completion P1, particular bearer P2, nonbrute explanation N2, unconditional intrinsic existential sufficiency N3, and essential integrity from actual disclosure and recipient warrant.

The following probes are applications of those results, **not new bridges**:

- The false report and correction can share an ultimate productive origin while differing in correctness. Common origin alone is not a discriminator of truth or endorsement.
- The same displayed target can help constitute the mismatch and, through actual understanding, explain its correction. Local coordination is not automatically an unexplained coincidence among ultimate resources.
- A direct presentation of source necessity would require stronger content than recognition of local dependence. Merely repeating that condition supplies no new such presentation.
- A necessary bearer may have contingent exercises. This distinction was already retained, including beginningless activity and non-well-founded subordinate ancestry.

The new investigation below tests an actual-explanation route against this exact programme. Its contribution is diagnostic and comparative. No new necessary-being theorem is claimed as established about reality.

## 3. Why this is a genuinely affirmative possibility

The explanatory question can start before an original bearer has been found. There is an important difference between saying that the admitted correction has some real productive explanation and saying that the entire relevant actual contingent field has one. A general explanation principle could make that transition rather than simply assuming the sought source.

Let E be the exact explanation relation that the transfer in the capsule promises, and let E* be a stronger whole-origin relation. The anti-regress premise must exclude *every E-explanation* consisting solely of the in-scope contingent members. A premise excluding only E* does not do that. This repairs the first draft's numbered reconstruction, whose transfer delivered an unspecified explanation while its fifth step excluded only a complete one.

There are two legitimate repairs. First, defend collective exclusion for E itself. Second, strengthen both the possible-explanation premise and its transfer rule to E*, and independently justify that stronger rule. The second is not licensed simply by the primary paper's restricted rule. Either repair must also establish actual concrete productive participation for the same E; a rule that permits noncausal realisation cannot be silently read as promising an efficient giver.

This is a useful logical discipline rather than a rebuttal by stipulation. A special existential target may be such that any genuine E-explanation already requires the requisite nonpresupposing scope. If so, that is the argument to defend. It cannot be inferred just from using the adjective global. Conversely, a strong E* can be worth defending even if ordinary scientific explanations are weaker. Its additional strength must remain visible.

The proposed restriction does not itself warrant every application. A possible explanation and an actual explanation remain different claims. Neither the meaningfulness of a question nor a successful local answer makes every possible answerable instance actually answered. The admitted correction vindicates its particular evidence-sensitive explanation; it does not supply a faculty-independent certification of a general modal transfer. A defender may offer basic rational warrant for that transfer. That warrant must be assessed at its actual content and scope, rather than dismissed because it is basic or accepted merely because it is called basic.

## 4. The joint-target test

The possible additional-source strategy must preserve the proposition to which the transfer is applied. It cannot preserve only a familiar endpoint while replacing the actual individuals or the complete receiving mode relevant to a later conclusion.

There are three distinct targets here.

**The local episode.** Its concrete subject, displayed object, cognitive content and evidence-to-revision relation are fixed. Explaining this gives genuine local credit. It does not by itself cover every actual productive prerequisite of its whole origin.

<!-- SOURCE_PRUSS_QUALITATIVE_START -->
**A qualitative existential inventory.** This deliberately abstracts from some particular origins.
<!-- SOURCE_PRUSS_QUALITATIVE_END --> Such abstraction can be a legitimate way to seek a necessary participant. It need not be accused of changing its own target if that target is declared from the outset. But the conclusion is correspondingly about the actual explanation of that inventory. It is not already a whole-history explanation of this particular correction as this warranted cognitive achievement.

**The full actual-origin target.** This includes all relevant productive modes and exercises whose actuality the P1 source must cover. Moving to that target requires explaining why the coarser inventory's explanation covers those modes as well. An account of how many instances of an intrinsic kind there are can leave individual origination and relational history unexplained. For example, a constraint on a fixed finite domain may explain its cardinality while presupposing the existential availability of its witnesses. This is a generic distinction, not a population-at-a-time counterexample to a timeless inventory.

For exact qualitative counts, adding possible explainers raises a further concrete question: do the added beings occupy new intrinsic types, or alter counts already fixed by the inventory? Finite multiplicity within each type does not show that a suitable new type is possible, that all the proposed creators can coexist, or that their effects jointly realise the whole inventory. Independent local production examples do not establish those collective modal claims. No assertion that the required enlargement is impossible follows; its warrant remains to be supplied.

This is a more specific discriminator than asking for another g-less scenario. A positive advance would exhibit and defend **joint explainability of the declared inventory**, with the origins and extra-source types tracked, rather than merely exhibit the separate explainability of each familiar item. An actual conditional model does not by itself establish that metaphysical possibility.

### 4.1 What finite multiplicity buys, and what it does not

Let T be all actual instances of one in-scope complete intrinsic description. If T has exactly n members, n finite, any subset S of T with n members is T itself. The proof is ordinary finite counting: a proper subset has fewer members. Thus an account that really produces n such witnesses cannot leave another actual instance of that same type outside its witness set.

There is a crucial qualification. Explaining an exact finite count is not automatically producing n witnesses. A fixed-domain cardinality constraint can account for the number while presupposing the individuals. This is only a generic count/origination distinction; the target here remains timeless. The finite result supplies **witness exhaustion**, not the stronger existence-origination bridge. Those two steps must not be conflated.

For an infinite cardinal, exhaustion fails. A countable set indexed by the integers and its nonpositive-indexed part have equal cardinality, although the latter is a proper subset. Consequently, a cardinality-based explanation cannot be assumed to account for every actual instance merely because it supplies a family of the stated size. A proposed account may obtain an infinite lower bound from one subfamily and an upper bound separately. To exclude that account or upgrade it to all-instance origination requires additional explanatory premises. This is a loss of an inference used in the finite case, not a proof of any actual infinitely duplicated field.

Replacing the quantitative target with *every finite subfamily* also changes the result. Consider a strict active-chain interpretation c_(n+1) → c_n, for n a nonnegative integer. Every finite set of members has a further member upstream of all of them. No member is upstream of every member, and none is unreceived. This preserves the distinction between actual local explanations and an explanation of the whole order; it is not a metaphysical-possibility certificate. The inference from a source for each finite target to one actual source for all targets is invalid without a further compactness or coverage principle. Compactness of a model construction would not itself establish that its additional witness belongs to the actual source domain.

Occurrence indexing has three different readings. Rigid names restore individuation but reopen the original origin-sensitive modal question. General relational descriptions can fix order or provenance without being intrinsic descriptions; their joint possible production needs a new argument. Genuine distinguishing intrinsic properties would support a finer qualitative partition, but attaching labels in our representation does not show that the actual beings have those properties. None of the three is a cost-free repair.

A stronger conditional repair replaces cardinality with an independently warranted **exhaustive qualitative presentation**: every actual target-preserving realisation inside the declared domain includes the entire domain. This is more than rigidity, which only rules out nontrivial automorphisms. It must also rule out proper target-preserving realisations. Such a condition can hold for an infinite structure. If a genuine distinguished member c_0 is supplied, every member is an iterate of a real successor operation f, and eligible substructures must contain c_0 and be closed under f, then every eligible substructure contains every f^n(c_0), hence the whole declared domain.

This example does not assume a first productive source. The distinguished c_0 could be the downstream target, with f(c_n)=c_(n+1) selecting its successive upstream suppliers; that productive ancestry has no first supplier. What the condition fixes is descriptive coverage, not causal foundation. Nor does a selected successor relation automatically include every actual productive contributor. Adding the distinguishing and relational facts changes the origin-neutral modal-witness obligations, and their truth and full target scope require independent warrant. Even a genuine exhaustive presentation leaves the explanation-to-existential-production and joint-possibility questions open. It is the strongest structural repair identified here, with no claim of actual applicability to the whole field of the admitted correction.

The scope conclusion is exact. A beginningless history, infinitely many different intrinsic kinds, or infinitely many acts of a finite number of substances is compatible with finite multiplicity in each substance-kind. The user's allowance for infinite activity therefore does not refute the premise. It also does not warrant that premise for every allowed ontology. An account admitting infinitely many intrinsically indiscernible contingent substances, or counting infinitely repeated act-tokens as beings, needs a separate applicability check. No actually warranted repair here has been shown to retain both the source's origin-neutral possibility warrant and the same exhaustive existential conclusion across that broader domain.

## 5. Collective explanation and the active infinitist

The serious infinitist has real, actually productive members. Every receiver genuinely possesses the efficacy it exercises, although that possession is received. No first powerless relay is awaiting activation. Both sides must retain the correction's real cognition, objective target relation and actual reason-sensitive production.

A demonstration that some conjunctions need more explanation than the explanations of their conjuncts defeats **automatic agglomeration**. It does not establish **universal failure of agglomeration**, still less establish that no genuinely non-well-founded active account can explain the relevant collective target. The logical form of this distinction does not rely on certifying the latter proposal as metaphysically possible.

The strongest source reply asks for original possession. In the rootless proposal, every contributor still receives the very efficacy under explanation. Naming all its actual contributors may therefore leave the full supply question undischarged. A root with intrinsic efficacy gives a positive answer: it has the relevant productive reality without receiving that possession from another. This is not an objection from temporal firstness or a requirement that all subordinate ancestry terminate.

The exact conflict is whether an active, non-well-founded explanatory pattern can itself be collectively sufficient without originally possessed efficacy. The restricted transfer does not independently decide that issue. If such a pattern is an adequate explanation, a general principle guaranteeing explanation may be satisfied by it. If it is not, the affirmative needs the substantive original-possession reason for excluding it. Putting the transfer in front of that reason does not increase the reason's force.

This leaves a genuine positive research direction: identify a feature of *productive reception itself* which prevents collective sufficiency in the stipulated active case, and warrant that feature without defining complete explanation as already rooted. A mere absence of a named root, or a finite first-source removal example, does not supply it. Equally, assigning actuality to every node is not a positive explanation of the whole pattern's actual efficacy.

## 6. A necessary bearer with contingent willing

This route is not committed to identifying the source's existence with its complete actual profile. A statement that the source exists can be necessary while a statement that it exercises a particular power is contingent. The actual explanation of an effect can include that contingent exercise. Necessary existence of the bearer does not make the entire explanans necessary. This is inherited from the Seventh analytical supplements, “Factivity and modal force” and “Bearer and act,” not a new-source paraphrase. [B4]

The inherited distinction between necessary bearer and contingent exercise remains in force. It does not show that a contingent exercise is inexplicable; nor does the word free establish categorical inexplicability. The scope exception in the source capsule therefore needs its own application warrant.

If categorical inexplicability is invoked, the exemption must be applied symmetrically. A rival may ask whether its active total configuration also has a categorically unexplainable realization while really explaining local effects. This request cannot be accepted merely by redescribing the target as inexplicable. It also cannot be refused merely because the preferred source's choice is allowed to remain inexplicable.

The discriminator is **explanatory surplus**. What does the offered basis add beyond the target's obtaining? In the strongest personal account, an actual bearer apprehends the target, adopts a fitting end, selects relevant means and produces the outcome for that reason. In a serious active-order account, actual powers, receptive relations and local cognitive processes must be specified; a symbol for whatever realises the outcome is not enough. Neither account earns global actual warrant by offering its preferred descriptions alone.

The source advocate has a further positive distinction available: the bearer's independent efficacy can be prior to its contingent choice, whereas the infinitist's whole pattern leaves every efficacy received. That is a reason-bearing difference. Establishing that this difference makes collective explanation possible in the first case and impossible in the second requires the original-possession argument just identified. The restricted principle cannot be used as a substitute for it.

A stronger requirement that the source's **necessary intrinsic nature alone** necessitate every actual choice would collapse those choices into necessity. This stronger requirement is not part of the route under test. Conversely, weakening explanation to allow contingent exercises cannot be used without notice in a different argument that needs exhaustive, existence-entailing intrinsic sufficiency. Different explanatory targets can warrant different relations; the relation must remain fixed at each inference.

Beginningless sequences are not excluded here. A necessary bearer can supply the standing efficacy for an unbounded sequence of contingent exercises. Showing that each exercise has an actual sufficient determinant is not showing that the whole actual sequence is itself necessary. A proof about the latter requires the further modal status of the determinants or of the complete sequence. No finite chain diagram settles that question.

## 7. What even a successful result would and would not establish

**A necessary participant is a real advance, if warranted.** The programme should not require complete common-bearer unity before crediting such a result. Original avenue 1 explicitly places necessary concrete existence before the later unity test. A de re existential witness is stronger than a necessarily occupied role.

**It is not P1 by itself.** An actual joint explanation could involve several necessary providers, none independently covering every actual exercise and receiving mode. A necessary contributor is not a complete original giver. The inherited uniqueness theorem applies to independently complete candidates; it does not merge joint contributors.

**It need not identify the inherited g.** Even if a particular complete actual source g is separately granted, a necessary participant h could be downstream of it in the actual account. To infer g's necessary existence from h's necessity requires an appropriately essential dependence on that very g, not only actual dependence. This returns a precise identity-sensitive transport question; it does not turn the new route into an illicit role/occupant inference.

**It is not N2 or N3 by itself.** Necessary existence does not automatically disclose an intrinsic nonbrute account of that existence. <!-- SOURCE_PRUSS_N2_START -->

**It does not settle original Knowledge, Wisdom or authority.** Those arguments require the source's adequacy in the relevant cognitive/practical respect and its actual identity as the bearer to which the attributes are ascribed. Explaining a qualitative population containing knowers is not itself being their single, intrinsically knowing original giver. A productive explanation of truth-directed function is not yet rightful practical governance or authenticated attestation.

**The actual local norm retains its credit.** The correction is better in the specified respect because it answers to the displayed target. Neither success nor failure of this new source route changes that local warrant. Nor does the local norm supply the missing global transfer by itself.

## 8. Comparison of the four requested routes

Direct particular recognition could in principle bypass an inferential totality construction. Its positive content would have to concern the same actual reality's relevant original, intrinsic and modal status. No new such presentation was found in this probe.

Causal indispensability identifies real contributors. Restricted collective explanation is the strongest new route inspected for raising that local credit toward an actual necessary participant. It still needs jointly warranted modal scope and collective productive adequacy. Those are more specific research targets than an undifferentiated demand for full PSR.

Norm/function fixing explains why the correction answers to this target and, with further function facts, why a capacity has its proper end. It does not identify an original bearer merely because the norm is objective. Its relevance to the ascent is to preserve the cognitive and normative explanandum against a merely behavioural replacement.

Comprehensive practical perfection could exclude a source-level counterfeit assurance. The accepted integrity article states its actual nondomination and complete-profile requirements. Neither an existential inventory nor the restricted transfer supplies that practical character. Conversely, the source's allowing or producing a recipient's error is not itself a source lie.

## 9. Disposition and next genuinely discriminating work

The inquiry found a materially different affirmative route, not an actual P1/P2 or N2/N3 closure. The main novelty is a scope diagnosis connecting possible-explanation transfer, collective active reception, source identity and contingent willing. The basic cosmological argument and its familiar objections are prior art, not discoveries of this tranche.

The next useful evidence or argument would do one of the following:

1. Warrant a jointly possible explanation of the declared full existential target while explicitly preserving the relevant origin/identity content and accommodating the extra explainers.
2. Defend a collective original-possession law against the genuinely active infinitist by identifying an actual reason why its productive contribution cannot be jointly sufficient.
3. Establish that the explanation promised by the transfer has the stronger whole-origin scope needed by P1, rather than only ordinary or qualitative existential explanation.
4. Give a principled explanation-kind distinction under which contingent willing is permitted, and show why the rival's proposed ultimate realization fails that distinction without simply restating the preferred conclusion.

Another local correct-output witness, another finite rootless diagram, another general source quotation, or a larger profile enumeration would not answer those questions. Until one is answered, source actuality, intrinsic necessity and normative governance must retain their separately warranted scope. R5 is not defeated.
