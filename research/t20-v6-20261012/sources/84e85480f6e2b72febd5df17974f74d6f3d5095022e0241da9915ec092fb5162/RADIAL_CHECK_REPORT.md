# Independent narrow check of the radial bridge

10 October 2026 UTC. A separate checker was given the proposed inequalities and assumptions, before the full draft was written. It checked only radial growth, positive-time representation, and boundary representability. It did not independently review the complete evolution/uniqueness theorem. The parent's later proof reading was participating review and is not represented as independent acceptance.

The checker found no counterexample or missing hypothesis beyond the explicitly stated linearity, d≥1, and T>0. Its mathematical conclusions were:

- Both parity classes of a_n²≤a_(n−1)a_(n+1) follow from square-functional Cauchy–Schwarz; the even case can use the positive bilinear form B(f,g)=A_t(Rfg).
- A vanishing interior radial moment forces a_1=0 and then all positive radial moments zero. This contradicts a_1′=2d a_0>0 at a nonnegative interior minimum.
- The Riccati coth envelope is correct. The map r↦r coth(rt) is increasing, so M=max(2,√(2d)) yields the stated dimension-uniform-in-n bound.
- Marginal Carleman divergence genuinely supplies a representing vector measure without initially assuming one.
- The zero-boundary weak-limit argument requires uniform integrability of every polynomial, supplied by continuity of higher radial moments. Weak convergence alone does not recover those moments.
- Boundary representability alone does not imply initial determinacy, trajectory uniqueness, or the measure-valued evolution equation. Those are separate proof obligations, addressed by Sections6–9 of the author's draft and requiring their own review.
