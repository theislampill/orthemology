# Fixed-cue acquisition, historical dependence, and failed transports

## 1. Declared physical interpretation

This is an original conditional diagnostic inspired by the inspected later primary argument, not an animal experiment or a reconstruction of the inaccessible 1993 chapter.

An encounter has two separately variable binary attributes: X is target appearance; N is noxiousness. During a presented taste trial the sensor C=X and discomfort signal P=N. The sensor’s target stays **appearance X**, never noxiousness N. W0=0. Learning obeys

    W(t+1) = W(t) OR (C(t) AND P(t)).

After training, avoidance output is

    E = Wfinal AND Ctest, with Ctest = Xtest.

The environment, sensor mapping, update law, target, and interpretation remain fixed across histories. Training exposures are supplied independently of the later response policy: no assumption is made that an animal forced to avoid would nevertheless ingest the next presented object. This is a controlled formal taste-trial protocol, not a physiological claim. The Boolean weight, persistent memory, signaling, discomfort, and learning organization are stipulated physical resources. Their evolutionary origin and consciousness are not explained.

The model therefore does not acquire its success by interpreting identical marks differently. No semantic-label argument is read by the implementation. Physical histories and explicitly named physical control mechanisms differ; semantic meanings do not.

## 2. Two conditional results

For every finite history H, induction on its length gives

    F(H) = Wfinal = OR over t in H of (X(t) AND N(t)).

Base case: the empty disjunction equals W0=0. Inductive step: appending a trial takes the previous disjunction OR its conjunction, which is the new disjunction. Hence E=F(H) AND Xtest.

Take H+=[(1,1),(0,0)] and H−=[(1,0),(0,1)]. Both have exactly one target appearance and exactly one discomfort episode. Nevertheless F(H+)=1 and F(H−)=0. With Xtest=1, E differs. Thus **current physical sufficiency of Wfinal and Ctest is compatible with causal dependence on the prior pairing of cue and outcome** in the stipulated structural causal model.

More generally, E=G(F(H),C) alone does not prove historical dependence: F or the relevant part of G may be constant. Dependence requires witnesses h1,h0,c with G(F(h1),c)≠G(F(h0),c). H+,H−,1 supply those witnesses here.

Jointly intervening to fix Wfinal=w and Ctest=c leaves E=w AND c independent of H. This mediator-clamp result is different from conditioning on observed W. Deterministic histories may supply no observational overlap at a fixed W; if an extended model also lets history affect Ctest, fixing W alone would not block every pathway. No arbitrary statistical-conditioning conclusion is claimed.

## 3. Discriminating controls

**Sensitization:** replace the update by Wnext=W OR P. It acquires target avoidance even after a non-target painful encounter (X,N)=(0,1). It also gives weight1 after both H+ and H−. This is why mere pain/no-pain comparison was insufficient. An independent pre-construction reviewer caught the initial proposal P=X·N, which made C·P=P on lawful trajectories; that proposal was rejected before coding.

**Disconnected learning:** W=0 regardless of H. The same target sensor and test-response equation remain, but the history fails to install any response role. This rules out treating an available indicator as automatically an effective learner.

**Preset:** W=1 regardless of H. After H+, the acquired and preset mechanisms have the same modeled test-time state and the same response for every test input. Under the alternative acquisition history H−, only the acquired mechanism changes. Present test output therefore does not identify acquisition provenance. This does not assert complete present physical duplication: the model equates only the state and transition function used during testing, not every physical feature of a retained learning mechanism.

**Harmless lookalike:** after H+, (Xtest,Ntest)=(1,0) produces E=1. C correctly indicates appearance; avoidance does not imply noxiousness. There is no variable for belief, falsity of a belief, rationality, or warrant. This is not a demonstration that a false belief lacks warrant.

Finite checks cover 341 histories of lengths0–4, 1,364 history/test cases, 2,728 mediator clamps, the four one-trial cases, matched-marginal histories, and the three controls. These overlapping checks are not independent experiments, empirical probabilities, or proof of naturalistic intentionality. The general induction is elementary mathematics; the earned increment is the disciplined historical-role test and its control structure.

## 4. What “as content” would additionally require

The equations establish that an appearance-tracking vehicle, participating in a specified learning process, acquires control. They do not identify a proposition as what the mechanism believes or show that an intentional property rather than a merely indicator relation explains recruitment. A model with an appended label “represents X” would not add this result. Nor may the fixed appearance signal silently become “this is harmful” when evaluating a harmless case.

After reading Dretske’s actual 1991 response, an additional faithfulness limit is explicit: W is a response disposition, not his proposed indicator function. This fixed-output model does not capture his claim that an indicator function can contribute to different behavior as other cognitive or motivational conditions change. No extra toy output mapping is added to pretend that this semantic issue has been solved.

The positive claim is consequently narrower than content naturalization. It is consequential because it refutes the specific inference from current sufficiency to historical irrelevance; it leaves the disputed indication-to-representation bridge exposed rather than assuming it.

## 5. T20 provenance/continuation comparison

The acquired/preset example shares a methodological structure with earlier T20 identification work: an observation map can erase a distinction that a richer, appropriately interpreted intervention reveals. The local readout maps distinct acquisition histories to the same test-time transition behavior. Any valid identification claim must say which observables and interventions are allowed.

But **intervening on past training is not a future continuation from a fully fixed present state**. The test does not prove that two completely current-state-identical physical systems diverge under identical future inputs and laws. It also does not use T20’s calibrated incidence-thinning instrument or reconstruct productive-route multiplicities. Importing that theorem here would require a new lawful mapping of interventions and roles. No such equivalence is claimed.

The earlier productive-identifiability report was directly consulted for its distinction between endpoint equivalence, interpretation-level provenance, and admissible intervention instruments. That is inherited methodological evidence, not a fresh verification of its mathematical dependency chain.

## 6. Original-source nonduplication comparison

The model has a past acquisition episode, a persisting dependent configuration, and a current trigger. It contains no independently original bearer, no whole-world productive provision, and no claim that each of two agents completely supplies the same created work. Its immediate sufficiency is conditional on sensor law, learning law, past formation, and present conditions.

A mapping from acquisition to one complete original source and triggering to another therefore fails to preserve the relata and the sense of completeness. Conversely, the possibility of ordinary historically mediated causation gives no licence to duplicate complete original provision. The mental-property problem also survives: even identifying mental and physical events does not establish efficacy of their mental properties. This model deliberately makes no such inference.

What transports is the requirement to fix effect, bearer, background, historical role, and intervention semantics before deciding whether two explanations compete. That discipline supplies a more exact comparison, not a proof that the two metaphysical problems are equivalent.
