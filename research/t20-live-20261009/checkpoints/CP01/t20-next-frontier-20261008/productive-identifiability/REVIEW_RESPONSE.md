route-convolution, matrix-inversion, determinant, oracle and transport controls.

eight general-root author groups. Review controls are additional assurance, not

Substantive clarifications made before the final freeze:

- Bounded-sample nearest-panel classification uses the maximum-coordinate norm.
- The omitted-coordinate lower bound assumes an oracle supplying only retained
  coordinate values. Full endpoint laws or paired raw samples permit recomputing
  deleted derived coordinates.

  whole-route gates with the same support-dependent success probabilities have
  the identical endpoint law, so physical gate architecture is not identified.

  guard reevaluation; their failures are tested separately.

  The inherited reactive code does not specify stochastic gates or cross-effect

  stochastic refinements make that limit explicit.

  incorporated into the exact all-profile iff proof and tested with nonnegative
  inventories obtained by splitting comparison coefficients.
- The original H fibre theorem excerpt was freshly retrieved by the reviewer and
  directly read here. The source-gap statement was narrowed accordingly.
