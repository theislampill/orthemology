## Blocking findings

### BG-F01 — “necessary” exceeded the finite frame

Existence in two declared worlds establishes frame-relative invariance only. It
must not be labelled absolute metaphysical necessity or a Necessary Being.

### BG-F02 — concreteness was represented by a status label

`external_actual` alone is not a theory of concreteness. The strongest formal
witness may be called an externally actual, causally efficacious model bearer
only because it participates in the declared effect relation. No general
abstract/concrete criterion is proved.

### BG-F03 — semantic content risked importing mentality

The model's truth-labelled content and interpretation table are primitive
semantic-contract coordinates. They are not knowledge, apprehension,
intentional content, or intrinsic notionality. The repair must use neutral
language and keep H7 open.

### BG-F04 — target authority and fittingness were unexplained primitives

The target key and fitting-action set are supplied to the model. Alignment with
them does not establish proper function, teleology, or Wisdom. Deep BG must not
claim that the impersonal rival explains norm authority.

### BG-F05 — one bearer risked carrier-boxing overread

Assigning all structural role labels to `g` proves numerical co-location in the
signature, not shared derivation, primitive reduction, or explanatory unity.
The repaired result must make Candidate G's parity burden explicit.

### BG-F06 — expansion independence is signature-relative

If conclusion predicates are unconstrained, arbitrary expansions are expected.
The value is exact nonentailment from a deliberately rich neutral reduct, not a
new general model-theoretic theorem. The repair must state every included
neutral correlate and deny metaphysical/evidential parity.

### BG-F07 — Creatorhood was not typed

An actualizer is not automatically a Creator. `CreatorClass` must be defined as
a scoped target predicate including at least agentive/intentional origination
under the declared bridge; otherwise its falsity is merely a vocabulary choice.

### BG-F08 — expression-token production was not Speech

Faithful token output does not establish speaker intention, speech capacity in
the source-relative sense, an occurrent divine act, Name authorization, or
revealed content. B15 remains open.

### BG-F09 — source-role compatibility could migrate Track N

A source-functional-role map under one candidate interpretation does not
authenticate the source, select the actual world, or transfer source-predicated
personality/Speech into a neutral entailment.

### BG-F10 — finite satisfiability is not metaphysical possibility

The executable structure proves consistency of a typed finite theory and a
nonentailment relative to its intended model class. It does not establish that
the impersonal rival is physically/metaphysically possible or equally likely.

### BG-F11 — bridge and theorem-family ancestry needed explicit custody

The result is a strengthened R5 common-model application and an instance of
reduct/expansion nondefinability. General mathematical novelty is zero. It must
be linked to B6–B16 without being assigned their historical identities.
