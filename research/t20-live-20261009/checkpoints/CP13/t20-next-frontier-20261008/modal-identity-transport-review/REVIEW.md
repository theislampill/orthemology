## Findings first

Theorems 1–4 are correct under their stated hypotheses. The main distinctions survive adversarial checking: a full faithful identity atlas requires trivial holonomy; one point section requires a common fixed point; an invariant normalized vector need not supply a point; and restricting the supplier domain can destroy receipt despite preserving the target's identity, existence, and received predicate.

The existential Back condition is exactly necessary and sufficient for preserving the specified Received assertion. It need not retain every supplier. Neither Back nor the analogous Realisation condition makes arbitrary restricted-domain quantification faithful.

Independent controls pass **175,130 exact assertions**. A read-only replay of the author's bound script passes **626,901 assertions**, with output identical to the saved author result. These are finite checks, not general proofs or evidence of actual metaphysical possibility.

Two precision requests made during review are explicit in the reviewed text: modal preservation uses a declared chart-induced assignment semantics, and empty outer domains use a separately declared extension rather than conventional nonempty-domain first-order semantics. The overly broad Barcan disclaimer was also corrected: constant outer-domain quantification validates both directions, while arbitrary E-guarded quantification need not. There are no unresolved blocking or wording findings in the final bound revision.

## 1. Theorem-by-theorem examination

### Theorem 1: accepted sections and the rooted fixed set

The tree paths determine every component uniquely from the actual component. For an arbitrary edge, the remaining equation is exactly the fixed-point equation for `t_w⁻¹ g_vw t_v`. Taking every non-tree edge in one orientation suffices: the reverse constraint is its inverse, and tree-edge constraints are identities. The root membership filters are exactly the pullbacks of the separate local acceptance sets.

The bijection therefore proves both the Boolean existence scalar and the natural-number count. Every satisfying tuple contributes exactly one; the count is not a product of independently chosen local witnesses. The conclusion includes a one-world graph, empty acceptance sets, and an empty carrier. Since W contains its actual world, an empty carrier gives no section rather than a spurious empty tuple.

Tree independence is correct even when the chart tables genuinely change: a section's actual value cannot depend on its chosen presentation. For a root point fixed by all loops, alternate paths agree on that point, although they need not agree on all other points. The linear-in-table-size complexity bound includes tabulating inverses, tree transports, cycle tables, and filters; it is not a claim that brute-force tuple enumeration has that complexity.

### Gauge covariance

The local relabellings cancel in adjacent composites. Consequently cycle permutations conjugate at the root, root accepted sets move by the actual-world permutation, and section tuples move coordinatewise. Scalar truth and natural counts are unchanged. This is a passive change of coordinates only when each affected local valuation and edge table is transformed together.

Importantly, no equation transports E, C, or Dep from one world to another. These may differ arbitrarily between worlds. Gauge covariance therefore does not secretly assume necessary existence or modal nonreceipt.

### Theorem 2: full faithful charts

All four conditions are equivalent, including cardinality zero. Identity holonomy makes the tree transports a global chart system. Conversely, charts telescope around every loop. The injective common-image representation forces each loop to be identity because equal images reflect equality. Connectedness ensures the fibre images coincide. Finally, every root value extends precisely when the common fixed set is the whole root fibre.

This is a theorem about the supplied correspondence system. It is not a proof that the labels denote the same real bearer. A nontrivial holonomy action with a fixed label gives a valid one-point section without giving an atlas for the entire local domain. Nontrivial holonomy is a contradiction only after every edge is interpreted as faithful literal identity for every local item.

### Theorem 3: normalized copyable points

The coefficient argument is exact. Diagonal entries require idempotent coefficients; off-diagonal entries forbid two distinct supported coordinates; the augmentation excludes zero. Over the reals this leaves exactly one coefficient equal to one. Over the Boolean semiring the support must be a singleton. The Boolean tensor product is being used in its declared free-basis coefficient interpretation, so the same support argument applies.

Parallelism and diagonal acceptance filters then recover the point-section equations. These are basis-sensitive operations: a general invertible linear change requires transporting the copy map, augmentation, filters, and extraction map, rather than retaining the old coordinate meaning.

The Z/6 example is correct: coefficients 3 and 4 are distinct nonzero idempotents, annihilate each other, and sum to one. Thus arbitrary-ring generalization would be false. The stated real/Boolean restriction is sufficient, although not maximally general.

### Theorem 4: exact Received Back condition

For the induced relation, any retained supplier maps to a local supplier. The only potentially missing direction is exactly the displayed Back implication. Equivalence of the positive assertions and equivalence of their Boolean negations therefore hold exactly when Back holds.

Injectivity of the retention map is appropriate for a faithful retained-individual interpretation, but is not itself needed for this elementary existential equivalence. This is an optional mathematical weakening, not a correction. Surjectivity is sufficient and unnecessarily strong for this single observable.

## 3. Modal-language scope and the Barcan distinction

The corrected chart rule is an explicit semantics: on moving from v to w, every free bearer value moves by `u_w ∘ u_v⁻¹`. It is equivalent to keeping the same global assignment while changing coordinates. Atomic valuation preservation, bijective quantifier reindexing, and this modal clause prove the asserted induction. The alignment graph itself is not modal accessibility.

This establishes preservation for the declared relational first-order modal language, including nested modalities and explicitly guarded actualist quantification. It does not establish that the 2009 G/PL comparison already has these modal constructors or supplies numerical-identity interpretation.

One important precision point: **constant outer-domain quantification validates both Barcan directions for its unguarded quantifiers**. Universal quantification over a fixed B commutes with universal quantification over accessible worlds. A blanket assertion that no Barcan principle follows would therefore be misleading. No corresponding unrestricted Barcan principle follows for E-guarded actualist quantification when existence extensions vary.

Independent controls exhibit both failures for guarded quantifiers. At a world whose domain expands from {0} to {0,1}, take the tested predicate true only of 0 at the destination: `∀x □P(x)` is true while `□∀x P(x)` is false. Reversing the existence extensions reverses those truth values. Both formulas agree under unguarded outer-domain quantification. The issue is semantic wording, not a flaw in the four numbered theorems.

For n=0, the explicit empty-existential/empty-universal convention gives compositional truth for closed formulas, while free bearer assignments do not exist. This is a declared extension outside the source's nonempty comparison-model class.

## 4. Strongest counterexample checks

1. **Normalized invariant vector without a point.** A swap loop on two labels has no common fixed label but preserves their uniform real average. Even all existence filters being true and augmentation being one do not turn that average into an individual. Copyability rejects it.
2. **Every loop separately has a witness.** The six-label Klein-four action has four group elements. Their fixed-label counts are 6, 2, 2, and 2, but their common fixed set is empty. This defeats even all-element separate fixed-witness tests, rather than merely checking two generators badly. The two-triangle graph realizes the action and has no point section.
3. **Coherent-point restriction erases receipt.** The swap of 0 and 1 fixes only 2, but an incoming edge 0→2 disappears when the retained bearer domain contains only 2. Copying E and C on 2 faithfully does not preserve their representation through Dep. A resource borne only by 0 likewise loses its Realisation witness.
4. **Necessity filtering erases receipt with trivial holonomy.** Identity charts, necessary target b, actual-only supplier c, and alternative-only supplier d preserve received status with locally existing endpoints. Restriction to necessarily existing bearers removes both suppliers. This is not a demand for one fixed supplier; the saved generic-reception interface already distinguishes those claims.
5. **Back is weaker than supplier closure.** If retained c and omitted d both supply b, retaining c preserves Received. Supplier multiplicity or a formula distinguishing d can still change.
6. **Even Back plus Realisation does not preserve all FullPremises.** Retain necessary b and omit actual contingent c. With empty C and Dep, receipt Back holds. Give an actual resource an intrinsic realization at b. Full ContingencyNeed is false because c is actual, contingent, and unreceived; its restriction is true. This directly confirms the report's warning about weakened universal formulas.
7. **Independent local isomorphism loses de re binding.** Identical singleton existence profiles up to independent local renaming can have different common global bearers. Transforming the alignment along with the renamed valuation restores truth, as a genuine passive gauge change must.

None of these finite fixtures establishes metaphysical possibility. Dep has its stipulated whole-reception reading; ordinary interaction is not silently substituted for that reading.

## 5. Attribution and primary-source check

The synchronization criterion is established mathematics. Gao, Brodzki, and Mukherjee's Corollary 2.1 equates full group-valued synchronization with trivial holonomy; their following remark gives the elementary propagation argument. This review checked the defining synchronization material and the selected corollary/proof/remark, not all of Section 2.1 or the whole paper. The report appropriately distinguishes a point section from full group-valued synchronization. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1610.09051).

Coecke, Pavlovic, and Vicary explicitly define chosen-basis copy and deletion maps, with copyable points characterized as comonoid homomorphisms. This supports the ancestry attribution. The report separately excludes the zero vector and proves its Boolean case directly; it does not import the Hilbert-space classification or a quantum interpretation. Only the cited introduction and opening technical passages were checked. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0810.0812).

Dasgupta 2009 brackets the relevant a priori modal issue at printed p. 37 and defers modal-language expansions at p. 63 note 40. Page 64 uses a nonempty extensional model domain. These passages support the report's refusal to derive modal or grounding preservation from the extensional comparison. [Primary source](https://shamik.net/papers/dasgupta%20individuals.pdf).

Dasgupta 2011's selected discussion at printed pp. 126–130 genuinely considers qualitative individuating roles and counterpart semantics, and separates necessitation from grounding. It is not a completed modal semantics for the 2009 G language. No full-paper reading credit is warranted or claimed. [Primary source](https://shamik.net/papers/dasgupta%20the%20bare%20necessities.pdf).

The inherited graded-interface fibre criterion, equality wiring, source-identity controls, and capacity/act/token distinctions are already present at the named prior locations. The earned contribution is the explicit adapter and its application-specific failure checks. This review does not establish a literature-wide priority or originality claim.
