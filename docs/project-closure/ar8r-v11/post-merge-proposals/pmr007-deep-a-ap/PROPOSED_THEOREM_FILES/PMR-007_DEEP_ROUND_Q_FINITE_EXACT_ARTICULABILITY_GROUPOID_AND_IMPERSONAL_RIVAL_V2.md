## 2. FEAG-1A — canonical translation groupoid

Define

\[
\tau_{ij}=e_j\circ e_i^{-1}.
\]

Then:

\[
\tau_{ii}=id_{\Sigma_i},\qquad
\tau_{ji}=\tau_{ij}^{-1},\qquad
\tau_{jk}\circ\tau_{ij}=\tau_{ik}.
\]

Every content predicate `P subseteq C` transports exactly:

\[
\tau_{ij}(e_i[P])=e_j[P].
\]

### Proof

All identities follow by cancellation of the bijections. Predicate transport follows because `tau_ij(e_i(c))=e_j(c)` for every `c in C`.
