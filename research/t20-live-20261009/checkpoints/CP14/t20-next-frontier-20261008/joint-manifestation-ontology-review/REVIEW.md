## Transport audit

1. **No physical-example shortcut.** Section 4 expressly permits generalization beyond physical motivating examples and asks whether explanatory structure survives. Its reference to physical bedrock limits the inference available, not the class of admissible recipients of the theory.

2. **No activation-to-createdness inference.** The report does not identify an activated state with a newly created intrinsic faculty. It respects the inherited allowance for successive proper acts. In Transfer A, the uncreated status of the participants’ acts is an assumption of the proposed rival mapping, not a conclusion extracted from the physics.

3. **No conditional-exercise-to-donation inference.** The dependence of A’s exercise on B’s activity is not automatically an efficient supply of A’s power by B. Section 4 and §5 item 2 explicitly preserve that distinction; a vicious reciprocal-enabling circle is not introduced without its missing efficient-receipt premise.

4. **No distinct-act-to-extra-created-trace inference.** Section 5 item 5 expressly refuses the creation-status and retention inference. No hidden created mixer is postulated merely because a product relation needs interpretation. Existing nonabsorption and trace results receive no new proof from this reading.

5. **Episode unity does not settle the created target.** Transfer A correctly identifies the category/coverage burden: an episode containing the stipulated original acts cannot simply be relabelled as the rival’s wholly created external result. Distinguishing an episode from its product is a legitimate response. Doing so removes the direct identity inference; it does not prove there can be no single product.

6. **Preserving intrinsic input is insufficient for preserving complete exercise.** Section 4 makes its point conditionally: if B’s activity partly constitutes the complete partnered exercise, removing B does not preserve that exercise merely by retaining A’s intrinsic record. This neither assumes that suppression is admissible for necessarily harmonious originals nor derives the inherited original-adequacy norm from the bookkeeping distinction.

7. **Many-partner and created-recipient cases remain live.** An interpretation with original A and B each active toward a created C can avoid making A’s own manifestation directly dependent on original B. The report admits it. What it must then establish is the exact joint productive interpretation and original-agent adequacy; requiring that account is reasonable. R2 ensures the numerical plurality is credited at its actual source strength.

8. **No perfection or sovereignty certificate.** The source cannot by itself supply unrestricted original power, pure-perfection eligibility, wise complete determination, universal supremacy, original-peer value, or action-guiding first-person recognition. The report does not claim it does. Conversely, the absence of those certificates is not treated as proof that every such extension is impossible.
