# Bounded scan controls for block 03

## Scope, witness and method

After completing the electronic reading and its five original deliverables, I inspected selected pages of the locally supplied [Adwa scan](https://archive.org/download/al-mulakhas/al-risala.pdf). The source metadata records 625 PDF positions and 24,103,666 bytes. The sampled text extraction produced only page-break characters; therefore identification and collation below use rendered page images, not alleged OCR matches. Each selected main-text page was rendered and visually inspected across its complete body and printed notes. The page list and original scan-receipt digests are in [factual scan coverage](../READING_COVERAGE.json). Three previously prepared catalogue images were also inspected.

This is a bounded textual control, not traversal of all 625 positions, of every critical note, or of a manuscript. Modern editorial assertions about a manuscript remain editorial assertions: the underlying manuscript was not inspected here. PDF position and printed page are separate. The printed page numerals are visible and checked except at PDF380, where no footer number is visible in the rendered scan. Its conventional printed373 identification is inferred from its placement between verified printed372/PDF379 and printed374/PDF381 and from uninterrupted argument, and is explicitly marked as inferred.

The principal controlled clusters are creator/perfection, necessary attributes versus efficient production, attribution versus producing a quality elsewhere, original agency versus conditions of particular acts, and the finite-source/common-Creator comparison. I also controlled the electronic part boundary and the six catalogue loci relevant to this range. Two exploratory pages beyond this range, PDF393–394, were inspected while locating the last cluster; they are recorded as out-of-range exploratory inspection and are not made evidence for full reading of the next range.

## 1. Creator and defect-free perfection: printed320–322, PDF327–329

The surrounding argument is continuous across these pages. Printed320 begins within L0324 and completes the alternative independent/dependent, sustaining/non-sustaining and complete/incomplete routes through L0327. Its note explains a manuscript transposition in the phrase about the dependent and the independent. Printed321 starts with L0328, then contains L0329–0332 and most of L0333. Printed322 completes L0333 and contains L0334–0337.

The key affirmative sequence is visibly present on printed321:

- Perfection without deficiency is considered as possible for an existent as such.
- Created possible dependants can possess life, knowledge, power, hearing, sight and speech.
- The inferior's possible perfection cannot be denied to the superior source merely as impossible for it.
- “وأيضا فكل كمال في المحدثات الممكنات المخلوقات فمنه” is present.
- The giver of another's perfection is more entitled to perfection.

Printed322 carries the circularity objection to borrowing perfection from an effect and the alternative involving another necessary source. It then concludes that the defect-free possible perfection is fitting and intrinsically grounded. Most importantly, L0337's limitation appears as the next paragraph on the same page: “وإثبات صفاته اللازمة له بطريق الإيجاب الذاتي هو الحق دون إثبات مخلوقاته”. There is no need to conjecture this constraint from distant material; the inspected page states it immediately after the positive argument.

The notes here concern grammatical readings: printed321 notes original “موجودا” against the edited “موجود”, and printed322 notes original “دلت” against edited “دل”. Neither note supplies a universal claim about every possible source or requisite. The creator/perfection cluster remains bounded by its actual theological subject and the defect-free-perfection filter.

## 2. Necessary attributes and requisites versus efficient production: printed323–324, PDF330–331

Printed323 begins at L0338, develops the criticism of composition-based arguments and reaches into L0344. It visibly contains the claim that requiring something included in one's own reality does not entail that this required item is an efficient producer or efficient cause. The thought experiment about a necessary whole requiring its parts is present. Printed324 finishes L0344 and covers L0345–0348.

Printed324 explicitly distinguishes two uses of “cause”: the essence's necessitation of attributes can be a sound meaning, but the proposed efficient cause of the world is “ليست من هذا الباب”. Its notes include a correction from masculine “وكونه” to feminine “وكونها”, matching the attributes as subject, and a grammatical “لا يتم”/“لا تتم” correction. These should not be obscured by electronic wording, but the central distinction is secure on the page.

An additional recapitulation is controlled at printed350/PDF357, corresponding to L0486–0491 (part). The complete page distinguishes externally assembled composition from multiple inherent meanings and repeats that requiring attributes is not requiring an outside originator. There is no printed note on that page which dissolves the distinction. These pages materially limit a reconstruction that treats every necessary requisite as an efficient source.

## 3. Creature-specific perfection and common predication: printed329–331, PDF336–338

The preceding debate over selective attributes is visible on printed329; printed330 continues through L0385. Printed331 contains L0386–0390, including common mental predication, non-equality of the concrete instances, and the explicit exclusion of creature-specific features even where they count as perfections for a creature. This supports the scope restriction already identified in the electronic reading.

Printed330 has an especially relevant note: above the words for hatred and anger in the exemplar argument the original bears the comment “فيه ما فيه”. This is a reported marginal reservation about those words, not a wholesale rejection of the ensuing perfection argument. It should nevertheless accompany exact reliance on that particular exemplar. Printed331's note reports a collation marker “بلغت المقابلة”; it does not identify a new philosophical premise.

## 4. Production is not inherence: printed352–355, PDF359–362

Printed352 controls the Throne Verse interpretation at L0496–L0501 (part). It includes the claims that intercession is permitted rather than independently imposed and that knowledge is taught by God. It also prints “وأما مجرد نفي الرؤية”, whereas electronic L0501 has “وأما مجرد الرؤية”. The new scan wording supplies the logically expected negation of vision in the author's argument that non-being itself is not praiseworthy. The footnote at this point addresses a different preceding word, “قدرته” versus the editor's “رؤيته”, so the observed electronic omission of “نفي” should be reported as a direct main-text difference, not attributed to that note.

Printed353/PDF360 contains L0501 (remainder) through the start of L0507. Printed354/PDF361 begins by completing the crucial sentence: God is not described by what he creates in another and which does not subsist in him, with colours, tastes and motions as examples. The same page then distinguishes attribute annexation, ownership and special association (L0508), and continues through L0512. Printed353's note corrects “بلى” to “بل”; printed354 has no relevant printed critical note.

Printed355/PDF362 gives L0513–L0517 (part), distinguishing regress in original efficacy from successive effects. Its full context prevents the “be” argument on printed354 from being treated as a blanket prohibition of all succession or infinity.

## 5. Original creatorhood versus this act's condition: printed356–358, PDF363–365

Printed356 completes L0517, then contains L0518–L0524. The full L0521 distinction is visually secure: original creatorhood depends on no other, but being creator of this may be conditioned on creating another. Printed357 contains L0525–L0529 and elaborates the difference between being unable to create anything until original agency is supplied and creating this after creating that. Printed358 contains L0530–L0536 (part), including the distinction between a letter type and a token in a particular person and time. These three pages have no printed critical notes altering those clauses.

The control thus strengthens, rather than removes, the limitation already recorded: independence of the comprehensive agent does not forbid ordered prerequisites in particular acts. It also prevents “Creator of everything” from being mechanically applied to the Creator and the attributes or acts included in his designation.

## 6. Comprehensive first source and finite conditional sources: PDF379–383

Printed372/PDF379 supplies the end of the false-binary diagnosis at L0594–L0599 (part). PDF380, conventionally printed373 but with its page numeral not visible, completes L0599 and contains L0600–L0603. Its final sentence visibly states that there is only the agent and its products and that no product is except from him. The immediately preceding context asks why a wholly unchanged first source would yield novelty and says its successive complete efficacy must be its own, not given by a distinct independent producer. The notes on PDF380 concern “يعلم” and “لا يفيده” in the source; neither changes the target's comprehensive scope.

Printed374/PDF381 has L0604–L0607 (part). A printed note reports a manuscript collation remark “قوبل بحسب الطاقة”, and another gives a grammatical correction concerning “يتوقف”. Printed375/PDF382 completes L0607 and contains L0608–L0611 (part). It explicitly distinguishes the philosophers' active intellect, whose effects require conditions from others, from the first comprehensive source. It then offers the sun example: removing a roof or cloud produces light and heat here without a new intrinsic act in the sun. The note reads source “شريك” against edited “شريكا”.

Printed376/PDF383 completes L0611 in its first paragraph and supplies the next branch of the dilemma. If necessitation depends on another, the proposed source is not eternally complete; further alternatives raise a prior circle or regress among producers. The remaining page continues the argument about complete efficacy and newly supplied conditions. I read this page as immediately necessary scan context, but do not claim continuous electronic reading of the following assigned range. Its exact post-L0611 electronic IDs must be supplied by that range's reader.

The bounded scan result confirms that the sun concession is genuine contextual limiting evidence. The text does not demand intrinsic change in every finite contributor whenever an effect occurs. Its special challenge concerns an alleged complete source of the whole causal order.

## 7. Received electronic part boundary: printed302–304, PDF309–311

Printed302/PDF309 covers the end of L0234, L0235–L0237 and the beginning of L0238. Printed303/PDF310 completes L0238 and L0239's authorial argument. It ends with the contrast between describing the Lord by negative attributes alone and by neither negation nor affirmation. Printed304/PDF311 begins directly “وهذا الثاني قول غلاة القرامطة الباطنية” and continues the quotation from al-Sijistānī through L0244.

The electronic colophon inside L0239 and separate title/author labels L0240–0241 are absent from this inspected Adwa transition. That absence establishes only a difference between the received electronic segmentation and this scan. It does not by itself identify which earlier print source generated the electronic boundary or prove that no other witness contains such paratext.

Printed303's notes matter: portions are described as marginal material in the author's hand; the phrase “من وجه” is reported as interlinear; and “مانعة من” is said to be unclear in the original, with the displayed text offered as a likely reading. Printed302 also flags unclear original wording and an editorial reconstruction. The mapped paragraphs can support general interpretation, but remain unsuitable for presenting every received clause as an unquestioned diplomatic reading.

## 8. Catalogue loci in this range

[complete contextual correction register](../variants/CORRECTION_REGISTER.json) records six entries with contextual identification, electronic old/new comparison, short discriminating electronic variants and full-line identity hashes and inspected authorial-page coordinates. They are substantial11–15 and lesser13. All six electronic passages follow the older side at the relevant locus. Substantial15 matches after joining electronic “ولو لا” to catalogue “ولولا”; this is not a conceptual emendation.

Two important complications are retained:

1. Substantial13 is catalogued at printed298, but “وهؤلاء يرفعون جميع الأمور الثبوتية” is visibly on printed297/PDF304. Printed298/PDF305 was checked and continues with the next argument. The catalogue's page reference therefore needs qualification rather than silent adoption.
2. Lesser13 prints “فأنتم تبنون أمركم” at printed294/PDF301, but note2 says this is the original reading and suggests “تثبتون” as the likely correct one. The electronic “تثبتون” therefore agrees with the older print and also with the newer editor's suggested correction. It cannot fairly be labelled simply an electronic error.

The other substantial controls are direct: L0193 omits the accepted-interpretation clause found on printed290/PDF297; L0220 reverses existence/non-existence where printed297 and its apparatus follow a transposition mark; L0234 lacks the mental/external paired clause on printed302; and L0236 preserves the garbled earlier wording where printed302 reads a criticism of al-Rāzī's conception through the example.

## 9. Other inspected observations and residual limits

Printed359/PDF366 retains “عن أبي موسى” in the main text at the start of the angels/jinn/Adam report, while its footnote cites the report from ʿĀʾisha. This is a scan-confirmed tension between body and annotation, not a silent correction or an independent new hadith verification.

Printed369/PDF376 reproduces the difficult repeated formulation at L0584 concerning created/originated remembrance. There is no directly relevant critical note on this page resolving it. The scan therefore confirms that the difficulty is not merely introduced by the electronic line, without resolving the intended wording. Printed370/PDF377 continues the doctrinal taxonomy and carries biographical notes; it was inspected as context.

The selected controls do not settle every candidate in the initial proposed control list. In particular, the detailed co-occurrence proof at L0432–0436, the wording at L0299 and other non-selected loci remain open. The newly read pages do not justify increasing the continuous electronic word count, claiming all-page critical-edition traversal, or treating all 119,021 words as diplomatically verified. The central substantive result remains bounded: strong actual common-Creator/perfection evidence, with explicit restrictions on causal mode, attribute inherence, requisite dependence and finite conditions.
