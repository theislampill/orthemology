# One source-bound mapping test: collective ground to productive target

## Question and verdict

Can the strongest actual proposal in Dasgupta 2011 §11 be translated into a singleton qualitative explanation of each individual-existence fact, and then used as either an instance or a counterexample to productive Completion?

**The first translation fails to preserve the source's grounding claim.** Modal consequence distributes to the individual truths; the grounding relation, on this proposal, does not. Neither accepting nor rejecting that first translation supplies the subsequent productive-resource commitments.

This is a direct test of a proposed interpretation against a published proposal. It is **not** a formal consistency proof for generalism, a model of actual GTR, or a metaphysical countermodel obtained by assigning an arbitrary grounding relation. No executable finite-world fixture is used or needed. The complete source plurality is retained rather than replaced by a convenient proper subset.

## 1. Exact input and independently distinguished assertions

Let:

- Q abbreviate the AG assertion that the complete qualitative world-state W⁰ obtains.
- Y be the complete plurality of individualistic facts about the manifold, under the interpretation contemplated on printed 151–152.
- e_b be the member of Y asserting that a specified region b exists.
- P be an ordinary PL existential assertion used as an extensional counterpart of some AG assertion. P and its AG counterpart must remain distinct for grounding purposes; note 74 permits P to be grounded in individual instances and denies that it is thereby fundamental.
- G(X,Y) abbreviate the proposed plural grounding explanation. This notation is shorthand for the source's sentential explanatory apparatus, not a claim that facts are independently existing abstract objects.

The crucial input is:

    G({Q}, Y).

The source simultaneously denies a separate qualitative ground for e_b:

    not G({Q}, {e_b}),

and more generally denies a qualitative grounding explanation of that individualistic fact taken alone. This is the proposed way of being nonfundamental without a singleton qualitative ground. No assertion that Q is autonomous, productively unreceived or necessarily obtaining has been inserted.

The displayed two-sphere AG example motivates the availability of qualitative descriptions preserving symmetric plurality. It is not substituted for a complete actual manifold state here. Its omitted totality clauses (notes 63 and 70) cannot be supplied by pretending that an existential formula already excludes other objects.

## 2. The constraint inventory

**Factivity.** As an actual explanatory assertion, G({Q},Y) is used with Q obtaining and all the Y facts obtaining. This is an interpretation of the factive explanatory use, not a separately proved or explicitly axiomatized theorem in the paper. We do not infer actuality from a satisfiable AG expression.

**Necessitation.** Printed 119 and note 11 state the original principle; printed 152 says to modify it for plural output. Its relevant consequence is that at any world where all the grounds obtain, all the grounded facts obtain. For the present input this says, schematically:

    for every w, if Q holds at w, every y in Y holds at w.

This is a metalanguage formulation for the potentially infinite plurality, not an assertion that a finitary PL formula contains an infinite conjunction. Note 11 requires the sentential version in modal theories where facts obtaining across worlds is not well defined.

**Irreflexivity.** Note 10 excludes explaining a fact by a collection already including that fact in the single-output formulation. No self-ground is introduced by the tested mapping. An appeal to a supposed grounding projection from a collection to one of its own members would need defence and conflicts with the reason not to equate ground with mere entailment.

**Transitivity.** The explicitly stated schema in note 10 is:

    if G(X,{z}) and G(U together with {z},{v}),
    then G(X together with U,{v}).

That rule is retained wherever its stated premises apply. It cannot apply G({Q},Y) as a ground of e_b without already treating a plural output as a singleton output. That is the disputed projection, not a harmless instantiation of the schema.

An extension composing **whole** intermediate pluralities would read:

    if G(X,Y) and G(Y together with Z,V),
    then G(X together with Z,V).

This is a natural explicitly stated reconstruction for assessing the proposed mapping, not a full plural calculus supplied by the paper. It still cannot yield G({Q},{e_b}) without an additional grounding premise taking the whole Y collection to {e_b}. Membership of e_b in Y supplies an implication of truth; it does not supply that explanatory premise.

The source changes grounding's logical form in note 72 without giving its complete revised axiomatization. We accordingly do **not** claim to have checked all of an unstated intended calculus. The mapping test is negative at a more direct level: its output contradicts the source's advertised interpretive condition. Establishing that the entire advertised proposal is coherent and adequate would require more than this test.

## 3. What follows, and the exact failed step

From joint grounding and plural Necessitation, for each particular e_b in Y:

    necessarily (Q implies e_b).

The truth consequence follows because if every member of Y is true, e_b is true. The following is **not** the same rule:

    G({Q},Y) implies G({Q},{e_b}).

That rule is right-distribution of grounding. It is the rule the actual §11 proposal rejects. Deriving it from the necessary conditional would reverse Necessitation, which the source also explicitly rejects. Treating Y as one conjunction and applying a logical conjunction-elimination rule inside G would likewise insert an unprovided explanatory rule.

Thus the result survives the straightforward replies:

1. Factivity gives actual e_b, not its singleton qualitative ground.
2. Necessitation gives the conditional modal truth, not the singleton ground.
3. The stated transitivity has no suitable intermediate singleton premise.
4. A whole-plurality transitivity rule also needs an additional explanatory premise, not just membership.
5. Equal extensions of Q and a PL rendering do not erase their different grounding roles. Fine-grained assertion labels must be preserved.

The test does not show that the proposal is metaphysically true simply because it asserts non-distribution. It identifies precisely why a distributive reconstruction would not be a test of this proposal. The positive argument offered for the non-distributive account is its global qualitative ontology plus the concern about explaining one individual's existence by the entire cosmos; a fuller defence is deferred in notes 71–73. A critic can dispute that defence. Calling the collective account inadequate on independent explanatory grounds would be a substantive objection; silently distributing it would not.

## 4. The Completion translation has further independent obligations

The unchanged definition is:

    for each received target t,
    some relevant resource r is actual and not received.

This does not mention G. To get it from an explanatory principle one would need an additional application argument. In a per-individual reconstruction, that argument might first try to extract a separate ground for e_b. The tested extraction fails as an interpretation of §11.

Even a successful extraction would leave these obligations:

- identify the target as received in the intended whole-existence respect;
- identify an **actual concrete intrinsic resource**, rather than an obtaining assertion or a universal;
- show the required productive relevance and nonreceipt;
- justify the bearer realisation and reception transport at that interpretation.

The source's joint qualitative explanation does not discharge them. Conversely, a successful joint qualitative explanation does not establish that no such resource also exists. Failure of a route to Completion is not the truth of its negation.

## 5. Strongest affirmative response: use the whole field as one target

The affirmative route need not pass through the rejected singleton projection. `T` is an abstract target type. It can be proposed to contain a whole field, and `original_bearer_of_completion` uses Completion only for the particular target supplied. `abc_same_witness` begins with one received target. The code requires neither distributivity of ground nor a distinct resource for every individual.

Therefore, suppose a defender independently establishes:

1. the relevant whole field is an actual received target;
2. an actual unreceived resource is productively relevant to that target;
3. the resource has an actual intrinsic bearer;
4. the declared reception transport applies.

Then the existing inference to an actual original bearer goes through. The non-distributive grounding proposal has supplied no counterexample to that conditional. The modal extension additionally needs its already explicit representation, contingency and constitutive-reception assumptions.

A whole target is a choice of the target's scope; it is not automatically one concrete individual or one intrinsic resource. Classifying the qualitative world-state itself as the bearer and resource would still need a supported interpretation. Equally, it cannot be excluded from every possible broad bearer interpretation just by calling it qualitative.

This response is stronger than trying to compel individual grounding. It leaves the original dispute where the inherited adjudication placed it: the actuality and exhaustive applicability of productive-resource Completion, and any enlarged Realisation/SupportTransport interpretation. **The new mapping obstruction does not independently defeat that whole-field route.**

## 6. Modal and empty-scope guards

Agreement on all of Y at Q-worlds is the source's fact-specific conditional claim. It must not be weakened to merely the same qualitative isomorphism type, nor strengthened into a construction of unique qualitative individuating roles. The proposal was designed to accommodate symmetric individuals.

There is no derivation of Q's necessity. If U is the class of Q-worlds, necessity restricted to U is conditional necessity. If Q actually obtains, U includes the actual world; if U is empty, its universal truths yield no actual individual. A zero-world frame is not an actual-world model. The two-distinct-witness AG/PL condition is false in an empty domain. A zero-place property or truth value is not itself a concrete bearer.

Even with necessary Q and authenticated fixed de re interpretation, necessary existence is not existence through one's own essence and not productive nonreceipt. Counterpart modal truth does not automatically use the inherited global B semantics. Domain restriction still requires the earlier receipt Back condition if lost supplier witnesses could alter the inference.

## 7. Targeted 2016 control

The singular PSR notation in *Metaphysical Rationalism* does not refute this account: note 9, printed 413, explicitly admits the non-distributive plural option and sets it aside for the paper. Its autonomy definition, printed 383–384, concerns fitness for a grounding question, not merely absence of a supplied singleton ground. Its note 44, printed 415, remains a qualitative-essential proposal without a specified actual autonomous world-state.

No inconsistency between the two papers has been established. Nor has this test provided the full plural PSR/autonomy theory needed to combine them. A purported original bearer cannot be extracted merely by classifying a singly ungrounded individualistic fact as autonomous.

## Earned result

The source has a real joint-grounding proposal and it must be evaluated in that form. The attempted right-distributive mapping is unfaithful. The per-target and whole-field productive routes still require separate metaphysical application premises. The unchanged conditional theorem and its global bearer type survive this appraisal.
