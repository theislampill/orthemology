# Radial quartic gradient reverse lift: intermediate evidence

This additive packet preserves the frozen author proof, exact controls, independent review and antecedent reports. It is intermediate evidence, not integration, protected change, upload, or T20 closure. Unfinished discretisation work is excluded.

## Mathematical scope

The theorem concerns the exact radial quartic generator F(x)=|x|^4/4 and a normalized, full-square-positive, coordinatewise C1 all-polynomial path. It recovers one actual initial probability from the supplied path without a finite-rank or initial-tail assumption. It does not assert that initial square positivity alone gives a representing law, or that initial moments alone select a unique path when the initial moment problem is indeterminate. It does not generalize to arbitrary coercive polynomial gradients or finite/truncated data.

The classical Nussbaum–Carleman existence-and-determinacy theorem is an inherited explicit dependency, not a new proof here: full multisequence positivity and coordinate marginal Carleman conditions give a unique representing measure. The source is Putinar–Schmüdgen, Multivariate Determinateness, arXiv:0810.0840v1, printed p.24, Theorem 25 and following Nussbaum attribution; in one dimension use Hamburger existence and Carleman uniqueness. Exact external source hashes are preserved without paper bodies or page-text redistribution.

The evidence tier is ordinary mathematics plus finite exact symbolic controls. No whole-theorem kernel verification, numerical approximation guarantee, stability result, or empirical/philosophical identification is claimed.
