# Independent review: nonconventional intended indication

7 October 2026. Additional adversarial research, not a closure judgement. Earlier testimony/constitution work and history-v2 remain unchanged and credited. This directory preserves the exact reviewed implicit-sign inputs.

## Result

The new model makes a genuine source-correspondence improvement: it supplies a conditional positive intended-indication route without an explicit earlier credential pact or source endorsement sentence. Intent is a latent planning variable, not a field read by the behavioral observer. The positive conclusion nevertheless depends on the chosen means–end and policy class.

A stronger independently executed rival identifies a specific limitation in that class. The single `communicate` weight binds motivation to correct a false claim to motivation to affirm a true claim. A stable policy with different positive and corrective motives reproduces all four calibration observations **and every action in all 128 Boolean contexts**, without an episode-specific exception. Its selected positive-claim intention differs in eight contexts, including the target. Merely collecting more observations in that same context/action menu cannot separate these two policies.

A concrete new option can discriminate them in the declared models: provide a cheaper, intelligible affirmative gesture that supplies no personal gift. The original communicative policy chooses the gesture; the stable courtesy policy still sends the mantle. This is a proposed conditional probe, not a historical experiment or a universal detector of intent.

## 1. Source control

Re-viewed the actual retained Nubuwwat scan pages 769 and 772, adding to this reviewer's earlier direct inspection of 768 and 770–771. No new programme-page credit is claimed.

Page 769 expressly describes a recognizable personal object sent without a prior token agreement, where known circumstances support the interpretation that it was sent as a sign. Page 772 allows contextually understood gestures answering a question without a prior pact. Pages 770–771 distinguish custody from communicative purpose and acknowledge alternative practical purposes; the suspension condition concerns a strong relevant alternative. These support the author's correction that explicit source instructions are one possible route, not a necessary template for every warranted indication.

No prior token pact does not mean no background language, pragmatic expectations or knowledge of the source. The model appropriately retains such expectations as substantive premises. It neither authenticates the historical examples nor proves that every actual recipient can articulate a planning model. The source's ordinary confidence is not defeated just by writing down a remote logical possibility.

## 2. What is observable, and what remains a supplied model

The designated observation fields contain question/custody/handover/retention/gesture actions and circumstances. Neither the selected plan's aim nor the source-policy weights appear in those observation fields. The complete research output also contains latent plans, policy and situation data for auditing; those must not be passed to the hypothetical recipient as if observed.

In the positive model, changing the communication weight changes the chosen action. This shows that the parameter does explanatory work inside the simulator, rather than being attached after the action as an inert label. It does not itself establish that an actual person's action had this cause. An experimental intervention that literally deletes another person's communication goal is not ordinarily available; the goal ablation is a structural model check. The environmental interventions are a distinct, potentially observable class.

The forward model stipulates that sending a recognizable object in the question context is a feasible means of inducing acceptance. It does not compute an actual audience's belief update or prove its successful uptake. An expectation of intelligibility can exist without a prior credential rule, so this is not automatically a disguised pact. It is an independently needed pragmatic assumption. Intended indication, successful uptake, actual commission, report truth and exceptionless veracity remain different claims.

### Positive check against hidden-belief leakage

The calibration fixtures supply source beliefs. To check whether the result secretly needs direct access to those beliefs, I independently varied the source's belief across all four calibration episodes and the target. There are 96 policies × 32 belief assignments = 3,072 combinations. Conditioning on the observed four calibration actions and target handover leaves 24 combinations across the same three policies. Every surviving target plan has positive communicative intention.

Thus, in this particular regular class, the result can be recovered while treating source belief as hidden rather than observed. It is not necessary to reject the conditional positive because a fixture records an internal belief. The result still depends on the regular planning class and its action/aim structure.

## 3. A stable asymmetric-motive rival

The author's selective-courtesy policy already demonstrates that finite calibration cannot validate every possible extension of its class. The independent control removes the special novel-episode branch and instead separates two ordinary motivations:

- Positive affirmation weight: 0.
- Correcting a believed false claim: 6.
- Giving a personal courtesy gift in response to social attention: 4.
- Repair and warmth: 4 each.

The policy is unchanged across all episode names. When the source believes the claim false, correction wins. When it believes the claim true and receives the recognizable public question, the gift wins without a positive-affirmation goal. When there is no question, the same practical conditions drive repair or warmth. With no relevant demand or need, retention wins.

The existing four calibration actions are reproduced. Exhaustive enumeration of the seven Boolean situation coordinates produces the same action as the original positive policy in all 128 contexts. There are no ambiguous action outputs. Eight contexts, including the actual target, differ in their selected positive-intention sets. In the new policy the purpose can be gifting, repair or warmth; in the original positive policy it is affirmative communication.

This is a controlled means–end rival, not an assertion that a real source has these motives. Its relevance depends on actual character and circumstances. But it is a materially more ordinary class extension than a policy explicitly special-casing the next test. The calibration's false-claim rejection observation does not, by itself, justify tying the strength of negative correction to affirmative communication.

The model target concerns adopting acceptance of the claim as an aim. Whether foreseen uptake, public responsibility, mixed motives or a broader theory of illocution counts as an intended assertion is a separate philosophical/semantic question. Changing that target requires changing both models explicitly; an observer must not silently equate a public communicative effect with one exclusive private motive.

## 4. A new action-menu discriminator

The original model permits retention, mantle sending and negative rejection, but no cheaper affirmative response. That asymmetric menu is another substantive modeling choice. I added one positive option:

- An intelligible affirmative gesture in response to the current question.
- Cost 0.5 versus the mantle's cost 1.
- It provides the communicative effect but no material/personal gift benefit.

At the target, the original positive policy now prefers the gesture, with utility 5.5 rather than 5 for mantle sending. The stable courtesy policy keeps preferring the mantle, with utility 3; the gesture scores −0.5 because positive affirmation is not a goal. The actions therefore discriminate these two policies.

This repair's premises are explicit: the gesture must really be available, intelligible, cheaper and unconnected to the alternative gift goal. If the source is distant, hands constrained, audience unable to see, or a gesture itself provides the relevant courtesy benefit, this specific discriminator can disappear. This is why the actual opportunity set and causal means–end expectations matter alongside character.

The gesture is a possible additional observation, not a fresh universal requirement. Nubuwwat itself permits answering gestures, while its token examples also allow a distant messenger context where a direct gesture would be unavailable. The experiment does not turn every implicit sign into an obligation to obtain a separate explicit endorsement.

## 5. Tie and compatibility semantics

The author's `compatible` predicate requires an observed action to be predicted by **every** maximizing plan. That is robust prediction, stronger than the usual claim that the observation is *possible* under at least one maximizing plan. The distinction matters when a model leaves ties unresolved.

An independent enlarged case with communication weight 1 makes keeping and sending both optimal. Observed sending is possible under the policy, but the current predicate rejects it. This is not a defect in the retained three-survivor calculation: I checked all 12,288 original policy/context combinations and found zero cross-action ties. The original grid's values avoid this distinction.

If future work admits action ties, it should either supply a independently warranted tie-breaking policy or retain every policy under which the observed action is possible. A robust-prediction filter can be useful for prospective guaranteed behavior; it must not silently be treated as ordinary abductive compatibility.

## 6. Defeasible status and source authority

The author's probability-sensitivity section correctly labels its rival weights as supplied and nonempirical. With the same observation likelihood under two policies, the observations do not alter their relative odds. It would be wrong to choose a small rival probability simply to certify the preferred conclusion, or a large rival probability merely because a countermodel was constructed.

The stable rival makes a more specific burden visible: is there ordinary independent reason to connect this source's corrective and affirmative motives, and are courtesy, repair or warmth actually live explanations here? Evidence of character, the source's relationship with the audience, recent circumstances, source knowledge and the available action menu can bear on that question. Such evidence can support ordinary defeasible knowledge without eliminating every logically possible imitation.

Nothing in an inverse-planning result supplies normative authority, divine veracity, legitimate institutional office or adoption of all future reports. Correctly recognizing intended communication is one relevant premise, not a replacement for those distinct questions. Conversely, a source-owned reliable assertion can warrant a new proposition without independent rediscovery of that proposition; this review does not add a redundant certification demand.

## Disposition

- The source correspondence and no-prior-credential positive route are supported conditionally.
- The original 96-policy, three-survivor result independently survives hidden-belief variation.
- A stable, non-episode-indexed rival defeats extending that intention conclusion across the existing action/context menu without additional regularity evidence.
- An expanded action menu provides a specific conditional discriminator for the two compared policies.
- The tie-semantic caveat is prospective; it does not invalidate the original enumeration.
- Historical source authentication, actual policy/character warrant, audience uptake and broader theological or institutional conclusions remain outside these simulations.

No closure approval or recommendation follows. These are further discriminating research results for the owner's later assessment.
