## Bounded primary-source scope

The continuous discussion distinguishes several senses of composition. Assembly by an external composer, prior separate pieces being combined, and liability to division are rejected. Affirmed properties do not by themselves establish those senses. The author also rejects atomist and matter/form analyses and criticizes indiscriminate affirmation or denial of an equivocal technical term. The located quotation concerns the separability/divisibility sense. These are source claims at the specified locus, not a claim that every asserted attribute distinction has been independently analysed.
