## Literal scope

Truth indices J and coordinate indices iota are separate finite types. The supplied data are a finite real truth-vector family v, strictly positive barycentric weights summing to one, and their center p. Each coordinate of p and every repaired c_n is in (0,1). Every c_n must also lie in the actual convex hull of v. The score family is fixed, coordinate-additive and has the inherited smooth positive-curvature assumptions.

The same c_n weakly improves every supplied truth state under every score, up to one nonnegative tolerance eta_n. Positive epsilon_n tends to zero and eta_n/epsilon_n tends to zero. The conclusion is that, for the specified direction h, there exists one tangent vector z such that h-z is Hessian-normal to the hull's tangent span under every score. The exact theorem is the eta=0 specialization.
