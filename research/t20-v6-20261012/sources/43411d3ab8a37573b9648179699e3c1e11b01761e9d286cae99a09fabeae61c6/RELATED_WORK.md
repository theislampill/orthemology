A primary-literature check identified close established ancestry: Richard Pettigrew, *Aggregating incoherent agents who disagree*, published online 16 November 2017, Synthese 196 (2019), 2737–2776, https://doi.org/10.1007/s11229-017-1613-7.

The inspected publisher text, especially Proposition 1, Theorem 10(ii), and Section 8, treats Bregman repair together with linear/geometric opinion pooling. Generalized KL repair is ordinary normalization, and a compatibility characterization selects generalized KL for geometric pooling. The paper also discusses all-world accuracy improvement from Bregman projection and warns that mathematical compatibility alone settles no philosophical conclusion. These are important ancestors of this packet's repair/interface method. [Primary article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11229-017-1613-7).

This packet's exact premises concern a single finite likelihood action, not pooling several people's opinions. Its weak contract uses only single-coordinate attenuations of subprobability inputs and permits coordinate-dependent entropy weights; its genuine-null-experiment contract removes that freedom. Neither distinction establishes field-wide originality. The report is a source-bounded constructive integration, with explicit proofs and a null-experiment countermodel. Do not advertise it as a newly discovered uniqueness of KL, a new proper-scoring theory, or a new general doctrine of Bayesian rationality.

The actual project predecessor is also explicit: Deep AX V2 already supplies joint Bayesian likelihoods, conditional factorization, exact-copy invariance, and the source/world gate. The result here concerns compatibility of that inherited machinery with the Bregman-repair interface.

The brief search was not an exhaustive priority review. No general originality claim is established, and no such claim is needed for the local constructive result.
