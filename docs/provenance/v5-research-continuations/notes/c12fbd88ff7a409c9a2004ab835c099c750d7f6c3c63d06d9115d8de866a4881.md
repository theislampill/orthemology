# Sixth-tranche sparse-prior structural extension

Author research contract, 2026-10-01. This directory is new work. Frozen fifth-tranche sources are read-only predecessors. No historical-priority claim is made.

## New primary theorem: distribution-free relative cluster smoothing

Fix 0<epsilon<=1/4 and 0<=delta<=epsilon/4. Let a_i>0, a_0<=1/2, a_(i+1)<=a_i/2, r_i=a_(i+1)/a_i, L_i=log(1/r_i), Z=sum a_i and S=sum r_i L_i. For every sequence of Borel probability measures nu_i supported on I_i=[(1-delta)a_i,(1+delta)a_i], define mu=sum (a_i/Z) nu_i.

Use the SAME EndpointProcess experiment and report interface: draw a real parameter once from mu, draw an independent probability seed, and generate an infinite iid conditional Bernoulli stream from independent uniform innovations. At every positive physical index n, report after the complete first n receipts. The two inherited literal inequalities decide failure; equality is accepted. No skip, abstention, additional receipt, stopping clock, or latent-parameter input is permitted.

The proposed theorem is the quantitative all-policy/witness sandwich

    exp(-1) S/(9 Z) <= inf_pi E_mu totalFailures(pi)
                         <= E_mu totalFailures(explicit cluster policy)
                         <= 30000 S/Z.

Necessity quantifies over all measurable real full-prefix reports and every fixed independent probability seed. Sufficiency is a single deterministic coherent policy, simultaneously defined at all stages. Extended nonnegative expectations are intended. Thus existence of finite actual expected count is equivalent to S<infinity, uniformly over all component laws nu_i.

The lower proof uses adjacent zero-word mass bounds, but NOT posterior unimodality or an adjacency equality. The upper proof uses an explicit clipped-log cluster selector and one-sided MGF estimates. The formal kernels and ordinary actual-process theorem will be labelled separately.

## New secondary theorem: finite-horizon endpoint crossover

For an arbitrary positive summable weight sequence w_i on a half-separated support, a common MAP report policy simultaneously attains the optimum at every finite horizon. Let C_N(theta) be that optimal actual count for theta delta_0+(1-theta)mu, s=1-theta, and M_n=max_i w_i(1-a_i)^n. Then

    C_N(theta)=s C_N(0)+sum_(n=1)^N min(theta,s M_n).

This formula requires no power-weight or unimodality assumption. For w_i=a_i/Z with finite baseline B=C_infinity(0), define t=theta Z/s and K(N,t)=#{i:1/N<=a_i<=t}. For 0<theta<1 and N>=1,

    s(K(N,t)-2)/Z <= C_N(theta)
      <= s B+s(K(N,t)+4+4 log2)/Z.

The count bounds concern TOTAL optimal cost, not the increment. The exact increment is between zero and theta N. For a_i=exp(-(i+1)^2), K is an explicit difference of square-root-log integer cutoffs; in particular C_N(theta)/sqrt(log N) tends to s/Z for every fixed 0<theta<1.

## Robustness examples and sharp limitations

- Uniform nu_i on I_i for any delta>0 gives an absolutely continuous prior, finite budget whenever S is finite, and infinite inverse moment. This is a genuine extension beyond atomic/comparable-on-the-same-support perturbations
- The component distribution can be adversarial, atomic, continuous, or singular within its fixed interval. The same upper witness does not need to know that distribution
- Endpoint contamination still destroys the finite budget because the interior inverse moment remains infinite. There is finite-horizon continuity and lifetime discontinuity, in the identical experiment
- Arbitrary weights can destroy posterior unimodality; nearest-neighbour adjacency identities must not be promoted without hypotheses
- The interval width must be justified. Unrestricted additive smoothing can fill the endpoint gaps or introduce endpoint mass and invalidate the guarantee
- This is an ordinary statistical theorem plus separately itemised Lean kernels and exact finite controls. It is not a verified executable, latency guarantee, generic robustness result, or almost-surely infinite-error conclusion
