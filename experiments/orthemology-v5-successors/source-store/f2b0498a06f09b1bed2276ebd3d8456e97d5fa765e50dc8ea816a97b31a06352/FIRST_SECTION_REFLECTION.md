## Tawhid printed 87–98 fully visually read

Direct local scan reading,12 consecutive full page images at 110 dpi, from the independently acquired CSIIslam scan. All Arabic authorial text and apparatus visible on each page were read. The exact scan URL and SHA-256 are given below; the printed-page ranges identify the bounded visual controls. This is a bounded second reading of corresponding E091–102, not a whole-volume visual read or a newly independent manuscript witness. It newly certifies these print locators relative to T19's extraction-only reading. The scan and electronic body are very close here and likely share the same modern edition ancestry; they are not independent historical witnesses.

The important result is stronger than a generic 'cooperation is dependence': printed 95 expressly permits a cooperator to be capable of acting alone in another situation. It argues that actual whole production and actual partial contribution differ, then printed 96 classifies the nonexercise of the whole power alongside another contributor as inhibition. Printed 97 directly rejects the ordinary contrary-wills inference as sufficient by itself to exclude cooperation. Printed 98 accommodates necessary concord in the premise space and routes it to nonduplication/perfection rather than denying it by stipulation.

This strengthens the historical attribution and narrows the live philosophical burden. The author knows the standing-power/actual-exercise distinction. The remaining issue is whether distinct agents' actual contributions necessarily subtract from an ultimate agent's intrinsic causal power or fit exercise, and whether a complete cause must exclude any nonexclusive co-sufficiency. The examples genuinely support partitioned contributions in familiar cooperative events. They do not by themselves compare every possible modal profile of cooriginal agents. Conversely that lack of exhaustive comparison does not erase the evidential value of familiar integrated order and the coherent common-source explanation.

Several awkward clauses are confirmed in the scan: printed 90's sibling/parent comparison lacks an expected contrast marker; printed 92 states the naqd example in the same-looking terms as absence-of-effect; printed 93's disjunction introduces a first case without fully stating both branches; printed 96 contains compressed/defective wording about a single power; printed 98's indistinguishable effect sentence is awkward. No amendment is made. The lucid printed 95–98 argument is cited at its actual scope, without demanding that defective ancillary sentences bear its decisive weight.

## Cross-section checks E183–186 and E211–216

Both complete electronic windows were directly read without truncation through their END markers: 646c338d75187bf7e7290a72a6688ae2fa8c501fb56b001d70807f2d2b2467fd and aad53f4bfc87f9f149256386f61581dc4548b8a9ea890d8851ad7ed9f4450f72. These are first-section reader readings of passages already covered in the middle reader's continuous assignment. They add no unique body coverage.

E183–186 rules out derivation of an agent's needed power from a partner who has that very power only by borrowing it back. It expressly allows joint realisation, supplied tools and strength generated by the cooperation itself. At E185 each partner has their own power, awareness, intention and end not derived from the other; attainment requires both intentions and acts together. The dual phrase refers naturally to the two described cooperators, not to an emended two-item list out of their four features. E186's 'with, not through' and 'with, not for its sake' give the exact explanatory/conditional distinction. These are positive authorial concessions, but do not say the cooperators are uncreated or that any arbitrary cooperative model meets complete-divinity criteria.

E211–216 adds a broader causal lexicon. A complete cause includes the requisite conditions and absence of blockers; a contributing cause may fail through obstruction or a missing condition. The text claims divine will alone is unconditionally complete, but allows real created efficacy and attacks reducing 'by means of' to mere simultaneous occurrence 'at'. The action/reception distinction is explicit: teaching without learning, burning-attempt without burning, striking-to-break without breakage. If one uses an accomplishment verb to include both agent contribution and patient reception, the sufficient complex contains both. These distinctions must control the language of independence throughout the appraisal.

The first-section reader has not visually collated every word of these ten pages. The middle reader separately performed the full visual 179–182 check; its receipts own that credit.

## Actual-order argument printed 125–129

These five full scan pages were reread visually. They confirm the difference between causal non-occurrence and normatively corrupted occurrence at 126, followed by a positive ordered-motion inference at 127–129. The author does not merely say alternatives are logically imaginable and stop; he argues that actual celestial regularity has the appropriate ultimate direction and a common creative mover. But the discriminating middle premises include that circular motion cannot be natural, that it must be voluntary, that the uppermost sphere's simple motion cannot yield varied motions, and that all nondivine ultimate ends corrupt motion. These are historically specific physics and strong causal/value principles. A contemporary common-Creator inference cannot inherit their confirmation merely from successful reading.

There are therefore two distinct questions. First, does any qualified original necessarily have a complete, exclusionary source mode? The volume argues for this through nonduplication, unimpeded exercise and ultimate worth, with the burdens recorded above. Second, is one common Creator of the actual connected order a sufficiently warranted comparative explanation? That need not exclude every conceivable rival deductively. Coordination, systematic interdependence, real derivative agency and coherent unity of ends supply affirmative support. My present assessment of this volume alone is that the second claim remains underdetermined too, but for a DIFFERENT reason: the actual discriminating argument is heavily carried by those substantive physics/causal/value premises, while the shared practical examples show the intelligibility of both common coordination and role-differentiated cooperation. Merely counting one source as simpler does not establish its explanatory advantage or the full personal/perfect profile. This does not count logical non-exclusion alone as defeat and does not deny genuine positive evidence for a common explanation. It is a bounded appraisal of this source, not a survey of all the evidence in earlier volumes or a verdict against basic recognition.

## Four full-page legal cross-checks

Printed/PDF 335,350,359 and 411 were directly inspected using the already retained high-resolution reader images. These are separate bounded first-section reader checks, not additional unique whole-body coverage.

335 distinguishes reward assigned for intention from reward due to the act itself. A statement comparing rewards does not by itself establish legal validity or permission; sitting prayer and solitary prayer still require separately warranted permissions. 350 argues that an act performed within a legally available time does not need a special intention to confer that availability; conversely mere intention cannot create a permitted time. Requiring followers to know an imam's prior intention may defeat the very concession and communal purpose at issue.

359 explicitly rejects simple defect-counting: two apparently lesser departures do not necessarily rank below one. Praying in a legally shared time and using a prescribed purification substitute differ qualitatively from performing in an intrinsically prohibited place. This is a historical ritual-law ranking, not a general theorem about divine perfection; it shows why the relevant type of limitation, not only its number, must be made explicit.

411 verifies the bodily-labour/capital partnership argument. Shared yield is compatible with materially different contributions; fixed-service hiring and partnership have different information/risk structures. The transmitted parenthetical 'then he said' and further omitted examples remain visible. This legal text supports disciplined classification of cooperation, not an inference that all partners possess equal power or can each independently produce the same complete effect.
