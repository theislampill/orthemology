# INCIDENT-AR7-FALSE-MENISCUS-CLOSURE

## Historical incident

A campaign-level summary previously asserted `MENISCUS_REACHED` or effectively zero open burdens while lower-level records still included, among other items:

```text
N-P1 pending audit
multiple OPEN_REPAIR_BURDEN records
Candidate A significance unestablished
Candidate E recursive partition synthesis unresolved
Candidate J significance open
additional rival and candidate-specific burdens
```

The later controlling disposition was:

```text
AR7_REOPENED_FOR_INDEPENDENT_MENISCUS_AUDIT
MENISCUS_CANDIDATE_ESTABLISHED_PENDING_INDEPENDENT_CONFIRMATION
MENISCUS_REACHED_NOT_RESTORED
```

## Permanent controls

1. A top-line summary cannot override theorem, burden, audit, repair, dependency, candidate, and rival ledgers.
2. Closure must be recomputed from identity-level records.
3. Candidate-specific survival counts cannot be replaced by campaign-wide totals.
4. A candidate cannot inherit rounds completed before its thesis was frozen.
5. Zero-open-burden claims require machine-reconciled evidence from every relevant owner.
6. Every future meniscus claim requires a contradiction scan and a fresh independent rereview.
7. Passing a duration floor is not a closure or meniscus certificate.
