## The proposed argument in exact stages

### E0  Actual exercise

Claim: a genuine actual correction or other rational exercise occurs.

Warrant: the admitted actual case. A truthfully identified exercise is a real occurrence, not merely a representation or a possible template.

Gain: actual reality is present before the source inference. This avoids one abstract-to-concrete transition for the initial witness.

Limit: neither its truth nor its received existential history follows just from its occurrence. No assistant consciousness claim or observational experiment is asserted here.

### E1  True evaluation and local reflection

Claim: a genuinely true evaluation has the objective conditions needed for that truth; a critique of its alleged total self-generation preserves its actual token and productive respect.

Warrant: truth adequacy and the exact admitted target, with Decision 0010's L/O/R distinctions.

Gain: a local, target-specific reflection demand is easier to warrant than global interpretation of an arbitrary formal process.

Limit: this is not a new acceptance of B1/H1 for Track T as a whole. Intrinsic-order rivals that deny total generation survive.

### E2  Real producer for this received exercise

Claim: a newly occurring exercise with received productive efficacy needs a real productive source, and an actual producer must exist.

Warrant: the particular dependence judgment, the distinction between an actual power and an uninstantiated condition, and the source's treatment of particular causal knowledge in Darʾ 3:119–121.

Gain: B2 and B5 can be approached together for this concrete causal target, rather than by reifying O and later seeking its instantiation.

Limit: the received character is substantive. The occurrence of a thought does not prove its own ultimate source or independently unborrowed efficacy.

### E3  Original productive possession

Claim: complete ultimate production cannot consist exclusively of endlessly received efficacy with no original possession or defensible holistic productive substitute.

Warrant: actual supply versus conditional transmission; the carrying-forward of productive need; complete agency in Darʾ /005 lines 59–64.

Gain: an affirmative ontology of productive completion, rather than a bare minimal-vertex postulate. It does not prohibit temporal beginninglessness or all infinite substructures.

Limit: this is the key metaphysical challenge to a genuinely non-well-founded productive ontology. The countable chain is consistent with weaker local premises. Its exclusion is explanatory unless the stronger completion doctrine is accepted.

### E4  Complete coverage yields independence

Claim: a particular actual productive ancestor g covers the full declared productive ancestry of x.

Additional structural conditions: ancestry is transitive and irreflexive in the same productive respect.

Checked consequence: g has no productive predecessor. No global well-foundedness premise occurs.

Gain: independence is derived from an informative scope condition. A covering source and a non-well-founded subordinate ancestry are jointly satisfiable in the checked model.

Limit: existence of a covering g is not proved. If ancestry includes endpoint-redundant actual causes, coverage is history-inclusive. Bare endpoint sufficiency does not supply it. The theorem uses a new ancestry predicate, not an asserted instantiation of every accepted ProductiveCompleteness premise.

### E5  Actual root yields necessary existence

Additional premise: every actual contingent bearer in the source-complete domain receives existentially.

Warrant offered: a contingent nature does not supply its own actual instantiation merely by being compatible with it; the Aṣfahāniyya's positive intrinsic-sufficiency reading.

Checked consequence: the same actual root exists at every represented alternative.

Limit: a brute contingent root rejects this explanatory premise without a formal contradiction. Conditional nature possession cannot be promoted to unconditional existence. The inference itself is prior SourceIdentity work, copied unchanged rather than claimed as new.

### E6  The same source is uniformly underived

Additional premise: generic essential reception, with fixed individual identity and the declared cross-world scope.

Warrant offered: genuine existential reception is constitutive of how the individual has actuality, not merely an accidental external accompaniment. Specific productive parents may vary.

Checked consequence: actual roothood plus necessary existence gives uniform roothood.

Limit: a bearer received at one world and autonomous at another contests this essentialist claim. Actual-only preservation is too weak. A root-specific backward-transfer restatement would be equivalent to the sought conclusion and add no independent warrant.

### E7  Universal common source and uniqueness

Additional premise: original actuality is borne by one source whose productive coverage includes the whole relevant dependent field, not only e's cone.

Warrant offered here: clarify the plural target, preserve all real productive prerequisites, and require common coverage to be earned rather than inferred from explanatory uniformity.

Gain: the totality question does not require a separate aggregate or joint possibility of all absences. This answers a reification objection.

Limit: distributive explanations do not entail a common bearer. The two-active-source model defeats local-to-global migration. The accepted uniqueness argument still requires HistoryComplete, UnborrowedActs and an actual ProductiveWitness. This extension neither deletes those premises nor claims they follow from the ancestry theorem.

### E8  Essential interpretation and attributes

Claim: intrinsic necessity and source-independence can coexist with real intrinsic attributes and contingent exercises.

Warrant: Darʾ 3:110–112 and Aṣfahāniyya's distinction between external efficient production and inseparable intrinsic features.

Gain: no Thomistic pure-act or universal no-composition premise is needed for the upstream inference.

Limit: no new Knowledge inference is made here. The separately frozen noninferential clarification governs the earlier Knowledge appendix.

## Rival dispositions

- An uninstantiated rule offered as a complete actual producer: excluded as a mismatch of explanatory role
- Complete same-token self-production in one strict productive respect: excluded under the retained no-go and faithful interpretation
- Actual received power confused with a wholly powerless relay: this caricature is rejected; the serious rival retains real power
- An actually realized countably infinite derivative chain: preserves the weaker premises; challenged by E3/E4, not contradicted by local causation or modal necessity
- A brute contingent actual base: disputes E5 and complete existential explanation; no formal impossibility claimed
- A necessary intrinsically productive concrete base: may grant E2–E6; impersonality and global integration remain separate
- Several productive independent components: refutes E7 if only local coverage was established
- A world-dependent sequence of source witnesses: insufficient for the fixed-bearer conclusion
- Inseparable attributes alleged to imply an external maker: the source texts reject that inference
