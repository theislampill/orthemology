## Blocking findings

### AL-F01 — desired predicates were counted as explained benefits

A predicate included in Architecture A but not independently derived is a target
import or open bridge, not explanatory coverage.

### AL-F02 — source compatibility and neutral explanatory support were mixed

Track-N source coherence is a source-regime benefit.  It cannot be counted as a
neutral derivational advantage.

### AL-F03 — primitive counts were invented from labels

`one nature`, `one power field`, and `abstract order plus actualizer` do not
carry canonical primitive counts.  The comparison must use identified
independent assumptions, not names or bearer counts.

### AL-F04 — a scalar ranking was imposed without an aggregation rule

Coverage, derivation, primitive burden, source commitment, target import, and
world bridges are typed coordinates.  No canonical weighting is authorized.

### AL-F05 — unresolved coordinates were treated as negative facts

Open H7/H8, proper-function, source-world, and rival-realizability burdens are
not established falsehoods.  The architecture matrix needs an explicit
`OPEN/UNKNOWN` status.

### AL-F06 — formal satisfiability was overread as metaphysical possibility

B/C common models show nonentailment in declared classes.  They do not establish
world actuality or equal prior probability.

### AL-F07 — source-extended B/C rivals were omitted

Deep AK showed that B1 and C1 can carry the selected source role bundle while
withholding an independently established modern personality predicate.

### AL-F08 — Candidate G's parity theorem was not applied consistently

Carrier unity earns no credit unless it reduces primitive freedom, derives a
new constraint, or excludes a joint profile under independently motivated laws.

### AL-F09 — established common neutral structure was understated

Deep AE/AG provide nonseparable cross-domain structure shared by personal and
impersonal expansions.  This is real integration but not a personal discriminator.

### AL-F10 — the architecture comparison lacked a robust-completion test

A champion should not depend on one favorable completion of open burdens.  The
packet must test all declared completions of its unresolved key coordinates.

### AL-F11 — source/world and owner-adoption ceilings were absent

No current proposal result is repository adoption, source authentication,
world truth, or owner adoption.

### AL-F12 — champion/meniscus overreach

No architecture has a proved exclusive central discriminator or has defeated
the strongest matched rival.  The correct eligible result may be
`RIVAL_PARITY_UNBROKEN`, not a champion.
