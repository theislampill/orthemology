## Begin with the question, not the archive

Orthemology's [main exposition][main] distinguishes a concrete case, the repeatable state-type used to describe it, and the governing standard bound to that case. Its question is not only whether a conclusion is correct, but whether the reasoning and handling that produced it were adequate under the declared analysis. The [formal reference](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#formal-reference) develops the framework and a separate multi-actor component.

The wider research cannot be reduced to an audit checklist. The sample below includes an affirmative account of recognition and correction, ordinary tensor-moment theorems, differential geometry, quantitative coordination limits and a connection between linguistic interpretation and empirical feasibility. These have different hypotheses and source roles. Reading one does not confer acceptance on all the others.

## How the principal works fit together

Begin with the [main exposition](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#main-exposition) for the concrete classification-and-correction lifecycle, then the [formal reference](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#formal-reference) for its record and composition contracts. The positive proposal is to preserve both the answer and the way it was produced, so that the repair addresses the actual defect. It is not a demand to formalise every thought before recognising anything.

The [ground-of-intelligibility companion](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#ground-of-intelligibility) asks a different question: what must be presupposed for true evaluation and correction to be possible at all? The [dynamic companion](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#dynamic-orthability) distinguishes available correction from an actor's ability to discover and learn it. The [Atharī companion](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#atharī-companion) adds its expressly revelational, creed-internal account of divine Speech and created reception. These are connected research questions, not successive levels of software certification.

**A substantive connection between avenues 1, 2, 3 and 8:** the explicit evaluation conditions studied by semantic fidelity make visible—but do not themselves settle—the norm-fixing and source questions. The principal arguments separate local rules, objective evaluability and creaturely representations. The step from these distinctions to a productive ground or to an attribute requires the premise named by that argument; the step from capacity for disclosure to actual Speech requires the companion's separate revelational basis. The [catalogue routes](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#philosophical-and-theological-manuscripts) expose both the positive arguments and the qualifications, rather than treating the avenue names as the connection.

## Recognition, sound cognition and correction

**Question:** How can a person recognise something correctly without first proving that every cognitive faculty is reliable, and what can reasoning contribute when recognition is obstructed?

The [Sound Reason substrate][sound] offers a positive answer. Its [basic-cognition account][sound-basic] and [proper-function account][sound-function] ground knowledge in truth-directed faculties operating in an appropriate environment, without requiring first-person access to an endlessly prior proof of their reliability. Its [corrective-reasoning account][sound-correction] gives argument a constructive role: clarify, remind and remove obstructions to recognition.

In its [broader use of **tawātur**][sound-tawatur], diverse, widespread and non-collusive concordance of rational and fiṭrī deliverances vindicates cognitive functioning itself. That is a substantive epistemological thesis, not merely a count of copied reports. The source argues it within its own account; this guide does not convert that account’s claims about all cultures or unimpaired cognisers into a newly verified empirical survey.

The [concrete/sound distinction note][concrete] contributes a different precision: actual instantiation is not the same predicate as epistemic soundness, and a correct output is not sufficient for an adequate truth-linked pathway. The [later retained-passage qualification][retained] keeps affirmative recognition and foundational appraisals while separating them from infallibility in every exercise and from additional productive-source premises.

**What the connection contributes:** recognition, cognitive vindication and correction form a substantive epistemic account. A source-dependence graph alone does not supply that whole account. These texts' philosophical assertions, source qualifications and stated premises remain their own; this reader layer neither authenticates all underlying primary passages nor diagnoses individuals as cognitively impaired.

### A positive foundational route

The [retained-passage report, section 2][retained-productive], explains a productive-adequacy argument: if the existence and productive actuality of an entire field are received, merely collecting its members—even without a first temporal member—does not supply an unreceived productive basis. The target is the wholly received field, not a fallacious inference that a cooperating whole must be as ineffective as each participant. The same report expressly allows real contributions and gains from cooperation.

This is the report’s affirmative source-bound route, not a new proof issued by this guide. Its premises concern actual receipt and sufficient production; their warranted application matters. The report also distinguishes a route whose bare conclusion is a Necessary Being from the later identification of the actual common Creator. Its later positive appraisals remain at their recorded, defeasible scopes; this distinction does not reset them to a blanket unresolved status. Nor does recognition have to wait for every person to traverse this particular proof.

Read the [three related works](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#recognition-and-correction), then the [remaining interpretation questions](CURRENT-FRONTIERS.md#recognition-and-foundational-application).

## Tensor histories and information geometry

**Question:** What information must a representation retain for a declared dynamical or geometric operation to remain correct?

The [tensor-moment work][tensor] studies full symmetric polynomial-moment records and their evolution under polynomial damping. Its [coercive-polynomial successor][coercive] gives a stronger written representability and continuation result under the stated all-polynomial square-positivity and regularity assumptions. This is actual conditional mathematics, not a proposal to give a metaphorical tensor shape to a mind. The complete path can identify an initial law even where the initial moment record alone does not identify a path.

The [finite-audit Fisher work][fisher] supplies an actual metric and gradient system on a positive joint probability law. Retaining conditional distributions and their mixture can discard source weights needed to preserve full ambient geometric observables. The [finite-sidecar corollary][sidecar] makes that loss precise on a fixed continuous audit-weight fibre.

**What these works contribute:** they specify different operation-preservation contracts, show when a retained representation is sufficient, and identify information that an exact supplement must retain. They do not assert that all interpretations need the strongest contract. A posterior-only task is weaker than preserving ambient Fisher norms.

The [selected results](RESULT-INDEX.md#tensor-moments) state the source hypotheses and distinguish the full moment path, finite records, ambient geometry and proposed noetic transfer.

## Coordination and information requirements

**Question:** How much information must a finite coordination record carry to explain sustained capture against increasingly dispersed noise?

For a fixed horizon, the [bounded coordination paper][coordination] gives an exact finite-alphabet Gaussian capture bound and a matching construction. Under its complete-mediation assumption, maintaining a fixed positive capture probability needs a mediator alphabet growing at least on the order of the square root of the horizon.

The point is not to count the bytes in a controller program. A program description or instantaneous readout need not include the entire mediator: side information and transcripts can matter. See the [finite-mediator result](RESULT-INDEX.md#finite-mediator) and its [application boundary](APPLICATIONS.md#coordination-records).

## A connection across research avenues

**Question:** Why is combining separately admissible answers sometimes an invalid way to construct a whole answer?

The [occurrence-feasibility join][join] connects **avenue 11, occurrence-sensitive language**, with **avenue 12, empirical and specialist validation**. In the selected linguistic records, complete source-relative interpretations form a restricted relation; multiplying the coordinate marginals creates unattested complete readings. In the missing-outcome problem, individually possible coordinate values need not form a jointly feasible endpoint.

**The common contribution is to retain the admissible joint relation before projection or optimisation.** A Cartesian relaxation may be useful as an explicitly labelled outer bound, but its extrema need not be attainable. The linguistic and statistical applications still need their own meanings, witnesses and justified feasible sets. One does not establish the other's empirical dependence or interpretative truth.

Follow the [worked comparison](APPLICATIONS.md#joint-feasibility-across-avenues) or the [underlying works](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#occurrence-and-feasible-completion).

## Finite observation, histories and rational correction

The [Twelfth work family](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#fresh-histories-access-and-source-warrant-the-twelfth-checkpoint) connects probability mathematics with the question of what an epistemic task actually requires. Its [fresh-history construction][t12-fresh] permits every sample position to be finite while every bounded position interval is null. Its [access analysis][t12-access] then identifies a positive, task-specific route to bounded capture: justified prospective success plus an appropriate resource tail. Coherence of a law, effective acquisition, justified reliance and actual sourcehood are different questions, but the papers do more than separate labels: they supply inequalities, constructions and a reporting protocol that connect those questions under explicit hypotheses.

The [total integer reporter][t12-integer] shows why an effective procedure is not automatically a tight-resource guarantee. The [policy clarification][t12-clarification] shows why a dominated course of action is not an impossible supporting architecture. The [worked finite-deadline example](APPLICATIONS.md#finite-deadlines-and-the-no-report-branch) makes the epistemic difference visible: omitting the residual information state changes the decision problem. This is a substantive connection between avenue 10's measure/effectivity distinctions and the source-specific epistemic and foundational applications—not evidence that a mathematical state variable has already been identified with real human cognition.


## Noetic correction, learning and collective operation

The [application studies](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#application-studies-contracts-and-runtime-outlines) connect the general framework to concrete analysis of discourse, subject-relative acquisition, runtime control and learning. Their contribution is not to identify a person with a transcript or to turn a numerical optimiser into an epistemic authority. The multi-target account separates the bearer of each claim; the represented-standard account separates a carried rule from its wording and case binding; the corrective example shows an addressed defect with a legitimately unresolved dependency.

These distinctions connect **objective norm-fixing (avenue 3)** and **semantic fidelity (avenue 8)**: admissibility under a configured standard, truth linkage, accurate source representation and a subject's warrant must not collapse into one score. The [worked HOLD route](APPLICATIONS.md#a-corrective-step-that-legitimately-ends-in-hold) explains a positive local repair and its retained limits. Applying it requires the actual targets, standards, evidence and dependencies, not just the avenue labels.

The [learning route](APPLICATIONS.md#learning-without-silently-changing-the-analysis) asks which earlier results survive inference, model change, repertoire revision or a new analysis. The [collective outlines](APPLICATIONS.md#agentic-coordination-and-writeback-outlines) distinguish shared information from transferred authority and preserve dissent. These are source-owned application contracts and proposals at their stated stages; the later [tensor/Fisher mathematics](#tensor-histories-and-information-geometry) supplies different, genuinely mathematical preservation questions whose noetic interpretation needs its own binding.

## When representations preserve answers but lose proofs

**Question:** If two descriptions evaluate to the same object, may a proof or type certificate be moved from one description to the other?

The [opaque-atom study][t13-opaque] supplies a negative example for a particular representation at a particular type. The [atom/application bridge][t13-bridge] shows a positive, separately specified repair for closed conversion. Neither result by itself answers whether *any* proof exists of every semantically true identity.

The next stage asks that stronger question. The [historical I/SKK note][t13-residual] distinguishes a semantic witness from a missing direct introduction rule; the [Fourteenth full-rule development][t14-separation] then proves noninhabitation through every rule, not just the obvious route. The [separate HasE development][t14-repair] restores the missed typed identity with two stated extensionality rules while retaining the old judgments and a `Raw`-carrier obstruction.

**The connection across avenues 8, 9 and 10 is substantive:** semantic fidelity determines which representation/equality contract a certificate must respect; the exact type-theoretic rules determine what counts as a derivation; and the [certificate/search analysis][t14-certificates] distinguishes checking supplied finite evidence from deciding whether evidence exists. Its closed-identity criterion enables a computability reduction rather than automatically supplying an algorithm.

The shared lesson is not that semantic claims are unusable or that formalisation is futile. It is to state precisely what is preserved, by which operation, under which judgment. A typed extensional repair can be useful without changing raw conversion or proving universal completeness. Read the [work sequence](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#identity-representations-and-finite-proof), [scoped results](RESULT-INDEX.md#representation-identity-and-proof-search) and [dated frontier distinctions](CURRENT-FRONTIERS.md#identity-obstructions-repairs-and-later-checkers).

## From general proof limits to a complete restricted service

**Question:** Which exact representation makes identity verification both semantically adequate and executable?

The [Fifteenth sequence](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#six-papers-in-the-fifteenth-mathematical-compendium) connects the earlier identity distinctions to exact classification and constructive algorithms. Certified Boolean endpoints have finite negative witnesses but no universal computable distinguishing-input bound; bare syntax adds a separate typability problem. Yet a declared finite grammar of natural arithmetic and zero tests admits a complete mask/coefficient checker and finite certificates, with an actual recorded Lean semantic-completeness theorem. A general obstruction and a restricted positive construction can therefore both hold.

This connects **avenue 8's semantic fidelity, avenue 9's exact calculus and avenue 10's effective procedures**. The contribution is to specify the service: endpoint syntax, certificate format, semantic relation and admitted expression grammar must match. Extending zero tests to equality tests changes the general problem again, while a one-variable subcase remains decidable. A finite Python/Lean comparison helps qualify an implementation but does not identify two wire formats or prove a parser. The [scoped result sequence](RESULT-INDEX.md#fifteenth-identity-classifications-and-constructive-subcases) keeps those distinctions and the positive algorithms visible.

## Collective competence and discriminating observations

**Question:** What additional observation could distinguish genuine use of two evidence inputs from a successful answer that used only one?

The [collective-observation paper](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#what-final-answers-can-identify) supplies a concrete connection between **avenue 6's warranted collective diagnosis and avenue 12's empirical validation**. Two constructed mechanisms can have the same whole final-answer law and different intervention behaviour. A linked passive observation can separate their laws when the required conditioning, freshness and error model hold; final answers alone cannot supply that distinction for the matched pair.

The positive contribution is an observation contract and statistical decision services, not just a warning about underdetermination. Known separation supports finite error bounds; without it, a one-sided sequential detector has a different lawful stopping promise. The [worked applications](APPLICATIONS.md#designing-a-collective-observation-without-claiming-attribution) explain the role of the extra observation and the no-null-answer boundary. Applying the mathematics to actual groups still requires a warranted model and observation design; that bridge is not supplied by the group's success rate.

## What a demonstrated success supports

The [Fifteenth programme report](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#fifteenth-programme-report) connects several avenues through a precise question: *what has this evidence established about this subject?* A measured group improvement is a genuine performance finding even when the contributing members remain unidentified. Likewise, a supported construction role can be renewed without having established preservation of a person's identity, an entire stateful organisation or independence from continuing infrastructure.

The [empirical reanalysis work](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#portable-reanalysis-and-the-paired-comparison) therefore complements, rather than supplies the participant data for, the [collective-observation mathematics](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#what-final-answers-can-identify). This connects **avenue 6's collective diagnosis** with **avenue 12's empirical evidence**, and the report's continuation assessments connect **avenues 5 and 7**. In each case the extra attribution needs evidence addressing the extra claim; the narrower positive result is not discarded.

For norm-fixing, the same report's [local-standard assessment](../../experiments/orthemology-v5-successors/source-store/69051582e889b364891ee1e6b59782fe025c0334eaa423e9fd822e5f256f08ee/Orthemology_Fifteenth_Research_Final_v1_20261005.md#a-proposal-for-natural-standards) offers an affirmative criterion of fittingness without equating a local norm, warranted belief about it, practical authority and identification of its ultimate source. Read it as a dated, witness-specific contribution alongside the existing [recognition and foundational route](#recognition-sound-cognition-and-correction), not as a replacement for later research.

## What an inquiry must preserve

The [composition and revision manuscripts](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#information-and-composition) connect representation sufficiency, observation and permitted correction. A joint property can be compatible with local records without following from them. A method can sometimes repair its access, but a useful first observation is not enough when the action destroys another distinction needed for the original question.

This gives a concrete bridge between restoration, provenance-resilient continuation and semantic fidelity (avenues 4, 5 and 8): preserve the target-relevant alternatives through the permitted inquiry, not merely the latest output. Applying that bridge requires a justified state/observation model, the relevant initial-state target and the actual permissions. The finite priced-probe account has a **non-destructive/resettable** contract; the composition paper's labelled-belief account treats state-changing operations. They cannot be substituted for one another without establishing the additional premises. See the [worked contrast](APPLICATIONS.md#choose-an-inquiry-without-erasing-its-target).

## From observation refinement to warranted reuse

Avenues **5, 8 and 10** meet in the original v5 convergence work: a representation may preserve each finite observation's guarantee yet lose an unrestricted guarantee at an infinite observation, while a relevant source revision can invalidate an issued licence even when the underlying inequality remains true. The [three mathematical extensions](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#observation-refinement-and-the-infinite-residual) provide exact semantic debt, an effective-presentation boundary and an operational reuse criterion—not one overloaded pass flag.

The constructive side matters: coherent finite debt is computable in appropriate restricted models; finite-state observers admit an exact decision procedure; unrelated revisions preserve a properly bound licence. Transferring these results to a cognitive or research system requires its actual observation laws, event repertoire, complete dependency reads and operational interpretation. No amount of version bookkeeping alone supplies those premises.

## Productive sourcehood and truthful action selection

The [Sixth sourcehood/Knowledge family](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#original-productive-sourcehood-knowledge-and-action-selection) begins with an actual corrective exercise and asks what supplies its received productive and cognitive actuality. Original productive possession, one complete covering bearer, necessary existence and cognitive adequacy are related but distinct questions. The positive original-giver argument compares the cognitive excellence itself, not only a source's output capacity. Complete creative self-Knowledge then supplies a separately qualified route to Knowledge of effects; its logical consequence is not divine acquisition by inference.

A concrete connection spans source/attribute questions and restoration: [truthful safe-action disclosure](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#truthful-disclosure-that-selects-a-safe-action) can remove an intervention obstruction left by correct diagnosis. It requires integrity knowledge, truthful transmission and preserved action identity. That mathematics does not itself authenticate the proposed source or establish its actual communication. It shows what the additional epistemic relation contributes rather than treating “disclosure” as an interchangeable label for provenance.

## From a warranted correction to its original adequacy

The [necessity and attribute family](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#necessity-objective-correction-and-attribute-ascent) develops a connection across avenues 1, 2, 3 and 4. A judgement's fittingness to its target, its epistemic warrant and the reality of the process delivering it are distinct aspects of one actual correction. The positive source argument asks for adequate original production of that whole occurrence, not just a relabelling of its outcome. The [necessity argument](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#original-actuality-and-existential-necessity) then makes its additional modal burden visible; the [world-bridge family](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#warranted-correction-and-complete-actual-origination) distinguishes completed effects from their whole actual origination.

The [norm-fixing work](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#objective-norm-fixing-and-warranted-common-repair) supplies immediate constructive value: justified target-preserving repairs can compose, under stated semantic and dependency conditions, without awaiting global agreement on every foundational question. [Agency, Wisdom and disclosure](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#original-sourcehood-agency-wisdom-and-disclosure) concern further real perfections and warrants, not merely more powerful classification machinery. These are connected affirmative arguments with separate premises, not a claim that a successful finite controller proves every conclusion in the ascent.

## Diagnosis, safe action and disclosure resources

The [restoration/disclosure family](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#safe-restoration-and-the-exact-cost-of-action-disclosure) connects restoration and provenance with collective diagnosis, identity and computability (avenues 4–7 and 10). Knowing which whole rule is wrong, having a persistent safe repair path and possessing a sufficiently informative message are different achievements. The positive fail-silent construction gives restoration without complete fault identification, under justified containment; the exact codebook theorems quantify the price of limited action-directed information.

The progression from [two messages](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#an-exact-two-message-frontier), through the [three-message family](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#three-messages-and-four-corrupt-roots), to the [complete classification](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#the-complete-fixed-codebook-classification) preserves both constructive results and their changing proof dependencies. [Timing](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#when-the-information-and-corruption-arrive) explains why preserving report bytes or root identities is insufficient when the adversary gains different information. Operational provenance is therefore more than a label, while philosophical sourcehood and truthful revelation still require their own arguments.

## Correction geometry becomes a condition on access

Avenues **3, 4, 5 and 8** connect in the [continuation repair programme](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#continuation-repair-geometry-and-information). It first asks when multiple fixed score systems can share a correction, then asks what information a controller must acquire to produce that correction without already knowing the true system. The answer is substantive: compatible geometries admit a common constructive repair; certain accepted repairs reveal a hidden norm parameter; a self-verifying action support can continue acquiring useful evidence while remaining eventually correct.

The corresponding failures have different causes. Incompatible geometries obstruct a common action; target-bad information acquisition obstructs eventual restoration; a changed representation or norm identity can invalidate the transfer itself. The [generated binary experiment](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#a-generated-experiment-joins-score-repair-and-causal-control) gives one fully specified positive mathematical composition. None of these results identifies its modeled norm with a warranted norm of actual human cognition merely by fitting a loss function.

## Three different ways a correction can succeed

The [critical repair and audit-cost sequence](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#critical-repair-safe-certificates-and-the-cost-of-learning) makes three contracts explicit. **Geometric existence** asks whether a common correction exists for the actual joint truth set. **Safe certification** asks whether every issued report meets the declared score guarantee with a controlled error probability; it can abstain. **Eventual restoration** permits early mistakes while converging to permanently correct reports. Its physical mistake count can have a different expectation under each parameter and under a prior.

This connects norm-fixing, restoration, semantic fidelity and empirical validation—avenues **3, 4, 8 and 12**—through actual mathematical operations, not labels. A complete local derivative record can still lose the exact smooth repair answer; a loss expectation can improve while every realised report fails; and changing when reports are counted can change a finite-budget theorem. The positive constructors, safe certificates, exact refinements and rate-optimal learner remain usable within their own stated assumptions.

## A source-dependent budget for learning

The [prior-sensitive work sequence](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#prior-sensitive-learning-width-and-executable-inference) asks when learning can succeed with a finite expected total number of mistakes, not merely eventually become correct on almost every path. It supplies positive sparse-prior and smooth-cluster constructions, an exact two-coin benchmark, and a terminating rational witness. It also shows why mixing two individually manageable sources can destroy a finite mean under unchanged information.

This connects **norm specification (avenue 3), restoration (4), provenance/information access (5), and effective construction (10)**. The chosen failure predicate and allowed receipts determine which guarantees transfer. A latent-parameter tolerance test cannot silently be identified with a nonnegative observed-symbol loss. A correlated side label changes what the policy knows; an independent seed does not. These are operationally precise distinctions, not a general judgement for or against probabilistic learning.

## From a restoration criterion to the exact law it governs

The [recursive-support development](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#recursive-support-and-the-same-physical-policy-law) connects an independently defined finite criterion to a real history-dependent policy in the **same** canonical action law. The [indexed-observer development](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#literal-indexed-observers-and-their-scheduled-runtime) connects literal code and its correctly scheduled evaluator to an exact output law. These are complementary contributions across restoration, semantic fidelity and computability (avenues 4, 8 and 10): a successful abstract policy is not automatically code, and correct code does not by itself justify the supplied target or licensing menu.

[Raw-tape concentration](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#concentration-for-the-actual-seeded-raw-tapes), conditional history laws and [actual controller cost](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#the-actual-generated-controller-has-finite-and-exponential-error-budgets) supply further links only when their source, history and clock interfaces match. An affine-tail implication alone does not establish that a particular controller has that tail. This makes positive construction usable without erasing its premise burden.
