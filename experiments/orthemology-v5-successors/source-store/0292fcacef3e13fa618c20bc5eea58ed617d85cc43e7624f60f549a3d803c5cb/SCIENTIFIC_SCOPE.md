## A1: exact original Certificate classification

For the delivered sparse fixture, the original Certificate compares raw digit
numerals. All 16 cycle cells, 16 target cells and four stage cells are covered.
Target payload zero means absence, one means a present empty set, and payloads
17 through 31 cannot meet the original contract. Decoder equivalence alone would
not suffice. Finite code injectivity and cell coverage justify radix reconstruction.

The unique bounded normal form uses cycle, target and stage moduli 2^16, 2^80
and 2^16. A supplied record is certified exactly when its normal form is the
literal record for the retained initial orientation. Every certified unbounded
record is that literal plus arbitrary multiples of these three moduli.
Classification does not choose the retained orientation. Given certified data,
the orientation can be recovered from its cycle bit at index 12. Certifying a
fixed literal would itself establish the corresponding retained-choice equality.

The malformed raw support input 4 distinguishes two certified records that agree
on all legitimate cells: it reads cycle bit 16. Consequently normalization does
not give equality of arbitrary-natural-input selector component functions.

## Evidence and limits

The original independent review rebuilt the exact 167-module inherited positive
runtime closure and checked its 13 endpoint axiom readbacks. It then cold-built
the exact literal dependencies and A1/A2/A3 sources. The accepted additions contain
138 declarations: 49 in A1, 72 in A2 and 17 in A3. The four independent control
modules contain 45 theorems: 15, 3, 17 and 10. All were checked with transitive
axioms restricted to propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound, or subsets thereof.
The pinned official external cache remains an explicit trust assumption.

Exact review receipts and source assessments are retained in `acceptance/`.
Command-bearing original execution receipts have separately labelled public
views in `evidence/`, each identifying its original digest. Such a view, a file
hash check, and an existing object check do not constitute a fresh kernel replay.
The portable wrapper emits a new result only for the stages it actually runs.

No closed evaluation of opaque initialCandidate, arbitrary-kernel executable
selector extractor, arbitrary raw-input extensionality, equality of bytes,
resource meters, cost or finite-fuel thresholds, or Python/CPython refinement is
established. Existing universal Lean task-stack/callFuel soundness and completeness
remain inherited evidence, not a new implementation-refinement contribution.

Historical full-mutant timeouts, interrupted full-denotation probes and optional
whole-proposition comparisons remain inconclusive and are not replayed. Earlier
development failures are not reclassified as mathematical refutations: A1 had two
author proof-script failures and one corrected reviewer auxiliary proof attempt;
