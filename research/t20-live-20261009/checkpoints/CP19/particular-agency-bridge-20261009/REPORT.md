# Particular cognition, wise necessitation, and original agency

9 October 2026 UTC. Additive source-and-inference investigation. No integration, closure, protected edit, new Lean result, or historical duration certification.

## Result

**Complete self-knowledge can disclose the original's particular productive activity without independently explaining that activity as practically directed.** The newly assembled source comparison identifies precisely what would have to be added: the source's cognition must enter its determination as apprehension of an end and of fitting means, together with actual willing. Merely making the knowledge more complete, more first-personal, or more exact about the selected outcome does not supply that relation.

This is not a refutation of the Darʾ knowledge–will argument. It is a failed-repair result: one cannot vindicate its disputed knowing-production-to-will step by identifying truth-matching knowledge with productive knowledge. Bayān 1:510–512 expressly distinguishes those functions; even knowledge that is a productive condition is not the entire producer. The positive agency argument consequently needs its substantive practical-perfection or purposive-production warrant at that step.

**That practical-perfection warrant is stronger than an unexplained preference for personality.** The source compares acting through informed discrimination with merely obtaining outcomes, and self-governance with either external compulsion or undirected preference. I give the former positive defeasible weight: understanding and owning the fitting reason for what one brings about is an excellence of the source, beyond the value of the result. This appraisal does not require a different effect with the entire actual determination held fixed, a first temporal event, or a featureless impersonal rival.

The particular-choice control also improves the precision of the affirmative and rival alike. Ibn Taymiyya allows a generic fitting end to be particularized by knowledge and availability without antecedently preferring that individual object for its own sake. Ibn al-Qayyim expressly maintains that choice remains choice when intrinsically determined by will and wisdom. Neither the causal necessity of an occurrence nor its necessary conformity to wisdom therefore discriminates personal agency from its strongest rivals on its own.

## 1. Baseline and what was not repeated

The earlier positive-agency return already authenticated Darʾ 10:140–146, attributed the initial selecting-attribute argument to quoted Ibn Rushd, and preserved the prior self-knowledge premise. The present return does not count those findings again.

The present question is prior to numerical uniqueness: **what distinguishes being completely aware of one's production from producing for an apprehended reason?**

## 2. Sources and voices

### Bayān: knowing about and knowing in production

The fresh connected electronic source is [Bayān, Islamicbook subsection 002](https://www.islamicbook.ws/amma/bian-tlbis-aljhmit-002.html). Current extraction L0074–0080 contains Ibn Sīnā's proposed emanation of the apprehended overall order, followed by Ibn Taymiyya's reply. [Islamweb item 186](https://islamweb.net/ar/library/content/415/186/تعقيب-المؤلف-على-ما-نقله-عن-ابن-سينا) independently supplies edition-linked pagination 1:510–512 for the central reply. Search returned its substantive body; direct open subsequently failed. No scan collation is claimed.

At L0076 the author says all knowledge matches its object, but only some knowledge contributes to its object's occurrence. His express example of nonproductive knowledge is divine self-knowledge: knowing oneself does not produce oneself. At L0077, the practical case differentiates the intended end, the desire for it, the means, and willing those means; knowledge concerns all four. Knowledge and power do not replace willing.

L0078 quotes Ibn Sīnā, then al-Rāzī, on the true king's independence and everything else's dependence. Ibn Taymiyya's L0079–0080 response demands positive capacities for knowledgeable governance and rejects treating absence of purpose as an automatic consequence of independence. The quoted definition must not be presented as the author's complete positive proof.

A further control at L0091 is unusually important: he objects that the opponent has not proved either that the necessary existent is generous or the proposed definition of generosity. A theological honorific cannot supply a missing philosophical premise. The ensuing critique of purposeless beneficence does not license simply inserting generosity into a thin necessary-existence premise.

### Darʾ: particular practical content

The retained [Darʾ subsection 017](https://www.islamicbook.ws/amma/dr-taardh-alaql-walnql-017.html), local L0915–0940, continues Ibn Taymiyya's response to al-Ṭūsī. It argues that knowing universal patterns does not yet identify the concrete objects and occasions of a particular action. L0924–0929 requires particular cognition and willing for the discussed practical realization.

Crucially, L0925–0926 allows the particular object to be selected because it is the available, feasible instance of a generic end. The end need not contain an antecedent preference for that very food, garment, or recipient. This is a human illustration of how specification can work; it does not assign limited human resources to God.

The earlier part, L0701–0745, expressly treats complete self-knowledge and knowledge of effects as entailments, without making one divine cognition efficiently produce the other. That boundary constrains the inference test below. These are electronic local coordinates; no new print-page alignment of the later al-Ṭūsī reply was established.

### Miftāḥ: intrinsic wise determination remains choice

The already acquired official Word witness, SHA-256 a5d0c62a4021602bb009ce5dabf3cadad3ee702610b12def6ba94b55bbd105f5, was reused. Bounded contiguous passages were extracted by the previously used UTF-16 method, with vowel marks removed, and checked against the [public electronic text](https://www.islamicbook.ws/amma/mftah-dar-alsaadt-003.html). This is not a general certified Word parser or a fresh manuscript reading.

Electronic 2:952–955 first states the opponent's objection that preference for the better removes choice, then replies that the source's own will and wisdom can necessitate its appropriate determination without an external coercer. Page 955's strongest particular-determiner wording has a reported manuscript variant; the editor adopts the ṭāʾ reading. The conclusion does not depend on suppressing that apparatus.

Electronic 2:905–909 includes the narrower permission to choose among equally weighted alternatives, while rejecting other alleged equal-value cases. It concerns creaturely practical and juridical options. It is **not** evidence that God faces that exact choice situation or that every complete divine practical context contains a tie. The legal and premodern medical examples are historical source context, not present advice.

Electronic 1:238–242 separately distinguishes productive and nonproductive knowledge and counts both as cognitive excellences. This corroborates the Bayān distinction; it is not an independent textual tradition or an additional discovered proof.

## 3. Executed operation A: remove the practical premise from the self-knowledge ascent

The strongest charitable starting argument is:

1. The concrete original is an actual producer with a determinate productive reality.
2. It knows that reality completely, including its powers and actual acts.
3. Complete knowledge includes the relevant consequences and objects of its actual productive relation.
4. Therefore it knows its particular products and their origination through itself.

Grant that conclusion for this operation. Do **not** restrict the producer to a static state, insist that it infer its effects sequentially, or assume that all its particular acts are eternal.

The candidate further conclusion is different: the producer brings about this particular product **because it apprehends and wills an appropriate end**.

### The failed substitution

Replacing the final practical relation by “it knows that it produces this” changes the claim. Factive knowledge of an act includes the act's occurrence among its truth conditions; this does not make that knowledge the reason for the act. Complete first-person ownership of the truth does not alter that direction.

The Bayān distinction decisively blocks a proposed *identification* of truth-matching cognition with practical production. It does not by itself furnish a counterexample to Darʾ's stronger claim about knowing producers. In particular, self-knowledge not producing oneself is not an example of an original knowingly producing an effect involuntarily. Those different targets must not be conflated. That self-knowledge does not produce its own object also does not show that it cannot contribute to producing another object.

The proper premise-independence result is therefore limited but consequential: **the displayed self-knowledge ascent supplies particular cognitive content; its premises do not state the cognition's practical basing relation.** Darʾ's knowing-production-to-will claim is the substantive bridge, not an automatic consequence of an increasingly rich knowledge operator.

A cognitive-but-practically-idle source is the relevant diagnostic objection: suppose an intrinsically structured productive reality has complete truthful cognition of its own determinations, while those determinations do not occur for apprehended reasons. This is not certified as metaphysically possible, an original perfection, or a complete countermodel. It isolates what a defense of the bridge must exclude. Adding only more correct cognition leaves that proposed distinction intact. Adding practical wisdom excludes it by a substantive further premise.

### Why this is not the old de se test

The old test principally examined uniqueness under an agency-bearing interpretation, while already distinguishing truthful self-ascription from additional practical basing. This operation isolates and source-tests that latter transition without claiming the distinction itself as new. It brackets the number of bearers and examines the transition from awareness to intentional agency itself. Neither the centered semantics nor the diagonal policy answers it. The independence claim also does not deny that two attributes can be inseparable in the actual original: that inseparability is what the stronger source argument must warrant.

## 4. Executed operation B: particular determination without a unique normative ranking

The source comparison supports three different questions:

- What makes this a fitting kind of undertaking?
- What identifies and enables these actual means and this occasion?
- What constitutes the producer's actual particular willing and its adequate determination?

Darʾ's availability example shows why the answer to the second need not be a superior intrinsic value attached to the individual object. Miftāḥ's equal-options case similarly shows that practical permission and causal determination are not the same relation. Neither example proves that the original's particular choice may be unexplained.

The resulting correction is stronger than merely observing that no numerical optimization theorem was stated: there are **affirmative source examples** of particular practical realization that do not start with an individually preferred object. Their transfer to an original is bounded. An original cannot simply borrow the human example's externally limited opportunity set as its ultimate explanation. If prior internally originated acts establish the present situation, those acts and their reasons remain part of the account; the opportunity set cannot hide an alien original selector.

Miftāḥ supplies the complementary control. A determination's necessary dependence on the subject's own wise willing does not make it involuntary. Therefore:

- An impersonal source does not become personal merely because its determinations are exact and necessary.
- A wise agent does not become impersonal merely because its appropriate determination is necessary.
- Necessary agreement between wise agents does not become coercion merely from the word “necessary.”

The discriminating property is the actual explanatory role of known fitting ends and owned willing, not modal looseness alone.

## 5. Executed operation C: test the affirmative practical-perfection repair

I next tested the positive source reply rather than stopping at the failed shortcut. The important fresh application is Bayān L0096–0103: the author refuses to identify genuinely excellent beneficence with the mere production of a fitting benefit. He calls giving with no end at all defective, then distinguishes seeking compensation from the recipient from intrinsically valuing beneficence. This is a substantive account of the producer's practical relation to its product, beyond the word “action-guiding.” It gives a reason why equally beneficial outputs need not amount to equally excellent modes of agency.

The source also supplies two limitations on its use. L0091–0094 forbids smuggling a contested attribute into an agreed honorific. L0097 describes natural directedness in the terminology of inclination and sometimes will; that weaker goal-directedness must not silently become conscious apprehension and owned willing. The strong impersonal rival may therefore possess intrinsic directedness without conceding the cognitive practical relation under examination. Nor is a naturally beneficial stone thereby a morally foolish agent: the source's comparison concerns the proposed characterization of excellent beneficence, not a moral condemnation of nonagents.

The relevant [Akmaliyya passage, Majmūʿ 6:129–130](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/22/591/), retained L0493–0500, supplies the complementary original-perfection comparison: informed wise discrimination and self-governance, as distinct from undirected preference or external governance. This inherited source supports the broader application; it is not counted as a newly discovered proof.

### Strongest affirmative argument

An original that knowingly brings about fitting ends through its own willing possesses a form of practical self-governance absent from a source whose cognition merely accompanies its operations. The excellence is not only that the final outcome is useful. It belongs to the source's relation to its reasons and activity: it understands and owns why it acts. Intrinsic wise constraint can express that excellence rather than detract from it.

The argument has no need to infer a defect from every relation, to condemn successive acts, or to posit an externally imposed demand. Within the independently argued pure-perfection framework, exclusion of this excellence because the candidate is essentially nonpersonal is not a neutral exemption. Nor must its defense first be reduced to a theorem about bare unreceived causation before it can receive evidential weight.

### Strongest structured impersonal reply and result

The relevant rival is a concrete underived productive reality with intrinsic differentiated powers and law-governed successive activity. It can claim adequate particular production and useful ordered effects; it is not a blank scalar, a mathematical description mistaken for an existent, or a natural object waiting for an unprovided trigger. It denies that rational self-governance is a required excellence of an original of its kind.

The practical-perfection comparison reaches this rival at its actual disagreement. Matching useful outcomes does not answer the comparison, because the comparison concerns the source's practical mode of production. Conversely, the comparison does not refute the rival merely by calling its operations blind. It needs the substantive judgment that informed owned action is a pure and relevant excellence at original scope.

My appraisal is positive at that stated level. Producing fitting benefits for understood fitting reasons exhibits an intelligible additional excellence over merely producing them, while the source's intrinsic-attribute and intrinsic-necessitation controls remove two familiar proposed costs: foreign completion and coercion. This gives a reason to favor rational agency that does not depend on failure to imagine structured impersonal causation. It is not a new deduction from independence alone or an independent second proof of the pure-perfection principle.

### Strongest necessary-concordance reply and result

A rival that grants two intrinsically knowledgeable, practically directed, wise bearers can accept this agency comparison. It may claim each acts for its own properly apprehended reason and that those reasons necessarily agree. Nothing in the newly tested agency bridge alone excludes it.

That is a discriminating outcome: the practical-perfection repair bears against the blind and cognitively idle proposals, but does not establish numerical uniqueness. A separate original-peer efficacy or authority argument is required against concordant rational agents. The report does not revisit or weaken the already assessed substantive case for that further norm.

## 6. What the completed operation changes

The return yields three usable results, beyond the inherited sourcehood stalemate:

1. It identifies a concrete false repair of the self-knowledge argument: practical direction cannot be supplied by upgrading truth-matching cognition or by importing the known actual choice into the knowledge premise. The earlier de se report already states the general basing distinction; new credit is limited to the source-bound causal-target and directionality audit here.
2. It supplies affirmative source controls separating particular practical specification from individual-object preference, and intrinsic wise necessitation from involuntariness. These control which arguments can discriminate agency.
3. It states and appraises the stronger surviving repair: practical self-governance as a pure original excellence, now tested through Bayān's explicit distinction between beneficial output and purposively excellent beneficence. The structured impersonal objection must address that comparison; necessarily concordant wise agents do not fail it merely through necessity or plurality. This is one supported repair, not a proof that every possible cognition-to-agency argument must use exactly this perfection premise.

No confidence percentage changes, new proof count, metaphysical countermodel certificate, or T20 closure follows. The completed source/inference operation has reached its bounded outcome. A further advance on this exact bridge would require an argument about the eligibility and purity of practical self-governance, or evidence of genuine purposive production, rather than another self-knowledge enrichment.
