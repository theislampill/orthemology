# Nonconventional intentional indication probe

Source distinction: Nubuwwat 768–772 allows an intended sign without a prior pact, recognised through circumstances and knowledge of the sender. 771 separately considers voluntary custody and communicative purpose; strong alternative purposes suspend inference. This is not a universal requirement to obtain an explicit source instruction.

Probe: a known source has a distinctive personal mantle. A publicly heard question disputes whether E carries the source's inventory message. The source can keep the mantle, send it visibly with E, or reject the claim by withholding and a negative gesture. The same visible sending can also implement repair, protection from cold, social courtesy, or deliberate deception. No earlier rule assigns mantle possession to a commission, and no source endorsement sentence occurs.

A small decision process scores feasible action-plans from explicit practical conditions and goal weights. The publicly visible act is generated by the selected plan. The target is whether the source chose it to get the audience to accept the present commission claim; the action log never reveals that target. Actual commission, source belief and report truth are separate. Counterfactual interventions remove the public question/audience or remove repair/cold opportunities. Separate previous choice episodes constrain the possible action/reason policies under a disclosed deterministic model; finite samples do not validate that model or prove character globally.

Positive criterion: within an independently justified candidate policy class and adequately observed context, the surviving plans for a voluntary public sending all pursue communication of this claim even though no explicit instruction or credential rule was given. Negative controls: maintenance, courtesy, appropriation, mistaken belief, deception, stronger question-conditioned courtesy, and a selectively mimicking policy. A defeasible calculation may retain nonzero rival probability under explicitly supplied likelihood assumptions; it is not a definition of knowledge or an empirical calibration.
