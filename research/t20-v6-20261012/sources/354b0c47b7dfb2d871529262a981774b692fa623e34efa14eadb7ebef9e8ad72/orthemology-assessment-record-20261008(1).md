## 2. Object and method

The object is not “whether the answer is right.” It is a concrete case undergoing consequence-bearing, rule-governed handling, where the path has to remain auditable after the result is in.

Candidate vocabulary, still unadopted, and the ordinary-language distinctions it is trying to keep apart:

- Orthemma: this concrete case, with an identity key and a version.
- Ortheme: a repeatable state-type the case may instantiate, relative to a declared, versioned analysis.
- Metaortheme: the repeatable governing standard.
- Metaorthemma: that standard bound to this case, now.
- Orthing: the whole governed process.
- Orthing episode: one dated run.
- Also typed separately: bearer or source identity, evidence versus currentness, executor, route, result, closure, residual, handoff or continuation.

Result correctness is not pathway correctness. The four cells are right-and-adequate, right-and-defective, wrong-and-adequate, wrong-and-defective. A stopped clock, a stale recovered directive that happens to match current policy, a copied report counted as a second witness, and a successor that keeps the name while the constitutive controller sits outside are instances.

Methodological order that survived the thread:

1. Recognition, fiṭrah, observation, or an inherited source argument supplies a candidate. These steer relevance. They do not certify themselves. The intuition remains corrigible.
2. Isolate the claim. Name the bearer and the dependencies.
3. Formalise that joint. Search proofs and countermodels.
4. Retain what survives and print the residue.
5. Return to the source or the case when the formalisation exposes a missing distinction.
6. Repeat.

This was distinguished from system-first construction: choose an ontology, build the apparatus, derive consequences, then force the case to fit. “Orthogonal” here means a different order of earning, not a rejection of argument or of revelation.

“Computationally auditable philosophy/theology” was accepted as a better description of the direction of travel than “philosophy, formalised.” Computation does not decide the philosophy from outside.

What ordinary dialectic does not reliably provide, and what was treated as a difference in kind rather than degree:

- Machine-enforced persistence: a later derivation that revives an eliminated alternative fails the check.
- Fair or exhaustive search: an existing finite proof, or a countermodel in a finite class, is not left to whether someone thinks to look.
- Retained countermodels and failed maps.
- Explicit residuals, so a certificate cannot be narrated as warrant for the premises.
- Transport across a map that preserves the relations the prohibition uses.

Medieval scholars could record eliminations. They could not bind them into a machine-enforced state in which a silent revival fails verification by itself.

## 3. Attrition, accretion, transport, response

Endorsed conditional claim:

For every obligation actually reduced to a sound, fair, recursively enumerable proof search, any finite proof that exists in that calculus is eventually recoverable by unbounded computation. For every obligation reduced to a finite class with decidable satisfaction, search ends in a countermodel or in exhaustion of that class. Failure to find a proof is not a proof of absence unless the space was finite and decidable, or absence is itself proved. A countermodel shows non-entailment from the encoded premises. It is a metaphysical possibility only with a separate admissibility warrant.

Three attritions:

1. Within-lane: proof search, finite model search, premise removal.
2. Mapping: candidate correspondences fail if they do not preserve identity, collapse two relations, reverse dependence, or keep syntax and lose the verdict. Failed maps are retained.
3. Transport: once a map survives, a prohibition need not be reinvented in every domain that instantiates it.

Research state, schematic:

\[
R_n = (C_n, P_n, K_n, O_n, \Phi_n)
\]

claims, checked proofs, retained countermodels, open obligations, tested maps. An investigation is an update \(R_n \mapsto R_{n+1}\). Cumulativity holds only relative to the accepted state. A source correction can restrict a map and reopen a closure that transport had treated as settled. Generation enlarges the frontier as well as shrinking it. A result constrains and informs the next justified response. It need not pick a single next action. A result guides the next search without being mistaken for the answer to it.

Guaranteed convergence and increasing structural leverage were kept apart. Conditional inevitability applies to reachable formal discoveries inside fair search, not to major truths. A truth may have no finite representation in the systems actually built. The map may not exist. What is recovered may be formally real and intellectually minor.

Parallel programmes under similar pressures (proof assistants, provenance, agent continuity, reproducibility) would strengthen leverage if translations preserve structure. Verbal rhyme is not convergence. Disagreement between independently constrained trajectories is information.

## 4. Classification that survived

- Above a private house framework.
- Emerging research theory and coherent interdisciplinary research programme. Single authorship does not remove either label.
- A substantive, finite, conditional mathematical research programme is present in the September 2026 drafts (see below). That is not independent disciplinary recognition.
- Proto-discipline and distinct field: not yet.
- One level up would require outsider reproduction of a nontrivial formal result, or a benchmark gain. Two levels up would require specialists in at least two target domains retaining a result after the local vocabulary is removed.

The present-state formulation that was kept:

> A large interconnected search landscape with many plausible sources of yield, and a method for accumulating discoveries without dropping the failures, constraints, and mappings that make later ones possible. A landscape under exploration, not a surveyed field.

### Established under stated assumptions

- Finite actualisation: \(\rho_A(e) = \eta_A(\xi_A(e)) + \iota_A(e)\) has finite word support and finite degree under Assumption F.
- Fibre criterion: a target factors through a representation if and only if equal representations imply equal targets.
- Proper marginals can agree while a parity expectation differs. Compression can destroy the joint fact.
- Contractions can erase a distinction. No nonzero deletion functional is invariant under all basis changes.
- Witnessed degree-lowering lowers active degree only by extending the historical ledger.
- Compositional no-disappearance: record-valid witnesses keep obligations traceable.
- Finite mixed Nash existence for a finite declared game. This is Nash–Kakutani, conditional on the declaration.
- Two-level damping criterion and a certified invariant error ball. Explicitly not a Navier–Stokes contribution.
- Continuation completeness on a finite model: horizon-\(h\) expectations are exactly recoverable if and only if the continuation span sits in the retained space. Defect is the dual gap. Minimal continuous encoder dimension is \(\dim \mathscr{C}_h - 1\).
- Pairwise sign model on \(\{\pm 1\}^n\): Walsh span of degree at most \(h+1\), dimension \(\sum_{k=0}^{\min(n,h+1)}\binom{n}{k}\). Full horizon continuous memory lower bound \(2^n - 1\).

### Proposed or refused

- Representation declarations, inquiry landscapes, and the strategic layer are proposals. \(\mathsf{H}_A\) need not be a subalgebra. Unbounded episode degree is not proved.
- The tensor-programme note rejects a privileged “orthemic tensor.” A prospective five-arm gate is blocked by specification ambiguity. A representation win cannot establish ontology, source truth, or theology.
- G0 is metaphor. G1 is an order-theoretic route-ranked candidate; a smaller burden count is not progress. G2, literal gradient flow, is future. A Riemannian layer is not in these files.

Inherited mathematics includes the tensor algebra, Nash–Kakutani, moment duality, and energy estimates. The Orthemic contribution claimed for the interface is: an episode is not its tensor; current sufficiency need not survive a new inquiry; degree may fall in the active state only if history records the lowering; continuation is exact recovery of a declared future contract, with a defect and an augmentation cost.

## 6. Interface test

Composite geometric state, after correction: the modelling contract may be \((\kappa, x)\), where \(\kappa\) retains identity, evidence, authorisation, provenance, and history, and \(x = \psi_N \circ \pi_N \circ \rho_A\). Preservation of a target by the pair does not require the smooth coordinates to encode every semantic distinction. Geometry-only claims still require the target to factor through \(x\). Injectivity of \(\psi_N\) on the image of the truncation makes geometric preservation equivalent to preservation through the truncated tensor. If \(\psi_N\) is not injective, the geometry is a strictly lossier channel.

Conditional dynamical fact, if a smooth objective and a metric are declared and the gradient exists:

\[
\frac{dJ}{dt} = -\lVert \operatorname{grad}_g J \rVert_g^2 \le 0.
\]

This is a theorem about the declared model. It is not a theorem that the inquiry improved. Proper functionalism can motivate a choice of \(J\). It does not supply \(g\).

Theology-to-mind map of standing capacity, particular exercise, and produced effect onto disposition, episode, and output: a useful candidate whose fidelity is unproved. Role injection does not preserve causal order, temporal separation, bearer identity, or continuation. Those relations are in dispute in philosophy of mind, so assuming them assumes part of the conclusion.

Illicit map, retained as a negative edge: essential independence of a completing bearer does not map to multiple realizability of a mental role. Dependence reverses. A necessary condition is rewritten as a sufficient one.

Riemannian contribution in that categorical transport: none beyond the typed relations and the fibre criterion. Scoped negative result only. It does not show that a metric adds little in general. Quantitative stability, perturbation margins, convergence, and continuation bounds remain the open place a geometry could earn its keep.

## 8. Three kinds of leverage

These were collapsed in one earlier reply and then separated.

- Within-domain epistemic leverage: a result cuts live alternatives inside one inquiry. Localising harmonious plurality as surviving a thinner stock (contingency, standing power, real secondary causation, concordant intention) and depending on a disputed bridge (complete original efficacy, non-absorption) is already this kind of leverage.
- Cross-domain mathematical leverage: a prohibition remains in force in another representation because a map preserves the relations it uses. Not shown by the T20 packet.
- Independent disciplinary validation: outside reproduction and specialist retention. Not shown. Its absence does not cancel a within-domain cut.

On the rival: dropping a disputed load-bearing premise is not the same as dropping an independently warranted base from which that premise follows. A model that yields two complete original producers only by omitting complete efficacy does not defeat the argument and does not confirm it. A model that preserved every premise of the strongest argument and still yielded two complete original producers would defeat it. No such model is reported.

## 9. Genetic objection

Origin of a hypothesis is not warrant. Why \(H\) was investigated, whether the encoded premises entail \(H\), and whether those premises are warranted are three questions. An expected conclusion on a route built so that only it could win is a defective path even if the conclusion is true. The reported withdrawal and later lower-confidence return of the common-source judgement is process evidence against a manufacture-only reading. It is not independent replication.

Independence is a ladder: one encoding, alternate encoding, different prover, hostile model search, independent source specialist, external replication by people who need not share the conclusion. Shared encoding choices can still correlate routes.

## 10. Judgement at the end of the thread

Orthemology is a persistent research ecology under exploration: results, mappings, residuals, and new questions constraining one another. A stored result can become more important later without changing, because a new faithful map makes it relevant elsewhere.

It has a substantive finite conditional mathematical core in the September drafts, and a source-constrained interface practice visible in the T20 addendum. Theology is correcting the formalisation. Mind has not yet received a faithful theorem. Geometry remains an open modelling proposal, legitimate on a composite \((\kappa, x)\) contract, not established as a dynamics of inquiry.

The characteristic work — accumulate a discrimination, refuse a false bridge, print the residue, generate the next obligation — is in evidence. Reusable cross-domain mathematics and disciplinary recognition are not.
