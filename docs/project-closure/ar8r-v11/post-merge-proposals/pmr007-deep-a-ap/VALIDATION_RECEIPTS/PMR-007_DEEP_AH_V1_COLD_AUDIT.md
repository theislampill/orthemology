## Blocking findings

### AH-F01 — textual co-predication was too quickly called a metaphysical binder

Lines 68–99 have one grammatical/source referent, but moving from that textual
proposition to a world-level common bearer requires source identity,
translation, proposition reconstruction, applicability, and world-adequacy
coordinates.  V2 must call this `TEXTUAL_COREFERENCE` and keep
`WORLD_COMMON_BEARER` separate.

### AH-F02 — creed and commentary authorship were partially flattened

The initial argument is al-Asfahani's creed; Ibn Taymiyyah affirms the package
as true while criticizing or repairing several proofs.  V2 must type every
edge by source stratum rather than attributing the entire graph to one speaker.

### AH-F03 — the graph mixed roles, evidential routes, and eligibility guards

`PERFECTION` is a qiyas eligibility/bridge coordinate, not simply another
attribute role.  `CREATION`, `SPECIFICATION`, and `CHOICE` are explanans/action
coordinates.  V2 must type nodes and avoid ontological inference from graph
connectivity across heterogeneous node kinds.

### AH-F04 — the speech edge had a disputed-source qualifier

Line 124 says engenderment entails speech according to the majority of the
Affirmation Folk, while legislative composition entails speech.  V2 must split
`COSMOLOGICAL_SPEECH_ROUTE` from `LEGISLATIVE_SPEECH_ROUTE` and preserve the
stated tradition-relative qualifier.

### AH-F05 — qiyas eligibility was underspecified

The selected perfection route requires pure perfection, absence of defect,
Creator relation, and source/method eligibility.  Generic creaturely
availability is insufficient.

### AH-F06 — local edge satisfaction was defined to ignore bearer equality

The plural-realization countermodel is valid only in the declared role-graph
semantics.  V2 must state that the graph records predicate-level dependency,
not causal realization, co-instantiation, or metaphysical grounding.

### AH-F07 — connectedness was representation-relative

The graph is connected for the selected nodes and supports.  It does not prove
that the full source architecture has a unique graph, a minimal six-role
representation, or representation-invariant connectedness.

### AH-F08 — the generic-unity criticism was overgeneralized

Lines 481–495 criticize the stated shared-necessity/jointness-and-distinction
argument.  They do not refute every possible unity or common-bearer proof.

### AH-F09 — source-relative exclusion risked tautological credit

If a source premise explicitly co-predicates roles of one referent, a plural
assignment fails that premise by definition.  V2 must classify this as source
model-class restriction and adjudication, not an independent unity theorem.

### AH-F10 — impersonal graph realization did not settle metaphysical parity

An impersonal formal system carrying the selected dependency topology proves
neutral-reduct nonidentification only.  It does not establish metaphysical
possibility, equal explanation, or source compatibility.

### AH-F11 — source authority coordinates were bundled

Source bytes, textual locus, translation adequacy, attribution, proposition
reconstruction, source acceptance, referent identity, and world adequacy must
remain separately typed.

### AH-F12 — theorem-family and novelty status needed tightening

The finite graph claims use standard connectedness, partition, and equality
constraints.  The result is a source-formal adjudication and Candidate-G/R5
bridge localization with zero general mathematical novelty.
