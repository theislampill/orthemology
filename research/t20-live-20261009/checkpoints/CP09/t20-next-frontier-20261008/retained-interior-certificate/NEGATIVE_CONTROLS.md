# Boundaries and rejected transports

1. A single arbitrary coordinatewise-monotone two-input route can be an OR of the staircase conjunctions and realize all positives/no connectors. The count theorem needs elementary coordinate-separable AND semantics. controls.py checks the two-positive example.
2. Two unary routes at threshold 1/2 realize the m=2 panel without any AB route. The certificate counts total occurrences; the stated pure-AB hard pair is a restricted inventory.
3. Redrawing thresholds breaks witness identity. It must not be substituted for retained-state replay. The written argument and independent review include a single-route redraw countercontrol.
4. A comonotone route has zero mass in both symmetric off-diagonal boxes. The inherited heterogeneous three-bit alias survives; full support of independent routes is needed for this sufficient positivity argument.
5. Full-support marginal route laws coupled as identical copies produce no E for m>=2. Route independence is unnecessary for the logical impossibility, but marginal support alone cannot replace a joint assignment-probability condition.
6. Open-lower boxes differ from exact witness sets on threshold axes. Continuous Joe margins make this null; arbitrary boundary atoms require the exact S_i sets.
7. For more than m occurrences, extra inactive routes and multiple occupancy invalidate m! product p_i as the full event probability. Inclusion-exclusion controls explicitly cover k=m+1 as well as k<m and k=m.
8. Infinite forward KL on a finite support-deficient transcript is not infinite usable information on every sample. Certificate probability is <=m!/m^m for exactly m iid routes. This is a bound for certificate-only occurrence waiting, not full-transcript testing.
9. Dropping connector misses destroys the count claim: with one always-on AND route, every positive and every connector is hit, regardless of panel size. The falsified Lean source that substitutes hit for miss is rejected at the contradiction step. This is a mutation-sensitivity control, not an additional accepted theorem.
10. The stronger m+1 cardinal conclusion is false: one threshold route can supply the one-positive, zero-connector m=1 event. The corresponding Lean mutation is rejected at the final cardinal inequality. Error recovery may print sorryAx in these rejected logs; it is absent from the accepted source and accepted trust-zero replay, and the mutated sources are never imported by it.

The kernel controls do not formalize the Joe CDF, mass formula, support/KL reasoning or probability of these countercontrols. They verify the declared deterministic source-specific theorem and show the unaltered proof rejects the two stated false edits.
