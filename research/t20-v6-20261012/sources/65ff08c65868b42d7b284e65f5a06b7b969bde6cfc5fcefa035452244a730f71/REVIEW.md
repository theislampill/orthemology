# Independent review: nonconventional indication and motive semantics

7 October 2026. Additional adversarial research, not a closure judgement. Earlier testimony/constitution research and history-v2 retain their credit. Reviewed author inputs and this review's initial, less precise classification are preserved separately.

## Result, including the necessary correction

The implicit-sign experiment makes a genuine source-correspondence improvement: its positive plan uses a recognizable object to answer a question without an explicit earlier token pact or endorsement sentence. The corresponding inference remains conditional on the planning class and independently warranted pragmatic circumstances.

The central semantic limit is sharper than an additional possible liar. The current `aim` classifier identifies a plan's **terminal goal** with its entire communicative intention. That identification is too strong. Courtesy can be the ultimate goal while inducing acceptance is an intended means or concurrent act. Conversely, uptake can be foreseen without being a chosen means, while the overt response still supports a public acknowledgment under an appropriate contextual interpretation.

My stable courtesy/correction rival matches all four calibrations and every action in all 128 Boolean contexts while changing the winning terminal-aim label. It therefore establishes **motive/means underidentification**. It does not, by itself, establish genuine non-endorsement or different public adoption. The cheaper-gesture probe separates preferred means in the two specified policies, not necessarily whether either source acknowledges the claim. Those stronger readings are withdrawn; the initial files remain in `before-means-end-clarification/` as a development record.

An additional independent means–end control executes the distinction rather than merely stating it. The conditional positive evidence is also retained: allowing source beliefs to remain hidden leaves the original three regular policies, all with a positive-communication terminal plan at the target. No contrary general conclusion about ordinary testimony follows.

## 1. Actual source correspondence

Re-viewed retained Nubuwwat scan pages 769 and 772, adding to this reviewer's earlier direct inspection of 768 and 770–771. These are repeat inspections, not new programme-page credit.

Page 769 describes a recognizable personal object sent without a prior token pact, with known circumstances supporting its use as a sign. Page 772 permits contextually understood answering gestures without a prior pact. Pages 770–771 distinguish custody from certification-purpose and allow suspension when a competing explanation is strong. Explicit credential instructions are therefore one possible sufficient route, not a universal condition.

The source does not require the recipient to know every ultimate psychological goal. An ulterior wish to reassure, please, help or honor someone can coexist with deliberately indicating that the messenger was sent. Its purpose question concerns whether the token is intentionally used as an informative/certifying sign, not whether communication is the actor's sole ultimate utility.

A prior token pact is also different from ordinary language and pragmatic understanding. Contextual knowledge can make a new sign intelligible. The model appropriately needs this background, but does not produce or authenticate it merely by marking a plan as communicative.

## 2. Observability and the conditional positive result

The designated behavioral observation fields contain question/custody/transfer/retention/gesture actions and circumstances. They exclude the source-policy weights and selected plan's goal. The full research output separately includes those latent variables for audit; it must not be given wholesale to the hypothetical recipient as observation.

Changing the communication weight changes the positive source action. This establishes structural dependence inside the simulator. A counterfactual deletion of a person's goal is not normally an observation a recipient can perform; it is a model check. Removing a question, audience or practical need is a different type of environmental probe.

The forward model supplies the expectation that a recognizable object sent in this question context can induce acceptance. It does not independently model or validate actual audience uptake. That expectation can exist without a prior credential pact, but remains a substantive applicability premise. Intending indication, succeeding in communication, actual commission, report truth, veracity and legitimate authority are distinct questions.

### Hidden-belief positive control

The author fixtures supply source beliefs. I independently varied the source belief across all four calibration episodes plus the target: 96 policies × 32 belief assignments = 3,072 combinations. Conditioning only on the observed four calibration actions and target handover leaves 24 combinations across the same three policies. Every surviving target plan has the explicit terminal aim of inducing positive acceptance.

Thus this conditional result need not smuggle the source's belief into the observations. In the specified class, the actions infer enough. A plan that really takes acceptance as its chosen end supplies a positive intended-indication example. The problem is the converse: a different terminal aim does not establish absence of intentional indication as a means or concurrent undertaking.

## 3. Stable asymmetry: a real policy-class limitation at a narrower target

The original `communicate` parameter gives the same motivation strength to affirming a believed true claim and correcting a believed false claim. The author already adds an episode-indexed selective-courtesy alternative. My independent extension instead uses stable motives throughout:

- Affirmative communication: 0.
- Correcting a believed false claim: 6.
- Personal question-responsive courtesy: 4.
- Repair and warmth: 4 each.

There is no special novel-episode branch. Negative correction wins on the false-claim episode. Courtesy wins on the true-claim target. Practical needs produce repair or warmth when no question is heard. Quiet conditions produce retention.

The policy reproduces the four calibration actions and matches the original positive policy's action in all 128 Boolean contexts. Eight contexts, including the target, have different selected **terminal-aim classifications**. More samples within that same context/action menu cannot separate these two stable utility policies.

This exposes a specific background assumption: rejection of a false claim does not alone warrant symmetry between negative-correction and positive-affirmation motives. But the result is about latent utility/planning structure. It cannot be presented as an established source-level non-endorsement rival until the plan's intermediate intentions and communicative act semantics have also been supplied.

## 4. Executed means–end controls

`means_end_controls.py` represents the selected causal recipe separately from foreseen effects. It computes the ancestors of the chosen final goal within that recipe. Three cases use the same outward mantle response:

1. **Communication as end:** mantle response → recognition → acceptance. Acceptance is the ultimate goal and an intended result.
2. **Courtesy through acknowledgment:** mantle response → recognition → acceptance → gratitude. Gratitude is ultimate; acceptance remains an intended means.
3. **Material courtesy with foreseen uptake:** mantle response → receipt of material gift → gratitude. Acceptance is a foreseen side effect outside this chosen recipe.

The two courtesy cases share the same ultimate goal but differ in whether positive uptake is a chosen means. Thus the ultimate-goal string is not an adequate classifier of intended indication.

These selected recipes are themselves stipulated source-planning facts, not privately observable data. They demonstrate what semantic information a correct model must represent; they do not authenticate an actual person's intention.

### Public acknowledgment remains a separate question

The script also supplies an explicitly defeasible public-evidence rule: an attended question followed by a deliberate, independently intelligible source response supports acknowledgment absent defeating qualification. No private terminal-goal field enters this rule. All three cases can share the same public evidence and its acknowledgment assessment.

This is a conditional evidential model, not a universal definition of assertion and not a proof that a particular real source has undertaken an assertion. It demonstrates why a hidden ultimate goal cannot simply turn public adoption off. Foreseen uptake, intended means, and publicly undertaken assertoric responsibility need not coincide under every theory.

An actual independently observed disclaimer such as a clarification that the gift is not answering the commissioning question provides an overt non-endorsement control. That changes the evidence. The script expressly refuses to claim that the old public observations remained identical. These tests have **not** established an identical-public-record pair with different actual source adoption merely by assigning courtesy to one plan.

## 5. What the new gesture probe does and does not establish

The original action menu includes retention, sending and negative rejection, but no cheaper affirmative answer. I added an available, intelligible affirmative gesture costing 0.5 instead of 1, with no personal/material gift benefit. The original communication policy chooses the gesture; the stable courtesy policy chooses the mantle.

This is a genuine conditional action discriminator for those two utility policies. It does not establish that one source adopts the proposition while the other does not. Both means can be acknowledgments; courtesy may employ either as a communicative means. `MEANS_END_RESULTS.json` explicitly retains the same contextual public assessment for the two intelligible response forms.

The probe also needs real evidence that the gesture is available, intelligible, cheaper and not itself the relevant courtesy benefit. A distant messenger case might not permit it. No general requirement for an extra explicit endorsement follows, and it is not a historical experiment on the source examples.

I also executed a controlled intervention on the three supplied causal recipes: block positive uptake while retaining material gift receipt. The communicative end and courtesy-through-acknowledgment both fail to be achieved; material courtesy still succeeds. This separates the goal dependencies within those stipulated plans. A real probe would need an actually available way to alter uptake without also removing the gift benefit, and evidence of the source's expectation of that alteration. It still would not automatically resolve public assertion semantics. An actual deployment needs a concrete communicative setting, not just more labels.

## 6. Action ties and compatibility

The author's compatibility predicate requires the observed action to be predicted by every maximizing plan. This is robust prediction, stronger than ordinary possibility compatibility when optimal actions tie.

An enlarged case with communication weight 1 admits both keep and send as optimal. Observed sending is possible, but that predicate rejects the policy. I independently checked all 12,288 original policy/context combinations and found no cross-action ties, so the original three-policy calculation is not affected. The warning applies to later enlargements.

A broader model should specify a justified tie-breaking mechanism, retain policies for which the observed action is possible, or explicitly continue using a robust-prediction target. A favorable latent tie cannot be chosen after seeing the outcome to certify an intention.

## 7. Ordinary epistemic status

The probability-sensitivity calculations correctly disclose that their rival weights are supplied rather than empirical. Equal behavior likelihoods leave relative odds unchanged. Neither a small weight selected for convenience nor a large weight assigned merely because a model was constructed establishes real warrant.

Source character, relationships, known circumstances, source knowledge, alternative practical ends and the actual opportunity set can justify a contextual inference. The source explicitly allows such ordinary recognition. A merely imaginable rival is not automatically a strong defeater. Conversely, a positive finite inverse-planning fit does not itself establish that every omitted policy is irrelevant.

Recognizing an intended acknowledgment supplies neither normative jurisdiction nor divine veracity nor universal adoption of later reports. The stronger source argument retains those separate premises. Reliable source-owned testimony can still warrant a new proposition without independent rediscovery or a redundant second proof of the same obligation.

## Disposition

- **No-prior-pact positive route:** supported as a conditional explicit model of a genuinely communicative plan.
- **Three-survivor inference:** independently reproduced even with hidden source belief.
- **Stable asymmetric motive rival:** executed; establishes motive/means underidentification, not completed source-level non-endorsement.
- **Courtesy and mixed intentions:** terminal-goal classification is insufficient; intended intermediate effects must be represented.
- **Cheaper gesture:** separates the compared utility policies under stated conditions; different public adoption remains unestablished.
- **Overt non-endorsement:** independently observed qualification supplies a discriminating control while changing the evidence.
- **Actual history, pragmatic competence, character warrant and broader authority/veracity:** not established by these simulations.

No closure approval or recommendation is issued.
