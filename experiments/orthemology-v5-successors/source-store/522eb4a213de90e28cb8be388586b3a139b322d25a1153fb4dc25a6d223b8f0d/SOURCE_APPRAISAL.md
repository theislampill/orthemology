# Opening-quarter source appraisal

Dated Nubuwwat-stage assessment, preserved with its actual evidence boundary. The later Safadiyya study and final current appraisal are separate records; this text is not retrospectively revised into their verdict.

Scope: NB-S001-L0001–0138,25,826 words in 13 windows, read continuously; bounded printed 237–243,322–324 and 357–360 returns. This appraisal covers that division. The combined appraisal assesses the entire received body through the four contiguous readings.

The strongest contribution is conceptual discrimination. A startling event, specialist skill, lack of a local competitor, an announcement of prophecy and generic divine causation are each insufficient by themselves to establish intended divine attestation. Ibn Taymiyya supplies more than these negative observations: a relevant comparison class, character/content/profile recognition, multiple routes of testimony and perception, and a source whose knowledge and wisdom can make communication intelligible. The source's allowance that a follower's wonder does not make all his statements infallible is an especially valuable restriction. His differentiation of kinds of providential support also prevents equating worldly victory with complete or immediate moral recompense.

The passage's best affirmative philosophical argument connects purposive arrangement, intelligent specification and knowledge. It fairly presses a critic who appeals to purposive order for knowledge while denying that an agent ever does one thing for another. The material-transformation and real-bearer discussions make it untenable to reduce this work to a brief miracle handbook. Love, lasting ends and real responsive relations have substantive roles; they are not automatically conceded external deficiencies.

I judge the conceptual criticism of bare novelty and generic production sufficiently warranted. I also judge a contextual and cumulative route to recognising intended attestation rationally legitimate in principle; no universal demand for a pre-agreed code or one obligatory syllogism follows. The opening does not sufficiently establish the full set of claimed historical memberships, global incapacity claims or a particular token's divine communicative intention. Its ideal of a proof entailing truth must not replace the work of identifying an actual proof. Character and teaching can provide positive evidence without alone excluding sincere error, retrospective embellishment or an alternative provenance. Contextual recognition deserves its proper defeasible force, but the present reader cannot inherit another observer's claimed necessary knowledge merely because the treatise reports it.

The source's claims about atomism, substances, material persistence and ancient physical processes require separate appraisal. The verified electronic omission at L115 demonstrates why neither wholesale trust in the electronic wording nor wholesale dismissal of its argument is appropriate. The author's polemical descriptions of schools and supernatural histories have not been independently checked against every opponent or event.

As later evidence for the actual-common-source question, this division contributes a substantive purposeful-agent model and explicitly separates capacity, exercise and product conditions. It does not itself discriminate every principled plural-original alternative. The earlier standalone assessment remains untouched; further comparison belongs to the new combined supplement, rather than retroactively presenting selected reinspection as whole-book reading.
