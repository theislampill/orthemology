## Strongest positive reading

Uniform dependence on one condition is sufficient, but is not the only repair. If an independently warranted deletion leaves precisely retained requisites of a genuinely possible e, none of those retained facts can itself entail not-e: e entails each requisite, and their entailing not-e would make e impossible. A necessary fact likewise cannot entail the negation of a genuinely contingent e. If every obtaining eligible witness must lie within that exhaustive remainder, absence-witnesses are excluded without requiring one shared requisite for all witnesses.

That conclusion earns genuine conditional credit. The exact possible-world construction and its exhaustive closure must be warranted; writing a partial inventory does not establish them. Conversely, a justified deletion principle could supply that warrant without a separate universal-dependence theorem. The appropriate assessment is consequently a bounded proof-obligation diagnosis, not a refutation of the whole strategy. A defender may provide basic or abductive reasons for the requisite modal premise; neutrality, empirical measurement and exhaustive rival elimination are not admission requirements.

## Target application

Even a fully repaired conditional-sufficiency argument does not alone identify actual E_A supply. An entailment licenses a truth consequence; adequate supply requires the relevant explanatory content. This distinction does not deny that a substantive necessary condition could also be explanatory, or that a nonproductive metaphysical account could answer the availability question. The bridge must be justified at that very respect.

Necessary existential truth also does not identify one necessary particular. Genuine existence of an actual necessary capacity bearer would deserve its own credit if independently established, without requiring actual exercise first. Productive participation, original whole-origin provision and P1/P2 remain further claims. The candidate establishes none of those antecedents.

The retained Tenth warrant for local nonpresupposition and prima-facie answerability is unaffected. The accepted Eleventh assembly and accounting distinctions likewise remain in force: grouping relevant facts does not identify an owner or original provider. These are inherited boundaries, not new results of this assessment. Ninth's distinction between conditional modal consequences and actual source-nature warrant is preserved.
