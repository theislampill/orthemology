# Rival repairs — Candidate G

## Plural primitives

**Objection:** a modal fact, brute actuality, primitive content relation, and brute norm can jointly explain all target roles.

**Repair:** do not count bearers. Demand evidence of cross-domain constraints and compare independently variable primitive profiles. A unified theory gains only where one architecture derives coordination.

**Counter-repair:** the pluralist may add primitive bridge facts or accept the coordination as brute. This remains coherent; Candidate G becomes abductive.

## Impersonal conceptualism

**Objection:** an impersonal conceptual field grounds content and modal structure; actual and normative linkage are further structural relations.

**Repair:** ask whether the field derives content-token identity, reason-responsiveness, and actuality linkage or merely labels them primitive.

**Counter-repair:** a sufficiently rich impersonal field may satisfy every functional role. Rich personality is not entailed.

## Holistic powers

**Objection:** one concrete power-network fixes possibilities and transitions immanently.

**Repair:** test whether truth/content and objective normative authority follow from the same nature or require additional primitive coordinates.

**Counter-repair:** relationally complete teleological powers may integrate them. This is a serious unified impersonal rival.

## Brute thick totality

**Objection:** the whole coordinated profile is necessarily brute.

**Repair:** carrier-boxing does not help, but brute-fact parsimony gives a defeasible cost when a non-ad-hoc derivation is available.

**Counter-repair:** cost is not contradiction; parity applies to any proposed divine ground whose nature is equally brute.

## Distributed agency

**Objection:** integrated rational roles can be realized collectively without one personal subject.

**Repair:** distinguish organizational integration from ownership, first-person unity, responsibility, and intentional agency.

**Counter-repair:** those richer features are not established by orthability alone.
