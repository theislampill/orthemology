# A bounded common operational model

## Result and ceiling

The binary-table history kernel and the typed criterion runtime can be joined through a common parametric event system. The original history system is recovered by an exact state/event correspondence. The typed specialisation uses the unchanged compiled source transition and complete successor. A further forward simulation covers arbitrary finite traces of the stated actual runtime operations: preparation, attempted landing, authentic serial certification and completed-certificate delivery.

This is a bounded mathematical/reference-execution result. It does not say that all Seventh Tranche models compose, that the whole compiled program is verified, or that a physical implementation meets the premises. Aggregate cancellation, runBatch, progress, fault discovery and unknown-root alias transport remain separate. N2, T0 and R5 remain open.

## Why a direct table replacement would fail

The original physical Table type has four values. The typed Plant contains unbounded natural-number versions and finite audit histories. No injective encoding of the entire Plant type into Table exists. This cardinal result is about the types; it is not a separate theorem constructing infinitely many reachable plants.

Literal state idempotence also fails. A fresh, correctly versioned installation can leave the visible exact rule unchanged while incrementing ruleVersion and appending ruleHistory. The correct replacement results are exact per-event effects, cumulative audit counts, goal preservation and exclusion of a fixed old action after all lawful finite continuations.

## Common interface and original correspondence

The common interface supplies policy, command, plant and requester types; epoch/path/recipient projections; a local admission predicate; and a deterministic admitted effect. Its only laws expose requester and epoch equalities already checked by local admission. It does not assume currentness, source preservation, goal preservation, correct effects or the desired safety conclusion.

Both specialisations discharge those laws from the exact predecessor definitions. Generic structural induction establishes authentic stored policies, no future policies, durable old-epoch revocations and durable selected full-command cancellation memories. Full policy identity at an equal epoch follows from equality to one authoritative serial source, rather than from numerical equality alone.

For the original specialisation, state maps are inverse, all ten event constructors are covered, and landing agrees even on damaged or unauthorised states. Every original finite trace lifts, and every annotated common trace lowers to an original trace. There is no reachability or safety restriction on this original correspondence. Prepare/land timestamps are erased only because original admission does not use time.

## Typed command and observation boundary

The complete original Envelope remains the command value. Its ordered path, action, nonce and entire successor remain part of identity. A subtype proves that the path is duplicate-free and in range; the projected finite-root set has exactly the original list length. Memory lists are compared by membership, which is all the compiled guards observe. The full command is never identified merely by a nonce or by its selected-root set.

The typed admission predicate calls unchanged localStep on the actual plant, stored local Policy and the same event-time annotation, requiring the exact full proposed successor. Actual runtime correspondence identifies that annotation with World.now. No root guard consults the common source function or World.effectivePolicy. No actor receives World.now through this construction: the exact proposal witness uses the actor's stored observedTime, and the repair actor uses the predicted installed plant rather than reading a successful world as an unexplained observation.

Time annotations are unrestricted mathematical inputs. These results do not prove their authenticity, monotonicity or relation to elapsed service time. An authentic actor clock must also admit the proposal; an actually valid lease alone does not rescue a non-admitting stored observation.

The accepted native fixture begins installation at raw epoch 2 and repairs at raw epoch 3. The offset construction does not rewrite those fields. With base=2, common control index e denotes a stored source Policy whose unchanged raw epoch is 2+e. Raw Policy, Grant, Action and Envelope fields still enter the original compiled checks unchanged. Initial alignment is proved for the exact initialWorld constructor, including the four-root parameters and empty completed-certificate list. It is a fresh post-boundary reference state; it certifies no arbitrary lifecycle preceding raw epoch 2. New owner certificates and tombstones arise at raw base+index. Soundness/completeness of the recorded post-boundary certificate stream and uniqueness of its previous epochs are maintained.

A different starting state with historical pre-base messages would require its own preserved-prefix/initial-alignment evidence.

## What the runtime relation preserves

Aligned relates the actual root count, fault budget, preparation quorum, revocation quorum, whole plant, complete effective Policy, continuing taint set and every root's stored policy and relevant memory. It also relates the complete recorded certificate history in both directions, with distinct previous epochs and each original ordered acknowledger list's exact root-set membership.

The relation is stated at idle macro boundaries. Its intermediate common trace may contain request and individual close-before-acknowledgement events. This is a forward simulation of the aggregate reference functions; it is not an assertion that an atomic local function already implements a distributed service.

Actual certifyTransition is simulated by request, the distinct acknowledgement sequence and complete. The caller must supply the full authentic next source record, not merely a policy with the next numerical epoch. A control demonstrates that the actual shape checker accepts a same-epoch altered Policy; therefore source authenticity is a necessary external premise, not a theorem of the shape check.

Actual deliver is simulated by primitive deliveries to the intact in-range roots it updates. Delayed and repeated completed certificates are included. Their full source Policy, rather than only their epoch, is bound by the maintained certificate relation. Faulty roots are left unchanged by that runtime function.

Actual prepare is simulated by one primitive preparation for every root whose exact pre-call signing predicate returns true. Partial preparations are retained even when the aggregate all-signed result is false. The single shared badSign Boolean is preserved. Preparations change only commitment memory, so the sequential primitive simulation does not change the eligibility that determined the runtime signing set.

Actual attempt is either one admitted primitive landing or an identity stutter. The single shared badOpen Boolean is preserved. The common damage classifier is proved inactive from reached-history admission; it is not used as an additional runtime gate. The entire resulting World update agrees in all related fields, including the complete plant successor.

RuntimeTrace includes bounded ordered envelopes, successful well-shaped certification under the authentic post-base stream, and all recorded or unrecorded delivery calls. Wrong-quorum preparation paths are represented and rejected. Duplicate/out-of-range raw paths are outside the envelope subtype, with separate actual-function identity lemmas for invalid-path calls. Arbitrary unauthenticated policy creation, direct plant mutation, arbitrary clock updates, aggregate cancellation and runBatch are not silently included.

The chosen path [1,2,3] is intact under the fixture's lifetime fault set {0}. This proves existence of a genuinely successful runtime trace and its common history. It does not describe how an actor discovers that path or guarantee that any scheduling strategy finds it. Closed fixture proofs use ordinary Lean kernel reduction. Their full byte-record comparisons require an explicit bounded compiler thread stack; no native_decide, custom axiom, unsafe declaration or external truth oracle is used.

## What remains external

The continuing fault set bounds the lifetime union of relevant failed duties, not a moving instantaneous adversary. Root-wide coherent veto across copies, atomic observation of the same complete plant and time, non-bypassable same-command mediation, authentic source custody, serial legitimate Policy issuance, and actual reservation/consent remain substantive premises. Shared corruptible support must be charged; multiple labels or copies do not establish independent roots.

Stable authorised windows, delivery, bounded service, correct proposal-admitting actor observations and enough remaining work capacity are progress premises. Safety and a successful chosen-path witness do not prove them. The finite-history cancellation results in the common model remain valid, but they do not by themselves prove an aggregate cancelWith simulation for the stronger runtime class.
