## Exact semantic contract

World W, Bearer B, Resource R and Target T are independently typed. W has an actual point; ex(w,g) means this same bearer exists at that represented alternative. This supplies an inhabited modal domain, not an accessibility logic or evidence that a finite fixture exhausts metaphysical possibility.

WholeReceived(w,g) denotes whole-existence receipt in the adopted ontology. ResourceReceived(r) denotes receipt of the concrete intrinsic resource instantiation in that same exhaustive existential respect at actuality. Neither means every token condition, efficient production alone, nor mere modal co-occurrence. ActualOriginalResource(r) is exactly ActualResource(r) and not ResourceReceived(r). It contains no bearer, necessity, independence, uniqueness or perfection clause.

ActualReceivedTarget(t) states the occurrence/application premise. Relevant(r,t) records the resource's role in that target's complete provision. Intrinsic(g,r) records that the very resource is instantiated in/constituted by g, rather than an arbitrary downstream effect.

Parameters:
1. Occurrence: some t satisfies ActualReceivedTarget.
2. Completion: every such t has a relevant ActualOriginalResource r. This is positive actual completion, not merely a prohibition on wholly received hypothetical adequate accounts. It is a metaphysical premise, not proved from local causality.
3. Realisation: every actual resource has an actual bearer intrinsically instantiating it. Its single original-resource instance is all the A proof consumes.
4. SupportTransport: an actual bearer's whole receipt, together with its intrinsic instantiation of r, entails ResourceReceived(r). This is ontological support in the same respect, not an efficient bearer-to-attribute edge.
5. ReceiptRepresentation: WholeReceived(w,g) iff the inherited existential binary Received(dep,w,g). This is an explicit adopted-ontology representation condition. It does not provide a global fact-to-bearer theorem or manufacture self-edges for general grounds.
6. ContingencyNeed: every actually existing bearer which is not Necessary is WholeReceived actually. The inherited interface has this general quantifier; the particular proof uses its g-instance. The philosophical appraisal is independent of checking this conditional.
7. ConstitutiveReception: any bearer received in some world is received at every world where that same bearer exists. A change of supplier is allowed. This is the general source-of-existential-adequacy judgement, not a conclusion-specific backward-transfer postulate.

A returns the original resource, its target relevance, and the same actual whole-original bearer instantiating it. B/C convert through ReceiptRepresentation and CALL the byte-exact inherited SourceIdentity theorems. The final conjunction retains that resource/bearer identity and Necessary plus UniformRoot, as well as broad essential nonreceipt.

## Discriminating controls and nonvacuity

- Full ABC: two worlds, two bearers and two resources, original source and genuinely received effect/resource. Nonempty occurrence, realisation and nonvacuous support transport; contingency-as-need may hold vacuously when all bearers are necessary, so a richer contingent-effect positive variant will also be checked if useful.
- Completion removal: Nat bearers, all actual and received; strict transitive ancestry dep(y,x) iff x<y, supplier x+1 for x. All actual resources are received and realised. Other major assumptions hold; no original bearer.
- Realisation removal: the same infinite acyclic all-received bearer ancestry; an actual original resource exists but has no intrinsic bearer. Other major assumptions hold; no original bearer.
- SupportTransport removal: the same ancestry; an actual original resource is realised by a received bearer. Other major assumptions hold; no original bearer.
- ContingencyNeed removal: two actual bearers, one supplying the other, both absent in the other world. Completion, realisation, support, representation and generic constitution hold; no necessarily existing bearer. Essential nonreceipt conditional on existence still holds for the original witness.
- ConstitutiveReception removal: two necessary bearers swap the supplier/receiver role across two worlds. Each world's relation is acyclic, the A witness is fixed, necessity holds, yet no uniform original bearer. Other major premises hold.

These are logical counterinterpretations, not certifications of metaphysical possibility. Nat controls are not finite truncations and do not rely on causal cycles. Infinite all-received ancestry directly tests the stronger completion premise against local supply.
