# Direct primary abstract check

8 October 2026 UTC. Root read the authors' complete abstract on PubMed for Dervic et al., Religious affiliation and suicide attempt, American Journal of Psychiatry161(12),2303–2308, DOI10.1176/appi.ajp.161.12.2303: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15569904/ . Full article was not read by root.

The abstract concerns371 already-depressed inpatients, comparing religious affiliation with nonaffiliation. It reports differences in lifetime attempts and several other characteristics, while finding no difference in measured depression or hopelessness. It describes association and possible protective factors. This endpoint cannot establish that atheism is a psychiatric illness or causes depression in the general population.

This direct check supports the empirical reader's endpoint correction. It supplies no diagnosis of any person, no clinical recommendation, and no metaphysical conclusion about religious truth. The author's normative or spiritual evaluation must be assessed separately from what this clinical study measured.
