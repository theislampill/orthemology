## Distinctions to preserve in any future use

1. **Logical interpretation versus metaphysical possibility.** A mathematical model witnesses independence from its stated premises. It does not, by that fact, certify source-faithful possible worlds. Adding a stronger never-idle premise can exclude this model without invalidating its reduced-signature theorem.
2. **Created effects versus emergent intrinsic acts.** Absence of all creatures is not, by definition, absence of all intrinsic divine activity. No source-faithful modal possibility of either total absence is newly established here. The tiny model co-varies each represented G-act and its effect by its chosen E table; that model-local wiring is not a source doctrine that every possible intrinsic act must have a simultaneously present separate creature.
3. **One kind versus the whole genus.** EmptyG uses one stipulated kind. EmptyEmergent ranges over every represented emergent constructor, including effects and the auxiliary kind; it is not a complete catalogue of all source-recognised activity. The model's explicit scope counterexample is sufficient to block substitution of the stronger formula for the weaker one.
4. **Every token ends versus an idle interval.** The each-token theorem has a token-dependent later-absence witness. It does not by itself give a shared empty time or an interval without any activity. The model's gap is an additional selected trajectory fact. No all-current-tokens-cessation claim entails that fresh tokens do not succeed them.
5. **Standing capacity versus actual succession.** Persistent possible fresh action alone does not impose actual activity at every time. The saved source audits independently record stronger genus, perfection and causal arguments. Their translation must still specify what counts as a time, boundary, interval and voluntary act.
6. **Genus persistence versus one eternal particular.** Always some activity does not mean one numerically fixed act eternally persists. Nor does it create a second independently subsisting necessary individual called the genus. The formal type and capacity remain predicates.
7. **Emergent does not imply Created or external receipt.** An intrinsic voluntary act can be temporally discriminated without being a separately created bearer or a newly externally bestowed essential perfection. The source's act/product distinction and the bounded model both preserve this. No blanket denial of intrinsic inherence or dependence on the agent is inferred.
