## 1. Central question

## 2. Controlled finite weighted systems

For each `h∈{A,R}` let:

- `S_h` be a finite hidden-state set of size `n_h`;
- `U` be a common finite intervention alphabet;
- `O` be a common finite neutral observation alphabet;
- `alpha_h` be an initial probability row vector;
- `M^h_{u,o}` be the nonnegative matrix whose `(s,s')` entry is the joint probability of moving from `s` to `s'` and emitting **next observation** `o` under action `u`;
- `sum_o M^h_{u,o}` be row-stochastic for each `u`;
- `1_h` be the all-ones column.

For an action-observation word

\[
w=(u_1,o_1)\cdots(u_k,o_k),
\]

set

\[
p_h(w)=\alpha_hM^h_{u_1,o_1}\cdots M^h_{u_k,o_k}\mathbf 1_h.
\]

A policy may be deterministic or randomized, but must use only the common public action-observation history and private randomness independent of the architecture label.

## 3. NIBE-1A — open-loop trace equivalence iff adaptive nonidentifiability

The following are equivalent:

1. `p_A(w)=p_R(w)` for every finite action-observation word `w`;
2. every finite-horizon deterministic candidate-independent adaptive policy induces the same transcript law under `A` and `R`;
3. every finite-horizon randomized candidate-independent adaptive policy induces the same transcript law under `A` and `R`.

### Proof

For a deterministic policy, a transcript fixes the actions selected at every prefix. Its probability equals a product of policy factors, each equal to `1`, and one model word probability. Equality of all model word probabilities therefore gives equal transcript probabilities.

For a randomized policy, condition on its private random seed or multiply by the common action-selection probabilities at each prefix. These policy factors are identical under `A` and `R`, so word-probability equality gives transcript-law equality.

Conversely, if some word `w` has unequal probability, the open-loop policy that issues its action sequence makes the associated observation sequence a distinguishing event. Thus deterministic adaptive equivalence implies word equality.

## 4. NIBE-1B — finite witness horizon

Construct the difference representation

\[
\alpha=(\alpha_A,-\alpha_R),
\quad
M_{u,o}=\operatorname{diag}(M^A_{u,o},M^R_{u,o}),
\quad
\beta=(\mathbf 1_A,\mathbf 1_R)^T.
\]

Then

\[
p_A(w)-p_R(w)=\alpha M_w\beta.
\]

Let

\[
V_k=\operatorname{span}\{\alpha M_w:|w|\le k\},
\qquad
V=\bigcup_kV_k,
\qquad
r=\dim V\le n_A+n_R.
\]

If `V_k=V_{k+1}`, then `V_k` is closed under right multiplication by every alphabet matrix, so the chain has stabilized permanently. Starting from dimension at most one, the reachable space is therefore generated by words of length at most `r-1`.

Hence:

\[
\bigl[p_A(w)=p_R(w)\text{ for all }|w|\le r-1\bigr]
\iff
\bigl[p_A(w)=p_R(w)\text{ for every finite }w\bigr].
\]

The coarser bound `n_A+n_R-1` is always sufficient. No claim is made that this bound is tight for every subclass.

## 5. NIBE-1C — exact gate-E characterization

A neutral adaptive discriminator exists exactly when some finite registered action-observation word has unequal probability. When one exists, a witness occurs by horizon at most `r-1`.

If the trace laws agree, Deep AQ `GATE E` is blocked for the entire registered experiment algebra. Breaking parity then requires at least one of:

```text
an enlarged action or observation experiment;
an independently warranted source-conditioned exclusion;
an independently defended prior or coding policy;
restriction of the intended model class by nontrace evidence.
```

A relabeling, learned representation, or compression of the same transcript experiment cannot create raw discrimination under a common channel.

## 7. Countermodel architecture

### AT-CM-HIDDEN-PERSONAL-TAG

Personal and impersonal expansions share the full neutral weighted system and differ only on an unregistered `Personal` predicate. Every neutral adaptive protocol has the same transcript law.

### AT-CM-ENDPOINT-SAME-TRAJECTORY-DIFFERENT

Two systems have equal endpoint success but one finite intermediate observation word has unequal probability. A trajectory experiment distinguishes the models without establishing proper function or personality.

### AT-CM-REPRESENTATION-ERASURE

A common observation channel merges the only distinguishing symbols. The represented systems become trace-equivalent. This is information loss, not evidence that the raw models were equal.

### AT-CM-CANDIDATE-DEPENDENT-POLICY

A policy reads the candidate label or uses candidate-specific action kernels. It can distinguish by construction but is not a neutral experiment.

### AT-CM-VERSION-DRIFT

A source or runtime version change alters admissible interventions or matrices. Trace equivalence at `v` does not automatically transport to `v'`.

### AT-CM-INCOMPLETE-INTERVENTION-ALGEBRA

The models agree under all registered actions but differ under an unregistered intervention. The theorem establishes experiment-relative nonidentifiability, not absolute metaphysical equivalence.

## 8. OSM, PRH, T299/T300, and architecture effects

### T299/T300

A successor causal/profile-blindness experiment must change the action-observation algebra or the candidate predictions. Repeating the retired configuration with more copies or seeds does not by itself break trace equivalence.

### Candidate A

### Candidate B

Event-local restorative receipts can be trace-equivalent while hidden long-run dynamics differ. Model-bound temporal interventions are required before stable-restoration claims are operationally distinguishable.

### Candidate C

Neutral trace difference or equivalence does not supply personality, Wisdom, Speech, or source-world truth. A constitutive-semantic or source-conditioned premise cannot be inserted as a neutral observation without separate authority.
