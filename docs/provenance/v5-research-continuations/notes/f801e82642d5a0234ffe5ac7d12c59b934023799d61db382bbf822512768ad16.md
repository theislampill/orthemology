## Fully derived fallback tail

Let m=|B0|, q=|State x Action|, D=|State| |Action|^|State|, r=p^D,
c=(min(eta,1))^2/2 and

    J_N = q(N+m-1) + 2m(N+m)

For every positive integer N and every natural k, the actual raw-stack law gives

    Pr[C > J_N kD]
      <= 2 q |State| exp(-Nc)/(1-exp(-c)) + J_N (1-r)^k

The compiled source keeps the extended-real arithmetic explicit and preserves
integer threshold/cardinality conventions. Taking k=N gives a quadratic
threshold with polynomial-times-geometric failure probability. The separately
qualified `PolynomialTailMoments.cutoff_union_tail_all_moments` proves every
natural power integrable from exactly this derived bound. It handles possible
infinite cost by the tail itself.

The linear coefficient is alpha=q+2m and beta=q(m-1)+2m^2. The segment rank gives
a slightly tighter bound than the ordinary addendum's conservative +2m term.
No sharpness claim is made.
