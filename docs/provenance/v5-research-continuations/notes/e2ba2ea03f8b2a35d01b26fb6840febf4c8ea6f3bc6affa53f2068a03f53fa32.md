# Intrinsic exercises, productive support, and complete sourcehood

## Answer to the constructive question

An act can be unborrowed from every other agent while still depending on its own bearer, power, and determination. Treating “unborrowed” as absolute autonomy would be a mistake. This leaves a defensible positive route: a source complete for **all the actual productive efficacy** of a field must account for intrinsic exercises as well as separately produced endpoints. Such accounting need not turn the source's acts into created objects or require a further act that creates each act.

The route does not automatically follow from completeness for externally produced substances. The difference is a scope and relation question, not a word choice. The older sourcehood theorem can be used under an activity-inclusive interpretation, but its world application needs independent support for that interpretation. The recovered third-tranche effect-side reception-mode/prerequisite argument remains the strongest narrower route from a dependent field to actual-mode coverage.

The positive conclusion is consequently exact: two distinct, genuinely unborrowed sources cannot both be individually complete for one and the same **activity-inclusive actual productive field**. What remains unproved is that the earlier existential argument supplies a bearer with that full scope, or that ordinary endpoint sufficiency necessarily has it.

## 1. The starting claim must be kept at its strength

We test two alleged necessary, independent producers A and B. Both actually produce the same numerical effect x, each by its own exercise, and each is alleged to have sufficient power without borrowing the other's power. The hypothesis is not simply two parts of one composite cause. It is not two formal necessary-existence labels. It concerns real actual origination and the strong claim that each is a complete ground.

Nevertheless, “each could fully produce x if it acted alone,” “each is an actual sufficient producer of x,” and “each supplies the whole actual productive actuality of x” differ. The last includes the actual ways x receives being. The first two do not transparently include every other producer's exercised efficacy. The argument must show which is the intended necessary-ground role and why the metaphysical inquiry warrants that role.

The accepted third-tranche argument already exposes this issue. It proves uniqueness from HistoryComplete, ProductiveWitness, and UnborrowedActs, and defends a route through real dependent reception modes and indispensable productive prerequisites. That is prior work. This supplement tests whether intrinsic act-dependence supplies a further route and identifies what it adds or fails to add.

## 2. Three relations, rather than one undifferentiated dependence

**Bearer support:** this particular exercise is the exercise of B; without B exercising its power, this very B-exercise does not occur. This can include intrinsic realization and ownership. It need not make the exercise a separately created object.

**External productive receipt:** another source supplies actuality or efficacy that B lacks independently. This compromises B's unborrowed productive status in that respect.

**Ultimate productive accounting:** a source supplies the actual efficacy of the exercise through original possession or derivative provision. This is stronger than giving a true description or a constitutive analysis, and weaker than requiring a separate efficient act that manufactures each exercise.

Bearer support does not entail external receipt. Nor does a true constitutive explanation alone entail ultimate productive supply. If A explains that B's act occurs through B's own power, A's *explanation* has not become A's *productive provision*. An account written by A is not the Account relation in the retained theorem.

The crucial source claim can therefore be formulated without violating the source/attribute distinction: B's actual exercise is not an autonomous agent or another original giver; its actuality is B's exercise of B's power. It is not externally furnished when B is an original source. But A cannot also be the complete original supplier of that very unborrowed efficacy while remaining an independent source distinct from B.

## 3. Fresh primary evidence for intrinsic action under power

Majmūʿ al-fatāwā 8:19–20 distinguishes acts occurring in the agent from transitive acts with effects, and treats the act itself as within power. Its discussion of rival positions must not all be attributed to Ibn Taymiyya's own view. The concluding affirmation of both kinds, and the general insistence that action is within power, support the relevant distinction. “Within power” (maqdur) is not translated here as “created.” [MF 8:19–20, hosted lines 906–912](https://islamweb.org/ar/library/content/22/793/فصل-في-قدرة-الرب).

MF 16:364–368 distinguishes creating from the creature and connects the act with the source's will and intrinsic determinant. It distinguishes standing kind from particular acts. This is evidence against both identifying the act with its external product and treating intrinsic exercise as furnished by an independent external completer. [MF 16:364–368, hosted lines 908–925](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/22/1683/فصل-ما-يدل-عليه-قوله-تعالى-باسم-ربك-الذي-خلق-وقوله-علم-بالقلم).

These passages support the possibility of a source-faithful intrinsic-support interpretation. They do not supply a modern all-purpose grounding relation or demonstrate that every bearer-supported exercise belongs to the same extension as externally received beings. Nor do they independently establish a source, authenticate revelation, or transfer the intended divine predicates to a neutral variable.

## 4. The positive activity-inclusive reconstruction

Here is the strongest version of the proposed new route.

1. An actual producer B is actually exercising productive power; mere standing capacity is not its actual production of x
2. That exercise's actuality is borne by B and occurs through B's operative power and determination; it is not an autonomous second agent
3. A claimed complete source of the **whole actual productive efficacy of the field** must account for this exercised efficacy, just as it must account for any other actual productive contribution in the field
4. If B's exercise is genuinely unborrowed, no distinct A supplies its ultimate efficacy; if A did supply it, B's exercise would be derivative in the very respect under examination
5. Therefore, if A and B both satisfy this activity-inclusive completeness and B actually acts, they cannot be two distinct independent complete sources

This does not assume that an effect has only one possible sufficient cause. It asks about the actual supply of an actual exercise. It does not assume that every real distinction is an external component, or that B's power is another maker. It also does not identify a creature who genuinely owns a derivative action with the ultimate source: step 4 applies only to unborrowed efficacy, not ordinary agency or semantic originality.

There is a positive reason for step 3 when the strong target is genuinely intended. A proposal that says it explains the whole actual productive reality, but omits the exercised efficacy of an actual producer, omits part of precisely what it promised to explain. The fact that the omitted producer is redundant for a narrower endpoint does not make its actual productive exercise fictitious. This is why a history-inclusive source role is intelligible and philosophically substantive.

The limit is equally important. Step 3 does not follow from the statement that A could alone produce an endpoint of x's qualitative type. Nor does it follow from a source-complete graph restricted to externally received bearers. It is a defended interpretation of a stronger source role. Its existence must be argued; it cannot be obtained by defining a role whose satisfaction already rules out every active peer.

### Does this establish Comp_D → Comp_H?

It does if D was independently warranted as the field of **all actual nonautonomous productive exercises and their dependent products**, and Comp_D means complete actual provision of their efficacy. Membership of B's act can then be established through its character as an actual exercise of B, without calling it an external creature.

It does not if D only comprises **externally produced beings** and Comp_D means that A provides sufficient production of each. Under that narrower reading, moving B's intrinsic act into D changes both the domain and potentially the dependence relation. The earlier strict ancestry theorem cannot validate the changed interpretation.

The activity-inclusive formulation is source-compatible, but that compatibility is not sufficient to justify every metaphysical application. It does not derive the extended D from actual warranted correction alone. It names a possible affirmative route which can preserve uncreated acts while addressing total actual efficacy.

## 5. The retained narrower route through the effect side

Suppose the rival uses a real mode m_B in the dependent effect: x's actual receiving-from-B. This is different from B's intrinsic exercise a_B. Its realization requires B really to exercise efficacy in the relevant productive role; an otherwise similar endpoint does not supply that receiving-from-B.

The third-tranche bridge then proceeds:

- m_B is part of the actual dependent field on this ontology
- A's alleged universal completeness reaches m_B
- B's actual efficacy is indispensable to m_B, though it may be redundant for x considered apart from that mode
- Complete independent provision of m_B cannot leave that indispensable **productive** efficacy wholly externally furnished
- If B's exercise is genuinely unborrowed, A therefore either lacks the advertised completeness or is not distinct from B in the relevant original-source role

This is stronger than merely moving all acts into D because they inhere in bearers. It begins with an effect-side dependent actuality conceded by the rival. It does not claim that A has to create B's uncreated act. It claims that A cannot independently supply a reception whose real productive efficacy comes from an independent unborrowed B.

The disputed premise is productive-prerequisite closure. It cannot be defended by saying every necessary condition is another efficient source. A correct description of x as taller than a mountain requires the mountain to have a certain height; the mountain need not supply any efficacy to x's height. In contrast, an actual reception-from-B is constituted through B's actual production. The proposed application is privileged by that productive content, not merely by linguistic reference to B.

There is still a serious rival reply. If m_B is only a derivative relational description rather than an additional real actuality, the route cannot insist on an extra entity. It must show that the actual enacted production is within the intended complete target even without reification. That is a legitimate inquiry into actual sourcehood, but it returns to the activity-inclusive scope premise. The narrower proof must not pretend the primitive-relation ontology already concedes the realist mode ontology.

## 6. The exact Suárez comparator and its limits

Fresh Latin inspection confirms that DM XXVI.4.13–16 distinguishes an action's source-specificity from its endpoint's numerical identity; receiving being need not mean that the effect could not persist under another sufficient source. The continuation, §§17–20, nevertheless rejects natural simultaneous total production by co-applied independent agents left to their natures. The permissive argument invokes absolute divine power/concurrence. DM XX.4.16 distinguishes the dependence-mode from the creature by one-way separability. These claims are historical comparators, not endorsement of their theology or a two-ultimate-source model. [DM XXVI, pp. 18–20](https://homepage.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/Michael.Renemann/suarez/d26.pdf); [DM XX, p. 32](https://homepage.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/Michael.Renemann/suarez/d20.pdf).

The relevant challenge is exact: **two distinct actions need not be two distinct endpoints merely because the actions have different source identities**. One cannot establish source uniqueness by assuming that inference without defending an action ontology. Conversely, a proposal preserving two distinct actual modes cannot make the second mode disappear from a genuinely mode-complete target by pointing out that its endpoint could remain.

We do not import Suárez's effect-side action-mode into the Athari source as an uncreated intrinsic action. Nor do we adopt his natural/absolute possibility division as our own modal theory. The new source check strengthens custody and prevents selective quotation; the basic comparator and positive reply already belong to the third tranche.

## 7. Four controls that block false shortcuts

**The intrinsic-attribute control.** A standing attribute is inseparable from its bearer. That does not make it an independently produced external object. Therefore “depends on its bearer” cannot, without further argument, mean “is in the externally received productive field.” This protects both the intended source ontology and fair treatment of rival intrinsic powers.

**The exercise control.** An actual exercise is within the source's power without requiring a second action-object that produces it. A theory can say B acts through B's power rather than posit an infinite series of acts creating acts. The new bridge uses actual efficacy, not an act-production regress.

**The recipient-ownership control.** A creature's genuine understanding belongs to that creature while its productive efficacy is received. Ultimate provision does not erase local ownership. If the unity argument identifies every subject of a supplied act with the source, it has substituted semantic ownership for unborrowed productive agency.

**The redundant-origin control.** A and B may each be endpoint-sufficient in a hypothetical ontology. That is not a counterexample after HistoryComplete has been granted. It instead asks whether the actual inquiry has independently earned HistoryComplete. Deleting B's activity while preserving x tests endpoint sufficiency; it does not preserve the complete actual productive situation.

These controls require no new formal graph theorem. They illuminate the precise metaphysical interpretation on which the retained theorem turns.

## 8. Effect on the world bridge

The intrinsic-act investigation improves the affirmative case by showing why original activity need not be either an autonomous unexplained object or an externally created object. A bearer can originally exercise power. Its act is real, source-dependent in the appropriate intrinsic sense, and unborrowed from other sources. A complete account of actual efficacy can cover such an act without treating every dependence as efficient creation.

It also prevents a false shortcut: nonautonomy alone does not put the act in the already fixed externally received domain. The needed relation must be independently interpreted as actual productive support, with its own scope justified. The older effect-side-mode route can avoid part of that expansion when the rival already grants real dependent receiving modes.

The strongest intended uniqueness claim therefore remains available and well motivated: no two distinct unborrowed producers are both individually complete for the same actual-mode-inclusive productive reality. A weaker rival can contest that role's existence or restrict completeness to endpoints. A stronger rival can contest real mode inclusion or productive-prerequisite closure. None can be refuted merely by counting necessary beings; none has been certified metaphysically possible by this analysis.

Finally, the anti-skeptical argument in the main paper does not itself choose the maximal activity-inclusive field. A warranted correction requires the appropriate target-sensitive epistemic route. It need not require that the subject has an ultimate account of every redundant source in that route's remote productive history. The complete-source demand must therefore be defended through its metaphysical rationale, rather than smuggled in as the meaning of ordinary warrant.
