actual causal execution. It continues the same v5 source boundary. All the Lean
files here are newly authored research modules outside the canonical repository.

## Kernel endpoints

### Finite observations

`StatisticalRepair.lean` and `RepairTestingBridge.lean` derive a finite Bernoulli
lower bound for arbitrary randomised repair outputs from the literal score
constraints. `BinaryConstructiveRepair.lean` supplies a sufficient strict-repair
cell. The stronger total-variation square-root bound, exponential simplification,
Hoeffding sample budget and its general adaptive postprocessing interpretation
remain ordinary mathematical arguments at their stated scope.

### Finite causal information

`FiniteQueryLocality.lean` defines the evaluator and proves whole-run and bounded-
event locality. `FiniteQueryMeasure.lean` derives the artificial fair-reference
construction. `CorrelatedOracleMeasure.lean` handles arbitrary correlations with
the seed under a common marginal; `EventualCausalRestoration.lean` concludes
countability for one actual eventual target. `ApproximateRepairIdentification.lean`
extends it to eta/epsilon tending to zero and to commonly dominated seed marginals.
`BlindGuessSharpness.lean` proves the actual zero-query controller succeeds with
exactly the initial atomic mass of the true parameter.

The final no-go is countability of positive-success parameters for EACH fixed
controller. It does not produce one parameter that defeats all controllers, or
say that every parameter has zero success. Approximate outputs need not be
coherent at every finite time; allowing them makes that obstruction stronger.
The exhibited uninformed linear-tolerance output is coherent.

### Actual positive model composition

`ProductObservationLaw.lean`, `IntegratedBinaryRestoration.lean` and
`GeneratedBinaryExperiment.lean` construct the two-standard experiment and derive
literal eventual repair. Their control dependencies are the separate eight-module
canonical full-support policy spine under `../crosswalk/lean/`.

The true model index selects the probability law, not the controller. Singleton
blocks make every block one repair action. Epsilon is fixed for a run. The
known norm-to-signal relation is a modelling contract rather than empirical
norm authentication. The mathematical selector is noncomputable Lean code over
real comparisons, not a retained executable deployment implementation.

## Physical action time helper

`PhysicalFlattening.lean` defines the least cumulative-length crossing and uses
it to concatenate actual finite executed-action blocks. Its main theorem transfers
eventual per-block goodness to eventual action goodness when cumulative lengths
are unbounded. Nonempty or eventually nonempty blocks imply that condition. With
nonempty blocks, the block containing action t lies among the first t+1 blocks;
the least crossing is invariant under changes after that finite prefix.

This is a deterministic helper. It does not by itself prove that a controller
emits nonempty blocks, that its stored records include an interrupted exit prefix,
or that a stochastic flattened stream is measurable. The control integration
must supply those bindings. The deterministic helper and its measurable successor have independent scoped
PASS under ../prior-art-challenge/physical-flattening-audit/ and
../prior-art-challenge/measurable-physical-audit/. The latter accepted receipt
is fresh-replay/REPLAY_RESULT.json. The separate recursive recorded controller
now derives the nonempty-block and observation-record conditions; its audit is
../recursive-action-audit/. These results concern a mathematical action-time
sequence, not hardware execution or a separately printed micro-step filtration
refinement.

## Actual rival transfer and common-observation boundary

`GenericFiniteQueryLocality.lean`, `GenericFiniteQueryMeasure.lean` and
`GenericQueryReference.lean` generalise the primitive to any countable inhabited
observation alphabet. Independent seed/oracle product laws are explicit here.
A genuinely full-support iid rival dominates the observed finite-query trace
law of any source oracle with that same independent seed. Positive finite-query
events therefore transfer to the actual rival, not merely to an artificial
third reference. The independent audit is `../generic-query-audit/`. This does
not by itself prove the arbitrary-policy simulator's probability-law equivalence.

`COMMON_OBSERVATION_BOUNDARY.md` gives a short classical full-path corollary:
finitely many eventual target demands under identical observed laws need a
common accepted action. The stronger statement uses one nonzero measure
dominated by all full-path laws. Its two Lean sources have independent scoped PASS in
`../common-observation-audit/`. The compact-space and closed-target successor
in `../compact-observation/` likewise has independent PASS under
`../compact-observation-audit/`. The compact conclusion is existence of a
common action, not convergence, actual selection, or a simultaneous all-target
tail. These are classical explanatory corollaries, not historical novelty claims.
