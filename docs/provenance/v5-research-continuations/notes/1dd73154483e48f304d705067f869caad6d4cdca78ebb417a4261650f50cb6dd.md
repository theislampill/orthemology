## Exact accepted statement

For finite row and receipt types, normalized nonnegative real rows, an arbitrary independent probability seed and N>=1, the probability that some deterministic raw prefix of some row/symbol at count at least N deviates from its true probability by at least eta is bounded by

    card(rows) * card(receipts) * 2 exp(-N c) / (1-exp(-c)),
    c = min(eta,1)^2 / 2,
    eta > 0.

The event on the left retains the original eta. Only the rate is clamped. For Markov rows indexed by state/action, the cardinality multiplier is state_count^2 * action_count. The proof admits zero row probabilities and nontrivial independent private seed spaces. An empty row type simply gives an empty deviation event; the controller application separately has nonempty observed/action carriers.

This rate is weaker than sharp Hoeffding. It supplies a proved concentration input for the separately reviewed ordinary controller-cost argument. It does not by itself prove expected controller cost, conditional sample laws after adaptive stopping, an executable sample complexity, or a physical clock bound.

## Source and probability audit

`FiniteWordDeviation` applies the retained centered-Bernoulli word MGF at deterministic eta and -eta, then bounds both tails simultaneously. The eta<=1 condition is the inherited MGF interface and is used explicitly. The upper estimate p(1-p)<=1/2 is deliberately conservative.

`RawWordLaw` obtains the entire finite Boolean word law from actual seeded stack-coordinate independence and singleton/complement probabilities. It does not infer independence from equal one-coordinate marginals. The map from a fixed row's distinct finite indices to stack cells is injective; the seed enters through the actual independent product law.

`RawPrefixDeviation` equates the finite word mean with the literal rawFrequency at exactly the same positive count. The centered-sum conversion uses count positivity. `RawExponentialTail` unions all deterministic counts N+k, all rows and all receipt symbols, and evaluates the nonnegative geometric series with a strictly positive denominator.

`RawUnrestrictedDeviation` uses event inclusion under a smaller tolerance. It extends the bound to every eta>0 without changing the controller's tolerance or the original bad event. This is important in the all-identical-row controller case, where the admissible chosen tolerance need not be bounded by one.
