## 2. From root identity to genuinely revised evidence

### 2.1 Fixed roots: idempotence belongs to identity, not repeated Bayes multiplication

In the provenance-learning owner, an active set E selects strictly positive immutable likelihoods L_i under a full-support prior. Copies of one acquisition share its root ID; two genuinely distinct roots can have identical likelihood vectors. Set union is idempotent, but multiplying a nonconstant positive likelihood twice is not. Indeed every semilattice homomorphism into the positive-vector group modulo common scale is trivial. This explains why root-aware merge cannot be ordinary likelihood multiplication. Its correct overlap law is

q(E∪F) = normalize(q(E) q(F) / q(E∩F)).

For disjoint roots the denominator is the prior, not a constant. With two distinct roots a,b and likelihood (2,1), both singleton stores yield posterior (2/3,1/3), but withdrawing a produces different next answers. Merging the same root twice leaves (2/3,1/3); merging distinct roots gives (4/5,1/5).

ET-01's exact quotient retains informative-root membership and has 2^k classes for k nonconstant factors. The independent review improves the witness length: if the current outputs agree, one named activation or deletion of a differing informative root distinguishes them. Identical vectors can thus give only k+1 current posteriors yet 2^k continuation states. Conversely, one log-odds direction can encode distinct subset sums injectively; low numerical rank alone is no memory theorem.

This is a retractable **accepted-factor store**, not a theory that historical observations should be erased. Product likelihoods require justified conditional factorization; selection into the store must be exogenous/ignorable or modeled. Changing calibration or source version is outside its fixed-factor alphabet.

### 2.2 Withdrawal and guards: the exact reset theorem has real hypotheses

ET-02 gives a general finite Boolean result. Begin with the semantic essential coordinates of the output, then repeatedly add the essential inputs of any action coordinate already retained. On the full Boolean cube, with all independent set/clear operations and the specified total coupled actions, agreement on the resulting set I* is exactly all-word output equivalence. It gives 2^|I*| states and |I*| fixed-width bits.

For certificate eligibility/minimum checking cost, initial essentials are the union of globally undominated full proof supports. Current residual supports are insufficient after withdrawal: stores {a} and {a,b} can have the same already-satisfied minimum-cost frontier, while deleting a leaves only the second supported. A guard can add a further coordinate absent from all current proof supports. Thus provenance has future control dependencies as well as present support dependencies.

Independent resets and the full state cube make the necessity argument work. Acquisition-only actions can merge states the reset theorem separates. Correlated states, partial action menus, observable refusals or changed source meanings require their real contract, not a pretend independent bit model. The later Pro D1 construction supplies the finite generalisation for exactly those cases.

### 2.3 A source can lose testimonial weight while its observed report remains informative

The source-audit owner, ET-03–04, advances beyond the accepted-factor store. It fixes a joint law of target Θ, retained background B, observed report Y and later audit A. Correct revision is P(Θ|B,Y,A). Deleting only Y while retaining A is exact **iff** Y is conditionally neutral on the supported target states after B,A are fixed. Learning “the source was a fair randomizer” does not establish that neutrality after a report-dependent audit selection.

The decisive model is concrete. A report raises the target probability from 1/2 to 7/10. An audit then says this source was random and that an independent 4/5-accurate verifier disagreed. Correct revision gives 1/5, because the observed report plus disagreement identifies the verifier's content. Audit-aware deletion gives 1/2. Dividing out the old report but keeping the old report-conditioned audit likelihood gives 3/31. The report is useful as a record of what was checked, without remaining trustworthy testimony.

A positive replacement uses the full report/audit joint factor, or the audit likelihood followed by the report likelihood **conditioned on that audit**. Shared calibration may require changing a block of surviving factors. A previously target-neutral record can become necessary when a later audit exposes its relation to another signal. This is a source-specific application of inherited missing-interaction mathematics, not a fresh parity theorem.

Proper-score accounting makes the loss precise under the declared model. The main wrong deletion has squared-probability regret 9/100. An implementation can improve overall expected score and still make this incorrect update; positive net gain does not certify every step. A null audit has no canonical “tiny fallibility” repair: arbitrarily small total-variation perturbations can preserve the target prior while producing any desired conditioned target law. This numerical boundary does not establish that any real exceptionless source failed.

## 3. Histories, predictions and the information an inquiry actually needs

### 3.1 Predictive quotient and posterior-only quotient are distinct

ET-05A carries the inherited H-Continuation construction through an actual source-audit instrument. For finite substochastic branch matrices M_a, retain a basis B of the invariant observable span generated by total mass u and target rows c under all future words. Signed reduced matrices are harmless on this cone; a vector outside it can generate negative “posteriors.” Impossible branches remain zero and are never normalised.

For normalised laws, equality of B records is exactly equality of all future word probabilities and defined target posteriors. In the audit model, a 16-state description reduces to eight target/audit masses for audit-only continuation. In the known-Bad slice, four joint target/audit masses suffice. Separate target and audit marginals still fail: the same neutral marginals lead to posterior 4/5 or 1/5 after the same audit. Re-reading that same audit is idempotent; two fresh independent verifier outcomes are a different experiment and can give 1/17.

ET-06 solves a strictly weaker comparison. To retain conditional answers but discard event frequencies, cross-multiply normalised outputs and close the paired tensor rows c⊗u−u⊗c under the **same** branch matrix in both slots. Vanishing paired rows plus a separate feasible-language check characterises all-word posterior equivalence. The feasibility check is essential: zero mass on one side makes the cross-products vanish even when the other side has a live answer.

For one fixed binary audit the posterior-only signature recovers the full joint law off independence, but collapses differing audit frequencies when both conditional target laws equal the current one. This is a genuine nonlinear quotient; its paired-test dimension is not the memory of one belief.

### 3.2 Expected inquiry needs branch weights, not just correct conditional answers

ET-07 supplies a three-outcome discriminator. Keep conditional target-one probabilities (0,1/2,1), but give audit weights (a,1−2a,a). ### 3.4 Positive cone instruments add a distinct rigidity boundary

The versioned coverage correction **ET-23** adds the current reviewed positive-instrument owner, rather than treating it as another instance of ET-05A. On a nonzero finite-dimensional **closed, convex, pointed, generating** cone K, every map with both T and I−T positive is scalar on each uniquely determined indecomposable direct summand. Thus every finite positive instrument whose sum is **identity on all K** has branches T_y=Σ_a c_ya P_a, with stochastic coefficient columns. With a strictly positive mass functional, outcomes reweight only summand masses and retain the normalized state within each surviving summand; an indecomposable cone permits only state-independent outcome probabilities. Zero-mass branches are zero vectors, without posteriors. Mass preservation or fixing one prior is insufficient.

For one fixed instrument, group summands by equality of their **complete outcome columns**. A nonempty closed convex family in the normalized base is stable under all defined outcomes exactly when its extreme points lie in individual group bases, equivalently when it is closed under group conditioning. The smallest stable closed convex family from a state is the convex hull of its occupied group-conditionals. This is neither a reachability nor a perfect-disclosure/stopping theorem.
