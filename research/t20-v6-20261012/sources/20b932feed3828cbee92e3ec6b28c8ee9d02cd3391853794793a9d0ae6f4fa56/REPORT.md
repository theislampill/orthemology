# Particular representation, completed efficacy, and beginningless succession

10 October 2026 UTC. Bounded source-led continuation of T20. Separate evidence only; no protected mutation, integration, closure, owner acceptance, new metaphysical model, or withdrawal of an earned result.

## Result

**The source requires an adequately differentiated actual production of a particular cognitive-volitional occurrence. It does not require that occurrence to descend from a first temporal act, a will-excluding external determinant, or one timelessly complete production of the entire successive history.** These are different requirements. An explanation that merely installs a selected representation remains insufficient for its claimed ultimate selection; an explanation need not eliminate every prior intrinsic act before any particular explanation can count.

**No actual infinite-regress inference was found in the controlling prior reports.** They expressly exclude a first-moment requirement, distinguish accomplished succession from incompleted same-act dependence, and reject transferring the creature's finite-bearer argument to an everlasting original. This is therefore not a correction of a demonstrated inherited error.

The substantive increment is a source-bound inference test of the representation-origin residual. Darʾ's express distinction between conditions for each particular and conditions for the collective world blocks a tacit aggregation into a single globally completed production. Its accompanying simultaneous-completion argument also shows why mere graph acyclicity would be inadequate. The resulting positive standard is **locally completed, intrinsically differentiated efficacy**, with accountable content at the scope claimed, rather than finite temporal ancestry. The general original account satisfies several structural obligations under that standard; the inspected sources still do not supply a new complete causal derivation of the particular content of an actual divine representation.

## 1. Inheritance and what is actually under test

The representation-dependency report and RESULT, the wise frontier note, particular-determination report, wise-reason-asymmetry report, and pinned CP20/CP23 reports were checked. Their relevant conclusions remain:

- The actual original is intrinsically necessary and essentially independent; its relevant provision is not received from an independently original donor.
- Real voluntary exercise belongs within completed production. Accurate cognition alone does not establish practical basing or produce its object.
- Prospective resolve, present execution, and achieved ends can differ without ignorance or alien completion.
- A local reason need not uniquely rank every possible entire history. An incomplete human description is not a completely indifferent determining situation.
- The ship/wall and achieved-test applications supply real local reasons. The own-act love/aversion analysis and transferred-guilt exclusion supply a further content-sensitive restriction. Their accomplished scope is not diminished by an outstanding stronger question.

The current representation report already says its demand does not require a first moment, separately subsisting antecedent, or full disclosed history. Its strongest-objection section *tests* the claim that another willing would never finish; it does not endorse that claim. CP20 already records why the finite-subject premise in Shifāʾ e175 cannot simply be transported to the original. No newly detected contradiction is being announced.

The live question is whether an unexplained-content objection licenses a stronger stopping rule than those qualifications allow. It does not. But refusing the stronger rule must not turn a bare statement of intrinsic ownership into a complete particular explanation.

## 2. Majmūʿ: the particular representation is neither all knowledge nor an outside object

The complete retained 16:297–309 text was read first, followed by 310–312. The sequence matters.

At 301 Ibn Taymiyya distinguishes the genus of action from willing and doing a particular. At 302–303 the criticised Karrāmī account already adds new wills in the subject, but derives them from an unchanged old will without a newly differentiating basis. **Thus intrinsic location and token novelty alone do not meet his requirement.** His fourth account begins with successive willings, not a formerly idle subject acquiring its first act.

At 303–304 he distinguishes willing an act for its future time from presently intending its execution. He also distinguishes prior knowledge of what will be done, knowledge of present doing, and knowledge of its being done, and endorses an additional cognitive determination. Consequently “the representation arose after not being” at 305 cannot mean that the subject previously had no knowledge of the intended event. Equally, prior knowledge cannot simply replace all the particular cognitive-volitional determinations that the same paragraph affirms.

There is an important epistemic restraint: the passage supplies these distinctions but does not give a canonical one-to-one assignment of each cognitive representation to each volition. Its 310–312 continuation refuses to make differentiated knowings/willings into separated countable components. Our role labels must not fabricate such an inventory.

The source's assertions about successive willing and enduring activity are not translated here into “at every time there is an actual token,” dense-time occupancy, or identity of a divine act with its created effect. Enduring genus/capacity, modal possibility, and token actuality require separate predicates. The author's stronger succession doctrine remains an attributed source claim; it does not refute the owner's distinctions by redefining those predicates.

The operative reason at 304 is knowledge of the differentiating meaning in the intended particular. That particular's relevant features are present in knowledge and conception, not as an already existing external object. At 305 the representation itself occurs through the subject's will and power; particular specification is attributed to determinants within the subject. The paragraph then excludes capacity common to the alternatives and generic self-specifying will as sufficient explanations.

These sentences jointly distinguish:

1. prior cognitive adequacy concerning the act;
2. a particular cognitive-volitional occurrence;
3. the apprehended feature's practical differentiating role;
4. voluntary origination of the occurrence;
5. production of the separate intended effect.

They are not five independently subsisting entities. Nor can all five be replaced by one undifferentiated “because God knows and wills” premise.

Primary source: [Majmūʿ 16:293–305](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/22/1672/), [306–309](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/22/1673/); the retained [continuous electronic transcription](https://quranislam.github.io/fatwa/ibn-taymiyyah/fatwa258.html) supplies 297–312 here.

## 3. The additional discriminator: particular completion is not one collective production

The precise Darʾ return was warranted because the existing Majmūʿ extract affirms succession but does not itself spell out the collective-versus-distributed completion inference. This is a rereading of an inherited locus, not a fresh doctrinal discovery.

The current retained web selection 104–160 contains al-Rāzī's reported argument at 104–108 and Ibn Taymiyya's response from 109. At 114–116 the response asks whether completion concerns each individual worldly occurrence or the collective world. It allows the former to depend on prior occurrences and denies that the collective world depends on one specified set to which the proposed division applies. Different portions depend on different conditions. At 125–135, contrast is drawn between conditions that would all have to complete the same productive efficacy together and successive particular productions. At 143–160 intrinsic successive acts are used against the inference from an everlasting bearer to an everlasting particular product.

The qualification at 114–116 is itself conditional and partly ad hominem, not only the preceding 68–80 argument: the author says “وعلى هذا التقدير” at 114, “عندهم” at 115, and “عندكم” at 116. Its use here is to defeat the opponent's aggregation inference on the stated supposition and concessions, not to derive an adequate infinite arrangement from no premises. The positive succession basis remains the author's explicit attribution of permissibility at 113 and, at original scope, Majmūʿ 16:303–305.

The exact referent is crucial. This is **not** denial of one common original, comprehensive original provision, or an adequate account for every particular. It denies the opponent's treatment of the entire successive order as though its productive completion were one joint event with the same fixed antecedent conditions. A common bearer can perform many differently conditioned actual acts. Sameness of source does not entail sameness of the complete productive condition for every effect.

The immediately preceding response at 68–80 separately distinguishes dependence on something outside the original from dependence on its own act. Some of that argument is explicitly ad hominem against an opponent permitting succession. It should not be promoted into an independent proof that any proposed infinite arrangement is metaphysically adequate. The clear positive Majmūʿ fourth account provides the original-specific succession commitment used here.

## 4. Executed application to the representation-origin residual

Let the target be a particular representation r in an already actual original's activity. This is a role designation, not an atomization thesis.

### A. The warranted requirement

If r is invoked to explain a particular executive act, its occurrence and relevant content cannot be treated as a cost-free, already selected input. The account must warrant the differentiating feature's practical role and the actual voluntary production at the claimed scope. Majmūʿ's criticism of newly added but unexplained wills makes this demand explicit. The question remains legitimate even if the representation belongs intrinsically to the original.

### B. The stronger inference does not follow

That obligation does not entail any of these further claims:

- Some representation must be the first temporal act.
- Every earlier representation must be eliminated from the explanation until only an unchanged standing state remains.
- An external producer must first make the original able or willing to act.
- All conditions for every successive representation must be jointly complete in one state or event.
- Human disclosure of the full history is required before any local practical explanation is warranted.

The fourth claim is particularly important. From “each particular has an adequate completed basis” one cannot infer “the entire succession is a single product with one jointly completed basis.” Darʾ identifies the change in explanandum, not merely our inability to calculate the answer. Applying that distinction here requires keeping r particular; it does not establish that a complete history has no further intelligible explanation or that every particular content has already been explained.

A finite proof, readable certificate, or well-founded justificatory derivation is a different object from the actual history it describes. Requiring the offered argument to warrant its premises does not require that the divine acts represented by those premises have a finite ancestry. Conversely, a finitely described sequence does not thereby acquire metaphysical adequacy. No such transfer is used here.

Nor is a temporal beginningless sequence automatically a productive circle. The source distinguishes predecessor conditions from conditions that never finish completing this very production. Conversely, an abstractly acyclic dependence diagram would not guarantee source adequacy: it could still indefinitely defer the concurrent completion of the same act. **No finite ancestry requirement and no unrestricted regress permission follow.** No arbitrary chain or countermodel has been constructed as a metaphysical proof.

### C. Practical direction does not become an efficient circle

The representation report's relation-typing result survives unchanged. Particular willing's following knowledge and the representation's occurring through will and power do not identify the executive willing with the particular exercise involved in the representation's origination. They also do not identify practical relevance with complete efficient production.

Hence a same-token productive circle needs additional premises about both identity and relation. Removing that alleged circle establishes only the failure of that objection. It does not certify a representation's particular content. Darʾ's completion condition supplies the further safeguard: absence of an established cycle is not enough if the offered productive basis remains perpetually uncompleted for this occurrence.

### D. Why no outside donor follows

A prior intrinsic determination can matter to a later intrinsic determination while the bearer and its essential agency remain unreceived. Such an earlier-to-later relation does not cause the whole bearer whose actuality it presupposes. Majmūʿ locates content and differentiation within the original; Darʾ distinguishes an own act from an outside condition. This answers the ownership/alien-completion objection at its proper target. It does not supply the missing detailed content by the word “intrinsic.”

## 5. Which positive obligations are actually met?

The source gives positive answers, not only prohibitions.

**Productive ownership:** the representation's origination is assigned to the original's own willing and power. No future external object must efficiently donate the representation. This settles the proposed external-donor dilemma within the source account; it does not independently re-prove comprehensive sourcehood.

**Intentional-temporal differentiation:** an act resolved upon for a future fitting time is not already intended for immediate execution. The earlier resolve therefore does not by itself require the external product earlier. The difference concerns what is willed and when, not an inexplicable failure of an already complete immediate producer. This is a real bounded explanation, already earned in the particular-determination report.

**Completed sequential efficacy:** the current original need not first complete all its past activity as a single simultaneous production. Earlier accomplished acts can be conditions of a later actual act without constituting a still-unfinished production of that same act. The new application removes a stronger, unwarranted demand from the representation residual.

**Content-sensitive local reasons:** the already completed ship/wall, achieved-test, and transferred-guilt applications remain adequate for their declared means, achieved-end, and exclusion targets. The new discriminator gives no reason to revoke them because they do not derive the whole prior history. No additional application is claimed here.

**Still not met by this return:** the exact complete causal account of why an actual particular intrinsic representation takes its fully specified content and occasion. Saying it arises voluntarily, has an intrinsic basis, or belongs to an everlasting series does not instantiate that stronger explanation. No newly supplied particular selector is being credited.

The advancement is thus consequential but limited: a stronger ancestry/aggregation objection is shown not to be source-warranted, while the legitimate differentiating-content obligation survives. This is more than changing the residual's wording; it tests a specific attempted inference using the source's explicit account of the relevant productive completion.

## 6. Strongest surviving objection and answer

A serious objection asks: “Even if there is no first act, why does this sequence contain this content here? If each answer merely names a prior selected content, the original question has been relocated.”

That is a good objection to an account offering only labels. It becomes too strong if it demands that every legitimate explanation eventually reach a first act or one state independently selecting the whole history. The sources warrant the first complaint and resist the second transformation.

A successful stronger particular account would have to show an independently intelligible practical/causal differentiator with its actual role, retain complete efficacy for the occurrence, and explain whatever dependence it claims without converting the entire succession into one simultaneous event. That may involve prior intrinsic activity. Nothing read licenses treating its being prior as either an automatic defect or an automatic explanation.

The present report cannot exhibit that full account for a new actual representation. Neither can the surviving question be used to withdraw the already warranted existence, independence, knowledge, will, wisdom or local reason-sensitive agency of the common original. Its target is the strength of a particular offered explanation.
