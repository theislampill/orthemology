# Negative controls and what the computation does not prove

1. A single shrinking corner event is insufficient by its ordinary two-cell chi-square value: its chi-square tends to lambda_n², a finite positive constant. The continuous contradiction additionally uses localized integrability of a hypothetical L2 likelihood ratio. The explicit finite witness uses many disjoint shells, not repeated counting of the same event.
2. At the literal face command a=b=1, both bits are deterministic in both worlds and the pair divergence is zero. The positive interior limit is not a boundary-value substitution. This is inherited from the predecessor and checked numerically as a separate limit control.
3. P1 is absolutely continuous with a strictly positive interior density, so infinite chi-square is not produced by a singular atom, diagonal mass, or baseline-impossible event. The complete density-mass argument is analytic, not a finite grid claim.
4. Finite forward KL is proved independently. An infinite chi-square upper bound for KL would simply be uninformative; it could never by itself prove infinite KL.
5. The frozen two-probe n^4 sample lower bound used KL under its exact channel and cost model. This successor does not prove it false under its original scope, nor prove any new n-dependent full-readout testing exponent.
6. The computed finite shell slope d_n becomes very small: for example the formula evaluations give d_8 approximately 1.19e-15 and d_32 approximately 6.92e-92. These are numerical illustrations of a proved formula, not an all-n asymptotic. They warn against mistaking fixed-n unbounded-resolution divergence for practical information gain.
7. The equality control c=1,m=n gives S=xy and identical laws, so both divergences vanish. It prevents carrying positive tail dependence to the independence endpoint. The main proof includes n=1,c=1/2,m=2 separately and does not confuse those two parameter choices.
8. The finite resolution witness uses exact command levels with denominators up to 2^K and at most 2K retained face probes. No fixed finite K yields infinite divergence, and no finite probe word yields exact arbitrary real minima.
9. controls.py uses exact fractions only for the c=1 equality controls. Its derivative, density, partition, monotonicity and limiting-value checks are high-precision floating computations on bounded parameter sets with explicit tolerances. The 8,996 assertions do not prove the all-n theorem, integrability, boundary mass absence, or limits; RESULT.md supplies those arguments. There are no stochastic trials, user data, empirical samples, or physical experiments.
