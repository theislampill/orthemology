## Two additional source-handling failures worth preventing

### A title is not enough to identify a book

A discovered repository entry labelled *Shifāʾ al-ʿAlīl fī ikhtiṣār Ibṭāl al-Taḥlīl* displayed descriptive material for the predestination/wisdom work. [A23] This is a concrete title/body mismatch in the inspected record. It does not prove that the entire hosting service is unreliable. A faithful acquisition should record the title page, author, editor and incipit before treating a filename as the work's identity.

### A related reply is not the missing reply

Dretske's 2006 *Information and Closure* is not the inaccessible 2005 reply to Hawthorne. The Oxford Marmodoro record advertises a final published file, but the direct request returned 403. Neither discovery earns full-body reading or version-collation credit. [M04, M10]

## Three changes to argumentative framing

**Original-creator versus ordinary-cause semantics:** the weak OR-table has real mathematical significance but does not supply unreceived original sovereignty. Its existence also does not refute a theory which grants ordinary derivative agency.

**Perfection without illicit property copying:** a source's being the creator of a creature's act does not make it the performer of that act. The qualified giver argument must specify the perfection and mode of possession. [A10, A18]

**Rational warrant versus source attribution:** a genuine Arabic passage establishes that the author advances an argument. Whether it is a good argument requires analysing its actual premises and inferential role. Conversely, calling a premise philosophical or basic is not a reason to discard its positive evidential force.
