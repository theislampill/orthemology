## Exact scoped claim

The witness-cover number gamma is the minimum number of grounds whose witness sets cover C. LOCAL holds exactly when gamma is finite; COMMON holds exactly when gamma=1. Unique local witnesses need not be one common witness unless the witness assignment is constant.

## Proof or construction

The cover definition gives the LOCAL equivalence immediately. A one-element cover is exactly a ground related to every content. When every content has one witness, COMMON holds precisely when those witnesses are identical.
