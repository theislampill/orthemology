# Truthful guidance and access to intended content

9 October 2026 UTC. Targeted philosophical reuse of the already-read Darʾ 5:269–286 unit, following the frozen evidence-withdrawal report. The first owner supplement was read completely from its named Library item, 88 lines. The existing scriptural supplement's philosophical and mathematical assessments were also read. No new scriptural history, new primary reading credit, or comparative scriptural verdict is claimed.

## The consequential addition

The proper-availability objection has a positive source-owned foothold. In Darʾ 5:280–285, Ibn Taymiyyah's established messenger-authentication argument concerns truthful **delivery, explanation and guidance**, not simply a source that sometimes utters true propositions. A purported fulfilment in which the commissioned audience receives the false teaching as the source's indicated message and has no clarification of the truth conflicts with that very account of the mission. Calling the communication divine does not neutralize this problem.

The source supports **access to the intended commissioned teaching through appropriate means**. It does not make unique historical reconstruction of an autograph a universal condition of communication. Which verbal details are themselves part of the intended teaching or required practice must be established rather than discarded as interchangeable wording.

This strengthens a specific previously open premise: the connection from an authenticated explanatory/guiding mission to a substantive communication-success condition. It does not, by itself, infer a particular perpetual preservation policy from maximal greatness alone.

The strongest **direct** textual implication concerns an uncorrected false indication attributed to the commissioned source. Extending that objection to wholly unavailable teaching is a **purposive reconstruction**: it becomes compelling where that very teaching was part of what the source undertook to make available to those recipients in those circumstances. The source unit does not itself decide every temporary uncertainty, later archival gap or detail outside that undertaking. This marks the exact gain rather than treating all forms of inaccessibility as already condemned by the passage.

## What the actual argument says

The relevant edition-labelled witness is *Darʾ taʿāruḍ al-ʿaql wa-l-naql*, Imam Muhammad ibn Saud Islamic University, second edition, 1411/1991, as identified in [Islamweb's catalogue](https://islamweb.net/ar/library/bookinfo.php?bk_no=413). Page numbers here are the electronic witness's displayed labels, not a new print-scan collation.

At 280–282 the author considers the reply that one can preserve revelation while consigning its apparent conflict to unexplained interpretation or indeterminacy. His answer turns on whether the revelation actually indicates the disputed content. If it does not, there is no stated conflict. If it does, simply suspending its indication does not preserve that indicated teaching. He contrasts an authenticated source that has explained the truth and guided recipients with a portrayal in which it manifests falsehood and fails to explain the truth. The argument then appeals to the rational grounds establishing truthful delivery and explanation. These are the author's positive premises, not merely an opponent's allegation. [Continuous argument](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/413/967/الوجه-التاسع-والعشرون)

At 282 the authenticity argument's scope reaches everything genuinely reported within the relevant reporting domain. Selectively accepting a false report as an authentic member of that domain cannot leave the same unrestricted factive premise intact. At 285–286 the domain is explicitly **reporting from God**, with falsehood and settled reporting error excluded by the messenger's commission and the purpose of delivery. Non-delivery acts are left to separate disputed treatment. This is not global inerrancy about every act, nor a newly derived proof of divine essential veracity. [Scope and mission](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/413/970/الوجه-التاسع-والعشرون)

This reading gives the source's affirmative argument its full force: where the authenticating ground and the report's inclusion and content are established, the resulting truth commitment is real. It also identifies the appropriate response to an alleged counterexample: investigate authenticity, interpretation, domain membership or the purported contrary proof. Keeping all those premises and affirming the contradictory report together is unavailable.

## The supported communication condition

The following is a reconstruction of the source's commitment, not a verbatim principle or an exhaustive theory of revelation:

**A commissioned truthful explanatory communication succeeds, for the recipients and teaching within its commission, only if its operative means make the intended teaching appropriately accessible; the source cannot count as having clearly guided them by presenting the contrary teaching without an available clarification.**

The components have concrete roles:

1. **Content.** Identify the actual claim, directive or practice being communicated. The source's attack concerns what the utterance indicates, not bare inscription identity. A different spelling or wording history matters here only if it changes commissioned content, attribution or a required verbal form.
2. **Audience and occasion.** Identify the recipients and circumstances to which the mission extends. The passage does not establish that every person in every later age must independently reconstruct every textual detail with equal ease.
3. **Explanatory means.** Include genuinely authorized contextual explanation, demonstrated linguistic conventions, reliable teaching and other warranted communicative means. An interpreter cannot rescue the condition merely by inventing an unexpressed intended meaning that recipients had no grounds to recover.
4. **Epistemic access.** The route must give appropriate grounds for uptake in the relevant circumstances. Mere metaphysical truth of a hidden proposition, or existence of a lost original somewhere in the past, does not discharge an explanatory mission to recipients for whom it is supposed to be available.
5. **Actual uptake.** Availability and successful reception differ. Refusal, inattentiveness or failure to act on understood teaching need not show that the teaching was unavailable. The source does not promise that every hearer will assent or act properly.

“Appropriately” is not permission to assume convenient conditions. Its interpretation must be grounded in the claimed mission and recipients' real capacities. Conversely, inconvenience or disagreement alone is not proof that the explanatory condition failed.

## The internal scope control

The already-read Darʾ 7:426–430 limits knowledge obligations by capacity and distinguishes commonly obligatory teaching from detailed knowledge borne by some. It allows different hearers to be differently prepared and warns that material suitable for some may mislead others. It thereby rules out a facile inference from clear guidance to every individual's independent access to every detail. It also prevents moving the whole burden into unattainable cognitive requirements while continuing to claim that the relevant obligation has been properly explained to that person. The latter is the present purposive application of his capacity-sensitive account, not an additional explicit preservation promise. [Previously read continuous unit](https://islamweb.net/ar/library/index.php?ID=560&bk_no=413&idfrom=1327&idto=1329&page=bookcontents_ver3)

A source-compatible access arrangement can therefore involve education or qualified communal transmission. To invoke such an arrangement in a case, its actual adequacy must be shown; naming a learned community is not itself evidence of reliable access. No numeric cost bound or uniform educational entitlement is supplied by this locus.

## Intended content versus a uniquely reconstructed original

Let A identify exact autograph or original-recitation history and let C identify the commissioned content relevant to this recipient's task. The existing fibre results already show that failure to identify A need not defeat recovery of C. The present source return supplies a philosophical reason to select C: delivery and explanation concern the intended teaching, subject to whatever exact words or recitations the commission itself requires.

Consequently:

- If the actual admissible wording histories differ but preserve the relevant intended claim and its warranted source attribution, failure of unique A does not by itself defeat this communication condition.
- If the differences affect the required teaching, a prescribed utterance or the authority to attribute that teaching, the discrepancy cannot be screened away by calling it merely verbal.
- If available warranted channels cannot resolve a materially conflicting teaching to the level required by the mission, the availability objection directly engages the explanatory/guiding premise. It is no longer merely an objection that textual reconstruction is inconvenient.
- A common sentence across manuscripts is not enough by itself. The intended meaning, authenticity and audience-relevant use still matter.

There is no new mathematical theorem here. Neither source nor inherited fibre theorem establishes that appropriate warrant always requires an infallible decoder across every imaginable history. A relevant indistinguishable-history argument must use genuinely admissible alternatives and the actual epistemic access condition. A total absence of warranted access and residual uncertainty compatible with knowledge are different cases.

## What this strengthens in the owner's criticism

The criticism becomes stronger when it identifies **an actual commissioned teaching for an actual intended audience whose warranted means fail the communicative task**, rather than merely an unrecoverable autograph component. Within the source's positive account, failure of explanation cannot be dismissed as irrelevant to truthful guidance. The access norm is anchored in the mission's own purpose, not imported solely from a preference for easy historical research.

A rival with well-warranted transmission may sharpen the comparison by showing that the relevant access task can be achieved under comparable conditions. That fact alone does not establish the rival's divine authority or show that a maximally great source must maximize one transmission metric at the expense of every other purpose. The source unit supplies no comparative optimization principle.

The further inference to maximal greatness becomes available only with the claimed mission, its scope, and the alleged failure established, together with the relevant perfection/purpose commitments. Those are genuine philosophical premises to investigate, not reasons to dismiss the objection. The present operation strengthens the mission-to-access premise; it does not assert its historical antecedents for either the Bible or the Qur'an.

## Supplementary recognition cannot be an empty rescue

An appeal to basic cognition is not automatically circular merely because the person cannot articulate a proof of every faculty. The inherited CP03 discussion, directly reassessed at Darʾ 8:530–535, expressly distinguishes knowing from knowing how one knows. A real recognitional or testimonial route may contribute epistemic access without reconstructing the original inscription. [Inherited primary distinction](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/413/1594/تتمة-كلام-البصري-وتعليق-ابن-تيمية-على-أن-أصحاب-المقالات-وضعت-كل-معرفة-غير-موضعها)

But a proposed overriding faculty must actually bear on the missing **particular content or attribution**. Merely renaming the same non-discriminating input cannot identify which of incompatible teachings was commissioned. If the faculty genuinely supplies new target-relevant cognition, that is an additional route needing its own substantive appraisal; if the claim of its authority already assumes the very disputed message it is supposed to authenticate, the proposed rescue has not discharged that dependency. Neither generic fiṭrah nor a retrieval failure by itself settles such a token-specific case.

The earlier positive recognition and attestation appraisals remain intact. No demand for an infinite chain of reflective faculty certification is introduced.

## Result and next discriminating question

This operation supplies a source-owned **purpose-sensitive content-access condition** and its audience/domain limits. It strengthens the owner's availability criticism precisely at the point where an explanatory and guiding commission is alleged to coexist with unavailable or uncorrectedly misleading commissioned teaching. It does not demand a unique autograph or decide any scripture's actual preservation.

The next useful question, if the programme later instantiates this connection, is concrete: **Which teaching or prescribed verbal form, for which commissioned recipients and period, lacks an adequate warranted route after the actual available explanatory means are included?** Answering that requires selected case evidence. It does not justify a blanket new historical campaign from this result alone.
