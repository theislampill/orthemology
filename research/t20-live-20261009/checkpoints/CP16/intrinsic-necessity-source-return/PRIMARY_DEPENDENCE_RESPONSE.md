# The primary response behind Ameri’s dependence citation

9 October 2026 UTC. This continuation checks the consequential attribution in Ameri 85. The Asfahani commentary was already part of the broader project through a supplied translation. This is a new direct Arabic/context/edition check, not discovery of a previously unknown book or a claim that the project had never read it. It does not initiate a separate corpus survey. It qualifies and strengthens the first REPORT’s appraisal.

## Finding

**Ibn Taymiyya does endorse and develop the dependence argument, but not by endorsing every step of al-Isfahani’s creed.** He supplies a missing actuality premise, distinguishes proving necessary existence from proving a creator, explains necessary-through-itself by intrinsic sufficiency, and gives an explicitly collective dependence argument. He then separately rejects the creed’s composition-based unity proof.

This is stronger than a modal joint-absence argument. The Reichenbach quantifier test in REPORT §2 must not be applied as though it disposed of this independent route.

## 1. What is retained, repaired, and rejected

### Retained: the dependence route has positive content

At 32, Ibn Taymiyya separates two premises: there are actual contingents, and a contingent does not exist without a necessary basis. His next pages explain why the latter is true rather than treating a necessary being as an arbitrary exception to a universal efficient-cause rule. This is an affirmative reconstruction of the cited route.

### Repaired: bare necessary existence does not establish the intended creator

The first premise has not been demonstrated by the quoted creed. Merely dividing existence into necessary and contingent categories can establish some necessary existence while leaving open what it is and whether it creates anything. A materialist can assent to a necessary reality without assenting to a distinct maker. The target therefore requires an actual contingent effect and a productive relation, not merely a modal predicate attached to some existent (32–33).

The supplied witness is observed origination of animals, plants, and minerals. They were absent and now exist; this establishes their possibility rather than necessity-through-themselves. The author regards proceeding directly from such origination to an originator as clearer and shorter than first proving possibility and then necessity (33–34).

This is **not** a general licence to identify every novel token with a separately created entity. The examples are ordinary observed products. The passage does not adjudicate the ontology of every divine action-token, nor require a first event in the complete history of divine activity. Importing either conclusion would exceed the read argument.

### Explained: intrinsic sufficiency, not a detached modal stipulation

The key exposition is at 34–35. What cannot supply its own existence needs another; what exists through itself has itself as sufficient for its existence. The argument is presented even under the hypothetical distinction of essence and existence, and then under the author’s preferred identification of the concrete reality with its existence. It does not posit an independently existing essence that manufactures its existence. There is no efficient self-creation, and no need to strip the self of necessary attributes first.

The modal consequence is connected to that claimed sufficiency. A list of worlds in which a symbol occurs everywhere has not yet presented the same explanation. Conversely, the primary argument is not refuted by observing that a modal list alone cannot do so: the author supplies more than the list.

### Collective argument: a derivative whole cannot acquire an original source merely by grouping

At 35–36, the complete cause of the entire contingent field cannot itself be merely one of that same field. If the proposed source still needs another, it is not the self-sufficient complete productive basis being sought. The collective mode of arrangement is explicitly considered and denied the ability to remove the constituents’ contingency/dependence. This is not an unqualified assertion that all aggregate properties distribute from the members; the relevant claim concerns the continued existential insufficiency of what is wholly derivative.

The author offers this as a simpler, more direct dependence argument than the creed’s lengthy formulation. He does not retract the conclusion. The phrase “endorsed” is therefore defensible for the reconstructed dependence result, provided its added premises and restricted target are retained.

### Rejected: composition-based uniqueness

At 37–43, the separate proof from absence of all composition to numerical uniqueness is rejected. Necessary existence excludes needing a cause outside the self; it does not eliminate everything cognitively distinguishable within the self. Concomitance differs from causal priority. A common abstract description of necessity is not one shared external component in two alleged bearers. The criticism removes that attempted derivation; it does not affirm plural gods or abandon unity.

## 2. Reconstruction at the strongest warranted scope

The following makes the inferential work explicit without pretending to be a formalisation certified by the author.

1. There are actual originated products, such as the observed cases above.
2. Those products do not exist through themselves: their previous absence is incompatible with their having intrinsically sufficient necessary existence.
3. Their origination requires productive actuality adequate to it; neither prior nonbeing nor the product’s own nonexistent activity supplies it.
4. A purported **complete productive basis** consisting wholly of still-dependent actuality has not removed the same existential debt. Relabelling the collection or adding its arrangement does not by itself supply an underived basis.
5. Therefore the adequate actual productive explanation cannot terminate wholly within that derivative field; it needs a basis that is not derivative in the same respect.

Steps 3–4 are the substantive productive/dependence commitments. They are not simply the claim that every truth has some explanation. Their positive rationale is that a borrowed existence cannot furnish its own unborrowed actuality, and a complete account cannot rely entirely on precisely the resources whose actuality it is meant to account for. That is substantial anti-bootstrapping reasoning, not a demand that every model be reduced to mechanism.

The source’s formulation at 35–36 reaches the **actor of all the contingents**, whereas the observed witness at 33 begins locally. Application to the whole requires the relevant field to include everything derivative in that existential respect. If a proposed “whole” omits an independently supplied requisite, its apparent completeness is false. Conversely, a merely local producer may remain contingent without contradiction. It has not been claimed to be the complete origin of all contingents, including itself.

The source does not state the T20 resource/bearer predicates. Moving from this rationale to exact Completion, Realisation, and SupportTransport remains an interpretive application requiring the proposed resources and targets to be identified. But it would understate the text to call it only a bare PSR slogan: actual origination and total productive adequacy are already explicit.

## 3. Provisional interface question for the separate application audit

The crucial test is where the proposed qualitative basis stands relative to the field whose existence needs explanation.

### A. A contingent obtaining configuration of the derivative field

If Q is itself contingent obtaining, or only the relational arrangement of the same derivative reality, the collective dependence argument applies directly in intention. Saying that no individual member is fundamental does not by itself discharge the whole’s dependence. The basis needs an explanation of its own obtaining; non-distributive grounding is not equivalent to intrinsic sufficiency.

Whether Q really has this status must be argued. Describing its mathematics through individual variables is not a proof that its metaphysical constituents are those individuals.

### B. An intrinsically sufficient necessary qualitative basis outside that field

If the proposal is instead an autonomous necessary essential basis, it is not a member of the **wholly contingent** field by its stated hypothesis. The anti-bootstrapping argument cannot refute it merely by applying a premise whose antecedent it denies. This does not establish the hypothesis. It requires actual evidence for the basis, its intrinsic necessity/autonomy, its existential force, and the direction of explanation.

This exposes the exact productive bridge: could such a basis adequately account for the actual received/originated field **without** supplying an original productive resource in the relevant respect? The primary argument offers a reason to require productive adequacy; the qualitative proposal offers a different explanatory category. Their disagreement cannot be settled by redescribing all explanation as production or every necessary ground as a personal agent. Conversely, a qualitative ground’s success would not by itself demonstrate that no original producer also exists.

The separately assigned productive-totality audit owns the developed application test. This source return identifies its question without pre-empting that verdict: is its obtaining still among the derivative actuality, or is it independently warranted outside it? This is more focused than demanding an entire alternative cosmos, and stronger than repeating the joint-absence quantifier test.

## 4. Limit concerning redundant originals

The argument just reconstructed excludes a wholly derivative complete productive basis. It does not by itself distinguish one intrinsically adequate original from two purportedly adequate originals. That further question is not resolved by importing the composition-based unity proof the author rejects. Nor does absence of external donation prove that each proposed original provides the whole basis. The current original-resource adequacy and nonduplication questions remain separate.

## Disposition

The primary return strengthens the affirmative dependence rationale and earns a source-grounded distinction between **necessary existence**, **actual creator**, and **unique creator**. It does not reduce the whole argument to Reichenbach’s modal move, convert observed creature cases into an unrestricted Emergent⇒Created schema, or claim the qualitative and each-complete rivals have been defeated. Ameri’s attribution is substantially supported at the dependence conclusion’s bounded scope; the primary reconstruction adds essential qualifications that the brief citation alone did not reveal.
