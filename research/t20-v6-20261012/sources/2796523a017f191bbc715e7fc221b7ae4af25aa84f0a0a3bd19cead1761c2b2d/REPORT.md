## 3. Executed claim test

One possible overreading of the antecedent-awareness passage would require prior direct acquaintance for every genuine particular identification, allowing descriptions, testimony, or effects to supply only impersonal existence knowledge. CP03 does not assert that requirement: it expressly disavows acquaintance-only reference. The test here concerns that possible source overreading, not a supposed error in CP03's strongest argument.

That reading fails against the source's own affirmative examples. Their outputs are expressly identifying knowledge. Recognizing an unseen historical person from interconnected characteristics is not reclassified as merely knowing that somebody exists. The source also permits acquisition of the distinctive property through mediated evidence. Restricting all these cases to firsthand acquaintance would add a requirement the author explicitly does without.

The positive result is consequently stronger than a permission to utter a referring description: mediated evidence can genuinely ground particular identification. It is weaker than unconditional reference success. The evidence must actually supply the identifying connection, rather than merely accompany assent to the selected name or description. The source's property and testimony examples specify what can do this work; no invented cognitive label is substituted for it.

The adjacent sale example blocks the converse overreach. Knowing that an exclusive description has a real satisfier need not confer every further capacity to connect that satisfier with another presentation. A buyer may know which contractually specified item is owed while lacking the knowledge needed to pick it out in a new encounter. This is our explanatory reconstruction of the contrast, not a separately authenticated buyer or a claim that the source supplies a complete modern theory of singular thought.

The two positions can therefore be held together: genuinely identifying evidence may be mediated, while a particular identity-link still requires its appropriate ground. Neither bare uniqueness nor mere vocabulary acquisition automatically supplies that link. No Boolean model or new formal proof is needed to establish this textual result.
