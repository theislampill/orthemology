The independent return changes the framing of several questions:

1. **Necessity and contingency:** *Darʾ Taʿāruḍ* 8:120–131 directly challenges a global, symmetric existence-versus-nonexistence “scale” and the casual assumption that all Avicennian modal categories have independently established extramental instances. **Crucially, *Darʾ* 3:288–289 directly rejects the alternative of an existent which exists neither through itself nor another:** reconstruct that affirmative argument, not just its terminology. *Darʾ* 3:18 and 240–241 also differentiate qualified necessity of divine attributes from one original self-sufficient Creator. Distinguish originated-effect causation, intrinsic existential sufficiency and necessity-through-self without smuggling any transition into a definition.
2. **One complete Creator:** *Jāmiʿ al-Masāʾil* 6:94–97, *Majmūʿ* 20:176–181, and *Darʾ* 9:343 explicitly develop one-numerical-effect/complete-cause, necessary agreement, realised complete power and differentiated creaturely agency. Give Ibn Taymiyyah's positive argument full credit. Equally, Schaffer's actual-overdetermination examples cannot just be labelled idle/additive, nor transported to two **unreceived, maximally perfect original sources** without a justified bridge.
3. **Qualitative grounds:** *Darʾ* 5:126–144 supports concrete/extramental anti-reification. Dasgupta 2009 concerns primitive **material** individuals; 2014 concerns irreducibly **plural grounding**; 2016 connects grounding PSR to **necessitarianism**. None is accurately reduced to “a mathematical equation creates the universe.” Test existential obtaining separately from constitutive explanation or the attribution of efficient production.
4. **Fiṭrah and warrant:** The owner's six `Unknown citation` placeholders cannot be promoted. A much firmer source path is *Bayān Talbīs al-Jahmiyyah* 4:571–572 and 4:586, plus *Darʾ* 7:302–303 / 8:347–348. Ibn al-Qayyim (*Aḥkām Ahl al-Dhimmah* 2:948–949) explicitly distinguishes innate **capacity** for recognition from actual articulated infant cognition. These sources support basic recognition and cognitive-volitional fitrah, but do not automatically prove any particular subject's factive warrant, absence of corruption, or Plantinga's complete proper-function conditions.
5. **Divine attributes and particular action:** *al-Akmaliyyah* in *Majmūʿ* 6, *Majmūʿ* 8, and *Majmūʿ* 12 provide positive arguments about original giver-perfection, wise determining willing, and successive divine acts. Avoid importing a first temporal moment of all creation, an eternally fixed particular effect, or a unique numerical maximisation score. Preserve real intrinsic attributes and their concomitance without separating them into externally produced part-entities.
