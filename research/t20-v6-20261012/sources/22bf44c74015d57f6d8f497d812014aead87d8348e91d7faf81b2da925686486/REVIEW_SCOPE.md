# Review and evidence scope

## Manuscript status

This is a research draft with 14 main sections, three appendices, 21 references,
and 11 explicitly stated mathematical results with written proofs (five
theorems, five propositions, and one lemma). The error-accumulation estimate is
also derived in the text. Standard arguments are adapted and combined; the
manuscript does not claim priority for those standard mathematical results.

No independent human peer review or proof-assistant validation of these results
was conducted. The finite examples were checked by the supplied Python script.
Those checks do not establish that the idealised models apply to an unspecified
natural physical environment.

## Navier–Stokes source boundary

The user's six-page Fefferman PDF is the official problem description with

The September 2026 OpenAI announcement and reported manuscript were consulted

## Mathematical review points

The robust-bit theorem uses a known linear model, a full input norm ball, a
finite horizon, and worst-case additive noise in a closed output ball. Its
strict inequality is essential: equality permits ambiguous observations.

The nonlinear result is sufficient, not necessary. Both signed inputs must be
admissible, and the remainder estimate must hold over their certified domain.

The no-channel result requires conditional independence of message and receiver
observations; added receiver randomness must not introduce correlated side
information. Physical coupling alone does not meet the input/output premise.

The information-cut bound assumes a complete influence record with the stated
conditional Markov property. It does not sum nominal channel rates or omit
prior receiver information. The transfer lower bound concerns fresh state.

Continuation is behavioural equivalence under stated execution, migration,
ordering, and effect contracts. It does not establish subjective identity or
uninterrupted availability when the contract permits pauses.

The failure-support result is relative to an exhaustive available-support
catalogue. Alternatives that cannot be reached or activated are not silently
counted as live replacements.

The universal discovery limitation concerns programmes with unbounded working
memory, or model classes with an effective input/output-preserving embedding of
them. It is not an undecidability claim for every finite physical experiment.

The viability construction is finite-state, known-model, perfectly observed,
and robust only to its declared disturbance class. The combined host-autonomy
theorem is a sufficient-condition composition, not an empirical demonstration.

## Attribution and thought-level claims

The paper distinguishes functional agency from phenomenal consciousness,
semantic messaging from direct neural interfacing, and receipt of a message
from identification of its author. Neither thoughts nor hypothetical contact
are used as evidence of an unknown entity. The cited neural-interface experiment
used designed measurement, communication, and actuation, not ambient thought
access.
