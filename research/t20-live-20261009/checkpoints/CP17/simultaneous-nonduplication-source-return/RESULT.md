# Simultaneous complete production: primary-source return

9 October 2026 UTC. Bounded source verification and argument reconstruction. This is a return to inherited readings, not a new independent witness, new theorem, model certification, source campaign credit, integration decision, or closure decision.

## Result

The primary texts expressly discuss simultaneous production. They cannot fairly be answered simply by saying that neither producer acts earlier. **Darʾ 9:363–365 and Minhāj 1:439 directly address two complete independent agents acting in the same state; temporal succession is not their governing premise.**

But the verified immediate contradiction is stated through *whole-alone actual attribution*. Darʾ 9:338 and Minhāj 2:176–177 explain it by saying that each supposedly produces the entire effect alone, while the other's producing it denies that aloneness. Minhāj 1:439 calls the proposed simultaneous case the effect's existing twice in one state. That latter claim is not a further derivation from two occurrence-entailments: it already depends on productive nonduplication/individuation.

The strongest substantive rationale remains the already-read Majmūʿ 20:175–177: unchanged effective activity, genuine additional contribution, and the claim that their complete joint manifestation must be more or greater, otherwise the added would equal the unadded. The source also motivates freedom from foreign productive completion as a perfection of original agency. These are positive reasons, not mere labels. **No newly verified passage here independently establishes the unrestricted simultaneous exclusion from intrinsic nonreceipt, complete power, and decisive will alone.** Nor does this return establish that two whole unreceived original producers are possible.

The expression *taḥṣīl al-ḥāṣil* was not found in the bounded Arabic primary passages inspected here. It should not be placed in Ibn Taymiyya's mouth as an exact quotation from these loci. The unspecified title *Sharḥ al-ʿAqīda al-Tadmuriyya* does not identify a commentator or edition; its attribution is separately controlled below and in the commentary report.

## 1. The predicates the text requires us to separate

1. **Intrinsic necessary power:** a producer's power is unreceived and belongs necessarily to its own constitution.
2. **Complete power and decisive will for a target:** a relevant actual determination is adequate and the intended effect follows.
3. **Entire original productive provision:** all productive work and required resources for the effect in the specified respect are supplied originally, without an alien original supplement.
4. **Exclusive entire attribution:** this producer is the only original producer of that effect in that respect.

The source argues from the first profiles toward the fourth. One may assess its arguments, but must not erase the transition by defining every occurrence of “complete” to mean “exclusive.” Conversely, merely having a sufficient created route through materials supplied by another original falls short of profile 3. A source-neutral occurrence entailment is thinner still.

The issue is one numerical effect, one relevant state/time, and one productive respect. A creature's performance and God's original creation of that performance are not coequal attributions in this respect. Nor must original creation be universally interpreted as creation without materials or as requiring a first created moment. Darʾ 9:340–341 explicitly discusses food, recipient powers, instruments, and other created causes; Minhāj 2:180–181 uses the human organism's material and sustaining requirements. The question is the original provision of those requirements, not their absence.

## 2. Darʾ 9:338–365: exact voice and argument

The received edited work is Ibn Taymiyya's *Darʾ taʿāruḍ al-ʿaql wa-l-naql*. [Shamela's edition metadata](https://shamela.ws/book/21506) identifies Muḥammad Rashād Sālim, Imam Muhammad ibn Saud Islamic University, second edition 1411 AH/1991, with print-matching pagination. The current Islamweb text identifies the same publisher and preserves the page markers. This is an electronic edition-page correspondence, not a fresh scan/imprint or manuscript collation. Do not conflate this with Minhāj's first 1406/1986 edition.

### Authorial turn and whole-alone contradiction

Immediately before 9:338, Ibn Taymiyya is quoting Ibn Rushd's *Manāhij al-adilla*. The `قلت` at the start of [Islamweb item1768](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/413/1768/) marks Ibn Taymiyya's own reply. His opening says the single individual product cannot be the act of two agents independently or cooperatively. The explanation is explicit: each independently doing the entire product alone excludes a partner, so positing another complete independent producer both affirms and denies that aloneness.

The next step assigns a genuine partner a different part of the product. The examples are building and carrying a beam. This is a substantive individuation/partition account of actual contributions; it cannot be reported as if it had already answered all noncompositional overdetermination cases simply by choosing additive examples.

9:339–343 then argues that created agents never supply the whole external effect from their own resources. Receptive bodies, organs, food, air, instruments, cooperating powers and absence of interference matter. That is a strong explanation of why ordinary causal production is not automatically original total provision. It does not independently exclude two originally complete nonreceived provisions.

### Necessary concurrence is considered, not overlooked

At 9:357, [item1773](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/413/1773/) distinguishes vicious efficient priority from harmless concomitance, using divine attributes as an example of the latter. It then expressly considers the proposal that each agent has its own power and will without receiving them from the other.

At 9:358, it grants that each original must have power necessary to itself, then argues that each's independence in the actual doing excludes the other from doing or sharing that product. Required forbearance or permission by the other is classified as an impairment of original power. Thus the source does not merely assume that necessary concurrence is reciprocal power donation; it attempts to close the intact-power alternative. The disputed classification remains visible in the argument.

### The simultaneous target is explicit

At 9:363, [item1775](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/413/1775/) says that while one performs the act alone, the other cannot be another lone producer of that same act. At 9:364–365, power is tied to being able to do the specified act if it is willed.

The opening of [item1776](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/413/1776/) is still on printed **9:365**, before the marker for 366. It explicitly denies two agents' power over one object and production of one product **in the same state**. This is the exact location corresponding to retained Dar segment016 L0753. Web segment numbers, local extraction labels, and print pages are different coordinate systems.

The same paragraph begins from the already-argued impossibility of the other being its producer. It therefore demonstrates the *intended simultaneous scope*, but is not a new independent ground of nonduplication. The subsequent distinction between one's own voluntary nonselection and a peer's prevention is important; simply saying “both choose to agree” does not answer the claimed limitation on actual whole efficacy.

## 3. Minhāj: the double-existence and completion controls

[Edition metadata](https://shamela.ws/book/927): *Minhāj al-sunna al-nabawiyya*, edited by Muḥammad Rashād Sālim, Imam Muhammad ibn Saud Islamic University, first edition 1406 AH/1986, nine volumes, print-matching electronic pagination. The readings below concern Ibn Taymiyya's argumentative body. Modern variant apparatus and bracketed editorial supplementation remain distinct; this return does not newly certify the manuscripts behind them.

### 1:438–441

[Item96](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/108/96/) expressly says at 1:439 that simultaneous independent power would require the world's/effect's existence twice in one state. The opening at 1:438 supplies the exclusive-attribution explanation: each complete independent authorship excludes the other's authorship.

The statement “twice in one state” is a direct simultaneous claim, not an assertion that A must temporally finish before B starts. Its force nevertheless depends on treating two allegedly entire original productions as incompatible individuations of the one effect. Two independently operative causes of one occurrence do not become two numerical occurrences merely from the entailments A→e and B→e. The source's thicker production principle must do that work.

### 2:170–183

[Item156](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/108/156/), especially 2:170–172, gives a genuinely independent anti-bootstrap rationale: conferring life, knowledge or power already requires the efficacy used to confer it. A completing condition of the very same productive exercise cannot be supplied by the dependent result of that exercise. This applies to part-causes and completion, not only whole causes.

That argument should receive its full credit. It excludes reciprocal productive donation of the powers needed for donation. It does not by itself exclude two already intrinsically complete, unreceived powers acting simultaneously; that hypothesis denies reciprocal donation.

At 2:176–177, the same item explains the exclusion of two complete influences on one effect through each effect's being produced **by this alone**. At 2:180–182, items156–157 connect product-condition dependence to a defect in the agent's own completed efficacy, with created materials and preservation as concrete illustrations. This is a defended source-perfection argument, not a universal no-materials premise.

### 3:304–329

[Items364–366](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/108/364/) were returned to continuously through the completion at 3:329. At 3:311 the source's analogies are two agents each sewing the same garment alone or each consuming all the same food. These express exclusive entire attribution; the food example adds an exhaustible target. They cannot automatically decide a nonexhaustible/noncompositional causal case.

At 3:321, the source separately insists that two bearers' intrinsic acts differ and that the external traces differ. Distinct intrinsic acts do not by themselves prove two created tokens; the subsequent distinct-trace claim is the additional productive-individuation thesis.

At 3:321–323 and 3:326–329 the author considers common benefit, foreknowledge, equal cooperating wills, a third director, and necessary coaction. He treats their required conjunction as external or circular productive completion. These are not omissions that a harmony proposal can expose by merely being harmonious. Their transport from concurrence to productive completion is nevertheless the issue to establish, rather than something to insert among the neutral antecedents.

## 4. Jāmiʿ al-masāʾil VI: actual independence versus solo ability

The scan return is documented separately in `jami/JAMI_VI_94_98_VERIFIED_RETURN.md`. The csiislam scan has the exact inherited SHA-256 `c5434abfa5585d899f443576ff31509b79904662aa8db490fe53c5b3bb133730`. Its printed 93–99 were visually inspected; one-based PDF page equals printed page in this interval. The IIFA copy's web PDF indices differ, so its indices must not be treated as the print page.

Printed 94 introduces two complete causes and distinguishes the contributors' own acts. Printed 95 explicitly defines actual `مستقل` as acting alone. It also admits that a collaborator might have the power to perform alone in another condition. Printed 95–96 then argues that if a helper really contributes, it produces part; the other therefore does not exercise the whole productive power in the joint case. Printed 96 classifies that as prevention even where solo power exists. Printed 97 explicitly says conflicting-will tamānuʿ by itself does not rule out cooperation. Printed 98 refers necessary agreement back to the earlier exclusion.

Thus this is the source's clear act/ability distinction, not evidence that it overlooked the distinction. The residual is the exhaustive partition of genuine co-production into partial products, plus its perfection-relevant classification as prevention. No occurrence of *taḥṣīl al-ḥāṣil* was found in visually read 93–99.

## 5. The strongest substantive rationale: Majmūʿ 20:175–177

The [retained item2032](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/22/2032/) is another targeted return, not newly discovered support. It says:

- Hold the actual agent's will, motion, instruments and effective condition unchanged.
- Genuine cooperation adds another productive agent.
- The full product must be more or greater than the unchanged individual product; otherwise the added is equal to the unadded.
- Complete power plus decisive will necessitates the intended effect; the text then moves to the effect's occurring through that agent alone.
- Two such alone-attributions conflict. This is called intrinsic mutual incompatibility rather than merely conflict between wills.

This is the most relevant positive answer to a simultaneous redundancy proposal. It asks what real productive work the peer contributes and whether the first complete manifestation is retained. Its general productive augmentation claim needs warrant; it is not entailed just by effect occurrence or by a positive local response coefficient. The existing nonabsorption report already establishes that boundary. This return adds no replacement mathematical control.

The same chapters' power-concentration/perfection considerations give additional motivation for adequate achieved efficacy. They are not an independent demonstration that every genuine simultaneous contribution must augment the complete effect. Retain the later Oneness reassessment's correction that a defective original candidate can be disqualified comparatively without becoming another numerical individual.

## 6. Taḥṣīl al-ḥāṣil and the unspecified Tadmuriyya commentary

A prohibition on originating an independently already-realized effect a second time is intelligible. Three interpretations must not be conflated:

- **Temporal repeat:** the very same origination has already occurred, and another is then proposed. This does not directly address the same-time proposal.
- **Explanatory saturation:** the effect is entirely originally produced independently of B; B supplies no further actual productive work. This can exclude B only with a defended account of productive saturation, not bare counterfactual sufficiency.
- **Two entailments / two self-locations:** both determinations entail one effect and both know their contribution. This does not by itself create two numerical existences or exclusive attribution.

No verified primary passage in the bounded Dar/Minhāj/Jāmiʿ return adds a distinct *taḥṣīl al-ḥāṣil* derivation resolving those alternatives. The source's simultaneous nonduplication conclusion is clear; the new tag supplies no independently established bridge merely by being renamed non-temporal.

The user supplied neither the exact alleged reconstruction nor a commentator/edition for *Sharḥ al-ʿAqīda al-Tadmuriyya*. This is a limited attribution obstacle, not a blocker to the primary-source analysis. `commentary/RESULT.md` documents two readable later commentaries and further bibliographical/access limits. The identifiable primary matn there characterizes the theologians' appeal to tamānuʿ and criticizes treating creative lordship as the whole meaning of tawḥīd; their subsequently marked expansions are later commentary. No commentarial claim is attributed to Ibn Taymiyya without that boundary.

A positively verified, separately labelled comparator is al-Ṣāwī on Q21:22: it assigns *taḥṣīl al-ḥāṣil* to the branch in which one producer precedes the other, while the simultaneous branch appeals separately to the prohibition on two producers of one effect. This is not an identification of the user's unspecified Sharḥ. Its source and exact verse URL appear in the commentary report.

The philosophical conclusion here rests on the explicit structures of the verified positive arguments, not on a phrase-search non-hit. An unfamiliar wording could name those same arguments, or could contain a further one; its exact attribution remains open pending the text.

## 7. Precise residual and evidential status

The remaining question is whether **entire unreceived actual original production**, without defining it as exclusive authorship, independently entails nonduplicate/nonabsorptive realization in the same effect and respect. The original-source rationale is stronger than ordinary causal sufficiency and stronger than a bare no-cycle axiom. It still requires a justified transition from complete efficacy to the exhaustive choice between extra work, foreign limitation, and no real peer production.

This source return finds no newly independent resolution of that transition. It does not erase the source's genuine prima facie perfection and productive-totality warrant, and it does not authenticate a harmonious plural-original alternative. Schaffer's strongest noncompositional case must be considered at its real strength in the separate analytical report; no additive-only dismissal is licensed here.
