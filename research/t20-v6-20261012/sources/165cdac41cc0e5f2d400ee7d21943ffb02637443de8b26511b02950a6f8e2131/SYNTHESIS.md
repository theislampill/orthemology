## 1. The contingency quotation was genuine, but its gloss was misleading

The latest agent's Arabic sentence about a possible which does not exist through itself is located at *Darʾ* 8:116–117. The immediate continuation disputes a symmetrical determinant requirement for nonexistence. This is stronger source verification than simply finding a vaguely similar passage elsewhere. [A03]

The affirmative route should therefore not depend on an imagined possible essence sitting outside actuality with equal probabilities of existing and not existing. Its stronger focus is actual received existence, intrinsic sufficiency, and the explicit rejection of the third option “neither through itself nor another.” The contextual source at 3:288–289 addresses that objection rather than overlooking it. [A01]

**What Dot can use:** a more source-faithful reconstruction, a corrected exact locator, and an explicit division of the remaining disagreement into complete existential adequacy, original-resource realisation, and the sufficiency-to-necessity bridge. A brute alternative must engage those claims, not merely add a root to a graph.

## 2. The world’s existence and its right order are not the same conclusion

The supplied *Oneness* translation differentiates rubūbiyyah reasoning from the exclusive-worship reading of Qur'an 21:22. The received *al-Dāʾ wa-l-Dawāʾ* excerpt likewise contrasts corruption/right order with sheer nonexistence. The latter was inspected through a primary search excerpt; full page access failed, and that limit is preserved. [I04, A08]

**What Dot can use:** an explicit target distinction among existence, causal integration, teleological fittingness and rightly directed worship. A proof or objection addressing one should not receive automatic credit against the others. The distinction can make both an affirmative argument and an objection more exact.

The overdetermination issue remains serious. Schaffer's actual redundant-process account is not reducible to two half-causes. But its causal relation does not supply the unreceived, whole-original adequacy of the proposed divine agents. [M03]

## 3. Necessary concordance now has a precise mathematical discriminator

Suppose each agent's own state completely fixes a common output: f(x) for A, g(y) for B. Mark which pairs of states are jointly admissible. On every admissible pair, the two complete determinations agree.

The resulting bipartite graph yields a simple theorem: **the common output is constant on each connected component**. A connected compatibility graph forces a constant common output. A diagonal graph permits variation with perfect concordance, but does not explain why only diagonal combinations are possible.

The packet proves the lemma and exhaustively checks all sixteen 2×2 compatibility graphs with binary and ternary labels. This uses standard graph/common-information ancestry rather than claiming a novel theorem. [D7; M12–M13]

**What Dot can use:** a sharper question than whether two wills might disagree. Does the intended original independence require enough joint admissibility to connect the graph? If so, what warrants that premise? If not, what explains the restricted modal relation? Modal correlation itself is not productive dependence.

## 4. Explanatory-fact uniqueness survives a strong replay—but has an important consequence

The independent implementation reconstructs checkpoint18's 15-fact Boolean model. It checks every one of the 32,767 nonempty pluralities, with proper parts, nonempty fusions, strong supplementation and the stated explanatory axioms. It preserves one total explainer while permitting three explainers of a designated local target. [I02; D3–D4]

An additional consequence is that total-explainer uniqueness is incompatible with unrestricted source-side explanatory padding when a proper larger fact is available. If every larger fact containing a sufficient explainer were automatically another full explainer, a proper fusion with the total explainer would violate uniqueness. The deliberately padded negative control fails the expected axioms.

**What Dot can use:** a concrete test of whether its explanation relation is relevance-sensitive or is being confused with ordinary logical implication. A theorem about an atomic explaining fact does not eliminate real divine attributes or identify a single concrete bearer without an independently justified mapping.

## 5. The Koons–Pruss probability argument has a precise assumption boundary

Checkpoint15 already distinguished an adversarial likelihood choice from an objective chance. This delivery adds the exact algebra and finite controls.

With e=P(caused and of character K), d=P(uncaused) and α=P(K given uncaused), the posterior is:

\[
P(\text{uncaused}\mid K)=\frac{\alpha d}{e+\alpha d}.
\]

At the same e and d, the package gives exact values 10/11 and 1/101 for two different α assumptions. Setting α=1 is a worst-case treatment, not an identity supplied by Bayes. The authors make that move explicitly; no accidental algebraic omission is alleged. [M01; D5]

A second result separates coherence from exchangeability, finite exchangeability from infinite iid-mixture representation, and probability representation from causal ontology. The two-bit law assigning equal mass to 01 and 10 is exchangeable but has negative covariance. Every iid Bernoulli mixture has covariance Var(Θ)≥0, so no such mixture represents this law. The general proof is provided; finite tests are not its sole support. [M09; D6]

**What Dot can use:** a source-grounded mathematical objection to overextending the representation theorem, without pretending to have refuted the entire epistemic PSR argument. The philosophical questions concern warranted probability assignments, relevant alternatives and the move from reflective assurance to a global explanatory principle.

## 6. A recent published PSR argument exposes a witness-scope issue

Harrop's *Sufficient Reason Vindicated* is a newly added original-argument source. Its main route uses an exhaustive positive/negative pair sharing a requisite; the later route weakens the negative-fact commitment. [M02]

The packet establishes two local diagnostics. A common requisite of exhaustive alternatives must hold throughout the modal scope, so deleting it is not free while those assumptions remain intact. And a world-dependent “some negative witness” cannot automatically become the same selected negative witness at every world.

A three-world control and two sufficient repairs make the latter issue explicit. This is not a full countermodel to every premise of the paper, and no whole-paper refutation is claimed. [D1–D2]

**What Dot can use:** a particularly apt test of the uniform-bearer and possible-to-actual explanation transitions. The new source is not merely another citation endorsing PSR; it yields a precise analytical task with finished mathematical work.

## 7. The wisdom source contains a positive temporal-perfection reply

In *Shifāʾ* chapter22, the reply to the alleged “acquisition of perfection” objection distinguishes what is fitting at a particular time from what would be inappropriate earlier. It also distinguishes intrinsic wisdom from externally conferred completion. This is a positive argument, not merely a prohibition on certain words. [A11]

Together with the creator/performer distinction in chapter20, it prevents two mistakes: making every temporally occurring act a newly supplied essential perfection, and applying the giver-of-perfection principle as an unrestricted rule that a creator must bear every property of its effects. [A10]

**What Dot can use:** explicitly indexed predicates for the bearer, standing attribute, particular act, created effect and fitting time. A scalar or static all-properties-at-all-times conception of perfection should not be silently imported into the source argument.

## 8. Proper function itself needs an epistemic qualification

Plantinga's D-to-D* correction distinguishes a belief maintained by properly functioning processes from a belief sustained by the appropriately truth-aimed processes relevant to epistemic defeat. [M08] That is useful comparison material for fiṭrah, not an identification of the two complete theories.

The newly recovered Arabic first-principles and fiṭrah locators supply affirmative source support. An additional *al-Radd* passage explicitly recognises that a truth may be immediate to one person, inferential to another, perceived by one and known by report to another. [A04, A15, A24]

**What Dot can use:** a model of episode-specific cognition and warranted basing, rather than a single predicate which turns every natural inclination into knowledge or every unanswered objection into global defeat.
