# Source appraisal: Jami al-Masa'il VI, middle treatises

Reader-facing study record, 7 October 2026. The interpretations and reading claims below belong to the recorded study and its bounded rereads. This edition of the notes adds no source-reading or proof credit.

## Result and calibrated assessment

This selected continuous reading covers treatise 5, treatise 6, and the preserved portion of treatise 7: electronic E135–251 where successfully acquired, then printed/PDF 248–252 for the inaccessible electronic continuation E252–256. The electronic acquisition gaps remain held; the independently acquired scan was already local. The E251/printed 247 junction and the entire cooperator locus printed 179–182 were visually checked. Detailed coverage and END confirmations are in [middle continuous notes](MIDDLE_CONTINUOUS_NOTES.md) and [middle coverage](MIDDLE_COVERAGE.json).

The strongest locally established result is a **typed distinction among origin of a power, exercise of a power, and joint realisation of a product**, applied also to intentions and ends. It is not a denial of cooperation, conditional efficacy, or finite agency. I regard that distinction as successful and important. The author's larger explanatory picture, in which an actual integrated order of derivative capacities and goods has one creator and a non-arbitrary ultimate good, remains a serious **defeasible** account; lack of a deduction alone does not refute it. But this selection gives substantially stronger control over its local causal distinctions than over an unrestricted claim that necessary existence entails every conceivable perfection in one intrinsic source-mode. Those should receive separate levels of endorsement.

## 1. What is actually being argued

### 1.1 Two directions of dependence, neither erasing the creature
At E151–153 the finite agent cannot produce his own entire originating basis: awareness, desire, volition, and capacity cannot each be explained by a prior human act indefinitely within a finite life. The argument explicitly distinguishes simultaneous complete-cause regress from contested successive conditions. It should not be represented as a blanket dismissal of every infinite temporal series.

At E157–160 the creature nevertheless acts **really**, through created capacity and will, and really benefits from action. God as creator does not cancel this agency; God as final object does not cancel creaturely pleasure or welfare. This is the most constructive synthesis of the section. It blocks both independent human origination and an account that leaves the human only a verbal label for divine action.

At E169–172, E217 and E200–201, intention and realised consequence can diverge. A person may not understand the benefit of the rule he obeys; a creature may pursue only pleasure while the creator's purpose includes bodily continuance or reproduction. Correct ultimate orientation is not a necessary psychological precondition for every action's occurring.

### 1.2 Circular derivation is not necessary coexistence
The full visual control is printed/PDF 179–182 = E183–186. The text permits reciprocal conditionality, disallows prior constitutive dependence in a closed derivation loop, allows instrumental assistance, and identifies added cooperative strength as a result of the combined activity. Print 181/E185 then states the dual description: each has power, awareness, intention and a sought object; the corresponding bases are not simply obtained from one another in a closed circle; fulfilment requires both intentions and actions.

The strongest charitable reading is not 'partners can never causally affect one another'. It is that their already operative enabling basis cannot be conferred solely by the very dependent product that basis must explain. Ordinary exchange and instruments are explicitly exempted, and produced cooperative power is explicitly acknowledged. This is a precise answer to an illicit slide from 'A cannot accomplish the product without B' to 'A has no proper contributing efficacy except what B first supplies'.

Print 182/E186 closes with the decisive contrasts: causes may be **with**, not **through**, one another, and ends **with**, not **for the sake of**, one another. The passage offers a common external cause/end as a coherent arrangement. It does not by that permission alone demonstrate that every necessarily coexisting pair must be the effect of exactly one source. Conversely, treating the permission as an endorsement of ultimate independent cooperators would be equally wrong: the passage is working within a creaturely order whose derivative bases the author has already attributed to God.

### 1.3 Contributing cause versus complete product conditions
E211–216 provides the terminological control. An incomplete productive tendency can fail because a condition is absent or an impediment is present. A complete condition set cannot fail without having been incorrectly described as complete. E214–215 directly rejects the denial of created causal efficacy: God acts **through** these means, not merely upon their occasion. E215–216 then distinguishes an agent's activity from that activity plus reception by a patient. 'Teaching' can name instruction that fails to produce learning; if its meaning includes achieved learning, the description has already included another condition.

This is substantial evidence against interpreting source-perfection by reading every failed finite effect as absence of any real active perfection in the finite source. It does not eliminate the further question of what the ultimate source supplies. The author states divine will is the sole individually complete cause, but in this selection that claim is supported by scripture and fitra rather than derived from the mere definition of a complete cause. The necessity-and-sufficiency language also needs its intended exhaustive scope; it cannot be exported without qualification to any arbitrarily selected sufficient route.

## 2. Strongest positive reconstruction of the wider case

The text's larger case is cumulative rather than one isolated syllogism:

1. Actual originated agents and events display dependence; the agent cannot be the self-originating author of every condition that makes him act (E145–146, E151–153).
2. These agents nevertheless possess determinate real powers, cognition, volition, and goods. Their effects occur through a coordinated order of causes, conditions, and appropriate recipients (E154, E184–186, E211–216).
3. Mere circular borrowing does not explain those possessed principles. A common source of the derivative order gives a coherent direction of explanation without requiring the observed powers to be fictitious.
4. Successful cooperation depends on fitted ends and lawful coordination, while unrestricted conflicting desires are self-defeating (E153–157). There is an intelligible distinction between a preference and a fitting object of preference (E192, E201–202).
5. One divine origin and final orientation unify this account, preserve derivative agency and benefit, and make sense of scriptural testimony to creation, worship, wisdom, and guidance (E157–160, E196–203, E217).

As an account of the **actual coordinated order**, common creation is not undermined just because another arrangement can be described without immediate contradiction. A unified origin fits the interconnectedness and matched capacities/goals the text emphasises, and avoids treating each dependency as ultimately self-explaining. Within the conceded necessary-being/basic-recognition starting point, I judge it a **plausible, provisionally preferable unifying inference**, rather than a result with no warrant. Its strength is conditional on the prior dependence judgments and the reliability of the scriptural/fitri evidence; this selection does not independently compare all rival explanations or determine their probabilities.

That positive judgment is narrower than an unrestricted source-mode theorem. Excluding circular derivation, recognising coordinated cooperation, and preferring a common explanation do not yet demonstrate that every possible perfection must be present intrinsically in one necessary existent, or that every necessarily coexisting item is derivative in exactly the same respect. E184–186's careful distinctions themselves forbid reaching that conclusion merely by redescribing joint product conditions as borrowed intrinsic powers.

## 3. Final causality: charitable force and remaining burdens

### 3.1 The intended force
The author is trying to establish an objectively adequate final object, not merely the fact that agents happen to like things. Unlimited whimsical satisfaction frustrates durable welfare; law and social cooperation are necessary; sincere truth-directed inquiry is epistemically superior to rationalization. A final aim needs a reason for fitting desire rather than being made right solely by the desire it explains. These are genuine positive considerations (E145–154, E160–172, E201–202).

He also explicitly distinguishes sincere worship of God from using worship only as an instrument to acquire wisdom (E140), and acknowledges weak historical evidence while separating the plausibility of its lesson. That is an important methodological virtue.

### 3.2 Three bridges remain substantive
- **Transient realisation to worthlessness as an ultimate end:** E166–167 and E200 argue that a vanishing pleasure leaves one afterwards as one was before, so it must serve an enduring end. The strongest reading concerns a complete policy of durable flourishing, which transient gratification alone cannot secure. The stronger claim that no finite realised good can have any noninstrumental value is not established merely by its later cessation. The selection supplies a teleological valuation, not a direct consequence of necessary existence.
- **Fittingness to a prior externally existent object:** E177 and E201–202 reasonably resist desire conferring its own warrant circularly. But objective fittingness, positive representational content, and an already existent ultimate object are distinguishable. Mecca before pilgrimage and a beloved before contact illustrate the proposed structure; they do not alone settle every prospective goal's structure. The account of nonexistent objects in E207–208 also stresses construction from positive cognition, without by itself proving that every represented end is already instantiated externally.
- **All motion to volition:** E174 and E197 rely on forced/natural/voluntary motion as an exhaustive framework, with natural motion restorative after displacement. The universal conclusion needs that physical/metaphysical framework defended; it is not supplied simply by accepting an originated human act or necessary existent. The identification of angelic administration adds scriptural support, which should be labelled as such.

These gaps warrant qualification, not dismissal of every part of the cumulative account. The local causal distinctions are stronger than these universal bridges.

### 3.3 Do not attack an abandoned or disclaimed formulation
E191 and E200–201 explicitly say misguided love and action can occur. The final-cause prohibition concerning a human's motive is normative/explanatory: the intended object is not a fitting complete end. It is not the prediction that no person can psychologically idolize a creature, mutually fall in love, or act for a harmful pleasure. This distinction removes a superficial contradiction but also shows why normativity must be justified rather than replaced by the law of noncontradiction.

## 4. Orthogonal arguments worth preserving

- **Evidence grades and devotional law:** E137–140 distinguishes the value of sincerity from a disputed forty-day report, unknown-isnad anecdote, corroborative analogies, and prohibited additions to seclusion. Pre-prophetic practice is not automatically a post-prophetic legal rule.
- **Semantic discipline:** E148 distinguishes an act of desire from its object. E165 broadens causal bā' without reducing it to efficient production. E213–216 separates predication/ahwal, active contribution, and achieved result. E223–226 differentiates uses of Islam/faith by scope and degree. E248–250 distinguishes descriptive truth from normative due realisation.
- **Absence and explanation:** E207–219 does not treat sheer nothing as an actor. It permits absence as evidence, elimination of a blocker as a condition, and prevention/removal of harm as a derivative goal. Sign, productive source, condition and purpose are not interchangeable.
- **Knowledge of nonexistence:** E208 expressly needs God's comprehensive knowledge for the inference from not knowing an existent to its absence. It supplies no general human argument from ignorance.
- **Unknown arrangement versus impossibility:** E188–190 distinguishes experienced pleasure from pain relief and rejects inferring the impossibility of afterlife pleasures from different worldly production conditions. The rebuttal of a modal exclusion should not be inflated into empirical proof of the promised arrangement.
- **Legal purposiveness:** E169–172 and E217–219 allow consequences to bear on interpretation; fighting already-engaged aggressors after failed reconciliation is distinguished from initiating warfare for every prior wrong. Legal absence-based formulations may suppress an agreed positive ground.
- **Moral independence from felt harm:** E238–251 and printed 248–252 deny that a violated right always harms its immediate bearer. Artifact proportion and function illustrate wrong allocation; failed hostile intentions and wrongs followed by greater good separate wrongful conduct from ultimate net harm. This is stronger than defining all wrongdoing by injury.
- **Divine self-sufficiency with love and joy:** E239–245 and printed 251–252 jointly affirm no creature supplies God's need and real divine approval/joy in repentance. They resist a crude creature-to-God mode of receiving benefit, but do not erase positive response or normative entitlement. This is an explicit source-mode asymmetry that must not be converted into a denial of the attributes it is meant to protect.

## 5. Source quality, textual limits, and unfinished material

The assigned edition information is Ibn Taymiyya, Jami al-Masa'il, collectionVI, editor Muhammad Uzayr Shams, Dar Alam al-Fawa'id, first edition 1429. The independent reviewer acquisition record supplies the manuscript bases: treatises 1–6 from JarAllah 1729, with significant editorial correction; treatise 7 from UmmalQura 1491 and incomplete. I have not independently collated those manuscripts. Modern numbering, references, supplied brackets and hadith grading are not the author's own apparatus.

Electronic extraction contains obvious glitches. The visual check of printed 179–182 corrects the electronic misrendering of editorial '[كل]' and exposes '[لا يكون]' as restored wording. It does **not** remove every awkward printed clause. The power/awareness/intention/result distinction rests on several convergent clear sentences, so the main interpretation is robust to the unresolved wording in 180; a definitive diplomatic quotation should still preserve it or flag it.

Manuscript blanks are expressly reported at E161,162,171,186,188,201,208,212, and a damaged word appears on printed 250. Additional suspect electronic phrases E234 and E249 were not visually resolved. These do not justify silent modernisation. The printed ending 252 breaks after 'وكذلك', with an editorial note that the copy stops there. No complete authorial resolution of that final attribute/right discussion survives in this witness.

Polemical descriptions of philosophical, theological, religious and ethnic groups are attributed positions, not independently verified evidence. Especially E237's allegation about Chinggis Khan's mother is not adopted as fact. The local conceptual merits neither validate those historical claims nor require them.

## Bottom line

I endorse the local source/contribution/product distinction, the reality of finite causal means, the possibility of reciprocal conditions without reciprocal origination, and several semantic/legal clarifications. I provisionally favour a common-Creator interpretation of the actual coordinated dependent order within the given necessary-being/basic-recognition starting point, while treating its warrant as cumulative and defeasible. I do not regard this selected argument as having established an unrestricted all-perfections-in-one-source conclusion or every universal final-cause premise without additional argument. This is a differentiated positive appraisal, not rejection merely because a deduction is absent.
