# Bounded modern-print scan controls

## Scope, method and evidence layers

After completing the original five continuous-electronic study files, I inspected 33 entire authorial-body pages and four entire correction-catalogue pages from the already acquired [Adwa scan](https://archive.org/download/al-mulakhas/al-risala.pdf). The PDF has 625 positions. Every printed page label listed below was read on its image; the observed relation PDF position = printed page + 7 is local to these inspected pages, not a guessed global mapping. Full-page images were directly inspected, including all visible notes and marginal material. Several pages were reread at 150 dpi where small apparatus required it. Rendering or text extraction was not counted as reading; the PDF's empty extraction was not used as evidence.

The controlled body pages are printed 474, 477, 480, 484–488, 492, 495, 504, 512, 528–531, 533–534, 539, 544, 552, 562, 567 and 570–579. They are not a continuous scan reading of the whole final range. The catalogue pages are printed 36, 37, 39 and 40 (PDF 43,44,46,47). They cover this range's substantial entries 21–39 and lesser entries 27–55, as well as other rows on those pages. This reader does not claim to have read the complete 39+55 catalogue; the whole register is covered by the separate apparatus study.

Four evidence layers must remain distinct: the exact received electronic line; the modern printed main text; the modern editor's apparatus reporting a manuscript or earlier printing; and the correction catalogue's abbreviated account. No manuscript image or older printed edition was inspected here. A modern apparatus claim about its manuscript is therefore reported testimony, not my manuscript verification. Where the catalogue conflicts with the body apparatus, both are retained.

## Opening and attribute controls

- **Printed 474 / PDF 481; S003-L0364 tail–L0370 opening:** The page contains the end of the common-work argument, the substance/accident question, the start of the assigned mixing argument and the mud example. It confirms the join through L0367. Lesser 27 is specifically the parenthesised إن after وهؤلاء يقولون at L0369; the note says it was absent from the printing. The page begins outside the assigned range, so that fragment is separately recorded in the scan receipt. The bracketed منهما near the top is an editorial clarification stated to be absent from the manuscript.
- **Printed 477 / PDF 484; L0376 continuation–L0381 opening:** The page continues the Najat quotation, with multiple notes comparing its text, before the author's response. It restores at L0380: ليست صفة لغيرها فلا بد من إثبات ذات قائمة بنفسها. The apparatus identifies this as text lost from the printing. This strengthens the concrete bearer/attribute distinction already present in the electronic argument, but does not by itself yield a universal source-transfer principle. A reported collation annotation is separate from the argument.
- **Printed 480 / PDF 487; L0391–0393:** The modern text has تجدد النسب والإضافات, with earlier تجرد reported. The electronic carries the latter. The change matters to the claim that new relations follow creation. The text also preserves the distinction between objections to theological inactivity and monist use of the primordial-state report.
- **Printed 484 / PDF 491; L0404 continuation–L0408:** The modern heading is التوحيد القولي العلمي; the older practical adjective is reported. The electronic's القول العملي belongs to that older adjective-family but is not an exact copy of the catalogue's entire old anchor. The later التوحيد العملي الإرادي remains the practical-volitional counterpart. A subsequent 300 dpi catalogue check found العلمي in both of its abbreviated anchors, unlike the body apparatus; the catalogue must not be silently harmonised to the body.

## Epistemic routes and concrete objects

- **Printed 485 / PDF 492; L0409–0412:** The whole argument against treating possessed attributes as assembled parts is present. The final comparison reads وأي موجود قدر في الخارج كان أكمل منه, whereas the electronic has في الأذهان. The note expressly contrasts the printing with the manuscript-backed reading. A further note describes an attached/interposed source leaf; its extent and provenance should not be turned into direct manuscript verification by this reader.
- **Printed 486 / PDF 493; L0413–0415:** The text explicitly permits innate/immediate, inferential and person-relative routes. Auxiliary premises are عوارض طريق المعرفة لا من لوازم طريق المعرفة. The apparatus's bracketed addition القول belongs to the earlier report of rival philosophical positions, not to a new proof premise.
- **Printed 487 / PDF 494; L0416–L0422 opening:** The whole classification of arguments is confirmed: false arguments are rejected, sound concise arguments approved, and elaboration can help particular people. The printed text retains the unexpected لا يقولون in L0420. Thus the apparent tension with the ensuing reductionist account is not proven to be an electronic corruption; it remains an interpretative or wider textual-control issue. The following paragraph explicitly accepts the true ethical and civic goods but denies exhaustiveness.
- **Printed 488 / PDF 495; L0422 continuation–L0426 opening:** The critique concerns knowledge of a mental absolute substituting for knowledge of determinate realities, followed by the soul's need for love. The modern list has الإحساس, with old الإحسان reported. Another note distinguishes a manuscript verbal form in the preparatory-discipline clause. These local readings do not change the two-faculty argument.
- **Printed 492 / PDF 499; L0438–L0442 opening:** The modern readings المشخصة and الخارجية are explicitly contrasted with الشخصية and الخارجة. The concrete-object argument and the hypothetical concession “even if perfection were knowledge alone” are intact. The page then opens the appeal to external observable punishments and rewards.
- **Printed 495 / PDF 502; L0451 continuation–L0455 opening:** The modern text twice uses الموجود where the electronic twice uses الوجود in L0452. This is lesser 30 and is attached to the concrete account of what the philosophers allegedly know. The surrounding passage concerns whether attained knowledge cancels prescribed worship, then returns to the prophetic call to worship.
- **Printed 528 / PDF 535; L0567 continuation–L0574 opening:** The particular referents Necessary Existent, Lord of the worlds and Creator are explicitly contrasted with general existence or thing. Natural, logical and intellectual universals are distinguished. The positive assertion is that the natural universal is instantiated externally as a determinate individual, not that external universality exists.
- **Printed 529 / PDF 536; L0574 continuation–L0578:** The four registers, reality, mental representation, spoken expression and writing, explain correspondence without identity. Qur'an96 and the example of a reader ignorant of a language accompany the argument. The page's hadith reference is modern documentation, not an independent authenticity assessment here.
- **Printed 530 / PDF 537; L0579–0581:** The no-external-existence clause lacks the electronic's extra conjunction, confirming lesser 43 at the second clause of L0580. The catalogue's wording is located in its actual contradiction example rather than through a global search for لا يكون. Notes also register an uncertain manuscript reading of the putative eternal exemplar and a number-agreement correction in “forms”.
- **Printed 531 / PDF 538; L0582–L0586 opening:** الحدود, not electronic الحدوث, is the modern reading at L0584. The context is logical definition and necessary properties, not a new statement about origination. The later preposition is منها rather than electronic عنها. Both changes are reported against the older printing.
- **Printed 533 / PDF 540; L0590–0593:** The modern text reads مسمى النفس والعقل and لأنها عقلت العقليات, contrasting the electronic قسمي and لأنهم. The account remains a criticism of projecting mental abstractions into separate entities, using the human soul as the model for philosophical terminology.
- **Printed 534 / PDF 541; L0594–0600:** The modern text retains وهو عرض قائم بنفسه without a correcting note. In context بنفسه can be read as “in his soul”, referring to the human subject, rather than as a technical claim of independent self-subsistence. It must not be declared a proved corruption or ontological contradiction. The whole page confirms the relational account of unseen versus presently perceived things and its distinction from mental abstractions.
- **Printed 544 / PDF 551; L0647 continuation–L0651 opening:** The modern clause is لا إلى حقيقة هي (على ما) في نفس الأمر; the apparatus describes tightly spaced manuscript letters and reports old علمها. The parentheses and remaining awkwardness must be preserved. The central distinction between the knower's middle term and an objective intermediary is nonetheless explicit in the full paragraph. A separate note says وبعض is attached from the manuscript margin.

## Practical philosophy, intercession and legal controls

- **Printed 504 / PDF 511; L0479–L0484 opening:** The modern word is الحكمة and the reported old word is الحكة. Both the 150-dpi body and catalogue contradict the provisional transcription المحكمة / الحكمة. The page also reads القوة العملية, where the electronic has القوة العلمية, a further relevant difference not captured by the numbered short entry. The modern سلطانه at L0479 is restored from the manuscript. The reading عام at L0484 is explicitly offered as the best of several possible readings of an unclear word; it is not presented as certain. The apparatus reports a marginal pointing mark and comment.
- **Printed 512 / PDF 519; L0506 continuation–L0509:** Lesser 36 is وأنها versus وإنما, not وأما. The new conjunction makes the delight clause another reported belief about the soul. Lesser 37 restores ظنهم, preventing the claim that perfection is merely knowledge from being mistaken for the author's assertion. The page also qualifies Abu al-Barakat's report of philosophical consensus and credits Aristotle's natural science above his theology.
- **Printed 539 / PDF 546; L0630–L0633 opening:** The new text restores أو غيره in the description of denied intercession and has partner في الفعل rather than في العقل. The catalogue uses الشفيع in its short anchor, whereas body and electronic have الشافع; the action/intellect contrast is the substantive one. Notes document hadith quotations and one phrase inserted for completeness. The main context permits intercession only within granted limits.
- **Printed 552 / PDF 559; L0691 continuation–L0693:** The modern analogy is the varying law for the same prophet, للنبي الواحد, not for “the same thing”. This links changing commands to a single prophetic religion. A substantial continuation note supplies parallels for the many names of Muhammad, while the page's own notes distinguish the manuscript and printed wording and a Qur'anic correction in the manuscript. These are editorial documentary claims.
- **Printed 562 / PDF 569; L0732–L0735 opening:** The new treaty wording is لازما لا ينقض, without ت, independently checked in a 300 dpi body detail. The body apparatus reports older لا ينقضي, agreeing with the electronic; the catalogue's old anchor instead reads لا ينقص. This discrepancy is retained rather than corrected by inference. The problematic sentence العقود اللازمة لا تكون مؤبدة كالشركة والوكالة is retained by the modern text without a note resolving its conceptual difficulty. The whole page gives the fixed-term/indefinite treaty context and a dispute about the four-month respite; it does not license extracting an unrestricted practical rule.
- **Printed 567 / PDF 574; S004-L0008–0012:** The main text has [أهل] الجاهلية. Note 1 says exactly: زيادة ليست في الأصل، وهي في المطبوع. This contradicts the catalogue's claim that the old printing omitted أهل. The electronic includes أهل, so its agreement with the catalogue's “new” phrase is not evidence of modern updating. The surrounding distinction between unreached people and rejecters of a messenger, and the criticism of answering error with error, remain clear.

## Final agency and devotion: the entire printed 570–579 sequence

- **Printed 570 / PDF 577; S004-L0021 continuation–L0024:** The whole passage rejects both an attribute-less intrinsic necessitator and arbitrary selection without a differentiating ground. Complete will with capacity entails the intended effect; necessity is through the will. A small supplied به is explicitly editorial, and على الآخر is reported as absent from the manuscript but present in the printing. The author's affirmation of natural causes and purposes closes the page.
- **Printed 571 / PDF 578; L0025–L0029 opening:** The author criticises reduction of causal relations to customary conjunction, affirms natures and powers, and treats intellect as an inborn capacity. Notes distinguish copying errors, the manuscript's amended expression for purposive lam, and the uncertain modern conjecture [وأبهموا] where the electronic reads وأتموا. The last is a modern best reading, not manuscript certainty. This is an additional defect/control point outside the selected catalogue rows.
- **Printed 572 / PDF 579; L0029 continuation:** The short page completes the recap about the prophetic teaching, correct reasoning and the dangers of importing speculative doctrine into religion. There are no footnotes; the page ends with a separator before the final chapter.
- **Printed 573 / PDF 580; L0030–0034:** The final chapter opens with philosophy as التشبه بإله, indefinitely, confirmed by the note contrasting old بالإله. The quoted Abu al-Barakat passage is visibly delimited. Several notes compare al-Mutabar wording and explain textual repairs; they belong to the modern apparatus, not Ibn Taymiyya's authorial argument.
- **Printed 574 / PDF 581; L0035–L0038 opening:** The quotation ends and قلت begins the author's exposition. Modern فتحرك النفس جسدها repairs electronic فتجد له النفس جسدها. The notes distinguish words supplied from al-Mutabar and an inferred [الآلهة] in the later exposition. Such additions must not be treated as unmarked authorial autograph text.
- **Printed 575 / PDF 582; L0038 continuation–L0043:** Shared philosophical uses of “god”, imitation of the intellect, the mover/exemplar argument and ambiguity introduced by translation are fully present. A one-word note distinguishes a manuscript grammatical form. The text's complaint is that this route has not established the Creator or the beloved object of worship, not that no human can imitate any good quality.
- **Printed 576 / PDF 583; L0044–L0049 opening:** Imitation can coexist with envy or rivalry and does not entail love and humility. The conditional dilemma about scriptural likeness is confirmed. The new الشرك after أعظم at L0048 is supplied from the manuscript and reported absent from the printing. A separate note changes the pronoun وهو to وهم for context.
- **Printed 577 / PDF 584; L0049 continuation–L0054:** The main text attacks assimilation to every divine name and reports a contrary wholesale denial. The modern apparatus is important contrary evidence: note 2 says al-Ghazali also tells the reader to take the opposite of such traits, including humility; note 5 quotes his interpretation of human “pride” as the knower's ascetic detachment from creation and everything other than God. These notes qualify the author's representation of al-Ghazali and cannot be omitted from an evidence-balanced account. Note 1 identifies the work, note 3 supplies hadith references and note 4 supplies al-Mazari's biography. They are modern editorial scholarship, not parts of the authorial polemic and not independently verified here.
- **Printed 578 / PDF 585; L0055–0059:** The central threefold partition is confirmed in full: knowledge, mercy and wisdom can commend a creature; divinity, domination and pride cannot; servanthood, need, dependence, humility and asking are creaturely excellences incompatible with God. The proper end is worship, love and reliance. The modern notes give Ibn Barrajān biographical material, an explicit preference to omit a transmitted له despite retaining it in the text, a cross-reference for the author's account of fana, and the report about dying with knowledge of divine unity. The self-correction about له warns against making “new edition” automatically equivalent to an uncontested text.
- **Printed 579 / PDF 586; L0060–0063:** The final report, synthesis and “God knows best” are followed by a separated colophon, matching the electronic attribution. Note 1 discusses weaknesses and supporting routes for the last-word hadith and mentions another scholar's favourable overall grading; that modern assessment is not a new authentication by this reader. Note 2 says the colophon is in the manuscript margin, reports بلغ مقابلة بحسب الإمكان والحمد لله on that margin, and reports a title at the manuscript's foot describing a refutation of philosophers and some heretical Sufis. These statements are apparatus reports about an unseen manuscript. This page visually verifies the received terminal argument and colophon; later index pages were not inspected by this reader.

## Consequences and retained limits

The scans strengthen the particular-bearer reading, the plurality of epistemic routes, the distinction of cognitive conditions from productive agency, the reality of conditional created powers, and the final classification of predicates. They also supply counterweights: limits on the reduction of al-Ghazali's position, candid editorial conjectures, and catalogue/body discrepancies. None transforms an actual common-Creator comparison into a universal every-original/every-requisite SourceMode claim.

[complete contextual correction register](../variants/CORRECTION_REGISTER.json) maps all 48 allocated entries with exact electronic contexts: 45 match the discriminating older variant, two (substantial 23 and 38) match neither catalogue anchor, and one matches the catalogue's new phrase subject to the explicit أهل conflict. Abbreviated anchors can differ in surrounding wording; every such qualification is stated. These counts describe this set of mapped variants, not the genealogy or purity of the whole electronic witness.

No entire-work scan reading, direct manuscript reading, external-source retrieval or new formal/Lean result is claimed. The original electronic reading remains 439 lines and 23,795 words. Additional scan coverage is measured in actually inspected pages, not inferred word counts.

## Final enlarged-catalogue reconciliation

A 300 dpi reinspection confirms substantial 29’s old wording is بالعذاب المنفصل والمشاهد, which the electronic follows; the earlier provisional والشاهد transcription is superseded. Substantial 23’s catalogue prints العلمي in both anchors, while the body apparatus reports older العملي and the electronic has that adjective. Substantial 38’s new reading is لا ينقض, without ت; catalogue-old لا ينقص remains different from body-old/electronic لا ينقضي. The final classification counts remain45 older,2 neither and 1 catalogue-new with the stated caveat, but the two neither entries are23 and 38. The additional detail views do not increase the 37-page coverage.
