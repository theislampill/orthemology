## Blocking findings

### AC-F01 — the formal theorem was an application of T254, not a fresh trilemma origin

The stabilizer inclusion and invariant-source nonselection result are direct
specializations of exact historical `AR8R-T254`; the target-application
firewall is inherited from `T255`.  V1 says this in several places, but the
headline “specification-resource trilemma” could still be read as independent
theorem credit.  V2 must make the ancestry canonical and assign zero new
general theorem credit.

### AC-F02 — the deterministic and stochastic scopes were not fully separated

A random seed may be represented as a resource for pointwise deterministic use,
but a stochastic kernel, spontaneous symmetry-breaking relation, or equilibrium
selection process is not automatically a deterministic map `E -> A`.  The
candidate must state whether it is analyzing the realized pointwise map, the
probability law, or an unmodeled selection relation.

### AC-F03 — “specifier” was equivocal between thin asymmetry source and personal volition

The formal theorem establishes only that asymmetry enters through a represented
resource, non-equivariance, or a failed correspondence/interpretation.  It does
not establish a subject, apprehension of alternatives, intention, or voluntary
choice.  The source term “specifier” cannot be silently normalized into the
formal asymmetry resource or vice versa.

### AC-F04 — source attribution needed creed/commentary separation

The specification-without-a-specifier argument first appears in the translated
creed being commented upon.  Ibn Taymiyyah then affirms the core predications
but later subjects specification arguments to detailed causal, regress, and
premise scrutiny.  V2 must label the exact source role and not present one line
as an unqualified neutral theorem endorsed in every formulation.

### AC-F05 — transitivity/no-fixed-point guards were buried

The no-selection corollary requires a `G`-fixed resource state and no `G`-fixed
alternative.  Transitivity is a sufficient way to obtain the latter for a
non-singleton natural action but is not the theorem’s general assumption.  V2
must state the exact fixed-point condition.

### AC-F06 — the personal/impersonal control was interpretation-relative

Interpreting the same parameter as brute, dispositional, or intentional shows
formal underdetermination only in an intended class that admits those
expansions.  It does not establish that all interpretations are metaphysically
possible, causally complete, or equal in prior probability.

### AC-F07 — the positive bridge included a near-conclusion rival-exclusion clause

`NIMP` (“no impersonal rival realizes the same profile”) is a comparative burden,
not a constructive premise that may simply be added to prove personal Will.
V2 must separate positive subject/intentional guards from the independent rival
adjudication.

### AC-F08 — uniqueness of outcome was not uniqueness of explanatory source

One selected alternative can have several jointly sufficient, overdetermining,
or nested resources.  The theorem does not establish one ultimate specifier,
one bearer, or one causal origin.  `ULT` therefore remains a separate grounding
premise.

### AC-F09 — semantic and normative descriptions of the alternatives were absent

The group action treats alternatives extensionally.  It does not establish that
a selector represents them *as alternatives*, knows their content, evaluates
ends, or chooses for a fitting reason.  Those coordinates must remain outside
the formal reduct.

### AC-F10 — the executable check did not independently test the T255 world firewall

The primary finite checker corroborates the deterministic stabilizer result and
personal/impersonal label parity.  It does not test resource completeness,
target-world equivariance, contrast reflection, truth, warrant, or source
application.  These must remain explicit nonclaims and receive source/formal
adjudication rather than checker credit.

## Required repairs

1. Recast V2 as a scoped `T254/T255` application with exact fixed-point and
   deterministic scope.
2. Separate realized deterministic seed models from stochastic kernels and
   equilibrium-selection relations.
3. Type `ASYM_SOURCE`, `SUBJECT`, `ALTERNATIVE_REPRESENTATION`, `INTENTION`,
   `BECAUSE_REASON`, and `VOLITION` independently.
4. Correct the creed/commentary source role.
5. Remove rival exclusion from the positive definitional bridge and retain it
   as an open comparative burden.
6. Add multiple-source/overdetermination, stochastic-relation, and semantic-
   alternative countermodels.
7. Freeze V2 and conduct a distinct group-action rereview.
