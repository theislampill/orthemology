representation-induced kernels and advances the hypothesis of convergence

representation-comparison contract.

representations. Among the tested computational models, CSCG reproduced both

paper also notes that high task performance can occur without the same full
representational geometry. Deep AO therefore treats trajectory and state
structure as task-specific model evidence, not metaphysical identity or proper
function.
