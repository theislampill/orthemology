# Six-lane V14 research intake

This directory preserves three public-safe proposal packages and records an
independent intake of three Deep Research reports. It is a custody and research
queue surface, not adopted theory.

## Six lanes

1. `central/`: exact 178-file Deep CK–CT sanitized proposal tree;
2. `specialist-a/`: exact 77-file formal/computational proposal tree;
3. `specialist-b/`: exact 29-file transcendental/noetic proposal tree;
4. Deep Research 20: theorem ancestry and prior art;
5. Deep Research 21: primary theological, metaphysical, and proper-function sources;
6. Deep Research 22: empirical, representation, and protocol research.

The Deep Research prose reports are not copied into the public tree because
they contain unstable tool-citation tokens and unadjudicated claims. Their
input hashes and bounded intake dispositions are recorded in
`external-evidence/AR8R-DEEP-RESEARCH-20-22-INTAKE-V14.yaml`.

## Controlling boundaries

- source files are immutable evidence;
- correction overlays govern overstatements and missing receipts;
- original Lean drafts remain preserved as failed source evidence;
- repaired Lean files are separately labeled intake repairs;
- finite checker success is not source truth, world truth, empirical utility,
  theorem novelty, or owner adoption;
- every proposal remains `owner_adoption: PENDING`, `external_review: OPEN`,
  and `repository_scientific_adoption: NONE`;
- historical identity is `NONE`, general novelty is `0`, integrated champion
  is `NONE`, and meniscus and natural closure remain unreached.

Private checkpoints, full reports, raw chats, Activity transcripts, browser
evidence, local paths, session identifiers, sandbox URLs, private uploads, and
bulk private archives are excluded.
