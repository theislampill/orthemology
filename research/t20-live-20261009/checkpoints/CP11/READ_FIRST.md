## Main result and the remaining gap

For the stated fixed-count uniform/Joe hard pair, the strongest new bound is

    0 < KL(P0 || P1) = O(log² n / n⁴),
    KL(P1 || P0) = infinity.

Under the matched adaptive retained-endpoint contract, this yields

    E0[started vectors] = Ω_α(n⁴ / log² n).

A finite uniform worst-case cap must also satisfy that lower order. The expectation is under the baseline P0, not the alternative. The existing count-test upper bound O_α(n⁴/log n), inherited from the tenth checkpoint, leaves a logarithmic gap. There is no Θ information claim, matching logarithmic order, leading constant or optimal-test result.

## Five preserved stages

1. Localized likelihood score. The common-stratum log likelihood has a controlled second-order polynomial expansion with |R|≤40(K/m+U)³ when K/m≤1/2 and U≤1/2. A genuine boundary counterexample rules out silently making it global. The separate review supplies a stronger safe constant 5, but downstream proofs retain the author constant 40.
2. Exact baseline moments. Fixed-count formulas for E U² and E(KU) expose compensated-count cancellation, giving E(K−mU)²=H_n+O(H_n²/n), and a score L1/probability bound. These formulas alone do not establish score variance, likelihood-tail integrability or KL. The polynomial is not exactly centered: its n=1 mean is −7/72.
3. Probability prior art and concentration. Last–Molchanov's Pareto-minimum Poisson-hull identities are explicitly matched. A source-guided proof pays the conditioning cost and derives a fixed-count exponential area tail under both hard-pair worlds. It does not transfer centered Poisson equalities unchanged, or equate raw moment bounds with cancellation-sensitive ones.
4. Full-skyline reverse KL, log-four. Both-world dyadic tails, the localized expansion, a global reverse-log envelope and entropy integration establish O(log⁴ n/n⁴), with an Ω_α(n⁴/log⁴ n) started-vector lower bound.
5. Log-squared strengthening. An exact posterior gives N|skyline=K+Poisson(mD). On U≤1/2 the fixed-count/Poisson skyline density ratio is less than 3; the exceptional fixed-count event is separately controlled. Compensated fourth and weighted second moments then sharpen the integrated score square, giving the displayed log-squared bound. Its own review is bound to the final author digest; the log-four review never substitutes for it.

In both information proofs, L is the unnormalized common-stratum likelihood, E0 L=1−p_n, and the correct identity is KL(P0||P1)=E0[−log L+L−1]+p_n. The positive singular-mass correction is preserved. The alternative-only K=n+1 stratum makes forward KL infinite; it is neither erased nor assigned a finite forward logarithm.
