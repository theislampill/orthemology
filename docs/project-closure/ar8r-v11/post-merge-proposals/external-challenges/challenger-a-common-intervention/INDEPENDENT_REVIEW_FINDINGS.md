# Independent review findings — Challenger A

**Disposition:** `GAP_WITH_SOUND_BOUNDED_CORE`

The finite-interface nonidentifiability argument is sound only when the
impersonal comparison class is explicitly closed under a guard-satisfying
history-state realization. It is a synthesis of existing common-intervention,
factorization, and architecture-parity families, not a new theorem origin.

Blocking or material gaps:

1. The architecture maps are design sketches. No machine-readable factor
   registry, total architecture crosswalk, common decoder, policy registry, or
   guard validator establishes an implemented factorial.
2. “Strongest” and “exhaustive” are unsupported. No manifest-backed projection
   proves that every current proposal family maps into the claimed four
   buckets.
3. Potentially continuous or unbounded latency, energy, memory, locality, and
   trajectory fields exceed the stated finite-alphabet theorem unless finite
   bins and bounds are frozen.
4. The one-cell deletion countermodel applies to unrestricted response-table
   classes; structural constraints can make an omitted cell recoverable.
5. The packet's internal reread is a challenger self-reread, not an independent
   repository fresh rereview.

Before stronger use, require executable factorial fixtures, a total
family-to-factor coverage matrix, typed architecture maps, finite measurement
contracts or a measurable-space reformulation, and a distinct independent
rereview.
