## Exact cross-locus finding

1. **Original-perfection premise is genuinely strong.** The supplied Aṣfahāniyya pp.127–129 argues from eligible pure perfection and the original giver to intrinsic necessary possession. The author is not merely asserting one good episode. The modern audit may question its warrant, but must not weaken the intended premise into occasional beneficence and then claim the stronger source argument refuted.

2. **Aṣfahāniyya pp.219–220 reports the Muʿtazilite chain.** The text attributes the ignorance/need account to that school, follows its bodily/temporal/attribute commitments, and attacks the resulting denials. This does not by itself show that Ibn Taymiyya repudiates every ignorance-or-need proposition. The speaker and target of the criticism must stay fixed.

3. **Aṣfahāniyya pp.221–223 gives an affirmative alternative.** The text expressly proposes exclusion of an incontestable confirming sign for a liar through divine mercy, wisdom and regular practice, appealing to benefits and praiseworthy ends. Its physiological illustrations and broad historical outcome claims are part of the source's rationale; they have not been independently validated as modern empirical premises.

4. **Aṣfahāniyya p.218 separates capacity from known nonoccurrence.** Its appeal to sound-disposition knowledge is not a inference from lack of ability. The argument claims certainty about what does not happen despite possible power to do it. Epistemic certainty, actual nonoccurrence and essential modal exclusion should therefore not be conflated in a reconstruction.

5. **The king-sign route at pp.228–230 is reported with named kalam ancestry.** Context may establish what act the king is performing, including his confirmation of the claimant. This is not permission to attribute the entire reported exclusivity claim to Ibn Taymiyya's own sole method; the next section recognizes multiple routes to knowing prophecy.

The complementary *Nubuwwāt* passage is decisive for attribution. In its own affirmative voice, paragraph 106 connects full knowledge of fitting action to choice, treats defective human willing through deficient knowledge/appetite, and treats wisdom as intrinsic through its relation to Knowledge and Will. Near the paragraph's end it says the king-sign route presupposes purposive wisdom, and confirmation is truth-indicating only with exclusion of divine lying. Thus identifying a confirming act and establishing the truth of what is confirmed are distinct **within the source's own discussion**, not only in a modern critique.

## What this warrants for the present research

The source has a substantive connected affirmative argument. It cannot fairly be reduced to a reported Muʿtazilite slogan or to bare information possession. Its load-bearing claims include a governing connection between correctly understood fittingness and willing, and the essentiality of Wisdom through the source's own nature. The full mixed rival must contest those premises, not claim to satisfy them in their strong sense while lying.

Conversely, the source's assertion that full knowledge precludes bad willing is the very philosophical thesis under assessment. A modern fully informed malevolent/mixed proposal denies that normative apprehension by itself fixes motivation, and supplies a bad but determinate preference. The passage's differentiation through what is preferred by the agent must not be equated with objective moral goodness without the accompanying cognitive/perfection rationale. The report earns source fidelity by displaying that bridge and defending it; a quotation of its necessity claim does not independently settle the rival's impossibility.

A purpose-based token result can earn narrower credit without universal moral perfection: if this specific attestation is genuinely selected as the adequate means to the recipient's knowing its exact asserted content, a fully informed instrumentally coherent source cannot knowingly use a false assertion in that role. That is a local practical-compatibility consequence, not a demonstration of actual source purpose, universal truthfulness, actual revelation, or recipient entitlement.
