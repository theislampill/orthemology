## Start with what is being preserved

An application should say what its variables represent, what operation is being performed, and what would make that operation adequate. The sample below contains **source-argued epistemic interpretation**, **ordinary mathematical constructions**, and **worked source-relative examples**. They are not interchangeable levels of implementation or empirical evidence.

This preview does not yet catalogue every application package. The existing [DAEE crosswalk][daee], [latent-state application][latent] and [agentic-runtime guide][agentic] remain their own scoped entrances.

## Recognition and corrective reasoning

The [Sound Reason substrate][sound] gives reasoning a constructive role when basic recognition is obscured: clarify the question, identify the obstructing premise or acquired distortion, and restore access to the cognition the account treats as basic. In that account, wider non-collusive concordance can vindicate cognitive functioning; it is not only a transmission-provenance check.

The [project distinction note][concrete] helps keep this from becoming a scalar “correct/incorrect person” judgement. Instantiation, configured pathway adequacy, truth linkage, source dependence and subject-relative warrant need their own bearers. The [later qualification report][retained] retains affirmative source-bound appraisals while distinguishing ordinary knowledge from infallibility.

**Application standing:** a philosophical account and project-level distinctions, not an empirically validated diagnostic instrument. A transcript, popularity measure or graph of reports does not establish a person's fiṭrah, proper functioning or metaphysical warrant. The reader must see both the constructive account and the additional premises needed to apply it.

## Noetic representations and numerical dynamics

The [tensor and coercive works](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#tensor-moments-and-coercive-dynamics) provide actual conditional mathematical descriptions of full moment histories. The [Fisher and sidecar works](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#fisher-geometry-and-retained-information) provide an actual probability manifold, metric and information-preservation obstruction.

A proposed noetic application must separately specify how a numerical state or audit law represents the relevant noetic structure. It must identify what the moments or labels mean, how they are observed, what assumptions justify the dynamics, and which result-preservation contract matters. Renaming coordinates “belief” or “soundness” supplies none of those bindings.

This distinction cuts both ways. The lack of a validated psychological interpretation does not turn the metric and theorem into mere metaphors. Conversely, a nonpositive derivative of a stipulated numerical loss does not establish epistemic improvement. The mathematical construction, proposed interpretation and evidence about actual cognition must remain separately described.

## Coordination records

The [finite-mediator paper][coordination] is useful when evaluating a proposed explanation of coordination under its declared Gaussian model. Its sharp fixed-horizon bound states the capture performance compatible with a complete mediator of a given alphabet size.

To apply it, establish the conditional-independence cut and account for side information. A controller's short code, finite local state or quantised display might not carry the whole mediation information. A matching retrospective construction is not already a forward-causal physical controller. The [result entry](RESULT-INDEX.md#finite-mediator) links the exact ordinary proof and the scope that must travel with the bound.

## Joint feasibility across avenues

The [existing join][join] gives a worked connection between occurrence-sensitive language and empirical completion. Its contribution is not merely that both subjects contain uncertainty.

In the selected [occurrence records][occurrences], the complete source-relative tuples are:

```text
(M, M, G)
(G, G, G)
```

The fixture labels `M` and `G` denote Messenger and God. Treating each coordinate independently also permits `(M,G,G)` and `(G,M,G)`, but neither has a complete witness in that selected relation. A partial `(M,M,unspecified)` occurrence does not authorise either addition. Direct exposition, attributed interpretation and reported preference remain different source roles.

The [missing-outcome analysis][missing] has an analogous feasibility issue. Suppose two binary coordinates each separately permit zero and one, but jointly only these completions are admissible:

```text
(0, 1)
(1, 0)
```

Their sum is always one. The Cartesian relaxation admits sums zero and two; those extremes are not attainable in the declared feasible relation.

**What transfers:** preserve the jointly admissible relation before projecting to coordinate answers or optimising an objective. The Cartesian set can still be a labelled outer relaxation. **What does not transfer:** the linguistic records do not prove dependence among actual missing outcomes, and the statistical example does not authenticate Arabic sources or decide an interpretative truth dispute. The [existing review][join-review] preserves that boundary.

## From reading a proposal to using an implementation

For any application reached through this guide, identify whether the target is a manuscript, worked fixture, reference implementation, executable package, or an empirical result. Follow the target package's actual maintained entry point and its declared environment.

Historical wrappers and archive-relative commands can describe a different workspace. The [source recovery guide][recovery] distinguishes exact source export from actual execution and retains unavailable-input boundaries. A source-resolver success is not evidence that a controller, cognitive model or experiment works.

The eventual full applications page must connect the remaining application families to their actual sources and tests. The current sample does not establish that all existing packages have been examined, run or accepted.
