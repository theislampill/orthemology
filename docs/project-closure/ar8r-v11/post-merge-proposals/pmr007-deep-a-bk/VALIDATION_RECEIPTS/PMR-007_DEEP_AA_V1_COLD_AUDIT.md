## Blocking findings

### AA-F01 — `N` is not automatically a norm or reason

The structural variable is an encoded score/control state. Its causal influence
on action does not establish normative authority, reasonhood, semantic content,
or fittingness. The repaired packet must distinguish `Score` from `Norm` and
carry Deep-X/Y guards separately.

### AA-F02 — SCM intervention is model-relative, not causal certification

`do(N)` semantics establishes causal relevance inside the frozen SCM. It is not
an empirical intervention receipt, a correct causal model of a runtime, or a
world-level causal conclusion. The authority ceiling must be explicit.

### AA-F03 — personal/impersonal expansions need an intended-class guard

Adding Boolean intentional predicates to one neutral SCM proves a logical
nonentailment only in an intended class admitting both expansions. It does not
establish metaphysical possibility or equal plausibility. The repaired theorem
must say so.

### AA-F04 — intentional bridge contract is transparent rather than probative

`UptakeAsReason`, `FittingEnd`, `SelectsBecause`, and `OwnsEpisode` are exactly
the missing target relations. Their conjunction conditionally yields the
intended label but does not independently prove them. No positive theorem or
novelty credit should be assigned to the contract.

### AA-F05 — truth/authority/world coordinates are carried, not established

Deep Z's common core treats them as independently valid typed owners. The round
must not describe them as established in the world merely because the SCM
assumes them.

### AA-F06 — Wisdom is stronger than intentional purpose

Wisdom requires at least eligible knowledge, fitting end, selection, efficacy,
nondefective coordination, and stable bearer/source conditions at the declared
scope. Intentional guidance alone is insufficient.

### AA-F07 — source and implementation boundaries

Track-N source predication must remain source-relative and nonmigratory. The
SCM does not validate daee implementation, OSM mechanism, human cognition,
fiṭrah restoration, or a divine attribute.

## Required repair

- rename the structural variable `Score` and type `NormAuthority` separately;
- narrow intervention claims to the frozen SCM;
- add intended-model-class and metaphysical nonclaim guards;
- downgrade the intentional contract to a bridge specification;
- carry truth/authority/world status rather than reprove it;
- separate intentional purpose from Wisdom;
- preserve Track-N and implementation firewalls.
