## Disposition

Accept the candidate at its stated scope. It preserves the serious positive case for original practical integration and supplies a modest new application to complete-profile comparison. Two alleged compensating advantages of an unfaithful exercise are excluded under the specified facts: avoiding a responsibility already incurred, and retaining an authority that the care relation never established. Neither exclusion establishes an eligible repair, the absence of all other losses, actual nondomination N, or essential nondomination.

The negative epistemic conclusion needs its exact reading. The paper does not newly establish N for the actual original source; it does not show that N has no rational support. The integrated original-giver proposal supplies a genuine prima-facie reason toward comprehensive practical integration, beyond the local judgment that the controlled lie is defective. At the granted source and practical-respect scope, this is also a reason in favour of the nondomination proposal. Its force need not be deductive or source-neutral. A describable intrinsic exception does not defeat it. This positive credit is substantially inherited from Seventh and Ninth, and the current inquiry must not advertise it as newly won.

No material correction to the frozen article is required. There are three important reporting guards: do not turn the inquiry's absence of a new N result into a verdict that belief in N is unwarranted; do not describe eliminating two comparison objections as establishing R_G; and do not turn bounded print inspection into critical-edition or manuscript certification. The article already contains the qualifications needed for all three.

## 1 The actual inherited premises

The accepted Ninth article and logical appendix were read, together with their exact acceptance and the supplementary warrant assessment. Their result is not an inference from local badness to metaphysical impossibility. R_G requires a genuinely same-bearer eligible complete repair that is strictly better in the warranted practical comparison. R_P is the stronger nonspoiling condition. N concerns the actual profile's nondomination among the eligible complete alternatives. The exclusion follows from the conjunction; neither conjunct was independently established for the actual original source.

The whole-profile requirement matters even when only one false assurance is repaired. A better sentence is not automatically a better complete exercise. A change affecting another context, a beneficiary or the source's legitimate practical interests remains part of the comparison. The full essence cannot be weakened to secure eligibility. Conversely, the direct original-perfection route can challenge a purported defective essence without first accepting it and searching inside its counterpart space. No same-bearer construction is compulsory for that different route.

The current Tenth article retains those distinctions. Its statements about absent overriding justification do not infer R_P, and its discussion of a single concrete improvement does not infer unrestricted R_G. Its modal paragraph does not infer essential N from actual N. The accepted Tenth organised-reliance and governance-v2 materials were also read rather than inferred from their titles. Their accepted credit is bounded normative responsibility and application-level coordination, not comprehensive practical priority.

## 2 The strongest positive reason deserves substantive credit

The best affirmative proposal is not that a producer must resemble every product or that possessing information mechanically causes a selected output. It identifies a positive practical achievement: an agent apprehends a genuine reason as favouring an action and acts from that apprehension. The normative significance belongs within the agent's explanation of what it is doing. It is not merely a proposition registered alongside an otherwise unrelated preference.

Original giving adds an intelligible explanatory consideration. If the received achievement is fittingly integrated agency, its original explanation should not silently discard the integration and retain only the causal production of its components. An original source acting through its own understanding and fitting orientation can explain why the recipient's achievement is practical excellence rather than accidental conformity. This supplies rational support for an integrated source account. It is not erased by the existence of an alternative verbal description.

The comprehensive extension has positive content too. Imperfect manifestations can exhibit an excellence whose unimpeded original mode is more complete than those manifestations. Fidelity at the specified respect need not be a collection of unrelated successful episodes. It can be a standing, nondefective ordering of the source's practical exercise. The exception would then be an internal practical deficiency, rather than merely the failure to produce one more optional benefit. The article gives this interpretation its strongest form; it does not define original as impeccable and announce a victory.

The intellectualist component makes the affirmative case sharper. Complete apprehension of decisive choiceworthiness can plausibly be understood as intrinsically directing cognition. Its object is already action-favouring; nothing forces us to treat it as motivationally inert information. If that direction is sufficient and no competing determination remains, positing an arbitrary contrary choice does not explain anything. Human weakness of will is a poor immediate rebuttal, since relevant attention, affective salience and practical understanding may be incomplete. The paper correctly accepts the burden on a reasonless exception.

These considerations provide prima-facie support extending beyond local eligibility. They can support a defeasible preference for comprehensive integration and the corresponding source-priority proposal. They may also rationally support a stronger basic judgment in someone who actually apprehends the source-qualified principle. It would be an error to demand a neutral algorithm, consensus, an empirical source survey, a proof of every premise from something more basic, or an independently constructed malicious essence before any such warrant counts.

The review does not claim an all-things-considered comparative verdict between complete metaphysical systems. It also does not transfer a conditional actual-source grant into independently identifying an ultimate source in the world. The epistemic credit is real at the argument's stated referent and scope, without those additional achievements.

## 3 What the investigation has and has not added to N

The distinction between practical preference and objectively decisive ordering remains relevant, but it must be used carefully. Merely selecting an end can make an action intelligible without showing why that end ought to govern. Conversely, practical apprehension as understood by the strongest intellectualist is not exhausted by the weak premise that an end is attractive. The author explicitly examines both and does not refute the stronger account merely by replacing it with the weaker one.

Original independence removes a donor, coercer or borrowed need only where those are independently excluded. It does not, solely by being independence, classify every intrinsic conative determination as correctly ordered. This observation identifies a limit of the nonneed inference. It is not evidence that the actual source possesses a competing malicious orientation, and it is not a proof that such an orientation is metaphysically possible.

The most important fair test is to grant the stronger proposal outright. If complete source-qualified practical apprehension is an essentially sufficient principle of fitting intention throughout the relevant domain, the controlled lie is excluded. Adding a supposedly intrinsic competing priority after that grant would revoke the grant. The candidate passes this test. It allows a constitutive account or a properly targeted basic recognition to supply precisely that content. It does not insist that sufficient motivation and complete practical understanding must always be separable.

Even a successful direct exclusion of the controlled lie is not automatically a proof of N. N is a claim about complete-profile nondomination, whereas nonlying or avoidance of the specified wrong is narrower. The positive support toward N comes from the comprehensive original-practical-priority proposal, not from reversing the Ninth exclusion lemma. A fully fitting intention also needs the appropriate connection to the stipulated pure-perfection comparison before it can do all of N's work.

The remaining question is which positive reason warrants that comprehensive identification. An account of informed purposiveness and absence of external interference does not by itself add the stronger content. Original practical priority can supply it, but then the explanation draws on that source-wide principle. This is a dependency finding about the offered argument, not an inference that the principle is arbitrary, circular merely because strong, or incapable of basic warrant.

Nor must every reason in a rationally warranted package be independently sufficient on its own. The original-giver, integration and independence considerations may reinforce one another. The paper's statement that there are not two independently earned warrants should therefore be read as rejecting double credit for the same disputed source-wide step, not as a general requirement of epistemic independence. The combined account can remain more illuminating and rationally attractive even when it produces no additional accepted N result. The article's opening combined account, its explicit prima-facie credit and its final inquiry-relative limitation support this reading.

The precise verdict is consequently differentiated:

- The local practical defect and eligibility of fidelity retain their inherited affirmative warrant.
- The comprehensive original-integration proposal has positive prima-facie rational support; the mere intrinsic-exception description is not a defeater of it.
- The present inquiry clarifies the proposed cognition-to-choice connection but does not supply a newly established actual N judgment with the complete comparison scope.
- The review does not establish that N is false, that a contrary essence is possible, or that a believer in N lacks justified belief.
- Essential scope remains a further content requirement unless the original warrant itself has that essential reach.

This is stronger than treating the account as an empty possibility, and weaker than treating the newly inspected argument as completing the source-wide warrant. It is faithful to the paper's §§3–4 and8 when read together. A short integrating report should state the positive credit expressly so that its headline limitation cannot obscure it.

## 4 The new comparison result

### Responsibility is the correct baseline

The organised-reliance result earns a special reason for proportionate care from the source's actual history of actively arranging legitimate bounded reliance. Mere purpose change does not cancel that history. When two current responses share that history, the responsibility belongs on both sides of the comparison. It is not first created by the faithful response.

That is a real correction to a proposed loss. A source that breaches its responsibility does not preserve a normatively unencumbered condition which the faithful alternative sacrifices. Fulfilling the reason can remain voluntary, original agency; its relational object is not an external donor of the source's being.

However, a common responsibility does not make the costs of performance common. Warning, transition or substitute provision could consume effort or affect other legitimate projects even when both alternatives lie under the same duty. The author retains this distinction: actual feasibility and collateral goods remain independently assessed. The excluded advantage is escaping the responsibility as a status, not every resource saving or accompanying benefit obtainable through a breach. This restriction is essential to the accepted credit.

The responsibility also has its inherited limits. Valid risk allocation, adequate release, informed refusal and changed circumstances can alter what care is owed. The comparison cannot quietly extend a discharged undertaking or turn bounded reliance into indefinite maintenance. Candidate §6 assumes the responsibility still applies rather than deriving it anew from sourcehood.

### Unsupported control is not an established perfection

A care responsibility does not by itself grant jurisdiction over the beneficiary. Where an equally adequate, reasonably available performance preserves the relevant legitimate goods without the disputed control condition, withholding it solely to obtain control does not establish the missing entitlement. The source's ability to originate and execute an act is different from a justified right to settle the recipient's decision.

Removing that unsupported condition therefore does not sacrifice a practical authority established by the care role. This is a negative comparison conclusion about the alleged advantage. It does not demonstrate that every exercise of control is bad, that consent is necessary for every obligation, or that actual pleasure accompanying control has no value under any description. An independently justified allocation of authority, real coordination burden, third-party claim or relevant collateral good would require a different assessment. The author preserves those alternatives.

The false-assurance and control-condition cases are also not interchangeable. A coercive demand may contain no false assertion. The comparison result can apply to that practical wrong without placing it under Lie or changing T0. R_G credit applies only when the candidate alternative actually repairs an in-scope lie while preserving the requisite assurance role. The candidate's §7 retains this distinction; a future summary must do the same.

### The two times cannot be collapsed

The corrected governance-v2 boundary is fully carried into the candidate. A source may wrongfully reject a less intrusive arrangement and then create innocent third-party dependence on its chosen implementation. The later dependence does not retrospectively justify the earlier selection. It can nevertheless produce real current reasons against an abrupt switch.

The historical comparison and current repair comparison consequently have different jobs. An earlier adequate alternative may expose wrongful manufacture of a constraint. It does not prove that a currently adequate, loss-free repair exists. Current innocent-party interests cannot be deleted merely because the source created them wrongfully. Temporary recipient cooperation can be fitting without recognising rightful command, and remedial responsibilities remain subject to actual means and competing reasons.

A whole-history alternative is not prohibited. It needs an explicit eligibility and fixed-case argument. If replacing the earlier arrangement removes the actual exposure or changes the assurance situation under review, it cannot be silently substituted for a current repair of the unchanged case. Conversely, preserving the actual history to assess current care does not vindicate that history. Candidate §6 states both protections clearly.

### Exact new credit

Under the stated conditions, two named supposed losses fail as objections to a faithful candidate improvement. If the same-bearer alternative and complete remaining assessment independently support strict superiority, the local alternative can contribute to R_G at that case. The new argument alone does not provide either of those further premises.

In particular, a failure to articulate another loss is not proof that no loss exists; a lesser genuine loss may permit R_G while defeating R_P; and a better available exercise does not prove that the actual source selects it. Actual N is the additional claim that excludes a dominated actual profile. The paper never earns N simply by making the comparison against the lie more favourable.
