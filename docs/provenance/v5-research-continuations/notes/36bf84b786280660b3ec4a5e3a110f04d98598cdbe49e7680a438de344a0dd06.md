## Literal statement

The objects are two **fixed smooth coordinate-additive score families** on a finite categorical simplex with at least three genuine outcomes. The assumptions on each generator are inherited unchanged from `SmoothScalar.Score`. One outcome direction i0 is fixed.

`CommonLocalNudges` quantifies over **every interior coherent p**. At each p there must exist positive epsilon_n→0 and directions d_n such that the inputs p+epsilon_n e_i0 stay in the open cube and every truth-state loss has a strictly negative actual directional derivative under both scores. The original directions need not be bounded or normalized, and need not point to coherent endpoints.

`CommonCoherentLocalNudges` keeps those universal and sequential quantifiers, but additionally supplies a shared interior coherent sequence c_n and requires strict descent in the direction c_n-(p+epsilon_n e_i0). It still does **not** say the endpoint c_n itself improves either loss.

Each universal property is equivalent to the same positive global scalar/per-coordinate affine generator relation, and hence to the exact **universal** common coherent-repair class. These are classification equivalences across every p, not pointwise equivalences at one input or assertions that a particular nudge endpoint is a common dominator or projection.

## Proof audit

The displayed Hessian expressions are actual Fréchet derivatives of the truth-state Bregman loss with forecast in the second argument. Strict descent makes every d_n nonzero. The proof normalizes the directions internally to unit norm and extracts a subsequential unit limit. No nonvanishing limit or normalization premise is assumed.

Continuity near p and the strict inequalities yield weak limiting inequalities. Their positive barycentric sum is zero, so all vanish. The same nonzero limit is normal for both positive Hessians. On a categorical simplex the normal space is a line; positive diagonal curvature therefore forces proportional Hessian coordinates. The already audited third-coordinate connectivity and twice-integration arguments give one global positive scale and closed-interval generator equivalence.

For sufficiency, the explicit reciprocal-curvature direction is

    d_i = (1 - sum b) / f_i''(b_i).

Its actual derivative at every truth state is exactly `-(sum b - 1)^2`. Affine-equivalent generators scale this sign positively. A previously proved interior perturbation sequence supplies valid inputs; no common-nudge oracle appears.

For coherent-directed nudges, reciprocal curvatures are normalized by their strictly positive sum S. The constructed endpoint is `c_i=b_i-epsilon*(1/f_i''(b_i))/S`. Its coordinates sum exactly to one. Continuity and positivity imply c_n→p, so a sequence shift puts every endpoint in the interior. The direction is the ambient reciprocal-curvature nudge multiplied by the positive factor 1/S, preserving strict descent. The construction is proved, not assumed.
