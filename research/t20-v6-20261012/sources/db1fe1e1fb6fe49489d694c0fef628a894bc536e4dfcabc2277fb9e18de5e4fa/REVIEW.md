**Qualified pass, with a needed scope clarification.** The report accurately identifies an election-and-fitness argument and does not derive arbitrary requirement-making power from causal origination. Its negative result is defensible as a bounded test for an additional constitutive explanation. ## The exact logical fork

The controlling scoped-original-authority report preserves three possible grounds for a determinate duty: a relevant selection entitlement; a substantive performance fact making the alternative inadequate; or authenticated truthful normative disclosure. It explicitly says the third does not require a second independent derivation of title. The reviewed report repeats this correctly.

Consequently, distinguish two questions:

1. **Does this additional selection-and-fitness argument independently explain the constitution of the contested title?** A negative or unresolved answer is coherent. Knowledge of beneficial differences does not alone establish obligatory rather than recommended grade, and a source's good choice is not automatically an exercise of every proposed jurisdiction.
2. **Is this particular obligation already warranted through an admitted route?** If independently authenticated source-owned disclosure establishes this obligation with its force, addressee, scope and conditions, the separate constitutive explanation is not a further prerequisite. Keeping it as a duty-blocking residual would contradict the retained testimonial route.

Similarly, if a relevant continuing right of control has already been warranted for this aspect of the owed response, the stipulated case falls under the retained scoped-control rule. General acknowledgement that some in-scope control is warranted does not, however, identify every disputed specification as within that scope. The present sources do not supply an identified new actual case deciding that coverage.

## What “otherwise adequate” may and may not hold fixed

The report's hypothetical is intelligible if it fixes adequacy relative to antecedent grounds and sets aside the contested specification's normative contribution. It asks whether the new address can change what satisfies the relation. This does not itself assume that the alternative remains adequate after a rightful specification.

Two distortions should be excluded:

- Defining the rival as adequate even after every admitted normative contribution would assume the disputed conclusion against specification from the outset.
- Defining any source-preferred option as uniquely required would assume the contested grade and scope in the other direction.

The actual distinction is not simply unknown benefit versus known equivalence. An authenticated obligatory address can supply an operative normative fact even where the recipient lacks an independent account of its underlying reason. Conversely, a beneficial preference or recommendation does not become obligatory through appeal to hidden wisdom alone. These boundaries are already available in the controlling report; no new general authority theory is needed.

## Recommended additive clarification

“Shifa also positively defends adherence to actual divine legislation through knowledgeable beneficial selection. Our negative result concerns only a proposed additional derivation of selection title from the election analogy. It does not suspend an obligation already warranted by authenticated normative disclosure or an independently established relevant control right. In an actual application, identify which attribution, force, scope, or constitutive premise remains unsettled; do not reintroduce a second title proof after the admitted route has supplied the obligation.”

This clarification preserves wise normative guidance without claiming that these excerpts independently settle every constitutive question. It also avoids making the residual a manufactured practical obstacle.

## Final disposition

No new selection-title theorem or independently authenticated obligation is established by this review. No first-reading credit or reversal of the earned positive authority appraisal is warranted. The precise result is that the report's negative title test is bounded and substantially supported, while its practical use must honor the sufficient routes it already accepts. The broad claim “no further title established” should not be paraphrased as “no binding guidance warranted.”
