# An ancestry-qualified actual-power route: resource and power-bearer consequences

Research candidate, 3 October 2026. This is original target-specific analysis, not an additional synopsis of Pruss2009. The source-side modal proposal remains owned by the accepted transfer-warrant paper, section 6, and its source locator T-POWERS. No actual necessary participant is claimed here.

## 1. Why examine this route

The joint-history question need not be decided before every possible necessary-participant argument can proceed. A modal-realization principle might extract an actual necessary concrete realization resource from an independently warranted possibility of existentially independent supply. An additional supplying-power condition on the selected resource would give an actual necessary capacity bearer. Either is an intermediate existence result; no actual exercise is required. The route must not receive credit merely from relabelling a possible independent supplier as an actual one, or from treating every concrete background resource as a bearer of the relevant productive power.

Fix the actual covering family Q. F means being a contingent concrete bearer instantiating a relevant member of that same family. Distinguish COV-B, coverage of every actual contingent concrete capacity bearer, from COV-R, coverage of every actual contingent concrete resource in the relevant realization base and any actual positive supplier when S is actual. The latter clause supplies the actual-S branch's domain without assuming a nonactual realizing base there. The minimal resource theorem below assumes COV-R openly. It is a stronger domain claim if the inherited coverage concerned only capacity bearers, and is not established merely by that inherited premise. A direct supplying-power-anchor version can instead use COV-B. Q does not change with the comparison history. A necessary bearer may instantiate a Q-kind while not being F, because the contingency qualification is separate.

The modal target S is stronger than the bare proposition p that there is an F. It positively includes a concrete supply bearer s outside F, a productive contribution by s to an F recipient, and the absence of F-instantiation from the full relevant existential-support history of s's availability. This includes productive ancestry and any relevant earlier stage of that same bearer retained through persistence. The shorter term F-ancestry below always has this stated support scope; strict productive ancestry alone could omit a same-bearer stage. The absence qualification covers the whole relevant realized history, not just s's classification at its last stage. If an earlier F-stage is required for a later differently classified stage's availability, that earlier F-instantiation violates the qualification. A merely internal account's claim that no boundary input is left unaccounted for does not by itself satisfy S. The positive bearer and its ancestry qualification are both part of the target. In a merely possible history s can be a contingent outsider to actual Q, and it can be supported by further non-F bearers. S does not assume s is necessary, intrinsically unreceived, or the sole source. Nor does it require every later participant to be non-F: s may produce F intermediates and work through them.

S must be independently intelligible and its possibility warranted. It must not be treated as already actual merely because p is actual. This route uses possible S openly, rather than feeding S into a principle whose only established actual input is p.

## 2. The strong realization principle

Consider the following substantive package, RP:

1. Every relevant merely possible S can be realized through powers actually possessed by concrete bearers, singly or jointly.
2. A realizing route begins from actual power-possession resources. Its relevant concrete background is either among those actual resources or produced through the realizing route. It does not silently import a merely possible independently available concrete source as free background.
3. Realizing a qualified causal history preserves the actual initiating resources' relevance to the qualified achievement. Producing one conjunct of a description while presupposing its independently supplied remainder does not count as realizing the whole achievement from that initiating resource alone.
4. Where an initiating actual bearer is F, its relevant F-instantiation remains in the antecedent support of the realizing route. Changing its classification later cannot remove that earlier dependence. Counterfactual substitution of a different prior classification is not automatically an exercise of this very actual F-based capacity.

5. For the selected possible realization, either s is itself an actual available concrete resource, or there is a positive support trace to s's existential availability anchored in at least one actual concrete entry resource r. The relevant occurrence of r is retained on that trace. If r is actually F, that occurrence's F-instantiation is retained as relevant existential support for s in the whole realized history. Mere whole-base relevance to S, or an F-resource relevant only to the recipient or trigger branch, is insufficient. r need not suffice individually for the supply; it may make its positive contribution as part of a genuinely joint resource.

RP is a stronger generative reading of actual-power modality than the claim that actual powers ground modal truths. It also says more than identity preservation of an initiator. Its target is a possible realizing path grounded in actually possessed powers, not the merely semantic truth of a possibility-description. There is no causal arrow between worlds and no claim that the possible path is actually exercised. No assumption that every actual history has a first event is included. The positive actual anchor is supplied by RP for the selected possible realization of this nonactual achievement; arbitrary actual beginningless histories need not themselves have such an entry resource.

## 3. Two typed conditional consequences

Assume possible S, RP, the appropriate fixed actual coverage, and the following antecedent-preservation judgement:

If a selected realizing route retains an actual resource's F-instantiation as positive existential support specifically for s's availability, that route cannot realize S's qualification that the relevant availability has no F ancestry. Further downstream production does not erase that occurrence from the selected route.

**Minimal concrete-resource consequence, using COV-R.** If S is actual, it directly includes an actual concrete contributor s outside F. COV-R makes s noncontingent, hence necessary. The positive bearer requirement is essential: the weaker claim that a history has no remaining unaccounted-for inputs would reopen the very joint-coverage dispute under examination.

If S is merely possible, choose the possible realization supplied by RP. If s is itself an actual available resource, retaining its actual F-classification at the relevant supplying occurrence would contradict S's outside-F clause; changing that classification later would retain the relevant earlier F-stage and contradict S's whole-ancestry clause. Thus that actual resource is outside F. Otherwise RP5 supplies an actual concrete anchor r whose positive support trace specifically supplies s's availability. If r were actually F, RP4–5 and antecedent preservation would retain relevant F-support for s and contradict S. Thus the actual r is outside F. In either case COV-R makes the selected actual concrete resource noncontingent, hence necessary.

The selected witness need not be indispensable across every possible realizing route. Its retained positive contribution on the particular route promised by RP is enough. Nor does the proof select an arbitrary member of the whole resource base: it selects s if already actual, or the actual anchor relevant to s's existential availability. No assumption that a necessary background resource actively supplies a power is made.

**Supplying-power-bearer consequence.** Add that the positively selected actual resource is a bearer of a supplying power, or independently establish that all relevant entry resources have that type. The same witness is then an actual necessary capacity bearer. One may run this stronger version directly with COV-B and an actual supplying-power anchor, without covering unrelated passive background. In the actual-S case s's specified actual productive role already gives the required type. In the merely possible case the active-bearer condition must be added; actual membership in a sufficient background is not a substitute.

Both conclusions concern an actual particular, not merely necessary occupancy of a role. Neither establishes actual exercise, unique production of the actual field, or intrinsic explanation of the witness's own necessity. The inference has not earned P1/P2, N2/N3, cognition, unity or integrity.

## 4. What is independently plausible here

A productive exercise does not supply the antecedent reality required for that very exercise by producing an output. This is the accepted local judgement, and it has genuine rational warrant. There is likewise a strong reason not to count the realization of a specially qualified achievement when the proposed route defeats that very qualification. A manufacturing process whose initial stage uses lead has not supplied an entirely lead-independent provenance merely because its final stage uses a different metal. This is a provenance-relevance judgement, not a claim that existence is a conserved material.

Resource completeness and achievement-specific relevance also have positive warrant. A sufficient capacity for the whole stated achievement cannot produce only its recipient while borrowing a merely possible independent supplier as free background. This is an adequacy judgement about what has actually been promised, rather than a global demand that every fact be explained. It does not establish the existence of an actual basis satisfying that promise.

If RP supplies a particular actual entry occurrence whose F-instantiation is retained as positive support for s's availability on the selected possible route, this local reason extends through that route: later production does not make the entry occurrence disappear from its history. This is not a universal but-for test across all possible routes, and merely earlier events do not qualify. The conditional extension requires neither a first event in the whole universe nor a neutral algorithm for evaluating every explanation.

The cleanest supported case has finite supply depth from actual resources to s: every step preserves its concrete ancestor's relevance, and repeated finite composition retains an F ancestor. The number of simultaneous resources need not be finite, provided their joint role and a relevant actual F contribution are independently established. No inference that arbitrary infinite plural dependence is well-founded is used. For routes with no independently established trace from the actual entry resources to s's availability, RP5 is a substantive further requirement, not a consequence of calling the construction a chain. The present argument does not establish that every absolute possibility has a finite-depth or source-traceable realization.

The strongest positive prospect is consequently real: a historically closed, independently warranted modal target plus a genuinely generative, source-preserving account of its possibility can yield a necessary concrete realization resource, and under the supplying-power condition a necessary capacity bearer, without first refuting every active non-well-founded history.

The target's reduced inventory burden and the inherited prima-facie reason to seek independent availability make it a rationally motivated proposal. They do not by themselves warrant an outsider to the comprehensive actual Q with the full support qualification. Familiar restricted classes have outsiders; the current Q is meant to cover every relevant actual contingent candidate. An analogy from a restricted class therefore needs a scope argument. S is lighter than the former complete inventory in one respect while stronger than ordinary outside-kind production in its ancestry respect. The research does not treat that tradeoff as uniformly weaker modal content or as new direct evidence for POS-S.

## 5. Why the present accepted premises do not already yield it

### 5.1 Classification transport

The actual kind-covering premise and the priority of membership to an actual exercise do not say that the bearer has that classification in every comparison history. Bare identity preservation therefore does not establish RP4. The same actual individual could, under the weaker premises, be differently classified in an alternative history. This is an inference control, not a certified possible case.

There are two distinct cases. An actual F bearer changes classification by exercising an F-based power; the earlier F-instantiation remains antecedent. Or the alternative history gives the bearer a different classification before the relevant exercise, with no F-based transformation in that history. Only the second defeats the simple preservation argument, and excluding it needs a modal warrant about actual-power realization. Origin-insensitivity is not essentiality and does not exclude it by itself.

### 5.2 Relevance and available background

An F agent might occasion an independently available producer's exercise without supplying that producer's existential availability. Merely observing that the F agent appears causally earlier does not show that S's existential availability depends on it. Conversely, if that independent producer is already an actual resource outside F, the intermediate necessary participant has already been obtained and must be counted.

If the independent producer is only possible, the claim that an actual F agent can realize S by activating it needs an account of that producer's availability. RP2 is meant to prevent treating that very disputed availability as free background. The weaker claim that the F agent can produce one recipient once the possible producer is supplied does not establish a capacity to realize the whole qualified S.

### 5.3 A target stronger than ordinary outside production

A contingent producer from a kind absent in actual Q could itself be produced by an F bearer. Its later production of an F recipient then establishes ordinary outside-kind production but not automatically the antecedent independence in S. A modal witness for the former is not silently a witness for the latter.

If the source is necessary, actual existence follows on ordinary modal assumptions from the source's possible necessity; invoking that fact would be a different route. The present proposal must not use possible necessity as an unacknowledged substitute for an independently grounded possible supply judgement.

### 5.4 The scope of realized histories

A power to bring about a later state is not a power to make the whole timeless history have had a different provenance. S's qualification must state whether it concerns the entire relevant productive ancestry or a bounded later occurrence. If it concerns only a later occurrence, a formerly F supplier can cease to be F and still act, but the earlier F availability remains in the wider history. If it concerns the entire ancestry, RP must really apply to such qualified histories, not merely to ordinary later states.

## 6. Relation to the Ninth exact-origin result

The qualified-achievement contradiction is inherited proof technique, not a new discovery. Accepted Ninth F1 already tests whether productive realization preserves an exact origin qualification or defeats it by supplying a receiving source. Its target-specific productive-coverage relocation remains controlling. Merely restating RP for S would not independently warrant its application.

The present conditional differs in target and conclusion. It does not begin with an actual identified original g or seek the necessity of that same g through reverse modal access. It begins with one possible outside supply bearer and one suitable recipient, with an explicit F-ancestry restriction. It seeks some actual necessary concrete resource, strengthening to a necessary power bearer under the stated type condition, without requiring that resource to be the possible s or an actually exercised contributor. The price is a new classification-transport obligation and domain alignment, as well as the inherited need to justify realization of the whole qualified achievement. This is a target-sensitive extension and discriminator, not a replacement necessity theorem or an escape from the Ninth result.

No Ninth-style equivalence between this entire RP package and the bare existence of some necessary bearer is asserted. A necessary bearer could lack the powers, trace or resource relations required to realize S. An independently justified general realization theory might nonetheless entail the required instance; simply asserting the target-specific instance would relocate the substantive force rather than independently earn it.

The single-recipient modal reduction is retained. No exhaustive actual inventory, exact multiplicity, unique original source, or first temporal event is required. Conversely, these reductions do not make possible S or RP automatically warranted.

## 7. Current judgement

The two-tier route is a valid and informative conditional bypass once its stated positive-target, domain-matched coverage, resource, selected-anchor relevance, support-trace and transport premises hold. Its strongest warranted element is antecedent preservation for a genuinely fixed realizing route, most transparently at finite supply depth. That identifies a positive local reason and the way it can support the necessary-resource inference and its power-bearer corollary. It must not be expanded into a principle for a whole arbitrary infinite capacity base whose connection to s has not been established.

The inspected accepted premises do not yet warrant the full RP package, broader COV-R, positive active-anchor restriction, or possible S. In particular, classification transport, exact witness typing and availability of merely possible auxiliary resources are substantive controls. The route therefore does not establish an actual necessary bearer in the current record. It narrows one promising affirmative strategy to independently assessable modal commitments without demanding actual exercise, global source uniqueness, or a refutation of every rival history.
