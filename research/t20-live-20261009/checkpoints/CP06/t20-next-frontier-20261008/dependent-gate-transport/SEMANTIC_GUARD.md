# Source-to-formal semantic guard

8 October 2026 UTC. Addendum to the frozen author packet; the original MANIFEST.json and its payloads remain unchanged.

Probabilistic independence of gate outcomes is different from whole-existence or original-source independence. The Joe alternative is a countermodel to a measurement inference under a relaxed stochastic response law. It is not a model of harmonious plural Necessary Beings satisfying the source's perfection premises.

The retained word "independent" in the result means independence of distinct route-pair random outcomes in the specified probability experiment. It neither asserts nor entails that a productive source depends on no foreign provision for its whole existence, power, act, or perfection. Conversely, the within-route statistical dependence does not by itself establish dependence between metaphysical originals.

The existence of a mathematical threshold distribution establishes a probability-model realization. It does not establish that any corresponding command is psychologically manipulable, physically realizable, metaphysically admissible, or compatible with complete decisive willing and unchanged productive conditions. Those are separate warrant obligations. The endpoint equivalence and minimum-compatible-count theorem cannot discharge them.

The broad dependence warning, occurrence interpretation limits, and source-admissibility limits are inherited from productive-identifiability and noise-aware-calibration. The explicit Joe construction locates a narrower statistical assumption boundary within that already qualified framework.

Research-event timestamps are milestone records only. No active-research duration, whole-paper read time, source coverage total, or novelty credit is inferred from them.
