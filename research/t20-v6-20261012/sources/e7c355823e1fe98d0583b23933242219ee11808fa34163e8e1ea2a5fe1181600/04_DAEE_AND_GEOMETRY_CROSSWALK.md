## 3. What the state and operators mean

The algebraic note uses a state of the form

```text
S = (N_space, N_sel_or_held, B_live, H, κ, Reg,
     Routes, SourceBasis, Gate, RenderGate)
```

The canonical IR is the actionable representation. It retains selected or held noetic frames, burdens, source conditions, registers, history and release permissions. It is not merely an unweighted proposition DAG and does not purport to encode all of an actual person's mind.

The operative sequence is schematically:

```text
surface discourse → Diagnostic IR → ∇ route selection
→ owned operation → Δ burden/register/dependency update
→ ∇· / ∇× field diagnostics → licensed LoopBreak when applicable
→ R(H,Δ) re-read → scoped continuation/closure
```

| Symbol or surface | Actual declared role | Invalid promotion |
|---|---|---|
| `∇` / route-gradient | Route-ranking functional over eligible actions under the current gates | Differential gradient of an identified scalar on a smooth manifold |
| `Δ` | Actual burden/register/dependency state change | Merely printing an improvement label |
| `∇·` | Post-change residual-pressure/accounting diagnostic | A physical divergence theorem or truth measure |
| `∇×` | Circularity/dependency/loop diagnostic | Literal curl or a universal differential obstruction |
| `LoopBreak` | Partial licensed transition with target, ground, delta and re-read | Permission to dismiss any inconvenient objection as a loop |
| `R(H,Δ)` | Re-evaluation of the actual changed history/dependencies | Automatic global closure or proof that no later question exists |
| `field_witness` | Machine-readable trace checked against source-owned fields | Self-authenticating truth or warrant certificate |
| `T_lang` | Bounded language-mediated coupling attempt | Guaranteed uptake, guidance or causal control of a person |

This typed operational status is enough to justify taking the system seriously while keeping its interpretations criticisable.

## 4. Registers, representation and identity

The source uses a noetic signal-state notation such as

```text
𝓝 ⊢ D₀ ⇝ Ψᴺ<N∈𝓝,m,τ,σ,♥,ξ,Ω,μ,κ,H>.
```

Surface discourse `D₀` is interpreted through an available selection space, and the selected N can remain held or underdetermined. The schema-light registers must have an operative effect on existing fields, routing, holds or rereading when live. “Schema-light” does not mean purely decorative, but it also does not imply that every additional symbol has already become a mandatory JSON field. That migration remains a separate contract decision.

The selected runtime field `Ψᴺ` differs from the diagnosed interlocutor field `Ψᴵ`. The latter is inferred from limited evidence and must remain uncertainty-bearing. Thin or compatible-with-multiple-readings discourse does not license forcing a person into one noetic family. The source explicitly withholds total identity, interior motive, sincerity, culpability, soul-state and guaranteed uptake without independent grounds.

Identity-linked commitments may stabilise a pattern or authority posture, but identity alone must not carry the verdict. These are constraints against precisely the unjustified psychological promotion that a simplistic “cognitive coordinate” reading would encourage.

Notation ownership matters. DAEE's κ dependency/history field, the geometric modelling companion κ, and an Orthemic record's exact authority structure may play related roles but are not automatically one identical datatype. DAEE's μ register and a metaortheme notation elsewhere also need their own declarations. Shared letters are not a proved interface.

## 5. Closure and the claimed termination discipline

The IR must govern dispatch before response release. A retrospectively written IR is not evidence that it actually governed the earlier operation. The initial burden set is enumerated before terminal accounting. A new burden found later is genuinely new/current-pass or next-pass material; it does not retroactively enlarge the original set to make a coverage story look complete.

The witnessed dependency graph records baseline and generated burdens, parentage, generation depth, roots, parallel groups and terminal dispositions. Every initial burden must be accounted for. A held-with-reason, carried-PARTIAL or carried-RECURSE result can satisfy honest accounting while leaving the substantive burden unresolved.

The algebraic note calls its finite-depth construction a termination proof for the MRP generation chain. Its own qualification is crucial: this is **a machine-auditable discipline for the witnessed finite graph**, not a theorem that every possible future interlocutor reply, unlimited generated burden sequence or arbitrary hosting-model execution terminates. Finiteness of each immediate menu alone would not prove global termination; the finite witnessed depth and actual graph obligations do the scoped work here.

Likewise, neutral burden divergence is linked to terminal accounting under the declared diagnostic definition. It is not an independently derived vector-calculus result or evidence that the interlocutor accepted the answer. COMPLETE collapse has stronger conditions than coverage completion and cannot be manufactured by printing STOP.

## 6. NLA and Shannon boundaries

The inspected checker explicitly distinguishes Natural Language Autoencoder-style reconstruction, ordinary algebra and Shannon communication. It requires source-owner/control effects and includes forbidden reductions such as treating entropy as truth, meaning or warrant, or treating NLA as generic linear algebra.

Reconstruction fidelity concerns whether an output retains relevant diagnostic/control structure. It is not the truth of the diagnosed worldview. Confabulation, degenerate bottlenecks, excessive expressivity and lack of mechanistic grounding remain possible failure modes. The source's checks do not establish arbitrary real neural-state recovery or intervention efficacy.

This complements Orthemology's fibre/continuation theme: a retained representation can be inadequate for the intended future question, and a fluent explanation can omit precisely the information needed to reproduce its actual decisions. But the thematic relationship is not itself a verified mathematical transport between the complete implementations.

## 8. A legitimate interface to geometry

A selected smooth subsystem could be encoded into a manifold M with metric g and objective J, while exact discrete facts and history remain in κ. A proposed correction should specify:

- what coordinates mean and which observables they preserve;
- which constraints cannot be traded away for a lower numerical score;
- which evidence/rule changes are discrete transitions rather than smooth coordinate changes;
- why the chosen objective relates to the intended correction;
- the actual regularity, step-size, feasibility and stopping assumptions.

For a genuine gradient flow, `dJ/dt = -‖grad_g J‖²_g` establishes decrease of J under the model. It does not establish source authority, sound cognition, original identity, global continuation or truth. The existing Hessian nudge supplies a concrete restricted continuous branch rather than a merely aspirational formula. It is naturally combined with discrete guards only after specifying a faithful hybrid interface.

The most useful immediate mathematical questions would be representational preservation, guard/feasibility invariance, quantitative sensitivity or a precise obstruction. A universal geometry of every noetic state is not needed before investigating a bounded case; a bounded case does not retrospectively certify all DAEE operations as geometric gradients.

## 9. Practical use for helping Dot

Use DAEE reflexively when it exposes a real issue: unearned burden, source-order mistake, role substitution, incomplete coverage, a circular objection or an unjustified closure. Permit the criticism to run in both directions. A result can show that a DAEE category, guard or framework premise needs repair.

Do not use its noetic vocabulary as a shortcut to judging the owner, an opponent or the model's interior moral state. Do not turn its restorative aim into a guarantee of behavioural steering.
