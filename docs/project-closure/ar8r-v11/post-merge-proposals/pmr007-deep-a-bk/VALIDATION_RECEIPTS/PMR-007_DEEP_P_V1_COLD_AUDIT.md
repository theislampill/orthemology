## Frozen inputs

The audit examined the V1 theorem packet, model owner, primary checker and result, Candidate E countermodels and premise defenses, the V11 proper-function typed matrix, Deep M/N/O, the Fable OSM source-verification V2, and the accessible Sun et al. source copy.

## Blocking findings

### P-F01 — “proper function” is under-typed

V1 sometimes moves between biological/etiological function, organizational function, generic designed function, and **epistemic truth-directed proper function**. TRPF-1 establishes only that neutral target-relative structure cannot identify the last predicate. It does not show that selected-effect or organizational proper function fails to exist.

**Required repair:** define distinct predicates `PF_SEL`, `PF_ORG`, `PF_DES`, `PF_EPI`, and `PF_FITRAH`; state the theorem only for `PF_EPI` or another explicitly truth/fittingness-bearing predicate.

### P-F02 — selected-effect history is not always neutral

A selected-effect account can include selection for successful world tracking. If that history explicitly encodes a truth-relevant environmental relation, it is not preserved by the target-relabeling pair.

**Required repair:** distinguish selection for installed reward/survival consequence from selection for truth-conducive performance. The latter breaks symmetry only by adding the truth/environment relation.

### P-F03 — causal role and organization may be environmentally relational

V1's neutral base presentation can be read as purely internal. Causal-role and organizational accounts can include organism-environment relations.

**Required repair:** permit arbitrary structural/environmental relations in the neutral reduct while excluding only the independently truth- or fittingness-fixing relation. The theorem is about omitted normative/semantic coordinates, not internalism.

### P-F04 — interpretation-pair closure is a real assumption

The theorem is conditional on the intended model class admitting both expansions. The executable pair does not establish that both expansions are metaphysically, biologically, or theologically possible.

**Required repair:** promote model-class closure to an explicit hard premise and classify the result as logical/model-relative underdetermination.

### P-F05 — generic design does not select a personal or wise designer

A design relation may be local, collective, derivative, finite, malicious, or merely target-relative. Truth-directed design still does not by itself establish one necessary personal ground or Wisdom.

**Required repair:** separate `DES_ANY`, `DES_TRUTH`, designer personality, designer wisdom, and transcendental ground identity.

### P-F06 — OSM source use needs a tighter ceiling

The source supports endpoint/trajectory discrimination among tested models and task-level learning. It does not measure a “complete trajectory” in the theorem's formal sense, prove CSCG uniquely in an unrestricted model universe, or investigate objective proper function.

**Required repair:** use OSM only as a bounded empirical example: final-state similarity can underdetermine learning mechanism, while measured trajectory can discriminate members of the tested class.

### P-F07 — Track N and fiṭrah need source/applicability typing

A fiṭrah-oriented function account is not supplied by a neutral relabeling theorem. It requires authenticated source, reconstruction, recipient/scope applicability, and a school-internal bridge.

**Required repair:** state those coordinates and preserve the M/T/N firewall.

### P-F08 — ancestry and significance are incomplete

The theorem's mathematical mechanism is already covered by symmetry/profile nonselection families. Its possible central value lies only in the joined proper-function adjudication, not a new theorem mechanism.

**Required repair:** classify the formal theorem as an application/compositional consequence of `AR8R-T31`, `AR8R-T55`, Candidate E, and the Deep M/N/O controls; assign zero general novelty.
