# 2. One original Creator: causation, sovereignty, agreement and explanatory scope

**Claim type:** source comparison, analytical reconstruction, and one precisely scoped mathematical bridge.  
**Main sources:** A05–A08, A10–A11, M03–M04, M12.  
**Core question:** what justifies moving from complete unreceived productive adequacy to sole actual original attribution?

## 2.1 Repair the target before assessing the objection

The relevant target is not merely `Causes(A,e) ∧ Causes(B,e)`. It includes a definite numerical effect, a definite productive respect, and the proposed sources' actual—not merely potential—original adequacy. An argument that switches among “agent,” “process,” “power,” “act,” “created product,” and “explanatory fact” can seem decisive while never preserving a single target.

For the comparison, use six distinct questions:

1. Could A produce e under appropriate conditions?
2. Would e occur if A acted and B did not?
3. Is A actually causally active in the production of e?
4. Does A provide all the productive requisites of the designated e, rather than one contribution?
5. Is A's adequacy unreceived in the relevant original respect?
6. Does A possess unrestricted original sovereignty over the whole field being investigated?

The answers need not coincide. In particular, a theorem about question 2 is not automatically a theorem about question 5. Nevertheless, evidence concerning ordinary causation may challenge a universal intermediate premise used in an original-creation argument. It cannot be dismissed solely because it is not itself a complete theology.

## 2.2 The source does not overlook harmonious agency

The Arabic discussions in *Jāmiʿ al-Masāʾil* 6:94–97, *Majmūʿ* 20:176–181 and the relevant *Darʾ* 9 passage distinguish numerical identity from sameness of kind, complete production from cooperation, and agreement from disagreement. The supplied *Oneness* translation gives a particularly accessible parallel at printed pp. 23–33. [A05–A07; I04]

The philosophical structure worth retaining is affirmative: intrinsic original power is not conferred by the peer; a necessary perfection cannot disappear merely through coexistence; requisite assistance or required forbearance is tested as a limitation of original efficacy. The consent branch is not omitted simply because it lacks overt conflict. The source considers whether consent merely conceals received completion.

It would be equally mistaken to announce that this settles the issue just because “independent” is translated as “sole.” A definition of exclusive production entails exclusivity without needing a creator argument. The substantive work is showing that the independently warranted original-agent profile entails that kind of production.

### The idle-agent branch in the agent's diagram needs correction

The diagram in the latest external packet labels a non-acting second agent idle and therefore incomplete. That inference is not valid without an additional premise. Possessing a power does not require exercising it in every instance. Voluntarily refraining, being unable, being prevented, and lacking original efficacy are distinct.

Nor is a divergent-will diagram complete if it lists only “both” and “neither.” Exactly one fulfilled will is the other familiar branch. Whether this defeats the other agent depends on the relevant standing and exercised-power assumptions. The repaired analysis must keep all three cases and must not assume contradictory willing is jointly possible simply because each contrary object is individually possible.

## 2.3 The strongest redundant-cause case is not two half-causes

Schaffer's paper defends actual redundant causes, not merely backup possibilities. Its treatment of event individuation and nonphysical cases prevents a blanket response that every example is additive material contribution. Its complete-process approach is nevertheless not an account of two absolutely unreceived perfect original bearers. [M03]

The earlier light-bulb analogy in this conversation was too loose. Depending on the circuit, both supplies may divide current, one may be disconnected, or each may supply an independently sufficient condition relative to a chosen coarse output such as “lit.” Those are different models. The analogy should not be retained as an authenticated instance of two simultaneous total productions of one finely individuated physical occurrence.

Our exact support diagnostic makes the problem visible without pretending to settle actual causation. For a Boolean output:

- `E=A OR B` has two minimal positive sufficient supports, `{A}` and `{B}`.
- `E=A AND B` has one minimal support, `{A,B}`.
- `E=A` has one support, `{A}`; B is irrelevant to this table.

These are statements about sufficiency under a specified mechanism. They do not choose an actual-cause theory or prove anything about unreceived agency.

Now expose a background condition:

`E=(A OR B) AND L`.

The minimal sufficient supports become `{A,L}` and `{B,L}`. Holding L fixed had hidden it from the earlier display. This illustrates why effect-relative sufficiency is weaker than complete original provision. It does **not** establish that L is an extra substance, a superior deity, or even an independently existing entity: it might represent a law, a receptive substrate, a constitutive condition, or simply an analysis parameter. The ontological interpretation must still be supplied.

This produces a useful requirement for a developed redundant-original rival: state what plays the role of L, and why each alleged original's actual adequacy is complete without receiving any relevant completion from it. Conversely, the affirmative side must not manufacture an L merely to force dependence. A source-neutral model should expose whatever the proposed rival actually assumes.

## 2.4 A new precision tool for necessarily concordant wills

The packet provides an elementary compatibility-graph theorem, with exhaustive finite checks and known mathematical ancestry rather than a novelty claim.

Let A's possible intrinsic determination states be a set X, B's be Y, and let `R ⊆ X×Y` say which pairs are jointly admissible. Suppose the entire designated output is determined by A alone as `f(x)` and by B alone as `g(y)`, with:

`R(x,y) → f(x)=g(y)`.

Construct the bipartite graph with X and Y as vertices and admissible pairs as edges. The common value is constant on every connected component. Hence a connected compatibility graph forces a single fixed output across the whole represented domain. Full independent recombination `R=X×Y` is sufficient, but stronger than necessary: connectedness already suffices.

A diagonal graph permits variable concordance: only `(0,0)` and `(1,1)` occur, and each side returns its own bit. No contradictory outputs occur. What the diagram does **not** explain is why those are precisely the admissible pairs.

This isolates a substantive question more precisely than “could the gods disagree?” To derive exclusion, one would need to defend a bridge from the intended original independence to enough joint admissibility, plus a nonconstant common output requirement and the two complete-determination maps. Necessary intrinsic concordance could contest the admissibility premise. Thus the theorem is a discriminator of assumptions, not a proof that modal correlation is productive dependence.

The underlying graph argument is elementary equality propagation. It is closely related to the support-component construction in Gács–Körner common information. Only bibliographic/original-author access to that ancestry is credited here; no claim of rediscovering a new information theorem is made. [M12] The philosophical use is a proposed application with its interpretation burden stated explicitly.

## 2.5 A source distinction changes what the argument is trying to prove

The supplied *Oneness* text distinguishes the familiar rubūbiyyah argument from the interpretation of Qur'an 21:22 concerning exclusive worship. Ibn al-Qayyim's *al-Dāʾ wa-l-Dawāʾ* additionally states, in the received p. 470 passage, that the verse does not simply say the heavens and earth would fail to exist: the contrast includes their corruption versus right order. [I04; A08]

This is not a peripheral translation point. Consider the following analytical form:

`Plural gods → not RightOrder`  
`RightOrder`  
`Therefore not Plural gods`.

That can be a valid argument. But replacing `RightOrder` with `Exists` changes its premise. Someone who demonstrates only that an existing cosmos is conceivable under plurality has not yet answered the order-premise argument. Someone who derives nonexistence from an order-premise has overclaimed.

“Right order” itself needs unpacking. Physical stability, integrated causal operation, teleological fittingness and rightly directed worship are distinct predicates. The source context cannot be replaced by an undifferentiated Boolean called `CoherentWorld`.

The comparison of a body governed by two equal souls, cited in *Miftāḥ*, is an analogy offered in the source tradition, not a independently validated biological theorem. The exact supplied edition/page was not freshly authenticated in this pass. It belongs in the pending quotation ledger, not in the list of proved scientific facts.

## 2.6 A creature's act and God's creation are not coequal duplicate production

The received dialogue in *Shifāʾ al-ʿAlīl* chapter 20 makes the creator/performer distinction especially clear. It also supplies a control on indiscriminate property transmission: creating a creature's action does not make the creator the performer qualified by that creature's action. [A10]

Our consequence is specific. A modelling relation must distinguish:

- original creation of the act and its received power;
- the creature's genuine performance through that power;
- the property borne by the performer;
- the resulting external product.

This is not occasionalism by default and not a ban on all real secondary agency. A model which flattens these into an untyped `Cause` relation has changed the argument before testing it.

Marmodoro is relevant here because the source discusses distinct activities and a shared causal event. The Oxford repository advertises a version-of-record file, but this session's direct archive access returned 403. The accepted-manuscript/final-publication comparison therefore remains unfinished, rather than a newly resolved access gap. [M04]

## 2.7 Present assessment and discriminating next steps

The source case is stronger than the statement that two gods might quarrel. Ordinary redundancy is stronger than two partial material contributions. Neither should be weakened to obtain an easy victory.

Three questions now deserve priority:

1. What is the full productive target, including its relevant enabling and receptive conditions?
2. Which argument converts complete unreceived adequacy into exclusive actual attribution, without defining exclusivity into the antecedent?
3. Which joint modal profiles must original independence permit, and what warrants that compossibility claim?

The graph theorem directly clarifies question 3. The support calculation clarifies question 1. The primary-source work and full-perfection argument remain central to question 2. A self-knowledge principle can expose a demonstrated shortfall in the resulting profile; it does not by itself establish that the shortfall exists.

No result in this report forces a downgrade of warranted assent to one actual common Creator, and no result establishes universal exclusion of every possible plural original. It does identify more exact propositions on which either judgement should depend.
