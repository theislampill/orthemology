# Decision 0039 — v5 Seventh–Sixteenth research successor layer

**Date:** 2026-10-05 · **Status:** proposed-candidate · **Authority:** owner-authorized repository integration into one pull request for separate review

## Problem

The Sixth registry records an accepted historical research frontier. The later
Seventh–Sixteenth inputs contain new mathematical, operational, philosophical,
source-critical, linguistic and empirical work, including later corrections to
specific earlier scopes. Changing the Sixth cutoff or treating compilation as
one general research verdict would lose both that history and the boundaries of
the new evidence.

## Decision

Add `experiments/orthemology-v5-successors/` and
`docs/provenance/v5-successors/` as a separately validated successor layer in the
same Orthemology v5 programme. Preserve both Sixth source/evidence trees exactly
at their accepted base. Keep the occurrence-centred core, verdict and notation
owners, companions, multi-actor/application branches and prior source history.
The new source index is navigation and evidence accounting, not an authoritative
theorem catalogue or an adoption of candidate operational replacements.

Bind every result to its statement, input contract, assumptions, conclusion,
limitations, exact source identities and source-specific review. Separate
custody, inherited evidence, fresh execution, research disposition,
implementation reach and external warrants. Original obligations retain their
exact owner and statement; later work changes only a matched scope through an
explicit successor relation. The original twelve research avenues remain
distinct from the older eighteen-milestone programme.

Store identical public bytes once while preserving source aliases. Suites own
their module-to-source maps and imports; unequal modules sharing a name remain
isolated. Replay descriptors bind inspected original drivers, argument meanings,
tools, dependencies, stages, budgets and controls. Fresh custom outputs and
qualified inherited dependencies are distinguished. Actual compiler rejection,
successful finite mutation detection and resource-inconclusive execution retain
their different terminal meanings. Logical proof closures exclude proof holes,
unapproved axioms and unsafe or partial dependencies.

Preserve original scientific source and attribution. Any public derivation has
an original-to-derived map and reviewed diff. Public projections exclude raw
research archives, original DOCX/PDF objects, third-party primary-text
collections, participant-level inputs, compiled objects and private execution
material. Exact archive selectors and external input locks are custody records;
they are not promises that the corresponding files exist in this checkout.
