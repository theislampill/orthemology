## 1 The actual collective mechanism pair

The source is T15's mathematical compendium, section “What final answers can identify about collective evidence use,” lines 4255–4602 and its finite/sequential sequel. Its inherited IDs are D18-T15-ANSWER-LAW, D18-T15-CAUSAL-CONTRIBUTION, D18-T15-PASSIVE-LINK, D18-T15-RECORDING-ERROR, D18-T15-FIXED-SAMPLE, D18-T15-SEQUENTIAL and D18-T15-TWO-ANSWER-LIMIT.

At a fixed stimulus x and announcer a, let

    X_B = x + sqrt(v) Z_B,    X_W = x + sqrt(kv) Z_W,
    alpha = (k-1)/(k+1),     beta = 2/(k+1),
    T_L = X_B + sqrt(r_a) Z_A,
    T_P = alpha X_B + beta X_W + sqrt(r_a) Z_A,
    Y_M = 1{T_M > 0}.

Here v>0, k>1, r_a>=0 are fixed finite parameters; the standard Gaussian noises are independent within and across trials. Because alpha+beta=1 and alpha²+k beta²=1, both scores have law N(x,v+r_a). Their complete answer sequences therefore have the same law under the same fixed or commonly exogenous whole design. L ignores X_W; P has positive sensitivity to an intervention replacing X_W by w. Equality in law is not equality of their answers under every coupling of noises.

This is stronger than equal average accuracy. It includes every answer-only statistic at the stated design. It is weaker than mechanism identity: changing the observation or intervention alphabet changes the comparison.

## 2 Exact transport through audit and adaptive inquiry

### 2.1 Faithful postprocessing cannot make the missing distinction

Let R be the entire available fixed-design answer record, including its design, and let K be any common randomized processing kernel that receives only R and common, model-independent auxiliary randomness. Its outputs may be deduplicated source stores, versioned copies, audit labels, corrected scores, selected retained records, or a whole replay transcript. Since Law_L(R)=Law_P(R),

    Law_L(K(R)) = Law_P(K(R)).

Proof: integrate the same kernel K against the same input law. This applies to arbitrarily complicated processing and is not a finite-memory theorem. If a later calibration audit brings model-dependent information not measurable from R and common independent randomness, the premise fails; the theorem then makes no equality claim. An audit of real mechanisms is allowed to be informative. A changed model-specific prior can also change posterior odds without being evidence extracted from these answers.

For positive pair priors, likelihood ratio one leaves their mutual prior odds unchanged. Both models may gain against other candidates. Unknown-parameter mixtures need matched induced response-law priors, as the older source explicitly says; pointwise parameter matching does not justify arbitrary marginal-likelihood equality.

ET-01's root bookkeeping prevents duplicate acquisitions from being counted again. It does not assert causal identification. In this example even correctly independent, genuinely new answer roots have ratio one between L and P. Conversely, distinct root IDs alone never prove the required conditional factorization. Printed source aliases, document locations, acquisition events and experimental trials must not be silently identified.

### 2.2 Adaptive fresh acquisitions remain nondiscriminating under a stated extension

The original T15 proof expressly excludes adaptive designs unless their joint laws are separately established. Here is the precise extension available at this model scope.

Before trial t choose (x_t,a_t), or stop, by the same nonanticipating policy using earlier chosen designs, recorded answers and independent private randomness. After choosing the design, draw a fresh independent Gaussian noise triple with the above fixed parameters in each model. No hidden summaries, model-dependent calibration observations, interventions, outcome-dependent recording channel or mechanism-specific access refusals are available. Include all observable policy randomness and stopping decisions in the transcript.

Then every finite adaptive transcript has the same law in L and P. Conditional on each common past and the chosen current design, both next-answer laws are Bernoulli(Phi(x_t/sqrt(v+r_a))). The policy/stop kernel is common. Induction on t therefore equates the joint transcript laws. Equality extends to measurable events of the infinite padded transcript by uniqueness of the probability law on its cylinder sigma-algebra; in particular it includes eventual stopping and any reported result.

Thus repeatedly requesting more answers, switching announcers or adaptively choosing stimuli from previous answers cannot identify which of this pair used W. This is an added conditional proof, not a claim about arbitrary endogenous human designs. A policy that directly changes X_W or observes its linked record is outside the theorem. Root revision remains honest when it reports this lack of discrimination rather than manufacturing a likelihood factor.

## 3 The positive observation and its exact inquiry value

### 3.1 One interaction really changes the observation contract

The inherited passive observation is Q=1{X_W>0} on the same trial as Y. It is assumed not to change the structural equations. A recorded Q' flips Q with probability epsilon, independently of the full model noise. For fixed design,

    Cov_L(Q',Y)=0,
    Cov_P(Q',Y)=(1-2 epsilon) Cov_P(Q,Y).

T15 proves positivity before flipping and strict separation for epsilon<1/2. It does not establish feasible passive measurement of an actual person's private state.

Use its exact illustrative specialization x=0, v=1, k=3, r_a=0. T15's angular calculation gives Pr_P(Q=1,Y=1)=5/12 and covariance 1/6. Put delta=(1-2 epsilon)/6. With rows Q'=0,1 and columns Y=0,1,

    L_delta = [[1/4, 1/4], [1/4, 1/4]],
    P_delta = [[1/4+delta, 1/4-delta],
               [1/4-delta, 1/4+delta]].

Every Q' and Y marginal is fair. Their difference is delta (1,-1) tensor (1,-1), a signed interaction of rank one when delta is nonzero, with zero one-coordinate contractions. Thus the join with T20 tensor/fibre work is literal and low-dimensional: marginal preservation loses precisely the retained mixed observable Q'Y. This is not a high-rank necessity theorem. Nor is it ET-20's four-slot truth/report calibration example; here the target is the chosen mechanism M and the observable pair is linked to one trial.

Keeping authentic Q' observations from separate trials and authentic Y observations from other independent trials also yields independent fair pairs in both models. Such separate acquisitions can be perfectly genuine yet fail to supply the needed within-trial relationship. This statement uses disjoint independent trials; it does not claim that every random shuffling of two arrays destroys all finite-array dependence. Trial identity and cross-variable linkage are part of the sufficient record. Reacquisition must recover the same relation or make a genuinely new linked trial.

### 3.2 Exact posterior and decision calculation

For this calculation only, take prior Pr(M=P)=Pr(M=L)=1/2 and squared-probability loss (q-1{M=P})². This is a stipulated mathematical decision problem, not a prior assigned to a real group. Before observing the pair the Bayes risk is 1/4. Answers alone leave it 1/4.

On a concordant pair Q'=Y, the posterior of P is

    q_same = (1/4+delta)/(1/2+delta).

On a discordant pair it is

    q_diff = (1/4-delta)/(1/2-delta).

The prior-predictive probabilities of concordance and discordance are 1/2+delta and 1/2-delta. The expected reduction in squared-probability risk is the variance of the posterior:

    V(delta) = (1/2+delta)(q_same-1/2)^2
             + (1/2-delta)(q_diff-1/2)^2
             = delta²/(1-4 delta²).

At epsilon=0, delta=1/6: the two posteriors are 5/8 and 1/4; the expected risk is 7/32 and V=1/32. At epsilon=1/4, delta=1/12: the posteriors are 4/7 and 2/5 and V=1/140. At epsilon=1/2 the independent fair recording channel erases the distinction and V=0. For a constant additional acquisition price c=1/64 in these same loss units, linked inquiry is better than skipping at epsilon=0 and worse at epsilon=1/4. Values for a half-scaled Brier loss would be half these values; no unit conversion is implicit.

This is a concrete older/current bridge to ET-07 and ET-17: a law that preserves all final answers is sufficient for the old answer objective yet insufficient for the proposed mechanism inquiry; the newly justified linked observation and its calibrated predictive weights determine whether acquiring it is worth its cost. It does not claim that the sensor exists, the price is realistic, the pair is exhaustive, or improved model score supplies authority to act.

### 3.3 Recalibration changes the same factor rather than adding a root

For one observed concordant pair, its likelihood ratio P/L is 1+4 delta. For a discordant pair it is 1-4 delta. Revising epsilon changes delta and replaces that root's factor. With a concordant pair and an epsilon revision 0 to 1/4, correct equal-prior posterior changes from 5/8 to 4/7. Treating the revised factor as a second independent acquisition instead gives odds (5/3)(4/3)=20/9 and posterior 20/29, which is a different and unjustified result. A true second independent concordant linked trial at the revised calibration would justify that product; renaming or rereading the first would not.

This exactly instantiates ET-01's acquisition distinction and ET-03/05's calibration/version frame, with an old substantive collective mechanism rather than a generic likelihood table. If calibration itself is learned through a report-dependent audit, use the warranted joint observation/audit law; replacing epsilon by a supplied exact value here assumes that calibration value is legitimately supplied. With uncertain common calibration, keep its model index coupled across trials rather than freely choosing a separate worst endpoint for each branch.

### 4.2 The exact effective boundary

Enumerate partial computable functions phi_e. At depth n let A_n contain e<n for which phi_e(e) halts within n steps with output 0, and B_n those halting with output 1. A length-n history sigma is admitted when sigma(e)=1 on A_n and sigma(e)=0 on B_n. This defines a decidable prefix-closed binary tree T. The total planner H(n)(e)=1{e in A_n} supplies a valid plan at every finite horizon. The limit chi_A is a mathematical infinite path. Any computable path f, however, appears as phi_k and must disagree with phi_k(k)=f(k), contradiction.

For each e, the padded H(n)(e) changes at most once and eventually stabilizes to chi_A(e). Nevertheless there is no total computable stabilization modulus m(e). Otherwise evaluating H(max(m(e),e+1))(e) computes an infinite path. There is also no computable unbounded sequence n_j of prefix-coherent H(n_j), since its coherent union would be a computable path. These are the actual inherited Proposition 5, not a finite-memory argument.

### 4.3 The failed transport is from recomputation to commitment

ET-14 preserves historical reconstruction, retrospective recomputation and prospective correction as different operations. Applied to a finite T16 horizon, a fully current dependency computation can correctly return H(n); there is no obstruction to that finite recomputation. Recompute at horizon n+1 and the candidate earlier choices may change. Even if every coordinate changes at most once and all recalculations are exact, their pointwise convergence does not supply a computable time when an earlier choice may safely be made irrevocable.

Hence the following proposed import is invalid at this actual older source: “exact current replay for every requested finite horizon, plus convergent successive corrected plans, supplies one executable indefinitely continuing lineage.” The T16 H(n) is an explicit counterexample. Replay correctness at a supplied horizon is retained. What fails is exchanging the horizon-indexed quantifier with a single committed supported policy.

A revision of an earlier candidate prefix is not the already executed causal past being revised. Rollback, restart or a branching population may be legitimate under a different declared contract, but must count the actual support, lineage and permissions they require. Neither source-root deletion nor a historical numerical reweighting authorizes rebuilding an earlier physical history. No failure of T20's existing replay theorem is alleged.

T16's strengthened tree L_h additionally requires each prefix to have an admitted T-extension of the supplied total computable finite length h(prefix). It remains decidable, has plans at every finite horizon and has exactly T's infinite paths, hence no computable committed infinite lineage. Each lookahead witness concerns T, not a recursively certified indefinitely viable L_h region. Arbitrarily large effective finite lookahead therefore does not repair the extrapolation.

### 4.4 The positive transport condition

For a decidable rooted binary admission tree, T16 proves equivalence of a computable path and a rooted, prefix-closed, pruned computably enumerable subtree R. Given a warranted enumerator for R, restart its enumeration at each current prefix, or retain all earlier emissions, and wait until either child of that prefix is available; choose that child. Restarting or retaining earlier emissions prevents missing a child emitted before its parent was selected. Pruning makes this search finite at each step; soundness keeps the next prefix inside the same continuing region.

This is the appropriate constructive join to T20 continuation contracts. A finite invariant with a computable closed next-choice rule is one special case. It is not necessary that all viable histories be enumerated or that an arbitrary supplied code's pruning promise be decidable. In the diagonal example the full indefinitely viable set is co-c.e. but not c.e.; the missing positive region cannot be extracted just by accumulating finite successes.

The theorem does not require finite physical memory and does not refute finite source-controller realizations. It does not derandomize external stochastic environments, turn a fixed-tree result into a CIRS statement, or provide a physical support-independent continuation guarantee. The source's broader randomised and population theorems retain their separate hypotheses and written/formal status; they are not rerun or needed for this bridge.
