## 2. Typed setting

Let `M` be a finite declared model or architecture class, `E:M→Obs` the frozen
complete profile, and `Q:M→Target` the unresolved target. A proposed operation
has an availability indicator `A:M→{0,1}` and, when `A(m)=1`, an outcome
`Y(m)`. Its **totalized signal** is

```text
X(m) = (A(m), Y(m))       if A(m)=1;
X(m) = (0, bottom)        if A(m)=0.
```

The complete extended profile is `T(m)=(E(m),X(m))`.

The mathematical map is not yet an eligible experiment. Eligibility separately
requires target-independent typing, common abstract semantics, version and
authorization custody, resource eligibility, implementation-map preservation,
and an explicit missingness/nonimplementation observation contract.

## 3. PCDF-1A — restricted-domain evidence does not become a global comparison

Suppose the operation is observed only on its domain
`D={m:A(m)=1}` and nonimplementation outside `D` is not itself an observed
variable. Then the experiment supplies a profile only on `D`. It can establish
at most a statement about `Q|D`.

For every proper nonempty finite `D⊊M` and target set with at least two values,
there are two target assignments that agree on `D` and differ on `M\D`.
Therefore restricted-domain evidence alone does not determine the global target.

This is a domain-of-definition result, not a claim that observed
nonimplementation is always irrelevant.

## 4. PCDF-1B — bottom-totalization is a new complete experiment

Once availability or nonimplementation is recorded, `X` is an additional
observed coordinate on all of `M`. The exact identification criterion is the
ordinary fibre condition:

```text
Q is identifiable from T
iff
T(m)=T(m') implies Q(m)=Q(m').
```

Thus a proper implementation domain can mathematically identify `Q`; V1's
counterexample takes `A=Q`. The scientific question is whether the availability
coordinate has an independent, target-blind operational meaning.

## 5. PCDF-1C — common-postprocessing barrier

If the totalized signal factors through the old profile, `X=g∘E`, then

```text
ker(T) = ker(E).
```

Indeed, equality of `E` forces equality of `X`, and `T` contains `E` as a
coordinate. Therefore bottom-totalization cannot repair an existing `E`-fibre
conflict when availability and realized outcomes are both determined by `E`.

Conversely, whenever `E` fails to identify `Q` but `T` identifies it, `X` must
be nonconstant on at least one conflicting `E` fibre. The successful coordinate
is therefore genuinely new information relative to `E`; it is not a free
normalization, quotient, relabeling, or correction of the old profile.

## 6. PCDF-1D — noncircular capability fork

A separating partial operation has exactly three evidential dispositions.

### Branch 1 — restricted-domain only

No total availability observation is made. The conclusion is restricted to the
implementing subclass; the original architecture class remains unresolved.

### Branch 2 — independently certified totalization

A common target-blind test establishes availability/nonimplementation and the
same abstract semantics across the compared architectures. The availability
certificate is then a **new experiment or anchor**. It may discriminate by the
fibre criterion, but must pass the same typing, version, authorization,
resource, source/world, and implementation checks as any other anchor.

### Branch 3 — target-defined totalization

Availability or outcome is defined by personality, intentional uptake,
Wisdom, the architecture label, or another target-equivalent predicate. The
resulting profile may separate perfectly, but the separation is target leakage.
No neutral architecture inference follows.

The formal theorem cannot decide whether a real capability certificate is
independent. That is an implementation, empirical, source, and world-level
burden.

## 7. Current U/I/P application

The frozen unified-personal `U`, one-bearer impersonal-powers `I`, and plural
`P` architectures share the registered neutral profile.


Accordingly, the absence of a currently common intentional-uptake actuator does
not itself prove that `I` lacks intentional uptake. Treating that absence as an
outcome either narrows the domain, adds a new independently certified
capability measurement, or leaks the target.

## 8. Cross-lane effects

### Proper function and Wisdom

A function-sensitive or fittingness-sensitive operation can be total at the
causal-role level while partial at the intentional or because-of-fittingness
level. The richer semantic operation cannot be smuggled into the common
experiment by reusing the same label.

### Transcendental and source ascent

A source-relative predicate or qiyās operation is not a neutral common
intervention unless the source, meaning, referent, truth, applicability, and
recipient coordinates are independently implemented for every compared
architecture. Source inapplicability is not automatically a negative neutral
outcome.

### Speech and grammar

The availability of tokens, syntax, or semantic articulation is a common proxy
surface. Speaker intention, speech capacity, occurrent Speech, Name
authorization, revealed wording, recipient warrant, and execution have distinct
domains and may not be totalized into one `speech=yes/no` bit.

### TAC/SAC, communication, and provenance

Missing receipt, unavailable capability, stale version, absent authorization,
and negative acknowledgment are different observations. Coding all of them as
`bottom` destroys the semantic contract and can manufacture apparent
architecture separation.

### Empirical representation and restoration

Architecture-dependent missingness is analogous to an overlap/positivity or
MNAR problem: absence can be informative, but only under a frozen observation
and selection model. Endpoint absence does not identify a latent restorative or
personal state by itself.
