## 2. Declared polynomial operators and actual protocols

Let d,m≥1, P=R[x_1,…,x_d], and let F_1,…,F_m∈P be fixed polynomial objectives. Put

    B_i p = −∇F_i·∇p − F_i p.

Each B_i is a real-linear endomorphism of P. It is not generally a derivation of the commutative polynomial algebra: B_i(1)=−F_i. Let A be the unital associative subalgebra of End_R(P) generated by the B_i. Operator multiplication means (BC)p=B(Cp).

Let u:[0,T]→R^m be piecewise constant with finitely many switches. For a real parameter ε near zero, evolve

    x′(s)=−ε Σ_i u_i(s) ∇F_i(x(s)),
    a′(s)=−ε Σ_i u_i(s) F_i(x(s)) a(s),  a(0)=1.

The initial law μ is compactly supported. Fix a sufficiently small common ε interval such that all characteristics from its support remain in one compact set through T. Ordinary local ODE existence provides such an interval for these finitely many smooth polynomial fields and a fixed finite control. The weights a remain strictly positive for actual trajectories. Define

    U_u^ε p(x)=a(T,x) p(x(T,x)),
    N_u^ε(μ)(p)=μ(U_u^ε p)/μ(U_u^ε 1).

No global existence, coercivity, actual source likelihood, survival probability, or warrant is inferred from this local setup. If u selects one objective at a time with nonnegative duration, it is the inherited forward characteristic correction; ε may equivalently scale all durations. Intermediate normalization cancels exactly. A chronological F then G protocol has observable product U_F U_G, not U_G U_F. Signed coefficients below are algebraic tools; they do not establish availability of inverse-time interventions.

## 3. Exact finite-jet tensor transport

Declare E=R^m with basis e_1,…,e_m and let T(E)=⊕_{r≥0}E^{⊗r} with concatenation. The linear assignment e_i↦B_i uniquely gives a unital algebra map

    ρ:T(E)→A,      ρ(e_i1…e_ir)=B_i1…B_ir.                 (1)

This is the inherited universal property applied to an actual specified map. It does not assert injectivity: equal objectives, commuting operators, and other operator identities can create a kernel.

There is also an exact weight-coordinate lift into the usual vector-field setting. On P[z], put ∼B_i=−∇F_i·∇_x−F_i z∂_z and ι(p)=zp. Then ∼B_i ι(p)=ι(B_i p), and by induction ∼B_w ι=ι B_w for every word. The homogeneous-z-degree-one subspace is invariant. This is an injective **linear** intertwiner, not a homomorphism of the polynomial multiplication algebras (ι(p)ι(q)=z²pq). Thus the killing term is actually carried by a supplied extra coordinate, rather than silently treating B_i itself as a derivation or importing a drift-only signature formula.

For N≥0 set R_N=R[ε]/(ε^{N+1}) and

    T_≤N(E)=T(E)/(⊕_{r>N}E^{⊗r}).

The quotient has truncated concatenation. As a vector space it is the finite direct sum of grades zero through N; for N≥1 it is generally **not** a subalgebra of the inherited T(E) with its original multiplication. There is an exact unital graded algebra map

    ρ_N:T_≤N(E)→A⊗R_N,
    e_i1…e_ir ↦ ε^r B_i1…B_ir.                            (2)

Here the target grade is formal ε degree. It is not polynomial-state degree, differential order, an audit level, or tensor rank. Products beyond degree N vanish on both sides, which proves the map is well-defined and multiplicative. For N=0 both the retained control and its action are just the unit.

For a word w=i_1…i_r define its chronological coefficient

    s_w(u)=∫_{0<t_1<…<t_r<T} u_i1(t_1)…u_ir(t_r) dt_1…dt_r,
    s_empty=1,
    S_N(u)=Σ_{|w|≤N} s_w(u)e_w.                           (3)

Splitting the ordered integration simplex at a concatenation time proves

    S_N(u followed by v)=S_N(u) S_N(v).                   (4)

For a constant control v of duration h, its contribution is the finite exponential Σ_{r=0}^N (h v)^{⊗r}/r!. Thus a finite switched protocol is an exact product of these finite exponentials in T_≤N(E). These are the standard Chen concatenation coefficients, with chronological letters ordered as in the inherited observable products. Some sources reverse their letter convention; equations (1)–(4), not an unchecked naming convention, fix ours.

### Theorem 1 — Actual response has the represented finite jet

For each N and p∈P,

    U_u^ε p = Σ_{|w|≤N} ε^{|w|} s_w(u) B_w p + O(ε^{N+1}) (5)

uniformly on the initial compact support. Consequently the degree-N jet of N_u^ε(μ)(p) is exactly

    J_N(u,μ;p)= μ(ρ_N(S_N(u))p) / μ(ρ_N(S_N(u))1) ∈ R_N.  (6)

The denominator has constant term 1 and therefore has a unique formal inverse.

**Proof.** Along each interval, the weighted pullback satisfies U_t′p=ε U_t(B_{u(t)}p), with B_{u(t)}=Σ_i u_i(t)B_i and U_0=I. Integrate this identity, substitute it recursively N+1 times, and split at switch times if necessary. The terms with r substitutions are precisely the ordered integrals in (5). The remainder is an integral with N+1 controls and a remaining weighted pullback applied to a finite word B_w p. There are only finitely many words of this length. Their polynomial values and the weights are uniformly bounded on the stipulated characteristic compact set, and the finite control has finite L1 norm. The remainder is bounded by a constant times |ε|^{N+1}. Integrating against μ preserves this bound. Its denominator tends to μ(1)=1, so actual division near zero and formal truncated division have the same jet. This proves (5)–(6). □

No infinite operator series is evaluated. An entire signature belongs naturally to a graded product/completion; it is not inserted into the inherited algebraic direct sum. The theorem claims a finite-order expansion with a local remainder, not universal convergence or exact finite-degree closure at a fixed nonzero duration.

For a polynomial output p of degree r, let D=max_i deg(F_i), assigning degree zero to constant/zero objectives for this bound. Since deg(B_i p)≤deg(p)+D, only initial polynomial moments through degree r+ND are needed for the numerator in (6), and through ND for its denominator. Those are symmetric state moments, paired with **ordered** control coefficients. This explicit bound is sufficient rather than optimal; it does not assert that a fixed finite moment state closes all future times or all outputs.

## 4. Projective faithfulness of normalized operator jets

The next theorem compares represented operator jets, not raw tensor words. It is algebraic and does not assume that arbitrary endomorphism jets preserve positivity or are physically realizable.

Let

    G_N={I+εA_1+…+ε^N A_N : A_j∈A} ⊂ (A⊗R_N)^×,
    Z_N={c(ε)I : c∈R_N, c(0)=1}.

The inverse in G_N is the finite geometric-series inverse. Z_N is a central subgroup. For a real normalized linear functional L on P, extend L coefficientwise to P⊗R_N, and put

    N_U(L)(p)=L(Up)/L(U1).                               (7)

The same formula acts on formal normalized functionals L with L(1)=1. Formal composition gives

    N_V(N_U(L))=N_{UV}(L).                               (8)

Thus this is a right action in chronological order. There is no claim that every formal element acts on actual probability measures.

### Lemma 2 — Only scalar operators are invisible to every law

For D∈End_R(P), define

    V_D(μ)(p)=μ(Dp)−μ(D1) μ(p).

If V_D(μ)(p)=0 for every finitely supported probability μ on R^d and every p∈P, then D=cI for one c∈R. Conversely every scalar operator has this property.

**Proof.** Put a=D1∈P. A singleton law δ_x gives (Dp)(x)=a(x)p(x) for every x and p. Polynomial equality therefore gives Dp=ap. For μ=(δ_x+δ_y)/2 the identity becomes

    (a(x)−a(y))(p(x)−p(y))/4=0.

Whenever x≠y, a linear polynomial separates x and y. Hence a(x)=a(y) for all x,y, so a is constant and D=cI. The converse is immediate. □

Diracs alone are insufficient: every nonconstant multiplication operator a(x)I is invisible on each singleton but changes a suitable two-point mixture. The all-laws quantifier is consequential, not cosmetic.

### Theorem 3 — Exact normalized equivalence is scalar projective equivalence

For U,V∈G_N, these conditions are equivalent:

(a) N_U(μ)(p)=N_V(μ)(p) in R_N for every finitely supported probability μ and every p∈P;

(b) U=c(ε)V in A⊗R_N for a unique c∈R_N with c(0)=1.

**Proof.** (b) implies (a) by cancellation. For the converse use induction on degree. Initially both constant terms are I. Suppose c has been chosen so that U−cV has no terms below degree k. Write U−cV=ε^kD+O(ε^{k+1}), where O here means algebraic divisibility. Since scalar multiplication of V has no normalized effect, the degree-k coefficient of N_U(μ)(p)−N_{cV}(μ)(p) is

    μ(Dp)−μ(D1)μ(p).

It vanishes by (a), so Lemma 2 gives D=d_k I. Replacing c by c+d_k ε^k eliminates that coefficient because V(0)=I, without changing the lower coefficients. Continue through k=N. Uniqueness follows by multiplying U=cV by the formal inverse of V. □

Thus G_N/Z_N acts faithfully on the family of normalized formal readouts, with real finitely supported inputs already separating the classes. For control tensors, the exact criterion is

    J_N(u,μ;p)=J_N(v,μ;p) for every μ,p
    iff ρ_N(S_N(u)) = c(ε) ρ_N(S_N(v)).                  (9)

This allows both the representation's operator relations and scalar normalization. It does not claim that the control signature itself is recovered or that the original histories coincide. On a restricted law family, or for only selected outputs, equivalence may be strictly coarser. For d=0 the polynomial algebra is R and every endomorphism is scalar, so the normalized action is trivial; the meaningful separation claim concerns d≥1 (or a separately specified function algebra separating a domain with at least two points).

## 5. Why normalization has a Lie quotient but no unit-killing associative quotient

On the affine space of algebraic functionals L with L(1)=1, let l_p(L)=L(p). The formula

    D_B l_p = l_{Bp} − l_{B1} l_p

defines a derivation of the algebra of polynomial cylindrical functions by the Leibniz rule. Each displayed expression uses only finitely many evaluations, so no topology or infinite-coordinate differentiability is being assumed. Direct application to the generators l_p gives

    [D_B,D_C]=D_[B,C],       [B,C]=BC−CB,                (10)
    D_(cI)=0.

For completeness, expanding D_BD_C l_p produces

    l_BCp − l_B1 l_Cp − l_BC1 l_p − l_C1 l_Bp
      + 2 l_B1 l_C1 l_p.

Subtract the expression with B,C interchanged; all cross terms cancel, leaving l_[B,C]p−l_[B,C]1 l_p. Equality on the generators proves (10) everywhere. Lemma 2 says that the kernel, even when tested only on all actual finitely supported laws, is precisely R I. Thus A/(R I) has a faithful Lie action in this sense.

By contrast, R I is not a two-sided associative ideal when A contains a nonscalar B: BI=B∉R I. Any associative-algebra quotient in which I becomes zero is the zero algebra, since B=BI. Hence there is no nonzero associative representation of the same unital operator algebra that implements the generator gauge by setting I=0. More concretely, choosing B+cI instead of B changes the product (B+cI)C by cC, generally a non-scalar operator. The vector-space quotient A/(R I) therefore has no induced associative multiplication.

This does not undermine associative chronological composition. Positive weighted deterministic flow operators with the required common domains have well-defined projective **multiplicative** composition; the always-defined formal near-identity version is G_N/Z_N above. For general positive kernels, normalization is restricted to nonvanishing branch mass, and products of nonzero kernels can be zero. Scalar shifts of infinitesimal generators and scalar multiples of complete propagators are different operations. Lie quotients handle the former; projective group/semigroup composition handles the latter. The normalized moment vector field is nonlinear in L even though the unnormalized operator representation is linear.

## 6. A degree-three discriminator with only positive forward stages

Take d=1, a≠0,

    F(x)=x²/2,       G(x)=(x−a)²/2,
    A=B_F,          B=B_G.

Both objectives are coercive, and their gradient flows are complete forward in time. Consider two chronological protocols, each of total duration 2t and equal total time t under each objective:

    P: F for t/2, then G for t, then F for t/2,
    Q: G for t/2, then F for t, then G for t/2.

Their control signatures through degree two are both

    1+t(e_F+e_G)+t²(e_F+e_G)²/2.

Expanding the three finite exponentials in the free associative algebra gives

    U_P−U_Q=−t³ [A+B,[A,B]]/8+O(t⁴).                    (11)

This identity is a standard low-order symmetric-splitting calculation, used here as a discriminating control, not a new splitting theorem. The inherited commutator gives [A,B]=a∂_x. Computing one further bracket yields

    [A+B,[A,B]]=2a∂_x+(2ax−a²)I,
    D:=−[A+B,[A,B]]/8=−a∂_x/4−ax I/4+a² I/8.          (12)

The last constant term vanishes under normalized readout. The nonconstant multiplication term does not. Since the two operator jets agree below degree three, normalization gives, for every compactly supported μ and polynomial p,

    N_P(μ)(p)−N_Q(μ)(p)
      =−a t³ [ μ(p′)+Cov_μ(x,p) ]/4+O(t⁴).             (13)

In particular, for p=x the coefficient is −a(1+Var_μ(x))/4. A singleton has coefficient −a/4. The law (δ_{−1}+δ_1)/2 has coefficient −a/2. The extra term is exactly the higher-order relative-weight effect that singleton tests miss; it is not a change of sign convention or a forbidden negative-duration commutator experiment.

### Exact characteristic check, beyond formal expansion

For the objective (x−c)²/2 and a stage with contraction r=e^{−s}, the state and log weight update are

    y↦c+r(y−c),
    k↦k−(y−c)²(1−r²)/4.

Put q=e^{−t/2}; write y_P(x),y_Q(x) for the terminal points, and w_P(x),w_Q(x)>0 for the full characteristic weights. Direct composition gives

    y_P(x)−y_Q(x)=a(q−1)³(q+1),                          (14)
    log(w_P(x)/w_Q(x))
      = a(2x−a)(q−1)³(q+1)(q⁴+1)/4.                   (15)

For t>0, (14) is nonzero, and (15) is a nonconstant function of x. Thus no common scalar multiplier relates these weights on all input points. For the two atoms ±1, the difference of their log weight odds between P and Q is

    a(q−1)³(q+1)(q⁴+1),                                (16)

which is nonzero for every t>0. The t³ coefficients in (14)–(16) are respectively −a/4, a²/8−ax/4, and −a/2, agreeing with (12)–(13). Terminal atoms are identified by their initial labels in this odds statement. Formula (13) concerns their actual normalized polynomial output, including both moved positions and changed weights.

Consequently degree-two control tensors are not sufficient for exact finite-duration continuation in this admissible example. This is a failure of the specified truncated control representation, not a lower bound for arbitrary state encodings and not a contradiction of finite known-support pure-tilt closure.

## 7. Readout boundary and compatibility with inherited positive results

Adding a constant c to F changes B_F to B_F−cI while leaving its gradient field unchanged. Exactly,

    U_(F+c)(t)=e^{−ct}U_F(t),
    N_(F+c)(μ)=N_F(μ).

The unnormalized mass μ(U1) is multiplied by e^{−ct}. Therefore the projective quotient is valid for conditional-state readout alone. If total mass is part of the contract, it must be retained. If separately justified probabilistic semantics makes it an actual survival/branch probability, that probability also changes.
