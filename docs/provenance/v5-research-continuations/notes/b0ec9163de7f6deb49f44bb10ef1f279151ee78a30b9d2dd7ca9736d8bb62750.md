# Smooth common-repair classification from finite truth dependencies

Status: exact ordinary theorem and proof, with the general finite-hull smooth necessity bridge separately kernel-checked in lean/FiniteHullSmoothRigidity.lean. The diagonal-stabilizer/component theorem and the connectedness/integration classification below are ordinary mathematics, not a Lean matroid-stabilizer certificate. Independent block review is in progress. No theorem-priority or canonical-adoption claim.

## 1. Setup and precise theorem

Let V be a nonempty finite family of truth vectors in [0,1]^n and C=conv(V). Let

    T=span(C−C), U=relint(C),
    l_i=min_(v in V) v_i, u_i=max_(v in V) v_i.

Initially assume every coordinate varies, so l_i<u_i. Consequently p_i lies in I_i=(l_i,u_i)⊂(0,1) for every p∈U. This permits endpoints 0 and 1 in V while keeping all tested baselines interior to the generator domain.

A nonzero vector of T is elementary when its support contains no strictly smaller support of a nonzero vector in T. Connect coordinates that occur together in an elementary support, and let B range over the resulting connected components. Let

    T_B={x in T: x_i=0 for i outside B}.

Elementary vectors span T, giving the coordinate-supported direct sum T=⊕_B T_B. A singleton component is a free coordinate under the present nonconstant-coordinate assumption. Call the other components constrained. Assume

    dim(T_B)>=2 for every constrained component B.

For each coordinate let f_i,g_i be finite continuous functions on [0,1], twice continuously differentiable on (0,1), with strictly positive second derivatives. Keep these same generator families fixed across every p and perturbation. Define F=sum_i f_i and G=sum_i g_i, using forecast-second Bregman losses.

The following conditions are equivalent:

(A) For every p∈U and every transverse direction h∉T, there are e_k>0 with e_k->0 and common coherent interior repairs c_k∈C∩(0,1)^n such that, for every truth vector v∈V,

    D_F(v,c_k)<=D_F(v,p+e_k h),
    D_G(v,c_k)<=D_G(v,p+e_k h).

(A_eta) The same requirement with nonnegative additive tolerances eta_k on both comparisons and eta_k/e_k->0.

(B) For each constrained component B there is one a_B>0 and constants b_i,d_i for i∈B such that

    f_i(x)=a_B g_i(x)+b_i x+d_i,
    for every i∈B and x∈[l_i,u_i].

There is no proportionality requirement on free singleton coordinates. No equality is claimed outside a coordinate's attained interval. Different constrained components may have different positive constants.

Moreover (B) supplies common STRICT repairs for every sufficiently small positive perturbation in each fixed transverse direction. The quantification is over coherent endpoint repairs. The codimension-one nudge equivalence is not imported into this general-hull theorem.

## 2. General smooth necessity, including sublinear tolerance

At a fixed p∈U, choose strictly positive barycentric weights w_v summing to one with p=sum_v w_v v. Every relative-interior point of a finite hull admits such a certificate. In the Lean bridge the certificate is an explicit input; no literal intrinsicInterior wrapper is silently claimed.

For common approximate repairs, weighted averaging yields

    D_F(p,c_k)<=D_F(p,p+e_k h)+eta_k ->0.

The coordinate divergence localization argument gives c_k->p even when endpoint derivatives are unbounded. Barycentric averaging alone would supply only a square-root error bound. The stronger all-truth-state argument is essential: on T, the positive part of the statewise first derivative family

    x -> sum_v max((p−v)^T Hess F(p) x,0)

is coercive. A nonzero tangent vector cannot make every term zero or nonpositive: the positive barycentric sum is zero, so all terms would vanish, making that vector Hessian-orthogonal to itself. Compactness of the tangent unit sphere supplies a uniform positive coercivity constant.

The actual statewise inequalities, the O(e_k) baseline variation, and a uniformly small differentiability remainder then give

    ||c_k−p||=O(e_k+eta_k)=O(e_k).

Normalize and extract a convergent subsequence (c_k−p)/e_k->z∈T. Dividing the score comparisons by e_k and using eta_k/e_k->0 yields nonnegative first-order gains at every truth vector. Positive weighted zero forcing makes each gain zero. Therefore

    Hess F(p)(h−z) ⟂ T,
    Hess G(p)(h−z) ⟂ T.

Thus the two positive-metric tangent projections agree on h. Since they already agree on T as the identity and (A_eta) tests every transverse direction, the projection operators agree everywhere.

This bridge is the kernel-checked part of the generalization: finite_hull_approximate_hessian_necessary, its exact specialization, and the derived normalized-repair bound. Truth-vector indices and coordinate indices are distinct types in the formal statement. Nothing assumes a categorical simplex, a coordinate-sum coherence rule, pre-established projection compatibility, or an O(e_k) repair estimate.

## 3. From equal projections to dependency-component ratios

Put H_F=diag(f_i''(p_i)), H_G=diag(g_i''(p_i)). Equality of their tangent projections is equivalent to the diagonal ratio map H_G^{-1}H_F preserving T. Inversion of the ratio makes no difference to preservation.

The classical diagonal-stabilizer result says that a diagonal map preserves T exactly when its entries are constant on every elementary-support component. The parent note Dependency-block norm rigidity supplies an ordinary proof via elementary vectors and diagonal spectral projectors, exact rational controls, and primary ancestry in Denham's Proposition 2.12. That result is not kernel checked in this tranche.

It follows that, at each p∈U,

    r_i(p_i):=f_i''(p_i)/g_i''(p_i)=a_B(p), for all i∈B.

So far a_B may depend on p. That is the precise remaining step which separability and rank at least two resolve.

## 4. Why a rank-two-or-higher component has one global constant

Fix a constrained component B. Since dim(T_B)>=2 and coordinate functionals separate its vectors, two restricted coordinate functionals x↦x_i and x↦x_j are linearly independent on T_B. Otherwise all nonzero coordinate functionals would be proportional and T_B would have dimension at most one.

The map T_B→R² taking x to (x_i,x_j) is therefore surjective. For any p∈U, a sufficiently small relative-open ball around p lies in U; its image contains an open rectangle around (p_i,p_j). Every pair (s,t) in that rectangle is realized by a coherent q near p. The component-ratio equality gives

    r_i(s)=r_j(t)

throughout the rectangle. Fixing t shows r_i is constant on the first interval; fixing s shows r_j is constant on the second. Consequently a_B(q)=r_i(q_i) is constant throughout a neighborhood of p in U.

Thus a_B is locally constant on U. The set U is convex and connected, so a_B is one constant a_B>0 throughout U. The coordinate image of U is precisely I_i=(l_i,u_i): the image is a nonempty open convex interval with closure [l_i,u_i]. Hence

    f_i''(x)=a_B g_i''(x), for every x∈I_i.

Integrating twice gives f_i=a_B g_i+b_i x+d_i on I_i. Continuity extends the identity to [l_i,u_i]. This proves (A_eta)->(B). Exact (A) is the eta=0 specialization.

The proof also rules out hidden dependence of the block constant on other components: local constancy holds on a full relative neighborhood in U, not only along a fiber with the other blocks held fixed. Connectedness then connects all such neighborhoods.

## 5. Constructive converse without assuming a product coherence set

Assume (B). Fix p∈U and b_e=p+e h with h∉T, for sufficiently small positive e so that b_e∈(0,1)^n and all varying coordinates stay in their attained open intervals I_i.

Let c_e minimize D_G(c,b_e) over the compact convex set C. Existence follows from continuity in the first Bregman argument. As b_e->p, these objectives converge uniformly on C to D_G(c,p), whose unique minimum is p by strict convexity. Hence c_e->p. In particular c_e is eventually in U and all its coordinates are interior.

Because c_e is relatively interior, every sufficiently short tangent displacement in either direction remains feasible. First-order optimality therefore gives

    (grad G(c_e)−grad G(b_e)) · t=0, for all t∈T.

On a constrained block, the affine generator identity implies

    [grad F(c_e)−grad F(b_e)]_B
      =a_B [grad G(c_e)−grad G(b_e)]_B.

Each T_B lies in T, so this rescaled residual still annihilates T_B. On a free singleton i, e_i∈T and the G normal equation gives g_i'(c_e,i)=g_i'(b_e,i). Since g_i''>0, the derivative is injective; c_e,i=b_e,i, and the F residual there is zero too. Summing over the direct-sum blocks gives

    (grad F(c_e)−grad F(b_e)) ⟂ T.

For every v∈C, the Bregman three-point identity now yields

    D_F(v,b_e)−D_F(v,c_e)=D_F(c_e,b_e)>0,
    D_G(v,b_e)−D_G(v,c_e)=D_G(c_e,b_e)>0.

Strictness holds because b_e lies outside the affine hull of C and therefore differs from c_e. This proves (B)->(A), and therefore all three conditions are equivalent.

Only the tangent space decomposes as a coordinate direct sum. C itself need not be a Cartesian product; the argument uses one genuine minimizer over C and local relative interior. It never assembles globally infeasible blockwise candidates.

## 6. Sharp scope and what is not classified

- Nontrivial rank-one components remain exceptional. The complementary binary problem and any faithful duplicated encoding of that problem have tangent rank one, irrespective of the number of encoded coordinates. The earlier quadratic/binary-log common-repair example shows that a blockwise affine conclusion is not necessary there
- Matching Hessian projection fields in a rank-one component should not be assumed sufficient for nonlinear common endpoint repair. The independent block reviewer is testing this stronger distinction separately
- Constant-coordinate singleton components were excluded from the initial statement. Their tangent coordinate is zero, so the normal-equation argument imposes no ratio constraint. They can be added with a separate convention for the degenerate attained interval; no affine-function classification on a nonempty open interval is available there
- The boundary of C needs tangent-cone analysis. Neither relative-interior proof nor free-coordinate reasoning should be transferred to a face without a face-preservation contract
- A common infinitesimal descent vector alone does not identify a higher-dimensional normal space. The categorical nudge theorem depends on codimension one and supplies no shortcut here
- The generators and coordinate/occurrence identities remain fixed before the universal quantifiers. Context-dependent redefinitions evade the conclusion, as the separate quartic fixture demonstrates

## 7. Finite membership matrices and the usable crosswalk

An exact finite truth or membership matrix supplies V, and therefore its rational affine tangent space, elementary-support components and their ranks. These structural data can be computed using exact finite linear algebra. They determine which sets of coordinate score ratios the theorem requires to agree, which free coordinates are unconstrained, and which rank-one components need separate nonlinear analysis.

This is a meaningful interface among logical membership structure, faithful occurrence identity, admissible score families and common correction. It consumes a supplied finite truth presentation; it does not infer the true semantic matrix from strings or observations. Duplicated/complementary events are handled by actual tangent rank and component structure rather than by counting coordinates.

The finite structural computation is not a decision procedure for arbitrary smooth-generator equivalence. Establishing f_i''=a_B g_i'' on an interval remains an analytic proof obligation. Approximate numerical matrices also require a separate stability/error model: an exact affine dependency can disappear under arbitrarily small perturbations. No unrestricted infinite-observer, original-intentionality, norm-authority or necessary-intellect conclusion follows.
