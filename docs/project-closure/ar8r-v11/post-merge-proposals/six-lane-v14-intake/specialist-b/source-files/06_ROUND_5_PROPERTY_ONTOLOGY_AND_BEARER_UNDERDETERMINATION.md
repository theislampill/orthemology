## 1. Axis repair

Two questions must not be merged:

```text
PROPERTY THEORY:
  What, if anything, are properties, universals, tropes, predicates, powers,
  resemblance classes, or conceptual classifications?

MIND–BODY PROPERTY DUALISM:
  Are mental and physical properties irreducible to one another even if one
  adopts some theory of properties?
```

The first is a theory-of-properties axis. The second is a mind–body
metaphysical axis. A project can be realist or nominalist about properties
without thereby accepting property dualism; conversely, a property dualist
still owes a theory of what properties are. The axes may constrain one another
in particular systems, but no formal profile in the repository licenses their
collapse.

## 2. Shared incidence reduct

Let `B` be a set of bearers and `A={K,W,P}` a set of role/predicate labels. Let:

\[
I\subseteq B\times A
\]

record which bearer is associated with which role. Deep G/CF can ask whether:

\[
\bigcap_{a\in A} B_a\neq\varnothing.
\]

This answers an incidence question under supplied bearer identity. It does not
answer what the roles/properties are metaphysically.

The same finite reduct can be interpreted as:

```text
nominalist:
  predicates K,W,P are true of b, with no reified universal;

trope theory:
  b bears particularized K-, W-, and P-tropes related by resemblance;

conceptualism/internal realism:
  b falls under K,W,P relative to an intellect/conceptual scheme;

immanent sparse realism:
  b instantiates sparse properties K,W,P;

powers ontology:
  b possesses dispositional powers whose manifestations realize K,W,P;

real-pattern view:
  K,W,P mark objective compressible patterns without sparse-universal
  commitment;

distributed plural realization:
  different bearers realize the roles with a coordination relation.
```

This is an explicit underdetermination witness, not a claim that every theory
is definitionally equivalent or that every theory can preserve every richer
intensional fact.

## 4. Common bearer does not settle property theory

Assume one `b` lies in every role set. The following remain open:

```text
whether K,W,P are universals, tropes, predicates, powers, or conceptual roles;
whether their co-instantiation is primitive or grounded;
whether they are necessarily co-instantiated;
whether b is numerically the same across worlds;
whether the roles are causally integrated rather than carrier-boxed;
whether b is personal;
whether b is concrete or abstract under the intended semantics;
whether source predicates apply to b.
```

A total intersection is therefore neither a theory of properties nor a full
unity theorem.

## 5. Attribute-locus source and ontology ceiling

The translated Aṣfahāniyyah’s attribute-locus rule is compatible with a
bearer-local predication requirement. It does not force one general ontology:

```text
nominalist paraphrase:
  the predicate applies to the locus exhibiting the relevant state;

trope paraphrase:
  the particularized quality belongs to that locus;

immanent realist paraphrase:
  the property is instantiated in that locus;

powers paraphrase:
  the relevant power is borne by that locus;

conceptualist paraphrase:
  the classification rule applies to that locus under the scheme.
```

The source rule can exclude a particular conduit-predication move within its
source architecture while leaving the property-theory axis underdetermined.

## 6. Proper-function anti-unification

Function theory and property theory are also different axes. For example:

```text
selected-effect function can be modeled nominalistically, tropistically,
realistically, or in powers terms;
causal-role function does not imply selected history;
Plantingian proper function adds a design-plan/truth/warrant architecture but
not a neutral proof of universals or property dualism;
fitrah-oriented function adds source-relative normative purpose but does not
settle whether the disposition is a power, property, trope, pattern, or
conceptual classification.
```

No function-fixing relation should be defined by whichever ontology is being
tested, because that would turn the discriminator into the conclusion.

## 7. Countermodels and rival readings

### SB-CM37 — nominalist/immanent-realist twins

Both models agree on every `I(b,a)` fact and every response profile. One treats
those facts as primitive predicate truths; the other posits sparse immanent
properties. The current profile cannot distinguish them.

### SB-CM38 — trope/resemblance twin

Particularized tropes reproduce the same local role incidence and similarity
judgments as a universal-based model over the finite domain.

### SB-CM39 — conceptualist twin

A scheme-relative classification reproduces the profile while denying that the
role unity is scheme-independent. Content determinacy does not alone establish
mind-independent sparse properties.

### SB-CM40 — powers twin

A brute powers field realizes the same dispositions and causal transitions as
a personal bearer. Causal unity does not establish mentality or personality.

### SB-CM41 — common carrier, no integration

One substrate carries K-, W-, and P-labelled modules, but each operates
independently. Total intersection holds; reason-mediated integration and Wisdom
do not.

### SB-CM42 — plural coordinated realization

No one bearer carries all roles, but a coordinated plural system reproduces all
external and cross-domain profiles. Extensional success does not establish one
bearer.

### SB-CM43 — transworld role section without numerical identity

Each world has exactly one bearer carrying every local role and the sections
transport coherently, but the supplied global identity map assigns different
bearers. Unique coherent role/bearer section is not numerical transworld
identity.

### SB-CM44 — property dualism independent variation

Two models share one property theory and all incidence facts while differing on
whether mental properties reduce to physical properties. Conversely, two
models share a property-dualist stance while differing on trope versus
universal ontology. The axes are not inferred from one another.

## 8. Information-gain diagnosis

The current reduct discards at least:

```text

power/manifestation structure;
```

coordinates without defining it by the preferred ontology. Merely adding more
instances of the same incidence or performance data leaves the fibre intact.
