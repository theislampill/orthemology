# Preceding context in Jami sixth collection

Reader-facing study record, 7 October 2026. The interpretations and reading claims below belong to the recorded study and its bounded rereads. This edition of the notes adds no source-reading or proof credit.

Printed 87–92 were directly read after the separate 93–98 reread. The combined twelve distinct pages are two bounded reread ranges, not a new whole-volume traversal.

The opening explicitly permits reciprocal conditions of coexistence while excluding reciprocal productive origination. It then distinguishes a final end's later realisation from its earlier role in knowledge and intention. Created cooperative agents are described as independently performing their own partial acts with jointly conditioned products; neither is simply the producer of the other agent. Shared ends can also coexist without one final end being the productive origin of the other. This makes the later perfection/inhibition comparison essential: the opening does not itself define all cooperation as mutual creation of the agents.

The legal analysis distinguishes type-level and numerical individuation of a ruling. It also warns against promoting every apparently suitable feature into an independently sufficient cause. Finding a ruling without an alleged cause can expose non-influence; a proposed replacement cause requires its own evidence rather than an ad hoc assertion. The resulting methodology is relevant to semantic interpretation and premise-removal tests, though the source is doing jurisprudential argument rather than providing a modern model-checker theorem.

These points strengthen the need to state contribution identity, causal level, condition, genuine cause and evidential independence separately. They do not erase the later affirmative claim that shared partial exercise lacks the fullness of sole exercise.
