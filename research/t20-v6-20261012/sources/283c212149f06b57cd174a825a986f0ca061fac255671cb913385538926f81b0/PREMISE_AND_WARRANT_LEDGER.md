## Fresh explanatory distinctions

**X1 Base versus link.** A proposed explanatory input is not the only possible form of explanation. An essential status or law can be proposed as the link of an empty-base explanation. This distinction has primary philosophical precedent and improves the analysis of both the affirmative and rival. It does not prove that a particular empty-base explanation exists.

**X2 Intrinsic typing symmetry.** An essence fact may be an instantiated character, an identity condition, an independently obtaining existential input, or a proposed empty-base link. The same criterion must classify the affirmative and the rival. Necessitating on one side and indeterministic on the other does not by itself establish an ontological difference.

**X3 Explanation versus epistemic warrant.** A proposition may be offered as an explanation of a fact without the explanatory structure establishing that it is true. Thus an intelligible source essence is not automatically a warranted actual essence. The converse also holds: lack of a deductive derivation of a primitive does not prove that it cannot explain anything.

**X4 The three target levels.** Constraint explanation, existential provision and contrastive necessitation differ. A complete constraint is not automatically realization; a real non-necessitating provision need not fix every outcome. The source's existential necessity must come from N3, not from equivocating between those target levels.
