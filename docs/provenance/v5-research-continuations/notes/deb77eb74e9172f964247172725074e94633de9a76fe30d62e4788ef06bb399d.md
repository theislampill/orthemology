## Checks made

1. Sufficiency: if a harmful candidate lambda is selected infinitely often, the candidate-specific cycle forces every support action infinitely often. Self-verification supplies at least one action distinguishing lambda from the true theta. Finite action-stack strong laws imply the theta/lambda log-likelihood ratio tends to positive infinity. Equal-law actions contribute exactly zero; finitely sampled unequal-law actions contribute fixed finite terms. Thus the candidate eventually cannot maximize likelihood. Finiteness of the candidate family turns individual finite harmful selections into eventual target persistence
2. Necessity: finite A makes the recurrent action set nonempty and lets one choose a support U with positive probability. The union over deterministic N of tails confined to U has that event as its limit. Full support transfers finite histories. Killed continuation kernels coincide for indistinguishable standards on U. Recurrence of any U action rejected by lambda therefore creates positive-probability failure under lambda
3. Randomization: the stated common behavioral reduction is sound in this perfect-recall setting because the scenario-specific likelihood of a fully observed action/observation history does not depend on which compatible private seed generated its actions. Its factor cancels from the posterior distribution of seeds. No hidden theta-correlated seed may be allowed
4. D1/D2/D3/D4 have the claimed support status. D4 is a useful asymmetric deletion: informativeness of a good action only in one standard does not suffice for a universally restorative policy
5. Full support is essential to the stated criterion. Zero-probability revealing observations can permit finite certainty and invalidate the tail transfer; relaxing that assumption needs a different theorem
6. A fixed finite-memory policy need not exist even when this theorem holds. Growing exact likelihood memory is substantive and matches the prior HS1 boundary

## Nonblocking precision suggestions

- In section 4 explicitly require measurable disjoint restorative tail events, standard Borel action/observation histories, and a common measurable behavioral policy for the continuous-space formulation. The existing paragraph acknowledges this but the theorem statement should carry it, so the generalization is not read as already proved for arbitrary spaces
- If calling per-action observation stacks independent, specify mutually independent stacks conditional on theta, or simply use the sequential controlled kernel assumption and its product-space realization. The stated full conditional law already suffices
- Keep the convention that the action is chosen before its current observation. Allowing access to the same action's current sample in advance would change both policy feasibility and the evidence interpretation
- The likelihood proof establishes almost-sure eventual behavior; it does not yield an integrable last-error time or any finite stopping certificate. The draft correctly disclaims those

## Interface with local repair rigidity
