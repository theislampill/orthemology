# A structuring-cause reply, with its remaining semantic burden

8 October 2026. Bounded successor to `reason-causation-bridge`; no protected edits, final integration, or T20 closure.

## Result first

**Immediate physical sufficiency does not entail historical causal idleness.** A history can establish the physical disposition whose present realization produces an output. It need not furnish an additional simultaneous physical trigger. The new fixed-cue test exhibits this distinction and discriminates genuine acquisition within the model from both nonspecific sensitization and a history-independent preset.

**This is not yet a successful account of propositional content causing rational inference.** Indication, acquisition of a response, present basing on understood grounds, conscious grasp of necessity, and warrant are different achievements. The inspected rival improves the causal account at the first two levels. Its actual reply also gives a substantive historical account of content’s explanatory role; this has not been reduced to a bare cue-label proposal. It does not settle the remaining rational/epistemic questions or every property-level exclusion objection.

The requested Dretske 1993 chapter could not be read beyond its public abstract. Its precise detailed comparison remains blocked. A complete publisher-hosted **later primary article, Dretske 2004**, was read instead, separately identified. Reppert 1998 pp.19–25 were freshly inspected. Baker 1991 and Dretske’s complete 1991 response were also read. The response was initially inaccessible through the publisher, then located through a university syllabus before this package was frozen. Exact reading boundaries and access recovery appear in READING_PROVENANCE.json. This is substantive supplemental research with a disclosed target-source gap, not a completed 1993 test.

## The strongest charitable reply

The full source digests and page locators are in PUBLIC_SOURCE_SUMMARIES.md. The following is the deliberately restricted entailment tested here, not a reconstruction of any author’s complete argument:

1. A present physical state and its current organization suffice for an output.
2. Therefore nothing explains why that state acquired this output-producing role.
3. Therefore its content cannot explain behavior.

The move from 1 to 2 is invalid. A sufficient present condition can itself have a causally relevant history. Exhausting the immediate inputs is not exhausting the explanation of how their efficacy arose. This remains so when the entire history is also physically realized. The reply need not insert an uncaused mental event at an earlier time or exempt learning from physical causation.

But rejecting 2 does not automatically reject every version of 3. Establishing a past physical contribution by a cue is weaker than showing the contribution of a property under a genuinely intentional description. That is the important remaining burden, rather than a demand for a second push.

The source’s strongest useful idea is thus explanatory differentiation with an actual acquisition link. A merely retrospective label is insufficient: if the mechanism’s configuration would have been unchanged without the proposed learning history, that history has not been shown to explain its acquisition. The preset control makes this difference explicit.

## Direct comparison with Reppert

The fresh comparison supports three distinct verdicts, not a single victory or defeat.

**Convention and relational properties.** The causal significance of a representational label assigned after the event is not the same as the causal significance of an organism’s physically sustained relation to its environment. Our model fixes that relation: C signals appearance X before, during, and after learning. The history changes the response weight, not C’s assigned meaning. A causal explanation of this fixed relational dependence therefore cannot be dismissed merely as a freely movable interpretation. This addresses a real weakness in generalizing from an irrelevant honorific property to every non-intrinsic property. It does not demonstrate that indication is original propositional intentionality.

**Causal relevance through content.** Two claims must not be conflated: an internal vehicle contributes to learning, and its meaning contributes in the way required for rational inference. The model supplies the first with an explicit selective learning law. It does not supply the second. Introducing a variable called “content” and defining it from the learning history would not fix this: the new variable would still need an explanatory bridge. Conversely, insisting that a real higher-level property must add an independent physical input would pre-decide the question against realization accounts. Both shortcuts fail.

**Logical necessity and actual rational basing.** Learning to avoid a perceived class is not an instance of accepting a conclusion because one understands an entailment. Our test contains no logical relation for the system to apprehend, no belief acceptance, and no defeat conditions. It consequently does not rebut Reppert’s stronger requirement by replacing it with animal conditioning. The previous distinction between necessary entailment and a fallible token realization remains sound, but no new account of conscious necessity-grasp is supplied here.

## The objection and the actual response

The complete exchange changes the appraisal: **the simple same-role circularity charge is not established against the reply actually given.** The source summary records Dretske’s temporal and type/token distinctions. Treating all the relevant causal roles as one would omit that answer. His historical-function proposal deserves conditional explanatory credit, rather than dismissal because it supplies no extra simultaneous push.

The independent causal point is straightforward. An earlier event can install a capacity whose later exercise helps explain a present outcome. A description of that earlier installation is not a claim that the present exercise caused its own origin. A loop appears only if distinct explanatory relations are collapsed: acquisition of a capacity, constitution of a historical property, and later manifestation of the capacity. This is a real answer to a purported contradiction, not a proof that every account formulated in these terms succeeds.

The remaining question is correspondingly narrower. Does the offered historical property really constitute the relevant intentional content, with its determinate satisfaction conditions, or does it identify only one causal antecedent of a contentful capacity? If the constitutive identification is adequate, repeating that the underlying facts are physical or indicational does not refute its explanatory role. Demanding an additional causal contribution beyond the proposed realization would beg the question. If the identification is inadequate, a genealogy of the vehicle’s powers alone will not rescue it. The defect must be shown in the identification, its scope, or its application to the actual reason-guided process.

Our model establishes neither side of that remaining identity/realization question. Its weight W is an acquired response disposition, not a definition of an indicator function or belief. This distinction is newly explicit after inspecting the actual response. Nor does the model incorporate a theory of current misrepresentation: its harmless-lookalike case preserves accurate appearance indication. Adding a free “meaning” label would not improve it.

The full 1993 chapter remains unavailable. This operation therefore does not assert that its detailed argument has been tested, or that the complete content theory has been vindicated. It does show why an assessment ending with Baker’s same-role allegation would understate the available primary reply.

## Why the new test matters, and its ceiling

The inherited model asked whether fixed semantic interpretation and local determination can coexist with form-sensitive inference. This new test asks whether identical present test behavior identifies the history by which a response role was acquired. Those are different questions.

Two training histories with equal totals of the same cue and the same aversive input yield different acquired weights when their pairing differs. A pain-only mutant fails that distinction. A preset can match all current test outputs of the trained mechanism while lacking the same acquisition dependence. The exact conditional theorem and finite checks are in MODEL_AND_TRANSPORT.md.

These findings give **positive, bounded credit** to cue-specific acquired control and to the distinction between acquisition and current triggering. They do not naturalize a mind. An accurate appearance signal can produce avoidance of a harmless lookalike; that shows that the signal and response do not entail harmfulness. It neither ascribes a false belief to the system nor proves lack of warrant. Warrant is absent from the model, not refuted by it.

## Disposition

The broad inference from present physical sufficiency to the idleness of every relational/historical explanation fails. A second immediate physical trigger is unnecessary for historical relevance. The stronger inference from this finding to causally efficacious propositional content, rational basing, or warrant remains unsupported by this operation.

The next discriminating work is to assess the historical constitutive account against a concrete case of content individuation and present rational basing, rather than repeat the answered same-role circularity allegation. The exact 1993 chapter remains a specific unverified target; the 1991 reply no longer does. Repeating the finite simulation would not settle those application questions.

No source or core worldview judgement is downgraded merely because fresh access was unavailable. Nor does an open semantic burden make naturalism and its rivals equally warranted. The retained positive common-source and recognition appraisals are not adjudicated by this toy mechanism.
