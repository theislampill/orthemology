AR8R-T141 — Burden-emptiness antitonicity and irreversible terminality boundary
Setting

Let B range over sets of authoritative model-actionable burdens ordered by inclusion. Define

Terminal(B)=1 iff B is empty.

Theorem T141.1 — antitonicity

If B subseteq B', then Terminal(B') <= Terminal(B).
Proof

If B' is empty, then B is empty. Equivalently, adding burdens cannot turn a nonterminal state into a terminal one under this predicate. ∎
Theorem T141.2 — irreversible-terminality boundary

If the system allows a transition from B=empty to a nonempty B', an irrevocable terminal flag cannot remain extensionally equal to Terminal along that transition.
Proof

At B=empty, exact terminality is true. At nonempty B', it is false. An irrevocable flag remains true and therefore ceases to match the authoritative predicate. ∎
Repair alternatives

Exact closure requires at least one of:

revocable terminal status;
a completion barrier excluding future admissible burdens;
a versioned terminal status scoped to a frozen burden universe;
or a stronger invariant proving every later state remains burden-empty.

A period of policy stabilization alone supplies none of these.
