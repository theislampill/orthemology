# One complete original Creator of the actual connected order

Publication scope note: Historical independent assessment, expressly PRE-MIFTAH/NUBUWWAT/SAFADIYYA. The dated judgement and first-person voice below are preserved; this is not a final after-all-books verdict. The partial-unblinding disclosure and all selected-reading limits remain in force.
## Independent, bounded philosophical assessment

7 October 2026. This assessment concerns the **actual relevant connected created order**. It does not ask whether every conceivable original reality must instantiate a universal complete-source mode, or whether no disconnected necessary reality could exist. It neither changes proofs nor makes a governance or publication decision.

## 1. Plain verdict

**I regard one complete actual common Creator as a positively favoured explanation, but I do not judge the inspected cumulative argument sufficient to settle that claim for defeasible belief.** The remaining underdetermination is about complete original authorship of this actual order, not merely about the logical possibility of unrelated necessary beings. My conclusion is not that unity has no warrant, that plurality is equally credible, or that the unity claim is false.

The common-source claim and the universal source-mode claim deserve **different graded verdicts**:

- **Actual common source:** a serious, comparatively favoured unifying account. Its affirmative evidence includes coordinated dependence across heterogeneous products, common operative conditions, coherent ends, continuity through replacement, and the economy of one sufficiently capable original agent. The present case does not yet discriminate complete single-source production strongly enough from the best intrinsically coordinated plural basis.
- **Universal complete-source mode:** not sufficiently warranted by the inspected argument. Its contested demand is that every qualified original's actual contribution must include the entire original provision of each connected product, rather than a fitting contribution whose result also includes another's original contribution. This is a stronger normative and metaphysical claim than the actual-order hypothesis.
- **At the stated upstream starting point:** actual original existential provision, a concrete original bearer, and genuine original efficacy are not suspended merely because numerical common-sourcehood remains unsettled. The recovered original-completion and bearer rationales give these claims substantive content. For this assessment I retain that defended starting point, including the stronger positive agency/perfection background, rather than repeatedly reducing the inquiry to bare necessity. This selected reinspection does not claim a new independent traversal of every foundational source supporting that background.
- **Successfully established local distinctions:** circular original donation does not explain the agency it presupposes; two agents cannot each actually produce the same whole exclusively; real cooperation does not erase distinct acts; dependence of joint attainment is not automatically receipt of each agent's intrinsic power; standing ability is not actual exhaustive production. These have much firmer support here than either broad unity thesis.

Thus both broad claims fall below my sufficiency threshold in this assessment, but for different reasons and with different positive standing. I do **not** infer the actual verdict directly from the failure of the universal principle. An independent cumulative inference to one actual source could succeed even if that principle remained unproved.

## 2. Inferential standard

The standard is sufficient **defeasible rational warrant**, not a theorem from premises neutral between all metaphysical schools. An intelligible substantive principle, a sound explanatory inference, or convergent signs can justify belief without excluding every describable competitor. Neither dissent nor absence of a deduction automatically defeats warrant.

I distinguish a conclusion's being the leading available proposal from the reasons being strong enough to take its central additional commitment as established, subject to revision. Here that commitment is **one bearer's complete actual original provision**, rather than some actual original provision or some common coordination.

My withholding rests on a substantive contrast problem. The relevant observations strongly favour coordinated explanation over unrelated or accidentally juxtaposed production. But much of their force is shared by one comprehensive agent and several intrinsically wise, necessarily harmonious agents. The residual economy advantage is real; it has not yet been shown sufficiently strong and stable against that principled alternative to settle numerical authorship. This is more specific than saying a rival can be drawn or described consistently.

A hypothetical rival need not first be proved metaphysically possible before it can expose a contrast the positive argument has not adequately explained. Conversely, merely describing it neither establishes its possibility nor gives it equal credibility. The test is what explanatory work the admitted considerations actually perform.

## 3. The strongest positive case

### 3.1 Begin with the complete actual explanandum

The relevant field contains genuinely acting beings, not powerless appearances. Their acts depend on powers, materials, receptive subjects, media and the absence of impediments. Shifa e143 itemises this dependence in ordinary perception and bodily action. Darʾ IX 016:621–650 likewise distinguishes a person's act from the bodily and environmental conditions of its success. Original explanation must account for the actuality of these resources, not merely append another receiver to an otherwise wholly received account.

The T17 original-completion rationale, especially P40–54, properly directs attention to that complete existential target. It also expressly separates an actual original bearer from one bearer supplying everything. I accept that distinction without weakening original completion into a demand for one unexplained token somewhere in the history.

### 3.2 Unity has substantive explanatory content

The source's central positive observation is not simply that there are regularities. Shifa e231–234 emphasises distinct contributions that fit together: organs serve a whole; different persons' activities answer one another's needs; materials sustain varied products; replacement preserves an intelligible pattern across time. Sawaiq printed232 appeals to the world's interconnection and coherent order. Minhaj II 114–119 uses the relation between a human being and the material and nourishment required for its existence.

A common wise source can explain both the differentiated products and their mutual accommodation through one comprehensive practical determination. This is more illuminating than describing every locally useful fit as an unrelated ultimate fact. On the retained positive agency background, one qualified original has adequate knowledge and eligible standing power; the explanation does not have to construct its competence from a collection of independently limited specialists.

This yields a real economy argument. Several original agents appear to require additional facts about allocation of contributions, shared constraints and concordant ends. One actual common original can supply those relationships within its own integrated determination. The gain concerns fewer independent explanatory commitments, not merely a shorter label.

### 3.3 Full actual sourcehood is a genuine excellence

Darʾ VIII P412–421 does not merely admire unused capacity. Ibn Taymiyya expressly endorses the comparison between a producer that originates and preserves its effect and one whose effect needs another preserver. Minhaj II 117 treats realised efficacy as a perfection, and II 100–120 argues that a necessary self does not receive an intrinsic constitutive perfection from an external source. The argument against partnership is therefore not adequately answered by saying that each partner remains able to act alone.

The positive point survives: actually supplying the complete basis of an effect is a fuller original role than supplying only part. Voluntary consent does not change the provenance of a genuinely foreign contribution. An assessment that treated all actual-role questions as questions only about a retained faculty would miss the affirmative case.

But recognising the fuller role does not yet settle whether undertaking the less comprehensive role is necessarily a defect of a practically perfect original. That is where the universal source-mode argument adds a substantive classification.

### 3.4 Why this is evidence for the actual hypothesis even without the universal norm

Even if perfect original cooperation were possible, it would not follow that the actual world has several creators. No distinct independently original contribution has been identified merely by observing many created agents. One common original remains the least ontologically expansive adequate hypothesis within the stipulated creator framework. This gives it a positive presumption over an otherwise identical account with gratuitously multiplied originals.

This is the best reason not to let objections to a universal perfection theorem erase the actual-order case. I give it genuine comparative weight. I nevertheless judge it a preference rather than a settled sufficient-warrant conclusion for the reasons below.

## 4. The strongest objections and what they establish

### 4.1 Noncausal grounding is not automatically efficient creation

Darʾ VIII P396–422 is unusually clear: an indispensable condition, an object of love or imitation, or a source of motion alone is not thereby the maker of the world's substance and attributes. Calling something the ultimate ground does not by itself show that it creates. T17 P44 retains the same modern distinction: broad existential derivation and efficient production are not interchangeable relations.

A complete original noncausal ground would satisfy a different explanatory claim unless a bridge to efficient originating agency were supplied. If the stronger productive-bearer and agency premises are retained, this is not an objection that simply annuls them. It is a warning against advertising a single ground as already a single Creator on generic sufficient-reason grounds.

It also matters inside the actual-order argument. Evidence of common intelligible organisation can point to a common organiser without independently assigning every material and productive requisite to that organiser. The source rightly demands more than an Aristotelian final attractor. The unity appraisal must apply that same demand to its own conclusion: **one common coordinating explanation and one complete original producer are not identical achievements**.

### 4.2 A plural original basis need not be an unexplained aggregate of receivers

Whole-field completion asks for an adequate original basis. A plural proposal can accept that demand while assigning original actuality to more than one bearer. It need not make an abstract collection perform an act, or claim that a sum of derivative realities becomes original merely by being collected. T17 P45 and P48–52 explicitly preserve these alternatives.

A genuinely unified holistic bearer could instead own all the relevant capacities, with differentiated aspects that are not independent original agents. That would be a real unity proposal, not a plurality simply renamed. But identifying such a bearer requires evidence about its unity. Shared necessity, coordinated behaviour, or one description of a set does not establish numerical identity.

The source's defence of inseparable divine attributes against an external-assembler argument makes this particularly important. It cannot be read as a general prohibition of every internal distinction, nor can its permission for one bearer's attributes automatically absorb distinct proposed bearers into that single bearer.

### 4.3 Cooperation and necessary harmony

The weakest plural proposals are successfully excluded. If neither agent has operative power until the other creates that very power, the alleged explanation is circular. Simultaneity does not repair the circle. If each will receives its original activation from the other's as-yet-unactivated will, no genuine original determination has been supplied. Foreknowledge that presupposes an event is not, simply as foreknowledge, a producer of that event. Minhaj III 259–276 and Darʾ IX 016:700–779 engage these issues directly; the author did not overlook agreement.

The strongest alternative is different. Each original possesses its own intrinsic power, awareness and intention. Their natures and intelligible reasons yield compatible contributions. The shared product requires both contributions; neither agent receives its constitutive agency from the other. Necessary harmony is not treated as a further efficient agent, and the truths known need not be produced by one knower into the other.

This is not proved possible. It must explain why agreement extends to the relevant detail, rather than merely repeating “wisdom.” Common good might fix a broad aim while leaving roles and means unsettled. A hypothesis that inserts a separate tailored necessity for each observed fit would lose much of its claimed explanatory economy.

Yet the single-source account also has to explain the specificity of its practical determination. “One wise source wills this order” is not by itself a derivation of why this rather than another order is fitting. A fair comparison cannot grant the single source an unspecified comprehensive determination while requiring the plural basis to derive every detail independently. Nor can distinct necessary bearers simply be assigned probabilistically independent natures or choices; numerical distinction does not establish that kind of independence.

The actual observations therefore discriminate more clearly against unrelated, uncoordinated production than against principled plural harmony. This is the strongest comparative objection, and the main reason the remaining economy advantage does not yet settle the claim for me.

### 4.4 Intrinsic acts and external product conditions

Jami printed181 supplies an especially strong internal control. Each cooperator has power, awareness, intention and a sought end; these bases are not obtained from the other, while attaining either end requires the two intentions and acts. Printed180 permits instruments and treats added cooperative strength as an effect of the joint activity. Printed182 distinguishes being with another cause from being produced through it. This is not an endorsement of uncreated peers, but it blocks a general inference from joint attainment to reciprocal origination of powers.

Darʾ VIII P415–419 similarly distinguishes the craftsman's intrinsic act from the materials' receptivity and preserving properties. The primary sources therefore supply both the positive fuller-producer comparison and the constraint on how dependence is transferred.

The strongest source-mode response deserves its full force: the agent's actual original role is foreign-completed even if its intrinsic act remains self-owned. I agree about the external role's incompleteness. The further claim that such a chosen role is an intrinsic or practical defect in every qualified original remains insufficiently established. A result that is intentionally joint need not contradict an internally complete intention merely because neither participant actually produced the whole alone.

This does not vindicate any arbitrary choice. Practical perfection constrains action, and consent cannot make deficiency disappear. What remains to be shown is why this specific form of differentiated original contribution is deficient, rather than merely less comprehensive in one respect. Shifa e207–209's proper-time account itself requires assessing what perfection belongs to an undertaking, not demanding every possible accomplishment indiscriminately.

### 4.5 Numerical nonduplication

Two agents' each **actually doing the entire same thing alone** is contradictory. Shifa e50 sharply distinguishes that case from jointly carrying something either could have carried alone. Jami printed94–96 also distinguishes numerical act, separate contribution and composite result.

A proposal of primitive nonexclusive double sufficiency is a further claim, not directly refuted by the word “alone.” T19's separate causal-nonduplication rationale has positive content: real additional production ordinarily has some determinate contribution, and gratuitous duplicate productive relations buy no explanatory gain. I regard that as a reasonable economy consideration, while not treating its universal scope as analytically settled.

The actual unity question need not turn on accepting that unusual rival. Even if I grant the stronger nonduplication principle for this assessment, complementary original contributions remain the relevant alternative. Nonduplication does not itself give one participant exhaustive coverage of every product.

### 4.6 Strict supremacy and pure-perfection eligibility

Sawaiq 002:1196–1210 gives a sound conditional: two beings cannot each be strictly above the other in the same respect. Absolute domination excludes an undefeated equal. This is stronger than mere maximal possession of an attribute and must not be reduced to it.

The positive rationale is also substantive. Unborrowed informed agency without vulnerability or an external veto is a real excellence. An explanation of ultimate agency that still needs external constitution is incomplete in the relevant respect.

The contested extension is from independence of another's domination to domination over every proposed independent peer. The original giver's superiority to its recipients does not already make every other reality a recipient. Nor is ability to annihilate an intrinsically necessary peer an eligible object of power while that peer's necessity is retained. Control over its intrinsic agency requires a corresponding argument about eligible targets. Otherwise the claimed relational perfection builds peer exclusion into the description used to prove peer exclusion.

Thus strict supremacy would decide more than the bounded actual common-source question if independently established. The inspected pure-perfection rationale does not, in my judgement, sufficiently establish that exact eligibility. This is a local judgement about a demanding relational perfection, not rejection of all positive perfection arguments.

## 5. Why the remaining positive evidence does not cross my threshold

Three considerations prevent a merely sceptical verdict: one qualified original is already a genuine explanatory resource; the order is integrated rather than merely similar; and additional original agents have no independently identified task that requires them. Those make the single-source account preferable to a casually multiplied or disconnected account.

Three limitations prevent my preference from becoming a sufficiency judgement here:

1. **The explanation's unity is clearer than the agent's numerical unity.** Shared intelligible constraints and ends can do explanatory work without themselves being a single efficient agent. The evidence does not yet adequately rule in which bearer arrangement realises that explanatory unity.
2. **Common coordination is weaker than complete original provision.** The actual order gives strong reason to seek a unified account, but assigning every constituent resource to one bearer remains additional. The act/product controls make that difference real, rather than verbal.
3. **The strongest plural proposal is not mere lucky consensus.** Necessary wise compatibility can preserve much of the same explanatory fit without circular power donation. Its additional bearers and role facts have a cost, but the present dossier does not show that this cost is sufficiently greater than the specificity carried by the single source's intrinsic nature and actual comprehensive intention.

This does not demand a measured probability, a scientific experiment on original beings, or an exhaustive rival cosmology. It asks for a sufficiently supported comparative reason, or an independently warranted source/bearer principle, at the particular inference where the conclusion adds complete single-source authorship. A new observation with differential force, a better account of why plural harmony incurs unavoidable extra unexplained structure, or a stronger independent bearer-unity argument could change the judgement.

Historical celestial and physiological illustrations are not essential to the positive case: ordinary reciprocal dependence is already enough to make it serious. But the selected reading does not independently verify all historical scientific details or a universal claim that every feature has the asserted final purpose. Those details cannot be used as silently certified extra confirmations.

Fitra and religious experience may also be genuine grounds of recognition; failure of one public argument need not erase a person's independently well-grounded belief. In this assessment, however, I have not independently established the discriminating reliability of a universal fitri recognition of numerical authorship. Likewise, treating independently authenticated revelation as evidence could materially change the question, but the truth of the scriptural unity claim is not assumed as a premise of this standalone philosophical comparison. Reporting that the authors regard it as certain is not a substitute for making the assessment.

## 6. Relation to the preceding assessments

I consulted the recovered T17 and T19 rationales and the current Jami/Shifa and source-mode appraisals only after direct primary reconstruction, subject to the disclosure in [01_PRIMARY_RECONSTRUCTION.md](01_PRIMARY_RECONSTRUCTION.md). I do not adopt either report's final verdict as authority.

The Jami middle reader's provisional preference and the first-section reader's withholding of sufficient discrimination are not necessarily opposed answers to one precisely fixed threshold. Both recognise evidence beyond bare consistency. My own verdict is explicit: **comparative preference for the actual common-source account, withholding sufficiency for its complete numerical authorship, and a separate adverse sufficiency judgement on the universal source-mode requirement**.

The older source-mode assessment's negative verdict cannot by itself settle this actual-order question. I have instead assessed the remaining integration/economy route on its own merits. My result happens also to withhold sufficiency, but that is a separate inference whose positive considerations and unresolved comparison are stated above.
