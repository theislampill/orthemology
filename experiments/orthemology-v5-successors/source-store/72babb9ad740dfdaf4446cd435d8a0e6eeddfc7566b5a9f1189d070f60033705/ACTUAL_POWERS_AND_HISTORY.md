# Actual powers, alternative prehistory, and essential grounding

Tenth source-level investigation, 3 October 2026. This study assesses an existing modal theory against the accepted joint-coverage result. It adds no formal frame model and changes no accepted file. The question is which actual reasons support the generative and source-retention claims, rather than whether another conditional can be written down.

## Finding first

The primary account gives the affirmative route more than a semantic appeal to actual truthmakers. Its finite productive interpretation supports a genuine actual entry source for a possible new supply. A source-relative realization can retain that entry while changing later historical facts. It need not freeze every past event or choose one universal source shared by every alternative.

The remaining qualifications matter. Retaining a source's identity is weaker than retaining the actual kind-instantiation occurrence relevant to the new argument. A genuinely occurrence-anchored interpretation supplies a positive rationale for the latter; a merely object-based account of modal grounding does not. The stronger interpretation must cover the complete historically qualified achievement, rather than only a later recipient.

The hybrid essentialist route is also substantive and must not be represented as a powers-only argument. Its strongest reading obtains necessary particular grounds from a richer existential premise. But its offered connection between contingent support and removability needs an exhaustive, jointly realizable support claim at the existential target. Its extraction of concrete grounds also needs a restriction on abstract essential grounding, beyond efficient-causal inertness alone.

These are content tests of specific arguments. They leave genuine positive warrant for finite generative realization, relevant entry retention and resource completeness. They do not supply new independent warrant for the whole qualified modal target or every absolute realization covered by the strong theory. No actual necessary particular is established by this study.

## 2. What the generative reading positively contributes

A power capable of producing a new concrete supplier is not merely evidence that a description is consistent. It is a feature of an actual bearer which can genuinely account for that supplier's actuality through an appropriate possible exercise. A finite sequence of such productions can transmit the relevant source relation without turning any recipient into a powerless relay.

This gives a concrete reason for selecting an actual availability anchor. If the supplier is not already actual, and its existence is genuinely produced through the specified finite route, the route includes an actual power-bearing entry source positively relevant to that existence. The inference does not proceed from the whole package's relevance to each of its parts. It follows the supplying path to the supplier. The H-GEN and H-CAUS loci therefore support the positive-anchor interpretation appreciably better than a reading on which actual reality only makes the possibility sentence true.

Nor does an inactive capacity fail to be actual. The relevant source can actually possess the power even if the possible exercise never occurs. Keeping that distinction gives the accepted necessary-capacity strategy genuine room to operate. An explanation of why the power exists and an actual use of it are further matters.

The accepted joint-power control remains important. Finite depth concerns iterated supply steps, not the number of participating sources or the whole prehistory of an entry resource. Joint contributors may supply a new producer even if no one member suffices alone. Selecting an actual necessary resource from such a basis still requires its positive availability-specific role. Calling it an active power bearer additionally requires the supplying-power condition; arbitrary membership in a collective does not provide that type.

Resource completeness also has independent rational force here. If an alleged power can produce the advertised recipient only after an independently available supplier has been provided, it has not yet accounted for the supplier's availability. For an achievement explicitly including that availability, citing the power and silently supplying a merely possible auxiliary is inadequate. This is a judgement about what the claimed capacity accomplishes, not a demand to explain all facts about the world.

The finite generative interpretation is consequently a positive result of the source-level comparison. It is not merely one more condition we would like a modal theory to satisfy. It is an intelligible account of what concrete productive realization amounts to, with a recognizable reason for preserving its actual supplying source.

## 3. Alternative prehistory without a frozen past

Two easy objections should be rejected. A present agent's inability to rewrite a completed past does not refute a timeless actual-power account. Nor does the absence of a later power-bearer from an alternative history show that the earlier history cannot be restored as a possibility.

An earlier actual source may have been able either to produce an intermediate agent or to refrain. If it refrains in an alternative history, that intermediate agent's powers are absent there. Yet the earlier source may retain the power to produce the agent, which could then produce the later result. Finite iteration makes the missing later ability available through the retained earlier one. This is why a serious criticism cannot simply point to the disappearance of a downstream bearer.

Such an alternative can change the prehistory of a later recipient. The relevant actual entry lies earlier than the changed segment. What is retained is that entry's existence and the powers used for the specified realization. There is no inference that every historical fact is fixed, that the same entry serves every possibility, or that all subordinate histories have a first event. A source can itself have a beginningless actual ancestry while its particular possible exercise is the selected entry for the achievement under discussion.

The H-RETURN rationale has a genuine application here. It is not defeated because another whole-history metaphysics can be described.

The extension to every absolute alternative is a different claim. Earlier exercises can determine whether a later bearer exists, which powers it receives, and which backgrounds coexist with it. Power possession's independence from its own present exercise does not establish independence from every exercise in its ancestry. Finite iteration can repair some losses through a retained earlier source; its success must not be assumed when the entire relevant availability base has been removed or changed.

There are therefore two acceptable positive positions. One can defend unrestricted return directly as a basic modal principle, with its full content visible. Or one can derive return within a genuinely source-preserving generative domain. The second gives a reason for the first only if that domain exhausts the absolute alternatives in question. This is an exact scope assessment, not a requirement that basic modal knowledge be derived from something more basic.

## 4. Identity retention and kind-instantiation retention

The accepted Tenth target fixes an actual family Q; F classifies contingent bearers within that family. The requested possible supply has no relevant F-instantiation in its full existential-support history. This includes a relevant earlier stage of the same bearer, not just its later classification or strict productive ancestry through different individuals.

Retaining an actual source's identity does not by itself establish retention of a nonessential classification in an alternative prehistory. This is an inference control, not a certified alternative history. Merely carrying the name of that source across the comparison does not carry every actual descriptive fact about it. This matters because an exact-origin exclusion can concern the identity of an added source, whereas the current kind argument also concerns how that source is classified at a relevant occurrence.

There is a principled stronger interpretation. Take the realizing entry to be an actual power-possession occurrence whose availability is positively relevant to the supply. If that retained occurrence is F, later transformation cannot erase it from the route's support history. The F-property need not itself be a separately efficacious cause; its bearer-occurrence must play the relevant supplying role. Retention of a constitutive power basis is one sufficient way of securing that role. A possible use of the specified actual occurrence is not a retrospective replacement of the very source conditions from which the route is said to proceed.

This is a defensible occurrence-based reading of genuine generation. It does not make all the source's incidental properties essential. It also need not preserve unrelated earlier facts. Its point is that an account claiming to use a given actual resource cannot gain the opposite ancestry qualification by silently substituting a differently constituted resource history.

The distinction remains important for interpreting a general modal theory. Actual bearer identity and an actual possession of some power do not by themselves establish which classified occurrence is retained in every promised whole-history realization. The H-CAUS and H-HISTORY loci support causal-source fidelity; they do not expressly supply a universal theory of the nonessential Q-membership of each retained power-occurrence.

Thus the strengthened generative reading has positive rational support, but its exact application must be stated. If the theory promises realization from the relevant actual power-occurrence, the accepted fixed-route principle applies. If it only supplies an actual ontological ground for a possibility realized through a different complete prehistory, that is a weaker relation. One cannot use the latter at modal coverage and the former at exclusion.

There is another charitable repair which need not preserve a whole descriptive stage. Suppose the retained relevant power-profile itself suffices for membership in some kind within Q. Then retaining that profile preserves Q-membership even if other features vary. This could have an independent basis in a real definition or a sufficiently discriminating power-based account of the kind. Mere explanatory priority of kind-membership to power-possession does not establish the converse. A nature may supply a power without being the only nature capable of supplying it. No metaphysically possible alternative bearer is asserted by distinguishing these directions.

The two strongest retention strategies are therefore precise: preserve an actually classified relevant entry occurrence, or preserve a power-profile with independently warranted Q-classifying force. Neither requires every accidental feature to remain fixed. Neither can acquire the needed force simply from the fact that Q covers actual candidates. The proposed covering family and the absolute contingency qualification must remain the same through the comparison.

Only one appropriately retained realization is needed for each genuinely possible S, not retention in every realization of S. A different upstream history therefore does not defeat the route if another eligible realization starts earlier and retains the required occurrence. The problem arises if the general theory supplies only a realization with a differently classified prehistory and gives no reason for the existence of an appropriately retained alternative. Actual bearer, actually possessed power-profile and actual classified occurrence are three different candidates for what is held fixed.

## 5. The strongest case for exhaustive actual-power coverage

Actual powers give genuine reality to many possibilities. Treating unexercised capacity as real avoids identifying possibility with mere verbal consistency.

A theory that uses those already needed powers can also have an explanatory and ontological advantage over a theory that simply adds ungrounded modal facts.
<!-- SOURCE_ASSOCIATED HAMRI_ADVANTAGE END -->

These are real reasons in favour of the H-GEN strategy.

They do not automatically establish that every nonactual absolute fact must be generatively realizable from such a basis. The rationale for including power-supported possibilities and the rationale for excluding every other kind have different scopes. At a whole-origin qualification, the disputed issue is precisely whether possible reality must have a generating route that preserves the requested support condition.

The stronger claim may be defended basically or as part of an abductively justified metaphysics. It is not irrational merely because the record contains no neutral algorithm to verify it. But the argument must then address the historical qualification itself. Familiar abilities to produce later objects support finite generation; they do not by themselves disclose whether every alternative complete ancestry is generated in that same respect.

This limitation is not a proof that the strong account is false. Nor is it a demand for an independently certified counter-history. It explains why the primary source adds contentful metaphysical commitments beyond the local power examples, and why those commitments must remain visible when applying the theory to the Tenth target.

A general rule about genuine possibilities also does not establish that the particular conjunction S is possible. A suitable outsider to fixed Q, the appropriate productive relation to an F-recipient, and the full support qualification must be possible together. An independently existing ground does not automatically have that productive capacity. A capacity to produce an F need not have the required independent support. This is why improved source-level backing for realization does not by itself discharge POS-S.

## 6. The necessity equivalence and three kinds of removal

<!-- SOURCE_RECONSTRUCTION HAMRI_2022 START -->
The H-EQ bridge identifies an obtaining fact's immunity to power-produced failure with its obtaining from independent essences. Its support discussion aims to derive a power for failure from the absence of that essential grounding.
<!-- SOURCE_RECONSTRUCTION HAMRI_2022 END -->

For a positive existential fact q, distinguish three operations:

1. Change or remove one selected actual support for q.
2. Eliminate every independently adequate support for q within the relevant account, including the alternatives capable of replacing the selected support.
3. Jointly remove the whole relevant contingent concrete support class while excluding replacement witnesses.

The first does not in general achieve the second. If two independently adequate witnesses are present, removal of one leaves q true. Even removing every currently active productive channel may leave an available backup that becomes active under the intervention. That is why the domain of supports must include exactly the alternatives relevant to the claimed failure.

The second does not establish that the third is metaphysically possible. A joint absence specification is not an ability to realize that specification, especially when it removes the very bearers of all proposed realizing powers. It also differs from the claim that each bearer can be absent in some circumstance. No empty-world possibility is asserted by drawing these distinctions.

There is a strong reading on which the support-removal inference is valid. Suppose q counterfactually depends on a collectively exhaustive support condition, and there is a genuine jointly realizable power to eliminate that condition without introducing another adequate witness. Then q would fail. The correct response is to credit that implication.

Its premise, however, is stronger than the observation that actual contributors are individually contingent or that one actual ground can be replaced. At the global existential target, the premise must contain the exhaustive joint removability which the weaker observations do not present. Giving that stronger content to the support relation is a legitimate philosophical proposal; it is not a derivation of the content from local contingent support.

This distinction bears directly on H-ABS. Merely changing an actual history is weaker than removing every member of that history from the comparison. A surviving member may provide an internal route for further production. The stronger absence is therefore not a dispensable rhetorical flourish: it is what prevents the realizing account from using an in-history source. Its warrant cannot be borrowed from the weaker possibility that one selected member or one detailed historical fact fails.

The H-ABS essence-externality proposal is consequently an additional serious argument. It should be examined as such rather than collapsed into a powers-only derivation. Its extension from particular external objects to generic existential consequences is examined next.

## 7. What essential grounding contributes, and the concrete-type step

It is important to distinguish three possible achievements:

- establishing some actual necessary concrete resource;
- additionally establishing that the selected resource bears a supplying power;
- establishing an actual being whose existence has the source's stronger through-essence status.

The first does not establish the second, and neither alone establishes the third. Conversely, a properly established concrete ground of the third kind would deserve necessary-particular credit without waiting for unity or actual exercise. It should not be reduced to a necessarily occupied role. The H-ESS premise has richer existential content than bare necessity; it is not something ordinary power possession already grants.

A necessary concrete-existence proposition concerns concreta; its grounds' type is a further question. This is the relevant test at H-CONC. Efficient-causal inertness is a reason not to credit an abstract object as an efficient producer. It does not by itself rule out every noncausal grounding or essential consequence of that object's nature.

The strongest externality reply concerns real definition. A nature that constitutively requires another particular being would not be independent of that being.
<!-- SOURCE_ASSOCIATED HAMRI_EXTERNALITY END -->

That is an intelligible reason to resist importing an arbitrary external object into an independent essence.

The generic existential case is different. A nature's having some existential consequence does not automatically make it depend on the consequent. In particular, a generic consequence need not name a particular recipient. The direction of explanation must be examined. Inferring reverse ontological dependence from necessitation alone would repeat a kind of error already exposed by the earlier contribution-routing study.

Consider conditionally an independently existing bearer whose nature necessarily includes some productive activity, while the particular exercises and recipients can vary. Its existence may then entail some effect without being ontologically explained by that effect. This is a programme-compatible control on a dependence inference, not an actual or certified possible source. It does not refute the exact-H argument: a generic productive consequence is weaker than the existence of a member of that fixed history.

There is also a genuine positive abstractness control. The existence or intrinsic structure of a proposition representing concrete existence does not by itself make its represented content true. Nor does an abstract specification alone supply a concrete instance merely by describing one. This blocks a purely representational substitute for existential explanation and gives the affirmative a substantive reason to ask what the alleged abstract ground contributes.

That bounded judgement is not a proof excluding every possible nonrepresentational essential ground of concrete nonemptiness. To reach the concrete conclusion from the essentialist bridge, the relevant type restriction must be defended at that wider scope. It could be an independently defended restriction on what abstract essences can ground, or a warranted link from this existential grounding to concrete productive capacity. It cannot be supplied merely by repeating that abstract things are causally inert.

No abstractly grounded concrete cosmos is certified by this test. The test identifies why an efficient-causal classification and an essential-grounding classification cannot be traded without explanation. It also preserves the stronger route's positive force: if the appropriately concrete through-essence ground is warranted, it supplies more than the accepted minimal necessary-resource conditional. That richer conclusion must be earned at its own premises.

## 8. Result at the programme's actual standard of warrant

The source-level study strengthens the affirmative case in a limited but genuine way. Finite actual-power generation is a concrete proposal for the positive availability trace. Source-relative counterfactual histories can vary later prehistory without freezing every historical fact. Relevant occurrence retention has a defensible generative rationale, and complete-resource adequacy is a justified constraint on the full claimed achievement.

The study also identifies specific remaining reasons the complete application has not yet been earned. Identity retention does not alone settle classified-occurrence retention. The support-removal route is sound under exhaustive jointly realizable dependence, but weaker local contingency does not supply it. The essentialist route adds positive existential grounding rather than deriving it from powers alone, and its concrete-ground conclusion needs the corresponding type restriction.

These are not demands for measurement, consensus or a demonstration ruling out every rival. They distinguish independently intelligible reasons from the stronger content needed at this target. The theory retains a serious rational motivation; its open application is not evidence that it has no merits or that competing completed metaphysics are equally credible.

No new actual necessary resource, power bearer or through-essence source has been established. No identification with the admitted correction's whole-origin source is supplied, and P1/P2, N2/N3 and later attributes are not completed by switching between these types. The accepted joint-history adequacy question remains open. What changes is the source-level assessment: there is a credible finite generative basis for the local route, and the decisive remaining work concerns exhaustive historical-modal scope, joint removability and the type of essential ground.
