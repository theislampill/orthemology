## Blocking findings

Connected interaction support rules out a sum of block-local functions.  It
does not rule out arbitrary nonadditive plural implementations, compositional
networks, latent-variable models, or other explanatory decompositions.  V2 must
call the result an **additive derivational-unity certificate**, not full
explanatory or metaphysical unity.

### AE-F02 — the coordinate system was load-bearing

Möbius support changes under recoding.  The profile coordinates must be fixed by
independent semantic/causal/source contracts before interaction support has
orthemological meaning.  An invertible gerrymandered change of variables can
manufacture or hide interactions.

### AE-F03 — a scalar outcome function was not the whole architecture

A theory may expose several constraint, outcome, transition, and explanation
functions.  V2 must define the union hypergraph for a finite family/vector of
registered functions and state which family is claimed complete.

### AE-F04 — connectivity does not establish causal direction or common bearer

The hypergraph is undirected support structure.  It does not identify causal
direction, a subject, a bearer, intentional ownership, or an efficient cause.
The personal/impersonal control must remain explicit.

### AE-F05 — hidden variables can change the decomposition

Marginalizing or omitting a latent coordinate can induce apparent interactions.
A connected observed hypergraph does not prove primitive nondecomposability
unless the coordinate/resource registry is complete for the explanatory claim.

### AE-F06 — arbitrary coupling is not automatically explanatory unification

A gerrymandered cross-term can connect every coordinate while explaining
nothing.  A Candidate-G gain requires independently motivated functions,
coordinates, and counterfactual restrictions—not interaction density alone.

### AE-F07 — observational samples do not identify the exact truth table

The exhaustive checker validates finite functions once the full table is given.
Sparse observations, correlation, neural alignment, or endpoint/trajectory data
do not recover all Möbius coefficients without additional design and
identifiability assumptions.

### AE-F08 — the minimal-component claim needed exact wording

The connected components are the unique coarsest blocks for **additive
separability relative to the registered function family and fixed coordinates**.
They are not necessarily ontological parts or minimal causal modules.

### AE-F09 — theorem-family and prior-art credit needed stronger ceilings

The proof is standard Boolean Möbius/ANOVA decomposition and interaction-graph
reasoning.  The Candidate-G application is useful but receives zero general
mathematical novelty and no historical identity.

### AE-F10 — personal architecture advantage remained unproved

A personal architecture can realize a connected interaction law, but so can an
impersonal law, powers field, or distributed system.  V2 must not describe
nonseparability as evidence for personality unless matched rivals and primitive
costs are independently adjudicated.
