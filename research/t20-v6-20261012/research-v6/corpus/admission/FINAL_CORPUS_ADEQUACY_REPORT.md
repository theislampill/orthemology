The classifier is a navigation and consistency aid. Exact owner records and their limits control; a matching word, line count, source hash, import, or receipt alone is never the reason to accept an entire scientific body.

## Why another indiscriminate pass is unlikely to help

The stopping basis is the finite unit-by-unit disposition, coupled to actual controlling arguments, imports, callers and selected data, rather than elapsed time, a shrinking association count, or successful test output. The admitted roles have no outstanding unassigned scientific body, and the terminal challenge has resolved the concrete contradictory scope metadata. Another undirected pass over the same qualified bytes is therefore unlikely to reveal a consequential omission. The productive next work is targeted recovery or examination of a specifically identified unavailable dependency, new evidence, or a challenged theorem/implementation claim. Such a discovery would reopen its exact scope; this report does not preclude it.
