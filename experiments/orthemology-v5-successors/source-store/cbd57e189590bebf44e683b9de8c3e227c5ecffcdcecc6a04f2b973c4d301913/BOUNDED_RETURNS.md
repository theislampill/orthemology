# Final-quarter bounded reflective returns

The complete 13-window continuous pass ended before these returns. Scan pages below were visually read in full with their visible apparatus, without claiming a full edition or manuscript collation. Electronic raw text remains unchanged.

## Printed 946–957 / S003L25–29: intent, designation and ability
These twelve pages preserve the inherent/intentional evidence distinction, envoy tokens and the capacity/performance critique. Printed 946's first-type evidence does not require prior knowledge that someone intended it as evidence. Printed 947 says token-signs might not have been designated signs; actual intentional designation matters. Printed 948 sharply differentiates impossible genuine evidence of falsehood from possible production of a similar occurrence without its being a genuine sign. Printed 952–953 states that failure to perform a possible truth-certification is not loss of the ability, and that undifferentiated power alone does not restrict a token to the truthful claimant. Printed 954 requires establishment of a proposed invariant association, rather than assuming it. Printed 955–957 concludes the internal critique of denying purposeful action while retaining intentional divine guidance.

The notes in this edition add citations, explanatory paraphrases and readings against خ; e.g 947 supplies [معه] from the older prints, 948 has a manuscript-margin qualification, and 955 [نصب] is chosen against a reported manuscript expression. No manuscript image at these points was independently deciphered. The separate editor's exposition is not the author's original paragraph.

Reflective result: the strongest reading is not 'anything caused by God adopts every accompanying claim.' It is a purposive, scope-sensitive certification model. Its coherent conceptual structure still requires an evidentially established historical application. The source's necessity claims about divine action elsewhere in the same passage do not erase its own distinction between a power and its actual exercise.

## Printed 1064–1071 / S003L63–66: mediated causes and a received discontinuity
Printed 1064 explicitly says universal dependence upon God would distinguish no miracle from other events. Printed 1065 rejects requiring absence of causes or angelic mediation. Printed 1066–1067 illustrates angelic mediation and ordinary gestation. The conditional beginning at the end of 1067 then meets an abrupt transition at 1068; the edited main text begins '[وقال] يقرر التوحيد...', with note 1 reporting an unclear manuscript word and taking the selected reading from م and ط. The electronic version has 'فإنه يقرر'. Thus the discontinuity is not securely repaired by this edition. It is registered without invented missing text.

Printed 1070–1071 assigns creatures genuine acts while withholding independent production of the full effect: Gabriel's blowing, Christ's shaping of clay and life given by God. This bears directly on mediated causal contribution, but it should not be inflated into an independent proof that a plural original basis is impossible.

## Printed 1085–1087 / S003L71–72: answered need and prophetic continuity
Printed 1085 confirms the crucial qualification that God may answer a distressed unbeliever; bare request-response is not by itself religious authentication. Its editor quotes a researcher's classification of special versus general signs; that is a modern commentator's gloss. Printed 1086–1087 continues the next criterion: conformity with the common teaching of prior prophets. This provides the immediate sequel and shows how the author supplements an occurrence with content and an established comparison class. It does not establish the external history of every prior tradition.
