# The connected mathematical and foundational programme

Working synthesis for reconciliation against the source-bound track registers. This draft assigns no new theorem or validation credit.

## What is being preserved

Orthemology's occurrence-centred architecture distinguishes the particular case, the analysis under which it is described, the governing standard bound to that case, the actual handling episode, its result and its pathway. Its mathematical programme makes several of those distinctions exact. The foundational programme investigates what warrants the descriptions, sources and standards in the first place. Neither programme reduces to a vocabulary for the other.

The through-T20 development is especially coherent when read as a sequence of questions about representations and the operations they must support. A representation may retain the present answer yet fail to retain a source attribution, an admissible future correction, the probability of an informative observation, a temporal dependency, or a proof's shared cost. A different failure occurs when a proposed representation has no realising object at all. These are distinct failures, with different repairs.

## Existence before sufficiency

A moment hierarchy first needs to be the moments of an actual probability law. Positivity of individual coordinates, valid initial data or success at selected finite truncations does not establish that fact. Once a law exists, uniqueness, computational recovery and suitability for a proposed evolution remain additional questions. The positive full-moment recovery and restricted-family constructions therefore complement the nonrealisability controls; they are not exceptions to an undifferentiated assertion that finite or moment-based mathematics cannot work.

The analogous methodological order in the foundations is not a theorem transport from probability. An actual productive target, bearer or communication must be warranted before a formal model of its relations receives an actual-world interpretation. Mathematical consistency supplies an admissible formal interpretation. It does not itself supply extramental actuality, necessity through oneself or the source identity required by an argument. This is a methodological constraint shared by the inquiries, not an inference from a moment theorem to a metaphysical conclusion.

## Present equivalence and continuation equivalence

The older tensor and continuation results already ask which distinctions an observation or compression preserves. Higher-order relational information is not generally recoverable from lower-order marginals. Later examples give that boundary practical mathematical content: pairwise proof-overlap data need not determine total shared cost; identical current posteriors need not determine the probabilities of future branches; separately retained model ranges need not preserve their coupling; and equal coarse states need not determine equal projected velocities.

The common fibre discipline is task-relative. Two states may be merged only where the declared future operations and observations do not require distinguishing them. A sidecar can retain the missing information, but its minimality is relative to the specified operation family. A label carrying an acquisition root can preserve identity without authenticating that root or proving that a subject's belief was actually based on it.

The current type-theoretic findings sharpen this point. Internal proof equality and external derivation history answer different questions. Proof contraction within a specified calculus does not identify source histories, costs, permissions or a knower's operative grounds. Conversely, provenance bookkeeping cannot supply a missing typing or induction principle. The unchanged HasE and deliberately strengthened systems must therefore remain separately named throughout the synthesis.

## Correction is a structured operation

A scalar loss alone does not specify a correction. The state domain, metric, admissible directions, constraints, source evidence, time semantics and numerical method all matter. The geometry results show this positively by constructing genuine joint position-and-weight metrics, restricted invariant families and constrained corrections. They show it negatively by separating divergence contraction from autonomous gradient projectability, frozen-time coercivity from uniform nonautonomous control, and total-variation continuity from finite Fisher path length.

Evidence reweighting and state movement likewise differ. Their commutation requires the appropriate transported likelihood and vector field, not merely the use of Bayesian vocabulary. The universal labelled audit classification and its fixed-group extension identify exact symmetry conditions. Within-group interactions may survive a restricted audit family even where the stronger singleton family would exclude them. Shared provenance alone does not establish the likelihood equalities that define such a family.

The full-law positive approximation results supply constructive repairs at their declared scope. A discretisation must preserve genuine nonnegative normalised laws and the relevant objective and audit relationships. A local Euler counterexample does not show that every already implemented guarded scheme fails. Tail control, finite-order local error and positive-time endpoint convergence also require different hypotheses.

## Law preservation includes timing and coupling

Matching possible transitions is weaker than preserving a stochastic process. For timed jumps, the relevant object can be the joint law of holding time, event or outcome, and successor state. Separate marginals can agree while deadlines or accumulated costs differ. Similarly, a fixed unknown credal model across branches is not the same optimisation problem as allowing independent adversarial model selection at each branch.

The synthesis therefore uses typed dependency arrows. A formal entailment, source-supported philosophical premise, semantic interpretation, implementation refinement and methodological warning are not interchangeable edges. Compatible deterministic, stochastic and geometric contracts can be composed only when they refer to the same state and operation semantics. Their individual validity is not a general hybrid completeness theorem.

## Computability and attained proof are separate achievements

Constructing a finite guarded proof catalogue can turn an abstract existence statement into an attained minimum under stated finiteness, support and price conditions. Shared proof graphs require preserving actual node incidences; independently cheapest branches need not give the cheapest shared proof. Enumeration of finite derivations is different from a total decision procedure for semantic truth. Almost-sure finite stopping is different from stopping on every path.

The literal compiler bridge provides another concrete distinction. Ordinary universal correspondence already had an owner. Later source-lowered AST kernel results strengthen formal coverage and derive the actual prefix-retaining probability law and atomic-defect formula. That achievement neither disappears because the host-runtime bridge remains separate nor certifies Python execution and serialised bytes automatically.

## How this supports the affirmative foundations

The source-based positive appraisals have their own arguments: actual original productive actuality and its bearer, intrinsic necessity, a complete common original of the relevant connected order, and Knowledge, Will and Wisdom. Their current warranted scope must be read from the latest controlling reports, not reset by historical pending headers. Formal gluing and dependency calculations organise which conclusions depend on which premises; they do not independently warrant those premises.

Cumulative recognition and testimony need not be replaced by possession of a formal proof certificate. The source work distinguishes recognised expertise, truthful commission, actual attributed content and the relevant reporting scope. The latest Ibn al-Qayyim reading explicitly preserves both content-attribution and reporter-truthfulness premises. It also treats further seeing or acquaintance as an addition to already warranted knowledge. The mathematical representations can expose loss of those distinctions under revision without claiming to establish their psychological or metaphysical actuality.

Normative questions retain similar specificity. A source's authority, a command's authenticity and force, its recipient and its scope cannot be inferred from successful optimisation. Where an in-scope obligatory disclosure is already warranted, an outstanding deeper explanation of selection title does not automatically suspend that practical warrant. Where a historical or philosophical objection remains unanswered, the synthesis preserves it at its actual target rather than spreading uncertainty across unrelated results.

## The consolidated continuation

T21 should begin from this differentiated state: exact inherited results, current source-bound appraisals, executable components, failed transports and sharply stated remaining interfaces. It should not retry an identity in an unchanged calculus after a scoped nonderivability result, forget a genuine AST theorem because host semantics remain open, or treat source collection as independent validation. The value of consolidation is to make the next consequential investigation accessible without reconstructing the archive history.
