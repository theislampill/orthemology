# Exact representation-premise check

10 October 2026 UTC. A narrow check requested during review of the new own-act example. No earlier record is edited.

## Finding

No mandatory “every voluntary act requires a newly originating cognitive representation” premise was found in the exact controlling formulations. Accordingly there is no evidenced inherited per-act requirement to revoke. The new report's initial compressed reference to the origin of “a new intrinsic representation” could invite that reading and has been made explicitly conditional. This is a precision correction to the new report, not a new failure of the inherited positive architecture.

## Primary scope

The retained Majmūʿ16:300–305 extract was read completely for this check. At303 the author develops successive willing, prospective resolve and executive intent. At304 he distinguishes knowledge of what will be done, what is being done and what has been done, and positively endorses an additional cognitive determination. At305 he attributes the discussed cognitive-volitional representation's origination to will and power.

These positive claims must be preserved. They neither say that the original was previously ignorant of the intended act nor provide a canonical inventory of new representations corresponding to every act of self-praise, every spoken clause or every created effect. The general account cannot be converted into such an inventory without further premises. Equally, declining that conversion does not negate the author's additional intentional-temporal cognitive differentiation.

The self-praise report therefore introduces no new r. Its actual result is a source-attributed practical explanation of own-act content. The remaining intrinsic act/volition-individuation question does not automatically become a demand to explain the genesis of an extra cognitive representation. If an offered stronger explanation does posit one, the inherited conditional origin test applies normally.

No formal mathematics is changed. No unsupported mandatory premise was found in a theorem or contract, and no new demotion of inherited research status is claimed.
