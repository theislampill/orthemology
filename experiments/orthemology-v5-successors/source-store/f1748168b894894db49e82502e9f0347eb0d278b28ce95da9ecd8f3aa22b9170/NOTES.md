# Nubuwwat bounded token and purpose reinspection

7 October 2026. Direct untruncated reading of NB-S002-L0103–L0114 (the source-bound RR3 selection), plus complete visual reading of printed 770–772 and their apparatus. This overlaps the whole-book study and adds no first-pass coverage.

## The argument's actual distinctions

L103–105 distinguishes a trace that evidences its cause from a deliberately designated sign, and within intentional signs distinguishes prior convention from contextual recognition without prior agreement. A private ring, garment or secret can function as an attestation without a previously negotiated code, if the sender's situation makes that purpose intelligible. This is a positive account of contextual warrant, not a demand for an infinite certification chain.

L106 / printed 771 expressly gives two premises: the alleged sender actually sent the token, and sent it to certify the bearer. It considers theft, loss, the sender's death, unauthorised seal use, and authorised transfer for repair or another purpose. Strong relevant alternatives call for suspension; familiar circumstances can also exclude them with warranted confidence. The author is not treating every imaginable possibility as a permanent defeater.

L107 / printed 771 then distinguishes knowing that the messenger was sent from the messenger's possibly lying about what the sender says. The immediate token act certifies the claim of having been sent. The sender's responsibility and the content adopted by that act need exact interpretation; do not silently enlarge certification of mission into authentication of every reported sentence. This is especially useful for Orthemology's authority and occurrence interfaces.

L108–114 defines a demonstrative sign as entailing its indicated truth and attacks overbroad criteria such as a locally unmatched extraordinary event. That definition is not yet a decision procedure for recognising a particular event as such a sign. The author separately appeals to contextual knowledge, necessary recognition, evidence reaching such recognition, and knowledge of a speaker's established truthfulness. He therefore has both an inferential definition and proposed epistemic routes, neither of which should be omitted.

L111–112 explicitly permits warranted confidence in a familiar truthful messenger without an additional wonder. The possession of special tokens is not made the universally mandatory first route to belief. Conversely motives or inconsistent content can disconfirm a claimant, but this account supplies no warrant to diagnose any present dissenting person as dishonest or self-interested.

## Textual controls

Printed 770 substitutes bracketed بصاحبهم where its apparatus reports على حيهم in earlier print witnesses; the electronic line's awkward phrase is not silently repaired in the raw source. Printed 771 marks the suspension sentence as duplicated in the manuscript. The singular يكذب is editorially supplied from earlier prints against manuscript يكذبون. These are meaningful edition-layer facts, not evidence that the electronic body was independently collated to every manuscript word.

The reported seal misuse by Marwan and particular prophetic-life stories remain transmitted historical examples. This selected examination does not independently authenticate their histories. The conceptual distinctions can be analysed without treating each anecdote as newly proved.

## Research consequence

The passage materially supports a source-authentication model with distinct custody, intentional transfer, certification purpose, certified content and defeater fields. It supports ordinary contextual recognition as well as explicit argument. It supplies neither a fresh Lean theorem nor automatic certification of any actual contemporary or historical message. A future formalisation must preserve the narrower mission/content distinction and avoid defining every observed source-caused event as truth-entailing attestation.
