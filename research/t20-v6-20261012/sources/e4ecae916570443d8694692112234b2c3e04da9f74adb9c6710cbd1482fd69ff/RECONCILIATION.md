# Reconciliation: general Sixth refinement is an optional successor

7 October 2026. This addendum revises the priority/obligation classification in REPORT.md and matrix rows D/G, without changing any scientific result. It uses existing instructions, source and reviews only. No new code, proof, model or research campaign was undertaken.

## Classification

The general incidence-to-Sixth ancestry refinement is **not an established unmet mandatory obligation**. It is an **optional successor after substantial completion of the requested D/G/H attempt**. Its OPEN status must remain visible, but neither that label nor the synthesis calling it the “next formal task” establishes that pursuing it now would strengthen the answer enough to justify the cost.

My earlier descriptions “highest formal leverage” and “clearest remaining tractable formal question” overstated the comparative case. They identified a technically describable extension, not evidence that the current result needs it or that it outranks the later B5/B6 and fronts 3/4 gaps. The foundations audit's prioritisation is better supported.

## Exact owner wording and work already earned

D says to “Reuse rather than rediscover the strongest existing structural work from Sixth onward,” distinguish the listed ancestry/coverage concepts, test local-coverage propagation, and determine which part is mathematics versus application. It does not explicitly require an equivalence or general refinement between every new representation and Sixth's relation. The reuse map, existing Sixth theorem, general incidence theorem and explicit semantic obligations answer those requests without pretending the interfaces are identical.

G says “Try to formalise” the three attribution levels and test preservation through genuine derived causes without collapsing agency. The general incidence/use framework, concrete mixed-occurrence and same-bearer model, retained created agency, and separate assertion-ownership controls constitute that attempt. They do not establish every possible causal semantics, but the instruction does not demand such a result as the condition of completion.

H asks for the strongest faithful conditional approximately of the stated form and exact scope for “one.” The implemented field theorem supplies a genuine general conditional route that deliberately avoids needing Sixth LocalCover. It does not acquire an extra obligation merely because another valid route was historically used.

This conclusion would change if the delivery claimed the incidence theorem *was* Sixth's theorem, claimed arbitrary mediate-causation refinement, or silently omitted the input interpretation burden. The inspected final report expressly disclaims those strengthenings.

## Already tested boundary, not just a future question

The finite joint certificate does not become a general refinement. Nevertheless, it is more than a compatibility label. J05/J06 establish interpreted positive reachability and incoming inventory for the supplied model. The independent joining review then adds an h→m productive edge while leaving component accounts unchanged. The theorems omitted_h_breaks_stored_reachability and omitted_h_breaks_joining_inventory show exactly what fails: the stored ancestry and the joining inventory no longer represent that incoming contributor.

Thus “can an unrepresented incoming branch invalidate the application even while the component formulas remain true?” already has a concrete checked answer. Another generic adapter or repetition of this omission control would not add a new discrimination. The existing result already earns a precise application boundary.

## What a genuinely new successor would have to ask

A useful general refinement would need a selected independently meaningful causal/process semantics that the current application actually needs to support, and a proposed representation whose adequacy is not already assumed. A possible future question is whether that semantics' independently justified local accounting conditions entail provenance preservation across transformations and complete ancestor coverage, rather than merely target-output coverage. Its value would lie in discovering a weaker sufficient bridge or a counterexample preserving those independently motivated conditions.

No such new selected interface or failed application is identified in the present audit record. Introducing one merely to create a fresh theorem would be speculative extension. Accordingly, there is **no demonstrated new discriminating question here that presently requires reopening this lane**. If the B5/B6 continuation yields a richer agency/production interpretation that genuinely needs the mapping, reassess then.

Keep the general-refinement boundary explicitly OPEN; treat the D/G/H attempt as substantially completed at its stated interface; do not reopen solely to remove that label. Resume the concrete B5/B6 and fronts 3/4 gaps first. The B5 continuation directly revisits T17 pure-perfection P65–68, so the optional ascent-interface question can be addressed there without starting a separate ceremonial proof or adapter campaign. The lack of a general adapter neither invalidates the existing theorem nor, on this evidence, defeats the bounded completion judgement.
