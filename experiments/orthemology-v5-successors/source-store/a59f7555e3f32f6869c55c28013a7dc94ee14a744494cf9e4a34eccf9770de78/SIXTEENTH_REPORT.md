# Orthemology Sixteenth Research Report

Research report · 5 October 2026

## What this tranche changes

The Sixteenth Tranche distinguishes three claims that had remained too easy to slide between: a succession of successful finite handoffs, the existence of some infinite continuation, and the availability of one effective policy that can keep making the required choices. Its central result gives an explicit setting in which every requested finite horizon can be planned, and an infinite mathematical path exists, yet no computable policy can commit to an infinite surviving lineage. The positive companion identifies exactly what effective closed-region evidence would suffice.

For the separately specified finite rational controller class, an independently accepted complementary development supplies complete signed (positive-or-negative) certification and direct submitted-witness controller extraction. It connects the admitted certificate to the returned policy and the original probability law.

A further result closes a carefully delimited identity problem. A language of one-variable natural-number expressions with addition, multiplication and nested equality tests has a complete, executable equality checker. Its successful checks do not automatically become proofs in the inherited identity calculus. Sixteenth proves a concrete obstruction in that calculus, then supplies a separate finite-certificate extension with full rule soundness and an exact empty-context completeness result for this expression language.

These findings connect continuation, provenance and copying to the computability and type-theoretic avenues. They also establish a useful limit on the verification programme: an abundance of correct finite checks cannot substitute for the particular effective or inferential bridge that a larger claim requires.

## The short explanation

### ELI5

Imagine a branching road. For any number you name, a planner can find a safe route that many steps long. But a route chosen for ten steps may conflict with the route chosen for twenty. It does not follow that the planner can pick one road now and keep extending that same road safely forever.

Sixteenth builds an exact mathematical example of that problem. It also explains what would fix it: a trustworthy, searchable collection of safe positions in which every position has another safe position to move to. Checking a few more steps ahead does not generally supply that collection.

For a separately specified finite kind of system, the result is constructive. A checked plan can now be turned into a working chooser, with a proof about the chooser actually returned. Alternatively, a complete checked record can show that no successful chooser exists within that model. The search can still be far too large to run practically.

The identity work has a similar lesson. Two little programs can always give the same answer while an existing proof language cannot certify that equality. We identify a precise case, then build a separate proof-language extension that accepts finite certificates and prove that its rules remain sound.

These results alone do not decide whether two people are numerically the same person, whether a real organisation has sufficient resources to continue, or whether an ultimate source exists. Those questions require their own evidence.

### ELI14

The crucial difference is the order of the requirements. “For every finite horizon, there is a computable plan” lets the chosen plan change when the requested horizon changes. “There is one computable policy that works indefinitely” commits to one compatible sequence of decisions. The first statement does not entail the second, even when every individual plan passes the declared checks.

The example also survives a stronger local rule: at every handoff, demand a genuine finite amount of further capacity, with the amount computed from the full history and allowed to grow without any fixed bound. That is still a finite lookahead condition. It is not the same as proving that the successor lies inside an effectively usable region closed under its own continuation rule.

The finite controller result belongs to a different class from the unbounded branching-road counterexample. It starts with finitely many candidate models, states and actions, but its controller may retain an unbounded history. Its positive proof follows the actual submitted witnesses into an actual policy; its negative proof covers all admitted policies, including randomised ones. Finite plant size does not mean finite controller memory.

For the expression language, a complete decision procedure does exist. Each expression eventually agrees with a natural-coefficient polynomial, with only finitely many exceptional input values. That description can be normalised and compared. Unequal descriptions yield a concrete distinguishing input. The calculus extension turns that finite semantic evidence into an admitted identity proof under precisely stated conditions.

## Effective continuation and preserved competence

### The counterexample

The construction uses a decidable, prefix-closed binary tree. Its finite membership test is effective; each requested finite level has an admitted member; and there is an infinite path in the ordinary mathematical sense. A diagonal argument prevents any computable function from producing an infinite path through this tree.

The implemented construction uses the actual bounded evaluation interface of the underlying partial-recursive code system. The evaluation bound is not described as a literal count of hardware instructions or Turing-machine steps. A separate committed-output argument turns an allegedly successful effective continuation process into a computable path. Its totality depends on the process really making unbounded committed progress, rather than on a finite successful test.

The continuation model carries a delayed semantic state, declared source lineage, a support boundary, and independently supplied permission. It does not obtain its negative result by silently withdrawing permission or changing the external input to something noncomputable. The fixed adverse example is always authorised and receives only a fixed computable stream of zero service inputs. Claims about a real organisation would additionally require evidence that its actual resources, causal organisation and authorisation satisfy the declared model.

This distinguishes a missing coordinate from a failure of all continuity. A completed finite handoff may genuinely preserve the specified semantic role and model-level lineage. The result says that those achievements alone do not establish an effective indefinitely repeatable renewal faculty. It does not refute a continuation criterion that already requires, and independently warrants, that very faculty.

The computability obstruction is classical. Jockusch and Soare study nonempty effective classes without computable members and represent such classes through recursive-tree paths. The contribution here is the explicit continuation-contract application and its scope, not a new general computability theorem. [Degrees of members of Π⁰₁ classes](<https://msp.org/pjm/1972/40-3/pjm-v40-n3-p09-s.pdf>).

### The exact positive condition

For this tree model, effective indefinite continuation is equivalent to the existence of an appropriate rooted, pruned subtree with decidable or computably enumerable membership. The word “pruned” carries the essential closure requirement: every admitted node has a successor inside the same admitted region. An effective enumeration then lets the continuing subject search for a successor whose membership is warranted.

This is an exact mathematical criterion, not an algorithm that decides whether every proposed organisation possesses it. The criterion does not manufacture physical resources, validate arbitrary source interpretations or certify every purported enumerator. When an actual case supplies the required effective and causal warrant, the criterion tells us why that warrant is enough at the stated scope.

### Finite lookahead

Let a total computable function assign a finite lookahead demand to each finite history. Strengthen admission by requiring every prefix, including the final handoff, to have an extension in the base tree of the demanded length. Membership remains decidable by finite search. The strengthened tree has exactly the same infinite paths as the original tree, so it still admits no computable infinite committed lineage.

The demand may depend on the whole history, vary between branches and grow arbitrarily quickly. There is no claim of practical efficiency. The terminating code for the demand is part of the assumptions; the checker does not solve arbitrary program totality.

The supplementary witnesses concern the base service. They need not themselves preserve the strengthened rule at every intermediate node. Requiring that recursive preservation would introduce the very closed-region obligation under discussion. The formal and written statements keep those two contracts distinct.

### Randomisation and populations

The written randomised argument strengthens this particular diagonal example: a computable fair-coin transducer produces an infinite path through it with probability zero. This is not a statement that every noncomputable path class has probability zero or that randomisation never helps a different continuation problem.

A further independently reviewed written synthesis locates the general probability-one boundary. For any fixed admitted-history tree, an effective private-coin machine that succeeds almost surely supplies an enumerable pruned output-support subtree and therefore a computable deterministic path. A separate construction also finds a computable successful input tape for the original machine, under the same promise. It does not merely fix an arbitrary seed and hope that seed succeeds.

The threshold is sharp. For each prescribed probability strictly below one, there is a decidable tree with no computable path in which fair-coin copying succeeds above that threshold. Thus arbitrarily high success probability and probability-one success have different effective consequences in this exact setting. These are fixed-tree private-randomisation results, not a general removal of randomness from policies facing external stochastic or adversarial choices. The full constructions and their resource and authority limits appear in the companion.

A population can change the continuation service. All admitted nodes at each finite level can be retained, creating an expanding population without computably designating one infinite surviving lineage. The written bounded-population argument rules out the corresponding closed-generation service when its width is bounded. It does not equate a bound on simultaneous activity with a bound on generations that may later be reopened, backtracked or recovered from checkpoints.

The object whose continuity is claimed therefore matters: one irrevocable lineage, an expanding population and a revisable search history are different objects. Credit earned for one should be retained at its own scope.

## Limits of general policy certification

The written classification separates several decision problems that sound similar in ordinary language. For syntax-total primitive-recursive binary-tree presentations, having nodes at every finite level is a lower arithmetical-complexity problem than having a computable infinite path. The latter is ${\Sigma{}}_{3}^{0}$-complete, including a restricted family in which every presented tree is mathematically infinite. Validation of a supplied ordinary computable path or suitable enumerator is ${\Pi{}}_{2}^{0}$-complete; validation of a supplied primitive-recursive policy has a different, ${\Pi{}}_{1}^{0}$ classification. Arbitrary decider presentations require an explicit invalid-input convention and have their own infinitude classification.

The formal package proves a substantial reduction underlying the computable-path obstruction. It constructs progress cuts, reset components and a noncomputable skeleton, then gives a primitive-recursive parameter transformation for a fixed computable relation. Every resulting tree in that reduction is valid and mathematically infinite. The existence of a computable path is equivalent to the selected quantified row-totality condition.

The full hierarchy normal-form argument and all seven written classifications are not thereby kernel-verified. Nor does the fixed-relation coding theorem export an arbitrary function together with its proof as native executable code. These boundaries are stated in the mathematical companion and source map.

An important consequence is that there can be no sound and complete effective system of finite positive certificates for computable-policy existence throughout that unbounded class. Useful sound but incomplete proof systems remain possible. This does not contradict the separate finite rational controller class from Ninth, whose positive existence certificate theorem has a different domain and whose finite descriptions may still support unbounded empirical memory.

The policy-existence classification has classical antecedents. Gasarch’s author-hosted [A Survey of Recursive Combinatorics](<https://www.cs.umd.edu/~gasarch/COURSES/752/S22/notes/rcombsur.pdf>), Definition 3.5 and Theorem 3.10, gives third-level existential completeness for recursive-branch indices. The present proof fixes the exact input presentation and organisational contract.

Cenzer and Remmel’s author-hosted [Effectively Closed Sets](<https://people.clas.ufl.edu/cenzer/files/Closed-Sets-Book-2017.pdf>), draft dated 3 May 2017, Definition 5.1.1 and Theorem 5.3.1, gives the fixed-bounded computable-member classification and credits Gasarch–Martin. ### Complete negative evidence

Every accepted positive body has dimension-dependent finite bounds on its paths, component links, node obligations and support nodes. The negative certificate contains an indexed refutation for every enumerated body. A false conjunction supplies a failing part; a false disjunction covers both alternatives; a failed bounded existential accounts for all listed candidates. Atomic leaves recompute exact arithmetic, equality, order or membership. There is no leaf asserting a semantic failure merely because an old solver said so.

### The actual returned controller

## A complete scoped identity language

### What the language contains

The selected language has one natural-number input, natural-number constants, addition, multiplication, and arbitrarily nested equality conditionals. Both sides of the equality test and both result branches may themselves be expressions in the language. The result is neither a global equivalence solver for arbitrary programs nor completeness for the entire inherited type theory.

Every expression denotes a function that eventually agrees with a polynomial with natural coefficients, except at finitely many inputs. Conversely, every function with such a description is expressible in the language. A canonical finite description consists of the unique eventual polynomial together with the finite exceptional values that genuinely differ from it.

Normalisation computes this description. Comparing descriptions decides extensional equality, and a failed comparison produces an input on which the expressions differ. The development also treats composition, including the important case in which an inner expression is eventually constant: the outer expression must then be evaluated at that constant using its complete value, not just its eventual polynomial.

The formal checker uses a deliberately conservative coefficient bound. The written proof presents a different signed-polynomial root estimate. Those are separate justified bounds; their numerical formulas are not presented as identical implementations.

### Why semantic equality did not already close the calculus

The literal compiled terms for the input expression and that input plus zero are extensionally equal under the specified semantics. They remain distinct normal forms in the inherited proof calculus. The confluence and identity-conversion results therefore exclude every proof witness of that identity in the current Has and HasPlus systems, not merely a short list of attempted witnesses.

The conclusion does not cover HasE. It does not depend on silently identifying different raw endpoints or replacing the current conversion relation. Its force is precisely that a correct semantic equality theorem and a complete checker do not, by themselves, supply an inhabitant in a calculus that has no corresponding introduction route.

### The certificate extension

The separate HasC development copies the inherited context, formula and proof rules with their original premises, then adds one introduction rule for a checked finite canonical certificate. It leaves the original conversion relation unchanged. A mutual proof establishes soundness for the full extended rule family, including dependent identity elimination, rather than checking only the new rule in isolation.

The semantic bridge is quantified over the relevant valuations. Empty-context evaluation is used only where the proof actually permits it; it is not substituted for related arbitrary environments. For the selected literal closed endpoints, arbitrary HasC proof existence, the corresponding semantic identity, and checker acceptance coincide in the empty context.

An unrestricted converse for arbitrary contexts would be false. The positive controls include a context carrying a false identity assumption to expose that boundary. The result claims neither conservativity over all old-language statements nor a general completeness theorem for arbitrary expressions, contexts or semantic domains.

The notation for finite type codes concerns finite type syntax. It does not imply that natural numbers or function spaces have finite cardinality.

## Foundational warrant

### The conditional temporal result

The original temporal analysis fixes one actual bearer and asks about its absence from an entire history. If every genuine possibility of that whole absence has at least one witness in an actually possessed power at a time in the specified order, and such a witness cannot undo the bearer’s already settled presence, the available witness times become decisive. This requires the selected temporal coverage premise, not a claim that every power or every witness is temporal.

With present-and-past preservation, an absence witness must precede every actual presence of that bearer. A bearer present at an attained first time therefore has no eligible absence witness. With strict past preservation alone, presence at the first time is insufficient because the equality case remains open. A separate beginningless route works when the same bearer has some actual presence before every proposed witness time.

These are conditional same-bearer results, not merely claims that some bearer fills a role. To obtain a necessary actual contributor to the selected correction, its actual contribution must also be warranted. Neither result establishes intrinsic existential sufficiency, sole sourcehood, essential truthfulness or the necessity of the correction itself. The full proof and exact temporal assumptions are in the mathematical companion.

### Temporal powers and the same bearer

Kimpton-Nye defends initial iterated potentialities covering later capacities’ ultimate manifestations. His derivation invokes witnesses of capacities’ origins, but his account also allows retrospective potentialities about completed events. These need not occupy the origin-enabling role used in that derivation. The initial-coverage claim retains explanatory motivation; it has not thereby been derived from the unrestricted possibility clause. His same-bearer necessity route works under present-and-past fixity and an attained first instant, rather than a finite age alone. Even granted initial modal coverage, actual productive participation in the correction remains a separate question. Werner’s later proposal expressly fixes the witness’s present as well as its past and separately requires earlier support for later potentiality content. This makes the strengthened temporal assumptions explicit. His interpretation of embedded modality preserves reference to actual objects and distinguishes a potentiality’s possessors from objects constituting its content. That provides a developed response to an S5 challenge, without turning every referenced object into a necessary existent. ### Nature unity

O’Connor’s 2004 identification argument begins with an ultimate necessary being and expressly distinguishes existence of itself from necessary causal reception. His 2010 reply presses for an intelligible connection between necessity and intrinsic nature rather than a brute assignment to heterogeneous states. These are positive explanatory considerations. They do not identify the temporal argument’s necessary participant as unreceived. Nor does differing intrinsic character alone establish contingent attachment of necessity. The stronger original-source grant changes the comparison: an actually received bearer then violates the premises. Actual originality, essential nonreception and intrinsic existential sufficiency still require their respective warrant. [2004, pp. 419–423](<https://toconnor.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/OConnor_And-this-All-Men-Call-God-2004.pdf>); [2010, pp. 311–313](<https://toconnor.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/OConnor_IsGodsNecessity10.pdf>).

### Productive unity

Aquinas’s commentary treats matter–form unity through potency and act while retaining efficient generation and allowing nonmotion existential causation. An intrinsic account of unity therefore need not eliminate productive explanation. The direct-primary comparison sharpens an already owned distinction rather than establishing a new actual supplier. A cause numerically distinct from a composite need not lie outside every constituent; the exclusion concerns an exercise presupposing the very actuality it would supply. [Sententia libri Metaphysicae VIII lectio 5](<https://www.corpusthomisticum.org/cmp07.html>).

### Warrant and fixed premises

A substantive basic judgement or a well-supported comparative explanation can warrant an actual metaphysical conclusion. Neither inquiry makes a neutral deduction, complete knowledge of the bearer’s constitution, or conclusive refutation of every described rival a prerequisite for all warrant. Describing a rival does not establish its metaphysical possibility or give it equal evidential standing.

The target must nevertheless remain fixed. A necessary contributor and a bearer already granted whole actual unreceivedness are different starting points. A criticism that assigns an actual supplier to the latter abandons that stronger grant. Conversely, actual nonreception must not silently become essential nonreception or an account of unconditional instantiation. The revised assessments preserve both the affirmative explanatory reasons and these differences of content. They add source-sensitive scrutiny without newly settling the original actual-warrant obligations.
