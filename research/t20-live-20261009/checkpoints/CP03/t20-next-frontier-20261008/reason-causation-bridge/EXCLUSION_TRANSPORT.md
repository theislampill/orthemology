# Why two exclusion arguments do not automatically transport

This analysis directly compares the current nonabsorption, wise-determination and warrant-standard-review reports. It reuses their source evidence and does not claim another primary-source discovery.

## The tempting shared form

Both discussions can be compressed into: if one basis already completely produces an effect, what causal work remains for another? That verbal resemblance is not a faithful transport.

The T20 common-source question concerns numerically distinct, independently original bearers, their own complete effective undertakings, a specified created product and independently justified norms against foreign completion or duplicate exclusive production. The report explicitly distinguishes a standing power from its complete actual efficacy, and an extra intrinsic act from an extra created effect.

The mind/brain question can concern two descriptions or properties of one dependent event. Physical sufficiency is commonly stated conditional on laws, current environment, organization and initial state. It does not by itself say that this local basis originally supplies its laws, recipients, actuality and every condition of the whole product.

## Failed premise map

A proposed map from “physical cause” to “complete original provider” fails at several exact points:

1. A brain state or algorithm is not thereby an independently original bearer.
2. A reason-level description has not thereby been shown to name a second numerically distinct producer. Identity and realization accounts specifically deny that assumption.
3. Physical sufficiency conditional on a background is weaker than full original productive provision of that background and its actual efficacy.
4. A norm concerning foreign original completion does not automatically forbid real created mediation or multiple levels of explanation. The common-source appraisal expressly retains created secondary agency.
5. A mental causal contribution, if real, need not be an additional created token over and above the token under its physical description. Requiring another such token would pre-decide the disputed ontology.

Therefore the original-source exclusion cannot be imported to prove that a physical explanation excludes a mental one. To make that import valid one would first need the very numerical-distinctness and productive-independence premises missing from the mind/brain case. Even a dualist account ordinarily treats finite minds and brains as dependent, so the original-peer premise would still be absent.

This rejects the original-peer import, not every property-level causal-exclusion argument. Even if one token event is both mental and physical, it remains a substantive question whether its mental properties make the required causal difference. Numerical identity or dependent realization alone does not settle that question. The separate content-causation bridge in INDEPENDENT_ASSESSMENT.md remains open.

The reverse import fails too. Demonstrating compatible descriptions of a single process does not show that two independently original agents can jointly produce the entire same created work while each retains the source's required complete efficacy. The implementation contains no original agents and meets none of those stronger eligibility commitments.

## What does transport

A methodological constraint does carry over: response sensitivity is not sufficient evidence of the claimed kind of actual production.

The nonabsorption report shows that local positive derivatives need not yield finite augmentation. Here formula-register sensitivity likewise does not establish content's causal role *as grasped by a subject*. Each observation can be genuine while its stronger interpretation is unearned. Neither result denies the stronger property wherever independent grounds establish it.

The wise-determination report supplies a second precise distinction. A conditional means certificate is not a complete normative justification or a full particular determining basis. Likewise, a sound inference engine is not the warrant for its own inputs or an account of why an actual subject accepted them. A causally sufficient determining basis can include reasons, willing and created means without equating those with an optimization score or rendering the means idle. The source's complete-cause vocabulary cannot simply be translated as “physics already did everything, so reasons do nothing.”

Finally the warrant-standard review prevents a standards error. Formal non-entailment is not a blanket defeater of a substantive, defeasibly warranted metaphysical judgement. The present failed transport neither downgrades the common-source appraisal nor upgrades a naturalist alternative to equal warrant. It shows that the two arguments address different relata and require different positive support.

## Result

There is no direct content-preserving reduction between the two exclusion claims. Their useful connection is the discipline of fixing the bearer, product, background, relevant dependence and meaning of completeness before drawing an exclusion. No metaphysical conclusion follows merely because both discussions use the phrase “two causes.”
