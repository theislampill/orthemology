# Existential warrant and intrinsic explanation

## Current result

N2 remains open. The positive argument gives a contentful reason to seek an intrinsic existential explanation, but the actual evidence does not establish that every ultimate existential question has an answer. Its successful completion would require productive completion, identification of a particular bearer, and actual warrant that this bearer's nature excludes absence. These remain distinct burdens.

The fresh advance is a more precise examination of what either side must establish. Efficient self-production, constitutive explanation, and the contemporary alternatives registered in SOURCE_GUIDE.md require different objections. The same standards apply to a proposed necessary source and to a contingent active basis. An explanation's intelligibility does not establish its actual truth, and actual truth does not establish necessity.

This is the current distribution projection of the repaired N2 research. The original reports remain unchanged and are identified in ORIGINAL_TO_PUBLIC.json. Historical report bodies and source-review bodies are omitted from this package. The projection makes no new scientific claim and does not establish cognition, truthfulness, unity, revelation, restoration, or metaphysical possibility.

## 1 The actual target and its warrant

The Sixth local-correction case concerns an earlier report that a displayed Minḥāj page did not identify its editor or edition. The accepted review corrected that report. The original document remained frozen, so the correction did not silently change its historical evidence. In this restart the exact local record was read and its hash checked. The host with the www prefix was also reopened successfully. Its header again displayed the editor Muḥammad Rashād Sālim, publisher Muʾassasat Qurṭuba, first edition, 1406 AH, and eight parts. An initial attempt without www failed; that failure was not treated as evidence that the metadata was absent. [Displayed host header](https://www.fnoor.com/main/articles.aspx?article_no=32248), returned lines 33–37.

The warranted conclusion is that the host displays identifiable metadata. It is not that the transcription has been collated against a physical first edition. This distinction supplies a particularly useful actual target: the reason for the correction is a real difference between what was claimed about a definite page and what the page displays. Its truth is not manufactured by the evaluator's preference.

Let e denote this actual correction under the admitted philosophical reading that it is a genuinely warranted cognitive exercise. The record demonstrates the evidence-supported correction as a human-readable episode. It does not by itself demonstrate consciousness or original cognitive ownership in every mechanism involved. Nothing in the argument depends on falsely promoting a machine trace into such a demonstration.

At the local level, the episode uses resources it does not itself originally supply: the page's displayed characters, accessible records of the earlier claim, the evidential comparison and capacities for reading and inference. Not all these dependencies are of one type. The page determines whether the report about its header is accurate; that is a truth and reference relation. The reading supplies claim-specific evidence; that is a warrant relation. Actual devices, persons and their capacities enable or produce the exercise; those are causal or constitutive relations. Their joint involvement does not make them one relation or one ultimate bearer.

Both sides in the comparison below must preserve this actual correction as warranted. A proposal that merely regenerates the correct names by luck, denies the page as the actual target, or treats every cognitive source as an inert relay has changed the case. Conversely, the success of this correction does not show that all of its remote causes have one metaphysical explanation. The local evidence fixes a real target without quietly closing the ultimate question.

The retained route distinguishes actual exercise E0, actual reception E1, productive completion P1, particular-bearer identification P2, explanatory aptness N1, exhaustive nonbrute existential adequacy N2 and unconditional existential sufficiency N3. The names matter less than their separate burdens. Knowing that some resource was received does not prove that all reception terminates. The predecessor also distinguishes complete endpoint production from complete actual origination. That distinction remains binding. A being with sufficient power to produce e, even every endpoint in a field, has not thereby been shown to provide every actual productive occurrence by which those endpoints came to be. Origin closure concerns that stronger target. This paper does not use N2 to repair a missing origin-closure argument or to manufacture a common source.

These are retained results. The fresh inquiry begins where the predecessor itself invited further work: can the actual existential explanation of an original bearer be warranted, and what exactly counts as its own intrinsic explanation?

## 3 A connected positive argument

### Actual supply is the starting point

The actual exercise cannot be supplied by an uninstantiated specification. If a capacity really produces or supports this correction, the relevant bearer or basis must be actual in the respect needed for its contribution. This is not an inference from imagining an ideal source. It begins with a concrete episode and the independently warranted dependencies of that episode.

The fact that the recipient genuinely owns and exercises a power is compatible with that power's existence being received. A developed source argument should welcome real derivative agency. A person who understands a text need not be reduced to a passive transmission point in order for that person's existence and powers to have an origin.

### Productive completion remains an additional claim

The affirmative next seeks an account of the whole actual supply, not just a separate answer at each local link. Its reason is that relocating an acknowledged received resource can extend its history without changing its received manner of possession. An original actual source would contribute what an endless description of still-received supply appears not to provide: actuality owned without another supply being presupposed for that very possession.

The affirmative must defend why complete productive provision requires original possession even against that account. We preserve P1 rather than pretending that source actuality alone establishes it.

### The bearer must actually be identified

Suppose productive completion succeeds. P2 must still identify a particular g as the bearer of the original actuality used in explaining e. Joint sufficiency does not establish individual sufficiency. A necessary role need not have the same occupant in every possibility. See also the whole-priority comparison M04 in SOURCE_GUIDE.md.

Let g therefore be the particular bearer delivered by an independently defended P2. This is a conditional stage, not a declaration that the present evidence has delivered g. Its actuality and originality must concern the same existence whose necessity is eventually concluded.

### The positive reason for existential adequacy

The strongest N2 rationale is that the ultimate explanation should not merely leave its indispensable existential resource unaccounted for. If g is genuinely contingent, g's actual presence and complete possible absence form a real existential contrast. A catalogue of what g can do when present does not answer that contrast. Calling the boundary fundamental identifies where explanation stops; it does not show why stopping there is adequate.

There are three positive reasons to seek an intrinsic answer. First, the original inquiry was occasioned by actual received being, not a wish that reality satisfy an arbitrary aesthetic preference. Second, the underlying question remains intelligible at the proposed ultimate resource: why is the actuality being used available at all? Third, an adequate answer through g's own nature would close the demand without borrowing another actual resource or making g cause itself. This gives the proposal a determinate explanatory benefit.

These reasons justify the inquiry and can support a defeasible preference for a successful intrinsic account over an otherwise comparable candid brute. They do not establish an unconditional right to an answer. Nor is the benefit earned simply by postulating that g has whatever nature would supply it. The proposed nature must be independently characterized and warranted. The working proposal's earlier formulation as a presumption of existential intelligibility is consequently retained only as a rationalist motive; it is not promoted to N2.

The Aṣfahāniyya passage supplies the historical positive rationale: being through oneself is presented as one's own reality sufficing for existence. The surrounding argument distinguishes bare necessary existence from proving the intended creator. MF 2:9–11 places the need/sufficiency contrast in particular recognition rather than requiring a prior universal syllogism. These are actual primary commitments, not a consensus or a substitute for contemporary warrant. The supplied English Aṣfahāniyya translation was also visually checked at printed pages 25–26, PDF pages 32–33. Its sufficiency and ipseity discussion matches the topic of the Arabic passage, while one sentence renders the Arabic modal phrase about admitting nonexistence as actual nonexistence; the argument here retains the Arabic modal force. This is a narrow translation observation, not a general judgment on the translation. [Aṣfahāniyya](https://www.islamicbook.ws/ageda/alaqidt-alasfhanit-001.html), relevant discussion at returned lines 61–76; [MF 2:9–11](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/22/131/?idfrom=&idto=&start=), returned lines 944–950.

### Intrinsic intelligibility without self-production

The affirmative should not say that g exercises a power which first makes its whole bearer actual. An actual exercise would already require actuality of g in the very respect supposedly being originated. It should instead say that g's manner of being is intrinsically existential: g does not wait for a further actualizer because the reality g is does not leave its existence open in that way.

This concerns what g is, not a productive event before g exists. MF 2:9–11 permits saying that no further ʿilla is present and referring need or sufficiency to the reality itself. That primary flexibility does not determine one modern explanation ontology. The comparison in SOURCE_GUIDE.md, M01, must therefore be read as a philosophical test, not an attribution of contemporary terminology or fact ontology to the primary author.

If this very g has an intrinsic status that unconditionally excludes its absence, g exists necessarily. This is a de re claim about the fixed bearer. Actual factivity alone is insufficient: the existence-entailing force and its attribution to g both need warrant. The fact that no producer has been observed supplies neither.

### Why contingent acts can remain contingent

The existence of g and a particular exercise a of g are different explanatory targets. To explain a, citing g as its already actual subject is legitimate. To explain the whole actuality of g, citing g's already exercised power is not a nonpresupposing origin. Thus rejecting self-production of g does not reject g's contingent activity.

The necessary existence of g does not establish every item of g's complete actual productive determination as necessary. The supplied Oneness translation, printed page 34/PDF page 41, discusses effect-necessity given complete will and power; it rejects an essence stripped of them. This supports distinguishing the actual complete determination from bare source existence. It does not independently prove the metaphysical contingency of particular divine volitions. ANALYTIC_SUPPLEMENTS.md gives the conditional inference check.

The connected positive conclusion is exact: an actual received exercise, independently established original productive completion, an identified particular bearer, and a warranted intrinsic nature excluding that bearer's absence would yield a necessary particular source. The reconstruction explains the role of each stage. It does not obtain the missing actual-world premises from that explanation.

## 4 The intrinsic-explanation test

The first question is what the proposed intrinsic item actually is. An instantiated character can explain an exercise while already depending on the bearer's actuality. If it is offered as an efficient exercise originating that same whole actuality, the objection of presupposing its subject applies. A constitutive explanation need not be earlier or independent, so that efficient objection does not automatically reject it.

## 5 A developed rival with real activity

### The actual local account

Let C be the proposed actual basis of the field containing e. It contains actual cognitive bearers, text-bearing structures, powers, evidence and relations of intentional inspection. Its derivative bearers are real. Their knowledge, will and power remain wherever genuinely intentional production is granted. Normative correctness still concerns the actual evidence and target; it is not whatever C happens to produce.

The correction occurs because a reader accesses the displayed header, understands its labels, compares them with the earlier report, and withdraws the false absence claim for that reason. This is the same local explanatory target preserved by the affirmative. It neither proves that C is the ultimate basis nor makes that proposal evidence-free at every local level.

In the holistic version, C's complete intrinsic condition explains its differentiated parts' conditions. Whole-priority does not make those parts unreal or the whole numerically simple. C might be indefinitely divisible without a smallest passive instrument. This application is our proposal for the admitted case; it has not established either C's actual priority or its existential necessity.
<!-- charge M04 end -->

### The candid contingent version

The cleanest N2 denier says that C's existence is primitive and contingent. Its intrinsic nature explains its operations; it does not explain its own actuality rather than absence. This is a cost the rival acknowledges. It rejects the claim that a real existential question is guaranteed an answer.

The affirmative cannot refute this view by saying C would have to create itself. It offers no self-creation. It cannot refute it by presenting an absent world as a false-belief situation either; if C and all thinkers are absent there is no mistaken thinker. Nor is the rival required to deny local evidential warrant. A primitive ontological boundary does not make every derivative belief accidental.

Its strength is that it preserves the inspected case and accepts a modest stopping point without positing an independently inaccessible necessary nature. Its weakness is precisely that it has no ultimate existential answer. Whether the explanatory deficit outweighs the costs of a rival intrinsic account is a comparative question. The present evidence does not make that tradeoff disappear.

### A noncausal completion

A stronger rival proposes an autonomous principle A with realization content: some eligible concrete field exists in virtue of A, while this particular C remains contingent. An axiarchic version gives objective value existential significance. Truth-directed cognition is a candidate good, but does not establish the eligibility of the whole universe. The rival must defend the value-to-realization relation and its application, not merely name them. Its local intentional and productive account remains the explanation of e. A is not an efficient agent. Under broad existential dependence, C is received, so this proposal contests identifying the autonomous explanation with a concrete original efficient bearer; it is not a refutation after granting C broad originality. This construction is ours; the cited sources do not establish it.

This proposal has an ultimate explanatory ambition that the candid contingent version declines. The decision between them and the affirmative requires actual warrant for the proposed ultimate commitments. Detailed specification makes the disagreement assessable; it does not make its premises true. The supplementary tests locate the evidence each account still owes.

A constraint can explain which histories are allowed without establishing that any concrete history is realized. The positive account promises an answer to the actuality of the productive resources. Its intrinsic nature must earn that promise. The active contingent account explains local production while declining the ultimate existential answer. The noncausal proposal accepts the further question but incurs the burden attached to its alleged realization principle. Neither labeling an account complete nor labeling it brute decides comparative merit.

The positive result is stronger only if its additional explanation is true and warranted. Its proposed gain cannot be purchased by stipulating a nature that contains whatever clauses the intended conclusion requires.

## 8 The exact surviving denial

The direct N2 denial is that this actual original field exists contingently and its existential presence has no further explanation. That denial preserves the correction and local evidence-sensitive agency. Its cost is an unexplained ultimate existential boundary. It offers no self-creation and creates no mistaken thinker in a situation where no thinkers exist.

The strongest nonbrute challenge is to the inference that every adequate ultimate explanation must be the intrinsically existence-entailing nature of one fixed concrete active bearer. Its two versions are the realization proposal in §5 and the fourth construction in SOURCE_GUIDE.md, M01. Each has explicit commitments requiring warrant. Neither is established as metaphysically possible merely because it survives the objections considered here.

The affirmative therefore needs actual ultimate existential intelligibility and the appropriate form of intrinsic explanation. The displayed correction supplies neither by itself. The primary method of particular recognition might be defended as a route, but extending that method to this ultimate bearer requires an account of its object, epistemic entitlement and defeaters.

A genuine advance would supply that actual identification and contentful nature, or a discriminating comparative reason to prefer it. A larger formal model, a useful role-description or the desirability of explanation cannot substitute for such evidence.

## 10 Integration and credit

The admitted local R5 interface is unchanged. This paper adds no warranted common bearer, ground, source-knowledge identity or complete origin-closure principle. An account may satisfy the local intentional-source requirements while leaving ultimate sourcehood plural, holistic or unresolved. The result is therefore available as a philosophical constraint on further arguments, not as an R5 defeat.

No downstream theorem may cite this paper as having established essential truthfulness or a repair oracle. Necessary existence, were it established, would still not supply a particular attestation, its content or a recipient's entitlement. Removing the present source argument leaves the local correction intact and leaves operational results which assume a cooperative informed source at their existing scope.

The Sixth distinctions concerning receivedness, autonomy, infinity and modal collapse are retained. SOURCE_GUIDE.md records earlier philosophical credit once. The present contribution is the symmetric scrutiny of intrinsic explanation and the actual-case comparison, with the missing existential warrant explicitly located. No lost Seventh result is reconstructed.
