# Tenth Orthemology research

Foundations and warranted continuation

## What the Tenth research changes

The Tenth investigation strengthens several affirmative arguments while narrowing the inferences they can support. The existence inquiry now has a smaller target, a warranted local distinction between producing another recipient and supplying a productive channel's antecedent availability, and a developed prima facie reason for treating existential availability as answerable. The new criticisms reach particular proposed explanations and inference rules. They do not establish an actual necessary source, and they do not show that belief in one is irrational. A direct, correctly targeted rational recognition remains an eligible source of warrant. \[E1; E2; E3; B1\]

The practical inquiry establishes a special reason for care when an intelligent organiser deliberately structures another subject's legitimate bounded reliance. That reason can precede a fresh assurance and persist after the organiser changes its purpose. Fair protective coordination can then give the recipient a bounded reason to cooperate. The organiser's responsibility and the arrangement's effects supply different parts of this argument; neither establishes a power to bind simply by choosing. \[PR1; G1\]

The continuation inquiry makes one preventive decision exact. When a recipient must decide a fixed attribution question with one permitted request, replacing a joint observation with two overlapping cards can leave every fact accessible yet make the task unfinishable within its allowance. In the controlled example, an endpoint witness repairs the handoff whereas a middle witness does not. An adequate retained verdict, an informative agreed selector, a surviving reader or an actually arranged budget change can also repair it. This gives the care argument a concrete application without turning read costs into moral premises. \[C1; C2\]

The mathematical work shows that unique infinite completion need not force forward convergence. A state can survive every finite-depth test without appearing in any compatible infinite history. The work supplies precise positive replacements for compact continuous systems, including genuinely varying coordinate spaces. A further ordinary mathematical result shows why a uniquely determined, uniformly computable limit with a known rate still need not admit a uniform exact-equality decision. These results support scrutiny of foundational explanations and finite recipient warrants; they do not create a new default implementation project. \[M1; M2; F1\]

The accepted joint-coverage study distinguishes a conditional necessary concrete resource from a conditional necessary power bearer. Its route tests how a possible ancestry-qualified supply could be grounded in actual resources. A source-level continuation strengthens the finite generative and retained-entry rationale while locating the additional historical and essential-grounding commitments. The practical-perfection study removes two alleged disadvantages of faithful repair while preserving inherited prima facie support towards comprehensive practical integration and nondomination. These studies do not newly establish the corresponding actual source conclusions. \[J1; J2; E4; PR2\]

## Explaining existential availability

### A smaller target changes both the opportunity and the burden

The earlier full-inventory question concerns all declared actual contingent bearers, with their relevant qualitative multiplicities. The smaller target fixes a family Q from actuality and asks whether at least one of its classifications has a contingently existing instance. The possible production required by the latter need supply only one suitable recipient. It need not reproduce the whole actual population, every identity or each exact count. Q must remain fixed when the comparison situation changes. \[E2\]

This reduction is strategically useful. It also removes an inference that the larger target could support. An explanation of one existential witness need not explain every other witness. Suppose a source's nature supports both its activity and the truth that some member of the target class exists. Those two dependences on a common basis do not establish that the existential proposition itself is prior to the activity. If the activity produces a different witness, there is no immediate local circle. Whether that production adequately answers the availability question requires further assessment. \[E2\]

The reduced target still needs actual contingency in its stated scope. A contingent exercise does not establish that its bearer is contingent. Nor does the contingency of every particular witness establish that the existential proposition itself is contingent: the witness could vary between possibilities. These are limitations on proposed applications, not certified alternative worlds. \[E2; E3\]

### The local affirmative judgement earns real credit

The strongest local reason concerns a productive channel rather than the bare logical form of an existential sentence. An activity does not, solely through its output, supply the antecedent existential availability required for that activity to occur. This judgement allows the received agent to be genuinely creative. It distinguishes production of a recipient from supply of the availability already used in that production. The kind-priority review accepts it as rationally warranted for an explicitly existential-supply question. \[E2\]

Its warrant need not be a measurement, an experiment or a deduction from some more basic norm. We can understand what a proposed account employs and what it supplies. That understanding gives a substantive reason to reject an answer which credits downstream production with its own antecedent availability. The judgement also leaves room for an account which supplies an earlier input from outside the restricted target. Original possession therefore retains a genuine explanatory attraction. \[E1; E2\]

Two further moves remain separate. The explanation promised by a transfer principle must be the same kind of explanation later assessed as inadequate. And a sound local judgement must not become a rejection of a whole joint history merely by changing its grammatical subject from one contributor to all contributors. Neither qualification cancels the local warrant. It locates the additional content required by the actual source inference. \[E1–E3\]

### Why the availability question has prima facie support

The transfer study develops a positive reason for answerability. The target concerns an actual feature of reality. The local channel distinction identifies a real explanatory respect which another received production can leave untouched. If an independently warranted possible answer supplies that respect, the demand is intelligible and attainable. Merely making the actual history beginningless does not explain why that demand should cease to apply. Together these considerations support a defeasible presumption that actual existential availability has an adequate answer. The target-relative anti-exemption argument is a genuine refinement of the earlier affirmative case. It develops inherited collective nonpresupposition and original-possession considerations without making its answerability judgement identical to the earlier preference. Their overlap should not be counted twice; reinforcing considerations need not each be independently sufficient. \[E3; B1\]

This argument does more than license curiosity. It identifies why one respect of actuality merits explanation and why one proposed exemption is insufficient. Its conclusion is nevertheless ampliative: removing a reason for exemption is not a deduction of actual answerability. The strongest received-efficacy reply challenges the claim that anything remains untreated. It offers genuine actual productions covering the recipients and contends that their joint coverage answers the question. That assertion is itself unproved, but it addresses the scope of the affirmative reason rather than merely drawing another infinite diagram. \[E3\]

A tempting causal derivation fails at a precise point. Removing every productive cause of a token effect differs from removing every account admitted as adequate to the availability question. In the latter test, the rival's internal productive causes may remain. Ordinary cause-deletion reasoning cannot simply be transferred to a narrower class of availability answers. A successful repair would justify the relevant causal individuation and comparison directly. \[E3\]

The actual-powers adaptation supplies another useful conditional check. Under an assumption that the actual target lacks an adequate availability answer, combine that absence with the target's obtaining. A possible adequate answer makes this combined claim contingent, even if the bare existential proposition is necessary. With a separately warranted modal comparison in which this combined claim is nonactual but possible, a powers theory could exclude it if its exact domain and achievement-preservation premises imply that realising it supplies the answer it denies. Those premises need independent support. Ninth already owns the qualified-fact contradiction technique; Tenth's increment is the reduced-target contingency and fidelity diagnosis. \[E3; B1\]

### What uniqueness from laws can and cannot supply

A mathematical representation can expose where existential content enters an explanation. Suppose every represented coordinate admits an absence value, the all-absence assignment lies in the domain, and each of the complete displayed receiving rules preserves absence. The all-absence assignment then satisfies the system. Such a system cannot uniquely determine a positive realisation. The result works for arbitrary infinite or branching networks and needs no first index or finite traversal. It is an ordinary mathematical implication. \[E1\]

The representation conditions matter. The rules must address existence rather than only a quality of already existing items; simultaneous absence must belong to the same represented domain; and no existentially loaded global constraint may have been omitted. If an independent condition excludes absence, that condition makes a genuine contribution and deserves separate assessment. A necessary nonempty field does not by itself identify one necessary occupant. The algebraic absence assignment is not a proof that an empty cosmos is possible. \[E1\]

This result sharpens one explanation audit without eliminating the actual-history rival. A concrete history includes actual bearers and exercises, and therefore contains more than an uninstantiated law package. A nonnecessitating causal account may also be adequate without uniquely determining its effect. The null argument consequently restricts a specified law-only adaptation; it does not establish that every adequate explanation has that form or that the full actual history is inadequate. \[E1\]

Likewise, existence can distribute across a plurality without explanatory adequacy distributing automatically. Asking how the local contributions compose does not posit another object beyond the bearers. A composition defence must say which presuppositions remain and why they are harmless at the target. The accepted criticism removes one proposed justification for unrestricted explanatory distribution, while leaving genuinely adequate joint explanations eligible. \[E1\]

### Joint coverage and the qualified modal route

The independently accepted joint-coverage tests preserve genuine received efficacy and independently supported infinite ancestry as a conditional control. Their function is to discriminate inference rules, rather than to certify a rootless cosmology. \[J1\]

The finite mixed-history control assigns each productive contribution its own target. One contribution can supply the bearer required by another. Treating every prerequisite of one contribution as a prerequisite of the entire account fabricates self-dependence. Package membership does not make each part relevant to every explained item. \[J1\]

The supported infinite-history control preserves local productive arrows while adding independent support. A rule that rejects the history solely from those arrows also rejects the supported case. It therefore needs either an additional anti-infinity premise or a more discriminating account of exhaustive availability-dependence. \[J1\]

The positive principle is fixed-antecedent preservation. Extending a specified productive route downstream does not supply the availability on which that route already depends. Completing an account upstream is different: it may supply an input previously left outside the account. The local principle remains warranted while its extension to exhaustive upstream discharge remains disputed. A sequence of changing inputs cannot simply be treated as one fixed unaccounted-for input. \[J1\]

The companion modal route seeks a narrower outcome than complete actual sourcehood. Its possible target positively includes an outside-class supplier, a suitable recipient and a restriction excluding the target class from the ancestry which supplies that supplier's relevant availability. That ancestry includes relevant earlier stages of the same bearer, not only productive relations between different bearers. Ordinary outside-kind production is weaker: its outsider could itself have been produced through the target class. The ancestry-qualified possibility must be warranted in its own right. \[J2\]

The proposed realisation principle then requires actual resources sufficient for that whole achievement. It cannot borrow a merely possible auxiliary supplier as free background. There must be a positively identified actual resource relevant specifically to the supplier's existential availability, with its relevant occurrence retained along the selected possible route. Relevance only to a trigger, recipient or undifferentiated conjunction is insufficient. If actual target-class membership survives on that support trace, later reclassification does not erase it from the ancestry. \[J2\]

Under the complete stated package, an actual resource outside the covering class is obtained. If actual coverage includes every relevant contingent concrete resource, the selected resource is necessary. A further supplying-power condition yields a necessary capacity bearer. Coverage limited to power bearers cannot silently classify arbitrary background resources, and passive background cannot silently become active efficacy. An unexercised necessary capacity bearer would still be a genuine intermediate existence result. Actual exercise, unity, intrinsic explanation and integrity are further questions. The conditional route can therefore establish its necessary-resource consequence while the adequacy of a proposed actual internal history remains undecided. \[J2\]

This accepted conditional route has more than syntactic interest. Its fixed-route preservation and resource-completeness requirements have intelligible rational grounds. Its smaller positive target can make the inquiry more assessable. What remains unearned is the complete modal target and generative reach over the relevant qualified ancestries, with classification transport and domain alignment. The research cannot promote these to an actual necessary particular merely because a conditional proof is valid. Nor does their present status warrant a global dismissal of the affirmative view. \[J1; J2\]

### Actual powers can support alternative prehistory

The source-level continuation earns a positive local judgement. A genuinely productive finite route to a new supplier has an actual entry relevant to that supplier's availability. This is stronger than saying that actuality makes a possibility sentence true. A capacity sufficient for the whole claimed achievement cannot supply only a recipient while borrowing its supplier as free background. The reason follows the supplier's availability trace, rather than distributing the whole package's relevance to every part. \[E4\]

Hamri's Causal fundamentality combines actual-power realisation with a richer account of independent essential grounding. Its return argument supports retaining an unexercised capacity across alternative exercises; its essentialist route seeks more than a merely necessary resource. \[E4\]

A retained earlier source can produce an intermediate agent in one history and refrain in another. Its iterated capacities can still make the later result available. The later recipient's prehistory can therefore change without fixing every past event or retaining one universal source for all alternatives. Nor does finite productive depth require finitely many joint contributors or a first event in the entry's entire ancestry. This gives the modal strategy a genuine source-relative application. It does not yet show that such applications cover every absolute alternative, especially one removing the whole relevant availability base. \[E4\]

For the fixed family Q, what survives the comparison must be precise. Retaining a bearer is weaker than retaining its actually classified supply occurrence. There are two principled repairs. One preserves a relevant actual occurrence with its classification, so later reclassification cannot erase that occurrence from the supply history. The other preserves a power-profile with independently warranted Q-classifying force. A kind's explaining a power does not establish the converse: that no other kind could have that power. The retained occurrence must contribute to the supplier's availability, but its classification need not itself be an additional efficient cause. \[E4\]

Only one eligible retained realisation is required for a genuinely possible qualified target S. Another history with a different upstream basis does not defeat the route if the required realisation is available. Equally, the promise of some realisation does not establish the required one. These distinctions give the strongest affirmative a workable retention argument while leaving its universal historical scope explicit. The actuality of the reduced existential target establishes its possibility, but not the richer source and ancestry qualifications of S. If S itself is actual, the separate direct coverage route applies. \[E4; J2\]

The richer grounding strategy deserves its own conditional credit. An appropriately established actual concrete being whose existence is grounded in its own essence would yield a necessary particular without waiting for unity or actual exercise. Two application bridges remain. Removing one support differs from eliminating every adequate support and replacement. The strong failure inference is valid when exhaustive dependence and joint removability are warranted; individual contingency does not establish that premise. Also, a proposition about concrete existence need not have concrete grounds merely because of its subject matter. Efficient-causal inertness excludes an abstract producer without by itself excluding every proposed noncausal ground. \[E4\]

Pure representation still supplies no concrete instance merely by describing it. That positive constraint helps test a proposed ground, while leaving substantive nonrepresentational grounding proposals to be assessed on their merits. An essence's generic existential consequence likewise does not establish reverse dependence on the consequent; the stronger claim about a member of one fixed history needs its own assessment. These are target-specific controls, not certified alternative metaphysical worlds. \[E4\]

The result improves the reasons for finite resource-complete generation and selected occurrence retention. ## Practical responsibility and source integrity

### Deliberately organised reliance gives a special reason for care

The practical result begins with a real recipient and an independently recognised epistemic good. An intelligent organiser actively installs, sustains or assumes a provision around which the recipient pursues a legitimate bounded activity. It understands the resulting exposure and controls a relevant means of response. Historical authorship, foresight or superior ability alone does not supply this relation. Under those stated facts, the organiser has a special pro-tanto reason for proportionate care. Pro tanto means a genuine reason whose final force depends on applicable competing reasons. Sixth already connected intentional governance with recipient goods, and Seventh already identified a particular assurance responsibility. The Tenth extension concerns prior organised exposure, persistence after purpose change and proportionate transition. \[PR1; B1\]

The rationale is relational. The organiser helped place the recipient's good under this particular dependency. It is therefore responsible for arranging an appropriate continuation or transition rather than assuming that an equally capable outsider will rescue the recipient. Both agents might owe an emergency rescue, but that coincidence does not explain who owed the advance arrangement. The source's own conduct identifies the bearer of that planning responsibility. \[PR1\]

The reason can arise before a new promise or assertion, and the recipient need not identify the ultimate organiser for the dependence to matter. It can survive the organiser's change of purpose because the attributable exposure persists. Possible responses include proportionate precautions, a usable substitute, timely warning or an adequate opportunity to disengage. The result neither guarantees success nor requires indefinite maintenance of every benefit ever produced. Legitimacy, risk allocation, feasibility and informed refusal remain substantive. \[PR1\]

The original-source comparison does not introduce a second absolute origin. A fault-producing secondary process can lie within the source's granted productive field while being independent of the particular interference assessed. Active organisation is a sufficient ground of responsibility, not a claim to have excluded every other ground in a whole-origin case. Creation also need not make the recipient worse off than never existing for its present structured exposure to matter. \[PR1\]

A responsibility does not itself predict compliance. Actual evidence of fitting concern and secure attribution can support the ordinary defeasible expectation already credited by Ninth. The exact token-purpose argument retains its stronger conditional scope. Essential integrity asks another question: whether this same bearer's nature excludes the relevant defect throughout the intended possibilities. A necessarily applicable norm does not by itself establish necessary conformity to it. \[PR1; B1\]

### A fair arrangement can change the recipient decision

Suppose two transition procedures are initially equally adequate. The organiser fairly selects one, gives warranted access to its plan and prepares other permissible activity around it. Uncoordinated mixing would now damage the shared task. A recipient's slight preference for the other procedure need not outweigh the avoidable burden on others. The recipient can acquire a bounded reason to cooperate even before a separate command authority is established. \[G1\]

This is a substantive positive bridge. The history explains why the organiser must prepare care. The fair arrangement's actual effects explain why the recipient should now act in a particular way. The recipient's lack of an unrestricted right to dictate means does not itself create a right of selection for the organiser. Fair formation and a real coordination burden must be established independently. \[G1\]

If a verified alternative preserves the task without an additional relevant burden, the demonstrated coordination reason may disappear. Another legitimate decision allocation could still bind the recipient, but it needs its own warrant. This harmless-alternative test diagnoses the present argument; it is not a universal definition of legitimate authority. Unsupported confidence in harmlessness, lucky outcomes and omitted rule-level costs do not satisfy its premises. \[G1\]

### The source alternatives must be assessed at both times

An organiser cannot justify an otherwise unsupported control condition by refusing an equally adequate, reasonably available less intrusive response without relevant offsetting loss. A recipient who declines unnecessary submission has not thereby declined adequate care. The actual alternative set includes options the organiser can provide but chooses not to offer. \[G1\]

The history of selection also matters. Suppose the organiser unjustifiably forecloses a less restrictive option and deliberately recruits innocent participants around its preferred arrangement. Their resulting reliance can create genuine present costs of change. Those costs do not validate the earlier choice or create command entitlement. They may nevertheless give the recipient urgent reasons for temporary cooperation, alongside fitting duties of remedy or reconfiguration for the organiser. Earlier costless availability does not prove that costless restoration remains possible now. \[G1\]

This prevents two errors in practical evaluation. Wrongful leverage does not make innocent people's interests unreal. Equally, temporary cooperation to protect those interests does not endorse the organiser's right to have created the situation. A recipient can reject an unwarranted demand, retain a claim to care and still heed well-supported evidence that continued reliance would be unsafe. \[G1\]

### The strongest original giver argument remains positive

The practical-perfection study received independent acceptance at its bounded scope. The positive warrant it discusses is substantially inherited. Genuine practical excellence involves apprehending a fitting reason and responding to it. Such integration can belong to an original agent without an external superior, borrowed power or reciprocal advantage. Original giving supplies a source-sensitive reason to locate the eligible excellence intrinsically in the giver, rather than allowing the whole practical excellence to appear only in the recipient. This gives prima facie rational support towards comprehensive practical integration at the granted source and fidelity scope. \[PR2; B1\]

The strongest intellectualist extension concerns the character of practical apprehension itself. A decisive reason is understood as favouring action, rather than registered as a neutral fact. If full practical apprehension is intrinsically sufficient for fitting intention, contrary willing would not express that integrated agency. At this scope, an arbitrary reasonless exception should not be inserted merely to preserve doubt. Familiar human weakness does not automatically refute the proposal, because incomplete attention, appetite and imperfect understanding can distinguish the human case. \[PR2\]

The exact remaining burden is comprehensive application to this source. Understanding what attracts an agent explains why an intention is determinate. Understanding the objectively decisive ordering explains what it is fitting to choose. The proposed integration identifies the latter as necessarily governing the former. Removing external coercion and need does not, on its own, establish that full intrinsic practical order. An unevidenced description of a contrary intrinsic inclination is not a defeater; neither is it excluded simply by renaming full information as full responsiveness. A direct rational warrant for the comprehensive source principle remains eligible. \[PR2; B1\]

The appropriate conclusion is therefore carefully graded. This investigation has not added a newly established actual or essential nondomination result. It has not shown an absence of rational support for nondomination, and it has not established a metaphysically possible defective source. The integrated original-giver argument retains its earlier affirmative force. The newly examined account clarifies the required connection rather than supplying a second independent warrant for the same comprehensive premise. Its considerations may reinforce one another; avoiding double credit does not require every reason to be independently sufficient. \[PR2; B1\]

### Two alleged costs of faithful repair can be removed

Ninth's complete-profile route uses R\_G, the premise that each in-scope lying profile has an eligible same-bearer complete alternative which is strictly better overall, together with N, actual practical nondomination. A particular repair supplies only the corresponding instance of that premise. R\_P is stronger: a repair that improves the relevant respect without any practical loss. Local wrongness alone does not supply either repair premise, and actual nondomination differs from essential nondomination. The case must preserve the same bearer with its full essence and examine the relevant collateral consequences. \[B1\]

The accepted Tenth comparison removes two particular objections once an eligible truthful alternative is independently supplied. First, truthful care does not newly burden an otherwise unencumbered source when its prior organisation already created the responsibility. Both options must be evaluated against the same history. Second, giving up a gratuitous control condition does not surrender an authority which the care relation never established. Fitting voluntary governance can remain the source's own action. \[PR2; PR1; G1\]

This is a bounded improvement in the comparison, not proof that every repair is feasible or that all other losses are absent. An earlier noncoercive opportunity does not itself supply a current loss-free repair after innocent reliance has formed. A whole-history alternative needs its own same-bearer and fixed-case eligibility. Genuine collateral goods must remain in the assessment even when they accompany a defective action. If a particular complete comparison establishes strict superiority, it can supply that instance of R\_G; N is still needed to exclude choosing the inferior profile. \[PR2; B1\]

## Warranted continuation through changed representations

### Original context and current force are different relations

The occurrence study asks one metalinguistic question: whether every selected output occurrence belongs to the original document's own unquoted authorial argumentative or expository voice. It does not ask whether the propositions are true or whether the current carrier endorses them. A present quotation can faithfully report an original assertion without becoming a fresh assertion or permission. Equal wording does not identify two source occurrences, while two copies of one context retain a shared original key. \[C1\]

The model assumes a justified source extraction with enter, leave and emit operations. A fragment may begin inside a nested context and return to a suspended parent. Symbolic execution records the required incoming depth, references used by selected outputs and the outgoing stack effect. Substituting the actual boundary contexts reproduces direct execution. Serial composition and branch reconvergence then derive which distinct original keys the next attribution question needs. Closed unobserved scopes can disappear from that task-specific effect. \[C1\]

The derivation does not authenticate the origin map. If the receiver lacks the map from an incoming placeholder to its original context, an identity or path observation must establish it before the live-key set can be computed. Such an observation can also reveal a role, and that information must be credited. Recovering arbitrary reply-to-objection links lies outside the small context machine. An ordinary parser with the same complete stack or evidence performs equally well. \[C1\]

### Exact read cost depends on the entire observation contract

A known negative role settles the answer as false. Otherwise, remove the known positive roles: the unresolved decision is the conjunction of the remaining original-role bits. Each permitted read has a cost and a justified set of roles it reveals. Under the declared contract, the minimum worst-case cost of a correct deterministic total decision equals the cheapest cover of the unresolved set. \[C1\]

The lower bound requires an admitted all-positive source and, for each unresolved key, an admitted alternative with only that key negative. The paired sources must agree on the recipient's entire initial retained packet, including old results, origin maps and witness metadata. Every read not covering the changed key must also return the same complete observable response. This includes side information, metadata, status, size and observable timing. Opaque handles exclude undeclared content lookup; they do not assert digest collisions. Authentication and adequate role disclosure are premises. Independence of target bits alone is insufficient if a cheap outside value reveals their conjunction. \[C1\]

Following a policy on the all-positive source explains the lower bound. If any relevant key is still uncovered, the corresponding negative source gives the same transcript but needs the opposite answer. A correct policy cannot finish there. A minimum cover supplies the matching upper bound. Negative sources may allow earlier termination; safe abstention does not count as a completed total decision. A valid task-specific certificate can change the initial holdings and remove the need for these reads. \[C1\]

### Copying does not destroy source interval structure

When each permitted observation provides adequate role evidence for an interval in the original source order, quotienting copied outputs by their shared original keys preserves interval coverage. Output reordering cannot turn that problem into arbitrary set cover. Let m be the number of unresolved relevant original keys and W the permitted interval-witness menu. Once justified coverage endpoints are known, the usual weighted interval recurrence takes $O\left(m\left|W\right|\right)$ candidate work and $O\left(m\right)$ storage for its dynamic-programming cost table. That storage bound does not include the source, credentials, witness menu or other input data. The algorithms are conventional.
