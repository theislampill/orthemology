# Source feedback on the proper-part self-explanation repair

## Finding

**There is positive, independently articulated source support for a productive anti-bootstrap repair. There is not yet source support for transporting it unchanged to every basic-natural fact under Koons–Pruss's general explanation relation.** The decisive inherited locus is **Minhaj 2:171–176**, particularly the explicit treatment of a *part-cause*, condition of the cause, and completion of the cause. This is appreciably stronger than a bare ban on cycles or a definition of exclusive production.

The source's rationale is that what produces must already possess the actuality and efficacy required to produce. An account cannot obtain that very actuality by an exercise whose completion already requires it. Adding other conditions around the target does not cure this defect. This gives positive evidential credit to a **same-occurrence, same-productive-respect prohibition**, including partial and indirect self-presupposition. It does not make syntactic or mereological inclusion in an explanatory fact into productive contribution.

Consequently, the mixed-wrapper control `E(c,{b})`, with `b < c`, is ruled out **if** its intended explanation uses b's very occurrence as a requisite supplying that occurrence. The source does not rule out the bare relational control merely from `b < c`. The missing map is exact: fact-parthood in a complete explanatory bundle → a productive requisite of the same explanandum in the same existential respect. The current theorem does not contain that map, and the source does not supply it for arbitrary facts.

This is a **new interpretive application of inherited readings**, prompted by the particular-explanans result. No first-reading, independent-manuscript, whole-volume, new-theorem, metaphysical-refutation, integration, owner-acceptance, closure, or timing credit is claimed.

## 1. Formal question retained without alteration

The bridge audit allows a wholly particular/basic-natural b and a universal u to be proper parts of mixed c, with `E(c,{b})`. c is outside the basic-natural class while its particular constituent b is inside. The displayed outside-explainer condition prohibits the explainer from being a part of a target; it does not prohibit a target from being a part of the explainer.

`BridgeRepairs.lean`, theorem `bridge_with_no_basic_proper_partial_self`, correctly makes the required extra premise explicit: no basic-natural p is a proper part of a fact completely explaining p. This assessment does not rerun the kernel or add a proof. It assesses independent source motivation for that premise and its faithful scope.

The required conclusion is stronger than the source-level proposition that a creator cannot make its own power by using that power. The formal predicate ranges over facts, not only productive resources, and its mereology does not specify which parts actually contribute to an explanation.

## 2. Primary evidence and its affirmative force

### Minhaj 1:438–441: completion of productive capacity

The entire reacquired item96, L001–009, was read continuously. L006 argues that reciprocal provision of what makes agents able to act is vicious circularity in agents and efficient causes. Power is required for action; if each receives its requisite power only through the other's power, neither account supplies the enabling actuality. L007 expressly distinguishes this from concomitance or mutual conditions with another producer, illustrated by fatherhood and sonship. L008 distinguishes an agent's own incompatible alternatives from requiring another's assistance or permission.

This is evidence against reciprocal *productive enabling*. The text also argues that a peer's required noninterference compromises original independence. That further classification cannot be silently generalized to every negative condition in every model. The narrower actual-power bootstrap is independently identifiable before that controversial extension.

Source: [Minhaj item96](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/108/96/). The reacquired HTML has exactly the inherited HTML digest. Its present local L-labels are fresh extraction coordinates, not printed line numbers.

### Minhaj 2:167–180: explicit part-cause and completing-condition argument

The entire reacquired item156, L001–041, was read continuously. The opening L004–020 separates necessary attributes, inherence, and concomitance from production by an external cause. A necessary attribute is not manufactured by its bearer merely because the bearer necessarily has it.

The affirmative argument at L021–024, electronically 2:170–172, is more precise than 'no circles': the recipient's life, knowledge, and power must be present for the purported act of conferring the other's life, knowledge, or power. If each completion waits on completion supplied by the other, the supposed efficacy presupposes the efficacy it is meant to produce. L023 explicitly discusses what belongs to the *completion* of a producer.

L030–034, electronically 2:174–176, repeats this at the level of existence. L032 says that neither an entire cause nor a part-cause can exert influence without existing. L033 excludes self-causation through being a cause, a part-cause, a condition of one's own cause, or what completes it. Its concrete reason is that, were creation to require the creature's participation, the creature would have to exist before being created, while not existing until created.

This passage **positively rules out a productive wrapper whose own completion requires the same target actuality**. It does not rely on counting creators, defining each as sole, or merely stipulating graph acyclicity. Its offered reason survives replacing a whole self-cause by one requisite among others. L022–023 simultaneously permit concomitant unproduced attributes and jointly created correlates. These qualifications are part of the argument, not exceptions invented to save it.

## 3. A consequential faithful premise

**Restricted productive non-bootstrap.** Fix an actual occurrence or an actual existence/efficacy condition x in respect r. A purported complete productive account of that very x-in-r cannot include x-in-r itself as a productive cause, a part-cause, or a condition that must already be actual for the productive basis to supply x-in-r. Nor may it obtain x-in-r indirectly through a completion whose productive adequacy already requires x-in-r. Otherwise the account spends the same actuality whose productive supply it purports to explain.

This formulation receives real source and rational support from Minhaj2's explicit part-cause/completion argument. It is not claimed as a new theorem of the explanatory axioms or as an exhaustive analysis of causation. Its crucial warrant is productive actuality/prior efficacy, not the orthography of 'self' or the mere presence of a directed graph edge.

Preserve the scope:

1. **A fact's explanation in general:** no unrestricted irreflexivity follows. Logical, essential, mathematical, or other nonproductive explanations require separate treatment.
2. **Productive explanation of actual occurrence/existence:** the prohibition applies when the target's same actuality is used as part of its own productive supply.
3. **Earlier act sustaining/enabling a later act or power:** not prohibited merely because the bearer persists. Earlier occurrence, later occurrence, and the origin of standing agency are distinct targets/respects. An origin bootstrap remains illicit; genuine indexed maintenance need not be.
4. **Complete same-occurrence self-presupposition:** prohibited even when hidden in a larger productive wrapper, supplied through a part-cause, or routed through reciprocal completing conditions.
5. **Logical/essential self-explanation of a necessary fact:** these passages do not condemn it as efficient self-production. They positively preserve necessary unproduced concomitance. This is not a claim that Ibn Taymiyyah endorses every modern account of logical self-explanation.

## 4. Applying it to E(c,{b}) without changing the question

There are three interpretations to keep distinct.

**Productive wrapper.** Suppose b states the very obtaining occurrence in question, and c's complete productive adequacy requires b already to obtain. Adding a universal u does not independently supply that missing actuality. Minhaj2's part-cause/completing-condition argument defeats this interpretation: the needed actuality cannot originate by being presupposed. No demand for a third maker of an abstract law is needed.

**Descriptive or certificate wrapper.** Suppose c merely conjoins b with another explanatory fact or law. Inclusion alone does not prove that b is doing productive work. The inherited effect/production/certificate study already shows why added cited facts can be explanatorily or logically redundant without changing productive support. Even if one ultimately rejects the bundle as a genuine full explanation, that rejection requires an explanation-adequacy or relevance criterion. Ordinary part-minimality does not suffice: the formal control already satisfies it. Minimality of complete explainers is not a test for the productive contribution of every part.

**Nonproductive explanation.** Suppose u supplies a logical or essential reason and c is offered under a noncausal meaning of E. A prohibition justified by efficient actuality cannot simply be substituted. Whether the target's nature is genuinely explanatorily sufficient is its own question.

Thus the source supplies a good reason to reject the *causally interpreted* mixed wrapper. It does not turn the displayed bare `part`/`E` structure into that interpretation. The finite control remains a useful explicit-axiom independence result and is not thereby certified a metaphysically genuine explanatory model.

**Separate redundant-route case.** This productive anti-bootstrap support does not by itself settle the separately tested A-only/B-only sufficient-route case: the target has not been shown to be an input to its own production, and neither route needs the other. Item156 L035–037's exclusivity/nonduplication branch remains a separate inference. The new positive repair therefore does not automatically reverse the latest common-source reassessment.

## 5. What would still have to be warranted for the formal bridge

The exact repairs can now be separated into obligations rather than left as an unspecified request for 'noncircularity':

- **Target/class transport:** why each selected Koons–Pruss basic-natural particular belongs to the source's productive-actuality class. Finite measurability or boundedness does not by itself mean createdness, received whole-existence, or a newly produced event.
- **Relation transport:** why a complete explanation of those facts is productive in the relevant sense, or why the same non-bootstrap norm independently extends to their nonproductive explanations.
- **Part/role transport:** why the target's parthood in an explainer makes its same occurrence an actually used prerequisite of the explanatory supply. Koons–Pruss's definition of partial explanation from parthood supplies formal nomenclature, not this causal interpretation.
- **Same-occurrence/respect transport:** why the fact contained in the explainer is exactly the actuality being explained, rather than an earlier sustaining episode, another intrinsic state, or an essential concomitant.
- **Totality and particularity:** retain the bridge's actual particular target, availability of the relevant plurality, and particular-constituent assumption. Anti-bootstrap alone supplies none of these.

The modern source explicitly allows self-explanatory necessary facts and defines its BNF/supernatural distinction by particularity, bounds, measures, and relevance entailment (supplied continuous witness L61–97, L140–173). That is not Ibn Taymiyyah's creature/Creator division. An uncreated successive divine act does not become a created or basic-natural fact just because it is particular or successively realized. Conversely, a finite/bounded fact is not shown to be a creature merely by that modern classification.

One can propose a general explanatory anti-presupposition norm, saying that a full why-account cannot borrow the very obtaining it is meant to render intelligible, except where the fact is intrinsically intelligible. The source's productive rationale makes that proposal worth defending; it does not finish its defense across explanation-types or show its coverage of all BNF facts. The epistemic defense of the modern strengthened PSR must therefore address these transports directly.

## 6. What the inherited formal analyses add, and do not add

`OriginalBearerBridge.lean` separates target occurrence, original completion, resource realization, and same-respect support transport. Its `Completion` premise already supplies a relevant actual nonreceived resource. It cannot be used as a neutral derivation of that completion from 'no self-production'. Nor does its `intrinsic` relation amount to arbitrary fact-parthood. No new kernel replay was attempted here.

The inherited explanatory-warrant analysis distinguishes local received provision from a complete account of its actuality. Its whole-account rationale reinforces the complaint against borrowing the explanandum's actuality. Its controls also show that acyclicity and global determination do not establish an original resource; the extension from finite self-bootstrap to an infinite all-received account is a further premise. The beginningless one-original control blocks compulsory least-generation of every act-token.

The provision and effect-production analyses likewise separate a sound occurrence certificate, actual operative routes, whole productive accounting, and individual exhaustive original provision. These distinctions prevent both cheap rejection of a merely syntactic wrapper and cheap acceptance of a stipulated E-edge as metaphysical production.

## Retained assessment

**Positive advance:** the exact new repair has a substantial source-relative analogue, with an explicit rationale covering part-causes and completing conditions. The antecedent cannot be escaped merely by embedding the target in a larger productive basis.

**Remaining gap:** extending that warranted productive prohibition to the modern theorem's general explanatory relation and BNF fact-mereology requires the listed semantic and classificatory premises. Necessary concomitance, real earlier-to-later support, and beginningless originally supplied succession remain available. Neither the primary source nor the formal repair is weakened to hide this boundary.
