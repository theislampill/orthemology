## Conclusion

The clarification about de se knowledge is right: the serious proposal is that complete self-knowledge exposes an incompatibility in the original-agency profile, not that the word “I” creates one. The source already supplies substantive arguments for that incompatibility. A response confined to the consistency of two accurately self-locating subjects does not answer those arguments.

After returning to the actual supplied PDF, I judge the case for one comprehensive original source of the relevant actual connected created order to have genuine, defeasible warrant. The strongest harmonious proposal does not presently have comparably established warrant for its full original-perfection interpretation. It has earned important logical and explanatory credits; it has not earned the conclusion that two fully perfected originals really can instantiate its joint productive relation. Calling the entire proposal a bare stipulation would erase those credits. Calling its remaining originality and perfection claims established would overstate them.

The precise surviving local reservation concerns the move from a peer's real contribution to a voluntarily selected common effect to foreign completion or restriction of each participant's *appropriate actual original efficacy*. This is the consent branch at printed 30–32, strengthened by the complete-efficacy argument in the other source readings. The source gives positive reasons for the move. I do not regard its unrestricted application to every proposed primitive joint manifestation as conclusively established. This is a reservation about the scope and application of a motivated norm, not a claim that the norm lacks any warrant because it has not been deduced from thinner neutral premises.

There is also a distinct universal-supremacy route. If every eligible original must originally ground, or have ultimate mastery over, every distinct actual external bearer in the same respect in which an original is independent or unassailable, two originals are excluded even under necessary harmony. The live question there is the pure-perfection warrant for that unrestricted domain, including a purported peer. Neither generic omnipotence nor first-person truthfulness alone establishes that domain.

The old identity-preserving acquisition objection to the power-concentration argument is no longer a sufficient standalone reservation. The later comparative-disqualification argument answers it. An essentially restricted candidate may fail a justified perfection standard without being capable of changing into another numerical individual.

## What the supplied argument actually achieves

### Intrinsic power at printed 17–22

The initial exclusion is strong against a pair whose members have no productive power except what each first receives from the other. Giving power already requires power. Reciprocal provision of the very efficacy needed to provide it cannot explain either member. An external giver would make the pair derivative rather than the exhaustive originals assumed.

The author expressly distinguishes this from ordinary collaborators whose independently present powers combine. Printed 18 distinguishes efficient-priority circularity from concomitance; printed 19 and 22 admit increased cooperative strength where antecedent power exists. Therefore this opening does not alone prove that every joint manifestation of already operative unreceived powers is impossible. That further conclusion depends on the subsequent original-efficacy argument. This observation respects the source's own distinctions; it is not a discovery that the author overlooked cooperation.

### Agreement and intact powers at printed 23–29

Printed 23 excludes two agents each being the exclusive entire actual producer of the same numerical effect. At that explicitly exclusive attribution the contradiction is genuine. The source then addresses an alternative-capacity proposal: each creator has the same powers before and during their coexistence, yet one's exclusive production would require the other's nonproduction. Printed 24 treats this required abstention as a restriction on power; printed 25 excludes removal of a necessary intrinsic power.

The exact promotion is visible at the beginning of printed 24: from the impossibility of two exclusive actual productions, the text infers that each can have the relevant power only when the other lacks it. The source's strongest meaning is unrestricted original efficacy, not merely two dormant human skills. Under that meaning, waiting for a peer's enabling or permission is a substantive defect. The weak generic inference from two abilities to two actual exclusive productions would fail, but attributing only that weak inference to the source would ignore its independence and perfection premises.

Printed 28 also invokes availability of contrary determined outcomes. Complete power and will cannot each guarantee mutually incompatible outcomes in one admissible circumstance. But separate standing capacities do not by themselves establish the compossibility of the two contrary *complete wise determinations*. Necessary harmony cannot be dismissed merely by constructing an off-diagonal action table. Conversely, defeating that conflict branch would leave the source's agreement and coalescence branches untouched.

### Coalescence at printed 30–33

The source explicitly considers effects mixing while each producer remains independently powerful. It demands an efficient account of contingent mixing. Without an external original, it assigns that account to one of the proposed pair and asks whether the other consents. Contrary intervention is restriction; consenting intervention is assistance. Both are then held to influence the other's determining power and will.

For contrary intervention the inference is comparatively direct: if A's complete unchanged determination entails the specified effect e, prevention of that same e is incompatible with that complete determination. The target, time and productive respect must stay fixed.

For consenting coalescence, the same short inference does not suffice. The peer may contribute to precisely the joint outcome intended, so no negation of e has yet been obtained. The source therefore needs its stronger claim: the peer's genuinely original productive contribution constitutes a perfection-relevant completion of A's actual efficacy, even where A's intrinsic faculties and own act have not been efficiently supplied or removed. The final paragraph of printed 31 asserts that extension, and printed 32 invokes the earlier exclusion of assistance.

That is the central inference I continue to qualify. It is not answered by “but they agree.” Nor is it answered merely by showing that A acquires no new intrinsic entity. Actual relational efficacy can itself be the relevant perfection. The question is whether the particular peer relation supplies foreign completion of appropriate original agency, rather than expressing an already adequate wise activity in a nonexclusive manifestation.

Given the exclusion of cross-domain cooperation, the actual interconnected order at printed 32–33 is powerful evidence against partitioned original domains. The modern application should rely on genuine operative connectedness, not just the weaker proposition that every entity has some neighbour. The physical illustrations are historical, and this return is not an independent empirical certification of the entire universe's connectedness. The argument's actual-world force is nevertheless substantially stronger than an appeal to simplicity or a prediction that rulers might quarrel.

### Determination and supremacy at printed 34–40

Printed 34 distinguishes intrinsic possibility from actual willing: complete power with complete will makes the specified effect occur, but unwilled possible things need not occur. Printed 36 restates the exclusion among exclusive whole production, mutually required help, and separated products. Printed 37–38 argues that a genuinely subordinate power depends on the stronger source's assistance or forbearance. Mere inequality between arbitrary power-sets is weaker than this domination ordering; the full original-lord and perfection background is doing work.

Printed 40 supplies the agreement argument again, with one director or a higher director in ordinary partnerships. Common reasons and correct foreknowledge cannot simply be relabelled a third efficient creator. At the same time, saying two subjects know the same reasons does not establish a complete original explanation of why these particular acts are fittingly determined. The author's priority challenge must be met at that explanatory level.

## Why the affirmative norm has real independent motivation

Three considerations survive the modern distinctions and supply positive warrant.

First, original explanation should reach achieved efficacy. An alleged complete source that contributes a trigger but relies on independently provided materials, receptive capacities, preservation or operative resources does not thereby explain the whole result. A ground's unreceived inner readiness is weaker than unborrowed achieved productive adequacy. This distinction is substantive and relevant, even when no new property is deposited inside the agent.

Second, adequate agency and freedom from required supplementation are intelligible pure excellences. The Akmaliyya compares self-sufficient power with needing an assistant, doing the whole with doing a part, and a fulfilled wise will with an impeded will. These are reasons about real efficacy. Describing them as a demand for exclusive credit or as vanity would be uncharitable and false to the text. The same source requires discriminating wisdom and mercy; it does not praise arbitrary maximal output. [Akmaliyya, especially 6:125–130](https://islamweb.net/ar/library/content/22/591/).

Third, essential nonreceptivity does not protect a deficient candidate from comparison. A being's inability to become sufficiently capable is not itself a reason to regard it as maximally capable. The source can deny that an essentially restricted bearer qualifies without proposing its numerical transformation. This directly answers the old B5 reservation when treated as a necessary condition of every eligibility argument. The stronger current rival already represents standing solo constructors, so the surviving question is its *actual* coactive undertaking, not an inability to acquire that standing repertoire.

These considerations warrant a positive assessment; they need not be proved from efficient nonreceipt alone before counting as evidence. They do not, however, settle by mere repetition every application of the stronger conclusion. A complete *joint* original basis explains why a created outcome occurs at the aggregate level. To show that this cannot be the basis of perfected original participants, one still needs the individual-adequacy or universal-supremacy comparison. That is where the rival disputes the source, and where the source's affirmative reasons should be judged.

The perfection-donor rationale has a narrower quantifier limit: each perfection having an appropriately perfected giver does not show that every proposed original is the giver or bearer of every perfection. The independent a-fortiori comparison remains available, so this limit does not defeat the entire argument. Equally, two incomparable partial maxima do not satisfy the source's requirement of unrestricted pure-perfection completeness.

## The strongest surviving dissent and its earned credit

The serious dissent says: both bearers have unreceived powers and own real acts, know themselves and the peer accurately, and knowingly select appropriate activities under an intelligible shared reason. Their joint manifestation supplies one common created outcome. Neither falsely claims sole actual original provision. The disputed conclusion is that this nonexclusive exercise is already a pure defect in either bearer.

The nine-candidate table in the older `MODEL_AUDIT.md` is historical. Its final prose-harmony row must not be used as a complete description of the later reactive-context, valued-cooperation and wise-determination interpretation. The latter has acquired actual structural and explanatory credits, even though it remains short of a full original-perfection account. This return relies on the accepted later reports for those model credits; it does not claim a fresh code execution or model replay.

This dissent has reasons beyond a label:

- Standing capacity, intrinsic exercise, completed effect and exclusive entire provision really are different. The source's own particular-act discussions preserve these distinctions.
- Necessary agreement need not be reciprocal efficient production. A common rational ground is not automatically another productive person or an external donor of the will.
- Wise selection need not actualize every available power. Printed 34 and the later particular-wisdom controls prevent identifying perfection with always taking the solo route.
- The retained exchange-invariant means rationale genuinely improves the explanation of concordant means given its end and practical assumptions. It should not be described as a missing coordinate or arbitrary matching output.
- Primitive original cognition is not uniquely suspect merely for being primitive. Its inherence, truth-correspondence and efficient receipt are distinct issues.

But these credits stop short of a warranted harmonious-original ontology:

- A primitive joint law must do more than assign two source labels to an output. Its actual productive interpretation, full created inventory and original-agent eligibility require justification.
- Algebraic response sensitivity, including local positive derivatives, does not independently prove actual productive contribution or unchanged full efficacy. The nonabsorption study expressly limits its polynomial in this way.
- A valuable relation with another person does not by itself explain why that person's *independent originality* is a perfection-relevant good unavailable through a created other. An original-peer end requires its own rationale.
- Accurate centered knowledge does not mechanically establish that the subject acts on the basis of the recognized first-person reason. The accepted de-se reviews correctly preserve that further basing obligation.
- Calling actual joint success “fully appropriate original efficacy” leaves precisely the source's classification in dispute. Calling it “foreign completion” also requires the defended norm and a faithful application; neither name alone settles the case.

The source's object-conditioned knowledge example provides a useful limited check. It treats actual knowing as perfection while distinguishing an object needed for knowing *of it* from an object donating the knowing perfection. Thus not every relational condition is foreign donation. But the peer in the production proposal is assigned a productive role in the undertaking's original basis, not merely the role of an intentional object. Transporting the epistemic example without that difference would not preserve the subject matter. The wise-determination continuation correctly locates this failed analogy.

My appraisal is therefore asymmetrical. There is genuine warrant for several distinctions and for a conditional harmony explanation. The further assertion that they jointly realize *fully perfected original agency* remains under-supported relative to the source's positive case. This is neither a full countermodel nor an equal-warrant alternative. It nevertheless identifies a legitimate inferential issue without needing to construct an entire metaphysical cosmos simply to question that issue.

## What de se recognition changes

The owner's clarification avoids an easy misreading. Grant the strongest recognition condition: each subject knows its own actual productive role and correctly recognizes the true normative status of its undertaking. If independent original co-completion is a constitutive pure defect, a practically perfect subject cannot knowingly enact it as a fitting exercise. Self-knowledge then exposes the problem and connects correct classification to conformance. It is not idle in that practical route.

But recognition must track the real classification. It cannot substitute for establishing it. If literal universal sovereignty is warranted, a supposed peer cannot truthfully regard itself as fully sovereign. If only unreceived powers, wise activity and successful nonexclusive production have been established, the first-person mode does not add the missing universal relation. The two accepted de-se reports already agree on this; their semantic enrichment is not a rebuttal to the source's full profile.

For the distinct universal route, the source's claim is stronger than ordinary coherent-task omnipotence. “Everything needs A and A needs nothing” has to include a proposed B among actual distinct external bearers, using the same original-dependence respect. Otherwise it does not yield the peer contradiction. No demand that A create B *while B remains uncreated* is legitimate; the Akmaliyya expressly excludes that contradictory task. The source instead disqualifies the whole peer hypothesis through its perfection comparison. That route is real, but naming universal supremacy as a premise does not itself establish its pure-perfection warrant.

## Revised disposition of the older reservations

1. **B5, bearer eligibility:** withdraw identity-preserving acquisition as a mandatory prerequisite of the strongest source argument. Retain only the logical observation that consolidation alone does not supply every de re claim. Comparative disqualification is a substantive additional route, and it answers the general essential-restriction escape.
2. **B1/B6, consent and exclusive completion:** retain one focused reservation about application. The source's norm has explanatory and evaluative warrant. What remains insufficiently established as a universal demonstration is that every primitive nonexclusive original manifestation constitutes foreign completion of each participant's appropriate actual agency. No-donation graphs or unchanged faculties do not defeat the norm.
3. **B2, necessary concord:** retain the distinction between rational co-determination and reciprocal efficient origination. Do not count it as an independently successful full-original alternative. The norm of unborrowed actual adequacy can still challenge a necessarily harmonious pair even where its common reason is not a third efficient controller.
4. **Universal supremacy:** retain separately the substantive domain/purity assessment. If warranted over all actual distinct external bearers in the relevant respect, the uniqueness inference is secure without conflict, merger or nonabsorption. The source gives more than a bare assertion, but ordinary power and nonreceipt cannot silently stand in for that stronger norm.

## What would genuinely distinguish the positions

A further source return is useful only if it advances one of these precise questions:

- Why is actual individual exhaustive original adequacy, rather than already unborrowed appropriate activity, a pure perfection required in every freely chosen undertaking of an original? A successful argument must preserve the source's own wise nonexercise and particular-act distinctions.
- Can a positively warranted ontology of joint manifestation specify whose act, resources and productive adequacy are involved, and explain why real peer contribution does not complete either subject's original perfection? A primitive relation may be legitimate, but its perfection interpretation cannot be supplied by a flag.
- Does independent-original peer participation have a reasoned value for perfect agency that ordinary created participation lacks, without presupposing the peer's eligibility or claiming that every relational condition is harmless?
- Is universal actual supremacy over every distinct external bearer itself an unqualified pure excellence, as distinct from complete governance of all created effects and from arbitrary domination? The domain and dependence-respect must be held fixed.

These are philosophical warrant questions. They do not demand an artificial deduction from neutral thin axioms, another finite action table, or repeated source reading counted as fresh corroboration. On the evidence assessed here, the affirmative common-source judgment is supported and defeasible; the strongest harmonious-original interpretation remains a serious but incompletely warranted dissent. De se transparency gives neither side permission to skip the substantive comparison.
