## 4. Why the proposed shortcuts do not add an operation

**Knowing production.** Omniscience can remove mistaken expectation; power can remove an inability to implement an adopted plan. Neither identifies which practical or communicative undertaking this occurrence instantiates. A source's knowledge of a hearer's uptake is not identical to adoption of its content. These distinctions do not deny the separately supported affirmative case for knowing, willing and wise agency.

**Wise provision.** The source's nondeception and recognisable-guidance arguments deserve their positive weight. But a known demand to follow this envoy and a known confirming sign cannot be inserted merely because guidance in general is fitting. The retained six-route reading already marks capacity, fittingness and actual mission as different dependencies. A new formal conditional would not independently supply the event's role.

**Veracity.** Once genuine owned assertion is warranted, the earlier integrity result has real force. Using it to infer ownership from the truth of an independently correct outcome would reverse its direction. Reclassifying an actually owned false assertion as a test does not evade its scope; classifying every test or caused utterance as an assertion extends its scope without warrant.

**Authority.** Authentication, assertion and obligatory address differ. A rightful directive requires its actual force, domain and recipient-specific application; a truthful duty report can convey warrant without creating the duty. The existing practical-authority result already makes both positive points.

**Proof compilation.** The latest source-sensitive compiler retains exact supports under declared guards. Its report explicitly says the ordinary authority Horn rules were already covered and that root identity does not establish truth or reliability. Supplying an adoption token to its grammar would represent a premise, not establish one. No compiler replay can replace the missing interpretation evidence.

**Cognitive adequacy.** A true result does not certify the recipient's actual basing or competent uptake. The independent known-premise/relevant-inference route is already achieved; this result adds no objection to it. Attestation application still needs the actual source, occurrence, resolved content and operative recipient route appropriately connected.
