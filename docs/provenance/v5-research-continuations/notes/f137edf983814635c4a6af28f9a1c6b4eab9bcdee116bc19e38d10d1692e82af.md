## 1. What the positive argument establishes at its stated strength

The argument is not merely “a missing bridge” relabeled. It offers a coherent rationale for a stronger source principle: a real intrinsic excellence is wholly received in a dependent subject; a complete original source cannot evade its adequate original basis by citing independent materials or partial contributors; and the comparison concerns the cognitive achievement itself, rather than productive range overall. Eligibility then separates truth apprehension from creaturely acquisition, prior ignorance, fatigue and error. This is a genuine positive metaphysical proposal.

Its decisive premise remains respect-specific original-giver priority. “Original qualitative adequacy” by itself demands an adequate intrinsic basis but does not establish that every such basis must be actual cognition. The author now says this explicitly. The main argument does not derive cognition from history-complete productive accounting, nor use the identity theorem to identify every created thinker with its source.

The main conclusion is therefore valid conditionally: genuine received cognition, independently warranted eligibility, the stronger cognitive priority doctrine and bearer locality entail actual cognition in the complete original giver. An opponent who grants only productive completeness has not already granted the priority doctrine. That residual disagreement is substantive and accurately identified.

The interpretation also preserves the role of upstream premises. Actuality, sourcehood, necessary existence, uniform underivability, complete coverage and uniqueness are distinct steps. The uniqueness result retains HistoryComplete, UnborrowedActs and an actual ProductiveWitness. Neither the local orthability no-go nor the cognitive argument silently supplies a deductive Necessary-Being result.

## 2. The impersonal challenge is fairly matched

The author grants the strongest relevant rival one positive, intrinsically determinate source with its own powers, no external substrate or lawgiver, and genuine recipient cognition. Its absence of cognition is not mistaken for absence of all positive nature. Ordinary partial-cause examples are not treated as decisive instances of complete original sourcehood.

The revised explanatory-disclosure argument also respects the semantic/ontological distinction. Individuating a power by its manifestation need not mean that a future effect constitutes the source's existence or efficacy. A manifestation-only name can still be explanatorily thin; that criticism is different from proving the nature contradictory.

The proposed cognitive explanation is properly charged for more than the word “mind”: its actual apprehension, relevant target coverage, purpose and effective production of a distinct subject all need warrant. Creative efficacy does not disappear because the source already knows. A developed impersonal theory with intrinsic teleology and normative powers must be compared at that strengthened level. The author makes no universal comparative victory claim against such a theory.

This is an appropriate acceptance limit. The positive account can be philosophically defensible and more informative than an unspecified variable Q without a theorem proving that no noncognitive categorical basis could suffice. The reviewer accepts the clarity and force of the offered rationale, not a source-neutral proof of the priority doctrine.

## 4. Constructive appendix: positive result and the source guard

The appendix has a valid, useful conditional conclusion. Knowledge of an actual complete determination A, Knowledge of its efficacy implication to X, and the appropriate epistemic closure entail Knowledge of X. Knowing generic standing capacity alone does not fix the actual exercise or its effect. An undetermined outcome or an omitted contribution falls outside the sufficient-determination premise and cannot be smuggled into its conclusion.

This is not necessarily circular. Apprehending a conditional involving X is not already knowing that X obtains. However, if A is described as “the completed bringing-about of actual X,” unpacking A already includes much of the target content. The packet avoids claiming that its finite labels or production function independently prove the metaphysical completeness premise. They illustrate information scope. The premise that the source truly comprehends the relevant actual determination and efficacy relation still carries the philosophical burden.

The freshly inspected [Darʾ section 017](https://www.islamicbook.ws/amma/dr-taardh-alaql-walnql-017.html) supports the distinction between self-knowledge and creating by Knowledge with Will and Power. It also contains an important additional guard at web lines 581–586: Ibn Taymiyya expressly rejects the source's acquiring effect-Knowledge through an inferential or causal derivation from self-knowledge. The entailment is used in our argument; it is not a process by which God learns a conclusion.

The appendix's formal closure principle is compatible with this guard. Its language about explaining effect-Knowledge and removing uncertainty must be read as logical/informational entailment, not divine psychological acquisition or a temporal learning sequence. This interpretation was communicated to the parent and author before completion. The author's separately frozen clarification now states it explicitly and attributes any uncertainty to the restricted formal description. The reviewer read that clarification and independently checked its additional citation to lines 627–629, which repeats the distinction. No change to the Lean theorem is needed; the guard is resolved by the companion clarification.

The consequence also avoids modal collapse. A stable implication from A to X does not make A necessary. Another alternative can contain B and Y. The appendix does not conclude that Knowledge of one effect establishes omniscient coverage, nor does it establish initial cognition or every required attribute of a divine source.
