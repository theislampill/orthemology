## 1. Source capsule

Alexandre Billon, *Dialectica* 77(4), 459–504, proposes completeness relative to explanatory laws; supercompleteness also addresses those laws. Genuine local explanations are presupposed. Law-only determination completes such a chain, rather than turning arbitrary entailment into explanation. Uniform bounded contractions on complete metric domains supply sufficient examples. His variables permit absence. Creative scribes produce books even without predecessor books; the ordinary stick example instead concerns conditional lengths. Fractal laws explicitly include existence, while §8 distinguishes the shape fixed by infinite iteration from that existential commitment. Footnote 28 leaves the broader existential question open. Sections 5.3–5.4 allow law-relative, near-world determination. Sections 8–9 motivate possible further explanations of laws and comparisons with equilibrium and essentialist explanation. [B]

This is a stronger proposal than simply stipulating an actual nonempty solution. Its philosophical assumptions and concrete explanatory examples deserve evaluation. Its advertised breadth does not itself settle the productive-capacity application.

## 2. The independently stated test

The following is our application analysis, not a new theorem attributed to the source.

Let V_i be admissible values at explanatory stage i, including an absence marker ⊥_i when absence is within scope. Let f_i: V_(i+1) → V_i represent the stipulated explanatory transition. For each k, take the range at stage i reachable by composing its next k transitions, without fixing a remote boundary value.

If every f_j maps ⊥_(j+1) to ⊥_j, then ⊥_i belongs to every such range: start from ⊥_(i+k) and compose. Therefore any intersection of those ranges contains ⊥_i. If that intersection is a singleton, its member is ⊥_i. The same point follows directly if an all-absent history is admitted: that history satisfies every absence-preserving transition, so laws admitting a second, actual present history do not determine a unique history.

This observation needs neither finiteness, a first stage, least generation, an actual-resource seed, nor an original bearer. It is elementary. Its research value is exclusively **premise location**. It is not an impossibility proof against all non-well-founded explanation.

In particular, at least four restrictions matter:

1. The value domain must faithfully represent the relevant alternatives. Adjoining absence to a law-admissible domain that already excludes it changes the hypothesis.
2. The transitions must genuinely preserve absence. A law with f_i(⊥) ≠ ⊥ falls outside this argument.
3. The represented target must include actuality, not merely a quantity conditional on existence. A zero numerical length or a false proposition is not automatically a marker for the absence of a concrete bearer.
4. The test concerns the laws actually specified. Removing an existential clause and observing nonuniqueness diagnoses that clause's contribution; it does not refute the original conjunction retaining it.

For the inherited homogeneous received chain, the actual-resource equation is D_i ↔ D_(i+1). Both globally absent and globally present assignments satisfy it. The new criterion thus rejects that bare law set without imposing least generation. The already observed D_0 selects a solution, but is a boundary datum supplied by the occurrence under explanation. Reusing that datum is not an independent law-only explanation.

This application is new only as the consumption of a particular published criterion. The old two-solution control itself is inherited and receives no new model credit.

## 3. The strongest law-supported rival must be preserved

Consider the stronger philosophical interpretation, rather than its absence-preserving weakening:

- There are genuine local explanations of received concrete capacities.
- Actual metaphysical laws have substantive existential content, and jointly exclude an entirely absent capacity field.
- Those laws fix a nonempty dependent history, without a separately provided original concrete resource.
- Their holding is either autonomous or adequately explained in a further account that does not insert such a resource.

The absence argument does not refute this conjunction. Nor does a demand that every explanatory reality already be an individual efficiently supplying actuality. That demand would predecide the disputed nonobjectual-ground/bearer bridge.

The rival now carries intelligible positive obligations: justify the lawhood and explanatory relevance of the existential content; show that it covers the very capacities and bearers at issue; and explain why its actual holding is adequate at the required level. It cannot earn those facts just by naming a sentence a law. Conversely, a warranted autonomous existential law would be a substantive challenge and must not be reduced to a mere description by assertion.

The proper result is therefore asymmetric. The **network-only** received rival has not obtained completion from the new criterion. A **law-supported** received rival has a more precise prospective route. Whether that route actually supplies adequate existential explanation is open here. Neither formal consistency nor this refinement establishes evidential parity with the accepted T17 rationale.

The existing ground-extension work already governs what happens next if such a law is independently warranted. An autonomous actual explanatory basis is not automatically an individuated bearer, and noncausal grounding is not automatically actual efficient provision. This inquiry does not repeat or supersede those controls.

## 4. Output creation is not a complete account of the creator's capacity

There is a source-sensitive reason not to dismiss creative examples as merely conditional. An unconditional output rule can genuinely account for the existence of an output within its nominated explanatory domain. That does not by itself put the producer's own existential constitution inside that domain.

To apply a creative-output example to the present field, distinguish:

- the book or other output;
- the producer's actual exercise;
- the producer's standing productive resource;
- the bearer of that resource;
- the law or explanatory connection governing the exercise.

A producer may create an output with no prior **output** while using a perfectly real standing resource. Erasing a predecessor book is not erasing the scribe. The extension to a world in which every standing resource and every bearer is wholly received is an additional interpretation, not a consequence of the book example.

Likewise, a mathematical law mapping absence to presence does not entail that there is an original individual. It may be interpreted as an unrepresented concrete producer, a nonobjectual existential ground, or an unsupported constraint. The point is to decide among those interpretations on evidence. The inherited empty-prerequisite control already warns about an unrepresented originating operation; it does not decide that every such operation has the same ontology.

## 6. Positive gains and exact residual

The new positive gain is a concrete test for explanatory variation: identify what can still vary with all nominated explanatory laws held fixed. Non-well-foundedness can remove that freedom in some explanatory settings. A blanket inference from infinite ancestry to explanatory failure is therefore not licensed by the formal structure alone. This is compatible with the inherited source's rejection of all-received original capacity, which was never simply an anti-infinity thesis.

The exact unresolved premise is:

**An actually warranted, nonobjectual existential law can completely account for the whole actuality of productive resources and their bearers, rather than only selecting their law-relative profile, without any nonreceived concrete productive resource.**

This statement is neither assumed false nor established by the examples recovered here. It requires an account of actual lawhood and its existential explanatory authority, faithful inclusion of the bearer/capacity field, and a declared standard for explaining the laws themselves. If the law is necessary and fixes the entire history over all metaphysical alternatives, that also imports a much stronger modal commitment than a near-world or laws-held-fixed result. None of those strengths follows from a diagram or fixed-point label.

## 7. Why the first two literature leads did not answer the question alone

Morganti's emergence proposal uses an analogy with probabilistic justification and infinite analysis. Its discussion explicitly separates reality's all-or-nothing existence from the progressive analysis of its structure. It does not claim to answer why there is something rather than nothing. This is a genuine positive alternative to merely collecting local explanations, but transporting a convergent analytical/probabilistic quantity into productive availability requires a further bridge. The continuous argument was read at journal pp. 558–567, §§2–4. It motivates taking the stronger global candidate seriously; it does not by itself provide the needed capacity criterion. [M]

Welchance's 2026 paper is useful as a scope control. It argues against alleged comparative advantages of foundationalism and proposes uniform explanation as a theoretical virtue. It explicitly allows the infinitist to leave the chain brute or explained in an unspecified way. That may challenge a bare comparison with unexplained foundations, but it does not positively explain this field's actuality. Nor does a comparison with bare foundationalism automatically address T17's additional necessity and constitutive-source arguments. Only the relevant explanatory-parity and uniformity sections were read. [W]
