# Independent review of the truthful agency bridge

Review date: 2 October 2026 UTC

## Decision

**Accept the corrected local purpose implication and the pointwise attestation-to-restoration interface as limited results. Do not accept an essential-T0 proof, an actual trustworthy-source conclusion, a metaphysical countermodel, or a new general restoration theorem.** The fresh lane is substantially careful about these limits. The main outstanding work is a substantive assessment of the original-perfection warrant, rather than another restatement that its conclusion needs comprehensive scope.

There is no unresolved logical defect in the purpose lemma when the erratum and its explicit linkage are included. The mixed source is an intelligible denial of a specified bridge, not a demonstrated possible necessary being. The source reconstruction recognizes the strongest doctrine, but its evaluation should distinguish two affirmative warrants and say more directly why the first alone does not establish the second's conclusion. It should also distinguish necessary epistemic knowledge from metaphysical necessity and bare ability from a possible exercise of the complete divine profile.

Two promotion blockers remain: the standing and essential practical-perfection bridge has not been independently established, and no actual episode supplies independently warranted source purpose, truthful content, reception and action binding. These are blockers to the stronger conclusions, not reasons to reject the limited checkpoint. The appraisal below identifies a genuine explanatory criticism and the strongest affirmative reply; it does not treat a demand for source-neutral proof as the sole measure of rational warrant.

## 1 Original perfection has two positive warrants

### 1.1 The source argues for much more than a capacity

A127, the last three main-text paragraphs, first identifies hearing and sight as perfections through a rational comparison. It then moves from an eligible pure perfection to its possession by the divine self: if perfection depended on something else for its affirmation, the source would need external completion; if it depends on the self alone, affirming that self affirms the perfection. A128, its opening paragraphs, adds that created perfection cannot have an original giver inferior in that same positive respect. A129 explicitly supplies two reasons: necessary independent existence has priority over dependent existence, and the creator of a recipient's perfection has priority in that perfection. The conclusion covers all eligible respects, rather than an average of good episodes.

Consequently an adequate review cannot start with an original capacity or a few actual good acts, observe that these permit an exception, and announce that the original-perfection argument has failed. The argument's conclusion is already intrinsic, standing and necessary perfection. C33, C62–69 and P9–13 correctly acknowledge this. P38–40 also correctly refuses to compensate for local practical inferiority by citing cosmic power or a greater number of truthful acts.

The finite character of an observed excellent act is not by itself an objection to the argument's generality. An example can disclose what the relevant perfection is, while a different metaphysical premise supplies universal source attribution. The burden is the truth and warrant of that metaphysical premise and its application to this respect, not the cardinality of the examples. C69 passes this test.

### 1.2 A precise criticism of the no-external-completion route

The following reconstruction makes the substantive issue sharper:

1. F is an eligible pure standing perfection of this source, not merely an action it could perform.
2. If this source lacked F, F could only be present through completion not already supplied by its own nature.
3. No such external completion can be required for an independent original source.
4. Its actual nature is therefore sufficient for F; where that nature is necessary, F is not contingently absent.
5. A knowingly counterfeit in-scope assurance is a contrary exercise incompatible with F.

Steps 2 and 4 need more than the proposition that every attribute or motivating concern actually possessed by the source is intrinsic. An intrinsic combination of accurate cognition, genuine concern and a malignant final end can be described without a foreign supplier. The source's practical arrangement can then be defective without its acquiring that defect from outside. Rejecting this description requires a principle excluding an intrinsic due-perfection privation or establishing the sufficiency of the source's nature for the entire fitting orientation. External independence alone does not state that principle.

This is a substantive explanatory objection: it challenges the completeness of the proposed explanation of how an eligible perfection could be absent. It does not merely point out that the conclusion was not written among the premises. It also does not establish that the mixed nature is possible. If F has already been shown to belong essentially to this very nature, a malignant exception is simply inconsistent with the granted F.

There is a second precision point. The question is not why every possible virtuous act must be actualized. An agent can retain truthful practical integrity while remaining silent, warning, or making a different true statement. The critical contrast is between not performing one particular good act and performing a contrary act that violates the standing perfection. P96–98 recognizes this. Calling the desired result “full manifestation” can obscure the distinction; “indefeasible governance of actual in-scope attestations” is more exact.

### 1.3 The strongest source reply must remain in view

The reply is stronger than “the source has no need.” The positive perfection is integrated practical agency: accurate apprehension of a fitting reason governing an appropriate exercise. Creaturely weakness, partial knowledge and competing appetite need not constitute that excellence. Removing those limitations should not remove the positive relation between the apprehended good and fitting agency. A knowing betrayal introduces a defect precisely in that relation. If a dependent recipient can genuinely avoid it, the proposed independent original giver is locally inferior in a pure respect it originates. A128–129 supplies an a fortiori reason against that inversion.

The mixed rival's having intrinsic capacities does not answer this argument. It concedes the relevant inferiority and disputes that original sourcehood excludes it. Nor does an endogenous malignant end become a countervailing good merely by explaining the action. These points support the affirmative route without first assuming a truthful revelation.

But the comparative judgment and the source attribution remain distinct. The controlled case makes the practical defect intelligible; it does not alone establish the metaphysical priority principle. The original-giver principle is not an instance of ordinary efficient production, since ordinary producers need not instantiate every property they cause. Its force depends on the independent source, pure-perfection and respect-specific superiority claims. The fresh lane should assess that joint rationale rather than offer the intrinsic-defeater description as though it had answered every affirmative premise.

**Recommendation:** retain both warrants separately. Say that the no-external-completion rationale is insufficient when detached from intrinsic sufficiency for this standing excellence; say that the full a fortiori rationale supplies prima facie non-circular warrant which the mixed rival has not refuted. Then locate the unresolved issue in whether this practical excellence and the source-priority principle warrant indefeasible governance for this very source. “Not established here” is supported. “No source-specific necessity warrant exists” would be false. “No rational warrant exists without a source-neutral proof” would impose an unjustified additional standard.

## 2 Necessary knowledge and inability require separate treatment

A218, the paragraph beginning with false claimants permitting injustices, expressly distinguishes knowing that something does or does not take place from knowing that God has power to bring it about. It argues that sound disposition recognizes what is fitting to divine lordship. A226, final paragraph continuing onto A227, explicitly discusses acts God has power over but transcends, including injustice. It rejects identifying divine transcendence solely with an absence of power to act.

These are important controls for the fresh lane's necessity language:

- Necessarily acquired or unassailable knowledge in the source's epistemology is not automatically a proposition about all metaphysically possible exercises of a fixed source.
- A prohibition grounded in wise will need not deny the source's power considered in abstraction from the complete practical profile.
- Conversely, power considered abstractly does not establish that a contrary exercise is possible while the complete wise nature and will are held fixed.

The source's character arguments at A206–210 purport to yield actual knowledge, not merely a frequency estimate over observed testimony. A208 even allows contextual indicators to establish truth in a particular report by a generally unreliable speaker. A210, final three paragraphs, joins known orientation to truth with further indicators excluding deliberate falsehood. One can dispute the warrant or scope, but should not weaken this into the assertion that finite data can only ever support an inductive guess.

C39 and C105 largely preserve this. The temporal-exposure observation at C103 is sound as a criticism of **eventual exposure alone**: a deception can be exposed after an irreversible action. It is not an answer to every A206–210 recognition claim or to the mercy, wisdom and regular-law argument at A221–223. The checkpoint expressly limits it in this way. Keep that limitation when reporting the advance.

## 3 The mixed rival is preserved at the correct strength

C73–81 and P21–32 retain relevant facts, normative understanding, real ordinary concern, successful derivative cognition, effective choice and absence of outside coercion. The exceptional end explains betrayal without presenting it as justified. This is substantially stronger than pure nonbeing, perpetual lying, instrumental stupidity, or a puppet world containing no actual knowledge.

It is also consistent with the Sixth's specified challenge. S13–31 grants necessary unreceived sourcehood, actual cognitive subjects and intrinsic ends while explicitly withholding a metaphysical possibility proof. S97–103 already distinguishes genuine benevolence from intrinsic comprehensive and essential practical completeness. S129–137 already allows accurate cognition and a false practical assurance without concluding that all derivative warrant collapses.

The fresh narrative still owes a modal account of how necessary sourcehood, intrinsic individuation, the origin of derivative virtues and a malformed practical orientation can coexist. Calling the orientations intrinsic does not discharge that debt. A local practical specification can reveal exactly which premise a rival rejects without constituting a countermodel to a full metaphysical theory. C81, P46, P74–76 and I68 correctly retain this limit.

The rival's evidential status is also properly constrained: a specifically targeted betrayal policy has additional unexplained structure; no comparative probability or actual evidence is supplied. Its bare specification does not defeat normal testimonial knowledge, establish equal probability, or establish an actual deceptive source. Its legitimate work is to test which grants entail which conclusion. Any use of it as a demonstrated metaphysical possibility would block acceptance.

## 4 The corrected purpose implication is valid but modest

The relevant claims are C85–99, E11–25 and P86–92. The erratum repairs a real ambiguity: possession of an epistemic purpose plus an act occurring at the same time does not imply that the act was selected to realize that purpose.

A precise local proof is available. Assume an actual attestation e of p, full relevant cognition, a governing aim that this recipient know p through accepting e, actual selection and performance of e as an adequate means to that aim, and refusal in that exercise to select a means known to exclude the aim. Suppose p is false. The source then knows that the recipient cannot know p through accepting e, because knowledge is factive. The actual selection of e as adequate conflicts with the stipulated means constraint. Thus the whole linked package excludes a knowingly false e.

This does not smuggle in actual achieved knowledge: the premise concerns an intended end and selected means, not successful realization. It also need not presuppose universal goodness or inferential processing inside the source. The repaired conditional is therefore valid. A true-p case can instantiate the package without contradiction; it is not universally empty. In the stipulated corrupt-root-1 case, however, the package cannot be jointly instantiated. The argument excludes that purported combination rather than furnishing evidence that an actual source has the required aim.

The practical premise does real work. A source can know the truth conditions, have some genuine concern for knowledge and nevertheless act for another end. It can also deliberately adopt an inadequate means. E23 excludes neither by authentication or vocabulary; it correctly requires the stronger linked package. Calling a public assurance “knowledge-giving” cannot supply its aim or adequacy.

**Warrant classification:** a valid conditional with coherent possible true-content instances; a narrow sufficient condition for local nonfalse attestation; no actual premise warrant supplied; no essential T0 obtained without necessary coverage of the entire assurance domain. P90–92 is explicit on all four points. The result is useful as an interface lemma, but its main agential exclusion remains assumed in the means-selection condition. It is not a new argument that an unreceived original source satisfies that condition.

## 5 The restoration import is correctly typed

I7–30 closely matches the actual D9–23 contract. In that contract the fault set is fixed; target, permission, version, named roots and action binding are held fixed; the sender has sufficient integrity knowledge; the receiver obtains a correct message; an intact selected root performs the adequate repair. The root label works through a pre-agreed code.

If T0 means noncounterfeit attestation rather than factual omniscience, the cognition clause is needed: an honest but mistaken source could otherwise assert false p. Under the listed cognition, integrity and actual event premises, the assertion is true. The independently warranted action contract connects p to safe restoration, and the recipient-warrant clause prevents external truth alone from being treated as justified reliance. If T0 were instead defined as universal factual truth of attestation, cognition would be redundant for this particular implication; the text should keep its operative meaning stable.

The three-way distinction is correct:

1. An assertion of actual integrity supplies truth-apt safety content. It can be coded rather than spelled out.
2. A genuinely bare directive may be harmful while every asserted proposition remains true. T0 alone cannot make it cooperative. If convention or context gives a directive an implicit integrity assertion, it belongs to the first class for this analysis.
3. An honestly described positive-error lottery does not assert the safety of every possible draw. Additional support restrictions could provide that stronger content, but truthful description of the distribution alone does not.

I30 correctly distinguishes a pointwise actual repair from the uniform policy required by D19. Essential nonfalsehood does not supply message availability, expressibility, sending, eventual receipt or action. I36–52's harmful command, silence, narrow diagnostic truth, changed snapshot and unbound root cases are sound deletion checks. The snapshot case expressly changes D's fixed-fault premise, so it is not a counterexample to the retained theorem.

The dynamic discussion in I56–60 is explicitly a reported live cross-lane contract. This review does not verify that mechanism's mathematical proof, reservation semantics or implementation. It receives no proof credit from this assessment. The valid point is semantic: a lifetime mechanism attestation has stronger content than a snapshot of one root.

## 6 What is new relative to the actual Sixth

The Sixth already contains the full motivational taxonomy, the original-perfection rationale, the mixed-rival response, the possession/completeness/modal distinction, the specific false-attestation test, the command/assertion/test distinction, the knowledge-versus-safe-action separation and the positive-error caveat: see S23–31, S65–103 and S127–155. It also explicitly identifies the need for unrestricted original practical completeness or independent actual communication evidence at S155.

The defensible fresh credit is narrower:

- A fully specified safety proposition and an agential reading of its counterfeit use make the inherited challenge more concrete.
- The linked epistemic-purpose lemma supplies a precise local sufficient condition; its erratum makes the act-to-purpose dependency explicit.
- Decision-time irreversibility gives an additional limit on the exposure-only component of character evidence.
- The consumption contract gives a sharper pointwise-versus-uniform and assertion-versus-directive formulation of the inherited operational interface.
- The supplied Asfahani loci provide fresh source comparison and improve the affirmative argument's textual reconstruction.

These are research advances in precision and source assessment. They are not yet a new proof of original practical completeness or a new independent warrant for an actual source episode. The sharpened communicative reason substantially develops S129–133 rather than discovering that false source testimony has a practical defect. Claims of novelty should say this explicitly.

## 7 Findings and required next work

### B1 Promotion blocker at the original practical bridge

**Target:** C57–69 and C113; P7–13, P38–52 and P102–104.

**Finding:** no independent derivation establishes that this source's intrinsic nature is sufficient for indefeasible attestation-integrity. The no-foreign-completion challenge is substantive, but does not defeat the combined a fortiori warrant.

**Required for stronger acceptance:** identify F as an eligible standing perfection without presupposing its source attribution; defend the source's respect-specific original priority and intrinsic sufficiency for F; show precisely why the false exercise is contrary to F. Alternatively provide a developed argument that this transition fails, without treating an unproved mixed narrative as a metaphysical countermodel. The present scoped checkpoint can stand while this remains open.

**Target:** C109–111; I19–30 and I64–68.

**Finding:** no actual source event, independent Aim/Link evidence or full recipient/action contract is established. Neither the valid purpose lemma nor essential nonfalsehood by itself supplies a communication.

**Required for an actual conclusion:** source- and event-specific evidence for the precise message, its scope and attribution, and the warranted route from its integrity content to the actual repair. A local metaphysical argument cannot substitute for those facts.

## Final assessment

The repaired package warrants a conditional local acceptance. It does not warrant a categorical anti-Taymiyyan result, a proven malicious necessary source, or a positive essential-truthfulness conclusion. The strongest useful next move is an explicit comparison of the two source-specific warrants and the exact standing perfection to which they are applied. The source offers a substantive, potentially non-circular rationale; the mixed intrinsic-end response explains why independence alone does not settle it. Neither side has earned the final metaphysical conclusion merely by stating its preferred full profile.
