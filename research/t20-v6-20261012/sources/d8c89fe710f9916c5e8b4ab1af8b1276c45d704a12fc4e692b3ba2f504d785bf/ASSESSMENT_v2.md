# Common-coupling frontier: design disposition

## Decision

**Reject the proposed ordinary weighted-Hall iff as a new project mathematical completion.** An earlier accepted control/restoration interface already owns a stronger result. The P01 successor's remaining-obligation label is a true statement of that Lean component's scope, not evidence that the whole project lacks the mathematical theorem or a rational witness-producing implementation.

The defensible remaining increment would be a specifically requested **kernel-certified finite rational transportation theorem and P01 adapter**, openly credited as formalization of inherited mathematics. No implementation is authorized by this assessment, and no recommendation is made to prioritize it over the stronger existing executable interface merely because the old P01 list calls Hall open.

## Precise optional formalization contract

Take finite types X and Y with Fintype and DecidableEq, tables mu : X -> Rational and nu : Y -> Rational, pointwise nonnegativity, equal total mass, and a supplied relation R : X -> Y -> Prop. Define N_R(U) as the finite set of y admitting x in U with R(x,y).

The contract is:

There exists a rational matrix kappa : X -> Y -> Rational, pointwise nonnegative, with row sums mu, column sums nu, and kappa(x,y)=0 whenever not R(x,y), if and only if, for every finite subset U of X, sum_{x in U} mu(x) <= sum_{y in N_R(U)} nu(y).

Include empty carriers, zero total mass and zero-weight atoms. Do not require normalization or nonempty carriers. Support positivity and off-relation zero are equivalent under nonnegativity.

For any indexed family R_i, instantiate R(x,y) := forall i, R_i(x,y). This gives one kappa satisfying every support condition, not a swap of forall i exists kappa_i. The abstract theorem may use classical decidability of this intersection; it supplies no algorithm for deciding membership in an infinite semantic family. For executable use, require a finite Boolean adjacency matrix or an explicit terminating decision procedure for the supplied intersection. A finite list of relations is one sufficient representation, not a completeness claim for all relation environments.

Concrete executable input objects, if later authorized, should be dimensions m,n; exact normalized numerator/positive-denominator rational tables indexed by Fin m and Fin n; and m-by-n Boolean adjacency entries. No floating tolerances, implicit labels, JSON frontend theorem or semantic-relation oracle should be claimed. A P01 adapter would use the finite outputSupport subtypes and restrict outputMass/outputCoupling; off-support extension back to Term must be proved explicitly.

## Feasible proof architecture in the retained snapshot

The read-only Mathlib c44e0c8 snapshot contains Mathlib/Combinatorics/Hall/Finite.lean and the theorem Finset.all_card_le_biUnion_card_iff_existsInjective'. Its finite indexed-set formulation avoids needing a fresh max-flow library.

1. Prove the natural-weight transportation lemma first. For integer masses a(x),b(y), form clone types L = Sigma x, Fin(a(x)) and M = Sigma y, Fin(b(y)); connect clones exactly when their underlying atoms satisfy R.
2. For any subset T of L, project to U in X. Its size is at most sum_U a. Its clone neighbourhood has size exactly sum_{N_R(U)} b. The weighted Hall hypothesis therefore implies ordinary Hall for all clone subsets, including partially selected fibres and zero fibres.
3. Invoke finite Hall to obtain an injective matching L -> M. Equal total masses give equal finite cardinalities, so that injection is surjective. Counting matched clone pairs yields a natural matrix with exact row/column sums and required support.
4. Clear rational denominators using one positive common multiple for both tables. Prove the scaled nonnegative masses are natural numbers. Divide the natural matrix by that positive denominator. Product-of-denominators suffices; no least-common-multiple optimization is necessary.
5. Prove necessity directly by exchanging finite sums, restricting to the neighbourhood and applying nonnegativity. Derive the intersection-family and finite-support/P01 corollaries without changing their source semantics.

This yields a **proof of existence with an exact mathematical witness built from a Hall matching**. Using Classical.choice to select that matching is not an executable verified solver. A separate exhaustive finite matching search could yield a total witness extractor after additional proof, but would be a new engineering scope and may be impractical after denominator expansion. A verified polynomial-time max-flow solver, extraction to Python, rational parser, bit complexity theorem and deployment contract are all excluded. Existing exact Python solvers should be reused where they meet the task rather than replaced by a weaker enumerator.

## What would actually be gained

Only the formalized contract would improve on two-point tests: arbitrary finite cardinalities, exact quantification over every subset, rational witness existence, and a machine-checked supported-coupling criterion without assuming shared syntax/seed. It would be evidence-level and interface-level completion, not a new mathematical insight, new ordinary project theorem, new general solver or broad avenue6 closure.

A coupling establishes mathematical compatibility of supplied marginals and support conditions. It does not establish physical independence, authenticity, collective epistemic warrant, admissible observations, actual available randomization, incentive compatibility, license or policy authority. The existing equality/flip counterexample and CR1's stronger constrained-interface warnings must be retained.
