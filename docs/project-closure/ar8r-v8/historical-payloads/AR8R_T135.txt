AR8R-T135 — Terminal-summary factorization
Setting

Let X be the authoritative campaign-state class, S:X->Y a summary representation, and

E(x)=1 iff the authoritative model-actionable burden set at x is empty.

Theorem

An exact summary-only terminality rule e:Y->{0,1} satisfying

e(S(x)) = E(x)

for every admissible state exists exactly when E is constant on every fibre of S.
Proof

This is the standard factorization criterion. If E=e∘S, equal summaries have equal E. Conversely, if E is fibre-constant, define e(y) as the common E-value of any state in the fibre. ∎
False-zero countermodel

Two authoritative states may have the same prose summary while one contains an open burden and the other does not. Then no summary-only validator can be both sound and complete.

Adding E itself as a summary field restores recoverability but is an oracle insertion unless it is derived fail-closed from the authoritative burden sources.
