# AR8R-RP-001-v1 — Common-model minimal-nonintegration control

## Provenance

This is a post-campaign role-preserving replacement associated with historical identity AR8R-T150. It is not the unavailable historical payload.

## Setting

Let `K` be a declared class of structures in a fixed signature, let `Γ` be the union of all currently registered route premises, and let `C` be the proposed integrated conclusion.

## Statement

If there is one structure `M ∈ K` such that `M ⊨ Γ` and `M ⊭ C`, then `Γ` does not semantically entail `C` over `K`. Consequently, the registered routes do not establish an integrated champion at that declared signature and model-class scope.

## Proof

Semantic entailment `Γ ⊨_K C` means that every `M ∈ K` satisfying every member of `Γ` also satisfies `C`. The stipulated common withholding model satisfies `Γ` and falsifies `C`, contradicting that universal condition. Therefore `Γ ⊭_K C`.

## Scope and nonclaims

- Separate countermodels for each route are insufficient; the theorem requires one common model for the union.
- The result is relative to the declared signature, premise union, and model class.
- A sparse-signature model is not by itself a proof of full metaphysical possibility.
- The theorem does not establish universal strict separation, source truth, or the falsity of `C`.
- The mechanism is standard semantic nonentailment and receives zero general novelty credit.
