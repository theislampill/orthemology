## 2. Reconciliation with the retained source commitments

### T17 P65–68: the affirmative grant must survive

T17 does not merely say that an original has whatever powers it happens to display. P65 separates giver-priority from the broader pure-perfection argument. P66 accepts additional genuinely feasible agency without need, contradiction, borrowed resource or impairment; P67 refuses an arbitrary declaration of an intrinsically limited nature as a sufficient reply. P68 equally explicitly keeps standing power separate from exclusive actual exercise. These clauses must be used together.

The final additive interpretation receives credit for granting each same rigid bearer the constructor for any natural-number vector and, more specifically, the sum of any two commands. This is a concrete relative-scope concentration witness. It is stronger than a free `Eligible` flag or a new hypothetical collective bearer. The finite 0–2 ceiling is only a search bound; it cannot be called a metaphysical eligibility limit. This interpretation contains no mechanically represented acquisition cost, borrowing, or conflicting intrinsic resource, so it fits a deliberately generous fragment of the retained positive-power grant.

It does not prove that all metaphysical effects are vectors, that every intrinsic act of another original is transferable, or that exclusive actual provision is a standing operation. T19 P106–111 explicitly protects those further distinctions. In particular, P111 does not require a general origin-essentiality thesis. A numerical effect's counterfactual identity cannot be disproved merely because its origin differs, but neither is it established by equal output coordinates. The report correctly makes this an interpretation issue rather than treating either view as settled.

### The late sources add real pressure without settling every transition

Majmu 20, retained rendered L908, argues from unchanged agency and genuine added productive work to a different complete result. L912–913 explicitly strengthens complete power and decisive will into the effect's production through the agent alone. L917–921 advances concentration, the existing subject's receptivity to fuller power, perfection, and outside dependence. Those claims are present; the experiment must not reduce the source to a weak material implication or to feared disagreement.

The directly read Minhaj 2:180–183 passages likewise identify realised efficacy as perfection and argue that constitutive perfection cannot be externally acquired by what is necessary by itself. Their substantive force is retained. What remains disputed is the application from an external result's requiring differentiated work to the agent's receiving constitutive or source-mode completion. T19 P58–63 and P75–78 expressly separate that norm and its repair from bare capacity and from arbitrary receipt of a token state. Treating M1/P2b or B5's disputed application as upstream assumptions of their own test would be circular.

T17 P74–81 and P108–120 distinguish intrinsic reasons, complete determination, external occasions, and wisdom-compatible willing. The directly read Minhaj V03L0268–0273 includes the objection based on common benefit and knowledge, together with the reply classifying necessarily conjoint willing as productive conditioning. The source does not overlook concord. Its classification is exactly what the experiment is permitted to inspect. The Safadiyya act-kind/token control at retained L623–627 does not make each token an eternally possessed constitutive perfection.

### Saturation

OR supplies the genuinely stronger adverse control: both actual commands remain full and each suffices for the output after the other's emission is suppressed. Neither owns every emitted token. This separates outcome guarantee from entire emission-process provenance at the chosen signature. It is not dismissed merely for denying B6.

But the test gains that separation through redundant output paths. Causal relevance somewhere is not indispensability in the actual saturated configuration. The complete emission process also differs after suppression. Treating solo output sufficiency as whole original ownership yields two whole owners and violates whole-target CND; treating whole ownership as ownership of all actual emissions yields no whole owner, making that whole-target CND condition vacuous. Primitive-emission CND remains nonvacuous. The two relations cannot be substituted without a source-to-signature bridge.

The Lean separation theorem conjoins saturation equations with an ownership model; it does not internally prove that the emission list is the unique metaphysically correct decomposition of the output's original causal basis. The report appropriately refuses to promote this finite product interpretation to a full model of the intended original-source profile.

The inherited T19 CND rationale concerns genuine entire original production in the same respect, not just multiple sufficient conditions. Its direct application to this saturating interface is unsettled. The report's conditional entire-provision-plus-CND exclusion is sound and nonvacuous; it is not an independent derivation of entire provision from standing power.

## 5. Concordance and the rectangular-control theorem

The rectangular theorem is valid: a command forcing q against every rival command cannot coexist in the mixed profile with the rival's command forcing a distinct r. It requires joint availability of that mixed profile. T17's wisdom controls prevent inferring rectangularity merely from each target's individual availability.

The executable concordance candidate does more than impose a bare diagonal relation. For each of four intrinsic preferred vectors, each agent independently maximises the negative Manhattan distance from that vector over its full command set. The maximum is unique, both calculations select the same vector, and the production map attains it. Neither calculation reads the other's will or a future product. There is no SourceMode term in that score. The structural graph has no peer-to-capacity/will edge.

The score contents nevertheless match by stipulation. An independent adverse control gives the agents different intrinsic preferred vectors: concord and one target's guarantee fail. The calculation thus provides a genuine deterministic reason-to-choice fragment, not an independently grounded account of why ultimate agents must share that reason. It does not model epistemic accessibility, knowledge, or foreknowledge; those remain unrepresented. A shared abstract parameter is not by itself a third efficient producer, but calling it intrinsic does not certify its metaphysical originality. Those are the precise residuals, not a claim that necessary correlation automatically means efficient donation.

### Why a factive label or predictor does not itself open another productive search

A finite factive annotation is available as a conservative extension: retain the same admissible contexts, commands, production function and ownership interpretation, and append a correct output prediction or a predicate recording truths of those contexts. Every formula in the old production/ownership signature keeps its truth value because its interpretation is unchanged. Accordingly, this addition cannot repair the additive fixed-command failure, remove the saturation/provenance distinction, change whole-target CND, or justify a formerly inadmissible mixed profile. This is a written semantic observation, not a new kernel claim or a completed model of knowledge.

A predictor computed from the two actual commands can be perfectly accurate while doing no explanatory work for their agreement. An own-reason-only predictor is correct in the stipulated matched-score cases, but matching reason contents and the relevant context domain remain the premise doing the work. If an outcome-dependent predictor is instead made an input to the wills, that changes the original equations and raises a new causal/fixed-point question; a factive label cannot certify its noncircular original status. Knowledge tracking its object is not automatically efficient receipt from that object, and knowledge of an already assumed concord does not independently establish it. These two distinctions respect the retained source objection and T17 P78–80.

A genuinely new route therefore needs an independently supported source-semantic principle grounding the matching intrinsic reasons and their accurate cognition of feasible targets, deriving the nonrectangular admissibility/complete-determination domain without covertly borrowing a peer's power or assuming the resulting agreement. It must also retain, or separately defend, the source's standards for a wise actual productive role and whole-effect original attribution. The current score supplies a calculation once its ranking is fixed; it supplies none of those new warrant premises. Merely adding epistemic vocabulary would relocate this same explanation burden and add no new discrimination, so its absence does not justify reopening the bounded campaign indefinitely.
