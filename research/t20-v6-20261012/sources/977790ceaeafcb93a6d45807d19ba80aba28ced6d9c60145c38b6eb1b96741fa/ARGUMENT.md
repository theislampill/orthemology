# Existential warrant and intrinsic explanation

## Result

The source argument can explain what a necessary original bearer would contribute without making that bearer cause itself or making all its acts necessary. Its strongest form concerns the actual availability of received productive reality, proceeds through independently defended productive completion and identification, and asks whether the existence of the identified original bearer is intrinsically intelligible. A genuinely existential nature could answer that question in a way a bare contingent stopping point does not.

The fresh result is a more demanding test of that last claim. **An explanation through a source's own nature need not be an earlier cause or a distinct grounding input.** It can instead be proposed as an explanation through essential status, or as an explanatory link with no prior base. This improves the positive reconstruction by avoiding a misplaced self-production objection. It also improves the rival: a contingent intrinsic or empty-base explanation cannot be rejected merely by the rule that an exercised power needs its bearer. The source advocate must defend the actual existential status attributed to the bearer, and why that status excludes absence, rather than obtain necessity from a causal presupposition objection alone.

The actual case supports the occurrence and local evidential reception of a correction. It provides no new independent identification of an ultimate bearer or inspection of that bearer's existential essence. Accordingly, N2 is **not closed** here. The positive route has an articulated rationalist rationale and an intelligible noncausal form, but the present evidence does not establish that reality satisfies its exhaustive existential demand. The strongest rival is developed below as a contingent active holistic basis with a noncausal existential explanation, together with the distinct brute and genuinely infinitary alternatives it must not be confused with. Its own ultimate explanatory commitments also remain unwarranted. Rival survival is not a possibility certificate or a proof of parity.

This is a provisional research checkpoint, not a final tranche disposition. It is fresh Seventh research from actual delivered Sixth bytes. It credits no lost Seventh work and changes no canonical status. Its narrow contribution is the intrinsic-explanation test, its symmetric application, and a connected actual-case comparison. It does not derive cognition, truthfulness, unity, revelation or restoration from necessary existence.

## 1 The actual target and its warrant

The Sixth local-correction case concerns an earlier report that a displayed Minḥāj page did not identify its editor or edition. The accepted review corrected that report. The original document remained frozen, so the correction did not silently change its historical evidence. In this restart the exact local record was read and its hash checked. The host with the www prefix was also reopened successfully. Its header again displayed the editor Muḥammad Rashād Sālim, publisher Muʾassasat Qurṭuba, first edition, 1406 AH, and eight parts. An initial attempt without www failed; that failure was not treated as evidence that the metadata was absent. [Displayed host header](https://www.fnoor.com/main/articles.aspx?article_no=32248), returned lines 33–37.

The warranted conclusion is that the host displays identifiable metadata. It is not that the transcription has been collated against a physical first edition. This distinction supplies a particularly useful actual target: the reason for the correction is a real difference between what was claimed about a definite page and what the page displays. Its truth is not manufactured by the evaluator's preference.

Let e denote this actual correction under the admitted philosophical reading that it is a genuinely warranted cognitive exercise. The record demonstrates the evidence-supported correction as a human-readable episode. It does not by itself demonstrate consciousness or original cognitive ownership in every mechanism involved. Nothing in the argument depends on falsely promoting a machine trace into such a demonstration.

At the local level, the episode uses resources it does not itself originally supply: the page's displayed characters, accessible records of the earlier claim, the evidential comparison and capacities for reading and inference. Not all these dependencies are of one type. The page determines whether the report about its header is accurate; that is a truth and reference relation. The reading supplies claim-specific evidence; that is a warrant relation. Actual devices, persons and their capacities enable or produce the exercise; those are causal or constitutive relations. Their joint involvement does not make them one relation or one ultimate bearer.

Both sides in the comparison below must preserve this actual correction as warranted. A proposal that merely regenerates the correct names by luck, denies the page as the actual target, or treats every cognitive source as an inert relay has changed the case. Conversely, the success of this correction does not show that all of its remote causes have one metaphysical explanation. The local evidence fixes a real target without quietly closing the ultimate question.

The retained route distinguishes actual exercise E0, actual reception E1, productive completion P1, particular-bearer identification P2, explanatory aptness N1, exhaustive nonbrute existential adequacy N2 and unconditional existential sufficiency N3. The names matter less than their separate burdens. Knowing that some resource was received does not prove that all reception terminates. Establishing an original basis does not prove that it is one particular bearer. Knowing that a bearer's existence is appropriately questioned does not establish that it has an answer. An answer through its own nature does not acquire existential content merely because it is called intrinsic.

The predecessor also distinguishes complete endpoint production from complete actual origination. That distinction remains binding. A being with sufficient power to produce e, even every endpoint in a field, has not thereby been shown to provide every actual productive occurrence by which those endpoints came to be. Origin closure concerns that stronger target. This paper does not use N2 to repair a missing origin-closure argument or to manufacture a common source.

These are retained results. The fresh inquiry begins where the predecessor itself invited further work: can the actual existential explanation of an original bearer be warranted, and what exactly counts as its own intrinsic explanation?

## 3 A connected positive argument

### Actual supply is the starting point

The actual exercise cannot be supplied by an uninstantiated specification. If a capacity really produces or supports this correction, the relevant bearer or basis must be actual in the respect needed for its contribution. This is not an inference from imagining an ideal source. It begins with a concrete episode and the independently warranted dependencies of that episode.

The fact that the recipient genuinely owns and exercises a power is compatible with that power's existence being received. A developed source argument should welcome real derivative agency. A person who understands a text need not be reduced to a passive transmission point in order for that person's existence and powers to have an origin.

### Productive completion remains an additional claim

The affirmative next seeks an account of the whole actual supply, not just a separate answer at each local link. Its reason is that relocating an acknowledged received resource can extend its history without changing its received manner of possession. An original actual source would contribute what an endless description of still-received supply appears not to provide: actuality owned without another supply being presupposed for that very possession.

That reason is not a proof that an actual non-well-founded order is impossible. A genuine infinitist offers real active suppliers at every link, not a chain of empty conditional instructions awaiting a first member. The affirmative must defend why complete productive provision requires original possession even against that account. We preserve P1 rather than pretending that source actuality alone establishes it.

### The bearer must actually be identified

Suppose productive completion succeeds. A second argument is still needed to identify a particular g as the bearer of the original actuality used in explaining e. Joint sufficiency of a plurality is not individual sufficiency. The priority of a whole is not the necessity of this numerical whole. A description such as the ultimate source of e can pick out an intended role without establishing that exactly one bearer fills it or that the same bearer must fill it in other possibilities.

For the necessity inquiry, therefore, let g be the particular bearer delivered by an independently defended P2. This is a conditional stage of a connected argument, not a declaration that the present evidence has delivered g. Its actuality and originality must concern the same existence whose necessity is eventually concluded.

### The positive reason for existential adequacy

The strongest N2 rationale is that the ultimate explanation should not merely leave its indispensable existential resource unaccounted for. If g is genuinely contingent, g's actual presence and complete possible absence form a real existential contrast. A catalogue of what g can do when present does not answer that contrast. Calling the boundary fundamental identifies where explanation stops; it does not show why stopping there is adequate.

There are three positive reasons to seek an intrinsic answer. First, the original inquiry was occasioned by actual received being, not a wish that reality satisfy an arbitrary aesthetic preference. Second, the underlying question remains intelligible at the proposed ultimate resource: why is the actuality being used available at all? Third, an adequate answer through g's own nature would close the demand without borrowing another actual resource or making g cause itself. This gives the proposal a determinate explanatory benefit.

These reasons justify the inquiry and can support a defeasible preference for a successful intrinsic account over an otherwise comparable candid brute. They do not establish an unconditional right to an answer. Nor is the benefit earned simply by postulating that g has whatever nature would supply it. The proposed nature must be independently characterized and warranted. The working proposal's earlier formulation as a presumption of existential intelligibility is consequently retained only as a rationalist motive; it is not promoted to N2.

The Aṣfahāniyya passage supplies the historical positive rationale: being through oneself is presented as one's own reality sufficing for existence. The surrounding argument distinguishes bare necessary existence from proving the intended creator. MF 2:9–11 places the need/sufficiency contrast in particular recognition rather than requiring a prior universal syllogism. These are actual primary commitments, not a consensus or a substitute for contemporary warrant. The supplied English Aṣfahāniyya translation was also visually checked at printed pages 25–26, PDF pages 32–33. Its sufficiency and ipseity discussion matches the topic of the Arabic passage, while one sentence renders the Arabic modal phrase about admitting nonexistence as actual nonexistence; the argument here retains the Arabic modal force. This is a narrow translation observation, not a general judgment on the translation. [Aṣfahāniyya](https://www.islamicbook.ws/ageda/alaqidt-alasfhanit-001.html), relevant discussion at returned lines 61–76; [MF 2:9–11](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/22/131/?idfrom=&idto=&start=), returned lines 944–950.

### Intrinsic intelligibility without self-production

The affirmative should not say that g exercises a power which first makes its whole bearer actual. An actual exercise would already require actuality of g in the very respect supposedly being originated. It should instead say that g's manner of being is intrinsically existential: g does not wait for a further actualizer because the reality g is does not leave its existence open in that way.

This is an explanatory claim about what g is, not a second productive event before g exists. There is no general requirement that every intelligible explanation be an efficient cause. A status or constitutive explanation can disclose why a further causal request is misplaced without supplying an earlier bearer. The proposal is therefore stronger than the slogan that a source has no incoming arrow, and it is not disposed of by a causal self-origination objection.

If that intrinsic status is truly factive and unconditionally existential for this g, g's absence is excluded. The conclusion is necessity of this bearer, not merely that something of its kind exists in every possibility. But the crucial qualification is substantive: a true factive existential essence is what has to be established. Neither conditional identity nor the nonexistence of an observed producer establishes it.

### Why contingent acts can remain contingent

The existence of g and a particular exercise a of g are different explanatory targets. To explain a, citing g as its already actual subject is legitimate. To explain the whole actuality of g, citing g's already exercised power is not a nonpresupposing origin. Thus rejecting self-production of g does not reject g's contingent activity.

Moreover, the necessary existence of g is not the necessary obtaining of every item in its complete actual productive state. An actual determination can be contingent even when its bearer is necessary. Complete productive explanation need not therefore be confused with proof that no alternative act could have occurred. The supplied Oneness translation, printed page 34, PDF page 41, explicitly discusses effect-necessity given complete will and power and rejects an essence stripped of them. That conditional productive determination should not be converted into necessity of the same act from bare source existence. If the affirmative allows non-necessitating explanation here, it owes the rival a fair hearing when that rival uses a non-necessitating explanation of concrete realization. The principled difference concerns the explanatory role of an already actual subject; it is not a universal ban on every alternative outcome.

The connected positive conclusion is consequently exact: an actual received exercise, an independently established complete original basis, an identified particular bearer and a warranted intrinsic existential nature would yield a necessary particular source. The argument now explains how the last stage could be intelligible without self-production. It has not acquired the missing actual-world truth of that stage merely by explaining its intelligibility.

## 4 Why the word intrinsic is not enough

The most important fresh test asks whether a contingent original bearer could be explained through its own nature without efficient self-production. Four constructions must be distinguished.

An **actual constituent character** of g may explain how g acts. If that character is instantiated only through g's already obtaining whole actuality, it does not provide an earlier or independent explanation of that whole actuality. The efficient self-production objection is sound here. Transferring it to all noncausal explanation would require a further same-respect priority premise.

An **identity-fixing condition** states what any instance of g would have to be. It can determine identity or exclude misidentification while leaving instantiation unsettled. Its intrinsic content does not provide an existential explanation merely because it is complete as a definition. This is the retained conditional-essence point, now applied to the strongest intrinsic rival.

An **autonomous fact about g** might obtain independently of g's concrete actuality and ground that actuality noncausally. If it is an independent existential input, g is received on the broad interpretation. Aboutness is not the same as being an actual intrinsic constituent. However, the affirmative must accept the same classification if it treats its own source's essence as a separate independently obtaining ground. Modal strength alone does not decide ontological identity.

An **explanatory link without a prior base** is different again. Here the claim is that g's existence is explained by its essential or lawlike status, not by an earlier existential input. The strongest version identifies the fact explained with its own explanatory link. An ordinary self-grounding arrow does not faithfully represent this proposal.

Kappes's work supplies a useful primary framework for this last distinction. It separates bases from links, considers empty-base explanations, and examines an essence/existence identity application. It also distinguishes metaphysical explanation from epistemic justification. Its candidate identities remain controversial; it does not establish a contingently self-instantiating concrete world. [Kappes, Self-Explanation and Empty-Base Explanation](https://doi.org/10.1017/apa.2021.8), §§5–7.

The present adaptation is our own. Suppose a rival calls the contingent actual existence of C an intrinsically generative status, which explains C without an independent base. That is not refuted just by observing that an exercised power needs C; no exercise of a previously actual C has been offered. But writing down a generative status is not enough. What makes it explanatory? Why does it belong to C? Why is its obtaining intelligible rather than a brute occurrence re-described as a law? If the purported law is contingent and unexplained, N2's question returns at that status. If the status explains itself, the rival must warrant the proposed fact identity and explanatory relation.

The affirmative faces a symmetrical demand. Saying that g is intrinsically existentially sufficient is a substantive characterization. If that characterization is true, its explanation need not be causal; but its truth does not follow from its formal admissibility. Treating it as a constitutive identity can answer one charge of circular production while leaving a different burden, actual identity warrant, intact.

This exposes two questions inside a compressed appeal to N2: why there is any nonbrute explanation of the ultimate existential fact, and why an intrinsic explanation must have the existence-entailing status needed for N3. The second cannot be hidden by defining adequate to mean necessitating. A defended restriction to factive essential explanation would answer it; the restriction itself requires argument.

## 5 A developed rival with real intrinsic activity

### The local account

Let C be a proposed actual holistic basis of the field containing e. C is internally differentiated. It contains actual persons or cognitive bearers, text-bearing structures, stable powers, sources of evidence, and the relations through which one bearer can intentionally inspect another target. Its derivative bearers are real. Their knowledge, will and power are retained wherever genuinely intentional production has been granted. No local intentional exercise is replaced by an impersonal label while the conclusion is still called intentional.

On this proposal the correction occurs because a cognitive bearer accesses the actual displayed header, understands the relevant field labels, compares them with the earlier report and withdraws the false absence claim for that reason. The material and cognitive capacities are grounded in C's actual differentiated nature. Normative correctness concerns the evidence and target; it is not whatever the whole happens to output. This explains the admitted local episode in the same granular respects preserved by the affirmative.

The account is holistic in that the field's complete intrinsic condition is prior to the full condition of its derivative parts. It does not infer that the parts are unreal or that the whole is numerically simple. Schaffer's priority monism provides a developed precedent for real parts with whole-priority and for basic heterogeneity. Its arguments do not establish that this numerical cosmos exists necessarily. [Schaffer, Monism The Priority of the Whole](https://www.jonathanschaffer.org/monism.pdf), especially §§1 and 2.3–2.4.

C could possess indefinitely many or infinitely nested constituents without needing a smallest passive instrument. Its local powers can be genuine even if their bearer is existentially contingent. At this stage the rival has offered an account of actual local supply and constitutive organization. It has not yet explained why C exists.

### The candid contingent version

The cleanest N2 denier says that C's existence is primitive and contingent. Its intrinsic nature explains its operations; it does not explain its own actuality rather than absence. This is a cost the rival acknowledges. It rejects the claim that a real existential question is guaranteed an answer.

The affirmative cannot refute this view by saying C would have to create itself. It offers no self-creation. It cannot refute it by presenting an absent world as a false-belief situation either; if C and all thinkers are absent there is no mistaken thinker. Nor is the rival required to deny local evidential warrant. A primitive ontological boundary does not make every derivative belief accidental.

Its strength is that it preserves the inspected case and accepts a modest stopping point without positing an independently inaccessible necessary nature. Its weakness is precisely that it has no ultimate existential answer. Whether the explanatory deficit outweighs the costs of a rival intrinsic account is a comparative question. The present evidence does not make that tradeoff disappear.

### The strongest noncausal completion

A stronger rival offers an autonomous noncausal principle A which existentially explains C. To avoid mere verbal substitution, A must have unconditional realization content: it cannot say only that any C which exists behaves in certain ways. The proposal is that some eligible concrete reality is actual in virtue of A, while the particular realization C and its history remain contingent. C then supplies the real local powers and intentional exercise; A is not a sentence which efficiently pushes C into being.

An axiarchic version gives this content a substantive rationale: some forms of concrete reality are objectively worth realizing, and such value has direct existential significance. The actual occurrence of truth-directed cognition offers one candidate good, while the realization of a whole world requires a much wider and independently defended evaluative account. A necessary principle could favor or require a class of eligible realizations without specifying this fixed token C. The detailed route from C to e then remains the actual intentional and productive story, rather than an inference from goodness directly to the header's names.

This is our deliberately demanding construction, not a doctrine attributed wholesale to one author. Mulgan's primary account identifies the axiarchic connection between goodness and actuality. Pearce explicitly treats axiarchism as an alternative to existence-only existential grounding and develops the case for complete non-necessitating grounding. Those resources motivate the construction; they do not prove its truth. [Mulgan, Beyond Theism and Atheism](https://doi.org/10.1111/phc3.12420), abstract; [Pearce, Metaphysical Rationalism Requires Grounding Indeterminism](https://doi.org/10.1017/apa.2024.17), §§1–4 and 7.

The price is substantial. The rival must warrant the value-to-realization relation, the eligibility of the actual whole, and the claim that the relation is genuinely explanatory rather than a re-labeled existential primitive. A local good does not show that this total cosmos is best or even that goodness has existential efficacy. The source advocate is entitled to press these burdens. It is not entitled to dismiss the account solely because A is noncausal or because different concrete realizations remain possible.

On broad receivedness, C in this account is received. The rival therefore contests identifying the ultimate autonomous explanation with a concrete efficient bearer; it does not grant broad originality of C and then refute the conditional necessary-source argument. If A's own status is offered as an empty-base explanation, the fourth intrinsic-explanation test applies. Moving the explanation into a link does not exempt its truth and identity from warrant.

This gives a serious actual explanatory proposal: the same concrete productive history explains e; a holistic condition explains the local resources; an alleged autonomous realization principle explains why there is concrete actuality; indeterminism allows the fixed bearer C to be contingent. Its assertions concern the actual case, not merely an invented possible world. Its actual-world ultimate premises remain unverified, just as the affirmative's intrinsic existential premise remains unverified. Explicitness is not truth, but it is enough to identify the exact substantive disagreement.

## 6 The genuinely infinitary alternative is a different rival

One must not strengthen the holistic rival by silently alternating between an original whole and no original actuality. A genuine productive infinitist instead proposes actual reception at every link, with every actual supplier active through another actual supplier. There is no first empty node and no temporal task of building infinitely many links sequentially.

This account can explain the local powers relevant to e through actual prior supplies. It challenges P1, not merely the modal inference after P1 and P2. A global constitutive treatment can also avoid making every analysis finite, but does not thereby answer the separate question of actual existential provision. Morganti develops the constitutive alternative and expressly limits its reach regarding why anything exists. Oberle challenges the transfer from removing a finite first cause to rejecting a genuinely infinite order. Neither paper is evidence that the actual world is an infinite efficient series. [Morganti, Dependence Justification and Explanation](https://iris.uniroma3.it/retrieve/e397d80e-d5d2-b0de-e053-6605fe0a1c76/ERKE%2010.1007_s10670-014-9655-4.pdf), §§3–4; [Oberle, Grounding Infinite Regress and the Thomistic Cosmological Argument](https://thomasoberle.weebly.com/uploads/3/8/1/8/38187281/thomist_paper.pdf), §§2–3.

The strongest affirmative question is whether every actual reception can be fully accounted for while all actuality remains received. The infinitist answers through the actual whole non-well-founded account, not merely through the formula each member has a predecessor. The affirmative asks why that is complete provision rather than an indefinitely articulated received field. The disagreement must be settled on the actual relation and target. Finite graphs, a removed first link and a theorem conditional on complete coverage do not settle it.

No further infinitary theorem is attempted here because that would displace the requested N2 work. P1 is retained as a live burden. Its independence is itself essential to assessing how much the existential argument has achieved.

## 7 Three explanation standards and their consequences

A constraint explanation identifies what an actual field may be like. It can be genuinely explanatory without being an efficient producer. Chen and Goldstein's minimal primitivism provides a developed example of governance by constraint rather than dynamic production. That is a useful correction to the assumption that every serious law account must be a hidden agent. It does not by itself establish an unconditional realization of the concrete field. [Chen and Goldstein, Governing Without a Fundamental Direction of Time](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.09226), §§3–4.

An existential explanation purports to account for the actual presence of the productive resources. This is the question N2 introduces after an original bearer is identified. Both the source's intrinsic sufficiency and the rival's realization principle must answer it. Neither wins by pointing to a sophisticated system of constraints which still presupposes a realization.

A contrastively necessitating explanation excludes every relevant alternative under the full explanans. It is stronger than complete productive supply when indeterministic explanation is allowed. Necessity of g is properly obtained only if the intrinsic existential status excludes g's absence; it must not be inferred simply from the completeness of g's account of a contingent exercise.

These distinctions yield a real comparative filter. Pure constraint accounts do not answer the claimed existential question merely by being mathematically complete. Active contingent accounts answer local production while openly leaving the existential boundary primitive. Noncausal realization accounts reach the existential question only by adding genuine realization content. Necessary-source accounts reach it only by earning an existential essence for the identified bearer. A formal account earns credit for exactly what it represents; no formal completeness substitutes for the truth of the added metaphysics.

The filter does not force equal merit. The best developed and independently warranted explanation could deserve preference. But a judgment of preference needs the actual content and warrant, not just a comparison between the words brute and necessary.

## 8 The exact surviving denial

The most economical denial of N2 remains: this actual original field exists contingently, and its existential presence has no further explanation. That denial preserves the local correction, local active powers and its evidence-sensitive warrant. Its cost is the openly unexplained existential boundary.

The strongest nonbrute rival denies a different step: every adequate ultimate existential explanation must be the intrinsic existence-entailing nature of a particular concrete active bearer. It proposes an autonomous realization relation, perhaps with no prior existential base, and lets the actual concrete bearer remain contingent. It must justify that explanatory relation and may fail. But its failure cannot be inferred solely from its noncausal type or from the possibility of alternative outcomes.

The intrinsic contingent version sharpens the dispute once more. If a rival says the bearer's own existential status explains it, the affirmative must show why the status is either merely conditional, subject-presupposing, externally supplied, or genuinely existence-entailing. An empty-base explanatory link is the additional case that prevents the first three labels from being automatically exhaustive. Showing that its proposed status lacks independent warrant is fair; calling it causally circular without representing its proposal is not.

The affirmative therefore needs two independently defended commitments: actual ultimate existential intelligibility and the correct account of intrinsic existential explanation. Its world-directed task is to show that the identified bearer really has the proposed status. The actual correction supplies neither commitment by itself. The source's particular-recognition method may offer a route to that knowledge, but the method's claimed extension to this precise ultimate bearer is a further substantive warrant, not a consequence of noticing a local causal dependence.

## 9 What would change this result

A positive advance would identify an actual g with a contentful intrinsic nature whose unconditional existence and productive relevance follow for independently warranted reasons. Such a nature should not be assembled by adding exists necessarily and supplies everything as unconstrained clauses. A successful recognitional route would need an account of its object, epistemic entitlement and relevant defeaters.

A different positive advance would show why the best actual explanation of the received exercise supports that nature over the developed holistic or noncausal competitor. It could be comparative rather than deductive, but would need actual discriminating reasons. Invoking the bare desirability of explanation or declaring one theory simpler without specifying its commitments is insufficient.

A rival advance would warrant an actual noncausal realization relation or a substantial intrinsic empty-base status, including its application to this field. A law of admissible descriptions is not enough. A novel necessary-sounding name for the existential boundary is not enough. For the axiarchic version, the crucial missing evidence is the connection from value to actual realization, not just a further example of value.

The present research does not meet any of those stronger thresholds. It does establish where they differ and removes a bad barrier to both sides: noncausal intrinsic explanation is not automatically efficient self-production. That is a genuine advancement of the constructive and adversarial argument, while N2 remains open.

## 10 Integration and credit

The admitted local R5 interface is unchanged. This paper adds no warranted common bearer, ground, source-knowledge identity or complete origin-closure principle. An account may satisfy the local intentional-source requirements while leaving ultimate sourcehood plural, holistic or unresolved. The result is therefore available as a philosophical constraint on further arguments, not as an R5 defeat.

No downstream theorem may cite this paper as having established essential truthfulness or a repair oracle. Necessary existence, were it established, would still not supply a particular attestation, its content or a recipient's entitlement. Removing the present source argument leaves the local correction intact and leaves operational results which assume a cooperative informed source at their existing scope.

The Sixth receivedness, autonomy, infinity and modal-collapse distinctions are explicitly retained. Dasgupta's prior distinction between conditional and existence-entailing essence is credited, as is the need for an additional existential-grounding premise. The fresh contribution is the symmetric scrutiny of intrinsic explanation, including link versus base and explanation versus warrant, applied to the actual correction and the developed rival. [Dasgupta, Metaphysical Rationalism](https://shamik.net/papers/dasgupta%20metaphysical%20rationalism.pdf), §§9 and 13.

There is no theorem of metaphysical possibility, no claimed unconditional Necessary-Being proof, and no reconstruction of missing Seventh results. The outcome is a source-bound research advance with a located residual denial.
