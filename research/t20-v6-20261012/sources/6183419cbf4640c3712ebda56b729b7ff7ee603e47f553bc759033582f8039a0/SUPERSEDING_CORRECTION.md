# Precise superseding correction to the inherited implicit-sign report

7 October 2026. The inherited file remains unchanged and retains its research credit. This correction applies to its stronger negative interpretation, not to its executed restricted positive case.

## File and statements affected

`../testimony-rivals/IMPLICIT_RESULT.md`, section 4, says that the selective courtesy policy "has ... no intent to make the audience accept the commission claim." Its summary also presents the policy as a stronger noncommunicative purpose rival. The corresponding `implicit_attestation.py` output field `intended_positive_claim` is computed exclusively by whether the selected plan's `aim` string equals the positive-acceptance terminal goal. `selective_courtesy_intends_positive_claim` inherits that classifier.

## Replacement reading

The selective policy has a different **terminal-aim label** in that model. The implementation does not supply its selected intermediate intentions or establish that acceptance is outside a chosen means–end route. A courtesy end is compatible with intentionally acknowledging a claim as its means. Therefore the old output does not establish nonindication or different public adoption.

The same restriction applies wherever a courtesy label alone was interpreted as absence of communicative intention. The independently preserved stable-motive review already recognized this correction. The new continuation supplies explicit adopted objectives, selected causal recipes, separate actual/expected uptake, and a new action discriminator; its own broader claims remain conditional on those structures.

## Further target refinement

The source's intentional indication/informing should not be identified with successful persuasion. The new hostile-audience control has intended indication and decoding without belief acceptance. The failed-decoding control has intended indication without successful decoding. Public adoption and actual truth are separate and are not computed by this new model.

## What is preserved

The old three-policy calibration result remains correct within its declared policy class, and the reviewer reproduced it with latent source-belief alternatives. A genuine plan taking positive acceptance as an end is a positive example of communicative intention. No critique of the old converse licenses discarding that positive result, the source's no-prior-pact route, or ordinary contextual knowledge. The old model's behavior and test results remain historical evidence of what it actually computed.
