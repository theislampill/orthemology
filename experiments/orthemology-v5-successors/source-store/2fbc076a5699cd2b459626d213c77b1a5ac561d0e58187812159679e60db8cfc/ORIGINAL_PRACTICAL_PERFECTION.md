## Result

The strongest affirmative argument is a combined account of original possession and intrinsically integrated agency. Its point is not simply that an original source knows which act is right. It proposes that the source's own practical apprehension is the internally sufficient principle of fitting choice, so that knowingly choosing a dominated practical exercise would be a defect in the very original excellence through which it gives genuine practical excellence to recipients. This is a serious rational proposal. The fact that it is evaluative, disputed, or not reducible to measurement supplies no objection to it.

The present investigation does not independently establish actual or essential nondomination N. Its substantive reason is specific. The explanatory work that makes intention intelligible is determination by an apprehended attraction or end. The work that excludes the controlled betrayal is determination by the objectively decisive ordering of reasons. Purifying the former of ignorance, coercion and external need does not yet explain why its ordering must be the latter. The strongest perfection argument supplies precisely that additional content, but the newly inspected integration argument does not independently warrant its source wide application. It returns to the same original practical priority claim with a richer account of what that claim means. This is neither a counterexample to the claim nor a judgment that belief in it lacks rational support.

A narrower advance is available at R_G, the eligible strict repair premise. The new Tenth responsibility and source alternatives arguments constrain the alleged cost of a repair. Where care is already owed, truthful performance does not first place the source under that responsibility. Where an adequate noncoercive performance is genuinely available, abandoning an unsupported demand for control does not surrender a practical authority previously established by the care relation. Those two supposed losses cannot, by themselves, block an otherwise eligible complete practical improvement. This removes particular comparison objections; it establishes neither the alternative's same bearer eligibility nor unrestricted R_G. Actual nondomination remains a further question.

## 1 The exact question and the inherited credit

Fix the source, recipient, root, version, interval, actual safety facts and the genuine assurance role. The source knows that the root is unsafe and falsely presents it as safe for irreversible reliance. The assertion is not a command, quotation or candid test. No undefeated justification for this false assurance exists in the controlled case. A truthful warning changes the response, not the target's safety. The source remains genuinely beneficent elsewhere. The case is a conditional philosophical comparison, not a discovered ultimate source event. [B1]

Ninth already earns the local defect judgment, the source eligibility of relational fidelity, and the conditional exclusion from R_G plus N. It also explains why a strict overall improvement need not be a loss free Pareto improvement R_P. Its complete profile comparison includes consequences in other contexts and preserves the same bearer with its full essence. A direct original perfection argument may instead challenge a proposed defective essence; it need not first admit that essence into a counterpart space. None of those results is new here. [B1–B2]

Earlier work already examines original giving, the no external completion rationale, the purification of creaturely limitations, the symmetry between object dependent good and bad purposes, and the distinction between a capacity and necessarily excellent exercise. It already reads the principal perfection and integration loci. We therefore do not present the fresh inspection as historical discovery, or a renewed statement of those distinctions as a new foundational result. [B3–B4]

The genuinely new input is narrower. Tenth establishes a special pro tanto responsibility arising from knowingly organised legitimate reliance, which can survive a change in the source's current purpose. Its independently accepted authority study also tests whether an alleged need for control survives the alternatives actually available to the source, both when a restrictive arrangement was selected and at the later recipient decision. These give concrete content to a practical comparison before any claim of impeccable source character. Their acceptance is at the stated bounded normative scope, not acceptance of a source wide metaphysical law. [B5–B6]

## 3 Why the affirmative proposal has real force

Consider an exercise which understands a recipient's epistemic good, freely undertakes a truth linked role and responds to the decisive reason for fidelity. What is excellent is not merely its successful production of a sound or a belief. Its understanding and action are fittingly integrated. The significance of the recipient's good is internal to the chosen action's object; it need not enter as an external command to the source. Honouring the reason therefore need not be obedience to a superior, purchase of reciprocal assistance, or repair of the source's lack of information. This inherited eligibility argument remains strong. [B1,B3]

The original possession proposal adds explanatory asymmetry. A received practical achievement cannot be the independent source of the original agent's possession of the very excellence it gives. If the original account leaves the whole fitting integration only in the recipient while offering merely indifferent efficient production in the source, something important about the declared practical respect has been left unexplained on that account. Appealing to greater force or more numerous effects does not address that respect. The source account has positive reason to include its own understanding and genuine fitting concern in the explanation of the received excellence.

The comprehensive extension is also intelligible. Finite agents need not exercise an excellence without every impediment; the original mode is not measured by the accidents of finite manifestation. Perfect fidelity need not mean reciting all truths, producing a uniquely best world, or never revising an optional plan. It can mean that whenever the source knowingly undertakes this truth linked role, its response is not corrupted by an intrinsically unjustified ordering. That excellence has an identifiable positive content. It is not an unlimited list of favours owed to recipients.

One can accordingly have a prima facie rational reason for original practical integration. A speculative exception has no automatic rebutting force against that reason. A directly apprehended source sensitive superiority could also warrant a stronger judgment without deduction from something more basic. The issue is what is actually apprehended. The comparison must include this bearer and the complete practical respect; it cannot silently replace original intelligible giving with necessary flawless governance of every exercise. That disciplined positive assessment is inherited, not withdrawn here. [B2–B4]

## 4 The strongest integration argument

The most promising further argument can be reconstructed without assuming a malicious essence:

1. Genuine intelligent action is explained through an apprehended end, rather than by the bare occurrence of a preferred output.
2. Full practical apprehension presents the actual relevant goods and their decisive ordering without ignorance, diverted attention or misappraisal.
3. In an intrinsically integrated original agency, this apprehended ordering is sufficient for fitting intention. A lower attraction does not become governing merely through an alien impulse, unmet bodily demand or borrowed practical constraint.
4. A knowingly false assurance in the controlled case opposes that ordering.
5. Such an assurance cannot be an exercise of that original agency.

The important proposal is step 3. It should not be dismissed as an arbitrary added axiom. It offers an explanation of why an ideal agent's action is its own rational exercise: what it accurately apprehends as worth doing genuinely governs its doing. The agent is not forced by something foreign to obey a proposition in its knowledge. The same intrinsically grounded agency understands and wills. For an original source, the account can seek to remove the incomplete integration characteristic of received agency while retaining intentionality and freedom from external control.

This supplies more than an exclusion label. It identifies the proposed connection's direction, object and modal basis. If the source's intrinsic practical apprehension is essentially this sufficient principle of choice, essential fidelity at the controlled respect follows. The conclusion would not require a separate survey of alternative profiles or construction of a better source. Nor would it make the source indifferent between fitting alternatives, or require one particular action where several are fitting.

### Apprehension of an attraction and apprehension of decisive choiceworthiness

There are nevertheless two different explanatory achievements in the proposed connection. To act for an end is to find something in the outcome attractive in the practical sense sufficient to explain pursuit. To act according to the decisive balance is to give each genuine reason its appropriate weight. A source's own preference is not a source external obstacle to its agency; it can be part of what needs evaluation.

The difficulty is not established by painting an evil label onto a formally consistent source. It can be located before specifying any source's alleged bad nature. Suppose we ask why this intention rather than another is determinate. A preferred end answers that question. Suppose instead we ask why this preference is fitting given all relevant reasons. The fact that it determines intention does not answer the second question. Making the preference completely explicit and its content completely known does not supply that answer either. The questions concern the actual explanation of willing and its practical authority, respectively.

A strong intellectualist account can answer that distinction rather than concede defeat. It may hold that complete apprehension of a decisive practical reason is itself sufficient motivation, and that the purported gap between lucid judgment and governing intention is an artefact of incomplete practical awareness. An ordinary weak willed human is not an immediate counterexample: attention, affective salience, habits and incomplete understanding complicate that case. It would therefore be a mistake to infer the account's falsity from familiar human misconduct or a verbally specified omniscient exception.

The strongest affirmative reason is that a practical reason is apprehended as favouring the action, not merely registered as one more neutral feature. A fully present understanding of decisive choiceworthiness can therefore be an intrinsically directing cognition. If nothing competes with that direction, an intention contrary to it has no remaining positive explanation. At that narrower scope the account has substantial force: arbitrary contrary selection should not be introduced just to preserve a doubt. The present investigation accepts this challenge to a reasonless exception.

There are two ways an advocate might extend it. One holds fixed an independently established governing end and argues that the source cannot knowingly choose what defeats that very end. That is the inherited token purpose route, with actual instrumental coherence doing real work. The other says that the fully apprehended objective ordering necessarily governs which ends become governing. The second is the candidate advance toward N. It cannot be evaluated as if it asserted only the first, and it is not refuted by imagining a different goal after its comprehensive direction has been granted.

The decisive question is whether original independence eliminates every competing inclination. It excludes a priority imposed by a donor, an external coercer or a bodily deficit only to the extent that those are independently excluded in the source account. It does not by that reasoning classify every intrinsic attraction as either a correct appraisal or a received force. An inclination can concern the agent's own possible activity, with the source supplying its efficacy. That description does not certify an actual or possible malicious source; it identifies an explanatory category that the independence premise itself does not address. Removing it requires a reason about the source's intrinsic practical order, not an additional proof that it receives nothing from outside.

Nor need the affirmative declare all attraction bad. A source may select among fitting alternatives for a determinate intrinsic reason without every choice being forced by a unique best outcome. The proposed law must explain why such intrinsic preference remains available within fitting choice while being unable to govern against a decisive reason. Perfect practical integration gives exactly that principled distinction. Its positive rationale is the superiority of self directed responsiveness to genuine value. The present difficulty is not that the distinction is incoherent or nonbasic; it is that unreceived intentional agency, considered apart from original practical priority, supplies no further ground for imposing that distinction on the actual source's complete intrinsic order.

But the source application still needs a reason that excludes a noncognitive priority without merely classifying it as cognitively incomplete. Calling a will fully informed has a clear object: no relevant truth is misrepresented or omitted. Calling it fully responsive to that information has a different object: no contrary priority governs. The strong account could identify them through a defended constitutive theory of practical apprehension. The present source premises concerning original cognition, intentional efficacy and freedom from external need do not themselves defend that identification. The additional explanation is the source's original pure practical perfection, the very source wide claim whose warrant is being assessed.

The result is not an epistemic veto on that perfection principle. A basic rational recognition of it can still carry the inference. It means that combining the pure perfection and integration arguments does not yet produce two independently earned warrants for N. The latter clarifies the inner structure of the former; its preference based differentiation argument does not independently turn that structure into objectively impeccable integration.

### The important positive stopping point

One should retain all of the argument's earned content: intelligent production has genuine end directed structure; fittingly caring for the recipient is eligible for an independent source; wise self government does not imply externally imposed servitude; and integrated fitting agency is superior at the very practical respect being compared. These are reasons for the affirmative view, not neutral vocabulary. The unresolved issue is whether the actual original source necessarily has that integrated excellence comprehensively, rather than whether the excellence is intelligible or worth possessing.

There is no new actual evidence here selecting a mixed essence. Nothing in this assessment establishes that one is metaphysically eligible. It instead explains why the newly examined purposive structure and absence of external impediments do not add an independent argument for the decisive comprehensive identification. The old burden has not been settled simply by restating it, and no closure result is claimed from that fact.

## 5 What absence of need can and cannot contribute

Absence of need can do genuine comparative work. If a lie is defended as required for the source's survival, replenishment, protection from coercion or access to a separately supplied power, independently established nonneed removes that defence. Likewise, if truthful care would otherwise exhaust a finite maintainer, a source with independently established relevant effortless capacity does not inherit that particular cost. The improvement is a reduction in actual reasons and feasibility obstacles, not an inference from the mere absence of a motive to all possible conduct.

Two precautions matter. Original possession is not automatically a proof of unlimited effortlessness or every proposed alternative's availability. And a purpose involving recipients need not be the source's receipt of an externally conferred standing perfection. These qualifications are already established in earlier work. [B3–B4]

The new care responsibility makes a further point possible. A source may have discretion not to create or initially offer a provision, yet acquire a responsibility through what it actually organises. Continuing that responsibility is not evidence that the source has become existentially dependent on its beneficiary. The source's own action explains why this recipient's reliance now matters in this particular way. Originality therefore does not annul the relation; neither does the relation import a second source of the original agent's being.

This blocks a specific alleged loss in a repair comparison. If a truthful warning or adequate release respects an already incurred responsibility, whereas the lie attempts to evade it, the truthful option does not first create the relevant restraint. The responsibility is present on both sides. The lie's practical success at ignoring it is not additional original independence which the truthful option destroys. This is more specific than observing that relational fidelity is a permissible source excellence. It uses the new persistence result to identify the correct baseline for comparing the source's options.

## 6 A narrower improvement in the repair comparison

Consider a source G which has knowingly organised R's legitimate bounded reliance. An interruption or transition now requires care. G has two candidate responses:

- F gives usable truthful notice and an adequate protective arrangement, preserving the target and the relevant independently justified interests.
- D keeps R's reliance through a false assurance, or couples its promised protection to an unsupported surrender of R's independent check.

Assume for the comparison that F is genuinely available to this same G, with its full essence preserved; that the practical effects beyond this episode have been examined at the relevant scope; and that no independently justified competing claim requires D. Those are substantive facts. We do not obtain them merely by stipulating that G is an original producer, that it can form a true sentence, or that D is wrong.

Now consider two alleged compensating advantages of D: it avoids G's becoming bound to care, and it preserves G's sovereignty by securing the recipient's submission. Neither advantage follows in this case.

First, the care reason is already present. A change of G's purpose and an absence of new promises do not erase the attributable exposure. F acknowledges and addresses that reason; D leaves it unmet. Treating F as creating a new burden and D as retaining an unencumbered original condition compares the options against different histories. It is not a complete profile comparison of the fixed case.

Second, responsibility to provide care has not itself established a right to make an unnecessary demand. If an equally adequate, reasonably available performance preserves the relevant goods without that demand, G cannot obtain the missing entitlement solely by declining to offer it. The freedom to originate and carry out an action is not identical to an independently justified right to make this recipient obey. F can remain G's own competent and voluntary performance. No external superior supplies its power or chooses for it. It follows that D's unsupported extraction of submission is not, by itself, a pure perfection of original agency which F sacrifices.

These arguments do not assume that every restriction is oppressive or that recipients are always entitled to their preferred procedure. Genuine third party interests, a valid allocation of decision making, an appropriate testing purpose, a costly accommodation or a target preserving coordination need can supply a different case. Such a reason must identify the affected respect and the real source side constraint. General appeals to sovereignty and authorship do not identify it.

### Selection time and repair time must not be conflated

The corrected Tenth authority result adds an important temporal control. A source may unjustifiably reject an available noncoercive arrangement, then recruit innocent participants around its restrictive choice. Their later reliance can create genuine present costs of switching. Those costs do not validate the source's earlier selection, but they are not fictitious merely because the source wrongfully created them. The recipient may now owe temporary cooperation to innocent others while the source retains fitting responsibilities for remedy or reconfiguration. [B6]

For R_G this makes two comparisons necessary when the history matters. An earlier alternative can show that the source had no right to manufacture the restriction. It does not by itself supply a currently available truthful repair with all relevant goods preserved. The current comparison must retain the innocent parties' actual interests and the costs of a fitting transition. Calling them consequences of a prior wrong does not remove them from the complete practical profile.

A whole history alternative may still be relevant if it genuinely preserves the same bearer and the fixed assurance case's targets and circumstances. That needs an explicit eligibility argument. One may not remove the earlier arrangement, thereby changing the actual exposure or assurance role, and silently use the resulting easier case as the repair of the unchanged present case. Equally, retaining an actual prior history for a current repair does not vindicate that history. Evaluation of the source's earlier choice and evaluation of the recipient's present action can diverge without contradiction.

Independent fair formation is therefore not established by sunk investment, and current remedy feasibility is not established by an earlier costless opportunity. These corrections further constrain the R_G comparison rather than proving it. They also prevent an artificial victory for either side: the source cannot turn wrongful preparation into rightful command, while the repair advocate cannot erase real dependent interests to obtain a loss free alternative.

Nor does the argument ignore every satisfaction accompanying D. An actual pleasure, protected project or other independently valuable condition has to be assessed on its own merits. We exclude the claimed practical rank of duty evasion and gratuitous domination, not every possible collateral good occurring alongside them. Satisfaction simply in getting one's way cannot be counted as an independently pure source perfection by renaming it sovereignty; if a separate good is alleged, the comparison must state it. A sufficiently strong contrary reason could remove strict superiority, while a lesser genuine loss might still permit R_G without R_P.

The bounded conclusion is therefore exact. Given the independently warranted same bearer alternative and complete relevant comparison, the two alleged losses just examined provide no obstacle to strict practical improvement. If the complete assessment independently excludes other obstacles to strict superiority, the improved option can supply R_G for that particular offending exercise. A critic's failure to name another loss is not evidence that none exists. We have not established the required assessment for every source profile. We have also not established N: the actual source's choosing an available inferior exercise is exactly what N would exclude.

### Why this is not a disguised freedom theorem

The result does not define eligibility by moral attractiveness. If a full nature positively excludes F, the counterpart route cannot silently delete that exclusion. Conversely, naming D essential does not establish the alleged nature's possibility. The direct original perfection argument may challenge such a nature, but the present comparison does not decide that dispute.

What has changed is the evaluation of purported losses after eligibility is independently secured. An agent can preserve original authorship, knowledge and power while governing its own exercise fittingly. Whether it actually can make that alternative exercise is a separate metaphysical matter. Preserving that distinction allows a real comparison result without laundering normative superiority into modal availability.

## 7 Scope of the result

The source wide proposal concerns standing practical agency; the controlled assurance concerns one exercise. An actual strict repair concerns one complete alternative to an actual profile; it does not establish that every offending profile has such an alternative. Actual N would exclude domination of that actual profile. Essential N requires the corresponding warrant throughout the same bearer's relevant possibilities. The source comparison never moves from one of these claims to another just because all concern one agent.

The responsibility result is also broader than nonlying in one way and weaker in another. It can assess deliberate withholding or negligent withdrawal without classifying silence as assertion. Yet it does not determine what the source actually does. A true warning can be issued in breach of a continuing duty, and a duty can persist when no truthful warning is issued. These facts matter when selecting a proposed repair, without changing the meaning of T0.

For actual recipient use, source identity, the current token's content, target, version, interval, the route of access and the relevant action permission remain separately necessary. The argument gives no actual D1 or D2. It establishes no revelation, source wide guarantee, new permission or operational R5 defeat.

## 8 Disposition and the next decisive question

No independently warranted actual or essential N is added by this inquiry. The direct original perfection argument retains its genuine rational appeal; an independently warranted basic recognition with the required content could succeed. The present investigation has not obtained that additional recognition or a new argument compelling its comprehensive application. Its failure to do so is a limitation of this investigation, not a proof of the belief's irrationality or an endorsement of a mixed source.

The bounded new step concerns R_G's comparison burden. When evaluating a candidate faithful repair, keep the source's already incurred responsibility on both sides and distinguish original voluntary agency from an unsupported jurisdiction over the recipient. Those adjustments eliminate two spurious compensating advantages. They can make a concrete strict comparison possible once the actual alternative and remaining consequences are independently supported. They do not establish that all repairs are eligible, that all possible losses are absent, or that the actual source chooses a nondominated exercise.

Further foundational progress should address one substantive question: why is the actual original source's full practical apprehension itself an indefeasible principle of objectively fitting intention? It would be useful to defend that connection through an independently motivated account of practical apprehension, source nature or intrinsic completeness. Another inventory of capacities, another formal malicious profile, or another stipulation that a wise source is wise would not answer it. For the repair route, the useful next work is instead a definite same bearer alternative with its real collateral effects, evaluated against the corrected responsibility baseline.
