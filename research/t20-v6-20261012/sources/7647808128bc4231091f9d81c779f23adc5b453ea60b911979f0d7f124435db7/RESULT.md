# A stronger harmonious rival: contextual power without sole provenance

7 October 2026. Substantive continuation of the sovereignty-choice study. The initial report, code, results and bindings remain an unchanged checkpoint. This is a **different primitive production law**, not the inherited UNION interpreter and not a semantics-preserving modification of it.

## Finding

**Yes: UNION's failure of full context-by-context output ability is a limitation of that law, not an unavoidable failure of every harmonious rival.** A stronger reduced interpretation can jointly retain:

- an executable full solo constructor repertoire in each same bearer;
- a successful means for every positive exact target in every fixed peer-exercise context;
- the actual shared fitting-goal hypothesis and peer-independent own-full-target policy;
- actual coactive success, non-peer-valued own-activity ends, and reason-derived factive cognition;
- unchanged own-exercise and exact-output success when the peer's exercise is suppressed;
- positive joint productive support with no efficient receipt into either original's standing power or cognition.

It still lacks a fixed own exercise guaranteeing the target against arbitrary peer changes. It still lacks entire individual original provision in every coactive production. Its stronger contextual ability also requires **non-invariant manifestation of a peer's solo effect across some joint contexts**. These are substantive limits, especially given Majmu's unchanged-agency/nonabsorption argument.

This is an earned joined reduced-signature rival, not a full metaphysical countermodel, a proof that its policies are necessarily wise, or a claim that all operational comparisons are admissible worlds satisfying the source's complete perfection package.

## 1. The new law, over positive exact created targets

Keep the same source-neutral two-event catalogue {e0,e1}. Its three nonempty exact created targets are:

- Q0 = {e0}
- Q1 = {e1}
- Q2 = {e0,e1}

These are alternative **complete created event sets**, not quantities of productive effort. Label0 denotes the existing created event e0, never nonbeing or inactivity.

An active intrinsic setting has a solo-output label. Two matching active settings yield that same target. Two different active settings yield the third target. Explicitly, the rows are:

- Setting0 against settings0,1,2 gives targets0,2,1
- Setting1 against settings0,1,2 gives targets2,1,0
- Setting2 against settings0,1,2 gives targets1,0,2

Idle is a separate absence of creative exercise. An active setting q with an idle peer yields Qq. Both idle yield no created events; that absence is not an additional positive target. The bearers and their standing powers need not disappear when an exercise is suppressed. Each retains the ability to refrain from its own exercise; no power to force global noncreation against an active peer is claimed.

The active law is the familiar three-element idempotent quasigroup, equivalently F(a,b)=2(a+b) modulo3, where2 is the multiplicative inverse of2. It is symmetric and invariant under all six permutations of its three outcome labels. Thus its three-label behavior is not an arbitrary priority assigned to A, B or one privileged outcome.

Quasigroups are defined by bijective left and right translations; idempotence is F(q,q)=q. The affine construction and idempotence condition are standard. I inspected those definitions and the short idempotence proof in Jedlička, Stanovský and Vojtěchovský, *Distributive and trimedial quasigroups of order243*, pp.1–2 and Definition2.4/Lemma2.6 on pp.4–5. No classification theorem or new Latin-square discovery is claimed. [Primary author-hosted paper](https://cs.du.edu/~petr/data/papers/distributive_and_trimedial_quasigroups_of_order_243.pdf)

### No fixed finite ceiling is doing the work

For a k-event catalogue, the number of nonempty exact targets is n=2^k−1, hence odd. Index them by the cyclic group of size n, let h be the inverse of2, and set F(a,b)=h(a+b). Then F(q,q)=q, and the response to peer b for desired target q is a=2q−b. The construction therefore extends to every such finite catalogue by one formula. The three-event/seven-target instance was also checked as an implementation control.

This does not establish power over every metaphysically possible effect. For larger catalogues the choice of target coding is an additional feature of the proposed law; the full arbitrary-label symmetry above is claimed only for the three-target case.

## 2. One joined interpretation, with goal and setting kept separate

The model is `src/reactive_context.py`. Each `Original` has a fixed bearer identity, fixed standing power and knowledge tokens, and an immutable, executable setting repertoire. Its `issue` operation really produces an exercise from that repertoire and rejects unavailable settings. The repertoire is the same for A and B and does not change with the peer or with the desired target.

An `Exercise` has two different semantic types:

- `TargetCode`: the intended exact created-event set
- `SettingCode`: the intrinsic exercise, identified by what it would produce solo

A contextual response may intend Q0 while using setting2. It must not be described as intending Q2 merely because2 is its setting, or as using the same operative exercise as setting0 merely because its goal remains Q0. Equality of goal and setting in ordinary concordant cases does not collapse these types.

For every represented ordinary reason-context q, the disclosed intrinsic policy is simply: **issue one's own full-target setting q with exact goal Qq**. Neither peer execution nor a created outcome is a policy input. As in the inherited rival, a fitting common Q and the worth of this own activity are hypotheses. The model does not derive their metaphysical necessity or derive the policy uniquely from output-only goodness.

The actual profile is (q,q). It produces exactly Qq. Both complete intentions and both own-activity ends are satisfied. Each end requires that bearer's own genuine participation and Qq; it does not value or require the peer's originality or participation as part of its content.

### Productive support and actual influence

For each output event the engine instantiates one direct rule from the actual positive intrinsic inputs. There are no earlier created inputs, emitted standalone products, hidden mixer, or earlier cancellation occurrences in the declared event ontology. In the coactive case the support of every event is {A,B}; when A alone acts, it is {A}.

This is not just an unexplained support label in an otherwise insensitive evaluator. Every change from one active setting to a different active setting changes the complete output, with the other setting fixed. At each diagonal actual case, for every created event and either agent, an alternative active setting can remove that event from the output. These are checked operational influence properties.

They remain different from **removal-necessity**. Deleting the peer's exercise at (q,q) leaves the remaining setting q and its exact Qq success unchanged. Nor do sensitivity tests by themselves establish the source's positive created contribution, metaphysical actual-cause attribution, or original completeness. The primitive direct productive interpretation and complete-event ontology retain those warrant burdens.

### Primitive cognition is represented, not manufactured

The standing knowledge and power tokens belong to the same original objects across the represented reason-contexts. Forecasts are computed from the assumed known intrinsic policies and the law before actual execution is supplied. Every displayed act, output, support, rule and intention result in the prediction agrees with the ordinary actual run. Accessibility is fixed by the known reason-context; every true proposition over these represented contexts is known. Inherence, intrinsic reason determination, truth correspondence, and efficient donation are separate relations.

No efficient path runs from the foreign act into either standing knowledge or power token. Adding a foreign-donation edge is detected. This is affirmative structural nonreceipt and factivity credit, not an assertion that finite context semantics establishes unrestricted metaphysical omniscience or necessary existence.

The ordinary epistemic model contains only the diagonal policy worlds. Off-diagonal capability comparisons and suppression comparisons are **not** thereby represented as ordinary all-premise wise worlds. Suppression makes the original fixed forecast of two coactive acts false; a covarying true description is possible in the changed diagnostic context, but does not follow from the unchanged normal forecast.

## 3. Exactly which power has been strengthened?

For each positive target q and each fixed active peer setting b, choose a=2q−b modulo n. Then

F(a,b)=h(2q−b+b)=q.

For an idle peer choose a=q. Both same bearers have this executable repertoire. Consequently each satisfies the displayed contextual ability:

**For every positive target q, for every fixed peer context b, there exists an own available setting a achieving q.**

This is stronger than the inherited UNION model, which could not achieve exact Q against a peer commanding an event outside Q, whatever own command was chosen. All of its positive solo targets remain available here. The changed law also preserves the diagonal coactive/solo equality that an ordinary XOR example does not establish.

It does not supply the stronger order:

**For every q, there exists one own setting a achieving q for every peer b.**

Every active row and column is a permutation of all targets. With at least two targets, none is constant, so no such setting exists. For fixed own setting a and desired q, the exact active-peer success envelope is the singleton {2q−a}; idle is additionally successful exactly when a=q. In particular, a full-target setting q succeeds unchanged against an active peer precisely when that peer also uses q; it also succeeds against idle.

The improvement is dimension-specific, not dominance in every sense of power. UNION's full-catalogue command guaranteed the full catalogue against every peer command. The new Latin law loses that uniform guarantee, while gaining a successful response to every fixed peer context for every positive exact target. Its “stronger” credit concerns that contextual capacity together with the retained actual-concordance/suppression bundle.

### The lost uniform guarantee is a class-wide tradeoff

This price is not merely an accident of the Latin table. Suppose A has a nonvacuously admissible response for **every positive exact target q against every fixed peer setting b** in one comparison domain. Suppose B had a setting b* guaranteeing some exact target r against every compatible A response in that same domain. Choose an eligible exact target q different from r. A's contextual ability supplies a compatible response a at b* with F(a,b*)=q, while B's guarantee requires F(a,b*)=r, a contradiction.

Thus full contextual responsiveness of A rules out every exact-target uniform guarantee by B in that common domain; symmetrically for B and A. At least two distinct exact targets and common quantifier domains are essential. No empty target or blanket Cartesian recombination beyond the given response witness is required. This is an elementary quantifier-duality result, not a new theorem of agency logic. It shows why UNION's robust full-catalogue guarantee cannot survive a repair granting the other bearer full contextual responsiveness over the same nontrivial exact-target field. It does not turn the operational comparison domain into all-premise wisdom-admissible worlds.

### There is no concealed move ordering

The contextual response is a mathematical witness for a fixed known peer context. It is not claimed to be a strategy that observes an unforeseen simultaneous move and then acts after it. In an altered diagnostic context where the peer's intrinsic policy is fixed and known, the response may be computed from that policy without a temporal observation of its actual execution. Whether all those contexts are source-admissible is separate.

The ordinary actual policy is peer-independent q, and the correct forecast of the peer's q follows from the assumed intrinsic policies. Thus ordinary coactive success does not rely on a secretly sequential game or on circularly solving for an unknown actuality by oracle.

### Mutual reactive policies have been solved

If A wants x and B wants y and both use the response equation, their settings must satisfy

a=2x−b and b=2y−a.

Hence a+b=2x=2y. Since2 is invertible, x=y is necessary. If x differs from y there is **no** simultaneous solution. Those conflicting complete intentions are not presented as an actual wise profile.

For common target q there are n solutions: any a paired with2q−a. Only one is diagonal, namely(q,q). Truthful knowledge of an eventual solution does not select that solution. The independent own-full-target policies select(q,q); the normative warrant for those policies remains exposed. In the three-target case the other two mutual-response solutions also execute successfully when **both goals are q**; they are not silently discarded. Their non-q settings, however, fail the same q intention if the peer is suppressed. They are cooperative alternative plans, distinct from the three ordinary full-target-policy worlds. This must not be confused with the diagnostic rows attaching goal b to a peer using setting b, where achieving a different q defeats that attached peer goal.

## 4. What two Boolean outcomes could not test

There is no two-element idempotent Latin table. Idempotence fixes the diagonal as0 and1. Requiring both rows to contain both labels then makes both rows(0,1), so a column repeats and is not bijective. The three-element table above satisfies both demands.

This is a familiar finite-class fact, confirmed independently by enumerating the16 two-input Boolean tables and directly checking the displayed three-target operation. It supplies no new mathematics credit. Its research significance is narrower: the earlier exhaustive Boolean control did not exhaust the class combining **full contextual responsiveness, agreement at every target, and unchanged same-setting solo success**. XOR separates quantifier orders, but its equal-input diagonal is constant and therefore does not itself provide this joined interpretation.

No claim is made that all even orders fail or that three alternatives exhaust metaphysical possibilities.

## 5. The cost: non-invariant solo manifestation

Repairing contextwise power is not free. If fixed peer setting T would produce a created event outside Q when alone, any successful response producing exact Q must leave that event absent jointly. Therefore the peer's solo output cannot be an invariant contribution that simply persists when another independently original activity is added.

### Class-wide retention obstruction

Let O(T) be the complete created set produced by fixed peer exercise T when alone. Let F(S,T) be the complete joint created set for any own exercise S. Assume the retention premise:

For every S, O(T) is a subset of F(S,T).

If some response S attains exact target Q, then F(S,T)=Q; substituting yields O(T) as a subset of Q. Equivalently, if an event belongs to O(T) but not to Q, that event belongs to every F(S,T), so **no choice of S can yield exact Q**. This proves the obstruction for every retention-preserving law, not just UNION and not just the displayed finite quasigroup.

The condition O(T)⊆Q is necessary, not generally sufficient: a law may always produce O(T) and never add the extra events in a strictly larger Q. UNION supplies a special sufficient constructor when the condition holds. The class-wide result transports only the necessary bound.

The finite control ranges over arbitrary two-choice output rows on all three nonempty exact targets, together with each nonempty O(T) and Q. Every row satisfying retention obeys the bound. No empty created target, additive physical quantity, causal-owner label, or previously emitted input is used.

Thus full contextual ability across targets that omit some peer solo event must abandon retention at those contexts. Applying this to original agents still needs independent warrant that a fixed intrinsic exercise fixes an external manifestation that is retained under a peer's activity. Unchanged command syntax and nonreceipt alone do not supply that premise. Ordinary created-agent interactions can fail it, and these off-diagonal comparisons are not worlds where two incompatible perfect intentions are both realized.

Example: B's fixed setting1 produces {e1} alone. A wants {e0}. A's response setting2 with B's unchanged setting1 produces {e0}, so e1 is absent. B's exact intention of {e1}, if attached to that diagnostic exercise, fails. No wise simultaneous opposite-intention profile has been established by this calculation.

This is **counterfactual nonmonotonic manifestation**, not an executed history where e1 was first created and then covertly erased. The model asserts direct production of the resulting event set. If intrinsic settings instead emit already created positive effects before their combination, those emissions and any cancellation would belong to the complete history and the present equality claim would fail. That different interpretation is not hidden inside this model.

This tradeoff gives the source's concern a sharper target. A law can repair output controllability by making each fixed exercise's manifestation context-dependent. The absence of an efficient edge into its standing capacity does not decide whether that is compatible with complete original efficacy.

## 6. Direct source comparison

### Minhaj1:438–441: foreign permission versus one's own alternatives

The retained complete Arabic was already directly read in the first checkpoint. Its final contrast distinguishes one's own choosing between opposed possibilities from needing the other to enable or refrain from preventing one's act. The new model meets a stronger **contextwise source-neutral** reading than UNION: every fixed peer setting admits an own means to every positive Q, so the peer does not make that Q wholly unavailable regardless of adaptation. [Primary electronic passage](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/108/96/)

It does not meet the interpretation requiring one already fixed decisive exercise to remain successful through arbitrary peer variations. Nor does it meet the opening passage's stronger exclusive-origin reading: while B actively supplies an original input, no available A-setting makes the output solely A-provided. The two target types must still be kept separate.

Thus the phrase “requires the peer's noninterference” needs a specified scope. It is false here as a claim that some fixed peer setting leaves no means at all for source-neutral Q. It is true as a claim that a particular already selected setting may be thwarted by changing the peer, and as a claim that sole-A original provenance requires the absence of B's original contribution. Whether the latter two are mandatory perfections of original agency is the substantive source norm, not an algebraic consequence of the first.

## 7. Changed judgement and remaining obligations

The first checkpoint's UNION result remains correct for its bound interpreter. What must not be generalized is its particular lack of contextwise control. A stronger primitive law repairs that defect while retaining the strongest actual concordance and suppression successes. It also strengthens the causal-interface case beyond a support annotation by making every active input extensionally influential.

The principal outstanding obligations are now precise:

1. Is this primitive, context-sensitive direct law compatible with original efficacy, rather than only a formally specified joint function?
2. Is every additional genuine original activity required to yield a distinct positive created manifestation, or is the coalescent realization permissible? Majmu's nonabsorption rationale bears directly here.
3. Does the requisite original sovereignty require uniform fixed-exercise control, contextwise available means, exclusive actual provision, or some independently defended conjunction? The source favors a strong conjunction; the bridge must be appraised at that strength.
4. Are the shared fitting target, each own-full-target policy and the primitive unreceived cognition metaphysically warranted for the same necessary originals? The operational construction does not answer this.
5. Does the declared event ontology include all actual created requisites? It may not erase genuine prior emissions or mechanisms; none are posited in the direct interpretation.

The stronger rival therefore receives **joined reduced-signature consistency and premise-discrimination credit**. It receives no certification as a full source-faithful metaphysical possibility and no refutation credit against the argument with nonabsorption and exclusive original completion already independently warranted. Those are the live philosophical questions; the repaired conditional power makes them harder to bypass with a weaker-capacity objection.
