# Metaphysical interpretation of the recent explanatory results

9 October 2026. Focused additive audit. Historical reports and proofs are unchanged. This report distinguishes source-faithful interpretation, valid conditional inference, and warranted application to extramental reality. It does not integrate or close T20.

## Finding

The requested semantic distinctions do not overturn the recent conditional proofs. Much of the recent work already protects them explicitly. In particular, the original resource–bearer interface does not make a bearer the productive cause of its intrinsic attributes; the qualitative-grounding reports do not identify different explanatory descriptions with separately subsisting things; and the modern PSR reports distinguish formal controls from metaphysically possible worlds.

Two more precise findings remain consequential.

1. **The modal formulation benefits from clarification; a material evidential error has not been established.** Calling the two-spells case an apparently coherent modal candidate avoids presupposing the possibility being assessed. Schaffer's modal intuitions and causal arguments can supply defeasible reasons, without independently establishing the candidate's extramental admissibility. The historical report already calls its evidence prima facie and denies that it authenticates two original resource bases; the root reassessment preserves that boundary. This audit has supplied no defeater requiring withdrawal of all that evidential weight.
2. **The unrestricted explanatory repair imposes a fact-mereological restriction whose relevance to intrinsic attributes requires an additional mapping.** Its already proved `part_of_total_explainer_is_itself` makes every fact-part of the total explainer identical to that explainer. Consequently, a proposed mapping that treats distinct intrinsic-attribute facts as its proper fact-parts is incompatible with that axiom package. Real attribute distinction does not itself provide that mapping. The consequence restricts applications of the modern proof; it does not establish that divine attributes are fictitious, separate producers, or removable pieces.

The right assessment is therefore discriminating. Preserve the inferential and source-relative gains; identify the application premises and sharpen language without manufacturing a substantive error from the absence of a conclusive demonstration. Neither a wholesale rejection of formal analysis nor a forced change or preservation of the common-source verdict follows.

## 1. What the supplied source distinctions require

The user's supplied attachment identifies the essential distinctions; authentication of the complete requested loci belongs to the accompanying source return. After the initial audit, focused primary controls were read: the complete retained Dar item178, electronically marked 1:293–295; the reciprocal-conditions passage at Dar 1:280–282; and Akmaliyya working lines L155–175, electronically marked 6:107–113. This is a new targeted semantic application of retained or newly acquired witnesses, not a new whole-work reading or an independent print-edition collation. The prior `source-noncirculation-feedback` report remains inherited evidence.

Four separations are required.

- A concept can distinguish an essence, an attribute, a mode of consideration, or an explanation without thereby exhibiting a separately subsisting external item. Conversely, an attribute can be real and genuinely distinct without existing apart from its bearer. Rejecting a bare attributeless substrate does not make knowledge and power identical concepts or unreal properties.
- Necessary concomitance does not by itself supply an efficient-causal direction. The fact that a living, knowing bearer cannot exist without its intrinsic perfections does not establish that those perfections first produce the bearer, that the bearer manufactures them, or that either borrows enabling actuality from an external peer.
- Truth or existence throughout a chosen class of worlds differs from necessity through oneself. The latter cannot be obtained just by declaring a modal domain, keeping an item in all its members, or making several predicates necessarily coextensive.
- A formally interpretable or imaginatively vivid description differs from a warranted metaphysical possibility. Formal nonentailment still exposes what an axiom set fails to establish. It does not, without further interpretation, establish what can exist outside thought.

The last distinction does not impose the further rule that a modal seeming has no defeasible evidential force until an independent demonstration establishes its object. Such a rule would itself need defence. The source warning blocks automatic promotion from conceiving to possibility; it does not by itself adjudicate every developed modal argument or analogy.

These constraints cut both ways. Calling two independently subsisting proposed agents attributes of one bearer does not make them intrinsic attributes. Calling a real intrinsic perfection another item does not make it a foreign donor. Neither plurality nor unity is settled by the analyst's noun count.

The primary controls expressly support this middle position. Dar 1:294 criticizes both making knowledge, power, and will identical and turning the qualifications of one human into several independently subsisting substances. Its affirmative contrast is one substance with multiple attributes ([Dar item178](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/413/178/), local L006). Akmaliyya L162 likewise preserves genuine attribute distinctness; L163–166 distinguishes independence from what is separate from the bearer from an attributeless existence, and L166–172 criticizes importing a newly stipulated sense of necessity or unity into what an argument establishes. These passages block identifying essence/existence with an undifferentiated absence of real attributes.

They also prevent a mistaken reply on behalf of necessarily harmonious peers. Intrinsic concomitant attributes qualify the same bearer; two genuinely independently subsisting original agents would not become one bearer merely by agreeing necessarily. Yet their numerical distinction alone would not show that either donates the other's power. The source's original-efficacy argument must establish that further productive conclusion separately.

Dar 1:281–282 sharpens this point. Its wording permits reciprocal conditions in general while using intrinsic attributes as the immediate example. It then expressly excludes a necessary-through-itself being's dependence on what is outside itself. Consequently, the conditional sentence that A succeeds only if B does not interfere is ambiguous: it can report mere co-obtaining, or an external enabling/completing condition. The sentence alone does not establish which. An actual productive or enabling bridge is needed; the intrinsic-attribute example cannot by itself validate two original agents. See `essence-attribute-source-return/sources/dar1-p281.txt`, L003–007, especially L005–006.

## 2. The distinctions the recent work already preserves

### Qualitative grounding

The 2011 appraisal expressly treats grounding as a sentential explanatory apparatus rather than a commitment to independently existing fact-objects (`modal-grounding-source/REPORT.md`, lines 29–35). Its three-stage distinction between algebraic obtaining, individualistic facts, and ordinary existential truths is explicitly a distinction of interpreted assertions and explanatory roles, not three new ontological objects (lines 85–97). The mapping audit similarly disclaims fact-object reification (`MAPPING_TEST.md`, lines 15–29).

It follows that keeping AG and PL assertions distinct for grounding is not already the criticized reification. Different senses or explanatory roles can matter even when no additional substance is introduced. The genuine disputed step is from a successful qualitative representation and its coordinated use to the claimed direction and sufficiency of metaphysical explanation. The 2014 appraisal already isolates exactly that step as abductive rather than a semantic deduction (`collective-explanation-adequacy/REPORT.md`, sections 3.2 and 4).

There is a further positive qualification. Dasgupta's 2009 proposal really does posit universals and states of affairs. It is not adequately answered by saying that its terms are merely notational. But its ontological proposal is also not proved by its translation theorem. The recent 2009 appraisal keeps those claims apart and rejects stipulating that an obtaining state is its own productive resource and bearer (`QUALITATIVE_ONTOLOGY_WARRANT.md`, lines 118–134). The requested source distinction therefore identifies an existing dispute about ontological warrant, not a newly discovered proof error in the formal translation.

### Intrinsic resources and the original bearer

The unchanged `OriginalBearerBridge.lean` states at lines 8–11 that resources are concrete intrinsic instantiations in the same existential respect and that there is no bearer-to-attribute causal edge. Its mathematical structure declares `B` and `R` separately for typing. That does not assert that every resource subsists independently of its bearer.

The consequential premises are precise:

- `Completion` already supplies an actual relevant resource that is not received (lines 35–36).
- `Realisation` supplies an actual bearer in which that resource is intrinsic (lines 38–39).
- `SupportTransport` says that whole-existence reception of the bearer would entail reception of its intrinsic resource in the declared respect (lines 41–43).

The theorem then uses contraposition: the resource is not received; if its bearer were wholly received, the resource would be received; therefore this bearer is not wholly received (lines 76–92). It does not infer that the resource causes the bearer or that the bearer causes the resource. A real inseparable attribute can satisfy this interface without becoming a second agent.

The source-faithful vulnerability lies in application. If `wholeReceived` were reinterpreted as any necessary co-instantiation, the premise would change. If `resourceReceived` were reinterpreted as inherence in a bearer, an intrinsic original resource could be classified as received merely because it is an attribute. Those translations would be defective. Neither is present in the inspected definition. Nor can the anti-bootstrap source argument supply `Completion` merely by ruling out a finite self-cause: the report already distinguishes this positive whole-account premise from noncircularity.

### Necessary existence and self-explanation

The interface separately introduces modal necessity, contingency-to-reception, and constitutive reception. `ContingencyNeed` and `ConstitutiveReception` are substantive assumptions (lines 50–57). `EssentialNonreceipt` is separately defined (lines 59–60), and its derivation actually uses constitutive reception rather than modal necessity (lines 116–122). Thus the formal proof does not silently deduce necessity through oneself from existence at every modeled world.

The domain matters as much as the formula. B is interpreted as bearers, not automatically every entity, act-token, attribute, or proposition. Broadening it to include an uncreated contingent intrinsic act-token would create an additional application obligation for `ContingencyNeed`: why should that item's actual nonnecessity entail whole-existence reception in the declared sense? Neither being other than the bearer nor being successively actual establishes createdness or receipt. Conversely, uncreatedness alone does not establish whole-existence nonreceipt. An act or capacity admitted as an R-resource still needs the stipulated intrinsic and same-existential-respect interpretation. These are protections of the current scope, not a demonstrated defect in the unchanged code.

The modal-grounding report similarly limits its conclusion to Q-worlds and expressly denies that restricted necessity establishes intrinsic existence (`REPORT.md`, lines 117–124). The Gale–Pruss audit distinguishes necessity of an existential proposition, a particular's necessary existence, successful production, and a necessary reason-state (`modal-psr-bridge/RESULT.md`, lines 65–85). Its report also identifies a literal scope slide in the 1999 source rather than copying it as a valid inference.

Finally, the plural theorem's conclusion `E(Q,{Q})` is self-explanation under the declared general relation. Its report explicitly refuses to identify this with absence of other explainers, necessary existence, or maximum independence (`plural-explanation-theorem-audit/RESULT.md`, lines 50–58). Applying efficient anti-self-production to every such E-edge would change the relation. Calling an E-edge self-explanation does not, in the other direction, supply existence through oneself.

## 3. The new restriction exposed by the unrestricted repair

The current particular-explanans report proves a restricted repair and, separately, a stronger route retaining an unrestricted total-explainer premise. The second route has a revealing consequence already visible in its checked code:

`BridgeRepairs.lean`, lines 55–74, proves that every part p of a total all-facts explainer q equals q.

The derivation is short and worth interpreting, rather than rerunning under a new name. Suppose p is a proper fact-part of q. Because q explains the total plurality F, the source-side definition makes p a partial explainer of F. Because q is actual, q belongs to F. Since p is actual too, output projection makes q explain p. Reflexive parthood then makes q a partial explainer of p. Antisymmetry says q is not a part of the proper part p. No Extended Circles excludes exactly this pair of partial-explanation claims. Hence p cannot be proper.

This consequence uses:

1. an explainer of the plurality of all actual facts;
2. partial explanation inherited by every fact-part of a full explainer;
3. member projection from total explanation;
4. No Extended Circles in its displayed asymmetric-parthood form;
5. reflexive, antisymmetric fact-parthood.

It does not need complete transitivity. It is not a consequence of the restricted basic-natural PSR alone. Nor is it the minimal shortcut deriving `E(Q,{Q})`, whose hypothesis footprint is weaker.

### Why this matters to the supplied attributes passage

Consider the following proposed interpretation, which is an additional proposal rather than a premise already supplied by the source:

- q is a complete divine explanatory fact;
- p is a distinct real fact concerning an intrinsic divine perfection;
- p is a proper fact-part of q;
- the same general E, projection, and circle principles apply.

That interpretation cannot satisfy the unrestricted package just described. The failure does not depend on p being separately subsistent or an external producer. It is induced by the explanatory/mereological axioms once p is mapped to proper fact-parthood.

There are several legitimate responses, none automatic. One may reject that fact-parthood mapping; distinguish intrinsic qualification from the facts' parthood; restrict which parts count as explanatory contributors; distinguish productive explanation from other explanatory relations; or decline the unrestricted totality/projection package. Each changes a named application premise. What one cannot do is invoke the proof as a neutral demonstration that real attributes must be denied or identified with their bearer.

Conversely, the supplied doctrine does not prove this modern package false merely by affirming real attributes. It has not established that the corresponding attribute-facts are proper parts of q under the particular fact mereology. Bearer qualification, real attribute distinction, conceptual separation, and fact-parthood are four different relations. An attribute may be real without being a detachable piece, while a fact about it may be distinct without being a proper part of a chosen total explainer.

The new gain is an exact incompatibility of proposed interpretations. The historical theorem and its proof remain valid.

## 4. The mixed wrapper remains a formal independence control

`RestrictedBridgeControl.lean`, lines 25–40, defines fact-parthood and directly stipulates its explanatory relation. It does not infer the relation from the mereological arrangement. The particular-explanans report calls the construction an explicit-axiom independence result and acknowledges that `E(c,{b})` may fail every adequate metaphysical reading (`RESULT.md`, lines 71–79).

The real finding survives: the displayed externality rule excludes c being a part of b, while allowing b to be a proper part of c. The displayed relational axioms do not themselves rule that out. Calling the labels obtaining facts does not give them a genuine productive interpretation. But denying that interpretation does not turn the reverse-parthood exclusion into a formal consequence of the weaker axioms.

The inherited source feedback already gives the right positive restriction. If b's same actuality is a productive requisite of a purported account that supplies b, putting b into a larger wrapper does not cure the bootstrap. If b is merely mentioned or conjoined, its parthood alone does not establish productive use. If the explanation is nonproductive, efficient-causal irreflexivity cannot be inserted without argument (`source-noncirculation-feedback/RESULT.md`, lines 75–113).

Real essential concomitance is therefore not newly rescued from the current report; it was expressly preserved there. The additional care now required is to keep that preservation in place when transporting the general No Extended Circles and partial-explanation-by-parthood package.

## 5. Calibrating the modal claim about redundant production

### What the historical reports actually claimed

The overdetermination report preserves a crucial distinction between an intact sufficient route, whole effect-relative original provision, and sole production. It says that neither Schaffer nor Hall establishes two entire unreceived original resource bases. It also refuses to count intrinsic uncreated acts or real causal relations automatically as additional created effects (`complete-overdetermination-audit/REPORT.md`, lines 23–35, 85–101).

The later root reassessment is likewise explicit: its changed appraisal is not a finding that two perfected originals are metaphysically possible (`COMMON_SOURCE_REASSESSMENT_AFTER_REDUNDANCY_20261009.md`, lines 7–9). There is no basis for alleging that it directly inferred possible plural gods from a Lean model or a fairy tale.

The sentence at report line 97 can nevertheless be expressed more carefully: it calls the spell example a coherent, intelligible causal possibility while appraising its prima facie modal force. Calling it an apparently coherent modal candidate avoids presupposing the disputed status. This is a wording and premise-visibility improvement. It does not establish that lines 97–101 or the root reassessment's lines 17–27 promoted the case to authenticated possibility: those passages explicitly preserve defeasibility and the stronger original-resource gap. ### What Schaffer's argument supports

A targeted return to the retained published text distinguishes three source moves.

- On printed p. 27, Schaffer rejects restricting analysis to actual physical laws and appeals to causal intuitions about fairy-tale spells. This is an explicit modal argument, not merely an unadorned stipulation. Its evidential force is contestable; its failure to demonstrate extramental possibility independently does not establish that it supplies no prima facie reason.
- The ordinary rock discussion preserves substantive reasons for causal attribution under redundancy. It also admits component production in the actual fine-grained history. That supports causation without indispensability, but not duplicate entire original provision of one fixed concrete effect.
- The two-level case is expressly conditional. Note 2, p. 41, leaves substantial independence and power assumptions unestablished in this paper. The later process argument, pp. 33–37, supports causal attribution given complete relevant processes; it does not by itself establish that a proposed nonphysical process can exist.

Source: Jonathan Schaffer, [Overdetermining Causes](https://www.jonathanschaffer.org/overdetermination.pdf), particularly printed 27, 29–38, and note 2 at 41. The present operation is a targeted rereading of the retained witness, not a new whole-paper reading or a new scientific validation.

### The exact distinction

There are two independent questions:

1. **Causal attribution within an admitted case:** if two relevant complete processes are actual in the case, does redundancy deprive one or both of causal status?
2. **Admission of the case:** what warrants that the described processes and their relations can exist, at the exact target and explanatory scope being disputed?

Schaffer's broader arguments give real support to an answer to the first question. Their application to the second remains defeasible and theory-dependent. Both can still matter to a modal appraisal. The conceivability of spell-casting does not automatically inherit all the warrant of ordinary causal-role and preemption arguments; conversely, exposing that distinction is not a defeater of every analogy between the cases. In particular, neither mode of argument establishes an instance of two unreceived original bases merely by describing each route as complete. The historical report already denies that stronger result.

The more carefully stated assessment is:

> The source supplies a developed challenge to an inference from actual causal contribution to indispensability or exclusivity. Its apparently coherent nonphysical candidate and causal-process arguments may provide prima facie modal evidence, conditional on the reliability and applicability of those modal judgments. They do not authenticate the candidate's possibility, and they supply no established duplicate whole-original instance.

This is not the demand that all possible causes be physical, nor the claim that modal imagination has no evidential role. It locates which evidence is conditional on an admitted ontology, which is analogical, and which claim still needs defence. A primitive affirmative account and a primitive rival should face the same interpretive standard. A stronger downward revision would require an argument that the relevant seeming or analogy is unreliable, defeated, or inapplicable at its stated evidential scope. This audit has not supplied that argument merely by citing the general mental/extramental distinction.

### Consequence for the root judgement

The recent withholding of a universal-exclusion verdict can remain rationally available because the argument's scope and its disputed nonduplication premise were independently identified. Failure to establish an inference can matter even without a certified metaphysical counterexample. That is an undercutting result, not automatically positive evidence for the contrary world.

The stated basis should remain transparent: a source-developed causal argument, a defeasible modal analogy, and an independently identified gap in the stronger original-production inference have different roles. They must not be redescribed as a verified extramental each-complete rival. But the historical appraisal already denies possession of that rival, so removing the stronger claim would not by itself remove its stated reasons. The prior draft's conclusion that an evidential overpromotion had been established was too strong. No forced downward or upward confidence revision, required preservation of the existing stance, or newly authenticated rival follows from this audit. Further changes require appraisal of the actual nonduplication and modal-warrant arguments.

## 6. The remaining formal controls retain their proper status

### Modal PSR actualisation

The H_P argument begins with an actual point and a domain of admissible possibilities, a fixed target, factivity, and negative-explanation completeness. Within that interpretation it derives actual explanation correctly. It does not create the domain's metaphysical admissibility (`ACTUALISATION_AUDIT.md`, lines 7–24). Its three-world failure control explicitly disclaims a metaphysically possible cosmos (lines 36–42).

Thus the needed revision is not to reject the proof because it uses worlds. It is to refuse entry into its domain merely on the ground that a description has been written consistently. Nor can necessity throughout that domain establish intrinsic existential independence. Both limits are already stated in the recent reports.

### Epistemic PSR

The local lamp construction grants causal interpretation and a chosen neighborhood, while expressly withholding their metaphysical authentication (`PREMISE_INDEPENDENCE.md`, lines 13–29). It demonstrates nonentailment from the selected local conditions to global PSR; it does not demonstrate that a brute background world is really possible or that the subject possesses reflective knowledge.

This is a useful distinction against both misreadings. The construction does not refute the authors' full metaphysical package, and the need for extra symmetry/proximity premises is not erased merely because the construction lacks a fully warranted ontology. The strengthened authors' causal and epistemic reasons must be judged directly rather than replaced by a count of modeled worlds.

### Plural PSR

The chain and regular-open controls retain their mathematical nonentailment roles. Their labels do not establish extramental facts, and their arbitrary explanatory edges do not establish explanatory adequacy. The result report explicitly marks those limitations. The positive total-explainer/self-explanation shortcut also remains valid at its declared general E relation. Neither outcome has thereby become a theorem about an unreceived agent or its intrinsic attributes.

1. The unrestricted total-explainer package cannot accommodate a proper fact-part of that explainer. Any proposed intrinsic-attribute interpretation must address this explicitly rather than treating attribute distinction as either fictitious or productive dependence.
2. The spell case should be described as an apparently coherent modal candidate. Its source-developed modal and causal reasoning may have prima facie force without authenticating extramental possibility. The historical reports already preserve the stronger whole-original limit; this audit establishes no requirement to withdraw all their defeasible evidential weight.

No fresh kernel run, new theorem, independent manuscript witness, overall exhaustion, integration, owner acceptance, or closure is claimed. The open work is now a substantive appraisal of the identified interpretations and source arguments, not additional formal controls with renamed predicates.
