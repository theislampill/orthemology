# Bounded scan controls: range 4

Every page listed below was rendered and displayed as a complete image, including the printed page number and all visible footnotes. I read the body and visible apparatus, not merely the target search phrase. The PDF has no usable extracted text layer for these pages; the control was visual. No external fetch, forbidden object retry, or manuscript consultation occurred.

## Main interpretative controls

### Concrete bearers and universals: print 423–426, PDF 430–433

The continuous four-page context corresponds to S003-L0130–L0145, with partial boundary lines. It confirms the distinction between the mental inclusion of genus/differentia and a concrete subject's attributes. Page 424 includes the end of L0135 and all of L0136; its decisive ending says that commonality is in the universal, universal only mentally, and externally instantiated only as a particular. Page 425 then supplies the four modes of existence/expression and the insistence that a person's attributes are not antecedent components.

The principal conceptual statements agree with the electronic reading. Page 423's notes distinguish some editorial grammatical repairs from the manuscript reading; page 424 and 425 each note a grammatical form of “growing”. These do not alter the bearer/universal interpretation. They do show why matching argumentative content is not identical to diplomatic word-for-word equivalence.

Page 426 confirms the received problematic “لا يتصوره في الذهن” in the triangle passage (L0142); no apparatus note on that phrase supplies a repair. The awkward negation is therefore not resolved by this edition. The argument's explicit mental/external distinction immediately following remains clear, but an exact proof should not quietly insert or delete a negation. Page 426 also confirms the fitra/revelation statement and begins the Qurʾānic creation-language argument, with modern notes on other minor readings.

### Intrinsic and continuing poverty: print 450–454, PDF 457–461

The five pages were read in full, including the dense apparatus comparing the Ibn Sīnā quotation with al-Najāt. Page 450 contains the quotation's ending and the author's “قلت”, then the intrinsic-dependence thesis. Page 451 continues it: no independent essence outside divine production, and no production of God's own necessity by God as if there were a separate producer/product pair. Page 452 develops the lexical distinction between finding, existence, and the existing reality, then explicitly states the creature's dependence before existence and throughout persistence. Page 453 distinguishes mentally conceived meanings from external realities and returns to the argument about preserving causes. Page 454 states the internal objection concerning a new preserving relation.

The universal poverty claim and the denial of a separate prior creaturely essence are confirmed. The principal arguments agree with S003-L0267–L0283. An important small variant occurs in L0276: page 452's main text reads “إلا بالحق” at the first occurrence where the electronic text has “إلا بالخالق”. Its modern footnote suggests “بالخالق” as a probable reading by reference to the end of the paragraph. The final dependence clause on that page does read “إلا بالخالق”. This is an editorial proposal, not manuscript verification by this reader. It does not turn the statement into creaturely self-sufficiency, but exact quotations must distinguish the two witnesses.

Several quotation notes on pages 450 and 454 explicitly use al-Najāt to repair or supplement wording. For example, page 450 retains a parenthesised “لا” before the clause about causes and notes that deletion is probably correct; the electronic text lacks that “لا”. This reinforces the need to distinguish the quoted author's argument, Ibn Taymiyya's transmitted quotation, and modern editorial reconstruction. No claim has been made that these pages establish an exact text of Ibn Sīnā independently of the edition's editorial decisions.

### Desired end versus efficient production: print 463–464, PDF 470–471

Page 463 begins within the philosophical quotation about imitation of the first good, closes the quotation, and visibly marks the author's “قلت”. Its apparatus records supplied words and differences against al-Najāt. Page 464 gives the complete paragraph corresponding to L0321: the desired object does not efficiently originate the power that desires or desire itself; the role is final rather than efficient, and the represented end can precede external realisation. It then moves to Aristotle's account and the dilemma about whether the sphere is dependent or self-necessary.

The main final/efficient distinction agrees with the electronic reading. The apparatus at the opening of page 464 reports a corrupt manuscript phrase involving desire and proposes the printed reading; this is an editorial intervention, not a new argument by the author. The context confirms that the author allows the object's existence to occasion willing while denying that such final attraction establishes the efficient maker of the faculty and act. This is limiting evidence against flattening all explanatory relationships into one source mode.

### Actual connected creation and permitted dependent agency: print 436–438, PDF 443–445

Page 436 gives the original-power circularity argument and two-independent-producer objection, then the conflicting-wills horn. Page 437 contains the interconnected-world argument (L0205) and begins the explicit permission of concurrent dependence among creatures deriving capacity from one cause (L0207). Page 438 completes that limiting case and distinguishes it from two alleged original creators, then resumes the eternal-possibility argument.

The central common-Creator paragraph and its creaturely-agency qualification agree with the received sequence. Page 437's modern footnote reports a disorder in the manuscript's page arrangement and identifies where the following material belongs. This is especially important: the coherent printed/electronic sequence should not be called a directly verified manuscript sequence. Page 438 notes a grammatical change around independent capacity. Neither note warrants ignoring the explicit admission that two created agents empowered by a third can have mutually conditional capacities.

### Single created unity and celestial plurality: print 473–474, PDF 480–481

Page 473 starts within L0359 and continues through most of L0364; page 474 completes L0364–L0367 and continues beyond the assigned electronic endpoint. Both complete pages, including notes, were read. The single human/horse/tree argument is confirmed, alongside the examples of builders, tailors, weavers, writers, farmers, and carriers whose acts are distinguishable. The common-Creator reading therefore remains supported, but ordinary collaboration has not been denied.

A consequential local correction is confirmed at L0359: the scan prints “اشتركا”, while the electronic witness reads “اشترطا”. The apparatus says that the latter is in the earlier printed edition and that the chosen reading follows the manuscript. We have verified the modern edition's report, not directly inspected that manuscript.

The extension on page 474 develops the mixing/transformation of bodies and celestial-motion proposal: the act that moves and mixes bodies cannot be separated from the resulting mixture in the manner required by the rival active-intellect scheme. It is further evidence of concern with inseparable action/results, but its electronic continuation belongs to the adjacent assignment. No completed conclusion for that new subsection is supplied here beyond the visible page.

## Other consequential controls and unresolved wording

### Print 469–471, PDF 476–478: verified substantive differences

The first major difference is at electronic S003-L0344. Page 469 reads a clause introducing what leading philosophers admitted with “من”, “أن”, and “تقوم به” in the appropriate places; its apparatus contrasts earlier printing and manuscript readings. The electronic text instead has the compressed wording “ما سلمه الأساطين الأول بحدوث الصفات والأفعال”. The printed context clearly concerns the admission that attributes and acts subsist in the first principle. Do not quote the electronic compression as an unambiguous diplomatic sentence.

The second, stronger difference is S003-L0346:

- Received electronic wording: “ووجود الفلك مع جميع حوادثه فممتنع”.
- Observed page 469 wording: “ووجود الفلك بدون حوادثه ممتنع”.

The modern apparatus explicitly contrasts the older “مع جميع” reading with its chosen manuscript-based wording. The distinction is consequential: the scan denies the sphere's existence without its occurrences, which fits the paragraph's argument from inseparability, rather than simply denying its existence with all of them. The received line remains unchanged in the electronic source; use the scan-controlled wording with an explicit witness label if relying on this step.

Page 470 confirms the epistemic-route limit in L0350: a route from existence establishes a necessary existent without thereby identifying it with the first mover or distinguishing it from the sphere. The page visibly prints “المغيرة” in the earlier sentence where the electronic text has corrupt inserted characters before “المتغيرة”; the apparatus reports “المتغيرة” in an earlier printing. The conceptually important route distinction does not depend on resolving that adjective. The difficult wording at L0353 about what correct proofs do not prevent is retained in this edition; no speculative correction has been adopted. Page 471 completes that sentence and presents the comparisons with Qadarite, naturalistic, and Magian accounts. Its visible notes include grammatical/word-form repairs, not an independent historical authentication.

### Print 403 and 421–422, PDF 410 and 428–429: anomalies not automatically cured

Page 403 verifies the act/product timing qualification: immediate following can be called concomitance, strict simultaneity cannot; hand/ring correlation is not complete productive causation. It also retains the awkward “ليس كله” wording of L0014 without a correcting note. That candidate remains unresolved rather than silently emended.

Pages 421–422 control L0121–L0129, including the non-being argument. The sentence at the page join retains “لأن نفس عدم السبب...” while the surrounding text denies efficient production of pure non-being. It is therefore not established that this is merely an electronic missing-negation error. The distinction “not every concomitant is an agent” and the surrounding denial are clear; the exact sentence needs further textual/philosophical attention. Page 421's apparatus also reports manuscript-page order disturbance. Page 422 supplies modern historical notes on Themistius and Alexander, which were read but not independently verified.

### Print 405 and 432, PDF 412 and 439: contextual controls

Page 405 confirms the survey distinguishing act from product and the three reported rival accounts; it has no footnote requiring a substantive revision of the reading. Page 432 confirms the transition through early anti-Jahmī reports; the narrator of the Muqātil anecdote is printed “مكي بن إبراهيم” where S003-L0177 reads “علي بن إبراهيم”. This difference affects transmission detail and reinforces the refusal to claim chain authentication. The page's one footnote reports a different small manuscript reading. The severe judgements remain quoted reports, not this reader's endorsements.

## Result and stopping point

The requested conceptual controls are complete at the initial pages listed above; the separately requested catalogue mapping then added the finite controls described below. They support the range's main distinctions and identify consequential witness differences without changing the received text. None of the unresolved anomalies was silently fixed just because its surrounding argument suggested an easy repair. The combined operation stops at 31 authorial body pages and four modern catalogue pages, all individually identified in the receipt. This is not a full edition collation, a manuscript reading, or a new formal result. Remaining candidates in the initial proposed control list are unverified unless explicitly linked to these controls.
