## 2. The research object

Orthemology investigates how concrete things or occurrences are recognised, distinguished, evaluated, handled and revised under governing standards, and what makes that handling actually correct and adequately warranted. Its mathematical research also investigates the representations, information, transformations, evidence costs and continuation conditions involved in inquiry itself. Its philosophical research asks about objective determination, intelligibility, warrant, normativity and ultimate existential grounding.

It is not exhausted by any one of these descriptions:

- a proof of God's existence;
- a database of arguments or an assurance-case vocabulary;
- ordinary formal verification with new labels;
- tensor calculus in which truth follows from numerical optimisation;
- a single completed theory of all cognition.

A good brief formulation is: **a developing mathematical–philosophical theory of warranted resolution and its conditions, with concrete audited episodes, independently scrutinised source arguments, conditional mathematics and a cumulative research ecology**. Its mathematics deserves evaluation on its actual statements and proofs, not dismissal because the programme is independently developed. Novelty, correctness, significance, empirical application and disciplinary recognition remain different questions.

### Basic vocabulary

An **orthemma** is a concrete situated case or occurrence. An **ortheme** is a repeatable type or distinction it may instantiate relative to a declared analysis. A **metaortheme** is a governing standard; a **metaorthemma** is its particular binding to an actual case. **Orthing** is the governed activity of apprehension, evaluation, handling and correction; an **orthing episode** is one execution. The terms remain programme vocabulary rather than proof of independently established natural kinds.

**Orthability** must not be collapsed into one sense. Local orthability concerns determination under a practice or declared analysis. Objective orthability concerns real determination and fittingness not manufactured by the practice. Representational orthability concerns the actual concepts, records and instruments through which an inquiry proceeds. A theorem about a declared record language does not automatically explain objective orthability.

At every layer preserve:

```text
actual case / reality
≠ observed evidence
≠ encoded representation
≠ inferred judgement
≠ governing standard
≠ adequacy of the route
≠ recorded verdict or closure
```

A correct result can arise by a defective route. An adequate route can confront misleading evidence. A record may be current for one candidate and stale for another. A carried HOLD can be honest terminal accounting without resolving the burden. These are not marginal caveats; they are part of the subject matter.

## 3. Method and cumulative ecology

The owner repeatedly pressed a methodological inversion from system-first speculative construction towards recognition/source-guided, **joint-first** investigation. Recognition, observation, fiṭrī cognition or a source argument supplies a candidate or a reason to investigate; it does not become unquestionable merely by supplying that starting point.

A typical sequence is:

```text
recognition/source/case → precise claim and bearer → explicit premises and relations
→ mathematical/formal joint → proof, countermodel or discriminating observation
→ retained result, failed mapping and residual → next justified investigation
```

The research state may be represented schematically by `R_n=(C_n,P_n,K_n,O_n,Φ_n)`: claims, checked proofs, countermodels, obligations and tested maps. This is a useful synthesis, not a single implemented universal research engine. Results can generate new obligations. An old theorem may acquire new significance through a later faithful mapping. A corrected source or meaning can restrict a former map and reopen a closure. Cumulativity is therefore disciplined, version-relative preservation, not immunity from revision or an assertion that all ledger entries only accumulate as truths.

Machine-enforced persistence can prevent silent resurrection of an eliminated alternative *within an implemented and sufficiently specified contract*. Merely saying that all future arguments must respect old results does not establish such enforcement everywhere. A checked implication does not make its premises true; a countermodel of an encoding does not automatically establish a real metaphysical possibility.

Finite decidable search can exhaust its specified class. Fair effective proof search can eventually find an existing finite proof in its stated calculus. Neither means every philosophical question terminates, computation is cheap, or an ultimate great discovery is guaranteed. The programme can produce important within-domain discriminations before any theorem crosses disciplinary boundaries. External reproduction is valuable evidence, not the source of a theorem's logical force.
