# Integrated active-rival test and the remaining collective explanation burden

Research result, 3 October 2026 UTC. Read with PREMISE_UNION_AND_TARGET_LEDGER.md. No canonical status changes. No metaphysically certified common rival, integrated impossibility theorem, or R5 defeat is claimed.

## Result first

No construction inspected or supplied here yet meets the full MEN-8 charter. The best existing personal-source challenge already preserves considerably more than the older structural models: one genuinely intelligent original giver, actual reason-responsive correction, intentional production of derivative virtue, and a contentful motive for one contrary assurance. Its unresolved point is joint metaphysical/explanatory adequacy, not a missing Boolean valuation.

The nexus lane's proposal creates a useful discriminating test at its EX clause, cross-referenced in §4 below. The active infinitist asks whether its complete productive configuration qualifies there. That request is not automatically legitimate: its account must contribute real productive content, and must defend why its global exception is the same explanatory kind. But it cannot be rejected merely by calling every received member passive or by observing that some local explanations fail to explain a conjunction. The remaining affirmative premise is a substantive collective original-possession claim.

This is a narrowed joint warrant test. It establishes neither side's ultimate metaphysics. It is not a restatement of a bearer-absence or role-replacement model.

## 1. Why the historical populated common model is insufficient

The inspected IR5CM-1 YAML numerically places modal order, a truth register, selector, norm, efficacy and expression on one bearer. It explicitly sets target authority primitively and excludes proper function/teleology and metaphysical possibility from its claims. Accordingly:

- numerical co-location is not explanatory integration;
- giving the norm an external valuation does not explain its authority;
- a selector is not thereby an act performed for an understood reason;
- marking a bearer present at two declared worlds is not warrant for absolute necessity;
- stipulating genuine recipient knowledge as a new predicate does not repair all those deficiencies.

These are the model's own limits, not new defeats of it. The stronger test must preserve actual intentionality and account-sensitive function-fixing rather than toggle those predicates to false.

## 2. The strongest already-developed common personal architecture

Retain the inherited three contemporary episodes, with one source M throughout.

A: a recipient inspects the same displayed source, understands its evidence, and corrects the false absence claim for that reason. M knowingly provides the recipient and an evidence-sensitive route because genuine understanding is a fitting end.

B: another recipient refuses a false integrity assurance because it understands the decisive reason not to induce harmful reliance. M genuinely values and productively supplies that fitting exercise.

C: M itself knowingly gives one false assurance under the exact unsafe-root, version, interval and irreversible-action contract. M's exceptional final aim is accomplished derivative agency being betrayed through entrusted assurance. It acts for that end while recognizing its normative defect. Ordinary genuine concern is retained; it is not merely bait, and would remain operative without C.

The reason-sensitive agency here has positive content: apprehended object, recognized reason, purposive selection, known means and effective institution. Function can be fixed by the actual truth-directed design purpose of the faculty in A/B, rather than by an assigned success score. A false later use does not retroactively erase that genuine function. The actual page and content fix correctness; M's endorsement does not make the false report true.

To stress the strongest source grant, let M account for every actual productive contributor and mode in the declared whole-origin target, including its proper unborrowed exercises. Do not shrink that target to endpoint replication. This fixes common productive sourcehood and preserves its conditional uniqueness theorem. It also permits real active derivative subjects; receiving a power is not being a passive instrument in every explanatory respect.

Necessary existence can be conceded to M for the integrity test, as in the predecessor. Alternatively necessity can be left unproved when the question is modal warrant. Those choices must not be confused: a described absent-M world would add a separate, unwarranted metaphysical claim. Nothing in the proposed new test needs one.

### Exactly what this architecture does and does not supply

It supplies, under the shared intentional-production grants, one explanatory arrangement for original good agency, derivative knowledge, local objective correctness, function and a contrary assurance. It is stronger than separate lane countermodels. It is also already substantially present in Seventh, so reconstructing it receives no new philosophical credit.

It does NOT supply a contentfully warranted intrinsic nature that jointly establishes necessary unreceived existence, original productive/cognitive adequacy, the metaphysical eligibility of the mixed character, and the ultimate authority of every relevant norm. Granting those facts separately is not an explanation of their compossibility. Its unusual betrayal aim has a definite explanatory role but no independent actual evidence. Calling that aim essential does not prove eligibility, and calling it defective does not by itself prove impossibility in an original bearer.

Its failure is not a discovered contradiction. It is incomplete positive warrant for the rival's ultimate explanatory commitments. The affirmative also needs more than that failure to establish its own necessary and perfect source.

## 3. A joint norm-grounding pressure test

There are three distinct contributions an account may make:

1. The source apprehends an obtaining truth or reason.
2. Its intention establishes a creature's design function or undertakes an assurance commitment.
3. Its intrinsic nature grounds the truth or normative authority in question.

The common mixed architecture supplies the first two in A/B under its grants. It cannot silently claim the third by pointing to its knowledge or causal completeness. Conversely, an affirmative cannot infer the third merely because the very same bearer performs the first two.

This matters when an objection says that the mixed source violates the norm it originates. A norm can constitute an activity's success condition without constituting an inviolable existence condition for every token of that activity. A knowingly false assertion can still be an actual assertion; its defect is assessed by the very truth-linked undertaking it abuses. A source's design can fix a faculty's genuine function while some exercise malfunctions. These are inherited distinctions jointly applied here.

A stronger source-nature account may identify the norm with comprehensive essentially fitting practical governance. Such an account excludes C. Its substantive burden is why this source has that comprehensive nature and why its norm-grounding relation has that force. The conclusion cannot instead be obtained from a weaker relation of knowing, designing or promising and then described as mere spelling-out of original sourcehood.

The rival has the converse obligation. If it invokes an independent normative object, value ranking or law that fixes all reasons, it must identify its status and the relation to M. If that reality productively supplies M's efficacy, strong independence/coverage may fail. If it is not a productive supplier, P1 alone does not refute it, but its normative explanatory work must still be supplied. Merely writing 'intrinsic norm–power coupling' leaves an undeveloped commitment. Local content–object correctness is better specified; it does not automatically answer every question of categorical authority.

There is no free normative completion on either side. The comparison needs one described dependence relation and its warrant, not a common label or an unexplained value assignment.

### A genuinely active candidate

Consider a beginningless family of actual bearers indexed by negative integers, with no earliest member. Each bearer really understands a target, intentionally produces the relevant successor's powers and evidence-sensitive environment, and exercises efficacy received from an earlier active bearer. Each local exercise has its complete actual determination. At the terminal local episode the recipient performs the admitted correction because the displayed evidence is understood.

This is not the claim that an infinite string of empty permissions turns itself on. At every link, there is actual knowledge, willing, power and a received relation supplying that power. No individual produces itself; no finite productive path cycles back to the same bearer. No first supplier is removed from a structure that stipulated one. The objective target is the same actual inscription and proposition, and local function is supplied by genuine intelligent institution, not by the investigator scoring an output.

The explanatory content offered for each link is a source's actual apprehended purpose and effective exercise. The aggregate candidate Q records these concrete productive exercises, rather than merely repeating that all nodes exist. P is the existential inventory of those bearers. The intended claim is that Q explains P while the whole realized configuration Q has no further explanation. This is a proposal for examination, not an established possibility or a complete explanatory success.

### The exact exemption comparison

Apply EX to the proposed Q. The infinitary candidate asks why its own complete realized productive configuration cannot occupy that explanatory stopping role.

There are at least three substantive differences a successful affirmative reply might establish:

- an original agent's Q draws on independently possessed efficacy, whereas the candidate's entire efficacy remains received;
- the candidate's Q merely rearranges or presupposes the very whole actuality P it purports to explain;
- the proposed kind of Q could have a further explanation, so it is not eligible for the restricted principle's exemption.

Each is an argument to be made, not a result of the word infinite. The first invokes original-possession priority. The second needs a defensible move from each act's dependence on its own bearer to a prohibited collective explanatory self-presupposition. Logical entailment of P by Q is insufficient by itself: a successful complete productive explanation can entail its effect. The third needs genuine modal explanatory warrant, not the bare conceivability of a larger diagram.

The infinitist must answer those objections. Stipulating that Q is intrinsically unexplainable is not enough. It must show why real productive reception can be non-well-founded and why Q adds explanation rather than merely renaming the active assignment. The proposed exemption is therefore a discriminator, not a license to hide an unexplained box.

### Non-agglomeration is not universal exclusion

A counterexample in which every member of a collection is locally explained while the whole has a further unanswered explanation defeats the general rule that member explanations ALWAYS suffice. It does not establish the universal converse that no infinite family can ever collectively explain its existential inventory.

To exclude the active candidate by that route, defend collective exclusion at E's exact target-relative adequacy standard: no E-adequate productive explanation of the declared inventory can invoke only contingent members whose existential availability is included in that inventory. This is not automatically the stronger whole-origin relation E*. This is stronger than irreflexivity of individual productive ancestry and stronger than the accepted local same-token no-go. Its reason can be that received efficacy never supplies original possession; that is a substantive metaphysical defense, not merely a finite-graph argument.

The candidate therefore puts pressure on collective exclusion for E itself. Excluding only a stronger explanatory relation does not answer it. Finding that exact burden neither disproves nor independently warrants the exclusion premise.

## 5. An exact inventory-witness constraint

For target I, any proposed added providers must preserve its exact counts.

Consequently the proposed providers must either have qualitative kinds not included in that fixed inventory, or fit into its counts without losing the required effects. Finite multiplicity of each actual kind does not alone establish either alternative. Nor does separate possible production of each target establish their joint possible production together with adequate providers.

This is a new application-side constraint on a proposed possibility witness. It is not an assertion that no such witness exists. An independently warranted possible extra provider with a suitably different intrinsic kind may meet it. But the inventory cannot silently shift between the actual list and the list in the possibility witness, and a merely qualitative duplicate does not establish identity of the actual complete productive source.

## 7. Recommendation and novelty boundary

Do not formalize a weaker Γ merely to obtain a model. Do not conjoin the old mixed-source story with a new absence world and call it an integrated advance. Do not report a failure to produce a metaphysically adequate alternative as positive evidence for necessary sourcehood.

Use the new collective-explanation route to demand a precise exemption comparison: what supplies actual efficacy, what is the exact unexplained item, why is that item intrinsically unexplainable if exempted, and why does the explanation of the inventory not presuppose its own target in the relevant productive respect? The same questions apply to the personal and infinitary accounts at their appropriate explanatory levels.

The present lane contributes a source-bound joint ledger, an exclusion of several invalid strong-grant substitutions, and this concrete combined warrant test with the exact-count witness check. It does not claim a new grand metaphysical theorem. MEN-8 remains open. No new Lean or enumeration has been run or proposed before a substantive joint argument earns that step.
