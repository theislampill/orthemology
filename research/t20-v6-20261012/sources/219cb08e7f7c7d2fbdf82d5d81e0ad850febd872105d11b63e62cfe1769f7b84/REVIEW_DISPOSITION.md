# Mathematical correction: application scope

3 October 2026 UTC.

Accept the mathematical correction at the existing uniform and nonuniform acceptance scopes. Unique infinite backward realisation does not generally require forward convergence. A value's survival at every finite depth does not generally provide a compatible infinite history.

Those failures alone do not refute a particular model with a continuous self-map of a nonempty compact metric space, a strict contraction with bounded image on a nonempty complete metric space, or an iterate whose image is a singleton. The application must retain the model's declared carrier and map. Contraction and eventual-compactness applications remain ordinary mathematics, not additions to the Lean-checked scope.

This is a source-application audit of the inspected witness, not final-version validation, independent discovery, or external specialist review.
