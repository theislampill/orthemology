# Warrant threshold for one actual common Creator

7 October 2026. Independent, bounded philosophical audit. AI-authored assessment; no human-specialist review is claimed.

## Verdict

My actual research-level judgement is **provisional acceptance of one complete original Creator of the actual connected created order**, with appreciably less confidence than I give the Necessary Being conclusion. I mean defeasible assent to that actual explanatory claim, not merely using it as a convenient research model. I **withhold assent to the stronger universal SourceMode claim** that every possible qualified original must completely provide every requisite of every connected effect to which it contributes. The former does not need the latter.

This differs from the earlier actual-common-source assessment. Its careful distinctions survive, but its final withholding gives too much practical weight to an insufficiently developed plural-harmony proposal relative to the positive explanatory resources it otherwise retains. It does not literally call that proposal proved possible or equally credible. The problem is subtler: its threshold for moving from preference to provisional belief is not sufficiently explained once one adequate original agent, integrated actual order, and the absence of an independently motivated task for additional originals are all credited.

This conclusion depends on a substantive background that I myself provisionally judge sufficiently warranted, rather than merely granting it for comparison. The specific grounds and limits are identified below. It would not follow from an arbitrarily selected necessary item or from the word “original.” Nor am I inheriting every attribute-ascent verdict in the Seventeenth or Nineteenth report. The common-source conclusion remains open to a serious comparative defeater, and the full Safadiyya assessment was unavailable at this audit's boundary.

## 1 The standard and the propositions

Sufficient defeasible warrant permits reflective belief when there is a positive, intelligible account of the actual target; its additional commitments do explanatory work; and the strongest **motivated** alternatives do not leave its advantage too fragile to rely on. It requires more than being the prettiest description in an impoverished list. It does not require deductive entailment, universal agreement, a numerical posterior, or an independently disproved rival for every imaginable alternative.

The decisive contrast is between:

- **N:** some actual particular bearer has whole-existence originality and intrinsic necessity, with genuine original efficacy relevant to the actual receiving field.
- **C:** the actual connected field has one bearer's complete original productive provision, compatible with genuine derivative contributors and successive acts.
- **S:** every possible suitably qualified original must instantiate an exhaustive, unborrowed source mode in every relevant exercise.

C does not assert that no unrelated necessary reality could exist. It does exclude a second irreducibly original productive contributor inside this field. An organising intelligence that works with independently original material would not satisfy C. Nor would labelling a collection of originals “one explanation.” A genuinely unified holistic bearer could satisfy C; numerical simplicity of every internal distinction is not being assumed.

C is therefore a substantive enlargement of N, and S is not simply its restatement. A failure to warrant S blocks a proposed universal route to C; it does not settle the separate cumulative case for C.

## 2 What background I retain and what I charge to the inference

I judge the defended whole-field original-completion rationale, the concrete bearer requirement, and genuine original efficacy sufficiently warranted for defeasible belief. This is my own bounded appraisal of the reasons, informed by selected primary checks; it is not a claim to have newly reconstructed every foundational source. The positive reason is that a completely received productive account cannot answer its own original existential requirement merely by adding more receivers. A real original resource needs concrete actual instantiation; the bearer of a wholly existentially received resource does not become original merely because it has genuine local power. This preserves the substance of T17 P40–54 and the primary Safadiyya distinction between received producers and a succession of effects (S001L31–34).

For N I also provisionally accept the contingency-as-existential-need principle. Its rationale concerns the whole reality's lack of sufficiency through itself, rather than a nonexistent thing making itself or a temporal transition imposed on every ultimate. Under that principle, the same actual whole-existence original is necessary. The brute-contingent-ultimate proposal identifies a substantive point of disagreement, but does not by itself defeat this positive adequacy judgement. This is an acceptance of a rational principle, not a modal theorem or a claim that all dissent is confused.

For C I additionally need **integrative creative agency**: knowledge and power adequate to the relevant differentiated order, and an intelligible capacity for coordinated determination. I take the means–ends and interdependence evidence to support that agency defeasibly. Nubuwwat S003L14–18 explains why the relevant organisation is the fitting arrangement of means, not regularity alone. Miftah 583–590 presents a connected provision of conditions, organisms and uses, rather than an abstract numerical preference. These arguments have genuine positive content even though their historical examples and their broadest claims are not all independently vindicated.

The enlargement of agency to this field is a **cost of C**, not a free result from N. My judgement is that the cost is warranted within this comparison: a common original agency supplies and organises the very resources whose original actuality already needs an account, and it joins their interdependence with their coordinated deployment. I do not first prove an unlimited creator and then recover unity by definition. Scope and unity are assessed together in the explanatory hypothesis. Its scope includes the original actuality of the field’s material, productive and preserving requisites, including mediate provision through real created agents. It is not merely an intelligence arranging foreign original materials. My affirmative assessment of this package would weaken substantially if the evidence supported only regularity or only an external organiser; it does not rest on bare efficacy alone.

I do **not** require independently settled omnipotence over every possible contingent object, absolute domination over every possible peer, universal veracity, exhaustive moral justification of every event, primitive causal nonduplication in all possible worlds, or S. The broad pure-perfection ascent in T17 has a substantive rationale: informed, unborrowed additional ability can be an excellence rather than a liability. But why every eligible excellence belongs to this bearer, and exactly which source-sensitive targets are eligible, remain additional questions. Safadiyya's displayed perfection passage, printed 122–123, supplies reasons for that ascent; it does not turn every such application into an already secured premise. I neither dismiss that argument nor count the report's acceptance as its warrant.

## 3 The positive comparison

The positive case has three connected parts.

**An original explanatory resource is already warranted.** C does not introduce an entirely unmotivated kind of cause solely to explain resemblance. It identifies the scope and arrangement of original provision whose actuality the retained argument already supports. That lowers one familiar cost of a common-creator hypothesis without making its numerical result automatic.

**The field exhibits coordinated interdependence.** The relevant effects do not merely look alike. They employ common operative conditions and sustain one another across different functions and successive replacements. A common knowing source makes this one intelligible practical determination encompassing different contributions. Real creaturely action remains part of the explanation. Shifa e231–234 gives the cumulative version, while Darʾ VIII P396–422 usefully insists that producing an indispensable motion or serving as a final attractor is not yet making the substances and their other properties. I apply that stricter target to C itself.

**Additional originals have no identified explanatory job that the integrated account cannot do.** If one source can originate the differentiated resources, another original introduces another independently possessed productive actuality and a division of original roles. Several sources may share a wise end, but that alone does not specify who supplies which materials, powers and processes. The single-source account locates the differentiation within one agency; the plural account must additionally locate and relate it across independent original bearers. The proposed bearer really owns the relevant capacities and originates their exercises. Its unity is a substantive hypothesis about agency, supported by integrated operation, not something established by drawing one box around a coordinated collection. If that unity were only a relabelling, the claimed explanatory saving would disappear.

This is a real economy advantage, though not an enormous quantified likelihood ratio. It is not permissible to assume that distinct originals choose statistically independently, that they probably quarrel, or that each observed fit requires a separate miraculous coincidence. Conversely, acknowledging those cautions does not erase the absence of a positive reason to multiply originals. Within the retained framework, integration plus economy warrants the modest further commitment to one actual original author. It is stronger than entity-counting in isolation.

## 4 How much weight the plural rival deserves

The best rival is not reciprocal manufacture of powers or two agents each exclusively producing the very same whole. It gives each original its own knowledge, power and intention, with intelligible reasons yielding complementary contributions. Jami VI E185, corresponding to the retained printed 181 discussion, explicitly separates each cooperator's agency from the jointly required attainment. That is an important conceptual control. It does not prove that independent originals cooperate, but it prevents automatic transport from joint-product dependence to borrowed intrinsic power.

Likewise, Darʾ's maker-and-preserver comparison gives genuine positive reason to value fuller actual provision. It does not alone establish that every perfect original must always undertake the fullest role rather than a fitting partial one. T19 P60–78 correctly exposes the added source-mode judgement. I do not use voluntary cooperation as an immediate refutation of it; I withhold its universal sufficiency because the respect in which a role is less comprehensive has not yet been adequately shown to be an unavoidable defect of its independently perfect agent.

The plural proposal therefore has sufficient content to expose a **premise gap** in an attempted deduction. It presently has less force as an **actual explanatory competitor**. “Their wise natures necessarily harmonise” names the proposed solution. It does not yet show a principle that both allocates original roles and gives a positive reason for their independent ownership. A common objective good could help explain matching ends. It need not settle differentiated means or numerical original provenance. An original-peer-essential relational good could supply a further reason, but ordinary goods of collaboration establish neither the originality of the participants nor the need for original peers.

The comparison must remain fair. One wise source also owes a determinate account; its unified will cannot magically derive every detail of this history. Internal differentiation may carry some of the same explanatory complexity as an inter-source arrangement. The plural hypothesis must not be penalised twice for worldly detail that both hypotheses already have to explain. My verdict rests on a **residual** advantage: the plural account adds original bearers and an allocation of ownership without an established compensating explanatory gain. The single source need not explain why this is the uniquely best possible world to explain its actual interdependent provision more economically.

Nubuwwat's ring passage provides a good epistemic discipline, not a direct argument for monotheism. In S002L105–107, visually controlled here at printed 771, suspension follows when an alternative arises **and has force**; mere describability is not made a universal defeater. The earlier common-source assessment recognises that rule verbally, but its final weighting does not sufficiently show where the proposed plural harmony acquires the force needed to block this bounded assent.

## 5 Consistency with Necessary Being acceptance

There is no formal inconsistency in accepting N and withholding C. N has a direct adequacy rationale; C adds a claim about the distribution and completeness of actual authorship. The same evidential standard can give them different confidence. It would be mistaken to reason that because one controversial metaphysical principle was accepted, every later one must be accepted too.

There is nevertheless a **calibration asymmetry to repair**. T17 accepts contingency-as-need despite a coherently expressed brute-actuality objection, because it judges the positive connection weightier. It also accepts some standing-perfection claims despite a merely described intrinsically limited original. At the unity stage, “a necessarily harmonious plurality could preserve the fit” cannot do more defeating work merely because the assessor is now asking about numerical scope. It needs either positive motivation or a specific undercutting diagnosis strong enough to undermine the single-source explanation.

The prior assessment supplies genuine local undercutters against S, the transfer of product-need to power-need, and a supremacy shortcut. It is less successful at showing that those undercut the remaining cumulative C explanation once adequate integrative original agency is retained. I therefore revise the actual verdict upward while leaving N accepted and S withheld. This is a comparative philosophical judgement, not a logical accusation against the previous assessor. No newly discovered quotation forces the revision. The remaining disagreement is whether the residual integration/economy advantage, after paying for comprehensive agency and removing the universal shortcuts, is enough for provisional assent. I judge it is; the prior assessor judges it too weak. There is no independently calibrated probability scale in this dossier that makes one threshold compulsory.

If the prior assessor instead withholds the adequacy of one agent for the actual field, that is a legitimate, distinct reason for greater caution. It should be stated at that point. It cannot simultaneously be treated as an accepted explanatory resource and left uncredited when the rival's extra bearers are assessed. My own affirmative verdict pays the scope cost described in section 2 rather than hiding it inside “necessary.”

## 6 Formal gaps and new source material

No weak countermodel has evidential authority beyond its represented premises. A model of several nodes with powers and matching outputs neither vindicates its original-bearer interpretation nor supplies a metaphysically possible plural world. Conversely, the critic need not prove that world possible to show that an alleged entailment used an unstated normative or causal bridge. Neither observation alone decides sufficient warrant.

Absence of deduction is not a reason for my remaining withholding on S. The reason is the unresolved classification of every partial original undertaking as defective. Equally, the availability of a valid deduction conditional on S would not establish S. C receives its own cumulative appraisal.

Miftah and Nubuwwat strengthen the intelligible-agent model and the legitimacy of cumulative recognition. They do not supply another independently successful proof of numerical uniqueness merely by affirming it. Miftah 587–588's two-souls analogy needs defended transfer conditions and explicitly defers fuller exposition. Miftah 1024–1027 distinguishes completing a product from acquiring the maker's perfection; its claim that all wisdom's sources and objects already derive from God belongs to its comprehensive theology and cannot independently prove that same scope.

The Safadiyya material actually checked adds the whole-field and positive-perfection rationales and a renewed unity argument, including explicit source qualifications. It does not yet alter my assessment of S. The full electronic traversal by others and an unfinished combined appraisal are not my own full reading or a hidden extra confirmation. Basic Creator recognition remains epistemically admissible; this audit does not certify that every claimed recognition is reliable, or independently authenticate a revelatory event.

## 7 What would change this judgement

C would lose its present sufficient warrant if a serious plural-original account supplied an independently motivated allocation principle and preserved equivalent integration at no greater explanatory cost, or explained some feature better. A convincing conceptual criticism showing that my one-agency account merely hides the same unexplained matching facts could also suffice, without a completed rival cosmology or a proved-possible plural world. Evidence of irreducibly separate original provenance, or a substantive failure of one adequate integrative source to explain the field, would likewise matter. A challenge to the underlying purposive-agent inference or to the whole-field/bearer background could lower confidence more fundamentally.

A stronger independent bearer-unity argument, a better account of why the actual source's competence encompasses the whole field, or independently warranted source-owned testimony with the relevant content could strengthen C. A sound defence that foreign completion of any qualified original's undertaking is necessarily an agency defect could strengthen S. None is assumed here.

The weakest seam in my affirmative judgement is precisely the passage from observed integrated provision to a genuinely single original agency with the required scope. Bare count-economy would not close that passage. I credit the cumulative original-resource and integrative-agency rationale; I do not claim that interconnection by itself individuates a bearer. This is why C receives lower confidence than N.

The immediate research need is a comparative explanation of **the cost of comprehensive single agency versus the cost and benefit of independently original plurality**, with the existing positive background fixed. Repeating that plurality is conceivable, counting another author who affirms unity, or replaying a conditional proof would not answer that need.
