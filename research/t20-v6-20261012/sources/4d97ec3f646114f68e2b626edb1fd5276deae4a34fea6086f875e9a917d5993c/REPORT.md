# Intrinsic representation: a dependency-elimination test

10 October 2026 UTC. One bounded continuation of the existing T20 investigation. No repository mutation, integration, closure, new primary witness, or new metaphysical model.

## Result

The constructive continuation is in **LOCAL_SHIP_APPLICATION.md**: the ship and wall cases instantiate a disclosed local means rationale, with the wall expressly attributing the future end to the Lord. Read that companion for the positive application; this report records the narrower dependency restriction.

**The cognitive-volitional representation in Majmūʿ 16:305 is itself an occurrence through the original's willing and power. It cannot be treated as an unexplained, ready-made particular selector and then credited with explaining the ultimate selection of that very particular. Conversely, the passage does not entail a same-token productive circle between representation and executive willing. That circle requires identifying two willing roles and converting practical differentiation into efficient production; neither conversion is supplied by the passage.**

This is an executed inference test of one newly foregrounded clause, rather than another restatement of the intrinsic/extrinsic distinction. It yields a precise application obstruction: moving the particular content into a cognitive representation does not discharge its determination, while an objection from reciprocal knowledge–will language does not establish circular origination. A faithful explanation must keep the origin of the representation, the apprehended reason's practical relevance, and the production of the intended effect distinguishable.

The inherited positive architecture of intrinsic, reason-sensitive activity survives. The new result is specifically an inference restriction, not a new proof that any particular representation is adequately determined or a new local means/occasion explanation. The accepted original productive provision, same actual bearer's intrinsic necessity and essential independence, complete common-original appraisal, and scoped Knowledge/Will/Wisdom appraisals are unchanged.

## 2. Primary context and attribution

At 297–299 the author presents wisdom as informed fitting activity, beyond bare power, will, or knowledge. At 301 he distinguishes the genus of activity from a particular act and its willing. At 302–303 he rejects both an unchanged indifferent single will and an account adding new wills without explaining their specification. These are described alternatives and authorial criticisms.

The fourth view at 303 is positively developed: the original has always willed through successive willings, with a particular willing at its proper time. It is not an initial transition from an eternally idle subject to the first mental event. The prospective/executive distinction then explains how an act can be intended for a future occasion without already being intended for immediate execution.

At 304 the author's positive account says particular willing follows knowledge of the relevant differentiating meaning. The intended particular's features are in knowledge/conception rather than an already existing external object. Immediately at 305 he adds:

“وتلك الصورة العلمية الإرادية حدثت بعد أن لم تكن”

The cognitive-volitional representation arose after not having been. The next clause attributes its occurrence to the original's willing and power, comparing this with the origination of separate effects through willing and power. He then attributes particular specification by quality and measure to determinants within the subject, and rejects bare common capacity as sufficient differentiation.

The immediate feminine pronoun in the specification sentence naturally refers to the representation, but it can also connect to the previously discussed things. Nothing here depends on making its antecedent uniquely the representation: the explicit representation-origination clause already prevents treating that representation as an unoriginated input, and the continuous argument demands grounds for particular specification.

At 306–309 the discussion defends prior determination and writing, then continues its account of the competing theological positions. At 310–312 differentiated knowledge and willing are distinguished from separated countable components. The interpretation below therefore uses role labels, not a census of independently subsisting divine act-objects.

## 3. Strongest affirmative argument and strongest objection

### Affirmative reconstruction

The already actual, knowledgeable, powerful original apprehends a fitting particular as intentional content. That apprehension has a practical role in its particular willing; actual willing produces the intended effect. The content need not be supplied by a future external thing. The intrinsic cognitive-volitional representation itself occurs through the original's activity. Thus both representation and product can belong to one original's internally governed activity, while remaining different explananda.

This is stronger than saying that the original knows whatever happens. It gives cognition a practical role and willing a productive role, grounded within the same bearer. Its positive value is preserved.

### Strongest objection tested

If willing follows cognition and cognition arises through willing, either cognition and willing produce one another, or the account always requires another earlier willing. The first seems circular; the second seems never to finish. An alternative reply would stop with one ungrounded selected representation and claim that cognition now explains the selection.

All three moves need testing. A blanket denial of every infinite succession would contradict the fourth view. A finite first-stage representation would be imported, not found. Equally, merely saying “the history is perpetual” does not state the differentiating reason for any specified content.

## 4. Executed derivation: preserve relation and witness types

Use the following role labels, with no commitment to separately existing components:

- g: the same actual original bearer.
- r: the cognitive-volitional representation under discussion.
- c: its intentional content, concerning a particular act/effect and occasion.
- w: the particular executive willing under discussion.
- q: the intended external effect, distinguished from both the original's intrinsic act and its representation.

Keep three relations separate:

- Content(r,c): what is apprehended.
- Guides(r,w): the apprehended relevant feature has a practical differentiating role in w. This is stronger than mere truth-matching awareness, but is not by definition complete efficient production.
- OriginationThrough(g,r): r occurs through g's willing and power. The Arabic supplies this claim, not a canonical one-to-one map from representations to prior volition-tokens.

For q, actual effective willing together with the relevant conditions has its inherited productive role. The particular passage does not require us to individuate every element of those conditions here.

### Step A: the alleged circle does not follow

The source gives Guides(r,w) and OriginationThrough(g,r). It does not give Produces(w,r), much less Produces(r,w).

Even if a formal implementation introduces a witness u for some particular willing involved in r's origination, it gets at most a relation involving u and r. Existentially introducing u does not establish u = w. Nor does Guides(r,w) license replacing it with the claim that r alone efficiently produces w.

Therefore a purported two-edge productive cycle requires both:

1. A warranted identification of the willing involved in r's origination with the executive willing w under discussion.
2. A warranted promotion of the representation's practical differentiating role into the relevant efficient-production edge.

Neither follows from the displayed premises. If an enriched account independently supplied both, the resulting productive-cycle question would have to be assessed. Their absence here is not a proof that no such cycle could ever be alleged; it shows that the simple reciprocal-language inference fails.

The source permits succession, and its distinction between resolve and execution is one explicit reason not to force every willing role into the same executive token. It does not follow that every representation must have a distinct earlier volition as its sole cause. Relation typing prevents both unwarranted conclusions.

### Step B: eliminating the intermediate representation preserves its origin obligation

Suppose an explanation offered is:

(1) r apprehends the distinguishing feature of c;
(2) that feature guides w;
(3) effective w produces q in the appropriate conditions;
therefore this explains the ultimate particular selection of q.

The conclusion exceeds the three premises if r's particular content is taken as an unexplained input. Steps (1)–(3) explain an execution conditional on that representation and its actual practical role. The source additionally locates r's occurrence within the original's activity. Replacing “q was selected” with “r represented q and guided its selection” does not explain why this particular r, rather than some other relevant intentional determination, obtains.

This is a dependency-elimination result: an intermediate does not vanish from an explanation's outstanding commitments when a later stage is derived through it. Either the offered account warrants the intermediate's relevant determination at the claimed scope, or the conclusion remains conditional on it. A known independent basis for r could discharge that commitment; its being intrinsic does not by itself do so.

This does not demand that every explanation reach a first moment, disclose an entire history, or provide a further separately subsisting antecedent. It also does not prohibit discovering a reason from an observed effect through an independently warranted relation. The target is the specific claim to have explained ultimate selection solely by inserting the representation.

### Step C: the positive account survives both tests

No externally actual q must efficiently donate its properties to r. No will-excluding earlier mechanism has been derived. No loss of the original's prior knowledge follows from a newly occurring cognitive-volitional determination. The source has expressly distinguished knowing what will be done from its present and accomplished intentional-temporal roles.

Cognitive practical differentiation and productive willing can therefore remain intrinsic roles in the same original's activity without the illicit productive cycle. And they can explain a local execution without falsely claiming to settle the whole content-origin question. That is a genuine affirmative interface constraint, not a new proof that the remaining particular account has been supplied.

### Step D: what would supply a stronger explanation?

The new representation-origination clause does not itself specify an additional local differentiator. The prospective/executive account already supplies a bounded local result: an intention for an act at its future fitting time is not an intention for its immediate execution. That explains why the existence of the earlier resolve does not already require the external product then. This is inherited positive work; no fresh credit is taken for it.

For a new account of the representation's particular content, the missing premise has a definite form. Identify an actual intrinsic feature or relation H, independently describable without “the feature belonging to the selected q,” and warrant both (a) its relevance to the apprehended end or operative occasion and (b) its actual differentiating role in the representation's taking content c. A merely common standing power does not satisfy (b); a merely factive knowledge claim does not satisfy the practical relation in (a).

H need not be a separate object, a temporally prior volition, or a unique numerical maximum. It could be an intrinsic practical relation within the activity. But saying only that *some* adequate H exists, although consistent with the affirmative general account, does not instantiate this particular explanation. And if H itself is supplied as an unexplained c-specific declaration, the claimed ultimate explanation still has the same outstanding content obligation. Ultimate adequacy requires a warranted account of the relevant determination at its stated scope, not necessarily a temporally first ancestor.

Thus the new operation identifies exactly what the local-to-ultimate promotion needs and why the inspected sentence does not supply it. It neither produces a new local reason nor proves that no one can supply one.

### Logical check and its limited status

A small explicit countervaluation checks Step A's invalid entailment. Even strengthen the source fragment by assigning a particular own exercise u to the origination of r. Set practical guidance to {(r,w)}, production to {(u,r),(w,q)}, and u distinct from w. All those strengthened role claims hold, while production has no directed cycle. This proves only that the displayed role premises do not logically entail a productive cycle. It is not a complete source model, a metaphysical possibility certificate, or an explanation of u, r, w, or q. Its role is precisely to prevent the inference restriction being mistaken for a proved positive metaphysics.

## 5. Exact formal-application consequence

A faithful adapter for this locus must not turn a field such as `represented_target = q` into a certificate of q's ultimate independent determination. Nor should it translate all three relations into a single binary causal edge and infer a cycle.

The minimal semantic contract must preserve:

1. Bearer ownership, which does not make the bearer produce its own entire existence.
2. Intentional content, which does not assert a pre-existing external product.
3. The practical relevance relation, which requires warrant beyond accurate representation.
4. Intrinsic origination of the representation, without a forced unique volition-token witness.
5. Actual effective production, with the act/effect distinction retained.
6. The scope of explanation: conditional execution or ultimate particular selection.

These are requirements for an application, not a detected code defect. The unchanged OriginalBearerBridge has no particular cognition/willing relation and explicitly disclaims a bearer-to-attribute causal edge. Its existing existential resource proof therefore neither commits the tested collapse nor repairs it. No change to that interface is indicated.

## 6. Change from prior state and stopping point

The prior report established the intrinsic location and intentional-temporal organization of particular agency. The present operation applies the adjacent representation-origination clause to two concrete proposed inferences: automatic productive circularity and automatic discharge of selection by cognitive representation. Both fail for separately identified reasons.

The useful remaining question is now sharper: when a particular intrinsic representation is offered as a ground, what independently warranted feature explains its relevant content and practical role at the scope claimed? The text's response is intrinsic differentiation, not a future external donor. The present passage does not identify the complete particular ground of an actual whole undertaking. Perpetual succession does not substitute for it, and neither does moving q into r.

No new full-history determination, independent original-adequacy proof, metaphysical countermodel, or refutation of all nonnecessitating agency has been obtained. The inability to obtain those stronger results is bounded by the actual premises read. It does not reopen any earned foundational or Wisdom appraisal.
