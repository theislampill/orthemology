# Proposed source-owned veracity boundary

Status: APPROVED BOUNDED DESIGN, corrected before implementation. Original proposal bytes are preserved in proposal-v1/. Root approved implementation on 2026-10-07 subject to the corrections in CHANGE_RECORD.md. No new philosophical verdict or publication is authorized.

## Recommendation

The useful bounded target is a **premise-dependency and equivalence audit**, not a newly independent proof of T0. Separate (i) semantic coverage of every genuinely owned assertion by informed, controlled adoption, (ii) the respect-specific defect of knowing counterfeit, (iii) the source-specific claim that this defect cannot belong to a fitting exercise, and (iv) generic actual practical conformance. Prove their conditional consequence and expose their individual failures in inhabited finite interpretations.

T17's affirmative, defeasibly warranted appraisal of exceptionless source-owned veracity remains intact, together with its continuing critic. The present coordinating reassessment of other foundational questions does not supply a new veracity verdict. If the semantic-coverage decomposition cannot be independently warranted, retain broad all-context owned integrity as the substantive accepted premise. A theorem obtained by naming that premise `Perfect` would add nothing.

## Exact proposed contract

Use distinct source, contextual occasion, occurrence, proposition, and exercise types. Fix the SAME source `g`. An occasion ranges over the intended domain of possible exercises; the adequacy of that domain remains an explicit application obligation. It must not be silently equated with an exhaustive metaphysical possibility space.

Primitive relations:

- `Assert(c,g,t,p)`: the actual occurrence is genuinely owned as an assertion, with contextual content p. It contains no truth, knowledge, practical perfection, or recipient-warrant conjunct.
- `KnowsFalse(c,g,p)`, with an explicit factivity law; this is not merely a belief label.
- `Aware(c,g,t,p)`: the source is aware that this occurrence presents this very contextual content.
- `DeliberatePresentation(c,g,t)`: the source actually controls/chooses this occurrence as its assertoric presentation. This deliberately has no content-p argument; awareness of the exact contextual content is separate. Available expressive power alone does not establish this exercise relation.
- `ActualExercise(c,g,e)`, `Fitting(c,g,e)`, and `DeclarativeShortcoming(c,g,e)`. None is defined by truthfulness. Assertion-to-own-exercise is a separate typing/realisation bridge.

Define `KnowingCounterfeit` descriptively as Assert + KnowsFalse + Aware + DeliberatePresentation. It contains no moral verdict. Do not identify knowing that p is false with knowing that one's present token asserts p. Do not turn accidental production or externally assigned content into deliberate adoption by definition.

Named premises:

1. K: for an owned assertion, false p entails KnowsFalse p. This is the exact relevant epistemic scope used, not an unexplained appeal to the word omniscience.
2. A: every in-scope owned assertion is covered by awareness of its contextual content.
3. C: every in-scope owned assertion is covered by actual deliberate assertoric presentation. A supplies awareness of its exact content. If ownership itself is interpreted as knowingly deliberate adoption under the exact content, A/C are semantic consequences of that strengthened interpretation and must not be reported as independent new attribute results.
4. D: knowing counterfeit has a respect-specific declarative shortcoming.
5. N: for this source, such a shortcoming is incompatible with a fitting owned exercise in every in-scope context. This is the substantive all-context purity/eligibility step. It cannot be inferred merely from D or from the existence of some fitting truthful alternative.
6. P: this source's actual exercises are fitting. This is generic practical conformance, with independent normative interpretation, not approval by definition of whatever occurs.

K/A/C are a proposed decomposition of the audit interface, not separately established T17 source sentences. D is strongly source-attested; N and P articulate the normative burden of the accepted cumulative appraisal. Their reality-warrant is not certified by finite interpretations.

## Proposed results

1. **Controlled-token theorem.** K, D, N, P and the exercise bridge imply truth for an owned token whose A/C facts are supplied. This makes the limited result available without pretending universal semantic coverage has been earned.
2. **Source-domain theorem.** Add A/C coverage for all in-scope assertions to obtain T0 over that declared domain. Exceptionless conclusion and defeasible warrant for its premises remain distinct.
3. **Failure diagnostic.** A false owned assertion, with the exercise bridge intact, is incompatible with the conjunction K/A/C/D/N/P. A disjunction locating a failed premise uses classical reasoning (or explicit decidable finite predicates). The useful deliverable is the exact premise-use and deletion ledger, not the trivial contradiction proof by itself.
4. **Honesty equivalence.** Given K/A/C coverage and factive KnowsFalse, T0 is equivalent to absence of knowing counterfeit among those owned assertions. Thus merely replacing T0 with source-specific no-counterfeit is not an independently weaker warrant. Do not claim the whole broader D/N/P package is equivalent to T0: it can constrain other exercises too.
5. **No converse.** A truthful speaker may perform a separate unfitting nondeclarative act. T0 does not imply generic practical conformance or the upstream attribute package.

Keep common-source uniqueness, graph coverage, causal nonduplication, universal benevolence, and truthful-means substitutability out of the theorem signature. They supply no logical force to this conditional. Essential independence and general pure-perfection ascent matter to the philosophical warrant for N/P; do not count them twice by passing unused assumptions.
