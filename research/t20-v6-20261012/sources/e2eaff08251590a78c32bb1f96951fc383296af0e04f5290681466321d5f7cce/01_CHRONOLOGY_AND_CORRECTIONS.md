## 4. What supersession does not do

Supersession does not delete old countermodels, failures, doubts or source interpretations. It changes the status with which they may be reused. An old head's HOLD is historically correct at its old scope. An earlier statement that witness extraction was unverified may accurately describe CP24 and become obsolete after the constructive update. A later result does not retroactively turn the earlier checkpoint into a proof it did not contain.

Likewise, the final conversation's stronger assessment of Orthemology is an argued appraisal, not a decree that every manuscript is novel or correct. Preserve disagreement and evaluate exact contributions. Neither sceptical understatement nor enthusiastic overstatement should become an unquestionable inherited standard.
