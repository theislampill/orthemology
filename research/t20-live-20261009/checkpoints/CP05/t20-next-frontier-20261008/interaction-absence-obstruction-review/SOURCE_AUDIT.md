## Attribution and bounded novelty

The parent supplied the hard family, deterministic oracle argument, known
observable Lipschitz refinement and randomized bounded-error candidate. This
review independently rederived and adversarially checked those claims, wrote
separate controls, and highlighted fixed-name and seed-independence quantifiers.
The multiplication-only positivity contrast is an equivalent check of the
already available mask quotient, not a separate discovery credit.

The new local conclusion is stronger than failure of one specific zero-case
routine. It rules out all always-terminating binary procedures within the stated
contract and all almost-surely terminating uniform-error procedures below 1/2.
It does not duplicate the exact full-law classification, positive-multiplicity
algorithm, grouped-mixture invariant theorem, or previous finite-sample bounds.

No broad novelty claim follows. The finite-transcript indistinguishability
principle is elementary and already present in the local ancestry. Independent
review is an evidentiary check of one result, not another independent result to
be counted as additional theoretical novelty.

## Negative transports checked

- Uniformly approaching Q laws are not identical Q laws.
- Valid rational approximation centers are not certified exact rational values.
- A common response modulus is not a common hidden-calibration modulus.
- Each finite inventory does not imply a known common count bound.
- A copied finite transcript is not automatically a fixed oracle; the new
  coarse-copy/fine-bisection definition supplies the missing total extension.
- A separate bad alternative for each seed is not a randomized lower bound;
  the shared-threshold event supplies a single seed-independent alternative.
- Internal protocol randomization is not empirical endpoint sampling noise.
- Conditional mathematical observation failure is not philosophical absence,
  physical unobservability, invalidity of an intervention, or source ownership.
- A scoped PASS does not authorize protected writes, integration, owner
  acceptance, release or T20 closure.
