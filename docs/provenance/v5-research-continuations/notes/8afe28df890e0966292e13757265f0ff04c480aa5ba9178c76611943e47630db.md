## Positive contribution and anti-skeptical scope

The argument properly starts from an actual, genuinely warranted correction and distinguishes what makes its content true, what warrants the subject, and what supplies the exercise's actuality. The positive explanatory demand is that an account of knowledge retain the apt target/evidence/subject relation rather than merely provide three coexisting items. The source route then motivates original possession of received efficacy while leaving completion, necessity, unity and source cognition as distinct further warrants.

This is philosophically substantive without being a new empirical demonstration. The admitted thick case cannot independently prove every condition it already includes. Likewise, the incompatibility between warrant and a condition specified to defeat that warrant is an analytic constraint. The revised packet explicitly withdraws any suggestion that this alone constitutes a new actual-world defeat.

The Koons–Pruss assessment is fair. Their anti-skeptical argument distinguishes scenarios from possible worlds and centrally concerns knowing that we know. Its generalization needs more than failure to explain an ultimate basis: cross-kind transfer, relevant closeness/exposure, certification scope and a collective explanatory step are load-bearing. The paper's conditional concession to neo-Humeanism and its subsequent challenge are not collapsed into either blanket acceptance or dismissal. The candidate also distinguishes safety failures, possessed/accessible defeaters and second-order uncertainty; mere unexcluded metaphysical alternatives do not automatically destroy first-order knowledge on every account.

The powers rival is strengthened symmetrically. Intrinsic local powers do not by themselves forbid unrelated misleading origins. A principled closure account is therefore an additional rival obligation, not a free consequence of a disposition. Yet demanding that obligation does not prove counterfeits possible or close. A developed necessary impersonal source may concede necessity/common sourcehood while contesting cognitive priority; it should not be treated as denying endpoints it grants.

The disappearance, caused-counterfeit and local-power-without-closure controls sharpen different joints. Absence of the whole basis with no subject is not itself a false-belief alternative; complete causal ancestry can coexist with deception; intrinsic receptor behavior does not settle every input's origin. These are relevant premise separations, not possibility certificates.

## Intrinsic acts and full productive scope

The supplement identifies a valuable distinction: unborrowed from other agents is not autonomous from one's own bearer and power. A source may really exercise its own efficacy without a second manufacturing act that creates that exercise. Ultimate productive provision also differs from a true explanation written by a subject.

For a genuinely activity-inclusive field, an individually complete source cannot leave an independent producer's actual unborrowed efficacy outside its provision. With the retained productive witness and proper-unborrowed-activity condition, the uniqueness argument follows. There is a positive rationale: omitting an actual productive exercise from a promise to explain all actual productive efficacy misses the very explanandum. It is therefore more than an unexplained uniqueness slogan.

Its scope cannot be obtained by moving labels. If the previously fixed domain D contains only externally received beings, the fact that an intrinsic exercise depends on its bearer does not put it in D in that same productive respect. The frozen packet explicitly preserves that limit. Compatibility with the Athari account of uncreated intrinsic acts is not an independent proof that the actual existential inquiry has earned the expanded domain or an individually complete bearer over it.

The narrower effect-side reception-mode argument remains meaningful: a real m_B can depend productively on B's exercise even when the bare endpoint survives without B. Universal dependent-field coverage plus productive-prerequisite closure then tests the source claim. Those two warrants are not supplied by every logical or relational prerequisite. A primitive-relation rival need not concede a new reified object simply because real production is admitted. The packet fairly identifies that reply rather than equating nonreification with denial of causal reality.

This whole-token/mode route already appears in the recovered third-tranche work. The frozen candidate credits it accordingly. The new contribution is source/typing stress-testing and direct primary context, not rediscovery of the retained proof.

## Primary evidence

Fresh primary inspection confirms the relevant distinctions. Aṣfahāniyya explicitly warns that a bare necessary-existent result can be granted without the intended distinct creator, then gives an intrinsic-sufficiency argument. Hume's material-necessity and member/whole objections in Dialogues IX are voiced by Cleanthes. Bird's essential properties are a serious powers resource, not a proof of one instantiated cosmic bearer. Dasgupta's autonomous/substantive distinction concerns grounding, not efficient production; Kleinschmidt defends explanatory power as a defeasible virtue, not a direct reply to every later PSR argument.

MF8:19–20 genuinely distinguishes intrinsic/transitive acts and treats acts as within power. The candidate does not translate maqdur as created or attribute all reported schools to Ibn Taymiyya. MF16:364–368 separates creating from creature, source determination from external completer, and standing type from particular acts. These support an intrinsic-support interpretation, not a new universal grounding ontology.

Suárez XXVI.4.9 and .13–16 allow the relevant absolute-possibility treatment of distinct complete actions and one numerical endpoint, and distinguish actual reception from indispensability. Crucially, .17–20 then deny ordinary natural simultaneous complete production under agents left to their natures. Divine concurrence is present in the permissive example. XX.4.16 distinguishes mode from endpoint asymmetrically. The final packet retains these guards and does not attribute two ultimate creators to Suárez or identify his effect-side mode with an Athari intrinsic act.

This is electronic primary-text/PDF verification, not critical-edition collation, universal scholarly consensus or full-system endorsement. The direct Latin check improves attribution custody; it does not independently validate the metaphysical possibility asserted by a historical author.
