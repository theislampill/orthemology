# Epistemic conditioning and original-peer production

Bounded independent source-critical review, 8 October 2026. No integration, new model, first-reading claim, or metaphysical closure.

## Verdict

**The passage licenses a distinction that a productive analogy must respect; it does not license the productive analogy's conclusion.** Its authorial counterfactual goes beyond the actual self-created-object case: knowing the posited object remains better than failing to know it even when its existence is supposed to be through another. Consequently, the author's argument cannot be reconstructed as an unrestricted equation of object-conditioned perfection with foreign receipt. But neither the admissibility of independently original peers nor the perfection and original adequacy of their joint production follows.

This is stronger than saying that the passage concerns only God's own created objects, and weaker than a positive original-peer counterexample to the actual-efficacy norm. The conditional may suspend the author's own stated ontology; its evaluation is not a certificate that its antecedent is metaphysically possible.

## 2. Five claims that must remain separate

1. **Efficient receipt:** B produces A's power, knowing act, or intrinsic exercise. Knowledge of B does not establish this direction of causation.
2. **Constitutive/perfection-relevant completion:** independently original B supplies part of what makes A's undertaking actually adequate. This is the strongest affirmative concern and need not entail a new intrinsic entity donated to A.
3. **Object specification:** the object supplies what this cognition is about and the conditions for its truth. An object's being necessary for knowledge-of-it does not, by itself, make it an efficient donor or establish the stronger completion claim.
4. **Primitive joint manifestation:** A and B exercise already operative, unreceived powers, and both genuinely contribute to the resulting created manifestation. The rival expressly proposes this, rather than reciprocal creation of its agents' powers or an unmentioned mediator. Whether that manifestation is fully appropriate original efficacy remains contested.
5. **Complete original provision:** every relevant originally productive resource of the undertaking is attributable to the same original. This can include genuinely efficacious created means originally supplied by that source. It is stronger than truthful cognition, intrinsic nonreceipt, successful local choice, or solo achievement of the same output.

L1049–1053 directly discriminate (1) from (3). They do not erase (2), identify (4) with (3), or establish (5) for either member of a pair.

## 3. The strongest arguments survive on both sides

The affirmative argument in `complete-efficacy-premise/RESULT.md` §§3.2–3.3 has a substantive rationale: perfection includes efficacy actually achieved, and original self-sufficiency should reach the original resources making that achievement adequate. A peer contributing original productive sufficiency can violate this norm without depositing a power-property in A. The reviewed knowledge passage does not rebut that narrower claim. Calling all relational success merely a truth-condition would evade it.

The actual rival nevertheless remains primitive joint manifestation, not borrowed readiness. Its ordinary diagonal undertaking succeeds with the same intrinsic activity when the peer is suppressed, while the joint occurrence genuinely has both roots and no individual entire provider. Same-output solo success answers a specified output need; it does not make the joint event wholly provided by A. Conversely, the two-root provenance alone does not settle that A has received a constitutive pure perfection. The affirmative bridge must classify this particular adequacy, rather than derive its classification from the disputed label.

The wider relational-participation end also remains possible as proposed intentional content. Solo output then fails that richer end. But knowing an already given object to which intrinsic cognition is appropriately directed is not yet a source warrant for valuing another **independently original** productive participant as an eligible pure end. The epistemic counterfactual provides no determinate cooperation reason, no exchange-invariance norm, and no grounding of a particular original-peer plan. The conditional means result in `wise-determination/REPORT.md` remains useful at its stated scope.

The transport fails at a definite location: it moves from **what an intrinsically sufficient cognition can appropriately be about** to **whose productive resources make an original undertaking sufficiently actual**. These need not have the same perfection norm. That does not prove no defended analogy is possible; it identifies what an adequate analogy still has to justify. No source-neutral demonstration is demanded as a precondition for crediting the source's own explanatory argument.

## 4. Exact changed knowledge and residual

**Changed or sharpened:** the author's response is not exhausted by L1051's own-created-object defense. L1052 supplies a conditional evaluative extension, and L1053 specifies its fixed-object comparison. This sharpens the boundary of the perfection norm beyond the earlier ledger's compressed summary. It rules out attributing a blanket object-condition-to-foreign-receipt inference to this passage. It does not supply an admissible alien-original world.

**Unchanged:** actual productive efficacy remains a genuine perfection; no-donation graphs are insufficient rebuttals; the rival must justify its original adequacy and eligible joint end; the affirmative account must defend applying exhaustive self-provision to this particular joint manifestation. The source's nonabsorption and whole-original-completion arguments remain substantive, disputed intermediate claims, not mere labels or newly secured consequences of this epistemic passage.

**Scope discipline:** no `Emergent → Created` shortcut is licensed. Newly realized knowing or efficacy, or a newly applicable relation, does not by that fact show that its bearer is created or that its perfection was donated from outside. Nothing here turns a primitive joint law into a created mediator, proves exhaustive created-effect coverage, or turns distinct jointly productive roots into one complete original provider. Nor does the absence of an emitted mediator prove the rival's metaphysical acceptability. This is a scope prohibition, not a claim about a named formal theorem.
