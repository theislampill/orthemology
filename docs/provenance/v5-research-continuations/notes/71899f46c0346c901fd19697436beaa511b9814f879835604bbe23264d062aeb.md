# What identical observations can and cannot settle

## Scope and classical status

This is a short classical consequence of finite intersections of almost-sure
events and absolute continuity. It is included as an explanatory crosswalk,
not as a new learning theorem or a proof of source authority. The Lean sources
are `lean/CommonObservationBoundary.lean` and its successor
`lean/CommonObservationFiniteActions.lean`; author compilation succeeds with
only propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound. Independent review passed in `../common-observation-audit/`; the accepted audit
archive SHA256 is
`401a41b34e9e5086f6946c9c9f0c26f74dc66a033505f13c887cf113d49fd5c5`.

## Exact full-path statement

Let a finite family of candidate standards have target sets G_theta in an
arbitrary action space A. On a common measurable path space, one output map
X(omega,n) describes the controller's action at time n. Let nu_theta be its
full observed-path law under standard theta. Suppose every nu_theta dominates
one nonzero measure mu, and the controller is nu_theta-almost surely eventually
in G_theta for every theta. Then the intersection of all G_theta is nonempty.

Indeed, each eventual-success event has full mu measure by absolute continuity.
There are only finitely many such events, so their intersection has full mu
measure and contains a path. On that path finitely many eventual conditions
hold simultaneously from some time onward. Any action after that time belongs
to every target. Neither finiteness of the action space nor measurable selection
is needed in this argument.

The especially transparent case is nu_theta=mu for all theta: identical complete
observations cannot support incompatible eventual requirements. Conversely, a
common accepted action gives a constant successful policy under arbitrary laws.
No identification of the true standard is needed if their targets overlap.

## Which direction of domination matters

The premise is mu << nu_theta for one NONZERO mu and every theta. It is not
nu_theta << mu. Two disjoint Dirac laws are both dominated by their mixture and
can reveal opposite targets exactly. Nor does domination of every finite
observation prefix imply domination of the full infinite observed law: infinite
iid Bernoulli laws with distinct parameters supply the familiar singular limit.
The finite-query theorem earns its transfer on the observed finite-query event;
it must not be replaced by an unrestricted infinite-law assertion.

Finiteness of the target family also matters when actions may be infinite.
Take standards theta in the natural numbers and G_theta={a : theta<=a}. The
single deterministic action stream X_n=n is eventually in every fixed G_theta,
but no one natural number belongs to all targets. This exact deletion control
is included in Lean. It does not say that every infinite-family variant fails;
a finite action alphabet rescues an arbitrary candidate family. If no action
is commonly accepted, choose one rejecting standard for each action. This
finite family already has empty intersection, contradicting the proved
finite-family theorem. The successor formalises this reduction and an
either-finite wrapper. Thus the counterexample requires both the actions
and the candidate standards to be infinite, under the stated domination
contract.

## Relation to source authentication

Treating two standards as candidates represents uncertainty about which one
has authority; it does not assert that both are authoritative. If the only
evidence available to an application has the same law under both candidates,
and their permissible outcomes are disjoint, no use of that same evidence can
satisfy both eventual requirements. Additional computation or duplicated
observations does not remove the ambiguity. An independent source-authentication
premise, informative acquisition or change in the target contract is required.

This is a conditional epistemic boundary. It proves neither that the actual
norm is inaccessible nor that it lacks a ground. It does not move from an
observational obstruction to an impersonal or intellectual metaphysical source.
Those conclusions require their own bridge premises.

## Compact closed-target successor

`../compact-observation/` replaces finite multiplicity by closed target sets
and eventual reference-law confinement to a compact action region. The same
nonzero full-path reference must be dominated by every candidate law. For each
finite target subfamily the earlier argument supplies a common point; compactness
then gives one point accepted by the whole family. Continuous score sublevels
and finite truth-hull quadratic repair are checked specialisations.

This is an existence conclusion. With targets [0,1/(theta+1)] and output
1/(n+1), each fixed target is eventually met, but their sole common point 0 is
never output. The independently checked successor does not claim convergence
to a computably selected common repair or a simultaneous all-target tail. It
also concerns fixed targets, not a changing sequence of tolerances or forecasts.
