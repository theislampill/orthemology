# Executable guarded exact-support tables

Construct a genuinely executable Lean finite candidate enumerator and numeric exact-support compiler, then prove equality to the unchanged classical guarded saturation recurrence. Executability requires explicit finite rule/judgement enumeration, decidable judgement/evidence equality, and a decision procedure for each fixed ordered guard. Rational occurrence-additive costs, exact supports, nullary rules, unsupported cycles, and position-sensitive/nonmonotone guards are preserved. No support-subset pruning.

Bounded-round correspondence allows arbitrary rational prices. Global minima and stabilization transfer only with nonnegative prices. The ambient evidence type may remain infinite because the grammar-local support is finite.

The new construction is the executable representation and correspondence, not discovery of the predecessor height/minimum mathematics. No Python correspondence, executable proof-tree reconstruction, efficiency claim, semantic/source admission warrant, integration, owner closure, or retrospective timing claim is intended.
