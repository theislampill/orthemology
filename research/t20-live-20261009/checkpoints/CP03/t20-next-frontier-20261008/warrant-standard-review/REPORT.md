# Same-standard appraisal: a defeasible common-source judgement

8 October 2026 UTC. A new philosophical appraisal, preserving the earlier eligibility review and historical reports. No repository change, owner acceptance, or research closure is implied.

## Judgement

**Under the expressly adopted T17 account of original actuality, necessary existence, essential independence and eligible original perfection, I now judge the cumulative case sufficient for defeasible acceptance of one actual complete common original source of the relevant connected created order.** I would state that judgement as provisional belief, rather than leaving it at an unspecified preference merely because the strongest coactive rival has not been proved impossible.

This is not a deduction of common Creatorhood from bare necessity, an acceptance of every source claim, or an independently established universal impossibility of plural originals. In particular, I do not take the stronger norm that *every possible original must ground every actual external bearer* to have been demonstrated solely by the comparisons inspected today. Its domain and purity still require distinct appraisal.

The field restriction is essential: this appraisal does not infer numerical uniqueness beyond the relevant connected created order, or silently include unrelated domains and idle putative bearers. Relevant connectedness means actual productive interconnection, not mere correlation, temporal adjacency or shared model notation.

The positive verdict concerns the actual explanatory hypothesis: the same source provides the original basis throughout that relevant connected created field, with real created mediation, rather than irreducibly foreign original contributions completing that basis. Its warrant is cumulative and defeasible. The stronger universal exclusion would rule out every appropriately specified plural-original alternative as such. Failure to establish that stronger exclusion is not by itself a reason to withhold the actual-world judgement.

The historical T17 withholding was not simply a mistake about standing ability. It expressly granted that ability and gave a substantive objection about actual co-completion. Today's nonreceptivity correction therefore does not automatically overturn it. My changed appraisal comes from reconsidering the positive actual-efficacy argument and explanatory comparison at the same threshold used for the other accepted metaphysical principles. The strongest rival earns important qualifications; it has not earned parity of warrant with the common-source account.

## 2. The common evidential standard

T17 P00020–P00024 sets a clear standard. A rational principle can support belief without first being derived from a vocabulary neutral between every metaphysical school. It must still have intelligible grounding, an articulated connection to the conclusion, and an answer to the relevant objection. A coherent contrary diagram can expose a deductive gap; it does not by itself establish metaphysical possibility, equal credibility, or defeat stronger reasons.

Applied consistently, this standard permits three different outcomes:

- a valid conditional inference whose substantive premises remain unaccepted;
- a sufficiently supported defeasible belief despite unresolved sophisticated dissent;
- a judgement that the articulated positive reasons are still insufficient, for an identified substantive reason.

It does not permit moving automatically from the first outcome to the third. Nor does it force the second simply because a source calls a principle evident. The present question is which outcome the actual comparative reasons support.

I find a real risk of standard drift in treating “no independent derivation from thinner nonreceipt premises” or “the refined rival has not been universally excluded” as the stopping reason for withholding. Neither is the standard T17 applied to contingency-as-need, constitutive existential independence, broader standing power, or exceptionless source-owned veracity. The retained reports often explicitly disclaim that overly demanding standard, so I do **not** diagnose every earlier withholding as inconsistent. Their substantive reasons must be evaluated rather than replaced by that diagnosis.

The corrective rule is symmetrical: formal independence is neither an affirmative warrant nor an automatic philosophical veto. A basic comparison needs reasons, but it need not lose its status as a basic comparison by being deduced from weaker principles before it may count.

## 3. What was accepted in F1, and why that matters

### Original completion and the actual witness

T17 P00041–P00054 accepts the need for original existential actuality at the scope of the received field, followed by a qualified bearer-realization and same-respect support argument. A merely all-received history does not supply a positively different original basis simply because its local transactions are coherent and its equations have a solution.

The positive judgement is not that every all-received formal structure has been proved impossible. It is that such a structure has not answered the exhaustive existential question on the adopted conception of received actuality. Its structural intelligibility does not put it on an evidential footing equal to the original-completion account.

### Intrinsic necessity and essential independence

T17 P00027–P00036 accepts contingency-as-need: genuine existential openness lacks sufficiency through the reality itself. It preserves a critic who regards that connection as intelligible but insufficient. P00056–P00061 similarly accepts a constitutive judgement about the source of whole existential adequacy. It expressly does not claim to have refuted every identity theory permitting a different manner of existence for the same individual.

These are positive, content-sensitive metaphysical appraisals. It would be inconsistent to preserve them while requiring common-source reasoning to eliminate every thin interpretation before any affirmative judgement is allowed.

There is also an important limit to the analogy. Whole-existence constitution is not the same as the mode of a voluntary particular act. The received bearer cannot be presupposed independently of its whole actuality; an already actual bearer can have different activities. That is a genuine conceptual difference, and it prevents transporting F1's conclusion directly into universal actual source coverage. The unity case needs its own actual-agency rationale.

## 4. The strongest positive common-source case

The positive case is stronger than economy in the number of names.

**First, the original source must actually explain.** Its knowledge, power and willing are not merely unreceived items alongside an otherwise completed world. Its productive activity supplies actual created reality. The maker-preserver argument gives this requirement concrete content: original production must account for the relevant recipients, dispositions and continued actuality, rather than borrow them without acknowledgment.

**Second, independent efficacy is an excellence of exercised agency.** The superiority of a knowledgeable capable agent includes its effective realization of the intended good. An internally complete command whose success is made adequate by independently original resources is not automatically the same perfection as genuinely unborrowed adequate achievement. No new intrinsic object must be deposited into the agent for its actual efficacy to be externally completed in a relevant respect.

Akmaliyya L217–240 supplies the focused comparison: unaided ability, whole rather than assisted partial achievement, effective willing, and wise discrimination are all positively appraised. The wisdom qualification matters. The claim is about the quality and original sufficiency of actual agency, not maximal production at all times or indiscriminate control.

**Third, the accepted power account removes a familiar cost of unification.** This comparison is not between one arbitrarily underpowered local agent and several specialists needed to complete its deficiencies. Each proposed original is already credited with comprehensive eligible standing power, knowledge and wise agency. A single common source therefore need not acquire the peer's special powers to cover the same created order. Multiplying originals does not answer an independently established shortage in the proposed common source's resources.

**Fourth, a common source provides a unified account of the relevant integrated actual order.** Created differentiation and real secondary agency can be retained. The positive economy is an economy of original explanatory bases and their mode of productive realization. The plural account adds distinct underived bearers and an irreducibly plural actual realization without an independently evidenced need for that addition. The new symmetric-means rationale can make coordination intelligible, so the addition must not be called an unexplained accident. But a reasonable conditional coordination policy does not establish that the original basis of actual creation is plural.

**Fifth, the rival's strongest new features do not remove that comparison.** Primitive cognition, own full-Q intention and a stable nonlinear law are legitimate structural resources. The single-source account also uses primitive agency, so primitiveness alone is no objection. The residual additional commitment is narrower: two complete intrinsic original activities have one irreducibly joint manifestation, while that sharing neither enlarges the source-neutral created result nor diminishes or foreign-completes either participant's appropriate efficacy. That account can be stated coherently at a reduced signature. Its perfection and productive interpretation are still precisely the substantive matters for which positive warrant is needed.

The one-source account has unsupplied details too. It does not identify a complete actual causal inventory, exhibit a mechanistic reduction of primitive agency, or derive every particular wise determination. Requiring those from the rival while exempting the one-source account would be unfair. Neither side has an independently authenticated microscopic model of original production. Those common limits do not count only against the rival. The differential issue is whether the extra irreducible coalescent realization has enough positive justification to offset the common-source account's independently motivated ideal of adequate unborrowed achievement. Nor may the favored side simply label g a complete provider and count the label as the needed explanation of coverage.

Taken together, these reasons give the common-source account more than a head count advantage. It satisfies the adopted original-explanatory target through a source already independently credited with the requisite kinds of resources, and exemplifies a positively intelligible unborrowed mode of achievement. The rival's interpretation shows how to resist the necessity of that mode. It has not supplied a comparably strong reason to think the actual order has the more distributed original basis.

This is why I judge the reasons sufficient for defeasible acceptance at the disclosed standard, rather than requiring a universal nonabsorption theorem first. The upgrade is to warranted provisional belief at this field scope, not merely to a possibility claim. Responsible reporting should state the accepted background and the defeasible status; it should not announce that reason has necessitated assent, that a kernel proved actual Creatorhood, or that every alternative reality has been eliminated. The judgement would be weaker outside the accepted F1 and pure-perfection framework; those commitments are material premises, not background words whose force can be silently discarded.

## 5. The strongest objection, taken seriously

The refined rival separates three claims:

1. A receives its power, cognition or intrinsic act from B.
2. This actual production is irreducibly joint.
3. A cannot obtain its source-neutral goal or fulfill its own activity without B.

Its interpretation denies the first and third while affirming the second. It therefore does not concede intrinsic donation or ordinary failure of goal attainment. If A's undertaking is genuinely to perform its own wise act within a joint manifestation, successful performance need not be the frustration of a will to act alone. The source also permits fitting particular acts, delayed attainment of loved ends and good means. An argument that treats every newly realized relation as deficient would be overstrong.

This is a substantial objection. It explains why the affirmative argument is not an analytic consequence of bare nonreceipt, and why a simple “the effect uses B, therefore B supplies A's existence” inference fails. It also prevents invoking an impossible task such as A's creating B while B remains intrinsically uncreated.

The objection does not, however, establish the more relevant positive classification: that this irreducible sharing is compatible with the fully qualified original perfection at issue. Its own-activity specification can be a defensible end-to-means construction without becoming a full account of the adequacy of that agency. A model cannot settle that classification by defining an achieved own end and then treating achievement as the only possible measure of original excellence.

The objection thus has asymmetric effects on two targets. It blocks claiming a demonstrated universal exclusion from the presently defended thinner premises. It does not erase the cumulative positive reasons for the actual common-source explanation. I retain the objection as a live limitation, but I no longer treat its continued articulability as sufficient grounds for withholding all affirmative actual-world judgement.

That does not shift an impossible burden onto the critic. A critic may expose a genuine misclassification, an ineligible comparison, or an explanatory failure without building a complete alternative cosmology. T17 P00036 explicitly grants that right for F1. The point here is that the identified distinction has not yet shown such a failure in the *cumulative actual-world inference*. It establishes that the rival cannot be excluded by a particular shortcut.

## 6. Comparison with source-owned veracity

The veracity case is the closest consistency control, not a proof of unity by analogy.

T17 P00087–P00098 accepts exceptionless integrity in genuinely source-owned assertions. It does so while recognizing that a knowing liar retains knowledge and expressive power, that finite rescue cases can justify false speech, and that no theorem has shown a truthful substitute for every conceivable good. Its affirmative basis is a positive classification of deliberate counterfeit as a deficiency of that very informed declaration, together with the original practical-perfection account. An unspecified further-good proposal does not outweigh that basis at the adopted defeasible threshold.

The analogous unity point is that retaining a faculty does not by itself show an unimpaired exercise. Standing solo power does not by itself answer the source's actual-efficacy comparison. Nor does the optionality of cooperation show that every chosen original role is fitting. Actual relational efficacy can matter even without a new intrinsic entity being received.

The disanalogy also matters. An assertion presents its content as true, so knowing falsehood has an especially direct relation to that undertaking's constitutive norm. Joint production does not, simply by being joint, contradict its own stated aim. The unity argument therefore needs the further conception of *original* adequate achievement. That makes its warrant less immediate than the declaration-specific integrity argument. It is a reason for lower confidence, not automatically a reason for no warranted belief.

The cumulative actual-source case supplies that further reason through genuine explanatory self-sufficiency, actual effective agency and unification without loss of accepted capacities. I find it sufficient, while granting that another assessor can reasonably judge its threshold differently. This is the same kind of disclosed defeasible disagreement T17 retained for contingency-as-need and veracity; unanimity is not being demanded selectively.

## 7. Universal actual supremacy and the limit of this verdict

The new eligibility work restores a further source route. If every actual external bearer must depend originally on any eligible original, a second underived peer is excluded immediately. That would establish a stronger conclusion without needing local nonabsorption.

The source offers a rational comparison supporting this route, but several things must not be substituted for it: ordinary power over coherent tasks, original provision of every created effect, absence of external need, or production of every possible good. None alone establishes universal dependence by all actual external bearers. A proposed necessary peer is not a coherent object of being created *while remaining self-necessary*.

I count the supremacy comparison as additional philosophical support for the common-source conception, not as an independently completed universal theorem of eligible necessary existence. It shares the perfection framework with the actual-efficacy route; repeated presentation is not two statistically independent pieces of evidence.

The present sufficient-warrant judgement is therefore narrower than assent to every step of an impossibility proof. It supports the actual common original basis of the relevant connected created field. It does not claim to have demonstrated essential numerical uniqueness across all possible realities, or separately established that every putative idle or disconnected necessary being is impossible. Those stronger claims need their own scope-sensitive warrant. The distinction is substantive rather than a weakening of the actual common-source conclusion into “one source is merely possible.”

## 8. What countermodel evidence must now do

No complete rival cosmos is required merely to challenge an inference. But the evidential claims must match the work actually performed.

- To refute a deduction, a structure must preserve its exact relevant premises and falsify the conclusion. If it omits the actual-efficacy or supremacy norm, it establishes that those norms do work, not that their positive warrant is defeated.
- To undermine the current defeasible appraisal, a rival may show why the proposed perfection is not pure, why original explanatory adequacy need not favor individual completion, or why the plural interpretation has comparable or superior affirmative explanatory support. A conceptual argument can do this; code is neither required nor sufficient.
- To claim a genuine plural-original metaphysics, the account must warrant its necessity, essential independence, unrestricted relevant perfection, actual productive interpretation, wise particular determination and complete created inventory together. Bare labels and an empty receipt graph do not supply them.
- To establish equal credibility, formal satisfiability is insufficient. Conversely, a failure to establish a full rival does not by itself prove the favored account. The positive common-source reasons above remain indispensable.

Relevant defeaters would include a source-independent reason that irreducibly joint original realization is an equally adequate or superior expression of the same pure agency; an independently warranted good showing a cost in the proposed actual-autonomy comparison; or a defect in the accepted F1/perfection package that removes the common-source account's claimed resources. Merely proposing another equation or preserving an already accepted distinction would not settle the appraisal.

## 9. Reflexive conclusion and changes

The useful reflexive question is: **Which additional epistemic demand enters exactly where the desired conclusion is withheld, and was that demand imposed on the already accepted principles?** This question is substantive here. It reveals that absence of a deduction or continued availability of a thin rival cannot serve as a universal veto without undermining the stated treatment of F1, standing power and veracity as well.

The same reflexive discipline also prevents affirming unity because it is source-native, because a critic has not supplied a complete cosmology, or because the owner favors it. It supplies no diagnosis of anyone's motives, rational faculties or disposition. The conclusion is an appraisal of reasons and their targets.

Changes made by this review:

- I affirm sufficient defeasible warrant for the actual common-source hypothesis over the relevant connected created field under the stated accepted background, rather than leaving its status at an unspecified preference awaiting universal rival exclusion.
- I retain the refined rival's structural gains and the genuine distinction between goal success, actual shared provision and receipt of intrinsic attributes.
- I retain withholding of a demonstrated universal plural-original impossibility from the thinner premises and of an independently established unrestricted supremacy norm.
- I correct no historical verdict retroactively. T17's narrower withholding and T19's affirmative source-mode appraisal remain what their texts say. This is a new assessment after the stronger rival and today's source corrections.
- I create no new formal model, source witness, doctrine of epistemic certainty, owner acceptance, or closure claim.

This is the sense in which the standard is now applied consistently: positive rational warrant is allowed to support a determinate but defeasible actual-world conclusion, while the stronger inference and application burdens remain visible.
