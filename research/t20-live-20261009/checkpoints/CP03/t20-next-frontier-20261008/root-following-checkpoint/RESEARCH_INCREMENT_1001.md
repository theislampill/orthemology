# Discriminating increments after the second checkpoint scope

8 October 2026 UTC. This note records new work and its limits; it is not a stopping or acceptance case.

## Statistical recovery

The new finite-catalogue proposal gives an explicit finite-observation contract, rather than relying only on exact population probabilities. Lagrange interpolation produces signed count contrasts whose expectations are component weights; their exact finite ranges give concentration bounds. A Bernstein derivative bound gives an explicit calibration allowance. The three-code example exposes the practical price: even a deliberately conservative successful guarantee uses40,000 independent pairs and tightly bounded calibration error. Those are derived sufficient requirements, not observations that were collected or an optimal resource estimate.

The unknown-label obstruction remains different: adding a sufficiently rare new route changes every latent histogram while making finite grouped laws arbitrarily close. Internal component separation and a positive weight floor alone do not isolate the discrete labels. This is a reason a finite catalogue or another justified label-isolation premise matters, rather than a generic warning that numerical estimates are noisy.

## Recognition and warrant

The newly read fiṭrah material makes a genuinely qualified epistemic proposal, not merely a popularity argument. It allows error, development and impediments. A criticism that rejects every natural inclination as untrustworthy would miss that qualification. The remaining classification question is whether a given presentation is a truth-apt cognitive recognition, a desire, an agency interpretation, or a mixed event. Common causal origin alone does not settle this distinction.

The direct Dar return also separates teacher independence from nondiscursive recognition, and general existential inference from acquaintance with a particular. Its correction of retrospective belief histories is important independently of its stronger semantic argument: a later proof can explain or reinforce knowledge without having generated it. Newly fixed reference through a unique description remains a substantive rival to a universal prior-acquaintance premise, not a completed refutation of the whole source account.

Prima facie rational standing, factive knowledge and scientifically validated reliability remain separate claims. Affirming that a basic Creator belief can be rational must not silently replace the stronger claim of actual knowledgeable recognition. Ordinary epistemic appraisal need not await a complete psychological mechanism, but empirical response tendencies alone cannot supply truth or warrant.
