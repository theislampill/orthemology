## M01
<!-- charge M01 start -->
Yannic Kappes, [Self-Explanation and Empty-Base Explanation](https://doi.org/10.1017/apa.2021.8) (2022), §§5–7.

Kappes distinguishes explanatory bases from links and explores explanations lacking a base, including proposed identity of result and link. Explanation and epistemic justification remain separate; the needed identities are disputed. No contingent cosmic realization is established.

Our fourth construction proposes that C's existential fact has an explanatory status without a prior input. It offers no prior efficient exercise, so subject-presupposition alone misses it. Yet its actual status and explanatory force require warrant. Rebranding contingent existence as a law supplies none.

The same test applies to g. Ontic identity of essence and existence does not establish identity between an existence fact and an explanatory-status fact. Shared bearer or truthmaker is insufficient. This is our safeguard when comparing the primary passage with the modern proposal, not a claim that the primary author accepted this fact ontology.
