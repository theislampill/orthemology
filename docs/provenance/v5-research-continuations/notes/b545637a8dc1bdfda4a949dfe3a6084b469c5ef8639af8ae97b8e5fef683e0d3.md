## 1. Exact experiment and claim

Fix 0<ε≤1/4 and an arbitrary measurable seed space with a fixed probability law ν. The latent parameter α is drawn once; the seed is independent of α and of the entire uniform innovation stream. Bernoulli receipts are the thresholded innovations, with conditional parameter α. A policy at positive report index n may use the entire ordered Boolean prefix of length n and the seed, and returns a real report. It cannot read α. For each n and word its seed map is measurable.

Literal acceptance is precisely

- D0(α,ε,q)=q²−1/4−αε(1+ε)≤0
- D1(α,ε,q)=(1−q)²−1/4+αε(1−ε)≤0

The random count is the nonnegative sum of the corresponding failure indicators over n=1,2,… . The report clock, both inequalities, and ε are identical on both positive sides and on every negative mixture. No changed tolerance or surrogate identification loss occurs in the terminal result.

The formal `FiniteBudget` predicate supplies a common measurable report family, with all outputs in [0,1], whose actual expected count is finite. The negative theorem quantifies over all measurable real reports, a strictly larger class. Probability of each prior and of mixtures with 0≤θ≤1 is separately proved by `minus_probability`, `plus_probability`, and `mixture_probability`; it is not included implicitly in the definition of `FiniteBudget`.

## 2. The finite endpoints and normalization

Let a_i>0, a_(i+1)≤a_i/2, (1+ε)a_i≤1/2, and let

Z=Σ_i a_i,  S=Σ_i r_i log(1/r_i)<∞,  r_i=a_(i+1)/a_i.

Half separation implies 0<Z<∞. Set b_i^−=(1−ε)a_i and b_i^+=(1+ε)a_i. Both factors are positive. The side supports remain half separated, positive, and at most 1/2. Their own normalizers are exactly Z^−=(1−ε)Z and Z^+=(1+ε)Z, so b_i^−/Z^−=b_i^+/Z^+=a_i/Z. The spacing terms are unchanged under either scaling. Forgetting to rescale the normalizer would give total mass 1±ε; both independent algebraic and rational countercontrols guard this mistake.

Each side therefore satisfies the inherited p=1 sparse point-prior criterion. `GeneralPointTransport.actual_count_transport` establishes equality of the actual count integrals after transporting the finite-prefix policy. Its proof first proves pointwise equality of literal counts, uses the genuine pushforward of the uniform threshold stream to the Bernoulli product law, integrates the normalized atomic prior, and rearranges the independent seed product. It does not assume an unproved conditional-risk equality or substitute a parameter-dependent policy. The ordered-word encoding is injective; it retains positions rather than merely the number of ones.

The inherited coherent criterion supplies each endpoint separately. The source-bound quantitative ordinary budgets 256S/[(1−ε)Z] and 256S/[(1+ε)Z] follow from `SparsePriorAnalytic.integrated_comparison`, whose upper constant is 128(p+1), the exact optimal-count witness, and the normalization factor 1/Z^± at p=1. ## 3. Why every proper mixture fails

For 0<θ<1 set μ_θ=θμ^−+(1−θ)μ^+. A direct literal-polynomial argument establishes within-pair incompatibility. Write t=q−1/2. Acceptance at b_i^− requires

t+t²≤a_i ε(1−ε²),

whereas acceptance at b_i^+ requires

t−t²≥a_i ε(1−ε²).

Together they imply t²=0 and then 0≥a_i ε(1−ε²)>0. The independent Lean probe re-proves this without invoking the author's pair-incompatibility lemma. Strictly positive ε and a_i matter; the zero-tolerance countercontrol is preserved.

At the all-zero prefix of length n, a fixed seed gives one common report for both members of each pair. At least one member is missed. Its weighted zero-word probability is at least

min(θ,1−θ)(a_i/Z)(1−b_i^+)^n.

This inequality is simultaneous for all i. It sums component masses; it does not presume that a report misses all but one mixture atom. Cross-pair atoms can in fact share an accepted report at loose base separation. Both author and independent countercontrols record this.

Because all terms are nonnegative, Tonelli allows the sums over n≥1 and i to be exchanged. The exact positive-time geometric sum gives, for each i,

min(θ,1−θ)(a_i/Z)(1−b_i^+)/b_i^+
= min(θ,1−θ)(1−b_i^+)/[(1+ε)Z]
≥ 3 min(θ,1−θ)/(10Z)>0.

The conservative constant follows already from a_i≤1/2 and ε≤1/4. Infinitely many component contributions force infinite expectation. The fixed independent seed is integrated only after a pointwise-in-seed lower bound, so seed randomization cannot evade it.

The new Lean implementation uses an equivalent shorter route: it identifies the balanced pair prior as (μ^−+μ^+)/2, applies the accepted universal paired-prior lower theorem, and uses actual-experiment linearity to show that finiteness under any proper mixture would force both component costs of that same policy to be finite. Their balanced cost would then be finite, a contradiction. No subtraction of infinities, optimizer existence or exchange of infimum and limit is used.

Quantifiers are important: a policy may be redesigned for θ, and the impossibility still holds. Conversely θ=0 is μ^+ and θ=1 is μ^−, each finite-budget. The formal hypotheses are 0<θ and θ<1 in ENNReal; they exclude both endpoints and θ=∞. Independent Lean endpoint identities check the orientation.

## 4. Ordinary corollaries

The side supports are disjoint. Within a pair b_i^−<b_i^+; between consecutive pairs,

b_(i+1)^+≤(1+ε)a_i/2<(1−ε)a_i=b_i^−,

since ε≤1/4<1/3. Thus the two countably supported priors are mutually singular. Under the sup-event convention, TV(μ_θ,μ_φ)=|θ−φ|: the event consisting of the minus support achieves the lower bound, and the signed difference (θ−φ)(μ^−−μ^+) gives the upper bound. Every TV neighbourhood of either successful endpoint therefore contains unsuccessful proper mixtures.

For a fixed finite horizon N, conditional expected count as a function of α is measurable and lies in [0,N]. Hence each policy's two prior costs differ by at most N|θ−φ|. Taking the infimum over the same policy class in both directions proves |C_N(μ_θ)−C_N(μ_φ)|≤N|θ−φ|, without requiring Bayes attainment. The fact that the optimal lifetime cost is an infimum of prior-linear costs makes it concave in the extended nonnegative sense; it does not make its finite domain convex.

If a true side tag is supplied before reporting, one may run the successful minus policy on minus-tag runs and the successful plus policy on plus-tag runs. Their mixture expectation is finite. The tag is correlated with the latent side and changes the observation interface. It cannot be smuggled in as an independent seed. Choosing between policies with a genuine independent seed, switching after receipts, or constructing an unrelated full-prefix policy all remain covered by the universal negative theorem.
