# Qualitative grounding after *The Bare Necessities*

8 October 2026. Prospective, bounded T20 source appraisal. T20 remains ACTIVE. No protected-file change, integration, closure, historical time-floor certification, actual-world countermodel, or new theorem about original bearers is claimed.

## Result

The completed 2011 paper supplies a more specific positive proposal than the previously read modal discussion: an algebraic qualitative world-state **collectively grounds the complete plurality of individualistic facts, without separately grounding each individual-existence fact**. This is a genuine account of proposed grounding direction and explanatory form, not just primitive-material-individual elimination. It explicitly preserves regions and symmetric numerical plurality.

This strengthens the *specificity* of the qualitative rival and changes the question it must answer. A requirement that it exhibit a qualitative ground for an individual-existence fact taken alone would exclude its advertised solution by assuming the distributive form it rejects. But the paper labels its proposal a toy theory, delegates the fuller argument for plural grounding elsewhere, and does not identify an actual autonomous ground of the obtaining world-state. It therefore adds no established actual counterexample to productive Completion, restricted Realisation, or the unchanged original-bearer theorem.

The consequential mapping test has a bounded negative result. Plural grounding plus Necessitation licenses individual **conditional modal truths**, not individual **grounding claims**. A translation that changes the former plurality into a collection of singly grounded existence facts misrepresents the source. This is a source-level mapping obstruction, not a consistency proof for the entire ontology or a metaphysical countermodel manufactured by omitting an axiom.

The strongest affirmative response survives this test: the existing target type can encode a whole received field, and the bearer theorem needs only one received target. It does not require grounding to distribute. If Completion, Realisation and SupportTransport are independently warranted for that whole target, the theorem still applies. What remains disputed is whether the collective qualitative explanation supplies, requires, or excludes an actual relevant unreceived intrinsic resource. The source proves none of those alternatives.

## 2. The whole argument, without changing its target

### Grounding, rather than bare modal covariance, defines the issue

Sections 1–2 distinguish whether space exists from its dependence on matter. Both sides may accept existence while disagreeing about what explains spatial facts. Ground is introduced as noncausal metaphysical explanation. Officially it is a sentential operator; talk of a relation between facts is convenient shorthand, not an independent commitment to a realm of fact-objects (printed 118–121).

The author assumes transitivity, irreflexivity and Necessitation for the discussion, while note 10 treats the first two as disputable. Necessitation runs from ground to necessary connection, not back. A substantivalist can accept a necessitarian theology while maintaining that spatial facts ground material relations. The necessity of everything would not settle this explanatory direction (118–120, 127). These are compatibility illustrations, not assertions of the author's theology or a historically certified interpretation of Spinoza; note 4 expressly disclaims the latter.

For GTR the question is likewise how facts about the physical manifold relate explanatorily to other physical facts. The mathematical manifold, metric and mass-energy field in a model are distinguished from what they represent. Several field ontologies are deliberately left open (121–122, notes 18–19). No inference from a tensor's existence to an extramental bearer is licensed.

### The Hole Argument and its accepted replies do not settle this issue

Sections 3–4 describe the Hole Argument and two replies: revise the relevant conception of determinism, or deny the distinct shifted possibilities. Dasgupta regards such replies as legitimate. He corrects the argument's model-to-world formulation: choosing to let two models represent one world does not itself establish that other distinct possible worlds do not exist (124–128; notes 27, 29–31).

Anti-haecceitism, qualitative individuating roles and counterpart theories are discussed as modal options. They are **not all the author's endorsed metaphysics**, nor do they automatically yield qualitative grounding. In particular:

- A qualitative role may necessarily characterize a particular without explaining that particular's individualistic facts.
- Counterpart theory concerns how modal predication works. A thick substantivalist can accept such a theory while denying qualitative grounding.
- Dasgupta grants that some counterpart version can do the intended anti-shift work; he explicitly leaves uncertainty about whether Brighouse uses Lewis's exact theory (128, note 35).

The thin/thick distinction is about whether the individualistic manifold facts have qualitative grounds. Thin substantivalism yields the relevant anti-shift claim through Necessitation; thick substantivalism can append the same modal claim for other reasons (128–130). This is the source's central reason not to infer ontological priority from an extensional modal representation.

### The source demands a positive alternative and gives defeasible motivation

Section 5 insists on two tasks: articulate the proposed qualitative base and show how it explains the individualistic facts. Merely rejecting primitive identity, pointing to invariance, or asserting discernibility does neither (130–135). This is important support for demanding actual interpretive content from a rival; it is not a permission to demand one's preferred individualist ontology as an admission condition.

The author treats ordinary existential facts as naturally grounded in instances and says that declaring them fundamental needs a new interpretation of their logical structure (131–132, note 38). The later AG proposal answers by specifying a different fundamental language, not by simply declaring ordinary PL existential facts basic.

Sections 6–9 supply the positive motivation: parallel Occamist arguments from physical redundancy and undetectability, conditional on the relevant physical laws being true and complete. The redundancy discussion corrects the overclaim that a feature makes no difference to any subsequent fact; absolute velocity affects later absolute velocities. The intended comparison concerns the truth of the laws and the relevant qualitative/observable structure (136–145).

The elimination preference is defeasible and comparative. It is not verificationism and does not eliminate theoretical entities merely because they are not seen directly (142). The nonmodal presentations use counterfactual or explanatory/causal interpretation of models, adapted to the interlocutor's commitments; the paper does not supply one universally accepted semantics of impossible antecedents (139–145). Note 52 separates why the equations are true from why they have the status of laws. Nothing here establishes an autonomous existential law.

Section 9 distinguishes reliable answers to available indexical questions from discrimination of the putative surplus structure. That distinction is useful beyond this task, but it does not provide an existence ground or a theorem that all individualistic knowledge fails (145–147).

## 3. The strongest positive proposal actually offered

### An algebraic qualitative base that preserves plurality

Sections 10–11 reject the local construction of each individual as an intrinsic-property bundle. The problem is not just conflict with a modal intuition: collapsing symmetric configurations discards qualitative structure the Occamist argument gave no reason to discard (147–149).

The offered alternative starts with simple qualitative properties and relations, including identity, and uses six property-forming operations. Its fundamental sentences assert that a constructed zero-place state obtains. The PL/AG correspondence supports representation of the qualitative systems expressible in the chosen first-order language (149–150). This repeats and develops the 2009 algebraic strategy; it is not a separate new physical experiment or an additional independent proof of generalism.

The displayed two-sphere example includes two distinct witnesses and reciprocal relational conditions. Its AG form is:

    cc(F¹ & pF¹ & R² & σR² & ~I²) obtains.

It distinguishes the two-sphere configuration from a one-sphere configuration. **The displayed existential statement alone does not say exactly two things exist or exclude all additional properties.** Notes 63 and 70 acknowledge the omitted totality clauses. No exact-two theorem is credited to that expression alone.

The source also intends to allow objects indiscernible even in the weaker qualitative sense under discussion (150). This must not be read as erasing equality or numerical plurality: identity and its negation remain in the language. Nor is a unique qualitative one-place role for every individual a requirement of this construction. There is a small vocabulary qualification here: to make the weak-indiscernibility claim nontrivial, one must read the relevant discernibility discussion as excluding the trivial use of inequality itself. If every identity-containing qualitative formula were admitted literally, inequality would weakly discern any two distinct objects. This interpretive qualification is not used as an argument against the ontology or in the mapping test.

### The new explanatory step is plural on the grounded side

At printed 151–152 the author changes the logical form of ground. His motivation is that an account separately grounding one person's existence in the complete qualitative cosmos has an unattractive explanatory consequence. His proposed response is collective explanation: the individualistic facts all together are qualitatively grounded, although no individualistic existence fact taken alone has a qualitative ground. Such a fact can therefore be nonfundamental without possessing a separate qualitative explanation.

This is not merely the familiar claim that **many grounds jointly explain one fact**. The consequential plurality is on the **grounded side**, and it is non-distributive. Nor is it merely a single conjunction for which ordinary conjunction elimination automatically yields singleton grounding. The source distinguishes the explanation relation from material implication throughout.

This proposal is more determinate than leaving ordinary individual discourse wholly unspecified. The 2009 appraisal correctly reported options left open in that paper; the 2011 paper now gives a specific realist, qualitative-first option with this explanatory form. Nevertheless the fuller argument for it is referred to another work (notes 71–73). The source's own brief motivation does not establish that joint explanation really resolves the dissatisfaction it diagnoses. A critic may reasonably ask why the collective account is adequate while the singleton question is treated differently; that critic must address the proposed plural form rather than simply restore distributivity by definition.

### Note 74 changes an important attribution

The ordinary PL existential facts are not the fundamental AG obtaining facts just because their representations are extensionally equivalent. In note 74, printed 158–159, Dasgupta expressly clarifies that ordinary PL facts may be grounded in individualistic facts in the usual direction. He acknowledges that using easier PL notation in the 2009 presentation misled some readers into thinking those PL facts were fundamental.

The result is a fine-grained explanatory distinction:

1. Fundamental qualitative AG obtaining.
2. The collectively grounded individualistic facts.
3. Ordinary existential PL truths that may be grounded in their individual instances.

These are not three new ontological objects introduced by this report; they are distinctions in the interpreted assertions and their proposed explanatory roles. Equal truth values or a translation preserving logical equivalence cannot collapse those roles. An extensional algebra that quotients away this distinction does not, without further structure, preserve the grounding explanation.

The inherited reports already distinguish AG primitives from simply declared fundamental existential sentences. Thus note 74 is a sharpened attribution and a useful constraint on later mapping, not proof that those reports made the opposite error. Likewise, the inherited adjudication did not derive Completion by a distributivity rule. Its residual request for a particular existence-grounding case should now allow an explicitly plural target and explanation; it should not insist without argument on singleton grounding as the only admissible form.

## 4. The one mapping test and the stronger affirmative reply

The full derivation audit is in `MAPPING_TEST.md`. Let Q say that the complete qualitative world-state obtains, let Y be the **complete** plurality of individualistic manifold facts, and let e_b be the member saying that b exists. The source proposes joint grounding G({Q},Y), while withholding G({Q},{e_b}).

Modified plural Necessitation yields that, at every world where Q obtains, every member of Y obtains. Hence it yields the conditional modal truth for e_b. This does not supply the prohibited singleton ground. The test retains fine-grained assertions even when their modal extensions agree; it does not replace Y by an extensional orbit, a selected pair, or a Boolean average.

Factivity is implicit in these actual explanatory assertions; the source explicitly assumes Necessitation, transitivity and irreflexivity, then modifies the grounding arity. Its original transitivity schema is stated in note 10 for singleton outputs; it does not fully axiomatize the new plural calculus. The audit therefore does **not** certify a model satisfying a complete author-endorsed plural calculus. It checks the exact failed projection against the actual proposal and explains why the stated transitivity does not supply it. No arbitrary relation is presented as a metaphysically possible world.

There are two additional breaks before productive Completion, even if a singleton explanation were available: an adequate metaphysical explanation is not already an actual unreceived concrete intrinsic resource, and metaphysical dependence is not already whole-existence reception. A qualitative ground would not, by its success, exclude a coexisting original producer either.

Crucially, **Completion itself does not impose singleton grounding or localist explanation**. Its target type is abstract, the same resource may serve multiple targets, and the original-bearer inference needs just one target with receivedness and Completion. A whole received field can be proposed as that target. Once the resource, bearer and transport premises are independently given for it, plural explanatory form supplies no objection to the theorem. The source has neither furnished nor defeated those premises for the whole-field interpretation. This is the strongest affirmative reply to the mapping obstruction and limits its importance.

The new finding is consequently narrower than a new central defeat of the affirmative inference. It prevents one misdescription of the rival, and prevents absence of a singleton ground from being counted immediately as brute, autonomous or original existence. The old actuality and category-application obligations remain.

## 5. Modal identity, necessity and autonomy

### What the source's necessitation conclusion means

Printed 152 claims agreement on the **individualistic facts**, not merely agreement that some indiscernible occupants fill an unlabelled structure. It is therefore not faithful to weaken this claim to a shared qualitative isomorphism class and then report the weaker claim as the author's success. Conversely, the AG/PL equivalence theorem by itself does not prove that stronger fact-specific modal consequence. The plural grounding thesis and Necessitation do the additional work.

The claim remains conditional on Q. The paper does not show that Q obtains at every metaphysical possibility, let alone that a particular region exists through its own essence or has unreceived productive power. Restricting to Q-worlds cannot certify intrinsic existence. If one independently supplied universal Q and an appropriate de re interpretation, one could obtain corresponding existential modal truths; originality would still require separate warrant.

Symmetry does not refute the proposal merely because no unique qualitative role singles out each sphere. Its strategy was designed to avoid requiring such local roles. Nor does that strategy manufacture a transworld identity atlas. The source offers no compositional modal semantics for AG, no construction of world-to-world counterpart maps, and no proof that a proposed correspondence means numerical identity.

Note 11 is particularly important: when fact-obtaining across worlds is not well defined, as in the counterpart setting it mentions, Necessitation should instead be stated schematically using the official sentential operator. One cannot import the same fact-domain/world interpretation into every discussed modal theory without argument.

### What changes in the modal adapter

Nothing in the full paper defeats the inherited adapter at its declared interpretation. Faithful charts and valuations preserve the selected extensional modal language. They still do not authenticate explanatory direction, fine-grained grounds or autonomy. The source provides a concrete reason these omissions matter, through the AG/PL distinction in note 74 and the non-distribution of §11.

No new holonomy or permutation construction is warranted. The paper does not supply actual correspondence data to feed that adapter. When a bearer domain is restricted, the inherited receipt Back condition and corresponding Realisation witness-lifting condition remain necessary at their stated scopes. Qualitative grounding provides no licence to delete suppliers, infer unreceivedness from an empty restricted edge set, or identify an invariant aggregate with one bearer.

### The targeted 2016 check prevents a false inconsistency

The 2016 PSR is written with a singular grounded variable, but note 9 (printed 413) explicitly acknowledges that facts can be grounded together although none has a ground separately, cites the plural-grounding work, and brackets the complication. Thus the singular notation is not evidence that the later paper repudiates the 2011 proposal.

Still, the paper does not there supply a complete plural PSR/autonomy calculus. A combined account needs to say which explanatory questions apply to individual facts, which apply collectively, and which facts or pluralities are autonomous. Absence of a singleton ground is not by itself the claim that its question is inapplicable, and therefore is not by itself autonomy in the 2016 sense. It is also not nonreceipt in the productive sense.

Note 44 (printed 415) remains a proposal that qualitative predicate-functor facts might occupy the essential explanatory role. The 2011 world-state construction adds no actual existence-entailing essence or proof of its autonomous status. Nor does it prove that this proposed synthesis is incoherent. The source-backed result is an articulated research option with distinct unfinished obligations.

## 6. Remaining boundaries and comparative appraisal

- **Not anti-theism:** the 2011 argument targets surplus manifold structure and the adequacy of substantivalism, not all beings or immaterial originals. Its theological examples illustrate compatibility, not disproof or adoption.
- **Not individual nonexistence:** printed 152 expressly retains regions. Qualitative-first explanation need not deny actual derived bearers. The global type B in the original bridge is not confined to primitive material individuals.
- **Not a full actual-GTR ontology:** §12 states that the finitary PL/AG result likely needs infinitary first-order and perhaps second-order extension. The source does not establish the entire actual manifold's representation in its toy language.
- **Not autonomy of obtaining:** Q's being the proposed most fundamental qualitative description is not a demonstrated autonomous explanation of why Q obtains. Relative priority and exemption from every further grounding question differ.
- **Not a productive resource:** a property, state, interpreted assertion, concrete intrinsic capacity, act kind, act token and created effect are not interchangeable. Naming a state as both resource and bearer supplies none of the intrinsic/reception warrant.
- **Particularized instances are not made qualitative by renaming:** notes 25 and 62 expressly exclude properties involving a particularized trope from the qualitative category at issue. This helps keep a concrete intrinsic instance distinct from a repeatable qualitative property, without establishing that every actual bearer is primitive or that every property must have the disputed individualist ground.
- **No vacuous necessity:** an actual world and actual Q must be given before the conditional modal result is used. An empty Q-world class, zero-world frame, empty individual domain or zero-place truth unit supplies no actual bearer. The displayed two-witness formula is false in an empty domain; it does not create actuality.

The source therefore materially improves **how the strongest qualitative rival is specified and evaluated**, while also placing a stronger limit on using it as a ready-made actual existential explanation. Its explanatory pluralism is a specific proposal unavailable from the earlier five-page screening. It is not independent confirmation of the same 2009 qualitative ontology, nor a warrant to stack multiple downward confidence updates simply because the same program appears in several papers.

The typed theorem is unchanged. Exhaustive application of productive Completion still needs a substantive argument, but this operation supplies no new actual counterexample. The whole-field version of the affirmative route remains open at exactly that premise-warrant level. No automatic parity, forced reversal, numerical confidence change, or immunity of the affirmative ontology is warranted.

The local operation ends with the whole-paper appraisal and the precise mapping obstruction. The cited 2014 *On the Plurality of Grounds* is a genuinely relevant lead **only for a bounded explanatory-form question**: what plural substitution/transitivity rules and account of individual-question adequacy defend the move from the collective ground to nonfundamental but singly ungrounded individual facts? That could determine whether the proposal survives the objection that collective explanation merely avoids the original existential question, and clarify a plural PSR reconstruction. It is not presently a lead to an actual autonomous obtaining essence: neither citation establishes that its body supplies one. If the next task is specifically to settle actuality/autonomy, reading 2014 solely to elaborate plural formalism risks broadening the theory without progressing that residual. No new source campaign is launched here.
