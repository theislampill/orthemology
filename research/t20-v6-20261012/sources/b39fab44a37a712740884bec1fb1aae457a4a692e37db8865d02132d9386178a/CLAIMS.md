# Accounting-bridge assessment: scope and credit

This selection follows the accepted assembly-attribution analysis without modifying it.

1. The positive target-relative reason for including an exercise indispensable to an actual receiving mode is inherited and retained. It does not establish an actual original source.
2. A proposed constitutive possession attribution must not be treated as an efficient donor relation without an argument. The reason against donated unborrowed power retains its proper application; a broader exclusive-subject interpretation needs its own warrant.
3. The inspected candidate does not establish the needed actual conjunction: distinct source-bearers, one same token proper exercise at the same productive respect, genuinely original possession, and full-origin productive accounting.
4. The application does not turn a shared power-kind, a power, an event, a receiving mode and a source-intrinsic exercise into one entity. It does not substitute a different counting relation for bearer identity.
5. No newly warranted actual P1/P2, original co-owner, necessary concrete participant, N2/N3, source attribute, disclosure or recipient-warrant result is claimed.
6. The accepted assembly conditional remains usable when its interpretation and premises are independently warranted. This assessment does not certify that interpretation for every constitution theory or prove composite sourcehood impossible.
7. The contribution is a source-grounded assessment of the exact accounting premise, not a new theorem, new owner criterion, metaphysical countermodel, or general hylomorphism result.

No new code, formal model, human study, external specialist validation, canonical edit, publication, or future-tranche work is included. Source inspection and argument acceptance have separate evidential scopes.
