## 3. What is already excluded: the exact uniqueness arguments

### 3.1 Act-accounting theorem

Fix the **same** target x, actual productive-mode interpretation, ultimate-accounting relation and unborrowed respect. The retained predicates are:

1. `HistoryComplete`: Complete(s,x) and ModeOf(x,m) imply Accounts(s,m).
2. `UnborrowedActs`: Proper(t,m) and Accounts(s,m) imply s=t. Proper concerns **proper unborrowed productive efficacy**, not merely psychological ownership of a thought or ordinary participation.
3. `ProductiveWitness`: an actual Complete(t,x) claim supplies some actual mode m of x properly exercised by t.

If A and B both meet Complete for x, choose B's witness m. A accounts for m by history completeness; unborrowedness identifies A with B. **Necessity, connectedness, worldwise well-foundedness, and an additional prohibition on two ordinary causes are absent from this proof.** [ACT L19–46; TOKEN §§4,7]

The witness is essential: two inactive objects can satisfy vacuous closure/exclusion statements. The scope is essential: endpoint-only sufficiency does not automatically account for another actually exercised mode. The act type is essential: if a finite agent owns a derivative thought, causing its bearer does not make the ultimate source the thinker. The retained exact `KnowledgeScope` interpretation expressly proves that promoting local derivative activity into Proper breaks UnborrowedActs. [ACT L63–76; KSCOPE L172–185]

The stronger universal-activity variant replaces target-specific closure by every admitted source accounting for **all actual productive modes**, plus each source's actual proper exercise. It excludes an active competing source even in an apparently disconnected domain. This does not derive universal scope from an observed connected sample or exclude every inactive necessary existent. [ACT L49–59; TOKEN §§8–9]

### 3.2 Local coverage and field unity

Let R be strict transitive productive ancestry, and `g ≤ x` mean equality or R(g,x). LocalCover(g,x) says g≤x and, for every y≤x, g≤y. A local cover is a root; two local covers of the **same target** coincide. This already excludes a two-root merger at that target. Connectedness is not the missing local uniqueness premise. [CONLEAN L10–58; CONNECTED §4]

For a nonempty field, local single-bearer coverage **at every node**, plus productive undirected connectedness, is equivalent to a least ancestor of the whole field. This is not a derivation of universal local coverage from a single corrective episode or from interconnection alone. A connectedness interpretation must be productive, and the declared field must include relevant predecessors to support absolute, rather than artificially relative, roothood. [CONLEAN L89–145; CONMAP P0–P4]

A unique root plus connectedness is insufficient on an infinite domain: a rootless incoming branch can feed the target alongside its unique rooted branch. For one target x, local coverage is equivalent to a unique root ancestor **and root support throughout x's ancestral cone**. The missing quantifier cannot be repaired with exhaustive finite tests, since finite strict cones conceal that absence. A covering root can nevertheless coexist with an infinite subordinate chain; no blanket ban on infinite structures follows. [CONNECTED §§5–7; CONLEAN L147–196; CONREVIEW formal-result section]

These graph results do not automatically instantiate the act-accounting theorem. Reflexive coverage of a singleton has no actual productive witness. A strict descendant is an ancestral witness, not yet a demonstrated proper unborrowed exercise. [CONNECTED §7; CONLEAN L198–207,254–269]

### 3.3 Strongest endpoint overdetermination and origin closure

The strongest retained comparator allows the **same numerical endpoint** x, distinct real exercises by A and B, each endpoint-sufficient, neither supplying the other's power. It need not be mere partial cooperation or a Boolean device. Nonetheless, endpoint sufficiency, even for every dependent endpoint, is weaker than individual completeness for **their whole actual origination**. [ORIGIN §§1,5,7]

The effect-side mode argument puts the real reception-from-B mode within the dependent target and identifies B's exercise as indispensable to that mode, even if redundant for x simpliciter. The later representation-neutral refinement does not require a separately reified mode: B's actual exercise belongs to the whole actual origination regardless of whether the ontology uses a mode-object or a primitive relation. A mode nominalist must still contest origin closure, actual unborrowedness, the meaning of ultimate provision, or individual completeness. Merely denying an extra object no longer answers the refined argument. [MODE §§2–6; ORIGIN §§3–5]

Conversely, replacing “x as actually receiving from B” with “x” inside an origin-sensitive dependency statement is not licensed by their numerical endpoint identity. Nor does an intrinsic act's external terminus make the act an externally created entity. The local original source can have intrinsic attributes and exercises without an external maker for each. [ORIGIN §§2–3; TOKEN §6]

**Exact verdict:** two sources that genuinely grant the complete actual-origin role are excluded under its retained premises. The endpoint-only rival remains a challenger to the **warrant and instantiation of that stronger role**, not a counterexample after it is granted. The positive completeness rationale is substantive and source-supported; it is not merely a renamed numeral “one.” Existence of a bearer meeting it remains a distinct burden.

## 5. Knowledge–Will–Power: the strongest local positive claim and its limits

### 5.1 Preserve the same owner's intentional exercise

The Third constructive route concerns the source's **own** selection: a reason is intrinsically understood, the exercise is guided by that understood reason, and the source's completed exercise is effective. Given those premises, that source has relevant cognition, intentional willing and actual efficacy. It is not an untyped assertion that one knower exists, another agent wills and a third power acts somewhere. [AGENCY3 §§2–3]

ASFAHANI printed p.39/PDF p.46 separates the route from volition through conception of the willed target, a teleological arrangement route, and the received-perfection route on pp.39–40. Printed p.41/PDF p.48 explicitly notices that “doer by choice” is a Will argument and does not by itself complete a separate Power proof. It then argues for the relevant power of the acting chooser. The source therefore supports positive same-subject act typing, but does not license importing intentionality from an arbitrary selector function.

**Local audit constraint:** once a rival grants g genuinely acts intentionally through its own apprehension and effective volition in this respect, it may not withhold every relevant cognitive/volitional/power predicate from g while retaining that act under the same meanings. If this g is independently the original source, the local predicates do belong to that source. Repeating the generic “global availability does not imply co-instantiation” control would miss the newly granted intentional-act premise.

The converse inflation is equally invalid: real cognitive recipients, a productive g and objective fittingness do not establish that g itself apprehends or intentionally acts. Even K(g)+Power(g) is weaker than cognition-guided efficacy: its cognition might be explanatorily idle. The Sixth integrated-original-giver principle is the defended bridge for that further claim, not a consequence of co-location. [ATTR §§4.1–4.2; ATTRMAP A0,W0; AGENCY3 §§4–5]

### 5.2 Apprehending a target is not knowing every asserted proposition

The source's conception-of-the-willed-object route concerns relevant intentional content. It does not alone establish truth of a future realized effect, omniscience, or truth of every proposition intentionally asserted. A person can intentionally assert a proposition they know false. Knowledge of the complete actual creative determination, known efficacy, and adequate epistemic closure separately yield knowledge of the determined effect. A chance law or thin standing nature does not fix a particular outcome. [ASFAHANI p.39; EFFECTK §§argument,efficacy,thin/thick tests]

The noninferential clarification controls interpretation: our entailment from complete self-Knowledge to effect-Knowledge is not a process by which the source acquires a previously absent cognition. [NONINFER §§exact clarification,formal effect]

### 5.3 The exact impersonal relative interpretation

`KnowledgeScope.lean` contains **one genuine joint formal interpretation** of its declared source schemas, genuine recipient cognition as semantic input, relational sanity and modal-union well-foundedness, with no source Knowledge or source thought ownership. It imports the unchanged HistoryComplete, UnborrowedActs and ProductiveWitness predicates. This establishes a source-cognition non-entailment for that specific package, not just a collection of unrelated countermodels. [KSCOPE L71–89,156–169]

It does **not** satisfy an independently warranted respect-specific original-giver cognitive priority or g's own intentional-exercise premise while denying their consequences. Neither those richer premises nor the full R5 strongest union belongs to its declared package. Its validity is relative interpretation, not a proof of impersonal metaphysical possibility. ## 7. T0 and restoration: prevent an illicit shortcut

T0 is role- and domain-specific: this identified source does not knowingly falsify an authoritative epistemic attestation in D. Actual address D1 and recipient warrant D2 remain separate. Mere disclosure capacity, factive source cognition and intentional control do not entail T0. [ATTRMAP T0,D0–D2]

The retained practical ascent has **five burdens**: relevant practical reasons for this source; eligibility of this very source for the excellence; original possession; comprehensive scope; essential governance. Some benevolence does not exclude mixed malice. An unneedy, fully informed source can be described as deliberately preferring a bad end without being wholly devoid of positive metaphysical resources. Pure-evil privation arguments or ignorance/need explanations therefore do not alone exclude it. [MOTIVE §§2–4,7]

A false attestation can be defective **as truthful testimony** even when psychologically successful as manipulation. Undertaking an epistemic role does not by itself prove that the speaker impeccably fulfills it. Conversely, if “authoritative attestation” is defined factively or as necessarily successful in giving Knowledge, the truth result may have been built into the premise. Seventh T0 must distinguish ordinary role/content identification from impeccable role performance.

For the action-integrity test, specify a proposition such as “root i is safe for this permitted intervention during this stated interval,” together with target/version, sender, actual message, interpretation, authorization, channel integrity and defeating evidence. Source knowledge of i's condition is not the recipient's evidence; truthfulness at one time is not persistence through a later corrupting event. A positive operational theorem consuming a truthful cooperative sender cannot be used to establish that sender's metaphysical truthfulness. [MOTIVE §6; ATTRMAP R0,T9; R5METHOD]
