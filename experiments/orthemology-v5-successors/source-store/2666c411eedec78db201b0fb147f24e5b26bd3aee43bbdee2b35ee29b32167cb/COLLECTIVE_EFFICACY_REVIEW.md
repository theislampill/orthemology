# Collective efficacy: focused supplementary review

3 October 2026 UTC. Contribution CE-1: source-neutral projection of the focused collective-efficacy challenge. This is internal model-assisted philosophical review, not external specialist validation, renewed acceptance of the underlying papers, or a metaphysical-possibility certificate. Its scope is distinct from the accompanying epistemic-standard review. The separately owned contribution CE-2 records the subsequent Arabic electronic-inspection check.

## Disposition

**Retain the accepted conclusion.** No overlooked, independently warranted bridge in the inspected inherited argument excludes the genuinely active non-well-founded account at the same explanation standard E used by the restricted transfer. The strongest affirmative argument remains substantive and has not been refuted. Its decisive extension is collective nonpresupposition for the declared existential target. That extension is not supplied by the admitted local no-self-production principle, nor by granting actual efficacy at each link.

The precise additional finding is a diagnosis of the strongest apparent shortcut: **lifting each act's dependence on its own bearer into dependence of the entire explanatory configuration on its entire inventory**, and then composing that lifted dependence with the proposed causal explanation. Both the lifting rule and the composition across dependence kinds require warrant at E. They cannot be recovered merely from local productive irreflexivity. This sharpens the already accepted boundary; it does not change its outcome.

There is no new P1/P2 or N2/N3 closure, no certified infinitist cosmos, and no R5/MEN-8 result. A warranted necessary participant would still deserve immediate credit before unity or full original-source identification.

## Exact target of the challenge

Let P be the declared existential inventory, and Q the rival's actual productive configuration. Preserve actual apprehension, intentional action, effective received powers, and the original evidence-sensitive correction. Q is offered as productive explanatory content for P; it is not just P with an explanatory label. For this challenge, grant the inventory's declared scope and set aside the separately recorded joint-possibility and witness-exhaustion objections. Otherwise those earlier objections could conceal the disputed collective step.

E must retain the same adequacy standard at possible explanation, actual transfer, and exclusion. E* is the stronger whole-origin standard. A test may defend target-sensitive requirements on E; it may not substitute E* only when rejecting the rival. This is the controlling distinction in F3, lines 38–46, and F4, lines 101–111.

## 1. What the original-possession argument actually establishes

The inherited six-step reconstruction is the relevant strongest argument, rather than a powerless-relay analogy: CE-H1 §3, lines 49–72. Its local premises retain genuine production by received powers. A donor that receives its own efficacy does not explain its own whole actuality merely by explaining its recipient. This is a real limitation of that donor's contribution. It does not make the contribution ineffective.

The affirmative's advantage is also real: an originally possessed productive nature supplies a positive mode of efficacy whose possession does not require another giver. It answers the question of received possession at a different explanatory level. That is more informative than naming an unexplained node. It is not, however, a derivation that every adequate E-explanation must include such a possession. The conclusion of the inherited reconstruction itself expressly retains the non-well-founded alternative as the point requiring adjudication; CE-H2, E3, lines 39–47, records the same limit.

For a received active power, distinguish:

- possession of a real intrinsic power by its present bearer;
- the source from which that possession was received;
- deployment of another's exercise as a merely instrumental channel.

The second does not erase the first, and does not by itself establish the third. An argument whose force depends on the third therefore needs an application premise. This is not a demand for a premise-free proof: it identifies the actual ontological commitment that would do the work. See CE-H3 §§3, 5–6, especially lines 47–53, 92–108; CE-H7, lines 37–46.

## 2. The strongest proposed exclusion, tested rather than merely requested

An individual act cannot be the complete efficient origin of the same bearer's actuality that makes that act possible. That is a sound local constraint at the specified productive scope. The temptation is to infer that Q cannot explain P because every exercise within Q presupposes some bearer counted in P.

Spell out the proposed inference. In the active chain, the existence and power of bearer b(n+1) support its exercise a(n+1), and that exercise productively explains the relevant actuality of b(n). Each local arrow is properly directed. No finite return to the same bearer follows. Adding bearer-to-act dependence does not turn the chain into a local self-production cycle.

To obtain a collective contradiction, one must add something like this:

Candidate principle (this review’s formulation): a configuration whose entire productive availability is supplied only by members of the existential inventory P cannot be a nonpresupposing E-explanation of P.

This is a **candidate general metaphysical principle**, not a definition used here to settle the question. It is not automatically equivalent to single-bearer P1: it can allow multiple independent contributors, non-well-founded subordinate ancestry, and contingent source exercises. It is therefore a stronger and more discriminating affirmative proposal than simply demanding a root by definition.

But the proposed justification needs two additional moves:

1. From each exercise's local bearer dependence to the whole configuration's dependence on the whole inventory in the relevant respect.
2. From that collective dependence to incompatibility with Q's explaining P under E, including a justified composition of bearer/constitutive dependence with productive explanation.

Neither is the local same-token no-go. Nor does the bare entailment Q implies P establish them. A successful actual productive account can entail its outcome without thereby illicitly presupposing it. Conversely, the fact that Q is a richer proposition than P does not make its explanation adequate. These are two different mistakes.

The substantive issue is **where existential presupposition occurs in the explanatory account**, not whether one can put all nodes inside a set. Saying that P as a whole must already be available before any member supplies another would impose the very global priority structure the rival contests. Saying only that each supplier must be actual is true and already satisfied by the proposed active architecture. The former is not obtained from the latter.

This is the strongest reason the present scope is correct. The disputed move remains necessary even after giving the affirmative real causation, strict productive priority, true bearer-dependence, and a genuinely existential target. The diagnosis therefore survives a steelman of both sides.

## 3. The inherited prerequisite bridge does not secretly close the gap

The best additional inherited candidate is the dependent-mode prerequisite argument, not the elementary ancestry theorem. Its scope is exact: a real reception mode requires the exercise through which that reception occurs; an independently complete source for that mode cannot leave its indispensable efficacy wholly outside its account. See CE-H5 §§2–4, lines 21–71, and §6, lines 97–107.

This has genuine affirmative force against two alleged independently complete origins. It does not establish that an E-explanation of an existential inventory is borne by an independently complete source. Applying the bridge to the rootless proposal merely brings each further received exercise into the required account. The stipulated Q already includes those actual exercises. To infer that this inclusion is necessarily inadequate is to invoke the collective principle just isolated, rather than to obtain it from prerequisite closure.

The target qualification is decisive. Covering the bare existence of a kind or bearer is not already covering every actual reception mode. If the latter target is warranted, the bridge becomes relevant; if not, importing it changes E's target. CE-H4 §6, lines 64–79, and CE-H1 §4, lines 85–89, already preserve that distinction. The connected-field theorem likewise starts with complete local coverage at every node; it does not derive those covers from actual connectedness or reception. See CE-H6 §§3–6, lines 33–41 and 54–88.

Thus this is an available substantive bridge with a different job, not a missed derivation of collective exclusion. Its positive content should remain intact.

## 4. Explanation of P, explanation of Q, and exemption

Three claims must remain separate:

1. Each local bearer has a genuine explanation.
2. Q E-explains P collectively.
3. Q itself has an explanation, or is categorically incapable of one.

The first does not establish the second. Failure of that entailment does not refute the second. The third does not establish either of the first two. In particular, an exemption for Q cannot repair a bad purported explanation of P.

The affirmative source account may offer an independently actual bearer whose efficacy is available for contingent exercises. The rootless account leaves every bearer's efficacy received. This is an important asymmetry, already retained in F3, lines 114–118. It is a reason to prefer the affirmative if the candidate collective principle is warranted, not evidence that the two accounts are automatically equal. Equally, the mere presence of contingency in Q cannot defeat the rival while a contingent source exercise remains an admissible explanans.

The same check defeats an overstrong non-agglomeration objection. Showing an explanatory shortfall in some collection rules out automatic assembly of local explanations. It does not show that this Q cannot satisfy E for this P. Rejecting collective self-explanation also requires care about whether Q and P are identical, or stand in the prohibited dependence relation. Neither is settled merely by the phrase “the collection explains itself.” F4 already locates these distinctions at lines 89–111.

## 5. Four distinct verdicts

**Genuine counterexample:** none supplied or certified. The active order is a discriminating rival description and a control on what weaker premises entail. It is not an established metaphysically possible world, an E-adequate explanation of P, or a rival preserving already-granted single-bearer P1/P2.

**Hidden premise if omitted:** a target-relative collective nonpresupposition/priority principle, with legitimate plural lifting and mixed-relation composition, or an independently defended strong instrumental account of every relevant received power. “Hidden” here describes an invalid compressed inference; the accepted papers already expose the dispute rather than concealing it.

**Stronger defensible argument:** defend the collective principle as a general account of existential productive explanation, rather than making completeness mean roothood. Its positive rationale is that explanatory supply should not collectively spend the entire existential availability it is supposed to account for. This is serious rational support and permits an affirmative basic-warrant defense. The stipulated active chain nonetheless shows why local possession/reception alone does not establish that collective dependence. No independently earned same-E application appears in the inspected materials.

**Merely restated burden:** “all are borrowed, so no power exists”; “no first node”; “the series is infinite”; “the totality has no source”; “every explanans must itself be explained”; or “Q is exempt.” Each either changes the active grant, repeats the disputed conclusion, strengthens the explanation demand without warrant, or leaves the adequacy of Q for P untouched.

## Final scope

Retain the accepted Ninth statement as a substantive open-warrant conclusion, not a defeat of affirmative metaphysics. The positive route is rationally contentful; the rival is not vindicated. The exact unclosed step is why productive reception excludes collective sufficiency at E, rather than merely failing to supply originally possessed efficacy by definition.

If that step and the route's independent target/possibility premises were established, a necessary concrete participant would be a real result without awaiting unity. Nothing in this review imposes P1/P2 or N2/N3 as the price of recognizing that intermediate achievement. No new formal model or canonical change is claimed. Subsequent primary electronic inspection is recorded separately in CE-2; it confirms this scope without amending the frozen report or accepted papers.
