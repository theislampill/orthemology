# Nubuwwat supplement to the earlier actual-common-source assessment

Dated Nubuwwat-stage assessment, preserved with its actual evidence boundary. The later Safadiyya study and final current appraisal are separate records; this text is not retrospectively revised into their verdict.

This is new evidence and reflection after the complete selected received Nubuwwat body was read. It does not edit the earlier standalone assessment or extend its original reading receipts. It does not claim a new full Miftah or Safadiyya reading by this assessor.

## What has genuinely been added

Nubuwwat gives a thicker positive model of an ultimate agent: knowing, willing, acting for fitting ends, teaching creatures, loving responsive goods, and distinguishing truthful guidance from deception. Its order-to-knowledge argument expressly includes the apt arrangement of means for ends. Its perfection argument is not solely a negative elimination of defects, and its account of temporal willing does not make every new act an externally supplied constitutional perfection.

That makes the one common knowing creator hypothesis more explanatory than a simpler numerical label. A unified source of order, intelligibility and truthful guidance would connect features that otherwise need a coordinated account. The book's contributions should therefore count positively in assessing that hypothesis; a disputed universal SourceMode principle does not cancel them.

The text nevertheless normally proceeds within the one-creator commitment when developing the account of divine guidance. Its criticisms of derivative causal regress, occasionalism and purposeless agency do not separately decide whether an original explanatory basis could be plural and necessarily harmonious. Created agents' genuine contributions and nonindependence are clarified, but a created-agent case cannot simply classify every conceivable original agent by analogy. The earlier actual/common versus universal/source-mode distinction remains necessary.

## Verdict under the same defeasible standard

On the source evidence actually assessed here, I continue to regard one actual common complete creator as positively favoured by an integrated explanatory picture, while withholding a judgement that this material has sufficiently discriminated complete numerical single-source authorship from the strongest principled plural-original alternative. This is an assessment of comparative support, not a deduction from the failure of a universal theorem. The new book strengthens the purposive-agent account and eliminates several weak portrayals of it; it does not independently complete the disputed unification step.

That verdict does not state that a plural basis is demonstrated possible, equally plausible or well explained. Necessary harmony needs an intelligible principle and cannot be a name for brute coincidence. But invoking one source also owes content: what capacities, purposes and ordering explain the observed coordination, and why is one complete numerical author better supported than a coordinated basis? Nubuwwat improves the content of the single-source model; it does less direct comparative work on the latter numerical question.

## A route that remains open independently of a universal SourceMode result

The book allows a person to recognise a trustworthy messenger, a message's truth or intentional certification before completing every speculative proof of divine metaphysics. That is philosophically legitimate in principle. The order of discovery and justification can differ across observers, and a later articulated proof can corroborate an earlier independently grounded recognition. No universal rule requires knowledge of a complete role-classification theorem before all testimony can carry warrant.

Accordingly, sufficiently warranted source-owned attestation of a specific claim about one common creator could contribute to that claim even while a universal source-mode theorem remained disputed. The relevant questions would be the identified source, truthful communicative intention, scope of commission, actual content and transmission. Those questions do not all collapse into numerical sourcehood. A human token's immediate authentication of 'he sent me' does not certify every later assertion, and the theological counterpart must preserve that scope.

The present reading finds an explicit source-owned account of those conditions, including origin/custody alternatives, purpose alternatives and warranted suspension. It does not yet supply an independently authenticated historical episode that this assessor has established as meeting them. Therefore this testimonial possibility neither closes the actual common-source question here nor is ruled out by the still-open metaphysical comparison. It remains a distinct substantive evidential route to investigate when the task reaches particular-source and particular-event warrant.

## Fair adverse and positive controls

- A merely possible counterstory is not automatically a defeater. The private-ring passage says suspension when the alternative arises and is strong; contextual knowledge can defeat it.
- Inability to certify by one proposed proof does not imply the claim is false or all other routes fail.
- The source's affirmation of known prophetic truth is not automatically an independent premise for every new audience. Its own discussion varies the required premises with what that audience knows.
- Reciprocity can be corroborative where there is independent evidential contact; repeating the same assumed authority in several forms adds no new ground.
- Generic divine causal production is not adopted assertion. The book says this explicitly and allows answers to distressed unbelievers and nonapproved providential agents.
- Attributed necessary knowledge is neither automatically transferable nor automatically worthless. The epistemic grounds, relevant competence and objective discrimination have to be considered.

## Boundary

The conclusion is limited to this complete received-body study and its specified print controls. No source-owned attestation episode has been declared established by an unseen external inquiry, no proof has been formalised, and the previous assessment's files have remained unchanged. Later Safadiyya and any independent historical investigation may add evidence.
