## Blocking findings

### BH-F01 — truth loss was treated as available rather than warranted

The graph theorem is conditional on an independently fixed, truth-linked,
scope-adequate `L`. It cannot establish the truth or authority of `L`.

### BH-F02 — the registry was static and complete by stipulation

Fundamental-cycle certification covers only the declared finite graph. Omitted
revision states or dynamically appearing edges can reintroduce holonomy or
miscalibration.

### BH-F03 — connectedness and component constants needed separation

For a disconnected graph the theorem is componentwise and the potential is
unique only up to one additive constant per component.

### BH-F04 — additive calibration was stronger than ordinal correction

Acyclic improvement admits a topological ranking without reproducing declared
cardinal increments. The theorem must not identify ordinal corrigibility with
additive truth-loss calibration.

### BH-F05 — proper-function nonentailment was under-typed

Arbitrary Boolean account expansions prove only that the current reduct lacks
the corresponding function-fixing relation. Once selected history, design,
teleology, or source purpose is added, an account-relative function may become
identified. The repair must state this exact ceiling.

### BH-F06 — success and reliability were at risk of proper-function promotion

Even perfect reduction of a true loss can be accidental relative to selected
effect, design, or purpose. Reliability is evidence only when linked to the
account-specific fixing relation.

### BH-F07 — selected effect and design needed truth-aim guards

Historical selection can fix a function that is not truth-conducive; design can
assign a role under an illegitimate or false target. Neither entails truth-linked
proper function without an independently warranted truth aim.

### BH-F08 — Plantingian and fitrah accounts were being treated too generically

Plantingian proper function includes a truth-aimed design plan and suitable
environment. A fitrah-oriented account is source- and school-relative and needs
authentication, translation, applicability, and world bridges. Neither follows
from the graph theorem.

### BH-F09 — anti-truth and unrelated-potential controls needed exact sign semantics

The repair must freeze the convention `d(u,v)=L(u)-L(v)` and keep exact but
anti-truth potentials distinct.

### BH-F10 — theorem ancestry and novelty ceiling were omitted

The mathematical result is a standard finite graph potential/cycle-space
certificate. The project contribution is its typed proper-function boundary.
General mathematical novelty is zero.

## Required repair

- make truth-loss authority and registry completeness explicit assumptions;
- state the disconnected componentwise variant;
- separate additive and ordinal correction;
- type each proper-function account by its fixing evidence;
- preserve Plantingian and fitrah source boundaries;
- add exact sign and dynamic-registry countermodels;
- record ancestry, zero novelty, and formalization ceiling;
- freeze V2 and conduct a distinct rereview.
