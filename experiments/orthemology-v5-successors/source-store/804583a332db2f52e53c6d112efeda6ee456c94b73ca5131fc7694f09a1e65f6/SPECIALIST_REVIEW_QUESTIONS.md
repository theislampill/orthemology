## How a response would change the report

For each reviewed issue, a response can:

1. Confirm the exact bounded conclusion.
2. Correct a source or mathematical error.
3. Supply an applicable missing premise or record and justify a stronger conclusion.
4. Show that the proposed stronger conclusion requires a different task specification.
5. Leave the issue unresolved, stating what observation or argument would discriminate it.

These are prospective review questions, not another experiment, a certification process or a claim of human-specialist validation.
