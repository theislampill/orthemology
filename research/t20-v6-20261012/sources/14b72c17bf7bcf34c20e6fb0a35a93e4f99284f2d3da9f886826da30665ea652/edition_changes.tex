\subsection{Mathematical lineage and the limits of novelty}
The first edition's eleven named results are retained with explicit repairs to scope and assumptions, and with stable labels in the source, although section numbers change. Their standard ingredients remain identified as such. The Gramian criterion is a linear-operator geometry argument; the cut bound combines error entropy and data processing; the migration and commuting-update results are induction arguments; the support result is a hitting-set characterisation; viability is a finite safety game; attribution is binary hypothesis testing. FLP is cited at its actual scope rather than reproved.

The additions in this edition are new \emph{to this exposition}, not asserted historical firsts. The fluid estimate is the energy method with Gronwall's inequality; its observation corollary is a norm bound. The uncertain-model margin is a robust-separation consequence. Task sufficiency is elementary fibre factorisation. Evidential JOIN, serial reliability, latent relabelling and finite-data nonidentification use elementary set, probability and function arguments. The held-out bound specialises the standard bounded-loss concentration method; finite-rank progress uses a decreasing integer variant. The synthesis claim is that these objects expose and connect the right obligations for the declared problem; superiority of the synthesis remains an empirical hypothesis.

\subsection{Scope of the executed verification}
The source package includes a standard-library Python script checking the retained finite and dimensionless examples together with new counterexamples and finite regression cases. The script checks its own outputs with explicit exceptions that remain active under Python optimisation. It makes no network requests, enumerates no device interfaces, and performs no physical experiment. A passing finite test does not replace a proof, establish a physical model, prove a universal negative, or certify that a private experience originated externally.

The general proofs were reviewed algebraically in this preparation, but were not checked in a proof assistant or subjected to independent expert review. The external NS proof and its formalisation were not independently verified. Research references and source terminology were checked at the scopes recorded in the accompanying source register. No arXiv submission, peer review, institutional endorsement or stable external release is claimed.
