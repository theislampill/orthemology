## Exact clarification

In the section “A narrower positive purpose argument,” make the practical linkage explicit:

**The actual attestation e is deliberately selected and performed by the source as the adequate means by which the recipient is to know this exact p through accepting that attestation.**

This condition accompanies the source's full relevant knowledge, its settled governing epistemic purpose, and its not knowingly selecting a means that excludes that purpose. Under those conditions, a knowingly false e is incompatible with the linked exercise: the source would know that accepting false p cannot give knowledge of p, while selecting e as adequate to that very end.

Alternatively, retain the existing three premises and state the narrower conclusion exactly: **the source cannot coherently select or perform the known-false attestation as realizing that governing epistemic purpose.**

The checkpoint's introductory formulation already uses the narrower means-linked wording. Its later sentence “it cannot knowingly give a false p-attestation in this exercise” must be read with the explicit practical linkage, rather than as following from a merely possessed purpose plus an unrelated act.

## Why this matters

Having a purpose does not make every concurrent act a chosen means to it. A source could preserve a concern for the recipient's knowledge while performing an act for another end, or acknowledge that the act is inadequate. Such an exercise is not excluded by a condition governing only the means selected for the epistemic purpose. The mixed-source rival uses exactly this distinction at its exceptional attestation.

The repaired implication still provides a narrower sufficient condition than universal benevolence. Independent warrant for the token purpose, its practical linkage, and its competence is still required. It does not derive that warrant from the attestation, does not establish actual communication, and does not establish essential T0.

The clarification follows an independent source-scope review of the frozen checkpoint. It is a precision repair to the local implication, not a withdrawal of the source reconstruction or the scope boundary.
