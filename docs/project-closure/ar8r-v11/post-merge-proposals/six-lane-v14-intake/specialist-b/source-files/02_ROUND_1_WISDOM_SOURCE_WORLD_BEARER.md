## 1. Frozen question

Deep CJ leaves objective fittingness, apprehension, intentional uptake,
selection because of fittingness, reliable realization, and bearer
applicability unjointly established. Deep CF supplies an extensional common-
bearer incidence criterion. Deep K already distinguishes proper function,
representation, reliability, agency, knowledge, objective fittingness,
because-of selection, means proportion, capacity, execution, ownership, and
stability.

The question is therefore not whether another bare conjunction can be coined.
It is whether the residue after Deep CJ is:

1. a new bridge;
2. an omitted bearer/episode index;
3. a reason-mediated dependence relation;
4. a source/world applicability failure; or
5. merely the already registered Deep K/BR guard package under new wording.

## 2. Typed separation

Fix types:

```text
World, Bearer, Episode, End, Action, Reason
SourceText, Edition, Translation, Occurrence, FormalPredicate, Recipient
```

For `w : World`, `b : Bearer`, `e : Episode`, `r : Reason`, `p : End`, and
`a : Action`, keep separate:

```text
F(w,p,r)       objective fittingness of the end/reason;
APP(w,b,e,r)   b apprehends the relevant fittingness/facts in e;
UPT(w,b,e,r)   b intentionally takes r up as a reason in e;
SBF(w,b,e,r,a) b selects a because r is fitting in e;
RR(w,b,e,a,p)  b reliably realizes a toward p in e;
APL(w,b,P)     the relevant predicate P is applicable to b in w.
```

`SBF` is intensional and explanatory. It is not equivalent to:

```text
response changes when a fittingness-labelled input changes;
selection covaries with a reward register;
selection and fittingness are both true;
or a system emits a reason-shaped explanation.
```

Define the packet-local episode contract:

\[
\operatorname{WIS\_EP}(w,b,e,r,p,a)
:= F\land APP\land UPT\land SBF\land RR\land APL.
\]

This is a **typed characterization**, not an analysis of every ordinary or
theological use of “wisdom,” and not a proof that any world contains such a
bearer.

## 3. Bearer and episode alignment

Two quantifier shifts are independently invalid:

\[
\forall G_i\;\exists b\;G_i(b)
\not\Rightarrow
\exists b\;\forall G_i\;G_i(b),
\]

and

\[
\forall G_i\;\exists e\;G_i(e)
\not\Rightarrow
\exists e\;\forall G_i\;G_i(e).
\]

Deep CF addresses the first at the level of finite incidence: a total
intersection is needed for one common bearer. It does not, by itself, establish
that apprehension explains selection, that selection belongs to the same
intentional episode, or that the source predicate applies to that bearer.

The repair is therefore:

```text
B_ID: all six predicates are indexed to the same bearer under the chosen
      bearer-identity semantics;
E_ID: the apprehension, uptake, selection, and realization belong to the same
      episode or to an explicitly typed integrated episode lineage;
BECAUSE: selection counterfactually/reason-explanatorily depends on fittingness,
         not merely on a correlated signal;
APPLICABILITY: the intended Wisdom predicate applies to that bearer in the
               relevant world under an independently warranted interpretation.
```

`B_ID`, `E_ID`, and `BECAUSE` are load-bearing specification coordinates. They
are not generated by the common-intervention factorization theorem.

## 4. Source-formal-world branch

A source occurrence and the episode contract must remain distinct. Let:

```text
SRC_WISE(s,o,bs)  source s, occurrence o predicates “Wise” of source bearer bs;
MAP_P(o,P)        occurrence meaning is formalized as P;
MAP_B(bs,b,w)     source bearer is mapped to actual-world candidate b;
TRUE_w(P,b)       P is true of b in w.
```

The Deep BY chain contributes nine typed guards:

```text
source regime;
custody;
version;
translation;
source-bearer identification;
actual interpretation;
predicate preservation;
bearer applicability;
target link.
```

Even with all nine, the immediate conclusion is only the world-directed formal
predicate selected by the interpretation. It is not automatically the six-
guard `WIS_EP` contract. A further **predicate-explication bridge** is required:

\[
P_{Wise}(b,w)\Rightarrow
\text{the intended contract or theological content at the declared scope}.
\]

Conversely, satisfying `WIS_EP` does not authorize a divine Name, identify a
revealed bearer, or establish source authority.

Recipient warrant adds a further coordinate:

```text
WARRANT(recipient, source, interpretation, world claim)
```

Truth and applicability may hold while a recipient lacks access, competence,
independence, defeater control, or authorization. Recipient warrant is not a
hidden tenth member of the formal conjunction; it is a separate epistemic
relation.

## 5. Narrow candidate and proof status

### SB-C1A — contract introduction

If all six predicates hold at the same `(w,b,e,r,p,a)` indices, then
`WIS_EP(w,b,e,r,p,a)` holds.

**Proof:** conjunction introduction. This is definitional and has no independent
mathematical novelty.

### SB-C1B — source-relative applicability

If:

1. a fixed source occurrence predicates `P_Wise` of `bs`;
2. the edition/translation/meaning/formalization chain is preserved;
3. an actual interpretation maps `bs` to `b` and `P_Wise` to `P`;
4. the source is true under the declared source-authority premise; and
5. `P` applies to `b` in world `w`;

then the source-relative branch licenses `P(b,w)` **conditional on those
premises**.

It does not license neutral source truth, the six-guard contract, Name
authorization, recipient warrant, personality, a Necessary Being, or revelation.

The residual-guided result is negative but useful: the repeated remainder is

## 6. Countermodels

### SB-CM1 — impersonal teleological tracker

A nonpersonal controller tracks a fittingness-labelled variable, selects the
registered beneficial output, and realizes it reliably. It matches every
current extensional Deep CJ profile. `APP`, `UPT`, and `SBF` are false under the
intended intentional semantics. Signal sensitivity is not intentional uptake.

### SB-CM2 — distributed plural reason pipeline

`b1` apprehends, `b2` evaluates/selects, and `b3` realizes. Each guard exists
somewhere, but the total bearer intersection is empty. Global
Will/Power/Knowledge/fittingness availability is not bearer-local
co-instantiation.

### SB-CM3 — episode split

One persistent bearer apprehends in `e1`, selects in `e2`, and realizes in `e3`
without a justified integrated-episode relation. Numerical bearer identity does
not by itself provide same-episode reason ownership.

### SB-CM4 — predicate weakening

A translation or formalization maps “Wise” to `produces_registered_benefit`.
The source sentence remains syntactically preserved while objective fittingness,
apprehension, uptake, and because-of selection disappear. Predicate preservation
is therefore load-bearing.

### SB-CM5 — referent ambiguity

Two candidate bearers satisfy the translated descriptive bundle. The source
predicate is true of one, while the neutral ground is the other. Without a
referent/target link, source truth does not select the neutral target.

### SB-CM6 — world-link deletion

The text, translation, formal predicate, and source-internal derivation are all
fixed, but the actual world does not instantiate the predicate. Located source
content does not create world truth.

### SB-CM7 — recipient-warrant deletion

The world claim is true, but the recipient has only an unauthenticated excerpt
or a known-defeated interpretation. World truth does not entail recipient
warrant.

### SB-CM8 — Name-authorization deletion

A positive meaning is true of the bearer, but the source does not authorize the
corresponding expression as a divine Name. The translated Aṣfahāniyyah locus on
`al-murīd` and `al-mutakallim` supplies a source-relative example of exactly
this separation.

## 7. Proper-function and ontology effects

No proper-function account supplies all six guards merely by being a function
account:

```text
Cummins/causal role: can supply system contribution, not selected history,
                     objective praiseworthiness, or intentional uptake;
selected effect: can supply historical function, including harmful or deceptive
                 functions, without current truth or Wisdom;
design: requires a legitimate designer and design specification; it does not
        follow from success;
Plantingian warrant: adds design plan, suitable environment, truth aim, and
                     objective reliability, but does not follow from behavior;
fitrah-oriented: remains source-relative and needs source truth, applicability,
                  impairment, operation, environment, and causal landing.
```

The episode incidence profile is compatible with nominalist predicates, tropes,
immanent properties, conceptualist classifications, powers, and plural
realization. The source’s attribute-locus rule constrains its own predication
architecture; it does not adjudicate the general theory of properties.
Property dualism remains a separate mind/body axis.

## 8. Source and school ceiling

The Aṣfahāniyyah translation supports, at source-relative scope:

1. a distinction between praiseworthy and blameworthy speech/volition;
2. a distinction between true attribute meaning and authorized Name;
3. an attribute-locus predication rule; and
4. an argument from praiseworthy ends toward Wisdom.

It does not establish the Arabic wording independently, an unqualified Atharī
consensus, objective fittingness in the neutral model class, actual-world
referent identity, or the joint truth of the AR8R guard package.
