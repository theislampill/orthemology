# Review dispositions and proof scope

The independent reviewer inspected the original printed Zsigmondy page, developed
an independent partition decoder and LCM-history gcd decoder, and checked the
finite and infinite-product arguments. The final freeze covers five mathematical
Markdown files and six core Python files. Export and verification helpers are
additional separately hashed evidence files.

The review sharpened several material boundaries:

- Order-e primes are triangular separators, not private against larger multiples.
  Descending elimination can return zero counts for spurious candidate orders.
- Complete new-prime valuations are retained in each gcd selector; the fourth
  coprimality check excludes partial prime powers.
- Resource limits have an inconclusive status, separate from invalid readouts.
- Hidden-guard witnesses may choose fresh indices outside a finite baseline.
  The helper's bound of2^n+1 refers to eligible index signatures, each containing
  n membership tests; it is not a bound of2^n+1 individual predicate calls.
- The second scope query changes the issued profile and reevaluates guards.
  Every route must affect the exact absence event being compared.
- There is no uniform positive equality tolerance across unbounded indices,
  but an already exact first readout supplies a candidate-dependent discrete gap.
- Deterministic finite stopping and limit learning are different guarantees.
  No randomized guarded impossibility is inferred from a seed-dependent witness.
- Finite rational coefficient independence survives clearing denominators;
  real weights, infinite products and trial mixtures are separately audited.
- The mixture pair's degree-two repair requires a shared latent inventory across
  two trial episodes, not just two unrelated marginal observations.

f_e^5<f_(e-1). This is a recorded correction, not a new result credited from the
