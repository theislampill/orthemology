# From anchored pairwise correctability to one repair for an arbitrary family

Status: ordinary corollary of DEPENDENCY_BLOCK_SMOOTH_CLASSIFICATION.md. No new kernel theorem is claimed. It uses the same finite truth hull, attained ranges, smooth additive generators, unconstrained free singletons, and rank-at-least-two hypothesis on every constrained component.

## Statement

Fix one anchor generator G. Let (F_k) be any family of admissible generators on that same fixed problem; the family may be infinite and no common bound on its curvatures or scale constants is assumed.

Suppose that for every k, the pair (F_k,G) has common coherent local repairs at every interior p and in every transverse direction h, in the precise sense of condition (A) of the block theorem. The same conclusion follows if each pair instead satisfies the existential sublinear-tolerance condition (A_eta), with its own sequences and error allowances.

Then, at each fixed p and transverse h, there is a sufficiently small positive perturbation interval and ONE coherent repair c_e for each admitted e in that interval which strictly improves every truth-state loss under G and under EVERY F_k simultaneously.

Thus the permitted interval and repair are common to the entire family. No uniform strictly positive lower bound on the improvements across k is asserted.

## Proof

For each k, the block theorem identifies constants a_(k,B)>0 and coordinate affine terms such that

    f_(k,i)=a_(k,B) g_i + affine_i

on the attained interval of each constrained component coordinate. The unrestricted singleton coordinates remain free directions in the affine tangent space.

Use the unique G-Bregman minimizer c_e over the genuine coherence set C. The anchor proof gives c_e->p and eventual relative-interior feasibility, with

    grad G(c_e)−grad G(b_e) perpendicular to T.

The neighborhood in which b_e and c_e lie in the attained coordinate intervals depends only on p, h, G and C, not on k. For every k, blockwise positive rescaling preserves the normal equations. In free coordinates the anchor normal equations imply c_e,i=b_e,i, so every other generator also has zero gradient residual there. Hence

    grad F_k(c_e)−grad F_k(b_e) perpendicular to T

for all k. The three-point identity yields the common strict improvement D_Fk(c_e,b_e)>0 at every truth vector. The same c_e therefore works for the whole family. QED.

## Why the quantifier exchange is licensed

The argument does not infer a joint intersection merely because pairwise intersections are nonempty. Each pair's universal correctability forces an exact structural relation to the same anchor. That relation proves that the anchor's single repair belongs to every member's admissible set. Both the anchor identity and the global fixed-generator/block hypotheses carry substantive content.

This differs from a claim that every standard separately admits a repair. That weaker premise neither identifies compatible score geometry nor supplies a common repair. It also differs from selecting a new anchor after seeing which output needs justification.

## Difference from the finite-family local approximate iff

The local Hessian-only converse in LOCAL_SMOOTH_APPROXIMATE_IFF.md sums first-order remainders over finitely many standards. An arbitrary infinite family need not admit a common sublinear remainder envelope without an additional uniformity condition.

The present corollary avoids that problem. Global anchored pairwise correctability has already forced exact blockwise generator relations. The final construction has zero allowed worsening and uses no family-wide remainder estimate. The positive scales may be unbounded or approach zero; strict statewise improvement for each member still follows, although a uniform improvement margin need not.

## Programme significance and limit

This is a concrete multi-standard bridge: universal joint correctability relative to one fixed standard can yield a single family-wide corrective law under the stated dependency structure. It does not choose which anchor equivalence class has objective authority, establish that an actual controller has access to it, or turn the law into original representation or a necessary intellectual bearer. Those remain separate source/application premises.
