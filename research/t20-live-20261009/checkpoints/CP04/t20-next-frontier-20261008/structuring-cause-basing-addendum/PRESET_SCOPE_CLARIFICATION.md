# Preset-control ceiling

This clarification governs use of the frozen structuring-cause-reply model without editing it.

Its preset control lacks the specific training-history dependence stipulated in that model. It does not follow that every preset, designed, inherited, or faithfully copied system lacks representational function, meaning, or warrant. Such systems may have other relevant histories and functional relations, whose eligibility requires its own assessment.

The original test establishes only that a matching current response map does not identify that specified acquisition route. It does not establish that an untrained mechanism is globally meaningless, and it does not import the programme's continuation/provenance conditions into Dretske's semantic theory.
