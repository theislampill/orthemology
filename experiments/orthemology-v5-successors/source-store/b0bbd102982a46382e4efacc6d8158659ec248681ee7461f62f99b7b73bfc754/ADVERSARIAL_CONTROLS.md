# Independent practical perfection controls

These are reviewer-authored conceptual controls for the research-v2 argument, not finite models, actual source observations or proofs of a possible malicious essence. No historical synopsis or quotation is added. Section references are to ORIGINAL_PRACTICAL_PERFECTION.md.

## C1 Intrinsically directing cognition

Grant that the agent fully apprehends the decisive practical reason as favouring an action, and that this apprehension itself supplies direction. Do not replace that grant with a merely passive list of true propositions. An unexplained contrary selection cannot be used to defeat the argument while everything else is held fixed. The paper explicitly credits this positive challenge. Result: PASS; §§3–4. This protects the intellectualist proposal from a deliberately weak reconstruction.

## C2 Sufficient constitutive apprehension

Strengthen C1: the actual source's complete practical apprehension is essentially sufficient for fitting intention throughout the declared domain. A contrary intention is then excluded. Adding an intrinsic defeating inclination would remove the strengthened premise, not refute the implication. Result: PASS; §4 recognises this successful conditional route and does not require the construction of an alternative source profile. The review does not certify the strengthened premise about the actual source.

## C3 Genuine but nonexclusive motivational direction

Instead grant only that apprehending the reason necessarily contributes motivation. That is weaker than C2. A competing intrinsic priority is not ruled out by this weaker grant alone, but its metaphysical possibility and actuality have not thereby been proved. Result: PASS; §4 separates the source-nature question from removal of external impediments. This is a premise-discrimination control, not an empirical counterexample to intellectualism.

## C4 Basic warrant without a further derivation

Suppose a thinker directly apprehends the stronger source-sensitive practical-priority principle with its required content. The fact that this apprehension is basic does not require an additional proof as a condition of counting as warrant. Nor does an imagined rival automatically defeat it. Result: PASS; §§3–4,8 expressly allow the route. Calling a weaker intuition basic would not change its content, but that is a different criticism.

## C5 Combined support without separate proofs

Suppose source-giving, practical integration and independence jointly make the complete account rationally preferable, while none is independently sufficient. A rule demanding three independent demonstrations would wrongly withhold their combined force. Result: PASS with reporting guard. The paper calls the affirmative a combined account and grants prima-facie support. Its refusal to count two independently earned warrants must not be read as a ban on mutually reinforcing or abductive justification.

## C6 The competitor has no evidence merely by being describable

An intrinsic contrary aim may explain the structure of a conditional challenge. Do not infer that it actually exists, is metaphysically eligible, is equally credible, or undercuts an independently warranted basic recognition. Result: PASS; §§4,8 explicitly retain all four limits. The paper's negative result is inquiry-relative, not a global verdict that N is an unwarranted belief.

## C7 Originality without effortless capacity

Hold original possession and absence of an external donor fixed. Do not add unlimited effortlessness, jointly realisable alternatives or costless repair. A proposed protective transition may still require facts not supplied by those grants. Result: PASS; §5 denies that nonneed alone supplies every alternative or removes every cost. If relevant effortless capacity is separately established, it can remove that particular obstacle and deserves the resulting credit.

## C8 Several fitting alternatives

Grant sufficient responsiveness to decisive reasons but leave two fitting optional performances available. A preference can select between them without becoming a defect. Conversely, the existence of such permissible preference does not establish permission for it to override a decisive contrary reason. Result: PASS; §4. Neither a unique best world nor a unique sentence is a premise of the accepted argument.

## C9 Token purpose versus governance of purposes

Hold fixed a source's independently established governing intention that this recipient know this proposition through this very attestation, together with adequate means and the relevant coherence condition. Deliberate known falsification defeats that purpose. Change the question to why the source must always select such fitting purposes. The token implication no longer answers the whole question. Result: PASS; §4 preserves the inherited successful local route without promoting it into N.

## C10 A duty shared by both options

At the current decision, the source has already actively organised the legitimate bounded reliance and the care reason persists. Compare a faithful response with a breach. The breach does not restore the pre-undertaking normative condition. Result: PASS; §§5–6. The alleged disadvantage that fidelity first binds the source disappears because it compares different histories.

## C11 Shared duty but different performance costs

Retain C10 and add a real resource expenditure or legitimate project loss in the faithful option. The responsibility is shared; the expenditure need not be. It must remain in the complete comparison and may matter to strict superiority. Result: PASS; §§5–6 retain actual capacity and collateral interests. The new result excludes duty-evasion as an original-independence gain, not every benefit accompanying nonperformance.

## C12 Proper release or expiration

Alter C10 so that the bounded responsibility was validly discharged, its relevant period ended or the recipient freely rejected every adequate response concerning a risk within its control. The persistence argument cannot simply carry over. General duties or other claims may remain, but require their own ground. Result: PASS; the inherited applicability conditions and §§5–6 require a still-applicable responsibility. No indefinitely owed maintenance is inferred.

## C13 Care without gratuitous control

Keep the source's responsibility, same legitimate goods and an equally adequate reasonably available noncoercive alternative fixed. The source withholds that alternative solely to obtain submission. Its own refusal does not establish the authority it then invokes. Result: PASS; §6. The conclusion removes the alleged status advantage of established authority; it does not establish all the other conditions for a complete-profile improvement.

## C14 An independent authority ground

Add a genuinely justified allocation of decision-making, or a real coordination burden not avoidable by the proposed alternative. The original no-leverage case no longer holds with all its premises. Rejecting an authority derived solely from care cannot annul the additional ground. Result: PASS; §6 expressly preserves valid allocations, target-preserving coordination and third-party goods. No universal consent requirement or universal recipient veto is introduced.

## C15 Collateral satisfaction

Suppose the defective option provides some separately valuable satisfaction or protects an independently legitimate project. Calling its unjustified dominance sovereignty cannot itself establish pure practical rank. Nonetheless a distinct collateral good must be assessed rather than erased by association. Result: PASS; §6 distinguishes the two. If outweighed, the loss can coexist with R_G while preventing R_P; if sufficiently weighty, it can change the strict comparison.

## C16 Manufactured sunk costs

At selection time, a noncoercive adequate arrangement is available without relevant offsetting loss. The source unjustifiably forecloses it and subsequently recruits innocent participants whose interests would now be harmed by an abrupt switch. Current cooperation may be fitting; earlier selection remains unjustified. Result: PASS; §6 carries the exact governance-v2 correction. Present dependence neither proves fair formation nor becomes unreal because wrongfully created.

## C17 Earlier alternative versus current repair

Retain C16. The earlier easy option is no proof of a currently available loss-free alternative. A whole-history comparison must separately preserve the bearer and the fixed assurance case; simply deleting the earlier exposure changes the target. Result: PASS; §6 distinguishes these comparisons and retains fitting remedy duties without promising impossible restoration.

## C18 Wrongful control without a lie

Let the source truthfully say that it will withdraw support unless the recipient surrenders an independent check. The threat may be wrongful while its assertion is true. The care/control comparison can still apply, but this is not an instance of Lie and its remedy is not automatically an R_G witness. Result: PASS; §7 preserves the difference between care and T0. Integrating summaries must not reclassify every abuse or silence as false assurance.

## C19 Two rejected losses with a third unresolved

Eliminate exactly the responsibility-status and unsupported-authority objections. Leave another genuinely relevant effect unexamined. Strict complete-profile improvement has not yet been shown, even if the critic has not specified the effect. Result: PASS; §6 explicitly denies that lack of another objection proves its absence. No unrestricted R_G or Pareto theorem follows.

## C20 An eligible improvement with a dominated actual choice

Grant a particular eligible strict repair and its complete comparative superiority. That grant does not establish that the actual source selects it or that no eligible profile is superior to its actual exercise. N still does independent work. Result: PASS; §§6–7. This is the inherited conditional dependency, not a new countermodel or proof of possible source wrongdoing.

## C21 Actual and essential scope

Grant actual nondomination at the controlled respect, leaving the corresponding other possibilities unassessed. Do not infer essential nondomination. Alternatively grant a genuinely essential source-integration principle: its modal content can carry the wider conclusion without another empirical survey. Result: PASS; §§4,7. The paper allows the latter route while declining to infer it from the former.

## C22 Recipient use remains separate

Grant the metaphysical principle in its strongest form. It still does not identify this occurrence's source, content, target, version, interval, access route or action permission. Conversely, these unresolved global questions do not cancel independently warranted ordinary local knowledge. Result: PASS; §7 and inherited Ninth boundary. No operational authority or new actual D1/D2 is earned by the review.

## C23 Fidelity is not the converse of nondomination

Grant that the source avoids the controlled false assurance, or even that every in-scope assertion is nonlying. A profile may still require assessment at other affected pure-perfection respects before N is established. Likewise, merely permissible intention need not be identical to maximal practical excellence under every candidate comparison. Result: PASS; §§4,6–7 make the direct conclusion fidelity and do not reverse R_G+N to derive N from it. Prima-facie support toward N belongs to the stronger original-practical-priority proposal, not to that invalid converse.

## Outcome

All twenty-three controls preserve the exact candidate's bounded claims. The controls do not independently prove the source-wide perfection principle, its negation or a realised metaphysical rival. The report should credit the actual rational force of the affirmative proposal and the two specific comparison corrections without promoting either into an unearned N or R_G result.
