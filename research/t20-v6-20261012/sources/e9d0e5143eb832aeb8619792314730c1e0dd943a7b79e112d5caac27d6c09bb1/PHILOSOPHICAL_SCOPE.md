## Constructive obligation

A defensible communication argument can have this form:

1. A specified source has a particular communicative intention: a particular audience is to have adequate warranted access to a particular content, under specified relevant circumstances.
2. The operative means, including any warranted supplementary means, fail that access condition.
3. The intended communication condition therefore is not fulfilled.

This is a substantive route to examining an attributed revelation, provided the intention and failure are actually established. To infer an incompatibility with divine wisdom, truthfulness or complete perfection, the argument must additionally establish why the identified failure contradicts the relevant source attribute and purpose. It is not enough to rank the ease of reconstructing two texts. Neither is a dispute about the extra premise a reason to assign it zero evidential weight without examining its support.

No new premise about divine purposes is established by these attachments alone. Existing positive source/attribute appraisals remain unchanged.

## Target distinctions that matter to the proposed comparison

- Knowing what a claimant said is different from having warrant that the claimant's assertion is true or divinely authorised. Stronger transmission evidence for a rival's wording does not by itself establish the rival's religious authority.
- Exact original wording, an earliest reconstructible textual state, a received edition, a standardised consonantal text and particular recitations are distinct target functions. Their comparison requires declared targets and evidence contracts.
- Failure to identify an exact wording does not by itself make a claim common to all admissible wordings unrecoverable. Whether a doctrine depends on the disputed wording is a further factual/logical question.
- A conjunction requires every conjunct, but a conclusion with alternative sufficient proof supports need not depend on the weakest available witness. Collection-wide consequences require the actual dependence argument.
- Repeated reports can share an ancestor. Additional reported witnesses only refine the target-relevant information when their admitted content or evidential relationship actually does so. Counts alone do not supply that demonstration.

These points preserve the proposed criticism's strongest form: inadequate warranted communication of the required content. They prevent replacing it with a merely numerical comparison of variants or manuscripts.

## The proposed additional-faculty objection

A rule that outputs a new answer from exactly the same retained input cannot remove a target-varying fibre while guaranteeing correctness throughout that history class. This applies to an alleged override that adds no target-relevant evidence.

A genuinely informative additional observation is a different mathematical input. Merely noting that basic faculties are involved in assessing it does not prove that it carries no new information. Conversely, calling an experience or assertion an additional faculty does not establish its truth-conduciveness, authority or relevance. The particular route must be assessed on its actual grounds. The supplied discussion does not settle every such route through a general circularity allegation.
