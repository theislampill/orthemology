# Sovereign control, independent choice, and the exact harmonious rival

7 October 2026. A bounded source-transfer study. This does not close T20, revise an earlier archive, discover a new theorem of agency logic, or certify a full metaphysical countermodel.

## Result first

The transfer is **conditionally successful and unconditionally insufficient**.

The source explicitly demands more than a power that succeeds when a peer permits it. Standard strategic effectivity gives that demand a precise candidate interpretation: an agent has an own choice that ensures the target against every relevant peer exercise. Two independently selectable agents cannot each possess this control over incompatible targets in one common nontrivial outcome field. Actual agreement does not invalidate that conditional result.

But the strongest inherited rival preserves a weaker, precisely identifiable power. For its actual full-Q command, **the maximal peer-exercise domain on which exact created output Q is guaranteed is exactly the collection of subsets of Q**. Against unrestricted target commands, neither agent controls every exact output. Against the subset-of-Q domain, both robustly ensure Q, including while both act, yet neither is the entire original provider of its jointly supported events. This is a more precise diagnosis than either “it has full power” or “it has no robust power.”

Consequently:

- If unrestricted, unimpeded sovereign control is legitimately retained as part of the original-agency premise, the rival **fails that premise**. Its solo-constructor theorem cannot be presented as satisfying it.
- If only intrinsic nonreceipt, ordinary standing constructors, necessarily fitting own activity, and actual exact success are retained, the standard choice theorem does **not** derive the stronger premise. In particular, nonreceipt does not entail a Cartesian domain of jointly admissible completed wills.
- Source-neutral outcome control and exclusive original provenance are different target types. The source's opening argument concerns the latter. Merely proving an output-control theorem does not reproduce the whole original-provision argument.

The new credit is one source-semantic discrimination and its exact admissibility-envelope calculation. Opposed-target failures, intrinsic power versus foreknown nonoccurrence, structural nonreceipt, and coalescent support were already present. The 12 checks below verify the discrimination; they are not 12 new discoveries.

## 1. Primary neighboring-field definitions actually inspected

Four original research sources were acquired and read at the specified loci. PDF hashes and read boundaries are in `SOURCE_BINDINGS.json`.

1. **Pauly, “Coalitional Ability in Multi-Agent Systems: A Logical Approach” (2001), pp.88–90.** The relevant notion is alpha-effectivity: a coalition has a strategy guaranteeing a target set regardless of outsiders' strategies. Disjoint coalitions' guarantees compose by intersection; impossible targets are excluded. Page90's displayed quantifiers and its distinction between complete outcome determination and mere effectivity were also visually checked. The representation theorem is not used here. [Primary paper](https://cdn.aaai.org/Symposia/Spring/2001/SS-01-03/SS01-03-011.pdf)
2. **Alur, Henzinger and Kupferman, “Alternating-Time Temporal Logic” (2002), §§2.1 and 3.1, pp.677–678 and 685–686.** Available move sets depend on the state. A concurrent move vector belongs to their Cartesian product. The next-step ability clause selects the coalition's moves before universally quantifying over the other moves. The article explicitly distinguishes collaborative possibility from adversarial guarantee. [Author-hosted paper](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~alur/Jacm02.pdf)
3. **Ming Xu, “Group Strategies and Independence” (2014), §1, pp.345–346.** Choice cells partition histories through one moment. Independence of agents requires every selection of one cell per agent to have a nonempty intersection. This is an explicit compatibility requirement on choices, not a definition of absence of efficient receipt. Page346 was visually inspected. [Primary chapter](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-319-01754-9_16)
4. **Horty and Belnap, “The Deliberative Stit” (1995), pp.588–589 and 592–593.** Deliberative STIT requires truth throughout the chosen cell and falsity somewhere among the moment's other histories. The second clause excludes historically settled propositions. It should not silently become a theological requirement that every wise voluntary act have an alternative under the same fully fixed intrinsic state. [Author-hosted paper](https://www.umiacs.umd.edu/users/horty/articles/1995-dstit-pub.pdf)

These sources concern formal models of agency. They do not independently establish Ibn Taymiyyah's metaphysical premises. The elementary incompatibility proof below was checked directly; no unpublished proof or uninspected representation theorem is being borrowed.

## 2. The exact source pressure and its two target types

### 2.1 Exclusive whole production comes first

Directly read retained `tranche20/cooperation-reassessment/islamweb-vol1.txt`, electronic **Minhaj 1:438–441**. The opening says that one source's independence in producing the world entails that the other did not share in it:

> استقلال أحدهما بالعالم ... يوجب أن يكون الآخر لم يشركه فيه

The ensuing simultaneous-independence contradiction is therefore about **each being the whole exclusive producer of the same effect**, not simply each ensuring a source-neutral proposition p. It would misrepresent this passage to replace its target silently by ordinary success at p. [Electronic primary locator](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/108/96/)

The text then distinguishes capacity only when the other is not acting from capacity while the other acts. Its explicit composability move is:

> إن كانا قادرين على الاستقلال، أمكن أن يفعل هذا مقدوره وهذا مقدوره

It follows this with the alternative that each capacity is conditioned on the other's enabling. The next paragraph separately introduces the qualitative motion/rest conflict. Thus the source contains both an exclusive-provenance argument and an incompatible-output argument; they must remain distinct.

At **1:441**, choosing one of one's own opposed alternatives is distinguished from having to receive the other's help or nonprevention. This is substantive positive warrant for a sovereignty standard. A power whose success is at the disposal of another original is less independent than a power whose alternatives are governed by its own agency. The source is not merely pointing out that people sometimes disagree.

### 2.2 What unrestricted power does and does not quantify over

Directly read **V02L0181–0185**, continuously preserved in `source-fidelity-review/MINHAJ_POWER_CONTINUOUS_EXTRACT.txt` and bound to `arabic-volume-study/minhaj-2-3/volume02/body.txt`. These are retained electronic line identifiers, not newly certified printed pages. The exact electronic source is subdivision004, body paragraphs17–21. [Electronic primary text](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/mnhaj-004.html)

The passage rejects “power over whatever falls within one's own power” as empty. Its affirmative scope is every intrinsically possible object of power, including what another agent can do. It separately says that an occurrence excluded by true knowledge may still be intrinsically possible and within power. It does **not** say that every possible target is an admissible completed will under one unchanged wisdom-and-knowledge context, or that all such wills of different originals can be combined.

Thus a faithful schematic reading includes

- for every intrinsically eligible q, the bearer has standing power with respect to q;
- the bearer does not need a foreign original to confer or complete its relevant efficacy;
- exercising complete independent production is stronger than merely having an available solo constructor.

The additional strategic regimentation, “for every q there exists one own exercise that succeeds against every peer exercise in the same domain,” makes the last two demands precise **only after its quantifier domain and notion of success are justified**. It is an interpretation of the source's strong norm, not a verbatim formula already proved by the all-power sentence.

### 2.3 Necessary concord is explicitly addressed

Directly read **V03L0268–0276**, especially0269 and0272–0273, from `primary-reading/actual-common-source-assessment/receipts/minhaj3-L268-L276.txt`. The retained coordinates are subdivision006, body paragraphs92–100; they are not independently collated print coordinates. [Electronic primary text](https://www.islamicbook.ws/tarekh/mnhaj-006.html)

The source considers necessary agreement, common benefit and knowledge of what will occur. It classifies necessarily conjoined willing/acting as reciprocal partial determination and rejects the corresponding efficient circle. This is the source's argument against the strongest harmony response. The inherited rival deliberately disputes that classification by using unreceived primitive cognition and peer-independent policies. Choice independence cannot settle the dispute by identifying every shared truth, intrinsic policy alignment, or modal correlation with efficient donation.

## 3. The control properties, with all quantifiers exposed

Fix one comparison context c, own moves A_c, peer moves B_c, admissible profiles D_c, and an outcome map F. Initially let success mean exact source-neutral output Q.

- **Joint attainability:** there exist a and b with F(a,b)=Q.
- **Solo capacity with an idle peer:** there exists a with F(a,idle)=Q. The peer may remain present with its standing power intact.
- **Reactive ability:** for every peer b, some own a gives Q. The successful a may vary with b. Access to the relevant peer choice and a coherent response policy are extra issues.
- **Uniform control / alpha-effectivity:** some own a gives Q for every peer b.
- **Actual agreed success:** the particular actual profile gives Q. This need imply none of the counterfactual claims above.
- **Entire original provision:** the particular event is wholly originally supplied through this bearer in the required respect. This is not one of the preceding output quantifiers.

The order difference matters. In the XOR game, for either target and every peer move there is a successful response, but no fixed own move succeeds against both peer moves. Complete knowledge of the actual peer move does not algebraically reverse those quantifiers. Conversely, alpha-effectivity is not defined by ignorance of the peer: it is a robustness claim about a displayed comparison domain.

The inherited cognition refinement explicitly varies reason-contexts rather than claiming alternatives under one fully fixed necessary intrinsic nature. One cannot take A's move from one reason-context and B's from another, form a new pair, and cite ATL's state-local product clause as a warrant for the pair's metaphysical admissibility.

## 4. The familiar impossibility lemma and its exact scope

Suppose A can ensure target set X, B can ensure target set Y, X and Y are disjoint, and the two guaranteeing choices have at least one common admissible completion. Every such completion would have to yield an outcome in both X and Y, impossible.

A Cartesian move domain or STIT independence supplies the common completion. Full rectangularity is stronger than needed: **compatibility of those two witnessing choices suffices**. The argument also works with nondeterministic nonempty outcome sets. Empty completion sets must not be credited as successful control.

Consequences:

1. Two independent agents cannot both uniformly control every member of one outcome space containing incompatible eligible targets. It does not matter whether their actual choices agree.
2. Two agents can uniformly ensure the **same** target. OR is the simplest case. The incompatibility lemma does not establish unique authorship of its concordant output.
3. The result is at most a uniqueness theorem for the specified control role. It does not establish existence of such a controller, unique original efficient root, necessary existence, wisdom, or entire actual authorship.
4. The theorem is familiar agency logic. The 16 Boolean tables are only a regression check, with no new-discovery credit.

For exclusive-provenance targets, “Q wholly through A with no independent co-provider” and its B counterpart are already incompatible. Their independent controllability would therefore be excluded by the same lemma. But proving that each is an eligible, unimpeded power of the proposed originals requires the original-efficacy premise under examination. Failure to produce “Q solely from A while B is an irreducible co-provider of Q” cannot itself demonstrate a power defect; that simultaneous description is contradictory. The source's stronger argument is that perfect original agency must not be placed under that foreign constraint in the first place.

## 5. Exact new discrimination on the SAME inherited interpreter

`src/choice_semantics.py` imports the existing `IntendedAct` and `execute_intentions` without alteration. That interpreter returns created output S union T for commands S and T, while recording productive supports separately. No new convenient production law replaces it.

### 5.1 Maximal peer domain

Let K be a nonempty domain of peer target commands. For a fixed own command S:

**For every T in K, S union T = Q**

holds exactly when

**every T in K is a subset of Q, and Q minus intersection(K) is a subset of S, and S is a subset of Q.**

Proof: equality forces S and every T into Q. Any point of Q absent from at least one T must be supplied by S. Conversely, each point of Q is either in every T or supplied by S, and neither input adds anything outside Q.

Since the inherited constructor includes S=Q, there exists an exact-Q guaranteeing command precisely when every T in K is contained in Q. In particular, the inherited full-Q command has maximal robust peer domain **P(Q)**. This is an elementary set calculation with a new use in adjudicating the actual rival, not a claim of mathematical novelty.

The executable test covers all 15 nonempty peer domains on the two-event catalogue and all four targets, checking all own commands through the inherited interpreter against the independent formula. It does not just repeat the old opposed-command example.

### 5.2 What passes and what fails

On the unrestricted two-event menu P({e0,e1}), either agent can construct every target when the peer is idle. Either can uniformly ensure only the full catalogue {e0,e1}. Neither can uniformly ensure exact {e0} against a peer allowed to produce e1, or conversely.

This failure persists when all allowed commands and both tested targets are nonempty. It does not depend on calling nonexistence a created thing, making an original disappear, or stripping its standing power.

But over P(Q) × P(Q), both full-Q commands robustly ensure exact Q, including at the coactive (Q,Q) profile. For nonempty Q, Q is not settled across this operational domain: the idle/idle profile yields the empty created set. Thus the shared guarantee need not be a vacuous settled-truth attribution even in the deliberative-STIT sense.

At actual (Q,Q), every event still has support {A,B}; the inherited exhaustive-provider relation assigns neither individual entire provision. Holding B's Q exercise fixed, no choice by A yields sole-A provision of those events. Therefore **robust source-neutral exact-output control can coexist with failure of exclusive original control**. This is directly relevant to the first part of Minhaj1's argument.

### 5.3 Two different restrictions must not be confused

- If all reason-contexts are gathered into a diagonal domain {(Q,Q)}, with Q varying, each coordinate slice identifies its target, but mismatching slices have no common completion. Calling the coordinate controls independently selectable violates STIT independence.
- If one actual fitting reason-context Q is fixed, the ordinary policy domain may be the singleton {(Q,Q)}. Independence then holds trivially, but only Q is historically available and Q is settled. This frame does not represent unrestricted standing power.

Neither observation disproves the inherited intrinsic power. They show that ordinary policy-admissible histories and the source's broader standing-power comparisons require distinct modalities. Importing the deliberative-STIT contingency requirement wholesale would also threaten a single necessarily wise original, so it is not an independently warranted theological test.

## 6. Appraisal of the source's sovereignty rationale

The affirmative rationale is serious. If an independently original peer can veto, redirect, or add to the very complete effect an agent decisively determines, then the agent's intended control of the whole effect is conditional on foreign restraint. Maintaining an unused command does not remove that condition. The maximal-domain calculation displays it exactly: foreign production outside Q destroys A's exact-Q success. The source's contrast between self-choice and foreign nonprevention has genuine explanatory content.

If that unimpeded control is required of every proposed original across the same eligible field, the inherited rival must not be described as preserving all the strong source premises. It fails. Accepting that original-agency norm makes the conditional unity argument valid; defining a program's constructor as “full power” cannot override the failure.

There are nevertheless two unclosed bridges.

**First, availability.** Intrinsic all-power does not itself make every counterfactual completed will compatible with every other under fixed perfect wisdom. In the rival, necessary concord is offered as intrinsic policy determination, not as a foreign veto. Its power to produce a source-neutral Q can be represented even when true knowledge and wise policy exclude actual departure. Minhaj2 expressly distinguishes such nonoccurrence from incapacity. The source's stronger classification of concord as reciprocal productive conditioning must therefore do work; the logic's independence axiom cannot substitute for its warrant.

**Second, original authorship.** When B's actual exercise is the same Q, the inherited A command suffers no source-neutral output obstruction, even with both present and active. The objection then concerns the purity and completeness of nonexclusive original exercise. The source takes whole unborrowed efficacy to be a perfection. That is a substantive and plausible standard, but alpha-effectivity at Q does not imply it, and nonreceipt of standing attributes is not equivalent to it. The rival's shared-support interpretation targets this distinction. It remains philosophically unestablished as perfected original agency, yet it is not refuted by a theorem whose premise is already exclusive or unrestricted sovereign control.

My resulting judgement is narrower than an unconditional exclusion: the source has a sound, intelligible strong sovereignty argument; the strengthened rival is not a countermodel to that argument with its strong premises preserved. The remaining question is whether those strong premises follow from the independently defended original-being/perfection commitments in precisely the counterfactual and actual-provenance respects required. This study improves that question's formulation without deciding it by stipulation.
