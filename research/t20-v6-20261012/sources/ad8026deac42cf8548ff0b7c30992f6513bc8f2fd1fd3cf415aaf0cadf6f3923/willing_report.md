# Intrinsic power, wise willing and foreknowledge: substantive continuation

7 October 2026. New research branch. Earlier archives and assessments remain unchanged. This report does not declare owner-level closure or a complete source-faithful countermodel.

## Findings first

The stronger semantics produces several genuine discriminations that the old matched-score experiment did not contain.

1. **A uniquely best created outcome need not uniquely determine its productive allocation.** For any objective that depends only on that outcome, every implementation in the same output fiber has the same normative status. With a full solo constructor in each bearer, sole-A, shared and sole-B implementations can all attain the same end. A common-good explanation needs a further allocation principle; omniscience does not by itself supply it.
2. **Complete foreknowledge need not involve ignorance at any world, yet it still need not select a world.** Each represented omniscient agent knows its own and the other's exact act at each complete world. All three additive allocations survive the mutual truthful-forecast equations. A merely informational ablation, clearly not a proposed divine condition, explains which facts the objective alone establishes.
3. **Explicit neutral rules can derive agreement without importing a desired source-mode conclusion, but their source warrant differs.** Anonymous planning selects equal sharing in divisible examples; it fails on indivisible odd targets. Weak dominance selects both active commands in OR, but this force disappears when the peer is known active. Equal capacities also permit different intrinsic practical roles mathematically, so numeric role-exchange symmetry is a new premise, not a consequence of equal power. None of these rules has been established as the source's necessary wise choice rule for plural ultimate bearers.
4. **A whole-end intention, a partial-contribution intention and an intention to produce the end through a specified peer are different.** A means-end planner can derive its partial command from its whole end and known peer contribution. This is stronger than attaching a goal label to an arbitrary command. It nevertheless does not establish that the selected partial exercise is the source's complete original determination of the whole effect.
5. **Factivity and productive relevance must be separated.** Freezing every omniscient truth while suppressing its object fails factivity. That does not defeat the source's fixed-productive-state argument: knowledge that merely accompanies a plan may covary while the productive command stays fixed. Knowledge that selects the means cannot simply covary while the same policy and command remain fixed. The executed controls distinguish these cases.
6. **Full-state modal entailment alone is an inadequate definition of productive completion.** A passive perfect knower's complete factive record also entails the output. That provides no productive contribution. An independently justified productive or explanatory relation is indispensable.

Thirty-one executable checks and fourteen Lean declarations support the displayed mathematical distinctions. They do not certify the metaphysical adequacy of an original bearer, intrinsic knowledge, objective practical ranking, or complete numerical created effect.

## 1. Exact source pressure, including the stronger reply

### What the Arabic actually argues

The directly read Minhaj V03L0263–0276 addresses equal powers, separate and conjoint exercise, necessary agreement, necessary disagreement, contingent agreement, common benefit, foreknowledge and partial causal circles. It does not merely suppose that wise agents might quarrel.

At V03L0269, the necessary-agreement case is classified as each being unable to will or act except with the other's willing and acting. Simultaneity does not remove the stated condition. V03L0272 then considers a common benefit and foreknowledge: actual created benefits are downstream from the makers; accurate knowledge of an occurrence tracks that occurrence, which depends on the relevant originating will. Each creator's own willing must instead be intrinsic or independently grounded in that bearer. V03L0273 explicitly makes the stronger claim that each will and act would be part of what makes the other willing and acting, and rejects a circle in part of the complete determinant.

Thus the source does reject necessary joint willing, and it does so through a productive/explanatory classification. It is incorrect to report that the source discusses only received standing capacity. It is also incorrect to report its classification as a theorem of bare modal correlation. The text itself distinguishes prohibited productive priority from permitted concomitance in the directly read 2:168–172 passage. Its uncaused-concomitance example concerns attributes of a bearer; extending that permission to independently subsisting plural bearers needs an argument. This report supplies no such extension merely from the example.

Majmu L908 specifies an unchanged comparison and concretely names will, movement and instruments when explaining why an ordinary collaborator can later produce a like result alone. L912–913 expressly argues from full power and decisive willing to production through the bearer alone, then applies nonduplication. The passage is stronger than 'the intended result occurs somewhere'. Its fixed comparison need not freeze every relational truth in an omniscient state. Nor is the passage explicitly an assertion that every surgical comparison is a metaphysically accessible world. The relevant unchanged productive condition can be studied diagnostically without either attribution.

### Additional direct controls

- T17 P65–68 retains unrestricted eligible standing power, feasibility without borrowing or impairment, and the distinction between capacity and exercise.
- T17 P74–81 retains complete intentional content and the common-benefit/knowledge reply. It requires a separate assessment of the productive-conditioning claim.
- T17 P108–120 retains reasons-responsive necessitation, wisdom-compatible willing, the distinction between necessity through a determination and global history uniqueness, and the unresolved stronger global premises.
- Minhaj V02L0181–0185 directly rejects a vacuous 'power over whatever is within its power' restriction. It distinguishes intrinsic impossibility from an occurrence excluded by true knowledge or other fixed conditions. Known nonperformance is not lack of standing capacity.
- Minhaj V02L0243–0246 distinguishes intrinsic creating from the created product and does not require a second act to efficiently create every creating act. It distinguishes a regress in sources of efficacy from a succession of act tokens. The present output sort therefore does not silently count both uncreated intrinsic acts as additional created emissions.
- Minhaj V03L0020–0025 affirms real wisdom while rejecting a simple transfer of human transactional needs to divine agency. This prevents an easy appeal to equal labor costs, energy conservation, or fairness owed by one ultimate bearer to another as though it were already source-supported. The same passage cautions that the full global wisdom is not exhaustively available to the human reader.

Source reading used retained exact Arabic, the bound T17 extraction, and two freshly inspected retained scan images of Asfahani printed pp.422–423 / PDF pp.522–523. No new source acquisition, whole-book reading, manuscript work or edition collation is claimed. Local V03 line identifiers are not certified printed-page coordinates.

## 2. Semantics and its limits

The experiment separates:

- the same rigid bearer and its executable standing operations;
- its intrinsic command/act;
- the created output at a source-neutral target;
- the intended end and any intended manner of achieving it;
- an objective ranking and a choice rule;
- truth-directed knowledge and the facts it concerns;
- reason-input dependence, efficient productive dependence and constitutive donation.

The scalar additive family provides each already identified bearer with the same constructor for every nonnegative scalar in the family. The finite searches use small domains; the unchanged interpreter and generic arithmetic cover arbitrary natural-number targets. The constructor is not 'power = true'. Concentration is performed by either same bearer issuing a+b, rather than inventing a third unified bearer or merely taking a set-union of alternatives. This is still an algebraic power family, not every metaphysically eligible effect.

Operational command combinations form a calculation domain. They are not assumed to be jointly wisdom-compatible completed wills. Admissible profiles are derived from specified outcome and policy constraints. This avoids a hidden rectangularity premise while still permitting counterfactual operation comparisons.

The scalar output is not certified as the complete numerical external effect. In particular, an OR gate can omit created machinery, emissions or other consequences under a physical reading. Whole-effect identity and actual support accounting are coordinated with the separate provision investigation. Its newer command-as-exact-created-event-set interpretation makes intended target sets generate the actual per-event rules and tests exact whole-output equality; it is an operational intention interface, not a reason-to-target derivation or a verdict on original provision. None of this report's knowledge or policy calculations resolves that issue by renaming the output.

Finite root identifiers, identity epistemic accessibility and missing efficient edges are not a model of the full necessary-by-itself metaphysical profile. In particular, identity accessibility proves comprehensive factivity but not that an original knowing act or its constitutive perfection is unreceived. The sources' latter condition remains a separate obligation.

## 3. Outcome fibers: a stronger common-good test

Let F map command profiles to created outputs. Let the normative ranking compare only outputs. If F(p)=F(q), then p and q have identical rank against every other profile. Consequently, if one is optimal, so is the other. A unique optimal profile is impossible whenever the optimal output has two distinct realizations.

This is a general statement, not an inference from a small numeric census. The Lean declarations `same_output_same_optimality` and `two_implementations_defeat_unique_plan` prove it with no axioms. The independent finite test checks all 16 two-input Boolean output maps and all nine ranks on two output values.

For addition with target 2 and objective minus squared target error, the unique optimal output is 2 but the optimal profiles are (0,2), (1,1) and (2,0). Both bearers can already produce 2. Therefore the common created benefit neither identifies the cooperative profile nor explains why these two intrinsic commands are 1 rather than one command being 2.

The limitation is precise. The source's practical perfection may rank productive acts or original authorship as well as created outcomes. Such a ranking can distinguish these profiles. If the source norm requires each actual ultimate undertaking to be fully originally provided by that bearer, it rejects the shared plan as a defect of actual efficacy, even though capacity and the desired external end are unchanged. The fiber result does not refute that norm; it identifies it as an additional substantive ranking rather than an outcome-only consequence.

Likewise, if complete effect identity itself includes the full causal history, then the three profiles may no longer occupy the same fiber. That is a genuine alternate interpretation, requiring the independent identity/provision warrant. The result is not allowed to settle that premise by stipulation.

## 4. Foreknowledge that is genuinely factive and complete

### Actual omniscience is preserved in the represented epistemic sense

The three complete profiles are distinct represented worlds. At each, both agents' actual epistemic accessibility is identity. They know every true proposition over the represented world set, including their exact own command, the peer's command and the complete represented output. The test checks every subset proposition. No actual omniscient agent is charged with ignorance of which allocation occurs.

A separate coarse frame holds fixed only shared objective and power information. It knows that output 2 occurs but not which allocation occurs. This is an information-ablation diagnostic of what those premises alone establish, not a proposed state of divine cognition or a chronological time before divine knowledge.

Metaphysical possibility and epistemic accessibility are different relations. Complete knowledge at each world does not imply that only one complete world is metaphysically possible. Conversely, these finite worlds do not establish metaphysical possibility under the full source commitments. The result is conditional underdetermination at the displayed signature.

### Foreknowledge as input, rather than an appended label

The more demanding test uses truthful forecasts in the choice equations. Each agent chooses the complement of what it knows the peer will contribute: a=2−kA, b=2−kB, with kA=b and kB=a. These are genuine reason-to-choice constraints. Their solutions are still the three profiles above, with an exact truthful forecast at each.

Factivity therefore does not add a contrastive selector in this family. The missing fact is not what either omniscient bearer knows at the actual world. It is what independently makes that complete combination actual rather than the other complete, equally outcome-optimal combinations.

The Boolean census of all sixteen pairs of unary forecast-response functions finds twelve pairs with one fixed point, two with two and two with none. A further ternary pair, a=min(b+1,2), b=min(a+1,2), has the unique solution (2,2) while retaining reciprocal input dependence. Thus existence or uniqueness of a mathematical fixed point does not establish a well-founded productive explanation. On an efficient-causal reading, that reciprocal graph is precisely the kind of partial conditioning the source rejects. On a non-efficient truth/constraint reading, the graph is not yet a source-faithful explanation of original actuality.

## 5. Three nontrivial allocation proposals and their objections

### Anonymous intrinsic planning

Supply an explicit rule: among optimal allocations, choose the one invariant under exchange of numerically interchangeable roles. For additive target 2 it uniquely selects (1,1); for rationally divisible q it selects (q/2,q/2). No chosen preferred-command score or peer's actual exercise enters that calculation. The source-neutral end plus the extra anonymity rule derive the matching choices.

This is not automatically a source-faithful rescue. Equal standing powers do not prove numeric interchangeability of all practical reasons, nor does perfect wisdom in the read passages require equal exercise by distinct ultimate agents. Indivisible odd targets have no equal integer allocation, so anonymity and achievement cannot both be maintained in that family. The obstruction is mathematical; it does not claim metaphysical divine effects are scalar integers. Human labor-cost or distributive-fairness justifications would import needs and relations that the source does not grant.

The exact source denial could also be stronger than 'the allocation rule lacks evidence': even perfectly reasoned equal sharing may fail the source's norm of complete original productive efficacy. That issue remains explicit rather than counted as an upstream axiom inside the experiment.

### Dominance-constrained wise choice

For OR with the objective that output 1 is better than output 0, active command 1 weakly dominates 0 over the full operational rival domain. Each can derive 1 without a selected score parameter or an actual peer input. The resulting pair (1,1) is derived, not declared to be the only admissible profile.

But strict dominance does not select it; both commands survive that rule. More importantly, when the peer is known active, both own commands yield the same good output, so even weak dominance no longer selects 1 on that restricted context. Actual omniscience cannot be simulated by uncertainty about a peer known to act. One could defend an intrinsic robustness norm over counterfactual operational alternatives despite knowing the actual peer. The source's distinction between power and foreknown nonperformance makes such counterfactual evaluation intelligible, but it does not establish that norm as mandatory wisdom or certify those alternatives as jointly admissible complete wills.

This proposal passes output-level unchanged-command suppression. It still relies on saturation and inherits the full-effect/provenance burden. Its success is not a new full cooperation model.

### Intrinsically different practical roles with equal power

A separate rational fixture assigns two intrinsic role weights, 1 and 2, for target 3. The disclosed rule allocates the target proportionally, giving the same rigid A command 1 and B command 2. Both already possess the full solo command 3. Renaming bearers while retaining their roles leaves commands attached to roles; exchanging the roles of the fixed bearers changes their commands.

This discriminates numerical labels, standing capability and practical role. Equal power does not by itself force identical reasons or an equal split. Yet the test does not discover any such actual roles, establish that they are pure and intrinsically necessary, or explain why their respective values are 1 and 2. The role condition is an exposed extra premise, not a self-warranting intrinsic label. If it comes from contingent created occasions, those occasions require original provision accounting; if it is intrinsic, its compatibility with complete original perfection requires a positive rationale. No arbitrary native limitation of power is introduced to solve the problem.

## 5A. Stronger rival: participation as a relational good

The strongest new rival does not claim that output-only wisdom somehow already selects cooperation. It rejects output-only evaluation as incomplete. Keep e/q as exactly the same created effect, and put a distinct condition in the total intended end: q is realized and both numerically distinct bearers genuinely act. Intrinsic acts remain outside the created-effect sort. This is therefore a legitimate richer intention proposal, rather than automatically an illicit relabeling of e as 'the effect with these creators'. If the two descriptions were silently interchanged in a proof, it would become exactly that illicit target change.

This proposal has more interpretive support than an arbitrary equal-share score. Directly reread T17 P85 discusses realizing fitting loved ends through one's agency rather than receiving constitutive improvement. P92 explicitly preserves goods belonging to another party or to the process that can be lost when the endpoint is produced directly. The two retained scan images of Asfahani printed pp.422–423 / PDF pp.522–523 were then inspected directly in this branch. Page422 quotes al-Razi's argument that omnipotence renders means pointless, followed by the author's reply that the intended wisdom of a thing need not obtain in that thing's absence. Page423's third answer explicitly allows a further wisdom in creating the means, including benefit to those means and other parties, illustrated by trade benefiting both providers and recipients. The argument defeats an unrestricted inference from direct endpoint power to dispensability of all means. Its context is created means and beneficiaries; it does not itself establish another uncreated original's intrinsic act as an eligible nondeficient end. This is genuine interpretive support for testing an enriched intention with the remaining transfer burden exposed.

### Executed consequence

For additive q=2 over indivisible commands, the end q plus both active has the unique realization (1,1). Neither anonymous equal-sharing nor a private preferred-command score is needed. For q=3 there are two realizations; over rational commands even q=2 admits many. Thus participation can supply a genuine selection reason in one family while failing to solve allocation generally. It is not being presented as a universal coordinating algorithm.

Either original same bearer can still produce the exact created q alone. The solo construction does not fulfill the broader relational end. The Lean result `solo_created_target_not_relational_end` makes this distinction explicit. Concentrating productive capacities is not an ability to make 'two distinct agents act' true while only one acts. Failure to satisfy that contradiction is not a missing eligible power. Nor may one replace the cooperative total end with bare q and claim the original complete intention is preserved.

This blocks an overly quick capacity argument. It does not establish the selected joint act as a perfectly original exercise. A determination directed at q-through-this-peer is conditional in its productive means even when the peer's participation is necessarily and perfectly known. The source may deny that it is complete decisive willing of q in the required original-efficacy sense, rather than denying that q is desired. The rival has to meet that stronger source classification.

### Two actors do not imply two original sources

A second graph test separates immediate act-bearer from original provision. In one case A originally supplies a creature C's capacity and willing; A and C have numerically distinct actual acts contributing to e. The original root support of all these created requisites is A. In the other case B's standing power and will are structurally unreceived roots; the same two-actor pattern has roots A and B. The graph merely displays the distinction, not a proof of either actual cosmology.

This respects the source's permission for genuine creaturely voluntary acts without identifying them with the creator's intrinsic act. A relational good of two distinct actors can in principle be originally supplied through one ultimate source in this representation. A good explicitly requiring two independently original actors cannot. The latter requirement is a substantive plural-original condition in the intended end, and its genuine purity/eligibility cannot be inferred from ordinary cooperative or process goods. It is not silently smuggled into e.

### The precise source objection remains strong

New direct reading of Minhaj V02L0109–0115, especially L0111–0113, gives more than a generic prohibition of response to circumstances. The source argues that need concerns obtaining or preserving perfection, that intrinsic necessities cannot be supplied through another, and that an independently original other cannot complete the first bearer's efficacy. Majmu L908–913 directly applies the latter concern to productive cooperation. A created condition already originally provided by the same source differs from a second unreceived original contribution.

It would overstate that argument to say every newly realized voluntary end is a newly acquired essential perfection. T17 P85, P116–120 and the intrinsic-act controls forbid that collapse. A successful relational end, a particular act token, standing power and constitutive perfection remain distinct. The strongest source reply instead asks why dependence on a foreign original exercise should count as a fitting actual perfection of an ultimate source at all. The rival answers that each is intrinsically able and willing, while their total relational good is intentionally joint; the source classifies the relevant actual efficacy as externally completed. The needed warrant is this source-specific perfection classification, not a theorem that every relation, act renewal or created occasion is prohibited.

Accordingly this is a materially stronger rival than mere matched scores. It preserves the separate created target and can give an intelligible coordinating end. It still lacks a source-supported positive case that independent-original participation is a pure, fitting end compatible with unborrowed complete actual efficacy. Conversely, the absence of that positive case does not by itself prove a universal impossibility theorem. The owner must appraise both the source's positive original-efficacy rationale and this precisely typed rival.

## 6. Complete intention, means and intervention relevance

The new `INTENTIONS.json` matrix makes three readings explicit.

### Only the partial contribution is intended

Executing one's own contribution satisfies that narrow content even when the whole effect fails. It cannot be credited as decisive will for the whole effect in P6. Full standing capacity does not expand the specified content automatically.

### The whole end is intended through a plan using the peer's known contribution

For whole target 2 and known peer contribution 1, the means-end equation independently derives own command 1. The actual result attains the end and the knowledge is true. Thus this is a real planning interpretation, not an unexplained mismatch between intention and command.

After diagnostic suppression of the peer's contribution:

- Freezing the entire intrinsic state leaves false knowledge and a failed whole end. This is not an admissible world of factive omniscience.
- Updating knowledge to 0 while freezing command 1 violates the displayed means-end policy.
- Updating knowledge and replanning yields command 2 and restores the end, while changing the operative exercise. It is the ordinary changed-state repair that the source's fixed comparison excludes.

These dependencies are typed. The peer's contribution is a condition of the product; its represented truth is a reason-input in this planner. Neither fact by itself says that the peer efficiently supplies A's standing capacity or that A acquires a missing constitutive perfection. The source's stronger judgement concerns complete actual efficacy and the original grounding of the will. This model has not independently established that the selected half-command qualifies as that complete determination.

### The end is intended through this specified peer

If the full content includes the peer's contribution, a solo command 2 may restore the scalar output but fails the intended manner. Calling that a preservation of the same complete will is a content substitution. The matrix checks this separately.

### Knowledge is an accompaniment rather than a productive input

An independently fixed symmetric plan can issue command 1 whether its accompanying relational knowledge says peer 1 or peer 0. Allowing the knowledge to remain factive under suppression while holding power, end and command fixed does not repair the additive output: it remains 1 rather than 2. The analogous OR plan does preserve output 1 with the same productive command and truthful covarying knowledge.

This control respects the source's actual fixed-condition discussion, which names will, movement and instruments rather than requiring every known fact to stay unchanged. It also exposes a separate identity question: assigning a fixed identifier to a knowing token whose relational content covaries does not prove that its whole intrinsic content is numerically unchanged. Nor does content covariance alone prove external receipt of a new perfection. The model treats both readings as proposals rather than settling the metaphysics of original cognition.

### Why full-state entailment cannot supply the missing productive bridge

A passive knower has no productive command or support but an exact record of output. Any two factive worlds with the same complete record have the same output. Lean proves this for arbitrary world and value types. Therefore entailment from an entire omniscient state would count this observer as a complete determinant if used without an independent productive relevance condition. The source's decisive-will/power argument is plainly stronger than that. This is a reason to strengthen the semantic interface, not to deny the source's causal argument by freezing irrelevant epistemic content.

The suppression comparisons remain diagnostics. The source's power, necessity and wisdom package has not certified all of them as accessible worlds; neither has a counterpossible diagnostic been ruled out merely because it is not such a world.

## 7. Temporal renewal does not automatically supply alignment

Three autonomous Boolean transition rules were examined over bi-infinite pair histories. A constant rule has only its concordant recurrent state. Identity allows four constant pair histories, including discordant ones. Negation allows both aligned and opposite-phase two-cycles. Each bearer uses the same transition rule without reading the peer, but the shared rule alone does not align their histories.

This is a small concrete distinction, not a theory of divine succession. It corresponds to T17's caution: locally determinate renewed acts do not by themselves determine one complete history, while absence of a first state does not establish multiple metaphysically possible histories. A justified global boundary, symmetry, convergence or unique-history principle would be further work; no unearned first state or shared phase has been supplied.

## 8. What these results establish about power concentration

Either same bearer can already issue the sum of the two additive commands. There is no operation newly acquired through cooperation in this family. The new planning test does not alter that conclusion: knowing a peer's contribution may select a different use of one's already full capacity without receiving capacity from the peer.

The source can still reject the cooperative actual exercise as lacking the required perfection of original efficacy. That objection is not answered by an unexercised solo option. Conversely, the source's accepted exclusion of acquired constitutive perfection does not, without a further argument, turn every dependent external product or variable act token into such an acquisition. The present work identifies exactly where these classifications must be joined rather than treating either as a default definition.

## 9. Obligations still requiring substantive judgement

1. **Original practical reasons and relational goods:** derive, justify or defeat a choice principle for the same ultimate bearers that distinguishes productive allocations. A shared uniquely best output is insufficient by the fiber theorem. Whole-history uniqueness, pure actual efficacy, independent-original participation as a relational end, or intrinsic de re roles could matter, but none is established merely by the finite tests.
2. **Original cognition:** interpret comprehensive true knowledge and its explanatory direction without silently making a future created event a donor of constitutive perfection. Identity frames alone do not meet this requirement.
3. **Complete determination:** decide whether the source's required actuality is independently sufficient productive exercise, whole original provision, a directionally explained efficacy relation, or some other source-defensible notion. Outcome guarantee and bare full-state necessity are both too weak to settle it.
4. **Whole-effect identity and support:** resolve whether an unchanged numerical created effect can have the relevant alternative actual support histories without omitted created requisites or a covert third efficient mechanism. The provision investigation owns this interface.
5. **Perfect actual source role:** assess the positive source norm that an ultimate source's actuality cannot require another's completion, together with the objection distinguishing external effect conditions, intentional reasons and constitutive receipt. This is a philosophical appraisal of an actual claimed perfection, not a request to add it as a convenient theorem antecedent.

These are an obligation map for owner judgement, not a closure declaration. The report has gone beyond a conservative factive label and a shared-score example, but it has not earned an unrestricted theorem that concord either must or cannot be efficiently conditioned. Further work should target the stated source-semantic warrants rather than merely enlarging the numeric domains.
