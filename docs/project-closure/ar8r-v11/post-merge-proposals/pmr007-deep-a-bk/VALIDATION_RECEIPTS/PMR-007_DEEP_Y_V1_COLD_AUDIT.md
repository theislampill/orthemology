## Findings

### Y-F01 — proper-function scope is too broad

The orbit theorem decides when one binary truth/world-fit coordinate is
recoverable from an extensional reduct. It does not characterize the whole of
proper function, which may also require history, environment, teleology,
operation, defeat, and warrant. The title and theorem language must say
**truth-linked proper-function coordinate**, not proper function simpliciter.

### Y-F02 — automorphism preservation is underspecified

The V1 text says `G` acts by automorphisms of `E` but does not state that every
registered account-relative evidence relation is preserved. A label swap is a
valid symmetry only relative to a reduct whose frequency, causal-role,
organizational, history, objective, and performance relations are all preserved.
The repaired setting must define the reduct and its automorphism condition.

### Y-F03 — “semantic anchor necessity” is nonunique and model-relative

The proof establishes only that an exact classifier needs information that
splits every truth-nonconstant orbit. Calling that information *the* semantic
anchor risks implying a unique metaphysical object. The repaired theorem must
use `truth-separating enrichment` as the formal term and reserve semantic anchor
for one interpreted witness.

### Y-F04 — selected-effect and design require because-of typing

Selected-effect function may include a historical `selected because of effect`
relation, and design may include an intentional aim. The V1 applications must
not say those accounts are extensional simpliciter. They fail to fix epistemic
truth function only when the registered because-of/aim relation is not itself
truth-directed and independently eligible.

### Y-F05 — definability is not validity or authority

Refining an orbit by a declared truth label makes exact classification possible,
but does not establish that the label is true, that its referent is correctly
identified, that the target is authoritative, or that the anchor is noncircular.
Deep W/X guards must be carried explicitly.

### Y-F06 — impersonal positive control needs an authority ceiling

The impersonal anchor-law model is a formal/model-class control. It shows
nonentailment over the declared class; it does not demonstrate metaphysical
possibility, actuality, or equal prior probability.

### Y-F07 — source and OSM relations need exact custody

The repaired packet must identify exact current-main and attached-source owners:
Candidate E/G, the proper-function matrix, T254/T255 ancestry receipt, Deep P/W/X,
and the Sun et al. source. OSM may supply permutation/trajectory constraints but
not semantic truth or proper-function validation. Track-N may supply a
source-relative anchor only under its authentication and referent-identification
ceilings.

## Required repair

1. Rename the formal target to `TL`, the truth-linked coordinate of epistemic
   proper function.
2. Define `Aut(E)` as preserving all relations in the declared reduct.
3. Replace unique “anchor necessity” language with truth-separating enrichment.
4. Type selected-effect and design because-of/aim relations.
5. Carry target-key authority, semantic-contract, referent, and world-validity
   guards separately.
6. Mark the impersonal model as model-relative.
7. Add exact source/hash and theorem-family custody.
