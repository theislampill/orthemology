## Exact proof review

For the active unguarded multiplicities at issued AB, the valuation matrix, with rows 2, 3, 5 and columns A, B, AB, is

    -1   1  -1
     0  -1  -1
     0   0   1

Its determinant is 1. Hence its integer exponent vector determines all three counts. The stated formulas agree with exact rational Gaussian elimination. Solo counts then determine the two guarded counts by subtraction. Positivity, exact rational reconstruction and nonnegative-count tests are appropriate membership checks; they do not establish the truth of the generative interpretation.

For multiple effects, no effect in U occurs precisely when every enabled route whose output intersects U fails. Independent route successes give the same product law with hit counts substituted for multiplicities. Complementing the hit query yields the subset-sum transform, and Boolean inversion recovers each nonempty output subset's count. The proof neither recovers numerical occurrence IDs nor identifies unrecorded intermediate causal history.

The three profile-omission witnesses work even with variable rates at every retained profile. Each additional route has an enabling condition true only at the omitted profile. The claim properly excludes pooled profiles, alternate encodings and narrower classes.

The signed output-coordinate witness is correct, including the U0 = E case where the complement is empty. Independent rational linear-system inversion reproduces the proposed signed vector for every nonempty omitted subset through five effects. Splitting its signs yields valid nonnegative inventories. The common baseline is sufficient to preserve the complete unattenuated endpoint catalogue.

## Required information-channel precision

The coordinate result means the observer receives only exact values q_S(U) for the retained U values. It does not mean that an experiment retaining the full joint endpoint law loses information merely because one derived column is deleted. A missing absence probability is computed from that law by summing endpoint masses. Likewise, retaining raw paired endpoint observations permits estimating it again.

The independent control checks an explicit two-effect witness: both retained singleton absence probabilities agree while the endpoint laws differ at the empty endpoint. The omitted joint-absence coordinate is immediately recoverable from those full laws. Thus the lower bound is a statement about a deliberately restricted exact-statistic oracle, not a universal lower bound on measurement protocols or encodings. The author has now stated this explicitly in both THEOREMS.md and RESULT.md.

## Gate and source-transport limits

The inherited coalescent, redundant and priority programs supply the deterministic support fixtures. They do not supply the calibrated stochastic actuator. All incidence gates, calibration, cross-occurrence independence, guard freezing and alias interpretation remain external premises.

The exact recovery needs the stated independent route-success laws. Independent incidence gates are one implementation. A single independent gate per route with success probability 1/2, 1/3 or 1/6, selected by that route's root support, produces identical endpoint laws. Consequently neither endpoint observations nor the theorem identify a physical incidence-gate architecture. The author replaced the overly strong "required" phrasing, added an equivalent support-calibrated whole-route mode, and added a corresponding control. Uniform route rates and a shared gate per bearer still have the reported obstructions.

The reactive-context preservation test checks fixed diagonal intrinsic exercises before attenuation. That is a legitimate, limited embedding. It does not preserve complete decisive willing in attenuated worlds or establish that those worlds are allowed by the intended source interpretation.

Two additional controls expose the boundary:

1. In a two-effect reactive context, one bundled route and separate per-effect routes have the same unattenuated event supports, but different joint attenuated endpoint laws. The inherited per-event proof/support records alone do not choose the stochastic bundling.
2. A compatible non-diagonal exercise pair can meet its joint target while fixed-act suppression changes its solo endpoint. Re-solving the response policy can restore the target, but changes the remaining intrinsic setting. That second comparison cannot be advertised as suppression with the original exercise held fixed.

These are limits on transport. They are not counterexamples to the stated conditional probability theorem.

Catalogue completeness is an equally load-bearing boundary. A genuine route that emits only effects outside the observed catalogue leaves every observed absence probability unchanged, even under perfectly calibrated independent gates. Such a route is outside the theorem's declared nonempty-output-subset-of-E class; it is not excluded by the data. The recovered anonymous histogram therefore does not recover full actual ground or the complete created history. The live RESULT.md correctly leaves catalogue completeness, hidden correlated causes, causal realism and source-level complete actual production unestablished.

## Finite-sample review

The k versus k+1 A-route total-variation separation is exactly 2^(-k-1) per informative fixed-rate trial. The product coupling bound and equal-prior discrimination inequality yield the stated necessary sample budget. Count n here as the total number of informative trials; if collecting n trials separately at A and AB, there are 2n informative draws for this pair. This does not supply a lower bound against freely adaptive rates.

For a known total count bound K, all panel probabilities have denominator dividing 6^K. Distinct models therefore have sup-norm panel separation at least 6^(-K). On the event that every coordinate error is less than one-third that separation, the true panel is the unique nearest panel in the sup norm. The variance bound and union bound give the stated sufficient probability guarantee for 0 < delta < 1. No within-trial independence across effect queries is needed. Approximate empirical rationals must not be factored as though they were the exact population probabilities.

In the independent exhaustive catalogue with two effects and at most two total routes, all 136 models have distinct panels, lie on the denominator-36 grid, and have observed minimum sup separation 1/12. That finite check supports implementation consistency; it is not the general proof or an optimal universal separation claim.
