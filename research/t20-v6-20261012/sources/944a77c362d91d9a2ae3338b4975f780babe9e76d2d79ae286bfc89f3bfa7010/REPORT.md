## The inherited cumulative warrant application

There are two different possible gains. First, an encountered performance can supply genuinely new evidence about a productive agent or its exercised capacity. Second, it may provide another operative route to a conclusion already known. Neither gain requires counting the producer's assertion and the observed performance as two independent testimonies. Nor does a new route automatically raise every numerical confidence or retrospectively validate an earlier recognition mechanism.

The inherited positive sufficient-case architecture remains specific: **a content-bearing specification and result must be available to an adequate comparison; their connection to the proposed production must be supported; and that comparison and causal ground must actually inform the conclusion.** The acquisition must not obtain its entire support for the disputed success from the very testimonial trust it purports independently to establish. These conditions can be satisfied without prior knowledge that all cognition is reliable. They concern what makes the inference work, not additional reflective duties imposed on an ordinary knower.

Where the intended gain is instead source reliability across a domain, a token-performance argument is not enough by its entailment alone. Any further inductive application must establish its own relevant scope and evidential route; merely accumulating self-certified successes does not supply that argument. This report does not settle the general philosophical status of every partly self-reliant calibration procedure.
