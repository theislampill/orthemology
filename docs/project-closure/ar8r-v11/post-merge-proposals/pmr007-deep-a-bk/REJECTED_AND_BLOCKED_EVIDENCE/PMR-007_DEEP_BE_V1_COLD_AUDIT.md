## Findings

### BE-F01 — interaction discrimination is not yet unification evidence

A joint law can differ while both architectures remain impersonal, distributed,
or brute.  A cross-lane interaction is eligible architecture-discrimination
evidence only when it was independently predicted and the rival cannot match it
under the matched model class.  It is not derivational or explanatory unity by
itself.

```text
severity: BLOCKING
repair: replace unification-credit language with an anti-unification firewall and a predictive-discriminator gate
```

### BE-F02 — one-coordinate parity and proper-subset parity are different guards

V1 correctly states both results but risks sliding from equal coordinate
marginals in CJID-1A to the much stronger equal-all-proper-marginals premise in
CJID-1C.  The top-order parity characterization does not follow from equal
single-coordinate marginals alone.

```text
severity: BLOCKING_SCOPE
repair: separate the two tiers in the theorem statement, model owner, and examples
```

### BE-F03 — joint sampling and unit identity are load-bearing

A joint law is meaningful only after the coordinates are bound to one declared
sampling unit, version, intervention epoch, source/world contract, and
provenance record.  Joining records from different mice, agents, worlds,
versions, or counterfactual regimes can manufacture a coupling.

```text
severity: BLOCKING
repair: promote the joint-unit and custody contract to the typed setting
```

### BE-F04 — the covariance formula is binary and coding-dependent

`TV=2|Delta Cov|` uses 0/1 coding, binary coordinates, and equal marginals.  It
must not be presented as a general covariance characterization.

```text
severity: BLOCKING_SCOPE
repair: state the coding and guard exactly and deny general covariance transfer
```

### BE-F05 — the top-order proof needs an explicit basis argument

The one-dimensionality claim is correct, but V1's proof sketch should identify
the zero-sum basis and show why every proper marginal annihilates all lower
Walsh coordinates.

```text
severity: BLOCKING_PROOF_EXPOSITION
repair: give a finite tensor/Walsh proof and a separate separable-test corollary
```

### BE-F06 — predictive independence and post-selection are under-typed

An interaction fitted after inspecting the outcome can discriminate the fitted
sample without supporting the architecture.  The candidate must require a
frozen architecture-conditioned joint prediction or a held-out enlarged
experiment, together with the Deep AN prior/likelihood and Deep AO
representation guards.

```text
severity: BLOCKING_EVIDENCE
repair: add preregistration/held-out and common-experiment requirements
```

### BE-F07 — OSM and PRH do not supply metaphysical architecture likelihoods

The OSM study supports a model-comparison lesson at its hippocampal task scope;
PRH supports representation-alignment hypotheses and limitations.  Neither
paper conditions data on the personal-versus-impersonal architecture pair.

```text
severity: BLOCKING_AUTHORITY

```

The primary checker exhausts small binary grids and directly implements the
candidate formulas.  Admission requires an independent Walsh-transform or
linear-algebra rereview across higher arities and random valid parity
perturbations.

