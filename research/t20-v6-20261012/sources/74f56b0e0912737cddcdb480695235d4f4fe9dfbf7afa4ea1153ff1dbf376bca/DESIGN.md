# Intrinsic willing, reasons and foreknowledge: continuation design

Started 2026-10-07 UTC. This is a new research branch; no archived judgement is revised here and no owner-level closure is declared.

## Questions fixed before implementation

1. Can source-neutral objective wisdom yield a unique created end without yielding a unique productive allocation? If so, factive foreknowledge must be tested as a possible selector, not merely appended as a label.
2. When each agent's full knowledge includes the other's exercise, is that exercise an epistemic truth condition, a contrastive reason-input, an efficient enabling cause, or constitutive donated perfection? These are separate relations.
3. Can an explicit, agent-neutral rule derive cooperation or redundant activity from independently specified reasons without a shared free preference parameter? Does the rule have source warrant?
4. Does necessity of concord in admissible worlds establish dependence under diagnostic interventions? Complete output and original productive support remain separate interfaces.
5. Can temporal renewal remove or merely relocate the missing determination?

## Source controls

Direct T17 P65–68, P74–81, P108–120 are retained. Direct Arabic Minhaj V03L0263–0276 and 2:168–172 distinguish original will, mutual productive conditioning, common benefit, factive foreknowledge, and uncaused concomitance. The source's direct rejection of necessary concord is not erased. Its inferential move to each will's being part of what makes the other willing is the tested bridge.

## Represented sorts

- Rigid bearer A/B; intrinsic power interpreter; intrinsic command/act; complete created output; objective reason; knowledge relation; productive dependence; reason-input dependence.
- Command availability is a Cartesian operation domain used to inspect constructors, not a claim that contrary complete divine wills are jointly metaphysically admissible.
- Wisdom-admissible profiles are derived from explicit objective and policy rules. They are not initially declared diagonal.
- Epistemic frames are sets of complete profiles with equivalence relations. Knowledge means truth in every accessible profile and is tested for factivity. Prechoice and postchoice information are distinct.
- A future occurrence is never evaluated as a prior efficient donor merely because it appears in knowledge content.
- Agent acts are not silently included in the created-output sort. Actual emitted created traces, if any, must be accounted for by the production collaborator's whole-effect interface.

## Rival rules

A. Outcome-optimal plans: maximize a source-neutral objective of created output. Test fiber multiplicity, unique end versus unique allocation, and whether exact foreknowledge removes fixed-point multiplicity.
B. Anonymous intrinsic planning: select the symmetric optimal allocation if unique. This introduces a disclosed normative choice rule; it is not yet source-authorized wisdom. Test failure on indivisible odd targets and success on rational divisible targets.
C. Individually dominance-constrained wisdom: derive allowed commands from the pointwise objective comparison over operational rival commands, without treating those diagnostic profiles as jointly admissible wills. Test strict/weak dominance and context restrictions. This may derive concord, but dominance in impossible contexts needs separate warrant.
D. Mutual forecast-responsive choice: solve factive best-response equations; test zero, one and multiple fixed points. A mathematical fixed point does not establish productive well-foundedness.
E. Autonomous recurrent choices: distinguish same intrinsic transition rule from phase alignment across a bi-infinite history; no first state is silently supplied.

## Evidence discipline

Logs record actual step starts/completions and tool receipt times, not a continuously active research envelope. Packing and waiting are not research-time evidence. Further viable discriminations remain open after each test.
