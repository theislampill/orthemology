# Does causal nonduplication add a route to essential independence?

7 October 2026. Bounded public projection of the T20 assessment. The accepted T17 conclusion is unchanged. Primary-source exposition is omitted; source attribution and role-specific reading scope are retained in CND_SOURCE_SCOPE_PUBLIC.json.

## Result

The new productive route has a valid conditional form, but its strongest interpretation does not add independent warrant to T17's already accepted essential independence of the same actual Necessary Being. The contribution of this inquiry is an exact premise audit: it identifies the role-retention and complete-provision premises needed by a CND route, proves the consequence without self-production, and separates it from necessary existence plus standing power with a nonvacuous formal assignment.

This result preserves T17's affirmative judgement. Necessary existence and essential whole-existence independence are already sufficiently warranted there under substantive rational principles. A proposed additional argument can fail to improve that warrant without withdrawing it. Neither the described received alternative nor a successful Lean check establishes a metaphysically possible rival or equal evidential support.

## 1. Exact starting witness and prior ownership

Fix the actual particular g warranted in T17. Its actual whole-existence originality follows from original existential completion and the qualified-resource/bearer principles; its necessary existence follows through contingency-as-need; its essential independence follows through the separate constitutive judgement. These steps are explicitly separated in T17 P00048–P00061. The constitutive argument says that the source of whole existential adequacy concerns this reality's constitution, rather than a manner subsequently added to an already actual neutral bearer. That rationale, its favourable appraisal and its recognised identity-theoretic objection are inherited findings.

T18 P00012–P00018 leaves that restricted affirmative profile intact while withholding an unrestricted extension through every autonomous noncausal explanatory fact. T19 P00051–P00056 independently appraises CND: entire original production of the same concrete contribution in the same causal respect is not primitively duplicated. That is a further substantive judgement about productive individuation, not a prohibition on multiple reasons, conditions or genuine levels of agency. T19 P00081–P00085 distinguishes proximate performance, participation in an original productive account and complete original provision. Those exact distinctions control this proposed transfer.

The old distinction between necessary existence and essential nonreceipt is not the new result. The new question is whether the newly accepted productive principle improves the reason for the latter. Nor does this inquiry reopen the actual-witness step owned by T17 or the unrestricted noncausal extension closed in T18.

## 2. Source argument and candidate reconstruction

The author's source-reading identities and ranges are recorded in CND_SOURCE_SCOPE_PUBLIC.json. This mathematical projection omits the primary capsule and source-specific exposition for separate consolidated selection. It makes no new primary-reading or source-warrant claim.

The source-to-candidate transition is analytical. T19 adds CND. The proposed new claim is that preserving an original productive role for this same necessary g could exclude g's being wholly supplied elsewhere. The point is to test whether this avoids the constitutive premise or merely reproduces its modal burden through productive language.

### First proposal: an extra supplier has no work left

The positive thought is worth taking seriously. If g has complete intrinsic existential adequacy, an additional alleged supplier must do some intelligible existential work. Supplying a contingent state does not supply g's whole existence. A merely unused alternative supplies nothing actual. Supplying the whole actuality while retaining complete intrinsic adequacy demands an account of the relationship between those two sufficient bases.

CND reinforces the request for determinate productive content, but does not itself settle this mixed case. Intrinsic existential adequacy is not an original production of g by g. Counting it as the first production would make self-sufficiency into efficient self-production. A broader principle excluding concurrent intrinsic adequacy and foreign whole supply is a different premise. Even that exclusion concerns coexistence at a state; exclusion of a switch between those modes invokes the already accepted constitutive rationale. T17 P00058–P00061 owns that inference.

### Stronger proposal: reason through g's genuine effects

This avoids self-production. Suppose the same g retains some entire original productive role in every relevant alternative. If g were wholly received there, an original supplier's provision would also enter the provenance of that effect. If it supplied the entire same contribution in the same respect, CND would identify it with g, contradicting its being a distinct supplier. The provisos about entirety, identity and respect are premises to establish, not verbal qualifications to discard.

## 3. Exact production-only lemma

Let w range over relevant alternatives, g designate the same particular throughout, e range over concrete contribution occurrences, and r fix the causal respect. The occurrence identity must include whatever timing matters to the attribution. O(w,a,e,r) means that a provides an entire original production of e in r. It is not defined as whole-existence nonreceipt of a. R(w,g) means receipt of g's whole existential actuality. For a productive-only conclusion it must be read as whole productive receipt; a broader ontological reading incurs an additional typing burden.

The premises are:

1. CND: O(w,a,e,r) and O(w,b,e,r) imply a=b.
2. PRI, productive-role retention: for every relevant w, there are e and r with O(w,g,e,r).
3. SUP, complete matching attribution: if R(w,g) and O(w,g,e,r), some h distinct from g satisfies O(w,h,e,r).

Take any relevant w and suppose R(w,g). PRI supplies a contribution and respect. SUP supplies a distinct source of that exact contribution in that exact respect at that same w. CND gives identity, a contradiction. Thus g is wholly unreceived at every relevant alternative.

The contribution can vary between alternatives: the premise is for every w there exists e,r, not one e,r in every w. No fixed transworld act token, unique history or simultaneous performance of all possible acts is required. A fixed effect label alone would not preserve the original efficacy; PRI asserts the efficacy itself. Likewise an effect under one respect and a supplier's contribution under another do not trigger CND.

The short Lean proof is `original_role_excludes_reception` in `CNDModalScope.lean`. `receipt_excludes_original_role` gives the diagnostic converse under CND and SUP: a received alternative cannot retain that original role. The first theorem does not use actual nonreceipt. It does not separately use necessary existence if PRI already requires an actual role throughout. If PRI is restricted to alternatives in which g exists, necessary existence discharges that restriction. This makes the location of the modal work explicit.

## 4. Discriminating assignments

The compiled separation uses two alternatives, two rigid source identities and nonempty production in each alternative. All alternatives are relevant, so no failure of symmetry, transitivity or reflexivity explains the result. g exists and has standing power in both. Actually g originally supplies contribution e, while h supplies another contribution. Elsewhere h originally supplies the contributions, g receives its whole actuality and performs a real derived exercise. A separate finite lemma checks g's exercised efficacy in both alternatives, so the diagnostic is not confined to an idle power label. CND holds in every alternative. Actual g is unreceived. Essential nonreceipt nevertheless fails in this interpretation of the selected formulas, because g's original role is not retained.

This assignment deliberately leaves out T17's constitutive mode-invariance premise, which excludes the switch. It is not an interpretation of all accepted T17 premises and is not advanced as a possible universe. Its use is to identify what CND does not supply. Retaining the name g, its existence, a power label and even an effect label does not preserve the provenance of its exercised efficacy.

A second assignment isolates complete attribution. Let g retain O in both alternatives, while h merely participates in the original account of the received alternative. CND and PRI can then hold while essential nonreceipt fails. The weak participation relation is not O. This purely relational interpretation does not purport to preserve strong original provenance together with T17's qualified-resource transport; it shows precisely why a participation-only premise cannot substitute for SUP.

A third control separates token-state receipt from whole receipt. The finite check allows the former predicate while denying the latter and retaining CND and original production. That assignment is a typing test, not a claim that every possible received token fits the stronger source-mode norm. Substantively, T19 P00077 and P00090–P00091 already distinguish a received state or occasion from received whole existence. An earlier effect occasioning a later act is not an efficient production of the bearer's existence or original power.

The accompanying executable scenarios also vary effect identity and causal respect. Different effects or respects admit two source attributions without violating CND; these variations expose errors that a bare count of source names would miss.

## 5. Does PRI improve the warrant?

On the strong reading, original production employs genuinely original existential resources instantiated in g. T17 P00050–P00051 already warrants that whole-bearer receipt carries its intrinsic resource's instantiation with it. The resource may be real, essential and locally sufficient while its possession remains received. Consequently, under that transport, an original role in every alternative excludes whole receipt in every alternative without CND or a recovered second producer.

PRI need not literally define essential independence, and an independent argument for PRI would not automatically be circular. But relative to the accepted transport it is already strong enough to do the required modal work. The sources read here do not newly establish PRI through CND. T19's source-mode premise at P00060 starts with an essentially independent qualified original; applying that norm to prove the same qualification would reuse a premise. Necessary generic activity, even if granted, says that activity occurs. It does not by itself establish that its existential possession remains original through every alternative. Standing power says still less about actual provenance.

On the weaker reading, g initiates or sufficiently performs a proximate act. Received organisms and agents can do that. The original supplier and the proximate actor are then distinct levels of agency, not two entire original productions in the same respect. Treating both as O silently alters the relation and contradicts CND's stated permission for genuine secondary agency. The weaker reading makes PRI easier to defend at the cost of losing SUP's relevance.

SUP has further independent costs. An original basis need not be one complete original supplier of the selected contribution. T17 P00045–P00054 distinguishes a minimal witness from complete common coverage; T19 P00082 supplies mediate participation, and P00083 adds more for complete provision. A plural basis cannot simply be renamed one h. Nor can an actual common-field result be applied to every alternative without the relevant premises there. Finally, if reception includes all ontological derivation, turning every such receipt into a productive h would reintroduce the broader bridge not earned in T18.

The strongest positive CND reconstruction therefore clarifies a genuine sufficient condition but does not presently improve on the accepted constitutive route. It is not judged defective because it is non-deductive, controversial or authorially asserted. The issue is the content of its proposed independent warrant: either it already imports the needed existential provenance or it lacks the production relation required by its concluding step.

## 6. Downstream relational scope

This result does not establish that original giving is intrinsically unrestrictable towards every other concrete reality. PRI has the form “in each alternative, some original contribution by this g”. Essential universal coverage would require every relevant other reality to derive from g. Those quantifiers differ even if PRI is warranted. CND constrains overlapping entire productions; it does not create overlaps with disconnected originals.

The broader Akmaliyya comparison remains a separately attributed evaluative argument about relational completeness; its exposition is not reproduced here. Nothing in the present CND transfer adds a reason that every intrinsic restriction needs an external limiter. T17 P00067 expressly rejects that shortcut. A genuinely new premise relating the constitution of original giving to unrestricted relational coverage would need its own evidence; no such premise was acquired in this bounded reading.
