# Prior-art boundary for the action-disclosure extension

Checked 2 October 2026. This is a bounded comparison, not an exhaustive novelty search.

## A direct classical comparator

Rao and Yehudayoff's authored *Communication Complexity* draft, printed p.45
(PDF page index44), considers a sender who knows a half-size subset and a receiver
with no private input who must select one member. It gives the exact ceiling of
log2(n/2+1) bits by an output-range lower bound and minimum-element construction.
The passage immediately precedes Claim1.38 and attributes the surrounding
repeated-relation example to Alon–Orlitsky1995. The verified teaching-hosted copy is:
https://aaignatiev.github.io/teaching/CC-book.pdf

Taking the permitted subset as the complement of the corrupt roots identifies
the same selection mechanism. The present budget-dependent zero-error result is
therefore a programme application/generalisation of a classical covering argument,
not a discovery of a new general communication principle. The exact static
restoration view, root/action semantics and source-warrant conditions supply its
orthemological application; their scientific novelty needs its own assessment.

Alon and Orlitsky's primary *Repeated Communication and Ramsey Graphs*, §3.1,
uses player-sequence covers for repeated subset/team instances. This establishes
relevant coding ancestry. The specific half-set selection paragraph above was
verified in the book; it is not being assigned an unverified theorem number in
the original paper. Author-hosted source:
https://web.math.princeton.edu/~nalon/PDFS/chann91.pdf

## Randomness and nearby problems

The positive-error continuation distinguishes a fixed private-random decoder
codebook from a public random codebook known to the informed sender. The exact
public-menu risk is a hypergeometric sampling quantity; its minimax proof uses
elementary averaging and products. That derivation has research value within the
specified action contract, but no historical-priority claim is established here.
The fixed-codebook general classification remains open outside the proved
boundaries and families in the continuation.

Universal Relation communication bounds must not be imported directly. In the
Kapralov–Nelson–Pachocki–Wang–Woodruff–Yahyazadeh problem, each party has a private
input, and the receiver seeks a differing coordinate; even the subset promise
retains that two-input structure. Here the conditioned common view is fixed and
only the sender learns the remaining corrupt set. Primary reference:
https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.00633

## Consequence for the programme

The worthwhile transfer is precise: a diagnostic message, an action certificate,
and a risk-minimising lottery recommendation have different required content and
warrants. Shared randomness changes the bounded-risk resource, whereas it does not
remove the exact zero-error message threshold. Authentication alone is not sender
truthfulness, and a communication bound is not authority to take a risk. These
interfaces can sharpen local MEN-1/MEN-6 candidates while leaving prior-art,
external specialist review, and every metaphysical R5 condition explicit.
