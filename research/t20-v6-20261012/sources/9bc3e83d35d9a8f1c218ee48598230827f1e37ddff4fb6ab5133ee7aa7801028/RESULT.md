## 1. The central result is differentiated sufficiency

The new work changes the programme in two directions. It supplies stronger constructive interfaces: overlapping audit families now have an exact gradient classification; restricted controls have an exact smaller sufficient record; whole moving grouped corrections have a second-order positive, descending, audit-covariant update; cached checking can be compiled into an attained remaining-cost optimum; and a four-trial uncertainty problem now has exact feasible bodies and solved continuation policies. At the same time it makes several false transports concrete: isolated response profiles need not support mixed histories, small geometric error need not preserve a semantic event, statically cheaper proofs need not reuse paid work, and one original provider does not identify the intermediaries through which its effects are explained.

The organising conclusion is **retain the distinctions needed by the declared task, and prove their preservation under its actual operations**. It is neither “keep everything” nor “present agreement is enough.” Three positive reductions matter:

- Restricted controls can require one extra continuous coordinate where unrestricted controls require three, on the same six-state law family.
- A valid proof cache can be compressed to exact-state availability for optimizing a terminal proof at one snapshot, provided real witnesses remain recoverable.
- Different full continuation laws can have exactly the same optimal ex ante robust prediction policy and value for every bounded horizon.

The corresponding expansions are equally precise. New controls can split the first quotient; source revision can split the second; conditioning on a specific history can destroy the third decision equivalence. These are changes in an interface contract, rather than contradictions between results. [R §§3–6; P §§4–5; U §§6–8]

It is useful to distinguish five preservation demands without pretending they form a universal strict hierarchy: present answers; future answers under a declared operation family; a chosen optimal decision/value; reconstructible witnesses; and effective, quantitatively stable execution. The implications depend on the domain, observables, legal policies, cost model, representation and topology. Preserving every lawful policy-loss functional certainly permits the same optimization over the same policy class. Preserving only one optimum does not reconstruct those functionals. Endpoint existence can be preserved while the submitted witnesses fail to compose. A uniquely identified law can still have a discontinuous event query.

This extends the inherited realisation–observation–operation–execution checklist with actual discriminating examples. It does not prove a single completeness theorem for all four stages. [B; F]

## 2. Common refinement has three different mathematical causes

### 2.1 What overlapping audits now force

Fix the full labelled domain

    Δ° × ∏ X_i,
    g = (1/α) Σ dw_i²/w_i + Σ w_i ||dx_i||²,

with α>0, connected open Euclidean position factors and C² energy E. For each partition P, admit **every** positive likelihood multiplier constant on its blocks. If R is the common refinement of finitely many partitions, simultaneous gradient covariance under those partition families is equivalent to

    E = C + Σ_(A in R) m_A V_A(r_A,x_A).

Thus interaction survives inside a refinement cell and nowhere across its cells. Crossing rows and columns on four labels have singleton refinement and force E=C+Σw_iV_i(x_i). [A §3]

The mechanism is stronger than group generation. Homogeneously extend E to independent positive masses by F(q,x)=(Σq)E(q/Σq,x). Each inherited partition classification gives an additive decomposition of F. Intersecting these decompositions makes each differential depend only on its common-refinement cell; homogeneous summands yield the displayed energy. This uses gradient integrability, the particular metric and the full connected product domain.

Row and column audit words themselves still preserve the odds ratio w11 w22/(w12 w21). They do not generate all singleton multipliers. A general equivariant vector field can depend on that ratio; the report gives an explicit one that is not a gradient for this metric. Conversely an odds-ratio-invariant energy need not have an equivariant gradient because the audits are not metric isometries. Hence the older action-generation obstruction survives while the previously unclassified gradient intersection is settled. The distinction changes a practical design decision: a system demanding both row and column covariance cannot retain a row-only interaction merely because its group orbit has a surviving invariant. [A §4; AR §§1–2]

### 2.2 Closed-convex instrument stability reaches refinement for another reason

ET-23 starts with a finite proper cone, its canonical indecomposable summands, a strictly positive normalizer and complete positive instruments summing to identity. Each branch acts by a scalar on each summand. Group summands by their entire outcome columns. A nonempty closed convex normalized family stable under several instruments is stable under conditioning on their common-refinement cells, and its least such hull from a state is the convex hull of its occupied cell conditionals. [A §5]

This is a direct application of ET-23, not another independent rigidity theorem: tag each instrument's outcomes and mix the instruments with fixed positive weights. The tagged instrument has exactly the common-refinement column partition; apply the inherited theorem. The short tagged proof was supplied collaboratively by the independent reviewer, while the alternative extreme-point and successive-conditioning proofs were reviewed separately.

The hull is not an attainable-history claim. Positive row/column outcomes retain odds ratio along each word; convex mixtures already need not, and boundary points may require closure. Nor does the cone supply the Fisher–transport metric. The valid cone-to-gradient application adds that geometry explicitly and enlarges from fixed outcomes to the full continuous multiplier family. An abstract order isomorphism must carry the metric too. [A §§5–6; AR §3]

### 2.3 Controlled histories reach refinement through actual concatenation

For fixed simplex support columns κ_j and positive law w, consider the inherited controlled Fisher equation

    wdot_j = −w_j(g·κ_j − g·q),   q=Σw_jκ_j,   g∈U.

Group columns having the same functional g↦g·κ_j on U. The exact record is the **full-output unnormalised barycentre** in each group,

It preserves every q-history under the allowed piecewise-constant controls. Conversely those histories determine the b_a: one separating control identifies the normalizer from its scalar projection of q, then distinct exponential coefficients identify all barycentres. Exact continuous trajectories are used; this is not a finite-sample identification result. For finitely many control subspaces with arbitrary real amplitudes and finite concatenations, integrated parameters fill their sum U. Their signatures therefore refine jointly. Crucially, stacking the sufficient records for isolated control families need not yield the jointly sufficient record. ## 3. A correction must preserve both its operation square and its promised observations

### 3.1 The new whole-state numerical interface

For a fixed retained partition, the classified energy and metric split as

    E=C+Σ_g m_g V_g(y_g),
    ydot_g=−grad_(h_g) V_g,
    mdot_g=−αm_g(V_g−Σ_k m_k V_k).

The internal y_g contains conditional weights and positions. Advance it by the qualified second-order descending discrete-gradient method. Write v_g=V_g(y_g), v'_g=V_g(y'_g), c_g=(v_g+v'_g)/2 and b=min_g c_g over the retained catalogue. Then use

    m'_g ∝ m_g / [1+αh(c_g−b)+(αh(c_g−b))²/2].

The elementary product identity

    E'−E=(m'−m)·c + (m'+m)·(v'−v)/2

joins decreasing-factor covariance descent to each internal descent. Trapezoidal accumulation of the changing loss supplies whole-state order two; evaluating only the final loss generally leaves an O(h²) local mass error even when both the internal solver and frozen-loss selection are individually second order. [J §§1–5]

The construction uses the existing grouped classification, positive reweighting and reciprocal Taylor machinery, plus the classical Gonzalez discrete gradient. Its accountable contribution here is the joined interface with explicit local solve, residual, descent and order obligations. The unavailable reported R3 could overlap; without its actual bytes no exhaustive internal priority comparison is possible. This packet supplies an independently accessible derivation, not a recovery or verification of R3, and no field-priority claim follows.

Audit covariance is an algorithmic contract, not just a formula identity. Internal root choice, stopping tolerances, acceptance tests, precision choices, baseline and time mesh must remain unchanged when an admitted audit changes group masses. A mass-dependent step can change positions, which a weight-only audit cannot reproduce. General implicit solving requires the certified local branch; at stationary input an arbitrary other root may move and lower energy. Compact interior control supplies the finite-horizon order theorem. Large-step positivity alone proves neither accuracy, collision avoidance nor rank preservation. [J §§2–6; JR]

There is a useful conditional application of the preceding refinement theorem: when E meets the full A-contract for several partitions, express it on the common-refinement cells and instantiate this update on those cells. For the Gonzalez construction, additionally require C³ chart potentials and C² mobility on its certified neighborhoods; A alone supplies only C² potentials. Alternatively supply internal second-order descent maps with J's required uniform bounds. Require all root, tolerance, precision and mesh choices to be invariant under the full refinement-cell mass action, not merely the original partition actions. With these extra hypotheses and J's compactness guards the resulting algorithm commutes with every positive refinement-cell audit, hence every original partition audit. This is a straightforward application of the two results, **not** a claim that an implementation built in an arbitrary coarser chart acquires the finer symmetry automatically. [A §3; J §§1–4]

### 3.2 Accurate law evolution does not mean all semantic queries are accurate

For a common Euclidean position carrier on a bounded region, the new update has order two in labelled coordinates, fixed smooth observables and W₁. It need not converge in total variation: different Dirac locations have TV distance one however small their coordinate difference. Nor is W₂ generically order two with variable masses: an O(h²) error in mass between two separated points gives W₂ of order h. The fixed-support high-order TV theorem and this moving-support W₁ theorem are compatible because they concern different operation and observation contracts. [J §6]

The semantic-geometry result makes the same issue visible in the **exact flow**, without numerical error. Specialize the inherited fixed-marginal Gaussian correction to

    μ_t=Law(Y, sqrt(s(t))G),
    s(t)=2(e^(3t)−1)/(2e^(3t)+1),   μ_0=Law(Y,0),

where Y,G are independent standard Gaussians. The whole Y-marginal is preserved, positive-time KL decreases, every polynomial moment is continuous, and W₂(μ_t,μ_0)²=s(t). For the fixed predicates A_a={z≠a}, a≠0, every A_a has probability one at every time. Their measurable universal conjunction H={z=0} has probability one initially and zero immediately afterward. [S §§3–5]

There are two safeguards against misreading this example. First, its start is singular with infinite KL. Original-time action diverges; a quadratic time reparametrization gives small finite-action endpoint competitors, not the unchanged gradient equation. It is not a counterexample under positive-density, finite-initial-energy hypotheses. Second, the repertoire is uncountable; countably many conull events remain conull. Finite source strings do not automatically enumerate the repertoire, although a separately interpreted universal quantifier may range over it. [SR §§2,6]

The law here is moment-determinate and even identified by its variance. The failure is not lack of enough parameters. The event-mass query is the discontinuous zero-test s↦1_{s=0}; ordinary real approximation names do not uniformly compute it. The full-law CV-R diffuse-mass defect remains one at both endpoints because the Y-marginal was already diffuse. That envelope therefore does not certify preservation of this fixed repertoire.

The positive repairs specify the actual obligation: ν≪μ is equivalent to preserving every μ-conull measurable event; TV bounds control every event probability; a narrower declared event family can use its own query pseudometric. ## 4. Uncertainty matters when it changes this decision, not merely when models differ

### 4.1 A same-target continuation game exposes a genuine difference

The uncertainty result holds fixed the observed history X1=X2=1, the reserved target Y=X4, the optional reading X3, the 0–1 loss, and its price c. Both model classes have exactly the same two-trial law (1/3,1/6,1/6,1/3), so the conditioning event has mass 1/3 everywhere. [U §§2,6]

In the fixed-calibration-mixture class C4, t=E[p³] and s=E[p⁴] range over

    2/9≤t≤5/18,
    12t²−4t+4/9≤s≤−6t²+5t−2/3.

Explicit atomic measures realize every point. An affine policy-loss functional has an exact maximization formula on this curved body. The four-exchangeable class E4 instead has triangle vertices (1/6,0), (1/4,1/4), (1/3,1/3), with t,s interpreted as joint-success probabilities, not an assumed latent calibration. Complete path-law probabilities are affine in these coordinates. This supplies the actual bridge from MOM-11's moment repair to ET-17's lawful common-policy evaluation. [U §§3–5]

For 0<c<1/2, C4 optimally skips and reports 1, with risk 1/3. E4 optimally mixes skip/report 1 with probability 4/5 and query/report 1−X3 with probability 1/5, with risk 2/5+c/5. At c=1/10 that is 21/50, versus E4's best deterministic risk 1/2. Exact primal and dual certificates prove optimality over all private mixtures. A full-support perturbed family retains the policy separation, so zero cells are not its essential cause. [U §6; UR §3]

This is a decision consequence of the representation premise, not merely a missing moment. It cannot be explained by switching targets, a null conditioning event or missing event frequency. The model is selected before the private coin and retained across all branches. Branchwise adverse-model pasting changes the game. Scalar worst-loss pruning also fails: the individually worse query/invert policy is essential to the optimal mixture. A four-trial sufficient state remains a finite-horizon state; fifth-trial controls separate laws agreeing through degree four. [U §§6,8]

### 4.2 A sharp counterweight: richer laws can leave the optimal decision unchanged

Before any observation, fix 0<μ<1 and μ²≤ν≤μ, with positive false-positive/false-negative prices a,b. Reserve a future target bit and allow bounded adaptive readings of other distinct bits, with at least one such reading legal. Deterministic mean endpoints are handled separately by skipping. The moment bounds are essential: a finite-exchangeable negative-covariance specification need not admit any calibration mixture. In both the fixed-calibration-mixture and full finite-exchangeable classes, one observation already attains the minimax risk

    R1=min[b(μ−ν),a(1−2μ+ν)] + min[bν,a(μ−ν)].

With first-query cost c≥0 and later nonnegative costs, the value is min(R0,c+R1), where R0=min(bμ,a(1−μ)). An explicit least-favourable calibration law makes even an oracle revealing p unable to beat R1; the same first-observation rule attains it throughout the larger exchangeable class. One least-favourable law bounds all lawful policies and bounded horizons simultaneously. [U §7; UR §4]

This does not say that one observation reconstructs the law or that no later history can make inquiry useful. The conditioned game above proves the opposite in its own contract. It says that preserving an actual optimization target can require less information than preserving all future predictive distributions. Restricted-control barycentres, snapshot proof-cache availability, and this saturation theorem are three positive examples of task-relative reduction, with different meanings of “sufficient.”

For the foundational inquiry this offers a methodological discipline, not a philosophical stopping theorem: a proposed alternative should be tied to the constructive obligation it changes. A mere mathematical variation need not change a selected decision; an actual dependence change may. The minimax calculation itself does not determine human warrant, relevant philosophical alternatives, fair inquiry costs, or when inquiry is owed. Those require their independently supplied grounds and norms.

## 5. Proof reuse connects inquiry to history without erasing proof identity

### 5.1 The cost of already checked work is a different objective

The inherited static whole-DAG theorem normalizes exact guarded states while reducing fixed nonnegative node charges. The new example executes that very normalizer: static cost falls 14→12, but remaining checking cost rises 2→12 because redirecting a cached parent's premise changes its immutable node identity. The cache-relative optimum instead costs 1. This is not a defect in the static theorem; paid work was outside its objective. [P §3]

For a currently valid dependency-closed cache C, the remaining bill is

    c_C(D)=Σ_(v in F(D)\C) price(v).

Cut a candidate proof at cached nodes and replace each by a zero-price macro with its **exact support** and recoverable witness. Apply the inherited finite whole-DAG compiler, then expand the macros using aligned identities. Cutting and expansion give both optimum inequalities and attained equality. The resulting witness needs at most |J|2^|E| newly checked nodes in addition to its retained cache. Effective enumeration additionally needs decidable guards and exactly computable price comparison; arbitrary fixed nonnegative real prices give attainment without automatically giving an executable comparison procedure. Ordinary support/Pareto pruning before nonmonotone guards remains invalid. [P §4]

At a single snapshot the planner may retain just which exact guarded states have a valid cached witness. That suffices for optimizing *some* terminal proof, not for pricing a specified immutable proof or reproducing every historical certificate. The distinction parallels endpoint existence versus submitted-witness compatibility in explanatory composition below.

Revision needs more. Under the explicit validity law “node v is reusable iff d(v)⊆L,” keep the inclusion-minimal dependency sets per exact state. This antichain exactly preserves macro availability over all declared activations/withdrawals. It is not necessarily minimal for terminal optimized prices, and the supplied helper implements rule-local dependency factors rather than arbitrary occurrence-specific receipt conditions. Rule names, evidence leaves and current validity bits do not authenticate the complete dependencies. [P §5; PR §3]

### 5.2 When acquisition first and checking later really is enough

The postponement theorem is pathwise: successful, non-informative checks may be simulated while queries proceed, then only the chosen terminal proof is checked. This never increases the declared cost if checking does not unlock queries, alter timing or event laws, consume evidence, change prices, or obtain irreplaceable early entitlements, and the terminal proof is reproducible from the terminal snapshot and surviving initial cache. The theorem preserves the actual query history and terminal result. It is a sufficient structural reduction, not a necessary-and-sufficient scheduling classification. [P §6]

That pathwise domination carries to expected loss under each fixed source model and its supremum; it does not legalize different worst models on hypothetical branches. Costs on mutually exclusive branches are paid contingently, while actual shared-prefix checking is sunk. If checking duration affects expiry or response laws, ET-19's joint holding-time/event/next-state contract must be added. The static cache table alone cannot provide it. [P §7]

### 5.3 Typed equality does not collapse the memory contract

HasE/HasJ proof uniqueness is at already fixed typed endpoints. It does not identify external derivation records, paid checks, source versions or actual intermediaries. Explicit Sigma labels remain distinguishable even when their proof field is irrelevant. The finite source-sidecar and rational reply-kernel theorems therefore preserve explicit data and supplied probabilistic contracts; they do not hide provenance inside an erased proof term. [PT §§3–4; P §7]

The CIRS boundary supplies a second positive/negative pair. Under common full-support observation laws, a stationary finite-memory controller can restore every standard exactly when their good-action sets have a common member. The common reachable bottom-component argument proves necessity; a constant common-good action proves sufficiency. The inherited CIRS and Sixteenth controllers permit unbounded likelihood/history memory and can restore disjoint targets. Finite algorithm descriptions are compatible with unbounded data. ## 6. One original provider does not erase the identities needed for local explanation

The foundational baseline remains affirmative at its earned actual-field scope: warranted provisional belief in one complete common original source of the relevant productively connected created order. Same-bearer necessity, essential independence, source-owned veracity and cumulative grounds keep their existing arguments and qualifications. The new mathematical work neither derives those conclusions from a thin graph nor resets them to mere formal possibilities. Universal exclusion of every idle or disconnected original remains stronger than the actual-field judgement. [F §§2–7; G §1]

Now represent actual secondary use by L⊆X×M and R⊆M×Y. Genuine endpoint composition joins on the same intermediary m. If a summary q:M→Z is used instead, composition permits m,n with q(m)=q(n). The summarized relation contains the true relation; it is exact iff every summarized endpoint has a genuine intermediary lift. If q is the common original-provider label, it is constant and the summary composition becomes dom(L)×ran(R). That may invent secondary routes while retaining every provider attribution correctly. [G §§2–3]

For fixed downstream R and arbitrary upstream L over nonempty X, endpoint composition is preserved exactly when downstream neighborhoods N_R(m) are constant on each q-fibre. For a declared continuation family retain the entire neighborhood profile. In finite M the smallest supplementary alphabet has max_z d_z labels, where d_z is the number of different profiles inside fibre z; labels can be reused across fibres. This count excludes decoder and witness storage. It does not cover arbitrary future additions to the relation family. [G §4]

If the question is instead whether **these particular two witnesses** compose, endpoint repair is weaker than needed: equal summary keys must imply equal actual middle identities on admissible cross-pairs. For unrestricted pairs that is injectivity. Equal endpoint answers cannot license substituting another unsubmitted intermediary. Nor is equal derivation-record identity required when distinct derivations genuinely refer to the same intermediary. Semantic sort, productive respect, scope and the remaining guards must still match. [G §5]

For n intermediaries with diagonal upstream/downstream use, the transfer matrix is I_n with ordinary and Boolean rank n, while every intermediary may have the same original provider. Quotienting through that provider creates the all-ones relation and n(n−1) false routes. Thus one original provider coexists, at this represented signature, with arbitrarily many indispensable mediation distinctions. Large routing rank does not count originals, and low rank does not warrant their unity. The report also exercises the unchanged provision engine; the false routes arise in the deliberately imposed quotient, not from an alleged defect in its existing root-attribution query. [G §§6–7; GR]

The joined epistemic architecture consequently needs three separately typed identity questions whenever all three are asked:

1. Who is the original productive provider?
2. Which actual secondary intermediary/input connects these explanatory records?
3. Which authenticated evidence acquisition is being copied, revised or newly added?

One common provider does not make two acquisitions copies; equal likelihood vectors do not identify acquisitions either. Retaining multiple acquisition keys does not imply multiple original providers or calibrated conditional independence. Typed proof equality cannot supply any missing actual identity premise. These are warranted distinctions of target and meaning, not demands for maximal storage in every application. [G §§1,5,8; F §§4,7–8]

## 7. Exact foundational use and the remaining interfaces

The earlier crosswalk is strengthened by specifying what a proposed application must actually transport.

- **Productive explanation:** retain same target/resource/bearer where the original-ground argument needs it; preserve secondary actual-use identity when composing local explanations. The new join theorem supplies a failure test and bounded repair, not productive actuality or a new unity proof.
- **Selective revalidation:** CP16/CP23/T17 dependencies already distinguish need-specific, constitution-specific and shared base challenges. Cache dependency profiles explain how a computational receipt can remain reusable under a declared revision model. They do not establish that a challenge is true, that a premise is philosophically indispensable, or that a subject relied on a surviving proof.
- **Testimony and content:** source, actual attributed content, reporting scope and veracity remain distinct. Calibrated complete laws may make their numerical aggregation explicit only after the model is warranted. The exchangeability comparison supplies no actual historical likelihood, no efficient-cause interpretation of p, and no new PSR defeater.
- **Correction and knowledge:** the exact semantic example shows that retaining a marginal, all pointwise conull predicates, continuous moments and energy descent can still miss a selected universal obligation. It does not model a person's beliefs or equate human knowledge with probability one. Positive source appraisals therefore retain their independent status.
- **Practical authority:** no optimum establishes authority, no price function decides what inquiry is owed, and no formal completion certificate replaces actual source-content access. Conversely a missing general numerical model does not suspend an already warranted in-scope obligation. [F §§5–8; S §7; U §9; P §9]

The live residuals are now narrower. Full non-Gaussian constrained-flow existence is not supplied by Gaussian semantics. General finite-solve guarantees require effective bounds and validated arithmetic. A changed operation menu needs a new closure analysis. A literal CIRS controller still needs its actual source/host/probability bridge. Real acquisition identity, source attribution and calibrated dependence remain application inputs. The historical expected-publicity objection retains its own source-critical target; none of these mathematical results answers it or spreads its incompleteness to unrelated affirmed conclusions.

What has improved is the ability to choose a consequential next investigation. The architecture can now say whether the missing object is a mixed-history coordinate, a semantic event oracle, a fixed-model policy vector, a recoverable checked witness, a live dependency, or an actual mediation key. It can also say when the richer object is unnecessary for the chosen task. That is a constructive advance in synthesis without a claim that every frontier is exhausted.
