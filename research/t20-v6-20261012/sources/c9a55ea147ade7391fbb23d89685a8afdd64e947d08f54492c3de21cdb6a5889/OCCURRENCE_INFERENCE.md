## Result

**Yes: a known report occurrence can become a premise of a competent factive inference even when its asserted content is not trusted.** In the exact-comparator case, knowing the token value Y=y, audit value D=d, and the truth-connected relation D=Theta XOR Y permits the content-using conclusion Theta=y XOR d. A random source's credibility is unnecessary for that inference.

The numerical condition a=1 does not establish those epistemic premises. The positive bridge requires independently grounded knowledge of the actual relation and the two occurrences, plus the subject's real competent use. A concrete comparator architecture explains how the relational premise could be known without first knowing the particular target value. No actual comparator or human episode has been authenticated here.

For a genuinely imperfect verifier, the same decoding establishes V=y XOR d, not Theta=y XOR d. This identifies the exact failed mapping. It does not establish that ordinary knowledge requires infallibility; it shows only that a fallible probability table does not itself supply the missing factive premise in this deduction.

## 2. Typed content and a concrete derivation

Fix one binary target Theta at one time. A particular source token s purports to report its value Y. A particular audit token a records D. Same-token linkage, target identity and time must remain fixed.

The premises have different contents:

- O_s(y): this identified source token occurred with the value y. This does not say that Theta=y.
- O_a(d): this identified audit token occurred with value d.
- C: this audit compares the very same source token with the very same target, and its correctly operating mechanism establishes D=Theta XOR Y for this episode.
- Ordinary binary algebra, understood and used by the reasoner.

For observed y=d=1, the ordinary conclusion is Theta=0. The uniform derivation is:

1. D=Theta XOR Y, from C.
2. d=Theta XOR y, by substitution of the known occurrence values.
3. d XOR y=(Theta XOR y) XOR y, by preservation of equality under the operation.
4. d XOR y=Theta, using associativity, y XOR y=0, and Theta XOR 0=Theta.

The inference uses the actual meanings and values of the premises. It is not the vacuous classical claim that any premise entails an independently necessary conclusion. For fixed y, changing Theta changes D under C, and changing d changes the inferred target. The target is an ordinary contingent bit with both alternatives available in the model.

The source's asserted proposition Theta=y is not a premise. Indeed, when d=1 it is false under C. The token-occurrence fact is nonetheless true. The audit status “Bad” is also unnecessary for this deductive step: it identifies the submodel in which the source report alone supplies no target discrimination before D is known, but the exact decoding uses occurrence and relation instead.

## 3. Where C's truth connection could come from without circularity

A coherent concrete realization is a fixed, understood comparator with two inputs: the target switch state and the logged source-token bit. Its output is 1 exactly when the two inputs differ. The recipient may see the source log and comparison output while not seeing the target switch directly.

The epistemic basis for C would concern the actual wiring/algorithm, reference linkage, and correct operation in the relevant episode. Appropriate independent inspection, an inspectable derivation of the comparator operation, and ordinary warranted evidence of the device's operative condition can support it. None is established by inserting the word “verified” into the record. A finite success sample alone does not prove exact correctness; a schematic circuit truth table alone does not prove that this actual device is connected as specified and functioning now.

Knowing the comparative law need not presuppose the target value. Before reading D, both target states remain possible with a fixed Y. The recipient can understand how the comparator transforms an unknown input without knowing that input. The physical target-to-output connection supplies the information; no prior recipient knowledge of Theta is covertly used to authenticate C. Nor is the random report trusted to certify its own audit. In the original verifier notation, one must additionally justify V=Theta for this episode; a direct correctly functioning comparator to the specified target is one possible exact realization, rather than a second ungrounded assertion of the target.

This is a conditional sufficient-case description with a specified truth-connected mechanism and described inferential use. It is not a reductive explanation of perception or a requirement that ordinary knowers independently prove every fact about their faculties. The source permits known factive grounds; our application must identify them honestly. It need not manufacture a general epistemology to acknowledge what follows if those ordinary epistemic conditions really obtain.

## 5. Exact failure at imperfect accuracy

In the fallible-verifier model, the exact mechanical equation is D=V XOR Y. The same competent substitution and cancellation proves V=y XOR d. Moving from this decoded verifier value to Theta requires something more.

Here the probabilistic calculation uses the known-Bad submodel: Theta and Y are independent fair bits, and the verifier has symmetric accuracy a with error independent of Y conditional on Theta. If 0<a<1, both target states remain possible after a fixed pair y,d. That submodel then gives P(Theta=y XOR d | y,d,Bad)=a. This equality is not asserted for the unaudited Good/Bad mixture. It does not give the episode-specific fact V=Theta. Even a very high a is not that fact, and setting a=1 in a file does not authenticate the actual-world law.

Conversely, failure of this infallible deduction is not proof that no actual recipient could know through a fallible perceptual or testimonial capacity. A further warranted actual-ground assessment may support knowledge. This finite probability model supplies neither that assessment nor its negation.

## 6. Preserved and failed source/world mappings

Preserved: source-token identity can warrant the occurrence premise without underwriting the assertion; a relational audit can bring two individually neutral occurrences into an informative joint ground; an independently warranted exact relation can support a real competent inference; an unreliable source need not render every fact about its behavior unusable.

Failed unless separately supported: bare authenticity to asserted truth; marginal randomness to conditional irrelevance; a statistical accuracy parameter to known episode-level equality; a type-correct model to actual device functioning; a mathematical derivation to human uptake; a contingent comparator target to necessary Creator-existence or exceptionless prophetic reporting.

The conditional bridge consequently gives a positive answer at its proper level. It explains one way retained occurrence information can do genuine epistemic work after testimonial trust is withdrawn. Its premises are substantive and inspectable rather than hidden in the term “audit,” and the earlier occurrence/content and competent-basing results receive their proper credit.
