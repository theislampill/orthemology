## Blocking findings

### AM-F01 — admissible-family transport was not defined

`I(S)` must be stable under isomorphism and transported contravariantly or
covariantly by an explicit convention.  Without this, a natural selector is
not even a well-typed object.

### AM-F02 — the selector theorem lacked an exact copy category

The proof must quantify over every labelled copy of one isomorphism class and
every isomorphism between copies.  Base-structure invariance alone is not the
full naturality statement.

### AM-F03 — fixedness and uniqueness were conflated

Existence requires at least one automorphism-fixed admissible interpretation.
Rigidity requires exactly one such interpretation.  These are different
claims.

### AM-F04 — semantic equivalence was untyped

Literal content labels, reference assignments, truth conditions, and induced
similarity kernels need not have the same equivalence relation.  The quotient
at which uniqueness is asserted must be declared.

### AM-F05 — interpretation rigidity was overread as intrinsic notionality

A unique isomorphism-natural interpretation relative to a frozen admissible
family can be supplied by an impersonal law.  It does not establish that the
structure is intrinsically notional, mentally hosted, or personal.

### AM-F06 — PRH-style kernel alignment was at risk of referential overread

A shared or increasingly aligned kernel does not fix a unique interpretation,
reference, modality-complete representation, or mental ground.  The paper's
information-loss, modality, metric, and incomplete-convergence limitations
must remain explicit.

### AM-F07 — OSM model fit was at risk of semantic or metaphysical transfer

The hippocampal study supports progressive orthogonalization and a selected
CSCG trajectory match.  It does not establish intrinsic semantics, unique
reference, proper function, personality, or an ultimate grammar.

### AM-F08 — the Taymiyyan mental/external distinction was migrated upward

The secondary reconstruction that universals/notions subsist in minds while
external reality contains particulars does not imply that modal or structural
order is itself a notion or that a constitutive ultimate mind grounds it.

### AM-F09 — theorem-family and novelty status were omitted

The core result is a standard fixed-point characterization for equivariant
choice over an isomorphism class.  Any value is the H7a/UG-T4 application and
scope repair; general mathematical novelty is zero.
