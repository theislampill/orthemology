# Nonconventional indication without a prior credential rule

7 October 2026. Additional source-correspondence experiment. The previous report and all models/results were preserved as history-v2 before this extension. No closure verdict.

## Result first

A positive contextual route can be executed without a prior rule assigning a token its evidential meaning and without an explicit source endorsement sentence. In the declared planning model, publicly sending a distinctive personal mantle is selected to answer a disputed commission claim. Its communicative reason is inferred from the source's information, alternative available plans, independently observed circumstances and a separately constrained source-policy class.

The physical handover alone still does not distinguish communication from repair, warmth or courtesy. Question-conditioned courtesy is a stronger rival: it matches the first question/audience interventions. A separately known-false-claim episode discriminates it from a truth-directed response in the regular policy class. A deliberately selective courtesy policy remains compatible with every supplied past observation and the current handover. The remaining burden is therefore substantive warrant for the policy class, source character, contextual interpretation and observed circumstances, rather than an additional Boolean named genuine sign.

## 1. Actual source distinction

Retained Nubuwwat print 768–772 was re-viewed, alongside RR3 L0103–0107. At 768 the author explicitly distinguishes intentional indication understood through knowledge of the sign-giver's circumstances from indication fixed by an earlier pact. At 769 the turban example stresses that no prior agreement established this sign. At 770–771 recently known possession and the absence of a different purpose support the interpretation; theft, unauthorized use, repair and sealing purposes are separately acknowledged. A strong live alternative leads to suspension. At 772 gesture can communicate in response to a question without a prior pact.

Therefore the earlier explicit-instruction model is one sufficient route, not a universal template. Replacing its instruction string with a field called contextual_intent would miss this source point. The present experiment instead executes choices among competing action-plans and computes what the outward observations discriminate.

The source does not demand elimination of every merely imaginable world before ordinary confidence is possible. The selective rival below identifies a limit of finite model identification. Its bare logical availability is not asserted to be an undefeated defeater of every real person's contextual knowledge.

## 2. What the model contains and does not contain

The household's question reaches P: did P send E with the inventory message? P has a distinctive personal mantle which the relevant observers recently saw P wearing. P can retain it, send it with E, or withhold it and reject the claim by a gesture. Sending may instead serve repair, protection from cold, an ordinary gift, a gift prompted by social attention, or deliberate deception. No past source credential rule is stored. P utters no endorsement sentence.

The source decision procedure forms feasible plans, computes their utility from goal weights and the concrete circumstances, and retains every maximising plan. The goals include helping the audience accept or reject the current commission claim, repairing the garment, warming the carrier and giving a courtesy gift. Each operation has a cost. The observed event sequence records only the publicly visible question/custody/handing/retaining/gesture acts. The plan's aim is not an observation available to the recipient.

This is a restricted means–end model. Its action-to-audience expectations are a substantive assumption: the source expects a distinctive object, voluntarily sent in this question context, to be intelligible as a response. The program does not prove that pragmatic competence or successful audience uptake from object identity alone. It does not learn natural-language semantics or impose a pre-agreed token credential rule. Source policy and interpretive expectations must be independently appropriate to the actual people and occasion.

The target is the selected plan's intention to have the audience accept the present commission claim. It is narrower than infallibility, ethical authority, adoption of all later reports or numerical identity. A positive interpretation of this intended acknowledgement can support the bounded claim that the source sent E, under adequate ordinary communicative and epistemic premises. None of those broader conclusions is silently added.

## 3. Executed positive and negative directions

In the positive fixture, a communication goal has utility 6 before the one-unit action cost; no repair or cold condition exists. Sending the distinctive mantle is the unique best plan. If the communication goal is removed while physical circumstances remain unchanged, the source keeps the mantle. Thus communicative purpose is doing explanatory work in the action process, rather than being attached as an inert metadata label after a handover.

The intervention table gives:

- Positive communication: actual send; remove the question, waiting audience or recognizability and the source keeps the mantle; remove practical needs and the source still sends; on a separately known false claim the source rejects it.
- Maintenance: actual send; removing question/audience/recognizability does not stop it; removing repair need does; the false-claim intervention still produces sending.
- Warmth/courtesy: actual send; removing question/audience/recognizability does not stop it; removing cold does; the false-claim intervention still produces sending.
- Question-conditioned courtesy: actual send; removing question/audience/recognizability stops it, just as for communication; removing practical needs does not. On the known-false-claim episode it still sends rather than rejecting the claim.
- Appropriation: the mantle reaches E after E takes it, while P selected retention. A witness of voluntary transfer discriminates this route from source sending; possession at the household alone does not.

These are explicit policy/context interventions in a toy model, not historical experiments performed on the source's exemplars. Their usefulness is specifying what information about circumstances or behavior would actually discriminate these purposes.

Equal reason scores do not licence a convenient choice of intention. A separate fixture ties a communicative plan and a repair plan with the same outward handover. The result preserves intention ambiguity, represented by null. An initial implementation collapsed that ambiguity into false; the failing test and correction are preserved in IMPLICIT_TIE_RED.txt and the later green replay. This correction affects the enlarged tie case, not the unique-plan positive fixture.

## 4. Independent choice episodes and their exact limit

The declared regular policy class has 96 profiles, varying communication, repair, warmth, ordinary courtesy, question-conditioned courtesy and deception weights. Four separate synthetic choice episodes are supplied:

1. No current question or practical need: P keeps the mantle.
2. A separately known false commission claim: P withholds it and rejects the claim.
3. Repair need without a current question: P sends it.
4. A cold carrier without a current question: P sends it.

These observations contain behavior and independently supplied circumstances. They never reveal an intention label. They are prior synthetic episodes, not endorsements of the target message and not a requirement that the recipient independently prove each new report.

Exactly three regular profiles survive all four episodes. Their weights differ, yet every surviving profile selects the communicative plan in the new positive scene. A separate closed arithmetic check reproduces these survivors. This supplies a nonvacuous conditional positive: within that warranted model class, previous action evidence and current circumstances can determine communicative purpose without explicit prior credential instructions.

A stronger policy behaves exactly like the positive policy in every calibration episode but treats the novel occasion as a special courtesy gift. It has the same outward action at the target and no intent to make the audience accept the commission claim. Thus finite calibration plus the token cannot identify the true policy over every extension of the class. This is a concrete competing means–end policy, not an assertion that modelled human conduct must always be deceptive or that all remote possibilities deserve equal weight.

The remaining real-world question is whether experience, known circumstances and character adequately justify the regular class or make the selective policy a strong live alternative. No newly acquired historical evidence here settles that. The model explicitly exposes this burden; it does not satisfy it by naming one profile trustworthy.

## 5. Truth, sincerity and defeasible weight

A source who mistakenly believes the commission occurred selects the communicative plan even when the model's independent mission-event history contains no commission. A deliberately deceptive source can also select a plan intended to induce acceptance of the false claim. Intended communication is therefore distinct from truth. Neither a source's motive nor the recipient's correct recognition of that motive validates exceptionless veracity.

For two hypotheses that match every available calibration and target observation, those observations have likelihood ratio one. A sensitivity calculation supplies illustrative selective-rival weights of 1/1000, 1/100, 1/10 and 1/2; the corresponding conditional purpose probabilities are 999/1000, 99/100, 9/10 and 1/2. These numbers are not empirical estimates, authorial probabilities, or a proposed threshold for knowledge. They demonstrate that defeasible confidence can be high while a logical rival remains, but its degree depends on independent background grounds that the code does not create.

The complete local suite has 24 passing tests, including seven new implicit-sign controls. The additional check enumerates 12,288 policy/context combinations, verifies that changed actual truth cannot secretly change planning when source belief is fixed, reproduces the three calibration survivors independently of the filter, and rejects four semantic mutations. No new Lean declaration or empirical calibration is claimed.

The source-correspondence gain is a worked positive nonconventional indication route plus concrete purpose rivals, instead of treating explicit authorization text as universally necessary. Real historical custody, pragmatic understanding, adequate character/policy warrant, actual source truth and the special divine-attestation application remain independent burdens. Earlier bounded work and its negative evidence retain their credit. Closure remains for the owner's approval.
