## Electronic pages 104–106 (continuous-first-pass)

E104 completes a layered account: Satan's deceiving is an external cause, the creature's own love, hate and withdrawal internal causes; God creates all, which occur by his will/power. Original self-sourced creature efficacy is excluded, but creature causes are named rather than erased. Divine will entails occurrence; absence of divine will entails nonoccurrence. Crucial distinction: antecedent enabling capacity includes sound organs, powers, available causes, conditions and access; this grounds obligation yet does not necessitate the act. Concurrent capacity necessarily accompanies/entails the act, is a special favour, and is not the condition of just obligation. Thus withholding actualisation is not equated with removing every ability. This directly controls any argument from mere possibility/power to actual efficacy.
E104–105 heart death and life: revelation is spirit and light added to the light/life of fitra, not simple identity with it. Attributes (mercy, justice, patience, generosity, beauty, etc.) are related to divine love for analogous virtues gifted to creatures; not an equality of divine/created attributes. Philological treatment of ikhbāt and its prepositions; Ibn Abbas/Umar shepherd glosses of haraj (blocked thicket) explain constrained reception of guidance. An unusual internal condition: receptive heart expands as knowledge and faith enter, unlike physical vessels. No external theological warrant should be claimed solely from these metaphors.
