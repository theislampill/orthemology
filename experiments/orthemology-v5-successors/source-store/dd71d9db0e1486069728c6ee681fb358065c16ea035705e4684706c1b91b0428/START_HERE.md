## Source and historical completeness

Ibn al-Qayyim’s مفتاح دار السعادة ومنشور ولاية العلم والإرادة is read here in the edition edited by Abd al-Rahman ibn Hasan ibn Qa‘id, third 1440/2019, Dar Ata’at al-Ilm / Dar Ibn Hazm. The electronic labels divide the body into 3–537, 538–1172 and 1173–1603; those labels do not independently establish physical binding seams in the composite scan. The whole-work selection does not depend on them.

Exact external source URLs, bibliographic identity, source hashes and earlier-access limits are in [source metadata](source_manifest.json); [boundaries](boundaries.json) describe the electronic and scan relationship. Complete received text does not mean that the promised conceptual programme on will was historically completed. Inherited lacunae and significant variants remain in [textual uncertainties](TEXTUAL_UNCERTAINTIES.json). Modern post-body indexes are outside the authorial-body selection.

## Findings and subjects preserved

The full notes retain Adam’s garden debate; learning, teaching and moral psychology; natural history and its historical scientific limits; wisdom, suffering, law and rational moral value; astrology, omens, names, sneezing and contagion; and the distinction between author, opponent, editor and electronic addition. The central philosophical return points include genuine created agency and joint partial causation (501–502; 1512–1515), beneficent delegation (461–462), completing the product without completing its maker (1024–1026), equal opposition and its unresolved productive/inhibitory priority premise (896–898, controlled by 910 and 1171), and ends of knowledge and action (511–514; 1164–1165). An actual common-Creator comparison remains distinct from a universal every-original/every-requisite rule.

Whole source books, extracted bodies, PDF/Word/HTML files, images, raw display records, conversations and scripts are excluded. The notes contain short quotations embedded in analysis, not standalone source extracts. The omitted Word witness is available at https://ibnelqayem.com/ar/media/download/86 and the omitted composite scan at https://ibnelqayem.com/ar/media/download/65; their exact hashes and coordinates are retained in the linked metadata. Checksums and range accounting establish stability and consistency, not independent proof of comprehension, authenticity or philosophical conclusions.
