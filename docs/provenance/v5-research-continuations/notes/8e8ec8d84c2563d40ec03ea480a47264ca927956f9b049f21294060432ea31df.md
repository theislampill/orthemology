## Primary sources checked

1. Andrew R. Barron and Thomas M. Cover, *Minimum Complexity Density Estimation*, IEEE Transactions on Information Theory 37(4), 1034–1054 (1991), DOI10.1109/18.86996. Author-hosted paper: https://isl.stanford.edu/~cover/papers/barron_cover_91.pdf

   The paper studies complexity-penalised likelihood and its statistical accuracy through resolvability. Its countable-model consistency and density-estimation results are relevant background for weighted model selection. Those facts alone do not classify the prior-averaged expected number of strict failures under the literal two-inequality loss in this packet. In particular, almost-sure eventual identification is not a finite prior-averaged mean-count assertion.

2. Jan Poland and Marcus Hutter, *MDL Convergence Speed for Bernoulli Sequences*, Statistics and Computing16,161–175(2006), author manuscript https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0602505

   This source is directly relevant to countable Bernoulli models and prior/complexity weights. It studies cumulative expected squared prediction error and how MDL convergence speed depends on the model class. Squared prediction error and literal cluster-identification failures are different objectives near a parameter endpoint, where separation in absolute parameter distance shrinks. This packet retains that distinction rather than importing a prediction-loss bound as an identification-count theorem.

3. Marcus Hutter, *Discrete MDL Predicts in Total Variation*, NeurIPS2009, conference source https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2009/hash/d3d9446802a44259755d38e6d163e820-Abstract.html and author manuscript https://arxiv.org/abs/0909.4588

   The source proves asymptotic merging of discrete MDL/MAP predictions with the true measure in a countable model class under very general assumptions. It does not by itself give the support-gap spacing criterion, uniform relative-cluster perturbation bound, or the finite-horizon endpoint-contamination law proved here. This is a scope comparison, not a claim that those results cannot be derived by another route.
