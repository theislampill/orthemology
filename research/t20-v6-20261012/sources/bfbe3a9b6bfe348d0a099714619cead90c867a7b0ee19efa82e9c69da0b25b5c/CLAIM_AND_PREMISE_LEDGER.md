## Claims

### TW10-1 The supply respect has a nonarbitrary motivation

**Claim:** The supply question evaluates the availability used by a productive channel, rather than selecting a preferred kind of agent. It can be intelligible for mundane restricted classes and permit contingent outsiders, plural contributors, indeterminism and unexplained background outside its target.

**Status:** Warranted clarification and positive target assessment. It does not establish that every ordinary adequate explanation must be an E_A answer, or that an answer actually exists.

**Owner:** Paper §2. The bounded channel norm is inherited from KP10-3, not newly discovered.

### TW10-2 Target-sensitive answerability has prima-facie rational support

**Claim:** Actual target availability, the independently warranted channel/availability distinction, an independently warranted possible supply answer, and resistance to an exemption based only on global shape jointly give a defeasible reason to expect an actual E_A answer.

**Status:** A genuine prima-facie reason for the exact explanatory respect, beyond mere permission to inquire. It is not an all-things-considered establishment of T_A's actual application. The possible-answer premise remains independently needed. The reason's basic presentation is legitimate; no second derivation, universal consensus or neutral algorithm is mandatory.

**New boundary:** Ninth credited original possession's appeal at complete supply, and the accepted kind paper warranted a bounded adequacy judgement. This paper assesses actual answerability at the newly specified relation, rather than presenting either earlier result as the transfer itself.

**Owner:** Paper §§3–4 and8. Source loci T-BASIC and T-TRANSFER identify relevant context; the four-step anti-exemption argument is our analysis.

### TW10-7 The strongest actual-powers adaptation turns on achievement preservation

**Claim:** Assume actual p_Q and no E_A answer, so q_A is actual. Possible E_A makes q_A contingently true even if p_Q itself is necessary. **Status:** Original target-specific adaptation and conditional inference. It begins with the actual target, confronts the strongest route directly, and identifies the q_A contingency gained from the already required possible answer. It neither refutes an actual-powers account of modality nor independently proves T_A. The modal comparison, exact theory domain and achievement-preservation premises remain substantive.

**Prior ownership:** Ninth F1 already supplies the exact-origin-fact contradiction technique and its premise-relocation test. This claim earns only the new target-specific contingency and achievement-preservation diagnosis. It supplies no newly warranted general productive coverage law.

**Owner:** Paper §6; T-POWERS is related source context.

**Owner:** Paper §7.

## Premises and their present status

- **A Actual target:** p_Q is true for the fixed actual Q. Granted at this investigation's scope; not inferred from a merely contingent exercise.
- **W Local supply relevance:** A productive output does not solely supply its channel's antecedent existential availability. Independently accepted at the explicit supply respect.
- **M Possible supply:** The same target has a genuinely possible E_A answer. Reduced witness burden is accepted; this modal application is not established merely by naming a creator.
- **N No arbitrary exemption:** Global shape alone does not justify treating the availability respect as answerless. This contributes a prima-facie reason; whether the active joint account actually leaves the respect untreated is disputed.
- **T_A Exact transfer:** Actual truth plus possible E_A explanation yields an actual E_A explanation. Has directly targeted prima-facie support; actual warranted application is not established here.
- **D_A Supply deletion:** In a supplied situation, absence of all obtaining E_A explanatory bases would remove p_Q. Separate from W and unestablished here.
- **B_A Relevant return:** The original p_Q-without-supply situation stays relevant in the nested comparison used to challenge it. Separate intuitive counterfactual commitment, unestablished here.
- **I_A Target individuation:** The causal/explanatory object is availability itself, and every cause of that very object meets the supply relation. A possible repair requiring its own positive warrant.
- **M_A Modal comparison:** An actually contingent q_A can be false while still possible in another situation. The tested actual-powers adaptation needs this modal commitment and a theory covering the composite explanatory-absence target. Given actual q_A, M already supplies q_A's contingency.
- **G_A Achievement preservation:** Realising q_A by a power necessarily supplies E_A for p_Q. Needed by the tested actual-powers adaptation, not supplied by token productivity alone.
- **C Actual coverage:** Every actual contingent eligible contributor is appropriately covered by Q. The accepted kind and origin conditions remain separate.
- **P Productive participation:** An actual E_A answer involves actual concrete productive participation. No analytic inference from existential grammar is claimed.
- **J Whole joint scope:** No wholly internal account adequately answers at E_A. Not inferred distributively from W.
