# Divine Knowledge and the role of our entailment argument

This clarification accompanies, and does not modify, Sixth_Original_Source_Knowledge_Author_v1_20261001.zip, SHA-256 83f1a917b40abc1e4d6ebac2df9fec31e1141440cf17bd57321efe9ebcab9cd9. It applies particularly to COMPLETE_SELF_KNOWLEDGE_AND_EFFECTS.md and CreativeKnowledge.lean.

## Exact clarification

The appendix's passage from complete self-Knowledge and Knowledge of actual creative determination to Knowledge of the effect represents a logical entailment that we use in reasoning about the source. It does not represent the source acquiring a previously absent cognition, performing an inference, consulting premises, or learning an effect by deriving it from prior self-Knowledge. Nor does it make the first cognition an efficient cause of the second. The source's Knowledge may satisfy the stated closure condition without any process of deduction occurring in the source.

Accordingly, the appendix's language about explaining effect-Knowledge should be read as showing that the alleged combination of complete self-Knowledge with ignorance of an included effect is incompatible with the defended completeness and closure conditions. It should not be read as a psychological or causal explanation of divine acquisition. Its talk of uncertainty concerns the information available in our restricted formal description, not divine uncertainty.

## Primary textual basis

Ibn Taymiyya expressly makes this distinction in Darʾ taʿāruḍ al-ʿaql wa-l-naql, in the section answering al-Rāzī about self-knowledge and effects: [hosted section /017, lines 581–586](https://www.islamicbook.ws/amma/dr-taardh-alaql-walnql-017.html). The source says that complete self-Knowledge entails Knowledge of creation, rejects saying that the first cognition produces the second, and distinguishes our inference from any inference needed by the source. The key negative phrase is: «فإن علمه سبحانه ليس باستدلالي».

The same page, lines 627–629, repeats the distinction: the relation permits our inference in either epistemically useful direction, while the source does not need to derive one cognition from another. The hosted number 017 is a website subdivision, not a printed volume reference. The retained capture is a digital transcription, not a critical-edition collation.
