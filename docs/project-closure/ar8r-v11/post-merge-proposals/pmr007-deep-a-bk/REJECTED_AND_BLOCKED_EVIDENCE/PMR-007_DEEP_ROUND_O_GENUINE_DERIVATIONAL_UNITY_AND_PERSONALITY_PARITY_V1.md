\[

\[

\quad A=y,
\quad S=x\oplus y,
\quad N=x+y,
\quad R=\mathbf 1[N>0].


1. **Shared derivation:** one two-bit state determines all five coordinates.
2. **Cross-profile restriction:** all coordinate marginals are nontrivial, but

   recombinable coordinate primitives.
5. **Novel exclusion:** equations such as `S=M XOR A`, `N=M+A`, and
   `R=[N>0]` exclude profiles admitted by a product theory.

On the Hamming-square revision graph, `N=x+y` is also an exact additive
potential. The witness therefore includes Deep N's positive structural
