## Rank criterion and exact certificates

Full column rank gives a unique rational solution; nonnegative integrality and exact readout reconstruction enforce membership in the model. A rational null vector at deficient rank scales to a nonzero integer null vector. Its positive and negative parts are distinct nonnegative inventories with identical full readouts. This remains valid if some failure factors equal one; a null vector can then be one-sided, with the empty inventory as one side. The unrestricted finite-count domain is essential to the necessity direction.

## Guarded-class necessity and sufficiency

Merely demonstrating a collision at the full profile would not establish failure of the all-profile guarded oracle. The included signed lift supplies the missing step correctly:

    delta(P,N) = (-1)^|N| z_P.

At a proper profile S, summing all eligible guards gives z_P times the alternating subset sum on R minus S, which is zero. At the full profile the surviving coefficient is z_P and its valuation image is zero by the original kernel relation. Splitting signs yields honest nonnegative route inventories. A common productive baseline leaves the observation equality unchanged. This proves the full guarded-class necessity claim, rather than leaving a discrepancy visible at a solo profile.

For the converse, full rank recovers aggregate support counts at each issued profile. For fixed P, the values at R minus V are exactly the subset sums of guard counts over N contained in V. Boolean inversion is therefore valid and keeps the positive support and absence-guard roles distinct. The implementation also rejects recovered supports outside the issued profile and checks the reconstructed panel.

The independent suite builds one complete all-profile collision directly from the small B versus C+AB relation, without using the author's kernel-lift function. It also reconstructs 40 independently generated guarded inventories with both repaired designs. These tests verify implementation consistency; the alternating-sum argument gives the general proof.

## Universal design

The 2-adic mask-design matrix has entries -|P| when P is a subset of T, and zero otherwise. Ordering nonempty masks by their integer values makes it lower triangular: P contained in T implies P <= T. Its diagonal is -|T|, everywhere nonzero. Thus it has full column rank for every finite root count. This supplies a second direct proof of the stated Boolean inverse.

Independent exact checks reproduce that triangular matrix through seven root indices. This extends finite verification of the certificate, not the theorem's scope or the author's tested decoder range. The author separately tests both decoders through four roots.

Zero attenuation rates leave issued roots present for absence guards. Treating a zero rate as deleting the issued act changes the model and invalidates this claimed transport. The universal family is a finite sufficient design, not a minimal number of probes and not a physically established actuator.

## Symbolic law and limits

For each nonempty support P, the polynomial 1 minus the product of its variables is primitive as a degree-one polynomial in any selected variable from P: its coefficients include the unit 1. It is irreducible over the fraction field of the remaining-variable polynomial ring and hence irreducible in the full polynomial ring. Distinct supports give nonassociate factors because all constant terms are one and their nonconstant monomials differ. Unique factorisation therefore identifies symbolic multiplicities.

This result does not imply that an arbitrary numerical evaluation remains injective. The rank-five rational probe is the explicit counterexample. Conversely exact full rank does not provide a uniform finite-precision separation or an optimal finite-sample instrument. Large multiplicities can still drive no-effect probabilities near zero. The current manuscript preserves those limits.
