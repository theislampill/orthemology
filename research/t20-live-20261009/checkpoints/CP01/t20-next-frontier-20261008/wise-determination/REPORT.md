## Findings

**The retained rival has a defensible conditional end-to-means construction that the previous menu test did not evaluate.** Given target q, genuinely equivalent practical roles, and an exchange-invariant joint-plan requirement, the unique goal-achieving plan is (q,q). Both existing local policies implement it without receiving the other's actual exercise. This does not require a unique value-maximizing outcome. It also does not prove that exchange invariance is a binding wise reason, that the richer end is eligible for originals, or that either actor supplies the whole actual work independently.

An apparent symmetry obstruction fails: the cycle among the three successful joint plans preserves coactive output but changes every solo manifestation. It is not a symmetry of the strongest retained model. Multiplicity of successful means therefore does not by itself establish arbitrary selection.

The targeted sources require more precision than “sufficient reasons demand a unique optimizer.” Majmu8 distinguishes complete causal determination from antecedent capacity and rejects unexplained particular specification; Bayan5 both criticizes generic reasonless-choice replies and affirms God's particular unreceived willing and optional action. Neither text supplies a theorem that every adequate normative reason-set has a single maximizer. Conversely, placing the chosen plan in a “will” or “reason” field supplies no independent justification for that particular selection.

Seven adapter checks and an independently developed exhaustive transport probe pass. The useful increments are the faithful conditional means result, rejection of the unfaithful transport, and a precise source/contract separation of justification from execution. The continuous 118–150 reading, with the 151–153 dialectical completion, preserves several different claims.

- **Antecedent power:** L294–298 (129–130) distinguishes capacity enabling an act from the contemporaneous capacity that entails its occurrence. Knowledge that an act will not occur does not remove the antecedent capacity. L299–300 separately distinguishes effective cosmic willing from command, love, and approval. These cannot all become one software boolean named “will.”
- **Actual causal completion:** L305–312 (133–136) distinguishes incomplete created causal conditions from the full determining basis and endorses the requirement of a complete determiner. At L311–312 the author criticizes those who allow an unchanged, equally related will to specify the particular event/time without a particularizing cause, while using the contrary principle against their opponents. This is an explicit causal-explanatory demand, not an optimization algorithm.
- **Means remain explanatory:** L313–318 (137–139) rejects reducing created causes to mere association and rejects the argument that a decreed effect makes its means dispensable. An effect can be decreed through those means. Holding the decree fixed while deleting a specified means is not automatically an admissible comparison.
- **Wise successive activity:** L332–340 (145–147) distinguishes effects originally supplied by the same source from foreign completion and says that absence before a fitting time need not be deficiency. This is reported dialectic, supported in direction by the author's explicit general argument at L312, rather than an unmarked verbatim authorial verdict at every sentence.
- **Do not freeze away optional acts:** L350–356 (151–153 and completion) distinguishes regresses in causes from successions of conditions/effects, and records the position that acts related to will and power have always occurred successively. This is part of the passage's response space. It prevents importing an unchanging complete act-history simply from unchanged standing perfection.

In this law that independently stated structural rule derives the existing diagonal, rather than copying the selected plan into a new parameter. It is reasonable as a proposed sufficient norm, not established as a necessary rule for all free or wise choice. This appraisal supports the means pattern at the declared scope; it is not a source theorem about pure original perfection.

The distinction between task and worldview matters. The means problem starts with q as its given task. Not deriving q's ultimate wisdom is no defect in that conditional derivation, just as a theorem can take its initial conditions as premises. Whether the actual originals would wisely choose that task is a different application question. The new result therefore materially improves the explanation of **which means, given q and the stated coordination rationale**, while leaving the source's actual-original-efficacy objection untouched.

The requirement is stronger than renaming A and B across presentations. The conditional result therefore supplies an intelligible instrumental justification of the inherited means recipe when these symmetry and epistemic premises hold. ## 3. Particularizing information is not automatically a reason

The adapter adds a diagnostic constraint d=a−b mod3. Given q and d, the unchanged law has the unique plan a=q+2d, b=q−2d. This is a conditional means certificate. **For fixed q, d and the selected plan are bijective.** Any successful plan can be reverse-encoded as its d. Consequently a supplied unexplained d adds selection information but no independent normative explanation. The general d construction is not a stronger wise-agent model.

Exchange sends d to −d. Only d=0 fixes this relation within the same unpointed context; d=1 and d=2 exchange the differentiated assignments. The zero condition has a separate symmetry interpretation when equivalent practical roles are warranted. Nonzero d receives no claimed source- or goal-independent meaning beyond its explicit algebraic coordination constraint.

The accompanying factorization test checks a precise information claim. A given role-sensitive family of selected plans can be reproduced from a coarse projection of its contexts exactly when its selected plan is constant on each projection fibre. Here the family does not factor through q alone but does through (q,d). This does **not** say that no q-only selector exists: the diagonal policy achieves q throughout all nine supplied contexts, while failing the extra nonzero-d constraints. It also does not prove full reasons were absent. The original summary may simply have omitted relevant data; whether the added datum is a genuine reason remains a separate question.
