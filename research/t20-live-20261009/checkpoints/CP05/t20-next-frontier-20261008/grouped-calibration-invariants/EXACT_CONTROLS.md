# Exact author controls

Run `python exact_controls.py` from this directory. It uses only Python's standard library and writes results/exact_controls.json. Saved stdout is results/exact_controls.log.

Fourteen deterministic control families pass:

1. Primitive vector/base recovery by the rational valuation/Bezout decoder for all 385 increasing supports of size one through four from counts 1,...,10.
2. Rejection of noncommon-power nodes, duplicate nodes and the excluded atom at one.
3. 252 pairs of integer-dilated worlds with optional zero mass, checking the complete sufficient moment panel and binomial count law.
4. Direct rational scale 3/2 between supports {2,4} and {3,6}.
5. The exact unrestricted scale set under varying known maxima.
6. Eta-restricted candidate scales and static strictly monotone piecewise-linear calibration extensions, including equality at a band boundary.
7. The shifted inherited two-repeat collision, including its exact third-moment discrepancy and different primitive vectors.
8. Fresh-resampling ambiguity within a 1/8 uniform calibration band and the strictly positive held-second-moment discrepancy.
9. General collapse of a fresh positive-count mixture to zero/one components while preserving zero mass.
10. Label-dependent calibrations matching nonproportional supports despite held counts.
11. A step calibration with no interior actual rate.
12. 891 rational parameter checks of general d/e global power maps and their exact maximal-radius inequality after an integer power removes radicals.
13. 1,386 adjacent transformed-F interval separations for residual scale catalogues.
14. Rational isolating intervals for the equal-weight gcd-one approximation countermodel, with moment-error bounds tending to zero.

The written proofs establish the arbitrary finite statements. These controls do not turn exact real roots or rational-ratio recognition into an algorithm from noisy observations. No finite-sample or empirical performance claim is made. The independent review has its own implementation, controls and exact source binding.
