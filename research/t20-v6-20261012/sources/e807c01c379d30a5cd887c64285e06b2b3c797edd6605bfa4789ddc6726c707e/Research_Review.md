## Central proof checks

The sharp total-variation bound uses the chord bound for an absolute-value function, followed by minimising `m + ab/m`. Its maximum occurs at the geometric mean, and the supplied two-point example attains it.

The audit-covariant field classification uses a global log-odds coordinate chart on the positive simplex. Positive likelihood ratios realise every translation. Translation-invariant vector fields are constant in those coordinates, giving the centred replicator form; the positional component must be independent of weights. Local flow uniqueness supplies the converse.

The separable-energy corollary requires the specified weighted Euclidean/Fisher metric. Weight-affinity follows from the weight part of the classification. Positional weight-independence then forces all off-diagonal position derivatives of the affine coefficients to vanish. Connected product domains permit the stated global separation.

The rational step's descent inequality is an exact covariance identity. Its sign follows from opposite monotonicity of potential and reciprocal weighting factor. No Taylor approximation is used to establish positivity, covariance or energy nonincrease; the Taylor expansion is used only for the local derivative at zero step.
