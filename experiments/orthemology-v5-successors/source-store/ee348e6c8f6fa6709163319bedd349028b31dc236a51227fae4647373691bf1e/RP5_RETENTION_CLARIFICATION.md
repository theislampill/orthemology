# Controlling clarification: retention in both RP5 alternatives

3 October 2026. This additive clarification makes explicit the reading already required by candidate-v2's JC10-5 and ACCEPTANCE_v2 §§2,4. The original candidate and acceptance remain immutable. This is a premise clarification, not a new theorem or new warrant for the premises.

## Scope and clarified requirement

For a merely possible S, the two alternatives in RP5 have the same retention requirement. Let x be the selected actual concrete resource: x = s when the supplier itself is used as an already available actual resource, or x = r when an actual entry resource supplies s's availability through a positive support trace.

**In either alternative, the selected realization must retain a relevant actual availability occurrence of x specifically in s's availability or its existential support. If the selected x is actually F, its relevant actual F-instantiation must be retained at that occurrence or at a positively relevant earlier stage within the full existential-support history.** The condition applies whether x is an initiating power bearer or relevant concrete background. Selecting a later non-F occurrence cannot screen off an earlier actual F-stage relevant to that availability, including a stage of the same bearer through persistence.

For x = s, retained availability at the supplying occurrence makes actual F-instantiation conflict directly with S's outside-F qualification. If that actual occurrence is earlier, its retained relevance to s's subsequent availability places it within S's full support scope. This does not say that s efficiently produces or grounds its own existence. For x = r, its retained occurrence lies on the positive support trace to s's availability, with the same full-history F condition.

Three claims must therefore remain distinct: the same individual exists actually and in the comparison history; that individual belongs to the actual resource base; and its relevant actual availability occurrence, including its F-instantiation if any, is retained in the selected realization. The first two do not by themselves establish the third. In particular, a differently classified alternative prehistory is not made a retained actual F-occurrence simply by naming the same bearer.

Positive availability relevance may be joint or enabling. It need not be an actively exercised supplying power, individual sufficiency, or but-for indispensability on this or every possible route. Mere membership in a background inventory still does not establish it. The supplying-power-bearer corollary retains its separate type condition.

## Inferential and epistemic consequence

Omitting this retention condition from either RP5 alternative breaks the corresponding extraction: the inference from the selected witness's actual F-instantiation to conflict with S would lack its required transport. The accepted conditional uses this condition for whichever alternative selects its witness. The branch where S itself is actual remains the separate direct coverage argument and does not need RP5.

The clarification supplies no independent global warrant for classified-occurrence retention, POS-S or absolute realization. It requires neither actual exercise nor a first event, preserves fixed Q and the two coverage domains, and establishes no actual necessary particular. No new source exposition or generic proof credit is introduced.
