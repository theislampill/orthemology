# Novelty audit — Candidate E

## Candidate nucleus

> If an objectively corrective transition is correct partly because the current token's complete alleged authority basis is inadequate in the relevant respect, the complete authority of that correction is not wholly constituted by that token basis. Recursive corrigibility must terminate in, or be globally fixed by, a retained meta-standard, a trans-state/type-level standard, a holistic normative fixed point, a primitive normative fact, or a denial/relativization of objective correction.

## Nearest lineages

1. Constitutivist accounts of agency and belief.
2. Enoch's shmagency/authority objection.
3. Normative regress arguments.
4. Foundationalism, coherentism, and infinitism about normative authority.
5. Naturalized normativity and teleofunction.
6. Fallibilism and revisability accounts.

## Shared mechanism

The result shares familiar anti-self-authorization and regress structure. It also shares the distinction between standards an agent currently endorses and standards constitutive of a type or practice.

## Proposed exact difference

The candidate makes three restrictions explicit:

- it targets the **current token basis**, not all constitutivism;
- it distinguishes ordinary self-amendment from correction that defeats the full alleged token authority;
- it partitions recursive correction into retained-meta, trans-state, holistic, primitive, and error/relative architectures without declaring every regress vicious.
