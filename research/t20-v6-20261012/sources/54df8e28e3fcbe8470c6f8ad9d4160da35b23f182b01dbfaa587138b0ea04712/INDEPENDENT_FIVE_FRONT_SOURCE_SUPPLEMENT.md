# Orthemology T20 — Independent source and inference supplement across five foundational fronts

**Research companion to the owner's five-topic source packet**  
**Research date:** 9 October 2026 UTC  
**Status:** Independent targeted source investigation; NOT a theorem, repository integration, warrant certificate, or closure.  
**Interpretive scope:** Historical reconstruction of Ibn Taymiyyah and related sources is distinguished from their truth, from rival modern metaphysics, and from an Orthemological formal interpretation. UK spellings throughout.

## Executive findings

The supplied five-part material contains valuable substantive leads but cannot safely be forwarded to T20 as five fully verified demonstrations. The independent source survey yields **five advances in argument quality**:

1. **Necessary Being:** The originator argument is better anchored in *actually originated existence* and *substantive original efficacy* than in a schematic essence whose existence/nonexistence are purportedly exactly symmetrical. Ibn Taymiyyah himself challenges technical usages of *mumkin* and some standard Avicennian possibilities; mere conceptual partition does not exhibit an externally existing member of every class. This directly corrects an overconfident passage of the original packet. **A further primary return also directly rejects a third option—existence through neither itself nor another—and must be given affirmative credit rather than discarded as wordplay.** [S01–S06, S23]
2. **Unity:** The allegedly neglected necessarily harmonious two-agent case is **explicitly addressed** in Ibn Taymiyyah. *Jāmiʿ al-Masāʾil* 6:94–97 offers particularly close reasoning about one numerical effect, ordinary cooperation, wholly independent capacity, actual action, and deficiency under cooperative restriction. *Majmūʿ* 20:176–181 and *Darʾ* 9:343 supply independently located textual anchors. This is stronger than the mere contrary-wills argument. But translating between causal overdetermination and **complete original sourcehood** remains a separate, substantive question. [S07–S11, R03]
3. **Qualitative grounds:** Ibn Taymiyyah sharply rejects making a mentally universal abstraction into a separately existing concrete item. But Dasgupta's strongest modern case invokes **plural constitutive grounds and qualitative facts**, not the proposition that a handwritten mathematical formula efficiently produces material objects. His 2016 grounding-PSR account additionally involves necessitarianism. These distinctions narrow the actual points of disagreement. [S12–S14, R04–R06]
4. **Fiṭrah/warrant:** Contrary to the supplied packet's six `Unknown citation` placeholders, there are strong, locatable primary passages. *Bayān Talbīs* 4:571–572 denies that recognising the Maker must depend on a technical syllogism; 4:586 discusses both recognition and love/obedience. *Darʾ* 7:302–303 and 8:348 confirm distinctions among innate knowledge, reflection, rational signs and prophetic report. None alone licenses automatic factive warrant for each individual's purported recognition. [S15–S18, R07–R09]
5. **Knowledge, Will, Wisdom:** *Majmūʿ* 8 directly opposes reasonless specification while defending purposive divine action; *Majmūʿ* 12 explicitly maintains the distinction between eternal willing capacity and successive chosen acts; *al-Akmaliyyah* 6 sets out a source-based priority-of-the-giver argument **with qualification for the manner in which a perfection belongs to its bearer**. This is much stronger and more precise than the original packet's schematic move from changing creation to an arbitrary first instant or to a mechanically unique optimum. [S19–S22, R10]

The conclusion is **neither** that the five theses have been proven in extramental reality, **nor** that their strongest primary-source arguments remain ordinary unverified philosophical conjectures. They have serious text-supported rationale which must be weighed at the intended semantic level. A formal countermodel earns **metaphysical defeater credit** only after its premises and interpretation survive that bridge.

### Evidence and provenance convention

- `P`: selected relevant text inspected in an online primary Arabic edition, with edition/print/scan collation NOT independently performed; quotation voice (author versus reported interlocutor) must be checked in context.
- `A`: modern original author article/paper, publisher record or targeted author-posted paper inspected to the extent stated; the whole article is NOT represented as exhaustively reviewed unless explicitly stated.

## Front 1 — Original actuality, contingency and the Necessary Being

### 1.1 An essential correction to the three-way taxonomy

The original packet presents a supposedly uncontroversial trichotomy and an *indifferent essence* with existence and nonexistence perfectly balanced. It then calls an uncaused contingent source contradictory *as a matter of definition*.

**The first sentence is too broad, the second risks begging the question, and the third requires discriminating premises.** Ibn Taymiyyah certainly argues that genuinely originated contingent things need originators and that an actually operative totality cannot be produced entirely by what first needs it to operate. But his critique of some philosophical constructions is unusually discriminating:

- In *Darʾ* 8:120–131, while discussing philosophers' division of existence, Ibn Taymiyyah asks whether the class said to admit existence and nonexistence has actually been established in the relevant extramental sense. Actual *ḥawādith* (originated events) are identifiable, whereas an eternally existing thing described as intrinsic possible-but-necessary-through-another is disputed. [S01, S02]
- *Darʾ* 8:121–122 specifically resists construing a possible thing's **nonexistence** as needing a determining cause just as its **actual origination** does. An absent thing is not an existing state created by a symmetric causal mechanism. The general balanced-pan picture cannot be imported uncritically. [S03]
- *Darʾ* 3:170 discusses Ibn Sīnā's argument that possibles and their totality must be explained, and the context makes speaker attribution essential: a passage summarising the Avicennian inferential package does not itself certify that Ibn Taymiyyah adopts every technical premise of the package. [S04]
- *Darʾ* 4:239–240 distinguishes dependence on an **efficient producer** from mere dependence on an inseparable constituent or recipient. The umbrella phrase *depends on other than itself* is ambiguous unless the causal sense is specified. [S05]
- *Darʾ* 5:103 rejects treating mentally distinguished quiddity and extramental existence as separable objects; when one refers to the actual particular, its existence is the existence of that very particular. [S06]
- *Darʾ* 3:288–289 **directly considers an objection that what does not exist through itself might exist through neither itself nor another**. Ibn Taymiyyah rejects that third possibility as evidently invalid, and then explains what he means by *existing through oneself*: not self-creation, but not needing another for existence. This is a **positive anti-brute-source argument**, not just a definition of the word *contingent*. A contemporary critic can dispute its rational warrant, but cannot fairly represent the primary author as merely overlooking that alternative. [S23]
- *Darʾ* 3:240–241 and 3:18 carefully distinguish **necessary existence in a broad qualification/concomitance sense**, under which essential divine attributes may be called necessary, from a **unique self-sufficient original Creator of everything other than Himself**. An argument that counts the attributes as separate original necessary deities by sliding between these meanings equivocates. These passages sharpen the essence/attributes safeguard and the plurality programme simultaneously. [S24, S25]

**Positive reconstruction worth pursuing:** begin with some actually occurring, externally instantiated exercise of productive efficacy. Show why a merely recipient or dependent source cannot complete the entire original adequate explanation of that occurrence. Identify the principle that upgrades an actual unreceived bearer to essential necessity **without** assuming the conclusion via a definition of *contingent*. The conclusion has positive content only if *intrinsic existential self-sufficiency* is independently supported, not merely a name for the empty predecessor set in a graph.

### 1.2 The current objection: brute contingent original

The rival is strongest when it grants actual existence and an intrinsic causal power, but denies the claim that **any original uncaused concrete bearer must exist necessarily in itself**. It can reject the broad PSR, propose a brute ultimate and ask why actuality must be *necessitated* as opposed to *uncaused*. This is not refuted just by calling that bearer *mumkin* and defining *mumkin* to need a cause. **However, Ibn Taymiyyah also positively rejects this third option in *Darʾ* 3:288–289**, arguing that what does not exist through itself must exist through another, and distinguishing *through itself* from the impossible claim that the entity efficiently produces itself. That argument should be reconstructed and critically tested on its merits, not reduced to terminological stipulation. [S23]

The relevant affirmative counterargument should bring to bear both the actually-originated-case rationale and this **explicit rejection of the third existential alternative**, arguing that actuality which is genuinely merely possible through its own intrinsic nature, yet has no existential source, leaves an explanatory deficit that is unacceptable for identifiable reasons. It should separately explain why this is **metaphysical incoherence**, **explanatory deficiency**, or **comparatively weaker theory**. These are not interchangeable verdicts.

The broader philosophical literature distinguishes strong and weak PSR, question-begging, explanatory loops and the possibility of ultimate brute facts. The Gale–Pruss 1999 argument uses a possible-explanation PSR and remains contested; Koons–Pruss 2021 argues dialectically from radical empirical scepticism for a restricted PSR about basic natural facts. Neither is reducible to a simple necessary-being syllogism. [R01, R02, R11]

### 1.3 Specific discriminating formal tests

**Test F1-A — actual root versus essential necessity.** Preserve actual concrete bearerhood and genuine original efficacy while interpreting essential necessity independently of world-indexed recurrence. Which additional explanatory premise rules out an actually unreceived but contingent bearer? A formal *absence* of contradiction does not certify metaphysical possibility; conversely a nonexistence-to-necessity theorem may be circular if contingency is defined as receivedness.

**Test F1-B — possibility vocabulary.** Assign independent predicates to: logical consistency, mental conceivability, extramental possible origination, modal existence across worlds, actual existence, dependence on an efficient source, and essential necessity. Exhibit the exact transport premise before replacing one with another.

**Test F1-C — direct three-way existential disjunction.** Use *Darʾ* 3:288–289 to distinguish (a) existence without an external producer, (b) self-production by a previously existing individual, and (c) the alleged existence of an entity which is neither intrinsically sufficient nor received from anything. Identify the independently defended source principle that excludes (c); do not assume that the mere syntactic phrase “without a source” establishes an actual possible bearer.

**Test F1-D — causal anti-bootstrap.** Reject an alleged origin of a given actuality in which that very actualised capacity is already required to generate itself. Do not reject necessary attributes co-present with their essence, or earlier divine acts contributing to later acts, merely because they are explanatory prerequisites.

**Open result:** One already permitted necessary bearer under declared philosophically warranted premises can be a serious positive appraisal. Formalising such a consequence is not itself a neutral proof of its premises. The original packet's exact purported *Darʾ* 3:147 quotation was **not authenticated word-for-word** in this investigation: treat it as Q pending comparison.

## Front 2 — Complete perfection, unrestricted sovereignty and the alleged two original Creators

### 2.1 Three complementary PRIMARY anchors independently located

**(A) *Jāmiʿ al-Masāʾil*, collection 6, especially printed p. 96.** The section *The one act in reality is not from two agents* is available in Arabic in a heading-specific edition view. Ibn Taymiyyah differentiates **one numerical action/effect** from two effects of the same kind, and independent causation from cooperation. He explicitly considers the case in which each partner could have performed the work alone in a different situation. In one simultaneous co-operative case, neither has in fact performed the entire original work on its own. He also addresses *necessary agreement*, not only opposed wills. [S07]

**(B) *Majmūʿ al-Fatāwā* 20:176–181.** The first part ties complete determining will and complete original power to the production of the specified effect; the second draws an explicit perfection-based inference from jointly completed effects to possible greater original efficacy. The source itself differentiates its intrinsic essential powers from their realised, partly supplied actual exercise. [S08]

**(C) *Darʾ Taʿāruḍ*, 9:343.** The passage insists that no created cause is independent in its products; it distinguishes each creature's own act from the different contributions of another. It expresses the intended nonduplication principle for one numerical effect. This is not a mere statement that contradictory wills cannot jointly succeed. [S09]

These independently locatable Arabic passages substantially confirm the existence of the strong authorial argument. They do **not** establish its premises for every metaphysically imaginable bearer by textual authority alone. But an objection that says Ibn Taymiyyah considered only quarrelling gods or only additive mundane partnership is inadequate.

### 2.2 Where Schaffer genuinely challenges, and where he does not

Jonathan Schaffer's *Overdetermining Causes* (2003) defends the attribution of **actual causal contribution to each redundant cause**, not just collective causal attribution. It includes nonphysical scenarios. The original paper has an author-hosted PDF. [R03]

A fair criticism of the supplied source packet: it cannot automatically categorise all overdetermination as *partial current, idle redundancy or wholly unreal causation*. Schaffer specifically argues against dismissing jointly active redundant causes that way. Equally, his ordinary causal relation does not confer **unreceived necessary essence, complete sovereign original productive efficacy, or whole-created-field coverage** on either cause.

The exact open inferential bridge is:

> Does the complete, originally adequate **actual production of the whole numerical effect** exclude a second genuinely independent complete original producer, rather than merely exclude a second contributor needed to finish an incomplete act?

The event-identity and cause-type translation must be defended. A logically sound theorem about an expressly *exclusive* operation trivially yields uniqueness; to acquire external warrant, its **premise that complete original adequacy entails sole production** must be supported without using the conclusion as the definition.

### 2.3 Strong test architectures

- **Sufficiency is not actual whole production.** Counterfactual ability, actual causal role, whole-effect production, efficient priority and unreceived complete efficacy must be separate predicates.
- **Token identity matters.** If two purported producers cause two qualitatively similar effects, that does not establish duplicate origination of one numerically identical effect; equally, one event cannot simply be redescribed as two because the theory assumes uniqueness.
- **Agreement is not automatically produced by a superior.** Necessary concordance, common reasons, intrinsically wise aligned willing, correlated states and reciprocally caused willing are not synonymous. The source offers arguments for stronger conclusions; a source-faithful countermodel must actually address them.
- **Capacity survives coexistence?** Test the claim that both have unborrowed, complete power in mutual presence, without assuming that possible contrary choices must be compossible.
- **Concentration/perfection step.** Test whether a hypothetical combination of all capacities into one more perfect bearer is metaphysically available to *each* original, rather than just representable in a thought experiment.
- **Connected field versus all possible entities.** Distinguish original domination of this actual causally linked creation, every created effect, and every necessarily existing object. A proof over one field should not be promoted to the others.
- **De se knowledge.** If another's contribution truly supplies A with needed original efficacy, A's perfect self-knowledge could reveal that deficiency; mere knowledge that another original exists is not causal or existential dependence.

**Main source opportunity:** The subsection in *Majmūʿ* 20:176–181 and *Jāmiʿ* 6:94–97 should be compared clause by clause with Schaffer's actual causal sufficiency criterion. The question is not whether *both* theorists use a word translated as `cause`, but whether each discusses the **same causal respect** and numerical product. This is substantive argument evaluation, not a semantic trick that pre-empts all objection.

**Unverified terms:** The Arabic expression labelled “essential mutual prevention / *tamānuʿ dhātī*” is supported conceptually in *Majmūʿ* 20:177, but polished English quotations and independent labels in the original packet were **not matched word-for-word**. The direct Arabic text, not the supplied English, controls attribution.

## Front 3 — Qualitative explanation, universals and the actuality of concrete reality

### 3.1 Ibn Taymiyyah: which distinction is actually established?

The direct source anchors are unambiguous at a bounded scope:

- *Darʾ* 5:103: a mentally abstract quiddity is not a second extramental entity over and above the particular. [S06]
- *Darʾ* 5:126–127: the universal qua universal exists in minds; the actual particular exists in a determinate qualified manner. [S12]
- *Darʾ* 5:134–135: a mentally possible constructed kind may fail to have extramental application; conceptual construction cannot alone establish an actually possible referent. [S13]
- *Darʾ* 5:143–144: a particular existent's extramental existence is the actual particular reality itself, not an independently subsisting generic *existence object*. [S14]

These support a strong **anti-reification rule**, not an inference that objective truth is manufactured by the mind, nor a denial of real intrinsic attributes. They also do not by themselves establish a positive original *producer* of all actuality. Such an inference requires a nontrivial premise about existential explanation and actual productive resources.

### 3.2 Three Dasgupta theses that must not be conflated

1. **2009 — “Individuals: An Essay in Revisionary Metaphysics.”** Argues for a fundamental world without **primitive material individuals**; it is not simply a universal no-bearer thesis and does not directly deny every conceivable immaterial necessary source. The article is accessible on the author's site. [R04]
2. **2014 — “On the Plurality of Grounds.”** Argues that *a collection of individualistic facts may be grounded together* in purely qualitative facts despite no member being independently grounded singly. This is a substantive challenge to a naïve distributive requirement: `ground(whole)` need not entail `for every member, ground(member)`. It is not an assertion that a mathematical description efficiently performs material creation. The original article identifies itself as *Philosophers' Imprint* **14(20)**, pp. 1–28; one author-page listing incorrectly says 14.14. Prefer the article itself and journal bibliographic cross-check. [R05]
3. **2016 — “Metaphysical Rationalism.”** Defends a grounding version of the PSR whose stated consequence is **necessitarianism** (every truth necessarily true). Its consequence differs dramatically from a theology that maintains genuine contingent particular creative determinations. The paper itself must be examined before transporting a general qualitative-PSR commitment into Ibn Taymiyyah's doctrine. [R06]

**Actual point of dispute:** Can a purely qualitative grounding basis *itself actually obtain* without an actual concrete productive source? Does the grounding theory purport to answer that existential question or merely a constitution/priority question about features of an already existing world? If its proponents offer noncausal constitutive explanation, arguing that they lack **efficient causal will** is insufficient unless independently warranted evidence demands an efficient origin at precisely that explanatory level.

### 3.3 Discriminating tests

- **Plural grounds:** Construct a model in which a *plurality* of grounded facts is explained together while none is singly grounded, and do not derive `ground(each)` from `ground(plurality)` without an explicit distributivity axiom.
- **What is actual?** Distinguish an abstract mathematical representation; a real but non-efficient constitutive truth; an obtaining qualitative fact; a concrete bearer; and a real origination event. Make all asserted mappings typed.
- **No conflation of insufficiencies:** “Does not provide an efficient origin” versus “explains nothing” versus “offers no genuine existential explanation” require different arguments.
- **Necessitarianism guard:** A Dasgupta-style PSR result implying all truths necessary would need to be qualified or rejected before being combined with distinct possible creative choices. This is a possible source of a real inconsistency that can be tested within Orthemology without caricaturing the opponent.

**Verdict:** Source-grounded challenge to the universals-as-extramental-objects move: strong. Universal refutation of developed modern qualitative-grounding alternatives by that move alone: NOT established. Independent case for an actual unreceived original resource: remains a distinct positive obligation.

## Front 4 — Fiṭrah, proper cognitive function and rational warrant

### 4.1 The owner-supplied evidence gap can be partly repaired

The submitted fiṭrah section contains six **unresolved `Unknown citation` entries**. Do not launder its attractive English paraphrases into Ibn Taymiyyah's direct words. There are, however, primary passages with precise electronic volume and page anchors:

- *Bayān Talbīs al-Jahmiyyah* 4:571–572: Ibn Taymiyyah argues that foundational acknowledgment of the Maker need not await the elaborate proof techniques of *naẓar* theologians; it may be innate/necessary and is readily acknowledged. [S15]
- *Bayān Talbīs* 4:586: distinguishes two aspects of human fiṭrah—cognitive acknowledgement and practical love/submission. It explicitly connects this to the prophetic report about every newborn's fiṭrah. [S16]
- *Darʾ* 7:302–303: distinguishes the natural acknowledgement of the Maker, elementary reflection, rational evidences included in prophetic communication, and report about particular unseen matters. [S17]
- *Darʾ* 8:347–348: likewise records discussions of natural or necessary knowledge of the Creator rather than the requirement that every believer complete a complex speculative proof. [S18]
- The Qur'anic verse 30:30 and the widely transmitted newborn-fiṭrah hadith are doctrinally central, but **the semantic and epistemic implications of their wording require exegesis**; merely listing them does not settle proper-function theory or factive warrant.

These passages preserve a positive Taymiyyan epistemology more complex than psychological pattern-detection. His primary account also includes **attention, love, action, signs, reasoning, testimony and possible corruption**. Do not automatically compress all of these into a single mental predicate.

### 4.2 Contemporary comparison: Plantinga and the scope of a model

Alvin Plantinga's *Warrant and Proper Function* (1993), ch. 1, supplies an explicit account of truth-aimed properly functioning cognitive faculties in an appropriate environment, with a favourable objective probability of truth. His later *Warranted Christian Belief* (2000) differentiates justifiability/rational acceptability from positive warrant and evaluates alleged defeaters. His theology is NOT automatically Ibn Taymiyyah's; the frameworks may agree on important epistemic functions without sharing their entire ontology or specific content. [R07–R08]

Carl Sharif El-Tobgui's *From Legal Theory to Erkenntnistheorie* (*Oriens* 46, 2018, 6–61) identifies *tawātur* as central to Ibn Taymiyyah's wider human cognitive assurance, not merely counting transmitted reports. The institutional/secondary abstract is inspected, not treated as a substitute for all of the article's arguments. [R09]

**Crucial difference between model possibility and knowledge:** a successful construction of a possible cognitive faculty that would be warranted **if** created by a truthful Maker is not thereby evidence that any actual person's faculty is as described. Conversely, a person's recognition need not be unwarranted simply because the person cannot produce a syllogistic proof on demand. The hard problem is identifying the truth-linked basis, effective operation and genuine defeater handling without relying circularly on an unverified design claim.

### 4.3 Candidate discriminators

- **Initiation versus basing:** A disposition can motivate attention to evidence while the evidence supplies the immediate warrant. Neither causal motivation nor coincidence guarantees inference from a known premise.
- **Knowledge versus endorsement:** `S believes p` + `p is true` + `S feels certainty` does not by itself supply a nonaccidental epistemic link.
- **Cognitive/practical roles:** fiṭrah may concern knowing and desiring/turning; do not infer action from a bare self-model or appropriate operation from accurate verbal claims.
- **Norm fixing:** identify what fixes truth-directedness rather than defining every successful output as properly functioning.
- **Defeaters:** model misinformation, distorted environment, testimonial copying/common-source dependence and thoughtful disagreement; avoid treating a psychologically striking objection as a semantic defeater automatically.
- **Cross-account mapping:** explicitly compare Taymiyyan fiṭrah to Plantingian proper function and contemporary basing/defeater theories, without identifying them by label alone.

**Verdict:** Strong positive Arabic anchors for fiṭrah as necessary/basic recognition and as cognitive-volitional orientation. No universal factive knowledge, absence of corruption or particular episode warrant follows automatically. The priority should be its best positive, non-question-begging epistemic argument and the strongest relevant objections.

## Front 5 — Knowledge, voluntary particular willing, wisdom and perfection

### 5.1 Strong primary sources, with an unexpectedly useful internal distinction

**(A) *Majmūʿ al-Fatāwā* 8, discussion of the wisdom of creation**: Ibn Taymiyyah challenges unmotivated selection in divine action while differentiating complete determining agency from an eternal essence considered as a blind mechanical cause. The text also debates whether an eternal, wholly completed cause could produce genuinely successive novel effects. In its detailed context, the interlocutors' reasonless specification is not silently adopted. [S19]

**(B) *Majmūʿ* 12:224–229**: clearly distinguishes timelessly complete necessitation of **one particular eternal effect** from divine voluntary activity involving successive acts. It expressly discusses indefinitely successive events without requiring every created particular to be eternal or assuming a single first creative moment. [S20]

**(C) *al-Risālah al-Akmaliyyah* within *Majmūʿ* 6, around pp. 76–78, 139–140**: explicitly argues from the giver of genuine creaturely perfections to the superiority of the ultimate source, while stating that divine perfection belongs to its bearer in an incommensurably appropriate way and that some perfections (e.g. complete lordship) are not shared with creatures as the same mode. It also opposes mutual productive donation of indispensable intrinsic perfection. [S21]

**(D) *Majmūʿ* 8:88–90**: the argument for wisely chosen ends is developed as a contrast with both a purposeless mechanism and an arbitrary will, not as a theorem that divine choices maximise one scalar numerical score. [S22]

### 5.2 Actual implications for the attribute ascent

- **Original life/knowledge:** If a real living, knowing produced bearer receives that positive actuality from an original source, an a-fortiori perfection argument may support source qualification. But the source's possession of an appropriate original perfection need not be the **same kind/mode** as a creature's cognitive episode. Test the priority-of-the-giver premise, not a copy-paste attribute transport.
- **Will:** An eternal essential capacity is compatible with contingent choice among eligible created effects, subject to separately defended particular-determination semantics. The existence of an ordered world is not by itself a theorem identifying a numerically unique willing subject.
- **Wisdom:** Particular willing needs an intelligible basis; that basis need not be an impersonal external efficient cause nor a uniquely maximised outcome function. Reciprocal concomitance of divine Knowledge/Will/Power is not their mutual productive origination.
- **Createdness:** The occurrence of an act in a temporal succession does not make an intrinsic divine act a **created product** by logical definition. Conversely, the source's intrinsic action cannot simply be equated with its distinct recipient effect.
- **Personal source vs structural laws:** A law, statistical regularity or qualitative ground might describe or constitutively explain patterns while lacking volitional agency. But identifying a requirement for willing must arise from the **target causal respect and origination claim**, not a stipulated aversion to all impersonal explanatory types.

### 5.3 Source-critical problems in the original packet

- The illustration that every constant, length, time or law has infinitely many exactly **equally metaphysically possible** settings is not demonstrated by the bare existence of varied numerical descriptions. This is precisely the mental/external possibility guard.
- A simple ‘first time of creation’ objection can misrepresent Ibn Taymiyyah's doctrine of indefinite successive created acts and effects.
- `The ultimate giver must literally exemplify each creaturely perfection` overstates the source. The *Akmaliyyah* distinguishes a perfection's intrinsic truth and **mode appropriate to the original bearer**.
- The positions of Ibn Taymiyyah's interlocutors in a discussion of purely necessitating causation are not automatically his accepted premises; voice and scope matter.

**Verdict:** Positive texts support purposive divine agency and a perfection-based ascent from source to Knowledge/Will/Wisdom, with strong theological and metaphysical commitments. The neutral entailment from existence or orthability alone remains unproved. That is not a reason to treat the positive texts as empty, nor to accept an incompatible rival just because it has a consistent formal model.

## Cross-front findings that should materially affect Dot's scientific decisions

### A. The symmetric 'scale' trope is not a source-safe global axiom

The source text actually distinguishes cause of **originated existence** from supposed cause of the mere **nonexistence** of a possible. It also contests philosophical usage extending *mumkin* to a wholly eternal reality thought intrinsically possible but necessary through another. This matters to front 1 and front 5 and suggests auditing any `ContingencyNeed` axiom's intended interpretation. The precise issue is not whether necessary being is true, but whether the proof attacks the right notion of contingency. **Crucially, *Darʾ* 3:288–289 also supplies a direct affirmative rejection of a third existential option; the source is not exhausted by its criticism of the scale trope.** [S01–S04, S23]

### B. Ibn Taymiyyah's multiple original-cause argument has independent textual depth

The actual Arabic books make a fine distinction among cooperation, dual fully adequate causes, actual whole-effect attribution, internally held power, particular voluntary determination and independent original perfection. A sophisticated overdetermination objection does not defeat it *by citation*, but cannot be excluded *by definition* either. The exact bridge deserves object-level examination. [S07–S09, R03]

### C. Fact-part PSR may tempt ontology transfer

Koons–Pruss's restricted explanatory fact calculus is about facts, their parts and explanation. An inference from `a total explanans has no proper fact-part` to `a concrete Necessary Being has no real intrinsic attributes` requires a semantic bridge not established by that calculus. The positive Taymiyyan case explicitly allows real but necessarily inseparable intrinsic attributes without calling them externally produced substances. [S05–S06, S14, R02]

### D. Necessary predicates are not automatically additional original Creators

*Darʾ* 3:18 and 3:240–241 explicitly separates senses in which divine essence and attributes may be called necessary from the claim that there is exactly one actual self-sufficient Creator of all else. This is not a verbal quibble: a model that counts inseparable intrinsic attributes as independent necessary roots has changed the domain and meaning of `OriginalBearer` and `Necessary` without proving the translation. [S24, S25]

### E. Dasgupta is a motivated rival, but its three theses are not the same

A plural/qualitative grounding of *individualistic facts* does not automatically show the whole obtaining order originates without an actual productive reality. A 2016 grounding-PSR yielding **necessitarianism** has an additional collision to assess with freely particular divine willing and contingent effects. Neither answer may be supplied by a one-line nominalist slogan. [R04–R06]

### F. Fiṭrah is not psychological reduction, nor an instant neutral warrant theorem

Primary texts favour innate recognition independent of complex scholarly syllogism and explicitly pair cognition with volitional orientation. Ibn al-Qayyim additionally makes the **capacity/potential recognition versus active adult recognition** distinction explicit when discussing children, which challenges any assertion that a newborn is already entertaining articulated theological propositions. They require source-sensitive epistemic reconstruction to distinguish an actual rightly functioning recognition from psychological conditioning. [S26] Rejecting the latter need not require abandoning the former. [S15–S18, R07–R09]

## Suggested focused next discriminators (not an administrative campaign)

1. **Contingency source-fidelity test:** Compare the strongest `actual unreceived → essential necessity` proof with *Darʾ* 8:120–131. Specify which concept of *mumkin* supports the argument, and whether the actual originated-case proof suffices.
2. **Two original producers test:** Directly pair *Jāmiʿ* 6:94–97 and *Majmūʿ* 20:176–181 with Schaffer's exact definition of actual redundancy. Require same numeric effect, same causal respect and genuinely unreceived original complete power before granting defeater credit.
3. **Essence/concomitance test:** Re-run the PSR fact-part interpretation with an explicit no-collapse case: one real essence with distinguishable necessary intrinsic attributes, none externally manufactured, while preserving objective factual distinctions.
4. **Qualitative plurality test:** Give *On the Plurality of Grounds* the strongest interpretation: group grounding without memberwise grounding. Then test whether it even claims to explain *actual original obtaining*, and on what grounds.
5. **Fiṭrah positive test:** Develop a concrete episode account of non-inferential recognition with truth-directed proper operation, corruption/defeaters, sources and signs. Audit the independence of its warrant premise from the Creator conclusion.
