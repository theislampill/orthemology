# Cross-front mathematics: what really connects, and what remains to prove

The strongest recurring T20 pattern is **operation-relative sufficient representation**: two original states may be equivalent for the current output but differ under a permitted future operation. A sufficient representation must preserve whatever the declared operation/future-query contract observes. This is a mathematical theme across several domains, not a licence to identify all their theorems as one theorem.

## B. What recent results add to that skeleton

**1. Historical source revision is not automatically a computational commute.** Suppose an audit multiplies labelled weights by positive likelihood factors and re-normalises. The recovered second synthesis (`08_SELECTED_ORIGINAL_REPORTS/RECOVERED_111MIN_CROSS_FRONT_THEOREMS.md`) supplies a written classification of continuously flowing positions and weights that commute with *every fixed positive audit*, under its declared smoothness/label/metric/energy assumptions. A discretisation can be positive, normalised and energy-decreasing yet break that commutation: its two-state Euler example differs by TV `1/117`. A rational positive audit-covariant step is offered as a constructive replacement. This is a **provisional research contribution**, independently testable but not automatically adopted by Dot or in the repository.

**2. Retaining data vs keeping an old calculation valid.** A correct source/version/provenance history tells us what changed, but may not make previously computed state motion, a proof cost or a source-likelihood normaliser remain valid. If the objective or metric changes with the evidence, retrospective correction generally needs recomputation or an invariant, with appropriate historical likelihood pullback. Recovered 111-minute work gives sufficient/necessary finite contracts and a dependency-directed recomputation proof. The alternative first synthesis gives a minimum-sidecar idea and a hybrid flow/jump proposal, but its iterator use and jump-timing gap were reported post-delivery and require qualification.

**3. Hidden interaction controls visible geometry.** For a linear retained representation `y=Lx` and fine positive metric `G(x)`, the projected co-metric `L G(x)^(-1) L^T` must be constant on represented fibres for *every* pulled-back coarse potential to admit the same autonomous reduced Riemannian gradient. The earlier synthesis states and proves this under its assumptions. Later Dot's leafwise and tensor-continuation results give concrete settings where hidden interaction parameters affect the next correction despite identical visible coordinates. The metric tensor is not identical to a provenance tensor.

**4. Full probabilistic normalisation is not truth-directed cognition.** Hessian repairs can lower incoherence for all listed categorical truth-state scores yet alter beliefs after evidence that should be uninformative. Nonseparable Bregman projection broadens a separable entropy conclusion; full stochastic-channel contraction narrows the allowed coherent geometry again to KL under more demanding conditions. Source semantics, potential function, prior/likelihood permissions, and proper functioning remain distinct. No blanket cognitive-field implementation is inferred.

**5. Finite summaries, valid laws and numerical fidelity are different guarantees.** The latest T20 ordinary theorems find true evolving law conditions and counterexamples for arbitrary polynomial moment compression, moving objectives, diffusion and quartic dynamics. Finite-atomic and invariant Gaussian Riemannian flows give positive cases. A direct Euler update can leave the moment cone, motivating positive-particle numerical schemes. A moving loss can decrease to zero while its trajectory escapes, motivating uniform time control and positive common-energy conditions.

**6. Full law and tail guarantees can be invalidated by evidence repair.** Recovered 111-minute source experiment: `μ(n) ∝ 1/(n+1)^2` has no positive exponential moment; a likelihood `(n+1)^2/4^n` yields geometric-tail posterior, whose exponentially bounded status is lost when evidence is withdrawn. Therefore probability-law *realisability* and an **exponential-tail certificate** are different. Its sufficient audited/transported envelope theorem supplies explicit constants for reusing an inherited continuation guarantee when the audit factors/transport have justified growth bounds.

**7. Type theory supplies its own limit to automatic resolution.** HasE original compiler identity can be true in a specified full-PER interpretation but independent of old derivation rules. Richer calculus can prove it, and source syntax/certificates can be effectively checked. General semantic truth cannot be enumerated/decided by such checking in the specified encoding, as shown via a program-nontermination reduction. The resulting methodology is **sound conditional proof investigation**, not universal philosophical oracle.

**8. Finite sidecar (first synthesis).** Start with a finite state set and a particular representation `r(s)`; refine its fibres until states differ only if some declared action, cost, probability or future output can distinguish them. This gives a coarsest right-congruent partition under the contract. If the maximum refined multiplicity within a single `r`-fibre is `M`, a fixed-length additional *label* needs `ceil(log2 M)` bits. This is finite probabilistic bisimulation/partition-refinement ancestry, not a compression of arbitrary real distributions into finite bits. The first packet's written proof and finite test implementation were replayed previously; independent theorem review remains distinct.

**9. Hybrid jumps must preserve time, not just endpoints.** If a research process has discrete source changes interleaved with smooth correction, a semantically faithful comparison can depend on when the jump occurred, action-enabled sets, branching likelihoods and cumulative cost. A prior synthesis reported a missing *jump-timing preservation* hypothesis in its original hybrid theorem, and one-shot iterator defects in code. These are **reported correction obligations**, not yet an externally qualified final replacement. The recovered second synthesis's audit-flow classifications do not automatically discharge them.

## C. Discriminating cross-domain tests that are worth doing

- **Cumulative-warrant fibres:** Two investigators share a conclusion `N` and formal target but differ in independent source support, fiṭrī/basic recognition or testimony. Do source withdrawals change their warranted status differently? If so `r` must retain that distinction. Defining actual `W_N` requires philosophical warrant, not a chosen arbitrary scalar.
- **Mechanistic interpretability / universal weight subspaces:** PCA/HOSVD can capture variance but lose the target functional output after future adaptation. Test whether a representation of neural weights is sufficient for an explicit family of task interventions; invariance of subspace coordinates and gauge equivalence must be addressed. The external `2512.05117v3.pdf` is empirical research on architecture-specific shared subspaces, not proof of cognitive understanding or Orthemology.
- **Faithful conceptual transfer:** Human-style concepts like understanding, agency or knowledge can be transported to artificial substrates only if the capabilities, counterfactual transitions and normative conditions relevant to the concept survive under a declared mapping. Mechanistic resemblance is evidence to evaluate, not the whole warrant.
- **Scriptural reconstruction:** Textual variants/manuscripts/reports form a retained panel; the fibre criterion can demonstrate inability to uniquely reconstruct one precisely specified text under admissible indistinguishable histories. It is not automatically `no epistemic warrant`, nor does a traditional claim of tawātur itself establish zero defect without justified independence/target assumptions.
- **Norm/source common-ground:** A conditional local-to-global lemma can connect target-local uniquely adequate sources over a justified connected overlap graph. The graph's edges must correspond to **genuine same-source adequacy**, not merely a bookkeeping edge or an aggregator called one source. The metaphysical premises still require substantive philosophical investigation.

## D. What these results do not establish

They do not establish a single Riemannian manifold for all human cognition; a causal ability to manipulate minds; empirical reliability of AI-cognition inference; unlimited finite proof or refutation search; metaphysical truth from type-checking; independent source authority from an MD manifest; or a guaranteed theorem transport merely because two vocabularies seem similar.

The most valuable remaining step is to identify **one exact typed interface** between existing results that supplies a genuinely new theorem, countermodel, repair or discriminating empirical test and can survive adversarial proof inspection.
