## Source propositions used

The Asfahani translation supplies two source-relative constraints used by Deep BY:

1. it reports the source architecture as affirming one creator with selected attributes including knowledge, power, life, volition, speech, hearing, and sight;
2. it presents an attribute-locus rule: when an attribute subsists in a locus, the derived predication applies to that locus rather than to a distinct cause.

The latter constrains a conduit account of Speech at Track-N scope: causing a created locus to utter is not, under that schema, sufficient to predicate the cause as the speaker.

El-Tobgui and al-Ujayri were used only to classify fiṭrah-oriented proper-function routes as source-relative architectures whose world-directed force depends on source, translation, referent, applicability, and metaphysical bridge premises. They were not used to turn fiṭrah into a neutral theorem premise.
