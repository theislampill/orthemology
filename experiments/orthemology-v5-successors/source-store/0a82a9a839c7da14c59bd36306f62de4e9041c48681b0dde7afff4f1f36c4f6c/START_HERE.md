Volume 2's [uncertainty metadata](volume02/TEXTUAL_UNCERTAINTIES.json) and [continuous log](volume02/READING_LOG.md) preserve those uncertainties without silent emendation. The selected reflection is not a second full-volume traversal. Reading one received body does not verify every reference, every edition or all manuscripts.
