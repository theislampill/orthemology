# Why complete original self adequacy warrants intrinsic necessity

9 October 2026 UTC. A constructive appraisal of the retained Orthemology argument. This is an additive philosophical result, not a new literature survey or a new machine theorem.

## Affirmative result

**The argument warrants affirming intrinsic necessity of the same actual original when the original is established as completely adequate in the respect of its own existence.** The warrant is the source's substantive rational judgment about existential sufficiency, clarified by the difference between explaining a bearer's obtaining and explaining what follows once it obtains. It is not merely a conditional result awaiting derivation from weaker premises acceptable to every dissenter.

The reason is precise. An account of this original's own actuality cannot obtain its entire existential force from the already-realized original. That would leave exactly the obtaining to be accounted for outside the asserted completeness. If the original's own reality is the complete existential basis, with no external existential contribution needed, its sufficiency cannot consist only in powers or properties it would possess *if it were there*. Nor can an exercise of those powers supply the bearer whose existence the exercise already requires. The positive alternative is a reality adequate to being through what it is, rather than one whose being remains an unexplained realization of an existentially open nature. Such reality does not admit nonbeing through itself. This is necessity through itself.

This reasoning gives affirmative weight to the self-adequacy judgment. It also locates the exact foundational commitment: **an actual bearer whose own reality is existentially insufficient cannot have that deficiency discharged by bare actuality with no intrinsic or external provision.** The argument does not manufacture existence-entailing intrinsic content by defining it into a name. It infers this existential character of an independently established actual original from the rational demand for complete existential adequacy. Its foundational status does not diminish that demand to an optional label or make the argument presumptively neutral.

The present increment is the appraisal and development of this inference, especially its resistance to a nonnecessitating self-actualization substitute and its restriction to the original's own being. The distinctions between productive adequacy for effects, intrinsic adequacy, and complete contrastive determination are already in the inherited work; they are not discoveries of this report.

## 1 The exact starting point

Fix the actual original **g** throughout. Its actual productive relevance and bearerhood are inherited from the original-resource argument under that argument's intended resource, realization, and transport meanings. Nothing below selects a different original for different alternatives.

Three claims must retain their distinct roles:

1. g actually exists and contributes an original productive resource.
2. g's own existence is wholly independent of external existential provision, in the relevant respect.
3. g's own concrete reality is completely adequate for g's existence.

The affirmative source does not leave claim 3 unsupported simply because claim 2 can be represented by negating a dependence predicate. It expressly supplies the no-brute existential judgment connecting independence to positive self-sufficiency. The direct *Darʾ* reply addresses the proposed neither-through-self-nor-another case and identifies existence through oneself with needlessness and necessity through oneself. *Iṣfahāniyya* supplies the positive explanation: the self is sufficient for its existence. [S1–S2]

Claim 1 alone is not claim 3. An original power for effects need not, simply by being specified as original, already explain its bearer's obtaining. This was settled in the earlier reports. Equally, an absence of actual incoming graph edges is not claim 2: it does not show that no external requisite is needed. And a formal negation of *whole* receipt must be connected to the relevant absence of external existential need rather than silently interpreted as the absence of only one especially strong kind of dependence. The present argument takes the intended complete-independence judgment seriously; it does not extract it from weaker notation.

The important affirmative point is that the source has already argued for the move to claim 3. The next step is appraisal of that argument, not treating claim 3 as an unfilled box merely because a critic can deny it.

## 2 What complete self adequacy says before adding modality

Self-adequacy is first an explanatory and ontological claim. The actual concrete reality supplies the full existential basis in the respect at issue; no external supplier or unexplained realization is being relied on to provide the actuality that the account purports to cover. It is not initially defined as existence at every possible world. Necessity is a further modal judgment: this same bearer cannot genuinely be absent.

There is a legitimate distinction between an account that uses actual g as an **epistemic starting point** and an account that uses g's unaccounted actualization as its **existential provision**. The first is what the argument does: actual products support an actual original; the argument then asks what existential character that actual original has. The second would be circular in the relevant explanatory respect: g is fully provided for because one has already granted g's otherwise unprovided obtaining.

Nor must the self be a producer of itself. A productive exercise is an operation of an actual reality. Intrinsic existential sufficiency concerns why no such production or outside existential supply is needed for the bearer. It is not a hidden operation directed from g toward g.

The primary text's concrete essence/existence identity supplies an important ontological control, but not the inference's warrant by itself. There are not two external items, an essence and an existence, with the first manufacturing the second. Yet ordinary creatures also have concrete essence/existence identity. Identity therefore does not prove self-adequacy or necessity. The positive sufficiency judgment is doing the work. [S2–S3]

## 3 Why conditional intrinsic content does not discharge this question

Suppose an account says:

> If this nature is instantiated, a bearer of it exists and has these powers.

It may be entirely true. It may illuminate what the bearer can produce. It does not yet answer whether the nature is instantiated, because that is already its antecedent. The same applies to a claim that an existing g possesses an intrinsic power sufficient for certain effects. That claim explains the effects only once the relevant productive actuality is available.

The defect is not that a good explanation must always exclude every alternative outcome. It is that this account has not addressed **its nominated existential target**. Instantiation has been used where instantiation was to be accounted for. Calling the nature fundamental, concrete, unreceived, beginningless, or identical with its own existence does not change this inferential structure.

Now apply that control to the actual original. If all g's alleged intrinsic existential sufficiency has only this conditional form, g's own obtaining is still outside that sufficiency. There are then two substantive options: the obtaining has some further existential provision, or it is left without adequate provision. The first relinquishes complete intrinsic independence. The second relinquishes the no-brute existential judgment and hence the claimed complete self-adequacy. Neither option preserves the complete self-adequacy with which the inference began.

This is why the source's phrase about identity being established by identity should not be isolated and reduced to “if g exists, g exists.” The phrase follows the positive assertion that g's own reality suffices for existence. The resulting reading is stronger than the isolated tautology, and the preceding premise supplies its strength. The scan at printed 35 confirms that order. [S2]

### What is positively established about intrinsic content

We should neither overclaim nor withhold the result. The argument does **not** construct a real definition of g, specify every essential attribute, or exhibit an independently existing proposition that produces g. It does infer something substantial about g's reality: **its sufficiency for being is not conditional upon an otherwise unaccounted obtaining of that very reality.**

This is an inferential discovery about the existential character of an actual subject, rather than a stipulation that a conceived essence has an instance. Knowing this character need not await an exhaustive account of everything g is. The reasons are the actual original's established actuality, its complete independence, and the rational sufficiency judgment. The content is earned to that extent; a name such as “necessary nature” alone would earn nothing.

If an actual original had merely an existentially open nature, its obtaining would be an additional existential issue not settled by that nature. The source's positive sufficiency claim denies that this issue can remain unresolved while the same reality is called completely adequate in its own existential respect. That denial is substantive and rationally warranted; it is not supplied by the grammar of the word “nature.”

## 4 Why a nonnecessitating exercise cannot supply the original itself

The strongest helpful clarification concerns an apparent escape: perhaps g is completely self-adequate while its existence is a nonnecessitating manifestation of its own power, just as an agent may produce an effect without the agent's standing nature making that effect necessary.

There is a real reason to distinguish these cases. For an effect, the producer is available as actual productive reality independently of the effect's obtaining. A productive exercise can therefore account for the effect even when the standing power is compatible with another exercise. Whether that explanation meets every further contrastive demand is a separate question.

For g's **own existence**, that same maneuver cannot be made. The power would be g's power, and its exercise would be an exercise by actual g. If the account says that this exercise supplies g's existence, it uses the bearer whose own actuality was the target. Temporal simultaneity does not repair the priority problem: a timeless exercise still belongs to an actual bearer. And calling the exercise intrinsic does not make the exercise's required subject existentially available before or independently of that subject's being.

A noncausal reformulation must also do the work. Saying that g, its intrinsic constitution, and its existence necessarily accompany one another can identify real inseparability without providing a separate producer. But inseparability conditional on their obtaining does not provide their obtaining. The primary text explicitly distinguishes intrinsic concomitance from productive dependence. [S4] A formula asserting reflexive self-grounding likewise does not independently establish the positive existential sufficiency claimed for the original.

The positive conclusion is therefore not a generalized deterministic principle. It is a restricted judgment about the original's own being:

**When no existential supplier is needed, and the intrinsic account is genuinely complete for the bearer's existence, there is no further contingent realization whose actuality is supplied by the bearer's exercise, by an external condition, or by unexplained occurrence. The self's existential adequacy is not merely one optional manifestation of an already actual subject. It is adequacy through the self.**

On this basis, nonbeing is not a genuine alternative left open by what g is. That is the warranted necessity conclusion. The exercise argument illuminates why the ordinary possibility of nonnecessitating production does not remove it; it is not advertised as an exhaustive formal classification of every imaginable explanatory relation.

### Is self adequacy itself only contingently possessed

An objection might say: g has complete intrinsic adequacy actually, but in an alternative there is no g and no adequacy. This description is correct for many ordinary intrinsic properties. It cannot, without further argument, be carried over to the property now in question.

If “having adequacy” means a qualification of g that becomes available only once g's otherwise unaccounted being is granted, it is not adequate for that being. The proposal has reverted to the conditional form already examined. If instead it means the full intrinsic existential basis itself, describing that basis as optional simply reintroduces the unexplained obtaining that full self-adequacy excludes. The latter move contests the positive sufficiency judgment; it does not give a case preserving it.

This is not an inference that every intrinsic attribute is necessary, nor that every mode of a necessary being is fixed. The argument concerns the reality's sufficiency for its own existence. Acts and other qualifications can have a different explanatory subject and a different modal scope.

## 5 The developed affirmative inference

The strongest argument can now be stated without a detached modal definition.

1. **Actual starting point.** There is an actual original g contributing to the provision of received actuality. The productive-totality and bearer arguments, with their independently stated meanings, supply this witness.
2. **Existential adequacy.** Actual being does not acquire adequate provision from mere possibility or from a wholly unprovided occurrence. Where the concrete reality is not adequate through itself, actual being calls for an adequate existential contribution beyond that insufficient reality. This is the substantive foundational sufficiency judgment defended in the primary argument.
3. **Complete independence.** For the original now under consideration, no such external existential contribution is required. Its actuality is therefore adequate through its own reality. This applies the positive judgment in step 2; it is not merely excluded middle applied to the word “dependent.”
4. **Completion of the right target.** Because the target is g's own obtaining, this adequacy cannot presuppose a further contingent instantiation of g that it leaves unprovided. It cannot be an exercise requiring actual g in order to supply g. The actual self is not a separate possible essence plus a received existence-component, but neither does identity alone settle its existential status.
5. **Intrinsic necessity.** Consequently g's own reality does not leave its being existentially open in the way a merely instantiable nature does. Its sufficiency for being is through itself; genuine nonbeing of that same bearer is excluded by this existential character. g is necessary through itself.

Steps 2–5 articulate one developed rational argument. They should not be represented as a newly proved chain of logically independent neutral axioms. In particular, the source's foundational judgment reaches exactly the alleged possibility of unprovided contingent actuality. The further analysis shows why that judgment coheres with the relevant distinctions and why familiar conditional and nonnecessitating explanations do not answer the same question.

The assessment is affirmative: **the clarified judgment supplies sufficient philosophical warrant to accept the conclusion on the present record.** The fact that it is basic in part, and that an interlocutor can refuse it, does not entail suspension. Conversely, the present appraisal does not show that every dissenter is irrational, that every modal intuition is indefeasible, or that the thesis is a theorem of logic.

## 6 Why this foundational judgment earns weight

The judgment is not being accepted merely because the author announces it. Its positive rational case has four connected features.

**It keeps actuality and admissibility distinct.** An admissible description, ability to conceive an object, or absence of contradiction is not itself actual provision. When the actuality of the purported original is the target, adding “it just obtains” supplies the target again, not the adequate existential basis. The brute proposal's failure here is explanatory, not a claim that a nonexistent item has performed an action.

**It applies the same existential standard at the terminus.** Dependent reality is not allowed to account for itself by borrowed actuality. The original is not exempted by declaring that no explanation is needed for a favored name. It has a different positive status: its reality is adequate through itself. A contingently realized root left unexplained lacks precisely that positive distinction. This is why necessary originality is a principled terminus rather than an exception to “everything has an efficient producer.” The latter slogan is not the principle being used.

**It respects the absence of a separate self producer.** The self-sufficient existent does not have to do something to become or remain its own existential foundation. Production belongs where existence is supplied. Intrinsic adequacy belongs where existence is not in need of that supply. The distinction prevents the regress generated by demanding a producer even for what is independently adequate.

**It survives the strongest relevant clarifications in the completed record.** Beginninglessness does not settle the question; concrete essence/existence identity does not settle it; an instantiated-nature conditional does not settle it; explanations of effects do not settle it; a world table does not settle it. None of these supplies a new reason that an existentially insufficient original could nevertheless have fully provided actuality without intrinsic or external provision. They leave the positive judgment in place.

These considerations are reasons for assent, not an argument that lack of a certified counterexample alone proves the thesis. They also do not certify that any assertion called evident is warranted. The source's foundational epistemology permits such clarification and assessment; it does not require an endless derivation of clearer judgments from more obscure ones. [S1, S5]

## 7 Consequences now earned and consequences not imported

### This bearer rather than some bearer in each alternative

The conclusion concerns g, the already established actual original. The argument investigates whether **its** existential basis leaves **its** absence open. It never replaces g with a differently selected source. Consequently, there is no inference from “each alternative has some original” to “one original exists in every alternative.” The varying-witness issue identified in the companion is relevant as a control but is not the form of this argument.

### A local sufficiency commitment rather than unrestricted explanatory determinism

The argument requires no thesis that every truth has a necessitating explanation, that every choice has a sufficient contrastive determinant, or that all the original's effects are necessary. Its anti-brute judgment concerns the existential adequacy of an actual bearer; its special application concerns a bearer whose own being is not supplied. This narrower scope preserves the possibility of contingent received effects without withdrawing the necessity conclusion for g.

### Knowledge of existential character without a complete real definition

The positive result need not wait for an independently constructed catalogue of g's entire intrinsic nature. An argument from actual original existence can warrant the conclusion that its being is intrinsically sufficient while leaving much about the bearer unidentified. That is a legitimate increase in knowledge of the original, not a definition used to generate its instance. It establishes neither personality nor omniscience by itself.

### No automatic unity or unrestricted bearer claim

Intrinsic necessity of g does not establish that only one such bearer exists. Nor may necessarily accompanying attributes be counted as additional original bearers. The retained necessity-domain controls remain operative. The independent unity and sourcehood arguments keep their own tasks.

### No new formal theorem

A formal implication from `Actual(g)` and nonreceipt to `Necessary(g)` once `ContingencyNeed(g)` is assumed remains the inherited inference. Reproving it would not formalize the philosophical warrant developed here. Introducing a predicate defined to mean necessity, or declaring an existence-entailing intrinsic fact without earning its application, would conceal rather than solve the issue. No new Lean theorem, model, or execution is claimed.

The formal application should therefore say that this report **affirms and develops the philosophical warrant for the relevant fixed-g ContingencyNeed instance under the intended existential meanings**. It does not certify every arbitrary interpretation of `wholeReceived`, every supplied world domain, or `ConstitutiveReception`. In particular, actuality plus failure of whole receipt is not silently substituted for complete independence if the chosen representation leaves genuine external existential need compatible with that failure.

## 8 What a genuinely new challenge would need to establish

The argument is not awaiting another assertion that a beginningless original can be labeled contingent. A new substantive challenge has two possible forms.

**A positive defeating case** would give reason to accept that this very original can be genuinely absent while preserving complete actual intrinsic adequacy for its own existence in the sense above. It must explain what makes that adequacy complete without borrowing the obtaining to be accounted for, adding an external existential requisite, or appealing to an operation that already requires the bearer. A defended nonproductive and nonnecessitating mode of intrinsic existential adequacy could be relevant if it really meets those conditions. Naming the category is not yet such a defense.

**A rational undercutter** could instead give an independently motivated reason to reject or distrust the foundational adequacy judgment itself. It need not establish an entire rival cosmology or preserve the very premise it is criticizing. But it must engage that judgment: for example, explain why actuality can lack adequate intrinsic or external provision without this being an existential deficiency, rather than merely state that brute actuality is permitted in a chosen notation. An adequate undercutter need not establish a counterexample; its relevance depends on its reason for weakening the inference.

The demand here is not that every critic prove a stronger conclusion than the affirmative side. The affirmative side bears and has offered a positive rational account. A critic who disputes its foundation identifies a real philosophical disagreement. What has not followed from that disagreement is an equal positive case for the disputed contingent original or an automatic return to neutral suspension.

The exact remaining premise is therefore best named openly: **the no-unprovided-actuality judgment in the respect of the original's own being, including the judgment that authentic self-adequacy is not merely conditional actualization.** This report affirms its warrant. It does not list it as an unanswered question whose mere existence cancels the result.
