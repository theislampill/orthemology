## 2. Exact constitutional counterhistories

All four have exactly the same **entire old observation menu**: service, vote packet, founding text, membership history, publication record, appointment record and technical history. Adding a separately retained proposal record repairs examples 2 and 3 but does not repair example 4. A genuinely witnessed common procedure record plus the supplied founding convention restores discrimination for this family. Numbering rows does not create an observation of the actual order. If the available timestamping cannot resolve the vote/publication sequence, the right result is unresolved authority under this convention, unless the actual convention supplies an order-independent rule or a later authorized cure.

The author independently reproduced these witnesses and then enumerated all 120 orders of the five events under a separately disclosed zero-delay constitution. That extension has 2 valid appointments and 118 histories retaining P; it is author-produced additional evidence, not counted as this reviewer's independent test population. Its global sufficiency statement remains relative to that constitution and observed minutes.

### Legitimacy alternatives remain live

A convention that permits independent appointment already supplies a positive counterexample to equating lawful succession with causal descent. The experiment evaluates a stipulated convention; it cannot choose ultimate legitimate authority by running that convention against itself.

## 3. Report adoption beyond sign-mission authentication

The author's challenge target is the narrower question whether this response was supplied to this bearer for the reporting mission. It is **not** a claim that all later reports thereby become source-owned or true. The source-emission/access correlations and the {instruction, dispatch} minimum remain correct for its original chosen histories.

The independent extension asks a broader question: is this **fresh report occurrence** adopted by the original source under an independently given public undertaking? It executes source policies, interpreted source utterances, revocation, source assertions, emissions, actual dispatch, carrier handover and report occurrences. It does not pass the answer as a target Boolean.

Eight negative controls retain the coarse received sign, nonce response and earlier instruction words:

- A source publicly revokes the adoption policy, then sends the response for maintenance.
- A source publicly withdraws the reporting mission and redirects the use to maintenance, under a policy explicitly conditional on that mission.
- The report falls outside the policy's domain.
- The same contextual words are rebound to a later proposition.
- The apparent commissioning instruction was quoted rather than issued as a directive.
- A lookalike performs the apparent dispatch.
- E hands the token to F, who purports to issue a fresh report without the nondelegable office capacity.
- The original undertaking merely permits reporting and does not adopt reports as the source's assertions.

Actual dispatch identity defeats the lookalike. The other seven share even that additional observation while fresh-report adoption differs from the successful fixed-mission case. A fully grounded history and declared source-act semantics distinguishes the finite cases.

Two important boundaries prevent overstatement:

1. The reviewer's `prior_instruction_words` intentionally retains the earlier words. The author's original `original_instruction` channel collects its source-instruction events. A newly observed contrary instruction would be new evidence in a faithful complete instruction channel. The review does **not** claim that its deliberately coarser channel is identical to the author's full observation function after adding arbitrary events.
2. The negative target concerns a fresh institutional/source-adopted report, not every route to learning its proposition. F can faithfully relay an original source assertion without inheriting E's office or originating a new authorized assertion. The control explicitly preserves the original P assertion when its content is unchanged. Losing current office authority does not erase historical testimony.

### Positive controls against excessive authentication

The extension also checks two genuinely different positive policies:

- An independently established prospective, mission-scoped adoption covers E's new content even when that proposition is not the earlier emitted token's content.
- An independently established unconditional standing adoption can remain effective when a mission changes. A change of private intention, or a changed mission by itself, is not automatically a revocation of a still-applicable public undertaking.

Thus a fresh token-level endorsement is not universally required. The criterion comes from what the actual original source undertaking means, including its public conditions and applicable defeaters. The model does not purport to resolve the full philosophy of private illocutionary intention.

## 4. Constitutional remit and source adoption are separate

The author added a useful joining control after this review began: a valid constitutional amendment changes the office's remit from inventory to safety and validly appoints N. A fixed inventory-only source-adoption clause does not thereby expand to safety. A separately acquired source clause explicitly covering authorized remit changes can do so. Both true and false ordinary report states are retained, keeping ownership separate from veracity.

I inspected that implementation. It derives appointment and remit from the amendment procedure, then applies one of two explicit source sentences. It does not infer the source sentence from the new constitutional state. This is the right separation, conditional on the stipulated constitution and actual source utterance. A current office cannot unilaterally amend a different source's earlier scope by proclaiming its own remit.

## 5. Observability and ordinary warrant

The event interpreters have access to ground histories. A real recipient does not automatically have that access. Statements such as `P_terminal_emits`, `actual_dispatch`, a founding rule and an ordered record are modeled evidence channels whose fidelity, source identity, timeliness and availability must be warranted by an actual acquisition route. They are not authenticated by their labels. A record copied from the claimant is not made independent by storing it in another field.

At the same time, the all-worlds determinacy calculation is stronger than ordinary defeasible justification. A logically possible lying messenger may survive an otherwise excellent ordinary testimonial situation. The source explicitly permits ordinary context/character-based recognition. Absence of an exact discriminator in a finite possibility model alone does not establish absence of warranted belief.

The independent script therefore includes a transparent probability-sensitivity illustration: the same logical possibility of a false rival can coexist with an illustrative posterior of 9900/9901 or 1/2 under different supplied priors/likelihoods. These values are stipulated, not estimates of historical reliability or a definition of knowledge. Their role is only to prevent promoting logical non-entailment to universal epistemic defeat. Actual ordinary-warrant assessment needs grounds for the evidential weights, dependence and stakes; duplicating a record supplies no independent likelihood factor.

## 6. Disposition and worthwhile remaining questions

- **Original finite family:** supported at its explicit boundary; retained baseline 13/13 controls pass.
- **Cross-history constitutional observation sufficiency:** fails under executed plausible extensions; repaired conditionally by a genuinely observed operative joint procedure and rule. The author reproduced and expanded this result.
- **Sign-mission evidence as sufficient for every later source-owned report:** not established; the broader target requires actual scope, occurrence and undertaking conditions. The author did not claim such unrestricted sufficiency.
- **Ordinary positive testimony and scoped prospective adoption:** preserved. No redundant independent proof of the same proposition or obligation is imposed.
- **Real revelation authentication, external constitutional legitimacy, numerical identity and indefinite renewal:** unverified by these experiments. They require different evidence, not more renamed bits.

A useful next discrimination is a concrete application in which the actual source policy and authority procedure are available: test a disputed revocation time, adoption-remit amendment, permitted relay versus fresh office assertion, or independently witnessed event order. If the task remains abstract, specify the broader event language and prove its observation-to-decision condition at that scope before suggesting robustness beyond the listed histories. The generic information-loss mechanism is already inherited and must not be renamed a new theorem.

This is not a closure recommendation. It supplies a corrected evidence map and stronger research controls for the owner's later obligation-by-obligation judgement.
