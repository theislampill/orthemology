## 1. The controlling reports do not commit the proposed antecedence conflation

The no-demonstrated-conflation finding is warranted at its stated scope. CP20 explicitly distinguishes normative fittingness, actual productive willing, and a complete particular determining basis. It allows that a coarse stated reason may leave relevant practical and productive information unexpressed; it does not equate that omission with an ontologically indifferent complete situation. Its account neither requires a uniquely best normative option nor excludes actual willing from production.

The productive-sufficiency report is even more explicit: complete production includes actual exercise, the source does not posit a separate necessary productive antecedent coercing God into willing, and temporal priority must not be substituted for productive sufficiency or justification. The particular-agency report separately preserves positive practical self-governance and distinguishes knowing the selected act from acting for an apprehended reason.

The phrase “further adequate differentiating basis” in CP20 consequently needs the modest reading supplied by the new report: explanatory content beyond merely restating that this exercise occurred. It cannot be expanded, without another argument, into a separately actual earlier entity or a will-excluding mechanism. The present review found no passage in the inspected controlling reports that makes that expansion. This is a targeted finding about those reports, not an exhaustive theorem that no shorthand anywhere could mislead.

## 2. The positive intentional-temporal distinction is source-faithful

Majmūʿ 16:303–304 directly contrasts willing that an act occur at a future time with executive intent when that time comes. The former's content is not that the act be produced immediately. This explains a real difference of intentional and temporal role without treating the earlier stage as ignorance of the later result.

The intended external act and its intended time can remain the same across the two roles. The distinction therefore must not be inflated into two unrelated ends or proof of a separate independently existing volition-object for every event. Nor does naming the future act and occasion explain why those were selected in the first place. The report expressly acknowledges both limits.

This is more than saying generically that an original may have successive acts: the source supplies a specific prospective/executive distinction. It remains less than a complete individuation theory or a derivation of the particular entire history. The new report earns that intermediate positive result.

## 3. Intrinsic cognitive grounds do not introduce an unactualized external cause

The 16:297–299 context makes wisdom more than mere power, willing or undirected knowledge: action is informed and directed toward fitting purposes. The later claim that particular willing follows knowledge therefore cannot be reduced to a passive factive registration of whatever occurred.

At 16:304–305 the intended object's differentiating features are present in knowledge/conception rather than in an already existing external object. Specification by quality and measure is explicitly located in grounds within the agent. Bare standing capacity, common to alternatives, does not by itself discriminate. This directly supports the report's distinction between an intentional object and an extramental efficient donor.

There are still different questions: the content apprehended, the intrinsic cognitive/willing activity, and the produced external object. They are not interchangeable nodes in a causal account. The report does not infer that an external object exists before it exists, that generic omniscience alone produces every effect, or that cognition replaces actual willing.

The source supplies an explanatory organization and its intrinsic location. It does not identify every actual differentiating feature for a particular divine undertaking. Calling an unspecified feature “the feature responsible for the chosen q” would merely redescribe q. The report catches this rather than using the source's generic relation as a certificate for its missing arguments. That application limit does not erase the positive structural account.

## 4. Necessitation through choice is not external compulsion

The direct Miftāḥ 2:921–923 reply identifies choice itself as the determiner and rejects the inference from necessity through choice to absence of choice. The relevant 2:953–954 argument likewise places fitting determination in the subject's own wisdom and willing. Its positive clauses do not need the disputed terminal reading at 955, whose apparatus remains visible.

Bayān 5:253–255 distinguishes unreceived divine willing from created willing and relates occurrence/nonoccurrence to willing/nonwilling. The new report relies on the clear positive and concluding clauses, rather than repairing the inherited quarantined strings. These passages substantiate including actual voluntary efficacy in the determining basis. They do not independently remove the source's demand for intelligible particular determination.

Conversely, Majmūʿ 8:135–136 and 16:301–303 do reject an unchanged generic will, indifferent in the relevant respects, as an unexplained selector of particular alternatives and occasions. Merely swapping the grammar of active determination for resultant preponderance does not create an exception in the source's argument. The new report correctly distinguishes this source-position finding from a universal refutation of every rival theory of agent causation.

The Shifāʾ finite-subject regress restriction and the refusal to replace will by bare capacity plus knowledge remain inherited controls. They do not force an external donor for an everlasting original's every intrinsic exercise. I checked the decisive retained e175–178 material for this boundary; no new whole-work reading is claimed.

## 5. Anti-atomization must preserve differentiation

The 16:310–312 continuation expressly discusses differentiation without separation. The water/continuous-quantity comparison contrasts that organization with separated counted objects such as coins. It is not an argument that every knowing and willing is numerically one identical act, that contents cannot differ, or that distinguishable occurrences cannot be indexed.

The corrected report now makes that limitation explicit. A finite index can be representationally legitimate without claiming a physical partition, separately subsisting attribute-bearers, a canonical intrinsic atomization, or a complete cardinal inventory. Conversely, this passage does not itself prove any chosen indexing or tokenization adequate for every future explanatory task.

Some demonstratives and the nearby quantity wording are awkward in the electronic transcription. Neither this review nor the corrected report silently emends them to derive a precise identity doctrine. The clear differentiation/nonseparation clauses and the explicit 312 contrast suffice for the modest conclusion. No conclusion rests on treating two web presentations of the same argument as independent historical witnesses.

## 6. The remaining exact-q demand is appropriately bounded

A demand for independently warranted differentiation is appropriate when someone claims to have explained why this particular act, means or occasion was determined. It is over-demanding if turned into a condition for retaining every general warrant for wise agency, or a requirement that all normative reasons uniquely maximize a numerical score. The corrected report explicitly avoids both moves.

Its proposed application test asks what target is being explained, what features can be described other than as “whatever was chosen,” how they matter practically and productively, and how much discrimination has actually been achieved. That is a test of the explanatory claim being made, not an assertion that a competent agent must possess or communicate a complete history-level theory.

A further interpretive guard is useful: “independently warranted” means more than reverse-encoding the selected outcome; it need not prohibit learning a cause or reason from an observed effect through an independently supported relation. Nor does it require the reason to subsist separately or be temporally earlier. Those stronger demands would exceed this packet.

The existing achieved-end example already gives a substantive local practical discrimination while leaving the initial complete undertaking unexplained. Its bounded success remains a success. Likewise, absence of disclosed exact-q grounds is not evidence that the grounds are absent, that Wisdom lacks warrant, or that a rival original has been vindicated.
