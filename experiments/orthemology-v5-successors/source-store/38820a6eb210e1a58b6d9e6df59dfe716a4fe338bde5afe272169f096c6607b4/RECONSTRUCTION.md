The current historical argument has three distinct layers. T17 affirmatively appraises an actual same-bearer Necessary Being, essential whole-existence independence, eligible standing power, relevant knowledge/practical perfection and exceptionless source-owned veracity. T18 retains those conclusions and closes an attempted unrestricted noncausal fact-to-bearer extension. Those are the final reports’ conclusions. They are not merely hypothetical suggestions and are not computationally certified truths about reality. The live philosophical dissent remains attached to its exact premise. The new coordinating reassessment is a separate dated overlay; it does not rewrite the T19 historical verdict.

The small conditional necessity and essential-reception inferences, and the exact local-cover/connectedness graph theorem, already have retained Lean owners. The main unformalised join is the passage from T19’s substantive causal/evaluative appraisals to the exact all-ancestor-cone local-coverage predicate and its actual productive interpretation. A theorem that simply assumes that strong predicate will not test whether the philosophical join earns it.

This deliverable is a source-grounded reconstruction, not the requested later formal implementation. No Lean source was written or compiled. ## B Necessary existence of the same witness

Fix A07’s g. B01 says that if this actual bearer is genuinely contingent in its whole existence, that existence is received. Its actual originality excludes that receipt. Actuality excludes impossibility. The report therefore concludes that this same actual bearer is necessary (T17 P00053–P00054).

The direct existing Lean formulation is even more economical: actual Root(g) plus actual existence and nonnecessity implying receipt yields Necessary(g), with classical contradiction explicit. `Necessary` quantifies over a supplied world type; `actual : W` makes that type inhabited. This formulation need not introduce an extra modal trichotomy predicate. If T20 instead formalises contingency as possible existence plus possible absence, the actual-point/possibility relation and classical equivalence must be explicit. A finite world index is not itself an exhaustive metaphysical possibility space.

The retained owner is `SourceIdentity.necessary_of_actual_root`, not a prospective T20 discovery. Its receipt predicate is an existential binary dependency edge between individuals. Treating it as every form of ontological derivation requires a representation argument; no self-edge trick should be described as actual causation.

B01’s warrant remains philosophical. Neither making it a Lean parameter nor deriving a contradiction under it proves its truth. The report accepts its rational connection and preserves a critic who withholds assent. The result does not yet establish essential nonreceipt, necessary particular creation or one necessary total history.

## D The exact existing structural theorem

Sixth `ConnectedUnity` takes a relation r that is irreflexive and transitive on an inhabited domain D. Its precise definitions are:

- Below(a,b): a=b or r(a,b)
- LocalCover(g,x): Below(g,x), and every y Below x is itself Below-covered by g
- LocallyComplete: every x in D has such a local cover
- Connected: every two nodes are joined by a finite undirected path in r
- LeastAncestor(g): g is Below every node
- Root(g): no r-predecessor

On that input, local completeness plus connectedness produces a common local cover, a least ancestor and a unique graph root. The reverse least-ancestor implication also holds. The full structural package is therefore extensionally equivalent to the common-least-source condition at this mathematical level. The substance must come from independent warrant for its interpreted inputs.

The exact all-ancestor-cone quantifier is decisive. One root somewhere upstream of x does not cover an unsupported branch feeding x. The existing countably infinite Fork control has connectedness and one root yet no complete cover. For one target, the exact repair is unique root ancestry plus root support for every ancestor; universally quantified unique root ancestry is enough only because it already supplies support at each ancestor. Replacing the infinite branch by a finite truncation changes that control.

T19 P00094 explicitly maps actual scope to P0, faithful strict productive ancestry to P1, local complete coverage to P2, productive connectedness to P3 and productive interpretation to P4. This is the directly read current retrospective mapping. The original Sixth author P0–P4 prose files are custody-only in the inspected checkout; they have not been quoted as though read. The actual Lean files and full independent connected-source review are available and inspected.

There are two P1 labels: Sixth ancestry P1 and the broader original-completion P1. They denote different claims. The graph uses A03 and D11 to keep them distinct.

Reflexive coverage does not mean self-production. A singleton can cover itself with no actual edge. Even a least-source graph does not determine an independently supplied act-accounting relation. Actual sourcehood, a productive witness and the faithful act interpretation remain separate.

## E CND is neither sufficiency nor solitary-production logic

The five-way discrimination is preserved:

1. A alone could produce a matching effect.
2. A’s actual exercise is sufficient without completion from B.
3. A exclusively exhausts the whole actual production.
4. A and B each purport to supply the same entire concrete production nonexclusively.
5. A and B contribute genuinely differentiated productive work.

The contradiction between two exclusive whole-alone producers follows from claim 3. It does not convert claim 2 into claim 3 or refute claim 4. T19’s scope correction preserves that point.

CND is an additional substantive principle at the same concrete contribution and productive respect. Genuine additional original production has differentiated causal content; primitive duplication of that entire production is excluded. It is not a prohibition on multiple descriptions, sufficient conditions, levels of agency or genuine differentiated cooperation. The report accepts the productive-individuation rationale defeasibly, while explicitly saying determinate content does not analytically imply nonduplication.

A formal definition of complete provision as an exclusive singleton provider would settle this dispute by definition. A faithful pre-CND language must allow multiple alleged entire providers of a fixed contribution/respect. Whether such a model is philosophically possible is a different question.

## F Source-mode perfection is a governing norm

T19 P00060–P00063 accepts adequate unborrowed actual provision as the fitting mode of a qualified original’s actual productive role. It is not merely the claim that A has the standing ability to do more. Conversely, retaining standing power does not vindicate every actual exercise. The source-mode premise excludes differentiated foreign original completion that CND allows.

The crucial evaluative move is from a fuller role being superior in a respect to its being the required mode of this qualified original’s actual agency. A critic can ask why freely choosing a narrower role cannot be fitting. Calling it an undertaking must not assign the whole composite role in advance (P00061). The report accepts the governing classification on its positive rationale and preserves that dissent.

P00076–P00078 expressly describes an inferential clarification or repair: the source itself claims independence in actions and effects, but local closure grounded in essential original mode is not a derivation explicitly completed in Minhaj 2:183. The strongest literal source inference from received efficacy to lack of necessary self-existence has a token/essential-constitution tension. The repaired immediate conclusion is incompatibility with the qualified original mode, not the illicit converse that any received token state makes a whole bearer received.

No timelessness of every token, indiscriminate activity maximisation, creation of every possible object or perpetual preservation of every effect is entailed.

## G Mediate attribution and the local closure join

If A originally supplies productive reality actually used by derived H to produce r, A remains in r’s actual original productive account. H remains a genuine proximal agent. This alone establishes neither A’s complete original provision of r nor A’s intentional ownership of H’s assertion.

P00083 adds source mode: if an independent peer supplies part of r’s actual original basis, mediation does not turn that foreign contribution into A’s supply or delete A’s own contribution. P00087–P00091 applies this to directly shared products, later modification, mixed derived processes and occurrence-sensitive conditions. An earlier act, the state it produces and a later act of the same bearer cannot be collapsed into one event to manufacture a cycle. Calling a genuine foreign productive resource an occasion does not remove it; treating every occasion or impediment absence as such a resource is equally unsupported.

Two interfaces should be compared rather than silently merged:

- Contribution/respect-level route: a qualified participating A must adequately cover all actual positive productive requisites of its actual role; a foreign peer’s real original contribution remains represented; CND then excludes primitive whole-contribution duplication. P00083/P00093 need not first make that foreign peer qualified to identify foreign completion of A’s role.
- Coarse effect route: if complete means only sufficient for a coarse effect, a partial foreign contribution can remain. Qualifying every contributor or assigning every complete role requires additional premises. P00097 forbids treating an unexplained or efficient-only contribution as already qualified.

The first reading is closer to the report’s contribution-sensitive account, but the exact role-to-result/all-ancestor closure still needs an explicit semantic derivation. No unique atomic decomposition is warranted. A representation may index arbitrary actual contribution/respect pairs if its completeness is separately stated. D16 records that adequacy burden.

## H The common provider and its bounds

The intended two-stage structure is:

1. Actual qualified productive source + complete original account + mediate provenance + source-mode norm + CND → substantive local coverage.
2. Exact local coverage + strict faithful ancestry + connected actual field → one common source, with productive interpretation separately supplied.

The first arrow is a substantive T19 philosophical application, not already proved by the graph library. The second already has a precise retained kernel theorem. If P2 is assumed outright in an omnibus theorem, CND, source mode and mediation can become formally unused because they belong to the justification of P2. That is an important dependency finding, not evidence that the philosophical premises are dispensable in the whole argument.

“One” means the same complete original provider of the warranted field, excluding an independent co-original contributor within it. It does not mean every necessary reality outside the field is numerically identical. The wider absolute-supremacy route needs its own unrestricted comparative premise. The activity-kind appraisal against a permanently idle qualified duplicate does not exclude a disconnected active original.

T19 P00148 supplies an essential final join. If the earlier attribute witness is an actual independent original contributor to this same field, the exclusion identifies it with the common provider. If participation is not established, two existential conclusions do not identify their witnesses. Alternatively, the accepted attribute rationale must be reapplied to the suitably qualified common source. The graph cannot smuggle in either identification.

### Anchored propagation alternative for review

A promising design candidate, not a newly established T19 theorem, uses separate source, effect/occurrence and concrete contribution/respect types. Let Req(e,c) say c is an actual requisite of e and Entire(s,c) say s provides the entire original contribution c. Entire must permit multiple alleged providers before CND is assumed. Participation(s,e) can be witnessed by a required contribution, while Complete(s,e) requires provision of every actual requisite. A qualified fixed source g’s source-mode condition would then carry participation to completeness.

A faithful productive crossing represented by a shared actual requisite can transfer g’s participation from a completely supplied effect to its neighbour. Induction from one actual qualified anchor through the connected field could propagate completeness; CND then excludes any peer’s alleged original provision of an already supplied contribution. This avoids universally qualifying every peer, and need not assume Sixth’s full all-ancestor LocalCover as a premise.

The substantive work is showing that the chosen Req/Entire/crossing relations faithfully express T19’s actual-role, later-material, differentiated-work and mediate-provenance claims. Shared topics or mere equality of coarse endpoints cannot count as crossings. Some derivative interactions may require a lifted mediation relation rather than literal membership of one contribution in both immediate effects. The proposed design does not settle those questions, and the coordinating assessment’s current withholding of the source-mode warrant does not prevent checking its conditional consequences.

### Faithful incidence and original contributor visibility

The anchored design also needs two directions that should not share one predicate silently. A represented Entire witness for a required contribution must soundly establish an actual source-mode role through real productive use. Conversely, every actual original contributor to a target must have a represented witness if the final conclusion is meant to exclude actual rivals rather than only indexed rivals. An originally supplied capacity merely present nearby supplies neither direction.

A mixed occurrence can join two distinct upstream effects even when those effects do not directly share a requisite: a later result retaining both inputs supplies the connecting target. The target sort must therefore contain the relevant concrete mixed occurrences and historical requisites, rather than arbitrary manufactured bundles. The requisite inventory needs same-token/same-respect soundness and completeness. Even after a typed incidence theorem succeeds, its completeness predicate will not automatically equal LocalCover on an independently interpreted ancestry graph; that comparison needs its own proof or must remain a separate formal route.
