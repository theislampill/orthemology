# Complete efficacy, intrinsic exercise, and the reactive rival

Targeted source-critical continuation, 7 October 2026 UTC. This study responds to the new quasigroup interpretation. It does not change the earlier archives or source files, certify a full rival metaphysics, or claim research closure.

## Result

The source does **not** generally identify an unchanged intrinsic power, will, or act with an unchanged complete cause of its external effect. Recipients, material powers, preservation, temporal conditions, and wisdom can belong to the completion of an operation. The inspected continuous Dar passages expressly distinguish these. Nor does the necessary perfection of original activity imply that every particular exercise is timelessly complete or that every newly attained end is an externally acquired intrinsic perfection.

However, **Majmu 20:175 really asserts the stronger nonabsorption principle for an unchanged productive contributor**. It must not be reduced to the much weaker claim that a fully specified complete cause has its effect. The source deliberately argues that two genuinely productive unchanged contributors cannot jointly produce merely the same complete created result as either produces alone. Its next argument deliberately links complete power and decisive will to production through that agent alone. The quasigroup rival contradicts those intermediate claims, including in its concordant actual profiles. It is not a source-faithful model of the complete affirmative package.

The unresolved point is their **independent warrant against this particular rival**. The strongest positive rationale is comprehensive unborrowed efficacy: an original must supply the actual adequacy of its chosen production, including whatever makes the product possible and sustained, rather than be an unreceived but partially adequate contributor. Importantly, **actual relational efficacy itself can be the perfection in question**. Demanding an additional donation of a new intrinsic entity would impose an unwarranted requirement on the source argument. Yet the inspected original-being arguments do not establish, without that stronger adequacy premise, that the rival's actual joint efficacy is an alien completion of a constitutive original perfection. The source expressly makes that transfer; the rival expressly disputes it. This pass has not independently settled a violation of the earlier anti-self-foundation, efficient-nonreceipt, or genus-of-perfect-activity premises.

The useful new discrimination is stronger than another report of the gap. Continuous Dar context separates **completion of original agency as such** from **completion for this particular act**. The middle Asbahani sequence, printed 418–419, positively conjoins successively attained ends, actual particular creative efficacy, perfection, and independence. These controls prevent an overly strong interpretation of the perfection bridge. They do not positively establish plural original agency.

## 1. Exact semantic distinctions

Four different things must be kept apart:

1. A bearer's standing intrinsic power and knowledge.
2. Its particular intrinsic willing and act.
3. The complete operative basis for a specified result: the relevant exercise together with all actual conditions, recipient dispositions, and absence of impediments.
4. Individually exhaustive original provision of that result, including any created conditions through which it is realized.

The third is not automatically the second. The fourth is not automatically the third. A complete joint basis can be described without assigning its entirety to either contributor. Whether such a basis can consist of coequal originals is precisely the disputed application.

There is a second distinction in the object: the **whole actual created result Q** is not the same as an alleged **created contribution cA within Q**. MajmuL914 and Dar9 0628–0629 describe cooperation through different partial work. That is positive evidence for real contribution plurality. It does not itself independently identify each cA in source-neutral terms, prove its unchanged manifestation across peer contexts, or establish that A originally supplies the whole Q. Conversely, a model denying sole Q-provision cannot answer a contribution-individuation argument merely by repeating that denial. The two proof obligations remain separate.

### تام and كمال التأثير

Dar subsection 013:640–655 explicitly rejects an eternally complete particular cause as an explanation of successive effects. At 645–646 its positive account says complete efficacy entails the effect immediately consequent upon its completion; this is not a doctrine of delayed effects from an already completed operative basis. At 652 the traveler's continuing intention remains while completion of the next segment has an additional temporal condition. At 654 the second will, word, or act is conditioned on the first's completion, and the text says that **with the first's passing, the complete producer for the second is completed**. [D1]

Consequently, “complete” here is effect-relative and operative. A standing bearer can retain its nature while the basis for a particular exercise is not yet complete. The illustrative temporal condition is not an alien donor of the original's standing capacity.

Safadiyya's retained 0623–0628 is especially explicit: the essence entails the **kind** of efficacy, not a particular act or product. Each effect exists upon completion of efficacy for it; that completion is attributed to the essence when conditions obtain and impediments cease. This confirms a distinction already present in Dar, not a new universal license for dependence on another original. The preceding 0618–0619 initially reasons under the opponent's simultaneity assumption; its wording must not erase Dar's own immediate-succession qualification. [S1]

### استقلال

In the unity passages, استقلال is stronger than an available solo constructor or success after peer suppression. Dar9 0627–0629 and Majmu L906/L909 define its relevant respect through the whole product's being through the bearer alone, excluding a partner in that original provision. The rival's ordinary joint run expressly lacks this. Describing its standing repertoire as complete cannot grant it the source's stronger independence by a change of terminology. [D3; F1]

Conversely, the incompatibility of two **individually exhaustive** providers does not by itself establish that all unreceived productive agents must be individually exhaustive. The denial of that inference is not the denial of causal nonduplication in its stated respect.

### حاله and فعله

Majmu L908 mentions changes in will, movement, instruments, and similar operative features, and closes with equality of the agent's two states. The wording is broader than sameness of a software token or standing power. It does not explicitly say that every external recipient condition is unchanged. Nor is its argument restricted to an already identical complete aggregate cause: adding the second agent is itself a change in that aggregate.

Dar9 0635–0643 is therefore an essential control. It expressly distinguishes intrinsic human action from its external result and attributes the latter to the act plus the stone, air, food's powers, bodily reception, organs, or assistants. This is not a universal law that an intrinsic exercise, independently of context, fixes an invariant external manifestation. [D3]

The source's strongest reading is: an **unchanged genuinely operative contribution** cannot simply disappear into another unchanged productive contribution. For original agency, complete control of the conditions is then supposed to block the ordinary contextual qualification. That is a substantive contribution-realization and original-adequacy norm. It is neither a merely verbal definition of completeness nor a theorem about all unchanged intrinsic acts.

## 2. The maker-preserver discussion: what completion includes

Dar subsection 013:221–267 contains Ibn Rushd's quotation followed by Ibn Taymiyyah's successive replies. The fifth reply at 257–258 and the express acceptance at 264 affirm the superiority of the maker who also preserves its product. The closing 266 affirms dependence in both origination and remaining. These are affirmative endorsements, not merely the quoted opponent's view. [D1]

But the second reply rejects producing a condition, such as motion, as equivalent to producing the world's substances and other attributes. At 261 the carpenter's or builder's intrinsic act is distinguished from the receptive bodies' powers to accept and retain composition. Their continuing retention is not credited to a continuing human operation that is not occurring. At 262 the original creator is distinguished as originator of the substances, attributes, and motions themselves.

This gives the affirmative position an independent **comprehensive explanation** rationale. A proposed original explanation cannot leave an independently available recipient, preserving power, or mechanism unexplained while claiming to explain the entire effect. It also makes continued existence part of the question.

It does not yield one fresh created sustaining token per root, or require each sustaining act to have a separately identifiable extra external effect. Still less does it imply that every new intrinsic act is itself a created product. Minhaj V02L0243–0246 expressly distinguishes intrinsic creating from the created thing and distinguishes succession of acts from regress of creators. [M4]

The strongest rival's primitive joint law must really explain the created result, rather than hide an independently created mixer or merely name a relation. Yet if its primitive joint productive relation is accepted for the sake of testing the inference, maker-preserver comprehensiveness alone does not choose between one complete original ground and a jointly complete basis of unreceived contributors. That further choice needs the original-independence argument.

## 3. The strongest independent original-being/perfection argument

### 3.1 Nonreceipt is more than necessary co-occurrence

Minhaj 2:167–172 distinguishes a property's inherence, necessary concomitance, and efficient production. The prohibition on reciprocal completion concerns one producer conferring what makes the other capable of producing. It is compelling where the very efficacy needed to confer that completion is itself produced by the recipient. Nothing could get underway in that explanatory order. Mere necessary co-occurrence is explicitly distinguished. [M1]

Dar9 0713–0722 directly considers necessary agreement, reciprocal efficient receipt, harmless concomitance, and the claim that each power is intrinsic. At 0721–0722 it argues that intrinsic original power must permit action through that power without needing another. The source therefore did not overlook the nonreceipt reply. Its next move, 0723–0725, requires independent production, and its strengthened explanatory argument at 0751 says that without an independently capable source there would be no occurrences at all. [D3]

The independent force is the requirement to terminate efficient enabling, not merely rename an ungrounded dependency. The disputed extension is identifying **a primitive joint manifestation of already operative unreceived powers** with the reciprocal enabling just excluded. A rule's dependence on two productive inputs is not already proof that one input produces the other's power or intrinsic act. Nor is a graph without such edges proof that no relevant perfection is externally completed.

### 3.2 The source expressly counts actual efficacy as perfection

Minhaj 2:178–180 is the strongest affirmative transfer. After discussing the outer sphere's recipients, materials, and other motions, it argues that its influence is conditioned by another's influence, and that **its efficacy belongs to its perfection**, because an efficacious being is more perfect than an inefficacious one. Thus dependence is not confined to a newly donated intrinsic power-property. The actuality and adequacy of efficacy count. [M1]

At 2:182–184 the source's general perfection argument excludes both impossibility and alien donation of a genuine eligible perfection in the necessary being. At Majmu L921 the effect-to-agent transfer is explicit: dependence in a product implies dependence in the intrinsic act and property from which it proceeds. [M2; M3; F1]

The strongest reconstruction is therefore:

- An original necessary bearer possesses its constitutive pure perfections through itself.
- Genuinely complete productive efficacy is a pure perfection, not mere correct prediction or idle intrinsic readiness.
- A chosen original undertaking must be actually adequate through its own original resources. Created means are compatible with this when their relevant adequacy is itself originally provided by the same source.
- An independently original peer supplying some of the undertaking's actual completion would leave that perfection externally completed.
- Such an undertaking is incompatible with the necessary original's self-sufficiency.

This is a serious argument with an explanatory and evaluative rationale. It must not be dismissed as a naked definition. But the third and fourth claims are the live bridge for the present rival. The first two do not establish that **every particular successful intrinsic undertaking** must already be individually exhaustive, nor that contingent relational joint realization necessarily supplies an intrinsic or constitutive perfection to its participant.

### 3.2a Relational perfection is the fair test, not an intrinsic-donation-edge test

The affirmative argument can be put without postulating a new property deposited in A: **A's actual productive efficacy is one of A's perfections; whatever irreducibly original resources constitute its actual adequacy must therefore be attributable to A itself.** A peer can violate this norm by supplying part of the actual original basis even if no new power, knowledge, or will is efficiently implanted in A. The relevant claim is about whose resources make the undertaking actually adequate, not about the numerical identity of a new intrinsic entity. Minhaj2:180 supplies direct evidence for that broader evaluative scope; MajmuL921 explicitly connects it with original self-sufficiency.

This strengthens the affirmative appraisal relative to any assessment treating the model's no-donation graph as a full rebuttal. The graph answers one causal-receipt question. It does not answer whether A is the original source of the actual sufficiency of this undertaking. Conversely, pointing to {A,B} as productive support does not establish that A has received a constitutive perfection, unless the broader norm or the relevant account of actual need is independently defended.

The norm has a concrete positive rationale: explanatory self-sufficiency should extend to efficacy actually exercised, rather than leave the bearer intrinsically ready but its original achievement completed elsewhere. The maker-preserver argument makes clear why merely supplying a trigger while borrowing independently available material or conservation does not constitute comprehensive original production. Created means ultimately supplied by A satisfy that rationale differently from an independently original B. The source is not prohibiting means as such.

There are consequently two quite different positive controls:

1. **Overstrong norm:** every new attained end, changed means, or externally related result is an alien acquisition of perfection. Dar's successive operations and Asbahani418–419 falsify that reading on the source's own terms. An effect can be newly realized through the original's own agency without perfect nature first being supplied from elsewhere.
2. **Narrow norm:** every independently original contribution to the actual sufficiency of A's chosen undertaking makes that undertaking externally completed in a perfection-relevant respect. Neither source control falsifies this. The passages affirm independence alongside attained efficacy and allow means whose original basis is not foreign. They never endorse an original peer's co-completion.

The present rival is not yet a positive metaphysical counterexample to the narrow norm. It is an explicit interpretation that denies its application while preserving the weaker structural conditions. To settle the point in its favor it must explain why A's real joint efficacy is already fully A's appropriate perfection rather than a partially external achievement. To settle it against the rival, the affirmative account must justify why this actual relational adequacy is a constitutive pure perfection demanding exhaustive original self-provision, even when A's same intrinsic activity and specified own end succeed under peer suppression. The source gives a substantial argument for this demand; this pass does not convert that argument into a derivation from the thinner nonreceipt condition.

### 3.3 Why the generic-perfection premise cannot silently do that work

Dar subsection 016:130–159 explicitly separates a condition for **this particular act** from a condition for **the origin of agency**. At 141 the contrast is “فعله لهذا المعين لا أصل الفعل”; at 144 the anti-regress conclusion concerns أصل الفعل, not performance of a particular thing. At 153–154 successive intrinsic volitions and acts are positively available in the author's answer to the eternal-world argument. [D2]

Dar016:338–398 then directly challenges the argument that passage from potential to actual must result from an external cause. At 354–356 it distinguishes a bare essence from an essence with its intrinsic acts. At 359–360 it allows becoming the actual maker of these particular occurrences without an external efficient cause. At 373–377 it expressly warns that renaming such activity “change” does not prove it impossible. At 391 conditions making a particular effect possible or wise belong to the analysis of complete power and complete will. The nearby 395–396 formulation is partly an argument against the opponent, so it cannot be universalized into authorial approval of every conceivable external impediment. [D2]

These passages independently block the shortcut: new actual efficacy, new loved end, or a different selected means therefore entails an originally deficient nature or externally received perfection. They do not block the narrower argument that **this particular sort of alien original completion** is defective. That narrower argument still has to establish its classification.

This temporal evidence must not be overused. The rival does not claim that its full-Q exercise is merely an early fragment of a later completed act. Nor is the affirmative case committed to timeless completion of every isolated micro-act. An original could supply an entire causally ordered means-complex through its own successive activities. What these passages establish is the distinction needed to formulate the present question accurately; they do not refute the claim that the **actual whole-Q original undertaking**, once complete, must derive its entire original adequacy from one bearer.
