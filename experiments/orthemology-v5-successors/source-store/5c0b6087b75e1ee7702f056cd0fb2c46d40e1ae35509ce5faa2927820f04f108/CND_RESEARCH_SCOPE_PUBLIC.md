# Same-bearer essential independence after T19

7 October 2026. Bounded research specification, projected from the original record. It is not a prospective preregistration or a claim to have preceded every reading.

## Question

Does T19's independently accepted causal nonduplication supply an additional reason for T17's same actual Necessary Being to be essentially whole-unreceived? Can a production-only argument improve on the already accepted constitutive inference without treating intrinsic existential sufficiency as self-production?

## Controlling baseline

The actual recovered T17–T19 final reports control the inquiry. T17 already affirmatively accepts original existential completion, qualified-bearer transport, an actual particular necessary witness and essential independence under its constitutive judgement. T18 restricts an attempted unrestricted noncausal extension without retracting that result. T19 retains it and adds a defeasibly warranted common original provider of the connected actual productive field under CND, source-mode perfection and faithful attribution. The present inquiry must not revive the earlier T16 verdict.

## Method and stopping condition

Read the source arguments in their retained context; distinguish authorial assertion, reconstructed argument and judgement of warrant. Provide an exact candidate claim, explicit premises, a bounded positive lemma and discriminating assignments. A formal assignment diagnoses inferential scope; it does not certify metaphysical possibility, evidential parity or a defeater of T17. Do not mount a large proof campaign for a short implication. Stop when the added premise cost and its relation to the already accepted constitutive warrant are clear enough for independent review.
