The seven exact bodies are rounds2,3,4,5,7,9,10, covering self-subsistence, qiyās/Speech, fiṭrah/restoration, property ontology, unity/identity, cross-school semantics and norm provenance. All bodies were read completely. Their frozen 9b80… authority and pre-primary-verification limitations are historical status; they must not replace the later source-based T17–T20 affirmative appraisal or generate new campaigns already completed.

## Useful retained contributions

Round2 distinguishes a root in a recorded relation from absence of an actual relevant incoming ground. Incoming completeness suffices for that elementary lift; edge soundness alone does not. But absence of an incoming edge under an uninterpreted relation is not positive actual existential adequacy, productive actuality, or necessity through oneself. Its 'nonborrowed contingent object' control only separates those weak formal predicates. It has no automatic defeater credit against the later stronger original-adequacy argument. A supposed complete frame must likewise be independently interpreted; a frame constructed to secure a result supplies no metaphysical warrant.

Rounds3 and9 distinguish positive qualification, authorised Name, act capacity, particular occurrence, content, created recitation and recipient warrant. Their six qiyās guards are explicitly a project synthesis, and the displayed conditional is modus ponens rather than a philosophical proof of those premises. Later source arguments may warrant the premises without any new logical operator. The early translated p.44 volition uncertainty and then-open Arabic comparison must be updated using the subsequent source-owned corrections; this reading adds no fresh Arabic witness. The shared vocabulary of rival ontologies is not evidence of faithful interpretation, truth or parity.

Round4 applies the inherited fibre criterion to restoration. Memorised correct output, coerced assent and genuine restored recognition can coincide on an output-only profile; absence of report can likewise conceal recognition. This is a conditional representation limitation, not empirical establishment that a particular recipient was impaired or restored. Account-specific proper-function conditions cannot be replaced by one scalar success measure. Nor should the proposed assessor's source/measurement certificate be mistaken for a requirement that every warranted knower explicitly possess a reflective proof of their own cognitive mechanism.

Round5 cleanly separates property theory from mind–body property dualism, and both from common-bearer incidence. Its common reduct can fail to discriminate these interpretations. This does not make all listed ontologies extensionally or intensionally equivalent, or prove their metaphysical possibility. Distributed realization changes the bearer-local incidence relation unless the observational reduct explicitly erases it. Reading the list as one fixed bearer-incidence countermodel across every architecture would be a semantic mismatch.

Round10 separates path-independent numerical correction from semantic fidelity, truth connection, function fixing, reason-guided uptake, authority, recipient warrant and execution. Its certificate stages are an audit specification, not universal definitions or new entailments between those notions. The potential theorem must keep the exact graph/holonomy hypotheses of its controlling mathematical source. Merely checking all directed cycles in an acyclic directed graph does not establish path independence between distinct paths; the historical shorthand must not broaden the already qualified theorem.

## A precise dependency refinement in round7

The unity certificate's guards are sufficient safeguards but are not always a minimal independent list. U1 says every world has exactly one eligible role-bundle bearer. If U2's transports preserve that eligibility on every edge, then the unique chosen eligible token at one endpoint necessarily maps to the unique eligible token at the other. Every selected-anchor holonomy therefore fixes that token automatically. No separate U3 path-independence premise is needed for existence/uniqueness of that selected role section under those stronger singleton-fibre assumptions.

Proof: choose the unique eligible token s(w) at each world. For an edge w→v, eligibility preservation puts T(s(w)) in the singleton eligible fibre at v, hence T(s(w))=s(v). Any other eligible section agrees pointwise by uniqueness. Composing edge equalities gives anchor fixation. This establishes only a section relative to the declared frame. It does not turn the chosen tokens into one numerical extramental bearer or discharge B_ID.

The packet's triangular-swap example says each world has an eligible carrier and each edge a bijection. A swap without a fixed eligible anchor requires a nonsingleton eligible fibre (or failed preservation). It therefore cannot establish U3-independence while retaining U1 and U2 as written. It remains a valid obstruction in the weaker nonsingleton-fibre setting. This is a correction to the dependency interpretation, not a rejection of the original holonomy theory or a new general section theorem.
