# Primary arithmetic source audit

The author downloaded both primary PDFs and used the PDF inspection workflow.
Only the listed passages received substantive reading credit.

- Karl Zsigmondy, Zur Theorie der Potenzreste, Monatshefte für Mathematik und
  Physik3 (1892),265–284. Printed p.283 is PDF page19. The specialised order theorem
  and its exceptions were visually read; the adjacent use of base4 directly
  confirms the required specialisation. Printed282 was also read in extraction.
  DOI10.1007/BF01692444. The archived PDF's MD5 matches the Zenodo record.
- Kálmán Győry and Chris Smyth, The divisibility of a^n−b^n by powers of n,
  Integers10 (2010),319–334. Lemma6 and Theorem13 were checked at printed323 and325,
  with the intervening primitive-divisor definition. The theorem page was
  visually inspected. This is corroboration, not a full article appraisal.
