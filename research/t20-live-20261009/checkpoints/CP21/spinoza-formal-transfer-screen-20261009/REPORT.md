# Spinoza formal transfer screen

9 October 2026. Focused T20 candidate-source operation. Read-only inspection; no repository execution, build, installation, kernel replay, integration, or metaphysical countermodel certification.

## Result

Retain this as a **rejected substantive transport with useful semantic controls**. The source supplies neither a new argument for numerical identity of original bearers nor a warranted plural-original alternative. Its narrow mathematical examples distinguish full attribute-profile agreement from overlap and distinguish distributed attribute realization from realization in every designated god. Those distinctions are worth keeping, but the relevant identity and ontology bridges are assumptions or interpretations, not results about Orthemology.

There is a consequential version split: the arXiv v2 text and the pinned repository implement different countermodel premise packages. The current repository repairs one acknowledged defect, while its phrase about a full stated register still includes four content-free axiom placeholders. This is a scoping restriction, not proof that the missing substantive constraints are false.

## Source synopsis

Nakamura investigates selected reconstructions of Spinoza’s Propositions V and XIV in Lean. His A22 distinguishes different substances by some attribute, yielding identity from agreement on all attributes; a finite countermodel blocks identity from merely sharing one. Another model satisfies attribute plenitude while blocking universal possession by each god. Adding god-uniqueness yields that universal possession. The arXiv version explicitly limits its countermodels to a predicate signature plus the selected augmentations, acknowledges fidelity problems, and leaves stronger PSR and Garrett-style reconstructions open. It offers a methodological case study, not an adjudication of every reconstruction. Its priority claims are not independently established here.

Source: [arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.02331v2), read continuously through abstract, §§1–9, and references. This synopsis is under 200 words.

## A22 and the exact identity question

A22 contains only substance status, inequality, and attribute incidence. It has no fact domain, explanation relation, sufficient-reason predicate, causal-production relation, or theorem deriving the incidence condition from a general PSR. It stipulates that different substances have different attribute profiles. Under classical logic this is an injectivity criterion for those profiles. Its six-line positive theorem consumes EthicaWorld and PSRSubstance alone, plus both substance hypotheses and pointwise equality of the complete profiles. [ModalForm 365–396](https://github.com/Nakammura/spinoza-ethica-lean/blob/6d4c0461265e50a3bbedb640fe60d9ad292b50a7/Ethica/Pars1/ModalForm.lean#L365-L396).

The difference is equality of two sets of attributes versus nonempty intersection. Neither expression means numerical identity of essence. The shared witness a is one identical carrier element used in both incidence statements; it is not merely two distinct intrinsic qualities falling under a shared concept.

A further formula-level qualification: A12 alone does not imply A22. Two attribute-free substances make the former vacuous and the latter false. A14 supplies the nonemptiness needed for the stated A12-plus-A14 implication. This is a transparent logical check, not a new replayed theorem.

## A15 and the uniqueness premise

The directly inspected positive proof is A25 plus A26 implies A15:

- A25 assigns each realized substance attribute to some IsGod witness, potentially a different witness each time.
- A26 identifies any two IsGod witnesses.
- Equality substitution transfers the attribute to the arbitrary IsGod witness in the goal.

A26 is not proved in that argument. It is weaker in scope than identifying every substance with God, but it remains the load-bearing identity assumption. [ModalForm 679–710](https://github.com/Nakammura/spinoza-ethica-lean/blob/6d4c0461265e50a3bbedb640fe60d9ad292b50a7/Ethica/Pars1/ModalForm.lean#L679-L710).

Do not infer unrestricted strength equivalence merely from this implication. Without a God-existence premise, A15 may be vacuously true in an attribute-bearing godless domain while A25 is false. Thus the inspected A25 countermodel demonstrates one failure of entailment; it does not alone establish that A25 is strictly weaker than A15 in every background. Counting one axiom versus two also proves no strength comparison.

There is a precise positive qualification: over a fixed background containing A22 and an existing IsGod witness, A15 implies A25 by that witness and A26 because A15 makes every god's full attribute profile equal. A22 then supplies identity. Together with the inspected converse, this establishes relative equivalence to A25 plus A26 by a short written argument, not a new Lean verification.

## Semantic transport to Orthemology

**Rejected transport 1: attribute profiles to numerical identity of originals.** The formal Attribute relation takes two objects from the same Thing carrier. Attribute is defined using Substance of the bearer and a primitive intellect-perception relation. sameNature is defined as sharing an attribute. A8 plus A10 forces every attribute object itself to satisfy Substance. The current models embrace this consequence and contain no modes. [Definitions 44–100 and 145–235](https://github.com/Nakammura/spinoza-ethica-lean/blob/6d4c0461265e50a3bbedb640fe60d9ad292b50a7/Ethica/Pars1/Definitions.lean#L44-L235), [Counterexamples 90–126](https://github.com/Nakammura/spinoza-ethica-lean/blob/6d4c0461265e50a3bbedb640fe60d9ad292b50a7/Ethica/Pars1/Models/Counterexamples.lean#L90-L126).

This cannot be imported as the ontology of real, inseparable intrinsic qualifications without a new interpretation. Conceptual distinctness does not make such qualifications separately subsisting original agents. Sharing qualitative perfection does not establish sharing a numerical intrinsic act, attribute, or essence. Restricting A22 to pure perfections would already assume that those perfections exhaust individuation; no inspected theorem supplies that restriction or excludes haecceitic or de-se distinctions. Allowing such additional discriminators, conversely, removes the premise of complete profile equality. Neither maneuver settles the disputed original-bearer identity bridge.

**Rejected transport 2: plenitude to a complete common productive source.** A25 is attribute possession, not producing all effects, explaining a total fact-field, or providing a common causal original. The positive proof uses assumed god-uniqueness. Importing it into a proof intended to establish source uniqueness would move the disputed premise into A26 rather than establish it. Nothing here changes the separate total-explanatory-fact result or supplies its missing faithful bearer map.

**Rejected transport 3: formal substance to original productive independence.** Substance is a conjunction of in-itself and per-se-conceived predicates. InAnother is unary: it does not identify the bearer of an inhering qualification. This is not the already differentiated Orthemology interface for productive receipt, intrinsic inherence, concrete bearer identity, and necessary concomitance. A library name cannot identify these relations.

**Rejected transport 4: finite formal possibility to metaphysical possibility.** Satisfaction of the selected classes is not a warrant for extramental multiple originals, divine attribute self-subsistence, or genuine redundant total production. The models neither refute a faithful full metaphysical package nor instantiate it. IsGod also uses an unrestricted absolutelyInfinite predicate and only existence of at least one attribute; actual infinite attribute cardinality is not encoded.

Prior results credited rather than reopened: essence-attribute-source-return/REPORT.md, metaphysical-semantics-return/ROOT_ASSESSMENT.md, and total-explainer-uniqueness/RESULT.md already preserve the relevant concept/token, intrinsic/external, and fact/bearer restrictions. This screen corroborates the need to apply those controls; it does not discover their absence.

## Verification language and structural conjecture

The manuscript’s general derivability-decidability claim is too strong. Lean checks supplied proof terms; unsuccessful search supplies no non-derivability result. Particular countermodel certificates can support a consistency-relative metatheoretic non-derivability argument. The [official proof introduction](https://lean-lang.org/theorem_proving_in_lean4/Propositions-and-Proofs/) distinguishes construction from checking, and the [kernel reference](https://lean-lang.org/doc/reference/latest/Elaboration-and-Compilation/) even cautions about possible checker nontermination. No general decision procedure follows.

The quantifier-shape extrapolation is also not an established invariant. Three consecutive universals are not three alternations; A12’s displayed universal implication is not A22’s existential-discriminator formula. More basically, universal content can be written with an existential equality witness, and universal-existential premises plus a bridge can imply universal conclusions. These elementary observations block a transport based on syntactic shape. They do not invalidate the source’s specific finite witnesses. Its proposed broad transfer remains a conjecture, not a proved PSR limitation.

Ancillary source-inspection caution: the A13 proof uses CausalAxioms, which extends the A13-containing Pars1Axioms; its class-level assumption footprint therefore is not target-free even though the displayed proof body uses A23 and modal bridges. No reverse equivalence proof was identified in the inspected A13 section. Do not import the typology’s equal-strength label as an independently verified result. This does not affect the minimal A22 or A25/A26 proofs.

## Stop decision

Retain the source-version discrepancy, precise assumption boundaries, and rejected transports as the result. The useful stopping point is reached; no broader Spinoza reconstruction, new Lean formalization, or literature-priority campaign is warranted by this screen.
