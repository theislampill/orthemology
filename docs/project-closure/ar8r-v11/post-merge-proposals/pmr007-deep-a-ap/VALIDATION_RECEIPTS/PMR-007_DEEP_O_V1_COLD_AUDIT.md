## Blocking findings

### O-AUD-F01 — “genuine unification” outruns the formal witness

The explicit map establishes finite cross-coordinate coupling, image
restriction, and coordinate compression **relative to a chosen presentation**.
It does not by itself establish independent motivation, explanatory priority,
or metaphysical unity. V1 repeatedly calls the witness “genuine” without
keeping the formal/explanatory distinction active.

**Required repair:** introduce a typed predicate such as
`FORMAL_DERIVATIONAL_COUPLING`; reserve `GENUINE_EXPLANATORY_UNIFICATION` for the
formal witness plus independently defended interpretation, primitive choice,
and world correspondence.

### O-AUD-F02 — primitive reduction is presentation-relative

The claim that two primitive bits replace five primitives assumes the plural
rival's five coordinates are independently primitive and that bit count is a
fair primitive-cost measure. A rival can redescribe its primitives or compress
the same relation.

**Required repair:** state the reduction only against the declared product
rival and under a fixed representation/cost convention. Preserve Candidate G's
`P5` rival-parity and `P6` meta-abduction burdens.

### O-AUD-F03 — target coordinate names do not supply semantics

Labelling outputs `modal`, `actual`, `semantic`, `normative`, and `selection`
does not show that the finite Boolean/integer values realize those domains.
Likewise, `N=x+y` is an exact graph potential but not objective normativity.

**Required repair:** classify the model as a typed formal countermodel schema.
Its central force is logical: any bridge exhausted by those structural
predicates is invalid. It is not a world model until domain-specific
interpretation maps are independently warranted.

### O-AUD-F04 — “underived” and “concrete actualizer” are stipulated labels

The model YAML marks an impersonal bearer as underived and concrete. No
metaphysical semantics or consistency theory for those predicates was supplied.

**Required repair:** remove them from the proof of the main theorem. Retain only
as an optional rival-extension schema showing which further predicates would
still require independent bridges.

### O-AUD-F05 — tie claim must be metric-scoped

Neutral-reduct isomorphism blocks strict ranking only for comparison rules that
factor through the neutral reduct. A source-sensitive, phenomenological,
mechanistic, or intentionality-sensitive comparison can rank the expansions.

**Required repair:** state the factorization condition formally. Do not report
unqualified equal explanatory cost.

### O-AUD-F06 — enumeration key overstates what the checker certifies

The primary result field `eligible_genuine_unification_maps` treats a weak
connected-influence filter as genuine unification. It certifies only a family of
finite structurally coupled maps.

**Required repair:** preserve V1 output as evidence, create a V2 checker/result
with corrected vocabulary, and ensure the candidate does not use the count as a
novelty or metaphysical argument.

### O-AUD-F07 — source-conditioned parity breaking needs a source-status ceiling

V1 says Track N can select the personal expansion but does not explicitly state
that source authenticity, translation, interpretation, and model application
remain separate guards.

**Required repair:** add the full source-formal-world firewall. Do not equate
direct source predication with a neutral derivation or world verification.
