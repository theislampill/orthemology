## Blocking findings

### AD-F01 — the factorization theorem belongs to the T366/fibre family

The exact proxy-invariance equivalence is the standard constancy-on-fibres
criterion.  It is useful here but receives no independent theorem origin or
general novelty credit.

### AD-F02 — semantic content was carried by the symbol `R`

Calling `R` a reason does not authenticate reference, truth conditions,
fittingness, source, target authority, or world adequacy.  V2 must call the
formal result registered-reason-variable mediation and preserve the semantic
bridge separately.

### AD-F03 — intervention on `R` needed a manipulability/cross-world contract

A surgical change to semantic content may require changing the world, evidence,
or source.  The SCM result is valid only for a declared variable-level model in
which the intervention is coherent and the remaining equations are held fixed.
It is not automatically a metaphysical counterfactual.

### AD-F04 — `P` must be declared a nuisance coordinate for the invariance test

A surface or token variable may legitimately carry additional evidence,
authority, or indexical content.  Proxy invariance is warranted only after `P`
is declared semantically and normatively irrelevant for the target claim.  An
unqualified invariance demand can erase real information.

### AD-F05 — system-level mediation was too close in wording to subjective because-of

The operational criterion establishes dependence on one interpreted variable
and invariance to declared nuisance variation.  It does not establish that a
subject apprehends content as a reason or is motivated by its fittingness.
`BFIT_SYS` must be renamed or explicitly firewalled from `BFIT_SUBJ`.

### AD-F06 — observational evidence cannot establish the intervention criterion

The formal SCM uses interventions.  Correlation, representation alignment,
trajectory similarity, or endpoint performance does not identify the causal
structure.  Hidden confounding and model-equivalent mechanisms remain live.

### AD-F07 — multiple admissible actions limit contrast sensitivity

When two reason states have overlapping or non-singleton admissible action sets,
conformity need not force an action change.  V2 must keep the exact singleton/
disjoint contrast scope and not generalize it to every semantic difference.

### AD-F08 — history, partial observation, and dynamic policy were absent

The factorization is static and memoryless.  A hidden history or changing policy
can make the same current `(c,r,p)` require different actions.  V2 must state
history sufficiency and fixed-policy guards.

### AD-F09 — personal/impersonal expansions establish only reduct-relative underdetermination

The inert expansion construction does not prove metaphysical actuality,
physical realizability, or prior parity.  It shows only that the registered
neutral evidence does not discriminate within the intended comparison class.

### AD-F10 — implementation and empirical transfer were not earned

The criterion is formal.  No daee, OSM, PRH, biological, or agentic runtime was
shown to expose independently intervenable semantic variables or to satisfy the
invariance test.  Any implementation or experimental application requires a
separate correspondence audit.

## Required repairs

1. Recast the theorem as a T366/fibre-family application with zero general
   novelty.
2. Add `SEM_VALID`, `R_INTERVENTION_VALID`, `P_NUISANCE`, `HIST_COMPLETE`, and
   `POLICY_FIXED` guards.
3. Rename the operational criterion `REGISTERED_REASON_VARIABLE_MEDIATION`.
4. Preserve the exact disjoint-singleton contrast scope.
5. Add semantic-variable misidentification, evidence-bearing proxy, hidden
   confounding, history, and implementation countermodels.
6. Freeze V2 and run a distinct partition/SCM rereview.
