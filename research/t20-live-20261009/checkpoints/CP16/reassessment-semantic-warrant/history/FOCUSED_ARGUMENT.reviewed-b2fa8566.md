# Common source confidence after preserving the meaning of complete production

9 October 2026. Focused assessment of the change between the two root judgements. Historical reports remain unchanged.

## Finding

The Schaffer reading supplies serious defeasible evidence for actual causation through redundant, individually sufficient complete processes. It does not independently establish duplicate entire production in the stronger respect used by the source, much less two bearers of complete unreceived original perfection. Its proper contribution is a developed alternative causal interpretation and defeasible pressure on a general nonabsorption rationale. That is less than a counterexample to the stronger original-specific bridge. It does not establish that withdrawing the earlier defeasible actual-common-source assent is required.

This does not automatically restore the earlier confidence or show that reducing it was irrational. Indirect evidence can weaken confidence without furnishing a complete countermodel. The original-specific premise remains substantive and contestable, and a deficient inference can be exposed without constructing a complete rival cosmos. But the objection must identify the step at its actual meaning, and explain the strength of the indirect evidence. Retaining prima facie modal evidence for Schaffer's proposal does not settle that appraisal.

## The proposition that must remain fixed

Four claims are different:

1. A can produce e without B under appropriate conditions.
2. A actually produces e through an intact individually sufficient process, although B also produces e.
3. A supplies the whole actual productive adequacy for this numerically specified effect, including the requisite resources and conditions, without foreign original completion.
4. A alone is the actual entire producer of e.

Schaffer gives more than standing backup capacity and more than a bare response table. His complete-process argument supports 2. His noncompositional spell proposal supplies defeasible modal evidence against dismissing every redundant process as secretly a vector sum. Neither result independently identifies his causal relation with 3. Claim 4 is explicitly exclusive; two distinct agents cannot both satisfy it in the same respect. The dispute concerns the warranted transition to 4, not that contradiction.

The source itself makes this distinction relevant. Oneness printed 23 explains independence by sole entire production and illustrates it with two alleged builders of the entire same house. Printed 24 extends its reasoning to created agents. This makes the general causal claim a legitimate potential target; restricting all discussion to original beings would insulate too much. Nevertheless, the extension still concerns whole/exclusive production. It does not turn ordinary causal membership into the source's intended antecedent.

The Arabic control is decisive against reading this as an accidental English ambiguity. Majmu 20:170–171 distinguishes each cause's sufficiency when alone from exclusive attribution during their concurrence. Majmu 20:175 then adds a real augmentation argument: unchanged effective addition must yield a greater or more numerous complete product. It expressly presents this as a general rule across voluntary, natural and motivating causes, not only originals. At 20:176–177 complete power and decisive will are expressly taken to entail production through the agent alone. These are the stronger substantive bridges; the source does not simply overlook the difference between ability and actuality.

## What Schaffer actually puts under pressure

The two-rock case is not a premise-preserving instance of each producing the entire finely specified work. Schaffer accepts that actual laws plus maximal modal fragility can turn it into joint causation. His separate component argument expressly says each rock causes a component and thereby causes the shattering. This is sufficient for his causal individualism and insufficient for the stronger attribution.

The two-spells case is more relevant. The apparent direct transformation need not compose separate forces, and it should retain genuine, contestable modal weight. Yet the inference from each spell actually causing the same metamorphosis to each entirely producing the same numerical transformative work remains unargued in the relevant stronger sense. An intact path ensures no missing intermediary in that path; it does not ensure that the path includes every requisite or supplies the whole actual productive basis. Calling the endpoint the whole effect does not supply that missing attribution. The spell story could be developed into a direct challenge, but its needed entireness interpretation is not established by the paper's argument for causal individualism.

This is not a demand for a complete account of the universe, actual physical laws, maximal fragility for every causal explanation, or extra created traces for every uncreated act. A warranted local case preserving the source's general numerical whole-production proposition would suffice to challenge its universality. What is missing is that local predicate mapping. The additional extension to unreceived original resource provision would then need its own warrant.

Accordingly:

- A weaker premise equating ordinary intact sufficient causation with exclusivity is legitimately undercut. If root used that premise, its use should be withdrawn.
- The source's stronger whole-production and original-perfection principle is not thereby defeated. Ordinary independence cannot replace its antecedent while retaining credit for refuting its consequent.
- The developed rival has analogical evidential relevance: it makes nonexclusive productive attribution intelligible and puts defeasible pressure on the intuitive universality of the general augmentation principle. This may affect confidence before a complete mapping is established. But the strength of that undercutting cannot simply be inherited from the strength of the process-level evidence; the mismatch remains relevant.

## Strongest affirmative and rival premises

The strongest affirmative argument is not a preference for exclusive credit:

1. Original agency must have achieved adequate efficacy, including provision and preservation of the conditions of its chosen created result; an unreceived readiness completed by foreign original resources is insufficient.
2. Adequate power, effective wise will, and independence from required assistance are pure perfections. Actual relational efficacy can itself be a perfection; no newly implanted intrinsic property is required for foreign completion to matter.
3. A genuinely operative original peer cannot add its activity while both originals retain unchanged entire efficacy toward merely the same complete work. Either the addition makes more work or the alleged entire efficacy is qualified. This is the nonabsorption/nonduplication bridge.
4. The relevant actual created order is operatively connected. If the preceding principles exclude original co-production and inter-domain completion, partitioned originals do not explain that connected order.

The first two premises have positive explanatory and comparative support in the source. Akmaliyya 6:125–130 compares comprehensive creation, unimpeded wise will and self-sufficient power with restricted counterparts. The third requires the strongest contested application; it is not derived merely by counting two contributors. The fourth preserves the source's actual-world evidential contribution and is conditional on the no-co-production result. This is stronger than economy alone.

The strongest rival must therefore claim more than two sufficient routes: each actual original relation is already adequate for the whole fixed effect, no independently supplied requisite completes either one's agency, and their concurrent real efficacy is nonexclusive without becoming partial, absorbed or merely potential. Schaffer motivates a less exclusive account of causation but does not establish that complete rival premise.

A direct residual objection remains available without such a countermodel: the positive comparison between needing assistance and not needing it does not alone classify a purportedly redundant peer as needed assistance. Nor does successful complete willing by itself establish sole actual attribution. Conversely, merely describing the peer as redundant does not prove that the strong source premise has been preserved. The issue is whether its real contribution supplies any part of the actual adequacy, and why that must count as foreign completion or attenuation of original efficacy. The source's stronger argument addresses this issue; it deserves appraisal rather than substitution by the weaker process claim.

## Confidence and standard

The older root judgement affirmed one complete original source of the relevant actual connected order at a defeasible research standard. It already reserved the unrestricted application of the original-efficacy norm. The later judgement declines a sufficient exclusion of the strongest redundant-original proposal and labels its actual assent provisional. These are not direct logical contradictories: positive actual-world assent can coexist with an uncompleted universal exclusion argument.

There is a genuine new distinction: a redundant rival is not the complementary joint rival previously criticised. That prevents transferring the previous rebuttal without argument. There is also genuine new causal literature giving the redundant interpretation more motivation. Recognising this can rationally weaken confidence. But the earlier judgement's strongest basis was original adequacy and perfection, not merely the failure of an ordinary joint-production model. The new literature has not shown that basis false or supplied an equally warranted original-agent alternative.

The later report already denies that Schaffer authenticates whole-original production and expressly targets an unqualified self-evidence claim. It should not be accused of having explicitly certified the stronger counterexample. The semantic review correctly preserved that qualification.

My result is therefore a needed clarification of the confidence basis: **direct counterevidence to the strongest original-specific premise is unestablished; indirect pressure on its general rationale is genuine but its strength remains to be assessed.** A lower overall judgement could be justified by that pressure or by reconsidering the original-specific premise's independent support. It should say why those reasons now fall short of the same earlier defeasible threshold. Withholding universal exclusion alone cannot serve as an unexplained increase from defeasible warrant to deductive certainty. Neither preservation nor restoration of a particular confidence label follows mechanically.
