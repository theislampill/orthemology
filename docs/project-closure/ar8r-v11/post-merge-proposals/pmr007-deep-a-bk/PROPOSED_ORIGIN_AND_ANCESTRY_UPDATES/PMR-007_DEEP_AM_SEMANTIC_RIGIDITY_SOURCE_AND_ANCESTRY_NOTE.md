## PRH use

The paper's formal comparison object is a representation-induced kernel, and
alignment is a relation among such kernels.  Its convergence hypothesis is a
hypothesis about increasingly shared statistical structure.  The paper also
states information, modality, metric, special-purpose, and incomplete-
convergence limitations.  Deep AM uses it only to motivate and constrain the
kernel/reference distinction.
