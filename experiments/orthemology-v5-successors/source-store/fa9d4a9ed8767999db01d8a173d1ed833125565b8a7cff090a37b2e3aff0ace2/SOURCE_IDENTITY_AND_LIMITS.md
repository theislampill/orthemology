### Manuscript and edition basis

At printed 112–114, the editor describes one manuscript in al-Kawakib al-Darari in the Zahiriyya library, Damascus, number 581, with an Islamic University microfilm 4472. It spans the end of the collection's volume 121, pp 221–251, and volume 122, pp 1–76. He names the copyist as Ibrahim b. Muhammad b. Muhammad b. Mahmud b. Badr al-Hanbali, 830 AH, and reports corrections, marginal supplements, comparison notices, 28–30 lines per page and the Umariyya collection stamp. These are the editor's manuscript reports, not independent catalog or manuscript authentication by this reader.

At 115–116 he identifies earlier editions, including Muhammad Munir Agha al-Dimashqi's Muniriyya 1346 printing, and the 1405 Muhammad Abd al-Rahman Awad printing. At 117 he defines the sigla: خ for manuscript, م for Muniriyya, ط for the 1405 print. He chooses readings he considers correct, even against the manuscript, and records differences in footnotes. Thus the main text is editorially eclectic, not a diplomatic copy, and the printed witnesses must not be counted as two additional independent manuscripts.

The photographs 121–125 visibly include the work's opening below the preceding work's ending and its ending before al-Sarim al-Munki. Those boundary features support what the editor describes but do not amount to full palaeographic decipherment, collation or confirmation of every claim about completeness.

### Is the historical work complete?

At 93–96 the editor quotes doubts voiced by Muhammad Rashad Salim and Abd al-Rahman al-Mahmud, then argues for completeness. His reasons include the author's ordinary practice of beginning with a section rather than a formal preface, the presence of earlier-discussion references within the received book, and the manuscript's before/after contexts. He regards the work as completed but not finally fair-copied. This is a defended editorial judgement with reported disagreement, not a fact certified merely by downloading the file.

The acquisition result is therefore: complete accessible RECEIVED edited scan, with a continuous authorial body and explicit end formula. No claim of a recovered authorial autograph, a fully verified historical original, absence of all internal lacunae, or manuscript-critical closure is made.
