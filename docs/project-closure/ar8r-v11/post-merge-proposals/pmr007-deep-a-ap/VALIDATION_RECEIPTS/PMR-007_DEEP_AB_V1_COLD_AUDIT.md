## Scope audited

The audit examined the V1 theorem packet, typed model owner, primary checker
source and primary results.  It treated the primary exhaustive PASS as evidence
for the finite selection facts only, not as authority for the philosophical or
metaphysical interpretation.

## Blocking findings

### AB-F01 — `Best` was called objective before its authority coordinates were typed

V1 says that `Best` is carried and does not establish its truth, authority, or
world adequacy, but later calls the policy “fittingness-sensitive” and its
selected actions “extensionally best” without keeping those coordinates in the
formal signature.  The result must distinguish:

```text
registered admissibility relation;
truth of that relation;
target-key authority;
world adequacy;
source or version applicability.
```

Otherwise an arbitrary, false, stale, or malicious table can be relabelled
`Best` and inherit language stronger than the formal result.

### AB-F02 — functional profile responsiveness was described as causal

The finite checker proves a table-level fact: changing the supplied profile
index can change the selected action.  A causal claim requires a frozen SCM or
intervention semantics in which the profile is an exogenous manipulable input,
the policy equation is held fixed, and no hidden/common-cause or model-change
interpretation is being imported.  V1 must either remove “causal” or state the
exact SCM-relative result.

### AB-F03 — selection *in accordance with* a fittingness table was not separated from selection *because of* fittingness

`EXT_FIT` and `PROFILE_RESP` are extensional.  They do not identify semantic
uptake, reason recognition, motivating explanation, first-person ownership, or
the counterfactual because-of relation used in the Wisdom bridge.  The V1
personal/impersonal parity is sound only when presented as intended-model-class
underdetermination, not as a metaphysical demonstration that the impersonal
realizer exists.

### AB-F04 — registry completeness and history sufficiency were implicit

`STABLE_EXT_FIT` ranges only over the declared static profile registry.  It does
not establish performance under unregistered profiles, temporal drift, stale
source bindings, hidden history, dynamic membership, adversarial messages, or
future policy revision.  These guards must be explicit.

### AB-F05 — the policy table did not by itself fix a common bearer

A fittingness representation, selector, executor, and source custodian may be
distributed.  V1 mentions the distributed control but does not incorporate it
into the positive bridge specification.  Common-bearer co-instantiation and
intentional episode ownership remain separate predicates.

### AB-F06 — ancestry and novelty needed a stricter classification

The finite choice and intersection facts are elementary.  The selector portion
shares a mechanism with Round 18; the Wisdom interpretation is a strengthening
and application of Deep K and Deep AA; the carrier-boxing control is governed
by Candidate G.  The result may carry central bridge value but no independent
general mathematical novelty or historical identity.

### AB-F07 — source role was insufficiently explicit

The Asfahani file is translated-primary-text access based on two Arabic
editions, not Arabic-primary verification by this project.  Its distinction
between praiseworthy and blameworthy volition/speech, and its source-level
predication of Wisdom, may constrain Track N.  It does not establish a neutral
formal entailment from policy conformity to divine Wisdom.

### AB-F08 — the primary checker was not an independent rereview

The primary checker uses one canonical least-element selector.  Admission
requires a distinct method that (a) checks all admissible selectors in a small
complete class, (b) checks the blind-policy criterion by an independently
implemented constraint method, (c) verifies the frozen hashes, and (d) tests
personal/impersonal reduct equality without reusing the primary routine as its
oracle.

   guards.
2. State the exact finite selection theorems independently of those guards.
3. Add a frozen deterministic SCM for the limited intervention claim, or reduce
   the claim to functional sensitivity.
4. State personal/impersonal parity as reduct-relative model-class
   underdetermination.
5. Preserve registry, history, dynamic, distributed-bearer, and source
   nonclaims.
6. Reclassify ancestry and novelty.
7. Freeze V2 and conduct a distinct rereview.
