# Independent appraisal of complete original sourcehood

Publication scope note: Historical independent source-mode reassessment and its dated Shifa controls. The principal appraisal predates the controls; read the two supplements in sequence. This is not a final after-all-books verdict. Nubuwwat/Safadiyya evidence and the final report remain outside this packet.

7 October 2026. Standalone philosophical reassessment after the selected Arabic-body study of Darʾ 8–9, Minhāj 2–3, and the received original al-Ṣawāʿiq. This is an independently owned assessment, not a formal audit decision or an alteration of earlier reports. The later requested Shifāʾ reading has not yet been supplied to this assessment.

## Verdict

I withhold sufficient philosophical warrant for the claim that every qualified actual original contributor must supply, without an irreducibly foreign original contribution, the complete actuality of each relevant product and thereby the whole connected field. The expanded primary reading substantially strengthens the affirmative case. It does not, in my judgement, close the disputed transition.

My reason is specific. Intrinsic complete power, an intrinsically complete actual exercise, and complete original provision of the external result are not the same completeness. The source gives substantive reasons for linking them, especially by treating realised efficacy as a perfection. But those reasons do not yet establish that a contributor’s fitting, self-determined partial undertaking is an intrinsic deficiency whenever its external result contains a contribution from an independent original. The separate strict-supremacy argument is genuinely stronger than weak maximality and must be assessed in its own right. I do not find the proposed control over every alleged necessary peer independently established as an eligible pure perfection. Taking strict supremacy as already qualified into the bearer makes peer exclusion follow, but does not settle this qualification’s warrant.

This is not a verdict that the arguments contain no positive reasoning, that harmonious originals are demonstrably possible, or that finite formal models refute the full metaphysical qualifications. One complete original source remains an intelligible, coherent and explanatorily attractive account of connected actuality. I do not judge the assessed cumulative argument sufficient for the stronger source-mode claim at issue. Necessary Being, complete actual common-sourcehood, and peer-excluding supreme divinity remain different achievements.

## The exact claim under assessment

An original resource is not merely a creature’s locally effective act. Its concrete actuality is not existentially borrowed in the relevant respect. A qualified original bearer is an actual self-subsistent reality with its intrinsic agency and perfections, rather than a bare abstract power or an empty label. These are substantial qualifications.

Nevertheless, at least five further distinctions matter:

1. Whole-existence originality of a bearer is not complete origination of every external product to which it contributes.
2. A power’s being intrinsically possessed differs from its being sufficient, through an actual determinate exercise, for an effect. Both differ from actual production of every requisite of a larger composite.
3. A condition required for an external product differs from something conferring the agent’s intrinsic power, will, or existential actuality.
4. Essential agreement can be proposed as noncausal concomitance. Reciprocal conferral of effective agency is a different proposal and faces a genuine circularity objection.
5. A highest degree permitting no superior can, considered by itself, admit a tie. Being strictly higher than every other candidate excludes ties. The Ṣawāʿiq argument claims the latter.

The source-mode conclusion concerns actual complete original provision, allowing genuine derivative secondary causes. It does not require God to be the creature’s intrinsic actor, every token act to exist eternally, all possible effects to be realised, or every necessary attribute to be efficiently manufactured. Those stronger and often incoherent readings are not the target.

## The strongest affirmative reconstruction

### Original actuality and its qualified bearer

Received activity is real, but conditional possession of efficacy does not supply the concrete actuality it presupposes. An exhaustive existential account cannot discharge its entire task by repeating received actuality throughout. A positive original basis is required; under the concrete productive-resource/bearer commitments, that basis has an actual original bearer. This remains stronger than a mere prohibition on finite causal circles or a preference for short explanations.

Minhāj 2:100–109 develops the bearer’s intrinsic completion. If a constituent of the necessary whole’s own completion is supplied by an independent other, the whole is not unborrowed in that respect. The argument works with both a putatively partless bearer and an intrinsically articulated qualified bearer. The latter’s life, knowledge and power can be inseparable without being externally assembled or mutually efficient causes. This is a substantive independence argument, not simply defining the word “God.”

### Real productive fullness is an excellence

A power that actually accounts for an effect is more than an unbestowed but idle partial capacity. Minhāj 2:117 explicitly says that efficacy is part of the agent’s perfection. Darʾ 8:412–421 explicitly endorses the comparative superiority of the producer that also preserves its product over a producer requiring a separate preserver. Darʾ 8:396–417 also explains why moving an already available bearer, furnishing an occasion, or being an object of imitation is less than originating its complete reality.

The strongest proposed extension is that adequate original agency is a mode of actual provision: when the qualified original acts as an original source, its actual efficacy cannot need an independently original positive resource to complete the result. The claim is not merely that it could perform more actions. It says that a supposedly perfect original’s actual sourcehood would otherwise be a qualified and incomplete sourcehood. This mode-level argument deserves assessment; pointing out that capacity does not logically imply action does not by itself answer it.

### Connected products cannot escape provenance

Materials, receptive powers, constitutive processes and preservation are real productive contributions. Calling them circumstances or occasions does not erase their source. Minhāj 2:114–119 uses interconnected products, parental matter, sustenance, air and water. Darʾ 9:638–644 shows why an ordinary agent’s own bodily action and external effects depend on factors it does not originate. An intended composite containing two independently original contributions really has divided original provision, even if both contributors consent.

The affirmative accordingly argues that product interdependence exposes a shortfall in the claimed complete efficacy of their ultimate producers. A dependent contributor can cooperate because its enabling actuality is supplied; an ultimate original cannot receive that same completion from a peer. This is the force of Darʾ 9:739 and Minhāj 2:117–119.

### Agreement does not create an original explanation

The sources explicitly address necessary agreement, common benefit, foreknowledge, cooperation, partition and turn-taking. Their case is not restricted to the possibility of hostile wills.

If each agent becomes effectively willing or powerful only through a contribution from the other, simultaneous occurrence does not remove the explanatory circle. Making the dependency only partial does not remove it either. A future benefit produced by both is not an independently existing efficient donor of the wills that produce it. Factive foreknowledge cannot, merely by presupposing the future agreement, supply an independent productive explanation of that agreement. Minhāj 3:272–273 argues all of these points.

The affirmative further contends that intrinsic complete efficacy entails genuine unilateral efficacy. Darʾ 9:720–725 explicitly starts from the proposed noncausal-concomitance answer and argues that an intrinsic power must enable acting without the other. It then argues that a second complete agent would either obstruct that independence or duplicate the same complete production. This goes beyond simply confusing concomitance with efficient circularity.

### Complete production and strict supremacy

The claim that two agents each wholly and exclusively produce the same numerical effect is contradictory under that ownership description. Genuine cooperation instead supplies differentiated contributions, as Darʾ 9:627–650 and Minhāj 3:264–272 expressly explain. The source then excludes irreducibly divided original provision by its stronger efficacy/perfection account.

Ṣawāʿiq adds a distinct route. At 002:1196–1197, if two candidates are equal neither is higher than the other; if unequal only the higher has the highest exemplar. Absolute subjugation similarly cannot leave an unsubdued equal. Printed 233 says complete overcoming entails unity because partnership is incompatible with its completeness. This is a strict, peer-excluding conception of supremacy, supported by the larger positive account of pure perfection and necessary self-sufficiency. It is not the weak assertion that nobody happens to be greater.

Once independently warranted, this stronger conception excludes a coordinate original peer without waiting for the source-mode classification of every composite. That is why the supremacy route cannot be dismissed merely by repeating the partial-role objection.

## Where the strongest objection remains

### Intrinsic agency and the intended result

Suppose, as a proposal to be assessed rather than a certified possible world, two original agents each possess their intrinsic actuality, power, knowledge and determinate willing without receiving them. Each performs a distinct intrinsic act. The two external contributions enter one composite result. No contributor receives its own intrinsic act or power from the other. The result’s needing both contributions is acknowledged, not disguised.

The objection is that this specifies an external composite dependency, while the source-mode argument concludes an intrinsic deficiency of each agent. That transition requires more than tracking real provenance. It requires showing that the all-incoming original provision of the composite belongs to each qualified original’s own fitting mode, rather than being a more extensive undertaking it could choose.

Granting broad eligible standing power makes this objection harder to dismiss, not automatically successful. The proposed contributor is not limited to a partial ability or incapable of complete provision. Nor does the proposal insist that wise agency must realise a special good obtainable only through an original peer. Its narrower challenge is whether voluntarily undertaking a less extensive original role has been shown unfitting in every relevant case.

The affirmative has a serious reply: actual sourcehood with an independent completion is less full in precisely its source aspect. Consent cannot turn supplied material into one’s own original material. I accept that comparative judgement. What I do not accept as sufficiently established is that every difference in actual causal comprehensiveness is an intrinsic deficiency of the qualified actor, irrespective of the content and fittingness of its act. “Less comprehensive original role” and “defective intrinsic original agency” are related but not interchangeable classifications.

### The source’s own act controls make the question substantive

Minhāj 3:238–242 says not everything within power is done; completed will and power secure the determinate effect. Ṣawāʿiq 003:1189–1192 and 004:16–36 distinguishes full standing perfection from compulsory production of each further enjoyable or fitting effect. Goods can belong to particular times and require other occurrences; appropriate nonperformance is not automatically deficiency. At 004:340, acts issue from prior complete perfection rather than completing an originally deficient God.

These passages do not prove that a wise original may cooperate with a peer. The source explains its own responsive conditions as lying within universal divine origination, and therefore denies that they are independently supplied perfection. But that coherence defence cannot independently establish the universal origination used in it. The passages force the assessor to justify the relevant respect of deficiency, rather than equating every unperformed more comprehensive role with deficient agency.

The decisive contested move remains from a product’s independent incoming contribution to a defect in the agent’s own fully specified original exercise. I find the source’s comparison meaningful and supportive, but insufficiently discriminating at that move.

### Necessary harmony and foreknowledge

The objection is strongest when it does not make each agent’s actual will a productive condition activating the other’s. If necessary intrinsic wisdom and knowledge are proposed to yield matching determinations, an explanation of that matching is still owed. An unexplained appeal to wisdom or foreknowledge is not a completed alternative theory.

Conversely, common content, essential concurrence and factive knowledge do not themselves identify a positive resource supplied by one agent to constitute the other’s power or will. The source permits nonproductive essential concomitance in one bearer: Minhāj 2:109 and Darʾ 9:717–718. This is not proof of permissible concomitance between independent bearers. It does locate the extra burden in excluding that extension, rather than in a general ban on all necessity relations.

Minhāj 3:272–273 moves from the concurrence of the two determinations to each being part of what makes the other willing. Darʾ 9:722–725 instead appeals to intrinsic power’s independence of another’s permission. These are reasoned moves. They are not already established merely by specifying the composite’s conditions. Their success depends on whether full intrinsic efficacy requires the robust unilateral control at issue.

### Numerical effect and modal power

Darʾ 9:748–762 explicitly distinguishes inability to perform an intrinsic contradiction from being blocked by another. That reply must be retained. It defeats a critic who simply calls every outside obstruction a logical impossibility.

There is nevertheless a target-identity question. “This result wholly produced by A alone,” the source-neutral result, and a qualitatively matching alternative result are different targets. If the first includes A’s exclusive provenance, B cannot also produce that very target alone by its description. That does not by itself show a deficiency in B’s eligible power. If the target is source-neutral, actual alternative or complementary production must still be excluded by a causal and modal argument. Likewise, separate powers over alternative outcomes do not automatically imply that the two choices are jointly admissible while every intrinsic perfection and the other’s fixed act remain unchanged.

This is not a formal countermodel refutation. It identifies why the same-numerical-product contradiction cannot, without further warrant, do the work of the complete source-mode and unilateral-control premises.

## Strict supremacy and the eligibility of the perfection

The positive rationale is substantial. Unrestricted authority to realise the good without borrowing, bargaining, vulnerability or external veto is a genuine improvement over finite agency. An ultimate explanation that remains answerable to a coordinate source can appear incomplete precisely as ultimate. A bearer with all such unborrowed positive perfections has a stronger claim to divine supremacy than a merely uncaused specialist. These considerations give strict supremacy real philosophical attraction.

But “not subject to another” and “able to subject every other alleged independent reality” are different claims. Two proposed equals each lacking an external superior would not, merely by that description, possess the second status. The question is whether the second adds an eligible pure perfection, rather than imposing the relational fact that no independent equal exists.

Three tests matter.

First, the power must have an eligible object. Power to destroy an intrinsically necessary peer is not an eligible possibility while that peer’s intrinsic necessity is held fixed. T17 P66 itself excludes power to annihilate an intrinsically necessary reality. A defender can instead mean control over the peer’s actions or effects, but must then show why unilateral control over an intrinsically self-sufficient agency is an eligible object rather than the very disputed denial of that agency’s independence. This is not permission to assume the peer possible. It prevents proving it impossible merely by counting that impossibility as an uncontroversial pure perfection of its rival.

Second, intrinsic fullness does not by itself settle comparative uniqueness. If nothing about a bearer’s intrinsic knowledge, power, wisdom, self-possession or fitting act is reduced by the supposition of an equal, declaring its lack of strict superiority a deficiency needs the positive unilateral-control rationale just examined. Relational perfections are not therefore unreal. The point is that this particular relation needs argument beyond the fact that absolute degree is admirable. A tied maximal degree and strict all-others supremacy must not be exchanged in the inference.

Third, source priority over actual recipients supports superiority to those recipients. Extending that priority to an alleged nonrecipient requires either the broader pure-perfection argument with this property’s eligibility established, or an independently established universal source relation. One cannot infer that every other bearer is already a recipient simply because the original giver is superior to whatever it gives.

I therefore accept the conditional force of the strict-supremacy argument and give its positive rationale more credit than the expression “stronger assumption” conveys. I still withhold sufficient warrant for the exact universal peer-excluding qualification. It offers a second philosophical route, but its disputed step converges with the first route’s demand for unrestricted unilateral source-control. Repetition under “highestness,” “dominion” and “complete efficacy” does not automatically provide three independent reasons for that same extension.

## What T17 actually owns

The original DOCX is authoritative. My direct check confirms that its selected paragraphs agree with the existing SOURCE_PASSAGES bindings. T17’s principle is not merely “there exists a partial unborrowed input.” P41–43 addresses complete existential provision of the received field and excludes an exhaustive account that only presupposes received actuality throughout. The positive adequacy requirement is real and must not be weakened away.

However, T17 itself separates that target from assigning its full completion to one individual:

- P45: joint completion does not identify one individually complete common bearer.
- P48: the actual particular original witness need not be individually complete for every contribution; a plural original basis leaves common coverage unsettled.
- P49–51: the needed bearer inference concerns the original resource’s concrete instantiation. If the bearer’s whole actuality is received, its intrinsic resource cannot be declared wholly original while omitting that support. This is not an efficient bearer-to-attribute production edge.
- P52: an original member of a plural basis supplies a witness; alternatively a genuinely original holistic bearer can supply derivative members. Neither option identifies every proposed plurality with one bearer.
- P54 and P61: necessity and essential nonreceipt do not themselves establish complete actual creatorhood.

Thus two corrections are required at once. It would understate T17 to reduce its whole-existence principle to a generic original contribution with no complete-account target. It would overstate T17 to make that complete-account target already entail all-incoming coverage by every actual original resource or its bearer.

The new typed adapter preserves a deliberately weaker formal witness interface. Completion says that each received target has a relevant actual original resource; ActualOriginalResource is actuality plus nonreceipt. The bearer bridge uses intrinsic realisation and same-respect support to obtain an actual whole-original bearer. It contains no provision relation ranging over every incoming requisite. The formal interface therefore exposes the witnessed consequence needed there; it must not replace the richer prose rationale or be expanded by the ordinary word “completion.”

In the contribution interface, Complete(g,e) explicitly means that g originally supplies every requisite contribution of e. SourceMode transports represented original participation to that coverage. These are different quantifier structures and different objects of completeness. This comparison is semantic inspection, not a new kernel verification or a judgement that the typed representation misstates its bounded purpose.

Sixth’s historical LocalCover is stronger and differently typed again: g belongs to x’s reflexive ancestral cone and lies below every member of that cone in an independently interpreted strict transitive relation. I directly inspected its retained definitions at source lines 10–25. An original-resource witness does not supply that all-ancestor coverage, and incidence Complete does not automatically refine to it. The possible route below describes substantive original provision and appropriate propagation; it does not claim to discharge Sixth’s exact LocalCover or its separate relation-faithfulness obligations. Upstream original-completion/bearer premises must not be confused with Sixth’s differently numbered graph premises.

## A possible stronger completion and bearer route

A new route could avoid saying that wisdom obliges every original to choose a maximally comprehensive role. It could argue instead about what an adequate actual original existential explanation is. Its strongest formulation would require the following commitments.

1. For each actual received concrete product, there is a positively sufficient original completion of its entire existential actuality, including every real productive requisite; an unreceived token that merely contributes something is insufficient.
2. That sufficient completion is realised by one actual original bearer, or by an intrinsic unity independently shown to be one bearer. It is not merely a jointly sufficient plurality of original bearers.
3. The completion is unborrowed at the intended scope. An irreducibly original contribution supplied by an independent other cannot be left outside the bearer’s actual provision while describing the bearer as the complete original completer.
4. Real contribution ownership is preserved through derivative mediation, and the field’s requisite inventory and productive connections are adequate. Numerical nonduplication applies in the same original-provision respect.

Under these premises, every requisite in a completed product belongs to its adequate original source. A source claiming another original contribution within that same fully covered product must coincide with the complete source under the relevant nonduplication principle. Appropriate productive overlap then propagates common-sourcehood through the connected field. Genuine derivative agents and their distinct intrinsic acts remain possible.

The route has philosophical merit. It focuses on actual adequacy rather than maximal quantity of action; it refuses a merely conditional or fragmentary existential answer; and it attaches real efficacy to a concrete bearer. It also locates the difficult step honestly. T17 warrants the complete-account target and some concrete whole-original bearer; it does not already warrant that every sufficient original completion is individually owned, rather than jointly realised. The proposed second commitment is a substantial bearer-unification premise. If the first and third are instead defined so that “original completion” already means one bearer’s exhaustive all-incoming provision, they contain the local coverage result rather than derive it.

A weaker version saying “some unborrowed actuality contributes to the account” cannot recover the route. A stronger version saying “all distinct original contributions are modes of one bearer” can recover it, but needs an independent reason to identify the bearer. Shared meaning, common necessity or necessary coexistence does not provide that identity; the sources themselves reject turning a shared universal into an externally common component.

A genuinely independent proof that there is only one whole-existence original/necessary bearer could supply the missing identification, provided every relevant original contribution is shown to instantiate such a bearer. The proof would then be a separate uniqueness route, not a rediscovery of LocalCover inside T17. The pending Shifāʾ reading is relevant to testing alternative bearer/necessity arguments; no result from it is anticipated here.

## Why the cumulative case does not change my verdict

The affirmative explains more than the bare absence of an obvious alternative. It connects original actuality, necessary self-sufficiency, real intrinsic perfection, actual efficacy, order and source ownership. It defeats powerless cooperator models, reciprocal original donation, two exclusive whole productions of one numerical effect, and foreknowledge invoked as an unexplained producer. It makes unified original sourcehood attractive without denying real derivative agency.

The surviving objection grants those gains. It challenges the exact identification of a voluntarily limited but intrinsically self-complete original exercise with defective original agency, and the eligibility of strict domination over every proposed independent peer. Those are substantive classification questions located by the sources’ own distinctions. They are not demands for a premise-free proof or an argument acceptable to every metaphysical school.

A single source reduces the number of independent original bearers and avoids a separate coordination account. That is a real comparative advantage. It does not, at the present level of argument, establish that every other profile is deficient in the relevant intrinsic respect or that the qualified anchor in question actually supplies every contribution in its connected field. A common-source account can be preferred as an explanatory proposal while the universal source-mode principle and strong actual-coverage conclusion remain insufficiently warranted.

My withholding is therefore affirmative-evidence-sensitive and revisable. It is not an assertion of equiprobability, no rational basis for monotheism, or a demonstration of a plural ultimate. A separately justified recognition, theological premise, or independent bearer-unity argument would be new evidence, rather than something this appraisal has already refuted.
