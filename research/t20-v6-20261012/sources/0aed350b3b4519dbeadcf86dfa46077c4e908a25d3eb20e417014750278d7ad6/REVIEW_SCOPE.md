## Proof-specific limits

The NS residual argument assumes a smooth exact trajectory over its stated
horizon, periodic boundary conditions, a complete residual bound, and bounded
linear observation. Its script example checks an associated scalar comparison
envelope only; it does not simulate or validate a fluid flow.

The robust binary theorem uses a Euclidean output norm and a full closed noise
ball. Its input norm budget is not automatically physical energy. The uncertain
model result is a sufficient, not necessary, common-decoder guarantee.

Evidence JOIN is sound only when the target, analysis and evidence constraints
have the stated scope and coverage. A nonempty intersection alone is not truth.
The abstraction result is relative to a fixed target on the attained image.

The finite-corpus and eventual-horizon impossibility arguments concern declared
unrestricted model classes; they do not rule out useful restricted research.
Conversely, finite enumeration cannot prove those general impossibility results.

The continuation result is behavioural, not an identity theorem about conscious
subjects. Viability is a safety property; progress has a separate certificate.
The combined host-independence result is conditional on real, jointly available
support and operator-governed continuation, with permitted termination separate.

The held-out concentration guarantee requires independently sampled cases and
candidate assessors fixed independently of those cases. It does not validate
physical model applicability or unrestricted adaptive test reuse.

## Responsible scholarly use

Before publication, domain reviewers should check the transfer assumptions,
physical scope and novelty against the relevant literature. Before any physical
application, the specific model, interfaces, resources, uncertainty, authority and
safety obligations need their own review. This package supplies none of those
case-specific approvals by implication.
