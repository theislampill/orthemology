# Original capacity and the limits of explanatory completion

7 October 2026. Continuing the authorised T20 original-explanation inquiry. This report preserves the existing affirmative T17 appraisal and conditional original-bearer theorem. It does not certify a possible all-received universe, replace the earlier research, or close T20.

## Finding

A criterion restricted to the existential availability of productive capacity can exclude an entirely received capacity account without prohibiting beginningless token histories. Its source-grounded rationale is that a complete account of the resources enabling production cannot obtain its complete existential adequacy merely by repeating receipt. This is a defensible substantive principle, with a positive explanatory motivation. It is not derived from local production, absence of cycles, modal necessity, or the impossibility of one agent acquiring its power through an exercise of that same unavailable power.

The new discrimination identifies two opposed illicit promotions. On the affirmative side, identifying all capacities under one genus does not convert an acyclic concrete donor chain into the same-agent constitutive circularity demonstrated in the source. On the opposing side, saying that every member has a strict explanation does not automatically supply a strict explanation of their totality. In a shift-invariant infinite field, unrestricted pooling of those local explanations into the same strict explanatory relation produces literal self-explanation. A rival making that stronger pooling claim must reject the pooling rule, reject the requirement of a further strict total explanation, or supply some genuinely different basis.

These are applications of elementary structural facts to the precise source/reconstruction boundary, not newly discovered mathematics. They narrow which explanations remain available. They do not establish universal original completion by a new theorem.

The strongest surviving rival can also make every particular capacity necessarily coexistent and necessarily received. Necessity of existence does not thereby become possession through itself. The original-completion judgement, rather than a dependent-to-contingent shortcut, must decide that case.

## 1 The source contrast and its exact scope

The direct primary rereading covers electronic Ṣafadiyya S002 lines 725–740, S003 lines 1–16, and the four complete held Adwa scan pages PDF404–407, printed397–400. The electronic text crosses a file boundary during the sentence continued on printed400. The scan controls the scope of that continuation. This is another examination of the same held witness, not an additional historical witness or manuscript collation. [S1–S3]

Printed397 distinguishes a condition of being able to act at all from a condition of performing a particular act. The latter may be a previous particular act. Printed398 uses a created parent preceding a created child as the permitted contrast. Printed398–399 then considers the proposal that the agent becomes efficacious by itself bringing about what first makes it efficacious. In that argument the premise that there is no effecter except that same agent is explicit. Printed400 continues: the agent's being an agent in the original respect need not depend on the occurrence of a particular effect; it may belong to its own essence, although a particular production can depend on another particular production.

Consequently, the strongest local contradiction is not merely:

- Each particular act has some earlier particular act.

It is:

- This agent can acquire the condition of its ability to produce anything only through its own production, while that very condition is unavailable without that production.

The latter makes the same concrete constitutive capacity explanatorily prior to its own availability. The former need not. No temporal first act is demanded by that distinction. Nor can a genuinely productive predecessor be relabelled a merely temporal accompaniment to save a proof.

The source also confidently excludes regress in effecters and in the origin of efficacy. Its wider conclusion must be preserved. However, the same-agent reductio displayed here is not, by itself, a derivation concerning every infinite field of distinct received agents. The supplied Oneness translation, printed20–21/PDF27–28, likewise declares the efficient-power regress impossible and refers to discussion elsewhere. Its footnote16 restates the ban; that editorial repetition is not an additional proof. [S4]

Printed399 itself contrasts treating the successive particular occurrences as a regress with treating the genus of origination as circularity; S003 line1 continues that passage. The operative context is the same agent’s acquisition of the origin of its efficacy. The diagnostic below does not deny that the author reasons about the genus. It tests whether that local explanation of circularity can be extended to any plurality of distinct received bearers merely by identifying their capacities under one type. The source does not present a graph quotient, and this report does not allege that all its wider anti-regress reasoning reduces to that construction. The existing T17 account expressly supplies a further whole-field existential rationale. [T17 P40–43]

## 2 The strongest affirmative case

The target must first be fixed. It is the actual existential possession of the productive resources used by an actual receiving field. It is not the existence of an abstract causal schema, the existence of some capacity somewhere, the entire specification of every token history, or one source's individually exhaustive production of every effect.

The positive case can be stated without appealing to a first temporal event:

1. Actual received efficacy is real. A derivative agent can possess genuine power and be a genuine supplier. This rules out the cheap reply that receivers have nothing to give.
2. The complete account includes the actuality through which those suppliers are able to supply. A conditional description of how an available donor transmits power does not itself account for that donor's existential availability.
3. Moving to another received donor supplies a genuine local explanation but does not change the received mode of existential possession. Repeating that move, however extensively, does not by itself supply possession through itself.
4. A complete existential account of that received capacity field cannot consist exclusively of such received possession. It needs actual nonreceived adequacy relevant to the field.
5. Under the separately defended real-resource and bearer commitments, that original actuality has an actual bearer; the receipt-transport argument secures the same bearer's whole-existence originality. Further constitutive and contingency-as-need principles govern necessity and essential independence. [T17 P40–61]

Step4 is the exact contested principle. Steps1–3 explain its motivation but do not logically force it. The rationale is stronger than a preference for short explanations: an original source supplies a different existential mode rather than one more donor. It also does not require beneficiaries to cease being received after they have been supplied. That already-corrected objection must not be revived.

A proposed **capacity completion criterion** can therefore say:

> Count an account as existentially complete for the relevant received productive-capacity field only if its actuality is not accounted for wholly by receipt within that field; the account must include actual nonreceived adequacy genuinely relevant to the field.

This makes the inferential commitment reviewable. Its reference to nonreceived adequacy is not an independently established discovery. To call the criterion necessary for adequate explanation is to accept the substance of T17 P41, with the rationale just given. Defining “complete” this way cannot add further warrant.

The scope restriction is independently motivated by the source contrast. In the permitted one-original beginningless history, the standing resource G is genuinely unreceived and actually contributes to each token, while token n also requires its productive predecessor. G discharges the original-capacity question without least-generating the entire token history. The already executed BeginninglessProvision control demonstrates precisely that compatibility; this report does not replay or rediscover it. [R2 §5]

Three further limits matter. One original relevant resource need not cover every target branch. Its existential relevance need not already be efficient productive attribution by the same bearer. And an original-capacity criterion does not establish one individually complete common Creator. The earlier actual-use and same-witness controls continue to govern those transitions. [R1 §§4a–4d; R2 §§3–4]

## 3 A received-capacity rival that survives the local argument

A useful rival must represent actual supply, not just unused arrows or equations. Consider distinct bearers B_n, their concrete standing capacities C_n, and actual donating exercises D_n, indexed by the nonnegative integers. For each n:

- B_n is actual and possesses C_n intrinsically.
- D_(n+1), an actual exercise of B_(n+1) using its actually possessed C_(n+1), supplies the whole existential actuality of B_n and hence the concrete instantiation of C_n.
- D_n is B_n's own real exercise after or while its received capacity is available. It does not supply B_n or C_n. D_0 supplies the observed downstream target.
- Every represented donation is actually used. All its required resources are actual. No separately conserved substrate, original bearer, or efficient law is inserted under a different name.

Bearer-to-capacity support here is the inherited ontological receipt transport, not a fictitious efficient-production arrow from a bearer to its attribute. The donor exercise is the productive link. The distinction prevents an actual-use label from silently doing work assigned to another relation.

This interpretation rejects neither real secondary efficacy nor real receipt. No bearer acquires its power through its own not-yet-enabled exercise. Every finite backward tracing moves to a distinct higher-indexed donor; it never returns to the same resource. It therefore satisfies the local anti-bootstrap condition and the qualified-bearer receipt condition while containing no original resource. It is an analytical counterinterpretation to the attempted entailment from those conditions, not a newly kernel-checked model or a demonstrated possible universe.

The interpretation can give each donor temporal priority to its recipient if that is separately required: B_n can be supplied at time −n, with B_(n+1) available earlier. Nothing in the narrow non-entailment depends on a contemporaneous cause or an eternally existing individual. This chronological assignment does not prove the metaphysical possibility of such complete creation or preservation by those agents. Those would be additional application questions.

The source rejects the entire received explanation on the stronger original-completion principle. The rival's serious response is not that receipt becomes originality through repetition. It is that the actual donor relations exhaust what a sufficient existential explanation requires: each actual bearer is supplied by another actual bearer, and no additional original mode is needed. That is **distributive explanatory completeness**. It differs from claiming that the field is a new independent object, that a law efficiently gives power, or that one receiver is intrinsically sufficient.

The rival grants the local premises preceding step4 and denies step4. It is not a countermodel to T17's complete accepted conjunction, which includes step4. Equally, demanding step4 as an entry condition for testing whether the preceding premises support it would insulate the disputed inference from examination.

### Necessary reception is the stronger modal version

The preceding interpretation need not admit the possible nonexistence of any B_n. As a stronger conditional test, suppose every B_n necessarily exists and essentially receives its whole actuality through D_(n+1). Every admitted world retains those receipt relations. This is an explicit modal hypothesis, not a discovery of necessity from the use of a rigid subscript.

Nothing in the local supply, anti-bootstrap, acyclicity, or qualified-bearer premises refutes it. If contingency means possible nonexistence, a contingency-to-need conditional has a false antecedent for these bearers. If the source uses contingency to mean lack of sufficiency through itself, the bearers remain contingent in that explanatory sense and their need is met locally by their donors. Neither reading automatically establishes an unreceived source.

T17 P56 already distinguishes necessary existence from nonreceipt and notes necessarily supplied reality. This continuation uses that existing distinction; it does not discover it. The necessary-reception rival presses original completion directly. An argument that all received members must be possibly absent, merely because they receive, would assume an additional bridge the inherited account expressly keeps separate.

Necessary existence somewhere is also not the same as the same necessarily existing original particular. In a different hypothetical family of worlds, each world can have an original witness while the witnesses vary. The modal form “necessarily, some original exists” does not alone yield “some one particular is necessarily original.” Conversely, the necessary-reception variant grants same-particular necessary existence while withholding originality. Neither comparison replaces the actual fixed-witness bearer and constitutive arguments of T17 P48–60. No modal countermodel is here certified metaphysically possible.

The preserved formal interface is explicit: OriginalBearerBridge.lean defines Completion separately at lines35–36; ContingencyNeed and ConstitutiveReception separately at lines50–57; and abc_same_witness at lines126–139 composes the same actual resource and bearer with the inherited necessity and independence arguments. The current rival denies Completion, not the validity of that composition. Essential nonreceipt conditional on existence already has its own theorem at lines118–123 and does not independently require necessity. These are cited inherited results, not fresh executions. [OBB]

## 4 What fails when genus is made a graph vertex

In the donor interpretation, each C_n receives from another particular capacity through an actual exercise. If all the C_n are identified with a single vertex called “the genus of efficacy,” the quotient has a self-loop. But an identification map can create cycles that are absent from the original graph. The same operation applied to all tokens in a beginningless productive history also creates a self-loop, although the source permits that history.

The diagnostic consequence is precise: a shared explanatory description is not identity of its concrete instances. The source's same-agent argument has an independently fixed bearer and the condition of that bearer's agency. It does not obtain the identity needed for its contradiction merely by calling many different resources instances of efficacy.

A stronger whole-field argument is still available. One may claim that the actuality of received efficacy *as such* needs explanation beyond the received field. But that is a claim about the proper explanatory target and its required adequacy, not a finite cycle discovered in the underlying donor graph. It needs the completion principle of §2 or another supported argument. This clarification prevents a reconstruction from borrowing the evidentness of the same-agent contradiction for a different collective claim.

There is a symmetrical limitation on a propositional test. Every p_n, “capacity n is actual,” entails the existential statement that some capacity is actual. It does not follow merely from that entailment that using p_n to explain another fact is circular. Sufficient explanatory grounds often entail their consequences. Explanatory dependence, premise use, and material implication must be distinguished. A test rejecting all explanations whose premises entail the target would reject valid explanations generally, including proposed original-source explanations.

## 5 A conditional obstruction to the strongest pooling reply

The following small mathematical argument tests a particular *additional* interpretation of the rival's completeness claim. It is not a universal proof against infinitism.

Let p_i, for integers i, be distinct actual facts about the availability of the relevant concrete capacities. Suppose the rival grants:

1. E({p_(i+1)}, {p_i}) for every i, where E is the **same strict complete explanatory relation** on nonempty pluralities of facts.
2. Infinitary pooling: for any nonempty indexed family of E-relations, E holds from the union of their grounds to the union of their explained facts.
3. Irreflexivity: no nonempty plurality S stands in E to itself.

Set S = {p_i : i is an integer}. The shift i ↦ i+1 is a bijection, so the union of the grounds and the union of the explained facts are exactly the same S. Pooling therefore gives E(S,S), contradicting irreflexivity.

This is a genuine conditional exclusion. The concrete predecessor graph has no finite directed cycle, so graph acyclicity does not repair this *set-level explanatory* conflict. However, no metaphysical claim is proved unless the interpretation warrants all three premises. In particular, ordinary actual productive support is not automatically strict complete explanation of the full local fact package. If a donor's exercise or some other condition remains outside the asserted local explanation, premise1 has not been established at that scope.

The equality of the two unions matters. Mere overlap does not suffice. As a finite nonvacuity control, E({p},{q}) and E({q},{r}) can pool to E({p,q},{q,r}) without reflexivity: the sets overlap but differ. A finite rooted chain therefore does not incur the same contradiction. Likewise, in the one-original token control, the union of complete rule inputs includes G as well as the tokens, while the outputs are the tokens. These unions are not identical. This observation only blocks the stated reflexivity argument; it does not establish that pooling supplies an adequate explanation of that whole history.

No new aggregate object is needed for the lemma: E can be a relation between pluralities. But moving from separate member explanations to one explanatory relation between their unions is nevertheless substantive. The lemma may not be used to pretend that the author already made that move, that the Humean critic accepts it, or that every infinite directed field is shift-invariant.

The strongest surviving distributive rival can therefore say: each member's receipt is genuinely explained, but there is no further strict plurality-explanation produced by union. That move is coherent at the stated structural scope. Its cost is that it cannot then advertise the union construction as providing the additional strict total explanation demanded by the affirmative criterion. The disagreement has been located rather than settled by renaming.

The primary contrast is informative here. In Dialogues part9, Demea requests a reason for the entire received succession, while Cleanthes in §9.9 holds that explaining the members suffices. His separate §9.8 claim that causation requires a temporal beginning is unnecessary to the stronger distributive objection and is not adopted here. These are positions of dialogue characters, not an unqualified attribution of every claim to Hume. [H1]

Leibniz's Ultimate Origin, opening discussion, explicitly permits an eternal series of copied books while denying that successive copies supply its full existential explanation. This independently illustrates the distinction between a temporal first and an ultimate reason. It does not establish the capacity-specific principle for T20, and its more general sufficient-reason and world-selection commitments must not be silently imported. [L1]

## 6 Where noninferential warrant may enter

The proper place for a basic or noninferential judgement is now identifiable. It can support the claim that exclusively received existential possession cannot be an adequate original account of productive capacity. That is the substantive content of step4, rather than a missing algebraic manipulation. A person need not derive every rational starting point from a premise set neutral between all philosophical schools. T17 P20–24 and P41–43 already permit that kind of positive, defeasible appraisal.

It is also possible that a person's warranted basic recognition directly supports an actual original source. Such a warrant, if genuine, could support belief independently of this regress reconstruction. It would not retroactively make the reconstruction deductively valid or identify every property of the recognized source. No report of such a person's experience is supplied here, and this research agent does not claim to possess a human fiṭrī experience.

Four safeguards prevent an appeal to basicality from becoming question-begging certification:

- State the exact content recognized. Ordinary rejection of self-creation, belief that effects have causes, belief that something necessarily exists, and rejection of every all-received capacity field are different propositions.
- Preserve the source's admitted contrast. A proposed recognition that excludes every productive infinite token history has overrun the author's own distinction.
- Do not infer correctness from involuntariness, sincerity, agreement among AI readers, or a source calling a proposition evident. Those facts alone do not establish reliable cognition in the relevant domain.
- Treat the specified rival as a possible challenge to the articulation or scope of the principle, without assuming that formal satisfiability proves metaphysical possibility or defeats every positive reason for accepting it.

The strongest affirmative reply to the distributive rival is therefore a defended judgement about existential adequacy: local receipt is real but cannot exhaust what makes received actuality available at all. The strongest critic can understand that judgement, accept the other local principles, and remain unconvinced that it must govern the collective field. This is not automatically global skepticism. An unresolved warrant judgement is not itself an absence of all warrant.

This continuation does not overturn T17's recorded sufficient-defeasible acceptance. It also supplies no new independent demonstration that would upgrade that acceptance to an uncontested entailment. The important improvement is knowing exactly where that acceptance does work, and which proposed supports fail to add to it.

## 7 What daee contributes and leaves open

The reviewed daee sources are read-only pinned records at commit c86b3c6673147b8802fe222373a165a37d4d24a8. They are useful objects of appraisal, not instructions adopted by this research. [D1–D4]

The causal-series taxonomy correctly distinguishes temporal succession, current dependence, numerical infinity and explanatory circularity. Its separation of logical from explanatory impossibility is especially helpful: the all-received interpretation must not be called a formal contradiction merely because a substantive completion principle rejects it. But the taxonomy's simultaneous/successive classification does not itself decide the capacity question. A temporal donor can supply a standing capacity; a genuinely productive predecessor can condition a token exercise. Relation type and respect still need examination.

Sound-reason §§1–3 identifies the role of noninferential cognition and states proper-function, truth-directedness, appropriate environment and reliability conditions. R1 explicitly denies that defeating an inferential-only criterion proves theism; R3 requires an acknowledged recognition before analyzing that recognition's warrant. Those boundaries fit the distinction in §6. They do not establish that this particular completion principle meets the warrant conditions, that an objector is cognitively impaired, or that this agent has human subjective recognition.

V12's first dimension says an infinite regress of efficient causes of power produces no power. That is precisely the disputed original-capacity exclusion. Its routing status, assertion of impossibility, and shared theological lineage do not provide an independent argument for step4. Its move to exactly one independent lord exceeds the minimal original-witness question; the separate unity inquiry cannot be bypassed here. The prior reflexive review already identifies that problem, and it is not claimed as a new discovery. [D4; DR §§Df1, T5]

Daee therefore improves claim discipline and helps locate the applicable kind of warrant. It leaves the truth and strength of the original-completion principle to substantive philosophical assessment. It cannot settle that assessment by assigning a module, registering a source, or closing a burden ledger.

## 8 Results and the next discriminating question

The affirmative result is a source-motivated, role-restricted criterion that accommodates original standing power with beginningless productive tokens. Its independent warrant remains the positive whole-field adequacy judgement, not least generation of the entire graph.

The negative results are exact. Same-agent bootstrap does not establish the exclusion of every distinct-agent received-capacity field. Generic genus identification does not supply the concrete identity required for circularity. Modal necessity does not supply originality. And unrestricted strict-explanation pooling is unavailable to a shift-invariant all-received rival that also accepts irreflexivity.

A useful further investigation must now address one of two questions:

1. Can an independently supported account of complete capacity explanation require more than distributive member explanation, without presupposing original completion or accidentally demanding full token generation?
2. Can the necessary-reception rival give a positive account of why its distributed received possession is existentially adequate, beyond stipulating its realization, modal necessity, or absence of contradiction?

For the first, the needed evidence is a reason for the relevant completeness principle and its capacity-specific scope. For the second, the needed evidence is an actual account of explanatory adequacy, including whether laws are descriptions, nonobjectual grounds, or concrete productive reality. If a law is offered as an autonomous actual basis, the inherited ground-to-bearer and grounding-to-production questions reappear; this report does not resolve them by reifying the law.

Another finite enumeration, the same least-fixed-point proof, a generic graph self-loop, or another agreement with the source would not answer those questions. Conditional mathematical work remains useful when it tests a specifically proposed aggregation or explanation rule against the preserved controls. The pooling argument supplies one such test, with its exact premises exposed. No exhaustive impossibility or research-completion claim follows from it.
