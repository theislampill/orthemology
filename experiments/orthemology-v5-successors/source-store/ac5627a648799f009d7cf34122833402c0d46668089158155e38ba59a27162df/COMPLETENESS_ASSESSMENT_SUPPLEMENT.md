## Verdict

The new assessment is a substantive improvement and introduces no new blocking defect. It addresses the principal evaluative recommendation in the review: the two positive arguments are separated, an explanatory criticism of the external-completion rationale is supplied, and the stronger original-priority reply is given its proper force. This improves the assessment of the bridge without proving essential truthfulness or the possibility of a contrary source.

## What the concurrent cases establish

Lines 19–27 make the relevant concession unusually explicit. The source does not merely produce outputs retrospectively labeled good. It recognizes the recipients' cognitive and practical goods, values them for their fitting character, and intentionally originates the conditions for them. The actual derivative virtue in B is present while the source's defective attestation in C occurs. Thus the model cannot evade the giving argument by removing all derivative virtue during the bad act, reducing good effects to indifferent mechanism, or treating every apparent virtue as a disguised instrument of malice.

The exact explanatory point is legitimate: those episode-level intentional dependencies do not include a requirement that the source's unrelated C be fitting. Adding such a requirement constrains the bearer’s entire practical profile, rather than filling a missing element in the stated local explanation of A or B. This makes the distinction between local explanatory completeness and comprehensive character substantive rather than merely terminological.

It does not answer the source's a fortiori challenge by counting two good acts against one bad one. Line 29 expressly accepts that a comprehensive priority principle would exclude C and that superior performance elsewhere does not remove C's defect. Lines 33–37 explain why purification is a reasoned positive reply, and line 39 preserves the de re eligibility dispute. The concluding paragraph correctly withholds both a modal countermodel and essential-T0 closure.

## One nonblocking wording refinement

At line 25, the statement that the causal or explanatory target is fully supplied should be read as: **the stipulated episode-level intentional dependencies are supplied under the shared source-effect description**. That wording is preferable to a formulation which might imply that a full metaphysical account of the source, its necessary individuality, or its production of consciousness has been constructed. Lines 27, 39 and 51 already disavow that stronger achievement, so this is a precision improvement rather than a new flaw.

## Remaining boundary

The reviewer still withholds essential-T0 promotion and any actual-source or restoration conclusion. The new assessment fairly develops why foreign-supply exclusion alone does not settle an intrinsic ordering defect, while admitting that the combined intrinsic-perfection and original-priority rationale is a substantive non-circular candidate warrant. Its strongest fresh contribution is this explanatory discrimination, not a proof that the source is or can be malicious.
