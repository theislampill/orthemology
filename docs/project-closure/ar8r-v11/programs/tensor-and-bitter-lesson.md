# Tensor representations and the Bitter Lesson challenger

## Historical disposition

A privileged ontological "orthemic tensor" is rejected as overclaimed. Boolean,
semiring, matrix, tensor, relation, and hypergraph encodings remain legitimate
finite-record representations, with zero general novelty merely from encoding.
Miettinen's Boolean Tensor Factorization and Sutton's Bitter Lesson are later
sourced methodological context, not recovered historical theorem bytes.
Their bounded source and publication status is recorded in
[`representation-method-source-status.yaml`](representation-method-source-status.yaml).

Two different frozen enumerations must not be conflated:

- search controllers A-G: human registry; novelty-prioritized adaptive;
  countermodel information gain; burden reduction; proof search;
  diversity/random control; and learned interaction priority;
- representation candidates A-F: relation/hypergraph; inherited human features;
  sparse piece-outcome; raw text; graph-plus-text hybrid; and random/diversity.

There is no recovered representation G. Controller G is not representation G.

## Completed bounded diagnostic

The historical controller evaluation ranked D, A, F, E, B, C, G on the
selectively audited retrospective corpus. G was not promoted. Because the data
were missing-not-at-random, policy-selected, and positive-unlabeled, this is noncausal and not a verdict
that learned representations fail.

P186 used 488 labeled observations: 461 pair interactions and 27 higher-order
interactions; 86 had independent review and 63 were completed prospective
controller selections. It excluded 62,374 Cartesian-only entries, 52,192
unobserved pair obligations, and 10,182 payload-blocked interactions from
ordinary negative labels. Its terminal disposition was
`DATA_INADEQUATE_COLLECT_PROSPECTIVELY`.

The v3 controller implementation failed irrelevant-identifier tests for B, C,
E, F, G, and the mixed policy. V4 removed identifier-derived features, random
seeds, tie-breaking, and parsed ordinals; frozen A-G and mixed-policy tests then
passed. Later payload-safe checks failed closed on semantic-key collisions. This
is an implementation repair, not predictive, causal, ontological, or meniscus
evidence.

## Frozen decision rules

Success requires either a preregistered predictive gain of at least 0.05 with no
independent-review loss and paired permutation `p <= 0.05`; at least 25-percent
compression retaining 95 percent of the best retrieval score and every hard
safety distinction; or an independently reviewed prospective higher-order
status-changing family missed by pairwise and human baselines. Underpowered,
non-equivariant, typing-losing, source-custody-losing, false-zero, or
status-dishonest results cannot promote a representation.

The prospective 120-observation, five-arm gate remains unrun. A bounded win may
change search policy or indexing only; it cannot establish ontology, source
truth, metaphysics, or terminology.

## Standing methodological control

Sutton's Bitter Lesson challenges fixed hand-authored taxonomies, while the
"same mistake relocated one level up" critique applies equally to hand-authored
learned axes. Neither argument proves learned-representation superiority.
