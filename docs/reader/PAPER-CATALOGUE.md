## Core exposition and formal reference

### Main exposition

**Orthemma–Ortheme Systems: An Analysis-Relative Architecture for Auditable Classification and Handling.** Read the [Markdown manuscript][main] or its [governed PDF][render-orthemma-ortheme-systems-draft].

**Why read it:** this is the principal account of how one concrete occurrence moves through observation, competing descriptions, evidence, placement, routing and correction. An *orthemma* is the situated occurrence; its *orthemes* are repeatable, consequence-bearing state-types under a declared analysis. The actual profile is distinct from both an observation and an actor’s inferred placement. Analysis-relativity does not let the actor manufacture what is true.

The [integration thesis](../../manuscript/orthemma-ortheme-systems-revised-draft.md#13-the-thesis-stated-as-an-integration-discipline) is deliberately specific: combine established classification, uncertainty, provenance and handling practices in a shared lifecycle and an auditable **episode record**. The six additions concern versioned lineage; analysis/actor/time indices; typed and expiring evidence; factorised candidates; per-burden closure; and episode reification. The manuscript does not claim that each neighbouring technique is new.

Its [stopped-clock validator](../../manuscript/orthemma-ortheme-systems-revised-draft.md#85-worked-example-the-stopped-clock-validator) returns a correct answer merely because a marker occurs in a log. Removing or spoofing the marker exposes a defective method. The [result/pathway matrix](../../manuscript/orthemma-ortheme-systems-revised-draft.md#84-the-result--pathway-matrix) also admits the converse: an adequate, sufficiently reliable procedure can produce a rare wrong result. Pathway adequacy is therefore non-factive; factive token truth linkage is reported separately. Undetermined required checks are neither passes nor automatic failures. The distinctions direct different repairs instead of blaming every error on the same component.

The [empirical programme](../../manuscript/orthemma-ortheme-systems-revised-draft.md#13-empirical-program) separates machinery benefit, episode-record benefit and vocabulary benefit. Its ready-to-run packets and conformance fixtures are not completed experiments or proof of mathematical completeness. These are this manuscript’s evidence boundaries, not a denial of later separately scoped research. The [limitations](../../manuscript/orthemma-ortheme-systems-revised-draft.md#151-limitations) also deny that task-relative success supplies the legitimacy of the task or its authority. Edit the Markdown, not generated TeX or the PDF; this exposition does not silently incorporate every later tranche.

## Philosophical and theological manuscripts

### Ground of intelligibility

**Orthability and the Ground of Intelligibility: A Transcendental Argument from Rule-Governed Resolution.** [Source][ground] · [Governed PDF][render-orthability-ground-of-intelligibility-draft].

**Why read it:** it asks what makes genuine recognition, error, fittingness and correction possible, rather than treating the engineering rulebook as its own ultimate justification. It distinguishes local analysis-relative aptness (**L**), objective conditions of evaluability (**O**) and created representation/application (**R**). The [labelled argument chain](../../companion/orthability-and-the-ground-of-intelligibility.md#42-the-chain-with-each-step-labeled) passes from *true* evaluations to their objective preconditions, not from an arbitrary local convention to a reified object called Orthability.

The affirmative reductio is that an intelligible construction of **all** objective evaluability would already use identity, possibility, lawful transition and success/failure conditions. It would therefore presuppose what it claims wholly to produce. The [adjudication](../../companion/orthability-and-the-ground-of-intelligibility.md#53-adjudication) makes its reach explicit: an opponent who instead accepts brute modal structure or refuses the intelligible-production model is not thereby demonstratively refuted. Reliability alone does not establish a Designer; the faculty argument separately needs irreducible teleology and causal adequacy. Intellect, Will, Power and Wisdom are not obtained by relabelling one result. The [step map][orthability-step-map] explicitly holds the common-premise Wisdom bridge.

The [disclosure argument](../../companion/orthability-and-the-ground-of-intelligibility.md#8-a-ground-capable-of-disclosure) supports at most a **conditional capacity** for intentional disclosure, not that the ground has actually spoken or that a particular text is its speech. The separate Atharī manuscript supplies its own revelational premises.

**Reading the historical claim correctly:** the source’s [later V11 authority qualification](../../companion/orthability-and-the-ground-of-intelligibility.md#later-v11-authority-qualification) says that this positive philosophical language is not itself the current audit verdict; it directs that verdict to Track T/B0–B16 and retains `NECESSARY_BEING_NOT_DEDUCTIVELY_ESTABLISHED`. That explicit qualification stays with this work. The [later source-bound foundational account](RESEARCH-ATLAS.md#a-positive-foundational-route) has its own premises and appraisals; this catalogue neither merges the assessments nor rewrites the older argument. Engineering conformance supplies no metaphysical proof.

**Why read it:** this is the explicitly Atharī/Taymiyyan articulation of divine Knowledge, Speech, linguistic possibility and created reception. It is not merely the preceding philosophical conditional with a religious title attached. ### Information and composition

**Information, Composition, and Self-Revision: Local Descriptions and Operative Access.** [Preserved PDF][information-pdf] · [Tracked TeX][information] · [Edition relationship note][lineage].

**Why read it:** this manuscript connects what a local description determines with what an observing method can learn by reconstructing its access. It does not infer an actual coupling from compatible component records. Its [relational strengthening theorem](https://github.com/theislampill/orthemology/blob/0784acddd4770f1190f758f264ba52b2b110bd27/theory/lineages/h-ics-v5/information-composition-and-self-revision-v5.tex#L81) separates an invariant that *some* exactly compatible composition can guarantee from one that *every* compatible composition guarantees. With nonempty compatible records, the first asks whether `G = J ∩ P` still projects onto every declared local relation; the second requires `J ⊆ P`. The parity family shows that even all proper subsystem records can leave a joint constraint undetermined.

For probabilities, compatible support is not sufficient for compatible weights. The [output-determination proposition](https://github.com/theislampill/orthemology/blob/0784acddd4770f1190f758f264ba52b2b110bd27/theory/lineages/h-ics-v5/information-composition-and-self-revision-v5.tex#L202) uses the span `D` of **feasible differences** between laws sharing the record: a channel has the same output law on that family exactly when it annihilates `D`. Replacing `D` by the whole record kernel requires the stated full-support condition. This matters at boundary laws, where algebraically possible directions need not be feasible perturbations.

Its [operative-access argument](https://github.com/theislampill/orthemology/blob/0784acddd4770f1190f758f264ba52b2b110bd27/theory/lineages/h-ics-v5/information-composition-and-self-revision-v5.tex#L241) distinguishes reformatting an insufficient observation from acquiring an informative relation. A permitted reconnection can make an already reachable parity observation immediately available. That is neither evidence created from nothing nor a claim to expand beyond every inherited operation. The [sure-finite-decision theorem](https://github.com/theislampill/orthemology/blob/0784acddd4770f1190f758f264ba52b2b110bd27/theory/lineages/h-ics-v5/information-composition-and-self-revision-v5.tex#L289) then gives an exact labelled-belief criterion for finite deterministic observation-based controllers. The labels retain the **initial** answer through state changes. In the destructive example, every pair has a distinguishing experiment, but neither first action preserves enough information for one policy to decide every initial possibility.

The version-bound workflow applies the same distinctions to candidate receipts: two success flags are insufficient when they omit the candidate identities. It requires the joint guard to remain valid through publication, not just individually consistent field reads. The source supplies conditional models and ordinary arguments, not a verified concurrent implementation, noise-tolerant controller, physical mechanism or metaphysical proof. It acknowledges the antecedents of its join, marginal and state-identification techniques rather than claiming each is new.

The lineage note establishes the title/abstract and companion relationship. It does **not** establish a byte-identical rebuild of the supplied PDF. The discussion above is grounded in the tracked TeX; that source is not silently promoted to the verified exact build input of the preserved edition.

#### The related reflexive-revision manuscript

**Reflexive Revision under an Evidence Budget.** [Tracked TeX manuscript][reflexive].

The [minimum-evidence-cost section](https://github.com/theislampill/orthemology/blob/0784acddd4770f1190f758f264ba52b2b110bd27/theory/lineages/h-reflexive-v3/reflexive_revision_evidence_budget_v3.tex#L47) specifies `V_g(S)`: zero when the remaining situations agree on the decision; otherwise minimise probe cost plus the largest remaining branch cost. Its theorem characterises exactly when a finite budget guarantees a uniformly sound answer. Identifiability and affordability differ, and affording the next probe alone does not ensure that the entire deciding continuation fits. Policy computation and validation of the model are **not** included in these probe costs. Hard constraints remain vetoes rather than compensable penalties.

The [reuse and reflexivity section](https://github.com/theislampill/orthemology/blob/0784acddd4770f1190f758f264ba52b2b110bd27/theory/lineages/h-reflexive-v3/reflexive_revision_evidence_budget_v3.tex#L86) explains why a retained record can settle one target yet fail a changed target. Under unchanged meanings, no new observation is needed exactly when the new decision is constant on each retained-record fibre. An injective record suffices for every Boolean target; a restricted future target family can need less. A common well-founded order can justify termination, but changing local losses alone cannot: the source's two-state example cycles while improving the predecessor loss at each step.

The [delivery-cost obstruction](https://github.com/theislampill/orthemology/blob/0784acddd4770f1190f758f264ba52b2b110bd27/theory/lineages/h-reflexive-v3/reflexive_revision_evidence_budget_v3.tex#L114) supplies a practical limit: complete agreement on task outputs cannot decide which representation is cheaper without an observation linking behaviour to delivery cost. An authorised probe of that missing coordinate changes the decision problem; repeating behaviour-only tests does not. This is a reason to ask the right discriminating question, not a result that either representation is actually cheaper.

Keep both titles and their source roles visible. The [lineage note][lineage] relates the PDF and TeX, but explicitly leaves exact source-to-PDF provenance unverified. ### Fresh innovation histories and original support

**Fresh histories, bounded observation, and original support.** [Complete written article][t12-fresh] · [Mathematical review][t12-concentration-review].

The article proves a Gaussian anti-concentration bound for an ordinary real-valued **finitely additive** joint probability. Independence and the Gaussian marginal's tail control suffice; tightness of the other variable and joint countable additivity are not required. Applying the bound to arbitrarily long fresh innovation blocks, all bound to one terminal law, makes every bounded terminal interval null. Thus even one warranted positive bounded-capture probability contradicts that package.

Its constructive counterbalance is equally important: a fixed ultralimit of uniform-anchor laws gives a coherent fresh history in which every sample position is finite, the full noise marginal is ordinary iid Gaussian, and every bounded position interval has probability zero. The article also supplies finite forward interventions and distinguishes a stable contractive process from its accumulating-variance obstruction. These are positive mathematical constructions, not merely warnings about evidence.

For an original-support argument, the article identifies an additional joint-realisability burden concerning the actual received support, stochastic mechanism and observation contract. It credits the inherited original-possession and recognition arguments without pretending that this stochastic route establishes an actual original participant, unique complete bearer or intrinsic necessity. The [source-specific frontier](CURRENT-FRONTIERS.md#fresh-histories-access-and-rational-policy) preserves that distinction rather than turning a local limitation into a verdict on every foundational route.

### Bounded acquisition and diachronic policy

**Bounded access and diachronic coherence.** [Complete assessment][t12-access] · [Separate final clarification][t12-clarification].

The positive operational result replaces a blanket preference for countable additivity with a specified acquisition task. If successful acquisition within resource budget `m` confines the target to a bounded range, then finite subadditivity bounds success by bounded capture plus the resource tail. A warranted positive success probability and a sufficiently small tail therefore force positive bounded mass. The law, information state, target and magnitude-sensitive task must stay fixed. A finite output alphabet or a correct actual answer does not by itself supply that contract.

A second construction uses a countable partition of positive-probability exact reports. Each report gives a specified event posterior zero although its prior is one half. Under the stated reporting and trading interface, two locally favourable transactions incur a fixed loss in every completed outcome; a quadratic forecast calculation supplies a related prospective-accuracy comparison. Every finite deadline retains a no-report branch and does not inherit the same completed-policy conclusion.

The final clarification is a **separate interpretative supplement**, not an edited edition of the article. It distinguishes mathematical incompatibility of positive bounded capture from domination of a particular completed policy: the history, reporter and losing behaviour are mathematically consistent. Abstention, precommitment, model revision and rejection of an unwarranted service assumption remain different responses. Outright resale can lock in the loss at report time without an extra executed settlement stage, while warranted ticket value and transfer conditions remain necessary.

### Total finite-input reporting

### Charged live interlocks and repair after quiescence

[Original argument](../../experiments/orthemology-v5-successors/source-store/c33e271b68f0ab941bab29cf8ca679ea53d1d26c108071e2d7a313ae222022ab/MODEL_AND_PROOFS.md) · [T07-DYNAMIC-V2 — proof and evidence scope](../../experiments/orthemology-v5-successors/groups/t07-technical/README.md#t07-dynamic-v2).

The dynamic model combines a physical-veto premise, versioned commands, revocation memory, cancellation and an executable repair strategy. With `n` genuine roots and a lifetime union of at most `B` faulty roots, a `q`-root path and `r`-root revocation set satisfy the declared safety and availability contract exactly when

```text
q + r > n + B
q ≤ n − B,   r ≤ n − B.
```

The minimum is `n = 3B + 1`, with `q = r = 2B + 1`. This is **not a new universal distributed-systems threshold**: the source explicitly imports familiar quorum mathematics and contributes its exact place in the fully charged repair mechanism. ## Independent evidence, faithful evaluation and finite controllers: the Ninth interfaces

These four work families develop different links in a trustworthy computation: an independently checked existence certificate, faithful modeled expression execution, a finite implementation of an accepted initial history, and an invertible change of complete command identity. A result at one interface does not silently fill a gap at the others.

### Finite common-policy evidence without rerunning synthesis

The finite typed-data calculus supplies an actual [checker syntax][t09-syntax], [soundness proof][t09-soundness] and [completeness construction][t09-complete]. For a valid fixed finite rational hidden-model input and query, **an accepted finite body exists exactly when the inherited deterministic common lawful almost-sure parity policy exists**. Any admitted measurable-seed winning policy also yields existence of accepted finite syntax. The new contribution is the independently checked evidence interface, not another claim to have discovered the inherited winning-region theorem. A winning query does not make every submitted body valid.

The checker validates positive dimensions and actual shape before enumeration, normalized rational rows, sparse menus with empty defaults, exact state/candidate keys, every submitted node and every positive receipt branch. Components require an attained even minimum and compare full numerical rows, not just positive supports. The path construction shortens repeated-state walks to at most `n−1` edges; equal endpoints admit an empty path. That bounds submitted evidence syntax, not stochastic hitting time.

[Checked composition][t09-composition] validates both original bodies before canonicalisation, retaining literal obligations rather than unioning components into an unproved witness. A new parent needs a separately checked absent support and all child obligations. Raw merge/sort/link can discard a malformed duplicate, so they are not untrusted-entry admission APIs. The [bound envelope][t09-binding] requires actual structured-input equality before a fresh check; an old acceptance bit, equal label or digest is not a substitute. Even a harmless input revision can invalidate the old envelope while a newly checked body remains valid.

The [ordinary verification-cost argument][t09-cost] is polynomial in **explicit materialised input, submitted AST and rational bit lengths**; certificates may themselves be exponential. It is neither a Lean machine-cost theorem nor a polynomial witness-size result. The [source-owned guide and evidence][t09-cert-guide] preserve formal components separately from that ordinary argument. A certificate does not extract a runnable controller or finite-state memory strategy. The separate 21-path shared-root runtime has no supplied encoding into these input tables. Model authority, a safe byte decoder, linked binary behaviour and physical safety remain additional application premises.

### Expression stacks, costs and the Python trust boundary

The [exact expression scope][t09-p1-scope] and [source/model map][t09-p1-map] connect a manually defined Lean stack machine to the pinned Python evaluator. The universal local result permits arbitrary pending work and pre-existing values: a successful expression leaves its value above the unchanged old value stack and does not execute the pending tail. It agrees with the expression semantics. Success costs one tick per leaf and two plus child costs per compound; a resource fault instead stops early with the same modeled error category and visible meter count. The [wording clarification][t09-p1-clarify] prevents treating available mathematical fuel as pops executed after failure.

Every popped item ticks before dispatch, including ready visits. Binary children execute left then right and reduction pops right then left. Variables bypass width checking; binary arithmetic precedes its result check. Power checks exponent plus one **before** shifting and has a distinct exponent-storage fault. Thus an expression can return a large register value under a small width budget, while a host allocation can fail before a modeled binary-result rejection. Adequate finite ideal bounds depend on the actual input and need not be cheap or physically safe to compute.

The admitted domain requires finite immutable correctly aritied tuples, exact builtin natural integers, ordinary dictionaries and valid natural budgets. Those are preconditions, not validations supplied by `Meter` or `eval_expr`; booleans, subclasses, malformed direct tuples and concurrent mutation are excluded. The [recorded theorem and finite evidence][t09-expression-guide] include independently written recursive-oracle and pending-stack controls, but AST/source identity and finite tests are not a verified CPython frontend. Parser, serializer, statements, loops, full runner and physical resource failures remain separate. The later tail-completion record preserves earlier failures and credits reused products only once.

### Finite reconstruction and an actual initial-state controller

The [finite primitive refinement][t09-refinement], [sampled-policy reconstruction][t09-history] and [uniform initial-state endpoint][t09-native] form one implementation lineage. Actual finite storage operations preserve shared-root mutation and distinct addressed-label receipts; a gate snapshot alone does not authenticate a policy. Callable reconstruction rejects missing or wrong-epoch samples, while proof-side finite representation recovers the **exact** accepted endpoint using full row-to-source equality. Successful reconstruction alone is guard-free: an illegal immediate completion can be reconstructed. Equal epochs or projected delivery events are weaker than authentic full policy inputs.

The positive operational result fixes one public ordered program **before** every matching hidden environment and permitted finite service decoration. In the specified empty epoch-zero ABC fixture, seven holds followed by 21 trials yield exactly 259 logical events, one installation and zero repairs, with the exact installed Plant. The 20-trial, 247-event prefix has no effect. This is an actual accepted trace of emitted events, not inferred from synthetic success flags; missing/excess annotations and malformed reply-array lengths reject.

The [original result and execution records][t09-controller-guide] distinguish fresh kernel components from compiled finite assertions. This entry point explicitly rejects even a valid epoch-one repair. Public-program independence does not imply transcript opacity, and the ABC installation is not the earlier 3,013-byte repair example. Arbitrary reachable-idle entry, unknown function-state serialisation, physical storage/time, arbitrary asynchronous fairness and standalone linked-C deployment are outside the supplied endpoint.

### Bijective whole-history transport is not in-place permission

The [transport reading guide][t09-transport-read], [selected-order involution][t09-transport] and [ordered-trace equivalence][t09-trace] give a separate mathematical result. For each chosen support, swap the old order with one coherently supplied duplicate-free order. Other orders and all non-order command fields survive. Transport every commitment, tombstone, receipt and corruption-payload key consistently. The [scoped proof and review route][t09-transport-evidence] does not authorise physical in-place conversion, signature reminting or old-key reuse. Counterstates carry their explicit reachability conditions. Raw native-list equality, arbitrary interface invariance, public-program composition, an executable serializer and actual authority remain separate questions. The isomorphism explains what must change together; it does not grant permission to make those changes.

## Remaining formal continuation developments and source ownership

### Concentration for the actual seeded raw tapes

[Read the retained statement](../../docs/provenance/v5-research-continuations/notes/211febf4a1cf053563e4cefd1adc21a4778932bc6e39c9c414c09c9020e86853.md). For finite action and symbol alphabets, normalised nonnegative row laws and an independent probability seed, the proof starts with the actual product-tape law—not an assumed observation process. It bounds the event that some action/symbol empirical frequency at some deterministic prefix length `n ≥ N` deviates by at least `η`. For `0 < η ≤ 1` and `N ≥ 1`, its bound is

```text
|A| |Y| · 2 exp(−N η²/2)
────────────────────────
      1 − exp(−η²/2)
```

The [raw-prefix declaration](../../experiments/orthemology-v5-continuations/source-store/82b3e06719e80fe4e1f0a77fd72e157bfc30ef3b42992b3e6a5587b859404e3b.lean) passes through the exact finite-word pushforward before the countable cutoff union. The [unrestricted-threshold component](../../experiments/orthemology-v5-continuations/source-store/29942f882e2e223ddabd7bf19d1b4dbf5deb0ec3b7287c1868c315d505d46182.lean) uses `min(η,1)²/2` for arbitrary positive tolerance. This is a useful, explicitly non-sharp exponent; it is not advertised as sharp Hoeffding. Adaptive acquired-row counts do not become deterministic-prefix samples merely because their names resemble the raw indices. The source's [recorded concentration review](../../docs/provenance/v5-research-continuations/notes/1dd73154483e48f304d705067f869caad6d4cdca78ebb417a4261650f50cb6dd.md) remains separate from both the subsequent stopped-cost construction and a fresh replay.

### What an affine tail does and does not imply

[Read the retained statement](../../docs/provenance/v5-research-continuations/notes/2d7ec3409db14cb075da9d60a8c0ebdcd564de32f3994a5e227fffacb31d4a42.md) and [formal tail-to-moment declarations](../../experiments/orthemology-v5-continuations/source-store/7f58b3c0270925b452c97ebeaaaa9383f7958f2c0c0b4fc6b578a7cde01ef4f7.lean). For a measurable extended-nonnegative cost `C`, a **supplied** tail `Pr(C > B + Vu) ≤ exp(−u)` for all `u ≥ 0`, with `V > 0`, yields finite expectation, almost-sure finiteness, positive exponential moments for `0 ≤ θ < 1/V`, and all natural-power moments. The actual coBüchi-count instantiation transports those conclusions to its named cost variable. It does not erase the tail hypothesis.

The proof first controls the extended integral; conversion to a real-valued cost occurs only after almost-sure finiteness has been established. Its displayed integer-envelope expectation estimate is deliberately conservative, not a claim of the sharp `B + V` bound. Boundary controls protect the open exponential range. The [existing review](../../docs/provenance/v5-research-continuations/notes/58500a16b7290bf5631b347fc94be113ebd7ec240e2169001761078f7392100c.md) and the later [actual-controller cost chain](#the-actual-generated-controller-has-finite-and-exponential-error-budgets) concern different steps: proving an actual tail is additional work, not a consequence of naming the abstract moment theorem.

### Recursive support and the same physical policy law

The [original recursive-equivalence declarations](../../experiments/orthemology-v5-continuations/source-store/fdd271bd99c4a7e1e79ddeba278e809ba9319936438fd4edb7c72e1bdecc426e.lean) connect three existence claims in one finite controlled-iid interface: an independently defined recursive support certificate, one lawful history policy with almost-sure eventual target goodness, and existence of one with finite expected total bad-action count. The common probability seed and policy are selected **before** quantifying the live hidden model. Fixed kernels may have zeros; the starting support must be nonempty. Public permission menus depend on current support, while target goodness is a different condition.

The [canonical sufficiency account](../../docs/provenance/v5-research-continuations/notes/a90fd14b7987f39e21e16116ee532abc16b396f58f42b90523a6934646c91815.md) explains the positive implementation: finite action trees, first-exit truncation, residual-tree replay and a normalised potential pay the current physical action in the original canonical law. The [same-law necessity/equivalence review](../../docs/provenance/v5-research-continuations/notes/0bba51eceac85ed0c16234042f29f9f8167857f5db4f51009ba0a941b3e44748.md) explains common-seed selection, finite-query transfer, patching unused singular rows and the requirement to handle **all** feasible strict-support successors. A favourable single branch is not enough. Safety is asserted on feasible, action-compatible histories, not forged transcripts or impossible empty supports.

Finite expectation is an **existence equivalence**, not a property of every almost-sure winner. A different constructive policy can supply the finite budget. Good actions may continue gathering information forever. The theorem neither authenticates the public menu's normative authority nor implements every continuum scored-repair problem. Its exact formal sources and recorded review remain available without treating this reader curation as another kernel replay.

### Literal indexed observers and their scheduled runtime

[Read the literal-source invariant](../../experiments/orthemology-v5-continuations/source-store/98c1923c5c9ef77094ca28a63c32866fae56ae04b7a1597da59874935d9b1167.lean) and [scheduled-runtime equations](../../experiments/orthemology-v5-continuations/source-store/73b3cb0b813ff303a6735c3cf8ac3c8dbbc1ed5c5dd335d4e0c239b8ddb23cc3.lean). The observer state is exactly `(stage, acquired sentinel word)`, starting from `(0,1)`. A transition appends the current input bit and the output uses the preserved numeric-index evaluator at the correct stage. The schedule computes a sufficient finite stack-fuel budget for each code and finite horizon; at tick `k` it uses horizon `k+1`. Consequently the whole runtime output stream, and then its fair-input law, equal the literal indexed source semantics.

The [retained source/runtime review](../../docs/provenance/v5-research-continuations/notes/9e774f1b1e3366ca616619923df8a934b894bcb3886a69bab1cae1eca8298994.md) distinguishes malformed syntax or wrong arity, whose specified semantics defaults to zero, from an unfinished valid evaluation, which returns `none`. Default zero is not proof that the intended source ran. The schedule proves successful ticks before applying an optional-value projection; its fallback is not silently treated as a bit. A local simulation of the concrete splitter yields exact nonatomic defect `1/2`, rather than an estimate from finitely many outputs.

The [earlier observer-core review](../../docs/provenance/v5-research-continuations/notes/8063ee424925df31d7bd8f6efb1f7d22c007fbb4e7723a66cd04902097b08550.md) keeps local synchronous and guarded simulation distinct from the later actual language implementation. No universal finite abstraction, single fuel bound for an infinite run, practical CPU bound, host/Python refinement or compiler for every P01 term follows. This is a source-owned research-software development with mathematical proof components, not a separate paper for each imported module.
