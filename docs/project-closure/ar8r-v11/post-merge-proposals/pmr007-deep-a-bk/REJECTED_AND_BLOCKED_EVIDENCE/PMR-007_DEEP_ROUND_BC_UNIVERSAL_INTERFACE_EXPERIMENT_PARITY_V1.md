## 1. Frozen candidate-independent experiment

Let `H={A,R}` be the personal architecture and its strongest matched impersonal
rival.  A finite experiment is a Markov kernel

\[
E:H\rightsquigarrow Z
\]

from architecture to a complete declared signal or transcript.  `E` includes
all observations and interventions that are actually part of the frozen common
protocol.  Source labels, target keys, priors, coding choices, and world-link
assumptions are not smuggled into `Z` unless independently warranted and applied
to both candidates.

A downstream representation, learned encoder, statistic, report, or randomized
post-processing is another common kernel

\[
K:Z\rightsquigarrow Y.
\]

## 2. UIEP-1A — garbling dominance

If

\[
E_2=E_1G
\]

for a common Markov kernel `G`, then every finite expected-reward decision rule
available after `E_2` can be simulated after `E_1`.  Therefore `E_1` has at
least as large an optimal expected value as `E_2` for every common prior,
action set, and bounded payoff function.

### Proof

Given a rule `d_2` after `E_2`, compose `G` with `d_2` to obtain a rule after
`E_1`.  The induced joint distribution of architecture, action, and payoff is
identical.  Optimization over all `E_1` rules can only do better. ∎

If `E_1` and `E_2` are mutual garblings, they have the same optimal value in
every finite expected-reward decision problem.  This is decision equivalence,
not identity of signals, representations, mechanisms, or meanings.

## 3. UIEP-1B — universal downstream noncreation

For every common downstream kernel `K`,

\[
E_A=E_R
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
E_AK=E_RK.
\]

More generally, mutual Blackwell equivalence is preserved under common
post-processing.  No downstream classifier, learned representation, language
model, crosswalk, profile, quotient, or decision procedure can manufacture an
architecture discriminator absent from the frozen experiment.

This subsumes the relevant consequence of Deep AO's data-processing result and
Deep AT's controlled trace equivalence at the architecture-comparison level.

## 4. UIEP-1C — controlled-system interface corollary

Suppose the personal and impersonal candidates have:

```text
- isomorphic Deep-BA canonical interface quotients;
- the same initial distribution under the isomorphism;
- the same action-conditioned transition kernel;
- the same declared observation/target/source/version output law;
- and one candidate-independent policy/intervention protocol.
```

Then the induced complete finite transcript experiments are identical.  Every
finite downstream expected-reward diagnosis or architecture decision has equal
value for the two candidates.

A different internal tag—`Personal`, `Intentional`, `Wise`, `IntrinsicMind`—does
not become evidence unless the common experiment contains an independently
motivated observation or intervention whose law differs.

## 5. Exact burden relocation

A neutral personal-ground advantage now requires at least one of:

```text
NEW_EXPERIMENT:
  a candidate-independent observation or intervention with different induced
  laws;

NEW_SOURCE_WORLD_LINK:
  independently warranted source reliability, referent, and actual-world
  selection that changes the admissible model class;

NEW_PRIOR_OR_CODING_DEFENSE:
  an independently defended prior/hypothesis language, explicitly classified
  as prior evidence rather than experiment output;

MODEL_CLASS_RESTRICTION:
  a non-question-begging metaphysical, causal, or semantic principle that
  excludes the impersonal expansion before observing the desired conclusion.
```

Search over downstream representations alone cannot discharge this burden.

## 6. Controls

```text
BC-CM1 SIGNAL PERMUTATION:
  mutually garbled experiments differ only by signal names; decision value is
  equal and literal meaning is not fixed.

BC-CM2 LOSS-DEPENDENT ADVANTAGE:
  one experiment may help a selected task but not establish universal
  dominance; the decision problem must be frozen before inspection.

BC-CM3 DIFFERENT INITIAL DISTRIBUTION:
  quotient isomorphism without matched initial law can remain distinguishable.

BC-CM4 INTERVENTION EXPANSION:
  observational parity is broken by one common independently motivated
  intervention.  This is legitimate new evidence, not post-processing.

BC-CM5 CANDIDATE-DEPENDENT QUERY:
  different interventions or encodings for A and R manufacture apparent
  discrimination and violate the common-experiment contract.

BC-CM6 SOURCE LABEL LEAKAGE:
  inserting `personal` or a preferred source-role label into `Z` restates the
  target.

BC-CM7 PRIOR REVERSAL:
  equal experiments leave posterior odds equal to prior odds; a preferred prior
  can reverse the winner and must be defended separately.

BC-CM8 MUTUAL GARBLING NONIDENTITY:
  decision-equivalent experiments can have different signal alphabets,
  geometries, encodings, carriers, and internal mechanisms.

BC-CM9 PERSONAL/IMPERSONAL TWIN:
  both candidates share every neutral experiment and downstream decision value
  while differing on de se ownership and Wisdom.

BC-CM10 SOURCE-RELATIVE FILTER:
  Track N can restrict the source-expanded class; this is source-relative
  evidence, not a neutral experiment consequence.
```

## 9. Nonclaims

The theorem does not show that the current experiment is complete, that the
candidates are metaphysically equally likely or possible, that expected utility
is the only rational decision model, or that no future source, intervention,
phenomenological, semantic, or metaphysical evidence can discriminate them.
It establishes only that common downstream processing cannot create evidence
absent from the frozen experiment.
