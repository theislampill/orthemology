## Finding

The strongest law-only rival survives the proposed absence diagnostic: an actually obtaining, genuinely explanatory, autonomous law may exclude universal absence and explain the relevant actuality without being an additional objectual producer. Its failure has not been established by noting that it is a law, or by deleting its existential force. Conversely, a uniqueness theorem alone does not warrant that ontology. The unresolved issue is the justified explanatory and ontological status of the full law, including its existential force and actual holding.

The strongest correction to an overly negative reading is therefore substantive: distinguish “not earned by this mathematical criterion” from “not a genuine possible explanation.” The strongest correction to an overly positive reading is equally substantive: distinguish a law-admissible pattern's unique specification from warranted actual explanatory coverage of the target, including concrete capacities and the laws themselves.

## 1. Strongest counterinterpretation, preserving the candidate's laws

Consider the following rival as a hypothesis, not an established description of actuality:

1. A nonobjectual law L actually obtains and has genuine explanatory status.
2. L is not merely an implication with unprovided antecedents: its full content excludes a world with no target instances, or supplies an instance without a prior target instance.
3. L and a locally explanatory non-wellfounded structure determine the relevant complete target history.
4. L's obtaining is autonomous, or otherwise receives an adequate explanation that does not require an original concrete bearer.

If these claims are warranted, asking “but what actual object makes the law work?” already assumes the objectual bridge under dispute. Likewise, changing L to an absence-preserving rule does not refute this rival. It evaluates a weaker hypothesis.

Calling an existence-forcing clause a “new premise” is appropriate relative to a bare contraction theorem or an absence-preserving recurrence. It is misleading if used to suggest that the source's full candidate never contained that clause. The clause's explanatory credentials remain assessable without deleting it.

There is a fair symmetry requirement: do not permit autonomy for an original bearer solely by stipulation while demanding a further bearer behind every autonomous law. Conversely, saying that a law is necessary or autonomous does not by itself establish its explanatory credentials. Both proposed stopping points need substantive support at the level of the actual target.

This is not a proof that the rival succeeds. It locates what could make it succeed and blocks a question-begging shortcut against it.

## 2. Keep five different targets separate

- **Target existence:** why at least one relevant instance exists.
- **Target specification:** why that instance has this value or form.
- **Local actual provision:** which actual capacities or resources contribute to each particular effect.
- **Global determination:** whether the laws leave more than one admissible target history.
- **Ultimate coverage:** whether the laws' actual holding and relevant background require further explanation.

A result about one target does not silently settle the others. The law-only rival is serious precisely because it proposes to supply existential and explanatory content, not merely consistency. But that proposed content must be assessed on its merits.

In particular, book creation without prior books can still depend on actual scribes, their capacities, and the relevant stage structure. It does not establish that every concrete capacity is received, or that no original bearer supplies production. An explanation complete for books need not be complete for scribes-plus-books-plus-laws.

Nor does “a common explanatory basis” automatically mean “a common objectual producer.” If common source is broad enough to include a nonobjectual autonomous basis, the rival may instantiate that broad thesis while contesting a stronger bearer conclusion. The disputed target must be fixed before counting the view as either a defeat or a confirmation.

## 3. Correct domains and the sound mathematical diagnostic

For a uniform mathematical transport, specify:

- a value space V;
- a total self-map f: V → V;
- a nonempty class Ω of law-admissible worlds or cases;
- state-value functions X_i: Ω → V satisfying X_i = f ∘ X_(i+1);
- whether V represents existence, a property conditional on existence, or an entire relevant state;
- the actual explanatory interpretation of the local links, separately from the functional equation.

The variables X_i are not thereby objects, productive resources, or bearers. A value identifying a book's content is not automatically a value identifying the book's concrete production capacity. Constancy across Ω is only as informative as Ω: if its construction fixes the target value independently, the determination result does not discover it.

### Sufficient singleton-image result

Let Π = intersection over n ≥ 1 of f^n(V). Every value in any coherent infinite backward history lies in Π. Therefore Π = {e} forces every coordinate of every admissible history to equal e. For a self-map, f(Π) is contained in Π, so a singleton Π supplies a fixed point e and its constant history.

This is a specification/existence result inside the mathematical model. It does not by itself establish that the modeled law actually obtains or that its links are explanatory.

For the familiar contraction route, retain the actual technical assumptions: a nonempty complete metric value space, a self-map with a strict Lipschitz constant, and the requisite boundedness (a bounded domain or suitably bounded image). Do not infer a model's nonempty domain from observed actuality and then count that stipulation as a newly explained existential fact.

### Absence-preserving obstruction

Suppose ⊥ is a legitimate value in V and f(⊥) = ⊥. Then f^n(⊥) = ⊥ for every n, hence ⊥ belongs to Π. Consequently Π cannot be the singleton {e} with e ≠ ⊥. Indeed, the all-absent history itself satisfies the recurrence.

This proves the proposed narrow discrimination. It does not prove that every genuine law must preserve absence. When the candidate's full L excludes the all-absent case or makes f(⊥) nonabsent, the premises of this obstruction fail.

### Do not extend domains for free

On the usual nonempty compact-set domain, a geometric contraction need not include the empty set as an ordinary finite-distance point. Extending the domain to include absence requires checking the map and metric again. If an extension fixes both absence and a nonempty attractor, it cannot remain a strict contraction under any ordinary finite metric separating those two values: their positive distance d would have to satisfy d ≤ c d with c < 1. This is a domain-change test, not a rebuttal of a candidate that explicitly rules absence out.

### Limited guardrail about a general iff

Do not export a blanket equivalence between singleton Π and uniqueness of coherent infinite histories without checking the complete Appendix A and its intended admissibility conditions. Arbitrarily deep finite predecessor paths need not assemble into one infinite path. A bounded discrete example can contain a unique fixed-point history plus an infinite forward trunk fed by finite branches of every length; trunk points survive every finite image although none has an infinite coherent prehistory.

This is a transport guardrail requiring full-text/domain verification, not a finding that the published paper is erroneous. It does not affect the sufficient singleton result, the contraction application under its assumptions, or the absence obstruction used above. No fresh countermodel is needed for the current report's main conclusion.

Φ(A) = {G} ∪ {t_n : G ∈ A and t_(n+1) ∈ A}.

Both {G} and {G} ∪ {t_n : n is natural} are fixed points. Thus the rules alone do not uniquely select the supplied actuality set. The source code selects the all-actual interpretation explicitly.

But this does not erase actual original contribution in that selected interpretation. The relevant theorem supplies a strict productive edge from the same original G to every token, together with an actual productive predecessor for each token. It is not a dormant-power or merely co-present-source example.

Accordingly:

- full global-history uniqueness is not necessary for original-stock contribution;

## 5. Exact unresolved transport and fair discriminators

To move from the mathematical result to an actuality-explaining law-only rival, establish the following separately:

1. **Target match:** the explained values include the existence and concrete capacities relevant to T20, rather than just shape, content, or conditional states.
2. **Full-law fidelity:** retain every actual candidate-law clause and background commitment. Identify which part excludes absence and why it is lawlike rather than a disguised restatement of the target.
3. **Explanatory warrant:** justify the local explanatory links and any law-only explanatory step. Derivation, necessitation, and structural uniqueness are not themselves certificates of production or grounding.
4. **Ultimate status:** justify autonomy or explain L's holding. An open proposal for law/meta-law regress is not already a demonstrated supercomplete package; nor is such a regress defeated merely by its being infinite.
5. **Ontology:** assess whether actual explanatory efficacy can be nonobjectual, without building the answer into a bearer axiom or an anti-law abstraction assumption.
6. **Comparative support:** show why this candidate fits the actual target at least as well as the live original/common-source account. Formal coherence alone does not decide a defeasible comparative appraisal.

There is also a modal-strength fork. If L is metaphysically necessary, Ω covers all metaphysically possible worlds, and the entire history is uniquely fixed by L, that full history is necessary too. If the result is merely relative to fixed contingent laws or a near-world domain, it establishes less. “Autonomous” and “metaphysically necessary” must not be interchanged. Whether this matters to T20 depends on its exact use of contingency, and cannot be settled by relabeling a law-relative result as absolute necessity.
