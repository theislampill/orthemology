# Joint manifestation: ontological credit and the original-agency boundary

9 October 2026 UTC. A bounded T20 source comparison. No protected-repository change, integration, closure, full rival-world certification, or research-duration claim.

## Result

There is a developed and independently motivated kind of causal ontology behind joint manifestation. It need not be an assigned two-input response law. The single new primary essay examined here earns that limited philosophical credit. It does **not** independently authenticate the retained rival's complete original-agency interpretation.

The decisive new discrimination is between **one causal episode containing distinct actual activities** and **one wholly created external product jointly originated by distinct uncreated agents**. The first does not supply the second by identity or relabelling. Nor does the actual adequacy of a power for its situated activity establish an agent's complete original adequacy for the undertaking's whole productive basis.

The result therefore strengthens the ontological seriousness of the general alternative while leaving the comparative verdict unchanged: the common-source argument retains positive, defeasible warrant; the full harmonious-original rival has not acquired equal warrant. The remaining reservation about universal application of the individual-original-adequacy norm is retained. It is not converted into a completed objection or a newly discovered contradiction.

## 1. Screening and the selected source

Before selecting a new source, I inspected the current Oneness return, the strongest-rival audit, nonabsorption, necessary-concordance, the inherited complete-efficacy report, and the Eleventh Rea comparison. The Rea comparison already distinguishes genuine constitutive power from original co-possession; it does not warrant original co-ownership. Repeating it would not advance this question.

Searches of the inspected T20 and inherited checkpoint records found no earlier developed Martin/Heil mutual-manifestation comparison. This is a bounded records result, not a claim about every historical archive. A short public screen considered Martin/Heil-related candidates. No full new Heil or Martin article was read, and snippets from critics were not used as evidence for their complete positions.

The one selected primary essay is Anna Marmodoro, *Aristotelian powers at work: reciprocity without symmetry in causation*. The [Oxford-hosted accepted manuscript](https://media.philosophy.ox.ac.uk/assets/pdf_file/0003/40188/Powers_at_work_18_09_13.pdf) explicitly states acceptance in September 2013. [Oxford repository metadata](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid%3A6b943a51-e410-4ed6-920d-713dd8b485f6) identifies the later chapter in Jonathan D. Jacobs, ed., *Causal Powers* (2017), pp. 57–76, DOI 10.1093/oso/9780198796572.003.0005. All locators below use the 21-page manuscript, not presumed final-publication pagination. The accepted text is primary evidence for Marmodoro's proposal; its quotations and interpretations of Martin, Heil, and Aristotle do not constitute separate primary readings of those authors.

This source was selected because it explicitly addresses activity individuation, common event unity, and the location of each participant's efficacy. Those are closer to the residual than an assertion that ordinary collaborators possess real powers. Its developed disagreement with a single-manifestation reading also prevents treating all powers accounts as one interchangeable doctrine.

All pages were read continuously in four sequential displays. Reflective returns checked pp. 3, 7–8, 12, 16, and 18. Pages 3 and 16 were also visually inspected. The extraction and images agree at the controlling distinctions. No complete visual traversal or verification of the essay's scientific illustrations is claimed.

## 3. What has now earned support beyond an assigned law?

### Numerical unity: one event is a determinate proposal, but the target matters

M2 supplies a substantive candidate for numerical event unity. This is stronger than deciding that equal output values will share a name. Yet it does not say that everything occurring in the event is numerically one act, one power, or one effect in every explanatory respect.

For T20, three targets must stay apart:

1. The joint causal episode involving the participants.
2. Each participant's proper activity within that episode.
3. The external created result for whose complete original basis the rival claims to account.

Unity at target 1 does not collapse target 2. Nor does it establish unity or exhaustive coverage at target 3. This is not a numerical-event-key objection: it asks what real occurrence the key denotes.

### Distinct powers and activities: real individuation, not yet original personal ownership

M1–M2 permit an interpretation in which distinct efficacy belongs to distinct powers, even though an episode is shared. That is genuine content for the general challenge to an automatic inference from common activity to one producer. It is neither a mere difference in names nor an attribution of two exclusive entire productions.

But T20's original-bearer interpretation adds more. It requires distinct subjects whose proper acts are genuinely theirs, unreceived standing faculties, and eligibility as complete original agents. A claim that an activity is the actuality of power p rather than q does not alone establish all those subject-level claims. This is an application boundary, not a denial that nonhuman or impersonal powers can be real causes.

The Eleventh co-ownership issue is consequently not reopened by sleight of hand. Distinct own activities within one event are not two distinct subjects' original co-ownership of one numerically identical proper act.

### Irreducible jointness: an exercise-level account is available

M1–M5 offer reasons to take the relevant actuality as constituted by mutually involved activities, rather than infer it from a response table. One can recognize those reasons without demanding a mechanical reduction of causal power. A primitive ontology earns support by doing explanatory and individuating work; not every primitive needs to be translated into more basic mechanics.

There is nevertheless a limit to the available credit. A common episode with multiple manifestations is not yet the retained rival's stronger claim of an external manifestation that absorbs no independently real created work when another unchanged original acts. The new reading gives no source-faithful solo/joint comparison preserving the rival's complete created inventory, full particular will, and appropriate actual efficacy. It therefore supplies no fresh nonabsorption counterexample. The inherited fibre and trace results remain exactly where they were.

### What constitutes each participant's actual efficacy?

A defensible interpretation suggested by M1 is that a participant is actually efficacious when its power is doing its characteristic work in the relevant partnered circumstances. Its actuality is not merely a forecast, a dormant repertoire, or a citation in an explanation. This answers the thin objection that contextual manifestation necessarily leaves every participant idle.

That answer also exposes the price of the interpretation. Role-relative actuality is not independent exhaustive origin. If B's proper activity is indispensable to the actual basis, then A's completed achievement still includes an original contribution A does not supply, under the rival's own declared attribution. Describing A's activity as genuinely actual does not decide whether this dependence is fitting for a perfected original. The source's positive actual-adequacy argument addresses exactly that further question.

## 4. The structure-preserving transfer test

No new formal model is needed. There are concrete individuation and typing questions to resolve before any numerical or modal exercise would be useful. The restriction is not that the examples happen to be physical or created. A general causal account can extend beyond its motivating examples. The question is whether the proposed extension preserves the account's actual explanatory structure while adding warranted original-agent content.

### Requirements that can be preserved, and those needing an extension

**Distinct manifestations:** these can be preserved as A's and B's distinct actual activities. They cannot simultaneously be treated as one numerically identical proper act merely because the surrounding episode is one. The retained rival need not make that mistaken identification.

**Ownership:** distinct power-indexed activities can survive the mapping. Assigning them to personal original bearers is an additional interpretation, not intrinsically a contradiction. The further claims of unreceived possession and complete original eligibility require warrant at their own scope.

**Alteration:** no generic anti-change objection is available. M1 blocks the claim that joint activation must always destroy or replace a participant's standing power. Moreover, the inherited original-source corpus permits successive proper acts. If a proposed extension specifically makes A's standing power efficiently received from B, that violates the relevant nonreceipt claim; mere transition into a particular activity does not establish such receipt.

**Partner-conditioned exercise:** this is the exact pressure on unchanged-act transport. If B's activity is constitutive of the partnered exercise through which A is actually efficacious, suppressing B cannot preserve that same *complete partnered exercise*. Retaining A's input, faculty, or intrinsic act record is insufficient to show otherwise. This conditional point is not a claim that suppressing B is metaphysically admissible for necessarily concordant originals.

There are two legitimate replies, with different costs. The rival can keep the same intrinsic act while allowing its complete exercise-description to differ between solo and joint contexts. That preserves its intrinsic-token thesis, but leaves the disputed actual-adequacy norm unanswered. Alternatively, it can take A's own manifestation to depend on a created recipient C rather than on original B, adding B as another contributor to a larger episode. This avoids the simple two-partner dependence test, but now needs a developed account of how those activities jointly constitute or produce the one target. A many-partner extension is not ruled out; it is not already established by naming the episode.

**Causal event versus effect:** this distinction requires the additional examination below. None of the preceding differences entails another created token or a hidden mixer by itself.

### Transfer A: identify the entire shared episode with the created effect

Suppose the proposed mapping takes the whole joint episode to be the rival's one wholly created result. Suppose also that it preserves the rival's attribution of the original participants' intrinsic acts as uncreated.

Then the mapping must explain how the episode includes those uncreated activities while being identical with a wholly created effect. A mixed episode containing original activity is not automatically a wholly created item. This is not proof that such episodes cannot exist. It is a reason the proposed identity does not follow from event unity alone.

The rival may instead distinguish a jointly enacted episode from an external product of that episode. That is a legitimate move. But then the episode's numerical unity no longer supplies, by identity, the product's numerical unity or complete productive ontology. A further product relation has been introduced and needs its own interpretation. No hidden created mixer is inferred simply because that explanation has not yet been given.

### Transfer B: map one participant to a created recipient

This can preserve a genuine reciprocal exercise without implying a second original. If A originally provides the relevant recipient and its powers, the mapping fits the source's allowance for created means. It cannot serve as evidence for the rival's independent-original peer merely because two real causal participants remain.

Created receptivity and independently original coprovision are different original-accounting claims. The existing source corpus already allows the former. Counting participants cannot resolve the latter.

### Transfer C: retain two original partners and their complete acts

This keeps the rival's important plural-original claim, but it must now justify why both subjects qualify at the complete original scope. A source of physically conditional role-adequacy cannot simply be promoted into a source of unrestricted original perfection. There is no automatic inference from a metaphysical bedrock within physical explanation to uncreated, necessary, comprehensively adequate personal agents.

Nor does necessary partnership settle this by itself. The implication from A's activity to B's activity is not an efficient explanation in which B manufactures the capacity A must use to manufacture B's capacity. The inherited vicious-circle objection may therefore not be applied without that additional premise. Conversely, removing a donation edge does not remove actual joint productive dependence.

Finally, preservation of complete wise determination is a separate demand from conditional physical necessitation. A mapping that retains only enough circumstances for a physical manifestation has not thereby preserved original wisdom, full practical basing, or all admissible determinations. It would be unfair to require disclosure of every hidden wise reason; it would be equally unfair to assume the disputed original-peer mode's perfection on that basis.

## 5. Does the efficacy classification become clearer?

Yes, but the dispute is not settled. The competing claims can now be stated without using an opaque perfection label:

- The rival's best claim is that A's proper activity is already fully actual and appropriate to its chosen role; B's participation helps constitute a shared episode rather than repairing a missing faculty in A.
- The source's best reply is that the relevant excellence concerns A's achieved original undertaking, including all indispensable productive resources, rather than merely A's successful performance of a participant-role.

The first can be intelligible without the second being false. The selected ontology supports the intelligibility of situated activity; it does not establish the normative equality of situated original participation and comprehensive original provision.

This result also constrains both easy dismissals:

1. **No additional intrinsic property is received, so no perfection is externally completed.** Invalid. Actual relational efficacy can be the perfection at issue. The inherited complete-efficacy reading gives direct source support for that broader scope.
2. **A partner is required, so A receives its very power from B.** Invalid without further evidence. Dependence in exercise and efficient donation of the standing power are different.
3. **There is one event, so there is one proper act or one original subject.** Invalid. The numerical target must stay fixed.
4. **Both are fully active, so both are fully perfected originals.** Invalid. Actuality of activity is not a certificate of unrestricted original eligibility.
5. **There are distinct activities, so there must be two additive created fragments.** Invalid without the creation-status, individuation, and retention premises. Distinct original acts do not automatically count as separately created traces.

The fifth control is particularly important. The new account must not be used to smuggle a per-root created-token ontology into the source's nonabsorption argument. Equally, the first control prevents its real-act account from bypassing that argument's stronger actual-adequacy norm.

## 6. Comparative judgment and stopping point

The new positive credit is **ontological motivation for a joint episode with genuinely differentiated actual efficacy**. This makes the general family more substantive than a stipulated response rule. It does not supply all the retained rival's commitments as a package, and it is not new proof that created collaborators have real powers.

The new limiting result is **lack of an established direct structure-preserving promotion** from that ontology to the rival's single created output and complete original participants. Preserving the shared episode, the distinct intrinsic acts, the created/uncreated distinction, and the whole-original target together requires more than the selected account establishes. This is a failed evidential transfer, not an impossibility theorem about every possible extension.

Consequently:

- The general joint-manifestation objection deserves genuine philosophical consideration.
- The actual retained rival still assigns joint original support without independent warrant for its full original-perfection interpretation.
- The source's reasons concerning achieved original adequacy, comprehensive maker-preserver explanation, and pure-perfection comparison retain their differential evidential force.
- The universal application question remains open at its precise scope. The selected article neither proves the strong norm from thinner premises nor defeats it with a warranted original case.
- Universal supremacy, original-peer value, necessary perfect concordance, and action-guiding de se recognition acquire no new independent warrant from this operation.

There is no decision-relevant reason, within this bounded operation, to add another response table, rerun inherited mathematics, or start a broader reading campaign. A later extension would be useful only if it defended the actual original-agency promotion identified above, or supplied a substantive argument about why individual entire-origin adequacy is or is not the appropriate perfection. Merely collecting more ordinary examples of reciprocal causation would repeat what is already granted.
