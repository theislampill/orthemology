## 1 A causal argument that survives the concession of indeterminism

The principal interpretive test compares the earlier general defence with the fallback at61–65. This is a concession within a larger argument which normally favours determinism. It should not be rewritten either as Ameri endorsing quantum indeterminism or as his being unable to reason without deterministic sufficient causes.

The strongest useful reconstruction is conditional:

1. Suppose a proposed event occurs only in an obtaining physical background with the relevant lawful capacities.
2. Suppose that background need not determine whether this particular event occurs.
3. The event's occurrence nevertheless entails that its required background is actual.
4. Therefore the case is not an example of occurrence with no enabling reality whatsoever, and cannot establish that stronger claim merely by being indeterministic.

The force of this reply is not exhausted by pointing out a linguistic difference between physical vacuum and absolute absence. It identifies what the proposed explanation still **uses**. A theory can leave the particular outcome nonnecessitated while presupposing an operative field, states, powers, or circumstances. The state of explanation is then importantly different from one in which there is no actual productive or enabling basis.

The argument also places a fair constraint on the original-source discussion. An explanatory account cannot introduce an actual field to obtain an effect and then count the field as absent from what must be accounted for. If that field's existence is itself received, its use does not by itself complete an account of all received actuality. This is the relevant positive pressure toward whole-account adequacy. It does not require rejecting probabilistic local explanations as explanations.

Three restrictions keep that pressure honest:

- The conditional alone does not show that every indeterministic model has exactly the background Ameri describes. No primary physics assessment of virtual particles, Copenhagen interpretations, Bell results, vacuum fluctuations, or cosmogenesis was performed here.
- Existential or nomological requirement alone does not identify an efficient contributor. For example, the mere truth of an equation is not already a concrete exercise of a power. What the theory actually supplies must be identified.
- The question why a particular event occurs, the question why its enabling background exists, and the question whether the complete received field has original resources are different explanatory targets. Progress on one should not be silently counted as completion of all three.

Thus this is a successful conditional rebuttal of a proposed inference, not a verified empirical counterexample to a physical theory and not a new global causal axiom.

## 2 Exact comparison with the unchanged interface

The entire existing `OriginalBearerBridge.lean` was inspected. No new formalization or kernel run was needed. Its explanatory vocabulary is interpreted in the inherited source-faithful sense; the code itself does not certify that interpretation.

### Occurrence and relevance

`targetReceived t` is not merely “a token appears.” A quantum transition, an ordinary creature's origination, and an intrinsic divine act-token cannot be identified through a single occurrence word. The source's physical examples may motivate an ordinary dependence question, but do not establish an unrestricted Emergent-implies-Created principle.

If an actual event requires backgroundB, B is actual. To use it as `r`, one must additionally identify a concrete resource token and explain its relevance to the received target in the same existential respect. A field-wide condition, a law-proposition, and an intrinsic instantiation of productive capacity are not interchangeable candidates. Several may have legitimate roles, but the roles need an argument.

### Completion

The definition is: for every received target, some relevant resource is actual and not received. It is not: for every target, one resource deterministically suffices to produce it. Neither a temporal-first-cause requirement nor exclusive entire attribution is built into that definition.

Consequently, **lack of necessitation is not the decisive obstruction to mapping Ameri's fallback into Completion**. A genuinely original probabilistic productive resource would not be excluded by the displayed formula merely for being probabilistic. Conversely, an actual necessary condition can be wholly derivative. Nothing in the necessity-of-background inference establishes its nonreceipt.

There are two possible next steps, with different ownership. If independent evidence establishes that this background contains an unreceived relevant resource, the bridge may be instantiated. If instead one invokes the impossibility of a wholly received complete productive field, the earlier productive-totality rationale is doing the work. The new quantum example may supply a local target and expose an omitted prerequisite; it is not another independent proof of that global rationale.

### Realisation

Realisation supplies an actual bearer in which an actual resource is intrinsic. Ameri's example does not prove that every entity mentioned in a successful physical explanation has that form. A concrete system's instantiated capacity is a plausible kind of candidate; an abstract mathematical law is not automatically such a bearer-resource pair. The conditional result cannot settle the ontology by relabeling either one.

Nor does the need for a bearer entail a separate productive cause of its intrinsic attribute. The current bridge expressly avoids that edge. Attribute reality, intrinsic instantiation, external assembly, and independent subsistence must remain distinct.

### SupportTransport

SupportTransport says that whole-existence reception of an actual bearer entails reception of its intrinsic resource in the stipulated same respect. A local event's need for its quantum background establishes no such generic transport. It also supplies no contrary instance. Whether the proposed capacity is inseparable from, received with, or merely accompanied by its bearer belongs to a distinct constitutive interpretation.

### The two downstream questions

Even after Completion, Realisation, and SupportTransport yield an original bearer, **ContingencyNeed** remains the separate premise connecting actual nonreceipt with the intended necessity conclusion. Existence of a required background supplies no shortcut to it. The thin-negative and thick-positive senses of through-self remain exactly as distinguished in `direct-anti-brute-return/REPORT.md`.

Similarly, the actual-original-production residual asks about two allegedly complete unreceived productive relations to one effect and respect. Nothing about the existence of a required background determines whether original entire production is nonduplicable. Making the local event deterministic would not by itself settle that issue either.

## 3 Rational intelligibility and the strongest brute rival

The fresh unit's most ambitious causal defence should be tested rather than bypassed. Its strands can be given a charitable reconstruction: rational inference needs intelligible connections; empirical investigation relies on stable causal order; and differentiated concrete natures possess differentiated powers rather than being a causally empty sequence of appearances. These are substantive affirmative considerations, not merely an appeal to habit.

There is a narrower semantic danger in the compressed argument that a proof denying causation itself relates premises and conclusion. Inferential support and efficient production are not the same relation. A logical consequence can hold between propositions without one proposition efficiently manufacturing another. If the target is the actual mental process, an operative reason-sensitive causal route may be required, but that is an additional interpretive premise. Neither interpretation licenses replacing all explanatory relations by efficient receipt.

The stronger reading therefore grants genuine causation in thinking and in the ordinary world. On that reading, the relevant rival is not an inert abstraction, global eliminativism about causes, or an event popping out of absolute absence. It is a concrete, qualified, beginningless, unreceived productive reality with stable intrinsic powers and appropriately connected downstream cognition. Its asserted brute status concerns **why that reality obtains at all**, not whether it produces its effects or whether the effects support knowledge.

Such a rival can accept the intelligibility and powers considerations while denying that failure of intrinsic existential necessitation creates an unsatisfied existential requirement. Describing the position does not establish its metaphysical possibility. It does show why refuting the elimination of causal powers does not yet refute that position: the latter preserves the very powers whose elimination the argument attacks.

The comparison with the already inspected epistemic-PSR literature is exact. Koons's 1997 treatment already distinguishes causation from necessitation. Koons and Pruss 2020/2021 additionally argue for symmetry, relevant counterpossibilities, and a priori coverage of the actual epistemic route. Those stronger arguments and their assessed limits are inherited; they are not present merely because Ameri says denying causality undermines knowledge. A bridge from a brute background to unreliable local cognition needs its own modal or epistemic argument. Conversely, the rival cannot secure reflective warrant by arbitrarily declaring all threatening alternatives distant.

The fresh Ameri unit therefore supports retaining causal provenance and rejects an easy indeterminism-to-nothing move. It adds no separately established anti-brute premise beyond the intrinsic-sufficiency argument already examined. This is a genuine failed transfer at a specified target, not a claim that foundational causal warrant must first be deduced from weaker premises or that the original affirmative appraisal is void.

## 4 Time and tradition require separate treatment

The full causal unit makes the temporal disagreement explicit. Its ban concerns the actual infinite past and successive accumulation, not only a regress of existentially received sources. The analogies involving unbacked cheques and indefinitely deferred permission have a dependency structure; a beginningless succession does not acquire that structure merely by having no first temporal member. A sequence of independently enabled events is not established as possible by this observation, but it is not refuted simply by importing the analogies' absence of an actual resource.
