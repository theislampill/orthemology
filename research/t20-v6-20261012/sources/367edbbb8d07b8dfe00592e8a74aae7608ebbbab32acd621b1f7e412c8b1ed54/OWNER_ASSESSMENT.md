The earlier mathematics classified correction energies compatible with one partition of sources. A partition can permit interactions within each group. The new deduction establishes what happens when several such audit requirements must all hold, under the same stated Fisher–transport metric and full product domain.

The energy must decompose over the common refinement of the partitions. For crossing row and column groups of four labelled atoms, every interaction across individual atoms is excluded from that gradient-energy class, even though the audit operations themselves still preserve an odds-ratio invariant and do not generate all individual rescalings.

This distinction matters: the stronger result comes from the gradient, metric and integrability assumptions. It cannot be inferred merely from the generated action group. An explicit nongradient field retains odds-ratio dependence, and an audit-invariant scalar energy has a gradient that fails audit compatibility. Those counterexamples identify exactly what supplies the rigidity.

### The allowed correction repertoire changes the exact memory requirement

The earlier controlled Fisher result allowed every coarse linear objective and therefore distinguished every aggregate support weight. The new restricted-control classification retains exactly the unnormalised full-output barycentre of each group of support points that the permitted controls cannot distinguish.

This gives an exact sufficient state and a sharp continuous-coordinate augmentation count. A six-point example requires one extra coordinate beyond the present output under restricted controls, whereas the full-control contract needs three. This is not a finite-bit claim or a practical sampling bound.

There is also a consequential composition failure. On a nine-point example, two laws have identical complete output histories under every x-only correction, and also under every y-only correction. Yet an x correction followed by a y correction separates them. Collecting both isolated response profiles still loses one necessary tensor direction. Their combined operation family requires its own closure.

This uses the inherited tensor/continuation machinery in a specified new control domain. It does not claim to have discovered the general principle that operations determine faithful representations.
