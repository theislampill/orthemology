AR8R-T132 — Same-token/same-respect reflection constraint
Typed predicates

For a target model m, let Prod_T(m) mean:

    one determinate production token, in one fixed explanatory respect, is the total producer of every contrast-fixing condition required for the determinacy of that very production attribution.

For a formal model f, let Forbidden_F(f) be the formal configuration ruled out by a proved nonaugmentation or self-dependence theorem.

Let I : M_T -> M_F preserve the relevant production token, explanatory respect, and dependence incidence.
Theorem

If:

    Prod_T(m) -> Forbidden_F(I(m)) for every target model m; and

    the formal theorem proves not Forbidden_F(f) for every f in im(I);

then not Prod_T(m) for every target model m.
Proof

Assume Prod_T(m). Assumption 1 yields Forbidden_F(I(m)), contradicting assumption 2. ∎
Failure conditions

The theorem licenses no target conclusion if the interpretation:

    maps different production tokens to one formal node;

    changes the explanatory respect;

    replaces constitution with mere observation;

    preserves outcomes while losing dependence direction;

    or omits a target countermodel from the image.

RB remains separately required for a world-directed reading when the formal interpretation could otherwise be merely representational.
Disposition

FORMAL_RESULT_CAN_BE_METAPHYSICALLY_CONSTRAINING: CONDITIONALLY_ESTABLISHED
DIRECT_TRANSFER: REJECTED
UNIVERSAL_STRICT_SEPARATION: REJECTED
NECESSARY_BEING: NOT_ENTAILED
