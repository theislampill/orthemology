# Valued cooperation on the retained reactive model

8 October 2026. Successor research only. No metaphysical closure or protected-repository integration.

## Result

A richer end can be stated before action selection and truthfully fulfilled in the retained model's policy-defined ordinary runs. The end includes both the created product Q and actual goal-directed participation by the designated peer. No output-success filter is needed. Both actors retain the old standing repertoires, structurally unreceived resources, and factual forecasts.

**This does not yield independent actual whole original provision.** Each ordinary created event still has both original contributors and no individually entire provider. “Two independent original resource roots” and “each independently produces the whole actual work” are different claims; only the former is represented.

The consequential new negative result is about reasons. A selector can use the intended participation requirement without consulting outcomes, but **peer-valued cooperation adds no local action-selection restriction beyond the old own-activity end**. Both require the actor to act. The old q-setting means policy is still doing the remaining selection work. Relation preference alone leaves nine joint profiles, six of which fail the product and full end. The richer end therefore explains why an otherwise endpoint-equivalent solo run loses something valued, but has not supplied the missing justification of the particular policy, target, or original-agent admissibility.

Sixteen checks pass. The first ten were preserved while six controls were added after independent review. Their counts are implementation coverage, not sixteen discoveries.

## 2. Product, end, act, forecast, and efficacy are separate

`End` contains the product code, subject, end kind, and optional designated peer identity. `Choice` combines that end with the inherited `Exercise`. The setting is an operative means code, not the intended product by definition. Local act-token strings remain inherited world-local handles; none is promoted to a global numerical-act identity.

`joint_relation` checks actual productive acts with the same intended product. In an ordinary joint-end context, both richer records additionally express the peer-valuing content. This is **goal-directed co-production with declared individual relational ends**. The predicate does not, for arbitrary inputs, certify philosophical collective intention, mutual awareness, or a comprehensive theory of shared agency. In the ordinary fixture the forecast correctly describes both richer records and acts, but finite factive description is not a full divine-cognition theory.

For each q, the ordinary profile (q,q) fulfills both richer ends. Its full forecast is derived from the supplied end records and known policies before an actual run is provided. That gives genuine conditional forecast truth. It does not establish that those policies instantiate source-level complete particular willing, or that they are sufficiently justified as wise.

No predicate named or functioning as source-level `CompleteWill` is defined by success. A source defender may deny that peer-dependent willing is complete in the required original operative respect even when the declared product and relation are actually attained. ## 3. The selection control: a genuine dependency, with a decisive limit

The initial adapter's `ordinary_choices` does not branch on rich end kind. Merely placing end records before that function would not show that richer reasons selected cooperation. The additive `selection_probe.py` therefore separates two stages.

**Participation stage.** Each actor independently considers idle and all three owned active settings. Product-only content leaves all four options. Both old own-activity and richer joint-activity require that actor's participation, leaving the same three active settings. This is a minimal necessary-means restriction derived from the declared content, not a general sufficient theory of rational choice. It does not select or command the peer.

Composing these declared local menus gives sixteen product-only profiles and nine own/joint profiles. This Cartesian construction is a disclosed model of these two specific policies; it is not inferred from nonreceipt or projected onto every metaphysically eligible counterfactual choice. For each fixed q, all nine joint profiles have two q-directed productive acts, but only three obtain Q. The other six fail the stated full end. Thus this stage is demonstrably not “admit exactly success.” Its incompleteness is visible, not hidden.

**Inherited means stage.** Applying the old q-setting recipe retains idle or q in the product-only case, and q in the own/joint cases. The product-only domain has four profiles, including both idle; three obtain Q. The own and joint domains are identical singletons (q,q), and both fulfill their declared ends under the existing law. This does not derive the recipe from relational value. The two other coactive response solutions also fulfill the richer end, so that value alone does not privilege the diagonal. No arbitrary unique optimizer or sequential response ordering is introduced.

A test replaces `Model.execute` with an exception during domain construction; both domain stages still construct normally. Another changes only the evaluated product observation: the ordinary policy domain stays fixed and its satisfaction claim fails. This latter record is expressly synthetic and internally not a replacement causal world. Together these controls establish procedural independence from output filtering, not metaphysical admissibility.

The strongest surviving selector conclusion is therefore narrow: relational content can be explicitly used as a necessary participation reason, but adds no action-selection explanation over the inherited own-activity rival. The wisdom of q, the means recipe, and the eligibility of the richer end remain substantive obligations.

## 4. Suppression and particular intention

For each q, suppress B's exercise while retaining the exact A `Choice` and `Exercise` objects. A's repertoire and product Q survive, as does the inherited own-activity end. The richer fixed end fails because its peer-participation condition is absent. The cut overrides B's policy-required act and disagrees with the ordinary full forecast; it is not another ordinary run with all wise reasons, cognition, and source-complete willing frozen.

Changing A's end to own-only makes the solo result satisfy that revised content, using the same underlying exercise. This is a change of intention content, not preservation of the complete particular intention. Adding a statement that B was present does not restore the actual relation. Changing B's intended product while retaining its setting can preserve the same physical output and support yet defeat the concordant-project condition. Neither diagnostic is proposed as an actual false-omniscient or perfectly wise world.

These tests stop two invalid transports: product preservation does not preserve every richer end, and a truthful forecast of the ordinary plan does not certify admissibility of every intervened plan. They equally stop the rival from declaring those cuts impossible merely because they fail the chosen end.

## 5. Strongest source argument, including concentration

Fresh continuous reading of the retained primary electronic text `majmu20-item2032.txt` L001–020 controls the application. At 20:175, L002 gives the unchanged-contributor nonabsorption argument and expressly distinguishes cases in which the actor's will, movement, or instruments change. At 20:176–178, L003–008 argues that actual joint production is not individually independent production and that complete power plus decisive willing entails the product through its bearer alone. The objection is explicitly intrinsic to simultaneous independent efficacy, not merely possible disagreement. [Primary text](https://islamweb.net/ar/library/content/22/2032/)

The retained Latin law permits the same A exercise to yield Q alone and with B while genuinely recording B's contribution. It therefore does not satisfy the source's nonabsorption/application premise. Enriching the end does not repair that law-level disagreement. Nor does success of both richer ends turn the two-source support record into two exclusive whole origins.

**The source also argues for concentration; that argument is not absent.** L009–013, electronic 20:178–181, holds that powers concentrated in one subject do not diminish productive capacity, then uses this to argue that the existing partners are eligible for a more independent condition. The current fixture already gives A the entire represented solo product repertoire. The added capacity-transport control therefore suppresses B, retaining A and Q; it does not execute a metaphysical merger or transfer B's powers into A. It is a response to the target-transport issue, not an implementation of the source's hypothetical consolidation.

That response grants source-neutral external work capacity while distinguishing it from the relational end “A and B do Q.” Losing a participant-sensitive end under a one-participant comparison does not show that productive power has decreased. Conversely, retaining external Q does not preserve that richer end. Whether original agents may fittingly choose such an end is the disputed applicability question. The predicate's identity sensitivity is not itself a refutation of the source's concentration or perfection argument.

Minhaj 2:161–164, item151 L007–017, freshly read, gives the perfection/nonreceipt rationale. L009 says a merely external conjoined item is not thereby the bearer's perfection. That prevents treating any achieved external end as automatically an implanted intrinsic improvement. It does not exclude relationally specified perfections. Item156 L038–041, freshly read as a bounded adjacent scope, explicitly treats actual efficacy as a perfection and argues from its dependence on another. Thus no incoming power/knowledge edge does not answer the full source challenge. [Item151](https://islamweb.net/ar/library/content/108/151/), [item156](https://islamweb.net/ar/library/content/108/156/)

The earlier audited Dar9:364–365, Minhaj3:52–53 and 320–328, Bayan5:253–255, and Majmu8 particular-reason obligations retain their inherited source bindings. ## 6. Actual interaction, original peers, and full inventory

Ameri's discussion of the experience-machine objection, printed pp.205–206, supplies a limited reason to take actual interaction seriously: reported or simulated experience may omit real interpersonal action and consequences. This warrants testing a richer end rather than assuming output-only evaluation. It does not establish that divine originals need such a relation, that an uncreated peer is required, or that the relation is a pure original perfection. This is a narrow application of that discussion, not a new general wisdom principle. [Primary PDF](https://almobadarah.com/uploads/books/d054b3190ecdfbaaef33369a.pdf)

The separate origin control represents A and a created agent C as distinct goal-directed actors. A supplies C and its capacity, willing, and act; local Q has one original root despite two immediate actors. This is a typed source-accounting comparison, not a proof of creature consciousness or a new metaphysical model. It preserves the explicit extra created inventory `{C_created,C_power,C_will,C_act}`. Consequently it does **not** preserve the complete created history of the two-original fixture, and cannot be advertised as producing the same whole created output.

It can satisfy a generic actual-other-agent condition at the local Q. It cannot satisfy the de re condition naming original B, or the strengthened requirement of a second independently original peer. Those are additional substantive contents. Real interpersonal value alone does not choose among them.

Finally, wanting a particular relation is not automatically making the peer an ultimate final object. Majmu20 L020 links two self-sufficient final objects to the earlier exclusion of two self-sufficient agents. No new source proof equating every subordinate relational end with that prohibited ultimate status was found. This leaves a specific transfer burden; it does not show that the source permits the proposed original-peer end.

It still lacks individual whole actual original production and a warranted identification of its end/recipe with perfectly complete original particular willing. Richer content clarifies the loss under suppression but does not improve policy selection over the inherited own-activity rival. The live source question is whether freely choosing an actual relation with another original can be a fitting exercise without constitutive borrowed completion, given the source's positive argument that foreign-conditioned actual efficacy itself is a deficient perfection. Neither the graph nor the desired end settles that question by definition.

No further table enlargement is warranted by this result alone. Sixteen passing checks and the present sources do not supply one.
