- Corner activity reveals the minimal support antichain, not all absorbed supports.
- A_min need not equal the interaction-anchored root set.
- H=empty must be included in mixed-grid inversion.
- Minimal-response upper bounds supply correct lower rate bounds only with a valid supplied count ceiling.
- A per-support bound is not the total budget governing the joint unary simplex.
- A count ceiling is an inequality, not an attained-total promise.
- Conditional covered-root gaps require every target root to have a known-count positive covering support, at common base commands with shared maps.
- Covered-root lower bounds do not require full-rank calibration identification.
- Fair positive-support discovery must not wait indefinitely on an absent candidate.
- A promise covering every known root cannot be weakened to covering only an unknown active subset without changing the proof.
- Without a cover promise, eventual actual cover certifies completion; nontermination never certifies absence.
- Finite count/support output does not reconstruct full calibration functions.
- Certified population approximation access is not a finite statistical sample.
