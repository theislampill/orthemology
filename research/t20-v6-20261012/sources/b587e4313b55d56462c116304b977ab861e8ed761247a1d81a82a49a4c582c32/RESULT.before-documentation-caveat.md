## 3. Conditional source application and countercontrol

Use C for “the item is accounted for” and P for “it is punished,” at the relevant fixed scope. The local argument permits a C-and-not-P case and does not exclude C-and-P. These two possibilities suffice to refute C entails P. They are not a complete semantics of the verses, a claim that every possible combination is realized, or a normative model of punishment.

The bounded repair has:

- Fixed original source occurrence, wording, attribution, scope, force, and established content.
- Before: a recipient takes the accounting content to force P.
- Operation: use the source's forgiveness/punishment distinction and the clarifying passage to reject that entailment; retain the old interpretation and its disposition in history.
- After: accounting remains affirmed, while P is not established by accounting alone. A separately warranted P claim is not thereby defeated.

The semantic premise is substantive: the authorial reading and source-to-claim correspondence must be adequate. The operation does not make them adequate by construction. It preserves established content only where that premise and the frame are warranted. Nor does it require every corrective episode to change a governing standard; a first-order interpretation update is enough here.

**Synthetic actual-rule-change control:** let an earlier rule really require P whenever C, and let a later valid rule remove that requirement. Keep the same source authority but change the actual rule version and content. The operative “P follows” output again changes from true to false. This is expressly not the Shifāʾ author's account. The comparison proves why unchanged source identity plus changed output cannot decide the repair/repeal question. One must inspect whether the earlier stronger norm was established, and whether its content/current force actually changed.

### What an application must additionally supply

For a substantive “interpretation repair, source norm preserved” claim, the generic witness must point to immutable, distinguishable before/after appraisal states and a justified interpretation mapping that identifies:

1. The same original occurrence and source role, with authenticated scope/content where claimed.
2. The established norm/content and relevant version/current-force frame, not merely the authority's name.
3. The old and new represented interpretations, and the exact judgement or inference each produces.
4. The evidence that supports the new interpretation and defeats the old entailment, without asserting blanket not-P.
5. The historical record and linked disposition of the old attribution and any genuinely affected obligations.

A mechanical consumer could compare typed, dereferenced frame fields at that declared interface. It would still need the substantive source/interpretation and authority premises.
