### U08 — beyond a finite proposal

The actual finite row correspondence is

`F(p)={Σ_i p_i r_i : r_i∈R_i}`, on `p∈Δ_m`,

with nonempty compact convex row sets. Compactness, invariance, nonempty compact convex values and upper hemicontinuity are proved, including a Hausdorff 1-Lipschitz bound. The precise external theorem used is Shmalo's simplex Kakutani statement, Definition 2.4/Theorem 2.5 of arXiv:1811.08454v1; the proof documents compare its hypotheses, rather than confusing it with Riesz–Markov–Kakutani.

A separate direct proof chooses rows, takes Cesàro averages and passes to a compact subsequence. Thus the constructed family's existence does not rest on an unidentified Lean Kakutani declaration. Candidate Lean source implements this route against the pinned compactness/linear-map infrastructure, but has not been executed.

The countable extension uses

`K_c={p∈ℓ¹ : p_j≥0, Σp_j=1, p_j≤c_j}`, where `c≥0`, `Σc<∞` and `Σc≥1`.

Uniformly controlled tails prove compactness in ℓ¹. Rows in this envelope preserve it, and the same selected-operator Cesàro proof produces a stationary law. This does not claim all countably supported safe laws form a compact domain. Shift on ℕ and irrational rotation provide exact counterexamples to that unsupported extension.
