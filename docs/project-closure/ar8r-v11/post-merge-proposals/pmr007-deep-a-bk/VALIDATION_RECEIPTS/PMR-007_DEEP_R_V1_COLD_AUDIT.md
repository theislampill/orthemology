## Blocking findings

### R-F01 — underived order is only a model label

The finite witness does not prove or instantiate the full historical anti-total-emergence derivation. It assigns an `underived` predicate to `o`.

**Repair:** call this an **underived-order placeholder compatible with the admitted conclusion**, not a proof of that conclusion.

### R-F02 — necessity and external actuality are stipulated

`Necessary(o)` and `ExternallyActual(a)` are rival premises. The model cannot be represented as advancing B4 or B5.

**Repair:** mark both as stipulated strengthening assumptions used to make the rival harder, and preserve the bridge statuses as unchanged.

### R-F03 — W/P/K are only functional role proxies

The Boolean role allocation does not establish Divine Will, Power, or Knowledge, nor the semantics of those attributes.

**Repair:** rename them `W_role`, `P_role`, and `K_role`; state that the model tests common-bearer logic only.

### R-F04 — integration links are not executable in V1

The prose says that content, selection, realization, and correction are linked, but the primary checker only verifies their components separately.

**Repair:** add an explicit typed link graph and executable checks for content-to-selection, selection-to-realization, order-to-admissibility, and norm-to-revision evaluation.

### R-F05 — common satisfiability is not explanatory unification

The witness may still be a composite architecture with primitive coordination. It establishes nonentailment from the formal union, not a superior explanation.

**Repair:** classify the result as an `R5_COMMON_MODEL_SATISFIABILITY_CONTROL`, preserve brute stopping points, and do not call it an integrated champion.

### R-F06 — finite consistency is not metaphysical possibility

A finite relational structure demonstrates first-order/model-relative consistency of the stated predicates, subject to the formalization. It does not prove that abstract objects, powers, or impersonal actualizers exist or are metaphysically possible.

**Repair:** make this authority ceiling explicit in theorem and admission status.

### R-F07 — source premises must remain outside U

The Track-N expansion is described but not typed in the model owner. Without a source toggle, the firewall is only prose.

**Repair:** add explicit `track_n_source_accepted=false` to the neutral witness and a separate source-conditioned expansion record.

### R-F08 — matched architecture comparison remains incomplete

The personal, powers/plural, and abstract-order architectures accept different premise classes. The witness shows survival of the rival but not equal prior probability, equal explanatory cost, or strict dominance.

**Repair:** add a matched premise/primitive/bridge table and conclude only `RIVAL_PARITY_UNBROKEN` at the current evidence boundary.
