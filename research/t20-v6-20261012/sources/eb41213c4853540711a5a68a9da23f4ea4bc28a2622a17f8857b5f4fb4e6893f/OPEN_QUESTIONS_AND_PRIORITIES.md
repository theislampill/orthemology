# Current open questions and defensible priorities

**Neither a task checklist nor a declaration of T20 scientific completion.** Use it to avoid losing important work or choosing the same already-settled question again.

## Foundational philosophical fronts

- **Objective orthability / actual sourcehood:** determine what independently warrants moving from real distinction and actual received reality to adequate original sourcehood and from that source to intrinsic necessity. Retain the already credited affirmative philosophical grounds; investigate only exact surviving joints, not generic rival scenarios.
- **One complete common original Creator:** evaluate full same-effect/same-act complete productive efficacy, independence, non-absorption, original agent identity and actual connectedness. Formal local-to-global source gluing is conditional on substantive overlap/source-individuation, not a substitute for them.
- **Knowledge–Will–Wisdom:** distinguish own intrinsic act, wise particular intention and produced effect, while avoiding the invalid inference that every voluntary action is acquisition of essential perfection. Seek named, independently warranted actual reasons where appropriate; do not demand explanation of every micro-event of world history from one source passage.
- **Fiṭrah, recognition, cumulative warrant:** what epistemic information must survive source correction and passage-specific defeaters? Legitimate noninferential recognition and source testimony should not be discarded because a formal proof record is incomplete; nor treated as knowledge merely from psychological tendency.
- **Source-to-norm and authority:** actual rightful direction vs counsel and permission; recipient condition, communicative intention, command adoption and scope. The extra publicity/attestation historical objection remains open at some source links.

Which invariants survive reweighting and moving objectives? Do not install a universal smooth model by definition.

Verify actual hypotheses, compare with Dot's existing results and stress-test failed interfaces.
- **Hybrid jump timing:** address the predecessor-reported gap. A trajectory matching postjump state isn't faithful if the contract observes different jump times, costs or STOP conditions.
- **Source-to-state version changes:** historical likelihood pullback, current source-version tables, dependency-directed replay and unconditional tail/realisability reassessment after evidence withdrawal. Test with authenticated data contracts.
- **Type-theoretic completion:** original HasE independence is recorded, while extended proof systems have conditional positive results and exact boundaries. Investigate soundness/expressive consequences of any proposed added principle, not re-proving the old target in unchanged calculus.
- **Infinite moments and learning:** distinguish determinate law, effective name, positivity and actual law realisability, supported transforms and long-time continuation. Identify where a finite hierarchy or positive interval certificate can be trusted.
- **Research-ecology theorem transport:** develop an explicit state/operation/semantic morphism between at least two otherwise distinct fronts; prove what is preserved, produce a countermodel for an illicit map, and record how the result changes a later research obligation. Simple verbal resemblance doesn't count.

## What should not be done automatically

- Don't infer that the owner authorises new PRs or releases. All previous selected publication PRs are merged, and subsequent private research still needs scope-controlled adoption.
- Don't commission another unlimited whole-corpus scan when one mathematical inference or source question is the user's immediate request.
- Don't equate 168 available ZIPs with 168 proven novel mathematical results.
- Don't treat an external model agreeing with Orthemology's vocabulary as independent validation.
- Don't repeat previous erroneous blanket claims (“DAEE only metaphor”, “entropic geometry is unique”, “all moment dynamics are realisable”, “original HasE is simply open”, “the Creator argument was erased by a modelling qualification”).

**Best research behaviour:** choose a specific consequential question, inspect exact accepted and rival statements, establish the sharp mathematical/source interface, perform a discriminating check, and preserve the evidence plus residual. T20 remains open even after a successful checkpoint.
