## 2. Candidate scoped Wisdom bridge

For a bearer `g`, action episode `a`, end `e`, and means `m`, declare the following coordinates:

```text
AG: g is an agent/intentional subject;
K:  g understands the relevant end, means, and circumstances;
F:  e is objectively fitting or praiseworthy;
W:  g selects e because of its fittingness;
M:  m is appropriately proportioned to e under the circumstances;
C:  g has the capacity to employ m;
X:  g nonaccidentally executes m toward e.
```

V1 proposes:

\[
WisdomEpisode(g,a)
\iff
AG\land K\land F\land W\land M\land C\land X.
\]

This is a role-level conditional bridge. It does not establish that any bearer satisfies the coordinates.

## 3. Nonentailment results

### K-N1 — proper function does not entail Wisdom

A selected-effect truth tracker or fiṭrī function can have a proper function while no designer or agent is present in the neutral model.

### K-N2 — success does not entail fitting end

A perfectly corrigible optimizer may efficiently realize a destructive target.

### K-N3 — fitting outcome does not entail wise agency

A nonintentional process can accidentally produce a beneficial outcome.

### K-N4 — Knowledge, Will, and Power do not entail Wisdom

A knowledgeable, effective agent can deliberately choose an objectively vicious end.

### K-N5 — praiseworthy intention does not entail competent means

An agent can will a fitting end on false beliefs or choose disproportionate means.

### K-N6 — distributed guard availability does not entail one wise bearer

Knowledge, fitting-end selection, capacity, and execution may be distributed across different bearers with empty total intersection.

### K-N7 — unique actualizer lineage does not entail Wisdom

The Deep-J root may be an impersonal powers field.

## 4. Source-relative adjudication

The supplied Asfahani translation distinguishes attributes or names that entail praise in themselves from speech and volition, which admit praiseworthy and blameworthy instances. It represents Allah as characterized only by the praiseworthy and invokes names such as the Wise, Merciful, and Truthful. This supplies Track-N source premises if the source and translation are accepted. It does not prove the neutral existence of a bearer satisfying `AG–X`.

The supplied al-ʿUjayrī synthesis interprets created suitability, guidance toward benefit, and teleological tendency as evidence of a Creator and Guide. This is a contemporary Atharī argument, not Arabic-primary verification or a neutral deductive bridge.
