# Root adjudication: what the rival evidence actually bears on

9 October 2026. This supplements, rather than replaces, both earlier root judgements. T20 remains active.

## Current judgement and its basis

I continue provisionally to favour one actual complete common original source. I do not currently claim an independently warranted plural-original countermodel, and I do not judge ordinary overdetermination to refute complete original sovereignty. No further downward revision is earned merely by the formal controls or by changing the meaning of explanation, dependence, or entirety.

The earlier change of confidence needs clearer explanation than “developed local counterevidence.” The relevant change is a reassessment of the warrant for the transition from complete original productive adequacy to sole actual productive attribution. Ordinary redundant causation motivates scrutiny of that transition; it does not settle its original-specific application. I remain unpersuaded that the examined grounds close that inference at its unrestricted scope. That is an assessment of the positive argument's warrant, not a conclusion that a substituted weaker proposition has been refuted.

There is an important qualification to this account of my history: the earlier affirmative judgement was already defeasible and already reserved a universal original-efficacy norm. Actual-world assent and completed exclusion of every plural model are different claims. The later report must not be read as showing the older position logically inconsistent, or as demanding universal deductive exclusion before any defeasible assent is possible. My present caution is not a newly imposed general standard that only deductive demonstrations warrant philosophical judgement.

## Exact propositions

Schaffer supplies defeasible support for causal individualism under redundant complete processes. The source's whole-production proposition additionally concerns the entire actual productive work of a fixed numerical effect, and its original-agent application further concerns unreceived adequate power, conditions, and perfection. Neither extra claim is certified by complete-path status alone.

The source nevertheless explicitly advances a broader augmentation principle: genuine unchanged additional efficacy should yield more or greater completed work. The imagined noncompositional transformation can exert prima facie pressure on that principle's claimed intuitive obviousness. Calling it only irrelevant ordinary causation would suppress a real argumentative connection. Its preservation of the full productive situation and the entire numerical work remains unestablished, so its evidential role is indirect and defeasible, not an authenticated counterinstance.

My remaining source-directed question is therefore precise: why must complete unreceived actual adequacy entail exclusive actual attribution, rather than merely freedom from indispensable foreign supplementation? The source answers through nonabsorption, actual efficacy as perfection, and complete power plus decisive will. These are substantive reasons. Neither a two-arrow model nor merely relabelling a peer “redundant” defeats them. Conversely, stipulating exclusivity into “complete power” would conceal the bridge requiring defence.

## Qualitative grounding and PSR

The new primary return strengthens positive warrant for actual productive-ground adequacy and contingency-to-need. Its plural target need not be reified as another thing. The qualitative literature offers genuine alternative ontological and explanatory proposals, but has not established the absence of an original productive resource at the intended receipt/production meanings. Its different explanatory relation receives no direct defeater credit against Completion without the missing mapping. A genuinely independent concrete field might instead satisfy the broad original-bearer interface without establishing personality or unity.

The fact-mereological restriction on the unrestricted PSR package remains a conditional transport constraint. It neither makes divine attributes unreal nor refutes an intrinsic-attribute ontology without a defended fact-part mapping. Modal persistence is not intrinsic nonreceipt; the whole received field cannot be silently restricted to bearers absent from at least one represented world.

## Disposition

The investigation has identified missing transports and sharpened the strength of indirect evidence. It has not demonstrated that the historical reassessment explicitly substituted a weaker proposition and certified a direct metaphysical counterexample. Nor has it shown that the confidence change was required. The current judgement remains open to the strongest source argument and genuinely meaning-preserving rivals. Valid mathematics, source corrections, both earlier judgements, and this clarification remain preserved.
