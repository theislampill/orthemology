# Formal reconstruction — Candidate E

## Current schema

Let Corr(t,s) mean t is an objective correction of s, and Fix_s be standards wholly constituted by s’s actual endorsements/dispositions. If Corr(t,s) can hold while t correctly rejects or repairs elements of Fix_s, then Corr does not supervene solely on Fix_s. Some normatively asymmetric standard has counterfactual authority beyond the current state.

## Quantifier and type obligations

- Separate tokens, types, relations, truth conditions, and explanatory standards.
- Do not infer a bearer, unity, intellect, or personality beyond the stated conclusion.
- Preserve partial emergence and rival stopping points unless independently excluded.
