## 2. Source taxonomy: precise support and limits

### Salīm 2:23 supports the first distinction

`RESULT.md:9,52–54` is accurate. The author explicitly distinguishes intransitive activity from product-requiring transitive activity. The case division concerns whether only the intransitive succession is beginningless or transitive succession is also beginningless; its first branch still introduces transitive acts later. Accordingly it does not establish a life that never produces anything externally, and does not certify such a being as an eligible necessary original. The report states both limits. The surrounding argument is directed against a coeternal particular act or creature; its conclusion is not a concession of plural divine sources. [Electronic Salīm text, 2:22–24](https://islamhouse.com/read/ar/الصفدية-272837).

The further output/field distinction is an explicit logical scope analysis, not an additional taxonomy that the report can attribute verbatim to 2:23. The report keeps that difference: its source control blocks equating every intrinsic act with a distinct product, while the localization condition is separately introduced at `RESULT.md:13–16` and `CONDITIONAL_SCOPE.md:55–62`.

### Salīm 2:142 is not reduced to the no-restart conditional

`RESULT.md:40,60–62` and `CONDITIONAL_SCOPE.md:23–29` preserve the stronger essential-genus assertion as well as the causal no-restart reasoning. The no-restart condition alone permits a bearer that never starts acting. The source does more than assert that condition, and the report explicitly says so. The final issuance-to-intrinsic-activity implication is not reversibly equivalent to a product for each intrinsic occurrence. The productive antecedent of the 2:145 discussion also does not erase the earlier intransitive category. [Electronic Salīm text, 2:141–146](https://islamhouse.com/read/ar/الصفدية-272837).

No extension from the established creator to every proposed necessary external bearer has been smuggled into those passages. `RESULT.md:44` separately states the already-established creator, every eligible original, and every necessary external bearer domains. That is a legitimate quantifier control, not a denial of the source's stronger philosophical argument.

## 4. The relational controls prove only what they claim

The all-integer definitions in `CONDITIONAL_SCOPE.md:33–41` are sufficient for the stated reduced-signature non-entailments:

- In Version I every indexed bearer has an actual act at every integer, but `b` has no production relation. Hence the activity relation alone does not entail some output.
- In Version II `b` produces a distinct effect for every represented act, while all of those effects are outside F. Hence even that stronger productive condition alone does not entail participation in F.

The constructions are defined for arbitrary integer indices; they are not finite-prefix experiments falsely generalized. Their disconnectedness and productive semantics are stipulated. They do not assign philosophical eligibility to `b`, establish modal necessity, authenticate original causation, or preserve all-created coverage. The author states these omissions explicitly and does not count them as defects in the positive source argument. This is premise isolation, not a source-faithful plural-world counterexample.

`B_F` together with `X_F` implies at most one eligible original by direct substitution; separate existence supplies exactly one. All-created coverage entails `B_F` only with the stated nonempty F and grounding-to-participation link. It does not itself establish exclusive participation. The all-external-bearer route requires the same excluded dependence relation and independently disqualifies a peer; neither activity nor field localization does that work.

**No substantive formal gap found.** A proof assistant or exhaustive finite simulation would not add the missing philosophical warrant and is unnecessary to check these elementary general arguments.
