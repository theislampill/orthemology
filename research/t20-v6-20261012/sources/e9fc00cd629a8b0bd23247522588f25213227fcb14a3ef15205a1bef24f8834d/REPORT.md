## 2. The affirmative architecture actually available

The argument starts with actual received productive activity. It does not start with the mere availability of an objective norm, a correct proposition, a possible nature, or the definition of a perfect being.

Its first substantive judgement concerns the right target. A complete account of received productive actuality must include the actuality of the derivative suppliers it employs. A supplier can genuinely explain a local effect while its own received actuality remains among what the complete account must cover. Neither indefinite repetition nor rearrangement of that received actuality performs the missing existential work merely by changing its presentation.

This is the positive anti-presupposition reason for original completion. It does not deny intermediate causal efficacy, require a first temporal event, or require the recipients themselves to become original after being supplied. The accepted T17 correction specifically rejects that last misunderstanding. Original actuality supplies a different mode of existential adequacy; it is not just a shorter ancestry diagram.

The second judgement concerns actual realization. Under the defended concrete-resource interpretation, an actual productive resource is a concrete intrinsic instantiation in some actual qualified reality. This is not a theorem converting any true explanatory sentence or norm into a concrete component.

The third judgement concerns support. If that bearer’s being actual at all is externally provided in the same existential respect, the bearer’s received actuality cannot be used to instantiate the resource while being omitted from the latter’s alleged original existential provision. The resource may nevertheless possess its own genuine efficacy. Dependence of its existential possession is not causal idleness.

The deductive conclusion is then short and already checked in the inherited interface. Completion supplies an actual nonreceived resource; realization supplies its actual intrinsic bearer; support rules out that bearer’s whole-existence receipt in the stipulated same respect. The same bearer can then enter the separately warranted necessity inference.

The proof does not establish the philosophical premises’ truth simply by checking their composition. Conversely, their status as substantive premises does not return the inference to unsupported suspension. The actual constructive warrant lies in the target-preserving adequacy and instantiation arguments just identified.

## 3. Source-sensitive test of the tempting partial-receipt repair

The exact question tested was whether a bearer could require an external existential contribution while housing an allegedly original power, thereby defeating the intended step from resource originality to complete existential independence.

The retained primary text already prevents one easy misreading. *Iṣfahāniyya* L184 allows the dependent existent to exist through another or through itself together with another. Both fail sufficiency through itself. Thus “received” at the relevant existential target is not restricted to total causal passivity or the absence of every intrinsic contribution. The text’s positive contrast is freedom from external existential need.

This does not establish a new formal equivalence by itself. It tells us how the intended application must be read. If “whole receipt” is interpreted instead as only a particularly strong quantity of foreign contribution, its negation will not establish complete independence. The later necessity report explicitly preserves that safeguard. No completed theorem has licensed the weaker reading.

The stronger proposed escape distinguishes an underived power-type from its externally enabled instantiation. That distinction is legitimate but does not defeat the accepted argument. Its resource is the **actual concrete instantiation**, not the type considered without its obtaining. Promoting type-level status to original actual possession would omit the very receiving actuality nominated by the argument. This response is already explicit in T17; it is not a new finding of this return.

Finally, pure concomitance is insufficient. *Iṣfahāniyya* L195–203 and Akmaliyya L120–134 distinguish real intrinsic qualification from external production and preserve necessary co-presence without an efficient causal edge. The accepted support argument respects this. It does not reason that any B occurring whenever A occurs is productively received through A. It reasons about the existential provision of a concrete intrinsic instantiation within the bearer whose **actuality in that respect** is being supplied. The qualifying ontology and explanatory target do substantive work.

This rereading therefore validates the source-meaning control and identifies its existing owner. It supplies neither an unrestricted dependence-distribution theorem nor an independent classification of every proposed noncausal ground. No new toy model would supply those philosophical meanings.

## 4. Objective norms, actual provision, and de re eligibility

These three roles remain distinct without diminishing any of them.

**Objective correction or norm availability.** A warranted judgement can identify a failure, a genuine good, or a fitting response. It does not, merely by being correct, produce the actuality it evaluates. The later normative reports already preserve the difference between a real claim and its actual fulfilment. They are not premises manufacturing an original bearer from a moral ideal.

**Actual productive provision.** This supplies the actual starting point and the whole-account question above. Actuality is epistemically given through the target; its original existential character is inferred under the accepted completion and realization account. The argument does not stipulate that a desired explanans exists by inserting existence into its name.

**De re bearer eligibility.** Once an actual bearer is available, the pure-perfection comparison can assess that bearer. The eligibility finding and independent review correctly reject a demand for identity-preserving acquisition as the only possible warrant: essential incapacity can itself disqualify a candidate under a justified comparative standard. But that standard does not replace the prior actual-witness argument. Nor does “each perfection has some source” distribute every perfection to each proposed source. The donor-quantifier limit and the separate a-fortiori warrant remain exactly as earned.

Thus neither a normative fact nor an explanatory proposition becomes a bearer part. The availability of a standard, the existence of its subject, and the warrant for attributing a perfection to that subject are separate claims with separate positive arguments. Repeating their distinction is a currentness control, not another research discovery.
