# Existential supply and non-well-founded explanation

Tenth Orthemology research candidate, 3 October 2026

## Findings

The most promising advance is a limitation on a purely receptive existential adaptation of law-only uniqueness. A non-well-founded system of genuinely recipient-dependent productive rules cannot, merely by uniquely determining qualitative values, supply the nonempty existential realization on which those values depend. If its complete law representation admits simultaneous absence and preserves it, absence is already a solution. A unique positive existential solution therefore requires additional existential content. This result applies to arbitrary infinite networks, allows real creative efficacy at every actual link, and does not assert that an empty cosmos is metaphysically possible.

A second result concerns a defence of explanatory distribution. That existence distributes across individuals does not establish that an adequate explanation of their existence distributes in the same way. Requiring a joint answer need not posit an extra aggregate object or deny any individual's existence. This removes one proposed justification of unrestricted distributed explanatory discharge; it does not establish that every distributed explanation fails.

Together these results sharpen the affirmative original-possession case against two stronger responses than the bare description of an active infinite chain. They do not yet show that every E-explanation of the declared existential inventory requires original possession. An actual productive configuration can be offered as an explanation even where laws alone do not uniquely determine it. The resulting E-versus-law-only distinction is essential. A necessary concrete participant remains unestablished by this investigation; if subsequently warranted, it should be credited before unity, complete source identification, intrinsic necessity explanation or integrity.

## 3 Existence distribution does not settle explanatory distribution

### 3.1 The legitimate starting point

For an ordinary plurality xx, its members exist just when each member exists. One need not add a further substantial entity, the collection, to make the plural statement true. The affirmative can grant that point completely.

The disputed explanatory claim has another operator. It asks whether an account explains the obtaining of those existential truths at the required E. Distribution of the existential predicate does not itself license moving the explanatory operator inside the plural demand. In schematic prose:

- Each bearer exists, so these bearers exist.
- Each bearer's existence has some E-explanation, so there is an adequate E-explanation of their declared joint existential target.

The first is ordinary existential distribution. The second is explanatory composition. To defend the second, one must specify how the local explanations compose and why their remaining presuppositions are harmless at the target. No reified aggregate is required to ask that question.

O-DIST is therefore insufficient as a defence of O-HEP at the present target. A collective explanatory demand does not mean that the members somehow exist collectively while none of them exists individually. It means that the target can require one adequate account of its existential content even though that content is realized distributively. Nor must a joint explanation be incapable of explaining any individual member. A jointly adequate account may have useful individual restrictions; the point is that adequacy of all those restrictions is not a proof of its joint adequacy.

This is an assessment of a particular offered reason. It is not a claim that the source's entire position is refuted, that plural grounding must be irreducibly collective, or that actual infinitism is impossible. A defender can posit a genuinely distributive E and give independent reasons that it discharges the demand. But defining such a lifting of local explanation does not show that it is the relation warranted by the restricted transfer's existential application.

### 3.2 Why this helps the affirmative without granting it victory

The original-possession advocate need not answer the challenge by inventing a mysterious additional object whose existence awaits explanation. It can keep exactly the declared bearers and ask whether their sources' availability has been accounted for in the relevant productive respect. The new analysis removes a false choice between a distributive answer and an extra collective entity.

Conversely, explanatory composition is not automatically invalid. An outside source might productively explain several bearers through a complicated or infinite structure. Several outside contributors might do so jointly. Even an internally distributed proposal might succeed if its target-sensitive adequacy is independently warranted. The objection establishes neither the affirmative nonpresupposition principle nor an equal weighting of the two theories. It identifies why a semantic fact about plural existence does not defeat an independently targeted rational presentation of the principle.

## 4 A stronger rival based on unique non-well-founded determination

The rival need not stop at O-HEP. It can offer a law-governed configuration whose only admissible completion is the observed one. This has an explanatory attraction: the eventual value is not merely stipulated at an inaccessible endpoint. A genuinely infinite constraint can rule out qualitative variation which every finite fragment permits. That possibility deserves full credit. Infinity is not by itself an explanatory defect.

The decisive question for this programme is whether a result about which value existent items have also determines that the bearers exist. Nonemptiness can enter through a variable's domain, a standing producer, an existence clause among the laws, a global admissibility restriction, or a law that generates existential actuality without received input. Those contributions are not equivalent. The distinction can be made precise without requiring that any admissible history have a first event.

### 4.1 The null-preservation lemma

Let I be any nonempty index set. For each i in I, let S_i be a represented-state domain containing a distinguished absence value 0_i. Let S be the product of these domains, so that the all-absence assignment 0 belongs to S. These mathematical indices do not assert the actual or possible existence of any bearer.

For each i, suppose a rule F_i specifies the state of recipient i from the represented states of its productive suppliers. Suppose each rule preserves absence: when all its suppliers are absent, F_i returns 0_i. The displayed laws require, for each i, x_i = F_i(x).

Then x = 0 satisfies every displayed law. Each equation reduces to 0_i = 0_i. Therefore those equations alone cannot have a unique solution in S which includes any existent recipient.

No first index, finite path, induction through a productive chain or traversal of infinity is used. The argument works for arbitrary branching and infinite networks. It does not presuppose that strict productive ancestry is globally well-founded. It is elementary mathematics, with no claim of general mathematical novelty or a new mechanized theorem.

For a nondeterministic formulation, let each rule specify permitted input-output tuples. It suffices that each rule permits an absent recipient when all represented suppliers are absent. The simultaneous null assignment then still satisfies all the displayed local constraints. The stronger condition that no positive received output is permitted without an actual supplier is natural for the intended pure-reception interpretation, but is not needed merely to establish null compatibility.

### 4.2 The productive interpretation is an additional argument

The familiar actuality condition for production supplies a real rationale: an altogether absent supplier does not perform the exercise through which a recipient receives existence. This condition allows actual suppliers to be intelligent, powerful and genuinely creative. It does not say that a received agent merely passes along a conserved quantity or lacks its own real intrinsic power.

Three representation obligations remain separate from that actuality condition.

First, the proposed rules must be defined on simultaneous-absence inputs. A mathematical extension to those inputs is not automatically a theory of metaphysical alternatives. Second, their outputs must represent the target's existential actuality, rather than only an acquired property, current exercise or amount of already possessed power. Third, the displayed coordinate rules must exhaust the alleged law-only account, rather than omit a global constraint or restrict the admissible domain in advance. Rejecting any of these representation claims does not require accepting production by an absent donor.

The application is thus a disciplined audit of a specific explanation. If a purported law-only account rejects null, ask which independently warranted law or domain condition rejects it and what that condition contributes. If it represents only qualitative states, it has not yet addressed the existential target. If it includes an original productive resource, count that resource. If it permits genuinely noncausal existential determination, assess that explanatory relation rather than calling it received production. A restricted domain of nearby alternatives may legitimately leave null outside its scope. B-DOMAIN therefore cannot be refuted by simply adding a remote algebraic solution. The target-sensitive question is whether the proposed restricted domain is appropriate to the existential claim being explained, rather than only to variations of already actual bearers.

### 4.3 The concrete application to the new source

B-EX supplies the decisive target-sensitive comparison. The operative existential input is not an unnoticed consequence of mere infinite repetition. It is already present in the governing package. Removing that input changes the account under assessment; retaining it permits legitimate explanatory work about the remaining structure.

The null-preservation lemma explains why this distinction matters for a purely recipient-dependent adaptation. A nonempty-domain uniqueness theorem can be correct while being silent about why that domain is concretely instantiated. If the same theorem is reused to explain the domain's existential nonemptiness, its domain restriction has changed from an admitted background to part of the requested conclusion. No charge of mathematical error follows. Nor is this an accusation that B-QUAL conceals its assumptions: that source qualification is precisely why the example should be given its bounded credit. The new application constrains reuse of the purely productive fragment; it does not repair an omission that the author failed to recognize.

The positive lesson is stronger than saying that another premise is absent. It identifies a substantive contribution which any proposed pure-reception law-only completion must contain: some determinant excluding the null realization. The determinant may be a productive source, a nonproductive existential law or a restriction with independent modal force. Those are different research commitments, and the original sourcehood argument should not treat them as the same conclusion.

## 5 The strongest replies and their proper force

### 5.1 The actual active order is not empty

Correct. Its actuality is granted throughout. The null assignment tests what the displayed rules determine, not whether the actual received powers exist. A rival that offers Q, including every actual productive act, has more than the laws alone. The lemma does not remove that content or prove that Q cannot E-explain P.

This reply limits the result correctly. It also prevents a law-only uniqueness argument from claiming explanatory credit which is actually supplied by stipulating the realized configuration. The question becomes what the added actual content explains and whether its use answers the entire declared target. That is the unresolved collective question, now reached after a developed proposed solution has been tested rather than merely described.

### 5.2 An empty universe may be impossible

Correct. Nothing here proves otherwise. A legitimate metaphysical law can restrict the domain so that the null assignment is not an eligible world. But the law's existential content is then doing work beyond the null-preserving productive rules. Its warrant must be stated. It cannot be extracted from a formal zero assignment, and its failure to name one necessary individual is not a contradiction.

In particular, a law guaranteeing that some concrete bearer always exists need not guarantee that this same bearer exists in every alternative. De dicto nonemptiness and de re necessary existence remain distinct. The present argument gives no new same-occupant bridge.

### 5.3 The nonempty explanandum is already known

Correct. The admitted correction establishes actual nonemptiness without a global metaphysical proof. It may also allow us, together with a receiving law, to infer the existence of an actual supplier. That is valid epistemic use of the evidence.

It does not follow that the correction is the productive source of its supplier. A downstream existence fact can entail upstream actuality while the productive relation points in the other direction. Appending the observed nonempty fact to the rule package may therefore remove the null mathematical solution without providing an original explanation of the fact appended. This preserves the real episode's warrant and separates evidential inference from productive supply.

### 5.4 An original source also enters as an existential assumption

Correct if the source is merely stipulated. The comparison must be symmetric. An unexplained original bearer can account for its effects while its own actuality remains primitive. It should receive genuine productive credit without being advertised as an intrinsic nonbrute account of its own existence. The null argument does not supply N2 or N3 for it.

There is nevertheless a relevant positive difference when original possession is independently warranted. The bearer then has actual efficacy otherwise than through reception from the target inventory. Conditional on that actual resource, the inventory's null configuration need not be preserved. A source-independent contribution has been identified, rather than a nonzero assignment simply asserted for the purely received field. Whether that contribution is necessary, unique, cognitively intrinsic or perfectly trustworthy remains a further question.

### 5.5 Complete explanation need not necessitate its effect

Correct. The argument rules out a specific E_L claim, not every E claim. A necessary bearer can have a contingent exercise, and an ordinary adequate causal explanation can be nondeterministic. Requiring law-only unique determination of every E-explanation would silently add a strong premise. The present investigation has not earned it.

The law-only route matters because it is an independently proposed answer to the problem of non-well-founded completion. The limitation on its purely receptive existential adaptation is useful even though the full law package can meet its own declared criterion and another active account can choose a different explanatory form.

## 6 Positive controls preserving the intended source route

An original contributor can sustain an infinite subordinate ancestry. For a receiving subinventory, once its actual outside contribution is included, the receiving network need not be absence-preserving. The argument therefore imposes no temporal beginning and no requirement that every subordinate causal path terminate after finitely many links. For P as the inventory of all contingent concrete bearers, an outside concrete contributor cannot itself be contingent. If an original contributor is contingent and belongs to P, its own existential actuality remains in the target; the positive control does not explain it for free.

Several original contributors can jointly sustain the target. Nothing here turns joint sufficiency into independent completeness of each contributor or applies the inherited source-uniqueness theorem to partial causes. The required existential contribution may be plural.

The target's ontology must remain fixed. If P inventories contingent substances, a necessary bearer's contingent exercise may belong to Q without becoming another substance in P. The null test concerns absence of productive suppliers in the represented existential respect. It does not remove every contingent fact or demand that a necessary source necessarily choose this effect. If a different argument inventories acts or all contingent facts, it has a different target and must repeat the adequacy assessment.

A genuinely nonproductive existential law remains a distinct option. It is not refuted by insisting that absent producers do not act. The affirmative causal route must defend why the actual existential target requires concrete productive participation; a law-grounded competitor must defend its real explanatory content. Neither side wins by calling its basis a law, a source or a complete explanation.

These controls show why the proposed criterion is not simply roothood by definition. The null-preservation result concerns a precise kind of supply claim and its resources. It tolerates infinite ancestry, multiple contributors, contingent acts, nonnecessitating explanation and noncausal alternatives at their declared scope.

## 7 The strongest basic-rational defence after these tests

The positive reason begins with a real difference in explanatory contribution. A received agent's actual exercise can explain a recipient's existence. The source of the giver's own possession remains a different existential respect. An account offered for the inventory's existential availability should not quietly move that same availability into an unexplained domain restriction and then credit only the receiving laws for explaining it.

This is a justified target-sensitive criticism in the law-only adaptation just examined. It does not rest on measurement, a neutral algorithm or universal agreement. It is supported by understanding what the account takes as input and what it claims to account for. This is actual warrant for a judgement about the offered explanation's resource placement, not new actual-world warrant that an original source exists. A merely describable infinite alternative does not defeat this criticism, and the two new rival defences do not answer it: existence distribution does not settle explanatory closure, while non-well-founded qualitative uniqueness can still require existential input.

The broader proposed principle is collective nonpresupposition: a productive account of the whole declared existential target must not obtain all of its existential availability solely by taking that target's received availability for granted. A direct rational appreciation of that requirement can have basic evidential standing. It need not be reconstructed as a finite derivation from local irreflexivity. It is independently intelligible because it distinguishes an answer to the target from an explanation of further features conditional on that target.

But the word 'taking for granted' must be earned in the actual Q case. Q claims genuine distributed productive discharge, not merely a law with missing inputs. There is no first inactive node. The positive source advocate cannot infer that the entire target is an antecedent collective resource merely from the fact that each act needs its own bearer. Nor does the opponent settle collective adequacy merely by saying that every local requirement is fulfilled. A basic presentation must concern this joint explanatory respect, rather than retroactively enlarging the sound local judgement.

The present research gives a stronger, concretely targeted affirmative reason against one developed law-only answer and defeats one semantic argument for unrestricted explanatory distribution. It does not find that a metaphysical infinitist world has been refuted or that the full E-application is now rationally compulsory. This is a differentiated warrant judgement: the specific application criticism is justified, the original-possession comparison retains real prima-facie force, and the universal E-exclusion remains open. Nothing here concludes that a person with a genuinely independent and correctly targeted basic presentation lacks justified belief merely because the investigative record has not reproduced that presentation.

## 8 A failed shortcut retained for accountability

We tested whether a logical or constitutive explanation of the inventory sentence's truth would automatically satisfy broad E while preserving no existential productive contribution. That shortcut was not established. Logical explanation of some compound propositions does not prove that an arbitrary witness-presupposing derivation is an adequate explanation of this exact timeless inventory. A conjunction padded with the conclusion, a mere list of satisfying witnesses, or an epistemic proof that the count is correct is insufficient by itself.

The source route's target-specific productive condition must be evaluated at its strongest content. It cannot be neutralized just because another explanatory kind exists elsewhere. This failed branch is not a new counterexample to the route and receives no substantive result credit. The already charged Ninth E/E* diagnosis remains the controlling prior result.

## 9 What changes in the programme

For avenue1, the new usable result is a resource-sensitive rejection of a specified law-only existential completion and a strengthened test for proposed active-infinite rivals. No actual necessary participant has yet been obtained. If an independent E-adequacy defence and the route's other target and transfer premises earn one, it should be reported without waiting for P1/P2 or unity.

For avenues2 and3, the result prevents a qualitative or functional determination theorem from being treated as an original existential source of the cognitive or normative bearer. It does not undermine the admitted correction's real cognition or target-relative correctness. Nor does it establish that a source's original production of those capacities supplies intrinsic Knowledge, comprehensive practical governance or normative authority.

For the joint rival assessment, the active order is stronger than an uninstantiated rule but weaker than a completed explanatory account merely because each link is labelled actual. The new work tests two contentful ways of defending its completeness and identifies what each can and cannot contribute. The rival remains a live challenge rather than a certified cosmos; the affirmative remains a live rational route rather than a completed necessity proof.

The next discriminating question is now narrower: can the actual configuration Q give a non-law-only E-explanation of P whose distributed reception discharges the existential target, or does a directly defended target-relative nonpresupposition judgement exclude exactly that explanation? Returning only to the uniqueness of qualitative values or distribution of existence will not answer it. Neither will another finite modal table.
