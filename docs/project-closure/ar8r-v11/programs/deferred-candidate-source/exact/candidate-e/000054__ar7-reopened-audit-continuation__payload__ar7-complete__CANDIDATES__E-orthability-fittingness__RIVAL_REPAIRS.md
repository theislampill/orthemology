# Rival repairs — Candidate E

## Constitutivism

**Objection.** Normative standards can be constitutive of belief, agency, or inquiry and need not be current endorsements.

**Repair.** Accepted. Candidate E now targets only token-autonomous authority. Type-level constitutivism is classified as a trans-state standard, not refuted.

**Counter-repair burden.** Determine whether recursively corrigible constitutive standards can be fixed without primitive normative facts or a holistic equilibrium.

## Shmagency / authority objection

**Objection.** Showing that a standard constitutes agency does not show why one has reason to comply rather than become a “shmagent.”

**Repair.** Candidate E does not derive authority from constitution. It identifies the source options once objective correction is assumed. Thus it avoids claiming that constitutive status itself supplies categorical authority.

**Residual burden.** The existence and authority of objective corrigibility remain premises, not conclusions.

## Naturalized normativity and teleofunction

**Objection.** Historical selection or organismic organization can make current tokens defective and corrigible.

**Repair.** Accepted as a trans-token horn. The argument asks whether this yields only biological malfunction or also categorical correctness, fittingness, and reason-giving authority.

**Residual burden.** A bridge from selected effect or organization to robust normative authority is unestablished.

## Reflective equilibrium and coherentism

**Objection.** Normative authority may arise from a holistic fixed point, so no external standard is necessary.

**Repair.** Accepted as a distinct horn. The theorem denies only unilateral token autonomy.

**Residual burden.** Determine whether multiple equally coherent equilibria make correctness relative, and what fixes truth-linkage across equilibria.

## Brute normative realism

**Objection.** Objective correction may be primitive.

**Repair.** No deductive defeat. Brute normativity supplies exactly the trans-token authority the theorem requires while resisting teleology and Wisdom.

**Residual burden.** Any preference for a unified purposive ground must be abductive and independently defended.

## Error theory

**Objection.** There are no objective correction facts.

**Repair.** Candidate E is conditional. An independent argument from unavoidable assessment practices, deliberative indispensability, or self-defeat would be needed, and none is assumed here.

## Social-practice constructivism

**Objection.** Objectivity can be practice-internal and survive individual correction.

**Repair.** This shows that agent-transcendence is not practice-transcendence. Candidate E now reports the exact level of transcendence instead of treating all objectivity as global.
