## New primary controls

### Boghossian and Richard

[Boghossian 2019](https://paulboghossian.com/pb/files/InferenceAgencyAndResponsibility.pdf), pp.101–124, argues from rational responsibility toward taking premises as supporting a conclusion. He distinguishes nonreflective reasoning from subpersonal transitions, allowing tacit guidance rather than demanding an explicit metajudgment. His reply to Richard distinguishes responsibility for a result or later correction from responsibility for the original transition. Crucially, pp.122–123 allow perceptual justification without taking, and the conclusion expressly leaves the positive analysis and regress worries unfinished. Thus his responsibility argument supports a desideratum; it cannot simply be installed as a completed reduction of guidance. The full chapter and notes were read.

[Richard's author manuscript](https://markrichardphilosophy.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/07/reasoning-and-agency.pdf), *Is Reasoning a Form of Agency?*, pp.1–14, was independently read, rather than inferred solely from Boghossian's quotations. It questions whether responsibility requires contemporaneous control and distinguishes a transition from later review. Its hypothetical continuity between adult and child mechanisms is not empirical verification of their identity. The manuscript leaves a general analysis of inference open. Its title and pagination differ from the published chapter; no final-version collation is claimed.

### Strongest constructive proposal

[Wu 2026](https://doi.org/10.1111/nous.70055), §§2–6, gives rule representations a biasing role: they select and configure a cognitive transition instead of joining its premise inputs. Automatic completion can coexist with control of the relevant aspect of an extended action. The proposal supplies a specific functional alternative to merely adding a further belief that the transition is licensed. His §6 explicitly distinguishes knowing a rule from possessing its integrated application capacity and leaves the learning implementation for empirical investigation. The broad learning-theoretic use of knowledge does not by itself establish warrant. His naturalistic reductio is a philosophical commitment, not empirical disproof of nonnaturalism.

This is positive causal-explanatory progress at hypothesis level. Its regress escape is conditional, not a demonstration that any selector is reason-guided: the selected premise-to-conclusion coupling must actually deploy the relevant contents. The argument from multiple admissible conclusions establishes a selection problem; it does not uniquely establish this implementation. His spatial-cueing analogy is offered as support for the hypothesis, not a completed deductive-reasoning experiment. Its declared Pavese/Dretske lineage prevents treating convergence as wholly independent corroboration. The full argument, both notes, and references were read in publisher HTML; figures were available as descriptions/captions, without successful image inspection.

### Executed empirical citation check

[Aitken et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.3001023), Results, Discussion and Experimental Procedure, provided the bounded check of one cited bridge. The final sample comprised eighteen participants. Coloured cues predicted grating orientation; the behavioural task judged differences between two gratings. The authors explicitly designed the tasks to avoid a direct expectation-to-response relationship. On diagnostic omission trials there was no grating and no task response beyond maintaining fixation. Expected orientation nevertheless modulated deep-layer V1 activity. The discussion separates expectation from attention and cautions that the between-task deep-layer contrast was not significant.

This supports preparatory, stimulus-specific influence. It does not directly test selection of an inferential response, deployment of a logical rule, or epistemic basing. That restriction is fixed by the experiment's actual design, not a fabricated psychological rival. It neither invalidates the neural result nor discredits the wider philosophical hypothesis. Main article text through methods was read; image-encoded equations, supplementary bodies, raw data and code were not reanalysed. No numerical replication is claimed.

## Exact inferential check

Here is our application of the inherited role distinction, not a new mathematical theorem.

Let Γ name the currently entertained grounds, r a represented rule, and q the accepted conclusion. The disputed regress starts only if every effective use of r requires adding a proposition about r to Γ and then inferring again by a further rule. That universal input-role requirement needs an argument. A control-role proposal denies it: r regulates which operation is performed on Γ. Accordingly, showing that r is not an input premise does not show that Γ is unused.

The converse shortcut also fails. A valid derivation from Γ to q describes support; a verified checker describes a computation that checks it. Neither alone establishes that a person's q-acceptance arose through that support. The distinction is between a relation holding and its actual operative use. The selector cannot earn this latter fact merely by being named a rule-controller.

The positive result therefore has a precise strength: the premise-adding objection does not defeat an independently realised control-role account. The residual challenge is to characterise and identify that realisation, including nondeviant content-use, rather than to reiterate the same regress. This does not demand a separate meta-inference by the reasoner, nor a prior theory of their own warrant. No primitive capacity is disqualified merely for being basic.

## What a further discriminator must preserve

For an actual identified reasoning task, independently establish what the representations mean, then separate three interventions: changing the rule-content while preserving the available grounds; changing grounds relevant to the selected rule; and changing only attention, response labels, or motor preparation. A useful result must identify the current coupling by which those grounds inform acceptance, including retained or redundant grounds. Endpoint accuracy, a fluent explanation, or a correct certificate alone is insufficient for that attribution. Failure of one but-for contrast is likewise insufficient when other grounds remain operative.

This is a prospective evidential specification, not a new human episode, executable cognitive model, or completed experiment. The present source check supplies no dataset with the required rule/ground manipulations, so no further numerical exercise is warranted here. The exact local obstruction is missing application evidence for the proposed current coupling, not an absence of any positive account.
