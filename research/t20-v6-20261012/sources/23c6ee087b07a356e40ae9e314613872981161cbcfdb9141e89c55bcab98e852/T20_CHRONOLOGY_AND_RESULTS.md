A spurious formal positive trajectory may violate later law validity.

Explicit 2-atom/three-moment description, finite information with known family, and limits at merging/boundary. - **Nonlinear/nonconvex gradients and computable stationary laws:** polynomial energy conditions support some more general flows, but no guarantee of global optimum or general convergence. Stationarity, unique stable law and computable selection need distinct assumptions. Long-time limits can lose information even when finite-time transport is faithful.
