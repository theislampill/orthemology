## Epistemic and moral limits

The author sometimes rejects convenient readings, insists on grammatical and contextual controls, acknowledges truths held by opponents, and distinguishes reported from chosen positions. These norms should govern use of this book too. Sweeping consensus claims, adversarial descriptions, miraculous narratives and natural-history particulars are source assertions, not certified external facts. Justice is not equated with bare possibility; responsibility requires intelligible access and relevant enabling capacity. The tension between original created receptivities, withheld effectual aid and deserved punitive closure remains a real philosophical question.
