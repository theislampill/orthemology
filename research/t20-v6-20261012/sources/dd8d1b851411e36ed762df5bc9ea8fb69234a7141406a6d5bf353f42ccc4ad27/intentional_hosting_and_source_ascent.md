# Intentional hosting and the source ascent frontier

## Finding

The most promising additional constraint is **constitutive, bearer-local intentional hosting**, strengthened by essential dependence when necessity is claimed. It is stronger than truth registration, successful correction, semantic invariance, and fittingness-sensitive control. Its ordinary motivation is substantial: a particular act of representing is somebody's act, and causing that act is different from being its subject. But that motivation applies most securely to representational particulars, not to an objective order or a repeatable proposition type. The positive ascent therefore turns on a specific ontological question:

> Does the necessary item supplied by objective evaluability have to be a necessarily instantiated, bearer-local representational particular, rather than a truth evaluated at every world, a representational type, an impersonal semantic structure, or a condition represented by contingent minds?

This formulation identifies where a real conditional bridge is available and where its main warrant burden remains. It also shows why “truth aim” is too coarse a proposed repair. Historical truth-conducive function can be impersonal. A subject's intentional commitment to truth already supplies mentality at that subject, but does not transfer mentality to its ultimate ground.

Two positive conditionals survive precise reconstruction:

1. A necessarily existing representational particular that essentially inheres in a particular intellectual bearer requires that bearer in every relevant possibility. This is a valid hosting-to-necessity inference, not a proof that necessary truth supplies such a particular.
2. A complete, actually ungrounded bearer is necessary if every actually existing contingent bearer has an actual ground of its existence. A weaker principle that contingent bearers *can* be grounded requires essentially ungroundedness instead. These are standard dependency results. They compress conditional burdens; they do not discharge the strengthened premises' warrant.

Neither result defeats the strongest one-bearer R5 architecture. What changes is the research target: defend or refute the **necessarily instantiated original-content premise at the proposed ground's own locus**, rather than adding further finite response profiles to R5.

## A serious rival must explain something

Deep BG supplies one structural bearer of modal order, truth registration, target key, norm, selection, efficacy and expression. It then establishes that its stipulated neutral reduct permits all assignments to nine further predicates. That correctly refutes entailment from that signature. It cannot by itself settle whether the all-false interpretation gives an adequate metaphysics of those roles. The next rival should consequently be stated as an explanatory architecture.

Consider the following enriched R5 proposal:

1. A fundamental impersonal powers structure determines physically and modally admissible transitions. It may be one bearer; no plurality of ultimate carriers is needed.
2. Particular organisms have producer–consumer arrangements. A state produced by one subsystem guides a consumer whose historically selected performance requires a specific correspondence with its environment.
3. Malrepresentation is possible because the correspondence can fail while the same producer and consumer continue to operate. Error is not defined as whatever the system dislikes after the event.
4. Local thinkers can possess original intentionality and reasons. Their existence does not make the underlying powers structure the subject of their thoughts. The proposal can accept genuine minds while denying an ultimate mental bearer.
5. Objective logical and normative conditions are primitive, essential to the powers structure, or handled by a separately specified realist account. The rival owes an account of this final clause; it cannot treat a table labelled “truth” as its explanation.

This proposal has costs. Teleosemantics faces content-individuation, historical, and novel-content problems; a fundamental powers ontology owes a modal account; irreducible value realism owes an account of authority. It is not established merely because these commitments are consistent in a finite signature. But it is substantially stronger than a mechanism with a detached `Personal = false` flag.

There is a corresponding positive architecture. One original intellect intentionally hosts the relevant contents; its knowledge of fitting ends explains its selection; its productive powers institute the faculty–world fit; objective standards are grounded in its nature rather than externally imposed. That can be a genuinely unifying explanation if the hosting, fittingness and production relations are independently defended. It too must explain why the proposed mind is necessarily truth-directed and normatively adequate. Calling its nature “perfect” cannot perform those derivations unaided.

The fair comparison therefore counts primitive or essential commitments on both sides. The impersonal model's unexplained norm is a real cost. So is an unexplained necessary intellectual nature whose truth and goodness have merely been stipulated. Equal numbers of predicates do not show explanatory equality, but unequal numbers of labels do not show superiority either.

## Why truth aim is not yet the separating constraint

At least four claims are often compressed into “aimed at truth”:

- **Output success:** the produced judgments are mostly true
- **Historical function:** truth-conducive correspondence played a causal role in retaining or reproducing the mechanism
- **Constitutive correctness:** the states are representations with determinate error conditions
- **Intentional commitment:** a subject takes truth as an end or as an undefeated epistemic reason

Their evidence differs. The first is distributional. The second is causal and historical. The third is a theory of content. The fourth involves an intentional subject. A strengthened intervention on current output can distinguish a truth-sensitive mechanism from an insensitive one without deciding the remaining claims.

Millikan's primary account grounds representation in conditions required for the historically normal success of a consumer, rather than in mere statistical regularity. This is a substantive reply to the demand for nonaccidental error conditions, not an assertion that any accurate signal already means something. [Millikan, “Biosemantics,” 1989, especially pp. 285–288](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Ruth-Millikan/publication/235734046_Biosemantics/links/54b9cd880cf253b50e2a9aa5/Biosemantics.pdf)

For a simple historical contrast, hold fixed a present mechanism whose response to target state 0 is 0 and to state 1 is 1. In history H1, retention probabilities are 0.9 following successful correspondence and 0.1 following its absence. In H2 a bottleneck retains the same mechanism with probability 0.9 independently of correspondence. The current input–output profile is identical; the proposed etiological fixing relation differs. This contrast is not a complete teleosemantic theory. It shows what evidence would actually advance the account-specific proper-function question: evidence about retention and mediation, not another current accuracy table.

Even H1 leaves a further question: was the mechanism selected for correspondence as such, for a consumer's resulting success, or for a correlated effect? A joint causal history and a content-individuation theory must answer that question. The toy retention table does not settle it by calling one coordinate `truth`.

Nor does selected function establish all-things-considered normative authority. Papineau explicitly separates a biological design history from prescriptive reasons and proposes deriving norms of judgment from moral or personal values. That view may be rejected, but it demonstrates that the naturalist can distinguish content fixing from norm authority rather than unknowingly conflate them. [Papineau, “Normativity and Judgement,” 1999, especially p. 21 n. 5](https://www.davidpapineau.co.uk/uploads/1/8/5/5/18551740/papineau-normativityjudgement-1999.pdf)

Plantinga's argument against naturalism is likewise more than a definition of proper function: it requires rejecting satisfactory naturalistic accounts of that notion. His own chapter abstract makes that conditional burden explicit. The existence of a truth-aimed design plan in a proper-function model must not therefore be silently converted into a demonstrated conscious designer. [Plantinga, *Warrant and Proper Function*, ch. 11](https://academic.oup.com/book/3187/chapter-abstract/144085408)

**Result:** historical truth-conducive function is an independently motivated additional constraint that can exclude accidental-success models. It does not exclude enriched R5. Intentional truth commitment excludes an impersonal *local subject* only because intentional subjecthood is now part of the explanation. Whether that subject is the ultimate ground is still open.

## The strongest hosting argument

The conceptualist case deserves reconstruction before criticism. A set of marks or an ordered tuple does not represent a particular state of affairs merely by having a shape. Representation has directedness, aspectual organization, and correctness conditions. Intentional mental activity supplies an independently familiar case of such unity. A theorist can reasonably ask whether positing sui generis impersonal representation explains those features or only renames them. That is an abductive argument for mental realization, not automatically a circular definition.

Anderson and Welty argue from necessary truths to necessarily existing propositions, then to mental items, and use essential thought–bearer dependence in the ascent to a necessary mind. They explicitly address the objection that each world might instead contain different contingent thinkers. Their 2011 footnote 31 supplies essential ownership, so it would be inaccurate to accuse them of simply overlooking the quantifier issue. [“The Lord of Noncontradiction,” §§5–8 and n. 31](https://www.proginosko.com/docs/The_Lord_of_Non-Contradiction.pdf)

Their 2013 reply also distinguishes human thoughts from the propositions they express. It defends an actualist truth-to-existence premise and denies that sharing propositional content makes human thought tokens numerically identical to divine ones. These replies remove two easy objections. They leave the substantive questions of propositional ontology, actualism and original mental realization. [“In Defense of the Argument for God from Logic,” pp. 2–9](https://www.epsociety.org/userfiles/art-Anderson-Welty%20(In%20Defense%20of%20the%20Argument%20for%20God%20from%20Logic).pdf)

Here is the relevant conditional in a notation that does not prejudge its inputs. Let W be the intended modal range, let t name one particular representational state, and let b name one bearer. Write E(w,x) for existence and H(w,b,t) for constitutive hosting. “Bearer-local” names a constitutive ownership relation here, not spatial location, material containment, a created temporal event, or a doctrinal account of divine attributes. Assume:

1. **Necessary particular:** for every w in W, E(w,t)
2. **Essential hosting:** for every w in W, E(w,t) implies H(w,b,t)
3. **Hosting factivity:** H(w,b,t) implies E(w,b)
4. **Mental realization:** this kind of constitutive hosting qualifies b as an intellectual bearer at w

Then b exists and is an intellectual bearer at every w in W. The proof is universal instantiation and modus ponens at arbitrary w. It requires no S5 possibility-to-necessity step and no assumption that only one mind exists.

This conditional does not define b as God, does not assume b's necessary existence, and does not use a target-specific “if b grounds truth then b is mental” axiom. Its hosting premise has independent motivation in ordinary representational particulars. The chief burden is instead premise 1 together with the ontological classification that permits premises 2 and 4 to apply to the same item.

The existence of necessary truth is not yet premise 1. At least four readings must be distinguished:

1. A proposition is true at every possible world
2. A proposition exists in every possible world
3. A representational type is instantiated by some occurrence in every world
4. One particular representational occurrence exists in every world

An actualist property-possession rule can connect 1 to 2 only with a further semantic identification: truth *with respect to* w must be a property that the proposition locally instantiates *in* w. A theory requiring all proposition types to be instantiated can connect 2 to 3. Essential ownership of each token does not connect 3 to 4. Nor does it directly connect 3 to the existence of one host common to every world.

That extra semantic identification is not forced merely by accepting Serious Actualism. Speaks develops a view of evaluation at a world intended to retain both Serious Actualism and contingently existing propositions. The distinction is relevant here even if his complete account is ultimately rejected: a defender of the ascent must argue for local truth-property possession, not just repeat that nonexistent objects cannot instantiate properties. [Speaks, “On Possibly Nonexistent Propositions,” 2012, §§1–3](https://www3.nd.edu/~jspeaks/papers/2012nonexistentprops.pdf)

This is not a bare logical objection to a doctrine already committed to necessary divine thoughts. It identifies what that doctrine must defend before the conditional becomes an argument from independently available premises.

### The cognitive type rival

A serious rival can accept the representational contribution of cognition while taking propositions to be repeatable cognitive event types. Soames distinguishes the representational properties of such types from actual tokening and explicitly permits truth at a world without existence at that world. His proposal also allows unperformed combinations of already available cognitive acts. Thus rejecting independent Platonic representation need not imply a necessarily occurring divine thought. [Soames, “Propositions as Cognitive Event Types,” author manuscript, pp. 8–10 and 17–20](https://dornsife.usc.edu/scottsoames/wp-content/uploads/sites/191/2023/11/NTP_Chapter_6.pdf)

The point is not that Soames's ontology is established. The conceptualist can challenge truth without existence or uninstantiated representational types. But the challenge must engage those views; the rival does not simply deny mentality by changing a predicate's truth value.

A further rival targets the supposed explanatory gap before adopting act types. Speaks's 2025 paper argues that the representational role of propositions need not be the same property instantiated by representing subjects; a theory that explains propositional attitudes can explain the former role without making propositions cognitive acts. Its linguistic and explanatory argument must be assessed, but it is another substantive alternative to inferring mentality from the word “represents.” [Speaks, “What Propositions Need Not Be,” 2025, especially §§3–5](https://jspeaks.site/papers/what-propositions-need-not-be.pdf)

A three-world illustration sharpens the difference. One type p is present at w0, w1 and w2. It has token t0 hosted essentially by h0 only at w0, token t1 hosted essentially by h1 only at w1, and token t2 hosted essentially by h2 only at w2. Every world has a genuinely hosted representation of p; every token has a fixed essential owner; no token or owner occurs in all worlds. The failure lies in replacing a repeatable type by one of its particulars, not in denying the local hosting principle.

### The locality rival

Even a mental basis of actual representation does not make every ultimate cause mental. Searle's primary account accepts intrinsic intentional states while explaining them through biological processes and locating them at the organized system level. That is a direct counterproposal to a blanket transfer from mental effects to mental causes; it is not a defense of mere syntactic simulation. [Searle, “Intentionality and Its Place in Nature,” 1984, pp. 5–6](https://www.newdualism.org/papers/J.Searle/Searle-Synthese-1984.pdf)

In the minimal relational picture, g grounds h and h hosts t. The conclusion that g hosts t does not follow. One may reject the naturalistic explanation, but a principle ruling it out must address why adequate grounding of original intentionality requires the very ground to instantiate the relevant intentional state. The ordinary premise that a thought has a subject only identifies h.

This distinction survives one ultimate bearer. If g is a fundamental field or a single concrete foundation supporting local subjects, making g the unique fundamental entity does not by itself make it the subject of every local experience or judgment. Mereological containment, causal ancestry, constitution of a substrate and intentional ownership are different relations.

### What would really defeat enriched R5

The strongest additional claim is now precise:

**Local original-content constraint:** the relevant fundamental ground has a truth-evaluable representational state in its own constitutive realization, whose content is not wholly derivative from downstream interpretation, a consumer history, an external semantic assignment, or a merely possible instance of a repeatable type.

Combined with a defended theory of original intentionality as intellectual hosting, this gives a genuine conditional B7 for that ground. It excludes the downstream-interpreter and mere-registration versions of R5. It is not harmlessly equivalent to observable semantic sensitivity. Its independent defense must show why objective evaluability entails this particular ontology of the ground, rather than already assuming that the ground represents the world.

At present the companion's T2 and Deep AI's H7a/H7b point toward this claim, but do not establish it. The strongest conceptualist argument gives it explanatory motivation; act-type theories and the locality distinction keep serious alternatives open. The result is a sharper contestable premise, not a neutral proof of mentality.

## Because of fittingness does not remove the locality problem

Reasons explanation is richer than covariance. A person may choose an act because they take its end to be fitting; in that explanation, the represented consideration contributes under its reason-giving aspect. A controller's output changing with a fittingness-labelled register does not by itself establish that explanation. Deep AA, AB, AZ, CI and CJ already establish the appropriate formal boundaries.

But the positive side should not be dismissed as just an undefined predicate. Ordinary explanations distinguish saving someone because their danger is a reason for aid from saving them accidentally or for a reward. Relevant evidence can include practical understanding, the organization of deliberation, responsiveness to objections, nondeviant causal history and stable integration of reasons. None is infallible, and some theories analyze them functionally. The resulting attribution is typically defeasible and theory-involving rather than an entailment from a finite transcript.

Grant the richest ordinary attribution to a local subject. It establishes a reason-guided act at that subject. It does not establish that the ultimate source of the subject chooses for those reasons. Nor does a reason-guided act alone establish that its reasons are objectively undefeated, that its end is good, or that the bearer has stable Wisdom. Those further burdens are not arbitrary extra hurdles; they distinguish intelligible cases such as sincere error, akrasia, limited knowledge and malicious planning.

The companion's irreducible-teleology premise T1 also leaves a logically wider alternative class than “intelligent designer.” If selection-for-fitness does not exhaust proper function, immanent teleology, objective reasons, nonreductive naturalism and primitive value remain candidates. T2 must positively eliminate or outperform those alternatives. A disjunction can be narrowed without being reduced to one member.

Accordingly, a better positive task is to defend a specific **explanatory exclusivity** principle: why must this objectively truth- and reason-directed relation be instituted by an intellect at the fundamental locus? The principle cannot be obtained by using intentional language to restate the relation whose intentionality is disputed.

## A bounded necessity advance

The bridge ledger's H2 says only that a restricted principle of sufficient reason licenses a positive ground. H4 separately requires underivability to entail necessity. Deep C already records anti-contingency/PSR as the required additional kind of premise; no new route is claimed here. [Deep C V2, §§4–5](https://github.com/theislampill/orthemology/blob/03e2604dfcef278b01c60d19b05370fcec4194b6/docs/project-closure/ar8r-v11/post-merge-proposals/pmr007-deep-a-bk/PROPOSED_THEOREM_FILES/PMR-007_DEEP_ROUND_C_INTEGRATED_COMPOSITE_RIVAL_AND_ASCENT_BOUNDARY_V2.md)

A useful refinement concerns the strength and scope of that principle. Let G(w,y,x) mean that y grounds x's existence at w, not merely one property of x. Let the relation range over all relevant grounds; a root in an incomplete record is insufficient. Let w0 be actual, with rigidly interpreted x.

Define:

- U0(x): E(w0,x) and no y grounds x at w0
- Uall(x): E(w0,x) and, wherever x exists, no y grounds x
- C(x): E(w0,x) and x is absent at some relevant possibility

Compare two target-independent explanatory principles:

- Pactual: each actually existing contingent x has an actual ground of its existence
- Ppossible: each existing contingent x has a ground of its existence in some possible world

**Conditional 1:** Pactual and U0(x) entail that x is necessary. If x were contingent, Pactual would provide a ground at w0, contradicting U0(x).

**Conditional 2:** Ppossible and Uall(x) entail that x is necessary. If x were contingent, Ppossible would provide a world at which x exists and is grounded, contradicting Uall(x).

**Failure:** Ppossible and U0(x) do not suffice. Three worlds and two possible bearers already witness failure while grounding is irreflexive and well-founded at every world. At w0, x and y exist and x grounds y. At w1 neither exists. At w2 both exist and y grounds x. Each bearer is contingent and possibly grounded; each world has acyclic grounding; x is actually ungrounded; neither bearer is necessary.

The failure is not dependent on permitting an empty world. A strengthened three-world, three-bearer witness has w0={x,y} with x grounding y; w1={y,z} with y grounding z; and w2={z,x} with z grounding x. Every world is nonempty and has one unique ungrounded root. Every bearer is contingent and grounded in some world, and none exists necessarily. Each world's grounding graph is acyclic. This is a witness to the quantified-premise gap, not a declaration that this ontology is metaphysically possible. With only two bearers, four nonempty worlds suffice for a no-necessary-bearer version; the exact checks record that refinement.

Three worlds are minimal for this kind of failure in a universal-accessibility frame with grounding factivity. The actual root cannot be grounded at the actual world. Contingency needs a world where it is absent. A grounding world can be neither of those and must be a third world. Two bearers are minimal if self-grounding is excluded.

This matters because weakening “has a ground” to “can have a ground” often makes an explanatory premise more attractive. The weakening changes which conclusion follows. Worldwise well-foundedness does not repair the modal shift; a relation can be acyclic in each world while its direction changes across worlds.

Modern necessity arguments use differentiated causal and modal principles rather than one undifferentiated PSR. Rasmussen's primary paper explicitly begins one path from possible causation and supplies additional argumentative steps. The small countermodel above is not a refutation of that paper or of all weak-PSR arguments. [Rasmussen, “From States of Affairs to a Necessary Being,” 2010](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/225502686_From_States_of_Affairs_to_a_Necessary_Being)

Pactual has independent motivation in the idea that contingent existence calls for an explanation of why it obtains rather than fails to obtain. Its challenge is equally specific: a critic may allow brute contingent existence, reject existential grounding, or accept only possible explanation. The standard principle is general rather than tailored to g, so the argument is not definitionally circular. Nevertheless, using it moves the anti-brute necessity burden into the premise. It establishes no new shared warrant merely by giving H4 a derivation.

A ledger could therefore record H4 as derivable under one explicitly strengthened H2 variant, while retaining H4 as open under the actual weaker owner wording. That is a research recommendation, not an owner edit. It does not make a necessary bearer concrete unless that bearer was already concrete; does not yield unique fundamental reality; and does not entail intellect. Enriched one-bearer R5 can grant both positive conditionals' conclusions.

## Source ascent under its own authority

Source acceptance can legitimately change the candidate class. It is not intrinsically question-begging for an Atharī argument to use authenticated revelational premises, any more than a causal analysis is question-begging for using independently warranted empirical premises. The crucial issue is what kind of authentication and warrant has actually been supplied, and which conclusion is being claimed.

Three matters should remain distinct:

1. Documentary authentication: these words are accurately transmitted from this human source
2. Source identification: the relevant disclosure originates from the proposed revealed bearer
3. Propositional warrant: the asserted predicate is true of that bearer, and the recipient has an adequate basis for accepting it

One can have the first without the latter two. Conversely, a theological epistemology can defend the latter by means other than a school-neutral deduction. The argument must declare and defend that route; it need not pretend every warranted religious belief was inferred from a neutral finite model.

The Atharī companion separates Allah's Knowledge, capacity and attribute of Speech, particular acts of Speech, and created acts of recitation. It also separates the bearer of an attribute from a created conduit. This is relevant to the hosting question: a source-relative locus rule may rule out attributing a created conduit's speech to a cause solely because it produced the conduit. That does not by itself prove a school-neutral causal-adequacy principle. [Atharī companion, §§3–6](https://github.com/theislampill/orthemology/blob/03e2604dfcef278b01c60d19b05370fcec4194b6/companion/orthability-divine-attributes-and-speech-athari.md)

The Specialist B packet reports translated-primary Aṣfahāniyyah loci concerning necessary existence versus Creatorhood, attribute locality and Names. The initial pass inspected that repository report rather than the supplied translation bytes. A subsequent targeted search located an Arabic digital rendering of the four-part locality argument; the separate Arabic readback records that narrower new access finding. It does not establish print-edition collation, the translation's exact base, or the six-guard package as one historical theorem. [Specialist B round 3](https://github.com/theislampill/orthemology/blob/03e2604dfcef278b01c60d19b05370fcec4194b6/docs/project-closure/ar8r-v11/post-merge-proposals/six-lane-v14-intake/specialist-b/source-files/04_ROUND_3_QIYAS_SPEECH_NAMES_AUTHORIZATION.md), [Arabic digital rendering](https://www.islamicbook.ws/ageda/alaqidt-alasfhanit-001.html)

Deep AX already gives the proper evidential alternative to taking source truth as a deductive premise: compare independently specified architecture-conditioned joint source likelihoods. It does not supply those likelihoods merely by authenticating a text. Deep CR then shows the operational consequence of warranted source restriction: the inherited standard family can narrow and restoration can become feasible. Neither result requires calling source custody itself a proof of world truth. [Deep AX](https://github.com/theislampill/orthemology/blob/03e2604dfcef278b01c60d19b05370fcec4194b6/docs/project-closure/ar8r-v11/post-merge-proposals/pmr007-deep-a-bk/PROPOSED_THEOREM_FILES/PMR-007_DEEP_ROUND_AX_SOURCE_TESTIMONY_LIKELIHOOD_AND_WORLD_GATE_V2.md), [Deep CR V2](https://github.com/theislampill/orthemology/blob/03e2604dfcef278b01c60d19b05370fcec4194b6/docs/project-closure/ar8r-v11/post-merge-proposals/six-lane-v14-intake/central/source-files/PROPOSAL_ARTIFACTS/deep_cr/PMR-007_DEEP_ROUND_CR_FITRAH_SOURCE_RESTRICTION_AND_RESTORATION_V2.md)

The refined source question is therefore: which independently warranted source-identification or content-preservation relation makes the revealed bearer the very bearer targeted by the proposed metaphysical bridge? Answering that may establish a source-conditional B7/B11/B15 attribution. It will not turn that attribution into a consequence of the neutral structural reduct.

## Checks and their limits

The accompanying `bridge_models.py` checks structural examples rather than assigning nine arbitrary personal predicates. Its current results are:

- 216 admissible three-world, two-object existence/acyclic-grounding structures examined
- 82 actual-root cases under Pactual, with no necessity counterexample
- 42 essential-root cases under globally imposed Ppossible, with no necessity counterexample
- 12 actual-root counterexamples under Ppossible
- No such Ppossible/actual-root counterexample in the two-world check, as the analytic minimality argument predicts
- 343 covered three-world hosting relations examined; 174 have no common host
- The necessary-type/worldwise-token example satisfies essential ownership of every token while lacking any necessary token or host
- The grounder–host distinction and the two historical retention mechanisms are explicitly instantiated

The first two positive implications are proved analytically above; exhaustive finite checking is only a consistency and deletion check. The model classes are stipulated. None of these counts estimates metaphysical probability or licenses a claim that all represented structures are genuine possibilities.

The scripts also do not claim that intellectual hosting can be detected by inspecting a relation table. The table illustrates what a defended constitutive interpretation would imply. Establishing that interpretation is the philosophical task.

## Research disposition

The positive route has not been reduced to an empty conditional. It has a serious independent motivation in the ontology of representational acts, and the essential-host inference is valid once its object is secured. But the conditional's most attractive ordinary premise is precisely the one least safely exported to necessary proposition types or global conditions of evaluability.
