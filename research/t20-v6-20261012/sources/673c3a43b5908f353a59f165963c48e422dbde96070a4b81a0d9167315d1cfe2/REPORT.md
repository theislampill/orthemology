## Result

**Ibn Taymiyyah offers a substantive alternative to a circular testimony-first argument: an observed effect can disclose a previously unacknowledged producer, and an appropriately contextualized sign can disclose sender and messenger together.** His second route explicitly does not require antecedent Creator acknowledgment. Thus corroboration need not be a loop in which an already trusted messenger first supplies the very source on which that trust depends.

The source's copyist example makes the first step intelligible without treating an assertion as self-authenticating. Its conclusion concerns a producer capable of the displayed copying. The adjacent royal example does different work, concerning the purpose of a contextual response. The theological joint route requires both aspects to be adequately grounded in the same occurrence. The copyist example alone supplies neither commission nor unrestricted veracity.

The new work is this exact dependency-and-target test of the source's two repairs, applied to corroboration under a specific challenge to antecedent Creator recognition. ## 3. What the copyist case actually establishes

The source fixes more than a generic surprising endpoint. The dispute concerns whether there is a copyist inside the house. The scene contains a blank sheet, a poem prepared in that situation on another sheet, a request to copy it, and the return of the sheet bearing that poem. The observer can compare the displayed input and output. This is an ordinary action-based discovery of an otherwise unseen producer in the described setting.

The relevant evidential direction is from the observed production to the producer. It does not run from accepting the alleged copyist's assertion that he exists to accepting that assertion because he exists. The claimant's speech specifies what response to inspect; it need not already be trusted as a report about the hidden person. Knowledge of the copying performance can therefore arise without prior assent that this copyist exists.

This is a substantive noncircular pattern, not merely two unsupported propositions relabelled as mutual support. Its ordinary grounds include perception of the materials and their change, retention of the comparison content, understanding the request, and competent causal interpretation in that setting. The scene's freshness contributes to identifying this responsive performance. It is not a cryptographic protocol, an entropy estimate, or a guarantee against every possible substitution. No modern experimental controls are silently inserted.

The conclusion must remain proportionate. Copying the poem can disclose a capacity to reproduce the displayed text; it does not by itself prove understanding of every meaning in the poem. Still less does it prove omniscience, moral perfection, uniqueness of the producer, or the claimant's appointment. The request in this example is to copy if a copyist is there, not to attest a commission. This is an important difference from the adjacent royal case.

Ordinary knowledge here need not wait for an exhaustive investigation of imaginary tricks. A warranted indication of actual substitution or a defective observation could matter. Bare logical possibilities do not erase the positive content of the described case. ## 4. The theological joint route and its additional work

The source's stronger proposal combines two evidential contributions: an effect indicates its capable producer, and its setting indicates that it was brought about to attest the claimant. The second cannot be supplied simply by calling the first a miracle. Conversely, the fact that two contributions occur together does not make the reasoning circular. One sufficiently informative encounter can disclose a relation and its previously unknown relata.

The royal example illustrates intelligible confirming purpose; the copyist example illustrates discovery without antecedent producer assent. The author uses them together to explain how an appropriately discriminating sign could do both. He also argues that the selected transformation manifests a source beyond created competences. The present unit does not independently reconstruct the entire warrant for that last exclusion, and this report does not replace it with the inference that failure by some performers proves inability of every possible creature.

Three application premises therefore remain substantive: what actually occurred, what grounds its attribution to the relevant source, and what grounds its confirming purpose in that setting. They can be jointly supported without a rigid psychological acquisition sequence. They cannot be made true merely by joining the labels “source” and “commission.” The received positive contextual-attestation results remain pertinent to the third premise; the earned original-source arguments remain pertinent to sourcehood. Neither is reset by this test.

For a present reader, learning the scriptural narrative and witnessing the narrated event are different evidential positions. If the narrative is accepted solely through the very divine authority being established, it cannot simultaneously be counted as an independent historical authentication of that authority. Independently warranted testimony may convey the event, but its actual grounds must be identified rather than invented. This operation provides no new transmission reconstruction.

This warning concerns the ground for accepting a reported occurrence, not the provenance of an argument. Earlier in this same connected unit, Ibn Taymiyyah expressly allows a route to be scriptural because revelation directs one to it, and rational because its correctness is known by reason. Learning an independently appreciable argument from scripture therefore need not presuppose accepting scripture's authority. The hidden-copyist reasoning can be assessed for its rational force without first authenticating the Moses narrative. This is inherited rational/revealed compatibility used here as a scope control, not another source discovery.

The positive anti-circle gain remains: the account does not universally require Creator assent before any source-disclosing sign can be understood. It permits direct evidential acquisition in the relevant sense. It does not establish that every actual sign interpretation has the needed warrant or solve CP03's stronger first-singular-reference problem.

## 5. Corroboration under the actual challenge

A challenge specifically to the origin or soundness of an antecedent Creator-directed seeming need not defeat the copyist-like route's ordinary perceptual and inferential grounds. If those grounds are genuinely operative and adequately support the theological application, they supply a route to the same conclusion without borrowing warrant from that disputed seeming. This is the concrete source-owned alternative relevant to corroboration.

But a challenge to perception, causal inference, memory, or contextual-intention recognition may reach this route too. Calling it external, scriptural, or miraculous does not make it independent of those capacities. Independence from prior Creator assent is narrower than independence from all reason or recognition. A universal-faculty undercutter cannot honestly be recast as a merely local objection to prior God-belief so that the second route appears to escape it.

Two further conclusions follow at the actual targets. First, this independent route could warrant Creator-existence without certifying the original seeming's acquisition history or restoring its warrant. Convergence on the same content is not retrospective certification of each converging mechanism. Second, one event bearing on producer and commission is not automatically two independent evidential acquisitions. Joint disclosure must not inflate a cumulative case by counting its linked conclusions as separate sources.

The latest CP16/CP23/T17 support-scope appraisal remains affirmative. Its rational arguments need not be reacquired through the challenged basic-recognition route merely because they concern the same Creator. Whether a particular challenge reaches their actual premises is determined by the already preserved dependencies. No additional proof of necessity or universal sourcehood is claimed here.
