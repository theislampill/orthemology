# What pathway/result mathematics can preserve here

## Inherited distinction

The fitrah warranting-basis clarification already separates causal occasion, adequate warranting ground, and retrospective attribution. The previous productive-identifiability result separately shows that an endpoint observation can leave productive interpretations unidentified unless an appropriately calibrated, admissible observation/intervention scheme is supplied. These are inherited results, not new theorems or fresh primary verifications in this addendum.

## Legitimate methodological connection

Current Q-belief, its truth, and a matching list of P/R/Q contents are a coarser description than the actual route by which the subject formed or maintained Q. An observation map that forgets which source was consulted or which content guided the relevant response may lose information needed for a basing attribution.

This is an application of the ordinary factor-through constraint: a property cannot be recovered from a readout if it varies among admissible cases that the readout identifies. The mathematical conditional itself is already known. The philosophical work is to justify the admissible cases and the property, rather than attach different “basis” labels to output-equivalent machines and announce a discovery.

## The failed direct map

The earlier formal route models use specified physical or abstract productive occurrences, guards, source supports, and calibrated interventions. A mental state with content P is not automatically one such independently gateable occurrence. Neither an introspective report nor a numerical route label establishes that P was the subject's epistemic basis. Furthermore, a causal path can lead through attention to an independently warranting source. Merely recovering that causal path still does not classify every edge as an inferential ground.

Consequently:

1. The existing endpoint inverse cannot recover rational basing without a validated encoding and intervention semantics.
2. A controlled change in evidence, motivation, or context is not a free reassignment of a state's meaning.
3. A manipulation that destroys semantic function or changes the very task cannot automatically count as a clean test of the original claim.
4. Equality of all modeled endpoints is not equality of complete present physical states, histories, epistemic relations, or cognitive architecture.
5. Distinguishing two paths need not imply that one outcome is unwarranted. Different paths can support the same proposition by different good grounds.

No direct transport to original-source nonduplication is available: present representations and learned organizations remain dependent processes, not separately original complete producers. Nor does this failure undercut that separate metaphysical argument.

## Why no new toy is earned

A new two-path script would either repeat the inherited information-loss conditional or stipulate the disputed basing predicate. A new evidence-support bit would encode a normative judgment; a “takes-as-reason” flag would encode a psychological condition. Neither would explain its own interpretation. The present work therefore stops before adding such variables. Its contribution is a source-grounded discriminator and a sharper application boundary, not a new formal certification of cognition.
