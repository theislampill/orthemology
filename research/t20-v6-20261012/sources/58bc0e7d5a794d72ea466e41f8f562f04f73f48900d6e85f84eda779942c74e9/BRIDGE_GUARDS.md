# P15 bridge guards

1. **Hard P14 dependency:** P15 consumes the completed P14 checkpoint. The bundled P14 input packet is not a substitute. If the P14 result is absent, stop `BLOCKED_P14_RESULT_ABSENT`; do not re-run P14.
2. **No forced positive inheritance:** `REJECTED_BRIDGE`, `PARTIAL`, or negative P14 results remain binding inputs. P15 must weaken or reject its own route accordingly.
3. **Relative result, not universal Hume theorem:** disagreement among admissible normative extensions establishes underdetermination only relative to the specified descriptive/normative partition and bridge class. It does not prove every version of Hume's is–ought thesis.
4. **Bridge class cannot be arbitrary:** if `B(D)` permits every imaginable valuation, disagreement is trivial. Admissibility conditions need independent motivation and nonvacuous positive/negative controls.
5. **No normativity smuggled into the descriptive base:** explicitly type which symbols/relations belong to `D`, which belong to the bridge/enrichment, and which are mixed or derived.
6. **Define entailment carefully:** if `D` and normative expansions have different signatures, use an explicit expansion/reduct semantics such as `D |=_B phi`; do not write ordinary model satisfaction ambiguously.
7. **Empty extension class is not automatically inconsistency:** `Ext_B(D)=empty` means no extension satisfies the selected admissibility regime. What follows depends on the regime; do not overinterpret it.
8. **“Canonical” needs mathematics:** choose a criterion—unique, definable from `D`, natural, equivariant under automorphisms, universal, etc. Mere absence of an obvious weighting is not a theorem of non-canonicity.
9. **Category language is optional:** reducts, fibres and sections may be useful, but only after categories/signatures/morphisms are explicitly defined. Do not use category-theoretic vocabulary as decoration.
10. **Scalar ranking reversal needs hypotheses:** specify the source order and allowed scalarisations. Positive linear weights can reverse rankings only under appropriate crossing-difference conditions; prove the exact statement.
11. **Preserve P14's symbol guard:** an application improvement cone is not canonical Orthemology trusted calculus `K`. Rename it.
12. **Feasible geometry is not evaluation:** strategy sets or mixed simplices do not define “better.” But this shows only that the chosen model lacks a comparison relation until one is supplied, not that normativity is metaphysically impossible.
13. **Stability is not correctness:** fixed point, Nash stability, convergence, truth, epistemic adequacy and normative optimality remain distinct. Supply countermodels and, separately, any sufficient bridge conditions.
14. **No automatic anti-naturalism:** a theory could independently constrain or uniquely determine a normative enrichment from descriptive structure. P15 must allow that possibility and state what evidence would defeat its underdetermination result.
15. **Supervenience is not identity:** even if normative differences require descriptive differences under some theory, that does not itself provide a reduction or derivation rule; conversely, P15's relative multiple-extension result does not settle global supervenience.
16. **No Moore/Hume conflation:** is–ought, naturalistic fallacy/open-question arguments, supervenience and preference-representation issues are distinct literatures. Keep them separated.
17. **General gap schema must be typed:** fact->value, evidence->ranking, observation->ontology, data->classification and model->decision may require different source/target signatures and admissibility classes. Do not universalise by metaphor.
18. **Bridge provenance is not authority:** a machine-readable certificate can expose assumptions and dependence; it cannot certify that a normative premise is substantively correct merely because it is explicit.
19. **No metatheory promotion:** P15 is application/meta-epistemic research. It grants no Girard/SCUU/OWOU, necessary-being, world-authority, or unrelated theorem credit.
20. **No global novelty closure:** P15 may survey literature needed for correctness and identify closest formal analogues. P09/U13 remains responsible for global novelty positioning.
