# Intermediate transport boundary and complete-flow repair evidence

This is a compact, standalone intermediate preservation packet. It is not an integration, an audit closure, a claim of empirical or philosophical identification, or T20 completion.

## Mathematical boundary

The pure-killing finite-rank theorem remains valid: fixed finite atom locations and finite locally integrable killing rates do not permit finite-time annihilation of a positive atom. Transport adds a distinct failure mode. The preserved damped-escape proof shows that smooth determinate moment snapshots, a uniform rank cap, ordinary tightness, and even strong fixed-order/exponential tail bounds need not prevent finite-time loss of an atom at spatial infinity. The explicit forward propagation is noninjective. Its separate reverse-inflow construction gives genuine forward nonuniqueness for the same initial law and full moment time jets in the unrestricted distributional/smooth-moment class, under its own reversed coefficients.

The constructive repair uses complete two-sided characteristics, appropriate regularity, and a locally uniform finite-rank promise to obtain a unique recoverable finite-atomic continuation. Exact hypotheses and distinctions between the linear and normalized equations are in the preserved positive proof; this summary does not weaken them. A second repair replaces two-sided completeness with forward completeness and a global lower killing bound on each finite horizon. It gives uniqueness and injectivity with an inverse only on the actual forward image, not surjectivity onto all finite laws. Autonomous coercive polynomial gradient/selection dynamics satisfy this second criterion, still subject to the rank promise. No novelty claim is made.

## Evidence and review boundaries

Author directories contain authored proofs and exact finite symbolic controls. Independent reviewer directories remain separate and retain their original acceptance text and byte bindings. Five predecessor proof/report files are retained at original relative paths because the positive author suite verifies their input digests. Only those predecessor reports, not their complete historical source bundles or suites, are included. All preserved files are byte-for-byte copies; annotations live outside those evidence directories. The proofs and independent reviews are ordinary mathematics. SymPy controls test finite exact algebra, not all hypotheses or the whole proof. There is no proof-assistant/kernel verification in this packet and no claim that computational PASS formalizes the general theorem.

The source is Curto–di Dio–Korda–Magron, arXiv:2211.04416v3, printed pp.21–23 (Lemma 4.1, Theorem 4.2, Example 4.5). The original escape characteristic is credited to Example 4.5. The full paper and page images are excluded. Their exact hashes and source loci remain in EXTERNAL_BINDINGS.json and the original review binding. Existing reports describe source inspection performed at authorship/review time; this archive's replay does not repeat or certify that full-source inspection. Paths to excluded material inside preserved original reports are historical references, not an assertion that the material is included.

## Offline replay

Requires Python 3 and SymPy already installed. No network access, installation, source download, or original-evidence overwrite is performed.

1. Extract the ZIP into a new directory.
2. Run `python replay.py --output /absolute/path/to/a/new-output-directory` from the extracted packet directory.
3. Inspect the new output's REPLAY_RECEIPT.json and each suite's stdout/stderr.

The output directory must not exist. Replay first validates the exact included-file manifest, copies evidence into the new output, executes both author suites and both independent suites there, and checks regenerated JSON results against preserved bytes. It validates original review bindings for every included referenced file and explicitly records excluded source bindings as unverified. Finally it verifies that the extracted evidence remained unchanged. It does not freshly check excluded source bytes, literature novelty, all-order proof correctness, or any kernel artifact.

MANIFEST.json lists every included file except itself; the ZIP's external SHA-256 binds that manifest and the full archive. The external replay receipt binds the extracted manifest, environment, exact suite bytes and generated outputs. No circular self-hash is claimed.
