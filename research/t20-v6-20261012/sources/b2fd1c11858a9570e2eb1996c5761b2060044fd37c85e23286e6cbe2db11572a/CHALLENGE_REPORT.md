# Independent challenge: the reactive original-agency rival

7 October 2026 UTC. Prospective research review, not a summary vote, closure decision, or change to a historical archive.

## Finding first

The new rival genuinely strengthens the capacity result. It defeats a generalization from the inherited UNION law: an active fixed peer need not make any positive exact output unattainable to the other bearer. The idempotent Latin law gives an executable response for every positive target in every fixed peer-setting context, while preserving actual diagonal concord and the remaining command's exact output after diagnostic peer suppression.

It does **not** satisfy the source's stronger original-sovereignty or whole-original-provider standard. More importantly, its success does not independently establish that the standard follows from nonreceipt and intrinsic power. The source's direct remaining objections are (1) nonabsorption of unchanged positive original activity and (2) complete original efficacy entailing production through the agent alone. These are precisely the additional substantive premises the primitive interpretation contests. They must not be replaced by a generic disagreement argument, nor silently removed from the source.

There is no discovered arithmetic or finite consistency defect at the declared scope. There are three essential transport limits, all of which the author has now expressly accepted in correspondence:

1. Contextwise response witnesses are not one fixed unimpeded exercise, and some responses change the operative setting while preserving only the goal.
2. The ordinary joined epistemic model contains diagonal reason-context worlds only. Off-diagonal and suppression comparisons are diagnostic operational profiles, not certified all-premise wise worlds.
3. Successful output and active causal sensitivity do not establish whole original authorship. Actual two-root runs expressly lack individual entire-provider attribution.

Accordingly, the research judgement should change about **which finite capacity objection is available**, without changing to “a source-faithful full-premise countermodel has been established.” The strongest remaining discriminator concerns the adequacy of actual coalescent original efficacy, not a larger response table or a missing created knowledge mechanism.

## 2. What the new law actually earns

On the same event catalogue {e0,e1}, label the three nonempty exact targets 0={e0}, 1={e1}, and 2={e0,e1}. For active settings, the law is F(a,b)=2(a+b) modulo 3. None is typed idle and is an identity for one active setting; both idle create nothing.

The active table is:

- A setting 0: outputs 0,2,1 against B settings 0,1,2
- A setting 1: outputs 2,1,0
- A setting 2: outputs 1,0,2

Every row and column is bijective, the law is commutative, and F(q,q)=q. The unique response to a fixed peer b for goal q is a=2q−b modulo 3. The owned repertoire contains all three settings, so this is an actual executable witness in the declared model rather than a Boolean capacity flag.

Thus every positive target can be obtained against every fixed active peer setting. This response capacity is stronger than the parent UNION fixture. It also preserves the diagonal actual output and the same remaining exercise's solo output. These properties coexist in one specified primitive law; objections to XOR's lack of diagonal idempotence do not defeat this construction.

It is not a pointwise strengthening of every power property. For fixed own setting a and exact target q, the largest guaranteeing peer domain contains just the active setting b=2q−a modulo 3, with idle added exactly when a=q. Hence the actual full-target command's envelope is {idle,q}. In the inherited UNION law, the full-catalogue Q={e0,e1} command guaranteed Q against the whole available catalogue; the new law loses that robust guarantee. It gains universal contextual response while retaining concordant success, not every earlier success condition. This is a different primitive interpreter with a precise tradeoff.

### The tradeoff is class-wide, not an accident of this table

Let D be the declared domain of admissible own/peer exercise pairs, and F their single exact output. Suppose there are at least two eligible exact targets, and A can achieve each q against each fixed peer b: for every q and b there is an a with (a,b) in D and F(a,b)=q. Then B cannot possess any uniform exact-target guarantee over that same domain.

Proof: suppose a B exercise b* guarantees r across all its admissible A completions. Choose q different from r. A's response premise supplies a nonvacuously admissible (a,b*) whose output is q. B's guarantee requires that same output to be r, a contradiction. No full Cartesian-domain premise is needed beyond those common-domain response witnesses and the guarantee's range. If both agents have full contextual response capacity, neither has any uniform exact-target guarantee.

Consequently the inherited UNION top-target guarantee cannot be combined with the new opposite-side full reactivity in one unchanged comparison domain. This is ordinary quantifier duality, not new mathematics. Its research value is preventing a rival assembled by borrowing mutually incompatible favorable properties from different models. The conclusion can fail when there is only one exact target, when the alleged response is inadmissible, or when response and guarantee silently range over different contexts. Those conditions must remain explicit.

The strengthened conclusion remains finite and typed: output capacity over this positive catalogue, not unrestricted omnipotence over every metaphysically eligible object. The generic odd-cardinality construction broadens the algebraic family, but does not establish the source's unrestricted metaphysical interpretation.

## 3. Concrete counterexample to sovereignty transport

Fix A's intended target at 0 and actual setting at 0. With B at setting 0, the result is {e0}. Replace B's setting with 1 while freezing A's setting. The result becomes {e0,e1}; A's intended exact target fails.

A can restore its goal against B setting 1 by changing its own setting to 2. That adapted exercise produces {e0,e1}, not {e0}, when B is idle. Therefore the recovery preserves the intended goal, but neither the operative setting nor its solo manifestation.

This establishes three different facts, which should never be credited interchangeably:

- The unchanged diagonal exercise survives **idle suppression** of the peer.
- Every fixed active peer setting admits **some contextual means** to the desired exact target.
- No fixed own setting controls **any** exact target against all three active peer settings.

The second is a genuine strengthening of power. It is not the third. Omniscient knowledge of b makes selection of a suitable contextual means intelligible; it does not reverse the quantifiers. Conversely, failure of a uniform action is not automatically failure of the source's intrinsic-power claim. The source-to-uniform-control bridge needs its own justification.

Minhaj 1:438–441 is stronger than a claim about occasional conflict. It asks whether the agent's power is conditional on the other's enabling or nonprevention and distinguishes this from the agent selecting its own alternative. The new law removes the claim that an active peer necessarily excludes some available means to q. It retains the fact that the successful means varies with the foreign exercise. Whether this is objectionable foreign conditioning for an original, rather than permissible knowledgeable means selection, is still a source-specific normative and metaphysical question.

The improvement has a necessary cost under a natural stronger condition. Suppose an unchanged peer setting b produces a nonempty set S_b alone, and require that its solo-created items persist under any added activity: S_b is a subset of F(a,b). Then no added exercise a can obtain an exact target Q that omits an item of S_b. Thus full contextual response capacity across such targets requires rejecting that persistence condition. The Latin law does so: B's unchanged setting 1 creates e1 alone, but A's adapted setting 2 with it produces only e0. This is an extensional nonmonotonicity of manifestation. It need not mean e1 was first created and then destroyed; the declared primitive law has no such intermediate history. The final author's nonmonotonicity test now records this same semantic cost. Its bearing on an original's agency still depends on whether the operational counterprofile is a relevant capacity comparison, not an automatically admissible completed wise history.

## 4. The simultaneous test is narrower than two perfect agents' success

The actual per-peer response test assigns B both goal b and setting b. In all six cases with q different from b, A succeeds and B's intention is false. This was independently enumerated in `INDEPENDENT_PROBES.json`.

That is not a bug in reactive ability. It is a counterexample to an inflated reading of the test as simultaneous successful agency by two independently complete wills. The same output cannot equal two distinct exact target sets.

Solving both response equations, without ordering one agent after the other, yields three solutions for each shared goal q and no solution for unequal goals. Across three shared goals there are nine solved active profiles. Only three are diagonal; only those three occur in the current ordinary epistemic model. The intrinsic policy, not factivity or the Latin property alone, selects them.

This does not assume that opposed completed perfect wills are metaphysically admissible. It simply keeps two claims separate: each has a repertoire supplying a response to a fixed exercise; both can jointly realize all independently specified completed ends. The latter is not supplied, and must not be inferred by recombining reason-contexts.

## 5. Cognition and nonreceipt: genuine credit, exact boundary

The model does represent primitive original cognition. Each rigid bearer owns a standing knowledge token, the forecast is computed from the known intrinsic policies and fixed law before actual execution is compared, and all represented ordinary worlds are factive. Accessibility is identity over the finite reason-context domain; every proposition over that domain is known. The efficient graph has no foreign incoming path to the power or knowledge tokens, and the donor mutation is detected.

It would be unfair to reject this solely because no created cognitive mechanism is supplied. A truth relation, a common reason, and efficient receipt are different relations. The model also does not claim that its fixed token exhausts the intrinsic content of knowledge across all counterfactual comparisons.

The important limitation is that `epistemic_model` has only the diagonal ordinary worlds. An active off-diagonal response, or removal of a peer contrary to the known actual policy, is not itself one of those worlds. The unchanged ordinary forecast does not remain true after the suppression. This blocks an “all premises remain fixed” claim for the full diagnostic state; it does not defeat the same-exercise output comparison.

Nor does it defeat standing power. The continuous Minhaj power passage explicitly distinguishes intrinsic possibility and capacity from nonoccurrence fixed by true knowledge. A rival can therefore distinguish an available operational means from an ordinary completed wise history without simply defining power as whatever occurs. It could also represent primitive factive cognition in additional hypothetical settings; no theorem here forces such cognition to be efficiently donated by a foreign act. What it cannot do is hold mutually incompatible completed target-success claims true together.

The nonreceipt graph supplies a declared structural interpretation, not an independent derivation that every metaphysically relevant dependency has been included. If a contextual `respond(q,b)` rule is promoted to an actual policy, the role of b in its explanation must be specified. The mathematical argument b is not by itself proof of efficient donation; omitting a donation edge is not by itself a proof that the source's stronger notion of original efficacy is preserved.

## 6. The strongest affirmative objection survives at the actual diagonal

The remaining challenge should be posed even at (q,q), where both intended ends succeed and no disagreement occurs.

Majmu L908 maintains that adding an unchanged genuinely productive equal must produce more or greater created effect; unchanged joint and solo results cannot simply coincide. The new law denies that nonabsorption principle at every diagonal. Active replacement genuinely changes output, so it is false that the second act has no causal sensitivity whatsoever. Yet replacing an active setting and removing an activity are different interventions. Replacement sensitivity does not establish that the peer's actual addition creates an extra item or increases the original output. This is exactly where the primitive law and the source diverge.

Majmu L909–915 then goes beyond difference-making. Complete power plus decisive will is said to require the numerical effect's existence through that bearer alone. Actual sharing therefore entails incomplete power in the relevant original respect. The code's two-root supports deny individual entire provision, so it does not preserve this stronger premise. Calling the standing repertoire complete cannot solve that problem by nomenclature.

Still, the law is not refuted merely by restating the very implication it contests. An independent warrant is needed for the passage from unchanged actual success and intrinsic nonreceipt to exclusive original production, or from actual nonexclusive efficacy to received intrinsic perfection. The source supplies a serious argument through productive adequacy, nonabsorption, and the superiority of self-sufficient efficacy. The current law supplies a consistent thin counterinterpretation to weaker structural entailments; it does not establish which metaphysical conception is right.

The following safeguards matter:

- `supports={A,B}` is explicitly stipulated original-production provenance, not inferred solely from presence. The active sensitivity tests make inert tagging an inadequate criticism, but do not prove this is genuine perfected original causation.
- `entire` is defined by singleton support. It faithfully records the declared lack of entire-provider attribution; it is not an independent proof of a universal causal-nonduplication theorem.
- `created_history` lists the complete created output **in the declared ontology**. It does not certify that a physical or metaphysically complete account could omit additional traces. No hidden created mixer may be presumed simply because one expects physical mechanisms; no completeness credit follows merely from leaving one out.
- The effect labels preserve source-neutral identity under suppression by interpretation. This review does not establish a disputed origin-essentiality or transworld numerical-identity thesis.
- An intrinsic act is not automatically an additional created item. Counting A's and B's distinct intrinsic acts as extra created products would change the source's creating/product distinction.
- Ordinary creatures' causal partnerships are expressly admitted in the source's concluding creator/creature contrast. Demonstrating multiple sensitive ordinary causes therefore would not establish two complete original causes. This candidate supplies a primitive original interpretation, whose original adequacy is the disputed point; the `Original` class name does not prove necessary existence or maximal perfection.
- `own_activity_ends` checks successful intended output plus participation. It does not independently establish that every such coalescent exercise is fully wise, maximally good, or required by perfect wisdom. A disclosed own-full-target policy is stronger than absent intention, but not a proof of its normative warrant.

The later capacity-concentration argument, Majmu L916–921, must also retain its actual antecedent. It begins from cooperative incapacity to act alone, inferred in the preceding argument. This rival denies that incapacity at the source-neutral output level. One cannot simply reapply the concentration argument to that weaker target without first defending the original-efficacy inference. Conversely, a lone bearer with greater sovereign authority might instantiate a perfection these paired bearers lack; eligibility of that stronger perfection for each identified original remains a substantive issue.

## 7. What changes, and the highest remaining discriminator

Change the research appraisal in these respects:

1. Withdraw any generalized claim that every active peer destroys some intrinsic target capacity. That was true for UNION, not for this primitive law.
2. Give the new candidate credit for one joined diagonal interpretation containing executable standing power, own intended activity, reason-derived primitive cognition, structural nonreceipt, and genuine active-setting output sensitivity.
3. Keep the exact limitations visible: contextual response rather than uniform control; three actual diagonal epistemic contexts rather than every response profile; joint output rather than sole original provenance.
4. Do not revive missing-cognition, accidental-agreement-only, merely standing unused-capacity, or inert-act criticisms in an undifferentiated form. The strongest current candidate answers their thin versions.
5. Do not reverse the stronger source appraisal merely because these omissions are repaired. The rival still contests nonabsorption and exclusive original completion, and its wise own-activity norm remains unproved.

The highest remaining discriminator is therefore:

**Can an original bearer's unchanged, decisive exercise, supported by intrinsically possessed standing power and proposed as wisely chosen, count as complete original efficacy when its actual numerical effect is nonexclusively produced, even though removing the peer preserves its exact output and replacing the peer's active setting can redirect it?**

Here only solo-target adequacy, standing repertoire, and contextual response capacity are established in the reduced model. The question does not concede the source's stronger complete actual sufficiency or the normative wisdom of the particular exercise.

A successful affirmative exclusion would need a source-grounded account of why such actual shared efficacy is a received or defective perfection, without assuming that every modal correlation is efficient donation, that intrinsic acts are created traces, or that every standing power must be an admissible opposed completed will. A successful rival would need to justify that its primitive joint production and own-activity norm genuinely satisfy original-perfection commitments, not just that the finite output table and labels are consistent.

Those are sharper demands than “add another setting” or “add another knowledge token.” No issue is found with the author's now-declared limited conclusion. The report should not claim a full metaphysical countermodel, proof-kernel certification, new agency-logic theorem, or audit closure.
