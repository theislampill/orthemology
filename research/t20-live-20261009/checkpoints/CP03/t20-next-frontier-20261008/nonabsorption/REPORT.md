## Result

The source's nonabsorption claim is stronger than numerical effect individuation, nonzero input sensitivity, or even **positive response to each input in a neighbourhood of the actual joint setting**. The new exact operation isolates that last distinction. It does not merely restate the inherited observation-fibre result or assign a support label to a proposed effect.

For the explicit polynomial

    F(x,y) = x + y - 3xy + x²y + xy²,

both partial derivatives equal 1 at the joint setting (1,1), and both are at least 73/100 throughout [9/10,1]². Nevertheless F(1,0) = F(0,1) = F(1,1) = 1. The decisive qualification is visible rather than hidden: **F(1,y) = 1 - y + y² is nonmonotone along the absence-to-full-activation path.** Its marginal response is negative below 1/2. This is an algebraic counterexample to a specified implication from local positive responsiveness to nonabsorption. It is not a model of genuine actual production, a complete created world, or perfected original agents.

A positive theorem identifies what would suffice: monotonicity along the entire relevant augmentation comparison, together with a genuine strict increase somewhere on that comparison, entails strict endpoint growth. In a complete-history account the analogous condition is faithful retention of independently justified extra contributions. The latter is the inherited trace route, not a new theorem here.

The source argues for precisely the missing strength through real effect-parts, unchanged effective activity, and original freedom from externally imposed limitation. Those are substantive arguments, not a numerical identity convention. The strongest rival rejects the original-specific transition from genuinely joint manifestation to diminished or externally completed efficacy. Neither response sensitivity nor the present polynomial discharges that rival's metaphysical burden.

## 2. The exact quantifiers

Let p record an original's specified intrinsic productive activity, let b0 mean peer absence and b1 the peer's full activity, and let W(p,b) describe external created work at an explicitly stated observational/ontological resolution. Distinguish:

1. **Intrinsic actuality:** the joint description contains an additional real intrinsic act of B.
2. **Operator relevance:** for some setting b, changing B changes W somewhere.
3. **Actual-neighbourhood response:** arbitrarily small changes near b1 change W at fixed p; a scalar version can even have strictly positive local derivative.
4. **Finite augmentation separation:** W(p,b1) differs from W(p,b0).
5. **Strict complete-work enlargement:** W(p,b1) is greater/more than W(p,b0) under a justified order on complete created work.

The source's 20:175 claim aims at 5, with an unchanged operative contributor and a genuinely additional contributor. It is not merely 2 or 3. Claim5 entails4 when the order is strict. Claim4 need not entail5: different work need not be greater work. Claims1–3 do not supply4 without a bridge. The polynomial below strengthens the known negative result by satisfying3 with positive actual-neighbourhood derivatives.

A generic demand that W be globally injective is unnecessarily strong. The needed separation concerns a specific productive augmentation relation and a justified invariant first contribution. Conversely, saying that every genuine addition must change this very evaluated W merely restates4; calling that statement “no idle contribution” does not derive it from an independently weaker non-idleness criterion.

Crucially, p must not change meaning halfway through the inference. Fixed intrinsic willing, movement, or recorded exercise is weaker than fixed context-complete productive efficacy. If p includes the already determined external manifestation or exclusive whole-work attribution, its preservation may exclude the rival at the outset. The source's broader original-efficacy argument is needed to justify that stronger premise for originals.

## 3. Exact diagnostic and positive counterpart

### 3.1 A genuine algebraic separation, with no declared causal support

The script `diagnostic/response_diagnostic.py` contains no actual-support relation, route inventory, perfection predicate, necessary-being predicate, or numerical-event-identity convention. It evaluates one integer-coefficient polynomial over exact rational numbers and differentiates its coefficient dictionary.

For F above:

- F(0,0)=0.
- F(1,0)=F(0,1)=F(1,1)=1.
- F_x = 1-3y+2xy+y²; F_y is its symmetric counterpart.
- F_x(1,1)=F_y(1,1)=1.

Both derivatives are at least 73/100 on [9/10,1]². A proof, rather than a grid inference, is available: F_x increases with x there because its x-derivative is 2y≥9/5, and increases with y because its y-derivative is -3+2x+2y≥3/5. Its minimum is therefore F_x(9/10,9/10)=73/100. Symmetry gives the other bound.

Thus the second input is neither algebraically unused nor locally masked at the actual corner. This remains **response sensitivity**, not authenticated actual causal production. Whether these interventions can preserve a particular original's complete wise willing is not answered by differentiation.

### 3.2 The missing global comparison is exposed

Hold A's input at1 and vary B continuously from0 to1:

    g(y)=F(1,y)=1-y+y²
    g'(y)=-1+2y.

The output first falls from1 to3/4, then rises to1. The exact integrated changes are -1/4 on [0,1/2], +1/4 on [1/2,1], and0 overall. The positive derivative at the actual endpoint does not entail a positive change from peer absence.

The path is a mathematical family of comparisons. It is not an actual temporal episode in which work was produced and then destroyed, and no such cancellation events are inferred. A source defender can say these intermediate comparisons alter the effective contribution or violate the no-foreign-limitation standard. That is a substantive, relevant objection. The diagnostic expressly does not certify an unchanged complete productive basis along the path.

### 3.3 Positive sufficiency theorem

If g is nondecreasing on [0,1] and has positive left derivative at1, then g(1)>g(0). For a sufficiently small ε>0, positive left derivative gives g(1)>g(1-ε); monotonicity gives g(1-ε)≥g(0). Equivalently, a continuously differentiable g with nonnegative derivative everywhere and positive derivative on a nonempty interval has positive total change.

This is not offered as a newly discovered mathematical theorem. Its application is the discriminating work: **local operative responsiveness is insufficient; the affirmative route additionally requires a justified prohibition of compensating limitation across the relevant finite comparison.** No assumption of a physical conserved quantity is needed for the elementary theorem. A scalar measure that faithfully represents complete work is, however, another substantive requirement.

A nonnegative-coefficient polynomial in activation variables supplies one sufficient algebraic case: if some term involving B is positive when activated, no other term decreases, and the result grows. Merely finding an algebraic representation with nonnegative terms is weaker, as the next control shows.

### 3.4 Basis signs are not causal events

The same F has the exact representation

    F(x,y)=x(1-y)²+y(1-x)²+xy.

All three displayed terms are nonnegative on [0,1]². Yet increasing y reduces factors containing (1-y). Nonnegative displayed terms therefore do not guarantee monotonic augmentation. Conversely, the negative coefficient -3 in the expanded representation does not prove that a real cancellation or annihilation occurred. These are two algebraic presentations of one response law.

Interpreting the three terms as independently real created contributions would be a further causal/ontological claim. The script deliberately does not do so. This blocks both an artificial physical counterexample and an artificial trace-based refutation of it.

## 4. Full history is a type obligation, not a dimension count

The same equality holds after an elementary profile lift W(x,y)=F(x,y)H for any fixed nonzero source-neutral profile H. The script checks a three-coordinate example. Replacing H by a time-indexed profile gives the same pointwise observation equality. This is only a consequence of the scalar identity; it does not certify a complete created history.

Three inventories must be separated:

- A **created-effect profile** describes selected states or properties of the produced thing.
- A **complete created history** additionally includes every real created production, interaction, destruction and preservation event, if such events exist in the ontology.
- The **total actual inventory** also contains the proposed uncreated original bearers and their intrinsic acts.

Adding B immediately changes the third inventory. That cannot by itself establish an increase in the first or second: B's alleged uncreated intrinsic act is not automatically an extra created token. Merely counting the two intrinsic act records therefore commits a type error if the conclusion concerns created work.

Conversely, if independently known extra created traces exist, leaving them out of H makes the profile coarse. Equality of H then does not answer nonabsorption at the full level. Our construction establishes neither that such traces exist nor that they do not. The inherited trace theorem remains valid once reality, individuation, unchanged manifestation, disjoint attribution and faithful retention have been justified.

A changed provenance relation also need not itself be an additional created entity. That classification requires an ontology of the relation. Calling the relation real is not yet identifying its status as a separately created item. Equally, denying it is a separately created item does not show that efficacy cannot be an agent's real perfection.

Numerical effect identity is separate again. The profile lift does not prove that the same token could have another origin. Nor would distinct counterfactual tokens with equal profiles establish a greater actual product. Majmu's comparison with a like product cannot be answered solely by assigning different origin-indexed names.

## 5. Strongest affirmative argument

The source's best case can be reconstructed without confusing an intrinsic command with an external completed act:

1. Genuine productive addition must have real efficacy, not merely be an extra explanatory citation.
2. An original undertaking supplies its completed efficacy from its own original resources, including relevant created recipients and means. Independently original peers cannot supply or impose the missing completion of that efficacy.
3. Holding that actual efficacy fixed, adding a genuinely operative peer cannot diminish, redirect or suppress the first contributor's complete manifestation.
4. The peer's positive real manifestation is neither sheer nothing nor a second exclusive entire production of precisely the first contributor's work. The latter would violate the separately stated exclusive-attribution condition.
5. With all real work faithfully retained, the complete joint result must contain more work, or the initial claim that both original productive states remain complete and unchanged must fail.

This is a substantive no-foreign-limitation account. It explains why ordinary bodily cooperation changes effort, why limited recipients matter, and why standing solo capacity does not certify actual whole execution. It is stronger than an appeal to numerical identity alone.

A coordinated source control strengthens this reconstruction. Akmaliyya, Majmu6:125–130, explicitly ranks comprehensive self-sufficient creation, power and unprevented wise willing above their restricted counterparts;6:137–140 distinguishes pure perfection from a gain in one respect purchased at a loss in another. The bearer-eligibility lane identifies these passages, and I directly read its retained canonical L216–240 and L258–274. This is shared evidence, not an independent discovery or full-epistle reading by this lane. [Primary epistle](https://islamweb.net/ar/library/content/22/591/)

Accordingly, the argument must not be weakened into a bare wish for exclusive credit, and a merely relative cooperative good does not automatically defeat it. Nor is inability to acquire another bearer’s power by an identity-preserving merger the only source test of eligibility. The live question remains whether optional joint manifestation actually diminishes the pure efficacy that this account ranks positively.

The present investigation does not derive steps2–4 from bare intrinsic nonreceipt. Step3 carries the relevant monotonic/locality commitment; step4 additionally needs a warranted account of positive contribution, not a source-labelled token supplied by definition. The source's proper-part argument and efficacy-as-perfection argument are its affirmative reasons. The polynomial does not refute those reasons; it shows why a weaker appeal to positive local response cannot replace them.

## 6. Strongest surviving rival and exact limitation

The preserved serious rival proposes intrinsic powers, acts, cognition and ends, with one directly joint nonlinear manifestation. Each participant can achieve the same source-neutral work alone, but neither claims simultaneous exclusive entire attribution to the actual joint product. It rejects the claim that a change in the actual productive relation necessarily supplies a missing constitutive perfection to either bearer.

Its strongest response is that contextual manifestation need not be a diminution of the agent's own fulfilled activity. Real participation could be irreducible without adding source-indexed external tokens, and its difference might lie in the productive relation rather than in the created work profile. It need not make the peer's participation an independently valued end.

That reply remains unestablished as a complete metaphysics. It must justify real actual production, original eligibility, sufficient particular determination, and the adequacy of its complete created inventory. Neither an empty efficient-receipt graph nor nonzero derivatives authenticate those claims. The polynomial is **not** substituted for the richer retained rival and inherits none of its tested knowledge/end properties.

The source's strongest answer is correspondingly precise: if the peer really supplies some of this actual production, A's realised original efficacy is thereby less than the entire productive adequacy of its undertaking; full power and complete willing cannot be so limited by another original. The unresolved issue is whether that classification follows independently for primitive joint manifestation. Calling the rival “joint” does not alone prove the classification, and calling its own end “fulfilled” does not alone refute it.
