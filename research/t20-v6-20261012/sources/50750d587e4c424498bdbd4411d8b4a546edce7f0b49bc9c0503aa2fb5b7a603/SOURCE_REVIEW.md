## 2. Efficacy: neither intrinsic-donation shortcut survives

Direct rereading of Minhaj 151 L007–017, 156 L001–041, 157 L001–004, 158 L001–006, and Majmu 2032 L001–020 supports the following controls:

1. Minhaj 2:162 distinguishes a perfection belonging to the bearer from a separate thing conjoined to it. It does not show that any relationally specified efficacy is extrinsic to the bearer's perfection.
2. Minhaj 2:167–172 distinguishes own inseparable attributes, mutual concomitance, and external productive completion. A generic “composition implies a foreign cause” reconstruction is not faithful.
3. Minhaj 2:180 explicitly identifies efficacy as a perfection and moves from conditioned efficacy to dependence of the subject. Majmu 20:181 explicitly transfers effect-dependence to the inhering act/attribute. The stronger premise is present and argued.
4. Majmu 20:175–178 expressly considers unchanged agents, harmonious cooperation, nonabsorption, and inseparable/mixed production. The objection is not merely disagreement between wills.
5. “Produced through A alone” and “A would suffice for output under a changed comparison” are not interchangeable. The source uses the former in its contradiction argument; certificate or counterfactual sufficiency does not silently earn it.

Consequently a reduced graph with no incoming donation of power, cognition, will, or act cannot refute the source's larger norm. Conversely, the mere fact that q has both A and B in its provenance does not without that norm prove that B supplies a deficient perfection of A. The report correctly locates the live issue in actual operative adequacy.

## 3. Material addition: the consolidation argument

Majmu 2032 L009–013 (20:178–181) offers an additional explicit argument. Its author claims that what is producible by two can be producible by one; concentrating the powers in one subject does not diminish their capacity and can improve it. He then infers each existing participant's eligibility for more complete independent capacity, and inability to give itself that completion.

The original WARRANT_ASSESSMENT discusses L013's effect-to-action transfer but does not reconstruct this preceding argument. An additive correction should distinguish:

- Work-capacity: a hypothetical unified subject can produce the same source-neutral external work.
- Bearer eligibility: either existing participant can possess that capacity while remaining the relevant bearer.
- End preservation: the transformation retains the subject-sensitive or peer-sensitive end actually under discussion.

The first does not by itself entail the second or third. Failure to preserve an end requiring a real other does not refute the source-neutral work-capacity claim. Nor does bare peer-dependence of such an end establish that the end is an eligible pure perfection of a necessary original. The correct next question is which features the modal consolidation must preserve and why.

This is a scope correction, not a new impossibility proof or a demonstration that the author's argument is missing. The source investigator acknowledged the omission in emphasis and undertook an additive correction on 8 October 2026.

## 4. Strongest surviving rival

The strongest rival should concede real joint original production, reject dual sole-exhaustive actual authorship, and claim independently possessed powers and intrinsic activity manifest jointly without completing one another's agency. It must provide a substantive account of that real productive manifestation. A support set, successful command, accurate forecast, or absence of receipt edges cannot supply the account by declaration.

The strongest affirmative response is that the adequacy of the actual undertaken production, not only possession of alternate solo capacity, must be unborrowed. If the peer partly constitutes that adequacy, voluntary partnership does not erase completion by another. This reply has force; the remaining task is independently earning that “partly constitutes” inference for genuine redundant co-production.
