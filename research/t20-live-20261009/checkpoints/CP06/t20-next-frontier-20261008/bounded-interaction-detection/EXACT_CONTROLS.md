# Exact interval controls

The author script uses standard-library integers and Fraction arithmetic. Its 18,101 assertions cover 520 world/name runs and 3,122 complete support decisions. Four fixed valid Cauchy-name styles return exact centers or controlled positive/negative/alternating rational errors; no random sampling or floating-point comparison is used.

The catalogue includes every two-root inventory with support counts in {0,1,2}, every three-root binary-support inventory, and four-root fixtures with overlapping minimal supports, absorbed higher supports, unused roots, very small interior rates and very small positive raw denominators. Empty inventory and M=0 branch without division by M. H empty is included in every relevant double-mask inversion.

The controls obtain minimal-support upper probability bounds through actual precision refinement, construct rational rootwise rate lower bounds, and propagate raw-Q input intervals through products and quotients until the known gap separates each support decision. They independently compare the recovered support family with the generating counts and verify the exact double-Möbius factor. They also enumerate finite residual count simplices, check their collapse criterion and every coordinate's remaining ambiguity, verify exact unary power identities, and show that a false ceiling can invalidate the key lower bound.

The largest precision parameter used by these finite fixtures was 256 bits. This is a run statistic only, not a uniform guarantee or a sample budget. The finite assertion count is not a proof of all-name termination; that proof is given in RESULT.md.

The multi-root and general unary sign lemmas are imported from their separately reviewed stages. This script does not duplicate those discoveries. Independent review supplies additional interval controls, including corroborating unary cuts and the conditional covered-root consequence, and replays the author output byte-identically.
