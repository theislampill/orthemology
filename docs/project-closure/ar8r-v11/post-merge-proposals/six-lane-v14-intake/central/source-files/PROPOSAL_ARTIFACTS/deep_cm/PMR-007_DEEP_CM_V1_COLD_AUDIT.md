## Blocking findings

### CM-F01 — “proper function” was defined too generously

The frozen reduct establishes reliable conformance to independently declared truth and fittingness relations. Calling that entire package “proper function” would settle a contested philosophical classification by stipulation. The result must use the neutral phrase `objective truth-linked functional fit` and compare proper-function accounts separately.

### CM-F02 — objective fittingness was carried, not derived

The fittingness relation is an input to the model. The candidate does not establish its truth, objectivity, authority, naturalness, or metaphysical ground.

### CM-F03 — observational/interventional completeness was under-typed

The primary checker covers the finite deterministic reduct, permutations of its four state indices, and an output flip. It does not cover every possible internal intervention, infinite history, stochastic architecture, physical implementation, or world-level experiment.

### CM-F04 — one structural bearer was conflated with one subject

One implementation locus does not entail de-se ownership, one intentional subject, or numerical identity under every ontology.

### CM-F05 — selected effect and design were not typed

An etiological selected effect can be impersonal. A design plan may involve a personal designer without making the designed system a personal agent. These accounts must not be compressed into one function predicate.

### CM-F06 — personal-target expansion coherence is conditional

The U expansion stipulates uptake, because-fittingness, de-se ownership, personality, and Wisdom. The model shows nonentailment from the reduct; it does not independently prove that those predicates are jointly coherent, actual, or sufficient for theological Wisdom.

### CM-F07 — source-relative fiṭrah route was missing

A fiṭrah-oriented account can supply a school-internal function/health norm only under source, translation, referent, applicability, and world guards. It cannot be treated as neutral evidence.

### CM-F08 — theorem-family and ancestry ceiling omitted

The nonidentifiability is an application of the fibre/reduct expansion family and materially strengthens the Deep-BP/Deep-BG/Deep-BR proper-function control by adding carried objective fittingness. It is not new general mathematics.

## Required repair

- rename the neutral package;
- classify causal-role, selected-effect, design, learned-objective, truth-linked, intentional, and fiṭrah accounts separately;
- state that objective fittingness is carried;
- limit the intervention scope;
- type the one-bearer and source ceilings;
- classify theorem ancestry and novelty; and
- record the central effect as survival of the one-bearer impersonal rival even after objective truth-linked fit is carried.
