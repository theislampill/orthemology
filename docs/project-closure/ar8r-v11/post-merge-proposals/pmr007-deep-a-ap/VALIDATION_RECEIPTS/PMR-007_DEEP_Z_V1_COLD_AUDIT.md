## Blocking findings

### Z-F01 — Boolean coordinates are abstractions of typed owners

Core A and Core B are structured objects, not truth bits. The V1 construction
must be described as a diagnostic abstraction over independently valid typed
owners, with projection back to those owners. A Boolean pass/fail vector is not
the common theory itself.

### Z-F02 — product existence does not establish joint satisfiability

A categorical product exists formally, but an intended integrated model may
have no object satisfying every component contract simultaneously. The repaired
claim must require a nonempty attained pullback/fibre over one full target key.

### Z-F03 — complete-cube independence is a stress model

The 64-state cube proves conditional diagnostic independence only for an
intended class that realizes all combinations. Actual domains may impose
logical dependencies. The lower bound and all-coordinate-load-bearing claim
must be scoped to complete-cube attainment.

### Z-F04 — release versus diagnosis is purpose-relative

A binary conjunction is lossless for the single release predicate itself. It is
lossy only for recovery of failure coordinates, burden routing, repair, and
explanation. The V1 wording must avoid treating one-bit release as intrinsically
invalid.

### Z-F05 — FABLE-R1-B06 is narrowed, not globally closed

The common product/pullback answers both Core A and Core B at a typed
compositional interface. It does not provide a reductive common primitive,
shared proof calculus, explanation of coordination, or adopted champion. The
burden should be split into a discharged structural sub-burden and a surviving
reductive/explanatory sub-burden.

### Z-F06 — added authority/truth/world axes do not belong to the original two-core claim

They are justified downstream extensions, not retroactive properties of Fable
Core A or Core B. The repaired packet must state the ancestry and avoid
backfilling them into Round 1.

### Z-F07 — product is not Candidate-G unification

The repaired packet must make explicit that coordinate conjunction is exactly
the carrier-boxing/product case Candidate G warns against. No explanatory or
metaphysical unity credit follows.
