# Additional separation from global exclusion

Supplementary inference analysis to the independent v1 review. 3 October 2026 UTC.

There is a direct content test for the bypass claim. Suppose an actual necessary resource has the relevant unexercised power, while a proposed actual F history is assessed separately for E_A adequacy. The realizing resource can ground possible S without being an actual exercised contributor to that F history. The modal premises do not, by themselves, assign the history's adequacy relation. Thus the corrected route can settle its conditional necessary-resource question while leaving the original whole-history question unadjudicated. This is an inference control, not a claim that the combined arrangement is metaphysically possible. It also explains why actual exercise must not be imposed merely to make the new result bear directly on the old question.
