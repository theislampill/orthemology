## Blocking findings

### AK-F01 — creed predication and commentary endorsement were collapsed

The creed's line 70 is an authored creed sentence; line 99 is the commentary's
translated endorsement.  The evidence record must distinguish them.

### AK-F02 — translated-primary access was overread as Arabic-primary custody

The project has the English translation and translator's source note, not an
independent Arabic critical-text verification.

### AK-F03 — source co-reference requires a proposition-reconstruction guard

Grammatical co-predication in the translation supports one source referent only
under translation and source-proposition reconstruction.  It is not a neutral
metaphysical theorem.

### AK-F04 — source truth and world applicability were omitted

Even an accurate source proposition does not establish that the predicates are
world-true of the proposed neutral ground without source acceptance,
referent-identity, and world-adequacy bridges.

### AK-F05 — functional/source agency was identified with philosophical personality

`Doer with choice`, knowledge, ability, volition, and speech may support a
Track-N agentive bundle.  They do not automatically establish de se ownership,
first-person subjecthood, phenomenal consciousness, or a neutral modern
personality predicate.

### AK-F06 — the title/attribute nuance was lost

The commentary explicitly says `al-murid` and `al-mutakallim` are not unqualified
Divine Names while affirming the corresponding attributes.  The packet must not
convert attribute predication into an unrestricted naming claim.

### AK-F07 — plural exclusion was too broad

Source co-reference excludes assignments in which no one bearer carries the
source roles.  It does not exclude a source-compatible impersonal or
philosophically nonpersonal role-bearer unless additional semantics are supplied.

### AK-F08 — neutral Track T and source-expanded Track N were conflated

The source bundle changes the Track-N intended model class.  It cannot be
credited as a neutral H6/H8 proof.

### AK-F09 — Architecture C could be source-extended rather than simply refuted

An abstract-order-plus-actualizer rival can add the source role bundle to its
actualizer.  This may increase primitive or bridge burden but is not a formal
contradiction.

### AK-F10 — architecture dominance lacked matched metrics

Containing more desired predicates is not explanatory dominance.  Coverage,
primitive burden, derivation, source commitments, and unresolved bridges must
remain separate; no canonical scalar score is authorized.

### AK-F11 — source-dependent filtering was confused with neutral rival defeat

The strongest valid result is source-relative model-class restriction plus
neutral nonmigration, not an integrated-champion theorem.

### AK-F12 — theorem-family and novelty ceilings were absent

The common-bearer set-intersection fact is elementary; the source and
architecture value is an adjudication/application with zero general
mathematical novelty.
