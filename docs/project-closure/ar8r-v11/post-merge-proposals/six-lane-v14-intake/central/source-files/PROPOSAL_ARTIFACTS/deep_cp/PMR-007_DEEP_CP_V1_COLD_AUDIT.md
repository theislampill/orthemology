## Blocking findings

### CP-F01 — “independently operationalizable” exceeded the evidence

The registry supplies formal implementation maps and declared contracts. It
does not supply physical, empirical, human, or metaphysical operationalization.
The conclusion must be `NO_CURRENT_ELIGIBLE_UI_EXPERIMENT_IN_THE_FROZEN_FORMAL_REGISTRY`.

### CP-F02 — finite open-loop check versus adaptive completeness

The checker exhausts words of length at most three. Equality for all finite
adaptive common-history policies follows only from the stronger fact that U and
I share the complete one-step initial/transition/output kernel. The proof and
executable evidence must be typed separately.

### CP-F03 — output completeness was overstated

The output tuple is complete only for the declared registry. It does not include
unknown internal variables, additional sensors, source-world facts, or every
future architecture-relevant output.

### CP-F04 — objective fitting status is a carried coordinate

The intervention flips a declared fittingness-status bit. It does not change or
certify actual objective fittingness, nor show that the architecture
apprehends it as fitting.

### CP-F05 — self-model token is not de-se ownership

Behavioral sensitivity to a self-model or center token is compatible with an
impersonal controller. The proxy control must remain explicit.

### CP-F06 — ineligible operation status is provisional

`toggle_intentional_uptake` and `query_de_se_ownership` fail the current common
contract. The theorem does not prove that no future independent
operationalization can exist.

### CP-F07 — formal map does not establish realization

The U/I systems are finite formal expansions. Their physical or metaphysical
possibility, actual implementation, and comparative prior probability remain
open.

### CP-F08 — source and empirical nontransfer

Track-N source predication and OSM/PRH representation findings are not neutral
common interventions. They require separate source/world or empirical mapping
and must not be used to inflate the registry.

### CP-F09 — architecture-specific interventions are changed experiments

An internal intervention available only in one architecture may be evidentially
valuable, but it is not a common intervention unless an abstract type and
semantics-preserving implementation exist in both.

### CP-F10 — ancestry and novelty

The result composes Deep BW/CK common-kernel parity, Deep BV finite fibre
identifiability, Deep CO common-postprocessing impossibility, and CI/CJ-style
conditional templates. It receives no new general mathematical novelty.

## Required repair

- restrict the conclusion to the frozen formal registry;
- separate structural all-policy proof from finite regression;
- type output and fittingness/self-model ceilings;
- preserve future operationalization and changed-experiment exits;
- maintain source/empirical firewalls;
- classify ancestry and novelty.
