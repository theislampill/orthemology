# Original authority: scoped selection and a source-owned conditional claim

10 October 2026 UTC. Bounded primary-source continuation. Separate research evidence only. No protected edit, integration, contemporary duty adjudication, elapsed-time claim, or closure.

## Result

**The selected Miftāḥ argument does not newly establish the original's right to select a particular otherwise-open form of an already owed creaturely response.** It does positively defend worship, practical obedience, and wise directive formation. Its argument against leaving humanity without moral direction should not be mistaken for a comparison against the narrower rival that accepts those achievements but disputes one selection title. The exact obstruction is local, not a demand for unrestricted sovereignty.

The adjacent self-obligation argument supports a different positive application: **a recipient's claim can depend on fulfilment of an actual creaturely response while its normative undertaking belongs to the original. That dependence does not by itself make the creature an external legislator or a donor of the original's essential perfection.** This is a bounded application of inherited source distinctions, not a newly discovered doctrine, a general promise theory, or a proof of temporal creation of an obligation. It does not close the creature-directed selection-title question.

## 1. The actual burden and inherited credit

The controlling original-authority report, its CP02 appraisal and quotation check, the T17/T19 paragraph bindings, CP23 intrinsic-normative-reasons report, directive-added-goodness report, token-force report, and recent wise-reason and intrinsic-succession reports were checked. All eleven files in the controlling report's INPUT_BINDINGS match their recorded SHA-256 values. This verifies received inputs, not every claim in them or current repository integration.

The following remain earned:

- The actual complete wise original is owed practical acknowledgement, love, worship and allegiance. This is a substantive defeasible appraisal, not mere praise for useful services.
- A warranted continuing right of control can be exercised within its scope to determine an otherwise-open use.
- Actual rightful address can add a real practical relation and goodness.
- Nonprohibition does not alone establish an irrevocable grant surrendering later control. Conversely, absence of a surrender does not establish universal retained title.
- A suitably authenticated source-owned truthful assertion of an obligation can warrant that obligation through T19's testimonial route, without a second independent derivation of its title.
- The actual address's content, force, addressee, time, conditions and attribution remain indispensable. An intercession is not converted into an obligatory command by its speaker's rank.

The earlier report's references to “every proposed requirement-making title” and “every conceivable action” would be overbroad **if used as the completion criterion for a local Orthemological obligation**. They are not needed. However, the same report explicitly identifies the exact local question, and CP02 expressly rejects requiring unrestricted duty-creation. No demonstrated historical error is corrected by merely repeating the narrower wording.

The live constitutive question is therefore: for this actual source and recipient, why does the owed relation give the source standing to make this feasible, wise form required rather than leave another adequate form available? One successful source-grounded answer at that scope would suffice. A universal jurisdiction theorem is unnecessary.

## 2. What the original-ownership passages actually argue

### Creation and command: 1:17–18; 2:1067–1076

At 1:17 the expression “له الخلق والأمر” is immediately explained: command is the revealed law, direction and religion. The argument concerns why an arena of actual testing and response belongs to the wise purpose described in the surrounding discussion. It is not a freestanding syllogism from causal possession to unlimited permission-setting power.

The developed replies at 2:1067–1076 distinguish the good of moral direction from abandoning humans to unregulated appetite and mutual predation. The source also explains why being able to confer benefits without an intermediate human response does not make a response-mediated good pointless. At 1071–1074 the opponent's “hand over the reins of choice” is disambiguated. Choice together with command is affirmed; absence of all command is rejected. The author explicitly makes creaturely choice a condition of the relevant accountability.

This is positive support for wise normative governance and real agency. It does not establish that every alternative to a disputed specification is abandonment to appetite. A recipient could accept owed worship and moral restraint while disputing a particular form's being exclusively due. Showing that lawlessness is bad does not, without another premise, show that this narrower position is lawlessness. This is an argument-scope check, not an assertion that the narrower rival is true or a defeater of the earned general appraisal.

### Original benefaction and worship: 2:878–881

In explaining Qurʾān 2:21–22, Ibn al-Qayyim connects worship of one's Lord with creation, continuing provision and the created setting of human life. At 879 he argues that being originated by this source makes worship of this source fitting and incumbent; the argument does not rely only on the occurrence of a command. The subsequent contrasts concern worship of the real source and worship of dependent or impotent substitutes.

The reasoning has substantive normative content: actual origination and provision identify whom directed acknowledgement answers, and why substituting another recipient fails that relation. Calling this mere power would understate it. Its direct target is the due worship relation and exclusive object of that worship. It does not separately compare two otherwise adequate forms of acknowledging that same source and explain why the source's selection makes one alone obligatory.

### Justice and ownership: 2:1121–1135

The source's third, endorsed account combines complete power, singular lordship, justice and wisdom. At 1133–1134 it rejects justifying punishment **without desert** by saying the recipients are within the punisher's power or ownership. The same passage positively argues that being the source's servants entails exclusive worship, magnification and reverence, and describes the beneficent master's relation to the recipient.

This is not a denial of rightful specification authority. Nor may an interpreter transport the argument against undeserved punishment into a general argument against command title. The narrower control is that the punitive discussion supplies no shortcut in which the word “ownership” alone eliminates the need to identify the normatively relevant relation. Its positive due-worship rationale remains intact.

The anti-bare-ownership punishment point and the due-worship doctrine are already recorded in inherited Miftāḥ notes. The recent wise-reason report already applies the former to transferred guilt. There is no new discovery credit for either doctrine here.

## 3. The strongest narrowed specification bridge

The strongest reconstruction is more serious than “a rightful command binds”:

1. This actual perfect source is already owed a practical response.
2. An actual address can introduce a new occasion and object of responsive conduct.
3. In a domain genuinely belonging to that owed response, an adequately conveyed wise specification can tell the recipient how to render it.
4. Refusing that specification while substituting another act would then fail to render what is actually owed.

Steps 1–2 are already positively supported. The inferential hinge is the transition from step 3 to step 4 when the other act was otherwise adequate. There are three importantly different ways that hinge could be warranted:

- **A substantive selection title:** the owed relation itself confers on its bearer control of this aspect of its discharge. This is the exact constitutive-authority premise sought.
- **A necessary-means or adequate-performance fact:** the alternative does not actually realize the same owed response in these circumstances. That can support a determinate duty without unrestricted control, but the non-equivalence must be shown independently of calling the selected form obligatory.
- **Truthful normative disclosure:** a properly authenticated assertion establishes that the obligation obtains. This is the retained T19 route, not a new constitutive-title proof.

Miftāḥ 1081–1089 expressly supports obedience and seeking what pleases the source. It therefore supports more than detached admiration. It does not follow that every more excellent response is required: its capacity qualification and the already checked recommended/obligatory distinctions remain. In the selected units I found no further discriminating argument for the first route that was not already contained in the accepted allegiance appraisal or the conditional retained-control rule.

This result does not impose a demand to deduce normative force from norm-free premises. Nor does it require the affirmative to defeat every imaginable rival before having warrant. It identifies why these particular additional passages do not add the missing selection argument. Owed allegiance must not silently be expanded into the exact selection power whose application is being tested; equally, its existing practical force must not be reduced to optional admiration.

## 4. Adjacent constructive application: conditional right without outside legislation

The self-obligation discussion supplies a more definite, differently directed case.

### The source sequence

At 1123–1124 Ibn al-Qayyim distinguishes the reported accounts of divine obligation. His preferred account neither subjects God to a humanly imposed external law nor treats every possible act as equally fitting. The grounds are the source's own perfection and the self-obligation it affirms.

At 1136 the passage expressly identifies a right of the worshipping recipients upon fulfilment of the stated condition. At 1137 it distinguishes an undertaking directing or restraining one's own conduct from an oath that merely confirms or denies a proposition. At 1138 it argues that self-demand is intelligible without a superior commander, and that the source's self-obligation includes its own willing, love and approval of the undertaking. At 1138–1140 the efficacy of the source's own-act love is distinguished from love of a creature's action. At 1141 fulfilment is related to praiseworthiness belonging to the source intrinsically, independently of acquiring perfection through a particular performance.

These are the author's endorsed account and reasoning, though introduced partly in the voice of the “middle” position he accepts. The surrounding quoted verses and reports are source evidence within that argument; no new independent authentication of their transmission is claimed.

### The executed inference

Keep the particular conditional relation stated at 1136 fixed. The application requires a warranted source-owned undertaking and warranted fulfilment of its actual condition; neither is obtained merely by naming a promise.

1. The source specifies its own treatment of the qualifying recipient.
2. The recipient's actual response matters to qualification under that undertaking. Simply knowing about the response is not substitutable for its occurrence.
3. The normative undertaking is nevertheless the source's own. The recipient does not become a superior legislator by satisfying its condition.
4. The source's perfection is not a reward supplied by the recipient. At 1141 the account locates the praiseworthy character of fulfilment in the source's already intrinsic character.

Consequently, **condition-dependence of this claim is not by itself external normative imposition, existential receipt, or acquisition of essential perfection**. The conditional dependence is real; it need not be denied to preserve original independence. This supplies a concrete normative application of the already retained distinction between a real achieved creaturely response and a donor of original perfection.

The scope is deliberately exact. The passage supports a conditional beneficiary right and an owned undertaking. It does not alone decide whether all such obligations are newly created by a temporally issued promise, whether this one has an earlier temporal stage without obligation, or how many intrinsic volition-tokens its realization involves. The described promise may disclose, enact or specify aspects of a normative relation; those stronger temporal-constitutive claims need further evidence. No withdrawal of the successive-act account follows, and no new instance of that ontology is proved here.

If the condition is not warranted as fulfilled, this conditional application does not establish the recipient's claim. It also does not establish its contrary or absence of other rights. If a purported undertaking lacks warranted authorship, the source's general perfection does not repair it. The source's actual scope and content, not an interpreter's preferred expansion, control the application.

This is not the requested authority over the creature's otherwise-open choice with the arrows reversed. Power to undertake one's own performance and power to require another's performance are different. The new application cannot be used as a substitute solution to section 3.

## 5. Credit and stopping point

The selected source doctrines were already available. The narrow additions within the checked records are the discriminating application of the governance-versus-lawlessness contrast to the exact local rival, and the application of the source-owned conditional right to the outside-legislator/foreign-perfection inference. The latter is a reuse of the prior achieved-response distinction, not a new general metaphysics or promise theory.

No new local selection title has been established. The next substantive success on that exact target would need a warranted selection entitlement in the owed-response relation, a non-question-begging actual-performance discriminator, or a relevant authenticated normative disclosure. More repetitions of the same passages do not supply a fourth route. The earned sourcehood, necessity, Knowledge, Will, Wisdom, due-response and testimony conclusions remain in place.
