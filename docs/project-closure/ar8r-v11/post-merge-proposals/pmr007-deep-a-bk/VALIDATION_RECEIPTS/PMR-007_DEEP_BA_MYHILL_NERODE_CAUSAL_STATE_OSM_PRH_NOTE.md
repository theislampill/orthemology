## PRH relation

PRH defines representation convergence through similarity of induced kernels
and hypothesizes convergence toward a shared statistical model of reality.
Kernel alignment and interface-state equivalence are different objects:

```text
kernel alignment can hold without exact action-trace sufficiency;
exact quotient isomorphism can hold under geometrically different encodings.
```

Deep BA therefore supplies a candidate held-out criterion for the Bitter-Lesson
comparison: preserve all declared counterfactual interface traces and compare
minimal quotients, while retaining kernel geometry as a separate metric.
