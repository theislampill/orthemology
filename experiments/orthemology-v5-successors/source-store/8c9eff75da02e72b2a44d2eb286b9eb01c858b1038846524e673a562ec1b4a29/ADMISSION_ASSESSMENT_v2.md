# Objective norm-fixing and actual productive sourcehood: admission assessment

3 October 2026. Bounded model-assisted research assessment, not independent acceptance of a new theory.

## Disposition

**Do not admit a new main inquiry on the presently identified bridge.** The actual correction remains positive evidence for target-relative, verdict-independent correctness and admitted reason-responsive agency. No newly identified ground or argument changes the relation between those achievements and the original productive bearer. This is a negative novelty finding for this candidate, not a defeat of objective normativity or original-source warrant.

## Exact inherited ownership

- **Sixth, Objective norm-fixing and warranted common repair, §§3.1–3.4, 8.1–8.4 and 10; Hard objections, §§E–G.** Owns the positive target-relative relation, the distinction between apprehending a reason, instituting a function and grounding normative authority, and the serious impersonal challenge. Its independent norm-fixing review v2 accepts local correctness and semantic warrant at that limited scope. It expressly leaves ultimate norm-to-source and source-to-authority bridges open.
- **Ninth, MEN-8 integrated-rival ledger, §3 (E0/E1, TYPE, FN/NF, UC, S-N), and §4 (C-unity/C-norm-ground); Integrated active-rival test, §3.** Already locates the specific division proposed here: an independent normative reality must explain its normative role; if it supplies the source's productive efficacy, original independence is affected; if it does not, productive completeness alone does not refute it. This is inherited analysis, not a newly identified Eleventh objection.
- **Ninth, Communicative integrity and original practical priority, “Relational exercise need not mean borrowed perfection” and “The remaining warrant.”** Already separates a relation's objective practical significance, internally grounded source excellence, actual compliance and essential practical priority.
- **Tenth, Original governance and due care for epistemic reliance, §§2–4; Original practical perfection, §§4–8.** Supplies a genuine reason-bearing relation and a bounded responsibility argument, while retaining the separate burden of comprehensive fitting intention. These results cannot be converted into an actual ultimate-source instance merely because the correction has a beneficiary.
- **Eleventh, Composition and the original productive bearer, §§4, 6–7; Original provision and constitutive possession, §§1–2, 4, with their separate acceptances.** Retains the inherited target-relative inclusion of indispensable productive efficacy, distinguishes that relation from constitution, and leaves actual P1/P2 unsettled. The accounting assessment expressly rejects inferring productive donation from every relation essential to describing the target.

## The strongest bridge considered

The proposed advance would run as follows: the subject actually corrects because it understands an objective reason; that reason belongs to the correction's actual explanation; complete original explanation must therefore include its normative ground; the normative ground and productive bearer must consequently be one reality.

The first statement retains the admitted target. The second identifies more than output covariance. The third is ambiguous between explaining the entire episode in every respect and supplying its whole productive origin. The final identity requires a relation-specific argument on either reading. A reason's being what the subject apprehends does not identify it with the source exercise by which the subject receives efficacy. The accepted accounting bridge covers the latter relation. It does not establish the former identity.

This is not a demand that reasons be causally irrelevant. The subject's actual apprehension may contribute causally to its choice; that apprehension is a concrete cognitive exercise with productive prerequisites. What makes its content true or its response fitting is a further explanatory respect. An account could integrate those respects, but their integration is the disputed inference, not something already licensed by including the apprehension in the actual history.

The actual witness sharpens the issue without resolving it: changing the claim changes neither the displayed header nor the fixed proposition's truth merely by changing endorsement. That supplies local objectivity. It does not identify an independently existing abstract normative bearer, establish its lack of productive dependence, or identify it with an original source. Sixth and Ninth already own precisely this positive local result and its limits.

## Why the candidate division also fails admission

A concrete reason-responsive recipient plus an abstract normative ground is a serious architecture to investigate if the latter has independent warrant. The current materials instead warrant a determinate content–object relation and retain several possible ultimate accounts. Adding an abstract ground solely to fill the open position contributes a commitment, not new evidence. Nor does its being abstract by description show how it supplies categorical authority, cognitive access or the relevant relation to the productive source.

The existing local account is not refuted by that absence: it does not need a separate universal called Correctness. Conversely, its local success does not defeat a source-nature account of ultimate normativity. The stronger intellectual account retains a real explanatory attraction through integrating apprehension, fitting end, means and institution. No newly supplied reason here warrants or defeats that integration at the ultimate scope. A direct rational recognition or abduction could do so without an experiment or exhaustive rival elimination; neither is supplied merely by reasserting the explanatory ideal.

## Consequence for original avenues 1–3

- **Avenue 1:** no new original productive completion, particular bearer or necessary participant. Local reason-responsive agency and inherited original-possession credit remain.
- **Avenue 2:** no new source-level cognition, comprehensive fitting intention or essential integrity. Recognising a fittingness relation is not already responding impeccably to it.
- **Avenue 3:** local objective correction remains warranted; ultimate norm-ground identification remains open. No concrete defeating case or independently grounded abstract division has newly been found.

The inquiry should stop at this admission gate. Reopening requires a definite additional warrant with an identified consequence: for example, a defended explanation of why this local normative relation must have the same original bearer as its productive realisation, or an independently warranted alternative ground whose relation to the actual cognitive exercise changes that inference. Repeating the inherited distinction, a formal relation table or another score-versus-reason example would not meet that condition.
