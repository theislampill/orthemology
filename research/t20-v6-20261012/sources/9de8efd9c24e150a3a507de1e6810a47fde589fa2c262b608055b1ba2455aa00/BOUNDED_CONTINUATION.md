# One bounded cooperation-bridge continuation

This is a recommendation arising from the read-only audit. It does not itself implement new research or authorise broader publication. Preserve all completed artifacts and work in a new successor directory.

## Intended result

Determine whether the strongest newly reconstructed B5/B6 cooperation route can receive a faithful, premise-preserving conditional/model interpretation using the already held sources and formal infrastructure. The result must tell us something the nine-old-model relevance audit did not: either which independently retained premises suffice, an interpreted candidate that actually preserves them while the bridge fails, or the exact semantic/warrant obstruction to such a test.

This is narrower than solving all original-source metaphysics. There is no requirement to produce a full metaphysically possible plural universe. A finite interpretation is only a relative-scope model. A failure to find one is not truth evidence.

## Freeze the test's roles before code

### Independently retained inputs

For the proposed originals, preserve at the intended subject scope:

- Rigid bearer identity across the relevant alternatives; actual existence; necessity and essential whole-existence nonreceipt under the separately defended constitutive package. This does not by itself include whole-field sourcehood.
- P2a: intrinsic power and will are not efficiently supplied, removed or sustained by another original. Do not define independence as having no peer or needing no external requisite in any chosen product.
- P3: no vicious efficient-priority cycle in the origination of the very capacities required for either originating act. Mere necessary correlation or a shared reason is not declared an efficient edge by definition.
- Eligible standing power at T17's actual scope: additional genuinely feasible capacity, without contradiction, borrowed intrinsic resources or impairment, is an eligible positive perfection. Standing ability is not automatic occurrence, ability to create an underived peer, or joint realisability with every fixed commitment.
- P5 where the equal-powers branch is used: equality and retention of the relevant target powers during coexistence. Declare the target domain; finite enumerated targets do not certify its metaphysical exhaustiveness.
- P6: complete power plus completed determinate willing entails its fixed specified outcome. State whether the predicate has causal-sufficiency content beyond material implication and independently justify that choice.
- Distinct real contribution tokens, fixed productive respect, actual effect identity, and same-respect CND where the inherited defended package requires it. Do not make EntireOrig exclusive before imposing CND, and do not confuse derivative agency with two original providers.
- Knowledge, wisdom and actual conformance at whichever limited semantic scope is required. An unexamined Perfect=true or StandingCan=true flag does not certify the intended full profile.

### Disputed downstream inferences

Keep these out of their own antecedents:

- P2b/M1 transfer: dependence of a completed external result is necessarily received completion of the original's intrinsic perfection or relevant original exercise.
- B5: co-bearability of a combined profile implies eligibility of each already identified bearer; that enlargement is a pure constitutive perfection; inability to supply the external effect establishes inability to complete oneself in the relevant respect.
- B6: complete determination of the target entails entire and then exclusive original actual provision of its basis.
- B2: the specified necessary concurrence is reciprocal partial efficient production, rather than intrinsic determinations with a downstream common result.
- Any universal source-mode or non-isolation premise whose derivation is precisely under test.

A principle can be retained downstream after independent warrant is explicitly accepted. Its presence in the source's complete theory does not make it an independently upstream premise of its own derivation. Nor may the critic delete an independently established upstream premise simply because it is inconvenient.

## Single reusable semantic interface

Use separate bearer, world/context, act/exercise, target/effect and contribution/respect types. Keep these relations distinct:

- has intrinsic capacity; capacity is efficiently supplied by another;
- eligibility for this bearer; realisability of a power profile somewhere; de re possible possession by this same bearer;
- standing can; complete actual willing/determination; occurrence of a target;
- intrinsic exercise; required external contribution; entire original provision; exclusive original provision;
- knows/understands a reason; intrinsic response to that reason; efficient cause of a will;
- whole-bearer receipt; receipt of a token state; source-sensitive history of a product.

Do not force a giant metaphysical theory into a one-shot encoding. First test whether the above interface can be given independently meaningful interpretation without using the questioned conclusion in definitions.

## Minimal candidate controls

### B5 co-bearability and identity

Construct or analyse a rigid-identity frame distinguishing (a) some bearer of the joint profile, (b) each original bearer able to acquire it, and (c) pure-perfection eligibility for that bearer. A generic U-without-R truth assignment is already understood and is insufficient as the new result. The new work must include the retained eligibility/perfection principle and expose whether it establishes the disputed R or presupposes it.

Two honest outcomes are possible without a full philosophical model: a nontrivial relative interpretation that retains a source-grounded eligibility rule, or an exact finding that the rule requires de re feasibility as an input and so cannot derive that feasibility here. The latter is progress only if demonstrated through the actual source-to-signature mapping and strongest affirmative application, rather than a generic claim that modal logic cannot decide metaphysics.

### B6 fixed complete determination

Compare the following at one unchanged target, causal respect and actual exercise background:

1. D(A,e) entails that e occurs.
2. D(A,e) entails that A independently supplies the entire original productive basis of e.
3. A's provision is exclusive.

Derive (3) from an independently warranted version of (2) plus same-respect CND/coverage as appropriate. Attempt a contribution-sensitive interpretation of (1) in which (2) fails, while genuinely retaining the fixed power/will semantics rather than switching to a different target or half-effort act. Reject it if preservation fails, with the exact failed antecedent named. If an interpretation of complete determination already includes (2), record that it proves the strong conditional but does not derive the classification from standing power and willing.

Ordinary saturating mechanisms and conditional joint goals may be useful diagnostic controls; neither counts as a full-original countermodel merely by its familiar causal form. The source's unchanged-state/additive-efficacy rationale must be assessed, not bypassed.

### Shared M1/B2 stress controls

Within the same candidate, distinguish a result needing both contributions from a power/act needing the other's efficient donation. Give any intrinsic-concord proposal actual functions or relations over the declared contexts, so it has more content than PerfectAgreement=true. Then test whether it preserves completed determinations, relevant target powers, source-sensitive histories and the adopted wisdom restrictions. A shared objective reason is not a third efficient giver merely because it is shared.

These are stress controls for the same bridge target, not separate projects. If the B5/B6 interpretation is blocked before this stage by a precise warranted representation problem, do not manufacture an arbitrary harmony model to fill a checklist.

## Verification and claim discipline

Where the semantics are faithful enough, use existing Lean 4.19 infrastructure or an explicit finite evaluator. Prefer direct finite checks and small lemmas to installing a solver framework. State all bounded domains. Bind code and theorem statements to the premise table. Test a positive inhabitant and a deliberate overclaim at the same signature. No model receives full-profile preservation credit through omitted predicates, vacuous universal domains or free labels.

The independent reviewer should examine the source-to-predicate map and premise preservation before interpreting a successful compile as meaningful. It should challenge both conclusions: that a model succeeds and that it has failed. The contested bridge may be false in a legitimate test; that is not itself a rejection reason.

## Outcome-based stopping rule

Stop when one of these is established for the frozen target:

- A faithful conditional derivation shows which independently defended antecedents carry B5/B6; any new philosophical premise and any simplification/redundancy is explicit.
- A concrete checked interpretation preserves the stated antecedents and falsifies the selected bridge, with finite/relative and full-profile limits stated separately.
- The strongest proposed interpretations cannot be made faithful without a precisely named unresolved eligibility, causal-sufficiency, will or perfection application premise. Show the obstruction by comparing the actual source commitments and the failed candidate(s). This is a narrowed-open-problem endpoint, not a claim that no possible model exists.

Do not use an arbitrary number of failed tries or an archive deadline as the stopping rule. Do not continue once the remaining step requires inventing an ontology of all possible originals, a new theory of practical reasons, or new evidence beyond this saved frontier. Record that scope limit and the decision it prevents. Reopen the frozen deliverable only after a substantive successor result exists.
