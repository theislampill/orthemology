# Edition 2 revision map

## The retained research question

Under what jointly satisfiable physical, informational and organisational
conditions can a specified process continue across changing realisations while
its original host becomes replaceable?

This remains distinct from independence from all physical support, consciousness,
personal-contact attribution, and unauthorised circumvention.

## Incorporated improvements

1. **Experimental lineage.** Added BitWhisper, Fansmitter, AirHopper and GSMem as
   primary precedents, preserving their endpoint-control and experimental scope.
   The paper no longer treats physical effects outside the ordinary network
   interface as a newly discovered topic.
2. **Adjacent disciplines.** Added TEMPEST's specific emissions-security meaning,
   molecular communication, PDE identification, and a clearer relation to
   physical neural computation. None of these is made coextensive with all of
   channel science.
3. **NS linkage.** Replaced an exclusively motivational bridge with an explicit
   smooth-flow residual-to-state estimate and bounded-observation corollary.
   These do not depend on the reported 2026 blow-up construction. A specific
   advantage transferred from that construction remains unestablished.
4. **Uncertainty.** Added a sufficient common-decoder margin with offset,
   linear-model and nonlinear-remainder uncertainty. Positive gain in each
   separately calibrated plant does not imply one decoder for an uncalibrated
   class; an unknown-polarity counterexample makes the distinction explicit.
5. **Orthemology.** Grounded the type/token vocabulary in the pinned current
   source. A concrete case is an orthemma; identification is an operation on it.
   State-types, observations, inferred profiles, governing types and case-bound
   governing tokens remain distinct. A learned coordinate is not automatically
   a warranted ortheme.
6. **Consolidation.** Distinguished representational abstraction, evidential JOIN
   and physical composition. JOIN is defined locally as scoped constraint
   reconciliation; it is not attributed as a universal canonical operator.
   Duplicate reports do not multiply evidence; empty intersections mean conflict.
7. **Neuralese.** Added the actual learned-agent-communication reference. A
   relabelling proof explains why external behaviour alone does not identify an
   intrinsic latent language, much less a universal biological one.
8. **Generalisability.** Stated a matched held-out performance hypothesis and an
   unrestricted finite-corpus nonidentification result. The proposed generative
   ambition remains a research hypothesis, not an implemented discovery engine.
9. **Composition.** Added a conditional serial reliability proof, shared-resource
   counterexample and split-role quantifier counterexample. A common schema is
   not a joint physical witness.
10. **Computability.** Corrected edition 1's misleading theorem title. The
    undecidability construction concerns whether some unspecified finite horizon
    eventually distinguishes inputs in an unbounded-memory model class. It does
    not make every fixed effectively simulable finite-horizon problem undecidable.
11. **Mixed-step continuity.** A pure migration has identity reference dynamics,
    hence Lipschitz factor one. A contraction factor from computation cannot be
    silently applied to it. Added the nonuniform product-sum error bound.
12. **Progress.** Safety invariance does not ensure task completion. Added a
    finite-rank progress certificate and a safe-idle-loop counterexample;
    strengthened the conditional host-independence theorem accordingly.
13. **Evaluation.** Added a simultaneous held-out bounded-loss result, provenance
    separation, matched baselines, abstention, negative examples, and separate
    failure conditions for the representation, composition, NS and continuity
    hypotheses.
14. **Scope.** Clarified that operational invariants concern authorised service;
    permitted termination is respected rather than defined as a failure to defeat.
    Content, physical capability, evidential warrant and authority remain separate.

## What was not carried forward as evidence

No inference was retained from the popularity of a term, an AI interlocutor's
agreement, repetition of a shared source, an allegation about a researcher's
motive, a personal experience, or a message asserting extraordinary authorship.
The review dialogue contributed questions and corrections, not experimental
confirmation. No comprehensive novelty claim is made for the individual results.

## Executed checks

The package contains 27 top-level finite and dimensionless checks. Both ordinary
and optimised Python runs passed. The written arguments cover 21 named results;
the finite checks are supplementary examples, not proof-assistant verification.
