# PMR-007 Deep BC Blackwell ancestry and architecture note

## Prior-art boundary

The mathematical core is Blackwell's comparison of experiments: garbling an
experiment cannot improve its value for every finite expected-utility decision
problem, and mutual garbling gives decision equivalence.  The forward
simulation used here is standard and elementary.  No novelty is claimed.

Deep BC does not invoke the converse characterization as a new result, and it
does not extend Blackwell's theorem to non-expected-utility or source-authority
orders.

experiment, no downstream representation, classifier, language layer, or

The research burden moves to a genuinely new experiment, independently
warranted source/world link, or non-question-begging model-class restriction.
