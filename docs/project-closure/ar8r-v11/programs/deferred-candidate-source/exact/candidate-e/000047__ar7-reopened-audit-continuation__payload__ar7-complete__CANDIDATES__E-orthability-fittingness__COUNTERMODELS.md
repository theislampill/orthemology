# Countermodels — Candidate E

## E-CM1 — Self-amending constitution

A constitution contains a higher-order rule authorizing replacement of every first-order rule and, on specified conditions, its own repeal. The later code rejects the earlier first-order rules.

**Attack.** Refutes the naive claim that a current standard can never authorize its own replacement.

**Repair.** Candidate E distinguishes retained meta-authority from radical correction relative to the full basis. The model occupies the retained-meta-standard horn.

**Disposition.** `SURVIVES; NARROWS TARGET`

## E-CM2 — Fixed arbitrary reward

An optimizer improves its score under reward `R`, including by accepting patches that make it more effective at maximizing `R`.

**Attack.** Shows that corrigible behavior can arise under a purely stipulated contract.

**Repair.** This is contract-relative improvement. It supplies no reason that `R` is objectively fitting and does not establish orthability in the robust sense.

**Disposition.** `NOT_A_COUNTERMODEL_TO_CONDITIONAL_THEOREM; BLOCKS_NORMATIVITY_INFERENCE`

## E-CM3 — Self-modifying reward with a retained corrigibility rule

The optimizer contains a meta-goal: accept reward replacement when an authorized operator signals. It may replace the entire first-order reward function.

**Attack.** Makes revision appear to transcend current goals.

**Repair.** Authority remains in the retained authorization rule. If that rule is itself objectively defective and corrected without relying on any retained rule, the radical case reappears.

**Disposition.** `RETAINED_META_STANDARD_HORN`

## E-CM4 — Evolutionary proper function

A heart, perceptual system, or signal malfunctions relative to effects for which ancestors were selected, even if its current token dispositions differ.

**Attack.** Supplies objective-seeming error without current endorsement or a mind-independent normative realm.

**Repair.** It confirms token transcendence while locating the standard in historical type-level facts. Whether selected-effect function yields categorical normative authority remains a separate dispute.

**Disposition.** `TRANS_STATE_NATURALISTIC_HORN; WISDOM_NOT_ENTAILED`

## E-CM5 — Constitutive truth norm of belief

Beliefs are correct if true, regardless of what an individual believer currently accepts as an epistemic rule.

**Attack.** Shows that type-level constitutivism can explain correction of tokens.

**Repair.** Candidate E is compatible with this. It does not claim all constitutivism fails; it asks what fixes the type and why its standard has authority.

**Disposition.** `TRANS_STATE_TYPE_STANDARD_HORN`

## E-CM6 — Reflective equilibrium

The authority of norms and judgments is fixed by a globally coherent equilibrium rather than by an external foundation or a single current state.

**Attack.** Evades linear foundational regress and supports endogenous self-correction.

**Repair.** This occupies the holistic fixed-point horn. The remaining question is whether coherence is truth-linked or merely system-relative; the structural partition does not refute it.

**Disposition.** `HOLISTIC_HORN_SURVIVES`

## E-CM7 — Brute normative facts

Irreducible normative facts determine that `t` corrects `s`.

**Attack.** Supplies objective authority without teleology, function, or a wise ground.

**Repair.** None deductive. The model occupies the primitive-normative horn and blocks the Wisdom bridge.

**Disposition.** `LIVE_RIVAL`

## E-CM8 — Social convention

A practice corrects individual departures relative to socially maintained rules and can revise those rules through recognized procedures.

**Attack.** Provides apparent objectivity and corrigibility without agent-independent standards.

**Repair.** Token participants are corrected by practice-level standards; procedural rule revision is retained-meta or holistic. The model blocks inference from local correction to robust objective normativity.

**Disposition.** `PRACTICE_RELATIVE_HORN`

## E-CM9 — Error theory

All robust objective correction claims are false, although speakers continue to use correction vocabulary.

**Attack.** Denies the existence premise.

**Repair.** Candidate E is conditional and therefore does not refute error theory. A separate indispensability or companions-in-guilt argument would be required.

**Disposition.** `LIVE_GLOBAL_EXIT`

## E-CM10 — Primitive recursively corrigible relation

A global primitive relation orders states by correctness. It is not grounded in any current state and has no further explanation.

**Attack.** Satisfies recursive corrigibility without teleology or a bearer.

**Repair.** It occupies the primitive horn and shows why the theorem cannot by itself establish a personal or wise ground.

**Disposition.** `LIVE_RIVAL; MENISCUS_BOUNDARY`
