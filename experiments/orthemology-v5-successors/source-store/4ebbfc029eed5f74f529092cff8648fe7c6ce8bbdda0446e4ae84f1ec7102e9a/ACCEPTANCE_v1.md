# Independent review: original provision and constitutive possession

3 October 2026. This review concerns only the separate accounting-bridge selection identified in `REVIEW_RECEIPT_v1.json`. The accepted assembly article and its review remain unchanged.

## Disposition

**Accept as a candidate-specific warrant assessment and clarification of the accounting inference.** The result retains the warranted target-relative inclusion of an exercise indispensable to an actual receiving mode. It distinguishes the argument against donated original efficacy from the further claim that a proper original exercise has exactly one subject. It establishes no new actual P1/P2, original co-owner, necessary participant or generally applicable ownership criterion.

The inspected account articulates substantive constitutive possession; it does not establish distinct absolute original owners of the same source-intrinsic exercise. [R11, §§2–5, manuscript pp. 13–15, 19–23.]

This assessment neither treats a theoretical label as a counterexample nor excludes a substantive interpretation merely because it changes the inherited vocabulary. The actual content and explanatory work must be compared.

## 1. Positive inclusion is warranted at its precise target

The actual target includes the subject's reason-responsive correction about the displayed bibliographic witness. A receiving mode admitted as reception through a particular source exercise cannot be realised without that exercise in its productive role. That is a fact about actual efficacy, not merely a consequence of attaching another relational description to the endpoint.

Accordingly, a purportedly complete original account of that mode cannot leave its indispensable efficacy supplied wholly outside the account. Otherwise the claimed accomplishment still uses a contribution whose productive availability the account has not supplied. This is a substantive local constraint on the claimed completeness. It does not require the source to cause every associated fact or to produce its own intrinsic actuality through an earlier action.

The article correctly retains this as inherited warrant. It is not newly established by the comparison. It also does not identify a bearer satisfying it. A collective account may include every relevant exercise without yet establishing that one bearer originally provides them. The account's closure, the power's possession and the source's identity remain different questions.

For an endpoint-only target, adding actual receiving modes would enlarge the target. For the fixed whole-origin target, retaining those modes preserves it. The article makes this distinction explicit and does not use endpoint redundancy to erase a mode's indispensable exercise.

## 2. No donation does not by itself settle subjecthood

If an alleged original exercise receives its efficacy from a different independent source, its original status fails in that respect. This reason remains valid even when the recipient genuinely owns and exercises an intrinsic power.

The reason establishes an exclusion of a particular productive dependence. It does not, without an additional relation premise, establish that every second possession attribution is that dependence. A proposed constitutive possessor may deny furnishing efficacy to the other bearer. Mere distinctness then does not complete the donation argument.

There is also a genuine affirmative rationale for subject-indexed exercise identity. A proper exercise is the actual agency of its subject, rather than an event whose actor can be changed without consequence. If two subject-indexed acts are realised in one event, identity of the event does not give identity of the proper acts. The final article now states this rationale positively. It is a reason-bearing account of act individuation, rather than an empirical measurement requirement or a restatement that another proof is needed.

Its universal application to a constitutive co-ownership theory remains additional. Defining every proper exercise as exclusively owned would secure the conditional consequence within that definition but would not independently assess a rival interpretation. Conversely, describing another interpretation does not rebut a warranted subject-indexed account. A basic recognition or a well-supported abduction could favour either relevant bridge; its actual content and application must be assessed.

## 3. The decisive application test

An original co-ownership claim needs the following claims jointly warranted:

1. There are distinct actual source-bearers, rather than merely multiple powers, aspects or a collectively denoted plurality.
2. The object allegedly co-owned is one token proper source exercise, not a shared power-kind, a multiply possessed property, a common event, or two acts with a common endpoint.
3. Both attributions concern original possession at the same absolute productive respect, not ordinary intrinsic possession or independence only from outsiders.
4. The asserted accounting relation supplies the full relevant actual origin, including indispensable receiving-mode efficacy.
5. The bearer identity relation used at the conclusion is the same identity relation used in the premises.

These are application requirements, not five demands for separate experiments. One rational presentation could warrant connected claims together. But the claims cannot be supplied merely by importing a term such as nature, fundamental, uniting or same into a stronger role.

Grounding, constitution and productive donation must therefore retain their respective types. Constitutive dependence does not automatically establish efficient reception. Equally, a power's intrinsic presence does not show that its availability has no further productive prerequisite. Genuine later supply cannot retrospectively supply the antecedent availability used by that very route; genuine upstream completion could address it. The final article preserves both sides of this fixed-antecedent distinction.

## 4. What changes, and what does not

The candidate assessment gives a concrete reason not to treat ordinary constitutive possession as already satisfying absolute original co-ownership. It also prevents the opposite shortcut: applying no-donation directly to an interpretation that has not asserted donation. The accepted assembly conflict remains available when the inherited productive-accounting and proper-exercise premises apply.

The live affirmative choice is now precise. Establish actual productive provision, and apply the non-borrowing constraint at that relation; or establish the proper exercise's subject-indexed identity at the proposed constitution; or defend a different original-possession relation with its own exclusion or co-ownership argument. None of these is won by changing an entity's name or moving contributors inside a boundary.

No new actual bearer is identified here. The local subject already has its admitted credit, and the original-possession argument retains its inherited prima-facie force. The result is a limited assessment of one candidate interpretation, not a new whole-origin completion, a metaphysical impossibility of composite sources, an established plural ultimate ontology, or an all-things-considered defeat of the affirmative.
