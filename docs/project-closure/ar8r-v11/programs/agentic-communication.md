## Historical boundary and later-program split

transclusive, and council architectures. It treated a received artifact as a new
local case: transclusion gives availability, not adoption; receipt is not
application; shared message is not independent evidence; executable form is not
authorization. Recipient-side re-orthing must recheck source, applicability,
authority, version, invalidators, privacy, and execution permission. Those are
program requirements, not historical theorem admissions.

access from transcript-derived postprocessing under fixed guards, and AR8R-T100
preserves the declared set-valued provenance-root quotient under pure copying.
