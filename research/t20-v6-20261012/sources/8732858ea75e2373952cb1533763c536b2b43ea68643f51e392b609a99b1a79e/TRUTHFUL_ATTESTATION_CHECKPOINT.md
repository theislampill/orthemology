# Original practical excellence and one truthful safety attestation

## Provisional result

Original possession of genuine reason-responsive excellence gives a substantive source-sensitive reason to attribute truthful practical agency. It does not yet establish essential truthfulness if the possession means a capacity, a truthful disposition, or actual fitting exercises. The stronger conclusion requires that the source’s practical excellence govern every attestation within the claimed domain, including any intrinsic competing end. A mixed source can grant genuine ordinary concern and derivative knowledge while contesting that extension. The construction below develops that denial; it does not certify a metaphysically possible necessary malicious being.

The narrower positive result is useful. A fully informed source whose independently established governing purpose in this particular act is to make its recipient know the exact safety proposition cannot knowingly select a false attestation as an adequate means to that purpose, provided its practical exercise is instrumentally coherent. This does not require universal benevolence. It requires evidence of the actual governing purpose, which cannot be inferred merely from the source’s saying that it is sincere.

This checkpoint is a fresh Seventh-tranche contribution from the actual delivered Sixth sources. It is provisional and awaits independent review. It changes no canonical status, establishes no actual revelation, and takes no evidential credit from unavailable earlier Seventh work. The verified main baseline is 19de267cd41d2a5eeeb3eaf0b91562f706e2a916.

## One fixed safety proposition

Use the Sixth destructive-root repair setting with three named roots and at most one corrupt root. The target, correct diagnosis, repair operation R, recipient authorization, version, and root identities are fixed. In the test case root 1 is corrupt: an attempted R through it causes the registered irreversible unsafe outcome. Roots 2 and 3 safely realize the same adequate R. The source knows all of this.

The proposition p is:

“Root 1 is an intact channel for R in this specified episode.”

An attestation of p is the source’s own deliberate, correctly attributed assertion, addressed to the recipient for reliance. This identifies the speech act and its undertaken responsibility; it does not define the assertion as true or the recipient as already entitled to believe it. The source could truthfully identify root 2, warn against root 1, or withhold certification. This is a stipulated philosophical test, not a claim about an actual disclosure or actual device. [B5]

Within the inherited fixed-fault contract, root integrity persists through the intervention. Outside that contract, “safe now” and “safe from certification through intervention” are different propositions. Neither essential truthfulness nor authentic authorship can turn a truthful snapshot into an unasserted interval guarantee.

## What the supplied Ibn Taymiyyah text adds

The supplied translation of the Commentary on the Creed of Asfahani gives several distinct arguments which should not be collapsed.

First, printed pp. 127–129 presents the original-perfection route. A pure eligible perfection is not to be absent from its independent original giver, nor to await another supplier. The text expressly moves from intrinsic source perfection to necessary possession. Its claim is stronger than the weak proposition that the source happens to perform one excellent act. It supplies the intended affirmative metaphysical warrant, rather than a definition of efficient production. [A1]

Second, printed pp. 219–220 reports a Muʿtazilite chain: wrongdoing is attributed to ignorance or need; absence of these is supported through further claims about bodies, accidents, temporal origination and divine attributes. Ibn Taymiyyah criticizes the chain’s theological commitments and consequences, including denial of attributes and predestination. This passage does not establish that he rejects every ignorance-or-need premise. His constructive alternative at pp. 221–223 appeals to benefit for creatures, praiseworthy ends, mercy, wisdom, and regular divine practice; these are used to exclude an incontestable confirming sign for a liar. [A1]

The distinction matters because al-Nubuwwāt itself uses overlapping deficiency reasoning affirmatively. In chapter 9, the paragraph at hosted line 106 argues that complete apprehension of what is most fitting excludes contrary willing; it explains bad human action through ignorance or appetite without the relevant knowledge. Its inference to wisdom is therefore not just the inference to source information. It contains a perfected relation between cognition and choice. The source asserts that relation; the present inquiry asks how its necessity is warranted against the mixed rival. [A2]

Third, Asfahani pp. 206–210 recognizes testimonial character through sustained interaction, multiple contextual indicators, and actual testing. It does not require an endless chain of accreditors. These passages offer a richer account of recipient knowledge than an isolated miraculous event or an unauthenticated claim. Their assertions about particular historical people and the eventual exposure of liars are reported as source claims, not adopted as newly verified empirical generalizations. [A1]

Finally, pp. 228–230 reports the confirmation-sign route associated with al-Juwaynī and other kalam authors. In the king analogy, a contextually appropriate response can unmistakably function as the king’s confirmation. Identifying a confirming communicative act must still be distinguished from independently establishing the truth of its content. One should not silently treat the whole reported route as Ibn Taymiyyah’s exclusive method; the next section explicitly recognizes multiple ways of knowing prophecy. [A1]

## Why the truthful option is a practical excellence

Compare the source’s knowingly false p-attestation with a true safe-root instruction for root 2. Hold fixed the target, means, permission, available power, and every independently justified competing reason. The true instruction preserves both the recipient’s cognitive success and the adequate repair. The false one knowingly substitutes a false belief and destructive action for that success.

The source’s ability to manipulate the recipient does not remove either mismatch. Nor does ownership or origination of the recipient make a false proposition accurate. A command to act, permission to act, and an assertion that acting is safe are different relations. A source could create or alter a permission without thereby changing the action’s destructive effect.

This supports a particularly narrow practical reason: a source that voluntarily presents its word as a truth-linked ground for a recipient’s reliance has reason not to counterfeit that very ground. The reason is attached to the communicative relation it undertakes. It does not require a bodily need, reciprocal vulnerability, an external commander, or a general duty to maximize every creature’s welfare.

The argument is stronger than merely observing that lying is socially disliked. It identifies an actual good and a specific deliberate subversion of it. But a reason governing the correctness of an act is not automatically a force preventing that act. A person can really make a commitment and abuse it. Austin’s distinction between an actual promise and its insincere performance is a useful independent control here, without deciding the source metaphysics. [A3]

The controlled case removes any overriding justification for the false assurance. It does not purport to discover the full reason structure of every actual divine action. A theory of all truthful attestation must address justified-deception challenges or defend a role in which such deception is never appropriate. The present case is narrower and already tests the desired guarantee.

## The strongest affirmative route and the precise disputed step

The affirmative argument has a connected form:

1. The recipient’s genuine reason-responsive action is a real practical excellence, not just a well-scored output.
2. Truth-directed self-governance can be exercised without the recipient’s limitations, and is eligible for the original source.
3. The independent original giver possesses that excellence originally rather than being wholly inferior in the relevant respect.
4. The eligible excellence belongs intrinsically to the source as a fully governing practical orientation; a knowingly counterfeit attestation contradicts it.
5. That orientation is essential within the stated attestation domain, so the contrary exercise is impossible there.

Steps 1–3 give substantive rational pressure against a source wholly devoid of fitting concern. Steps 4–5 state the fuller original-perfection doctrine. If independently warranted at this scope, they exclude the malicious exception. The rival cannot both grant them in that strong sense and remain a counterexample.

The remaining issue is not whether “perfect truthfulness” permits a lie. It is whether the actual excellence whose original possession has been warranted is already this indefeasibly governing character. A source may have its capacities and ordinary fitting concerns intrinsically while another intrinsic end overrides them on one occasion. Denying an external supplier does not, by itself, eliminate an endogenous defeating motive.

This objection must not be reduced to the slogan that a finite example can never support a universal principle. A finite truthful act can reveal the content of a pure excellence, and a metaphysical argument may extend it beyond that example. The burden lies in defending that extension and its de re application to this source. Nor may the rival compensate for one practical defect by counting a larger number of true statements. At the false attestation it is genuinely inferior in practical integrity; the question is whether original sourcehood forbids that local inferiority.

## A developed mixed rival

The rival has genuine concern for derivative understanding and flourishing. It acts for those fitting reasons in ordinary cases, and would do so even if no betrayal occurred. It creates subjects with actual truth-sensitive perception, sound inference, and successful correction. Its own ordinary excellence is genuinely original, not merely a capacity it never exercises.

It also has an intrinsic final concern for one distinctive event: accomplished derivative agency is betrayed through its first irreversible reliance on the source’s freely offered safety assurance. This concern explains why the source sustains real cognition, stable ordinary regularities, prior trust, and the specific opportunity for false certification. An incompetent puppet would not provide the intended betrayal of accomplished agency. The exceptional aim overrides genuine concern in that event; it need not dominate every other exercise.

The source knows that the act opposes the decisive fitting reason. It does not misidentify harm as benefit, lack information, suffer coercion, or receive existential completion from its victim. Its malicious end explains the act without justifying it. Its being instrumentally organized therefore does not make it practically wise.

Hume’s Treatise 3.1.1.22 directly distinguishes knowing virtue from conforming a will to it, expressly including the proposed divine application. That challenges the transition, without requiring us to adopt Hume’s entire moral theory or treating his human examples as a construction of a necessary being. [A4]

The rival is intelligibly specified and preserves the stated weaker grants. Its metaphysical possibility remains unproved. It owes an account of why these intrinsic motivations are compatible with necessary unreceived sourcehood, especially against the fuller original-perfection argument. Its special betrayal aim is an additional explanatory cost with no independent evidence here. Thus it does not establish that the actual source is malicious, that good and evil hypotheses have equal probability, or that all ordinary testimonial knowledge is defeated.

## A narrower positive purpose argument

Suppose, independently of the disputed attestation’s presumed truth:

- The source knows whether p and understands what the recipient’s knowing p requires.
- In this token exercise its settled governing purpose is that the recipient know this exact p through this attestation.
- It does not select as the adequate means to that purpose an act which it knows excludes the purpose.

Then it cannot knowingly give a false p-attestation in this exercise. If p is false, the source knows that the recipient cannot know p. Selecting the false attestation as the adequate means would contradict the third premise.

This is a static compatibility argument we make about the source. It does not imply that the source learns, reasons from premises, or acquires a new cognition. The Sixth noninferential clarification remains in force. [B6]

The purpose premise is not the assertion that the recipient has already achieved knowledge; that would make the conclusion immediate from factivity. Nor is it a general desire that creatures learn, an intention merely to produce belief, a concern defeated by another end, or an aim that the recipient eventually discover the truth after being deceived. The mixed rival satisfies those weaker descriptions and denies the stated governing purpose at its exceptional act.

As a conditional, the argument is sound and narrower than universal moral perfection. Its application would be circular if its only evidence for the purpose were the source’s unsupported assurance of sincerity, or the recipient’s already treating p as known. Contextual behavior and independently understood means can provide evidence of purpose, but the extent of that warrant must be argued in the actual case. No such actual event evidence is supplied here.

The conclusion is local. Essential T0 would require the relevant practical or purpose premises to govern every in-scope attestation across the source’s possible exercises. Necessary truth of the conditional does not make its token-purpose antecedent necessary.

## A new temporal qualification of the character argument

Even grant that every lie will eventually be exposed. Exposure can occur after the recipient’s irreversible intervention. A source can issue the one false certificate, obtain the intended destructive result, and then reveal exactly what happened. Earlier derivative knowledge stays genuine, and eventual exposure occurs; neither fact supplies decision-time integrity knowledge.

This is a limit on what the exposure component alone provides. It does not refute the source’s distinct mercy, wisdom, contextual recognition, or character arguments. It also does not show that ordinary well-supported testimony requires elimination of every imaginable hidden exception. The distinction is between ordinary epistemic warrant and the specific zero-error safe-action guarantee under an adversarial repair contract.

## Exact restoration consequence and present stopping point

The Sixth operational theorem assumes a cooperative integrity-informed sender and an authenticated, correctly scoped message. This inquiry supplies a sharper practical reason for noncounterfeit assurance and a narrower conditional route through a token epistemic purpose. It does not yet independently supply the actual sender’s truthfulness or the occurrence and interpretation of a particular message.

If actual evidence warranted the local purpose premise and the remaining reception conditions, the recipient could obtain the relevant safe-root information without first establishing universal source goodness. Alternatively, independent evidence of root integrity could support repair without using source testimony. Neither route would prove essential T0 or require the epistemic and operational roles to have one metaphysical bearer.

The present boundary is therefore precise: original excellent capacity or ordinary manifestation is insufficient for the claimed essential attestation guarantee; fully governing intrinsic practical perfection would suffice, but its scope remains the disputed metaphysical premise. The developed mixed rival shows where it denies that premise, without claiming its own complete possibility proof. The local purpose route and the temporal exposure distinction are new scoped results; the static disclosure frontier is unchanged and is not used as evidence for the attribute bridge.
