## Result

**The revised candidate earns the bounded intermediate normative result.** An agent's active organisation of another subject's permissible bounded dependence, understood exposure, and relevant control can give that agent a special pro tanto reason for proportionate protective and transitional care. That reason can require preparation before an interruption and persist after the agent abandons its original purpose. It need not begin with a new assurance or a promise, and the recipient need not know the organiser's identity.

The result is supported by a substantive relational rationale, not only by defining a duty-bearing role. A real recipient good is deliberately placed within an arrangement that shapes the recipient's options and exposure. Responsibility for those attributable arrangements gives a rational basis for directing the transition question to the organiser, beyond the reasons applying to an equally able uninvolved helper. In the controlled absence of defeating considerations, knowingly omitting an available fitting precaution is therefore assessable as a wrong even without false speech.

This is a prima-facie normative judgment at the stated factual scope. It neither establishes the conditions in an actual ultimate-source relation nor certifies a universal moral theory. Direct rational assessment of the relation is a legitimate form of warrant here; requiring mathematical proof would misidentify the task.

## Material review requests resolved

1. **Descriptive organisation.** Section 2 and P2 distinguish active provision, shaping of routes, induced continuation and accepted handoff from authorship, foresight, power and passive nonintervention. H does not acquire G's exact relational ground merely by noticing the dependence.
2. **Independent legitimacy.** Section 2 and P3 separate warranted expectation, permissible reliance and a claim on G. They distinguish valid risk allocation from mere foresight, coerced acceptance and unilateral disclaimers. Acceptance of natural-failure risk does not imply acceptance of negligence. This avoids defining P3 by the conclusion.
3. **Care rather than an outcome guarantee.** The result, P5–P6 and C1/C6 now consistently concern proportionate care. Reasonable precautions may fail without breach; unexcused omission may be wrongful despite a fortunate outcome. The original feasibility qualifications are made operationally clear.
4. **Discharge and limits.** Section 4 and cases G–H/N–Q cover independent failure, disproportionate cost, informed refusal, compensation and shared provision. Adequate release is a sufficient route at its scope, not a necessary condition for every justified disengagement. P7 and C4 preserve that distinction. Compensation is not retroactive permission for an irreversible injury.
5. **Several organisers and technical forks.** Allocation tracks actual contributors, control, undertakings and recipient interests. Technical record duplication does not by itself create a new subject or an unrestricted guarantee. Genuine distinct recipients retain their distinct interests. No allocation theorem is claimed.
6. **Creation and motivation.** Section 4 permits a direct due-care reason concerning an existing created subject without comparing its life to nonexistence. It does not infer identical maintenance/compensation rights from the absence of that baseline. Section 8 and P1 also preserve responsibilities of a manipulative organiser without inventing a benevolent truth-service history.
7. **Timing.** Planning before interruption is distinguished from an additional, unproved obligation before any dependent relation exists. Current-purpose abandonment leaves the relevant history and exposure intact; it does not logically cancel them.
8. **Source and attribution controls.** The revised source capsule incorporates the relevant risk-allocation caveat. The constitutive discussion and C8 now use the same structural-rational terminology. No actual disclosure, source authentication or access permission is obtained from the duty.

The author's final source-scope check also identifies an important application qualification: independence of the malfunction must concern the assessed tampering or withdrawal, not an absolutely independent productive origin outside a granted whole-origin G. A bounded ordinary-organiser case can isolate the history from an externally caused fault; the full original-source application may contain additional causal grounds of responsibility. The proposed organisation-based reason can be sufficient without being the only ground there.

No material correction from the v1 review remains unresolved in this v2 packet.

## What kind of advance this is

The strongest conservative classification is a **bounded relational-duty application**, possibly described as a nonpromissory undertaking or role. That classification is not a refutation: the active conduct is specified independently, and the reasons why it matters are defended rather than hidden in the label. Conversely, it supplies no additional duty merely by invoking the word governor.

Original productive status itself is not the differentiating normative ground. A derivative organiser can meet the relevant conditions; an original maker who meets only bare authorship cannot obtain the whole result from that fact. Genuine benevolent purpose locates the paper's affirmative source case but is not necessary to every such responsibility. These observations sharply limit what the result contributes to practical authority.

The inherited Sixth/Seventh/Ninth ownership set out in ARGUMENT_REVIEW_v1.md remains unchanged. The new contribution is the more precise temporal and action scope of responsibility: advance planning, transition, possible wrongful withdrawal without a false token, and persistence after purpose change. Persistence of duties in general is not a newly discovered moral thesis; the useful increment is its defended application to this pre-assurance support relation.

In particular, do not reverse the direction of the result. A duty **owed by** G to R is not an authority **possessed by** G over R. The recipient need not obey, accept unwanted assistance, or treat the organiser's claims as true. An unrequested benefactor acquires no jurisdiction merely by supplying a benefit. An independently rightful authority needs its own warrant. The candidate now consistently preserves these distinctions.

## Stronger outcomes still unestablished

- The principle can identify a reason or, with the relevant balance, an obligation. It does not predict that its bearer will respond fittingly.
- The source's duty does not provide an eligible strict complete-profile repair, actual nondomination, or their essential scope. R_G, N and essential T0 remain distinct.
- An acknowledged knowing breach does not refute the duty; a story about such breach does not establish the metaphysical possibility or actuality of a mixed original source.
- The same-channel history, actual recipient exposure, supported domain and risk allocation must be established for a concrete application. Virtuous governance elsewhere does not establish them here.
- A due-care requirement does not authenticate an actual address or create a truth-linked recipient route. D1/D2 and actual permissions remain independent.

These are present scope limits, not reasons to withhold the bounded normative credit already earned.
