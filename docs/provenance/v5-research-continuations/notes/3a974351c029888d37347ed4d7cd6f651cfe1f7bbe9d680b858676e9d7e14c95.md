## Positive source rationale

The paper does not rest its case on an arrowless node. An actual exercise with independently warranted reception requires an actual supplier in that respect; a conditional rule does not itself instantiate powers. This is an intelligible world-directed starting point. Its positive explanatory question concerns the availability of actual received being, not a demand that every fact have an efficient cause.

The best reading of intrinsic existential sufficiency gives the proposed source a positive nature that excludes its complete absence. That would explain why no further actualizer is required. It differs from merely stipulating that an unexplained contingent bearer happens to exist, and could earn a real explanatory advantage if independently established. The source need not efficiently manufacture itself in order for its nature to explain its existence.

The paper correctly refuses to infer that existential force from identity alone. An actual bearer is necessarily itself under its actual supposition; essential unreceivedness says that whenever it exists it has no external producer. Neither claim alone makes it exist in every possibility. Similarly, necessity can be received through a necessary supplier, so necessary existence alone is not original possession. These distinctions are essential to the proposed argument's truth conditions.

A factive existential essence is not rejected merely for having existential content. The live issue is whether the relevant nature has that content and whether the inference to it is warranted. This is a fairer and stronger positive treatment than an automatic prohibition on all essence-based existence claims.

Replacing such recipients with inert relays would weaken the opponent unfairly. Likewise, an actually infinite productive order is not a finite chain from which its first source has been removed, and no temporal traversal is implied by the infinite description.

The strongest positive completion argument asks whether the total account has supplied the actuality it repeatedly locates as received. This is a substantive objection about complete provision, not merely dislike of an infinite explanation. Yet an actual non-well-founded rival answers local reception with real production at every link. A constitutive hierarchy and an efficient productive history also have different explanatory targets. ## Self-production and modal scope

The bearer-first self-production objection is sound under its explicitly declared whole-existence origination standard. An intrinsic exercise requires some actuality of its bearer. It cannot explain all of that bearer's actuality while simply presupposing that same actuality as an operative productive prerequisite. A coherentist may reject the noncircular explanatory standard, but the candidate no longer claims that mere coexistence of bearer and act yields a bare logical contradiction.

The exception handling is important and correct. Earlier stages may maintain later stages; a beginningless self-maintaining history revives the completion issue; a process ontology may reverse the bearer/act explanatory order; a noncausal account may supply a different explanation type. Candid brute contingency proposes no self-production at all. Refuting the targeted causal repair does not refute these alternatives.

The asymmetry also protects contingent acts from an illicit modal collapse. A necessarily existing bearer need not have a necessarily fixed complete actual determination. Necessity of the bearer is not necessity of every item in an actual sufficient productive state. Conversely, any permission for non-necessitating explanation must be tested symmetrically against rivals.
