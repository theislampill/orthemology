## Necessity rechecked

Positive finite histories have a common behavioural continuation across their surviving models. For an independent common seed, the model-dependent transition product factors out of conditional seed weighting. Merely equal seed marginals with hidden-model correlation would not suffice. A residual positive failure probability at a feasible history would contradict original almost-sure success. Therefore every selected action's proper-support successors must be in their own lower-support winning regions, and unchanged-support successors must stay in the current universal winning region.

On a no-exit tail, any pair used infinitely often must have zero actual exit probability. Repeated trials otherwise force an exit. The same conditional argument forces every positive successor of an infinitely used pair to recur. After transient pairs disappear, the actual path gives an internally closed strongly connected component. Eventual acceptance makes all its recurrent pairs good.

For a matching live model, the required equality is equality of all full rows on that component. Choose a positive finite prefix after which only the component is used and every retained pair recurs. The prefix has positive mass in the other live model, although its mass may differ. Killing at the first outside pair or revealing exit gives equal continuation cylinder laws, hence equal probabilities for the measurable infinite staying/recurrent event. A pair bad for the other model would recur on a positive-probability event there. This proves self-verification without asserting equivalence of whole infinite laws outside the component.

These facts give the post-fixed-point inclusion for the actual winning region. Induction on proper supports and monotonicity then yield necessity of the computed region.

## Statistical sufficiency rechecked

From every active state there is a simple internal path to a target or candidate exit state. Internal edges are positive in every live model. At the final pair, either its full row differs, giving a mismatch sample, or the actual row also permits the revealing exit. This gives the conditional block bound q=(p/a)^N and mean next-milestone wait L=N/q.
