# Origin identity, eligible power, and unchanged preservation

7 October 2026. A bounded T20 continuation. This report adds modal interpretation tests and a primary-source transport check. It does not establish plural perfected originals, alter prior archives, or authorize closure.

## Result first

**Origin-essentialism is a serious objection to the numerical reading of the initial-suppression comparison, but it does not finish the original-efficacy argument.** If this concrete q essentially originates through A and B, suppressing B's productive contribution at q's origin cannot leave that very q. A qualitatively matching q-prime can leave A's profile-directed end satisfied while defeating a rigid de re intention to produce q. Those are different results; the rival cannot advertise the first as the second.

**A new preservation control survives even this stronger identity requirement.** It keeps the very AB origin event, numerical q, both proposed original bearers, and the whole represented pre-probe history fixed. It changes only B's later q-preserving exercise. A continues to preserve q. Essentiality of origin holds; essentiality of the complete sustaining history fails. Excluding this case requires a further sustaining-essentiality, policy-admissibility, or original-efficacy principle. It does not follow from origin-essentiality alone.

The primary philosophical origin proofs also have a precise transport limit. Their compossibility and origin-uniqueness premises cannot simply be substituted for the disputed original-power bridge. In particular, **AB→A changes overlapping productive bases**, and entire/exclusive material origin is not the relation of being one efficient contributor. Extension beyond matter is possible in principle; the required hypotheses remain to be earned.

## 1. Exactly what the affirmative origin thesis would say

Keep five types separate: original bearer A; A's intrinsic act alpha; the created bearer q; q's originating episode sigma; and q's later sustaining episode. A modal world is another argument, not another productive agent.

The strongest relevant hypothesis is **efficient-origin essentiality**:

> Whenever this numerical q exists, its originating episode includes these same concrete original productive roots A and B.

An even stronger version fixes the exact episode sigma, not merely the root identities. Neither version guarantees that a replacement q-prime is produced after suppression; that success is supplied separately by the productive fixture. Neither version follows merely from ordinary necessity of identity. The proposal concerns which properties q could have while remaining q; ordinary identity logic does not select origin as one of those properties.

The affirmative rationale is substantial. Concrete productive ancestry may help constitute which actuality has come to be. A matching outcome specification need not select the same produced individual. The other root is then indispensable to this q's originating, even when some exactly matching individual would arise without it. This is a metaphysical claim about q, not automatically a verbal trick. It deserves separate defence rather than dismissal as provenance bookkeeping.

Assume it for the test. Also assume that the suppression comparison removes all B-contribution to q's origin, rather than just one B-act while another replaces it. Then:

1. q's occurrence requires B's contribution at origin.
2. The A-only origin profile excludes that contribution.
3. q cannot occur under that profile.
4. If A nevertheless produces a matching object there, it is q-prime, not q.

The general Lean lemma `peer_essential_target_not_solo_eligible` checks the incompatibility of an essential peer condition and a solo condition. `changed_origin_excludes_same_effect` checks the corresponding origin-invariance consequence. They are conditional theorems; neither proves the origin premise.

A required caution: if A and B necessarily exist, q's existence implying B's existence is uninformative. The live dependence is upon B's **particular productive participation**, not merely a necessary bearer's presence. Every world in the fixture keeps both bearers present. Suppression never deletes a purported necessary being.

## 2. What follows about intention, success, and deficiency

### Profile-directed and de re ends

The retained interpreter uses source-neutral event keys. In its original declared interface, both A and B command the exact set Q, and the output is exactly that key-set. Numerical transworld identity is additional interpretation; equality of strings is not evidence for it.

This lane imports that interpreter unchanged and distinguishes:

- **Exact profile target:** realize precisely Q's represented created characteristics, with A's own genuine productive activity. Under origin-essentiality, q-prime can satisfy this target.
- **Rigid de re target:** produce this actual numerical q. Under origin-essentiality, q-prime fails this target.
- **Counterpart target/comparison:** evaluate a modal claim through an explicitly selected counterpart relation. This is neither strict identity nor an automatic interpretation of every intention.

The first remains a legitimate candidate end. But if earlier language about “full Q” was intended to promise numerical sameness of every actual effect, the origin-essentialist enrichment withdraws that promise. It cannot rescue it by silently shifting from numerical exactness to qualitative exactness. Conversely, the origin defender cannot silently insert a rigid numerical target into a policy whose disclosed end is profile-directed.

### Effect dependence is not yet received perfection

The origin thesis can make B indispensable to **this q's origin**. It does not by itself show:

- that B produces any intrinsic resource, cognition, will, or act of A;
- that A cannot realize its profile-directed end without B;
- that failure to originate q through A alone is absence of an eligible pure perfection of A;
- that every jointly manifesting activity must be individually exhaustive original provision.

These are not evasions of the actual support facts. Actual q is jointly supported, and A does not wholly originate it. The missing move is from that fact to the relevant intrinsic deficiency of A. In the typed models, effect-origin dependence coexists with an empty intrinsic-receipt relation and unchanged A-command. This is a reduced signature-independence result, not a proof that such an agent is metaphysically perfect.

If origin-essentiality is true, “this AB-originating q produced without B's originating contribution” is impossible. The impossibility is earned from the origin premise; it need not be analytic in the words used to refer to q. But an omnipotence claim normally ranges over eligible possibilities, so inability to realize that impossible combination cannot *without another premise* establish a defect in power.

A defender can instead propose a constitutive norm: a perfected original agent must never undertake an exercise whose actual concrete target essentially needs an independent original peer. That norm would exclude the rival without requiring the impossible combination as an eligible task. It is the strongest affirmative continuation. Its warrant must come from the account of perfected original agency, rather than from effect identity alone. The rival can reply that freely undertaking genuine activity with its own satisfied end need not receive an intrinsic perfection. Origin-essentiality sharpens this disagreement but does not settle it.

The distinction also prevents a tempting reverse argument. A's act alpha being a cause of q may be an essentially peer-involving **relational** fact because q has that origin. It does not follow that the intrinsic alpha is efficiently received from B. An additional account connecting relational efficacy to constitutive agent-perfection is needed.

## 3. The new fixed-origin preservation test

### Construction

The earlier `preservation_modes.py` compared AB at every represented stage with A at every stage. That changes the initial productive support, so it does not answer an origin-essentialist objection to numerical identity.

The new Python control instead compares:

- Actual: AB originates q; AB preserves q; AB preserves q.
- Probe: the **same AB episode** originates q; the same AB preservation occurs; A preserves that same q while B omits only its q-preserving exercise.

The immutable origin-event object and numerical bearer object are literally shared in the model. The first two stages' support records and scheduled exercise records are checked equal. A's stage-specific scheduled exercise records stay equal throughout. In the shutdown control, a later scheduled attempt has no successful preserving effect; no resurrection is credited. Both bearers exist at every stage, and fixed background activity is retained so that withholding B's q-directed exercise is not B's global inactivity. Background activity is declared context, not a new argument establishing its particular wisdom or a hidden contribution to q.

The unchanged retained recurrence supplies continued existence only if q existed previously and some sustaining exercise is active now. If all q-sustaining action ceases, q ceases; later activity does not resurrect it. Thus the probe does not treat q's continued existence as autonomous after origin. Its current support changes AB→A, while q's historical origin remains AB.

This is a genuine strengthening over the old fixture: **the identity being kept fixed is compatible with exact origin-essentiality**. It is not a certificate that q can really be co-preserved by independently perfect originals. Identity and nonreceipt are explicit enrichments, and the common past is the entire represented prefix, not a newly verified model of the complete cosmic past.

### What the test proves and what could block it

`essential_origin_does_not_imply_sustaining_history_essential` is a checked countermodel to the logical entailment from origin invariance to later-support invariance. It retains q through both later branches. The abstraction can be placed after any fixed prefix; no claim about the actual modal freedom of the bearers is thereby earned.

Three possible responses are importantly different:

1. **Sustaining-history essentiality:** each later sustaining support is essential to q, or the complete actual causal history is essential. This rejects the model's identity interpretation. It is stronger than origin-essentiality.
2. **Fresh actuality-token essentiality:** the very continued-existence actuality at the probe stage has essentially AB as its source, although the bearer q remains. This shifts the target sort to a sustaining effect-token. The existence and individuation of that token still need warrant; merely adding a time coordinate does not derive one distinct created effect per sustaining root.
3. **Necessary-concord restriction:** intrinsic policy or wisdom makes B's omission inadmissible. This rejects the probe as an accessible possibility, without showing any failure of numerical identity. The Lean control represents this restriction separately. Neither identity claims nor the absence of a policy-admissible probe establish received efficacy.

There is no claim that A in the probe becomes the sole historical originator of q. A is sole **current** sustainer in the indicated episode. Its preservation still presupposes the already existing q and its fixed AB past. Calling that the exhaustive provision of q's entire existence would conflate respects.

The source's maker-preserver reasoning remains a live affirmative standard. A being that both originates and continuously sustains is assigned the fuller productive role. Here A is an actual joint originator and then a current sole preserver. Whether its earlier shared origin is already deficient is precisely the original-efficacy question. The fixture does not replace that standard with human craftsmanship or mere physical retention.

## 4. Primary origin proofs: exact transport, not authority by analogy

### Kripke

*Naming and Necessity*, printed pp. 113–115, especially p. 114 n. 56, contrasts a matching table with that very table. The proof sketch concerns material origin and expressly requires that the two production possibilities do not interfere. Its distinctness step compares products made in one world and then uses necessary distinctness. It is not a proof that every efficient contributor, or every later sustaining episode, is essential. [Primary text](https://neuroself.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/naming-and-necessity-full-text.pdf#page=121)

### Rohrbaugh and deRosset

The author-hosted July 2004 version of *A New Route to the Necessity of Origin*, §§4 and 8, uses compossibility, necessary distinctness, and origin uniqueness. Its origin relation is entire-and-exclusive material origin. Overlap forces restriction; even disjoint material can have entangled productive histories. Section 2 distinguishes origination from later survival. Footnote 10 permits nonmaterial applications where creatibility and resistance to nonlocal prevention hold. Consequently their argument cannot fairly be dismissed as necessarily material-only. [Author manuscript](https://www.uvm.edu/~lderosse/NewRoute.pdf)

**New application analysis:** To use that schema here, independently establish all of the following:

- a source relation whose single-world uniqueness has the relevant meaning for original efficient production;
- compossibility of the two **numerically specified** production possibilities, not merely two similar endpoints;
- the relevant noninterference/independence of their complete productive bases;
- the appropriate modal persistence of the specified objects and roots.

AB and A overlap in A and its surviving act. Overlap of source sets is not itself a proof that no suitable independence principle could hold, but it prevents importing the published non-overlap argument unchanged. At the target q the productions are also intentionally co-directed, not simply two remote manufacturing processes. Their relevant independence must be argued.

Replacing material origin with “one efficient contributor” invalidates the uniqueness premise: a product can have several contributors without two entire origins in the stated sense. Replacing it with **sole entire original production** brings CND into view, but then the coalescent rival has neither A nor B as a sole entire origin in the actual world. Replacing it with the aggregate basis {A,B} can support an invariant aggregate origin if the remaining assumptions are established; it does not turn the aggregate into a third original agent or show its members receive capacities from one another.

The legitimate gain is therefore conditional. These primary arguments supply a respectable pattern for deriving origin restrictions from independently motivated causal-modal premises. They do not supply the missing efficient-source interpretation simply by mentioning origins.

### Lewis and the strongest flexible rival

Lewis's 1983 postscripts, printed pp. 42–43, allow counterpart relations to vary with relevant respects and context, including relations that privilege origins. His treatment preserves the distinction between ordinary identity and modal counterpart evaluation; it does not make counterparts numerically identical. [Primary postscripts](https://www.andrewmbailey.com/dkl/Counterpart_PS.pdf#page=6)

For this test there are two distinct rival options. A strict transworld-identity interpretation may deny efficient-origin essentiality while preserving q across support changes. A counterpart interpretation may keep distinct q and q-prime and evaluate the relevant qualitative power through counterparts. The first owes an account of origin flexibility; the second owes an account of why its correspondence captures the actual power/intention question. Neither is established merely by assigning equal keys. The counterpart control here covers effect comparison only, not a complete Lewisian ontology of all worlds and necessary agents.

It would also be unfair to say that every counterpart-directed end is simply a wish for someone else's result. A philosopher can analyse a de re modal property through counterparts. But a strict-origin essentialist rejects that as the relevant analysis, and the model must not conceal that disagreement. The reduced tests do not adjudicate the entire theories of modal reference.

## 5. Return to the Arabic argument and legal individuation

Direct rereading of Majmu retained L906–916 reinforces the prior appraisal: the argument is stronger than numerical origin-essentiality. At L908, unchanged productive agency plus a real extra contributor must yield **more or greater**, not even a merely like result. L909–913 then moves to entire efficacy and the transition from full power/decisive will to sole actual production. The text is not fairly reconstructed as relying only on the identity of q across worlds. [Majmu 20:175–178, retained electronic witness](https://islamweb.net/ar/library/content/22/2032/)

Numerical difference alone supplies no quantitative or qualitative nonabsorption. The origin-essentialist fixture has one actual q and one counterfactual q-prime, each with the same represented profile. It does not have two created bearers in the actual world, a greater actual created amount, or two independently individuated effect-parts. Origin-essentiality could undermine one rival argument without independently establishing Majmu's stronger conclusion.

Miftah main electronic pp. 89–92 was directly reread. Page 91 distinguishes a generic ruling from the particular ruling associated with its ground, with ownership by sale or inheritance among its examples. This gives a source-backed model of ground-sensitive individuation. It does not independently show that a physical bearer or its later persistence has an equally ground-sensitive numerical identity. [Institutional edition witness](https://ibnelqayem.com/ar/media/download/65)

The useful legal lesson is limited but real: unchanged practical manifestation can coexist with changed individuated grounds. It prevents treating visible sameness as an automatic identity proof. The unsuccessful stronger transport would turn every producer-relative attribution into an extra source-neutral created item, or every change of continuing support into replacement of the enduring bearer. No such theorem follows from the legal case.

Shifa E146 was also directly reread. The reported Abu al-Husayn response distinguishes attributions of one effect to different powers; Ibn al-Qayyim rejects its sufficiency. His positive settlement distinguishes the Creator's producing from the creature's dependent causal operation, including the creature's capacity as an effect of divine power. That asymmetric account cannot be substituted for two proposed unreceived originals. Nor may its acceptance of creaturely causation be advertised as a counterexample to the common-original-source conclusion. [Retained electronic witness, section 001](https://www.islamicbook.ws/amma/shfa-alalil-001.html)

Dar subsection 013 local lines 257–267 was directly reread for the preservation control. It endorses continual creaturely dependence and rejects reducing creation to motion or to the preservation of a human-made arrangement. The fixture is responsive only to the difference between essential origination and essential sustaining history; it does not overturn that affirmative maker-preserver norm. [Retained electronic witness](https://www.islamicbook.ws/amma/dr-taardh-alaql-walnql-013.html)

## 6. Difference from CND and complete-production nonduplication

CND concerns **one world, one effect, one productive respect**: two distinct agents cannot each have the stipulated sole entire production of that effect. Efficient-origin essentiality concerns q's identity across worlds. The two do different work.

- Actual joint q can have origin {A,B} under strict origin-essentiality while neither A nor B is an entire individual provider. No duplicated complete production follows.
- Flexible origin can allow q to have one sole origin A in one world and one sole origin B in another while preserving CND separately in each. Thus same-world CND alone does not fix transworld origin.
- Origin-essentiality changes q into q-prime under the initial suppression, if its premises apply. It does not derive per-root tokens or individual whole-provider coverage.
- Current sole sustaining provision cannot be promoted to sole origin or whole-history provision. Every CND attribution must retain its time and respect.

These are type and premise controls, not a repetition of the earlier fibre theorem. The new discriminating claim is the failure of origin-essentiality to imply later sustaining-essentiality, with a fixed numerical bearer and fixed origin episode.

## 7. Earned conclusion and remaining obligation

The strongest affirmative origin defence earns a conditional **de re correction** to the original birth-suppression claim. Its strongest noncircular next step is a norm of perfected original undertaking: why must the actual concrete target of any such chosen exercise be individually exhaustively realizable by that bearer, rather than jointly realizable with an independently satisfied qualitative own end?

The rival earns a narrower survival result: qualitative full-Q success is compatible with an origin-essentialist reinterpretation, and numerical later preservation is compatible with exact origin-essentiality. It still owes the metaphysics of genuine coalescent original efficacy, wisdom, and admissible choice. The diagnostic construction does not settle those obligations by itself.

There is no new universal exclusion and no full plural-original model. The precise obstruction is no longer simply “maybe different origin means different q.” The remaining choice is between an independently defended original-efficacy norm, a stronger essentiality of current sustaining actualities/history, and a rival that allows primitive joint manifestation without receipt. These alternatives require philosophical warrant of the identified kind; another endpoint equality or origin label would not supply it.
