# P14 bridge guards

These are anti-conflation constraints for the research run.

1. **Symbol collision:** the PDF's order/improvement cone `K` is not Orthemology's fixed trusted calculus `K`. In any proposal rename the former to `C`, `K_order`, or another unambiguous symbol.
2. **Scalarisation is not automatically refinement:** a map to `R` may be noninjective, monotone, order-reflecting, or none of these. State which property is proved. CV-R concerns coherent observation-refinement laws and its own defect semantics; do not identify the two by vocabulary.
3. **Comparability is not information:** because `R` is linearly ordered, a scalar representation can compare images even where the source order does not. Distinguish order preservation from order reflection/embedding and from mere score assignment.
4. **Admissibility namespaces differ:** a PDF response set, a cone-order improvement relation, Orthemology `Adm_Γ`, operational licences, and analysis-relative legal wiring are not identical without a bridge definition.
5. **Mixed policies are not deployed probability semantics:** the simplex geometry in the PDF does not establish measure-theoretic CV-R/CV-C results, sampler semantics, or executable law support.
6. **Finite Nash is standard conditional mathematics:** compact convex mixed strategy space is not sufficient by itself; payoff/best-response assumptions matter. Do not claim novelty for Nash existence.
7. **Vector equilibrium needs an exact order:** specify cones, strictness, closure/convexity assumptions, and the selected equilibrium notion. Do not hide a scalarisation inside notation.
8. **Equilibrium is not truth:** provide at least one formal countermodel or separation argument showing stability can coexist with epistemically bad/false outcomes under unsuitable encoding.
9. **Functional loci are modelling abstractions:** no empirical claim that human cognition literally decomposes into the listed faculties is authorised.
10. **Worked religious example is schematic:** its numeric profiles cannot be used as evidence about hadith, inscriptions, Islamic history, or source authority.
11. **No metatheory promotion:** P14 is an application-layer candidate. It grants no new Girard/SCUU/OWOU, universe, self-instantiation, or necessary-being result.
12. **No novelty promotion:** global novelty remains P09/U13 territory. P14 may identify closest literature and standard facts needed for correctness, but must not close U13.
