## 1. Direct contextual findings from the Arabic

### 1.1 The unchanged-agent argument is stronger than token identity

Majmu 20:175, retained L908, contrasts solitary and combined productive activity under an unchanged relevant agent condition and explicitly mentions will, movement, instruments and similar features. It claims the combined product would be more or greater, and uses qualitative equivalence (*mithl*), not only numerical identity. Merely distinguishing two histories or asserting origin-essential identity therefore does not answer it. Nor does retaining a standing capacity or an immutable Python act object establish that the actual productive exercise is unchanged.

The provision engine's coalescence policy changes from the AB-joint rule to A-solo when B is removed. This is a useful diagnostic of the distinction, already acknowledged by its author. It is not satisfaction of a fixed-operative-production antecedent. The redundant program avoids that particular rule switch but uses idempotent event-key output; that identifies two rule derivations with one event in the interpreter. Numerical effect identity is thereby proposed, not metaphysically earned.

This is not a reason to insert nonabsorption or provenance injectivity as an independently established premise when those are the inference under test. It is a reason to disclose the exact primitive and assess its warrant. The source explicitly argues for nonabsorption; merely denying it is not an independent refutation of another premise.

### 1.2 Three relevant sorts appear in Minhaj

V03L0272 distinguishes the intrinsic act borne by A from that borne by B. It then says that in the detached created thing, A's effect in it is not numerically B's effect in it: there must be two effects. The phrase is not simply a statement that two numerically different created substances must exist. A created bearer/product, an effect or contribution in it, and an intrinsic act must therefore be distinct sorts in the review.

A many-to-one map from acts to a created product leaves open two distinct causal contributions to that one product. It need not falsify this sentence. The live inference comes next: if the effects condition one another, the source classifies the sources as deficient in independent efficacy; if they do not, it treats their products as separate and nonconcomitant. The source also expressly admits ordinary distinct intrinsic acts whose effects mix, at V03L0268. Its intended exclusion concerns the original-lord profile.

A model can consequently fail in either direction. It can collapse the two contributions into one event label and claim to answer the source, or it can bake original-owner tags into numerical product identity and claim the source's conclusion is analytic. Neither maneuver provides the missing justification.

### 1.3 Necessary concurrence is not already efficient receipt

Minhaj 2:168–172 expressly distinguishes intrinsic attributes, harmless concomitance, external efficient donation and a priority circle in what completes an agent. The plural-concurrence argument at V03L0272–0273 adds the substantive claim that each will and act would be part of what makes the other willing and acting. This classification is argued for; it is not a generic truth that co-occurring states cause one another.

The perfection argument is equally explicit. Minhaj 2:180 calls efficacy a perfection and externally conditioned efficacy a received perfection. Majmu L919–921 transfers possible concentration of powers to each existing bearer, then transfers external-product dependence to intrinsic act/quality dependence. These are the strongest source claims, and a thin unbestowed-power label does not preserve them. Conversely, the conclusion of the disputed transfer cannot be imposed as an upstream premise against a test of that transfer.

### 1.4 Knowledge follows the occurrence in the explanatory argument

V03L0272 says knowledge of an occurrence follows the occurrence, which follows its producer's will. In context, foreknowledge is rejected as an independent explanation of the very concurrence whose truth it presupposes. Nothing here alone warrants temporal learning from an independently existing future, or efficient donation of the knowing perfection by its known object.

The prior T17 P79–81 already makes this distinction. The new controls add discrimination; they must not be presented as discovering an omission that the earlier reading had already addressed.

## 2. The frozen-omniscience problem and its proper limit

The willing author correctly shows that changing a peer's actual contribution while freezing correct knowledge of that contribution produces nonfactive states. Such states are not admissible metaphysical worlds of an omniscient agent merely because an intervention function returns them.

But the rejection is not a plurality-specific impossibility result. My independent control performs the same freezing on a single source that knows its own chosen effect: replacing the effect while preserving its old knowing content also violates factivity. No rival original is needed. It would be invalid to infer that the source therefore lacks every alternative ability.

A further direct source control now strengthens this distinction. Minhaj V02L0181–0185, read continuously after the willing author identified it, explicitly distinguishes impossibility in itself from nonoccurrence fixed by true divine knowledge, report or writing. V02L0182–0184 nevertheless calls the latter possible in itself and within power. Known nonperformance therefore cannot simply be classified as deficient standing capacity. This does not make contrary complete divine wills jointly admissible, or establish a specific plural-original cooperation policy.

There is a general reason, beyond the finite examples. If admitted worlds satisfy K(e)→e, restricting them to a fixed state with K(e) makes e hold in every remaining world. This is true even when the subject is a passive observer and has no productive role. Thus outcome entailment over fixed complete factive information cannot define productive sufficiency.

Three different comparisons are needed:

1. Freeze the relevant productive command, end and power, but recompute truthful relational knowledge of a causally irrelevant peer. This can leave the productive outcome unchanged. It is a conditional diagnostic, not proof that knowing-token identity or intrinsic nonreceipt is metaphysically preserved.
2. When knowledge of the peer is an input to the means-selection rule, changing it while freezing the command can violate that policy; replanning may preserve the end while changing the actual exercise. It does not refute the unchanged-exercise argument.
3. If full intention includes the manner “e through B's contribution,” a solo realization of e fails the specified manner. If intention is just e, the manner restriction must not be added after the test. Neither specification by itself proves intrinsic deficiency or perfection.

Majmu's enumeration of will, movement, instruments and similar relevant conditions does not explicitly specify freezing all omniscient contents. Reading it that way is an additional interpretation, and one that trivializes the modal comparison unless justified. Reading it so weakly that every changed productive policy counts as unchanged is equally unacceptable.

The live task is a relevance and individuation account of power, willing, knowing and actual exercise. “All interventions are illegitimate” and “every intervention yields a metaphysical world” are both unsupported.

Proof support is computed from executed rules, improving on arbitrary owner tags. But the classification of a rule as genuinely productive, a guard as a condition rather than a donor, and a rule derivation as an extramental effect remain interpretive commitments. Joint-created-mechanism and A-created-mechanism controls genuinely expose added created conditions; they do not establish that every nonentity production rule needs a third efficient creator. P7 requires an explanation of actual contingent mixing, not automatically another entity that creates an abstract mathematical law.

### Intrinsic willing

Identity accessibility supplies factivity and all represented true information. It does not supply constitutive nonreceipt of the knowing act. A symmetric allocation, intrinsic role weighting or weak-dominance rule can select behavior only under its disclosed normative semantics. A source-neutral good that determines a total outcome does not by itself determine who produces which part. Exact foreknowledge at each candidate world does not remove this multiplicity.

The author now distinguishes reason-input dependence, epistemic truth conditions and productive donation in its intentions matrix and initial completed RESULT.md, which was read in full. Its later §5A relational-good addition was also directly reviewed. Twenty-four willing tests were independently replayed before that addition and 31 passed in the later relational-good replay. This is the right refinement. It still leaves the source-warrant of the selection rule and the intended scope of perfection to be argued.

### Relational-good refinement and direct scan check

The strongest enriched-intention rival retains exactly the same created q but includes both agents' participation in the total end. Same-q solo ability does not satisfy this richer end. That is a legitimate proposal of intentional content; it is not automatically a source-endorsed perfection.

This directly supports distinguishing endpoint from total good. It does not directly support making another uncreated original's intrinsic act a necessary, pure and fitting end of the first. ### Causal abstraction

The HP code implements the modified actual-value contingency and minimality test for candidate root conjunctions only. In the parallel case A∧B is a modified-HP cause, and A and B are each *part of a cause*. The primary paper explicitly uses that terminology; neither is thereby causally irrelevant. None of these roles is complete original provision.

The full fine states differ, so the root packet correctly rejects full-created-process equality. Its projection fails an intervention-commutation test involving a hidden route variable. Coarse input/output agreement therefore cannot be promoted to a strong abstraction or original-provision identity. The paper-based methodological result is useful precisely as a rejected transfer.

## 4. Realistic further work and precise obstructions

1. Complete a single interpretation joining the provision engine's act annotations with the willing lane's end/manner/means distinctions. State which parts of the earlier standing-power and perfection premises are interpreted, which are granted, and which remain unmodeled. Without this join, the separate controls cannot become a full original-source model by aggregation.
2. Assess a three-sorted direct-coalescence candidate: two intrinsic acts, distinct effects/contributions in one created product, and no omitted created mediator. Explain the actual occurrence, relevant capacities and will content. The exact contested bridge is the transfer from these distinguishable contributions to intrinsic dependence, not an act/product ID mismatch.
3. Evaluate the source's nonabsorption rationale for source-neutral complete effects, including whether productive efficacy is necessarily additive or cancellation-free. Physical conservation models and finite monoids discriminate interpretations; neither supplies a universal metaphysics of pure creation. An appeal to saturation needs an account of the actual efficacy and all created consequences, not just the output bit.
4. Defend or reject the de re applicability of concentrated power as a pure perfection of each existing bearer. Assigning unrestricted scalar constructors helps test consequences once granted; it cannot prove their applicability or the necessity of their exercise. The source's concentration examples do not by themselves settle bearer identity.
5. Determine which relations of knowing, intending and acting can vary without borrowed constitutive perfection. A targeted local search for explicit knowing-content change in selected retained Arabic corpora produced no decisive passage in its displayed matches. Separately, the willing author identified V02L0181–0185, which I directly checked and which decisively blocks treating fixed-knowledge nonoccurrence as lack of standing power. The narrower ontology of an intrinsic knowing token with changing relational content remains unestablished. Neither the negative search nor the positive conditional-power passage exhausts that question.

The obstruction is not lack of larger finite cardinalities. It is the missing justified semantic map across these causal, intentional and metaphysical sorts. That map may be defended philosophically, challenged by a stronger premise-preserving interpretation, or shown to require a specific new source premise. All three remain legitimate research work. This review withholds any declaration that the four fronts are exhausted.
