# Causal abstraction does not recover original productive provision

Ongoing T20 frontier research. This is a methodological discrimination and rejected transfer, not a claim that the Ibn Taymiyyan argument is refuted. No research closure is asserted.

## Question

Can equality of a complete coarse input/output intervention table, or a standard actual-causation classification, establish that two candidate originals produce the same numerical effect in the same complete productive respect?

## Direct result

No such general inference is valid. Three explicit finite process models agree on A, B and E under all 108 combinations of exogenous context and interventions on those three variables. Their productive route variables differ. Left preemption has HA=A and HB=B∧¬HA; right preemption reverses the priority; parallel production permits both route events. E is the disjunction of the route events. At actual A=B=1, their route states are respectively (1,0), (0,1), and (1,1).

A separately implemented modified-HP check, restricted to candidate input conjunctions A/B, returns {A}, {B}, and {A,B} respectively. The conjunction-output control returns individual causes A and B. Four initial tests and a later fifth concordance test pass. These are finite Python checks, not kernel proofs. The oracle implements the stated clauses, not every variant of actual causality, arbitrary causal models or all candidate endogenous causes.

All 108 matches concern the full declared *coarse* intervention table. They do not concern the full created history. In an ordinary physical interpretation, the route events are additional created events. This control therefore cannot be advertised as a countermodel with exactly the same complete created field, much less the same original productive provision. The provision/identity lane separately investigates models that do not introduce such created route events.

## Why the transfer fails

Let π map fine states to observations. A predicate P has an observation-level representation Q with P(s)=Q(π(s)) exactly when P is constant on each fibre of π (restrict Q to the image if π is not onto). The forward implication follows by equal-image substitution. Conversely assign each observed image the shared P-value of its preimages. This is elementary factorisation, not a claimed new mathematical discovery.

In the actual comparison, the observation (A,B,E)=(1,1,1) is identical while the predicate 'the A route occurs and the B route does not' differs. Therefore no function of that observation can recover this route fact. Using the entire permitted intervention table as observation does not help: that too is identical across the three models. An independently justified mechanism or finer observation is required.

The projection also fails a stronger intervention-preservation test. The cylinder image of do(HA=0) under projection to A/B/E leaves all coarse valuations possible, so its induced coarse intervention is empty. Yet in the left model with context (A,B)=(1,0), do(HA=0) changes E from 1 to 0. Thus this projection does not commute with every fine intervention that its cylinder map would admit. Restricted root/output agreement is not a strong causal abstraction.

## Necessary concord makes the permissible test set substantive

A second exhaustive control enumerates all 16 Boolean laws from two inputs. Restricting admissible input profiles to (0,0) and (1,1), and observing output 0 and 1 respectively, leaves four laws: conjunction, disjunction, first-input projection and second-input projection. Both off-diagonal queries are required to distinguish all four. Neither is in the declared admissible set.

This is an exact information limitation. It does not prove that originals must be concordant, that off-diagonal worlds are metaphysically impossible, or that their comparative causal powers are meaningless. It shows where an intervention-based assessment needs independent admissibility justification. A counterpossible diagnostic can still reveal a law's formal behaviour without becoming an admitted source world.

## Return to the Arabic argument

The retained Majmu 20:175 passage (display L908) claims more than numerical-origin essentiality: under unchanged productive conditions, genuinely combined efficacy must yield a greater/more extensive product rather than merely the same output. A coarse Boolean 'effect exists' observation can conceal just such differences. The process examples therefore do not defeat that substantive nonabsorption principle.

Darʾ retained L627–640 also distinguishes a producer's intrinsic act, differentiated created contributions, and the complete external effect. The formal word 'event' in a structural causal model is a variable assignment; it is not automatically the numerical extramental effect of that passage. Nor is modified-HP 'part of a cause' synonymous with complete original production. No semantic mapping in the present probe establishes those identifications.

The new useful finding is consequently a rejection: importing a mature actual-causation definition or a saturated truth table does not supply the missing original-provision semantics. The positive requirement is to identify the target's causal/process granularity, the relevant act/effect identity conditions, and the interventions appropriate to the source's unchanged-agent comparison. These remain concrete input requirements for the parallel source-based investigations.

## Realistic next tests and limits

The provision lane can now test whether direct coalescence with no created intermediate retains a meaningful entire-effect relation. The willing lane can test whether the relevant intervention changes determining knowledge or intention, rather than merely an unrelated factive accompaniment. Those are useful next discriminations. Repeating more OR truth tables would not answer either question.

No result here settles the reality of necessary originals, legitimises a rival metaphysics, authenticates an institution or establishes numerical identity across possible worlds. The exact missing map, rather than an unexplained 'semantic gap', is recorded above.
