# Integrating the positive constraints into the source-ascent research ledger

## Recommended research update

The new work supports **conditional target and comparison-rule determination**, not an automatic personal-source ascent. This is a positive advance over treating every added condition as another uninformative predicate. Accuracy-dominance can rule out incoherence under an explicit normative regime; stronger observed-outcome score constraints can select a logarithmic comparison rule. The open source question is then more precise: what independently warranted relation makes this objective norm or original representational particular a state of the fundamental bearer itself?

This note is a proposed research interface. It does not change B0–B16, any public owner status, the R5 verdict, or the two sealed P15-derived returns.

But a necessary truth, a proposition type, an instantiated type and one particular necessary representational state remain different objects. ## What each added constraint actually buys

| Additional constraint | Positive exclusion or conclusion | Load-bearing warrant still required |
|---|---|---|
| Truth-conducive correspondence causally mediates retention | Excludes an otherwise identical accidentally retained success mechanism from that historical function class | Content individuation; why the selected correspondence supplies the intended proper function or normative authority |
| Positive weighted quadratic accuracy plus available alternatives and anti-dominance norm | Incoherent credence vectors are impermissible under that regime; coherent vectors are undominated | Truth-state space, accuracy geometry, comparison authority, availability and other constraints |
| Differentiable strict propriety plus observed-outcome-only scoring, relative interior of full ≥3-outcome simplex | Logarithmic score up to positive scale and outcome constants | Why that evaluation restriction suits the task; already-coherent domain; fixed outcome law; boundary conventions |
| Constitutive original hosting of a representational particular | An intellectual bearer at the actual locus under the defended hosting theory | Why the item is such a particular; why it is necessary if necessity is claimed; why its host is the proposed ground |
| Intentional choice because the agent recognizes fittingness | A genuinely reasons-guided local act under a nondeviant explanation | Objective adequacy of the reason and end; bearer identity; stable Wisdom rather than one act |

These conclusions do not all have the same evidential form. The accuracy and score characterizations are logical/mathematical conditionals. The account of historical function is causal and theory-dependent. Hosting and reasons explanation require ontological or constitutive interpretation. Explanatory preference for a unifying personal source is abductive. None can be upgraded simply by borrowing the word “necessary,” “local,” “truth” or “source” from another row.

## A newly explicit policy boundary

Let B be an admissibility class of positive accuracy weights. There are three different targets:

1. `State target`: b is coherent
2. `Per-standard availability`: for every w in B some coherent c_w improves b
3. `Common licensed transition`: one available c improves b under every w in B

The accuracy theorem fixes target 1 and yields 2, under its premises. The two-standard rational counterexample refutes the automatic step from 2 to 3, even for randomized coherent repairs evaluated by expected Brier loss. The narrower weight interval supplies a positive 3 with a nonzero uniform margin. Thus score uncertainty need not destroy repair, but its width and geometry matter.

This is a concrete instance of P15's admissibility semantics. The machine can disclose whether the claimed common transition is supported by the declared class. It cannot decide that the class includes every legitimate norm. A source or independently justified norm-fixing argument may narrow B and make a common transition possible; the warrant for that narrowing is a separate input. This preserves the real operational value of source restriction without confusing it with source truth.

## Adversarial standard for the next substantive source argument

A proposed positive ascent should identify the exact object it is explaining: a proposition's truth across worlds, a representational type, an actual thought, a norm, a selection process, or a because-of-fittingness act. It should then state the source relation: causal production, metaphysical grounding, constitutive hosting, content fixing, norm authoring, or warranted testimony. If the relation changes during the argument, supply the connecting premise and defend it against the enriched R5 proposal.

The strongest positive case can claim that a necessary intellect unifies original content, norm recognition, fitting selection and efficacious action better than primitive impersonal semantic and normative facts. That is an intelligible explanatory hypothesis. The rival can counter that the intellect's necessary truthfulness, goodness, internal unity and content structure are themselves substantive commitments, while local minds and impersonal foundations divide rather than erase the explanatory work. Comparing those commitments requires a theory of explanation and source evidence. Predicate counts or finite expansion freedom do not settle the balance.

The sharply refined question is therefore:

> Which independently warranted feature of objective truth or norm-governed intelligibility requires original representational realization at the fundamental ground's own locus, rather than determining a norm that local thinkers instantiate and recognize?

The new accuracy constraints show that some useful normative structure can be fixed without already answering that question. They therefore prevent both an overstrong personal-source inference and an overstrong claim that normative targets can never be derived from independently motivated additional premises.
