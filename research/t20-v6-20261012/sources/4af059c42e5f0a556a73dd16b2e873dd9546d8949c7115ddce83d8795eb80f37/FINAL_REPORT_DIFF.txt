At 114–116 the response asks whether completion concerns each individual worldly occurrence or the collective world. Different portions depend on different conditions.  The exact referent is crucial. This is **not** denial of one common original, comprehensive original provision, or an adequate account for every particular. It denies the opponent's treatment of the entire successive order as though its productive completion were one joint event with the same fixed antecedent conditions. A common bearer can perform many differently conditioned actual acts. Sameness of source does not entail sameness of the complete productive condition for every effect.
