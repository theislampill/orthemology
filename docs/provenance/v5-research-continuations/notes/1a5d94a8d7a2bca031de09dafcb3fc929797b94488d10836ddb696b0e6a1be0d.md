# Common endpoint repair and infinitesimal nudging: exact relation

## Literary context

## Precise universal-nudge condition

Use the same finite categorical simplex and two fixed coordinate-additive Score families as S2, with at least three genuine outcomes. Fix one outcome index i0. At every interior coherent p, require positive e_n -> 0 and direction vectors d_n such that b_n=p+e_n e_i0 lies in the open cube and, for every n and every truth vertex v,

    grad_b D_F(v,b_n) · d_n < 0,
    grad_b D_G(v,b_n) · d_n < 0.

These are actual score derivatives, not a proxy quadratic loss. This frozen formal condition permits an arbitrary ambient descent direction. Konek's stated nudge points toward a coherent endpoint, which is an additional geometric restriction. Neither d_n nor b_n+d_n in the frozen condition is required to be coherent. A sufficiently small positive step in d_n improves all the finitely many losses, but a coherent common endpoint is not assumed.

Then the following four universal conditions are equivalent:

1. The displayed common local-nudge condition
2. F and G differ by one common positive scalar and coordinate-specific affine terms
3. The exact common coherent endpoint-repair condition of S2
4. The displayed strict common derivative inequalities with d_n=c_n−b_n for one interior coherent c_n under both scores

The conclusion also links to S7 through their common affine-equivalence characterization. It does not assert pointwise equivalence between a nudge and a coherent endpoint at one fixed forecast.

## Necessity

Strict descent makes each d_n nonzero. Normalize it to unit norm and extract a unit subsequential limit d. Continuity of the Hessians and b_n -> p yields

    (p−v)^T Hess F(p) d <= 0

for each truth vertex, and similarly for G. Their positive p-weighted averages are zero. Every inequality is therefore equality. Hence Hess F(p)d and Hess G(p)d lie in the normal line span(1). The same nonzero d lies in both positive-metric normal lines, so those lines coincide. For positive diagonal Hessians this means their diagonal entries are proportional at p. The existing third-coordinate connectivity argument gives one global proportionality constant and twice integration gives the affine generator relation.

The compactness proof normalizes the directions internally; it does not assume a nonvanishing limit or bounded original directions. The key codimension-one fact is special to the categorical simplex. Merely sharing one normal vector need not identify higher-dimensional normal spaces of a general truth polytope.

## Constructive sufficiency

For an incoherent interior b, define

    d_i = (1−sum_k b_k) / f_i''(b_i).

Then every state-loss directional derivative has exactly the same value:

    grad_b D_F(e_j,b) · d = −(sum_k b_k−1)^2 < 0.

If F=aG+affine, a>0, the same direction is strict descent for G as well. This construction requires no score-dependent nearest-point oracle. The formal proof uses the already established local interior perturbation sequence to keep every b_n in the score domain.

## Coherent-endpoint-directed wrapper

Konek's nudge is aimed at a coherent vector. That extra restriction is now formalized separately rather than silently attached to the ambient premise. Put

    S(b)=sum_i 1/f_i''(b_i),
    r_i(b)=(1/f_i''(b_i))/S(b),
    c=b−(sum_i b_i−1)r(b).

Then sum_i c_i=1 and, as b->p in the interior, continuity and positivity of the second derivatives give c->p. Hence c is interior coherent for sufficiently small perturbations. The direction c−b is the positive scalar 1/S(b) times the earlier reciprocal-Hessian direction. Each actual state derivative is therefore

    −(sum_i b_i−1)^2 / S(b) < 0.

The formal construction proves these facts and shifts the perturbation sequence so that every listed c_n is interior. Necessity follows by forgetting the extra coherent-endpoint restriction. Thus the precise universal classification also holds for coherent-endpoint-directed nudges, without asserting that their endpoint itself already improves every loss.

## Independent pointwise separation control

Use quadratic metrics I and diag(1,2,2), b=(1/2,7/20,7/20), and d=(-13/10,-1,-1). For the audit's generators without a factor 1/2, the three actual directional derivatives are (-1/10,-7/10,-7/10) for I and (-3/2,-1/10,-1/10) for the second metric. ## What this changes about positioning

The endpoint-versus-nudge distinction remains a genuine difference between the raw premises, but it does not separate their universal classifications in this fixed additive categorical regime. The most cautious research claim is a checked categorical classification and precise bridge to the earlier local-improvement problem. It would overstate matters to present common-gradient reasoning, binary curvature symmetry, or the common-improvement problem itself as new. Whether the exact categorical theorem has appeared elsewhere remains a literature question, not something settled by this proof.
