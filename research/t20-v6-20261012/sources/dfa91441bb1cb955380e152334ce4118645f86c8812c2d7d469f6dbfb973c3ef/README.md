## Distinct claims and assumptions

- Ordinary mathematical proof: a positive Euler step of the full moment hierarchy at a finite positive atomic law with a moving atom fails full square positivity. This is not the same as Euler in a finite coordinate chart followed by law reconstruction.
- Constructive repair: start from a known valid positive finite atomic law, move particles under explicit transport-step guards, reweight positively, and reconstruct moments. The repair does not recover an unspecified law from arbitrary incomplete data.
- Exact code evidence: rational arithmetic and finite symbolic controls test the supplied implementation and examples. They do not constitute a general theorem-kernel verification, a global numerical-convergence theorem, or a stability/rounding guarantee.
- Reversibility: retain the stated known step and admissible domain/guard assumptions. An injective guarded particle transition does not imply global surjectivity or reconstruction without its step information.
- Deployment: no actual DAEE implementation was inspected, changed or deployed. The Euler obstruction is an explicit mathematical scheme discriminator, not an assertion of a defect in an existing product.

The continuous-geometry, radial reverse-lift and two-atom antecedents retain their own assumptions. In particular continuous realizability does not itself certify a numerical method; full-path positivity differs from initial-law existence, and local finite-coordinate recovery differs from full-hierarchy Euler.

## Offline replay

With Python 3 and SymPy already installed, extract into a fresh directory and run:

    python replay.py --output /absolute/path/to/a/new-output-directory

The output must not exist. Replay verifies every included-file hash and the configured accepted/input digest bindings, copies evidence into fresh output, runs configured author and independent controls, compares regenerated reports byte-for-byte with their preserved portable targets, and verifies the extracted packet was not changed. Execution logs and the replay receipt are written in the new output. Live originals are checked separately in the external seal receipt.

Whole modern papers, source page images and broad baselines are excluded. Any source URLs, versions, loci, limited source readings and local ancestry-search descriptions are inherited historical records, not newly validated external content. Prior formal results mentioned in antecedents retain their original narrow scope and are not kernel-rebuilt here. Only suites explicitly named in REPLAY_CONFIG.json are freshly executed; prerequisite reports do not imply reproduction of their entire historical projects.
