# Audit selection as a genuine higher-order information interface

10 October 2026 UTC. Root derivation of the mathematical worker's audit/verifier example, with classical parity-synergy ancestry. This is an application of inherited higher-order information and continuation distinctions, not a new general tensor theorem or an empirical cognitive model.

Let Theta,Y be independent fair bits. Let V equal Theta with accuracy a in (0,1), independently of Y conditional on Theta. The report Y comes from a known randomising source. The audit returns D=1 when V differs from Y and D=0 otherwise. Put rho=2a-1.

The complete distribution is

P(Theta=t,Y=y,D=d) = (1 + rho (-1)^(t+y+d))/8.

Each one-variable and two-variable marginal is uniform. Consequently neither the report alone nor the audit alone supplies target information, and even the complete collection of pairwise marginals fails to distinguish this law from the fully independent law. The order-three signed interaction is

E[(-1)^Theta (-1)^Y (-1)^D] = rho.

Here 'order-three' means three tensor slots; no tensor-decomposition rank claim is made. The joint probability array is an ordinary three-index tensor. Marginalisation contracts an index against the all-ones covector; this kills the signed triple component because the sign vector sums to zero. Contraction against the three sign covectors recovers rho. These are genuine linear operations on the specified finite joint distribution.

Given Y=y,D=d, the posterior probability of Theta=1 is (1-rho*(-1)^(y+d))/2. Deleting Y while retaining D gives 1/2. For F(p)=||p||^2/2, the Bregman excess loss of the deleted report is rho^2/4 at each outcome, hence also in expectation. At a=4/5 this equals 9/100; Y=1,D=1 gives target posterior1/5.

Two report histories Y=0 and Y=1 have the same current target posterior, but the same future observed audit value d gives different target posteriors when rho is nonzero. Thus collapsing them is not continuation-safe for this admitted audit operation. This does not contradict the older fixed likelihood-store quotient: its permitted actions are named set/clear/merge of immutable factors, not a future observation whose likelihood depends jointly on the retained report and target. Current neutrality is not neutrality under an enlarged, explicitly specified observation family.

This supplies a concrete structure-preserving bridge among the audit generative model, an inherited higher-order-information obstruction, and posterior-update continuation. It does not justify retaining all conceivable historical data, prove an actual human knowledge judgement, authenticate a source or verifier, or assign a probability distribution to necessary truths. The allowed future audit and its conditional law remain substantive premises.
