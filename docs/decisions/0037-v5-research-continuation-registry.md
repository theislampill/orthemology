## Decision

Add an origin-bound continuation registry, strict source/status validator and
suite-isolated replay interface for the same Orthemology v5 programme, through
sixth-tranche checkpoint 3. Retain all 42 intake families as navigation without
promoting the assessor's map to an authoritative theorem catalogue.

Exact sources, derived prose, retained reviews, fresh component checks, ordinary
proofs, runtime reach, repository adoption and external warrants have separate
fields. Preserve source/module bindings within each suite; share only identical
bytes. Fresh checking cannot be satisfied by a stale object, a differently named
source, a permissive axiom or an unrelated declaration readback.

## Owners and limits

The [architecture](../architecture/V5-RESEARCH-CONTINUATIONS.md) states the claim
boundaries and [provenance index](../provenance/v5-research-continuations/README.md)
links the actual source/review material. Existing occurrence-centred core,
terminology, verdict, multi-actor, application and historical v5 owners retain
their roles. The obligation crosswalk adds exact successor links without
rewriting historical statements or globally closing their residuals.

The strongest sixth cost tail remains ordinary reviewed mathematics with formal
components. Full chronological aggregation and general-controller P02 extraction
remain open. Philosophical premise burdens, empirical validation and external
human peer review are not discharged by compilation.

## Reversibility and verification

The change is additive and confined to a local integration branch. Current
navigation, status and manifests are regenerated through existing tools; the
historical construction base and untouched source hashes are preserved. No
self-referential HEAD digest enters generated state. Rollback is removal or
reversion of this additive candidate, without changing the frozen originals.

Deterministic tests cover source identity, namespace collisions, unsafe paths,
stale receipts, illegal evidence promotion, supersession cycles, missing tools,
compiler identity, child-environment isolation and exact readbacks. Existing
repository, native v5 and PDF gates remain required and distinct from formal
suite replays. No licence, identity, release, DOI, terminology, publication or
remote-write decision is made here.
