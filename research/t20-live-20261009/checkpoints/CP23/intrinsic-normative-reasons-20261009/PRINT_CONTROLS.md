# Printed controls and speaker boundaries

These are bounded transcriptions from inspected primary page images, not a manuscript collation. Modern editors' notes are reported as notes, not automatically adopted.

## Darʾ, Salim second edition1411/1991,8:491–494

Printed491 ends: «بل قام به ما يستحق به العذاب».

Printed492 begins: «ثم إن الله بكمال رحمته وإحسانه لا يعذب أحدا إلا بعد إرسال رسول إليهم وإن كانوا فاعلين لما يستحقون به الذم والعقاب». The same paragraph calls the acts «سبب الذم والعقاب» and reiterates nonpunishment until a messenger.

Printed492, reported first school: «وهؤلاء يجوزون أن يعذب الله من لم يذنب قط، فيجوزون تعذيب الأطفال والمجانين». The electronic Darʾ014 has an extra «لا» before «يجوزون»; the print does not. This sentence reports an opposed school, not Ibn Taymiyya's endorsement.

Printed493, third account: «وطائفة تقول: بل هي متصفة بصفات حسنة وسيئة تقتضي الحمد والذم، ولكن لا يعاقب أحدا إلا بعد بلوغ الرسالة». The following authorial preference begins «وهذا أصح الأقوال».

Printed494, authorial conclusion: «الفعل محمود ومذموم، ودل على أنه لا يعذب أحدا إلا بعد إرسال رسول». The electronic witness's omission of «إلا» is not in this print. A new quoted author, Abu Muhammad ibn Abd al-Basri, begins immediately afterward; it is not the voice used to establish the preceding preferred position.

The report's “pro tanto basis” is an analytical gloss. It should not be cited as the source's own technical terminology, nor should this sequence become a claim of automatically completed punitive liability.

## Jāmiʿ VI, printed234–252

Printed236: «ولكن عبادته وحده حق استحقه عليهم لذاته».

Printed240 argues against injury-only injustice by appeal to opponents' own rational-gratitude commitment; the transition «ومعلوم أن ترك الحق الواجب ظلم» does not independently prove every alleged right. The report preserves this internal-argument scope.

Printed244 says the “putting a thing in its place” definition needs specification and that this does not make the general account useless. This is a source admission that correct application still requires identifying what is due.

Printed246: «فليس كل من وقع الظلم في حقه يكون متضررا به». The construction examples follow.

Printed251: «فإذا كان الظلم في حق المخلوق مما يضره به ومما لا يضره به ... فالظلم في حق الله تعالى أولى أن يكون كذلك». The discussion then joins God’s right to the creature's own good, without making divine benefit the aim.

Printed252 ends «وكذلك»; its sole editorial note says the manuscript's text ends here. No missing continuation has been invented.

Apparatus cautions read: printed248's report about food is assessed as weak; the Abu Dawud report continuing249 is disconnected; printed250 has an illegible word with an editorial suggestion. None carries a new independently authenticated argument in this return.

## Jāmiʿ VI, printed186–193

Printed187 explicitly qualifies the claimed impossibility of reciprocal ultimate love by «بإرادة صحيحة، وقصد صحيح، ومحبة صحيحة». Corrupt desire is allowed as an occurrence. Printed188's healthy/disordered taste analogy is a normative epistemic comparison, not an independently verified medical mechanism. The final-cause proof starting192–193 continues beyond this bounded reading and receives no new full-proof credit.

## Asfahāniyya, al-Saʿwi edition, printed444–450

444 introduces al-Razi's report of his opponent.445's equal-pay truth/lie illustration remains inside that report.446–447 supplies al-Razi's criticism.448 begins Ibn Taymiyya's «قلت». His own448–450 reply defends real evaluatively relevant properties and rejects a command-only interpretation.

450's final note identifies the end of the material unique to manuscriptṢād beginning399 and a return to other witnesses. This is the editor's textual architecture, not a new independent source.
