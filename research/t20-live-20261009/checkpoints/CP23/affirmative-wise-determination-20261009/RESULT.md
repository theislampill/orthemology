# Achieved foreknown goods and particular wise execution

A concrete source-based practical inference is now explicit. Majmu 8:428–436 affirms prior divine knowledge and real created causes, then explains an almsgiving test in which the actual approving response to the solicitation is the good sought. The angel subsequently tells the person to retain his property because the interaction was a test.

Knowing the response in advance does not replace its actual occurrence. When that foreknown good is achieved, literal transfer is no longer needed to obtain that same response. The stated purpose therefore explains a particular changed directive without requiring God to learn an answer or receive essential wisdom from a creature.

This removes one instrumental rationale; it does not independently prohibit every later collection or prove a unique possible ending. The literal transfer was never this test's intended endpoint. The result concerns an originally directed created sequence, not an inferred separately indexed divine micro-volition or the complete determination of the whole history.

The ingredients were inherited. The new credit is their worked application to particular original wise execution, with the end, actual response and later directive independently identified. REPORT.md supplies the argument and its source, causal and modal boundaries. The standing affirmative appraisal is retained. T20 remains active.
