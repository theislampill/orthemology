# Adversarial controls for joint existential coverage

3 October 2026 UTC. These are independent inference and scope controls on the hash-bound candidate-v1. They are not representations certified as metaphysically possible. They add no primary-source exposition and do not repeat the candidate's source-associated setups. Read with `INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_v1.md`, which states the controlling disposition and repairs.

## A Contribution and whole-history controls

### A1 Branch relevance does not distribute through package membership

Keep the exact supplying assignments in main §3. Test the two operations separately: lifting a later contribution's prerequisite to every contribution; distributing a combined account's explanatory role to every contained member. Neither operation follows merely from the combined account being genuine. The earlier supplying branch remains available without the later branch's prerequisite in its relevant basis. Result: the unrestricted shortcut fails; ordinary relevance-preserving composition survives.

### A2 Real cooperation is not collateral package membership

Alter only the supplying relation so that two contributors really cooperate in one production. Both may then be relevant to that production. Result: the review does not reject joint causation or impose one independently complete cause for every effect. It rejects treating all items in a larger record as cooperating causes without a relevant supplying relation.

### A3 Contingent exercise does not entail contingent bearer

Keep the target as a contingent-concrete-bearer inventory. Make a source's exercise contingent while its bearer is necessary. Result: the source is not brought into F by the exercise's contingency. An argument about all contingent facts or act-tokens must declare a different target and re-establish coverage. This control protects the intended source route without granting its actuality.

### A4 Local projection is insensitive to added independent support

Use main §4's supported-history control without changing its local arrows. A rule which reads only those arrows returns the same result before and after the support variation. Result: it cannot implement the narrower exhaustive-availability exclusion. An anti-infinity premise could still reject both descriptions, but would be an additional commitment.

### A5 Upstream completion differs from a fixed downstream extension

At each restricted stage allow an additional supplying contribution upstream of the old boundary input. There can be an input outside each restricted account without an identical input outside their union. Result: fixed-resource preservation does not automatically apply to the completed account. Neither does this quantifier observation establish that the union supplies the relevant availability adequately.

### A6 Actual production cannot be removed for a law-only test

Preserve the actual acts of the rival's proposed explanans. The null assignment concerns a separate law-only representation whose domain and equations admit it. Result: null compatibility of those equations is not a proof that the actual active history fails E_A. Conversely, the acts' actuality does not certify E_A. The two contribution standards remain distinct.

## B Positive target and classification controls

### B1 The actual-S branch has a positive witness

Grant S as actually obtaining and retain its concrete outside-F supplier. Under capacity-bearer coverage this witness is an actual noncontingent, hence necessary, power bearer. Result: an immediate intermediate necessity consequence is earned conditionally. No identity with a separately stipulated whole-origin source, unity or intrinsic self-explanation is needed.

### B2 Delete the positive supplier

Replace S by the assertion that no availability input remains unaccounted for. A whole-history adequacy proposal might satisfy that negative description without selecting an outside-F bearer. Result: the necessity inference disappears. Positive S is an essential premise, not decorative wording.

### B3 Permit a different Q in the realization

Keep actual coverage but assess the candidate supplier against a newly chosen classification family in the alternative. Result: outside-F status no longer refers to the predicate whose actual coverage supports the conclusion. The proof changes its middle term. Corrected arguments must keep Q fixed.

### B4 Preserve Q and identity but change earlier classification

Retain the actual bearer's identity while allowing a comparison history in which the bearer never had its actual classification before the relevant exercise. Actual kind priority alone is compatible with that weaker description. Result: RP4 cannot be derived from identity or origin-insensitivity alone. No metaphysical possibility of this description is asserted.

### B5 Transform after a retained F occurrence

Instead preserve an actual F stage as an existentially relevant input and allow subsequent change of classification. Result: endpoint non-F status does not erase that retained occurrence from whole-history provenance. Source-preserving realization defeats S on that route even though the endpoint is outside F.

### B6 Make Q broad enough to cover every eligible contingent kind

The existence of an outside contingent producer is not guaranteed merely because only one recipient is sought. Result: reducing the target lowers one burden but does not prove POS-S or provide a new kind. A possible necessary supplier would take a different modal route and cannot be substituted without notice.

## C Relevance and resource controls

### C1 An actual outside supplier is triggered by F

The F resource is relevant to exercise, while the supplier's existential availability is independently actual. Result: F's trigger role alone does not defeat S's existential-ancestry qualification. If the supplier is an actual outside-F power bearer, coverage already yields the intermediate necessary-bearer conclusion. The argument must count the supplier rather than attribute its independent availability to the trigger.

### C2 A merely possible outside supplier is treated as background

Grant only that an actual F agent could occasion the supplier's exercise if the supplier were available. Result: no resource-complete power to realize the whole S has been shown. The conditional ability to act in the presence of a new independent source cannot supply that source for free. RP2 and the positive availability trace must do real work.

### C3 The base contains non-power-bearing concrete background

Partition the actual resource domain into power bearers and other background. Cover only contingent members of the first domain. Result: coverage cannot be applied to an arbitrary background witness. Even a non-F background witness need not be necessary under that narrow premise. This is R1's domain control; it makes no claim that background alone can really produce a supplier.

### C4 The base has broad coverage but the selected witness is not active

Add coverage for every contingent concrete member of the relevant base, and select an actual non-F background witness. Result: necessary concrete existence follows. Possession of a supplying power by that witness does not follow merely from inclusion in a sufficient capacity basis. The active-bearer corollary needs its own type premise.

### C5 Relevance belongs to the recipient branch

Make an actual F resource relevant to the recipient's production or to initiating an exercise, while the supplier's existential availability belongs to a different part of the account. Result: relevance to the whole conjunction S does not locate that F resource in the supplier's existential ancestry. RP5 must select an availability-specific trace. Otherwise the modal route repeats the package-relevance error it set out to avoid.

### C6 The entire base is called a trace without a positive entry witness

Allow an arbitrary infinite resource collection to be labelled relevant, but specify no actual entry resource connected to the supplier's availability. Result: neither the fixed-route norm nor the candidate's finite-depth rationale establishes the required connection. This is an open realization premise, not a counterexample to a properly anchored trace.

### C7 Overdetermination and alternate routes

Allow more than one sufficient route or redundant contributing resources. Result: absence of a universal but-for dependency does not by itself defeat a retained supplying contribution. The proof needs a positive relevant connection in the particular realization promised by RP, not one individual indispensable across all possible realizations.

## D Positive repaired-route and closure controls

### D1 Finite supply depth from a typed actual anchor

Grant a finite relevant trace from an actual entry witness to the supplier's availability. At every step preserve that witness's supplying relevance and any earlier F-instantiation. Result: an F anchor is incompatible with S. Under coverage of that witness's declared domain, the anchor is an actual necessary concrete member. If it is a supplying-power bearer, the stronger capacity conclusion follows. The proof uses no actual exercise assumption.

### D2 Infinite width with an independently relevant entry witness

Allow arbitrarily many simultaneous resources but retain one positively connected actual entry witness. Result: mere cardinality does not defeat the preceding inference. Selecting the witness must be warranted by the source trace; it cannot be manufactured from the word “jointly.”

### D3 Infinite depth without an independently established anchor

Allow no first supply step and no verified connection to an actual entry witness. Result: finite trace preservation does not establish the missing bridge. Applying the proof would require a new generalized trace premise. The control certifies neither the eligibility nor the adequacy of such a realization.

### D4 Necessary actual power with no actual exercise

Grant an actual necessary bearer of the relevant power while stipulating only a possible appropriate exercise. Result: the repaired conclusion is satisfied without identifying an actually exercised source of the actual field. Rejecting this as insufficient for the capacity conclusion would incorrectly impose actual participation as an extra requirement.

### D5 Bare necessity does not imply RP

Grant some actual necessary concrete bearer while withholding any connection between its powers and S. Result: the conclusion alone does not give resource-complete realization, ancestry fidelity or classification transport. Therefore no simple equivalence between the full RP package and the bare necessity conclusion has been proved. The inherited F1 relocation warning remains applicable, but its exact equivalence is not automatically a theorem here.

### D6 Independent warrant is still needed for the corrected premises

Grant the repaired entailment as logically sound while leaving POS-S, absolute realization coverage, resource completeness and transport unsupported about actuality. Result: no actual necessary resource or bearer has been established by this research. Conditional existence credit is retained without promoting an open modal application into programme closure.

### D7 The modal capacity conclusion leaves an active actual history unadjudicated

As an inference control, retain an actual necessary resource with an unexercised relevant power, alongside the opponent's proposed active F history. The selected possible S may be grounded in that resource's power while the proposed adequacy of the actual F history is left as a separate relation. Result: the repaired modal route does not by itself derive that this actual internal account is E_A-inadequate. Its necessary-resource conclusion can coexist, at the level of stated premise content, with leaving that adequacy relation open. This is not a metaphysically certified coexisting architecture. It identifies why the conditional modal bypass is different from universal collective exclusion.
