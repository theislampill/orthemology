## 1. Residual-guided hypothesis

Several ascent attempts repeatedly leave the same residual:

```text
local correction consistency does not establish an objective norm;
objective fit does not establish apprehension or uptake;
current success does not establish proper function;
proper function under one account does not establish truth or warrant under
another;
a source standard does not establish world truth or recipient warrant;
a common bearer does not establish reason-mediated integration;
a stable potential does not establish a personal or wise norm source.
```

Deep N already proves the standard additive-potential/zero-holonomy criterion
for declared correction increments and explicitly withholds authority,
fittingness, completeness, proper function, and personality. The missing object
is therefore not another potential theorem. It is a **norm-provenance vector**
that prevents one scalar “norm” from doing several jobs.

## 4. Why one scalar correction term fails

Suppose every state receives a number `N(v)`. The same numerical structure can
be interpreted as:

```text
an arbitrary coordinate choice;
a loss function selected by an engineer;
a selected-effect fitness proxy;
a social approval score;
a source-command compliance rank;
a truth-conducive epistemic norm;
an impersonal teleological potential;
a personal agent's purposive standard;
a plural governance compromise.
```

The numbers and cycle equations alone do not choose among these
interpretations. Treating `N` as “objective normativity,” “proper function,” or
“Wisdom” is an interpretation leak.

## 5. Countermodels and deletion tests

### SB-CM87 — flat arbitrary gauge

All cycle sums vanish and an exact potential exists, but the labels were chosen
arbitrarily. `C1` holds; `C4–C12` fail.

### SB-CM88 — complete stable false standard

The graph is complete and stable, and labels perfectly encode a false doctrine
or systematically misleading target. Mathematical exactness and semantic
fidelity to a standard do not establish world truth.

### SB-CM89 — truth-linked score without function

A score correlates reliably with truth, but the mechanism has no selected,
designed, or constitutive function to track truth under the account being
claimed. Truth connection does not automatically fix proper function.

### SB-CM90 — selected function with false output

A mechanism was selected or designed for a truth-directed function but fails in
the current environment. Proper function history or design does not guarantee
current truth.

### SB-CM91 — causal-role success without truth aim

A component successfully performs its current systemic role, but the role is
manipulation, compression, or social coordination rather than truth tracking.
Cummins-style function does not import Plantingian truth aim.

### SB-CM92 — Plantingian vocabulary without design/environment guards

Reliability is observed, but design-plan, proper-function, suitable-environment,
and truth-aim fields are absent. Calling the process “warranted” omits the
account’s load-bearing coordinates.

### SB-CM93 — source authority without recipient warrant

A source is granted genuine authority and truth, but a recipient lacks access,
competence, route reliability, or defeater control. `C10` does not entail `C11`.

### SB-CM94 — recipient warrant without execution

The recipient is warranted and assents, but does not perform the correction.
`C11` does not entail `C12`.

### SB-CM95 — impersonal exact optimizer

An impersonal mechanism computes the exact truth-connected potential and
reliably realizes optimal outputs. Intentional uptake and selection because of
apprehended fittingness remain false.

### SB-CM96 — plural norm administration

Several coordinated agents or powers maintain different coordinates of the
certificate. The system-level vector is complete, but no one bearer locally
instantiates all reason and authority roles.

### SB-CM97 — per-version flatness, diachronic drift

Each time slice has zero holonomy and an exact potential, but standards drift
between versions without a warranted transport. Local exactness does not yield
one stable norm.

### SB-CM98 — omitted transition

All registered cycles close, but an unregistered transition creates nonzero
holonomy. A frozen finite check does not establish global correction
completeness.

### SB-CM99 — personal source without Wisdom

A personal author chooses a stable standard for arbitrary or vicious ends.
Personality and purposive authorship do not entail objective fittingness or
Wisdom.

### SB-CM100 — praiseworthy result without because-of selection

An action has a praiseworthy end, but the bearer reaches it accidentally or by
blind optimization rather than apprehending and selecting because it is
fitting. Outcome quality does not establish the Wisdom episode package.

## 6. Proper-function comparison

| Account | Additional function-fixing evidence needed | What current success alone misses |
|---|---|---|
| Causal role | independently frozen system boundary and contribution relation | history, truth aim, authority, warrant |
| Selected effect | selection/reproduction history and etiological relation | mere present stability or success |
| Design | designer, design plan, intended operating conditions | designer existence and plan cannot be inferred from performance alone |
| Learned objective | training/reward history and target identity | truth and authority of the learned objective |
| Plantingian warrant | proper function, design plan, suitable environment, truth aim, objective reliability | social success, runtime closure, or generic reliability |
| Fitrah-oriented | exact source account, original normative disposition, corruption/occlusion model, restoration and truth bridges | output improvement or reminder response alone |

The row distinctions are substantive. They should not be merged into one
`ProperFunction : Prop` without an account tag and evidence record.
