# Communicative integrity and original practical priority

## Result

The new advance is an alternative proof organisation and diagnostic for the missing warrant. A source's defective assurance can be excluded by **complete-profile local dominance** without first constructing a single globally improved character. The route has two substantive premises: an eligible strict improvement which does not neglect any compensating loss, and a warranted claim that the actual original bearer is not practically dominated in the relevant pure-perfection comparison. Neither premise follows just from productive capacity, knowledgeable assurance or original giving.

A newly examined primary locus also improves the local affirmative case. The relational character of fidelity is not by itself a creaturely limitation: the standing excellence can belong to the source while its exercises concern recipients and determinate truths. A valid comparison must preserve that practical respect. Deleting the relations until only causal power remains loses the excellence; copying every contingent human feature imports irrelevant limitations.

This establishes a conditional exclusion route and a more exact diagnostic for its application. It does not establish essential T0, the mixed source's metaphysical possibility, actual disclosure D1 or recipient warrant D2.

## The fixed case and the inherited boundary

In the inherited controlled hypothesis, keep the actual original giver, recipient, root, version and interval fixed. The root is unsafe, and the source knows that. It deliberately asserts safety as an authoritative ground for irreversible reliance. It understands the reliance and has the productive ability to answer accurately. Its intentional beneficence elsewhere is genuine, and there is no undefeated overriding justification for this false assurance.

The comparison may change the selected response to an accurate warning or appropriate true alternative. It cannot change the safety fact, silently repair the root, turn an assertion into a command, or declare that the false token was never really an assurance. Nonlying T0 remains distinct from full relevant knowledge: a sincere but mistaken assertion illustrates why the former alone does not establish the assertion's truth.

Eighth already distinguished local excellence, same-bearer eligibility, jointly eligible character, actual possession and necessary governance. Seventh already distinguished semantic commitment from compliance and already gave the exact purpose-linked token implication. Those distinctions remain controlling; repeating them is not the present increment. [B1]

## Primary-text control

Ibn Taymiyya's *Majmūʿ al-fatāwā* 6:137–140 addresses relative perfection. Its self-description examples separate a rightful true claim from a false claim by someone lacking the attribute. It also separates criticism of harmful truthful speech from criticism of falsehood. The closing comparison concerns defect-free perfection applicable to its bearer, rather than identical outward performances. At 6:138 he expressly affirms that all divine reports, words and acts are good and praiseworthy, in accordance with wisdom and justice. The unresolved warrant in our reconstruction is not a reservation attributed to him. Earlier, 6:107–108 rejects an impossible demand that all successive acts be eternal; appropriate occurrence and appropriate omission can each express perfection. These electronic loci support a context-sensitive comparison, not a requirement to maximise every output. They do not themselves establish the modern complete-profile repair premise. [S1]

The supplied *Asfahani* translation distinguishes speech and volition generally from their praised and blameworthy forms, and distinguishes the direct pure-perfection argument from comparison with created instances. These are compatible with treating the source's practical quality, rather than sheer power to produce speech, as the object of assessment. [S2]

## Relational exercise need not mean borrowed perfection

The relevant excellence is not merely forming a true sentence. It is understanding a determinate truth-linked responsibility and giving that understanding its proper practical effect. The source itself may freely undertake the relationship by inviting reliance. The source's independence from external commands, reciprocal vulnerability or social sanctions does not remove the responsibility simply by removing those particular explanations of it.

Three claims must therefore be distinguished. An act may originate in the source's own knowing and willing. Its standing practical character may be internally grounded. Yet the success of its communicative exercise can still depend on relations among its content, the facts and the recipient's uptake. The first two claims do not require the third to be nonrelational.

This permits a substantial affirmative reply. To identify fidelity as a suitable source perfection, one can preserve the formal practical relation while setting aside dependence, bodily need and receipt of one's being. No external superior need command the source to avoid consciously counterfeiting the evidential ground it elects to present. The recognition can be rational and qualitative; it need not be a measurement.

The comparison still needs discipline. Its content, knowledge, assurance role and relevant reasons must remain fixed. If changing only the original/derivative status of the speaker is said to reverse the local assessment, the advocate must identify what relevant reason changed. The phrase 'relations differ' does not do that work. In the controlled case, the source's status does not make the unsafe root safe or supply the excluded overriding justification.

Conversely, local recognition of a defect is not recognition that this actual bearer cannot instantiate it. Actual agency can understand a norm and act contrary to it. The strongest original-priority argument therefore supplies an additional claim: the original giver is not merely causally responsible for the positive constituents of fidelity, but is itself essentially entitled to the pure practical superiority at issue. That claim has affirmative explanatory content. It must not be weakened to efficient-causal copying and rejected on that basis, or silently inserted into the meaning of 'original'.

## Complete-profile local dominance

Fix the actual bearer s. Let K contain only complete exercises or profiles eligible for that same bearer with its full essence preserved. A profile includes the other contexts and pure-perfection respects affected by the choice. The actual profile a belongs to K.

Let x ≺ y signify a justified strict improvement in the source's practical perfection across those complete profiles. A transparent sufficient interpretation is Pareto improvement: no relevant pure perfection diminishes, and at least one improves. The order is about source agency, not the number of creatures or the total amount of pleasure produced. It need not rank every pair of profiles.

The generic lemma uses two premises:

- **R_G, eligible strict repair:** any in-scope lying exercise in an eligible profile has a genuinely eligible repair which preserves the assurance role and satisfies x ≺ y in the justified complete-profile order.
- **N, actual nondomination:** no eligible complete profile is strictly better than the actual profile a in that comparison.

The stronger application proposed here uses **R_P, nonspoiling repair**: the repair improves fidelity without reducing any other relevant pure-perfection respect anywhere in the complete profile. When the comparison recognises that Pareto improvement, R_P supplies R_G. R_P is not inferred merely from a verdict that the lie lacks an undefeated justification.

Suppose a contains an in-scope lie. R_G provides an eligible y with a ≺ y. N excludes such a y. Therefore a contains no in-scope lie.

This elementary proof does not require a total ranking, one uniquely best world, finitely many contexts, a process that reaches a maximum, or the prior construction of a single character repairing all contexts together. It uses one complete improvement for a hypothetical offending context. It does not establish that any nondominated profile exists: actual a and N are premises.

For essential nonlying, the corresponding eligibility, repair and nondomination claims must hold throughout the relevant same-bearer possibilities. The same repair need not work in every context or world. But actual nondomination cannot be promoted to essential nondomination, and the fixed case's absence of an overriding justification cannot be promoted to every possible authoritative assertion. The conclusion has exactly the domain and modal scope of the warranted premises. [A1]

This is an alternative comparison route, not a requirement imposed on every affirmative argument. A direct original-perfection argument can challenge an allegedly defective essence itself. It need not first accept that essence and locate a better exercise within it. Its burden is then the direct source-priority warrant, with the required practical and modal reach.

## Three controls that discriminate the premises

The following are mathematical interpretations of stated premises, not metaphysical constructions of an original source.

**Local eligibility plus nondomination.** Use two fixed assurance contexts. Write 1 for an accurate warning that the known unsafe root is unsafe, and 0 for a deliberate false assurance of safety. Both are genuine assertions; a third, fixed beneficent episode remains genuinely intentional.

Take K = {(1,0),(0,1)} and compare the two fidelity coordinates componentwise. Each context has a same-bearer eligible truthful response. Neither complete profile dominates the other. Thus either actual profile is nondominated, yet each lies somewhere. Repairing one lie spoils the other coordinate. The invented coupling is not an established possible essence. It shows that local eligibility plus nondomination does not entail the missing complete repair.

**Repair without actual nondomination.** If K contains all four binary profiles, every false response can be repaired without spoiling another. Nevertheless the actual profile could be (1,0) under those weak structural premises. It is dominated by (1,1). Productive origin, semantic commitment and knowledge of the normative balance do not supply N by definition. The full affirmative source-priority principle would exclude this assignment if independently warranted; it is not among the model's satisfied principles.

**Repair without a realised maximum.** For countably many contexts, consider profiles truthful at only finitely many of them. Any false coordinate can be repaired while preserving all previously truthful coordinates. Every resulting profile still has an improvement. There is neither a nondominated profile nor an all-truthful member. This proves no metaphysical possibility claim; it clarifies why repair alone is not an existence theorem for complete integrity.

A bounded enumeration independently checked the finite version across all 65,808 nonempty binary profile sets with one to four contexts, covering 525,348 choices of actual profile. It found no violation of the conditional lemma and many failures of the weaker local-eligibility inference. The short proof, rather than that finite bound, supports the unrestricted mathematical implication. [A2]

## The remaining warrant

Avoiding a prior global construction does not establish that R_G and N are logically weaker or independently easier to warrant than the earlier comprehensive-priority claim: R_G retains complete-profile eligibility, and N quantifies over all eligible complete alternatives.

The fixed absence of an undefeated justification is not automatically a Pareto-repair theorem. An inferior act could retain a genuine lesser good which a truthful alternative loses, or a complete-profile constraint could couple its other acts. A justified strict overall comparison could still rank the truthful alternative higher and supply R_G. That is a different route from R_P: it needs its own comparison warrant and cannot be described as a no-loss repair.

The new diagnostic forces a specific answer. Given a candidate lie, an advocate who retains N must identify an eligible strict improvement to invoke the lemma; a rival who retains the lie and N must show where that alleged complete improvement fails. A same-bearer incompatibility or genuinely relevant loss is a substantive answer. A bare appeal to source power, ownership of the recipient, or the relational grammar of assurance is not.

A supposed essential delight in betrayal is consequently neither a completed counterexample nor something refuted merely by describing a better source. Within the counterpart route it may block eligibility. The direct original-priority route challenges its admissibility as a source essence. The rival must defend that admissibility; the affirmative must defend the law excluding it. Nothing in the finite controls settles that metaphysical dispute.

Direct rational recognition remains a possible form of warrant. It could apprehend the actual original giver as necessarily incapable of the relevant practical inferiority, without an empirical survey or a deductive chain of more basic premises. The precise content matters: recognising this bearer as underived, knowledgeable, causally comprehensive or locally beneficent is not yet recognising its necessary practical nondomination. Disagreement or lack of a numerical measure does not by itself defeat the stronger recognition. Nor does labelling a judgement 'direct' establish that it has that stronger content.

The present investigation gives the original-priority claim a controlled application and a differently organised conditional exclusion proof. It has not independently established R_G (including the stronger R_P route) and N for this actual source, or a direct source-priority law with the required essential scope. This is a limitation of the result, not a demonstration that no rational source-integrity warrant is possible.

## Recipient use and retained conclusions

Even a warranted essential-nonlying result would have to be connected to the actual event: who performed it, what was asserted, which root and version it concerns, whether the source has the relevant knowledge, and how the proposition bears on the proposed action. An authenticated second claim of integrity does not establish those links merely by repeating the source's assurance.

The theorem concerns a source's possible practical profiles. It is not an actual disclosure, a source-identity test, an actuator-safety observation or recipient warrant. The inherited exact token-purpose route also remains available at its stated antecedents; it is not inferred from the public assurance role.

The affirmative advance is that relational fidelity can be specified without making it borrowed completion, and a candidate lie can be excluded locally without constructing a globally improved character. The limitation is equally exact: whole-profile eligibility and warranted original practical priority remain substantive. Essential T0, actual D1/D2, N2 and R5 are unchanged, and the mixed rival's metaphysical possibility remains unproved.
