# Independent finite-law derivation

Fix finite represented states C, finite outcome codes O, and finitely many enabled state/action pairs E. Each K_(c,a) is a rational probability distribution on O × C. Choose D>0 divisible by the reduced denominators of all aggregate joint probabilities. D=1 works if E is empty; this does not authorize any positive-length run. For each enabled row assign exactly D K_(c,a)(o,c') seeds to joint reply (o,c'). These nonnegative integer counts sum to D. Thus a total lookup on seeds 0,...,D−1 exists. Arbitrary ordering is harmless. Computing three coordinate projections from that same assigned joint lookup preserves their joint correlation; separately sampling the marginals need not.

For any pre-transition operating history H and selected legal action A=a with positive probability, let μ_(H,a)(u) be the conditional law of the seed. The conditional law of the deterministic joint reply r is exactly

    sum { μ_(H,a)(u) : lookup(currentState,a,u)=r }.

Consequently the exact local compatibility criterion is equality of these fibre masses with K_(currentState,a)(r). Conditional uniformity μ=1/D is a sufficient condition by counting. It is not necessary: mass may change arbitrarily within equal-reply fibres. Uniformity of each seed before conditioning, or conditioning only on coarse state, is insufficient. Selection of A using the current seed may break compatibility even when that seed was independent and uniform before selection. No rejection of harmless within-fibre correlations is justified.

For a matched retained-information policy (including randomised policies with the same conditional action distribution), equal initial represented-state laws plus this local compatibility imply equal laws of every finite action/joint-reply history by induction: multiply each prefix probability by the policy action mass and the prescribed joint transition mass. Zero-probability histories need no conditional distribution choice. Literal source adequacy transfers this deterministic seed-realisation computation to observations, not a probability measure or random choice constructor into the source calculus. Adaptive finite realisations are covered by the universal theorem for each realised finite action/seed list; adaptive policy implementation is not thereby constructed.

If operating history contains more than the compared retained history, the full-history condition is stronger than needed but remains sufficient by averaging. Calling it necessary for retained-history equality would overstate the theorem. The exact iff above concerns each conditioned local reply distribution, not global equality relative to an arbitrary coarser observer.

For D1 transfer, an independently established quotient q:S→P* supplies the representative-independent joint outcome/next-block probabilities. Rational concrete probabilities imply rational finite sums on blocks. Rational quotient probabilities alone suffice even if their concrete decomposition was not rational. Encoding each quotient block injectively as (retained coarse, tag), with required label/menu/cost interpretation retained externally, permits the lookup construction. Closure must hold on the actual code image; a enclosing rectangular bound alone does not show that a hole is a valid quotient code. Empty menus, illegal actions, or terminal states need explicit policy/stopping treatment. A total zero fallback is not a legality witness.

The finite-source theorem plus external finite law does not construct an authentic random source, an infinite-execution measure, arbitrary-real exact finite-uniform sampling, or substantive philosophical warrant. Every probability of a deterministic image of a finite uniform seed is rational; irrational probabilities cannot be realised exactly by this fixed finite architecture. For a finite rational contract the global lcm exists but may be very large; existence has no efficiency implication.

Proof-slot separation remains bounded by the declared source architecture. With a common explicit seed, an ordinary source proof slot cannot add distinguishable data beyond the inherited proof-irrelevance result. A host inspecting raw proof syntax and choosing different seed laws introduces an external channel. Neither the previous cardinality minimum nor this rational lookup theorem rules out arbitrary such external channels; randomness does not itself recover an erased source distinction.
