# AR8R-FABLE-R1 — typed extraction of the OSM source, and four countermodel classes

Status: research finding. The source is Sun et al., *Learning produces an
orthogonalized state machine in the hippocampus*, Nature, DOI
`10.1038/s41586-024-08548-w`, already integrated in this repository as a bounded
source. The paper's bytes are not committed and are not reproduced here.

Its existing repository treatment — bounded empirical exemplification and
comparison, never validation of orthemology, proper function, noetic structure, or
metaphysics — is unchanged and reaffirmed.

## Typed extraction: what the measured object actually is

The paper runs two tracks in parallel — a biological/measured track and a
model/simulated track — and the repository's prior crosswalk did not separate their
types sharply. Doing so changes what may be inferred.

- **The world state is imposed, not inferred.** It is set by the experimental
  apparatus, finite, and Markov under a fixed-speed idealization.
- **The observation type splits.** The animal-facing sensory stream and the model's
  small symbol alphabet are different objects. Because the models consume one
  symbol per step, a simultaneous cue-and-reward event had to be **serialized** by a
  modelling convention. That convention is unmeasured and, as recorded in
  `AR8R-FABLE-R1-NEGATIVE-RESULTS.md` (N3), it is load-bearing for the headline
  trajectory result.
- **"Orthogonalized" is formally decorrelation.** The measured object is a family of
  pairwise correlations between mean-centred population vectors, thresholded. **No
  subspace principal angles are computed anywhere.** The reported angle is a
  monotone reparametrization of the correlation, not independent evidence. Because
  the correlation is centred across units, near-zero values are compatible with
  substantial raw overlap of a non-negative code.
- **The "state machine" reading is an interpretation of a similarity statistic.**
  No transition matrix is estimated from the neural data. The topological reading is
  qualitative, with no topological statistic computed.
- **The trajectory is a three-element order statistic** with at most thirteen
  possible values — a maximum discriminative capacity of roughly 3.7 bits.
- **There is no causal intervention on the nervous system.** All biological results
  are observational; the only ablations are on models.

## Four countermodel classes

Each is finite and explicit enough to instantiate in simulation.

1. **Same endpoint, different trajectory.** Two learners differing only in a
   per-position rate vector reach bit-identical endpoints by opposite crossing
   orders. Small enough to enumerate exhaustively.
2. **High performance, wrong geometry.** Add to the representation any vector in the
   readout's kernel: predictions are *exactly* unchanged while the correlation
   structure is driven to degeneracy. Performance-preserving, not merely
   performance-approximating.
3. **Trajectory fit, wrong mechanism.** A multiplicative and an exponential update
   rule can be made to agree **exactly at every measured epoch**, not merely at the
   endpoint, by matching one rate parameter.
4. **Task alteration.** Three automata agreeing on every pre-alteration measurement
   — behaviour, endpoint, and full trajectory — diverge under a geometry stretch.

Classes 2 and 3 are stronger than the usual cautionary versions: they are exact
agreements, not approximations, so no amount of measurement precision separates
them.

## Forbidden transfers reinforced

- A model latent state is not an ortheme.
- Rodent hippocampal measurements do not establish human noetic structure.
- No neuroscience result supplies a metaphysical or theological premise.
- Comparative model fit does not identify a biological mechanism.
- Endpoint agreement does not establish pathway adequacy; trajectory agreement does
  not establish mechanism identity.
