# Recovering an adopted indication objective: a separating probe and its exact limits

7 October 2026. New local research continuation. Prior packets, reports, code, and negative results remain unchanged. No closure verdict or historical experiment is claimed.

## Result first

The goal/means distinction now produces a substantive inverse-planning result rather than another hidden label:

1. A stable material-courtesy planner and a stable mixed planner with an explicitly adopted concurrent indication objective reproduce all four inherited calibration actions. They also make the same **unique** choices under the complete declared channel-blocking, gift-removal, and action-menu intervention family. Their selected target plans differ in whether indication is pursued. An analytic interval, not the finite test count, establishes this nonidentification.
2. A genuinely new, sufficiently cheap post-failure retry distinguishes the two concrete planners. The adopted indication objective changes the choice; a positive but unadopted appraisal does not.
3. Two matched delivery-route crossover probes are cardinality-minimal for recovering the binary objective in a narrower, explicit class. Hidden source beliefs or an unmatched private route value defeat that recovery.
4. Communicative intention, audience decoding, acceptance, proposition truth, and public adoption are kept separate. An indication-directed plan can be selected for a hostile audience that never accepts the proposition, and can fail to be decoded.

These are conditional claims about an explicitly represented planning interface. They do not identify every realistic human intention, authenticate a historical person's goals, or show that the primary source's contextual inference fails.

## 1. Strongest source argument, strongest relevant rival, exact evidence

### Source argument retained

Nubuwwat 769–772 was re-viewed in the retained page images. A distinctive personal object can intentionally indicate that its bearer was sent without an earlier token pact. Knowledge of the source, possession, dispatch, circumstances, and plausible purposes can make that use intelligible. Page 771 separately asks whether the source sent the object and whether it was sent to certify the bearer. Repair/sealing or unauthorized possession can be live alternatives; a strong alternative prompts suspension. Page 772 explicitly permits contextually understood answering gestures.

That is stronger than the thesis that an object becomes a credential only after a prior explicit authorization sentence. It also does not require identifying every ultimate psychological motive or experimentally excluding every imaginable rival. The certainty/knowledge claims remain the source's; this program does not reinterpret them as supplied numerical posterior thresholds.

The source's indication/informing language also does not collapse into the hearer's actual acceptance of the proposition. An understood affirmative answer can be rejected by a hostile hearer. Failed decoding can leave a source's communicative attempt intact. The simulation's decoding variable means **interpreting the event as presenting p**, not factive knowledge of the speaker's actual intention.

### Concrete rival pair

The inherited stronger stable rival uses question-responsive material courtesy, negative correction, repair, and warmth. This continuation compares two versions:

- Material-only affirmative target: an adopted goal of gratitude through the material gift, value 4; correction, repair, and warmth operate in their appropriate contexts.
- Mixed affirmative target: the same goals, plus a separately adopted indication objective with value ε, where 0 < ε < 1/2. The target plan jointly pursues decoding of the answer and material gratitude.

The mixed plan is not called communicative merely because it enjoys a side effect. Its adopted goal set, selected causal recipe, and subsequent retry behavior represent pursuit. A separate control retains the positive appraisal but removes the indication commitment; it no longer plans the indication path or retries for it.

Both policies use stable context conditions, without a novel-episode exception. The four old calibration actions are keep; reject the false claim; send for repair; send for warmth. Both reproduce them. This is consistency with those synthetic observations, not evidence that a real source has either motivational structure.

### Evidence that would change the question

Relevant additional evidence includes an actually available lower-cost communicative route, independently supported equality of material benefits, source awareness of the channel's operation, behavior after failed decoding, and good grounds for ruling out an alternative practical purpose. The formal matched-route result needs those premises. Mere possession of the object, additional samples from the same nondiscriminating menus, or a renamed terminal goal does not supply them.

## 2. What is modeled as intention and what is observed

The source has appraisals and an explicit set of **adopted planning commitments**. Only adopted objectives enter the bounded goal-directed planner's score. This is an explicit modeling premise: a liked side effect is not automatically an adopted objective. It is not offered as a general expected-utility theory of all human behavior.

For a selected action, the planner records the positively weighted adopted goals with positive expected attainment and the paths it selects toward them. The relevant paths are:

- Act → attempt to indicate → audience decodes p
- That path → audience accepts p
- That path → audience accepts p → gratitude for reassurance
- Act → material benefit → gratitude for gift

An attempted answer is intended in the model when it lies in the selected goal/means recipe. Decoding can be the end; it can also be an intermediate means to belief or reassurance. Gift gratitude is a separate route. Mixed plans retain both paths. Positive values on uncommitted outcomes remain appraisals, not selected intentions.

Actual decoding and actual acceptance are separately computed from the environmental channel. Source expectations can be mistaken. A source can intend an answer while actual decoding is zero. Truth of p does not appear in the planner, and there is no public-adoption classifier or rule declaring one of the policies an actual nonendorser.

The `observe` function emits the chosen action, environmental menu/costs/channel conditions, material effect, and actual decoding/acceptance probabilities. It excludes policy weights, commitments, source belief mode, selected recipe, and intention. Full latent plans in RESULTS.json are **research audit output**, not observations supplied to the hypothetical recipient.

The class excludes some further possibilities: a source might value the mere expressive performance of an answer even when no decoding is possible, have lexicographic commitments, act habitually, use context-dependent unmodeled tie-breaking, or plan with richer beliefs. The positive recovery result does not silently cover those cases.

## 3. Exact nonidentification with mixed goals

### Admissible interventions D

There are three actions:

- K: keep; cost 0; no gift or answer
- M: send mantle; cost 1; material benefit g, where g is 0 or 1
- A: affirmative gesture; cost 1/2; no material gift

For each response action the decoding probability can independently be any real number in [0,1]. The acceptance probability conditional on decoding can also vary through [0,1]. The source correctly understands these channels in this result. Any nonempty subset of the three actions is allowed. Including subsets without K only strengthens the comparison; the equivalence already holds for the ordinarily relevant menus that retain refusal/inaction.

Interventions can therefore block mantle decoding while preserving its gift benefit, block the alternative gesture, prevent acceptance while preserving decoding, remove the material benefit, or change the available action menu. Costs and the payoff of an existing material gift remain as stated. No arbitrary manipulation of the source's goal is counted as a recipient-performed experiment.

### Analytic proof

For the material planner, scores are:

- K: 0
- M: 4g − 1
- A: −1/2

For the mixed planner with indication value ε, add εd_M to M and εd_A to A, with d_M,d_A in [0,1].

If g = 1, the mixed ordering is M > K > A, since M ≥ 3, K = 0, and A ≤ −1/2 + ε < 0.

If g = 0, the ordering is K > A > M. K > A follows as above. Moreover:

A − M = 1/2 + ε(d_A − d_M) ≥ 1/2 − ε > 0.

The material planner has precisely those same strict orderings. Restricting identical strict orderings to any nonempty common menu preserves its unique best action. Thus **every intervention in D**, including the continuum of channel probabilities, produces the same selected action and public outcome in the two policies. At the unblocked target M, the mixed recipe includes attempted indication and the material recipe does not.

The result does not depend on favorable tie-breaking or hidden misbelief. It is a substantive causal-plan countermodel at this observation interface. It does not infer actual nonendorsement from a courtesy label.

The upper bound is sharp for the full declared family: at ε = 1/2, with the gift removed and an effective gesture available, A ties K. The complete maximizer set changes. The implementation retains that tie rather than forcing a convenient answer.

A further mixed-goal extension permits direct decoding, acceptance, and reassurance objectives with total weight below 1/2. Their effective communicative score is v = α + q(β + γ), bounded by that total. The same inequalities apply. This is a bounded inverse-choice region, not a transported maximum-entropy or reward-shaping theorem.

## 4. The new separating retry, and graded nonidentification

After an unsuccessful attempt, suppose the source learns the old channel failed and has a new effective channel costing 1/8. The material gift has already been delivered; the retry supplies no further material payoff.

The committed mixed policy with ε = 1/4 chooses retry because 1/4 − 1/8 > 0. The material-only policy keeps because −1/8 < 0. The positive-but-uncommitted appraisal control also keeps. This operational consequence is why the mixed objective is not inert metadata.

That intervention separates the concrete policies. It does not provide a finite universal test for an unknown positive ε. For any finite series of strictly positive retry costs, an ε smaller than every cost yields the same refusal pattern as ε = 0. With geometric costs c_n = 2^(−n), every positive ε is eventually detected by a strict profitable retry, provided the channels remain feasible, effective, correctly understood, and all other motives/costs stay fixed. No finite all-negative prefix establishes ε = 0. A genuine positive cost floor c_min leaves every 0 < ε < c_min undetected by all such retry probes.

These are elementary inequalities used to sharpen the represented experiment. There is no claim that arbitrarily cheap human channels, unlimited repetitions, or a psychometric measurement scale are actually available. The model supplies no prior probabilities over ε. Graded loss of alternatives and a remaining identification interval are distinct from both certainty and complete ignorance.

## 5. Minimal two-probe recovery in a narrower class

A different intervention family supplies routes X and Y, each delivering the same gift at cost 1. K remains available. Material value exceeds that cost, so refusal is dominated. There are no unequal private route payoffs. In probe 1, X carries the answer effectively and Y cannot. In probe 2, this channel assignment is swapped while material value, cost, and source objectives remain unchanged.

Assume the source understands each assignment and uses a stable context-independent priority to resolve truly equal scores.

- Positive adopted communication-dependent value v gives responses (X,Y).
- Zero such value gives either (X,X) or (Y,Y), according to the stable priority.

The two observations therefore identify the binary target in this class, without identifying whether decoding is the terminal goal or a means. The intervention set is minimal for **uniform** recovery from one selected action per probe with initially unknown tie priority. A single X in probe 1 can be the positive planner or an indifferent material planner preferring X. A single Y in probe 2 has the corresponding collision. Some individual one-probe outcomes are informative; no one probe identifies all members.

This is not a universal minimum across every imaginable experiment or every human planning theory. If an observer could obtain the full maximizer set rather than a selected action, a single matched menu would distinguish a strict singleton from a tie. Repeated stochastic choices would require a separate response/noise model; a finite switch is not conclusive under arbitrary random tie-breaking.

### Why its assumptions matter

Two executed countercontrols preserve the public crossover observations while keeping indication in the target recipe:

- **Hidden belief:** the source believes both routes still communicate, perhaps through an unobserved bypass. Channel swaps do not change its expected advantage, so it keeps choosing X like the material policy. A real environmental block does not establish that the source understood it.
- **Residual route value:** a stable private value of 1 for X dominates the indication value 1/4. Even with accurate channel beliefs, both the mixed and material policy choose X twice. Physically equal gifts and nominally equal prices do not establish equality of effort, discomfort, symbolic value, or other relevant payoffs.

The crossover is thus a genuine sufficient discriminator under disclosed exclusion, belief, and stability premises; those premises are not discovered by running the code. No empirical matched-route experiment was performed.

## 6. Hostility, failure, and ties

The mixed source retains an indication plan when it knows the audience will decode but reject p. Its terminal communication objective is decoding, not persuasion. Actual acceptance is zero. In the unknown-block control, the source expects decoding, selects an indication path, and actual decoding fails. These distinguish the attempted act from successful uptake, as well as successful uptake from belief.

A separate exact tie fixture gives an adopted indication score of 1, a mantle cost of 1, and no material benefit. K and M are both optimal. Their chosen recipes yield intention values false and true respectively. Observed M is possibility-compatible; it does not license deleting the other optimal action from the model before the observation or pretending that intention is robustly certified across all optimizers.

The source's publicly undertaken responsibility may require contextual assessment even when a private objective is absent or unknown. This experiment deliberately leaves that semantic/epistemic question open. Neither two different recipes nor equal public observations settle it by definition.

## 8. Remaining live frontier

A real application would need an identified communicative setting and evidence about feasible alternative acts, material/social benefit invariance, costs, source channel beliefs, and which objectives the source adopted. The inherited source argument allows ordinary contextual and character knowledge to supply such warrant; it does not demand this exact laboratory protocol. A merely constructed material rival is not automatically a strong real defeater.

The new result closes a narrower modeling mistake: terminal motive differences cannot stand in for intended-indication differences; a commitment/means model supplies a real distinction, and common environmental probes still fail to recover it in an explicit nontrivial family. A carefully matched crossover or a sufficiently cheap retry can recover more, subject to separately warranted premises. Actual source-purpose attribution, publicly undertaken responsibility, broader veracity, and the special divine-attestation application remain unresolved by these tests.
