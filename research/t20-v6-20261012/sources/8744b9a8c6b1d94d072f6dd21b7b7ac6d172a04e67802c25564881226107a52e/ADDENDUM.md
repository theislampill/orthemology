# Additional continuous reading: Tibyān 1:281–287

11 October 2026 UTC. Supplement to the delivered report; the report and its archive remain unchanged.

**Page 287 strengthens the textual basis for the positive cumulative rationale already retained.** Ibn al-Qayyim connects divine greatness and just government with the incongruity of sustained support for an alleged fabricator of divine commission who changes religious obligations. His specified profile includes assistance, answered prayers, opponents' fate, and elevated standing. These are substantive features, not merely a repeated “confirming” label. This is further same-author corroboration, without new historical authentication or independent-witness credit.

Pages 281–282 finish the eighth reason and supply the ninth and tenth reasons for his preferred reading of 42:24. Pages 283–286 then distinguish certainty grounded in warranted truthful testimony, direct sight, and experiential contact. His Abraham discussion explicitly treats requested seeing as an addition to prior knowledge, not evidence that knowledge was absent. That supports the already-owned positive result in *Responsive demonstration after affirmed belief*, §§3–4; this author's account does not erase the interpretive qualifications preserved there.

Scope: directly read the edited electronic continuation, lines 2222–2263, through 1:287, and checked the next chapter's opening at line 2266/1:288. Authorial exposition and modern apparatus remain distinct. [Edited primary text](https://islamhouse.com/read/ar/التبيان-في-أيمان-القرآن-265620).

The delivered report's positive contextual and cumulative appraisals stand. This addition introduces no single-event objection, rival defeater, or new residual.
