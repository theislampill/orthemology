## Mathematical qualification

1. **Fixed L, R, q:** the displayed lift condition is exactly Cq ⊆ C; C ⊆ Cq always follows from choosing the same middle identity. The witness k can differ from the two submitted witnesses. Thus fixed endpoint exactness is strictly weaker than witness compatibility, and can hold without neighbourhood constancy. Under constant q, independent existential choices produce dom(L) × ran(R), including empty-relation cases.
2. **Uniform over all L:** with nonempty X, unequal neighbourhoods within a q-fibre admit a singleton L counterexample. Equal neighbourhoods let the original left witness serve as the lift. Nonempty X matters: if X were empty, all endpoint compositions would be empty regardless of profiles. The report states the needed assumption.
3. **Declared continuation family:** equality of the entire profile is necessary and sufficient, one continuation relation at a time. This is not stability under arbitrary new continuations. The author explicitly excludes that extension.
4. **Minimal finite sidecar:** a refinement of a pre-existing q must distinguish every different profile inside each q-fibre, but labels can be reused across different fibres. Consequently the alphabet minimum is max_z d_z, not total profile count over all fibres or total intermediary count. Enumeration of each finite fibre attains the bound, and ceiling(log2 D) is the fixed-length bit count for nonempty D. The storage caveats are material: decoder, relation, retrieval, and witness-level identities are outside this count. The empty-M case is not assigned an undefined logarithmic memory claim.
5. **Particular submitted witnesses:** equality of summaries is sound precisely when equal-summary admissible cross-pairs have equal middle identities. For unrestricted pairs this is injectivity; for fixed active left/right domains it is weaker. This does not demand equality of derivation records. Additional guards, scopes, and temporal respects are separately required, as the report states.
6. **Unbounded ordinary and Boolean rank:** the diagonal family has ordinary rank n because its n coordinate columns are independent. Every Boolean rectangle contained in I_n contains at most one diagonal entry, so at least n factors are needed; n singleton rectangles suffice. Assigning all intermediaries one provider does not change these facts. Quotienting by that provider yields the all-ones relation, rank one, and n(n−1) spurious routes. These are arbitrary-n written proofs corroborated by finite controls, not an extrapolation from n≤20 alone. They measure the declared derivative relation and cannot count original bearers or establish their metaphysical multiplicity.
