The strongest positive control is printed418–419: the wisdom for which an act is performed is attained after it; against the objection that an unending sequence never obtains its end, the author says «بل لا تزال الحكمة المطلوبة تحصل دائماً». Successive beloved attainments are called «الكمال الذي يستحق أن يوصف به»; printed419 continues that the beloved intention obtains through his act while he is «غني [عن] كل ما سواه». The bracketed preposition is supplied by the edition and noted as missing from its manuscript.

Printed419 also distinguishes creative volition from commands to servants: «ما يريد هو أن يخلقه لما يحصل به من الحكمة التي يحبها، فهذا يفعله سبحانه ولا بد من وجوده». This is particular efficacy, not merely an abstract genus of activity. It does not explicitly identify every isolated intrinsic act with an entire sufficient causal complex.

Printed414–415 seventh reply positively prefers wise activity attaining a wisdom at its loved time to purposeless activity. Printed415 ninth reply’s «لو سلم لهم أنه مستكمل بأمر حادث» is hypothetical. Printed417 contains an explicit opponent-directed الزام. Printed416,422,424 introduce al-Razi’s second, third and fourth arguments. The fourth argument’s temporal dilemma at424 is an objection, not the author’s answer in this retained range.

Printed420 requires a created thing’s concomitants and the absence of its contraries. Printed422–423 allows creating means to realize additional wisdom. Printed424 explicitly affirms purposes for all divine acts after rejecting a weaker concession that would already refute the opponent.
