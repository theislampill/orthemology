# Claims, premises and ownership

3 October 2026. Revised candidate for independent review. Candidate-v1 is preserved; this version addresses the independent review's R1–R3 and source-accounting correction. No claim below establishes an actual necessary bearer or closes the programme. Locations refer to the two accompanying papers.

## JC10-1: relevance-preserving composition is required

**Claim.** Indiscriminate lifting of a branch's existential prerequisite to an entire explanatory package, followed by unrestricted redistribution of the package's explanatory contribution, generates a spurious self-dependence even in a finite, independently supplied history.

**Support.** Main paper section3. The explanatory roles in the necessary-bearer/contingent-exercise control are explicitly allocated. The antecedent used for producing the second recipient is not the antecedent used for producing the first.

**Status.** Sound inference criticism under the control's declared productive grants. It is not an actual-world proof that the proposed necessary bearer exists, nor a metaphysical-possibility certificate for a rootless history.

**Prior ownership.** E21 is the prior owner for the marked setup. The programme-specific adaptation is identified in main paper section3. Source-associated setup is charged conservatively.

## JC10-2: local plural arrows do not discriminate independent supply

**Claim.** A proposed forbidden-circularity rule whose triggering conditions mention only the active local arrows also rejects a supported infinite subordinate ancestry. Such a rule cannot by itself express the narrower received-only availability exclusion sought here.

**Support.** Main paper section4. The independent support changes whole availability while preserving the local arrows.

**Status.** A conditional discriminating control. An advocate may instead defend causal finitism or reject the supported control; the paper does not prove that stronger theory false. No first-event premise is supplied.

**Prior ownership.** K7; O25. The necessary-source-supported control is the present application, not a claim of generic novelty. Separate author identities remain in the source record.

## JC10-3: fixed-antecedent preservation is positively warranted

**Claim.** Further downstream products do not remove a fixed, relevant antecedent F-resource from the supply history of the route that actually uses it.

**Support.** Main paper section5; capacity paper section4. The reason concerns productive direction, a retained concrete input and the exact achievement. It allows genuine received efficacy and does not interpret existence as a conserved substance.

**Status.** Rationally warranted bounded principle. Finite supply-depth application is the clearest case. An infinite route can qualify if the fixed source's actual relevance is independently retained; this paper does not prove that every infinite history has such a source.

**Limit.** Upstream expansion can supply an earlier boundary input and change the relevant basis. Its indefinite completion is not automatically the downstream operation. Applying the principle after replacing the fixed input by a changing succession of inputs is not warranted by the bounded judgement.

## JC10-4: whole-history E_A adequacy remains open

**Claim.** The concrete controls defeat specific proposed derivations of collective exclusion. They neither establish internal E_A adequacy nor erase the original-possession proposal's prima-facie rational force.

**Support.** Main paper sections5–6,8; accepted Ninth late warrant review and Tenth kind/transfer studies.

**Status.** Differentiated epistemic judgement. It is not equal weighting of competing metaphysics, denial of basic warrant, or a requirement that every rival be proved impossible. The disputed content is the relevance and scope of the whole actual history.

## JC10-5: typed conditional necessary-resource extraction

**Minimal claim.** Suppose a qualified supply target S is possible; COV-R covers every actual contingent concrete member of the relevant realization resource base and any actual positive S supplier; and the selected possible realization has the stipulated complete actual resource basis, positive anchor specifically supplying s's existential availability, and full-history classification transport. Then the positively selected actual concrete resource is outside F, hence noncontingent and necessary by COV-R. If S is actual, its positively specified actual outside-F supplier directly yields a necessary bearer under COV-R's actual-supplier clause; COV-B also suffices for that direct branch.

**Power-bearer corollary.** Add that the positively selected actual resource bears a supplying power. The same actual witness is a necessary capacity bearer. This stronger version can instead use COV-B directly, without asserting coverage of unrelated passive background. The positive type restriction is additional in the merely possible branch.

**Support.** Capacity paper sections1–3. In the selected possible realization, the actual source witness's retained F-instantiation would contradict the same ancestry-qualified achievement. The witness must be relevant to supplier availability, not merely to recipient or trigger production. Relevance on that route suffices; indispensability across every possible route is not assumed.

**Status.** Two valid conditional arguments under their explicit domain-matched premises. The source-trace restriction prevents importing the disputed global exclusion through an arbitrary infinite base. The actual-S branch explicitly includes a positive outside-F bearer; the earlier negative-boundary wording was insufficient and was repaired before candidate-v1. Candidate-v2 additionally repairs the resource/bearer mismatch and explicitly selected positive anchor identified in review.

**Conclusion limit.** Minimally, some actual necessary concrete realization resource. Only the additional supplying-power type condition yields a necessary capacity bearer. The witness need not be the possible supplier s, an actually exercised contributor to the actual field, one unique source, intrinsically self-explained, or perfect.

**Prior ownership.** Ninth F1 owns the qualified-achievement contradiction and productive-coverage relocation technique. Tenth's accepted kind paper owns the smaller existential target; the transfer paper owns the prior actual-powers/achievement-preservation boundary. The current contribution is the positive ancestry-qualified one-recipient application, typed resource/power-bearer conclusions, selected-anchor restriction and classification/trigger/background controls. It is not a new general modal theorem or a claim of a new target-instance equivalence.

## JC10-6: transport and resource controls are substantive

**Claim.** Actual identity and kind priority do not alone preserve Q-membership in another history. A causally relevant trigger does not automatically supply a producer's existential availability. A merely possible independent auxiliary source is not a free actual resource.

**Support.** Capacity paper section5; main paper section7. The whole relevant history, rather than the final classification of an endpoint, controls F-antecedent retention.

**Status.** Inference and content controls. No counter-history is certified metaphysically possible. No actual necessary bearer has been earned merely by identifying these obligations.

## Exact premise status for JC10-5

- **COV-B:** fixed Q covers every actual contingent concrete capacity bearer. Assumed for the stronger conditional with a supplying-power anchor; universal actual coverage is not established in this packet.
- **COV-R:** the same fixed Q also covers every actual contingent concrete resource in the relevant realization base and any actual positive supplier when S is actual. This broader scope is explicit in the minimal resource theorem. It is not automatically supplied by COV-B and is not established here.
- **POS-S:** one positive concrete outside-F supplier and one suitable recipient, with no relevant F-instantiation in the supplier's existential-support history, including a retained relevant earlier same-bearer stage, are jointly possible. Stronger than ordinary outside-kind production. Not established here.
- **RP1:** the relevant nonactual qualified achievement has actual concrete causal capacities capable of realizing it. A proposed general modal principle; its absolute coverage is not established here.
- **RP2:** relevant concrete background is actual in the capacity base or produced through the route. A legitimate adequacy condition on a sufficient capacity for this specified achievement; actual availability of a base satisfying it is not established.
- **RP3:** actual initiating resources retain achievement-specific explanatory relevance. A trigger or partial conjunct-production does not satisfy the condition merely by appearing in the history. RP5 further restricts the selected anchor to supplier availability specifically.
- **RP4:** the actual F-instantiation is retained at a relevant stage in the whole realizing history. Valid within a stipulated source-preserving realization; not a consequence of identity, ordinary actual kind priority or origin-insensitivity alone.
- **RP5:** either the positive supplier is an actual available concrete resource, or the selected possible realization has a positive supply trace to its existential availability anchored in an actual concrete entry witness. This is stronger than aggregate relevance. A supplying-power type condition on that witness gives the power-bearer corollary. Finite-depth trace preservation is warranted at its stipulated productive scope. General realization coverage including every timeless or infinite alternative is not established.
- **AP:** retained positive F antecedence on the selected trace to s's availability is incompatible with S's explicit no-F-ancestry qualification. This is a warranted qualification-preservation judgement once the same source trace and target are fixed. It needs no all-routes indispensability premise and is not a free-standing foundationalism principle for arbitrary actual histories.
