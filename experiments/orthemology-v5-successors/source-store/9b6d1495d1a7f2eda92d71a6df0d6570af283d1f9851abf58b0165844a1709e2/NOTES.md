# Separate bounded rereading of Shifa selected controls

Historical appraisal recorded during the Shifa study on 7 October 2026, before the later Miftah, Nubuwwat and Safadiyya studies. Its stated judgement and reading limits are preserved; subsequent final appraisals are separate.

Directly read e49–52 and e205–208 continuously. These are electronic page labels, not a newly authenticated print pagination.

E49–52 preserves real subordinate agency while rejecting its autonomy from divine creative power. E50 explicitly distinguishes three permissible forms of shared production from the remaining contradictory description in which each producer actually performs the whole independently. In particular, shared carrying with the capacity of either carrier to carry alone is allowed. This is not permission to infer actual separate sufficiency of each partial exercise from that counterfactual capacity. Some neighbouring clauses are visibly damaged; their missing wording has not been repaired or made decisive.

E51–52 identifies true elements on both disputed sides and requires retaining them together. Divine comprehensive power and genuine human power, willing and action are not treated as exclusive alternatives. Creating an act and the creature's doing it have different subjects and descriptions. These distinctions directly support the formal separation of productive provision, intrinsic act and assertion ownership, while their universal divine interpretation remains a source claim.

E205–208 is a positive argument for wisdom, causes and purposive agency, not a concession that agency is idle standing capacity. E207 makes the classification issue explicit: agreement that a source lacks deficiency does not settle whether the contested property is a deficiency. The response distinguishes externally supplied perfection from intrinsic wisdom and from realising a loved result at its fitting time. Its rejection of an outside provider invokes no-other-creator at that point; this is a coherent defence of the whole doctrine there, not an independent premise-free derivation of universal sourcehood.

E208 positively ranks actual creating above not creating and compares knowing, power and perception. It also refuses to demand an impossible simultaneous or eternal token occurrence as a perfection. The appropriate question is therefore the scope and fittingness of the actual exercise, not simply whether more outcomes can be imagined.

The passages strengthen the affirmative source account and sharpen its distinctions. They do not by themselves establish that every incompletely comprehensive original role is deficient. The philosophical stance recorded at 08:40 UTC on 7 October 2026 is not changed merely by this selected control. Whole-book assessment remains with the separately recorded continuous study; this is a separate bounded rereading, excluded from its first-pass count.
