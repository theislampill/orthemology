The finite predicate-functor/tensor result, however, treats one extensional model at a time. This report supplies that bounded adapter. Its main distinction is between:

1. a full globally faithful identification of all local indices, which requires trivial holonomy;
2. one compatible point section, which requires a common fixed point of holonomy, filtered separately by existence and other predicates;
3. a nonzero or normalized parallel vector/tensor, which can exist even when no compatible point section exists.

The third item is a substantive tensor readout hazard. An invariant average over permuted individuals is not an invariant individual. In the declared individual basis, adding the copyability equation Δz = z⊗z and normalization εz = 1 exactly distinguishes point sections from orbit sums or averages. This is standard basis/copy structure, not a discovery of individuals in a vector space.

A second hazard concerns the old receipt interface: keeping just the holonomy-fixed individuals can delete a supplier that witnesses a retained individual's receipt. A coherent surviving bearer does not, by itself, preserve quantification over bearers or `ReceiptRepresentation`. Full-domain faithful charts, or separate quantified-formula preservation on a restricted domain, are required.

The graph/holonomy mathematics and the copyable-basis fact are standard. ## 2. Declaration: identity indices are separate from existence

Let W be a finite nonempty set with a specified actual world a. Let Γ be a connected undirected graph on W. An oriented edge v→w is equipped with a bijection g_vw:D_v→D_w between finite outer-domain index sets, with g_wv = g_vw⁻¹. These are indices available for interpretation, not a claim that every indexed individual exists at that world. The common cardinal n may be zero.

At each world independently declare existence E_w⊆D_w, whole-reception C_w⊆D_w, any other local predicates, and any local relations. None is required to be invariant along g. In particular, neither g_vw(E_v)=E_w nor a corresponding equation for receipt or intrinsic resources is assumed. Imposing those equations would already insert modal persistence premises. The graph is an alignment graph, not the accessibility relation of a modal logic and not a causal graph.

For the point-section theorem choose arbitrary acceptance sets A_w⊆D_w. Existence is the case A_w=E_w; existing nonreceipt is A_w=E_w\C_w. A point section is a tuple s=(s_w) with s_w∈D_w and

    s_w = g_vw(s_v) for every oriented edge v→w.

It is accepted when s_w∈A_w for every w. This is presently a correspondence-consistent tuple. Calling it one numerically identical bearer across worlds requires the semantic interpretation stated in §5, not merely these equations.

Take a rooted spanning tree at a. Let t_w:D_a→D_w be the composite along the tree path from a to w, with t_a=id. For every oriented edge define its rooted cycle permutation

    H_vw = t_w⁻¹ ∘ g_vw ∘ t_v : D_a→D_a.

Tree-edge H is identity. It suffices to retain one orientation of each non-tree edge. Let K be the simultaneous fixed set of all these permutations. Equivalently, K is the fixed set of the group they generate, which is the rooted holonomy group. Let

    F = K ∩ ⋂_(w∈W) t_w⁻¹(A_w).

Existence and nonreceipt are filters in this expression; they were not built into the alignment equations.

## 3. Finite section theorem and exact tensor contraction

**Theorem 1.** Evaluation at a gives a bijection between accepted point sections and F. Its inverse sends b∈F to s_w=t_w(b). Consequently the Boolean scalar

    Z = OR_(s∈∏_w D_w) [AND_w 1_Aw(s_w) AND
                           AND_(v→w∈edges) δ(s_w,g_vw(s_v))]

equals

    OR_(b∈D_a) [AND_edges δ(b,H_vw(b)) AND AND_w 1_Aw(t_w(b))].

Using natural-number sum and product instead computes the number of accepted sections, which is |F|. The displayed δ factors are alignment tensors on two world-local axes. No independent existential contraction is allowed to discard a vertex axis before its required equality bindings have been used.

**Proof.** Along the unique tree path, the edge equations force s_w=t_w(s_a). Every remaining edge then says t_w(s_a)=g_vw(t_v(s_a)), equivalently H_vw(s_a)=s_a. The acceptance conditions give exactly the other intersections in F. Conversely any b in that intersection defines a tuple satisfying every edge and every filter. Its actual component is b, so the maps are inverse. Each summand in the network is 1 precisely for an accepted tuple. Boolean addition detects existence; natural-number addition counts those tuples. There is at most one tuple per actual index. This proves all claims, including the empty-domain case. □

The rooted-cycle test is finite: n candidate root values are checked against |W| filters and |E| constraints. With explicitly tabulated bijections, inverses, and tree transports, a direct implementation costs O(n(|W|+|E|)) operations. The uncontracted product-domain network is only an alternative specification, not the efficient algorithm. Finite checking is not a certificate that the maps mean numerical identity.

Changing the spanning tree does not change the section set or its actual values. Thus K and F, as subsets of the fixed actual fibre, are unchanged, although their generating cycle permutations and the convenient t_w representatives can change. For b in K the transported point is independent of the chosen tree path. Full path-independence on every local index is a stronger statement.

**Gauge covariance.** For arbitrary local relabellings π_w:D_w→D'_w, put

    g'_vw = π_w ∘ g_vw ∘ π_v⁻¹,
    A'_w = π_w(A_w),
    t'_w = π_w ∘ t_w ∘ π_a⁻¹.

Then H'_vw=π_a H_vw π_a⁻¹ and F'=π_a(F). The map s↦(π_w(s_w)) is a bijection of accepted sections. Both Z and its natural-number count are unchanged. For arbitrary predicates and relations, transport each world's own valuation by its appropriate π_w on every argument. This is passive coordinate change, not an equation identifying predicates in different worlds.

**Proof.** Adjacent π_v⁻¹π_v cancel in path composites. Substitution proves the H conjugacy and the filter-preimage equation. The section equation transforms to π_w(s_w)=g'_vw(π_v(s_v)); the inverse relabelling gives the inverse map. □

## 4. Full coherent identity, one point, and an invariant tensor differ

**Theorem 2.** The following are equivalent:

1. Every rooted cycle permutation H_vw is identity on all of D_a.
2. There is a set B and bijective charts u_w:B→D_w such that g_vw=u_w u_v⁻¹ for all edges.
3. There is a common set B and injective maps j_w:D_w→B satisfying j_w g_vw=j_v for every edge, with B equal to their common image.
4. Every local root index extends to a unique point section.

**Proof.** (1) gives (2) with B=D_a and u_w=t_w. (2) telescopes around loops and gives (1), and its inverses give (3). In (3), a loop gives j_a H=j_a; injectivity implies H=id. Connectedness makes all j_w images equal. Theorem 1 without filters identifies (1) and (4). □

This is a complete mathematical criterion for faithful global labelling of the specified local correspondence system. It does not authenticate that labelling's worldly referents. If interpreted as literal identity for every local item, nontrivial holonomy is inconsistent with faithfulness: a loop would identify distinct elements of one fibre. Such data may instead encode path-dependent counterpart transport or inconsistent proposed matching. A fixed point in a nontrivial holonomy action supplies one path-independent point only; it does not make the entire system an identity atlas.

### Parallel vectors can be false positives for bearer existence

For each D_w take either the real free vector space with basis e_x or the free Boolean semimodule on that basis. Let P_vw(e_x)=e_(g_vw(x)). A parallel vector family obeys z_w=P_vw z_v. This alone does not require any common fixed point of the underlying permutation action.

On a triangle, take D_w={0,1}, tree maps identity, and the remaining edge the swap. There is no point section. Yet z_w=e_0+e_1 is a nonzero parallel vector over either coefficient system; over the reals (e_0+e_1)/2 is also parallel and has augmentation 1. Every existence filter can be true. Neither nonzeroness, normalization, nor passing these filters turns the vector into a bearer. More generally, real invariant vectors of a permutation action are constant on its orbits, so nonzero invariant vectors exist whenever the finite fibre is nonempty, even if no orbit is a singleton.

Declare the basis-dependent copy map and augmentation by

    Δ_w(e_x)=e_x⊗e_x,          ε_w(e_x)=1.

**Theorem 3 (point readout).** Over R or B, a vector z satisfies

    Δ_w z = z⊗z,             ε_w z=1

if and only if z=e_x for exactly one x∈D_w. Consequently normalized copyable parallel vector families, additionally satisfying M_Aw z_w=z_w for the diagonal filter M_Aw, are in bijection with the accepted point sections of Theorem 1.

**Proof.** Write z=Σ_x c_x e_x. Equality of off-diagonal coefficients gives c_x c_y=0 for x≠y. The diagonal equations give c_x=c_x². Over R each c_x is 0 or 1, and normalization forces exactly one 1. Over B, the off-diagonal equations likewise leave at most one supported index and Boolean augmentation forces one. Conversely any single basis vector obeys all equations. Parallelism and the filters then become exactly the point equations and membership requirements. The zero vector is excluded by normalization, including when the fibre is empty. □

Permutation relabellings preserve these declared maps: Δ'_w P_w=(P_w⊗P_w)Δ_w and ε'_w P_w=ε_w. This is the copy/equality structure already used in the extensional tensor result, applied to cross-world readout. A general linear change is allowed only if Δ, ε, filters, and semantic extraction are transported too; entrywise one-hot meaning cannot be retained in an arbitrary new basis by assertion. No cloning, physical observable, or ontology follows from the algebraic copy map.

The coefficient restriction matters. Over Z/6Z the vector 3e_0+4e_1 is normalized and copyable: 3²=3, 4²=4, 3·4=0, and 3+4=1 modulo 6. It is not a single basis vector. Theorem 3 is not asserted for arbitrary rings or semirings. The copy map duplicates an index in a representation; it does not create an additional extramental individual. A point readout is appropriate to this one-particular-bearer claim, not a required account of every distributed organizational or apoautotic continuation.

### Even individual loop tests may be insufficient

A stronger control uses six labels arranged in three pairs A, B, C, and two rooted loop generators. Generator h1 fixes pair A and swaps within pairs B and C. Generator h2 fixes pair B and swaps within pairs A and C. They commute; h1h2 fixes pair C and swaps the other pairs. The generated group has four elements. Every group element individually has a fixed label, yet no label is fixed by the whole group. Thus even “each loop has some fixed witness” does not give one globally compatible bearer. The condition is a common fixed point. A connected graph consisting of two triangles sharing the actual vertex realizes these generators with identity tree edges. This also shows why nonzero traces or separately nonempty loop-fixed subspaces are inadequate readouts.

    existsAt(w,b)       := E_w(u_w(b))

    Received(dep,w,b)  iff  OR_(y∈D_w) Dep_w(y,u_w(b)).

### Exact minimal witness-lifting condition for Received

Let j_w:B_ret→D_w be any injective retained-domain maps, with induced relation

    Dep_ret(w,c,b) := Dep_w(j_w(c),j_w(b)).

At a specified pair (w,b), define the receipt back-condition

    [∃y∈D_w, Dep_w(y,j_w(b))]
       ⇒ [∃c∈B_ret, Dep_w(j_w(c),j_w(b))].                 (Back)

**Theorem 4.** Full local Received and retained-domain Received agree at (w,b) if and only if (Back) holds there. Consequently their negations agree as well. For all retained targets/worlds, use the corresponding universal family of (Back) conditions.

**Proof.** A retained witness maps to a local witness directly. The reverse implication is exactly (Back). This gives the equivalence; Boolean negation gives its complement. Conversely the equivalence's local-to-retained direction is (Back). □

This is existential witness lifting, not a demand that every supplier be retained. Full surjectivity is sufficient but stronger. For example if both retained c and omitted d supply b locally, retaining c suffices for Received's truth even though the edge from d is lost. It would not preserve a formula specifying d, counting suppliers, or distinguishing all suppliers. To infer full-world nonreceipt from apparent retained nonreceipt for a selected b, (Back) is required at precisely the worlds/targets under consideration. Without it, absent retained edges supply no closed-world absence certificate.

To preserve `ReceiptRepresentation` with C copied faithfully, this condition is needed at every pair where that representation equivalence is being claimed. The saved definition quantifies all w and g, without an existence guard. A narrower assertion for an actually existing witness may restrict its check to the worlds used by that assertion, but must be labelled narrower. The constitutive whole-reception premise remains separately required; (Back) adds no efficient-causal or metaphysical warrant.

For actual resource Realisation the corresponding condition is, for each resource r in the needed scope,

    [∃y∈D_a, E_a(y) AND I_a(y,r)]
       ⇒ [∃b∈B_ret, E_a(j_a(b)) AND I_a(j_a(b),r)].

Only actual resources need this for the saved Realisation premise. It again needs some appropriate retained bearer, not every local bearer. These selective back-conditions preserve the specified existential statements. The full-language assertion above retains its bijective-chart hypothesis.

## 6. Source-relative loss control and semantics not claimed
