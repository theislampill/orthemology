# Arabic reading methodology

This supplement records how the Arabic reading informed premise formulation and how models and objections prompted returns to the sources. Exact received-body boundaries, overlaps, editions, lacunae and scan limits remain in the archive-root `t20/reading/READING_INDEX.json`. The JSON companion uses its stable work, return-point and evidence IDs. No original guide or reading record is revised.

The audit adds no primary reading or proof replay. Existing contextual checks cover the twelve currently decision-relevant topics. The remaining questions concern warrant, interpretation, actual attribution or applicability; rereading solely to fill a stage label would not resolve them. This judgement is bounded by the retained corpus and present questions.

## Reading stages

### AR-S1 Continuous first pass

Traverse the selected received body in order, with actual textual or visual receipt boundaries. A received fragment or selected volume remains a fragment or selection. Original first-pass receipts only. Text extraction and scan reading retain their separate scope.

### AR-S1R Local return during the wider first pass

Revisit a completed section or volume while the larger selected body is still being traversed. A real bounded reread where receipted, but it does not satisfy the post-whole-structure condition of AR-S2.

### AR-S2 Reflective primary return after whole selected structure

The record establishes that the selected body was understood as a whole before this direct primary return. Knowledge of the whole historical work is not implied. Only the receipted return; a later synthesis of notes earns no additional primary-reading credit. If temporal ordering is uncertain, do not promote the return to this class.

### AR-S3 Question or model triggered primary return

An existing receipt links direct primary inspection to a newly salient question, premise, model, countermodel or objection. This category can overlap AR-S1R or AR-S2. The trigger and date must be evidenced. Mere relevance or a later juxtaposition does not establish a causal research history.

### AR-S4 Methodological synthesis and audit

Inspect existing receipts, interpretations, dependency maps and results to describe their relationship. Zero new primary-reading credit and zero new proof-replay credit unless a separate new receipt records such work.

## Twelve premise checks

### AR-T01 Necessary Being

**Context:** Asbahani main 47–107, especially 65–68, 96–104; Safadiyya S002L0043–0046 and the intrinsic-attribute discussion. The first context was read continuously by direct images, not inferred from a keyword hit. Index: ASBAHANI A-T20-04; SAFADIYYA SF03.

**Effect:** The concrete bearer and its intrinsic qualifications must be distinguished from a mental common description. Necessary existence alone does not identify the complete actual Maker.

**Formal link and limit:** OriginalBearerBridge A/B/C map: completion, realisation, support transport and contingency-as-need remain substantive premises; the same witness is tracked. The conditional proof does not independently warrant contingency-as-need or constitutive mode-invariance. Current provisional Necessary Being acceptance is inherited from the dated appraisal. Cases: AR-X01, AR-X02.

### AR-T02 Original sourcehood

**Context:** Asbahani 65–68 and 96–104; the full Akmaliyya answer, especially its concrete self and inseparable attributes discussion; Darʾ 8 P0396–P0422. Index: ASBAHANI A-T20-04; AKMALIYYA; DAR D03.

**Effect:** Intrinsic self-sufficiency is not efficient self-production. A qualified original resource, its bearer, a proximate act and complete common provision must retain separate types.

**Formal link and limit:** A original_bearer_of_completion; C essential_nonreceipt_of_actual_original; the field bridge needs further G/H premises. A minimally warranted original bearer does not by that fact supply every contribution or every disconnected domain. Cases: AR-X01, AR-X02, AR-X03.

### AR-T03 Causal nonduplication

**Context:** Darʾ 9 section 016:627–650 and 700–779; Shifaʾ e49–54 and e143–151; Miftah main 90–91. Index: DAR D04; SHIFA H02; MIFTAH MF04.

**Effect:** The same numerical contribution, same respect, entire original production and merely having ability to act alone are not interchangeable. Type-level similarity or differentiated cooperation does not duplicate the same complete production.

**Formal link and limit:** CNDModalScope requires role retention and complete matching attribution; AnchoredSourceBridge obtains coverage before applying FieldCND to uniqueness. CND does not supply modal role retention, connectedness, source-mode perfection or exhaustive scope. Finite consistency is not metaphysical possibility. Cases: AR-X02, AR-X03.

### AR-T04 Source mode perfection

**Context:** Darʾ maker/preserver and harmony contexts; Minhaj realised efficacy and voluntary particularisation; Sawaʿiq strict supremacy and responsive acts. Index: DAR D03, D04; MINHAJ M02, M03; SAWAIQ S01, S03.

**Effect:** The sources positively argue from effective capacity and appropriate perfection; they do not merely overlook harmony. The disputed transfer from product interdependence to producer receipt or defect is exposed rather than treated as automatic.

**Formal link and limit:** F separates qualification, classification and conformance; G separates use soundness and role applicability; H adds anchor and genuine connectedness. Deletion controls diagnose these dependencies. Universal SourceMode remains insufficiently warranted in the 14:34 assessment. Actual common original provision is provisionally accepted on separate residual integration/economy grounds, with lower confidence than Necessary Being. Cases: AR-X03, AR-X04.

### AR-T05 Created agency

**Context:** Jamiʿ printed 179–182, E183–186; Shifaʾ genuine secondary efficacy; Miftah 501–502; Safadiyya intrinsic act, produced product and source account. Index: JAMI6 J02, J03; SHIFA H01, H02; MIFTAH MF02; SAFADIYYA SF04, SF05.

**Effect:** Possessing a power differs from jointly attaining an end. Original support need not erase a created agent’s own act, nor adopt that agent’s false assertion.

**Formal link and limit:** JointFinite mixed_occurrence_real, J12_support_not_ownership and J13_genuine_agent_identity; field contribution types preserve agency. The finite interpretation establishes compatibility and exposes equivocation. It does not identify the actual productive account of a particular event. Cases: AR-X04, AR-X11.

### AR-T06 Wisdom

**Context:** Asbahani main 396–468; Shifaʾ e205–209 and e219–221; Miftah main 1024–1026 in its wider moral-value and suffering discussion. Index: ASBAHANI A-T20-02; SHIFA H03, H04; MIFTAH MF03.

**Effect:** A fitting timed response or completion of a product need not confer the maker’s intrinsic perfection from outside. Intrinsic conditions and source-created conditions are not independent donations.

**Formal link and limit:** The F conformance/classification split and JointFinite actual fitting nonspeech exercise retain this distinction. The content of an all-context fittingness norm, including whether a proposed overriding good can license a deficient assertion, remains a substantive warrant question. Cases: AR-X04, AR-X07.

### AR-T07 Veracity

**Context:** Asbahani 411–419, 422–423, 430–431 and 444–450 distinguishes reported Muʿtazilite reasoning, al-Razi’s criticism and Ibn Taymiyya’s reply. Sawaʿiq adds broader meaning and evidence-ranking controls. Index: ASBAHANI A-T20-02; SAWAIQ S04.

**Effect:** A false source-owned deliberate presentation, deficient exercise, all-context unfitness and actual practical conformance are different predicates. The rescue or overriding-good objection targets the no-fitting-shortcoming premise.

**Formal link and limit:** VeracityBoundary controlled_token_true and veracity_of_coverage; deletion_N and truthful_not_conforming keep the proof and converse limits explicit. Arabic discussion does not independently prove the entire modern K/D/N/P signature. Source knowledge/ownership coverage and N require warrant, not a kernel substitution. Cases: AR-X07, AR-X11.

### AR-T08 Attestation

**Context:** Nubuwwat S002L103–114, printed 768–772 and 777–781, especially 771 and 779, with custody and strong alternatives preserved. Index: NUBUWWAT NB02, NB03, NB05; SAFADIYYA SF01.

**Effect:** An extraordinary effect, intentional sign use, immediate mission certification and the full content of later speech need separate evidential links. A strong rival explanation can justify suspension.

**Formal link and limit:** Grounded attestation ownership and unanchored-cycle controls; the sign-converse/scoped-delegation comparison is contextual, not a new completed token-authentication theorem. No historical episode, particular revelation, private token or full message content is authenticated by these model or reading records. Cases: AR-X05, AR-X08, AR-X11.

### AR-T09 Authority

**Context:** Miftah 460–462 distinguishes delegated acts and unrestricted titles; Asbahani 725–728 contextualises authenticated report and learning. Index: MIFTAH MF02; ASBAHANI A-T20-05.

**Effect:** A truthful report of an actual duty can warrant it without a second proof of that duty. Constitutive jurisdiction, prospective power and present applicability remain separate.

**Formal link and limit:** T20Grounded.Controls testimony_needs_no_second_jurisdiction, prospective_power_is_lost and past_duty_does_not_supply_current_applicability. Actual attribution, the content and duration of a commission, and present applicability are external premises. Cases: AR-X05, AR-X08.

### AR-T10 Testimony

**Context:** Asbahani 608–615 and 676–682, including the boundary of the quoted al-Ghazali passage; Nubuwwat multiple routes and responsible assertion; Darʾ later proof versus first recognition. Index: ASBAHANI A-T20-05; NUBUWWAT NB04, NB-O6; DAR D01.

**Effect:** An accurate later account may preserve or strengthen knowledge without being its original cause. Multiple carriers or routes need a dependency analysis before being counted as independent.

**Formal link and limit:** Grounded support/retraction and quotient controls; inherited ordinary undefeated-report entitlement remains separate from strong source-owned certification. The model does not estimate historical transmission reliability or force every ordinary report to have prior speaker certification. Cases: AR-X08, AR-X09, AR-X10.

### AR-T11 Copying and transmission

**Context:** Asbahani 389–391 separates speech attribution, carrier voice and occurrence; Safadiyya’s speech/recitation discussion remains a broader return point. Index: ASBAHANI A-T20-05; SAFADIYYA SF-O1.

**Effect:** Faithful content preservation does not reproduce the acoustic event, create an independent origin or identify a source from content alone. Repetition of one realised observation does not accumulate independent information.

**Formal link and limit:** The GroundedQuotient and AliasCuts modules preserve the appropriate root support; MATH-122-APPLICATION gives a separate finite copied-observation control. A real origin map and independent channel-noise evidence must be supplied. The trace model intentionally leaves source labels unobserved; it is not a theorem about all possible evidence for authorship. Cases: AR-X06, AR-X09.

### AR-T12 Identity and continuation

**Context:** Asbahani concrete bearer and speech/carrier controls; al-Daʾ 24–27 on conditions, and 474–476 on direct act and generated consequences. Index: ASBAHANI A-T20-04, A-T20-05; ALDA.

**Effect:** Track which bearer, target, resource, claim meaning and conditions the particular continuation must preserve. A new carrier or qualified role successor can preserve a claim without being the same occurrence. Preserving bytes or an effect label alone does not preserve scope or provenance.

**Formal link and limit:** ABC same witness and JointFinite J01 canonical identity; derivable_after_retraction_iff; trace known_word_mask_ambiguity. No general theory of personal identity, historical continuity or arbitrary-context theorem transfer is established. Actual semantic transport and source identity remain obligations. Cases: AR-X01, AR-X06, AR-X10.

## Repeated witnesses and current assessment

- A reread can correct an interpretation or expose a premise without adding an independent witness.

- Distinct readers of the same received text do not multiply manuscript testimony; agreement is not a warrant vote.

- A translation, electronic transcription, scan and excerpt can represent overlapping witnesses; their custody identities and dependencies remain in the reading index.

- A repeated observation is not an independent sample. A genuinely independent acquisition or channel requires evidence of that independence.

- First-pass continuous coverage, bounded visual controls, editorial apparatus and historical reconstruction remain different achievements.

- Study seals establish the recorded coverage and file identities, not historical truth, metaphysical possibility or philosophical warrant.
