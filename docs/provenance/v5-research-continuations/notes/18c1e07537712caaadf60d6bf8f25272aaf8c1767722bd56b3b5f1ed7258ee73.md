## Mathematical and interface review

1. **The input criterion is substantive.** RecursiveWinning is an inductive witness predicate on nonempty finite model supports. It requires nonempty finite action lists licensed by the current support menu, recursive winning at every nonempty strict observation successor reachable by a listed action, and candidate-wise progress or live self-verification. It does not assume a successful policy or almost-sure restoration. The hypothesis remains a real restriction on models, targets and menus.

2. **The support update is exact strict positivity.** It keeps θ precisely when θ was live and P θ a y > 0. Impossible symbols may produce empty support and need no recursive certificate. A positive-mass exit under a currently live true model produces a nonempty strict subset retaining that model. The stopped exit witness identifies an actually executed action and symbol. Prior continuing observations have not changed support. The derived rank is support cardinality minus one and strictly decreases on positive-mass exits.

3. **The stage interface is genuinely linked.** recursiveWinning_iff_stage equates the inductive criterion to the maximal-support StageCertificate using the exact continuation menu recursiveAllowed. That menu admits an action only when all its nonempty strict successor supports win. Because those supports are strictly smaller, the finite bottom-up interpretation is well-founded. The theorem is not a necessity result about all possible policies.

4. **Extraction supplies the earlier obligations.** WinningPhase is the finite subtype of winning supports. Classical witness selection supplies initial candidates and lists, with a fallback live candidate for inputs outside the support. Every extracted plan is nonempty and menu-licensed, even on such unused candidate inputs. Support preservation, progress/self-verification and strict rank are derived rather than passed into the final endpoint as extra hypotheses. The policy uses the common likelihood model and past phase history; the true θ parameter is absent from its definition.

5. **The actual outcome record survives reset.** recordedUpdate pairs the updated controller state with the OLD phase, selected candidate and full truncated StopObs record. Thus the exiting observation is kept when controller memory resets. The transition is the pushforward of the normalized full stopped-prefix PMF; exit mass is not discarded or renormalized. Its infinite path law is the previously reviewed Ionescu–Tulcea construction.

6. **The executed action block is the stopped prefix.** An immediate exit exports one action; a continuing record exports that action followed by its recursively recorded prefix. No suffix of the abandoned old plan is executed after a support-changing observation. List order and multiplicity remain intact. The actual bad-action count is dominated by the planned charge, and the live mask cannot erase a selected plan's charge because the selected candidate is always live.

7. **Charge transfer treats the indexing correctly.** A new record appears at the next state. recorded_expected_charge_bound compares n+1 recorded-state charges with n prior-state planned charges plus the explicit initial-record charge. The initial placeholder is empty. This yields a finite expected total actual bad-action count and almost-sure eventual zero actual bad count, rather than merely a planned-action result.

8. **Non-stalling is proved on the required event.** The earlier eventual-nonempty result alone would not justify the fixed finite-window decoder. MarkovZeroEvent instead proves a single almost-sure event on which EVERY post-initial record is nonempty. It uses the actual one-step zero integral and a countable intersection over times. The decoder correctly skips x(0) and reads x(n+1). No nonempty-block premise remains for the caller of the final restoration theorem.

9. **The final action-time conclusion is literal.** recursive_physical_decoder_eventually_good says that, under recursiveRecordedLaw, eventually every decoded physical action belongs to good θ. It assumes raw nonnegative normalized finite-alphabet action laws, finite Θ, finite Y, a winning initial support containing θ, and a fallback action d. The fallback is unused on the almost-sure nonempty event. recursivePhysicalAction_measurable additionally assumes a countable measurable action type with measurable singletons. Measurability is total, including invalid paths.

10. **The scope remains a mathematical controller.** Finite support tests use exact real law equality/positivity and Classical.choice; witness extraction and arbitrary-real likelihood comparisons are not an execution refinement. The total decoder is a finite-record-prefix function. No universal Python-to-Lean refinement, formal necessity theorem for the recursive criterion, or equivalence to every externally supplied policy law is inferred. The source law assumes conditionally fresh raw observations through its stopped-block/product-kernel model; it does not establish an empirical sensor law.
