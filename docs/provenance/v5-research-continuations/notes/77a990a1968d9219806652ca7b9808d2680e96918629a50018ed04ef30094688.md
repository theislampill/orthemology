# Original sourcehood and the independent warrants for agency wisdom and disclosure

## Result and authority boundary

The source ascent becomes more informative when it ceases to be a single ladder whose later names merely repeat “adequate ground.” Actual productive sourcehood warrants efficacy in the produced respect. Actual cognition requires the additional original-giver argument already defended in the accepted sixth packet. Intentional agency requires a further integration: the source exercises efficacy through apprehended content and ends. Wisdom requires warranted good or fitting ends and appropriate responsiveness to their reasons. Disclosure requires a further intentional relation to a recipient. These are connected explanatory achievements, but neither the words “complete source” nor the co-location of attributes supplies all of them.

This continuation makes three positive advances beyond the earlier Knowledge result. First, it constructs an integrated account of agency that distinguishes standing power, determinate willing, reasons for willing and effective production. Second, it reconstructs Ibn Taymiyya's actual Knowledge–Wisdom argument and states its strongest perfection-based defense, including precisely what a motivational externalist can deny. Third, it unpacks the retained conditional disclosure-capacity argument into explicit abilities concerning content, intended uptake and an effective channel, and separates capability from authentication of an actual revelatory address. This clarifies the earlier perfection argument; it does not replace an allegedly unqualified canonical inference.

The strongest impersonal and plural rivals are given positive natures, genuine powers, real knowing recipients and genuine target-relative correctness. Aristotle's intrinsic teleology, Spinoza's thinking but nonvoluntarist source, Plotinus's productive source beyond intellect, and a dispositional-essentialist base are not interchangeable. None is refuted merely by failing to look like a human planner. The best surviving discriminator is whether an independently supported original source explains norm-responsive production through its own apprehension and fitting purposive exercise, rather than only producing recipients that do so. That discrimination remains substantive and defeasible where its perfection and integration warrants are contested.

## 1 What the accepted source conclusions provide

The accepted sixth Knowledge, upstream-source and connected-unity packets are conditional achievements with different inputs. Their sourcehood is productive, not merely conceptual entailment. Their identity results require the appropriate actual productive witness and complete accounting. The connected-field result needs local single-bearer coverage at every node and genuinely productive connectedness. It does not infer a single complete bearer from the bare fact that independent branches cooperate. Necessity adds the actual contingent-reception principle; uniform underivability adds essential reception. Intrinsic attributes are not external producers simply because they are distinct or inseparable.

For the following argument let g be the same actual source for a declared productive field, under those inherited warrants. Three meanings of completeness must be distinguished.

- **Existential coverage:** g supplies all the actual productive efficacy required by the target and its declared ancestry
- **Contrastive determination:** the complete actual source exercise determines why this outcome rather than a relevant alternative occurs
- **Cognitive comprehension:** g apprehends the relevant content of that exercise, its efficacy and its effects

The first is not the second. A source of an objectively chancy event could supply every power while not fixing the particular result through its prior state. The second is not the third. A determinate noncognitive nature could fix an outcome without apprehending it. The third does not establish that comprehension guides production: a source may be described as both knowing and productive without the account having explained how its cognition belongs to its productive exercise.

The accepted complete-self-Knowledge appendix validly infers effect-Knowledge from genuinely known actual complete determination, known efficacy and the appropriate closure of comprehension. That is our inference about the source, never a claim that the source learns by making an inference. The present argument retains that distinction. It also retains its scope: knowledge of a generic standing capacity does not determine a contingent particular exercise; knowledge of actual effects does not by itself establish knowledge of every possible truth.

## 2 Power begins with actual efficacy and has an independent scope problem

If g actually produces e in a specified respect, g is not wholly powerless in that respect. Here the positive warrant is the actual exercise itself. Denying all efficacy while retaining real production would withdraw the productive interpretation. This gives a minimal sense of power before intentional selection is established. It corrects the impression that Power must first be inferred as the implement of an already established Will.

That result does not yet give omnipotence, two-way ability, control over alternatives, effortless production or ability to disclose. An actual source of one cognitive subject might lack the power to produce every logically describable kind of subject. Actual coverage of all produced realities does not by itself cover every unactualized possible reality. A source able to produce only this outcome could still be productive and unreceived. If all standing natures necessarily yield their actual output, power and actuality are extensionally coextensive without thereby being intentional.

The Taymiyyan positive continuation is the perfection argument applied to efficacy: impotence in an otherwise eligible respect is a lack; a complete original giver of real power cannot be inferior in that pure productive respect; and the independent source's own nature, rather than an external supplier, must sustain its eligible perfection. MF 6:76–79 supplies that pattern. MF 6:85–87 explicitly limits possible perfection by coherent possibility and freedom from dependence-producing deficiency. The argument therefore does not make contradictions achievable tasks. Producing an independently unproduced product is not an additional impressive capacity.

Ṣafadiyya 2:109–110 states the stronger source doctrine directly: possibility rather than actuality marks the scope of Power; not doing something because it is not willed differs from being unable to do it; and producing contraries alternatively differs from producing their contradictory conjunction. These are positive doctrinal and philosophical claims to assess, not consequences of an already produced effect. The page markers in the retained IslamHouse transcription are trailing markers, so the passage is located before (2/109) and (2/110). [Ṣafadiyya, paginated host](https://islamhouse.com/read/ar/الصفدية-272837).

A genuine universal-power conclusion needs three further defenses: which possibilities are genuine tasks; why each is eligible for this source; and why the powers are jointly compatible with its intrinsic nature and other perfections. Pointwise eligibility is not automatically their unrestricted joint realizability. Moreover, being able to actualize either x or y does not mean being able to actualize the contradictory conjunction x-and-not-x in the same respect. These are modal obligations, not concessions to an independently existing realm of task-objects.

The rival with intrinsic dispositions has a positive answer to the first step. Its powers are real constituents of its nature. It does not need inert matter plus an external law-command. Bird's dispositional essentialism treats laws as following from essential relations among fundamental properties; “monistic” there means that fundamental properties are of one dispositional kind, not that there is one necessary concrete substance. Adding a necessary concrete bearer is our rival construction, not Bird's conclusion. Nor does necessity of a property's law entail that the property is instantiated. [Bird, “Monistic Dispositional Essentialism,” sections 1–2, author PDF pp. 1–2](https://www.alexanderbird.org/Research/Papers/Monistic_Dispositional_Essentialism.pdf).

Thus real actual efficacy establishes scoped power; unlimited power belongs to a further original-perfection argument. Neither conclusion is obtained by first calling the output “selected.”

## 3 Knowledge remains distinctive but does not absorb agency

The accepted original-giver account begins with genuinely received truth apprehension and argues that its complete original source cannot be entirely without that eligible intrinsic excellence. The report need not repeat that argument in full. Its force remains respect-specific: a power to produce knowers is not itself knowing. Its disputed warrant remains equally specific: original qualitative adequacy alone leaves room for an intrinsically noncognitive but cognition-producing nature; original-giver priority excludes that substitution only if independently defended.

Two new qualifications matter for the continuation. First, “intellectual” means genuine apprehension of content, truth and relevant relations. It does not here designate a separately subsisting hypostasis, serial reasoning or a newly established scriptural divine Name. The source's own vocabulary of Knowledge must not silently become every feature associated with human deliberation. Second, recognizing the same bearer of Knowledge and efficacy is weaker than explaining an act as guided by that Knowledge. Attribution and integration are different.

Consider a constructed source whose cognitive aspect perfectly mirrors everything that occurs while an independent intrinsic disposition fixes every output. Its cognition is real and belongs to that source; yet the proposed explanation of the output does not use its cognition. Merely announcing that both attributes are g's therefore does not establish intentional production. Conversely, an intentional source need not deliberate in time or move from ignorance to understanding. Its apprehension can enter a static or noninferential explanation of why it produces this intelligible effect for this reason.

This distinction makes Spinoza a particularly strong rival. Ethics II.1–3 affirms Thought as an attribute and an idea of the divine essence and its consequences. Ethics I.31–32 classifies actual intellect and will as modes and denies free willing as the source's creative way; I.33–34 connects effects and power to the necessity of the divine nature. It would be inaccurate to call this simply a noncognitive source. The challenge is whether cognition and productive order require purposive choice among genuine alternatives. [Spinoza, Ethics I.31–34 and II.1–3](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/3800/pg3800-images.html).

A Taymiyyan response cannot rest on a gratuitous ban on real attributes or every form of intrinsic determination. It must defend the existence of alternative eligible exercises and the excellence of exercising power through one's own apprehended ends. It must also explain the actual integration of those attributes without making the source dependent on a separate intelligent supplier.

## 4 From differentiated actuality to intentional agency

### 4.1 What differentiation alone establishes

Suppose g could produce either x or y, and x occurs. There is a contrastive question: what explains the realized alternative? Calling the occurrence “selection” can mean only that one alternative is actual. In that thin sense there may be no selector in the psychological sense. An intrinsically probabilistic power, spontaneous symmetry-breaking event, deterministic trajectory with a further intrinsic state, or plural interaction are offered positive accounts. They are not all equivalent, and some may fail the demanded contrastive explanatory scope. None becomes a will merely by producing one result.

Even a complete sufficient determinant of x need not apprehend x. A nonintentional differentiated nature may do the work. The inference “not necessary from an undifferentiated standing nature, therefore chosen by a mind” leaves unexamined both richer intrinsic natures and genuine chance. A defender of contrastive sufficient reason can reject a brute realized chance outcome as incomplete, but must then defend that stronger explanatory requirement. The bare sourcehood relation does not supply it.

### 4.2 The positive intentional account

The better positive proposal connects independently motivated resources:

1. The actual explanandum includes rational correction: a subject apprehends a target, recognizes why one response is mistaken, and changes its action because of that reason
2. That is more than an observer's utility ranking of outputs; the subject's grasp of content and reason is part of the actual exercise
3. The original source is eligible for apprehension and effective self-directed exercise without borrowed understanding, unfulfilled bodily need or external coercion
4. Respect-specific original-giver priority applies to this integrated excellence, not only to the separate presence of cognition and force
5. Its original possession supplies a positive way of accounting for derivative reason-responsive agency: the source can originate a recipient and conditions for the recipient's own distinct exercise through a known and intended determination

The conjunction is important. If the relevant excellence is acting intelligently for an apprehended reason, assigning original Knowledge to one independent root and original efficacy to another does not yet place that integrated excellence in an original agent. Equally, merely writing both predicates on g does not show that one guides the other. The source advocate's advance is a connected account of **content-guided original exercise**, not a list of attributes.

Why consider this explanation better than “a nonintentional nature produces rational agents”? It identifies an original occurrence of the distinctive achievement being explained; it connects differences in understood reasons to differences in fitting exercise; and it can account for why a recipient's grasp of reasons is relevant to the intended kind of effect. The same content appears at the origin and in the explanandum without requiring the two subjects to be identical. The account does not eliminate the primitive efficacy needed to produce a numerically distinct recipient. Its additional explanatory content is purposive integration.

The rival's strongest reply is that original intentional possession is unnecessary: intrinsic powers and objective relations can produce genuinely content-guided recipients without an original instance of that guidance. This reply preserves the explanandum. It directly rejects step 4 or claims a different positive realization basis; it does not deny that rational correction occurs. Our argument gives a reason to prefer the integrated source under the original-priority doctrine, but it is not a source-neutral derivation from production alone.

The comparative claim has a useful limit. Intrinsic norm–power coupling may already explain why changes in targets or reasons make appropriate differences to outputs. The intentional theory earns additional credit only if its independently warranted apprehension and practical orientation explain that coupling, rather than merely redescribe an equally primitive fit as “intended.” A candidate discriminator is cross-context flexibility: the same source understanding accounts for the appropriate production of distinct reason-sensitive capacities under changed ends and circumstances. Yet a developed impersonal dispositional essence could also support that counterfactual pattern. We have not demonstrated a pattern available only to intentional agency. The positive advantage is consequently conditional on the original-priority and practical-integration warrants, not an unsupported likelihood claim or a primitive-counting victory. Both accounts owe an intrinsic account of efficacy and of the normative relevance of its exercise.

There is also a second, teleological route. If arrangements are independently established as intentionally ordered to ends, then explaining those arrangements as someone's intelligent exercise is appropriate. But biological functionality, natural teleology and intelligent intention must not be conflated. Aristotle Physics II.1 and II.8 supplies intrinsic principles and for-the-sake-of structure without requiring observed deliberation. A claim of nonaccidental fittingness has not yet excluded that alternative. Historical anatomical examples in al-Nubuwwāt are illustrations of its argument, not fresh biological evidence or a refutation of evolutionary explanation. [Aristotle, Physics II.1 and II.8, especially 199a–b](https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/physics.2.ii.html).

## 5 Will and contingent exercise without a pure act premise

### 5.1 What Ibn Taymiyya actually argues

Ṣafadiyya 2:96–110, especially 2:103, 105–106 and 109–110, is more discriminating than the formula “Will selects.” It contrasts an alleged bare essence stripped of attributes and acts with an essence characterized by Power, Will and actual exercise. A complete determinant in the latter sense can entail its effect while remaining an intentional agent. It also rejects treating one wholly undifferentiated old will, equally related to alternatives and times, as a sufficient explanation of their actual differentiation. Its positive account admits renewed determinations; it does not require all particular acts to be beginningless. [Ṣafadiyya, hosted section 002, especially lines 630–635, 658–685 and 697–700](https://www.islamicbook.ws/amma/alsfdit-002.html).

Four relations should therefore be kept separate:

- The source has a standing capacity
- The source has a determinate intention concerning x
- A consideration is a reason for that intention
- The complete exercise, including adequate Power and settled Will, is sufficient for x

A motivating reason need not, just as a reason, be a complete necessitating cause. In the cited discussion, the claim about a complete determinant is stronger than the mere claim that choice is rationally motivated. Conversely, a complete determinate willing need not be identical to the source's invariant generic essence. The causal necessity of the effect given the complete exercise does not by itself make that exercise necessary in every possible world.

MF 6:85–86 also rejects the inference from a particular act's not occurring eternally to deficient agency. Some acts are such that their successive occurrence belongs to their appropriate manner. The absence of an act before its fitting occasion is not automatically a missing perfection. The argument is expressly unlike a blanket prohibition on potency, ordered acts or renewal. [MF 6:85–87, al-Risāla al-Akmaliyya](https://quranislam.github.io/fatwa/ibn-taymiyyah/fatwa81.html).

### 5.2 The remaining modal question is genuine

Suppose the source necessarily exists and necessarily has perfect practical understanding. Does it necessarily choose this precise act? Neither yes nor no follows merely from those attributes. If there is exactly one admissible best act in every fully specified circumstance and the source necessarily chooses it, that may constrain the resulting alternatives very strongly. If several fitting actions remain and none is uniquely required, a further account of determinate exercise is owed. Saying “free will” does not settle why this one is realized; saying “complete determinant” does not establish that the full determinant is itself necessary.

The present result is thus a scope clarification and constructive architecture, not a completed solution to every modal-collapse problem. The source account must specify the level at which alternatives remain: different freely exercised determinations of the same standing nature; different permissibly chosen ends; an eternal ordered series with each act dependent upon earlier intrinsic determinations; or another defensible account. It must not treat a chain of externally supplied perfection as the explanation of an unreceived source. Nor must it mistake the possibility of an infinite sequence of acts for a proof of an infinite regress of received sourcehood.

A decisive unresolved test is to hold the source's entire necessary nature fixed and ask which differing actual exercises the theory genuinely allows, and why. A two-world diagram with A in one world and B in another checks only that the stated premises do not syntactically forbid this; it does not establish metaphysical possibility. This limitation is especially important against Spinoza, who denies that the alternatives under discussion are genuinely available.

## 6 Wisdom is more than optimization and more than awareness of value

### 6.1 Three success notions

A device can maximize an assigned objective while that objective is vicious, mistaken or irrelevant. A subject can know the best available means to an end while misunderstanding the end's worth. A subject can also recognize that an end is good yet refuse to pursue it. Accordingly:

- Instrumental competence concerns means relative to a specified end
- Practical wisdom concerns knowing and appropriately pursuing genuinely fitting ends in the relevant circumstances
- Moral perfection adds an unrestricted and adequately defended relation to moral reasons, justice and the good of affected beings

These need not be three unrelated capacities, but none should be obtained simply by changing the name of the preceding one. A local instance of sound correction establishes a real epistemic achievement. It does not establish that every output of its complete source promotes every creature's welfare or that the source owes creatures whatever a human agent owes another human agent.

### 6.2 The primary Knowledge to Wisdom argument

In al-Nubuwwāt, Ibn Taymiyya argues that coherent arrangement of means for ends supports Knowledge, Will and Wisdom together. He then argues that a fully knowing agent apprehends what is fitting and superior; ignorance or appetite explains vicious human action; and a perfect divine agent is free from those defects. He also maintains that effective willing is differentiated by what the agent prefers or loves, and that the source's perfection excludes arbitrary or foolish preference. Finally he connects this wisdom to justice, truthfulness and the authentication of prophetic testimony. [Al-Nubuwwāt, hosted section 003, lines 20–24; parallel chapter 9](https://www.islamicbook.ws/amma/alnbwat-003.html).

This is the author's affirmative argument, not merely a quotation of an opponent. Its assertions of necessity or agreement report his view; they are not independent contemporary demonstrations that every rival accepts the premises. The anatomical and salinity discussion is not relied on as present empirical confirmation. The chapter combines rational arguments with scriptural identification, which must not be silently treated as the same epistemic route.

The most charitable philosophical reconstruction is stronger than “knowing facts produces virtue.” A source whose apprehension is complete cannot err about relevant ends or means. A source that is self-sufficient need not distort its practical judgment to satisfy a borrowed need or external master. If its agency is itself a pure original perfection, its exercise is appropriately responsive to the reasons it perfectly apprehends, instead of being alienated from its own understanding. An act motivated solely by a privation or by a mistaken elevation of a lower good would then contradict that specific practical perfection. This offers an intelligible positive explanation of wise self-directed action.

A stronger positive value argument can be given without identifying “good” with whatever is effectively chosen. A genuinely apprehended good is a reason in its favor, not merely an object catalogued by the knower. Perfect practical agency responds to that reason in its proper weight alongside other genuine reasons. If no defeating reason exists, knowingly acting against the all-things-considered fitting alternative is a failure of practical integrity. This gives the orientation premise content: it governs the relation between true evaluative judgment and exercise, rather than simply describing success at a desired outcome. The premise still goes beyond theoretical Knowledge. A motivational externalist can grant the whole evaluative truth and deny that apprehension alone makes it motivationally effective.

Concern for derivative beings has a related positive case. Their capacity for knowledge, flourishing and suffering makes their states genuinely better or worse in relevant respects. An informed powerful agent can recognize those goods and harms without needing them to repair its own existence. Deliberately creating and governing such subjects gives those realities a determinate place within the source's own field of action. The proposed pure excellence is intelligent beneficence: appropriately respecting and promoting real goods where no stronger fitting reason defeats them. Compassion understood as borrowed distress is not required. The original-giver account can then argue that genuinely good beneficence in a wholly received creature is not absent from its eligible complete original source. This is a new practical-perfection application, not a deduction from the bare existence of suffering or cognition. It still owes an account of competing goods, responsibility and the scope of creature-directed concern.

MF 6:129–130 reinforces the positive account. It distinguishes subjection to an external commander from the source's own wise self-commitment, and calls discriminating Will in accordance with Knowledge and Wisdom a perfection. The preceding 6:129 comparison specifically contrasts purposeless unrestricted willing with action for a loved Wisdom and mercy or beneficence toward others. Its 6:131 continuation answers the objection that recipients are unworthy of a message by appealing to powerful mercy and beneficial teaching or guidance. These passages directly support the positive practical and communicative-perfection route; they do not demonstrate actual mercy or a historical mission to a reader who has not accepted that route. The point is not that goodness is an independent efficient creator constraining God. Rather, what is worth doing and how to do it can belong to the source's own knowing and loving character. MF 8:146–153 additionally distinguishes actualized purpose at its fitting occasion from allegedly acquired existential perfection. [MF 6, displayed pp. 129–130](https://islamweb.net/ar/library/content/22/591/مسألة-تفصيل-الإجمال-فيما-يجب-لله-من-صفات-الكمال), [MF 8, displayed pp. 146–153](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/22/806/مسألة-حسن-إرادة-الله-لخلق-الخلق-وهل-يخلق-لعلة-أو-لغير-علة).

### 6.3 What remains independently warranted

The reconstruction contains three real premises: objective fittingness; a nondefective relation between apprehended reasons and the source's motivation; and an account of which ends or concerns are included in perfect agency. None is a mere consequence of a propositionally complete knowledge set. Hume's Treatise 2.3.3 supplies a direct challenge to the bare inference from cognition to motive: knowing means and consequences does not alone supply concern for the end. Ordinary human weakness does not refute the stronger perfection account, because it is precisely that kind of distortion the account excludes. A stronger counterproposal is an intrinsically coherent, informed source whose ultimate concern excludes or opposes creature welfare. The proponent must explain why such concern is a privation or failure of fitting agency, rather than simply give it the label “imperfect.” [Hume, Treatise 2.3.3.1–7](https://davidhume.org/texts/t/2/3/3).

The original-giver route can help here if derivative practical integrity is independently identified as an eligible pure excellence. A source which originally accounts for genuine recognition and pursuit of good would, under respect-specific priority, possess that excellence in an unrestricted original manner. But the application needs its own argument: the fact that a creature successfully pursues one epistemic good does not by itself identify every ultimate good, establish their joint compatibility, or show that the source has exactly human obligations. Cognitive priority must not carry the entire moral conclusion without this new work.

Nonneed must not do asymmetric work here. Excluding every satisfaction in cruelty merely because it is an actualized satisfaction would also threaten the source's allowed satisfaction in a fitting good act. The relevant distinction has to concern the end's worth and the agent's relation to reasons, not the bare fact that an act or satisfaction occurs. Likewise, unbiased understanding is not already a positive concern for the good of derivative beings. The account needs reasons why those beings' goods matter to perfect agency, how those reasons interact, and what, if any, stronger reasons can justify permitted harms.

The source's preference is also not interchangeable with objective goodness. Al-Nubuwwāt's movement from what is preferred to Wisdom is plausible only with a properly characterized perfect preference. A malicious optimizer is a countercontrol to mere efficient preference; it is not a counterexample to a premise explicitly requiring perfect responsiveness to actual good. The latter premise still needs warrant, and cannot be counted as both the definition and proof of Wisdom.

Finally, the source texts distinguish effective cosmic willing from approval or love. A means can be allowed or brought about for an end without being loved in itself. Jāmiʿ al-masāʾil 6:207–209 sets causal sufficiency beside the end loved or approved. This prevents the inference “it occurred, therefore it is morally endorsed.” It does not by itself establish that each observed harm is justified. A serious application must allow adverse evidence to count and cannot rescue every conceivable history by an unspecified hidden purpose. [Jāmiʿ al-masāʾil 6:207–209](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/416/688/فصل-قاعدة-في-إرادة-العدم-والإعدام-واستطاعته-وفعله).

## 7 A positive capability criterion for possible disclosure

### 7.1 Why intelligibility is insufficient

A world can be intelligible without being an address. A natural sign can carry information without intending that anyone recognize a speaker. A knowing agent can choose silence. An expressive agent can lie. A truthful statement can reach a recipient who lacks reason to identify its author. These are four different gaps, and general intelligibility closes none of them by itself.

The canonical philosophical companion §8 correctly stops short of actual revelation. It already assumes a knowing, willing, powerful source adequate to all communicative orthability, and explicitly invokes a perfection/causal-adequacy premise. Its “nothing in its nature excludes” wording must be read within those stronger assumptions, not isolated and criticized as if it inferred capacity solely from ignorance of an obstacle. The exact paragraph and source hash are retained in evidence/canonical_disclosure_scope.txt. The present contribution is a positive capability decomposition and application test. If adequate Power has independently been shown to cover every relevant communicative possibility, and such a possibility is positively established, the inference can be valid conditionally. But lack of a demonstrated contradiction is not alone a proof of that possibility; and “adequacy” should identify which powers and joint realizability are being assumed. The following criterion becomes a constructive demonstration in an application only when the required arrangement and powers are independently exhibited or justified; writing the criterion does not establish those powers.

Suppose there is some determinate content p, a possible recipient r, and a realizable sign/reception arrangement s. Assume:

1. g genuinely apprehends p and the pertinent signification and reception relations
2. g can intend r to apprehend p through recognition of g's address, rather than merely cause an unowned mental state
3. g has effective control over a suitable s and its relevant recipient-side enabling conditions
4. exercising that control is compatible with g's nature and does not require an external independently supplying perfection

If those capabilities and their joint exercisability are independently warranted, g has a positive route to intentional disclosure of p to r. This is a conditional realization argument. In particular, “can intend,” “realizable arrangement” and “effective control” are substantive ability premises, not conclusions supplied merely by naming a knowing source. The conclusion concerns a capability specified through an intelligible act and means. It does not require a mouth, a human sound-production process, temporal deliberation or an independently preexisting language. Existing conventions can be known and used; intelligible signs can also be instituted. But actual productive coverage of the present field does not automatically establish every alternative communicative arrangement, so step 3 must be warranted at the relevant modal scope.

The already-retained original-giver priority route supplies a separate perfection-based defense: genuine truthful communicative competence is an eligible positive achievement in a recipient; an independent complete source of that excellence cannot be wholly incapable of it in an appropriate original manner. MF 6:79–80 uses the contrast between powerless muteness and just, straight action and address, explicitly distinguishing laudable speech from speech as such. This makes ethical and intelligible address a richer excellence than the mere production of sound. That argument remains conditional on eligibility and the original-priority principle; it does not determine an actual utterance. [MF 6:79–80](https://quranislam.github.io/fatwa/ibn-taymiyyah/fatwa81.html).

### 7.2 Actual revelation requires a different evidence set

A particular revelation claim needs evidence that an event of address occurred, that this source is its author or authorizer, that its intended content is p, and that the recipient has an adequate route to receive and interpret it. The truthfulness premise concerns an authenticated intentional assertion. It cannot be applied indiscriminately to every event that a universal source causes, permits or sustains. A source can produce agents who utter falsehoods without itself asserting those falsehoods.

Al-Nubuwwāt's analysis makes the crucial positive contribution: a purported sign authenticates a messenger because of the relation between the sign, the source's purpose and the truth of the mission, not merely because the event is astonishing or locally unmatched. The account insists that a true indicator must discriminate truthful mission from false claim. Wise and nondeceptive agency is what makes purposive certification informative. The chapter's argument that a wisely sent messenger must have an adequate identifying indication is a conditional from actual mission and recipient obligation. It is not an argument that an unknown necessary source must already have sent a particular messenger. [Al-Nubuwwāt, hosted 003, lines 7–10 and 23–24](https://www.islamicbook.ws/amma/alnbwat-003.html); [chapter 9, opening distinctions](https://ar.wikisource.org/wiki/النبوات/9).

The norm-fixing joint can be stated without silently assuming benevolence. An assertion that p is truth-correct only if p. When offered as authoritative evidence, it purports to supply a truth-linked epistemic reason. Knowingly false certification defeats that epistemic function even if it efficiently serves a deceptive goal. This identifies a constitutive epistemic defect; it does not prove that a knowing agent cannot deliberately perform the defective act. Exclusion requires an independently established truthfulness or no-defect principle in the relevant authoritative communicative role, together with evidence that this occurrence belongs to that role. Generic Knowledge, spectacular Power and absence of an observed motive to lie do not supply it.

Purposeful withholding, an adversarially directed test, literal truth accompanied by a misleading inference, and false authoritative testimony must therefore be investigated separately. A source's not revealing every truth is not already a false assertion. Misdirection may raise distinct moral questions even where no false proposition is asserted. None of these categories licenses treating an apparently false authenticated promise as “really a test” without contextual evidence. Nor may “recipients entitled to rely” be defined factively so that their entitlement already entails the testimony's truth and is then used as an independent authentication premise. Fallible reasonable reliance can occur in a deceptive situation. The warranted authority, intended content, recipient situation and role-specific truthfulness each require their own support.

This also exposes a potential circle. If the source's truthfulness is known only from the very message whose divine origin is being established, the message cannot by itself authenticate that origin. One may use an independently defended perfection argument, cumulative historical and testimonial evidence, or an independently authenticated prior communication. Whatever route is proposed must be stated and tested. Definitionally calling an occurrence a “divine sign” merely inserts the conclusion.

No revelation claim is established here. The result is a more explicit account of the retained capacity argument and its application obligations, together with a separate analysis of what additional evidence would warrant actual disclosure. It does not newly establish actual capacity from the weaker original-source conclusions alone. Uncreated Speech, a particular Arabic wording, its source-side bearer, and created human reception belong to the separate school-labeled and historical inquiry; none is inferred from an abstract possibility of meaningful communication.

## 8 The strongest rivals and what would actually discriminate them

### An intrinsically powered impersonal base

Grant one necessary, unreceived concrete bearer, real noncognitive powers, real derivative cognitive subjects, and actual target-relative truths. It may have necessary dispositional relations without an external lawgiver. Against it, original-giver priority and content-guided source agency offer a positive rationale, but absence of a known mechanism for originating cognition is not a contradiction proof. If the rival offers only the predicate “produces all this,” its account is thin. If it offers a developed intrinsic realization account, compare its explanatory relations with the intentional account rather than counting its entire positive nature as nothing. The same demand applies to an unexplained divine “creative power.”

### An intrinsic teleological order

Grant genuine flourishing, malfunction and natural finality, rather than reducing them to user preference. Aristotle's Physics shows why the choices are not limited to conscious planning and blind accident. The relevant test is whether natural purposes and their realization require an original apprehender, or can be immanent in beings whose natures do not deliberate. The existence of real function does not decide this. The integrated-original-agency argument contests the sufficiency of the impersonal realization at the origin; it must not pretend the rival has no final causes.

### A thinking but nonvoluntarist source

Spinoza already supplies Thought and a productive necessary order. The additional dispute is over genuine alternatives and purposive choice. An argument establishing cognition alone therefore leaves this rival alive. The Taymiyyan emphasis on standing perfection with renewed determinate acts supplies an alternative architecture, but its modal possibilities and explanatory account of those determinations remain to be defended.

### A source beyond intellect

Plotinus Ennead V.2.1 grants an original productive One whose fullness yields Intellect without the One being one of the products. This is not inert nothingness or a bare label for ignorance. It contests the assumption that the source must exemplify intellectual perfection in the recipient's category. The Taymiyyan answer is that genuine apprehension can be affirmed without borrowed objects, creaturely acquisition or limiting bodily organs; production without apprehension is not automatically a higher possession of apprehension. The issue is which positive account of original perfection is defensible. A bare assertion that the superintellectual nature is incoherent does not resolve it. [Plotinus, Enneads V.2.1](https://www.ellopos.net/Elpenor/greek-texts/ancient-greece/plotinus/enneads-5.asp?pg=18).

### Irreducibly plural original contributors

Grant independent intrinsic powers, mutually compatible natures and genuine joint production. Their cooperation need not mean that each receives its entire being or power from the others. Hume Dialogues V.8–9 directly challenges the inference from coordinated design to one bearer of every required attribute. The accepted connected-unity theorem rules out plurality only when its stronger single-bearer coverage and productive premises are warranted. It cannot be invoked against a plural account which precisely denies those premises. [Hume, Dialogues V.8–9](https://davidhume.org/texts/d/5).

The intentional source account has a possible unifying advantage: one act explains why representations, ends and powers are fitted together. The plural reply is that their intrinsic modal compatibility and relations explain the same fit, without one source producing every contributor. The useful test is whether that relation is merely a description, an additional independently actual coordinating power, or an intrinsic fact of the contributors. Only the second introduces a further productive resource; necessary concomitance must not be turned into efficient reception by stipulation. Unity requires a positive reason to prefer or require one original exercise, not just fewer names on a diagram.

## 9 Consequences for warranted restoration

The attribute continuation changes the explanatory options for a real episode of repair. Under the integrated-source account, a finite knower's ability to recognize and repair an error can be an intended derivative instance of apprehending and responding to truth. Under an impersonal account, the same local achievement can be grounded by intrinsic faculties, real targets and natural or epistemic norms. The epistemic warrant of the particular repair still depends on the target, the evidence, competent uptake and available defeaters. Neither ontology permits a false update to become correct merely because it converges, runs efficiently or is called restoration.

Source Knowledge of p does not entail a creature's knowledge of p. A truth-preserving and properly attributed reception route must connect them. Source Wisdom, if established, may support an intended cognitive-function account; it does not identify the intended function of every artifact or endorse every realized policy. Source disclosure, if independently authenticated and correctly interpreted, can add evidence and obligations unavailable from generic metaphysics. That is a real possible change to the epistemic inputs, not a retroactive proof that every prior controller decision had divine warrant.

The shared restoration analysis gives a sharp operational instance. In its D/S model there are n = 2f + 1 authenticated report/repair roots and at most f corrupt roots. Majority reports can identify the true hidden governor while a corrupt root reports truth and destructively actuates. Source-level knowledge of the target leaves that action-integrity uncertainty untouched; with no corrupt roots exposed (k = 0), the stated first-attempt minimax failure is f/n. After k discrepant reports expose k corrupt roots, the stated conditional value is (f − k)/(n − k). A genuinely authenticated, correctly scoped communication establishing that a particular root is safe would change the available epistemic state. A bare capacity of the source to make that disclosure would not. Alternatively, independently established fail-silent semantics, under which each operation is either the true repair or the identity, permits the restoration worker's f + 1 distinct-root strategy without requiring a revelation or prior fault identity. The attribute argument changes that theorem's application only by supplying a warranted premise about the actual action channel, not by renaming the source of truth. These operational claims are crosswalk imports from the separately frozen restoration argument (restoration-crosswalk/frozen-v1/ARGUMENT.md and SUMMARY.md), not proofs in this source report; their review status belongs to that packet.

The root units in that operational model are acquisition and actuation fault domains, not ontological ultimate sources. Common metaphysical sourcehood does not collapse their fault units or establish a probabilistic dependence relation; distinct carriers do not establish fault independence either. The restoration theorem uses a warranted static fault budget, not a probability factorization. A newly established actual shared-failure relation could require contracting the root supports and recomputing the guarantee. Merely identifying one ultimate Creator supplies no such engineering fact.

The crosswalk therefore preserves two burdens: an ultimate explanation of why genuine cognition and norms are possible, and a local explanation of why this belief or repair is warranted. Their unity can be a substantive explanatory proposal, but one burden cannot be discharged by renaming the other. No claim here licenses canonical closure, mandatory repair choice, a privileged controller, or a revealed-content conclusion without its own evidence.

## 10 Present disposition

The argument now supplies an ordered set of independently intelligible positive warrants, not merely a list of missing premises. Minimal Power comes directly from actual production. Knowledge retains its accepted respect-specific original-giver rationale. Agency adds an argument from the original adequacy of genuinely integrated reason-responsive exercise. Will is treated as determinate intentional exercise whose complete efficacy must not be confused with its invariant standing nature. Wisdom has a constructive perfection-based defense and a precisely located motivational and value dispute. Disclosure has a positive conditional realization criterion, whose ability premises still need independent warrant, and a separate authentication problem.

The most important unresolved work is to establish the original integration warrant against intrinsically powered and teleological rivals; defend perfect practical responsiveness without presupposing benevolent agency; articulate genuine alternative acts under necessary standing perfection; and supply independent evidence for any particular communicative mission. Finite scope controls can locate invalid entailments, but do not decide those metaphysical and historical questions. The surviving research is substantive precisely because it concerns those positive resources and their relations.
