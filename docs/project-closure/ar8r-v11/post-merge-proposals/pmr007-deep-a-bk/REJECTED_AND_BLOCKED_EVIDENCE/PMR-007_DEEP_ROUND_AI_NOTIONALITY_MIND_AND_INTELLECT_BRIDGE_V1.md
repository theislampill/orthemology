## 2. Typed language

Keep separate:

```text
STRUCTURAL_ORDER(o):
  an externally or modally interpreted relation/order object;

NOTIONAL_CONTENT(n):
  a universal, concept, or meaning as mentally abstracted/hosted;

REPRESENTS(m,n,o):
  mind/intellect-role m carries notional content n of order o;

CONSTITUTIVELY_GROUNDS(m,o):
  m is the ground of o rather than a downstream representer;

  o is underived at the declared scope;
```

## 3. Source-relative premise candidate

`NM-HOST`:

```text
Every notional content token/type in the declared source ontology is hosted in
some mind/intellect-role.
```

This formalizes the source-reconstructed `universals exist only in the mind`
claim.  It does not say that every external structural order is itself a
notional content.

## 4. Candidate bridge decomposition

### NMIB-1A — hosted-notion result

From `NM-HOST` and `NOTIONAL_CONTENT(n)` it follows that some mind/intellect-role
hosts `n`.

### NMIB-1B — structure-to-notion gap

`STRUCTURAL_ORDER(o)` and `UNDERIVED_ORDER(o)` do not entail that `o` is a
notional content.  An impersonal abstract or modal structure can satisfy those
predicates while no mind exists in the formal model.

### NMIB-1C — representation-to-grounding gap

Even if a created mind represents a universal abstracted from an underived
order, representation does not entail that the mind constitutes or grounds the
order.  The direction may be the reverse: external particulars/order supply
the basis from which a created mind abstracts content.

### NMIB-1D — conditional intellect-ground bridge

A mind/intellect-role ground follows only after adding both:

```text
H7a STRUCTURE_TO_NOTION:
  the relevant determinate order is intrinsically notional/semantic rather
  than merely structural;

H7b CONSTITUTIVE_HOSTING:
  the host of that notional content constitutively grounds the order rather
  than merely representing it.
```

An underived intellect additionally requires:

```text
H7c UNDERIVABILITY_TRANSFER:
  if an underived order is constitutively grounded by m, then m is underived.
```

One intellect requires a uniqueness/co-reference premise; personality,
agency, Wisdom, and Speech require further bridges.

## 5. Positive conditional construction

`AI-POS-H7` freezes one order `o`, one content `n`, and one intellect-role `m`
with:

```text
STRUCTURAL_ORDER(o)
UNDERIVED_ORDER(o)
NOTIONAL_CONTENT(n)
REPRESENTS(m,n,o)
CONSTITUTIVELY_GROUNDS(m,o)
UNDERIVED_INTELLECT(m)
```

The positive result is valid only because H7a–H7c are explicitly included.

## 6. Mandatory countermodels

```text
AI-CM1 ABSTRACT-ORDER:
  underived structural order; no notional-content or mind predicate.

AI-CM2 DOWNSTREAM-ABSTRACTION:
  external order and particulars precede a created mind that abstracts and
  represents a universal; representation direction is downstream.

AI-CM3 MULTIPLE-REPRESENTERS:
  two minds represent the same order; no uniqueness.

AI-CM4 IMPERSONAL-INTELLECT-ROLE:
  a formal conceptual container hosts content but PERSONAL, AGENTIC, and WISE
  remain false.

AI-CM5 SOURCE-PREDICATION:
  a source may directly predicate Knowledge of one referent, but this supplies
  a Track-N premise rather than a neutral derivation from structural order.

AI-CM6 SOURCE-AUTHORITY-DELETION:
  secondary reconstruction or translation without source/world guards cannot
  establish the external existence of the source-predicated intellect.

AI-CM7 UNDERIVABILITY-NONTRANSFER:
  an underived truth/order can be represented by a derived mind; underivability
  of content/order does not transfer to the representer.
```

## 7. H7 and rival disposition

The source-reconstructed mental status of universals can support a conditional
bridge from **notional content** to a mental host.  It does not establish that
the underived modal-determinate order is itself notional, that a mental host
grounds it, or that the host is underived, unique, personal, or agentic.

The strongest live rival remains:

```text
necessary or underived impersonal structure
+
created or local minds that abstract/represent it
```

This rival satisfies the source-reconstructed mental/external distinction while
withholding a mental ultimate ground.
