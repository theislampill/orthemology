What this establishes

same lower-order conclusion can arise under a discriminating or self-sealing governing rule;

a correct actual output can be Gettier-like because the route fails on nearby admissible cases;

causal burden landing requires an operation/no-operation contrast;

stable restoration is a property of the governing route and target field, not merely the current sentence.

What this does not establish

that any person or school instantiates this model;

that disagreement is evidence of corruption;

that the finite binary model captures whole-person fiṭrī restoration;

that changing one inference rule causally restores human cognition.
