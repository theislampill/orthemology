## II. Comparative argument

Let `E={M,A,S,N,R}` be the orthability explanandum set and let `T` range over candidate theories. Define:

```text
Cov(T): target coverage
Prim(T): independently variable primitive degrees of freedom
Pat(T): number and complexity of derivational patterns
Con(T): independently testable cross-domain constraints
Fit(T): fit to the target cases and countermodels
AdHoc(T): target-specific auxiliary structure lacking independent motivation
```

No scalar score is assumed canonical. A rational-source theory `H_U` is comparatively superior to a plural rival `H_P` only if, all things considered:

```text
Cov(H_U) ≥ Cov(H_P)
Prim(H_U) < Prim(H_P) or Pat(H_U) is genuinely more reusable
Con(H_U) > Con(H_P)
Fit(H_U) ≥ Fit(H_P)
AdHoc(H_U) is not greater enough to erase the gain
```

The comparison is partial-order rather than forced weighted summation.
