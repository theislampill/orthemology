# Strong cooperation/coalescence: countermodel and inference audit

Assessment date: 7 October 2026 UTC. Independent bounded formal-research assessment for the current reassessment. This is not a formal research-programme closure, a new kernel proof, or a metaphysical-possibility certificate. Historical source and proof artifacts were read, not edited.

## Direct verdict

**FULL COUNTERMODEL: NOT PRODUCED.** No inspected current model has been shown to preserve every independently upstream premise of the exact cooperation/coalescence inference being challenged while falsifying that inference. “Full” does not include the disputed conclusion as one of its own mandatory antecedents. In particular, if the derivation of P2b is the test, denying P2b alone does not disqualify a model. No new model is certified here.

The source already considers agreement, assistance despite intact powers, necessary concurrence, a third cause, influence/restriction, and the connected actual order. Answering it with “two agents could agree” or a finite matching-outcome table is inadequate. The retained formal controls are useful for their stated weaker signatures. Their mathematical correctness does not enlarge those signatures.

This finding has two limits. Failure to produce a countermodel does not prove the source premises or their applicability. Conversely, the absence of a complete formalisation does not itself undermine their philosophical warrant. The affirmative actual-common-Creator assessment and the stronger universal SourceMode assessment must be considered separately.

## 1. Exact target and non-equivalent notions

The actual target is C: one original bearer completely supplies the relevant actual connected created field, including its genuinely operative requisites and mediate original provision. C excludes another irreducibly original productive contributor in that field. It does not by itself exclude an unrelated necessary reality outside it.

The stronger target S says that every suitably qualified original, in every admissible circumstance and every relevant exercise, must supply the entire original basis of each effect in which it participates. C does not imply S, and a failure of an attempted proof of S does not refute C.

Keep these notions distinct:

1. **Intrinsic independence:** the agent's constitutive power and will are not efficiently supplied, removed or sustained by another. This definition does not say “there is no peer.”
2. **Effect/goal conditionality:** the jointly intended result requires another contribution. This is not yet efficient production of the first agent's intrinsic power.
3. **Finite alternative ability:** some supplied history contains solo production of an outcome label. This is strictly weaker than unrestricted power over every eligible target with the other agent present.
4. **Unrestricted target power:** ability extends to every eligible specified target, with the relevant intrinsic resources retained. Eligibility, history and the causal respect must be fixed; they cannot be changed in the middle of an inference.
5. **Complete will:** the fully specified determination that, together with complete power, brings its specified effect. Merely wanting the same endpoint does not say which original contributions the agent actually produces.
6. **Perfect agreement:** agreement compatible with the full perfection profile, not an added Boolean label or unexplained list of matching choices.
7. **Actual contribution and effect unity:** distinct real productive contributions can occur in one concrete effect; “same outcome type,” “same effect token,” and “same original contribution in the same respect” differ.
8. **Causal nonduplication:** two distinct sources do not each provide an entire same original contribution in the same respect. This is stronger than merely defining “solitary” to exclude a collaborator, unless the relevant entire-production attribution already contains exclusivity.
9. **Complete control/sovereignty:** a substantive claim about the scope and unborrowed efficacy of determination. If it includes complete original provision of every requisite, this must be stated and defended as a premise. It must not be smuggled into the word “independent.”

## 2. Source-linked premise and inference map

The P/B/O identifiers coordinate with the direct-source reconstruction. Their wording here disambiguates the formal use; it does not claim that every principle has been independently established.

### Background premises

- **P1, power required for action:** productive agency requires power. Oneness printed 17–22 and 34; extracted PDF pages 24–29 and 41, especially text lines 830–1034 and 1458–1492.
- **P2a, intrinsic original agency:** a proposed original lord's constitutive power/will are intrinsic and not efficiently supplied by another. Oneness printed 20–21 and 31, text lines 938–993 and 1344–1377.
- **P2b, independence of productive completion:** the source also argues that a necessary original cannot receive its productive perfection through another's contribution or leave. This is stronger than P2a. Its place is downstream when the derivation from intrinsic independence/perfection is under test; at a later inference it may be used as a premise only if separately retained.
- **M1_EFFICACY_PERFECTION:** Minhaj 2:180–183 expressly values realised efficacy as perfection and treats dependency for that efficacy as incompatible with necessary self-sufficiency. Separate the comparative excellence of efficacy from the further transfer of a condition of a joint product into received intrinsic productive perfection. The final source reconstruction verifies this source-specific rationale; the model audit does not assume its transfer simply to reject a model challenging it.
- **P3, no vicious efficient priority cycle/regress:** reciprocal efficient origination of the very powers needed to do the originating cannot start; an uncompleted regress of that kind supplies no original source. This is not a ban on every infinite history of effects or every noncausal concomitance. Oneness printed 18–21, lines 848–1008.
- **P4, same-effect exclusion:** two agents cannot each actually and exclusively make the entire numerically same effect. Oneness printed 23–24, lines 1039–1095. A broader primitive anti-overdetermination CND requires its additional substantive interpretation; it is not secured merely by the word “alone.”
- **P5, equal powers and persistence:** in the equal-makers branch, each can do what the other can, and the relevant powers remain intact in their mutual presence. Oneness printed 23–25, lines 1039–1100 and following. This is not merely a finite list of nonactual matching endpoint witnesses. Equality alone says the target sets match; their unrestricted eligible scope also needs the independent-lord/perfection qualification and must not be inferred from equality alone.
- **P6, complete determination:** complete power plus complete will for a specified effect entails that effect. An unwilled target may remain possible and within power. Oneness printed 31 and 34, lines 1344–1380 and 1467–1480; Asbahani printed 147–148 as checked by the source reconstruction.
- **P7, efficient explanation of contingent coalescence:** an actually occurring, nonnecessary coalescence has an efficient agent; in the stipulated exhaustive two-original setup, ultimate creative agency does not lie outside the pair. This permits real derivative intermediaries; one must trace their original provision rather than mistake them for an independent third original. Oneness printed 30, lines 1329–1340; Asbahani printed 145. The source's prior assistance/exclusive-production split does the work of rejecting an irreducibly collective option. P7 must not silently be strengthened from “an efficient agent” to “exactly one complete sufficient original producer.”
- **O1, actual cross-connected order:** the actual products and their operative conditions are interconnected. Oneness printed 32–33, lines 1394–1437. Modern universal application and precise occurrence/requisite individuation are separate interpretation burdens. The translator's note 26 about every conceivable separate world is not the author's main-text observation.

### Substantive cooperation bridges being tested

- **B1, optional coalescence/influence:** optional mixing of products across two original domains is either with the other's willing, hence assistance, or against it, hence restriction. In either case the author argues that it influences the other's determining power/will. Oneness printed 30–32, lines 1334–1393; Asbahani printed 144–146. This is an explicit argument, not an omitted possibility.
- **B2, necessary concurrence/partial cause:** necessary concord of the relevant willing/acting makes each a partial efficient condition of the other's willing/acting, yielding a vicious cycle, unless a third complete efficient producer accounts for both. Minhaj V03L0269–0273; extended partnership argument V03L0274–0276. The author explicitly considers intrinsic willing and concomitance, then argues for this causal reading.
- **B3, simultaneous target-powers:** from the exclusion of two actual solitary entire producers, the source excludes concurrently unrestricted equally targeted original powers. Oneness printed 23–25; Asbahani printed 140–141; Dar IX 016:720–725, 747–756 and 766–779. This requires a substantive account of target eligibility, independence and actual provenance, rather than the invalid generic inference from two abilities to two actual entire productions.
- **B4, scope/induction beyond two (supplemental identifier):** a third efficient provider of the first pair's constitutive powers would deprive them of ultimate original status; further supposedly original partners require the same exclusion argument. The text's relevant third cause is an efficient producer/enabler, not simply a shared abstract plan, logical truth or objective reason. Oneness printed 19–22 and 40 with notes 19/32; Minhaj V03L0274–0276. Extending the two-agent result to every possible plurality requires the relevant exhaustion/induction assumptions, not an unexplained jump from two to arbitrarily many.

### Additional source constraints from the Majmu sequel

The newly targeted Majmu XX passage adds two substantive constraints: **B6_EXCLUSIVE_COMPLETION**, complete power and will entail production through the agent alone (printed 176–177); and **B5_TRANSFER**, powers spread across two can be borne together, with their concentration used to argue that each partner admits more complete independent power (178–180). The latter is presented as rationally evident and supported by cases of united persons, members and bodies. These are stronger than merely preserving intrinsic faculties. [Primary text, printed 174–183, especially 176–181](https://islamweb.net/ar/library/content/22/2032/فصل-العلتين-لا-تكونان-مستقلتين-بحكم-واحد-حال-الاجتماع).

Keep three analytic steps separate: possible unified realisation of a power profile; the same existing partner's eligibility for that profile; and the conclusion that lacking it defeats necessary self-sufficiency. Generic modal logic does not identify them. Likewise outcome attainment and exclusive original production are distinct predicates. The source supplies an argument for their connection; a critic must address it. None of the inspected models represents these added constraints. A model making the powers essentially non-unifiable would contest B5_TRANSFER, not preserve it.

### Strong conditional exclusion

Assume two distinct exhaustive original lords A and B, P1–P7 at their intended strong meanings, plus the applicable B1/B2/B3 classifications and the strengthened complete-power interpretation where used and no admissible higher efficient provider.

1. If their same numerical effect is each's entire solitary actual production, P4 excludes it.
2. If cooperation supplies either agent's constitutive power/will, P2 excludes receipt; reciprocal supply also contradicts P3.
3. If the coordination is necessarily interconditional in the B2 efficient sense, P3 excludes the reciprocal partial-cause cycle; a third complete producer falls outside the two-original hypothesis and defeats the ultimate status of the pair.
4. If coordination/coalescence is contingent, P7 requires efficient explanation. The prior exclusive/assisting split cannot simply be omitted. Under B1, consent gives the excluded assistance and opposition gives the excluded restriction. A foreign abstract coordination law is not a third efficient cause just because it is called “third.”
5. Thus the putative original domains cannot have the cross-coalescing relation. O1 contradicts their division across the actual connected field.

This is a valid **conditional argument at the stated causal/classificatory meanings**. Two tests must not be conflated: a model of the complete intended doctrine, and a countermodel to an inference deriving part of that doctrine. The latter preserves the independently upstream package and falsifies the exact bridge being challenged. Falsifying P2b, SourceMode, B1, B2, B3, B5_TRANSFER or B6_EXCLUSIVE_COMPLETION is therefore not itself a disqualification when that is the inference's conclusion. A deficiency counts against a candidate only if it concerns a genuinely independent retained antecedent, such as the required original-bearer semantics, specified target capacities, complete actual determination or faithful contribution interpretation. The current candidates remain uncertified at those antecedents; this audit does not reject them merely because they omit or deny the disputed bridge.

## 3. Precisely where the strongest challenge remains

### 3.1 A changed effect and contraposition

Let D_B(e) be B's unchanged complete will and power for the fully specified effect e, and let P6 be D_B(e) ⇒ e.

If A really prevents that same e, D_B(e) and not-e cannot coexist. This supports the restriction branch without requiring a probability of disagreement.

If A's contribution instead fulfils the jointly specified outcome B intended, not-e has not been established. A created object's acquiring a further state is not automatically the negation of B's intended effect. One must identify the complete target and show that the allegedly unchanged complete determination would have excluded that state or would itself already have supplied its complete original basis. This is the exact burden in extending the restricted contraposition to consensual coalescence.

The source's reply is strong: complete independent lordship concerns the entire original role, not just an unchanged faculty or a proximal partial act. Under that substantive profile, deliberately choosing an externally completed undertaking may already violate the relevant independence/perfection requirement. A candidate must contest that classification with reasons while preserving everything independently established; it cannot evade it merely by calling the choice voluntary.

### 3.2 Necessary agreement and efficient conditionality

Modal co-occurrence is not generally identical to efficient origination. B2 makes the stronger assertion for these proposed independent powers/wills. The Jami VI E185 control explicitly distinguishes each cooperator's own power, awareness and purpose from attainment requiring both acts; E184–186 keep conditions and efficient causes apart. Dar IX 016:621–650 likewise retains distinct actual contributions and describes absolute lordship as maintaining a thing from all its aspects.

Those controls constrain the reconstruction; they do not establish that two original perfect lords satisfy their weaker model. The source itself treats creaturely cooperation differently and directly presses the proposed original-concurrence case. A two-node DAG or parallel intrinsic will stipulation therefore does not answer the actual argument until it explains why the strong original target-powers, complete determinations and actual effect ownership survive.

### 3.3 Common reasons and the third cause

A proposed shared objective good is not thereby an efficient source of either agent's will. That prevents one equivocation. It does not establish its adequacy to account for all concordant decisions, role allocation and actual coalescence. Minhaj V03L0272 explicitly challenges appeal to the product's benefit or foreknowledge as an origin of the wills that produce it. A serious counterproposal must specify how intrinsic reasons determine concord without covert reciprocal efficient production or a foreign completing source, and must meet the strong original-agency constraints. None of the current formal models supplies that account.

### 3.4 Modal target availability and B3

From “A can achieve q” and “B can achieve q” one cannot infer that both actually produce the entire same token q. From each one's ability over a contrary outcome one cannot infer a jointly possible opposed-will profile without the relevant modal-compossibility premise. The source's intact-power and necessary-agreement arguments are intended to do more than those weak modal moves. Dar IX 016:747–756 explicitly considers conditional alternative powers and rejects the analogy with one agent choosing between contrary acts: another's required abstention is treated as an external limitation, whereas the agent's own choice is not. Lines 776–779 explicitly condemn partitioned cooperation as a perfection defect. These are substantive arguments and must be evaluated at that strong level. A response cannot merely repeat voluntary agreement. Equally, a proof must distinguish the source-neutral target q from the source-indexed target “A exclusively produces q while B also produces q”; the latter already contradicts P4. Its ineligibility alone does not show lost power over every eligible q. No existing model supplies the full independent-control semantics needed to settle this dispute.

## 4. Formal actual-field route without silently universalising

A precise relational presentation uses sources s, actual occurrences e, and contribution tokens c in fixed productive respects. Write R(e,c) for a genuinely operative requisite and O(s,c) for entire original provision of that contribution. Derived performers remain distinct from original providers.

An adequate actual application needs:

- each relevant requisite to have an actual original provision account;
- actual occurrence admission and faithful exhaustive requisite representation;
- an actual participating original anchor;
- connectedness through genuine shared operative requisites;
- local exclusion of distinct original owners across a single genuinely unified actual occurrence, with same-token CND where applicable.

If the strong coalescence argument warrants that actual local exclusion, original-owner identity propagates across each overlap path: choose an original owner at the anchor; each shared requisite retains that owner; local exclusion transfers it to every requisite of the next occurrence. Induction gives one complete original provider throughout the connected actual field. Visibility then excludes an unrepresented second original contributor.

This need not assert S across every metaphysically possible field. It is stronger than observing a connected graph: the local source-exclusion proposition and faithful original-provision semantics do indispensable work.

The existing AnchoredSourceBridge implements a related conditional route:

Qualified + Classification + Conformance ⇒ ModeSatisfaction;
UseSound + RoleApplicable + ModeSatisfaction ⇒ SourceMode;
ActualAnchor + SourceMode/ModeSatisfaction + faithful overlap paths ⇒ GlobalCoverage;
GlobalCoverage + FieldCND + Visible ⇒ ActualUnique.

No existing theorem in that package proves P2b/M1's transfer, B1/B2/B3 or the additional B5_TRANSFER/B6_EXCLUSIVE_COMPLETION from independently interpreted intrinsic-power, volitional, perfection or sovereign-control antecedents. Here “perfection” and “sovereign control” cannot include the exact disputed conclusion without making its test circular. Conversely, the theorem's conditional validity does not depend on a successful philosophical countermodel. The source trilemma would supply warrant for its application only after the relevant interpretation/classification obligations are met.

The correct present conclusion is asymmetric: the earlier models do not establish that the strong source argument fails; the inability to find a full countermodel does not establish that all its premises succeed. The exact matters still to test are B1's transfer from consensual cross-product coalescence to a defect in complete independent determination, B2's conversion of the specified necessary concurrence into partial efficient production, and B3's inference from actual-production exclusion to incompatible simultaneous unrestricted target-powers. The source argues for all three; the additional Majmu transferability and exclusive-completion arguments also have to be met. Any further criticism must meet those arguments and their complete power/sovereignty background, and any acceptance must credit their substantive rationale rather than a definitional no-peer shortcut.
