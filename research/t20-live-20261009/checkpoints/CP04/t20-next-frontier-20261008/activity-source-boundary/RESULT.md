# Essential activity, created output, and the field boundary

8 October 2026 UTC. New bounded T20 research. Earlier records remain unchanged. No protected integration, owner acceptance, universal metaphysical closure, or historical-duration certification.

## Result

**The residual can be narrowed, but essential activity does not by itself remove the created-field restriction.** Under an essential-activity norm applying to every eligible original, a completely inactive candidate is disqualified. An actually active candidate which contributes nothing to this created field is not disqualified by that norm alone. Turning activity into field participation requires an additional productive-scope premise.

The decisive direct source control is al-Ṣafadiyya, Muḥammad Rashād Salīm 2:23. It explicitly distinguishes intransitive acts, which do not require a product, from transitive acts, which do. It then considers conditionally a beginningless succession restricted to the former, as well as a beginningless transitive succession. This is stronger evidence for the distinction than simply observing that “act” and “effect” are different words. It is **not** an endorsement of a forever externally nonproductive necessary being, nor proof that the author rejects perpetual creation elsewhere. The source's case division prevents assigning an external product to every actual intrinsic act by definition.

Two scope transitions must therefore be distinguished:

1. **Global activity → some created output.** This needs a transitive-productivity/perfection premise beyond activity generally.
2. **Some created output → participation in this connected field.** This needs a localization, connectedness, exhaustive-inventory, or comprehensive-creative-scope premise.

Even granting perpetual transitive productivity leaves the second transition open. Conversely, to connect all eligible originals to a uniqueness result for this field, it is not necessary to prove that every act has a product: a justified *some-output-in-this-field* premise for every eligible original is enough. The existing appraisal does not yet establish exclusive field participation as a universal impossibility theorem, so completing this scope bridge alone would still not turn its defeasible actual-world judgement into such a theorem.

## 2. Completely inactive is not the same as field-inactive

There are three cases, not two:

- **Globally inactive:** no actual voluntary intrinsic acts anywhere in the candidate's history.
- **Intrinsically active but externally nonproductive:** actual successive voluntary acts, without any original production of a separate created effect.
- **Productive outside this field:** actual created products, but no original contribution to the relevant connected field F.

The first case violates a universally applied essential-activity requirement. The latter two do not violate that requirement merely by their description. This is an inference boundary, not their philosophical accreditation.

A further qualification matters. The causal reasoning at 2:142 argues that stripping the agent of all successive voluntary activity at a time prevents a later intrinsic or external novelty, because no sufficient causal change would obtain. For an already established creator that actually produces novel events, this blocks the proposed inactive beginning or interval. **A no-restart conditional alone does not refute a second bearer stipulated never to start anything.** The text also expressly states the inseparability of successive voluntary matters; applying that stronger essential-genus claim to every proposed original requires the eligibility/perfection bridge.

The inherited Nisāʾ lesson 33 record gives the explicit activity-as-perfection rationale. The Akmaliyya also compares actual succession with inert incapacity and treats the necessary ground as entitled to eligible pure perfection. These provide the relevant substantive argument. Under the adopted unrestricted-perfection framework, a genuinely globally idle purported divine original cannot be kept eligible simply by stipulating that its essence permits or requires inactivity. The completed nonreceptivity review already answered that maneuver. Nevertheless, bare modal necessity or lack of an efficient producer is not, without that framework, a demonstrated universal always-active theorem.

The quantifier domains are explicitly different. (A) GA(g) concerns the already established creator g. (B) ∀o [EligibleOriginal(o) → GA(o)] extends the essential-activity norm through the original-perfection framework. (C) ∀b [NecessaryExternalBearer(b) → GA(b)] is broader again unless every such necessary bearer has independently been shown eligible in the relevant sense. A fact about g does not quantify over all originals, and (B) does not yield (C) without that extra eligibility bridge. “External bearer” here means a distinct concrete bearer, not g’s intrinsic attribute, act-token, or a reified genus. The present result does not identify all three domains.

This is the first narrowing of the old “idle/disconnected” residual: **the completely idle candidate is no longer the useful residual once the essential-activity norm is applied universally to eligible originals.** What remains to test is field-disconnected actual agency, together with the warrant for its eligibility.

## 3. What the continuous activity passages entail about products

### The explicit taxonomy at 2:23

The key statement is “فالفعل اللازم لا يقتضي مفعولا والفعل المتعدي يقتضي مفعولا”. The public-domain Arabic wording belongs to Ibn Taymiyyah's own exposition, not a reported opponent. The surrounding 2:22 discussion argues for unceasing action from intrinsic determination and absence of an external enabler; 2:23 then distinguishes the two kinds of action. His case division permits the argument against a coeternal particular creature to proceed whether only intransitive activity is beginningless or transitive activity is also beginningless. The first branch expressly has transitive acts beginning later; it is not a branch in which no transitive act ever occurs.

Two conclusions are warranted. First, “actual act” does not analytically mean “act with a distinct created product.” Second, the general perpetual-genus statement cannot be substituted for the separate transitive-genus statement without a bridge. Two stronger conclusions are **not** warranted: that an intransitive act can have no external consequences or presuppositions, and that an entire externally productless perpetual life is metaphysically possible for an eligible original. Neither is proved by this taxonomy or case division.

[Primary electronic Salīm text, 2:20–24, especially 2:23](https://islamhouse.com/read/ar/الصفدية-272837).

### The direction of the 2:142 causal claim

The crucial conclusion at L2256 says that nothing proceeds from the agent except with voluntary matters subsisting in it. Its direction is **external issuance requires intrinsic activity**, not **every intrinsic voluntary occurrence requires an external issued product**. The preceding “whenever something of this subsists” proposal at L2254 belongs to the examination of a purported bare eternal cause plus changing conditions. It must not be extracted as an unrestricted productive equivalence while dropping both the reply and the 2:23 taxonomy.

The 2:145 description of the action under discussion as creation or origination likewise concerns the paired acts of producing a particular and its successive concomitants. Its antecedent is that productive case. It cannot erase the earlier intransitive category. At 2:146 the author distinguishes an essential attribute belonging as an individual or as a genus from a successive act; the point excludes one fixed eternal act-token, not the existence of intrinsically subsisting acts.

### The later explanatory control

Shūrā lesson 6 L236 affirms continuing activity and preserves particular will. It cites everlasting creaturely life as a future example, and permits pre-cosmic acts from power, while distinguishing agent, act and product. Thus it does include a substantive future-creature claim about the one divine creator; it is not confined to merely abstract capacity. It supplies no rule that *each* voluntary act has a product, no newly demonstrated perpetual-transitive-genus principle for *every proposed original*, and no rule localizing all products to this connected field. The inherited Nisāʾ and Ghāfir controls remain useful against collapsing intrinsic speech into an external product.

[Official Shūrā lesson 6 transcript](https://old.binothaimeen.net/content/14192).

## 4. The strongest competing inference

The strongest affirmative reply is not “all new acts are creatures.” It is this:

1. Every eligible original must satisfy unrestricted, deficiency-free perfection, not merely unreceived existence.
2. Adequate actual wise creative efficacy is a pure perfection; standing power alone does not exhaust it.
3. A bearer that does nothing for the actual created order lacks the appropriate comprehensive original efficacy, even if it exercises intrinsic acts.
4. Therefore a merely intrinsic or disconnected candidate cannot qualify as an original peer.

This is a serious source-supported line. Akmaliyya L217–223 ranks dependence of everything over dependence of only some things; unaided achievement over assisted achievement; and comprehensive creation over restricted creation. L235–240 combines efficacious willing with wise discrimination. It would distort the strongest inspected source to claim that it has only an always-active premise and no broader challenge to a nonparticipating peer.

The precise work is in **premise 3's scope and application**. It may mean:

- each eligible original must actually ground every created bearer;
- each must make some original contribution to this field;
- or every actual external bearer, including any putative uncreated peer, must depend on it.

These are not the same claim. The first rules out a nonparticipant in a nonempty created field but still leaves the coactive application problem. The second is a weaker scope bridge sufficient if exclusive participation is separately established. The third already rules out peers directly and bypasses both activity and the field argument. The eligibility domain addendum already records that last route; this report does not rediscover it.

The strongest opposing response is also narrower than an exemption by essence: actual wise agency might be complete in its own appropriate intrinsic exercise or distinct undertaking without being original provider of this field. **That response challenges the relational scope of a proposed pure perfection. It does not establish the response's truth merely by being sayable.** It must explain why comprehensive created efficacy is not a relevant pure excellence, or why its absence here is compatible with the same original perfection. A label such as “perfect but disconnected” supplies none of that warrant.

The source's allowance for fitting particular nonperformance is relevant but not decisive for either side. Akmaliyya L075 and L159–160 make an act's occurrence or absence fitting to wisdom. This blocks maximal production of every possible good, but does not establish that permanent exclusion from all original created efficacy is wise or perfect. Conversely, the value of creative agency does not by itself dictate this particular created token at this time. General activity, some external productivity, comprehensive actual coverage, and plenitude must stay separate.

## 5. The small conditional result

`CONDITIONAL_SCOPE.md` supplies an exact mathematical reconstruction, with a short all-integer relational control rather than a finite simulation or a new generic nonreceipt model.

The first control retains actual successive activity for both designated candidate bearers, creates the connected field through one, and assigns the other only intrinsic acts. It demonstrates solely that the activity subtheory does not entail external production. The second assigns external created output to **every** represented act of both candidates, with the second producing a distinct connected component. It demonstrates that even a stronger per-act production rule does not entail participation in F.

Neither control carries a certified necessary essence, unrestricted modal agency, a philosophical purity argument, wise particular reasons, an authenticated productive semantics, or a warranted complete created inventory. Both omit the broader all-created-coverage and universal external-bearer supremacy norms. Treating those omissions as a full source-faithful plural cosmos would be exactly the mistake this task forbids. Their usefulness is only to identify which premise does which work.

The positive conditional theorem is correspondingly minimal: if every eligible original participates in F, and every eligible original participant in F is g, then every eligible original is g. The participation bridge can come from some created output plus justified field localization, or from the stronger all-created-coverage norm. **The current sufficient-warrant judgement supplies neither a demonstrated exclusive-participant theorem nor the universal participation bridge.** Its defeasible acceptance of one common actual basis remains untouched.

## 6. Revised residual and next consequential question

The old report's boundary should now be read with greater precision:

- The globally inactive candidate is excluded **conditional on applying the essential-activity/perfection norm to every eligible original**. The causal no-restart argument alone has a narrower role.
- An intrinsically active but field-nonproductive candidate is not excluded by the activity premise alone. It has not earned genuine eligibility or positive metaphysical credibility.
- A perpetually productive but field-disconnected candidate additionally survives the bare activity-to-product bridge. Eliminating it requires actual scope/connectedness, comprehensive created efficacy, or universal external-bearer supremacy.
- Granting every actual created effect a common original basis does not by itself classify every other actual bearer as created. Nor does “one complete common source” by itself mean “no other original participant.”
- No new act is made a creature, no perfection is made externally received, and no genus is made an independent extramental individual by this analysis.

The most economical further scope question is therefore: **Why must every genuinely eligible original make an actual original contribution to this nonempty relevant created field?** This is weaker than universal external-bearer supremacy and need not require a product for every intrinsic act. The source's comprehensive-creative-perfection comparison is the strongest inspected route to it. Appraising that exact relational scope, together with the still-separate exclusive-efficacy application, would change the universal conclusion. Another proof that bare activity can be represented without an output would not.

This result narrows the universal-uniqueness residual without reopening settled distinctions, weakening accepted eligibility conditions, or treating source disagreement as an equal-warrant rival cosmology.
