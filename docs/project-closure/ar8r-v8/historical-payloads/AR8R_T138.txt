AR8R-T138 — Many-sorted minimal-nonintegration model
Setting

Let Sigma_M, Sigma_T, and Sigma_N be pairwise disjoint many-sorted signatures, possibly sharing only a fixed neutral interface Sigma_0. Let Theory_M, Theory_T, and Theory_N each have a model whose Sigma_0 reducts agree.
Theorem T138.1 — conservative combination control

There is a combined many-sorted model satisfying all three component theories and the agreed neutral interface.
Construction

Take the disjoint union of the nonshared sorts and interpretations, identifying only the fixed Sigma_0 reduct. Since no other symbol or axiom crosses tracks, each component sentence retains its truth value. ∎
Theorem T138.2 — no integration without a bridge

Suppose a proposed cross-track conclusion C is false in one such compatible combination. Then the union of the component theories does not entail C.
Proof

The combined structure is a countermodel to the entailment. ∎
Scope

This is a relative-consistency and nonentailment control, not a theorem that the real domains are disjoint. If current-main objects genuinely share identity, dependence, or truth conditions across tracks, those cross-axioms must be stated and tested. The result shows only that component success plus guarded coexistence does not by itself force an integrated metaphysics.
Disposition

R5_MINIMAL_NONINTEGRATION: STRENGTHENED_BY_EXPLICIT_MODEL_CONSTRUCTION
GENERAL_THEOREM_NOVELTY: REJECTED
INTEGRATED_MENISCUS: NOT_ENTAILED
