## The distinction in simple terms

### For a young reader

Imagine a game with a written rule book. Someone says that no sequence of legal moves can reach a certain square. A computer has now checked every kind of move used in the argument. That is stronger checking of the argument, not a change to the game. Two proposed extra rules create a different game. In that game, a checked sequence does reach the square.

When we learn about the world, we must also keep the question steady. Seeing a useful action, understanding why it works, and knowing when to trust it are different things to find out. We can give an achievement its proper credit without pretending that every further question has already been answered.

The present mathematics compares rules for typed programs. It does not decide whether two people, institutions or minds are numerically the same. ### Aplin and colleagues

Aplin and colleagues followed individually identified great tits through changed food rewards. Among birds previously preferring an established technique, 49% changed preference toward the better-rewarded alternative within 14 days. Both techniques already existed; the old one still worked. This is genuine adaptive choice correction, without an explanatory report being required. Switched birds supplied useful group-level social information, supported by repeated production, population change and sequential learning analysis. A particular switcher-to-recipient causal chain is not resolved. The favoured account combines social influence with personal reinforcement; fitted parameters do not uniquely reveal mental mechanisms. The door had been widened before the equal-low and unequal-reward phases; one population had a year-long interval plus renewed exposure. The case supports bounded adaptive and contributory competence, while leaving diagnosis, recipient qualification and guarantees unestablished.

### Vogel and Dussutour

Vogel and Dussutour's Physarum experiment supplies a physical transfer case. After three-hour fusion with salt-exposed material, a previously untrained fragment was separated and crossed a salt bridge with reduced aversion relative to untrained-pair controls. One-hour fusion did not show that pattern. This supports an acquired, immediately exercised response in the separated recipient region. It does not establish abstract encoding, unmixed material continuity, or whole-organism identity. No delayed recipient retention or onward relay was tested. Nonsignificant trained-control differences are not equivalence tests, and physiological carryover remains mechanistically unresolved. The main paper and supplement were read; raw-data downloads were inaccessible.

### Boussard and colleagues

After six days of salt exposure, Physarum samples retained reduced salt aversion following a month as sclerotia and reactivation, including after water-only drying. This demonstrates a learned behavioural disposition surviving an actual dormant transition. Sodium enrichment accompanied training and persisted in dormant samples, but the assays did not trace individual ions. Two-hour salt loading improved subsequent expansion without establishing shorter onset latency; supplementary modelling did not select a salt-specific interaction. Sodium is therefore a plausible contributing material state, not a proven necessary, sufficient or unique memory carrier. Differential one-year mortality prevents extending the behavioural finding to a year.

### Kriegman and colleagues

Kriegman et al. (2021) report frog-cell swarms whose spheroid offspring, after selection, culture and transfer to fresh donor-cell supplies, themselves assembled further offspring. Eight seeded swarm trials produced one or two generations from spheroidal founders and two to four from manually shaped C-founders; one C-founded trial stopped with fungal contamination. The longest sequence reached F4, demonstrating three offspring-swarm construction steps after the founders’ first step, without establishing F4’s onward competence. The C-shape was not copied: renewed task performance survived its loss, while size and yield declined. Three cell-only controls produced no motile offspring. This is finite, supported renewal of a collective constructive capacity, not evidence that AI software, learned information, individual identity or indefinite replication transferred. Microcircuit utility was simulated.

On the accepted minimal reading, this is an actual bounded constructive subfaculty with a defensible supplied-route faculty interpretation. The preserved object is the exercised role; full morphological fidelity, transferred qualification expertise, uniform future service and whole-organism identity do not follow.

## How the contributions fit the programme

The common discipline is to preserve the object and strength of a claim through each inference. Formal validity concerns a specified judgement. A capacity attribution concerns what an agent can do within a declared arrangement. A metaphysical attribution concerns what a particular bearer is or must be. Progress in one inquiry can sharpen another’s question without supplying its missing evidence. These levels should remain connected through explicit arguments, not merged by shared vocabulary.

A useful research record can separate three entries: the proposition supported, the grounds available for it, and the stronger proposition still under examination. This avoids two errors. One is erasing a modest positive result because it does not answer the largest question. The other is allowing a sequence of individually reasonable steps to change its subject or modal force unnoticed. The programme should preserve affirmative warrant while locating the exact additional burden.

For empirical work, a future design should identify the same carrier across acquisition, an experimentally specified change and later contribution. The task, support boundary and success criterion should be fixed in advance. A claim about recognising a fault needs a discrimination test; a claim about benefiting a successor needs suitable downstream observations. These are proposed requirements for the particular stronger claims, rather than universal prerequisites for crediting practical competence.

For foundational reasoning, the appropriate next question is what consideration would warrant the precise disputed attribution to the actual bearer. Basic recognition and integrated explanation remain eligible forms of warrant. Neither another formal conditional nor the mere description of a rival automatically decides the matter. A reason must be evaluated at its actual content, including its positive force, without demanding an unrelated complete theory as an entrance condition.
