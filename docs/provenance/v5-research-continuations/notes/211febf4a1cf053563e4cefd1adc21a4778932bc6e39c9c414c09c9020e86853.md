# Actual raw tape exponential bound

This source-only successor derives an explicit concentration rate for the exact seeded row-stack law used by the accepted hidden-parity controller. It adds five modules and fifteen selected declarations. The full reachable custom closure has 29 source modules.

For a finite row index type A and finite receipt type Y, any independent probability seed, normalized nonnegative real row probabilities, 0<eta<=1 and N>=1, the probability of some row/symbol empirical prefix of length at least N deviating by at least eta is at most

    card(A) card(Y) 2 exp(-N eta^2/2) / [1-exp(-eta^2/2)].

The dimension factor becomes state_count^2 * action_count for Markov rows indexed by state/action. The rate is deliberately weaker than sharp Hoeffding. It is derived from the already accepted elementary centred-Bernoulli MGF inequality, not assumed as an extra concentration premise.
