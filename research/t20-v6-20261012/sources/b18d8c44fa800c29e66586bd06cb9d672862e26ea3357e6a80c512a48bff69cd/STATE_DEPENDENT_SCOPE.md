# Scope control for possible corrective-dynamics applications

Participating interpretation guard, not a new general theorem or a claim about an implemented DAEE process.

The stationary-law results concern one fixed selected linear stochastic operator P. They do not establish contraction for a state-dependent selection P(p), even if every row of P(p) lies in the same cap envelope for every p.

For example, let c=(3/4,3/4), so Kc contains the Bernoulli interval [1/4,3/4] and D(c)=1/2. For p∈Kc choose every row of P(p) equal to p. Then each individual selected kernel is cap dominated and has a unique fixed point; nevertheless the nonlinear update F(p)=pP(p)=p fixes every member of Kc. The dependence of the selection on p is the missing hypothesis, not a counterexample to the proved fixed-operator theorem. This example is an application-scope warning, not a new programme of state-dependent policy optimisation.

Stability and computability alone do not establish truth, proper function, epistemic basing or source authority. No empirical or noetic identification of the Markov model has been established here.
