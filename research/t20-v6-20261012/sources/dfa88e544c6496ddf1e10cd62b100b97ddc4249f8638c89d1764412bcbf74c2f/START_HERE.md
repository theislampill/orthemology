## Verify integrity with Python standard library

After extracting the ZIP, change into this top-level folder and run:

    python3 tools/verify_supplement.py .

To verify the ZIP's CRC, safely extract to a temporary directory and verify every extracted member hash:

    python3 tools/verify_supplement.py ../Orthemology_T20_Closure_Audit_and_Research_Supplement_v1_20261007.zip

MANIFEST.json covers all payload files except itself and SHA256SUMS. SHA256SUMS additionally binds MANIFEST.json; the separately supplied whole-ZIP digest binds the entire archive. These are transport/identity checks, not proof of the research claims.
