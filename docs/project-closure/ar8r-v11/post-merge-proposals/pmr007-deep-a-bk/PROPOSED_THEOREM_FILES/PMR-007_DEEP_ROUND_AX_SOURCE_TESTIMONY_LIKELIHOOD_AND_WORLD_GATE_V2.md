## 2. STWG-1A — exact joint source-evidence update

For any observation `y` with `P_R(y)>0`, the posterior odds are

\[
O_y=O_0\frac{P_A(y)}{P_R(y)}.
\]

Thus the realized source observation changes the A/R odds iff its joint
likelihood ratio is not one.  If `P_A=P_R` on the complete source observation
space, no candidate-independent source observation updates the odds.

When `P_R(y)=0<P_A(y)`, the extended likelihood ratio is `+∞`; when
`P_A(y)=0<P_R(y)`, it is `0`.  Outcomes impossible under both candidates are
not conditioning events.

This is conditional Bayesian evidence within the declared candidate and
likelihood model.  It is not actual-world truth.

## 3. STWG-1B — conditional root factorization

Suppose the complete observation has authenticated root coordinates

\[
Y=(Y_g)_{g\in G}
\]

and the roots are conditionally independent under each architecture:

\[
P_H(y)=\prod_{g\in G}P^H_g(y_g),\qquad H\in\{A,R\}.
\]

Then

\[
\frac{P_A(y)}{P_R(y)}
=
\prod_{g\in G}
\frac{P^A_g(y_g)}{P^R_g(y_g)}.
\]

Without conditional factorization, the joint ratio controls and the product of
marginal ratios may overcount, undercount, or reverse the evidence.

## 4. STWG-1C — copying and translation

### Copying

A deterministic tuple of copies, paraphrases, or repeated presentations derived
from one root is a function of one root observation.  Treating those outputs as
separate independent roots is invalid.  Exact duplication preserves the one
root likelihood ratio and does not exponentiate it.

### Translation or representation channel

For a common candidate-independent stochastic channel `K(z|y)`, the
pushforward source distributions are

\[
(K_*P_H)(z)=\sum_y K(z\mid y)P_H(y).
\]

Total variation satisfies

\[
TV(K_*P_A,K_*P_R)\le TV(P_A,P_R).
\]

The channel may preserve or erase architecture discrimination; it cannot create
distributional discrimination absent from the raw source experiment.  This is
a distribution-level statement.  No monotonicity of every realized pointwise
likelihood ratio is claimed.

