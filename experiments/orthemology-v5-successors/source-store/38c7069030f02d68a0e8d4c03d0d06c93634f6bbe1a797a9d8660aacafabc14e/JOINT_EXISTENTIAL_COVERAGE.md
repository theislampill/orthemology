# Productive coverage, antecedent availability, and a necessary-resource route

Tenth research candidate, 3 October 2026. This continues the accepted kind-priority and transfer-warrant investigations. It does not amend them or the accepted Ninth boundary. No actual necessary participant, P1/P2, N2/N3, or defeat of every active infinitist account is claimed.

## Finding first

The strongest wholly internal account has genuine explanatory content: every actual recipient is produced by an actual agent exercising a real received power. The remaining question is not whether its links are switched on. It is whether that entire actual history explains the existential availability employed in all of those productions.

Two concrete controls rule out an important family of affirmative shortcuts. First, flattening contribution-specific dependence into dependence of an entire explanatory package fabricates a circle even in a finite history where a necessary bearer contingently produces one agent, which produces another. Second, a plural-priority rule based only on local productive arrows also rejects an infinite subordinate history independently sustained by a necessary bearer. Neither control certifies a rootless cosmos. Both show what a principle adequate to this programme must preserve.

There is nevertheless a positive, narrowly warranted preservation principle: extending a fixed productive route downstream does not erase the antecedent existential availability on which that route operates. The principled challenge is that completing a history upstream is a different operation. It can supply an input previously taken as background. The finite mixed history makes that difference intelligible without relying on a toy interpretation of infinity.

A separate conditional strategy can avoid adjudicating every whole-history account. One ancestry-qualified possible outside supplier, together with a genuinely source-preserving realization grounded in actual powers, can require an actual necessary concrete realization resource. A further supplying-power condition on the selected resource yields a necessary capacity bearer. Neither requires actual exercise. The companion argument identifies the exact positive target, coverage domains, positive support trace and transport conditions. The qualified-achievement proof technique is inherited from Ninth F1; the smaller target and new typed transport controls are the present extension. Those premises have not all been warranted in the present record.

## 1. The actual target and three distinct relations

Fix the actual family Q of covering kinds. F is the condition of contingently existing with the relevant Q-membership. The existential proposition p says that some F exists. Actual coverage places every actual contingent concrete causal candidate in F. No new family is substituted at another history. Actual membership, membership in an alternative realization, and necessary existence of a bearer remain distinct.

The actual history H contains concrete source powers, their exercises and their productive relations. It is richer than an inventory of existing bearers. It is not merely a set of uninstantiated laws. Grant that the source for each actual contingent recipient is effective, and that no source individually creates the very existence required for its own act. No first event is assumed.

Three relations are relevant:

1. A bearer's existential availability is required for its exercise. Depending on the ontology, this may be a constitutive or existential dependence claim; it is not merely causal production of a later outcome.
2. The exercise produces the recipient. This is an actual productive relation. Its reality is not cancelled by the source's received power.
3. An obtaining witness can ground the truth of an existential proposition. This concerns how the proposition obtains, rather than automatically supplying its witness's existential availability.

A common basis may support both an act and p without p itself grounding that act. Likewise, successful causation may entail its effect without illicit presupposition. These accepted constraints remain in force. The present argument tests ways of combining the relations, rather than treating their names as interchangeable.

## 3. The finite mixed-history control

<!-- SOURCE_ASSOCIATED_SETUP ELGIN_RATIONALIST_CONTINGENCY START -->
Let a necessarily existing bearer N contingently exercise a power to produce b. Then b exercises a real power to produce c. The joint account contains both acts and explains the existence of b and c. But b's existence is an antecedent requirement of one act in that account. If that requirement is lifted indiscriminately to the whole account, and the whole account is then credited as explaining b in every one of its parts, a spurious self-dependence appears.
<!-- SOURCE_ASSOCIATED_SETUP ELGIN_RATIONALIST_CONTINGENCY END -->

The correct allocation is exact. N's act supplies b; b's act supplies c. b is required for the second contribution, not for the contribution by which b itself is supplied. A description containing both contributions is not one undifferentiated productive event. Its explanation of b does not use every fact needed for its explanation of c.

This is a substantive control on proposed mixed-relation principles. It rules out the following unrestricted inference: because a target witness is required somewhere in H, and H explains the target witnesses, that witness explains itself. It also rules out treating mere membership in an explanans package as relevance to every explanandum covered by that package.

The control is compatible with real joint causes. If several agents genuinely cooperate in one production, their cooperation can be relevant to that production. What cannot be assumed is that every item in a larger account has that role. The requisite question is which actual contribution supplies which existential availability, including the availability of any genuine cooperative group.

Nothing in this control requires N's exercise to be necessary. The actuality of the exercise can be contingent while N's existence is necessary. An objection that inventories every contingent act together with contingent bearers has changed the target. It must repeat the argument for that new inventory rather than silently applying this bearer-level test.

The infinitary account receives a limited benefit. When contribution-specific relations are retained, adding each act's dependence on its own bearer does not turn an indefinitely upstream productive history into a finite same-witness cycle. That removes one proposed derivation of collective exclusion. It does not show that the history supplies the whole existential field, or that every question about its actuality has an answer.

## 4. Why local plural priority is too broad

Consider a genuinely active beginningless subordinate history, with every recipient produced through the next upstream received agent. For the control, also grant a necessary bearer whose contingent exercise independently sustains the entire subordinate history. There is no first subordinate event, and the local agents remain genuine productive contributors.

Partition the subordinate history into alternating groups. Independent support preserves each local arrow. A rule inferring forbidden collective circularity solely from those arrows still rejects the supported history; it cannot distinguish received-only availability from availability independently supplied.
<!-- SOURCE_ASSOCIATED_SETUP KOONS_OPPY_2020 END -->

This is not a general refutation of causal finitism. An argument may openly reject every such infinite subordinate history, but its additional anti-infinity commitment must then be defended. It cannot present the result as merely extracting original possession while preserving the programme's allowance for independently supported infinite ancestry.

A restricted affirmative repair must instead track exhaustive relevant availability-dependence. It must ask whether the proposed group gets all of its relevant existential supply from the other group, not merely whether some genuine local productions run between them. Including a necessary original contributor can change the former while leaving the latter untouched. This is a real ontological difference, not an exemption based on a preferred name.

The repair has affirmative force: one should not infer a wholly internally supplied field when a genuinely independent resource is present. But the converse is the disputed whole-history judgement. To show that an account has no independent contributor is not yet to show, by ordinary plural causation alone, that every local supply remains globally unexplained.

## 5. The strongest positive principle, precisely bounded

Here is a principle supported by the actual character of productive channels:

**Fixed-antecedent preservation.** If the existential availability of a concrete F bearer is a relevant antecedent of a specified productive route, adding further products downstream within that route does not supply that antecedent availability through those later products.

Its warrant is not a prohibition on factual explanations or a demand for a temporally first cause. The proposed later production already employs the specified antecedent. Its success can create a numerically new recipient with new powers; that success is not the source of the giver's availability for that same route. Overdetermination by another route does not reverse this route's direction.

For finite extensions this is a genuine rationally warranted judgement, not merely a coherent stipulation. Where an infinite route independently retains the same antecedent resource and its explanatory role, describing further downstream stages also does not erase it. The conclusion concerns the retained input; it is not a theorem that every infinite account has such an input.

The strongest objection identifies a different operation. An account can expand upstream by supplying something previously treated as an input. In the finite control, adding N's act to the explanation of c supplies b's availability. The larger account is not simply the old route with more effects appended. Its relevant explanatory boundary has changed.

An active infinitist proposes to perform that kind of upstream discharge for every actual contingent witness. Its entire history is not obtained merely by adding downstream products while retaining one fixed unexplained bearer. Therefore fixed-antecedent preservation cannot be applied by treating a changing sequence of inputs as one persistent input. At every bounded stage there may be an F input outside that stage, without one and the same input remaining outside the completed account.

This does not vindicate the completed account. It shows what its strongest affirmative critic must address. The critic needs a reason why exhaustive upstream discharge still fails to account for the availability of any F, despite the reality and scope of every local productive contribution. The familiar local reason survives intact, but its antecedent is not automatically present in the whole-history proposal.

## 6. Is the remaining availability question legitimate?

Yes. No extra collective object has to be posited in order to ask whether the complete account obtains all of its productive availability only through the very field whose existence it is meant to explain. A necessary or otherwise independently available source can provide a real explanatory advantage at this respect. Its powers are available without requiring another instance of the target class to furnish that availability.

The original-possession proposal consequently retains genuine prima-facie support. It is not defeated by describing an internally active order, and it need not await a neutral algorithm or a proof that every rival metaphysics is impossible. The insight concerns explanatory contribution and direction, not numerical finitude.

But legitimacy of that question does not settle what the full internal history contributes to it. The rival can maintain that actual productive origins cover every obtaining witness, without treating the whole field as an existential input prior to all its members. This reply is substantive because it contests the occurrence of the very antecedent presupposition on which the affirmative norm operates. It is more than an appeal to an infinite diagram.

The reply remains incomplete as positive metaphysics. It needs an account of why this contribution structure is adequate to the specified existential question. The union of true local stories cannot certify that simply by being called the whole explanation. Conversely, their inability to produce an independent original-possession item by definition does not show that they fail to explain p at every defensible adequacy standard.

The difference from a law-only completion is important. The null-preservation test in the accepted companion paper diagnoses what purely receiving laws determine. Here the actual history contains actual productive acts. The present controls neither erase those facts nor infer a possible null world from an algebraic assignment. A claim that the actual history has more content is correct; a claim that this additional content is adequate global supply is the proposition under assessment.

## 7. A possible route around whole-history exclusion

The companion ACTUAL_POWER_ROUTE.md gives a two-tier conditional argument without first rejecting every internal history. Its modal target positively includes one concrete outside-F supplier, one F recipient, and independence of the supplier's relevant availability from F ancestry. Here F-ancestry means the full relevant existential-support history, including a relevant earlier stage of the same bearer retained through persistence. A contingent outsider in that possible history is allowed; the target does not presuppose that its possible supplier is necessary.

If such a target is actual, coverage of actual contingent capacity bearers identifies its positively specified outside-F supplier as necessary. If it is merely possible, choose the possible realization supplied by a sufficiently strong actual-power theory. Either the supplier is already an actual resource, or a positive supply trace to its existential availability is anchored in an actual concrete entry resource. Retaining F-instantiation at that relevant source occurrence would contradict the qualified achievement. The selected actual resource is therefore outside F.

The conclusion's type now matters. If actual coverage extends to every contingent concrete member of the relevant resource base, the selected resource necessarily exists. That broader coverage is an added premise if the inherited coverage concerned only capacity bearers. If the selected resource is also an actual bearer of a supplying power, the same argument yields a necessary capacity bearer; that stronger version can use capacity-bearer coverage directly. A necessary concrete background resource must not be relabelled as active efficacy merely because it belongs to a sufficient basis.

The positive content is fixed-route antecedent preservation, applied to a qualified achievement. The anchor must supply the supplier's availability, not merely be relevant to a trigger, recipient or the achievement as an undifferentiated package. Its relevance on the selected possible route suffices; it need not be indispensable across all possible routes. The scope is easiest to warrant for finite supply depth, although neither a finite number of events nor a first event in the whole universe is imposed. A larger infinite realization needs its positive connection to actual entry resources independently established. Treating an arbitrary internally closed history as a realizing route with no verified entry would simply reintroduce the original dispute.

The controls are exact. The actual bearer can retain identity while changing a nonessential covering classification in an alternative history. An F agent can occasion an already available supplier's exercise without supplying that supplier's existential availability. A merely possible auxiliary supplier cannot be counted as a freely available actual resource. And producing a recipient is weaker than producing the whole ancestry-qualified achievement. These are limitations on the proposed inference, not metaphysically certified rival worlds.

The conditional strategy retains the smaller existential target: no complete actual inventory, exact counts, unique source or actual exercise is required. Its qualified-achievement proof technique is inherited from Ninth F1. It changes the target and sought conclusion, and introduces classification transport and resource-domain alignment as new burdens. No equivalence between the whole realization package and the bare existence of a necessary being is asserted. This is a potential narrowing of the affirmative route, not a second claim that the original global exclusion has been established.

## 8. Terminal judgement of this investigation

The entire actual received history cannot be condemned by indiscriminate explanatory packaging, mixed-relation transitivity, or local parity arrows. The finite mixed control and independently supported infinite control give contentful reasons for that conclusion.

The history also does not acquire an E_A certificate from memberwise actuality. The original-possession comparison retains positive rational force, and the fixed-antecedent norm is warranted at its proper scope. Exhaustive upstream discharge is a different operation; its adequacy at the existential-supply respect remains unresolved here.

The strongest newly isolated positive route is conditional necessary-resource extraction from a qualified possible supply and a positively anchored, source-preserving realization. A supplying-power condition strengthens the result to a necessary capacity bearer. Neither demands an actually exercised or unique original source. The full modal target, coverage and realization premises are not presently independently established, so no necessary particular has yet been earned about actuality.

This is a substantive open result with rejected derivations, a warranted bounded principle, and a more precise affirmative alternative. It is not an assertion that the rivals are equally good, that basic rational warrant is unavailable, or that a necessary-source belief is irrational. Further work should confront the contribution structure or supply a genuinely warranted qualified modal realization; another schematic infinite graph would add no relevant evidence.
