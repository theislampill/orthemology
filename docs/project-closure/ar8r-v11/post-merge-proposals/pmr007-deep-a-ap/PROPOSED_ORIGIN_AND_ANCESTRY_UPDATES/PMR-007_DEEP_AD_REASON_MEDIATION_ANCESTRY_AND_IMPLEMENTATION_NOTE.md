# PMR-007 Deep AD — ancestry, source, and implementation note

## Formal ancestry

The nuisance-fibre theorem is a direct instance of the recurring target-
factorization family and the present T366 semantic reconstruction.  Deep AD
adds no new abstract factorization theorem.  Its central contribution is the
composition of three previously separate burdens:

```text
semantic-anchor validity from Deep Y;
intervention-sensitive causal architecture from Deep AA;
fittingness conformity from Deep AB.
```

The resulting `R_MED_SYS` contract is stronger than any one of those inputs but
remains system-level and profile-relative.

## OSM, PRH, and implementation boundary

The Sun et al. OSM paper reports progressive hippocampal decorrelation,
orthogonalized task-state representations, and a CSCG model that best matched
both the endpoint organization and the observed learning trajectory among the
models tested.  These are empirical and computational representation results.
They do not identify an independently intervenable semantic-reason variable,
truth-linked target authority, first-person uptake, or Wisdom.

The PRH paper studies cross-model and cross-modal kernel alignment and proposes
a shared-statistical-model interpretation while retaining information-loss,
modality, metric, and convergence limitations.  Alignment is not the
intervention criterion in Deep AD.

Current daee/OSM crosswalks may name source, target, route, and state variables,
but a runtime corresponds to `R_MED_SYS` only after a separate audit validates:

```text
R semantics;
P nuisance status;
intervention correspondence;
policy stability;
history sufficiency;
and outcome/target authority.
```
