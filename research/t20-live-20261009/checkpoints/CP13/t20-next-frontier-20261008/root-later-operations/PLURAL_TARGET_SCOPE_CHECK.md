# Root countercheck: plural grounding and the bearer interface

2026-10-08, 23:10 UTC. Prospective interpretive investigation; no new theorem credit.

Reading Dasgupta 2011 §§10–12 and notes68–74 identifies an explicit irreducibly plural grounding proposal. A joint grounding claim does not, in that proposal, distribute into separate qualitative grounds for each individualistic fact. Necessitation of each fact does not turn into grounding of each fact.

However, the retained OriginalBearerBridge.lean must be confronted at its strongest scope. `T` is an unrestricted target type. The existential bearer conclusion uses one target supplied by `FullPremises.occurrence`, not a proof that every individualistic fact separately has an antecedent qualitative ground. A target can, at the level of the signature, represent a whole field or plurality. Consequently blocking a singleton projection is not yet a refutation of every affirmative route through Completion.

If the whole plurality qualifies as a received target, and Completion is independently warranted for it in the declared concrete-resource sense, the existing inference can use that target without distributing qualitative grounding to its individual members. This is an interpretive possibility, not a warrant that the plurality really has whole-existence reception, is adequately represented by one T, or possesses an original resource. The abstract signature does not settle these questions.

Conversely the mere presence of an alternative joint qualitative ground does not prove the nonexistence of concrete productive resources. No false Completion counterexample follows from such coexistence without additional incompatibility or resource-absence evidence.

The source researcher has been asked to retain both the failed singleton projection and this whole-target response. A philosophical critique must locate which actual bridge it contests; it cannot present an optional distributivity premise as indispensable to the existential conclusion.
