# Independent review of joint existential coverage

3 October 2026 UTC. Fresh substantive review of `candidate-v1`, whose seven research/metadata files and manifest are bound in `REVIEW_INPUTS_v1.sha256`. This is internal philosophical assessment. It establishes no actual metaphysical possibility, necessary being, external specialist validation, or programme closure.

## Disposition

**Accept the bounded substance of JC10-1 through JC10-4 and JC10-6. Require a scope and typing revision before accepting JC10-5 as stated.**

The main paper correctly preserves contribution-specific supply, the distinction between upstream discharge and downstream extension, and the affirmative force of fixed-antecedent preservation. The supported-history test discriminates against an arrows-only exclusion without claiming that an independently supported infinite history is metaphysically possible. The whole-history adequacy question remains open for substantive reasons, rather than because every metaphysical claim needs an external certificate.

The proposed capacity route has a valid and useful core, but its complete actual resource base includes concrete background. Its actual coverage premise explicitly covers capacity bearers. Those are not yet the same domain. The stated reductio passes from coverage of contingent capacity bearers to coverage of every concrete resource, and its conclusion then passes from a necessary resource to a necessary capacity bearer. Both passages require an explicit premise or a narrower conclusion. A second precision is needed in the source trace: relevance to the whole qualified achievement must not be substituted for relevance to the supplier's existential availability.

A sound repair retains two levels. With coverage of the whole relevant concrete resource base and a positively anchored supply trace, the conditional yields an actual necessary concrete resource. A further active-bearer condition on the trace yields an actual necessary capacity bearer. Neither conclusion requires actual exercise, and neither is established about actuality until the substantive possibility and realization premises are warranted.

The phrase “actual path of causal realization” in the capacity paper §2 also needs correction. In this argument the path is a possible realization grounded in actually possessed powers. Calling the path actual risks upgrading a capacity result to an exercised-production result which the paper otherwise carefully withholds.

## 2 Contribution routing and the finite control

The main paper §3 control is adequate for its assigned inferential job. A prerequisite attached to one contribution does not automatically become a prerequisite of every contribution in the explanatory package. Conversely, an explanans package's covering multiple targets does not make every contained fact relevant to each target. The proposed unrestricted lifting-and-redistribution inference fails at those two transitions.

This criticism does not depend on denying ordinary explanatory composition. One can form a genuinely adequate combined account and retain an exact restriction of that account to the supplying branch for a particular target. The objection is to erasing the restriction while using package membership as if it encoded productive relevance. There is no reason for ordinary joint production to fail merely because a larger record contains a dependent later act.

The control also respects the bearer/act distinction. An account can employ a contingent exercise whose bearer is necessary. The exercise's contingency does not put its bearer in the contingent-bearer inventory. If an opponent instead inventories every contingent event or fact, the explanandum and coverage premise change. The present control does not purport to settle that different argument, but it prevents that change from being made silently.

There is a useful asymmetry in the evidential force. The control can refute a purportedly universal inference under its own expressly permitted productive grants. It is not thereby an independently established possible world, a proof that all finite source accounts are adequate, or a proof that an unbounded internal account succeeds. Its strength is the exact relevance error it isolates.

**JC10-1 disposition:** accept as a source-associated target application and inference criticism. Do not credit a new generic conjunction or explanatory-distribution technique.

## 3 The supported-history discriminator

The main paper §4 test preserves the local productive structure while varying independent support. A rule whose antecedent can inspect only the unchanged local structure cannot distinguish the two support conditions. That is enough to show its inadequacy as a formulation of the narrower claim that all relevant availability is received only within the subordinate field.

A defender has two legitimate responses. It can enrich its premise to refer to exhaustive relevant supply, or openly defend an anti-infinity thesis which rejects the supported case as well. The first is a substantive new dependence claim; the second is a stronger metaphysical commitment. Neither is supplied by inspecting the same local arrows again.

The word “independently” does real work in the control. It must mean independent in the existential-supply respect, not a second name for one member or a redundant description of the local producers. Given that declared grant, preserving the local arrows is compatible with altering complete availability support. The paper is correct to make the test conditional instead of treating the grant as a demonstrated metaphysical possibility.

Nor does the control establish that an infinitely supported history is impossible. A successful causal-finitist argument might have independent premises not tested here. The result targets what local plural arrows alone discriminate; it does not evaluate every argument against infinite productive ancestry.

**JC10-2 disposition:** accept at its exact discrimination scope, with prior generic ownership retained.

## 4 The positive preservation judgement

Fixed-antecedent preservation deserves positive rational credit. If a specified route genuinely uses a concrete resource for the very exercise that produces its downstream outcomes, adding those outcomes does not make that same use disappear from the route's provenance. This is a judgement about productive direction and the same occurrence. It does not require existence to behave like conserved material, a first cosmic event, or an exhaustive account of every possible causal ontology.

Relevance is essential. Merely locating an event earlier in time is insufficient. Likewise, an overdetermined effect can remain genuinely explained by a route without making each contributor counterfactually indispensable. The judgement should concern an independently established supplying role, not a universal but-for test.

The upstream/downstream distinction is correctly drawn. An expanded account can introduce a new contribution that explains an input of the old restricted account. That expansion changes the account's explanatory boundary; it is not only the addition of more consequences of the previously fixed input. This is why the local norm cannot be lifted by changing the input at each stage and then speaking as though one unchanged input survived outside the whole account.

Two different quantifier patterns must be separated. A restricted account may have some external prerequisite at every finite stage. It does not follow that one identical prerequisite remains external to the union. Conversely, the loss of a uniformly external witness does not prove that the union has adequate existential supply. These are distinct conclusions, and the paper preserves both distinctions.

Finite supply depth offers the transparent positive case: the same resource relevance can be traced through finitely many productive steps. Infinite width does not automatically defeat that argument if an actual member's relevant contribution is independently fixed. Infinite depth also is not disqualified merely for being infinite, but the connection to an actual entry resource then needs independent warrant. The numeral “infinite” neither supplies nor erases that connection.

**JC10-3 disposition:** accept. Its local positive norm is inherited in substance from the accepted kind/transfer work. The current contribution is its precise route/boundary application and its control against misidentifying upstream completion with downstream continuation.

## 5 The whole-history judgement

An actual active history has more content than uninstantiated receiving laws. The law-only null result therefore cannot condemn it by deleting actual productive acts from the explanans and assessing the remainder. At the same time, adding those acts does not automatically certify an adequate answer to the existential-supply question. It makes the whole-history adequacy claim a substantive proposal.

The paper permits a real explanatory comparison. Original possession can supply an intelligible advantage at a relevant possession or availability question. That advantage need not wait for every rival to be proved impossible. It also does not, without further application warrant, establish that every adequate account of the same existential proposition must meet precisely that independent-supply standard.

The rival is treated fairly in the strongest relevant sense: its members actually act, and it disputes whether the whole field is an antecedent resource in the way one channel's bearer is. That challenges the scope of the offered argument. It does not by mere description rebut a correctly targeted basic insight into collective nonpresupposition, and it is not a certified adequate metaphysics.

**JC10-4 disposition:** accept as a differentiated open-warrant result. It neither collapses the affirmative's prima-facie reason into mere permission to ask nor assigns equal credibility to completed rival theories that have not been supplied.

## 6 The exact capacity-route target

The positive target S is appropriately stronger than p. It requires a concrete supplier, a suitable recipient and the ancestry qualification. A negative statement that no input remains unexplained would not suffice: a proposed internally complete history could satisfy that negative description on its own disputed adequacy view. Including the positive supplier makes the actual-S branch nonvacuous and prevents substitution of that whole-history dispute for a witness.

The target still allows a contingent outsider relative to the fixed actual Q. It does not secretly start with possible necessity, an intrinsically unreceived unique supplier, or the reproduction of the full actual inventory. Thus it retains a genuine modal reduction. The supplier in one possible witness also need not be identical to the actual resource extracted from a realizing base.

The reduction has a price. Ordinary production by something outside Q does not establish independence of that thing's relevant existential ancestry from F. A source can change classification, or be produced through a target-class lineage, and later act outside the endpoint classification. The complete qualification must remain part of the possible achievement to which RP applies.

No modal witness is earned by merely writing S. A broad Q might leave no contingently existing productive outsider; a warranted possible outsider might still have F ancestry; and the conjunction of all positive and historical qualifications might require new modal support. The paper correctly leaves POS-S open.

The actual-S branch is otherwise sound. Its positive supplier is already an actual concrete power bearer outside F. Under the stated capacity-bearer coverage, its actual existence and noncontingency give necessary existence. This is a particular existential consequence, not necessity of role occupancy. The review's typing concern concerns the merely possible branch and the complete realizing resource base, not this direct actual branch.

## 7 The blocking resource-domain issue

Let R be the complete actual concrete resource base of the selected realization, and let B be its actual concrete bearers of productive powers. RP2 explicitly allows relevant concrete background among R. The candidate does not explicitly establish R = B. Let COV-B be the candidate's claim that every actual contingent concrete capacity bearer is F.

COV-B licenses the inference from “this member of B is contingent” to “this member is F.” It does not license that inference for an arbitrary member of R outside B. This remains so even if the background is important, causally relevant in some broad sense, or belongs to the sufficient basis of an actual power. Being part of a sufficient basis is not already possessing the power in question.

The candidate reductio begins with all concrete bearers of the relevant resources being contingent and says coverage makes them F. That step requires coverage of R, not just B. Its last line then concludes a necessary capacity bearer. Even if coverage of R were supplied, the immediate reductio would identify a necessary member of R. It would not identify a member of B without a further active-bearer premise.

There is a strong reading under which the intended argument works: every relevant positive supply trace originates in an actual bearer of a supplying power. On that reading the witness selected by RP5 belongs to B and COV-B applies directly. But that reading must be made explicit rather than smuggled in through the word “resource.” The current inclusion of concrete background makes a blanket reading of all resources as active bearers unsafe.

A typed inference control suffices to exhibit the issue. A domain can contain covered active bearers and separately typed actual background. Coverage of the former imposes no classification or modal condition on the latter. Likewise, selecting a non-F member of the union does not select an active bearer. This is a control on quantified domains, not a metaphysically possible background-only creation scenario. Nothing here asserts that passive material can really produce an independent supplier without a relevant power.

**Mandatory correction R1:** state which domain coverage quantifies over, which domain the trace witness belongs to, and which conclusion is licensed. Do not silently enlarge Q or its coverage after the reductio.

## 8 A second source-trace precision

RP3 concerns relevance to the qualified achievement, which includes both supplier availability and production of a recipient. A resource can be genuinely relevant to the latter without being relevant to the former. RP5 must therefore select a positive support trace specifically to the supplier's existential availability, not merely infer such a trace from the whole base's relevance to S.

The required trace must be anchored in at least one actual concrete entry resource, with the relevant occurrence retained through the realization. Otherwise an arbitrary infinite base can be called “the realizing base” while the very entry-resource connection needed by the proof is left unspecified. A merely aggregate support assertion must not be made to perform the illicit relevance redistribution criticized earlier in the main paper.

This does not require one entry resource to be indispensable across every possible realizing route. Choose the particular possible realizing route promised by RP. A retained, positive supply connection within that route is enough. Nor is mere temporal precedence enough, and nor is counterfactual necessity required for an independently established contribution.

**Mandatory correction R2:** make the anchored, achievement-specific supply trace explicit. For the stronger capacity-bearer result, the anchor must itself be an actual bearer of a supplying power; relevance to a trigger or recipient branch is not a substitute.

## 9 The valid two-tier repair

### Necessary concrete resource consequence

Assume fixed Q and F, possible S, the full realizing-resource conditions, and coverage COV-R of every actual contingent concrete member of the relevant realization base. Assume also that the selected possible realization has the positive anchored source trace just specified, with actual F-instantiation retained in the relevant whole history.

If S is actual, its supplier is already a positive actual outside-F resource. If S is merely possible, choose the realization supplied by RP. If the supplier is an actual available resource, the outside-F and whole-history qualification rule out retaining it as an actual F supplier at the relevant availability occurrence. Otherwise choose the actual resource anchoring the positive supply trace. Its F-instantiation, if present, would be retained in the ancestry of the supplier's existential availability and violate S. Thus some actual relevant concrete resource is outside F. COV-R makes that actual resource noncontingent, hence necessary.

This is a valid conditional necessary-particular result. It needs no actual exercise. COV-R is an additional scope obligation if the inherited coverage only concerned capacity bearers; it is not already established by the candidate's original COV-B.

### Necessary capacity bearer consequence

Add that the positively selected actual resource is itself a bearer of a supplying power. Alternatively, independently establish that the relevant actual resource domain consists of such bearers. Then the same witness is an actual necessary capacity bearer. In the actual-S branch its specified productive role already supplies that type. In the merely possible branch the added source condition must do so.

One can also present the strong argument directly using COV-B and an actual active anchor, without covering unrelated passive background. What matters is an explicit common domain for the selected witness, the coverage premise and the conclusion. The weaker and stronger theorems must not trade domains halfway through.

The strong consequence remains appreciable before actual exercise, source uniqueness, complete actual explanatory participation, P1/P2, N2/N3 or intrinsic self-explanation. Those later requirements are not admission conditions for crediting necessary concrete existence at the conditional level.

**JC10-5 disposition:** revision required for candidate-v1; the corrected two-tier conditional has a sound proof under the added explicit premises.

## 10 Transport and resource controls

Actual kind priority and identity preservation alone do not preserve classification in an alternative whole history. Keeping Q fixed fixes the family of predicates; it does not make every actual instantiation essential. A future transformation from an actual F stage differs from a different entire history in which that stage never occurred. RP4 may rule out the second as a realization from the specified actual resources, but its modal warrant is an added commitment.

A trigger can affect whether an available supplier exercises its power without supplying the supplier's existence. If the supplier is an actual outside-F power bearer, the necessary-bearer conclusion is already available under COV-B. If it is merely possible, counting it as background has not established an actual resource-complete realization. If an actual background object is invoked instead, the resource-domain correction above determines what coverage and conclusion follow.

The whole-history clause does not imply that a present agent can rewrite a completed past. RP must be a theory of possible qualified histories, not only future states, if S requires whole ancestry. Conversely, a criticism based only on the limited future powers of familiar present agents does not refute every timeless theory. The correct issue is whether the exact qualified history belongs to the proposed theory's justified domain.

**JC10-6 disposition:** accept, supplemented by the resource/bearer typing discriminator. The controls are inference tests and do not certify their alternative histories as metaphysically possible.

## 11 Does RP merely restate the global exclusion

The bounded repair is not the assertion that every adequate actual history contains an independently available original source. It concerns the realization of one specified possible S from actual resources. A source-preserving finite-depth realization can conflict with S's ancestry clause even if some unrelated actual beginningless internal history is left wholly unadjudicated. In that sense this is a genuine conditional bypass of universal whole-history exclusion.

There is nevertheless a substantial relocation risk. Once COV and S are fixed, a source-preserving generative realization of S cannot be supplied entirely by the covered class. Calling such realization “possible by actual powers” does not independently establish that the actual resources outside the class exist. An actual-power theory needs independent reason to cover this positive, historically qualified target, including its background resources and classification transport.

The strong RP package is not shown here to be logically equivalent to the bare conclusion that some necessary bearer exists. That conclusion alone does not guarantee that the bearer has the required powers, can realize S, supplies the relevant trace, or meets the resource and history conditions. Ninth F1's exact target-instance equivalence should not be copied as a theorem for this different target without proof.

What is inherited from F1 is the qualified-achievement test and the warning about coverage carrying the substantive force. The present conditional gains a smaller target and does not start from an identified actual original bearer. It introduces its own transport and resource obligations. An independently supported general realization theory could genuinely establish the needed instance; merely asserting that instance under a new name would not.

## Rational support must be graded rather than withheld wholesale

The open verdict does not rest on the absence of a demonstration. Several parts of the proposed package have different and positive epistemic standing.

First, fixed-route preservation is rationally warranted at the specified scope. Its reason is the intelligible role of the retained concrete input in that very production. A basic appreciation of that role can suffice. The finite-depth case gives transparent application, not a demand that all metaphysical knowledge take finite deductive form.

Second, resource completeness and achievement-specific relevance are warranted adequacy constraints on a claim to realize the entire S. If a promised realization produces only the recipient while borrowing the independent supplier, it has not done the whole advertised job. If the supplier is merely possible, its availability cannot count as an actually supplied resource merely because it occurs in the target description. These criticisms have affirmative content: they identify what a sufficient realizability claim must include. They do not establish that any actual basis meeting the conditions exists.

Third, whole-history retention has rational force within genuinely source-preserving realizations. A subsequent stage cannot erase an earlier relevant supplying occurrence merely by changing the endpoint's description. The further claim that every relevant absolute possibility has such a realization is different. Familiar future-directed power cases give motivation for treating actual resources as grounds of some possibilities; they do not by themselves establish the reach of that rationale over alternative complete ancestries. The candidate's present limitation is this exact application gap, not the general illegitimacy of a powers-based modal theory.

Fourth, the smaller positive target improves assessability. It need not reproduce all actual individuals, counts or origins; its supplier and recipient make the intended accomplishment intelligible. Those are genuine advantages. But they are not, by themselves, new evidence that the supplier can be outside this comprehensive fixed Q with the specified ancestry. Restricted classes can have familiar outsiders, while the present coverage assumption may exclude every actual contingent concrete candidate. Transferring plausibility from an ordinary restricted-class outsider to the comprehensive target requires a reason. The added ancestry clause is also stronger than ordinary outside-kind production, so the new target is not uniformly weaker in every modal respect than every predecessor target.

The inherited original-possession comparison still supplies a prima-facie rational reason to seek an independently available basis. It can make this target a motivated proposal rather than an arbitrary stipulation. The candidate contributes a sharper discriminator of that proposal; it does not supply a new independent presentation of POS-S or of unrestricted RP coverage. These claims could receive basic or defeasible support without a neutral algorithm, external certification or a proof excluding every rival. What is missing from this record is the positive reason with this exact target and scope, not certainty as such.

Accordingly the route should receive more credit than bare syntactic validity: it has a warranted local preservation principle, warranted resource/relevance constraints and a rationally motivated reduced target. It should receive less credit than a newly warranted actual necessary particular, because the conjunction of its conclusion-bearing modal and ancestry commitments is not independently supported here. Neither promotion nor negative dismissal is justified merely by naming those commitments.
