# Actual original efficacy: a source-grounded warrant audit

8 October 2026 UTC. Research continuation for T20; no final integration, owner acceptance, or global exhaustion claim. This assessment concerns the substantive bridge from an unreceived bearer, power, and willing to exhaustive actual original production. It does not repeat the separate numerical-identity or peer-valued-end model work.

## Result

The source supplies a serious argument for unborrowed actual efficacy, not just a stipulative use of “original.” Its best reason is that the exercised efficacy of a self-sufficient necessary agent cannot have its actual adequacy supplied by an independent original. Unreceived possession of a command, accurate knowledge, or a standing solo capacity does not establish that stronger perfection. Created means are different when their relevant productive basis is originally supplied by that same agent.

The universal application to the strongest coalescent rival remains unestablished. Two transitions require assessment: (1) from genuine joint actual production to each participant's lack of individually sufficient productive efficacy, and (2) from that lack in the product to external completion of the participant's own action or perfection. These are explicit, argued source inferences. They are not consequences of an empty efficient-receipt graph, nor does absence of such receipt refute them.

The new source operation was a continuous comparison of Minhaj 2:157–184, especially the pre-existing necessary-being argument at 161–164, with the actual-efficacy application at 180–182 and Majmu 20:174–184. It improves the scope of the warrant rather than overturning the previous qualified judgement. No new metaphysical countermodel or universal exclusion proof is claimed.

## 2. What “within the thing itself” does and does not establish

At Minhaj2:162 the author says, “كمال الشيء هو من نفس الشيء وداخل فيه”. This is his affirmative explanation, not a philosopher's proposition quoted for refutation. The surrounding argument concerns the necessary being and the perfections that belong to it; the next sections disentangle meanings of composition, part, otherness, and dependence. The account accommodates either a partless bearer or a bearer with inseparable attributes. It is not a generic composition prohibition.

The contrast is between a perfection belonging to the bearer and an external item merely conjoined to it. “Within” must not be read spatially. It does not show that a relationally specified actual efficacy cannot be a perfection of its subject. Nor does it require a newly deposited intrinsic entity whenever an agent's efficacy is externally completed. Such readings would contradict the intended application at180 rather than interpret it.

The useful constraint is narrower: an external product does not become the agent's constitutive perfection just because the product exists, is valued, or includes the agent in its provenance. The argument still needs to identify the relevant efficacy of the agent. Conversely, identifying efficacy through a relation to an external product does not disqualify it from being the agent's perfection. The issue is what constitutes the agent's actual operative adequacy.

The broad electronic sentence before this explanation, section151 L008, has an awkward connective in the retained edition. No new inference here depends on repairing it. The clear L009–016 argument controls the reading.

## 3. The source's strongest bridge, reconstructed charitably

### 3.1 Independent motivation before cooperation

Minhaj151 L007–016 argues that an eligible pure perfection cannot be received from outside by a being necessary through itself. A necessary bearer includes its constitutive attributes; dependence of a constitutive requisite on an external producer would make the bearer dependent in that respect. Reciprocal donation cannot solve this when each donor's capacity to donate is the very thing still awaiting completion. Section156 L017–024 distinguishes an uncaused attribute's inherence from efficient production and permits concomitance while rejecting that prior explanatory circle.

This is genuine independent motivation for self-sufficiency. It is stronger than defining the necessary being as whatever has no incoming edge in a graph. It does not yet identify every nonexclusive exercise as deficient.

### 3.2 Why the external result bears on the agent

The celestial example at156 L040–041 is not merely about intrinsic powers. Its efficacy requires bodies, connections, and motions that it has not itself provided. At180, “فتأثيره من كماله” supplies the key evaluation: being efficacious is superior to being inefficacious. The source therefore treats actual effective agency as a perfection and infers dependency where that efficacy is completed by independent conditions.

Majmu2032 L013 makes the transfer even more explicit: dependence in effects implies dependence in the agent's inhering act and attribute, because the effect proceeds from them. This is the strongest textual answer to a rival that only denies a new donation of power, knowledge, or will.

The comparison “efficacious rather than inefficacious” establishes the worth of efficacy; it does not by itself rank two efficacious arrangements or prove that nonexclusive attribution is a privation. The latter work is performed by the no-foreign-completion argument once the relevant dependence has been established. This is why that comparison is substantial support without being the whole proof.

The positive philosophical reason is persuasive in ordinary complementary cases. If A's undertaken production would lack its actual requisite efficacy unless an independently original B supplies it, calling A's intrinsic intention complete does not supply that missing efficacy. Choosing the partnership willingly does not change where the requisite productive adequacy comes from. Neither simultaneous timing nor correct foreknowledge removes the requirement.

### 3.3 Why the source thinks all genuine cooperation falls under that case

Majmu2032 L002 defends nonabsorption: add a genuinely operative contributor while the first contributor's productive state remains unchanged, and the complete product must manifest a difference. Otherwise the productive addition has been treated as its absence. L003 then classifies actual sharing as lack of individual sufficiency. L006–008 supports the same result through complete power plus decisive willing, including inseparable mixed production. L007 explicitly distinguishes this from conflicting-wills tamanuʿ.

Minhaj157 L003 likewise reasons from mutual assistance to the source of ability. The intended point is not simply that simultaneous equations cannot be solved; assistance is taken to supply part of what makes each agent actually able to perform the common work.

These passages establish that the source addresses harmonious actual cooperation. They also locate the premises doing the substantive work. Merely reciting effect-entailment from a complete cause does not independently establish nonabsorption or individual exhaustive attribution.

## 4. Dependency operation: which implication is earned?

This is an explicit proof-dependency audit, not a new executable world model. Keep four claims separate:

1. A's bearer, intrinsic powers, and particular willing are not efficiently supplied by a peer.
2. A possesses its relevant eligible perfections through itself.
3. A's actual exercise has all the productive adequacy appropriate to its undertaken result without completion from an independent original.
4. The determinate created event is exhaustively originally provided through A, including any created means.

Claim1 alone does not entail2. The source adds necessary existence and its independently defended perfection account; with those premises it argues to2. A thin efficient-root interpretation of “original” must not silently inherit those additional premises. It argues from2 to3 using efficacy-as-perfection and the effect-to-inhering-action transfer. It identifies the relevant completeness in3 strongly enough to reach4. Once4 means exhaustive original provision, two simultaneous exclusive entire attributions to distinct originals are incompatible in the same respect. That conditional contradiction is not in dispute.

There are two dangerous substitutions:

- Replace3 with the weaker claim that A has an intrinsic capacity, goal, or record whose outcome is successful in the ordinary context. That does not reach4. The fixed-volition addendum already explains why policy-domain success and frozen-record reuse cannot supply the missing source-level completeness predicate.
- Define1, or the general word “original,” by4. The desired application has then been imported into the classification. It is not new support for2→3.

The exact open step for the strongest rival is the application of2→3, together with the claim that any genuine co-production violates3. These are two aspects of the same productive-adequacy problem, not two extra administrative obligations. A valid positive resolution would explain why irreducible peer participation in the common result necessarily completes A's own efficacy. A valid negative resolution would explain how A's real efficacious activity is already adequately its own while that very result has a genuine additional original producer. A graph, label, or stipulated support set does not decide either explanation.

## 5. The strongest surviving rival and its limits

The serious rival concedes genuine joint original production. It does not claim two exclusive entire providers. It proposes independently possessed power, cognition, practical direction, and intrinsic activity, with one directly joint manifestation. It denies that the peer supplies any constituent of the other's intrinsic agency, while conceding that the actual common production includes both. Suppression tests can distinguish these formal descriptions but do not certify a metaphysically admissible comparison preserving the source's complete particular willing.

This rival challenges the application of the first transition above: nonexclusive actual provenance need not mean that A's own efficacy is deficient or received. A can lack exclusive authorship without lacking the specified source-neutral end or its own fulfilled activity. But absence of outcome dependence is weaker than a source-grounded explanation of real actual contribution. The rival still must make sense of genuinely productive co-instantiation without merely declaring B an actual cause or hiding a created mixer.

The best affirmative reply is that unborrowed perfection concerns efficacy actually exercised, not merely a repertoire of alternate solo performances. If the very productive adequacy of this undertaking is constituted partly by B, A has borrowed completion regardless of intrinsic nonreceipt. That reply has real explanatory force. It becomes question-begging only if the disputed “partly constituted by B” is inferred from joint provenance alone, with no independently justified account of productive adequacy or nonabsorption.

The best rival reply is not “all relationships are harmless.” It is that an actual joint productive relation can be a manifestation of two already efficacious powers without one completing the other's power or activity. This is an explicit denial of the source's stronger causal norm. It is not yet evidence that the proposed powers are metaphysically possible in necessary perfected bearers.

The 2:162 control prevents one shortcut against the rival: an external conjoined result is not automatically a constitutive perfection. The 2:180 application prevents the opposite shortcut: efficacious agency is not exempt from perfection reasoning merely because its description is relational. Read together, they demand the discriminating account rather than settling it by the word “intrinsic.”

## 6. Particular volition and perfection: no illicit universalisation

The necessary-being argument cannot be applied by treating every contingent particular act-token or external end as an eternally necessary constituent of the bearer. The inherited Dar/Safadiyya/Asbahani readings positively distinguish the essential genus of wise activity from each particular exercise and its fitting conditions. Their finding is preserved; it was not reverified as a new full-source reading here.

Accordingly, the affirmative norm is conditional: when the particular original undertaking is complete, its original productive adequacy is unborrowed. It does not require every possible product to exist, every end to be timelessly realised, or every particular intrinsic volition to be a created effect. The rival cannot defeat that conditional norm merely by pointing out that particular acts are optional. Equally, the source defender cannot infer a prior deficient nature from the novelty of an attained end.

Calling original self-provision a perfection rather than a capacity does not resolve these quantifiers. The desired conclusion concerns the adequacy of the chosen exercise; the antecedent necessary-being argument concerns the relevant perfections of the bearer. The source's actual-efficacy argument is the bridge between those scopes.

The explanation is substantially motivated by comprehensive productive self-sufficiency and the rejection of externally constituted agency. It is not merely a naked stipulation or a disagreement-only argument.

The inference from thin nonreceipt to exhaustive actual production is not established without the stronger efficacy norm. The continuous return did not reveal an additional independent derivation that removes that norm's disputed application to irreducible redundant co-production. It did reveal why neither a purely intrinsic-donation rebuttal nor an external-product-to-perfection shortcut is faithful to the argument.
