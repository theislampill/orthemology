The richer content is integrated into semantic evaluation and prediction, but the implementation does not establish that it explains why cooperation or the diagonal policy is chosen. 2. **Goal-directed co-production is weaker than general shared intention.** `joint_relation` checks actual q-directed acts and support. In arbitrary input runs it does not additionally require both agents to intend the relation, know the other's intention, or form a collective commitment. The ordinary fixture does supply richer ends for both agents, but these distinct concepts must not be merged in the general claimed result.

3. **Forecast equality is fixture-relative.** The forecast executes the disclosed ordinary policies with the known law before receiving actual execution. Equality in the three ordinary contexts is checked. This is a representation of policy-grounded factivity, not a derivation of omniscience, truth of the policy assumptions, or factivity across an intervention that violates them.

4. **The observation perturbation is intentionally synthetic.** It changes `evaluated_product` while preserving the base execution. It shows that choice generation is not filtered by the wrapper's final success predicate. It is not a causal-law countermodel or an admissible alternative metaphysical world; the module flags it accordingly.

5. **Receiving a richer end's satisfaction is not receiving a constitutive perfection by definition.** The failed peer-sensitive end under suppression shows the stated content is relation-dependent. Whether that end is eligible, wise, essential, or deficient for a perfected necessary agent requires an argument beyond satisfaction code. Conversely fulfilled joint activity and no incoming power/knowledge edges do not establish the source's complete original efficacy.

6. **Created peer and original peer are different comparisons.** Generic interaction can be represented with one original root and a real derived peer, but the inventory changes and a designated B-original end is not preserved. This control does not vindicate a reduction of the entire original rival or its exact will to one source. It blocks only the unqualified inference from a real-other relation to two original roots.
