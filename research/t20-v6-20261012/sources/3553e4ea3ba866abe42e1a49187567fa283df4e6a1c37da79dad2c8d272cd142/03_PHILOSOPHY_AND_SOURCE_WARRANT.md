## 2. Actuality, reception and explanation

## 3. Complete original efficacy and unity

The strong unity question is whether two distinct original peers can each retain complete, unreceived productive efficacy for the **same numerical undertaking**, including a concordant case. It is not exhausted by the possibility of opposed wills.

The research separates solo capability, response against a fixed peer and a uniform guarantee regardless of another's permitted action. Stronger harmonious models improve on weak examples but can still fail the intended complete-efficacy or non-absorption condition. Different permissibility/counterfactual domains change what a model actually tests.

A domain fixed independently by a policy can make a substantive success claim falsifiable. A domain selected after the fact to contain only successful cases can guarantee success by construction. That is a definition-dependency distinction, not a theorem that either domain is metaphysically admissible.

Complete original efficacy and non-absorption must receive reasons, not be packed into a definition that excludes the rival. Equally, a rival does not defeat the cumulative case by omitting the disputed bridge without addressing its supporting warrant. A thin satisfiable structure is not automatically a coherent possibility at the intended metaphysical meanings.

The source distinctions include original provision versus created means; intrinsic act versus an effect in another bearer; own attributes versus foreign received completion; and complete actual efficacy versus merely possessing a standing power. Actual productive efficacy can be a perfection without requiring acquisition of a new externally supplied intrinsic property.

Numerical act identity must not be replaced by equal values or strings. Two calculations can agree, a forecast can be correct, and a tree leaf can occur twice without those facts creating or identifying the same productive act. These controls may be mathematically elementary yet philosophically important at the right joint; they do not settle the entire causal ontology.

Ibn al-Qayyim's Shifāʾ al-ʿAlīl chapter 22 supplies a positive reply to simplistic acquisition-of-perfection objections: what is fitting at a particular time need not have been fitting earlier, and intrinsically wise activity need not receive essential completion from an external creature. This is not simply the claim that every perfection must be actualised in every respect at every instant.

CP23 develops a concrete wise-execution application from Majmūʿ 8:428–436: a foreknown approving response to an almsgiving solicitation can itself be the good sought. Knowing it in advance does not replace its actual occurrence. Once the response occurs, literal transfer is not needed to obtain that same response; the later instruction to retain the property can therefore be purpose-sensitive without God learning an answer or receiving wisdom.

The result removes one instrumental rationale. It does not prohibit all later collection, identify a unique possible ending, index a distinct divine micro-volition, or specify the entire history's unique determination. Its ingredients were inherited; the new credit is their worked application with independently identified end, response and changed directive.

## 5. Noetic warrant and fiṭrah

Fiṭrī recognition is not merely another explicit derivation from propositions. Foundational warrant need not be preceded by a proof of every premise. But a natural tendency, persistent conviction, agreeable outcome or reliable-looking behaviour is not automatically warranted cognition.

Proper function, reliability, truth-directedness, appropriate conditions and actual operative grounds are related but nonidentical. An observer can attach a correct argument to someone's true belief without showing that the person arrived at or sustains that belief through competent use of those grounds. The source programme on necessary truth sensitivity and operative factive grounds matters here.

The independent deep-dive's Plantinga D→D* comparison distinguishes persistence through properly functioning processes from the specifically truth-directed processes relevant to epistemic defeat. It is a comparison with one author's framework, not an identification of Plantinga with Ibn Taymiyyah or fiṭrah. Likewise, a model's self-audit cannot certify its own correctness or justify attributing human spiritual experience to itself.

DAEE can be used reflexively to ask whether an interpretation was source-faithful, an objection was fairly assessed, a burden was invented, or a framework's own rule needs correction. That is optional critical permission, not enforced theological agreement. The method itself remains an object of inquiry.

## 6. Testimony, source ownership and institutional continuation

Intending to communicate differs from intending to cause assent. Identical observable behaviour can be compatible with different intentions under a declared model. A discriminatory retry or matched experiment can distinguish selected plans only under its extra cost/belief assumptions. Hidden costs can restore non-identifiability.

An authentic source, a carrier of its words, a reliable witness and an authorised occurrence are separate. Existing source-veracity reasoning does not establish that an actual new communication was adopted by that source. Recipient uptake and warranted practical authority require their own bridges.

This also explains why reliable preservation is not institutional continuation. A successor may inherit records, labels and permissions on paper while the constitutive control or enacted authority lies elsewhere. The bounded constitutional models are useful, but their stipulated succession criteria are not a general metaphysical identity theorem. Preserve the organisational front even when the latest updates concentrate on sourcehood and proof compilation.
