## 1. Narrowed target

This round does **not** characterize proper function simpliciter. It isolates one
load-bearing coordinate:

```text
TL(y):
  response/function role y is truth-linked or world-fitting in the declared
  epistemic respect.
```

A complete proper-function account may additionally require function-fixing
history, proper operation, suitable environment, teleology, praiseworthy end,
defeater status, warrant, and source authority.

Deep X establishes that accepted radical target authority cannot be wholly
constituted by the defeated token closure. Deep W aligns valid records only over
a supplied full target key. Neither result establishes `TL`.

## 2. Declared reduct and exact symmetry condition

Let `Y` be finite. Let `E` be one declared account-relative reduct containing
**all** evidence relations the account uses at this stage: frequency,
causal/organizational role, selected history, design assignment, learned
objective, success surface, or whichever subset is actually asserted.

`Aut(E)` consists only of permutations of `Y` preserving every relation in that
reduct. A binary classifier `F:Y->{0,1}` is `E`-definable only if it is constant
on every `Aut(E)` orbit.

Let `TL:Y->{0,1}` be a separately typed truth/world-fit coordinate. It is not
silently included in `E`.

## 3. PFSA-1A — orbit-constancy characterization

There exists an `E`-definable exact classifier `F=TL` iff `TL` is constant on
every `Aut(E)` orbit.

**Proof.** Every reduct-definable classifier is orbit-constant. Thus variation
of `TL` within one orbit is an obstruction. Conversely, when `TL` is constant
on every orbit, assigning each orbit its common value gives an exact invariant
classifier.

This is a finite definability/symmetry theorem, not a metaphysical account of
truth.

## 4. PFSA-1B — truth-separating enrichment

If `TL` varies inside an `E` orbit, every exact classifier must use information
that refines the orbit so the enriched fibres are `TL`-constant. Call any such
information a **truth-separating enrichment**.

A **semantic anchor** is one interpreted enrichment whose referent,
semantic-contract, target-key authority, and world adequacy are independently
warranted. Therefore:

```text
truth-separating enrichment:
  necessary for exact TL classification relative to the reduct when an orbit
  collision exists;

semantic-anchor validity:
  not established by the orbit theorem;

KeyAuthority and WorldAdeq:
  separate Deep-X / Deep-V-W coordinates.
```

No unique kind of enrichment is selected by the theorem.

## 5. Account-specific discrimination

### Frequency, causal role, organizational role, learned objective, success

When those registered relations admit an automorphism that swaps a
truth-aligned role with a false-aligned role, they do not determine `TL`.
Exact task success can therefore coexist with a destructive or false installed
target.

### Selected effect

Selected-effect function fixes an effect through actual selection history. It
fixes the truth-linked epistemic coordinate only if the selected effect itself
is independently identified as truth-linked in the relevant respect. Selection
because of survival, reproduction, or another effect does not supply that link.

### Design

Design fixes a role through an intentional assignment. It fixes `TL` only if
the design plan is independently truth-aimed and normatively eligible.
Malicious or deceptive design is a countermodel to design simpliciter.

### Teleological, Plantinga-style, and fiṭrah-oriented accounts

These may include truth-aim, proper-operation, environment, design/source, or
normative-end relations. Those are precisely the added relations doing the
symmetry-breaking work. Their source truth, referent, applicability, and world
status remain separate burdens.

## 7. Candidate-G unification effect

A validated semantic anchor can create a genuine cross-profile restriction
between semantic and normative coordinates. This can satisfy Candidate G's
formal unification criteria more strongly than carrier boxing.

It still does not entail a personal realizer. A personal architecture and an
impersonal semantic-law architecture can share the same enriched neutral
reduct. Personality requires evidence or a relation not invariant under that
common reduct, such as intentional uptake, first-person ownership, or a
source-conditioned personal predicate.

## 8. Transcendental and proper-function effect

The following package remains insufficient for `TL`:

```text
process-relative underived modal order;
external actuality;
exact correction potential;
contract alignment;
scoped target-key authority;
endpoint or trajectory task success.
```

A truth-separating semantic/world relation remains a distinct bridge. Even
when `TL` is supplied, full proper function still needs the account-specific
history, operation, environment, teleology, defeat, and warrant conditions.
Neither `TL` nor target authority entails Wisdom or personality.

## 9. OSM/CSCG and representation boundary

Sun et al. report progressive differentiation of sensory-similar task states,
and in the tested comparison the CSCG most closely matched both selected final
organization and the learning trajectory. Latent labels and state-machine
nodes remain permutation-conventional; the paper does not identify a
world-semantic truth relation, a norm-authority source, or proper function.
Endpoint/trajectory evidence may refine `E`, but cannot by itself validate the
semantic anchor.

## 10. Track-N boundary

Within Track N, an authenticated source locus and correct referent
identification can supply a source-relative truth aim or fiṭrah function. This
is a source-expanded model class. The formal theorem neither authenticates the
source nor converts the source predicate into a neutral Track-T consequence.
