# Contextual testimony and bounded enacted continuation

T20 resumed research, 7 October 2026. AI-authored analysis with executed finite reference models and nine new Lean-checked declarations. This resumes the original fronts 3 and 4; it does not revise the immutable earlier memorandum, packages or their historical claims.

## Results first

1. A controlled compositional translation preserves an original contextual proposition and source force while changing the carrier, encoding and present speech act. Identical words interpreted with a new `I`, `you`, `now` or `here` can instead express a different proposition. The distinction is tested with separating truth valuations, not just different metadata.
2. A recipient can learn an old duty from authenticated, relevantly veracious source-owned assertion without a second jurisdiction proof. An old directive, a quotation, current authority, applicability and executable action remain separate. A prospective attestation route can own genuinely new content when an independently established rule covers the current occurrence and the actual successor occupies the bounded reporting office.
3. Historical evidence and an operational service have different minimal support families. In the executed binary service, an authentic archive can survive the loss of present operation or the ability to construct the next successor. The exact all-retractions criterion is: every minimal epistemic support contains an operational minimal support. This is a checked application of inherited finite monotone-support mathematics, not a newly discovered general theorem.
4. A constructed successor implements the declared service through two actual model migrations, with changed encoding and retired predecessors. A separately installed twin matches every tested input/output sequence yet has no predecessor-to-endpoint migration event. Intervening on the predecessor's state changes the actual successor but not the fixed independent twin.
5. These results do not equate operational resilience with organisational identity. For a deliberately constituted service office, a separately declared chain-of-appointment convention licenses a narrow institutional continuation judgement. A legitimate but temporarily unavailable successor can retain that office even while its service contract fails. The same convention is not earned for arbitrary real organisations.

## 2. Occurrence semantics instead of word identity

An original occurrence has event identity, speaker, addressee, time, place, domain, force and a formula. The formula language has location and duty atoms, negation and conjunction. Its context resolves indexicals. For example:

- At(I, here, now) in (Principal, Operator, 0, lab) denotes At(Principal, lab, 0).
- Duty(you, inspect, now, 2) denotes Duty(Operator, inspect, [0,2)).
- The same duty formula at time 1 denotes the different interval [1,3).

Encoding A uses positional constructors. Encoding B uses differently named, rearranged constructors. Their translation is structural. In Python these have different JSON surfaces; in Lean they are distinct inductive types. `compositional_contextual_translation` proves that the abstract translation preserves the resolved formula at the supplied original context, by induction over the formula. `relay_preserves_source_act` preserves source occurrence, source force and contextual proposition while the new carrier occurrence is a quotation.

The parser is not itself a formally verified compiler: the Lean theorem concerns the declared inductive encodings; Python JSON roundtrips are finite execution evidence. Python also treats `here`; the compact Lean fragment formalises person/time indexicals with an explicit place parameter. The two scopes are not silently identified.

Proposition equality is syntactic equality of resolved formulas in this controlled semantics, not a claim of complete logical-equivalence decision. Structurally different but logically equivalent formulas can be rejected by this sufficient fidelity test. Conversely, agreeing in one world is insufficient. Put At(Principal, lab, 0), and no rebound atom, into a valuation. The original claim is true; rebinding speaker, place or time makes the corresponding claim false. The Lean counterexample independently verifies a speaker/time separating valuation. This shows substantive content discrimination rather than name comparison alone.

### Force and original context

The carrier's current force and the original source's force are separate fields. A present quotation can convey an earlier assertion without making a second original assertion. A source quotation is not flattened into endorsement of its content. A quoted directive remains a report of a directive; converting its original force to assertion fails preservation. This finite model handles one indexed source act and a carrier reporting it; it does not model every form of nested natural-language discourse.

### What the recipient may infer

The special-source route accepts a resolved proposition only when the original force is Assert, the carrier preserves original event/context/force/content, the event is independently authenticated, the source's veracity covers this domain, interpretation is adequate, the testimonial rule is admissible and there is no undefeated blocker. These epistemic premises are explicit application inputs. Machine acceptance does not authenticate them.

A fixed relay uses the original speaker's authenticated assertion. General employment supplies no prospective ownership. For new reports, the rule specifies owner, carrier, domain, force and time interval. The implementation requires an independently grounded rule event, no revocation, an eligible original occurrence, and membership in the actual executed successor history. It obtains that membership from current exclusive allocation, a live endpoint, an executed constructor ancestry chain and the endpoint's bearer label. The API has no arbitrary owner override. The independent twin's copied lineage text cannot satisfy this route.

The complete grounded rule also refers to the intended office history. Another World with an identically named successor and its own valid migration cannot inherit that rule. Full-record anchor maps prevent substituting different content, owner, domain or interval while retaining an authenticated event ID. Registered-object checks prevent substituting an unregistered same-ID token for the actual parent or endpoint. These guards close internal model confusions; they do not authenticate external-world histories.

This integrates front 3 with front 4 only at the stated model boundary. The Python World is trusted execution evidence for the toy office; it is not a forged-record detector for arbitrary external input. A real application still needs evidence that its alleged constructor/registrar history really occurred, that the reporting rule was genuinely undertaken, and that the source's asserted veracity scope applies. Those are not established by jointly consistent fields.

### Source ownership does not guarantee truth

The relevant veracity premise is a scoped property: every genuinely source-owned assertion in the relevant domain/context class is true. It is not merely a badge attached to a principal or an employee's successful appointment. In a separating valuation, the successor issues an in-scope duty report that is false. The independently grounded adoption rule and actual role membership still make it source-owned in this model. Precisely for that reason, the principal's scoped veracity property is false in that valuation. Ownership was not defined as truth and no contradiction was hidden by refusing the ownership judgement.

The recipient routine can return a candidate conclusion if supplied a falsely asserted veracity premise; its acceptance does not validate that premise. The diagnostic evaluates the source-side property over the declared finite set of assertions and shows that the full soundness premises cannot all hold in the false-report case. In a true-report valuation they can hold. This is not a demand that the recipient independently prove each reported duty: the diagnostic is outside the recipient's acquisition procedure. Actual general veracity beyond the finite universe remains an application premise, not inferred from finitely many successful reports.

### Historical duty and current action

The historical proposition Duty(Operator, inspect, [0,2)) can be warranted without a current grant to legislate or a separate proof of the very duty reported. Reporting an obligation and instituting one have distinct routes. A bare constitutive directive needs standing at its original occurrence; the testimonial duty route has no such extra duplicate-jurisdiction argument.

Current action additionally checks the actual recipient, interval and implementation. At time 1, Operator can act under that duty if implementation is available. At time 10, for another recipient, or without the actuator, the old proposition does not supply the missing fact. Revocation blocks new prospective adoption without retroactively erasing the old assertion. Failure of the exceptional-source route is not a denial of the retained ordinary defeasible-testimony route.

## 3. A service that actually changes state and migrates

The declared mission is a two-state register office. Read returns its state; an authorised toggle changes it; an intruder's toggle is denied without changing it. Both source states are tested. A successor stores the bit directly or inversely, so equal abstract state need not mean equal concrete representation.

The constructor reads its actual parent's current state, allocates a fresh endpoint, encodes that state, transfers the unique active allocation, emits a migration event tied to that endpoint, and removes the parent's live storage. It is not a prewritten trace attached to an independent installation. A second transfer from a retired parent is rejected.

For each first encoding, the model executes every request word of length zero, one or two over {read, toggle, intruder-toggle}. There are 13 words. It then actually migrates the changed state into the other encoding, retires the first successor and executes every second word from the same 13-word family. This is 2 source states × 2 encodings × 13 × 13 = 676 complete histories for each fully successful profile. A failed profile can terminate at its first concrete counterexample. The request horizon is explicitly finite; no unbounded policy follows.

### Two model histories with matching observations

An independently installed inverse-encoded twin with initial bit 1 matches the migrated successor from source bit 1 on all 13 service words. It has its own installation and lawful service appointment in the toy comparison, but no event connecting that endpoint to the predecessor's migration. Copying the lineage label does not create such an event.

Intervention holds the twin's independent constructor fixed and changes the parent bit from 0 to 1. Migrated outputs are [0,1]; twin outputs are [1,1]. This identifies an actual generative dependence inside the model. It is stronger than a declared parent-ID field and narrower than real-world causal identification. A different independently built twin designed to track an observed parent would be another causal model requiring its own provenance analysis.

### Future competence and unavailable construction

An archive-only profile keeps the historical assertion while failing service. A profile with engine, current state and grant but no builder serves all two-request words correctly yet cannot construct the next successor. A faulty implementation can return the correct first toggle result and then fail the following read. Another preserves its initial readout but fails to change state. These are executed failures, not conclusions inferred from the labels of the profiles.

## 4. Comparing support families on the same roots

The common root universe is archive, engine, state, grant, builder and reserve. A root is an assumed fail-stop failure domain, not necessarily one indivisible physical object. Static removals occur after the first installed migration and before the tested service/next-migration phases. Surviving resources remain available throughout the finite horizon. No time-varying reliability or failure probability is inferred.

For the selected historical proposition q, the retained T20 least-grounded support algorithm calculates:

E = {{archive}, {reserve}}.

Each is an already authenticated and adequately interpreted historical-record route under fixed background epistemic premises. The historical content is indexed by the relevant source initial state; identical support shapes across the two states do not make their propositions identical. A new prospective report is a different acquisition event, not an alias of an old office-holder's report.

The operational predicate O(A) is computed by attempting all model histories under the resources contributed by retained roots A. The minimal true sets are extracted only after executing all 64 root profiles and checking upward closure against every ordered subset/superset pair.

### Ordinary decoupled storage

The archive contributes evidence only. The reserve is an explicitly atomic service/evidence bundle. The other four roots respectively provide engine, current encoded state access, current exercise/transfer capability and constructor. Execution produces:

C = {{reserve}, {engine, state, grant, builder}}.

Thus E alone does not guarantee O under all removals: {archive} contains no C-support. There are 15 evidence-preserving failing retained profiles. The complete all-root profile nevertheless executes the ordinary positive migration and next migration successfully. Positive continuation is not made dependent on the optional co-survival construction below.

### Optional coupled fail-stop class

In the alternative class the archive root also provides an independently declared atomic service bundle. Nothing about the historical proposition changes; the resource model changes. Execution yields:

C = {{archive}, {reserve}, {engine, state, grant, builder}}.

Every minimal E-support now contains a minimal operational support, and all evidence-preserving profiles execute the finite contract. This is a conditional engineering fact about co-survival. It does not make an old historical receipt an observation of present readiness. If the alleged bundle can retain its record while losing its runtime, grant or constructor, the atomic premise is false and the decoupled counterprofile returns. Common-fault coupling is optional, not a definition of warranted continuation.

### Exact theorem and proof

For a finite universe U, epistemic predicate E and upward-closed operational predicate O, the following are equivalent:

1. For every removed subset Δ of U, E(U minus Δ) implies O(U minus Δ).
2. Every inclusion-minimal E-support S within U contains an inclusion-minimal O-support C.

Forward: retain just S, by removing U minus S. Since E(S), operation holds. A finite successful set has an inclusion-minimal successful subset C. Reverse: if retained A supports E, choose an inclusion-minimal E-support S within A. By the condition, it contains a minimal O-support C. Upward closure gives O(A). The Lean proof uses T20's existing finite-minimal-subset lemma, and a corollary instantiates E with actual `Derivable base rule A claim`.

No new hypergraph or reliability mathematics is claimed. The significance here is that C comes from independently executed service transitions and permissions, while E comes from retained grounded evidence. Merely reusing E as C would assume the disputed bridge. Equal minimum cut cardinality is also insufficient: {{a}} and {{b}} each have minimum cut size one, yet retaining a while losing b preserves one side alone.

### Why monotonicity is load-bearing

The positive resource semantics is fail-stop and enabling: optional extra availability does not add a mandatory unsafe controller, change the specification or allocate a competing role. The exhaustive check verifies this property in each selected finite class. It is not an axiom of arbitrary organisations.

A separate executed variant adds a mandatory hostile controller that makes writes stale. The complete safe harness works without it and fails with it. Its minimal successful support is still contained in the failed larger profile, so upward closure reconstructed from minimal supports overpredicts success. This refutes applying the support-only criterion to that nonmonotone class. The theorem requires O monotonicity; finiteness alone does not suffice. The formal theorem does not require E monotonicity, although the instantiated T20 derivability is monotone.

## 5. What continues institutionally, and what remains unsettled

Three judgements must not collapse:

- A historical assertion remains warranted.
- A service currently executes the declared finite task family and next migration.
- A bearer holds the institution's successor office.

The toy founding convention is independent of operation: a registrar establishes one office at genesis, appoints a bearer, and transfers it uniquely through the authorised constructor event, retiring its predecessor. A live currently allocated descendant holds that office; temporary resource unavailability does not by itself undo the appointment. This is why `is_current_successor` contains no operational success predicate. The reference model checks that a legitimate but currently unavailable successor still satisfies the office rule while O fails.

This convention has a limited justification. A reporting/service office can be deliberately constituted by publicly specified appointment, scope and succession rules. Such rules settle who can speak or operate in the office without requiring material identity of carriers. Under that institutional type, observed satisfaction of its actual governing convention is relevant constitutive evidence. The present convention was chosen to make this bounded type explicit, not derived from behaviour or invented to make all successful machines identical.

It follows only conditionally that the executed authorised migration continues this office's mission through the tested horizon. The independent twin may be an equally good service and hold another lawful appointment, yet lacks this particular chain. Conversely, if an actual institution's constitution permits replacement by a fresh independent installation and new appointment, lineage from this constructor need not be necessary. That alternative convention would support a different verdict without changing the input/output evidence. The experiment does not decide which convention governs an unexamined organisation.

The unresolved application premise is exact: identify the real institution, its legitimate constitutive/succession rule, the mission and bearer boundary it actually fixes, and evidence that the alleged transition instantiates that rule. It must also be established that the concrete resources and policy semantics realise the relevant continuing obligations. No Python identifier, signed record, Lean lemma or conjunction named GenuineContinuation provides that warrant. This is a narrowed positive conditional result and underdetermination result, not a universal organisational-identity theory.
