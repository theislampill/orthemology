## 1. Frozen formal setting

Let a finite group `G` act on a finite set of alternatives `A`, a finite
resource set `E`, and an output set containing `A`.  Let

\[
F:E\to A
\]

be a deterministic `G`-equivariant selector:

\[
F(g\cdot e)=g\cdot F(e).
\]

For every resource state `e`, equivariance gives the stabilizer inclusion

\[
\operatorname{Stab}(e)\subseteq \operatorname{Stab}(F(e)).
\]

This is the one-input specialization of the exact historical `AR8R-T254`
joint-resource stabilizer theorem.

## 2. SRVN-1A — invariant-source nonselection

If `e` is fixed by every element of `G`, then `F(e)` must also be `G`-fixed.
Therefore, when the action on `A` has no globally fixed alternative, no
`G`-equivariant deterministic selector can choose one alternative from a fully
`G`-invariant resource state.

### Proof

If `g.e=e` for every `g`, equivariance gives

\[
g.F(e)=F(g.e)=F(e).
\]

Thus `F(e)` is fixed by all of `G`.  If no such alternative exists, the selector
cannot be defined at `e`. ∎

## 3. SRVN-1B — specification-resource trilemma

A determinate unique output that lowers stabilizer relative to the focal input
must be accounted for by at least one of:

```text
1. asymmetry in a represented resource, parameter, boundary condition,
   history, seed, or selector state;
2. non-equivariance in the operation;
3. process-correspondence, contrast-reflection, resource-completeness,
   or interpretation failure.
```

This is a scoped application of `AR8R-T254/T255`, not a fresh general theorem.
A stochastic unique realization is not an exception when its random seed or
noise state is represented as part of `E`; the realized asymmetry then resides
in that resource.

## 4. SRVN-1C — asymmetry source does not entail volition

The formal trilemma identifies **where symmetry-breaking information or
structure must enter**.  It does not determine the metaphysical type of the
resource.

Consider the transitive natural action of `S_n` on `A={0,...,n-1}` and let
`E=A` with the same action.  The identity map

\[
F(e)=e
\]

is equivariant and yields a unique selected alternative for every resource
state.  Interpret `e` as any of:

```text
a brute boundary condition;
an impersonal law parameter;
a dispositional or powers state;
a represented stochastic seed;
a distributed coordination state;
or a personal preference/intention.
```

All formal selection, stabilizer, and counterfactual facts are unchanged.  In
an intended class containing both personal and impersonal interpretations, the
formal resource profile does not entail volition, intentionality, personality,
or Wisdom.

## 5. Positive volition bridge specification

A personal-volition conclusion requires additional premises that do not follow
from equivariant nonorigination alone.  At minimum a candidate must address:

```text
ULT:   the asymmetry source is ultimate for the declared specification;
INT:   it represents or apprehends alternatives as alternatives;
OWN:   one subject owns the selector state and the resulting act;
CTR:   the subject has alternative-sensitive counterfactual control;
WHY:   selection occurs for a reason or end, not merely because a parameter differs;
AUTH:  the end/selection norm is independently authorized;
FIT:   the end is objectively fitting or praiseworthy;
NIMP:  no impersonal, powers, brute, plural, or lawlike source realizes the
       same complete explanatory profile at matched cost.
```

Even `ULT+CTR` does not by itself establish `INT`, `WHY`, or `FIT`.
