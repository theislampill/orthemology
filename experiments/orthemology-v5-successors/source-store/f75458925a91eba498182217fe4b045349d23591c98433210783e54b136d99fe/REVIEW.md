# Independent bounded review of the cooperation bridges

Assessment: 7 October 2026 UTC. Scope: the current source reconstruction and model audit, with direct reading of the complete supplied Arabic Majmu al-fatawa 20:174–183, the supplied Minhaj 2:180–183 material and its immediate conceptual controls, and selected existing formal definitions. This is an independent assessment of warrant, not a new Lean proof, a metaphysical-possibility certificate, or formal research-programme closure. Evidence identifiers and reading ranges are in EVIDENCE_BINDINGS.json.

## 1. Result

The strongest current argument is considerably stronger than “cooperators must disagree” or “connected effects need one source.” It argues that complete, intrinsically sufficient original efficacy is incompatible with being completed by an independent partner; that a partner unable to supply the whole has a realizable perfection missing from it; and that necessary self-sufficiency excludes that deficiency. The Arabic expressly addresses cooperation. The Majmu sequel also explains why the disagreement-based presentation is not its decisive argument.

My bounded conclusions are:

1. The exclusion of two actual **exclusive entire producers of the same numerical effect in the same original respect** is deductive. So is exclusion of reciprocal efficient provision of the very capacities needed for either provision, given the no-priority-circle premise. Neither result alone excludes two intrinsic capacities or two contributions to one result.
2. **B6_EXCLUSIVE_COMPLETION is compelling when complete power and complete willing designate an agent's fully exercised, independently sufficient original production of the target, and causal nonduplication is retained.** Its conclusion does not follow from the weaker proposition that complete power and willing entail occurrence of an outcome. Exclusive provenance is additional content, explicitly defended in the source.
3. **B5_TRANSFER contains a substantial perfection argument, not just an assertion, but its de re transfer remains insufficiently established by the supplied illustrations.** Possible concentration of a pair's power profiles in one bearer, either existing partner's eligibility for that concentration, and the dependence of that partner's own constitution are different claims. The text moves through all three; the illustrations most directly support the first.
4. The positive reason for accepting these bridges is the superiority of **unborrowed complete efficacy** over effectiveness supplied only by an external completion. The strongest reason for withholding their unrestricted application is that a limitation of an actual chosen undertaking has not, just by being a limitation of that undertaking, been shown to be a privation of the agent's essential power or essential genus of activity.
5. The standard existing models do not establish a premise-preserving counterexample to the strongest source argument. Their limitations are independent of merely falsifying the disputed bridge. This negative model result supplies no independent proof that the bridge is true.
6. I would not use these passages alone to close the universal exclusion of every potentially fitting partial original undertaking. They give substantial pro-unity support and valid conditional exclusions. The narrower, defeasible actual-common-source assessment can still be supported by a cumulative argument; this bounded review neither derives that assessment from the absence of a countermodel nor refutes it by withholding the stronger universal bridge.

## 2. What the primary text actually argues

Majmu 20:174 starts with the incompatibility of independence **in the effect** and partnership **in that effect**. At 175 it strengthens this with an unchanged-agent argument: adding a genuinely operative equal contributor cannot leave the total product equivalent to the product of one unchanged contributor; otherwise the additional agency would make no difference. Its human lifting/cooking examples allow the same person to accomplish an equivalent result alone only after a change in willing, motion, instruments or other relevant state. [E01, web L906–908]

At 176–177 the source reasons that actual partners each need the other for this product. If either had complete power and complete decisive will, the product would exist through it alone. That would contradict actual participation by the other. It calls this an intrinsic incompatibility of two complete agencies in one product, expressly distinguishing it from one agent intending motion while the other intends rest. At 177–178 it distinguishes separate products/parts from an inseparably mixed product. The latter cannot be explained by merely assigning independent, separable parts to each producer. [E01, L909–915]

At 178–180 the source asks whether each deficient partner can acquire independent power. It argues that the product's possibility does not depend on whether one or two bear the power; the power in two can be concentrated in one; concentration improves rather than diminishes power; therefore each partner is capable of fuller independence. At 180–181 it argues that the agent cannot itself achieve the relevant completion, and concludes that its deficiency reveals dependence inconsistent with necessary existence by itself. The concluding generalisation identifies independence, perfection and necessity on one side, partnership, deficiency and need on the other. [E01, L916–927]

Minhaj 2:180 gives the connecting rationale explicitly: efficacy is a perfection, an efficacious being is more complete than a nonefficacious one, and efficacy supplied conditionally by another is a perfection for which the first depends on another. The next pages apply this to the interconnected actual world, reject reciprocal assistance that would supply original powers, and argue that a necessary existent's genuine perfections must be intrinsic rather than acquired. [E02, lines 40–41; E03, lines 1–4; E04, lines 1–2; parallel E05, V02L0117–0120]

These are arguments for a strong account of original lordship. They are not an accidental omission of harmonious agency. Equally, reporting their explicitness or intended demonstrative force does not establish their truth.

## 3. Type discipline: four levels, not one

The following must stay distinct throughout the assessment:

- **Essential capacity:** the agent's intrinsic, standing ability, whose existence and preservation are not efficiently received from an external source.
- **Genus of activity:** the agent's being actively productive as a kind or ongoing character of agency. An essential genus does not make every particular exercise essential.
- **Act-token:** a particular willing or productive exercise belonging to the agent. Its specification, timing and mode can differ without creating or removing the standing capacity.
- **Created effect:** the external product, including its constituent contributions and operative conditions. Dependence of this product on another product is not yet efficient origination of the first agent's capacity or act-token.

This distinction is not imposed against an undifferentiated source. The supplied Safadiyya passage expressly distinguishes the essential genus of efficacy from a particular efficacy, act or product. It rejects moving from the former to the eternal or essential status of a particular created thing. [E06, lines 0623–0627]

Consequently, Minhaj's efficacy-as-perfection principle is strongest when a proposed agent's **intrinsic productive adequacy** requires an external supplier. It does not straightforwardly say that every relational accomplishment must be an essential constituent of the agent. A further argument must show that the shared result completes something constitutive in the original agent, or that selecting that mode is itself incompatible with its independently established perfection.

Nor does the distinction settle the issue in the critic's favour. The source can argue that a particular exercise of genuinely original sovereignty must express unborrowed efficacy, even though that particular exercise is optional. Optionality does not convert a defective exercise into an adequate one. The precise question is whether the shared mode is defective **of the agent**, rather than only nonexclusive **of the undertaking**.

The causal respect also matters. One original source can provide an effect through genuine derivative agents. Original complete provision is not the assertion that no secondary agent acts. An alleged second irreducible original must not be confused with a derivative contributor, and two levels of causal attribution must not be treated as duplicate original provision in one respect.

## 4. B6_EXCLUSIVE_COMPLETION

### 4.1 The deductive core

Let D(A,e) mean A's complete power and complete determination for e. Let E(e) mean that e occurs. Let O(A,e) mean that A supplies the entire original productive basis of e, and let X(A,e) mean exclusive original production by A.

The weak P6 is D(A,e) implies E(e). Two instances of this implication entail the occurrence of e; they do not entail X(A,e), X(B,e), or A = B.

If instead the premise is D(A,e) implies O(A,e), and same-respect nonduplication says that O(A,e) and O(B,e) imply A = B, the exclusion is straightforward. Alternatively, if D already entails X, distinct agents satisfying D for this very effect cannot coexist. These are genuine deductions. The work lies in interpreting and warranting D's original-production scope and, where needed, the nonduplication principle.

Majmu explicitly makes that stronger move, especially “لوجب وجود المفعول به وحده” at L912. It would be unfair to criticise the author as having supplied only E(e). It would also be inaccurate to call X(A,e) a consequence of ordinary outcome entailment alone.

### 4.2 Strongest reason to accept it

“Complete” has causal content. If another irreducibly original contribution is necessary for the very efficacy that A is alleged to exercise completely, A's operative cause is incomplete in that respect. Merely saying that A welcomes the contribution does not supply the missing efficacy. There is a real distinction between choosing not to exercise an available ability and needing another cause in order to possess or complete the claimed exercise.

On this reading, B6 does not rest on a chance of disagreement. It compares two descriptions of one concrete completed agency: all its original requisites are from A, and an indispensable original requisite is from B. Those descriptions conflict. This gives the source's intrinsic-incompatibility argument genuine force.

The unchanged-agent consideration also blocks an easy human analogy. A person doing half the work jointly and all the work alone need not have the same complete operative act, even when standing strength is unchanged. The model must preserve the relevant **exercise**, not merely a label for enduring ability.

### 4.3 Strongest reason to withhold its broad application

Two different upgrades still require warrant:

1. From intact, unrestricted **standing power** and a determinate wish for the resulting state to complete **actual original production** of its entire basis.
2. From a causally sufficient actual contribution to **exclusive** actual original provenance, if “sufficient” is being used without exclusivity.

The first cannot be settled simply by relabelling every complete wish as a determination to produce the entire original basis alone. A will may be fully determinate about a specified partial act. Conversely, a critic cannot silently replace complete determination of the whole effect with willing “my part, provided the other supplies theirs” and claim to have retained the same premise. The antecedent must be fixed before testing the inference.

The second is the anti-overdetermination issue. The proposition “two causes cannot each be the sole actual cause” is analytic. The proposition “two actual causally sufficient causes cannot both contribute to one result” is stronger. Majmu's additive-efficacy rationale supports that stronger claim, but an arbitrary familiar saturated mechanism would not be a full countermodel: ordinary mechanisms are derivative and need their own conditions. Such mechanisms show only why the stronger claim cannot be extracted from the word “sufficient” without its causal-metaphysical interpretation.

Finally, the target must remain fixed. If a proposed target is individuated as “this product with B's independent contribution,” A's producing precisely that target alone is contradictory by specification. This does not yet establish that A has lost power over a source-neutral outcome. On the other hand, substituting a similar outcome in another history does not preserve the same numerical act/effect token. Both shortcuts are invalid.

**B6 verdict:** warranted as an exclusion of two completed, unborrowed, same-respect original productions; not established merely from intact capacity plus outcome-directed willing. Applying it to all cooperative undertakings depends on the additional perfection/sovereignty classification. That classification is argued for, not mechanically obtained from P6.

## 5. B5_TRANSFER

### 5.1 Its actual inferential sequence

The text is best separated into five propositions:

- **U, unification:** a productive capacity distributed across A and B can be realised in one subject, without loss of the relevant power.
- **R, de re eligibility:** each of these existing partners can possess the fuller independently productive capacity.
- **F, perfection:** that fuller independent capacity is a genuine improvement of that very partner, rather than merely a different individual's capacity or another task specification.
- **I, external completion:** the partner cannot itself effect the relevant completion; the source's argument is that inability to complete the external product makes inability to complete itself still clearer.
- **N, intrinsic necessity:** a subject whose own constitutive perfection depends on external completion is not necessary and sufficient by itself.

Given R, F, I and the substantive understanding in N, the final exclusion is coherent. U by itself does not give R, F or I.

### 5.2 What supports unification

The source offers two reasons rather than simply assuming arbitrary interchangeability. First, the product's intrinsic possibility should not turn on how many bear the producing power. Second, unifying power and coordination in a single subject removes dispersion; ordinary organised bodies, members and united agents illustrate increased effective power. [E01, L917–919]

This is strongest for a **pure productive capacity** whose exercise has no constitutive requirement for numerically different original bearers. If all the original resources are jointly realisable and no relevant incompatibility prevents their concentration, it is reasonable to regard their unborrowed possession together as a perfection. A proposed partner whose limitation is merely the arbitrary allocation of part of that capacity has no obvious claim to unrestricted lordship.

This matters philosophically. It puts pressure on a plural-original account to explain why its division is principled and compatible with perfection, rather than treating the word “intrinsic” as a permission to posit any limitation whatsoever. The source's unity argument therefore has more content than an economy preference for fewer names.

### 5.3 What the illustrations do not yet show

A group becoming one organised body can supply a **new collective bearer** without making each prior member individually capable of what the body does. Likewise, a description of one possible bearer with the union of two profiles does not entail that this very A or this very B could possess the union while retaining the relevant identity and constitutive character.

The crucial passage is L919's move from supposing the two to be one bearer of both powers to concluding that each can be receptive of independent power. That move is explicit, but not supplied by the illustrations alone. Literal numerical fusion of two existing necessary individuals is particularly unhelpful as a warrant: if A and B remain the same two distinct individuals, they have not become one; if only a new subject remains, the conclusion about each old subject has not followed. The strongest charitable reading is therefore concentration of the **power profile**, not literal identity fusion. That reading still owes the de re transfer.

A principled limitation or incompatibility is not automatically established by inventing a label for it. But neither is its absence established by saying a unified bearer is conceivable. The source needs a reason that these original subjects are eligible for every jointly realisable pure productive perfection. This can be a defended metaphysical principle; it is not generic modal logic.

A second step deserves separate scrutiny. Being unable to produce an external result alone does not, by a general ordering of task difficulty, entail inability to alter or improve oneself. The source's “a fortiori” is most plausible where the improvement is precisely acquisition of the missing original efficacy, and no cause can create the power it already requires for that acquisition. Stated that way it uses an anti-self-origination principle. For arbitrary improvements, the comparative claim is not secured merely by an external failure.

Finally, possible improvement is not automatically dependence for **continued existence**. Necessary-by-itself in the source is richer than the modal proposition “exists in every possible world,” so reducing the source to that proposition would miss its claim. Even under its richer account, however, one must establish that the missing item is a perfection belonging to the subject's own self-sufficient constitution. An optional relational attainment is not obviously such an item. This is where B5 joins the Minhaj efficacy-perfection rationale.

### 5.4 Does the Minhaj perfection argument close the transfer?

Minhaj 2:182–183 argues that a genuine perfection cannot be impossible for the necessary existent, since that would allow a possible being to surpass it; if contingent and acquired, the perfection would introduce outside dependence; hence the perfection must belong intrinsically. It also argues that the producer and giver of a perfection is more entitled to it. [E03–E05]

That is a substantive account of maximal intrinsic perfection. It can close R/F/N **if** the proposed concentrated power is a pure perfection applicable to each alleged necessary original. The question is not whether unborrowed agency is good; it is whether that principle has already independently established the full applicability and essentiality now needed.

The “giver is more entitled” argument is particularly strong for existence, life and the productive resources actually supplied by that giver. Its unrestricted application to the complete union of resources across alleged independent sources needs the unity/attribution argument currently under examination. One cannot first assume that every perfection in both sources' products comes from each alleged source and then use that assumption to prove that each must be the whole source.

**B5 verdict:** unification of suitable profiles has a serious positive rationale; de re eligibility and constitutive perfection remain the weakest links in the supplied argument. I withhold treating B5 as a fully established general theorem of intrinsic necessity. If unrestricted eligible-target power is already independently warranted for each agent, the transfer branch becomes largely unnecessary; B6 and the classification of actual exercise still do the decisive work.

## 6. Necessary concurrence, actual cooperation, and the prior-premise test

The immediate Minhaj controls distinguish intrinsic attributes and harmless concomitance from efficient receipt or a vicious causal priority circle. The source explicitly permits some co-occurrence without reciprocal origination. [E02, lines 8–24] Its rejection of necessary coordination between original lords is therefore a further classification argument, not a universal axiom that modal co-occurrence always entails causation.

A pair of intrinsic powers acting on a downstream result does not exhibit a causal circle merely because that result needs both. To invoke the no-circle premise one must show that each other's contribution is a condition of the other's **productive capacity or completed original exercise**, not merely of the shared downstream result. Majmu's B5/B6 and Minhaj's efficacy-as-perfection are the substantive arguments intended to justify precisely this move. They must be assessed together, without pretending that the source failed to notice it.

The current PREMISE_MAP usefully distinguishes P2a, unreceived constitutive power, from P2b, no externally completed productive efficacy. It would nevertheless be circular to reject an objection to the derivation of P2b solely because the objection fails P2b. The legitimate challenge preserves independently established upstream commitments and tests the target bridge. A countermodel to the **entire theory including P2b and the bridge** is not required to question its derivation.

This does not rescue the current models. Their independent gaps remain substantial:

- **Anchored split/mixed tables:** the definitions model contributions, operative requisites and coverage. Qualification is a predicate; neither full intrinsic power nor completed willing nor perfection is interpreted. These are useful counterinterpretations to a weaker incidence theory, not representations of full original lordship. [E09–E10; E07–E08]
- **Finite ModalAbility:** the two declared outcomes really have alternative solo-production witnesses, and the actual table really splits their pieces. But ability is an existential over supplied alternative histories. The model has no interpreted complete will, no unrestricted eligible-target semantics, and no proof of independent control in all relevant circumstances. History is part of the contribution token. It cannot establish sameness of numerical effect across the alternatives. [E11]
- **CND role-switch:** the alternative explicitly makes the first bearer wholly received. It tests whether actual role plus existence at two indices forces essential nonreceipt, not whether intrinsically unreceived original powers can cooperate. [E12]
- **Token-receipt control:** useful for distinguishing a received state from whole-existence receipt, but it contains no pair of fully qualified cooperative originals. [E12]
- **Ground-capacity, premise-removal and outside-field controls:** according to the inspected audit, these separate support from actual production, delete identified assumptions, or place another source outside the connected field. They do not supply the required two actual originals inside this field with the full agency package. I did not independently replay their proofs in this assessment. [E07–E08]
- **Necessary-harmony prose:** a shared good need not be a third efficient producer, but naming a shared good does not yet explain all concordant determinations, the distribution of original work and compatibility with the source's perfection standard. It remains a conceptual challenge, not a certified full interpretation. [E07–E08]

No inspected standard model therefore defeats the source's strengthened argument as a full-premise counterexample. This conclusion is about the models' evidential reach. It does not make B5 or B6 true by default.

## 7. What follows for one actual common original source

If distinct original owners cannot occur within any relevant unified actual effect, then faithful original-provider representation and connected overlap of operative requisites propagate a single owner across the actual field. Same-respect nonduplication and visibility then exclude a hidden second in-field original. The inspected AnchoredSourceBridge proves a corresponding conditional structure from explicitly parameterised classification/conformance and coverage premises. It does not establish their metaphysical warrant. [E09]

The empirical/material connectedness premise supplies **where** a locally justified exclusion propagates. It does not supply the exclusion. Nor does a field-bounded conclusion rule out every unrelated necessary reality outside that field.

There are thus three different legitimate levels of conclusion:

1. **Deduction conditional on full original efficacy and its classifications:** strong and informative.
2. **Metaphysical warrant for identifying every shared original undertaking as defective:** defended by the source, strengthened by the Majmu/Minhaj passages, but not fully secured by the supplied concentration and perfection arguments in this review.
3. **Defeasible acceptance of one actual common source from a cumulative account of original provision and integrated agency:** may remain reasonable without pretending that level 2 is closed. Its warrant must be assessed positively, not inferred from the failure of current countermodels.

My strongest affirmative consideration is that original sovereignty plausibly requires its completed efficacy to come wholly from its own resources; unexplained partition among alleged originals can conceal a real insufficiency. My strongest reservation is that the source's transition from a shared particular outcome to an externally completed intrinsic perfection has not yet been independently established for every allowed voluntary exercise. The reservation is specific to that transfer. It is neither a generic appeal to harmonious plurality nor a finding that the source ignored cooperation.
