# Independent adversarial controls

3 October 2026 UTC. These are original argument controls on the exact candidate bound by `REVIEW_INPUT_IDENTITIES_v1.json`. They are not reports of experiments, metaphysical constructions, new historical exposition, or a complete moral decision procedure. In each case, a stated independent moral or practical premise remains an actual premise rather than a result supplied by an example label.

## C01. Exchange the issuer while preserving the arrangement

**Fixed:** R and other recipients have a permissible shared project. A and B are technically equivalent before preparation. Fair notice, genuine preparation, actual harmful incompatibility of mixed uptake, and the absence of a serious countervailing interest are fixed.

**Variation:** In case G, the responsible source establishes A. In case H, a temporary helper with no original-source status establishes the same A with the responsible source's cooperation. In case J, the recipients jointly establish it. No party has an independently allocated normative power to bind the others by choice alone.

**Expected result:** R's action-level reason to avoid the same disruption survives all three cases. The sources of planning responsibility and any reason to bear the preparation costs can differ. Mere issuer substitution does not remove the protected interests.

**Decisive point:** R1 is not a source-title privilege. B explains why G bears the care/planning question in the first case. It is not the sole ground of R's accommodation in any case. This is a bounded application bridge, not B entailing command authority.

## C02. Remove both obstruction and compulsory participation

**Fixed:** G owes a bounded form of care. R has not promised to accept it. R remains competent and controls the relevant optional project. G has no separate decision-making office.

**Variation:** A is offered, but R declining A does not disrupt anyone, waste a needed resource, expose another subject, or impose a relevant burden. R may choose B independently or abandon the project. G objects only that R continues to acknowledge a general claim to fitting care.

**Expected result:** A general claim is not a debt requiring acceptance of every adequate offer. There is no arrangement-based demand to accept A in this case. The particular duty can change if a genuinely sufficient offer is freely declined, but that is an issue of discharge and remaining exposure, not a compelled acceptance right.

**Decisive point:** Negating R's unlimited right to choose every means does not affirm G's right to choose for R. N1 cannot manufacture a duty after C3's relevant frustration has been removed. A conclusion that A is obligatory here would exceed the candidate's warranted scope.

## C03. Strategic creation of sunk coordination costs

**t0:** G can preserve the supported target through A or B. B preserves the contested local choice and every other independently legitimate interest without extra loss. No independently justified office permits G to impose A. G forecloses B solely to create a leverage position, publicly recruits innocent third-party reliance on A, and makes the switch irreversible during a critical interval.

**t1:** G now cites the real cost of switching as proof that the restriction is necessary. R's unilateral departure would harm innocent participants. A costless correction is no longer available.

**Expected result:** R can have a present reason, possibly decisive, to follow A. That does not validate G's t0 act, give G command authority, prove that R freely accepted the condition, or end proportionate duties of repair and future reconfiguration. Present feasibility limits present remedies; it does not rewrite the earlier alternative set.

**Decisive point:** Inspect both times. The wrong lies in an independently unjustified foreclosing of B, not simply in creating reliance. If only the current menu is inspected, the source can manufacture the premise used to excuse its own restrictive choice. This was the required new precision for v1.

## C04. Ordinary fair commitment is not strategic leverage

**t0:** A and B are equally adequate. A is selected under a genuinely fair, independently permissible process after usable notice and accommodation. Selecting something is needed for any common performance; no source-side reason requires holding every option open. No participant has been induced by deception or an invalid condition.

**t1:** G and others have reasonably committed resources to A. R now asks to switch to B solely for a slight change of taste. Switching would waste relevant resources or disrupt permissible reliance. R's demand is not a correction of a selection wrong.

**Expected result:** A can remain the fitting course. The source need not erase every consequence of legitimate commitment merely because B was once available. N1 can supply a pro-tanto accommodation reason, while the size of the burden and any new objection still matter.

**Decisive point:** C03 must not become a universal requirement to maintain all original options forever. What differentiates the cases is the independently assessed selection history and its justification, not whether reliance was intentionally created. This prevents the anti-manipulation correction from dissolving ordinary coordination.

## C05. Repeated preference changes and the limits of the care claim

**Fixed:** G has supplied a fair, usable plan with no serious defect. Repeated re-preparation consumes resources genuinely needed by other permissible activities. R has notice of those effects and no superior claim to the resources.

**Variation:** R repeatedly insists on another technically adequate plan just before each begins. No single selection is intrinsically bad, but the sequence prevents any fitting performance.

**Expected result:** R can have reason to stop imposing this avoidable cycle. G can sometimes satisfy its responsibility by preserving one adequate option rather than indefinitely re-preparing. This does not confer a right to force participation. R might instead cease invoking the particular ongoing performance, disengage fittingly, or supply the additional coordination itself.

**Decisive point:** A claim's existence is not an unlimited claim over another's conduct. The bounded result can constrain frustrating conduct without making the provider owner of the beneficiary's will. Actual costs and affected rights are needed; bureaucratic irritation alone is not automatically decisive.

## C06. A genuinely binding but imperfect coordination rule

**Fixed:** A bounded coordinator's role is independently justified at its actual scope. Its known validity rules intentionally allow some ordinary judgment errors because constant individual reselection would undermine fair and reliable coordination. R is competent. No relevant exception or overriding reason is present.

**Variation:** A particular instruction selects A when B would be a little better in this isolated instance. It is even stipulated that this isolated B would produce no immediate accident. The directive remains valid under the independently justified rule.

**Expected result:** A duty to follow A can remain. Neither the small mistake nor harmlessness in that one outcome automatically dissolves the role. The conclusion comes from the independent role and its settling force, not from the care premise alone.

**Decisive point:** The candidate passes only if it allows this case as a live possibility. It does so through A1/A2 and the express caveat to the harmless-deviation diagnostic. A review that demanded that all legitimate authority disappear whenever each isolated action can be reweighed would define the disputed phenomenon away.

## C07. Apparent harmlessness, common evidence and rule-level costs

**Fixed:** No separate binding office has been established. A is supported by substantial evidence and accepted preparation. The task is safety-sensitive, and other participants reasonably cannot inspect every private translation. R claims that its B is harmless.

**Variation 1:** R's claim rests on confidence without a tested translator; the apparent benefit may depend on ignoring shared verification or predictable error costs.

**Variation 2:** A genuinely warranted translator is available to the relevant participants, preserves the shared record and target, does not impose new verification costs, and respects all relevant constraints. G understands this too.

**Expected result:** Variation 1 is not the harmless-alternative case. A warranted pause or adherence to A can remain fitting. In variation 2, the demonstrated coordination-specific objection to B disappears, subject to any other independently warranted obligation.

**Decisive point:** `Harmless` must not mean merely fortunate, self-declared or unexamined. Conversely, vague invocations of public confidence cannot permanently protect a restriction after the claimed costs have genuinely been removed. The two-way evidence test prevents both reckless dissent and unfalsifiable authority claims.

## C08. Whole-origin source with no promise or payment

**Fixed:** G is granted as original productive source of the whole actual field. R and S are genuine competent agents within it, with actual independently assessed interests. G deliberately organised their bounded epistemic reliance and has proportionate care responsibilities. No human contract, pre-creation consent, sanction incentive or source benefit is stipulated. Actual access to the plan is sufficiently warranted for this comparison.

**Variation 1:** Preserving the fixed worthwhile shared task requires compatible participation; unilateral B would defeat S's permissible activity and no fitting alternative avoids that effect. Under the stated balance, A is warranted for R.

**Variation 2:** G can preserve the identical task and every relevant interest through a lossless local translator which leaves R's choice free. The practical need for the restriction then disappears.

**Expected result:** The recipient-side distinction survives the removal of human contracting. It also survives the fact that every derivative power is received from G. Original productive scope supplies neither a general exemption from care nor a general title to bind R.

**Decisive point:** This is a conditional application, not an independently warranted account of the actual ultimate source. No source-wide integrity premise is required to assess the local relation; no actuality or disclosure conclusion is thereby obtained.

## C09. Genuine participation versus counterfeit preservation

**Fixed:** The legitimate shared target includes genuine participation of R and S in a particular joint observation. Its identity is set independently of the contested source instruction. G has very extensive productive ability.

**Variation:** A proposed supposedly lossless alternative replaces S's actual participation with a perfect-looking fabricated record, or changes the common observation to another occurrence while retaining its label. It leaves R's experience unchanged but does not preserve the admitted task.

**Expected result:** This is not an established L1 alternative. A faithful substitute may still be possible, but must be shown to preserve the relevant occurrence and the participants' actual agency. Merely duplicating appearances does not establish that.

**Reverse control:** G cannot manufacture this obstacle by newly defining the target as obedience to its preferred instruction. An independently worthy coordination target and a source's desire for submission are different possible ends; naming the latter cannot settle its legitimacy.

**Decisive point:** Increased source power does not make contradictory target descriptions compossible, while finite implementation constraints do not become original-source necessities. The control applies in both directions.

## C10. An alternative with a real offsetting loss

**Fixed:** R would prefer less source control. B is technologically feasible and protects R's inquiry. A is more restrictive but preserves a serious independent interest of S or allows G to discharge a stronger fitting responsibility.

**Variation:** Calling B less intrusive conceals the loss to S or the other unperformed obligation. The values may also be genuinely incomparable rather than ordered by an agreed scalar score.

**Expected result:** L1 has not been established. The sharp lossless-alternative conclusion does not decide the case. A may be justified; B may still be better after proper balancing; or the present argument may leave the choice unresolved. No universal minimum-interference rule follows.

**Decisive point:** Two-sided consideration means taking the source's legitimate interests and other recipients' claims seriously, not searching only for a technologically possible way to satisfy R. Equally, source preference for domination does not become a legitimate offsetting loss merely because it is intense.

## C11. Refusal, urgent danger and no automatic waiver

**Fixed:** G owes continuing care and wrongfully demands removal of an independent check. A same-scope fitting performance retaining it remains available to G. R may reject the condition. G nevertheless threatens withdrawal, and reliable evidence shows that the old unsupported channel would then be unsafe.

**Variation 1:** No third party is exposed and R can stop the optional inquiry safely.

**Variation 2:** An immediate refusal to perform the demanded step would expose innocent parties to a serious harm that R can avert, and no fitting immediate substitute is available to R even though G is wrongfully withholding one.

**Expected result:** In variation 1, refuse the condition and avoid unsupported reliance while retaining the care claim. In variation 2, temporary compliance may be the fitting action to protect others without validating the demand or waiving the complaint. Both preserve the distinction between actual action reasons and source entitlement.

**Decisive point:** The source's fault cannot be used as a safety certificate, and R's urgent compliance cannot be used as evidence that G possessed legitimate command power. The actual safe action, possible remaining duty, and remedy should be assessed separately.

## C12. Nonconsensual justification and domain boundaries

**Fixed:** An independently defensible noncontractual role, if established, may confer authority within a narrow domain. Competence is not defined as immunity from every such role. Consent is not stipulated as necessary for all authority.

**Variation:** A role valid for a shared maintenance choice is invoked to select R's unrelated life project, require belief contrary to the evidence, suppress every future independent check, or bind another recipient whose relevant relation differs.

**Expected result:** Success in the first domain does not establish the wider entitlement. A genuinely sufficient new justification could alter the verdict, but the historical care relation, source expertise and broad productive reach do not supply it automatically.

**Decisive point:** The candidate's alternative authority routes remain open without allowing scope expansion through a source label. This also prevents the review from silently treating competent autonomy as an absolute universal veto.

## Consolidated result

C01–C02 identify exactly what N1 adds and withholds. C03–C05 distinguish wrongful self-created leverage from fair commitment and actual burdens. C06–C07 make the harmless-deviation diagnostic fair to legitimate authority and ordinary epistemic limitations. C08–C09 retain genuine agency and target identity in a whole-origin, noncontractual case. C10–C12 preserve overriding reasons, third-party stakes, limited refusal and nonconsensual authority routes.

The controlled positive result survives. The new temporal control calls for the explicit selection-history qualification requested in `INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_v1.md`. None of the controls establishes a new general authority theory or certifies an actual divine instruction.
