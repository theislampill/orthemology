For arbitrary q, every Step (W q) u must be generated by a Step q q' and yields u = W q'. The outer S has exactly two arguments and the inner K exactly one; neither is a root redex, and S/K constants have no steps. Exhaustive inversion of the five Step constructors establishes this directly. Induction on Red gives:

Red t u → ∃q, Red omega q ∧ u = W q.

If W q were Normal, then q would be Normal: any Step q q' lifts through Step.right K and Step.right (app S K), contradicting wrapper normality. Apply inherited omega_no_normal_reduct. This direct contradiction route avoids an unnecessary choice/existence lemma. Therefore:
