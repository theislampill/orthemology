# Common source and divine action reassessment

Author: dot. Date: 7 October 2026. This is a philosophical and source-fidelity reassessment following the late contextual readings. It adds no new kernel theorem or empirical observation. Exact source boundaries and edition controls are recorded in the accompanying locus audits.

## Present judgement

I continue provisionally to accept one complete original source of the relevant actual connected created order. The new readings strengthen the source-based case and substantially improve the formulation of the live objections. They do not justify reporting an unconditional machine-verified metaphysical conclusion. I still withhold assent to the stronger universal source-mode principle across every possible original agent and undertaking.

My separate provisional sufficient-warrant acceptance of at least one actual Necessary Being is unchanged. This bounded common-source and divine-action audit does not add a fresh independent proof of that earlier conclusion.

My earlier criticism must not suggest that harmonious cooperation was overlooked. Ibn Taymiyyah addresses agreement, necessary agreement, foreknowledge, common benefit, intact powers, differentiated contributions, assistance and coalescing effects. The remaining question is whether his defended transitions from these arrangements to deficient or received original efficacy are sound at their intended scope.

I have not produced a countermodel preserving the full independently established antecedent package while falsifying one of those exact transitions. The nine inspected candidates do not do so. Their weaker separation results remain valid at their recorded signatures. This failure is not a proof that every source premise is true, and I do not treat an unspecified harmonious pair as an established alternative of equal explanatory merit.

The test must not be made circular: the disputed exclusion of externally completed efficacy cannot be inserted among the independently established premises and then used to disqualify every challenge to its derivation. The source map's P2b is a downstream commitment when B5, B6 or the realised-perfection argument is being tested. The current candidates fail the upstream test for additional reasons, including their missing complete-will, perfection and unrestricted-control semantics.

## The actual argument

The original lord is characterised by intrinsic, unborrowed power and will, rather than by a temporarily uncaused contribution. Two agents cannot each be the exclusive entire actual producer of the same numerical effect in the same respect. If their contributions are different, the source asks whether either has complete efficacy independently of the other's contribution. Mutual provision of the very powers needed to provide anything creates a vicious efficient circle. Merely simultaneous existence or relational correlation is separately recognised and is not condemned as such.

The argument then addresses powers retained in the partners' mutual presence. An ability that requires another original's enabling or abstention is held to be externally conditioned; the source distinguishes that from one agent's own voluntary selection among contrary acts. Necessary agreement is examined as a purported arrangement of original wills. The appeal to common benefit or foreknowledge does not, in the author's argument, supply an independent original explanation of the wills producing the beneficial event.

The coalescence argument applies these conclusions to effects. Against another source's complete determination, interference would contradict its unimpeded efficacy. With its consent, the interaction is treated as assistance and assessed under the prior cooperation exclusion. Thus genuinely independent original domains would remain separate. Their proposed separation conflicts with the interconnected created order. This is a structured argument for exclusion, not just an expectation that rivals might quarrel.

Majmūʿ 20:175–181 makes two further steps explicit. Complete power and decisive will are taken to entail production through that agent alone. Also, power distributed across two bearers can be concentrated in one; the source uses this to argue that each partner admits a fuller power, cannot provide its own missing completion, and therefore is not intrinsically necessary and self-sufficient. Both steps require assessment in their own right.

## What follows logically and what needs metaphysical warrant

The contradiction between exclusive entire production and an additional actual productive partner is logical once those predicates have their stated meanings. Likewise, if complete determination entails a particular outcome, an outcome genuinely incompatible with that determination cannot coexist with it. Identity propagation through a faithfully represented connected field is also a conditional mathematical result once local original-owner exclusion is supplied.

Other commitments are substantive: the causal nonduplication principle at the intended productive respect; the exclusion of vicious efficient dependence; the applicability and extent of complete original power; the explanatory account of contingent coalescence; the impossibility of received essential perfection; and the bridges from cooperative efficacy to defective original lordship. Calling a premise metaphysical does not show it is weak or arbitrary. Here the author gives arguments for it. Conversely, those arguments are not supplied by a Lean definition or by bare modal logic.

## The exact inferences I still find under-warranted as universal claims

First, attaining an intended outcome and exclusively producing its complete original basis are different claims. Majmūʿ 20:176–177 explicitly connects them through complete power and decisive will. This is compelling if the specified determination is already an exercised, independently sufficient original cause of the whole effect. It is not derivable merely from the weaker statement that the intended outcome follows from the agent's power and will. A cooperative proposal must specify the same target and the actual extent of the will; changing from the whole effect to a conditional partial contribution is not preservation of the stronger premise. Equally, a proof must justify why that stronger exercise profile follows from every relevant original agent's essential capacity.

Second, a possible bearer of both partners' powers and either particular partner's capacity to possess that combined profile are distinct modal claims. Majmūʿ 20:178–180 argues for their connection through concentration and greater perfection. I accept that distributed powers are often jointly realisable and that requiring a peer's productive completion is a serious challenge to absolute self-sufficiency. I do not yet regard the general move to each existing bearer's eligibility, followed by its ontological dependence, as established solely by the examples. A proposed exception would need to preserve the independently warranted power and perfection profile; merely declaring the powers non-unifiable would deny the disputed premise, not refute the completed conditional argument.

Third, necessary concurrence does not, in logic alone, amount to reciprocal efficient production. The source explicitly argues that the relevant original wills cannot have this concurrence without mutual conditioning or another complete cause. I withhold the universal inference until the distinction between an intrinsically determined agreement and an efficient condition of the other's willing is fully warranted. This is not a claim that a sound model of intrinsically concordant original lords has been supplied.

These are reasons to qualify the universal demonstration, not reasons to erase the positive actual common-source assessment. The unified account retains explanatory advantages from complete original provision, integrated conditions and ends, and the absence of an established gain requiring plural original ownership. Those advantages are defeasible; neither parsimony alone nor failure to exhibit a rival makes the conclusion certain.

## Divine action and the global cessation control

Essential power, the genus of voluntary activity, a particular kind, a particular act-token and a created effect must remain distinguishable. A particular act's beginning does not logically entail creaturehood, the beginning of its bearer, or receipt of a new perfection from outside. The source material expressly distinguishes acts subsisting in the divine agent from separated created products. These distinctions prevent a premise about externally completed efficacy from being applied indiscriminately to every renewed voluntary act.

There is also an important limitation on the earlier empty-extension control. Ibn ʿUthaymīn's Nisāʾ lesson 33 adds the substantive premise that a period of total divine inactivity would be a defect. His statement about continuing capacity is accompanied by an always-active claim. Thus a model retaining capacity while making the entire extension of divine activity empty does not preserve that fuller doctrine. Nonperformance of one kind is a different matter. No passage inspected here says that every kind or every token must always be actual.

The contextual al-Ṣafadiyya discussion around the user's Arabic anchor also argues that successive voluntary matters cannot simply be stripped from the agent, with a fresh act then appearing without its requisite cause. That is an additional causal argument to examine, not a consequence of the word “capacity.” The anchor occurs at 2:145 in the Muḥammad Rashād Sālim edition, with continuation at 2:146 onward. The [IslamHouse edition text](https://islamhouse.com/read/ar/الصفدية-272837) identifies Maktabat Ibn Taymiyya, second edition, 1406 AH, and explicitly preserves printed pagination. This is a direct electronic edition-page verification, not a new scan collation. Wikisource /11 and /12 are segment locators only.

The checked empty-extension construction therefore retains its role as a logical separation for its explicit reduced assumptions. It is not a countermodel to all of Ibn Taymiyyah's or Ibn ʿUthaymīn's commitments, and it was not a certificate of historical doctrine or metaphysical possibility. Its existing theorem statements need no alteration on this account. The philosophical application and source-mapping limit must accompany them.

## What changed

My affirmative, defeasible assessment of the actual common-source claim is unchanged from the 14:34 appraisal. My account of its strongest source support is more specific and stronger. The new Majmūʿ continuation prevents a vague “source-mode” objection from standing in for analysis of the actual exclusivity and power-concentration arguments. The full-countermodel verdict is expressly negative: none has been produced.

The divine-action audit now has an explicit source reason why a capacity-only global cessation model is not a model of the fuller doctrine. This narrows application credit without retracting the formal separation. Renewed acts and externally received essential perfection remain distinct. Historical appraisals and sealed mathematical evidence are preserved; this dated assessment is their source-contextual successor, not an independent second witness or a retroactive rewrite.
