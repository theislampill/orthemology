## Result

**The direct source already addresses rejoicing at repentance specifically, with an owned-exercise argument rather than only a conjunction of conditionality and independence.** Majmūʿ 8:145 identifies the very response, attributes the servants' existence, guidance and assistance to the original, and retains their actual performance. At 146 it explicitly distinguishes being complete through one's own unassisted activity from being completed by another, comparing the former with completeness through one's own attributes or self. It then argues that absence before the fitting occasion need not be deficiency and that ability for wise successive activity is itself a perfection.

These arguments were already continuously read and substantively assessed in the saved wise-determination and productive-sufficiency records. The present contribution is recovery and predicate-specific application of that earned explanation. The recent rejoicing report's statement that its own necessary-condition test did not independently settle intrinsic receptivity must not become a programme-wide claim that no developed answer exists.

The positive account does not require a new representation for each response, identify rejoicing with a particular volition, or supply its complete efficient mechanism. The fairly stated case-specific objection remains a possible challenge to the account's classification of owned realization; it has not thereby overturned the independently earned essential-independence judgment.

## 2. What the direct Arabic says

The retained [Majmūʿ 8 electronic witness](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/22/805/) contains vocalized and unvocalized display layers. The saved selection uses the latter, retaining website link artifacts rather than silently repairing them. Current contextual reading covered 141–147, with 144–147 inspected in both displayed layers; these are duplicate presentations, not independent witnesses. No print scan, manuscript, or transmission authentication was performed.

Pages 141–144 defend actual divine love and rejoice-at-repentance language against its reduction to merely creaturely relations. Historical polemic and cited traditions remain source reports, not newly authenticated events. At 145 the author says, expressly of rejoicing at repentance, love of those who approach through additional worship, and approval of the early believers, that foreign need or completion should not be inferred: He created, guided and assisted these persons until **they did** what He loves, approves and rejoices over. The next sentence attributes the loved attainments to His power, willing and creation.

The discussion identifies this as the argument of the majority who affirm wisdom connected to the original, loved and pursued in its action. At 146 the ensuing replies are introduced with “they said.” They are the positively developed defense in this context, not an unnoticed change to the earlier denial-of-wisdom opponent. The present attribution is accordingly to Ibn Taymiyya's developed majority defense; not every dialectical concession in the larger unit is treated as an independent adopted thesis.

The third reply gives the decisive distinction:

> وإذا قيل كمل بفعله الذي لا يحتاج فيه إلى غيره كان كما لو قيل كمل بصفاته أو كمل بذاته

If one says that He is complete through His own act, in which He does not need another, this is like saying He is complete through His attributes or His self. The preceding clause connects this to its occurrence through His power and willing without a partner. This is stronger than claiming that any condition whatsoever is harmless merely because it is called a condition.

The fourth reply distinguishes a new realization's prior absence from absence of what fitting wisdom required then. The fifth at 147 argues that power for wise temporal realization is better than incapacity, although temporal things cannot be eternally actual as temporal things. These are substantive evaluative and modal reasons. They do not derive an exact time or show that every chronological absence is fitting.

## 3. The recovered argument applied to the fixed case

Keep R as the servant's real repentance and J as the specific rejoicing over it, without changing either predicate.

1. Shifāʾ edited 2:208 supplies the actual-condition relation J requires R and the comparative excellence of this response.
2. Majmūʿ 8:145 addresses this same kind of rejoicing. It does not answer by deleting R, replacing it with foreknowledge, or assigning repentance to God instead of the servant. It expressly retains created guidance, assistance and the servant's actual performance.
3. The developed argument locates the entire original provision within the same original's power and willing. Its distinction at 146 is between realization through that owned activity and perfection furnished by an independently needed other. This is the source's declared account of the case, not a transfer from the cognitive representation at 16:305.
4. The fitting-time argument prevents moving from “J is an excellent realization” to “J's absence on every earlier occasion was an already existing deficiency.” The response's condition and fitting scope must be preserved. No claim about all possible anticipatory joy follows.
5. Within the earned original-source and essential-adequacy account, this provides positive reason to classify J as conditioned realization of owned agency rather than alien replenishment. The condition is real; the source of original adequacy and the mode of realizing its excellence are not replaced by that condition.

The third premise does substantive work. “God made R” alone would not establish nonreceptivity in every conceivable subsequent relation. Here it is accompanied by the explicit own-act/no-foreign-need account and the independently earned original-source profile. Conversely, an assertion that J must be foreign completion merely because actual R is relevant overlooks the distinction the author directly argues at this predicate.

This is a warranted application of the existing account, not a neutral deduction from the word “condition,” a Boolean model, or a newly independent proof from norm-free premises.

The associated power comparison must keep its target fixed. Rejoicing over actual repentance with repentance removed is not the same specified exercise with an otherwise eligible object left intact. Shifāʾ edited 2:181–182 already distinguishes a condition constitutive of a requested realization from an inability to produce a possible target; unlimited power does not require an incoherent removal of that realization's condition. That constrains an alleged loss-of-power inference, but does not reduce actual rejoicing to an external semantic label. Majmūʿ's owned activity, guidance and assistance account carries the positive explanatory claim about the real realization.

## 4. The fair surviving objection and exact limit

The revised preceding report correctly permits a critic to challenge **this realization** as receptive or completing, using the author's claim that its presence is more perfect than its absence. The critic need not posit an originally deficient essential faculty or a universal theory of every condition.

The recovered answer nevertheless constrains that criticism. It must address the case's actual source-owned provision and the source's distinction between excellence realized through one's own adequate activity and excellence furnished by a needed other. It must also explain why this excellent occurrence had to be present before its fitting condition, if prior lack is part of the objection. Merely repeating “a creaturely condition occurs” or “something new occurs” does not independently establish the disputed receipt classification.

A narrower criticism could accept fitting-time realization yet contest the classification of this internal response's dependence on R. The local passages do not provide a detailed efficient account of how J occurs or an independently individuated sequence of intrinsic acts. The majority defense does, however, explicitly give its original provision and self-exercise classification; lack of the detailed mechanism does not erase that developed reason or demote the inherited essential-independence judgment to neutrality.

Neither a micro-mechanism nor an exact clock selector has been established as a further necessary condition of warranted assent. Their absence is a limit on a stronger claim to have disclosed the complete implementation, not a newly imposed deficit in the present account. Calling the occurrence “acquisition” without showing the disputed productive receipt does not supply that stronger criticism.

No new mechanism should be manufactured by substituting Majmūʿ 16:305's representation-origination claim. Nor is a fresh proof of every form of intrinsic nonreceptivity produced here. The live distinction is between having this positively defended account and claiming to have independently disclosed its entire efficient implementation.
