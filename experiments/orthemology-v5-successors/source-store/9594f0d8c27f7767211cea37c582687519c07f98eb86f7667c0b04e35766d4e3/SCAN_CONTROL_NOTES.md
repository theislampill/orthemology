# Bounded visual scan controls: range 2

## Scope and method

After the five electronic-reading deliverables were completed, a subsequent pass performed selected controls against the already acquired [Adwa scan](https://archive.org/download/al-mulakhas/al-risala.pdf). I rendered and visually read the full visible bodies and notes of **21 main-text pages**, with printed labels individually checked: **169, 172–180, 185, 188–189, 197, 203, 215–217 and 244–246**. Their 1-based PDF positions are **176, 179–187, 192, 195–196, 204, 210, 222–224 and 251–253**. I also displayed catalogue PDF positions 42–45, printed 35–38, and used the relevant correction entries. Crops of a few already displayed passages/notes were used to check small type; they add no new page coverage.

The PDF has 625 positions and SHA-256 `97d79af373636137d0845f68702b5a9aa11935046e6451b6d1b0df8d89cd1a51`. Position-minus-seven agrees for these inspected labels; this is not asserted for uninspected pages. Some additional images were rendered while locating passages but **were not inspected**; the receipts, not the image directory, define the reading claim. There was no OCR text layer for the tested page. Source-body judgements below rest on visible images, not inferred text extraction.

This is **not** a full print reading, a direct manuscript collation, or a diplomatic equivalence for every electronic line. “The exemplar/original says” below always means **the newer edition's editorial report** about its exemplar, not my direct inspection of that manuscript. Editorial apparatus and marginal headings are distinct from the author's argument.

## Findings affecting interpretation

### 1. Createdness sentence: a material supplied negation

- Electronic locus: **SF-S001-L0263**.
- Direct control: **printed 169 / PDF 176**, complete visible page.
- Electronic: `فإنه لا يكون إلا حادثا بها لامتناع دوام الحوادث بل لامتناع صدور المحدثات وما لا يتقدم عن موجب بالذات`.
- New print: `فإنه لا يكون إلا حادثًا [لا] لامتناع دوام الحوادث، بل لامتناع صدور المحدثات، وما لا يتقدم عن موجب بالذات`.
- The bracketed **لا** is supported by note 2: `من هامش الأصل وكتب بجوارها: (صح)، وفي المطبوع: (بها).` Note 1, attached to the earlier `إن`, says `كتب فوقها بالأصل: (ص).`

This confirms that the received electronic wording follows the older **بها** reading at this point and omits/replaces the crucial negation. The newer reading denies that impossibility of continuing events is the reason used in this sentence, and instead appeals to the impossibility of new effects from a necessitating cause. It should not be cited as an unqualified assertion that a continuation of events is impossible. The note establishes an editorially reported marginal correction, not access to an autograph.

Catalogue substantial item 7, on PDF42/printed35, also visibly includes `لا لامتناع`; the initial shared transcription's new anchor accidentally omitted **لا**. The corrected reading must retain it.

### 2. Human action and acquisition: `مكتسب`, not `وليس`

- Electronic locus: **SF-S001-L0270**.
- Direct control: **printed 172 / PDF 179**, with preceding and following discussion on printed173–175 also read.
- Electronic: `فإنهم يقولون إنه فاعل مجازا وليس حقيقة`.
- New print: `فإنهم يقولون: إنه فاعل مجازًا مكتسب حقيقة`.

The newer text distinguishes the opponents' claim that the servant acts metaphorically but **acquires really**. The electronic wording loses the positive attribution of acquisition. This does not overturn the author's criticism of ineffective “acquisition”, but it matters when reconstructing what he says the opponents affirm.

**Catalogue locus error:** substantial item 8 visibly cites printed **174**, line11, with `مكتسب` replacing `وليس`. The relevant sentence actually appears on **printed172**, near the foot of the body, and its full context identifies **SF-S001-L0270** beyond reasonable doubt. Printed174 instead concerns recitational voices and contains no such change. The mapping therefore follows the directly inspected body, while retaining the catalogue's discrepant reference rather than silently repairing the catalogue.

### 3. Act/product distinction, created powers and continuing need

- Electronic loci: **SF-S001-L0271–L0276**.
- Direct controls: **printed173–175 / PDF180–182**.

Printed173–174 confirms the distinction between God's act subsisting in him, the separate product, and the human act that is genuinely the servant's. Printed175 affirms powers and natures in animate and inanimate bodies, continuing dependence for occurrence and persistence, and created causes' need for cooperating causes and vulnerability to impediments. It retains `بمشيئته القديمة` in the ongoing-dependence sentence. The print has `فما كان بالأسباب فإنه خالقه وخالق سببه جميعًا`, where the electronic text has `فالله خالقه`; the substance agrees, but this is not a character-for-character identity claim.

The recitational-voice discussion is also present in full adjacent context. It reports a rejected argument that generated sounds are not within human power; it is not the author's own affirmation that a reciter's ordinary voice is God's voice.

### 4. Embryo comparison and duplicate removal

- Electronic loci: **SF-S001-L0277–L0279**.
- Direct control: **printed176 / PDF183**.

The sentence `مع أن الجنين المخلوق في الرحم أكمل من الرحم وقواها` is visible and agrees with the received text. The printed active-intellect discussion proceeds once from “they require evidence that it is a cause” to “what they say in establishing the active intellect is false”; the electronic text repeats an intermediate clause. The comparative source/perfection point is therefore secure as a local argument about a proposed natural producer, but its conversion into an every-source/every-property axiom remains unwarranted.

### 5. The universal wording remains unresolved

- Electronic locus: **SF-S001-L0284**.
- Direct controls: **printed177–179 / PDF184–186**.

Printed178 likewise reads `فهذا أيضًا لا يوجد في الخارج كليًّا يمنع تصوره من وقوع الشركة فيه`, without an additional **لا** before `يمنع`. The surrounding argument about the absolutely simple existent being mental, and external existence being particular and individuated, agrees in substance. **This control does not correct the suspicious universal-definition phrase.** It shows that the problem is not confined to the received electronic copy. No conjectural negation has been inserted into either source or notes.

### 6. Ibn Sīnā quotation: `قرر` and several reported quotation variants

- Electronic locus: **SF-S001-L0300**, continuing into L0301–L0302.
- Direct control: **printed185 / PDF192**.

The body has `فلم يعارض العقل فيما قرر فعله أو رؤياه منها`, whereas the electronic text has `قدر`. Note6 says `كذا بالأصل، وفي المطبوع: (قدر)`. This confirms lesser catalogue item14 contextually, not through an isolated global word match. The same page's apparatus lists several differences from the cited *Ishārāt*: these are quotation-source variants, not necessarily corrections to Ibn Taymiyya's wording. The empirical argument still distinguishes observation of an event from subsequently seeking its cause.

### 7. Two occurrences of `يخلقه` are intentional in the newer reading

- Electronic locus: **SF-S001-L0306**.
- Direct control: **printed188 / PDF195**, with printed189/PDF196 read for the continuing note and adjacent sentence.
- New print: `وإن كان ما يشاؤه ويخلقه يخلقه بأسباب يخلقها وبحكمة يريدها هو سبحانه وتعالى`.
- Electronic: `وإن كان ما يشاؤه ويخلقه بأسباب يخلقها وبحكمة يريدها هو سبحانه وتعالى`.

Catalogue lesser item11 explicitly reports that the earlier editor removed one occurrence as apparently redundant. The newer body retains the relative clause and its resumption: what he wills and creates, he creates through causes. This matters to syntax and preserves the distinction between the object of creation and its causal mode; it is not evidence for autonomous causes.

### 8. Ordinary kinds may occur by unusual causes

- Electronic loci: **SF-S001-L0320–L0321**.
- Direct control: **printed197 / PDF204**.
- New print: `وكذلك شفاء المريض والزلزلة والخسف، وإن كان قد يحصل بأسباب غير معتادة كما يحصل نوعه بأسباب معتادة`.
- Electronic: `وكذلك شفاء المريض وحدوث الخسف والهلاك يحصل بأسباب معتادة كما يحصل نوعه بأسباب معتادة`.

The newer reading expressly concedes an unusual causal route to an event whose kind also has ordinary causes. The subsequent distinction between extraordinary **kind** and extraordinary **degree** is present. The range's critique should therefore be framed as a dispute over adequate and exhaustive explanation, not as a claim that every healing or earth movement must have an ordinary cause on every occasion.

### 9. Humans rather than only soothsayers

- Electronic locus: **SF-S001-L0334**, with L0335–L0336 in adjacent body.
- Direct control: **printed203 / PDF210**.
- New page begins `لكثير من الإنس ... بكثير من الأخبار`; the electronic phrase is `كالجن المخبرين لكثير من الكهان بكثير من الأخبار`.
- Note1: `كذا بالأصل، وفي المطبوع: (الكهان).`

The new reading broadens the stated recipients to humans rather than confining the phrase to soothsayers. The author's larger distinction among psychic, demonic and angelic information routes remains in view. This is a correction to the claimed recipients, not independent verification of the demonic reports.

### 10. Intercession means asking God, not `ورسوله`

- Electronic locus: **SF-S001-L0381**.
- Direct controls: **printed215–217 / PDF222–224**.
- Electronic: `والشفاعة عندهم ليس معناها دعاء الله ورسوله كما هو مذهب المسلمين`.
- New print: `والشفاعة عندهم ليس معناها دعاء الله وسؤاله كما هو مذهب المسلمين`.

Printed215 plainly confirms **وسؤاله**. The single note on that page references al-Bukhārī no.3218 from Ibn ʿAbbās for Gabriel's visits; it is not a textual note on this word. The correction itself is additionally documented in substantial catalogue item10. Printed216 completes the contrast with attachment to intermediaries and a mirror/light analogy, then begins the discussion of al-Ghazālī and his critics. The control therefore preserves both the word and its actual explanatory context.

The apparatus on printed216 records contested attribution of *al-Maḍnūn*, including a report from al-Dhahabī which rejects its attribution to al-Ghazālī. This is an **editorial** caveat and supports caution about using the polemic as straightforward biography. The continuation page217 was read, including its visible biographical notes; the last note continues beyond that page and remains bounded accordingly.

### 11. Necessary existence does not yet identify the distinct Maker

- Electronic loci: **SF-S002-L0040–L0047**, especially **L0043–L0046**.
- Direct controls: **printed244–246 / PDF251–253**.

Printed245 presents naturalist denial, the theological eternalists' alleged implication, and the objection that their abstract beings exist only mentally. It opens the concession that a necessary existent is admitted by all sides. Printed246 continues:

`فإثبات موجود واجب بنفسه لا يتضمن الإقرار بالصانع إن لم يثبت أنه مغاير للعالم`.

It also confirms that the disputed method's result is at most a necessary existent, and that what matters is its being other than this world. The subsequent critique of denying attributes to exclude the composite world is present. Thus the electronic range's distinction between a bare necessary-bearer conclusion and the particular Creator conclusion is **directly print-controlled**. It is not merely a thematic paraphrase of an anti-universal passage elsewhere.

## Limits and effect on earlier deliverables

The electronic notes remain a faithful account of the text actually received. They must now be read alongside these controls wherever a corrected clause is used. The important updates are the supplied negation at L0263, real acquisition in the opponents' reported view at L0270, the restored resumed verb at L0306, unusual-causes concession at L0320, humans at L0334, and asking God at L0381. The particular-Creator distinction is positively verified, while the suspected universal-definition difficulty remains unresolved.

The controls reinforce rather than erase the appraisal's caution: distinguish roles and quantifiers; keep the Creator's actual causal system separate from a universal every-original/every-requisite SourceMode thesis; do not turn reported opponents' claims into the author's own position. No new Lean result, whole-work verdict or full-page-edition correspondence follows.
