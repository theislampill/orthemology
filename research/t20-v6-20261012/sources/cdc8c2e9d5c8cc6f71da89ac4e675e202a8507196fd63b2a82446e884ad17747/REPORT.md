## 3. The constructive inference

### Proposition versus total cognitive content

The central proposition remains affirmed across the exchange. That does not prove that **all** propositional content is unchanged: an encounter can also disclose this occurrence and its features. The sight interpretation concerns a mode of encounter and additional grounding; the reassurance language concerns settledness of heart. These roles can cooperate without being identical.
