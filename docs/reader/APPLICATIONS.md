## Start with what is being preserved

An application should say what its variables represent, what operation is being performed, and what would make that operation adequate. The sample below contains **source-argued epistemic interpretation**, **ordinary mathematical constructions**, and **worked source-relative examples**. They are not interchangeable levels of implementation or empirical evidence.

The [application work catalogue](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#application-studies-contracts-and-runtime-outlines) now accounts for the fifty files in the baseline `applications/` directory as ten work-level entries and their supporting records. That is one bounded collection, not every application scattered across the research corpus. The [DAEE crosswalk][daee], [latent-state application][latent] and [agentic-runtime guide][agentic] remain their scoped source entrances.

## Choose the repair after separating the defects

In the principal manuscript's [result/pathway matrix](../../manuscript/orthemma-ortheme-systems-revised-draft.md#84-the-result--pathway-matrix), a correct result and an adequate method are separate questions. The [worked stopped-clock case](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#main-exposition) explains why rechecking the same successful output is not enough: the relevant adverse control changes the misleading cue and tests whether the method still tracks the property it was meant to assess. A deficient method needs a method repair; a rare false output from an adequate method needs correction without automatically declaring the entire procedure unsound. This is the source's diagnostic use of the distinction, not a new performance trial.

The [multi-actor note](../../theory/orthemic-multi-actor-conflict-note.md#3-formal-residue-assessed-against-the-existing-core) adds a related comparison problem. Actor A's desired profiles and actor B's desired profiles need not inhabit the same space. Pull both targets back to the shared set of reachable occurrences before asking whether one occurrence can satisfy both. Two differently informed descriptions of one game position do not become two positions, and two isomorphic goals do not become the same goal. This preserves a meaningful test of cooperation or conflict instead of intersecting unlike objects. Neither example licenses a clinical assessment of a person.

## Recognition and corrective reasoning

The [Sound Reason substrate][sound] gives reasoning a constructive role when basic recognition is obscured: clarify the question, identify the obstructing premise or acquired distortion, and restore access to the cognition the account treats as basic. In that account, wider non-collusive concordance can vindicate cognitive functioning; it is not only a transmission-provenance check.

The [project distinction note][concrete] helps keep this from becoming a scalar “correct/incorrect person” judgement. Instantiation, configured pathway adequacy, truth linkage, source dependence and subject-relative warrant need their own bearers. The [later qualification report][retained] retains affirmative source-bound appraisals while distinguishing ordinary knowledge from infallibility.

**Application standing:** a philosophical account and project-level distinctions, not an empirically validated diagnostic instrument. A transcript, popularity measure or graph of reports does not establish a person's fiṭrah, proper functioning or metaphysical warrant. The reader must see both the constructive account and the additional premises needed to apply it.

## Noetic representations and numerical dynamics

The [tensor and coercive works](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#tensor-moments-and-coercive-dynamics) provide actual conditional mathematical descriptions of full moment histories. The [Fisher and sidecar works](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#fisher-geometry-and-retained-information) provide an actual probability manifold, metric and information-preservation obstruction.

A proposed noetic application must separately specify how a numerical state or audit law represents the relevant noetic structure. It must identify what the moments or labels mean, how they are observed, what assumptions justify the dynamics, and which result-preservation contract matters. Renaming coordinates “belief” or “soundness” supplies none of those bindings.

This distinction cuts both ways. The lack of a validated psychological interpretation does not turn the metric and theorem into mere metaphors. Conversely, a nonpositive derivative of a stipulated numerical loss does not establish epistemic improvement. The mathematical construction, proposed interpretation and evidence about actual cognition must remain separately described.

## Coordination records

The [finite-mediator paper][coordination] is useful when evaluating a proposed explanation of coordination under its declared Gaussian model. Its sharp fixed-horizon bound states the capture performance compatible with a complete mediator of a given alphabet size.

To apply it, establish the conditional-independence cut and account for side information. A controller's short code, finite local state or quantised display might not carry the whole mediation information. A matching retrospective construction is not already a forward-causal physical controller. The [result entry](RESULT-INDEX.md#finite-mediator) links the exact ordinary proof and the scope that must travel with the bound.

## Choosing an equality contract for a proof-carrying representation

The [identity work sequence](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#identity-representations-and-finite-proof) gives a concrete design comparison. An exposed polynomial and an opaque atom can evaluate to the same raw term yet differ in whether they carry the specified type certificate. Replacing one with the other therefore needs more than evaluation equality. The [bridge construction][t13-bridge] supplies one particular closed-conversion remedy; it does not preserve every proof claim by declaration.

A second question concerns `I` and `SKK`. The original semantics agrees at the selected polymorphic carrier, but the old derivation rules do not inhabit the corresponding identity. The [typed HasE repair][t14-repair] is a separately named way to derive that identity while keeping raw conversion and the old judgments unchanged. A tool using the repair would have to identify its rule set, check its side conditions and retain that distinction in exchanged certificates. The reader guide proposes no runtime implementation or automatic migration.

For a proof-checking interface, [finite supplied certificates][t14-certificates] and open-ended proof search are different services. A checked supplied proof can justify acceptance at its own scope; unsuccessful search is not itself a checked refutation. This is an application of the written distinction, not an implementation or new decision procedure delivered by the refactor.

## Joint feasibility across avenues

The [existing join][join] gives a worked connection between occurrence-sensitive language and empirical completion. Its contribution is not merely that both subjects contain uncertainty.

In the selected [occurrence records][occurrences], the complete source-relative tuples are:

```text
(M, M, G)
(G, G, G)
```

The fixture labels `M` and `G` denote Messenger and God. Treating each coordinate independently also permits `(M,G,G)` and `(G,M,G)`, but neither has a complete witness in that selected relation. A partial `(M,M,unspecified)` occurrence does not authorise either addition. Direct exposition, attributed interpretation and reported preference remain different source roles.

The [missing-outcome analysis][missing] has an analogous feasibility issue. Suppose two binary coordinates each separately permit zero and one, but jointly only these completions are admissible:

```text
(0, 1)
(1, 0)
```

Their sum is always one. The Cartesian relaxation admits sums zero and two; those extremes are not attainable in the declared feasible relation.

**What transfers:** preserve the jointly admissible relation before projecting to coordinate answers or optimising an objective. The Cartesian set can still be a labelled outer relaxation. **What does not transfer:** the linguistic records do not prove dependence among actual missing outcomes, and the statistical example does not authenticate Arabic sources or decide an interpretative truth dispute. The [existing review][join-review] preserves that boundary.

## Finite deadlines and the no-report branch

The [bounded-access paper, section 6][t12-access], supplies a concrete test of what counts as acquired information. In its stated report model let `A` have prior probability one half, and let `Q_m = 1 - 2^(-m)`. Each exact report has posterior zero for `A`, but a finite deadline also leaves `D_m`, the event that no report has arrived.

Substituting deadline **two** into the source's formulas gives

```text
P(H₁) = 1/4; P(H₂) = 1/8; P(D₂) = 5/8.
P(A | H₁) = P(A | H₂) = 0.
P(A | D₂) = 4/5.
```

The weighted posterior is still `0 + 0 + (5/8)(4/5) = 1/2`. The appropriate finite-deadline forecast has prior quadratic loss `1/10`, compared with `1/4` for the constant prior forecast. Deleting the no-report cell would erase precisely the information state that restores finite-partition averaging. These numbers are a checked substitution into the existing analytic formulas, not observed experimental data or a new result.

The [total integer construction][t12-integer] makes completed reporting available under its supplied-input interface; that does not make its runtime law tight. The [clarification][t12-clarification] preserves the distinction between the dominated completed policy and impossibility of the supporting architecture. Applying this example to actual inquiry requires the law, information protocol, operational access and scoring/action norm to be warranted together. It does not redefine reason-responsive correction as merely a favourable score.


## Choosing the bearer of a noetic claim

The [multi-target note][app-targets] separates a discourse, the subject at a specified time, a runtime or control state, an emitted response, and the subject's later uptake. This prevents a statement about one bearer from quietly becoming a statement about another. The [evidence-bound example][app-claims] attaches claim, target, analysis version and evidence instead of treating a transcript as transparent access to its author.

A useful application therefore asks **what was inspected and which claim that inspection can support**. Correcting a response is not by itself evidence of a changed belief; an admissible control step does not establish the truth of the proposition; and a subject's later acquisition needs its own evidence. These distinctions give correction a more precise target without reducing the positive account of recognition to a diagnostic score.

The [qualitative tawātur example][app-tawatur] keeps one proposition's identity fixed while giving different subjects different access and warrant dispositions. Its objective truth is not adjudicated by the example. Dependence among reports, the proposition's truth, acquisition by a particular subject and the broader cognitive-vindication account in the [recognition discussion](#recognition-and-corrective-reasoning) are related questions, not interchangeable predicates. The file illustrates those distinctions; it does not establish a historical proposition or prescribe a universal count of transmitters.

## A corrective step that legitimately ends in HOLD

The [existing corrective-transition example][app-correction] starts with a criterion-import defect and a separate unresolved source-independence dependency. Its hard constraints prohibit soul-state assertions and burden deletion, and require provenance and uncertainty to survive. Only after that feasibility filter does it rank the two eligible routes. It selects criterion clarification, addresses that defect, and **carries the source-independence dependency forward**.

The whole-state reread therefore returns **HOLD**, with `runtime_closure: false`. The example records an admissible, pathway-adequate transition, leaves strict soundness unclaimed and records human uptake as unobserved. It is a constructive example of making a real local repair without pretending that the whole problem is solved. Removing the residual dependency to manufacture closure would fail its declared burden-accounting contract.

The [sound-descent comparison][app-descent] explains the order of operations: hard constraints decide admissibility before a declared partial order ranks feasible candidates. Incomparability, newly discovered burdens or an unanswered prerequisite can justify a hold. A shorter burden list is not automatically a better state. Its early G1/G2 interpretation is not the literal [full-source Fisher system](RESULT-INDEX.md#fisher-geometry) studied in the later mathematics; the two accounts need their own scopes.

The [typed semantic-operator contract][app-operators] makes the runtime account inspectable. Route pressure returns a ranking witness; an event transition returns a control-state delta; field and loop diagnostics require their own typed inputs; whole-state reread precedes closure. Its factive claim-relative strict-soundness predicate is distinct from nonfactive admissibility and pathway adequacy. The familiar symbols do not license replacing these typed operations with arbitrary differential calculus, nor do they reduce the separately established conditional geometry to a metaphor.

**Use boundary:** this is an authored fixture and offline contract, not a fresh run of an external agent or evidence of human restoration. The [work entry](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#feasibility-first-corrective-descent) connects the maintained v2 example, historical v1 example and supporting checks without counting the examples as additional papers.

## Learning without silently changing the analysis

The [latent-state study][app-learning] distinguishes **inference within a fixed model**, **representation/model learning**, **state-repertoire revision**, and **a change of the governing analysis version**. Those operations can affect different earlier claims. A learner's changed estimate need not be a world-state transition; a changed model need not be a newly discovered normative standard.

The [update-coupling contract][app-coupling] records what an update invalidates, which cases reopen and what transport to a new analysis requires. In particular, silence is not a transport argument, and a blanket declaration that all earlier verdicts survive is forbidden. Earlier analyses remain separately citable. The study's many-to-many profile relation and analysis-relative merger criteria also prevent a numerical threshold from silently identifying two psychological states or proving their orthogonality.

**What this contributes:** an update can be assessed together with the evidence and prior conclusions it affects, rather than being celebrated simply because the numerical representation changed. The source-reported computational exemplification remains distinct from biological evidence, a unique account of a human mind or a metaphysical conclusion. The [catalogue entry](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#latent-state-learning-and-governed-updates) holds the work description and its supporting records; this page shows the use route.

## Agentic coordination and writeback outlines

The [Somnus candidate inventory][app-somnus], [Hermes writeback profile][app-writeback] and [collective/transclusion profile][app-collective] are **outline-only, unimplemented downstream proposals** in these sources. They nevertheless specify useful prospective contracts: bound what a reconsideration process may change; keep receiving material distinct from adopting it; and distinguish independent convergence, exchange followed by local reassessment, and coordinated council work.

The writeback profile still requires the named upstream source, licence, commit and tests to be verified. The collective profile retains local authority, provenance and dissent: transmission to another participant does not itself authorise a local write or transfer the sender's acceptance. These are proposed engineering distinctions, not an already deployed memory service or an executable scheduler.

A contributor should therefore read the [three work entries](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#somnus-candidate-inventory) and their complete profiles before forming an implementation obligation. This refactor makes them discoverable; it does not implement them, activate a service, grant writeback authority or import current external repositories into the bounded census.

## A complete finite test without arbitrary-program equivalence

The [restricted identity paper](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#a-complete-restricted-identity-checker) gives a concrete service for exchanging natural-number expressions whose literal compiled endpoints use the stated P01AC semantics. Consider its two-variable example:

```text
e: if x+y=0 then 7 else (x+1)(y+1)
f: if x=0 then (if y=0 then 7 else y+1)
   else xy+x+y+1
```

Their four zero/nonzero cells have the same respective polynomials: `7`, `y+1`, `x+1`, and `xy+x+y+1`. This is not random testing: complete mask normalisation is justified by the grammar's nonnegative arithmetic and zero tests. The [source example][t15-paper-4] and [Python reference][t15-json-reference] make the input and certificate inspectable; the [Lean checker][t15-checker-lean] has its own complete typed interface.

A contrasting source example compares `x` with `x²`. Agreement at zero and one is insufficient: the positive cell's degree-bound grid includes `1,2,3` and finds the witness `2`. Arity zero has an empty-tuple **input**, not no inputs. Missing that cell or using a fixed arbitrary test budget would break the contract.

Use the procedure for its admitted expression syntax; bind external endpoints literally to the compiler output and preserve the chosen certificate representation. Resource exhaustion is inconclusive, not an inequality verdict. Neither the theorem nor the Python parser recognises arbitrary endpoint programs as members of the fragment. The [separate root-test/univariate interface](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#equality-tests-and-the-univariate-exception) is a distinct extension, not an undocumented relaxation of zero tests.

## Designing a collective observation without claiming attribution

The [collective paper][t15-paper-6] constructs one-input and two-input mechanisms with the same final answers in law. Keeping the final score does not help for this matched pair. Keeping one **same-trial** threshold bit from the otherwise hidden second evidence value can help, provided the stimulus/announcer conditioning and non-disturbing, independently noisy recording assumptions are justified.

In the source's mathematical illustration, joint bit/answer success has probability `1/4` under the one-input mechanism and `5/12` under the two-input mechanism. With the specified known gap `1/6`, 216 independent fixed-design repetitions and a joint count threshold of 72 bound each error by `exp(−3) < 1/20`. These are derived design numbers—not a report that a study of 216 people or trials was run, and not certainty from a finite observation. [The observation-contract guide][t15-attribution-guide] states the assumptions and derivation.

### A one-sided monitor is not a two-answer judge

When the marginals and positive separation are unknown, the paper's exact-real sequential covariance rule can control false positive selection under independence and eventually detect each fixed positive covariance almost surely. It may continue indefinitely under the null. Turning a timeout into a negative verdict is not licensed by that theorem: a sufficiently near-null alternative can resemble any finite null stopping prefix.

The next design decision is therefore what service is needed: a known-gap fixed-sample test, a one-sided sequential detector, or a two-answer procedure under genuinely stronger separation assumptions. Actual causal attribution additionally needs a warranted mechanism model. Linked association alone does not select an actual participant's causal role, and a floating-point implementation needs its own conservative numerical qualification.

## Using a reanalysis without turning it into causal attribution

The [portable reanalysis work](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#portable-reanalysis-and-the-paired-comparison) offers a concrete pattern: preserve the paired experimental unit and comparison direction, check input identities before parsing, retain the specified weighting and missingness treatment, compare separately written numerical cores, and expose only the declared aggregate output. Its [driver](../../experiments/orthemology-v5-successors/source-store/20110e73fd822e21651064578946cdf910edc493c973953b2c7d1bb74481dd94/reanalyse.py) checks timing missingness without using it to remove response observations, and protects the input files against output overwrite, including a hard-linked destination.

That procedure can reproduce the supported performance contrast without recovering the unrecorded private responses in immediate discussion. The [linked-observation design](#designing-a-collective-observation-without-claiming-attribution) is a distinct proposal for discriminating specified mechanisms; it is not a finding already latent in a second copy of the aggregate. A repeat computation is not another participant sample.

## Assessing a supported continuation claim

The report's [finite-construction assessments](../../experiments/orthemology-v5-successors/source-store/69051582e889b364891ee1e6b59782fe025c0334eaa423e9fd822e5f256f08ee/Orthemology_Fifteenth_Research_Final_v1_20261005.md#finite-construction-and-renewed-function) supply a useful claim ladder: a running successor; renewed ability to perform the declared construction role; preservation or adequate reacquisition of consequential state; further continuation; and independence from a specified source or support. Evidence for one level should not be made to answer all the others. Nor should support dependence erase a narrower capacity actually evidenced.

This connects to [governed learning and transport](#learning-without-silently-changing-the-analysis): preserving an executable role does not automatically transport every claim, commitment or analysis binding. The report's two external-study assessments remain at their own protocols and witnesses. This guide does not execute self-replication, endorse the agentic outlines as deployed systems or claim a same-person criterion.

## From reading a proposal to using an implementation

For any application reached through this guide, identify whether the target is a manuscript, worked fixture, reference implementation, executable package, or an empirical result. Follow the target package's actual maintained entry point and its declared environment.

Historical wrappers and archive-relative commands can describe a different workspace. The [source recovery guide][recovery] distinguishes exact source export from actual execution and retains unavailable-input boundaries. A source-resolver success is not evidence that a controller, cognitive model or experiment works.

Remaining application material in other research collections still needs work-level reconciliation. The fifty-file directory mapping does not establish that all repository applications have been examined, run or accepted.

## Choose an inquiry without erasing its target

The [revision-cost work](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#the-related-reflexive-revision-manuscript) has a positive efficiency example: with four address bits and sixteen data bits, read the address and then its selected bit. Five adaptive queries suffice, whereas a fixed individual-bit query set must include all twenty bits. This is the source's exact probe-cost comparison, not measured elapsed time or a promise that all adaptive inquiry wins.

The [composition work](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#information-and-composition) explains why this style of reasoning needs a different model when probing changes the subject. In its three-initial-state example, each pair can be separated by some experiment; either first action can nevertheless merge opposite initial labels irreversibly. A known *current* state after erasure does not recover the *initial* answer. Resettable copies would change that example's premises. The paired result is useful when choosing a diagnostic or a software inspection: establish what the operation preserves before treating all individually informative probes as one safe strategy.

For a revision of the testing method itself, the source separately asks whether the predecessor permits the change and whether retained evidence still answers the successor's question. Neither an old receipt nor a cheaper representation supplies its own authority. These are model-based design lessons, not measurements of this repository's maintenance cost.

## Refinement budgets and licence renewal

The source's [fair-bit example](../../experiments/orthemology-v5/mathematics/REFINEMENT_CALCULUS.md#3-infinite-chain-a-necessary-residual) has zero defect at every finite observation and defect one at the complete stream. A prefix-testing service cannot claim that its finite checks establish the stronger full-stream certificate. Conversely, an almost-surely terminating observation with countably many outputs is a different, zero-defect outcome object under these assumptions.

For reuse, the [operational transport theorem](../../experiments/orthemology-v5/mathematics/ABSTRACT_OWOU.md#4-exact-licence-transport-theorem) explains why disabling and re-enabling a permission cannot revive an old captured version. A new semantic transport proof may justify reissue, but is not itself the old licence. These are source-model illustrations, not measured deployments or a theorem about distributed revocation.

## Delivering a controller without changing what it observes

The [four-bit runtime wrapper](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#from-supplied-machines-and-histories-to-literal-p02-execution) marks accepted receipts and serialises the pre-receipt state/action. A waiting bit must not be treated as a fresh observation. The [full-phase compiler](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#a-complete-boolean-phase-source-with-a-canonical-law-bridge) requires finite selector certificates and the actual environment binding before importing a decoded winning-law theorem. Giving the policy the model index or raw sampler state changes the information problem.

For resource planning, use the cost actually proved. An [exponential logical-error bound](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#the-actual-generated-controller-has-finite-and-exponential-error-budgets) says neither that the last error occurs early nor that physical waiting, memory or CPU are bounded. Likewise [correct receipt-law projection](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#exact-rational-receipts-do-not-preserve-every-physical-stream-property) can discard nonatomic randomness in the physical output. These examples let an agent select a usable theorem contract rather than turn every successful implementation layer into a blanket guarantee.

## Diagnosis, integrity information and safe selection

Correctly learning a governing rule does not automatically identify a safe actuator. The [action-sufficient disclosure construction](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#truthful-disclosure-that-selects-a-safe-action) separates these tasks and supplies a minimal zero-error message menu under a static fault model. The certificate must retain its referent and action binding: a correctly transmitted number can still select the wrong root after an unverified permutation. An authentic but false message is not an integrity certificate.

The [integer confidence-budget evaluator](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#exact-integer-confidence-budgets) is another positive bridge to use: it gives exact conservative arithmetic thresholds once the controller-law and parameter assumptions have been justified. It is not a replacement for checking those assumptions or a forecast of typical physical runtime.

## Common correction before complete foundational agreement

The [semantic-repair account](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#objective-norm-fixing-and-warranted-common-repair) shows why an archive and its derived transcription may need different operations. Preserve the original source unchanged; correct the transcription against it. That is a genuine joint repair once the two bearers and their targets are distinguished, not permission to force the same bytes simultaneously to remain and change. Under uncertainty, a “repair” justified for one hidden interpretation is insufficient when other retained interpretations can still make it wrong.

The same precision prevents overreach from the foundational work. A fault-tolerant implementation may need several operationally independent components even while its metaphysical source account affirms one complete Creator. Conversely, a diagram with many operational inputs does not establish many original metaphysical sources. The [world-bridge](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#warranted-correction-and-complete-actual-origination) and [attribute](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#original-sourcehood-agency-wisdom-and-disclosure) texts state the different dependence relations instead of identifying them by vocabulary alone.

## Selecting a safe repair is not the same as diagnosing a defect

In the [action-integrity construction](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#action-relevant-integrity-after-exact-diagnosis), an accurate unanimous report need not reveal which actuator is safe. Repeating the same report or copying one root adds no new integrity. The constructive alternative is to justify containment: if a failed attempt genuinely does nothing and preserves future options, trying enough distinct roots can restore the whole rule. A “failure” label without those semantics is insufficient.

The [codebook results](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#the-complete-fixed-codebook-classification) can guide a stipulated action interface, but the [timing comparison](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#when-the-information-and-corruption-arrive) must travel with it. Independence established before an adversary observes a private choice cannot be carried past disclosure of that choice unchanged. These are source-bound design consequences, not claims that any current Orthemology runtime already implements the assumed containment or adversary model.

## A rule update, a payload repair and an execution grant

The [criterion-installer example](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#installing-a-criterion-is-not-editing-the-draft) is useful when a system's current validator accepts something the retained task excludes. Installing the adequate rule changes actual decisions but does not silently grant permission to alter the rejected data. The [compiled two-stage reference](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#compiled-installation-and-repair-with-distinct-grants) then supplies that separate operation under its own current grant.

For a fault-prone deployment, the [live-interlock and attribution studies](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#charged-live-interlocks-and-repair-after-quiescence) identify additional conditions that an implementation must actually satisfy: genuine shared-dependency accounting, non-bypassable complete-command gates, durable cancellation, independently serviced control paths and the correct consent model. The model's constructive repair is not evidence that a real platform already implements those conditions.


## Retrying a repaired process without replaying a cancelled command

The [actual-batch work](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#actual-batch-success-and-complete-envelope-freshness) offers a worked protocol-design lesson: failed attempts can change cancellation memory even when the target data are unchanged. Delivering a delayed policy therefore need not make the old actor's envelopes usable. The exact surviving-trial theorem states when another eligible path will land; it does not reveal the hidden fault map to a real client.

This is an application of stated source-model theorems, not an implemented distributed service. A deployment must supply authentic policy/observations, full-command identity, durable root-wide memories and its actual service/interference contract. The [protected shared schedule](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#a-protected-schedule-is-not-an-unconditional-time-bound) is a different classical reference construction, with logical event counts rather than physical deadlines.

## Repairing while learning rather than learning before every repair

The [generated binary experiment](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#a-generated-experiment-joins-score-repair-and-causal-control) makes the norm-to-observation law and permissions explicit: each ordinary repair also supplies fresh evidence, and the common controller eventually chooses score-correct repairs. The [finite-query obstruction](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#a-finite-query-restoration-obstruction-and-its-positive-sharpness) instead makes continued information acquisition target-bad. These are different design contracts, not a positive theorem contradicted by a negative one.

For an agent application, identify which actions genuinely preserve the target, which observations are freshly acquired, and how the target relation is justified. Counting copied reports as independent observations invalidates the inference mechanism. For multi-standard correction, the [anchor corollary](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#one-anchor-can-repair-an-entire-compatible-family) gives a constructive route under actual structural compatibility; pairwise intersections of arbitrary repair sets are not enough.

## Do not optimise the wrong success event

The [two-receipt expected-score counterexample](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#improving-expected-loss-while-every-possible-report-fails) is a useful test for an agent evaluation design. A lower average score at every fixed truth does not imply that any emitted report meets the all-truth repair condition. State which event is required before choosing a learner or a metric.

A [safe rational certificate](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#an-executable-rational-certificate-under-an-actual-source-law) is constructive but permits abstention. An [always-issued empirical policy](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#always-reporting-can-be-eventually-correct-without-all-time-safety) provides eventual correctness with early errors. A [sparser declared evaluation clock](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#changing-the-evaluation-clock-buys-a-different-budget) can reduce counted errors by buying additional observations, but cannot conceal required intermediate outputs. Actual receipt freshness, permitted actions, norm identity and physical cost remain implementation obligations; these are not empirical claims about a deployed agent.

## Choose a loss and reporting interface before transferring a learning guarantee

The [sparse-prior construction](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#spacing-can-decide-whether-a-finite-error-budget-exists) and [rational implementation](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#an-executable-rational-witness-with-an-honest-resource-boundary) make a positive design route concrete: exploit the declared source geometry, retain the actual receipt prefix, and select a rule whose tolerance and component-shape assumptions match the application. A finite mean is stronger than eventual correctness; a cap that stops reporting changes an infinite compulsory-report contract.

The [nonconvex-prior example](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#two-successful-priors-may-have-no-successful-proper-mixture) supplies a useful model-selection check. An unlabelled mixture of two successful composite priors is not automatically handled by randomly choosing their successful procedures. Supplying the true side label makes combination possible, but adds information that an independent random seed cannot replace. Do not infer an actual system has the theorem's infinitely many small signal scales without evidence.

At critical width, a correct implementation can still be used under the wrong statistical promise: [uniform whole-cluster mass and edge concentrations](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#at-critical-width-the-internal-shape-matters) can have opposite lifetime outcomes. The runtime cannot establish the distributional promise from a finite word. [Finite-horizon continuity](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#stable-finite-horizons-can-conceal-infinite-lifetime-cost) is therefore not a certificate of a finite lifetime budget.

## A policy, a transcript and a programme are different inputs

A support certificate can yield [one lawful physical-action policy](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#recursive-support-and-the-same-physical-policy-law) for the stated finite controlled-iid problem. Its pointwise safety condition concerns feasible **action-compatible** transcripts. Feeding a forged history with inconsistent action fields into a replay function does not challenge that theorem; it violates the input contract. An implementation must either establish compatibility or reject the supplied record.

For [literal indexed programmes](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#literal-indexed-observers-and-their-scheduled-runtime), invalid syntax with a specified default, valid evaluation still waiting for fuel, and successful execution of the intended source must also remain distinct. Exact per-tick scheduling proves semantic output equality, not a practical latency bound. These are useful positive interfaces for an agent choosing which evidence to demand before execution; they do not authenticate its installed binary, environment or normative authority.
