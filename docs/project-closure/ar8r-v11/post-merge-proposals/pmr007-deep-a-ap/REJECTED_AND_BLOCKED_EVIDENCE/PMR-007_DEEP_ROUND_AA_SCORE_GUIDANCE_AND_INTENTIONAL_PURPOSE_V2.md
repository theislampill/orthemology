## 2. CGIP-1A — internal causal-guidance characterization

Within the declared deterministic SCM:

```text

iff
f_A(0) != f_A(1).
```

**Proof.** Under `do(Score=0)` and `do(Score=1)`, `A` takes `f_A(0)` and
`f_A(1)`. The interventions differ exactly when the function values differ.

This is causal relevance inside the frozen SCM. It is not empirical causal
certification or proof that the SCM is complete and correct.
