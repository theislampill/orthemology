## Selecting the next investigation

Choose the strongest feasible operation that can change a live result or premise. State its current positive argument, surviving rival or obstruction, exact source/evidence basis, and discriminating operation. Rank by substantive opportunity and significance across the programme, not by ease of generating another theorem or proximity to a theological conclusion.

The following are candidate entry points, not a mandatory sequence or five new campaigns:

### A. A concrete warranted correction interface

Use an actual source-bound case already represented in the foundational work. Establish the relevant observations, source roles, version changes and justified challenge scopes. Determine whether the formal sidecar or support system preserves the distinctions needed for the case. A successful finite model earns application credit only as far as the interpretation is defended.

### B. One unresolved source-critical or practical inference

Pursue the exact expected-publicity comparison if responsive historical material is available, or a concrete particular-Wisdom/authoritative-content application with usable evidence. Preserve the positive baseline and avoid returning to already addressed weaker objections. If an unavailable source blocks this operation, preserve that local obstruction and select another worthwhile frontier.

### C. A genuinely effective timed or coupled-model representation

Select a bounded non-finite timing family, repeated uncertain-kernel family or nonlinear corrected family. Prove an effective equality, approximation or optimisation contract that preserves the relevant joint semantics. Distinguish finite-dimensional coordinates, exact attainable sets and conservative outer bounds. Do not reuse a vertex theorem outside its affine domain.

### D. A source-to-execution bridge

Use the existing literal compiler/kernel/probability results to close one precisely specified gap between source semantics, lowered representation, host execution, serialised data and external randomness. Bind the actual implementation and relevant dependencies. Repeatedly proving the same source-level theorem is not progress on the host gap.

### E. Mathematical growth where a consequential opportunity exists

Extend the inherited representation, geometry, moment, continuation or inquiry results where a specific new hypothesis class or preserved structure yields a worthwhile theorem, obstruction or algorithm. Give independently consequential mathematics its own credit. Do not force a noetic application or continue sampling refinements merely because they are locally productive.

## Evidence and stopping discipline

A mathematical proof, source-supported interpretation, successful finite experiment, implementation replay and actual-world warrant remain separate. Repeated readings can correct interpretation but are not independent witnesses. A countermodel must preserve the proposition it challenges; a sophisticated rival is not automatically a live obligation. A critic can nevertheless undercut a real premise without constructing a complete alternative universe.

For an individual investigation, stop when the stated discriminator has a justified result, a precise obstruction, or no worthwhile remaining authorised operation. Then reassess the wider programme. A theorem, package or blocked source is not automatically a programme-wide stopping point.

Track active scientific work prospectively. Do not retroactively certify T20's six-hour floor or count packaging, waiting, inventory and repeated verification as new research. Any T21 duration requirement must be taken from the owner's actual authorisation; this charter does not invent one.
