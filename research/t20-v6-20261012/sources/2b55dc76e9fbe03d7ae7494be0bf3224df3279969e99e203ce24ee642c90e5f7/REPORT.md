## 1 The actual primary entry is explicit

The retained electronic Iṣfahāniyya passage begins by distinguishing two premises: possible beings actually exist; possible beings require a necessary existent (embedded L175–180). It does not take the first premise to follow from the intelligibility of the second. At L179 the author insists on establishing an actual possible being when the intended conclusion is a producer of possible beings. At L181 he points to witnessed animals, plants and minerals, then prefers the more direct recognition of an originator to a longer modal detour. This is a substantive rational and experiential entry, not a definition of a perfect being. [S1]

The passage also contains an important scope distinction. L178 accepts a route beginning from actual existence and dividing necessary from possible existence, but expressly says that proving a necessary existent in that way does not by itself establish a creator of the heavens and earth. Thus even the source's own minimal actuality-to-necessity route cannot be substituted without comment for the actual productive-ground route.

At L183–187 the source supplies the further positive existential judgments: openness to being and nonbeing lacks self-sufficiency; a reality requiring another, including itself together with another, is not sufficient through itself; collecting dependent beings does not remove the relevant need. Those judgments receive the retained affirmative appraisal. They are not hidden contents of “correction,” and their substantive status does not demote them to unsupported labels.

The source's use of witnessed originated beings must not be generalized to “every newly occurring act is an externally created bearer.” The current semantic audit expressly forbids that transition. Temporal succession, acquisition of information, and reception of a bearer's whole existential actuality have different targets. [S2]

## 3 What an actual correction does and does not warrant

### Actual obtaining

If a genuine correction episode is known to have occurred, an actual occurrence is warranted. This does not require first settling its ultimate explanation. The warranted actuality belongs initially to the episode, or to its independently identified concrete bearer or produced token. It need not belong to every object mentioned in the correction. A correct denial, a mathematical correction, or a judgment that something is impossible does not instantiate the denied or impossible object.

Where actual human apprehension is independently warranted, its being an episode of apprehension has more content than a corrected inscription. Where only a calculation, proof check or textual replacement is established, that result supplies no additional evidence of AI subjective experience or factive cognition. Nothing in this return uses such a premise. Ordinary perceptual or testimonial warrant for an occurrence also need not be redescribed as a claim to this assistant's subjective acquaintance. The source tradition's distinction between actual external particulars and mental representation is pertinent; the present operation does not authenticate a new recognitional case. [S8]

### Productive receipt in the right respect

Calling an occurrence a correction does not independently show that its whole existential actuality is received. Learning a proposition concerns epistemic acquisition; that fact is not yet whole-bearer reception. A newly exercised standing power need not itself be newly acquired. Nor does an event's temporal beginning by itself establish an external supplier of its bearer.

A concrete produced token or activity can instead enter through ordinary evidence of its actual production: an inscription made through an identified writing operation, for example. The relevant evidence concerns what supplied this token's concrete occurrence, not just its ordering after another token or the correctness of its wording. This is an illustration of the application, not a newly investigated instance. The token's production does not make its producer powerless or show that the producer's whole being was produced by that same local operation.

For a selected actual episode, the occurrence and receipt claims therefore need their own appropriately scoped evidence. The inherited source already supplies such an experiential entry through actual dependent productive reality. There is no reason to make a correction episode the uniquely permissible witness, or to reauthenticate ordinary actual reality merely because the formal bridge has an occurrence field.

If only an actual qualified bearer is known, the source's existential division remains available: independence is assessed through the accepted positive self-sufficiency judgment; existential need is assessed through the received-ground route. But the primary L178 warning still applies. This minimal route to a necessary existent is not, without the corresponding productive warrant, the proof that this existent supplies the actual correction's productive ground.

### Explanatory continuation

Once an actual receiving target has been warranted, the positive continuation is already earned at its defended scope. Complete provision must account for the derivative suppliers' actuality rather than presuppose received actuality throughout. A concrete original resource must be actually instantiated. The bearer whose received actuality is required for that same instantiation cannot be omitted when declaring the instantiation original. These are the accepted target-preserving arguments, not inferences from temporal adjacency or normative truth. [S3, S6]

This provides the relevant actual original bearer. The necessity argument then concerns that same bearer's own existential adequacy. No standard, true sentence or abstract criterion is made into an efficient component; nor is the bearer said to produce its intrinsic attributes. [S2, S7]

### Cognitive excellence and bearer eligibility

A genuinely warranted cognitive correction can exemplify an intelligible excellence of truth-disclosing cognition. A merely successful formal transition does not establish that full exemplification. Neither form, by itself, identifies which original possesses every perfection.

The accepted eligibility argument already provides the further warrant. The a-fortiori comparison and pure-perfection appraisal apply to an independently established actual original; essential incapacity is evaluated rather than excused by assigning a restricted kind. The donor argument retains its separate scope: a source for each perfection is not every perfection in each proposed source. The current knowledge-ascent report accepts comprehensive cognitive perfection through the independent pure-perfection route, including knowledge concerning nonactual objects without producing them. These strong positive results remain credited. They are neither supplied nor defeated by the availability of one corrected statement. [S9–S10]

## 4 Exact disposition

The original connection has two distinguishable readings.

1. **Objective standards alone yield a concrete productive original.** This is not the retained source argument. Neither formal consistency, objective validity nor genuine comparative excellence alone supplies the missing actual instantiation.
2. **A warranted actual episode gives access to real activity, and the accepted existential and perfection arguments explain and qualify its original ground.** This is an available source-faithful route when the occurrence, receipt and relevant productive relation are established. It retains substantial metaphysical premises openly. Objective correction can disclose the target and assess cognition; the independently warranted actual productive situation carries the existential entry.

The second reading does not weaken the established affirmative conclusion. It explains how that conclusion was earned. The first reading is not an additional obligation silently owed by the second. No new general ontology comparison, rival campaign, bare-world control, or renamed Lean implication would improve this currentness result.

The completed operation is therefore bounded: retain the accepted actuality, explanation, necessity and perfection appraisals; correct any presentation that makes orthability itself the actual-occurrence premise; preserve the distinction between an actual correction and warranted cognition; and stop this scope. A future specified correction case could be assessed on its actual evidence, but no such case is invented or left as a newly manufactured programme obligation here.
