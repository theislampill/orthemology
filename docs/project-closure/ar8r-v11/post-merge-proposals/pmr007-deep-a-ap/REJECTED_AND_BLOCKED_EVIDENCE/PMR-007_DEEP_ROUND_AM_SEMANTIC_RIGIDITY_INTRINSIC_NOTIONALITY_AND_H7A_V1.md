## Candidate setting

Let `S` be a finite relational structure on carrier `X`, let `C` be a finite
content set, and let `I(S)` be a family of interpretations `i:X→C` regarded as
admissible for `S`.  An interpretation assignment over labelled copies of `S`
is called structural when it commutes with isomorphisms.

## Candidate theorem

A structural interpretation assignment exists exactly when one admissible
interpretation is fixed by every automorphism of `S`.  If exactly one such
interpretation exists, the semantics of `S` is rigid.

The intended bridge claim is that rigid semantics supplies intrinsic
notionality, and therefore materially supports H7a in the notionality-to-mind
program.

## Initial controls

- a symmetric two-node structure with a required nonconstant interpretation;
- an asymmetric structure with one distinguished role;
- equal similarity kernels under a permutation of content labels;
- one impersonal interpretation law;
- multiple local grammars converging on one structural profile.

## Initial nonclaims

No claim of divine Speech, revelation, a unique ultimate mind, personality,
Wisdom, or world-level source identity is made.
