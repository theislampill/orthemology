## Which kind of frontier?

A research question, a missing source, a proposed application and an unfinished repository integration are not the same frontier. This page follows the selected sources' boundaries; it does not assign new scientific verdicts or present a complete programme-wide status.

For repository integration and publication standing, the existing [STATUS.md](../../STATUS.md) remains the project-level entrance. For this preview's incomplete editorial work, use the [qualification record][qualification].

## Recognition and foundational application

The [Sound Reason account][sound] positively relates basic recognition, proper cognitive function, tawātur as cognitive vindication and corrective reasoning. The [retained-passage report][retained] retains source-bound affirmative foundational appraisals rather than requiring an indefinitely prior proof of every cognitive act.

The remaining questions must be stated at the correct level. The philosophical account's proper-function, non-collusion, generality and source premises need their own warrant; an apparent majority or an independent-source graph does not automatically establish them. A foundational application also needs its actual productive/source premises. Ordinary recognition without universally prior reflective proof does not imply infallibility in every exercise.

**What advances this frontier:** a source-grounded explanation of the required bridge or premise, at the scope the claim actually needs. A new citation count, a software test, or a blanket downgrade of every affirmative appraisal does not do that.

The distinct [concrete/sound note][concrete] retains its own narrower source qualifications. Where two documents have different strengths or roles, show the difference rather than silently rewriting one into the other.

## A historical tensor frontier has a written successor

The [tensor predecessor][tensor] records a bridge limitation beyond its quadratic/sum-of-squares conditions. The [coercive-polynomial successor][coercive] explicitly addresses that bridge in Theorem G. Its conclusion follows under the full path regularity, normalisation, all-polynomial square-positivity and coercivity hypotheses.

The new reading surface must therefore not present “general coercive representability is still only proposed” as the unqualified current account of these two works. Conversely, it must not treat the successor as a finite-data algorithm or a newly checked Lean theorem.

**What still requires work for an application:** relate any finite observation or truncated record to the full functional hypotheses; justify the intended variables and dynamics; and retain the distinction between a unique law for a complete path and potentially multiple paths for an initial record. These are different targets from the bridge already addressed in the written successor. See the [source-located theorem](RESULT-INDEX.md#tensor-moments).

## Exact geometry versus weaker retained tasks

The [fixed-fibre sidecar result][sidecar] settles its exact contract: preserving selected ambient Fisher norms distinguishes the audit weights, so a positive-dimensional fibre cannot be represented by a finite-valued exact supplement. The fixed-fibre continuous-coordinate count is sharp under the stated assumptions.

That result does not settle all neighbouring questions. A finite discretisation changes the task to a restricted or approximate one. Posterior-answer-only preservation is weaker. A separately chosen reduced metric changes the contract. A globally continuous minimal chart across varying-rank strata is not asserted by the fixed-fibre corollary.

**What advances a neighbouring question:** first specify which answers, velocities, norms or dynamics must be preserved, and which approximation is allowed. Do not infer a universal memory requirement from the strongest contract, or erase the actual geometric result merely because a noetic interpretation is not established. The [controlling source][fisher] separates these preservation levels.

## Complete mediation and actual coordination

The [finite-mediator bound][coordination] gives an exact fixed-horizon constraint and a matching construction. An actual coordination explanation must still identify the complete mediation relation, including side information, rather than substitute the size of a program or an instantaneous controller state.

The source also distinguishes attainment at a single horizon from a prescribed state marginal or one common, forward-causal history across horizons. **The useful next step is a faithful model binding**, not repeating the already supplied bound or claiming that its retrospective construction establishes physical coordination.

## Fresh histories, access and rational policy

**This is the Twelfth sources' particular application boundary, not a new programme-wide foundational verdict.** The [fresh-history article][t12-fresh] already proves its concentration obstruction and constructs a coherent diffuse history. The [bounded-access paper][t12-access] identifies a positive operational premise: warranted success in a magnitude-sensitive task, combined with sufficient resource-tail control, forces bounded capture. The next application needs that target, law, information state and actual acquisition contract—not merely a finite answer or a realised successful correction.

The [integer protocol][t12-integer] has advanced a different question: it supplies an all-tapes-total finite-input procedure. A remaining objection cannot simply say that this integer report is only measurable and no computation exists. Its input supply, hidden-information interface and maintained service remain application burdens; Gaussian exact-real acquisition is a separate question. Do not relabel pointwise finite execution as a tight runtime bound.

The [final clarification][t12-clarification] distinguishes two conclusions. Positive bounded capture contradicts the fresh diffuse law under the joint assumptions. The completed-report calculation instead shows domination of a policy. The architecture, reporter and losing behaviour remain mathematically consistent. A stronger original-support inference through that policy route must address the agent's possible abstention, commitment, model revision or justified rejection of the service assumption—not merely show that the architecture could run the reporter.

The [local chain-foundation candidate][t12-chain] likewise narrows its actual-warrant obligation to one complete incoming-closed ancestor cone, without supplying that witness. Its root conclusion must not be silently upgraded to a unique complete necessary bearer. These source-specific limits do not revoke the [later retained foundational appraisals][retained] or require independent foundational routes to pass through this stochastic example.


## Joint feasibility is not supplied by marginal ranges

The [occurrence/feasibility join][join] already gives the common relational lesson. Applying it elsewhere requires the actual admissible joint relation and the justification for that relation. The missing-outcome work's sharp endpoint claim depends on feasible completions; the linguistic record's complete rows depend on the particular source occurrences and their interpretative roles.

The join explicitly keeps an earlier entry/grammar-versus-sense applicability contract separate. The later complete-tuple calculation does not close that different question. **What advances the application is a justified joint witness or completion model**, not freely combining coordinate answers or transferring an empirical dependence claim from a linguistic example.

## Application contracts and the work required to realise them

The [corrective-control example](APPLICATIONS.md#a-corrective-step-that-legitimately-ends-in-hold) already represents a useful partial repair: the addressed criterion defect does not erase a separate source-independence dependency, and the final posture remains HOLD. What it does not establish is actual human uptake or an externally executed runtime. Its [typed operator owner][app-operators] and [dated runtime boundary][app-runtime] should accompany any claim about implementation or soundness. A later runtime inspection requires a separately identified source revision; the old pin is not a live-status assertion.

The [learning contract][app-coupling] requires explicit per-claim transport across analysis changes and preserves earlier versions. Extending an implementation means supplying and checking those transport/invalidation/reopening obligations, not asserting that all old verdicts remain current. Neither the early route-ranked G1/G2 account nor the actual later Fisher construction should be used as a blanket status for all noetic dynamics.

The [agentic-runtime profiles][app-runtime-outlines] explicitly remain unimplemented outlines. Their next work is proposal-specific source and authority verification followed by bounded implementation and testing; publishing these profiles does not satisfy it. These engineering/interpretation obligations are distinct from proving another theorem or reclassifying any original philosophical argument. Full repository-wide application coverage is also still an editorial obligation of this refactor.

## Source availability and editorial completion

The [source recovery guide][recovery] distinguishes recovered proof bodies from held or unavailable material and from historical evidence whose original scope remains limited. Its record of binary variants without decoded-equivalence qualification must not become a claim that their texts are all duplicates.

Within the refactor, the [owner map][owner-map] routes the full baseline candidate population, but the representative crosswalk does not yet classify every substantive work. Completing those dispositions, broader avenue explanations and source-specific update rules remains **editorial work**, not permission to reconstruct missing research or rebuild a scientific registry.

The full frontier page will need the original obligation, argument and supersession owners across the declared collections. Until then, the selected questions above must not masquerade as all of Orthemology's open research.
