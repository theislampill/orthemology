## Corrected or made explicit

1. **Freeze the claim:** define the kernel, input histories, state/output metrics, tolerances, horizon, resources, failure assumptions and permissions before comparing realisations. Non-vacuity does not require a frontier language model.
2. **Joint realisation:** correspondence applies to the assembled configuration. A constituent need not independently implement the whole kernel. Human participation through a specified observable interface does not require access to private mental states.
3. **Actual output correspondence:** state commutation alone is insufficient when a physical display or output is wrong. The physical output map is checked separately against the abstract observation rule. An inverted-output control exposes the omission.
4. **Behaviour versus parameters:** fixed-model behaviour, parameter identity, separately trained similar performance, and continuation of a current execution are different claims. Training method does not itself settle equivalence or continuity.
5. **Substitution profile:** the post-v2 T0–T5 shorthand is not an ordered theorem or an evidence ladder. Material, computing mechanism, architecture, support dependency, and maturity must be declared separately.
6. **Literature-negative claims:** replace the unqualified claim that no alternative stateful processor exists with a scoped assessment. Fluidic toggle memory and molecular automata are actual published precedents. They do not establish the present paired migration or a large-model implementation.
7. **Precision and scale:** do not compare model parameters directly with physical degrees of freedom. Add a scoped robust-state packing bound. Low-bit weight inference is not bit-identical or all-operations low-precision inference.
8. **Error direction:** an error upper bound that grows does not prove error growth or impossibility. Keep certified, defeated and unresolved outcomes distinct. Add a finite-trace distributional bound with explicit common-history premises.
9. **Established comparison methods:** refinement mappings, approximate simulation and contract-based design are genuine prior art. The proposed representation must be compared against a strong conventional baseline, not a deliberately uninformed specialist.
10. **NS scope:** retain the ordinary residual-to-observation derivation and current reported-source distinctions. No method-specific physical advantage from the 2026 construction is established; the new stateful witness does not rely on it.
