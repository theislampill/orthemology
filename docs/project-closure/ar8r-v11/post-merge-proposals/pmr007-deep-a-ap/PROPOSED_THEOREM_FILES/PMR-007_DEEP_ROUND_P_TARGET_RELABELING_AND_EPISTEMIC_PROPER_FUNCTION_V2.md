## 3. Neutral structural reduct

A neutral reduct `B` may contain all finite structural and environment-facing facts that do **not themselves settle** the disputed truth/fittingness coordinate:

\[
B=(Q,Z,A,\delta,o,r,\pi,H,\rho,d,N,F,\operatorname{Tr},E_{str}),
\]

where `E_str` may include causal-role, organizational, organism-environment, historical, and trajectory relations. The reduct can therefore be rich and relational rather than purely internal.

It omits only the independently load-bearing coordinates:

```text
TL: objective truth linkage of the target/output in the relevant respect;
FIT_O: objective fittingness or praiseworthiness of the governing end;
SEL_T: selection specifically because truth-conducive variants produced the relevant effect;
DES_T: design/intention specifically aimed at truth or sound resolution;
SRC_AUTH: authenticated source status;
SRC_REC: correct source-proposition reconstruction;
SRC_APP: recipient/scope applicability;
BECAUSE_F: selection or action because the end/reason is fitting;
WIS: purposive Wisdom;
PERS: personality or first-person subjecthood.
```

## 4. TRPF-1A — target-relabeling nonidentification

Let `K` be an intended model class containing two interpretation expansions `M+` and `M-` of one complete neutral reduct `B`, with:

```text
B(M+) = B(M-);
TL(M+) = true;
TL(M-) = false.
```

Let `G` be any predicate invariant on neutral-reduct isomorphism classes:

\[
G(M)=g([B_M]).
\]

Then

\[
G(M^+)=G(M^-).
\]

Hence `G` neither identifies nor entails `PF_EPI` over `K` when `PF_EPI` differs across the pair.

### Proof

The two models occupy one neutral-reduct isomorphism class. By invariance, `G` takes one value on that class. The truth-linked epistemic-proper-function verdict varies by construction. Therefore the latter does not factor through the neutral reduct. The closure assumption is essential: the result does not establish that both expansions are metaphysically, biologically, or theologically realizable.

## 6. TRPF-1C — trajectory evidence is mechanism-sensitive, not norm-fixing by itself

If `M+` and `M-` preserve the complete observed trajectory, adding that trajectory to the neutral reduct does not break the proof. By contrast, two mechanism candidates with the same endpoint but different trace languages can be discriminated by a measured trace in their symmetric difference.

The accessible Sun et al. paper supplies a bounded empirical example: several tested computational models achieved decorrelated final representations, while the observed order of regional decorrelation discriminated the CSCG from the other tested models. The source does not establish uniqueness over all possible models, objective fittingness, `PF_EPI`, divine design, fiṭrah, or Wisdom.
