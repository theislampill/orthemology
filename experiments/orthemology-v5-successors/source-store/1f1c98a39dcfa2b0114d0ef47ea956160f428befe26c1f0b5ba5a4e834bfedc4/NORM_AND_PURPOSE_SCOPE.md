# Norm authority and the truthfulness purpose

## The same fact can play different roles

The safety fact p_i can be true because of the actual root's integrity. Evidence can make p_i knowable to the source. A normative reason can require fidelity when the source attests p_i. An authority relation can authorize the recipient to execute R. The source's purpose can motivate the assertion. The assertion can supply evidence to the recipient. No one of these relations is a substitute for every other one.

The local practical reason defended in this lane concerns knowingly counterfeiting the truth-linked reliance one voluntarily invites. It does not establish a complete ultimate grounding theory for that reason. It remains compatible with different views of why the relevant truth, agency and good have their normative status. This is the same basic distinction retained by the Sixth norm-fixing account, now applied to the exact attestation event.

## Three source-sensitive explanations and their limits

A source can **apprehend** an obtaining fittingness relation. This explains what it knows, not yet why its will must follow that relation.

A source can **institute** a role or create a normatively relevant arrangement. For example, it can give a recipient permission to act, establish a signaling convention, or make an artifact for a purpose. The existence of that institution does not make every claim about its operation true. A valid permission can coexist with a broken actuator, and a clear codebook can carry a false report.

A source's own **intrinsic nature** may be proposed as the ultimate ground of a norm. That is a stronger metaphysical account. If it also establishes the source's necessarily integrated practical perfection, it may exclude mixed malice. But source-nature grounding and source conformity must be stated at their actual scope. The fact that one bearer grounds the standard and has power does not by itself prove that every particular act expresses the standard without defect. A merely necessary preference cannot be identified as fitting only because it is necessary.

The affirmative original-perfection theory offers an account of why these roles are integrated: the source knows the good and its own effective willing is intrinsically responsive to that good. This is genuine explanatory content. The inquiry does not reject it merely because another theory uses separate terms; it asks what warrants its comprehensive and essential application.

## Why the source's ownership does not change the proposition

In the fixed test, saying p_i does not by itself change whether i is compromised. If the source actually repairs i before the relevant assertion, the truth conditions may change and a later assertion may be true. That is a different event with an additional efficacious act. It must not be silently inserted to rescue a knowingly false attestation in the stipulated bad-root case.

Similarly, a present act of appointment may constitute a person's authority from that moment. It does not automatically establish a claim that the person had already been appointed earlier. A sign that expresses confirmation may identify the confirming act without constituting every fact affirmed. The source texts' king analogy must therefore retain the actual content and temporal reference of the claim under assessment.

The same caution applies to a creative command such as “let this root be safe.” If an independently warranted completed exercise really makes the root safe, its effect can support repair by a causal route. Mere omnipotence or a meaningful utterance is not evidence that this particular efficacious exercise occurred. The causal route can be valid without establishing universal truthful testimony, but it imports its own evidence of action and effect.

## Exact purpose identification

Public assurance, sincere belief, the intention to produce belief, the intention to produce knowledge, and the execution of an act as the chosen adequate means to that knowledge-giving aim must remain distinct.

The local purpose implication consumes the final two together with relevant source Knowledge and means coherence. It does not infer them from the public assurance role. A source can falsely present itself as serving knowledge; that is how the developed mixed rival's event is both genuine communication and a betrayal. Filtering such events out of the “authoritative attestation” domain by definition would change the target rather than prove T0.

The narrow route therefore supplies a candidate additional epistemic input: independently warranted token-purpose linkage. It does not constitute norm authority, does not authenticate its own message, and does not establish the recipient's present permission or physical ability to repair.
