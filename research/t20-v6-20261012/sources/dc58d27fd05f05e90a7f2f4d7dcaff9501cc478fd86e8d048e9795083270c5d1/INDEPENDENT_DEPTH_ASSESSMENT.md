# Independent assessment of T20 synthesis depth

Date: 11 October 2026. Read-only assessment of the delivered T20 edition. This report is a separate reassessment artifact; it changes no delivered source, claim, receipt, or v2 package.

## Executive judgment

The delivered edition is a substantial, intellectually informed consolidation. It is not merely a byte inventory or a reliable installation package. Its chapters preserve important positive results, explain several real mathematical connections, and distinguish hypotheses that would otherwise make apparent unifications false.

It does **not** demonstrate a fresh whole-corpus discovery investigation of the stronger kind now requested: sustained comparison across contributions, selection of the strongest new joints, attempted derivations or counterexamples at those joints, and independently reviewed resulting mathematical or foundational advances. Most substantive connective results already existed in the sources that the consolidation reconciled. The root synthesis makes their common architecture legible; it does not claim, or supply evidence for, a new research result from that architecture.

These judgments should not be collapsed into “the consolidation failed.” The original consolidation contract expressly allowed finite completion without solving all open problems or adding unrelated research. The narrower original deliverable and the broader intellectual ambition are different standards. The honest description is: **a strong current, accountable baseline with genuine interpretive synthesis; an additional cross-front research pass remains warranted under the broader request.**

No blanket demand for pairwise comparison of every source is appropriate. Nor would new theorem counts, more replay, or a new vocabulary by themselves remedy the depth gap.

## 3. What intellectual synthesis was actually achieved

### 3.1 Real interpretive gains

`MATHEMATICAL_SYNTHESIS.md` is a 93-line connected account, not a list of filenames. Its organizing distinction between realisation, observation, operation and execution is useful. It connects:

- moment realisability to the need to specify a genuine represented object;
- present equivalence to stronger continuation equivalence;
- source identity, stochastic joint laws, timing and model coupling to the information future operations need;
- gradient correction to its metric, constraints and allowed audits;
- formal dependency transport to separately warranted foundational premises.

Its most important intellectual virtue is that it does not force all branches into one theorem or turn mathematical preservation into historical, psychological or metaphysical warrant. It also retains affirmative foundations and constructive mathematical repairs instead of producing a catalogue of impossibilities.

The four track chapters and cumulative crosswalk contain significantly more content than the short root synthesis. Older restoration, collective diagnosis, copying, occurrence-sensitive language, empirical studies, finite-additive reporting and specialist boundaries are positively represented. Calling them absent merely because they are not prominent in the root mathematical narrative would be inaccurate.

## 5. Strongest feasible further investigations

These are bounded continuations anchored in explicit existing joints, not a demand to invent unrelated research or exhaust every possible pair. Each should first check close inherited results and credit any result already owned.

### A. Multiple audit families: classify the actual generated action

**Sources:** GEO-16/17 and `geometry/GEOMETRY_MOMENTS_SYNTHESIS.md` §§7.1, 9; ET-12; the finite-generator supplement.

**Concrete unresolved joint:** One fixed partition allows arbitrary within-group interactions, while singleton audits force a stronger separable structure. The source explicitly notes that overlapping row/column partition actions retain an odds ratio and do not necessarily generate independent singleton rescaling. “Overlapping partition families remain an unclassified extension” is a stated residual, not an invented problem.

**Bounded operation:** Fix a small overlapping family, compute the log-rescaling action and its invariants, then classify a justified class of gradient energies commuting with the combined action. Check whether intersections of the individual classifications are sufficient and whether nonseparable invariant interactions survive. Use exact symbolic countercontrols on a 2×2 family, then state precisely which general finite-family extension the proof supports.

**Why high leverage:** It connects the source-audit operation algebra with the geometric classification and can produce an independently consequential theorem. It tests a live distinction rather than restating that operations matter. It does not infer the correct audit family from shared provenance.

**Stopping evidence:** A correctly scoped classification or a precise obstruction plus exact control, followed by independent review. No claim about actual source semantics without separate evidence.

### B. One actual commissioned-content correction trace

**Sources:** FND-026/030/031/032/034; ET-01/03/14/18/20; the actual Tibyan revision/addendum and commissioned-content reports.

**Concrete unresolved joint:** The sources distinguish the report occurrence, particular attributed content, commission, veracity, scope, recipient access, and later custody. CF-13 stops at a candidate application. The strongest already worked revalidation concerns CP16/CP23/T17; it does not instantiate these particular communication distinctions.

**Bounded operation:** First compare the exact FND-033/ET-18 application and commissioned-content reports against the proposed target. Stop if the relevant combination is already demonstrated. Only where a genuinely unworked distinction remains, select one already documented source correction or the actual Tibyan cumulative-content revision. Construct an explicit source-owned trace: what proposition changed, which premises supplied attribution and veracity, which inference is defeasible or deductive, which duplicated documents share a ground, and which warranted challenge changes which downstream claim. Test merge, withdrawal, correction and retained-content operations against that trace. Keep actual source relations and hypothetical interventions visibly separate.

**Why high leverage:** If the source comparison identifies a real missing application, this can turn a candidate semantic link into a defended application and clarify a foundational argument. This is not yet a demonstrated omission of such an application from every held source; it is a high-value targeted check. The CP16/CP23/T17 combination itself must not be redone as though absent. It need not fit invented reliabilities, demand probability-one knowledge, reauthenticate all source volumes, or manufacture a new defeater. A purely Boolean toy tagged “warranted” would not suffice.

**Stopping evidence:** Exact controlling passages and versions, a justified interpretation of each modeled relation, a worked changed conclusion or proved preservation result, and an independent scope review. If the sources cannot justify an operation, that precise failure is an informative outcome.

### C. Couple bounded uncertain-kernel planning to a horizon-specific moment body

**Sources:** ET-15/16/17; MOM-11; H-Continuation; the cumulative crosswalk's finite-exchangeability control.

**Concrete unresolved joint:** MOM-11 repairs the two-trial fixed-calibration law and explicitly fails for a third new trial. ET-17 requires preserving the same model throughout policy evaluation, rather than maximizing branches independently. A new horizon or modest adaptive menu asks for a representation and optimization contract, not merely another moment identity.

**Bounded operation:** Specify one short adaptive inquiry problem with one calibration chosen once. Derive the exact attainable moment/path-law body needed for its complete legal policies, retain model-indexed losses, and solve or certify the robust optimum. Compare with the tempting branchwise/endpoint shortcut. Check the model class against the inherited finite-exchangeability counterexample: a finite exchangeable law need not be an iid mixture, so the Hausdorff moment body cannot silently represent all such evidence.

**Why high leverage:** It puts positive moment representation, coupling, and policy value into one demonstrated object. It can reveal a useful algorithm or genuine obstruction without claiming arbitrary uncertain kernels are solved.

**Stopping evidence:** Exact feasible domain, policy semantics, attainable loss contract, a sharp discriminator against the invalid shortcut, and independent verification. Simply extending the binomial expansion from two trials to three is too small to count as the proposed intellectual advance.

## 6. A tempting “new bridge” that is already mostly inherited

A useful check on novelty is the proposed connection between ET-09 finite sidecar bits, MOM-01 controlled finite-support continuation, and GEO-06's log-density leaf descriptor.

- GEO-06's source already proves that `(E_p[phi], [log p] mod V)` is a smooth bijective description of every positive law, with dimensions `d` and `n−d−1`. Held object: `catalogue/objects/6d/6d5c3c38f6e89c8f74f3b5d58cabcace7b9cae0eb2d6f8bf3a2f6ef4f4aef724`, around lines 191–199.

Consequently, contrasting finite-state tag cardinality with continuous law-space augmentation is worthwhile exposition. The no-finite/countable-sidecar observation on a positive-dimensional fibre is elementary once injectivity is required. It should receive connective-corollary credit, not be advertised as a major new memory theorem. This example illustrates the kind of primary-source comparison a deeper pass needs before declaring an emergent result new.

### A potentially useful restricted-control extension

A further candidate is to replace MOM-01's full control space with a declared subspace U while still observing the full channel mean q. Group the support columns by equal U-projections. Their unnormalised full-output barycentres, rather than necessarily every individual weight, appear to supply the relevant exact continuation state. The inspected MOM-01 source does not state that grouped classification; it explicitly warns that restricted controls can invalidate its full-control converse. H-Continuation already supplies the abstract operation-relative span/augmentation ancestry.

This is a candidate scope-specific extension, not a verified result of this assessment and not a new general minimality principle. A proof would need necessity, sufficiency, lawful full-q readout, the U={0}/pure-gauge cases, and a precise coordinate lower-bound contract. It is more discriminating than merely restating the full-control dimension bound.

## 7. What should and should not be corrected

**Retain:** The v2 baseline, original authorship, substantive positive appraisals, controlled negative results, reading limitations, and passed bounded reviews.

**Correct any broader completion claim:** Do not say the entire corpus has been freshly read, all cross-contributions have been intellectually exhausted, or new independent advances arose from consolidation unless additional evidence establishes that. The package itself mostly avoids those claims.

**Do next under the broader request:** Run a small number of well-chosen cross-front investigations, chosen by significance and supported joints. Record inherited antecedents, attempted combination, discriminating result, counterexample/failed attempt when applicable, and review. A negative result or demonstration that a tempting bridge is already inherited can be a valuable result if it changes the research direction.

**Do not demand:** A reread of every archived object, all-pairs theorem comparison, a fresh global Lean rebuild, speculative metaphysical transport, invented numerical source reliabilities, or more discoveries merely to make counts rise.
