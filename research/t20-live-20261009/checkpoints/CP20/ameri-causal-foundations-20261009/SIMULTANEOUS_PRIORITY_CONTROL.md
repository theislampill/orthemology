# Self production and simultaneous external causation

9 October 2026 UTC. Focused reflective return within the Ameri investigation. The source wording was read continuously with the causal unit31–86 and objections133–190, then verified on rendered41 and151–152. This is not a newly read second book or a new nonduplication argument.

## Finding

The self-production reductio on41 establishes that a nonexistent effect cannot supply its own productive power. It does not, without another premise, establish that a **different actual cause cannot operate contemporaneously with the effect**. The page moves to externality and then excludes contemporaneity with a backward reference to the preceding reasoning. That second move is not licensed by the self-production contradiction alone.

The book nevertheless explicitly affirms simultaneous causation on151. A responsible synthesis therefore preserves a locally overbroad or inadequately distinguished sentence while crediting the later source–act and causal–temporal distinctions. It must not describe Ameri's considered position as a general denial of simultaneous external causation.

## Source and local reasoning

At41 the subject is the emergence of an entity after absence, with a particular time requiring determination. The proposed determinant is first divided into inside and outside the emerging entity. The inside alternative is rejected because productive power would be attributed to that entity before it existed. The text then takes the cause to be external, excludes its being contemporaneous or later, and concludes that it exists before the entity. The temporal framing includes contrast between available times and a prior cause; this is stronger than merely saying that the effect cannot efficiently produce itself.

Its sensible core is a priority-of-actuality requirement: a nonexistent bearer has no actual power with which to produce itself. **Externality** blocks that particular contradiction when the alleged productive bearer is genuinely distinct and actual. The remaining question is whether its required actuality has to be temporally earlier or can be contemporaneous while explanatory priority is asymmetric. The internal-source reductio does not settle that question.

No metaphysically certified simultaneous case is needed to identify the inferential omission. We are not claiming to have proved that every such case is possible. We are asking which premise would exclude an externally actual simultaneous cause, rather than assuming that the self-production argument supplies it.

## The strongest reconciliation

Page151 directly addresses the challenge to creating time before time exists. Its explicit response distinguishes causal priority from temporal priority and treats the productive act as concurrent with its effect. It also distinguishes the cause's priority from the absence of temporal separation between act and product. Page152 says divine priority is by essence rather than by time, and denies a prior temporal interval before the ultimate beginning.

Three readings should be separated:

1. **Standing source versus occurring act.** An agent can exist antecedently while its act is concurrent with the product. This makes good sense of many ordinary examples and explains why claiming act/effect simultaneity does not imply self-production. It reconciles some of the early and later vocabulary.
2. **Explanatory priority rather than temporal priority.** A source's actual being is prior as an explanatory requisite without a distinct earlier time. This is the explicit stronger allowance in151–152 and is appropriate to the book's ultimate beginning-of-time case.
3. **A universal requirement of temporal antecedence.** If41 is taken literally as applying this requirement to every cause, it conflicts with the later allowance. But imposing that universal interpretation on the whole book would suppress the passage specifically written to answer the timing objection.

The first reconciliation alone is insufficient for the first-time case, since Ameri expressly declines to posit an earlier temporal interval. The second supplies the intended distinction, but makes the early phrase excluding a contemporaneous cause too broad unless restricted or rephrased. Hence the most defensible verdict is **a missing local qualification**, not a demonstrated wholesale incoherence.

This is also not a case for quietly changing the printed text. The source really does say what prompted the objection. The report preserves both passages and identifies the scope needed for reconciliation.

## Relevance to T20

The retained simultaneous-production investigations already distinguish a bearer, its actual activity, the produced terminus, and temporal ordering. They do not authenticate duplicate entire original production merely by admitting simultaneity. The Ameri return adds no missing exclusivity premise to those investigations.

In particular:

- A distinct cause's contemporaneous actuality is not efficient self-production just because the effect is actual at the same time.
- Lack of temporal priority does not erase asymmetry of productive receipt.
- Two distinct simultaneous causes need not be arranged in a productive cycle merely by their simultaneity.
- Conversely, two contemporaneous actual routes do not thereby each provide the entire unreceived productive basis of one effect.

The book's later discussion supports the first distinction. It neither establishes nor refutes the strongest nonexclusive-original rival at the fourth. A proposed proof that excludes that rival using only41's self-production reductio would therefore have changed the target: the rival claims contemporaneous external productive relations, not a product supplying its own nonexistent actuality.

## Related concomitance control

The convex/concave dish illustration on39 is an argument against inferring independent metaphysical possibility from separately considering two aspects. It is not evidence that one external efficient producer makes another, nor that all necessary accompaniment is productive dependence. Even if the source uses causal vocabulary loosely in describing the two sides, its relevant evidential function is necessary co-instantiation under the stated geometry. This use has positive force against the inference from imaginative omission to real separability. It does not establish a direction of efficient production.

The neighbouring intrinsic-powers argument likewise has a strong anti-eliminativist target: removing every differentiated causal power changes what concrete natures are alleged to be. A qualified brute original or necessarily concordant pair can grant real powers. The further original-sufficiency or nonduplication inference still requires its own premise.
