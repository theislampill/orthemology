## 1. Exact source use

Sun et al. report that several tested computational systems can attain selected
final OSM-like representational structure, while the CSCG default comparison
most closely matches both the final organization and the ordered learning
trajectory. The source also reports that alternative sensory serializations can
preserve final learned transition structure while changing the learning order.

This round uses the article as a primary empirical/computational neighbor. It
does not reproduce the experiment or code.

## 2. Endpoint/trajectory refinement theorem

Let `E` be final endpoint status and `T` the registered trajectory-match label
on a finite declared comparison class. If two instances share `E` but differ in
`T`, no exact endpoint-only certifier decides `T`. The joint profile `(E,T)`
trivially decides the registered label, and any independently measured
trajectory signature that is constant exactly on the target classes can refine
the endpoint profile enough to restore certification.

The registered source extraction contains endpoint-matched CSCG/RNN instances
with different trajectory labels. Endpoint fit is therefore insufficient for
the reported trajectory comparison.

## 3. Model-family and encoding control

The registered CSCG default and alternative-serialization instances share a
model-family label and selected final structure while differing on the reported
trajectory match. Model family alone therefore does not decide the trajectory
result. Encoding, objective, architecture, and training procedure remain part of
the model identity and evidence contract.
