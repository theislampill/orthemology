## 4. Close check of the revised actual-target q_A inference

Let A retain its objective explanatory meaning. The inference in §6 has the following conditional structure:

1. Actual p_Q is granted. Assume no A for reductio. Therefore q_A, namely p_Q together with no A, is actually true.
2. M provides a possible A. Any such situation falsifies q_A. Thus q_A is actually true and possibly false: it is contingent. This remains valid even if p_Q is necessary.
3. Add the separately stated modal commitment that an actually contingent truth can be false but possible in another situation. At that comparison situation q_A is nonactual and possible.
4. Apply the proposed powers theory only conditionally on its covering that precise composite state, including the explanatory-absence component, and requiring an eligible genuinely realisable capacity or chain for it.
5. Add achievement preservation: any such realisation gives an E_A answer to p_Q. The realisation would then satisfy both A and the no-A component of q_A. A genuinely realisable capacity for that contradictory achievement is excluded.

At the stated assumptions, this is a valid conditional route against the original no-A supposition. It does not infer actual exercise from actual capacity. It also does not derive q_A's contingency from individual witnesses or from assuming that p_Q itself is contingent.

The stated open premise is exactly where the philosophical work lies. Token production can establish an F and thereby p_Q without automatically establishing adequate supply of its antecedent availability. To obtain the contradiction one must defend E_A achievement preservation, not substitute truthmaking or a merely enabling channel for the relevant achievement. Conversely, the possibility of leaving some powers unexercised does not answer this conditional argument.

Recommendation R2 is resolved by this exact test. It provides a stronger assessment than the initial capacity/exercise observation while retaining the independent modal comparison and theory-domain burdens.

The added g/s control is also sound. The actual basis of a possible producer's capacity need not be that producer; the possible producer's location outside Q does not automatically locate the actual basis outside Q. Excluding a Q-dependent mediated realisation requires the independence/preservation argument.

Recommendation R3 is resolved: an independently established actual capacity bearer outside the class covered by the actual contingent-bearer premise would already warrant a necessary being at that premise's domain. Actual exercise is an additional condition for explanatory participation, not an admission price for that distinct existence result. Neither antecedent is established here.
