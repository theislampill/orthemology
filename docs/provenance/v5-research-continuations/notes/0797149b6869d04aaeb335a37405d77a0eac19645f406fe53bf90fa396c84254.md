## Reading the statuses

**Strict consequence** means that the stated interpretation and premises entail the stated conclusion. It does not mean those premises have been independently established of reality. **Defended warrant** identifies a positive explanatory or perfection rationale open to substantive philosophical assessment. **Additional prerequisite** marks a condition whose truth cannot be imported from an earlier rung. **Historical or school identification** is a claim about a source or a declared theological route, not a neutral metaphysical theorem.

## Connected positive argument rather than isolated conditionals

The cumulative account begins with one actual rational correction. The actual exercise is produced, is a knowing of truth, and is a response to an apprehended reason. Under the defended source account, its efficacy is wholly derivative from an original giver. Cognitive original-giver priority motivates an original apprehender. Extending that same source-sensitive comparison to **integrated** reason-responsive exercise motivates intentional source agency. This is an additional application, not a theorem obtained by conjoining Knowledge and Power. If objective fittingness and original practical integrity are independently warranted, the same source can explain why apprehended reasons guide its chosen production. Adequate Power completes that determination; its sufficiency need not identify the particular act with the source's necessary generic nature. A disclosure route is then intelligible through actual content, intended uptake and effective means, provided those abilities really belong to the source. Actual attestation requires additional historical evidence.

This integration has philosophical content: one source's understanding and practical orientation can explain the relation between norms and production. Its limit is equally substantive. A rival can propose intrinsic normative–dispositional relations which explain the same coupling. To establish comparative superiority, show what the source account explains through independently warranted apprehension and orientation that the rival must otherwise leave as separate primitive fit. Merely comparing one word, “mind,” with several words, “powers, norms, relations,” is not a measure of explanatory unity.

These are bridge challenges, not certified metaphysical possibilities or rival proofs of the actual universe. Aristotle, Spinoza and Plotinus are represented only for their relevant resources; their whole systems are not collapsed into the reconstructed intrinsic-powers or plural models. ## Concrete unresolved tests

### T1 Original integrated agency

Specify what is irreducibly present in an actual reason-responsive exercise, independent of a theological interpretation. Then defend why its complete original basis must have integrated apprehension-guided efficacy, rather than merely capacities which originate it. A decisive improvement must address intrinsic normative powers, not just unexplained behaviour or a substrate supplied by something else. Stopping condition: either a defended bridge capable of surviving that rival, or an explicit comparative-warrant result with the surviving denial located.

### T2 Comparative explanatory fit

Hold fixed actual cognitive subjects, true target relations and successful corrections. Compare (a) a source with known ends and means plus creative efficacy and (b) a source with an intrinsic normative–dispositional coupling. Identify independently constrained counterfactual differences or explanatory dependencies. Do not assign “simpler,” “more likely,” or “less brute” merely because one model uses an intentional label. Stopping condition: a genuine discriminating consequence or a clear finding that the current premises underdetermine the comparison.

### T3 Alternative acts under a necessary source

Hold fixed the source's entire necessary nature. State exactly which differences between actual exercises remain genuinely possible, whether their reasons necessitate them, and whether a uniquely best act exists. Distinguish explanatory reasons, efficient determinants, permissions and final purposes. Test whether the position covertly moves from contingent A to necessary A, or whether A's variation is merely left brute. An act-sequence cannot be counted both as sufficient and as explanatorily absent. Stopping condition: a coherent modal account with every added premise marked; no table alone counts as its metaphysical proof.

### T4 Perfect practical responsiveness

Construct the strongest fully informed, unneedy source with a malicious or indifferent final end. Ask why it fails an independently warranted pure practical excellence. Remove error, bodily appetite and external coercion from the challenge. Do not exclude its satisfaction in a bad end solely because that satisfaction occurs, while allowing renewed satisfaction in good acts. Distinguish unbiased rationality, objective value, concern for others, and role-specific duties. Stopping condition: an explicit value/motivation warrant or an acknowledged surviving gap.

### T5 Power over possibility rather than only actuality

Give the domain of genuinely possible tasks and show source eligibility and compossibility of the claimed powers. Actual produced scope is insufficient. Include a task known to be possible but not actually performed, and explain the warrant for the source's capability rather than saying no limitation is known. Stopping condition: a defended extension to the exact modal domain or a scoped Power conclusion.

### T6 Capability rather than noncontradiction of disclosure

Independently exhibit or justify one suitable content, receiver, signification route and jointly exercisable source control. The phrase “source can intend disclosure” cannot itself be the proof of that ability. General intelligibility is only a prerequisite. Stopping condition: a warranted realization route or a conditional capability decomposition with the missing powers explicit.

### T7 Authentication and truthfulness

For an actual message candidate, identify the event, purported sender, intended content, source-role evidence and reason for thinking the source's attestation is truthful in that context. Compare fraud, extraordinary but noncommunicative events, truthful withholding, adversarial misdirection and false authoritative testimony. Do not infer truthfulness from lack of an observed motive to lie or define reliance entitlement factively. Stopping condition: the bounded evidence justifies an attribution and transmission claim, or the actual-revelation conclusion remains unestablished.

### T8 Plural source integration

Give the plural rival all its stated intrinsic powers and actual relations. Distinguish relations of necessary concomitance from efficient furnishing of another root's power. Determine whether a single original exercise is positively required, or merely preferred by a unification taste. Stopping condition: singular coverage is warranted or the plural-origin denial remains live.

### T9 Restoration action relevance

In the D/S setting, specify which received proposition establishes safe actuation, not merely correct diagnosis. A revelation of θ alone does not identify a safe root; a warranted statement i∉C could. Alternatively verify fail-silent semantics independently. Stopping condition: a relevant new input to the local theorem, or an explicit finding that the source attributes leave local restoration knowledge unchanged.

## Computational controls and their limited role

`tests / check_scope_controls.py` supplies finite semantic-independence controls for the prohibited edges and a few direct conditional implications. “Knowledge,” “intentional control” and “good end” remain stipulated semantic predicates; the checker is not a consciousness detector, a theory of moral goodness or a proof that these structures are metaphysically realizable. No finite check establishes omniscience, a Necessary Being or revealed testimony. The checker makes it harder to accidentally promote a dependency while editing the argument; the philosophical work resides in the warranted premises above.
