## 2. NCBD-1A — actual underivability does not entail necessity

The premise

\[
\exists x\in D_{w_0}\,[ROOT(w_0,x)\land UNDERIVED(w_0,x)]
\]

does not entail that every declared world has such a bearer, nor that one
numerical bearer exists and is underived throughout the frame.

## 3. NCBD-1B — worldwise unique roots do not entail one necessary bearer

Even

\[
\forall w\;\exists!x\in D_w\,[ROOT(w,x)\land UNDERIVED(w,x)]
\]

does not entail

\[
\exists!g\;\forall w\,[ROOT(w,g)\land UNDERIVED(w,g)].
\]

The worldwise roots may be distinct local bearers. This is the quantifier and
identity firewall already exposed by the modal transport program.

## 4. NCBD-1C — coherent lineage does not entail numerical identity

A coherent root section under transport establishes one role/lineage section.
It does not identify its local values as one numerical bearer unless an
independent cross-world identity bridge `B_ID` is supplied.

A counterpart bijection may map `r_0` to a distinct `r_1` while preserving every
registered root role.

## 5. NCBD-1D — necessary abstract order does not entail concrete instantiation

A model may satisfy

\[
\forall w\;ABSTRACT\_ORDER(w)
\]

while every local concrete-bearer domain is empty or contains no bearer
exemplifying that order. Thus necessary abstract/modal order does not entail a
concrete necessary being without an exemplification/actuality bridge.

## 6. NCBD-1E — exact guarded bearer-descent package

concrete root bearer** only under all of:


Under those guards, `g` is a necessary concrete root **by the declared modal and
identity semantics**. The result establishes no guard independently.
