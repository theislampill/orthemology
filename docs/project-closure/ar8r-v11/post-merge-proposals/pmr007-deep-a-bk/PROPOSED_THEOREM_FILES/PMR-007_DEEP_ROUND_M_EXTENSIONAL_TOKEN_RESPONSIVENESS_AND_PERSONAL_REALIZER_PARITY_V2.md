## 1. Frozen boundary model

A finite deterministic boundary transducer is

\[
\mathcal T=(Q,Z,A,\delta,\alpha,q_0).
\]

`Z` contains registered **reason tokens or consideration inputs**; the name does not yet establish genuine semantic or normative reasonhood. The boundary profile `Prof_B(T)` contains:

```text
the finite sets Q,Z,A;
the complete delta and alpha tables;
the initial state;
and therefore every finite input/output/state trace at the declared boundary.
```

The profile may also record weak structural coordinates such as a common formal container, target-relative stability, and a declared action-fit relation. It excludes intrinsic mentality, first-person subjectivity, intentional uptake, and selection because of objective fittingness unless those are independently observed or stipulated.

## 2. ETRP-1A — canonical boundary-table presentation

Every finite deterministic boundary transducer has a canonical table presentation with the same `delta`, `alpha`, initial state, and therefore the same finite boundary traces. Equality for all finite traces follows by induction on input-word length.

If a distributed implementation composes to the same boundary transducer, the table presentation preserves only `Prof_B`. It does **not** preserve unregistered internal interventions, causal decomposition, latency, resources, provenance, or physical realization.

## 3. ETRP-1B — profile-fibre and reduct nonentailment

Let `K` be a declared model class over an expanded language containing `MENT`, `PERS`, `INTENT`, or `FIRST`. Let `Prof_B` forget those coordinates. If `K` contains two admissible expansions `m_P,m_I` with

\[
Prof_B(m_P)=Prof_B(m_I)
\]

but

\[
PERS(m_P)\ne PERS(m_I),
\]

then personality does not factor through `Prof_B`. Hence no theorem whose premises and conclusion-relevant evidence are entirely `Prof_B`-measurable entails `PERS` over `K`.

The same holds for `MENT`, `INTENT`, and `FIRST` whenever they vary on a profile fibre.

This is the declared-profile fibre criterion and ordinary reduct invariance. Its force depends on the intended model class admitting both expansions. An independently defended bridge axiom may narrow `K`; it cannot be assumed from the profile itself.

## 4. Token responsiveness versus reason responsiveness

Keep separate:

```text
ZRESP:
  output or transition varies under interventions on registered Z tokens;

SEM:
  the tokens carry the relevant truth-conditional reason content;

FIT_O:
  the governing end is objectively fitting;

UPTAKE:
  a bearer apprehends the content as a reason;

BECAUSE_F:
  selection occurs because the end or consideration is fitting;

SCOWN:
  representation/evaluation/selection/execution occupy one declared formal or physical carrier;

IOWN:
  one subject intentionally owns the reason-guided episode.
```

`ZRESP` does not entail `SEM`, `FIT_O`, `UPTAKE`, `BECAUSE_F`, or `IOWN`. `SCOWN` can be satisfied by a carrier-boxed impersonal controller and does not entail `IOWN`.
