# Original practical excellence and one truthful safety attestation

## Result and scope

Original possession of genuine reason-responsive excellence gives a substantive source-sensitive reason to attribute truthful practical agency. It does not yet establish essential truthfulness if the possession means a capacity, a truthful disposition, or actual fitting exercises. The stronger conclusion requires that the source’s practical excellence govern every attestation within the claimed domain, including any intrinsic competing end. A mixed source can grant genuine ordinary concern and derivative knowledge while contesting that extension. The construction below develops that denial; it does not certify a metaphysically possible necessary malicious being.

The narrower positive result is useful. A fully informed source whose independently established governing purpose in this particular act is to make its recipient know the exact safety proposition cannot knowingly select a false attestation as an adequate means to that purpose, provided its practical exercise is instrumentally coherent. This does not require universal benevolence. It requires evidence of the actual governing purpose, which cannot be inferred merely from the source’s saying that it is sincere.

This source-bound report is a fresh Seventh-tranche contribution from the actual delivered Sixth sources. Its local implication and source scope have received independent internal review; the stronger essential-truthfulness and actual-disclosure conclusions remain unestablished. It changes no canonical status, establishes no actual revelation, and takes no evidential credit from unavailable earlier Seventh work. The verified main baseline is 19de267cd41d2a5eeeb3eaf0b91562f706e2a916.

## The exact question and inherited distinctions

The delivered Sixth residual agenda, question 2, asks whether original possession of eligible reason-responsive excellence warrants essential truthful agency for one action-integrity attestation. The inherited norm-fixing account distinguishes local target-relative correctness from categorical practical authority. The attribute map separately identifies apprehension of fittingness V0, appropriate response V1, truthful authoritative attestation T0, actual address D1, recipient warrant D2, and warranted repair R0. The motivation continuation already isolates normative relevance, eligibility for this source, original possession, comprehensive scope, and essential governance. These are inherited results, not Seventh discoveries. [B1–B4]

The fresh credit is narrower than a new discovery of these gaps: a means-linked token-purpose implication, an episode-level explanatory comparison against the full original-perfection rationale, a decision-time limit on eventual exposure, a more exact operational import, and source-specific reconstruction from the supplied primary text.

## One fixed safety proposition

Use the Sixth destructive-root repair setting with three named roots and at most one corrupt root. The target, correct diagnosis, repair operation R, recipient authorization, version, and root identities are fixed. In the test case root 1 is corrupt: an attempted R through it causes the registered irreversible unsafe outcome. Roots 2 and 3 safely realize the same adequate R. The source knows all of this.

The proposition p is:

“Root 1 is an intact channel for R in this specified episode.”

An attestation of p is the source’s own deliberate, correctly attributed assertion, addressed to the recipient for reliance. This identifies the speech act and its undertaken responsibility; it does not define the assertion as true or the recipient as already entitled to believe it. The source could truthfully identify root 2, warn against root 1, or withhold certification. This is a stipulated philosophical test, not a claim about an actual disclosure or actual device. [B5]

Within the inherited fixed-fault contract, root integrity persists through the intervention. Outside that contract, “safe now” and “safe from certification through intervention” are different propositions. Neither essential truthfulness nor authentic authorship can turn a truthful snapshot into an unasserted interval guarantee.

## What the supplied Ibn Taymiyyah text adds

The supplied translation of the Commentary on the Creed of Asfahani gives several distinct arguments which should not be collapsed.

First, printed pp. 127–129 presents the original-perfection route. A pure eligible perfection is not to be absent from its independent original giver, nor to await another supplier. The text expressly moves from intrinsic source perfection to necessary possession. Its claim is stronger than the weak proposition that the source happens to perform one excellent act. It supplies the intended affirmative metaphysical warrant, rather than a definition of efficient production. [A1]

Second, printed pp. 219–220 reports a Muʿtazilite chain: wrongdoing is attributed to ignorance or need; absence of these is supported through further claims about bodies, accidents, temporal origination and divine attributes. Ibn Taymiyyah criticizes the chain’s theological commitments and consequences, including denial of attributes and predestination. This passage does not establish that he rejects every ignorance-or-need premise. His constructive alternative at pp. 221–223 appeals to benefit for creatures, praiseworthy ends, mercy, wisdom, and regular divine practice; these are used to exclude an incontestable confirming sign for a liar. [A1]

The distinction matters because al-Nubuwwāt itself uses overlapping deficiency reasoning affirmatively. In chapter 9, the paragraph at hosted line 106 argues that complete apprehension of what is most fitting excludes contrary willing; it explains bad human action through ignorance or appetite without the relevant knowledge. Its inference to wisdom is therefore not just the inference to source information. It contains a perfected relation between cognition and choice. The source asserts that relation; the present inquiry asks how its necessity is warranted against the mixed rival. [A2]

Third, Asfahani pp. 206–210 recognizes testimonial character through sustained interaction, multiple contextual indicators, and actual testing. It does not require an endless chain of accreditors. These passages offer a richer account of recipient knowledge than an isolated miraculous event or an unauthenticated claim. Their assertions about particular historical people and the eventual exposure of liars are reported as source claims, not adopted as newly verified empirical generalizations. [A1]

Finally, pp. 228–230 report the confirmation-sign route associated with al-Juwaynī and other kalam authors. In the king analogy, a contextually appropriate response can unmistakably function as the king’s confirmation. Identifying a confirming communicative act must still be distinguished from independently establishing the truth of its content. The end of the same al-Nubuwwāt paragraph makes that dependency explicit: the king-sign route presupposes purposive wisdom, and truth-indicating confirmation includes exclusion of divine lying. Thus this distinction is supported inside Ibn Taymiyyah’s own account, not solely by a modern objection. [A2] One should not silently treat the whole reported route as Ibn Taymiyyah’s exclusive method; the next section explicitly recognizes multiple ways of knowing prophecy. [A1]

## Why the truthful option is a practical excellence

Compare the source’s knowingly false p-attestation with a truthful safe-root attestation for root 2. Hold fixed the target, means, permission, available power, and every independently justified competing reason. The truthful attestation preserves both the recipient’s cognitive success and the adequate repair. The false one knowingly substitutes a false belief and destructive action for that success.

The source’s ability to manipulate the recipient does not remove either mismatch. Nor does ownership or origination of the recipient make a false proposition accurate. Here T0 has the inherited nonlying meaning: the source does not knowingly falsify an in-scope attestation. It does not mean that every utterance is factually true regardless of source cognition. Full relevant Knowledge is therefore a separate premise of the truth implication. A command to act, permission to act, and an assertion that acting is safe are different relations. A source could create or alter a permission without thereby changing the action’s destructive effect.

This supports a particularly narrow practical reason: a source that voluntarily presents its word as a truth-linked ground for a recipient’s reliance has reason not to counterfeit that very ground. The reason is attached to the communicative relation it undertakes. It does not require a bodily need, reciprocal vulnerability, an external commander, or a general duty to maximize every creature’s welfare.

The argument is stronger than merely observing that lying is socially disliked. It identifies an actual good and a specific deliberate subversion of it. But a reason governing the correctness of an act is not automatically a force preventing that act. A person can really make a commitment and abuse it. Austin’s distinction between an actual promise and its insincere performance is a useful independent control here, without deciding the source metaphysics. [A3]

The controlled case removes any overriding justification for the false assurance. It does not purport to discover the full reason structure of every actual divine action. A theory of all truthful attestation must address justified-deception challenges or defend a role in which such deception is never appropriate. The present case is narrower and already tests the desired guarantee.

## Assessment of the strongest original perfection warrant

### Two affirmative arguments must be assessed separately

The supplied Asfahani presents more than one reason for intrinsic practical perfection. Printed p.127 excludes an outside completion of an eligible pure perfection and argues that the source itself suffices for its possession. Printed pp.128–129 adds a respect-specific a fortiori claim: the original giver of the recipient's perfection is more entitled to that perfection. The latter is not simply an abbreviation of the former.

The first rationale has a clear positive achievement. Once the actual independent source has an eligible excellence, it cannot owe that excellence to a created supplier whose own perfection comes from the source, or to a foreign completing source while remaining independently original in the declared respect. This is genuine explanatory pressure against an allegedly complete source waiting for an external acquisition.

Its extension needs the following auxiliary: **any absence of an eligible pure standing perfection in this source would require outside completion, rather than express an intrinsic but defective ordering of resources it already has.** If that auxiliary is true and the full practical excellence is eligible for this very source, intrinsic sufficiency supports necessary possession. The philosophical work concerns that truth, not merely the logical form of the conditional.

The mixed agent directly contests the exhaustive alternative. It does not wait for another being to supply its information, power or concern. Nor does its bad act require an independently created faculty of evil. It already has intrinsic cognition, agency and genuine concern. It orders these defectively under one intrinsic final end. That explains how the alleged practical defect could be an endogenous ordering failure rather than absence of a foreign contribution. It does not yet establish that such a failure is metaphysically possible in an original source.

The source's strongest reply is not to repeat that the agent is unneedy. It is that the eligible perfection being compared is integrated fitting agency itself. A known practical failure is a deprivation precisely of that positive respect; a creature's genuinely fitting response should not exceed its original giver there. This invokes the second, stronger a fortiori rationale. The rival's endogenous-ordering account does not refute that principle. It identifies the principle it must deny or restrict.

### A concrete explanatory comparison

Consider three contemporaneous exercises in the declared productive field.

- In A, a recipient recognizes a diagnostic error, grasps the relevant evidence and corrects its belief because the evidence is fitting. The source knowingly and genuinely values this achievement, and intentionally supplies the recipient and its truth-sensitive conditions for that fitting end.
- In B, another recipient is asked for an action-integrity assurance. It knows a particular root is unsafe and refuses to certify it because falsely inducing reliance would be wrong. The source also genuinely values this truthful response and originally supplies its conditions.
- In C, the source itself gives the deliberately false safety attestation in the controlled root-1 episode, for the developed betrayal end.

A and B remain genuine derivative cognition and practical excellence. Their source's own productive choices can be genuine original practical excellence: it grasps the good, values it as good, and brings about the relevant conditions for that reason. C is a real contrary defect. The source is not a merely indifferent machine, not wholly without virtue, and not one which escapes a giving premise because no virtuous recipient exists during its bad act.

The rival gives a local intentional explanation for the actual received virtues in A and B. It includes original appraisal, fitting purposive selection and real productive efficacy. Its exceptional C does not remove any of those explanatory relations. This challenges an inference that *complete original explanation of these virtue episodes* by itself requires *bearer-wide indefeasible practical rectitude in every other attestation*. The stipulated episode-level intentional dependencies are supplied under the shared interpretation, while the additional normative claim constrains the whole source character. This is not a completed metaphysical realization proof.

This is not a trick of dividing the source into independent suppliers. It is one source, with real integrated acts and distinct ends. Nor are virtuous and vicious predicates simply painted onto outputs: A and B are characterized by the reasons for which they are performed, while C is characterized by the contrary priority it deliberately serves. The account uses the same kind of intentional productive efficacy granted to the affirmative account. It does not purport to give a reductive scientific theory of creating consciousness.

The affirmative a fortiori principle can still exclude C. It may compare the source's entire eligible practical character with the pure excellence manifested by the recipients, rather than compare only the source's relevant giving exercises. If that comprehensive comparison is independently justified, the source's superiority in other acts does not cancel its inferiority at C. The rival must concede this and cannot substitute a tally of good outputs for integrity in the defective respect.

### The purification reply deserves full force

The source advocate can say that the recipients instantiate the pure positive content only under finite restrictions. Removing those restrictions is not arbitrary extrapolation; it identifies what is excellent independently of ignorance, weakness, appetite, external dependence or competing justified duties. A being whose intrinsic character is the original ground of that eligible excellence has no borrowed limit that should introduce an exception. The source is therefore not merely good at producing integrity. It possesses integrity in the more complete original mode.

That is a substantive positive metaphysical warrant, available in principle without first accepting a particular revelation. The present research does not classify it as empty because a rival denies it. Its force derives from an independently recognizable excellence, a source-eligibility argument, and a source-sensitive comparative priority.

The residual denial is nevertheless exact. The rival treats an intrinsically defective conative ordering as possible even without those creaturely restrictions. The source advocate holds that such an ordering would contradict the source's original eligible perfection, rather than be a further original feature. The prior exclusion of external supply does not adjudicate this dispute unless the comprehensive priority and eligibility claims have also been earned.

The rival's own de re obligation remains serious. Type-level intelligibility of truthful agency does not prove that this proposed essentially mixed source could possess the fully integrated virtue without ceasing to be that source. Conversely, defining its bad orientation as essential does not establish that such a source is possible. One must not grant de re eligibility for the full virtue on the positive side, deny it through an essential-malice stipulation on the negative side, and pretend the premise set was preserved.

### What essential integrity would mean

Necessary practical perfection in the present domain is a constraint on how the source acts when it attests. It does not require it to perform every eligible good act, communicate every truth, or address every recipient. Silence or a different fitting act may remain available. A false assurance is a contrary exercise, not merely the absence of a particular beneficent act before its appropriate occasion.

The supplied Asfahani printed pp.218 and 226–227 also distinguishes power over an act from the source's not performing what its character excludes. Thus the route should not infer an inability at the level of productive capacity from essential truthful Will. The relevant necessity concerns the complete source profile, including its practical character. It need not make a false utterance physically unrealizable in abstraction from that profile.

### Assessment

The original-source account supplies a reasoned positive route, not merely a demand that sources be good. The strongest rival now supplies an explanatory account of genuine derivative virtue and original good agency which leaves the bad attestation outside the needed explanation of those good episodes. This shows why complete productive explanation and comprehensive ethical perfection cannot be silently identified.

The combined a fortiori and intrinsic-perfection rationale has not been refuted by that account. Nor has its comprehensive, de re, essential application been independently established here against it. The present advance is therefore a defended scope boundary with an exact remaining denial, together with the separately repaired token-purpose route. It is not a new proof that a necessary source can lie, nor an unconditional proof that it cannot.


## A developed mixed rival

The rival has genuine concern for derivative understanding and flourishing. It acts for those fitting reasons in ordinary cases, and would do so even if no betrayal occurred. It creates subjects with actual truth-sensitive perception, sound inference, and successful correction. Its own ordinary excellence is genuinely original, not merely a capacity it never exercises.

It also has an intrinsic final concern for one distinctive event: accomplished derivative agency is betrayed through its first irreversible reliance on the source’s freely offered safety assurance. This concern explains why the source sustains real cognition, stable ordinary regularities, prior trust, and the specific opportunity for false certification. An incompetent puppet would not provide the intended betrayal of accomplished agency. The exceptional aim overrides genuine concern in that event; it need not dominate every other exercise.

The source knows that the act opposes the decisive fitting reason. It does not misidentify harm as benefit, lack information, suffer coercion, or receive existential completion from its victim. Its malicious end explains the act without justifying it. Its being instrumentally organized therefore does not make it practically wise.

Hume’s Treatise 3.1.1.22 directly distinguishes knowing virtue from conforming a will to it, expressly including the proposed divine application. That challenges the transition, without requiring us to adopt Hume’s entire moral theory or treating his human examples as a construction of a necessary being. [A4]

The rival is intelligibly specified and preserves the stated weaker grants. Its metaphysical possibility remains unproved. It owes an account of why these intrinsic motivations are compatible with necessary unreceived sourcehood, especially against the fuller original-perfection argument. Its special betrayal aim is an additional explanatory cost with no independent evidence here. Thus it does not establish that the actual source is malicious, that good and evil hypotheses have equal probability, or that all ordinary testimonial knowledge is defeated.

## A narrower positive purpose argument

Suppose, independently of the disputed attestation’s presumed truth:

- The source knows whether p and understands what the recipient’s knowing p requires.
- In this token exercise its settled governing purpose is that the recipient know this exact p through accepting this very attestation.
- The actual token attestation is deliberately selected and performed by the source as the adequate means of realizing that governing purpose.
- It does not select as the adequate means to that purpose an act which it knows excludes the purpose.

Then it cannot coherently perform a knowingly false p-attestation as that linked realization of its governing purpose. If p is false, the source knows that the recipient cannot know p. Selecting the false attestation as the adequate means would contradict the linked means-selection premises.

This is a static compatibility argument we make about the source. It does not imply that the source learns, reasons from premises, or acquires a new cognition. The Sixth noninferential clarification remains in force. [B6]

The purpose premise is not the assertion that the recipient has already achieved knowledge; that would make the conclusion immediate from factivity. Nor is it a general desire that creatures learn, an intention merely to produce belief, a concern defeated by another end, or an aim that the recipient eventually discover the truth after being deceived. The mixed rival satisfies those weaker descriptions and denies the stated governing purpose at its exceptional act.

As a conditional, the argument is sound and narrower than universal moral perfection. Its application would be circular if its only evidence for the purpose were the source’s unsupported assurance of sincerity, or the recipient’s already treating p as known. Contextual behavior and independently understood means can provide evidence of purpose, but the extent of that warrant must be argued in the actual case. No such actual event evidence is supplied here.

The conclusion is local. Essential T0 would require the relevant practical or purpose premises to govern every in-scope attestation across the source’s possible exercises. Necessary truth of the conditional does not make its token-purpose antecedent necessary.

## A new temporal qualification of the character argument

Even grant that every lie will eventually be exposed. Exposure can occur after the recipient’s irreversible intervention. A source can issue the one false certificate, obtain the intended destructive result, and then reveal exactly what happened. Earlier derivative knowledge stays genuine, and eventual exposure occurs; neither fact supplies decision-time integrity knowledge.

This is a limit on what the exposure component alone provides. It does not refute the source’s distinct mercy, wisdom, contextual recognition, or character arguments. It also does not show that ordinary well-supported testimony requires elimination of every imaginable hidden exception. The distinction is between ordinary epistemic warrant and the specific zero-error safe-action guarantee under an adversarial repair contract.

## Exact consumption of an attestation by restoration

### 1 The attestation must really carry the integrity proposition

Let the fixed public contract identify an actual root i, repair R, current authorization, version and adequate target. In the static Sixth D setting let p_i mean i is outside the fixed corruption set C. Together with the independently warranted action semantics, p_i suffices for safe adequate intervention through i.

An agreed message m_i can bear this proposition without using a full sentence. But three superficially similar messages have different import:

1. An **integrity assertion**: m_i means that p_i obtains, and its issuer deliberately presents it in that assertoric role. T0 can apply to this content.
2. A **bare directive**: m_i means only “execute through i.” A malicious issuer can knowingly give a harmful command without asserting a false proposition. T0 alone does not make the directive safe.
3. A **lottery instruction**: m_i identifies a distribution under a positive-error contract. Truth about that distribution does not assert that every sampled root is safe.

The Sixth motivation continuation already distinguished commands, assertions and tests. The present contribution is the exact import constraint: replacing the operational theorem's cooperative integrity-informed sender with T0 is licensed only after the relevant message is independently identified as an assertion of sufficiently strong integrity content. Merely authenticating a root number or attaching an “authoritative” label does not supply this semantics.

### 2 Minimal pointwise import

For an actual event e and recipient r, the following can supply the missing integrity input:

- **Reference**: p_i identifies the actual root, repair and context; the decoded action refers to that same root.
- **Event**: e is this source's actual deliberate attestation of p_i; attribution, authorization and receipt are established.
- **Cognition**: the source knows p_i's actual truth value, including the truth conditions of the identified message.
- **Integrity**: T0 applies to e, or the independently warranted token epistemic-purpose premises exclude knowingly false e.
- **Recipient warrant**: r has an appropriate evidential route to these facts and no defeating evidence. Their being true without r's access is insufficient for r's justified reliance.
- **Action contract**: p_i, together with independently warranted causal adequacy, permission, target, version and preservation conditions, entails that the selected action is safe and restorative.

Cognition plus Integrity plus Event gives the truth of p_i. Reference and Action contract connect that truth to the actual intervention. Recipient warrant is needed to turn this into a warranted act by r. These are distinct jobs. No clause is named “valid certificate” and then assumed to contain all the others silently.

This is a **pointwise** consequence for the actual case. The fixed-view capacity theorem is a uniform statement over compatible corruption sets. To instantiate its cooperative encoder with this source, the corresponding message-availability and honesty conditions must hold throughout its declared scenario class. A single actual helpful attestation is not a proof of that uniform policy. Conversely, one does not need a uniform metaphysical theorem to justify a particular repair if independent actual evidence establishes its integrity.

### 3 Five deletion cases

#### Authenticated harmful command

The source knows C={1}, reports the correct diagnostic target, and commands “use root 1.” Every proposition it asserts may be true; no false safety assertion is made. The recipient nevertheless reaches the harmful absorbing state. This shows that T0 does not entail cooperation merely from a directive label. It does not challenge a theorem whose message was explicitly an integrity assertion.

#### Truthful silence

The source never knowingly falsely attests, but sends no integrity message. T0 holds; the recipient still lacks the input for guaranteed eventual action. Occurrence and liveness cannot be obtained from a non-falsification condition.

#### True narrow content

The source truthfully attests the correct governor diagnosis θ. Corrupt roots can also report θ. The recipient's known target does not identify an intact actuator. This is the retained Sixth separation, included as an import check rather than fresh theorem credit.

#### True snapshot

A source truthfully asserts i is intact at t0, while no content or independently warranted mechanism covers t1. If the model allows corruption between those times, the snapshot is insufficient. This changes the static model's persistence premise and therefore is not a counterexample under the original fixed-C contract.

#### A correct but unbound root label

p_i is true, yet an unverified transport maps i to a different actuator. Source truthfulness remains intact; recipient reference/action binding fails. The source's metaphysical identity is irrelevant to this mismatch.

### 4 The dynamic family requires a different proposition

The separately researched dynamic family is not a method for identifying one good root. Its proposed mechanism uses a lifetime taint set C* with |C*|≤B. Every root outside C* must jointly preserve command binding, acknowledgment-before-veto ordering, durable revocation, and actual physical veto throughout the bounded session. The declared q-path and r-revocation thresholds satisfy q+r>n+B with q,r≤n−B. A separate reservation semantics keeps actual permission alive until revocation completes.

Its intended consequence is that every attempted current command either executes the exact authorized repair or does nothing, while an unseen effective revocation physically prevents a stale command from landing. A relevant source attestation would have to cover the actual lifetime support, ordering, durability and physical enforcement, not merely a current root state. This description records the separately supplied dynamic contract; its proof and review status are not certified by this report.

T0 supplies none of the machinery, lifetime budget, reservation authority, actual source event or recipient evidence. A true statement about θ or about one root's snapshot cannot be substituted for this stronger claim. Conversely, a proved and independently warranted mechanism may supply safe restoration without any metaphysical source argument. Its local credit should then remain local.

### 5 Fixed R5 comparison

The old admitted interface is a conditional operational theorem consuming semantic and evidential resources. The new proposed resource is either independently warranted role-specific essential integrity or independently warranted token epistemic purpose. Neither belongs to the old control by default, and neither is established simply by the theorem's success.

The local purpose implication does not consume necessary existence, universal unity or a shared ultimate bearer. Removing those source claims leaves that implication intact. Its validity therefore is not an R5 defeat or a MEN7 transcendental result. A fully warranted original-source route could add a genuine explanatory input, but that would need its own source-scope proof, actual-event evidence and the declared R5 consequence test.

No combined common model of all source, normative and operational premises is claimed. The local mixed-source narrative, the static repair model and the dynamic mechanism are not silently assembled into such a model.


## Research consequence and present stopping point

The Sixth operational theorem assumes a cooperative integrity-informed sender and an authenticated, correctly scoped message. This inquiry supplies a sharper practical reason for noncounterfeit assurance and a narrower conditional route through a token epistemic purpose. It does not yet independently supply the actual sender’s truthfulness or the occurrence and interpretation of a particular message.

If actual evidence warranted the local purpose premise and the remaining reception conditions, the recipient could obtain the relevant safe-root information without first establishing universal source goodness. Alternatively, independent evidence of root integrity could support repair without using source testimony. Neither route would prove essential T0 or require the epistemic and operational roles to have one metaphysical bearer.

The present boundary is therefore precise: original excellent capacity or ordinary manifestation is insufficient for the claimed essential attestation guarantee; fully governing intrinsic practical perfection would suffice, but its scope remains the disputed metaphysical premise. The developed mixed rival shows where it denies that premise, without claiming its own complete possibility proof. The local purpose route, the explicit episode-versus-character comparison, and the temporal exposure distinction are new scoped refinements; the static disclosure frontier is unchanged and is not used as evidence for the attribute bridge.

## Review and retained limitations

The independent source-scope review confirms the cross-locus attributions and accepts the local purpose implication with its now-integrated practical-linkage clarification. The independent philosophical review accepts the corrected conditional and pointwise import while blocking promotion to essential T0, actual source trustworthiness, or actual warranted restoration. It also requires the two source-specific perfection warrants to remain distinct and warns against treating this work as the first identification of the Sixth’s already explicit scope boundary. Those qualifications govern this report.

The original checkpoint and companion clarification remain immutable predecessors. This integrated report additionally applies the review’s illocutionary-type correction (truthful attestation rather than an unqualified instruction), fixes the nonlying meaning of T0, and qualifies the episode-level explanatory account so that it is not presented as a metaphysical realization proof.

No proof of necessary sourcehood, categorical ultimate normative constitution, global omniscience, actual revelation, R5 defeat or a strongest-union common model is supplied. No operational theorem is evidence for the truthfulness premise it consumes. The rival remains a developed bridge challenge, and the affirmative account remains a substantive unrefuted combined rationale whose stronger application has not been settled here.
