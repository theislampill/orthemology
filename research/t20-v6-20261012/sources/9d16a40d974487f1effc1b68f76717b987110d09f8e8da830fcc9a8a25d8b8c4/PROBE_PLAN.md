# Stronger testimony and institutional rivals

Research spike, 7 October 2026. Reproducible experimental scripts are research instruments, not production software. The existing T20 finite cases keep their credit. No closure judgement is requested from this worker.

## Questions fixed before execution

1. Does voluntary source-to-carrier token transfer, including actual causal dependence, establish that the source used it to certify a mission? Test attestation against repair, sealing, theft, finding, and a truthful sending claim followed by a false extra report. Observe physical transfer separately from an independently retained prior instruction. Do not infer infallibility from ownership.
2. Does a fresh challenge response establish origin and purpose? Test a fixed replay, an adaptive counterfeiter, and an authorized maintenance operator who can obtain a real current source response without being commissioned as a reporting envoy. Compute which rivals survive increasing evidence; preserve indistinguishability when every allowed observable matches. This is an ordinary finite token analogue, not a prophecy-authentication experiment.
3. If a constitution can be amended, can the same service and same fresh appointment be lawful in one executed constitutional history and unlawful in another? Evaluate the prior amendment rule, then current voters, timing, publication and appointment. Add a legitimate independently installed replacement and a causally descended but unauthorized bearer. Do not equate conventional validity, ethical legitimacy, physical descent and numerical identity.
4. Can a lossy discourse export make different source acts and contextual contents observationally identical? Generate retained fragments from original contextual assertion/quotation/report structures, then expose independently retained framing/context. Test truth valuations and non-endorsing reports rather than distinguishing tags only. Translation is controlled syntax, not an Arabic translation-quality claim.

## Chosen method and realistic stopping condition

Execute small, enumerated transition histories and observation functions. Start with concrete counterexample tests. Compute surviving rival sets and minimal sufficient additional observation families for a specified judgement. Include failure when an observation channel is compromised and withhold any certainty claim for excluded but still materially plausible rivals. No new general fibre theorem, cryptographic construction or computability theorem is claimed.

The targeted work stops after these source-motivated instances have replayable positive/negative outcomes and precise residual evidence burdens, or if a specific required premise cannot be independently supported. It does not decide the larger programme's closure. The original owner retains that decision.

## Evidence quality rather than additional labels

A model observer may project a witnessed physical action, retrieve an earlier independently stored instruction, inspect a founding constitution, or read an unabridged discourse prefix. A Boolean named authentic, legitimate or intended is not an observation. The accuracy, access rights and independence of these observation channels are explicit application assumptions. Model truth is separately evaluated on states. Counterfactual worlds may share every displayed token, signature label, output, and current service state while differing in unobserved history.
