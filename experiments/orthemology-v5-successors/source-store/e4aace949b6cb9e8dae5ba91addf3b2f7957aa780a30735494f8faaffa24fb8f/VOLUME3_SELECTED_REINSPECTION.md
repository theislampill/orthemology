# Separate selected reinspection of volume 3

Dated study-stage record, 7 October 2026. Its provisional and historical statements are preserved; this is not a later after-all-books verdict.

This is a separate reader's selected examination after the primary volume 3 reader had continuously traversed the indicated passages. It does not add unique first-pass volume credit and is not a whole-volume second traversal. Final volume-wide appraisal awaits the primary reader's complete body coverage.

## V03L0069–0080

The opponent explicitly formulates the certification problem in two premises: the extraordinary act was done to certify the claimant, and anyone certified by God is truthful. The response first notes that the broad preferred political group includes wisdom-affirming schools; it does not pretend every school holds one theory. It then rejects an unsupported claim that prophecy can only be recognized by extraordinary signs.

Several distinct proposed answers are surveyed. L0075 offers immediate recognition through a king's requested exceptional action; L0076 describes an argument from otherwise rendering the king/source unable to certify anyone; L0077 treats the event as enacting commission rather than asserting an independently truth-apt report. These must not be conflated. The latter is an alternative theory's move from assertion to institution, not automatically proof of every proposition a commissioned person might utter.

L0078 reconstructs how a theorist denying final-cause explanations might still posit co-willed sign/truth entailment. It expressly states that the author does not himself favor the no-wisdom theory. A local dialectical defense of an opponent against a worse objection is not his comprehensive adopted position.

L0079 contains strong affirmative veracity reasoning. Producing a creature's wrongful act is distinguished from the divine source itself falsely certifying a claim. An act functioning as a certification of a liar would be a divine falsehood; falsehood is treated as a rationally evident defect, truthful speech as perfection, and the divine exclusion of defect as independently warranted. This is not simply a stipulative stipulation that whatever the selected source does counts as true. The actual epistemic burdens remain the perfection/defect premise, the event's function as assertion-like certification, the claimant and content identified, and the reliability of the report of the event. L0080 criticizes relying only on consensus while borrowing a rational defect premise one has elsewhere disallowed.

## V03L0101–0110

The source affirms real creaturely power while requiring assisting causes and absence of obstacles. A complete determinant of an actual new exercise must explain why it occurs then; unchanged standing power alone does not distinguish occurrence from nonoccurrence. L0102–0104 applies this to created willing and contests selective use of an unexplained-preference allowance for divine action while rejecting it for human action.

L0105 distinguishes a regress in efficient sources or in completion of the current productive ground from succession of effects. A supposedly new enabling completion arising only with the very act whose production awaits it is the problematic same-priority circle/regress. Endless succession of intrinsic voluntary acts is treated separately. L0108–0110 consequently combines perpetual divine ability and voluntarily renewed intrinsic acts with temporal beginning of each external created object. The source is not saying that an intrinsic exercise must be manufactured as a second independently subsisting bearer, nor that an eternally fixed token output constitutes all agency.

The account also rejects treating motion of a hand as a clear example of an originating efficient act simultaneous with its effect: the examples can involve conditions, or contiguous temporal succession. This matters when mapping formal relations: condition, completed productive exercise, resulting effect and source-bearer are not interchangeable slots.

## V03L0236–0246

This range distinguishes bare ability from its actual exercise through determinate will. Complete will plus power necessitates the intended result, yet that does not make the agent an impersonal timeless necessitating essence. L0239 explicitly says divine will is not from another; created will is contrasted with it. L0240–0242 disambiguates 'necessitating by essence' so that necessity of an actual result given complete free will is not confused with an eternally fixed world independently of will.

L0243–0246 discusses sovereigns' helpers, creaturely acts and celestial motion. Cooperating rulers rely on contributors they did not create and whose abilities exceed the ruler's own productive reach. The source's demand concerns adequate actual productive accounting, not merely possession of a title or a standing partial capacity. L0246 makes the contrast decisive: intrinsic acts of the self-necessary do not issue from another source. A new exercise can belong intrinsically to the original actor without the bearer or its intrinsic attribute being manufactured as a separate product. The continually changing exercise of a created celestial actor, by contrast, is said to require an external sufficient source because that actor is possible/derivative. This is a source-mode distinction with substantive premises, not a uniform formal edge called creation between all objects whatsoever.

The passage also distinguishes initiating/coloring/planting from being the complete originator of the resulting color or growth. An effect's occurring in accordance with a person's choice does not alone establish complete productive ownership. These affirmative constraints strengthen the intended strong source conception and constrain its typed reconstruction.

## V03L0266–0276

The source gives two Quranic arguments and says each is independently operative: separate divine domains would divide, and unequal rulers would subordinate one to another. It treats the world's integration as inconsistent with independently self-contained products. Ordinary collaborators retain different intrinsic actions and partial powers; their joint transport of a beam does not make one numerically identical act reside in two bearers.

Crucially, necessary harmony is not omitted. L0269–0273 considers compulsory agreement, compulsory disagreement, optional agreement/disagreement, and simultaneous corresponding wills. It interprets an inability to will or act except with the other's willing/acting as a condition contributing to each one's completed productive sourcehood. The condemned loop is therefore in part of the productive determinant, not merely numerical coexistence. L0271 allows cooperation-made-possible by an external third giver in created cases, while denying such a superior source for the proposed pair of ultimate creators.

L0272 also addresses a shared good and common foreknowledge. The candidate good is itself downstream from the supposed creators, so it cannot simply supply an independently antecedent governing explanation. Knowing the future event answers to what is known, while the event answers to its producer's will; foreknowledge is not thereby the productive origin of its own known occurrence. This is a genuine source-direction objection, not a missing obvious reply. The remaining appraisal question is whether the proposed coordination model really turns each will into a productive condition of the other's will, or merely describes a lawful joint outcome. The source offers reasons for its stronger classification; neither side may settle that classification by renaming the relation.

The social analogies involve a directing ruler, lawgiver, accepted expert, antecedent agreement, or alternating leadership. Those examples explain familiar collaboration but do not automatically prove that every metaphysically possible coordination must instantiate that finite list of social arrangements. Conversely, merely writing a harmonized countermodel does not establish its metaphysical possibility; volume 2 L0138 explicitly warns about that inference.

## V03L0194–0202

The text argues positively for divine truthfulness as perfection, with separate arguments for theories of intrinsic involuntary speech and intrinsic voluntary wise speech. The latter places the normative burden on the impossibility of intrinsic chosen defect rather than treating every created false human utterance as a false utterance by God.

The conclusion does not license treating every unusual event as certification. L0201 distinguishes divine 'sending' in a broad providential sense, including a liar's actual existence and activity, from authenticated prophetic commissioning. L0202 explicitly permits extraordinary happenings with false claimants where their falsity is independently exposed. A true sign entails its indicated truth, but classifying an encountered event as such a sign is a further evidence task. The variety of paths for discerning both truth and falsehood is expressly affirmed.

## V03L0410–0424

The closing discussion distinguishes two premises of authoritative guidance: there really is an infallible authority, and this particular instruction truly comes from that authority. The fallible chain of living teachers or books cannot be bypassed by asserting the original authority's perfection. L0414–0415 similarly rejects confidence as its own evidence: a purported guide who guarantees safety might be deceitful or ignorant, while a cautious guide can be rationally preferable. Suspension pending evidence is explicitly commended.

L0416 separates a sound universal conditional about salvation from knowledge that an individual satisfies its antecedent. L0417–0418 surveys testimony, corroboration and concordant dreams as possible grounds under the author's own epistemic/religious commitments. It also distinguishes a dream from self-talk or deception. This is not an independent empirical guarantee that any number of correlated reports must be true, and duplicated hosts do not thereby become independent witnesses.

L0421–0424 distinguishes absolute messengerly authority from limited institutional or scholarly authority and returns to the epistemic accessibility of the command actually transmitted. The explicit third-volume colophon is included in this independently reinspected ending. No present-day group accusation or source authenticity claim is adopted solely because it occurs in the polemical text.
