## 2. Qiyās al-awlā as an explicit rule, not a hidden theorem

For a fixed proposed bearer `d` and candidate predicate `P`, Deep G preserves
six project-level guards:

```text
IP   intrinsic praiseworthiness/perfection of P;
DA   total absence of P is a defect in the relevant respect;
NCL  creaturely limitation is removed rather than projected;
SRC  a relevant source relation licenses attribution to d;
NC   the capacity/attribute is nonconduit and bearer-local;
NEQ  attribution is nonequalizing and does not assimilate Creator to creature.
```

Define:

\[
Guards(d,P):=IP(P)\land DA(P)\land NCL(P)\land SRC(d,P)\land NC(d,P)\land NEQ(d,P).
\]

A school-internal candidate rule must be written as an additional premise:

\[
QAW\_RULE(d,P): Guards(d,P)\rightarrow DivineQual(d,P).
\]

Then:

\[
QAW\_RULE(d,P)\land Guards(d,P)\Rightarrow DivineQual(d,P)
\]

is only modus ponens. It does not establish the rule, the guards, the bearer,
the source, or the actual world. The six-guard package is an AR8R synthesis
unless a primary source is located that states them jointly. No such joint
primary locus was established in this tranche.

## 4. Source-locus adjudication

### 4.1 Positive meaning versus authorized Name

The translated Aṣfahāniyyah at printed p. 13 says that the meanings of
`al-murīd` and `al-mutakallim` are true while those expressions are not thereby
among the known Beautiful Names. It further distinguishes speech and volition,
which admit praiseworthy and blameworthy specifications, from Names that entail
praise.

This is a strong source-relative counterexample to:

\[
QUAL(b,P)\Rightarrow NAMEAUTH(s,n_P,b).
\]

It does not prove a universal theory of divine naming independently of the
source architecture; it does support the repository firewall between positive
qualification and Name authorization.

### 4.2 Attribute locus and conduit speech

At printed pp. 101–104, the translation presents a rule that an attribute’s
predication returns to the locus in which it subsists; a cause that makes
another locus utter is not thereby the speaker under that schema. The
first-person pronoun issue is used to resist identifying caused utterance in a
tree with the cause’s own Speech.

Formally, the source-relative rule is closer to:

\[
Subsists(P,b)\land EligibleDerivation(P,n)\Rightarrow Predicable(n,b),
\]

plus:

\[
Subsists(P,b)\land b\neq c\Rightarrow \neg PredicableFromThatToken(n,c).
\]

This constrains conduit models at Track-N scope. It does not by itself prove:

```text
that the translated rule is Arabic-primary exact;
that all Atharī authorities state it identically;
that the neutral world contains the proposed bearer;
that the relevant attribute actually subsists in that bearer;
that a general immanent realist theory of properties is true;
or that every caused utterance has the same source-theological status.
```

Nominalist, trope, immanent-realist, powers, and conceptualist paraphrases can
all encode a locus-sensitive predication rule. The source rule therefore does
not settle the general theory of properties.

### 4.3 Praiseworthy ends and Wisdom

Printed p. 227 offers benefit and praiseworthy ends as signs of mercy and
Wisdom. This is a source argument. It does not jointly state or world-establish
the six Deep BR guards, and it does not eliminate impersonal teleology,
selected-effect explanations, or a source/world referent failure.

## 6. Countermodels and deletion tests

### SB-CM18 — all guards except source relation

`P` is intrinsically excellent, absence is a defect, creaturely limits are
removed, capacity is bearer-local, and attribution is nonequalizing. No source
relation identifies `P` with `d`. The source-relative divine conclusion does
not follow.

### SB-CM19 — conduit capacity

A cause produces every speech token through distinct created loci but has no
bearer-local speech attribute under the source architecture. Delete `NC` and the
qiyās package overgenerates.

### SB-CM20 — capacity without occurrence

A bearer can speak but never performs the relevant act. Capacity does not
establish actual Speech.

### SB-CM21 — occurrence without positive qualification

A speaker lies or commands injustice. Speech occurrence does not establish a
praiseworthy specification or the Name “Wise.”

### SB-CM22 — positive attribute without Name authorization

The meaning of willing/speaking is true, while `al-murīd`/`al-mutakallim` is not
authorized as a Beautiful Name in the cited source treatment.

### SB-CM23 — occurrence without revealed wording

A true and praiseworthy speech act occurs, but it is not the particular revealed
wording under investigation. Occurrence does not identify content.

### SB-CM24 — wording/grammar twin

A personal architecture and an impersonal generator produce the same wording,
morphology, syntax, semantics, and recitation profile. Linguistic identity does
not identify mentality, uncreatedness, one bearer, or revelation.

### SB-CM25 — uncreated content / created token

A school-relative model treats the divine Speech/content as uncreated while a
human sound, inscription, memory trace, or recitation token is created. Deleting
the type distinction yields false token-level conclusions.

### SB-CM26 — authenticated wording without recipient warrant

The source and wording are authentic, but the recipient lacks access to the
chain, misunderstands the occurrence, or has an undefeated defeater. Source
truth is not recipient warrant.

### SB-CM27 — recipient warrant without execution

A recipient is warranted about the wording but does not recite, obey, or
instantiate the commanded action. Warrant and execution are different.

## 7. Proper-function and noetic effects

A speech-production mechanism may have a causal role, selected effect, learned
objective, or designed function while lacking truth, authorization, intentional
uptake, or a revealed source relation. A recipient’s reliable repetition may be
memorization rather than proper noetic function or restored fitrah.

Conversely, source-relative proper function cannot be inserted into a common
intervention as a neutral label. Doing so makes the intervention target-
dependent: the preferred architecture receives “authentic revelation” as an
input while rivals receive only text.
