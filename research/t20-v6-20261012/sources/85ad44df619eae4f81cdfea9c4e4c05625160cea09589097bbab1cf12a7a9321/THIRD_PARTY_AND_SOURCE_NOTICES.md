# Source and authorship notices

The `sources/` directory contains exact copies of selected textual members of user-supplied research archives. Their original authorship, copyright, licence, quotation boundaries and historical statuses remain attached to those originals. Inclusion here does not assign a new licence or change who produced a result.

The original archive/member and SHA-256 bindings are preserved in `inventory/SOURCE_REPORTS.json`. Source copies are content-addressed to avoid silently overwriting different revisions. Original archive files, compiled Lean dependencies, fonts, binaries and external academic PDF bodies are not redundantly embedded by this packet builder.

The guide, cross-front synthesis, foundational crosswalk and finite Python reference implementation are assistant-produced contributions for this handoff. They are separate from Dot's findings. Internal self-checking and separately implemented computational oracles are not external or human independent review.

The quoted T20 examples, including report/audit information, shared-proof overlap and inquiry-value reversal, retain their inherited credit. The corresponding regression implementations do not claim rediscovery of the underlying results.
