## Target-specific assessment

The actual reason-responsive correction remains the starting point. Its evidential and productive reality is retained. The issue is whether that reality warrants an account of existential availability at the precise E_A respect, rather than only the genuine received production of the episode. Tenth E2 §§4–6 and E3 §§2–5 already distinguish those questions.

A commitment to giving reasons for one's explanatory standards can regulate inquiry without specifying which concrete resources actually supply the target. An epistemic reason for treating two questions differently is not automatically an existential producer; conversely, the absence of a completed account of that distinction is not evidence that no reason exists. Suspension is distinct from an asserted exemption. An affirmative judgement may still receive basic or ampliative warrant; no demand for exhaustive rival elimination or experimental verification follows.

The strongest retained affirmative reason is that additional productive activity does not, solely through its output, supply the antecedent availability on which its channel depends. That local relevance assessment is already accepted. Extending it to a complete joint history requires the same target-sensitive application considered in the inherited papers. A different source for the general attraction of explanation does not erase this distinction.

Even an accepted explanation of the existential proposition leaves further questions about explanatory relation and scope. Necessary participation, complete productive coverage of this correction, identity of a particular bearer, and existence-entailing intrinsic necessity are separate achievements. They are not rejected for being rationally apprehended; none is supplied simply by reiterating answerability. Ninth's integrated active-rival assessment remains relevant because it retains genuine cognition and efficacy while testing collective adequacy, rather than replacing the episode with a Boolean output.

The present discriminator is consequently narrow: does the offered reason positively establish the relevant availability relation, or only put pressure on someone who denies that relation? No new positive relation-specific reason was obtained in this pass. This closes this candidate as a non-admission, without weakening the inherited prima-facie warrant or making a general impossibility claim.
