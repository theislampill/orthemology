### Deletion controls

Each conjunct is load-bearing for this **declared contract**:

- delete `Src`: the source-relative authority is unavailable;
- delete `Des`: a selected-effect or dispositional truth tracker can duplicate the neutral function profile without personal design;
- delete `Aim_T`: designed deception may be highly reliable relative to its installed end;
- delete `Fit` or `Op`: a correct output may be a lucky malfunction;
- delete `Env`: a properly constituted mechanism may fail outside its normal environment;
- delete `Und`: proper operation and truth can coexist with undefeatedness failure;
- delete `Acc_T`: proper design without success at this episode is not episode-level success.

This minimality is relative to the chosen conjunctive contract, not a theorem that every possible proper-function theory must use exactly these predicates.

## 5. Result FPF-1B — neutral-profile nonidentifiability

Let the registered neutral operational profile be

\[
q(w)=(accuracy,reliability,corrigibility,coherence).
\]

Suppose source history, designer intention, fiṭrah health, authority, and defeater status are not coordinates of `q`. Then there are two worlds with the same `q=(1,1,1,1)`:

1. a Track-N-positive world satisfying every `NPF_T` conjunct;
2. an impersonal selected-effect or powers-based truth tracker with the same output, reliability, corrigibility, and coherence, but no personal designer and therefore `¬NPF_T` under the declared Track-N contract.

Hence `NPF_T` is not constant on the fibres of `q`. Therefore no deterministic classifier using `q` alone can recover `NPF_T` exactly.

This is a direct application of the established profile/fibre factorization family. It is not new general mathematics.
