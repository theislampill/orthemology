## Will and Wisdom

The actual complete exercise can necessitate its effect without the generic standing nature necessitating that exercise. The packet neither treats a motivating reason as automatically a necessitating cause nor declares that contingent choice needs no account. It preserves the difficulty of genuinely alternative fitting acts under necessary standing perfection, especially against a necessitarian rival. A two-world control is not offered as a metaphysical possibility certificate.

The Wisdom defense is stronger than the slogan that complete information makes an agent good. It combines objective fittingness, appropriate response to apprehended reasons, freedom from distorting dependence, and intelligent beneficence. The pro-tanto-good argument gives the orientation premise normative content: recognized goods count in favor of acts, with defeaters and competing reasons. The source's intentional creation and governance place derivative goods within its field of action. Extending original-giver priority to such beneficence is a substantive practical-perfection application.

The remaining burden is honestly stated. Theoretical cognition alone does not establish motivation, cross-agent concern or its proper scope. A fully informed, unneedy malicious or indifferent orientation is the stronger challenge than ordinary human weakness. Nonneed cannot eliminate satisfaction in cruelty merely because satisfaction occurs while permitting renewed satisfaction in good acts on the same criterion. The packet now states this symmetry. Nor does cosmic occurrence entail moral approval or prove that every harm has a justifying hidden purpose.

These qualifications preserve a philosophically serious positive case. They do not reduce it to a bare stipulation; equally, accepting the case as argued is not accepting its decisive perfection premises as compulsory for every rival.
