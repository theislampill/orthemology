## Blocking findings

### AI-F01 — secondary reconstruction was treated too much like direct source law

The broad claim that universals/notions subsist only in the mind is supplied
here principally through El-Tobgui's scholarly reconstruction.  The Asfahani
line is a narrower translated-primary use inside one unity argument.  V2 must
separate them and retain the external source-review burden.

### AI-F02 — structural order, notional type, and mental token were conflated

An external or modal order can be represented by a mental universal without
being identical to that mental token.  V2 must distinguish the order, its
notional representation, and the hosting relation.

### AI-F03 — `mind/intellect-role` equivocated between mental host and ultimate ground

A faculty or entity in which a notion subsists is not thereby the constitutive
intellect that grounds the order.  V2 must type `MENTAL_HOST` separately from
`INTELLECTUAL_GROUND`.

### AI-F04 — the positive bridge imported its conclusion through H7b

`CONSTITUTIVE_HOSTING` is exactly the disputed move from mental representation
to grounding.  V2 may retain it only as an explicit hard premise and must not
present the resulting conditional as evidence for H7b.

### AI-F05 — underivability transfer was unsupported

An underived truth/order can be represented by a derived mind.  H7c is a new
bridge premise, not a consequence of source notionality.

### AI-F06 — uniqueness was too broadly stated

The formal guard establishes at most one constitutive host, not that only one
mind or representer exists.  V2 must state the exact uniqueness target.

### AI-F07 — the positive result was called an underived intellect too quickly

At most it yields an underived **constitutive intellectual host** in the
formal vocabulary.  Consciousness, knowledge in the rich sense, first-person
subjecthood, personality, and agency remain absent.

### AI-F08 — source-to-world coordinates were underdeveloped

Source bytes, locus, translation, attribution, proposition reconstruction,
source acceptance, and world adequacy remain separate.  The source
reconstruction cannot establish an externally actual mind without them.

### AI-F09 — the strongest rival needed to preserve the source ontology

The serious rival is not simply `order with no mind`; it is external impersonal
order plus derived local minds whose universals represent that order.  This
preserves the mental/external distinction while withholding a mental ultimate
ground.

### AI-F10 — source direct predication and neutral derivation were not fully separated

Asfahani may source-predicate Knowledge of one referent.  That can filter a
Track-N model under source guards, but it cannot be credited as a neutral
`structural order -> intellect` proof.

### AI-F11 — the formal check's personality coordinate was intentionally free

This is a valid nonentailment control only at the declared finite propositional
scope.  It does not establish metaphysical or explanatory parity.

### AI-F12 — theorem-family and novelty ceiling needed tightening

The result is a dependency-minimal bridge decomposition and source-formal
adjudication using standard propositional/model-theoretic methods.  General
mathematical novelty is zero.
