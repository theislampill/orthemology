## Core exposition and formal reference

### Main exposition

**Orthemma–Ortheme Systems: An Analysis-Relative Architecture for Auditable Classification and Handling.** Read the [Markdown manuscript][main] or its [governed PDF][render-orthemma-ortheme-systems-draft].

**Why read it:** this is the principal account of how one concrete occurrence moves through observation, competing descriptions, evidence, placement, routing and correction. An *orthemma* is the situated occurrence; its *orthemes* are repeatable, consequence-bearing state-types under a declared analysis. The actual profile is distinct from both an observation and an actor’s inferred placement. Analysis-relativity does not let the actor manufacture what is true.

The [integration thesis](../../manuscript/orthemma-ortheme-systems-revised-draft.md#13-the-thesis-stated-as-an-integration-discipline) is deliberately specific: combine established classification, uncertainty, provenance and handling practices in a shared lifecycle and an auditable **episode record**. The six additions concern versioned lineage; analysis/actor/time indices; typed and expiring evidence; factorised candidates; per-burden closure; and episode reification. The manuscript does not claim that each neighbouring technique is new.

Its [stopped-clock validator](../../manuscript/orthemma-ortheme-systems-revised-draft.md#85-worked-example-the-stopped-clock-validator) returns a correct answer merely because a marker occurs in a log. Removing or spoofing the marker exposes a defective method. The [result/pathway matrix](../../manuscript/orthemma-ortheme-systems-revised-draft.md#84-the-result--pathway-matrix) also admits the converse: an adequate, sufficiently reliable procedure can produce a rare wrong result. Pathway adequacy is therefore non-factive; factive token truth linkage is reported separately. Undetermined required checks are neither passes nor automatic failures. The distinctions direct different repairs instead of blaming every error on the same component.

The [empirical programme](../../manuscript/orthemma-ortheme-systems-revised-draft.md#13-empirical-program) separates machinery benefit, episode-record benefit and vocabulary benefit. Its ready-to-run packets and conformance fixtures are not completed experiments or proof of mathematical completeness. These are this manuscript’s evidence boundaries, not a denial of later separately scoped research. The [limitations](../../manuscript/orthemma-ortheme-systems-revised-draft.md#151-limitations) also deny that task-relative success supplies the legitimacy of the task or its authority. Edit the Markdown, not generated TeX or the PDF; this exposition does not silently incorporate every later tranche.

## Philosophical and theological manuscripts

### Ground of intelligibility

**Orthability and the Ground of Intelligibility: A Transcendental Argument from Rule-Governed Resolution.** [Source][ground] · [Governed PDF][render-orthability-ground-of-intelligibility-draft].

**Why read it:** it asks what makes genuine recognition, error, fittingness and correction possible, rather than treating the engineering rulebook as its own ultimate justification. It distinguishes local analysis-relative aptness (**L**), objective conditions of evaluability (**O**) and created representation/application (**R**). The [labelled argument chain](../../companion/orthability-and-the-ground-of-intelligibility.md#42-the-chain-with-each-step-labeled) passes from *true* evaluations to their objective preconditions, not from an arbitrary local convention to a reified object called Orthability.

The affirmative reductio is that an intelligible construction of **all** objective evaluability would already use identity, possibility, lawful transition and success/failure conditions. It would therefore presuppose what it claims wholly to produce. The [adjudication](../../companion/orthability-and-the-ground-of-intelligibility.md#53-adjudication) makes its reach explicit: an opponent who instead accepts brute modal structure or refuses the intelligible-production model is not thereby demonstratively refuted. Reliability alone does not establish a Designer; the faculty argument separately needs irreducible teleology and causal adequacy. Intellect, Will, Power and Wisdom are not obtained by relabelling one result. The [step map][orthability-step-map] explicitly holds the common-premise Wisdom bridge.

The [disclosure argument](../../companion/orthability-and-the-ground-of-intelligibility.md#8-a-ground-capable-of-disclosure) supports at most a **conditional capacity** for intentional disclosure, not that the ground has actually spoken or that a particular text is its speech. The separate Atharī manuscript supplies its own revelational premises.

**Reading the historical claim correctly:** the source’s [later V11 authority qualification](../../companion/orthability-and-the-ground-of-intelligibility.md#later-v11-authority-qualification) says that this positive philosophical language is not itself the current audit verdict; it directs that verdict to Track T/B0–B16 and retains `NECESSARY_BEING_NOT_DEDUCTIVELY_ESTABLISHED`. That explicit qualification stays with this work. The [later source-bound foundational account](RESEARCH-ATLAS.md#a-positive-foundational-route) has its own premises and appraisals; this catalogue neither merges the assessments nor rewrites the older argument. Engineering conformance supplies no metaphysical proof.

**Why read it:** this is the explicitly Atharī/Taymiyyan articulation of divine Knowledge, Speech, linguistic possibility and created reception. It is not merely the preceding philosophical conditional with a religious title attached. ### Information and composition

**Information, Composition, and Self-Revision: Local Descriptions and Operative Access.** [Preserved PDF][information-pdf] · [Tracked TeX][information] · [Edition relationship note][lineage].

**Why read it:** this manuscript connects what a local description determines with what an observing method can learn by reconstructing its access. It does not infer an actual coupling from compatible component records. Its [relational strengthening theorem](https://github.com/theislampill/orthemology/blob/0784acddd4770f1190f758f264ba52b2b110bd27/theory/lineages/h-ics-v5/information-composition-and-self-revision-v5.tex#L81) separates an invariant that *some* exactly compatible composition can guarantee from one that *every* compatible composition guarantees. With nonempty compatible records, the first asks whether `G = J ∩ P` still projects onto every declared local relation; the second requires `J ⊆ P`. The parity family shows that even all proper subsystem records can leave a joint constraint undetermined.

For probabilities, compatible support is not sufficient for compatible weights. The [output-determination proposition](https://github.com/theislampill/orthemology/blob/0784acddd4770f1190f758f264ba52b2b110bd27/theory/lineages/h-ics-v5/information-composition-and-self-revision-v5.tex#L202) uses the span `D` of **feasible differences** between laws sharing the record: a channel has the same output law on that family exactly when it annihilates `D`. Replacing `D` by the whole record kernel requires the stated full-support condition. This matters at boundary laws, where algebraically possible directions need not be feasible perturbations.

Its [operative-access argument](https://github.com/theislampill/orthemology/blob/0784acddd4770f1190f758f264ba52b2b110bd27/theory/lineages/h-ics-v5/information-composition-and-self-revision-v5.tex#L241) distinguishes reformatting an insufficient observation from acquiring an informative relation. A permitted reconnection can make an already reachable parity observation immediately available. That is neither evidence created from nothing nor a claim to expand beyond every inherited operation. The [sure-finite-decision theorem](https://github.com/theislampill/orthemology/blob/0784acddd4770f1190f758f264ba52b2b110bd27/theory/lineages/h-ics-v5/information-composition-and-self-revision-v5.tex#L289) then gives an exact labelled-belief criterion for finite deterministic observation-based controllers. The labels retain the **initial** answer through state changes. In the destructive example, every pair has a distinguishing experiment, but neither first action preserves enough information for one policy to decide every initial possibility.

The version-bound workflow applies the same distinctions to candidate receipts: two success flags are insufficient when they omit the candidate identities. It requires the joint guard to remain valid through publication, not just individually consistent field reads. The source supplies conditional models and ordinary arguments, not a verified concurrent implementation, noise-tolerant controller, physical mechanism or metaphysical proof. It acknowledges the antecedents of its join, marginal and state-identification techniques rather than claiming each is new.

The lineage note establishes the title/abstract and companion relationship. It does **not** establish a byte-identical rebuild of the supplied PDF. The discussion above is grounded in the tracked TeX; that source is not silently promoted to the verified exact build input of the preserved edition.

#### The related reflexive-revision manuscript

**Reflexive Revision under an Evidence Budget.** [Tracked TeX manuscript][reflexive].

The [minimum-evidence-cost section](https://github.com/theislampill/orthemology/blob/0784acddd4770f1190f758f264ba52b2b110bd27/theory/lineages/h-reflexive-v3/reflexive_revision_evidence_budget_v3.tex#L47) specifies `V_g(S)`: zero when the remaining situations agree on the decision; otherwise minimise probe cost plus the largest remaining branch cost. Its theorem characterises exactly when a finite budget guarantees a uniformly sound answer. Identifiability and affordability differ, and affording the next probe alone does not ensure that the entire deciding continuation fits. Policy computation and validation of the model are **not** included in these probe costs. Hard constraints remain vetoes rather than compensable penalties.

The [reuse and reflexivity section](https://github.com/theislampill/orthemology/blob/0784acddd4770f1190f758f264ba52b2b110bd27/theory/lineages/h-reflexive-v3/reflexive_revision_evidence_budget_v3.tex#L86) explains why a retained record can settle one target yet fail a changed target. Under unchanged meanings, no new observation is needed exactly when the new decision is constant on each retained-record fibre. An injective record suffices for every Boolean target; a restricted future target family can need less. A common well-founded order can justify termination, but changing local losses alone cannot: the source's two-state example cycles while improving the predecessor loss at each step.

The [delivery-cost obstruction](https://github.com/theislampill/orthemology/blob/0784acddd4770f1190f758f264ba52b2b110bd27/theory/lineages/h-reflexive-v3/reflexive_revision_evidence_budget_v3.tex#L114) supplies a practical limit: complete agreement on task outputs cannot decide which representation is cheaper without an observation linking behaviour to delivery cost. An authorised probe of that missing coordinate changes the decision problem; repeating behaviour-only tests does not. This is a reason to ask the right discriminating question, not a result that either representation is actually cheaper.

Keep both titles and their source roles visible. The [lineage note][lineage] relates the PDF and TeX, but explicitly leaves exact source-to-PDF provenance unverified. ### Fresh innovation histories and original support

**Fresh histories, bounded observation, and original support.** [Complete written article][t12-fresh] · [Mathematical review][t12-concentration-review].

The article proves a Gaussian anti-concentration bound for an ordinary real-valued **finitely additive** joint probability. Independence and the Gaussian marginal's tail control suffice; tightness of the other variable and joint countable additivity are not required. Applying the bound to arbitrarily long fresh innovation blocks, all bound to one terminal law, makes every bounded terminal interval null. Thus even one warranted positive bounded-capture probability contradicts that package.

Its constructive counterbalance is equally important: a fixed ultralimit of uniform-anchor laws gives a coherent fresh history in which every sample position is finite, the full noise marginal is ordinary iid Gaussian, and every bounded position interval has probability zero. The article also supplies finite forward interventions and distinguishes a stable contractive process from its accumulating-variance obstruction. These are positive mathematical constructions, not merely warnings about evidence.

For an original-support argument, the article identifies an additional joint-realisability burden concerning the actual received support, stochastic mechanism and observation contract. It credits the inherited original-possession and recognition arguments without pretending that this stochastic route establishes an actual original participant, unique complete bearer or intrinsic necessity. The [source-specific frontier](CURRENT-FRONTIERS.md#fresh-histories-access-and-rational-policy) preserves that distinction rather than turning a local limitation into a verdict on every foundational route.

### Bounded acquisition and diachronic policy

**Bounded access and diachronic coherence.** [Complete assessment][t12-access] · [Separate final clarification][t12-clarification].

The positive operational result replaces a blanket preference for countable additivity with a specified acquisition task. If successful acquisition within resource budget `m` confines the target to a bounded range, then finite subadditivity bounds success by bounded capture plus the resource tail. A warranted positive success probability and a sufficiently small tail therefore force positive bounded mass. The law, information state, target and magnitude-sensitive task must stay fixed. A finite output alphabet or a correct actual answer does not by itself supply that contract.

A second construction uses a countable partition of positive-probability exact reports. Each report gives a specified event posterior zero although its prior is one half. Under the stated reporting and trading interface, two locally favourable transactions incur a fixed loss in every completed outcome; a quadratic forecast calculation supplies a related prospective-accuracy comparison. Every finite deadline retains a no-report branch and does not inherit the same completed-policy conclusion.

The final clarification is a **separate interpretative supplement**, not an edited edition of the article. It distinguishes mathematical incompatibility of positive bounded capture from domination of a particular completed policy: the history, reporter and losing behaviour are mathematically consistent. Abstention, precommitment, model revision and rejection of an unwarranted service assumption remain different responses. Outright resale can lock in the loss at report time without an extra executed settlement stage, while warranted ticket value and transfer conditions remain necessary.

### Total finite-input reporting

**Existing family `V5::CV-R`.** [Exact observation-refinement accounting](../../experiments/orthemology-v5/mathematics/REFINEMENT_CALCULUS.md) defines defect as the mass outside positive singleton atoms.
