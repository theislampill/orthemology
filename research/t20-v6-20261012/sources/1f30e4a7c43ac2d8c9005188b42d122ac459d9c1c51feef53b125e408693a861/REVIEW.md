1. **“Native recognition” is source-attributed, not a new innateness demonstration.** Printed572 explicitly says that knowledge of kinds of truth/falsehood, good/evil, and truthfulness/lying is known through **الفطرة والعقل الصريح**. This local guide comparison neither independently proves psychological innateness nor identifies a selected person's first, noninferential acquisition episode. The report's explicit no-new-episode and inherited-premise limits control §4.

2. **Scope is evidential, not a rigid subject taxonomy.** The field-specific phrase supports the ordinary expert comparison. It does not declare that every justified inference must remain within a modern departmental boundary. Connected competence and broader warranted recognition can support broader testimony; the report says this. In particular, the source's richer prophetic application cannot be refuted simply by narrowing its premises to ordinary grammatical skill.

3. **Truthfulness is not identical to competence.** The integrated571–572 argument separately adduces beneficent intention and truth-directed conduct. The report correctly preserves those additional grounds. The medicine/grammar comparison by itself is not a proof of every speaker's sincerity or immunity from error.

4. **Recognition need not master its object.** The source's recipient can appreciate a better explanation without being able to originate it, and can learn new content through subsequent reporting. Nothing here requires a consciously articulated domain certificate, exhaustive capacity validation, or knowledge of every later proposition in advance.
