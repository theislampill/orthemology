# Observation routes and rational policy

This clarification accompanies the Twelfth checkpoint's *Bounded access and diachronic coherence* v3, especially §10. It preserves that text's mathematics and bounded conclusion, and distinguishes the two routes before either is used in a stronger foundational argument.

## Different negative conclusions

A warranted positive bounded-capture premise is mathematically incompatible with the stipulated fresh-history law. The completed-report trading construction instead shows that a specified policy is uniformly dominated: abstention leaves the trader better off in every completed outcome. The history, reporter and losing behaviour are mathematically consistent.

The trading calculation therefore gives a reason to reject the joint probability, update, access and action-guidance package under the relevant rationality requirement. It does not uniquely determine which commitment must change. An agent can anticipate the whole policy and abstain, make an appropriate commitment, revise its operative model, or reject an unwarranted service assumption. Each response has its own conditions and epistemic costs.

A support architecture's ability to realise the reporter and trading opportunities consequently does not make that architecture impossible. An original-support argument through this dynamic route would additionally need warranted operative-prevision and rationality commitments for the actual epistemic explanandum. It must explain why the relevant rational responses cannot preserve that explanandum within the proposed support architecture. Productive scope and actual-support realisability remain further requirements. The bounded-capture route retains its separate mathematical incompatibility.

## Total execution and the completed loss

The integer protocol gives a genuine total supplied-input procedure. Every valid input and every private bit tape yields a report after finitely many rounds, including the all-tails tape. Its non-tight runtime law does not mean that some stated run never finishes. Actual availability of the input and maintained service is a separate application question.

For the two-trade loss, outright resale of the same ticket, or issuer buyback cancelling it, locks in the loss at report time. Actual later disclosure and settlement need not be an extra executed stage. The ticket must still have the stipulated warranted value if retained; the report-time transfer opportunity and information conditions must hold. The checkpoint's reveal-after-sale arrangement remains a valid, stronger implementation.

This refinement supplies no new actual original source or necessary bearer. The foundational article v4 already confines its mathematical contradiction to the positive bounded-capture route and requires no corresponding correction.
