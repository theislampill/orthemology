## Substantive appraisal and remaining premise work

The received original offers substantially more than generic causal dependence. Its affirmative case grounds divine predicates in pure positive perfection, gives the creator superior entitlement to the nondefective perfections he bestows, and argues that complete sovereignty and strict unsurpassable highestness exclude a coordinate equal. The force of those arguments cannot be met merely by proposing harmonious cooperation: their central premise is that dependence-free, unshared absolute dominion is itself required perfection. A fair appraisal must assess that premise and its applicability to every proposed original source bearer.

The book permits essential distinctions within one necessary qualified bearer without an external assembler. It distinguishes bearer, attribute, voluntary act, created product, genuine creaturely action, and epistemic versus ontic dependence. This blocks crude composition and creature-erasure readings, but does not automatically classify every modern plurality of original-source candidates as one bearer's attributes.

Responsive love, anger, joy, hearing and prayer response are defended as real without an outsider supplying a missing constitutive perfection. Prior complete perfection grounds action; action need not perfect a previously deficient divine agent. The necessary efficacy of names and wisdom is strong, while the text also distinguishes successive goods, requisite conditions and decisive willing from mere possible enjoyment. Necessarily efficacious nature does not, without further premises, entail every token product eternally and simultaneously, or establish a unique token cosmos.

The wide reading also preserves the author's linguistic theory, criticism of misleading reformulation, indirect speech, legal disagreement and consensus cautions, historical allegations, natural history, testimony and miracle arguments, psychology, devotion, and theodicy. Quoted opponents, dialectical concessions and narrated reports remain distinguished from adopted conclusions. Historical scientific claims and sectarian accusations are not independently certified by being read.

Neither a verified formal conditional nor accepting Necessary Being alone certifies all of the positive perfection, complete supremacy, universal source-sufficiency and revealed-identity premises. These remain explicit objects of appraisal rather than predetermined verdicts. The two reflective appraisals give the strongest affirmative reconstruction and the exact remaining warrant questions.
