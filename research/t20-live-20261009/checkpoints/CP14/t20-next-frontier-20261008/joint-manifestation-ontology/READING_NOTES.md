## Sequential reading record

All 21 manuscript pages were read in order, in four displays: 1–5, 6–10, 11–15, and 16–21. Page-specific points for reflective checking, expressed as analytical questions rather than a substitute text:

- Pages 1–5: Does actuality require a separate categorical anchor? Keep a power, its activated state, and any further result separate. Do not mistake fundamental powers for original personal bearers.
- Pages 6–10: Is existential partnership an unrestricted metaphysical theorem? Check the qualifications to the opening formulation. Distinguish directedness and ontological dependence from efficient donation. Do not infer that nonreification makes a dependency harmless.
- Pages 11–15: Which modality is in use? Does conditional success under physically specified circumstances preserve a complete original wise determination? Distinguish a claim against quantitative transmission as an analysis from denial of all real physical transfer.
- Pages 16–21: What precisely is numerically one, and what remains multiple? Does a difference in causal role survive within a common event? Is an external created product the same category as the participants’ activity?

## Initial interpretive hypotheses to test by return

1. The essay can earn general ontological credit beyond an uninterpreted response rule without earning original-agent eligibility.
2. Its causal event unity might support a common episode while leaving an intended created-effect identity unproved.
3. Actuality of each participant’s own activity is a serious answer to an idle-label objection. It does not yet settle exhaustive original adequacy.
4. Existential and activation dependence must not be promoted directly to a vicious efficient circle, or demoted to merely linguistic dependence.
5. The strongest contribution may be an exact transport limit, not a new rival that reverses the comparative judgment.
