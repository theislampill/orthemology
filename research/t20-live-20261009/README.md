## 9. Original provision, nonduplication and the scope of unity

Whole-existence explanation asks for an adequate original basis of received productive reality. It does not follow from local causal relations alone, and a strong brute-original rival is not the same as a causally inert item: it may possess actual productive powers while its own existential adequacy remains disputed. A foundational principle need not be derivable from weaker premises to have warrant. Equally, an attribution to a historical author does not answer every philosophical objection. A counterexample must preserve independently warranted surrounding commitments and intended meanings, without being required to assume the very premise under dispute. [S0249] [S0319] [S0433]

The affirmative intrinsic-necessity appraisal keeps the bearer fixed and uses an explicit modal bridge from existential self-adequacy. It does not infer existence from a definition or identify modal persistence with essential nonreceipt. Whole-existence nonreceipt, nonreceipt in a particular act and efficient-causal independence are different predicates. Realisation and SupportTransport still need a typed same-respect interpretation. An anti-eliminativist argument does not, by itself, defeat selective brute original obtaining. [S0389] [S0622] [S0618]

Original completion of a field and one-bearer coverage of that field are different achievements. The historical Nineteenth appraisal accepts, defeasibly, a common original source for the actually productively connected field under its whole-accounting premises. It explicitly adds causal nonduplication, a stronger norm of unborrowed adequate original provision in actual exercise, and faithful mediate attribution. Connectedness propagates a warranted local identity or coverage condition; it does not supply that condition. The claim is field-relative, not numerical uniqueness of every necessary reality. [S0182] [S0241]

Causal nonduplication concerns two alleged entire original productions of the same concrete contribution in the same respect. It allows differentiated cooperation, multiple sufficient conditions, descriptions and levels of real secondary agency. The positive rationale is that genuine additional production has determinate productive content—a resource, requisite, reinforcement, receptivity or preservation—while unused ability is not a second actual contribution. Determinate content does not analytically entail nonduplication. The source accepts the additional principle on a defeasible individuation rationale and explanatory economy, while preserving a critic who finds that rationale insufficient. The critic need not exhibit a possible plural universe before the objection has force. [S0182]

Primitive double sufficiency is therefore kept as a serious distinct proposal: two actual unborrowed exercises allegedly supply one whole result without differentiated work, missing portions or idle backup. A contradiction follows if one already grants that A alone exclusively produces the whole and B also produces it. That does not show that nonexclusive sufficiency means exclusive production. The maker-and-preserver comparison directly concerns needing foreign completion; primitive duplication claims surplus unneeded concurrence. The intervening scope objection is preserved even though the later cumulative appraisal is affirmative. Schaffer's effect-relative overdetermination is relevant to this distinction, but does not itself exhibit two perfected unreceived original bearers. [S0182] [S0001] [S0207]

The stronger source-mode norm is separate. It regards adequate unborrowed provision as the fitting mode of a qualified perfect original's actual productive role. An intrinsic transformative act may be complete while the resulting composite needs material from another source. Causal accounting establishes that incompleteness of original coverage; the judgment that the chosen role is deficient needs the additional evaluative norm. A critic can ask why a less comprehensive freely chosen role cannot be fitting. Proper-time action and relational goods sharpen that question. The affirmative appraisal answers with its own positive account of original practical excellence, not by treating intact capacity, success or the absence of a peer-essential good as decisive. [S0182]

Essential standing perfections, an essential original mode, an enduring genus of activity and this token act must remain distinct. A token may be fittingly absent before its time and fittingly present at its time. The stronger general-activity premise can exclude global inactivity without making every token beginningless. A capacity-only empty model does not countermodel the fuller doctrine. The compressed unity passage and the wider action doctrine create an acknowledged interpretive tension: the reconstruction of an original mode across passages is an inferential clarification, not a derivation explicitly finished at the one local passage. Faithful mediate attribution explains how an original resource remains in the account of a later result produced by a real derived agent. It does not identify the original source as the proximal or moral owner of every derived act. If a later modifier actually needs another source's material, unused ability to create different material does not remove that actual requisite. Occurrences must remain typed: a prior act, its result and a later responsive act are not one bearer-level event. ## 12. Evidence frontiers, inquiry and compilation

The inquiry model begins with a fixed finite catalogue of valid derivations, persistent evidence and immutable source, rule and scope commitments. Each candidate carries a required evidence support and a nonnegative cost. Given evidence already held, the residual support and cost-pair frontier discards only candidates dominated for all future evidence states. Retaining the support/cost pair is essential: a representation keeping only present cost or ignoring price loses future distinctions. The canonical frontier theorem is the kernel-checked part reported in the later record, with thirteen principal and four control axiom sets and rejection of cost-blind, current-cost-only and infinite-price mutations. [S0391] [S0167] [S0208]

A Bellman recurrence over finite permitted tests then gives the minimum worst-case **execution** cost for completing a valid proof/evidence witness. The test outcomes are order-independent under the declared model. The state retains commitments and evidence, and the terminal case reconstructs a concrete valid witness rather than returning a bare Boolean label. This result does not include the cost of compiling the catalogue, planning the strategy, authenticating evidence or establishing the external truth of source rules. Unsupported does not mean false. The finite examples distinguish verification from search, immutable provenance from equal content, and deterministic worst-case cost from randomized expected cost. [S0421] [S0165] [S0444]

Compilation adds a restricted constructive result. With finite grounded substitutable rules and nonnegative costs, tree witnesses stabilize within the stated finite number of judgment rounds.
