# Claim and premise ledger

This ledger records the investigation's own argument status. H-keys are location pointers, not additional historical synopses. Accepted predecessor claims retain their original scope and ownership. “Positive warrant” means a reason in favour of the specified content; it does not mean certainty, consensus or a procedure eliminating every rival.

## AP10-1: A supplying route has an availability-specific anchor

**Claim.** A genuine finite productive realization of a nonactual supplier from actual powers gives a stronger rationale for a selected actual availability source than whole-package relevance alone. The trace must concern the supplier's being available, not just an effect or triggering occasion.

**Assessment.** Positive rational support under the genuinely productive, resource-complete interpretation. This is substantive source-level support for the accepted RP2/RP5 strategy. It is not a logical theorem from the word “power.”

**Conditions and controls.** Joint contribution need not be individual sufficiency. Finite iteration depth does not bound plurality width or an entry resource's entire ancestry. The actual fact branch and actual S branch remain distinct. Minimal resource and supplying-power-bearer conclusions retain their separate coverage premises.

**Bindings.** Paper§2; H-CAUS/H-GEN/H-FACT. Prior owner: accepted Tenth joint-coverage candidate-v2, ACTUAL_POWER_ROUTE§§2–4 and its independent acceptance. No new extraction proof is claimed.

## AP10-2: Prehistory can vary while an earlier entry is retained

**Claim.** Alternative exercises can change a later recipient's prehistory while retaining an earlier relevant entry and an iterated route. Missing downstream agents alone therefore do not defeat the availability of a return realization. This supports a source-relative scope without fixing every historical fact.

**Assessment.** Warranted at the retained-source scope. Exhaustiveness over absolute alternatives remains an additional metaphysical claim. A source may have beginningless ancestry; no first temporal event is inferred.

**Conditions and controls.** Do not confuse possession's priority to one exercise with independence from all earlier exercises determining possession. Some losses can be repaired by moving to an earlier actual entry. That repair cannot be assumed after removal of the entire relevant base.

**Bindings.** Paper§3; H-GEN/H-RETURN. Prior owner: Ninth F1's prospective-versus-absolute distinction. New credit: the explicit source-relative repair and its limited application to the current supplier trace.

## AP10-3: Two precise routes to classified support retention

**Claim.** Either retain an appropriately classified actual entry occurrence, or retain a power-profile with independently warranted Q-classifying force. Both can secure the required F-support contradiction without fixing every accidental feature.

**Assessment.** The occurrence-based generative interpretation has positive rational force. Actual bearer identity alone is insufficient. Kind priority to powers does not establish the converse power-profile-to-kind bridge.

**Conditions and controls.** Only one eligible retained realization is required per genuinely possible S. A differently classified alternative prehistory does not defeat the argument if another eligible route retains the required entry. The F-property need not itself act as a cause; the F-bearing occurrence must be on the relevant supply trace. Fixed Q and the stated contingency qualification cannot change during the proof.

**Bindings.** Paper§4; H-CAUS/H-HISTORY. Prior owner: accepted kind and joint-coverage papers. New credit: the source-level assessment of the two strongest transport readings and the one-realization precision.

## AP10-4: Generative backing does not itself exhibit the target

**Claim.** A general realization rule does not establish POS-S. The outsider, recipient, productive relation and complete support qualification must be possible together. No target conjunct can be silently supplied by another.

**Assessment.** This is a limitation on the current inference, not an impossibility claim about S. General theoretical economy and concrete modal grounding can give a theory real support while leaving its specific historical scope unresolved.

**Conditions and controls.** Actual p establishes no richer S merely by being its conjunct. The actual-S direct branch remains available if its stronger content obtains. The smaller target does not itself provide evidence for that target.

**Bindings.** Paper§§2,5; H-GEN. Prior owners: Ninth F1 productive-coverage scrutiny and accepted Tenth smaller-target/conditional route.

## AP10-5: Three support-removal scopes are different

**Claim.** Removing a selected support, eliminating every independently adequate support, and jointly removing the whole relevant concrete support class are different operations. The strongest exhaustive, jointly realizable dependence premise can validly support failure of the existential target.

**Assessment.** Credit the strong implication. Local contributor contingency does not establish its global application. This diagnoses a particular warrant obligation without refuting the entire modal account.

**Conditions and controls.** Replacement witnesses and dormant backups matter. No joint absence or empty-world possibility is inferred from an absence specification. The exact-H test is stricter than changing a historical detail; surviving in-history resources can change the result.

**Bindings.** Paper§6; H-EQ/H-ABS. New credit: target-specific selected/exhaustive/whole-class scope discrimination and the strongest valid support reading. Generic multiple-witness and non-distribution principles are not claimed as new discoveries.

## AP10-6: Necessary grounding needs its own ontic type

**Claim.** A necessary concrete resource, a necessary supplying-power bearer and an actual through-essence being are different achievements. A genuine concrete ground of the third type would deserve necessary-particular credit. An essential-grounding argument cannot borrow an efficient-causal type restriction without an appropriate bridge.

**Assessment.** Conditional affirmative credit for the richer route; the present investigation earns no actual instance. The type question remains substantive even after concrete nonemptiness is accepted.

**Conditions and controls.** A named external constituent of a real definition differs from a generic existential consequence. Necessitation alone does not establish reverse dependence. A conditionally necessary productive activity is an inference control, not a certified actual or possible source and not a refutation of the exact fixed-H argument. Pure representation is insufficient for existential explanation, but this bounded judgement does not exclude all nonrepresentational abstract essential grounding.

**Bindings.** Paper§7; H-ESS/H-ABS/H-CONC. Prior owners: Ninth F1's intrinsic-grounding distinction and accepted Tenth resource/power typing. New credit: explicit relation/type routing and the generic-versus-fixed-history consequence test.

It does not establish POS-S, universal classified-occurrence transport, exhaustive joint removability, or the required concrete type of essential ground. No actual necessary particular, actual exercise, unity, P1/P2, N2/N3 or later attribute is established. The earlier joint-history adequacy question remains open. These limits concern the content of the outstanding reasons, not a demand for neutral algorithms or conclusive refutation of every alternative.
