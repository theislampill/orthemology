# Finite omission check: duplicate result and abridgement limit

11 October 2026 UTC. Additive clarification; accepted report unchanged.

## Result

**Substantially duplicate.** Accepted request-context-provenance §1 already identifies the shorter Shuʿba/Saʿid occurrence accounts alongside the explicit request accounts and limits what their silence establishes. Reinspection confirms variation in reporting extent, but does **not** establish a documented act of abridging a particular fuller original. No additional defeat of Rida's expected-publicity objection is earned.

## Finite textual comparison

- [Muslim 2802c](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2802c): Shuʿba → Qatada → Anas; the main body reports splitting into two parts, and the Abu Dawud variant locates it in the Prophet's lifetime. Neither body mentions the request. Abu Dawud here is a transmitter in Muslim's printed chain, not a citation to the Sunan collection.
- [Bukhari 4868](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:4868): Musaddad → Yahya → Shuʿba → Qatada → Anas; short occurrence body. Its Arabic chapter heading does not add the request; the website's expanded English heading is not another Arabic testimony. This additional displayed canonical locus confirms the same Shuʿba branch, not a new event witness.
- [Tabari 54:1](https://quran.ksu.edu.sa/tafseer/tabary/sura54-aya1.html): the already-inspected six-report Anas block contains request bodies through Saʿid, shorter bodies through Shuʿba, and a Saʿid route through Abu Dawud with explicit Qatada hearing followed by an equivalent-report reference. The request-positive Saʿid branches run through Yazid and Bishr b. al-Mufaddal. The equivalent-report clause points to the preceding short occurrence report; it does not identify which longer original was reduced.
- Bukhari 3637/3868/4867 and Muslim 2802a,b remain the already-verified request-positive presentations. They are not independently originating request-event sources merely because they explicitly preserve the detail.

## What follows, and what does not

The coexistence of these presentations is actual textual evidence, not a made-up example of selectivity. The collectors' juxtaposition also shows that they presented occurrence-only versions without thereby excluding the request they elsewhere accepted. This bears against treating every unmentioned detail as denied or treating every body as an exhaustive description of the episode.

However, common Qatada–Anas attribution does not by itself prove a common full utterance was shortened. These could reflect different narration occasions, reporting extent, or other transmission histories not established here. No manuscript deletion, narrator's declaration of abbreviation, or independent report of the shortening operation was found in the finite bodies. Earlier wording “abridged” or “abbreviated” should therefore be understood descriptively as shorter presentation, not as a newly authenticated editing event.

Nor does an intra-Qatada variation establish why other Companions' surviving reports omit the request, how many witnesses heard it, or what wider transmission should have survived. Without that bridge, these examples cannot assign an evidential weight or transmission probability to Rida's stronger absence argument. They do not answer audience clarity or independently authenticate the historical request.
