# PMR-007 Deep T V1 cold audit

Disposition: `REPAIR_REQUIRED`.

## Frozen input

The audit used only the frozen V1 theorem packet, model owner, primary checker,
primary results, and their hash receipt. It did not treat the author's
explanatory gloss as an additional premise.

## Blocking findings

1. **T-F01 — completeness overreach.** V1 calls the selected seven-coordinate
   profile the “complete current visible neutral profile.” It is only the
   **registered profile frozen for this comparison**. Other current evidence,
   future observations, or a newly justified discriminator may refine it.

2. **T-F02 — source compatibility is not explanatory dominance.** Under H12 and
   H16, source-incompatible rivals can be excluded from a declared Track-N
   model class. That is conditional admissibility/classification, not a proof
   that Architecture A dominates those rivals on neutral explanatory grounds.

3. **T-F03 — nonexhaustive rival class.** A, B, and C are mandatory comparison
   architectures, not an exhaustive metaphysical partition. “Unique” may mean
   only unique among the frozen three.

4. **T-F04 — desired-target loading.** Architecture A's personal, wisdom,
   speech, and revelation coordinates are intended conclusions or architecture
   commitments. They are not current neutral derivations merely because they
   appear in its record.

5. **T-F05 — Pareto-coordinate commensurability.** Set inclusion over named
   premises and brute stops is a declared diagnostic. It does not establish an
   objective cross-kind cost order, probability, simplicity ranking, or
   truth-conducive explanatory metric.

6. **T-F06 — scope of nonselection.** The fibre result blocks certification from
   the frozen registered profile. It does not prove permanent empirical,
   metaphysical, or source-level underdetermination. A new independently
   warranted discriminator can split the fibre.

7. **T-F07 — theorem-family credit.** The nonselection mechanism is the existing
   profile/fibre factorization family, and the Track-N result is the existing
   conservative-reduct/nonmigration mechanism. General novelty credit is zero.

8. **T-F08 — source authority ceiling.** H12 source authentication and H16
   referent identification remain `REQUIRED_NOT_ESTABLISHED`; the Asfahani
   material is translated-primary access, not Arabic-primary verification.

9. **T-F09 — repository and adoption overreach.** Current-main milestone and
   PMR proposal artifacts remain proposal-only, externally unreviewed, and
   owner-adoption pending. A research admission cannot convert them into
   current adopted theory.

10. **T-F10 — review independence.** The checker and rereview are same-session
    procedural controls. They cannot satisfy the external-review or independent
    model-lineage burden.

V1 must be preserved as a superseded candidate. Admission is eligible only
for an authority-relative nonselection result and a conditional source-class
admissibility result with all of these restrictions explicit.
