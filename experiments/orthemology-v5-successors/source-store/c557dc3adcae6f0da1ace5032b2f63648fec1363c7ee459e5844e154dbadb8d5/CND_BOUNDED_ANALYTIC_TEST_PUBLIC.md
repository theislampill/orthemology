# CND and essential nonreceipt: independent bounded test

Date: 7 October 2026. Status: analytical test within T20, not repository adoption or a fresh historical discovery.

## Result

T19's accepted causal nonduplication (CND) provides a substantive independent reason against concurrent duplicate **entire original productions** in the same respect. It does not, at that exact scope, supply a further independent warrant for essential whole-existence nonreceipt of T17's fixed necessary bearer. The strongest affirmative bridge has useful explanatory content, but its modal completion still needs T17's already accepted constitutive judgement; applying CND directly to intrinsic sufficiency additionally changes its explanatory type.

This is not a return to withholding the Necessary Being or essential-independence conclusions. T17 accepts both with sufficient defeasible warrant; T19 retains them. The question tested here is what additional warrant CND contributes, not whether the earlier result survives.

## Starting point and exact target

Fix T17's actual particular bearer a. Original completion and qualified-bearer commitments warrant its actual whole-existence originality; contingency-as-need warrants necessary existence of that same witness. The additional constitutive judgement warrants essential nonreceipt. These are distinct steps, all accepted in T17, paragraphs P00048–P00061, especially P00053–P00060. T19 P00012 and P00150 expressly retain that outcome.

The target is: this same a cannot receive its whole existential actuality in an alternative in which it exists. It is stronger than actual nonreceipt, necessary existence, or the exclusion of two concurrent original producers of one effect. T19 P00111 itself separates actual productive accounting from origin-essentiality across alternatives.

## The strongest affirmative bridge

1. Actual whole-existence originality is positive intrinsic existential adequacy, not an absent observed supplier or an inventory of powers conditional on instantiation. This is T17 P00027–P00030 and P00058.
2. CND contributes a separate positive consideration: a further genuine original production must have determinate productive content. An extra source label, unused alternative or duplication of the very same entire production supplies none. The rationale concerns productive individuation, not simply the number of visible effects: T19 P00051–P00056.
3. Suppose an alleged supplier receives explanatory credit for a's whole existence while a's intrinsic adequacy is retained intact. Ask what exact actuality or requisite the supplier provides. If nothing, there is no relevant supply. If only a later state, relation, occasion or exercise, whole-existence reception has not been shown. If the supplier supplies the entire existential actuality, the account must explain its relation to the supposedly retained intrinsic adequacy.
4. The most favourable extension says complete intrinsic existential adequacy and complete external existential supply cannot be two independent, concurrently operative answers for the same whole actuality. Genuine supply would therefore replace the former mode rather than merely supplement it.
5. If the existential mode constitutes this particular reality, such replacement cannot preserve the same bearer. Hence a is essentially nonreceived.

The bridge is a serious cumulative proposal: it forces a putative redundant supplier to specify its work and exposes equivocation between preserving a necessary existent and preserving its original existential mode. But its last two transitions do not come from CND as accepted in T19.

## Where the bridge is substantive

**The relation-type transition.** CND quantifies over entire original productions. Intrinsic existential adequacy is not one such production of a by a. An inseparable qualified nature is not an external producer of its bearer or a collection of attributes the bearer first manufactures. Counting an intrinsic explanatory basis as the first producer and an external supplier as the second would introduce efficient self-production into a framework that expressly rejects it. T17 P00027 and P00051 provide this control; T19 P00051 expressly excludes a generic prohibition of multiple sufficient reasons, descriptions or levels of agency.

Step 4 can instead be offered as a broader anti-surplus principle, covering intrinsic existential adequacy and external original production without pretending they are the same relation. The productive-individuation rationale gives that extension a motivation, but T19's acceptance of CND does not itself warrant the extension. T19 P00055 makes the pertinent distinction unusually explicit: received-only accounts concern an original-provision shortfall; primitive duplication concerns alleged surplus supply, which adequacy discriminates against less directly. The proposed mixed-relation extension must therefore receive its own positive appraisal and objections. Calling it CND does not discharge that work.

If “complete intrinsic adequacy” already means that no external contribution genuinely supplies this actuality, excluding such a contribution is correct but merely unfolds that granted mode. The essential question then remains whether that mode must persist across alternatives.

**The modal transition.** Even granting the broader anti-surplus principle, its conclusion is coexistence exclusion within a given alternative: retained autonomous adequacy and genuine foreign supply cannot both obtain there. It does not show that the same a cannot instead be original in one alternative and received in another. Step 5 supplies that exclusion. Its reason is precisely T17 P00058–P00060: the source of whole existential adequacy belongs to this reality's constitution; there is no already actual existentially neutral bearer to which either mode is subsequently added. That is a warranted inherited reason, not an independent CND consequence.

An opposite argument through a's effects has the same boundary. If a produces e after receiving its actuality from b, faithful mediate attribution includes b in e's original account. But a then acts derivatively in that alternative; a and b are not two independent original producers of e. Calling both original has already assumed the invariance under investigation. T19 P00081–P00085 preserves precisely this original/proximate distinction.

## A bounded separation test

Consider only necessary existence, actual originality, and CND, temporarily leaving aside T17's accepted constitutive premise. In one schematic alternative a is original for e. In another the same necessarily existing a receives its actuality from b and derivatively produces e; b alone supplies the original productive basis. No alternative contains two entire original productions of e. CND therefore has no violation to identify. If the wider original-completion framework requires b to be necessary, b can exist in both alternatives and have a disconnected original role in the first.

This is a diagnostic of premise scope, not a demonstration that the described alternatives are metaphysically possible, equally credible, or compatible with all accepted T17 premises. In particular, T17's constitutive judgement excludes the proposed switch. That is the point: the premise doing that work can be identified without retracting its warrant. T17 P00022 and P00059 supply the appropriate methodological cautions. T19's essential source-mode argument cannot be imported to remove this limitation without importing its own essential-independence starting qualification (P00060 and P00097).

## Exact increment and limits

The genuinely independent rational narrowing is T19's exclusion of **concurrent duplicate original productive contributions**, supported by its productive-individuation appraisal. Applying that result to a proposed original production of a's existence is legitimate only where the two genuine productions are independently specified. It is not a new argument against an alternate-world single supplier of a.

The distinctions between a necessary recipient and an intrinsically necessary bearer; whole-existence receipt and acquired states; productive causation and qualification; and actuality and essentiality are already owned by T17. Merely redescribing that taxonomy in CND vocabulary supplies no new warrant. The strongest additional work is the explicit dependency test above: it shows exactly why an apparently new bridge either extends CND to a new mixed explanatory domain or relies on the previously warranted constitutive transport.

CND remains affirmative on its accepted defeasible appraisal. The continuing adverse CND assessment remains substantive (T19 P00101–P00104). Nothing here downgrades it to a definition, turns dissent into a demonstrated rival possibility, or infers necessary creation, a fixed whole history, unrestricted numerical uniqueness, or generic exclusion of noncausal existential grounds.

## Supplement: precise relational separation and the production-only bridge

Added 7 October 2026 after the initial result. This supplement tests a stronger positive candidate separately; it preserves rather than replaces the original memo. It uses the recovered reports already read, with no new source survey or implemented proof claim.

### Source → exact candidate claim

T19 P00051 supplies CND for entire original production of the same concrete contribution in the same causal respect. T19 P00082 supplies faithful mediate inclusion in an effect's original productive account; P00081 and P00083 distinguish that inclusion from complete original provision. T17 P00050–P00051 supplies the transport from whole-bearer reception to received instantiation of its intrinsic productive resource. T19 P00067–P00068 distinguishes an essential original mode, generic activity and particular acts; P00077 prohibits reversing whole-existence transport from just any received state.

The candidate claim is: **if this same necessarily existing g has at least one entire original productive contribution in every accessible alternative, and whole reception of g would transmit the very same entire original production to a distinct original supplier, CND excludes whole reception of g in every accessible alternative.** Its productive witness may vary between alternatives. It invokes no production of g by g and no efficient-production relation between g and an intrinsic attribute.

### Premises, all with their exact quantifiers

Let W be a set of alternatives, w0 the actual alternative, A(w0,w) accessibility, and g a rigidly designated individual. Use:

- E(w,x): x exists in w.
- R(w,x): x's **whole existential actuality**, not merely some token state, is received in w.
- O(w,x,e,k): x supplies the entire original production of concrete contribution e in causal respect k in w.
- C(w,x,e,k): x contributes to e's original productive account in that respect. C is weaker than O.

The background has necessary existence, `∀w [A(w0,w) → E(w,g)]`, and actual whole nonreceipt, `¬R(w0,g)`. Do not encode intrinsic existential adequacy as O.

**Worldwise CND:**

`∀w,x,y,e,k [(A(w0,w) ∧ O(w,x,e,k) ∧ O(w,y,e,k)) → x = y]`.

**Productive-role invariance (PRI):**

`∀w [A(w0,w) → ∃e,k O(w,g,e,k)]`.

This is `∀w∃e,k`, not an invariant effect `∃e,k∀w`. Nor is it merely standing ability to produce, actual-world activity, necessary existence, or necessary generic activity considered apart from original provenance.

**Strong supplier/attribution bridge (SUP):**

`∀w,e,k [(A(w0,w) ∧ R(w,g) ∧ O(w,g,e,k)) → ∃h (h ≠ g ∧ O(w,h,e,k))]`.

SUP includes more than the existence of some original supplier somewhere: its h is distinct from g, and its target, causal respect and **entire original production** exactly match the witness of PRI. A bridge concluding only C(w,h,e,k) is insufficient for CND.

### Formal consequence

Take any accessible w and suppose R(w,g). PRI yields some e and k with O(w,g,e,k). SUP yields distinct h with O(w,h,e,k). Worldwise CND gives g = h, contradicting h ≠ g. Therefore `∀w [A(w0,w) → ¬R(w,g)]`.

This is a valid, small relational consequence. Actual nonreceipt is not used in that proof; nor is necessary existence separately needed if O entails existence and PRI already holds everywhere. That exposes where the modal work resides: PRI universally requires the original productive role, rather than transporting an actual role by CND. If PRI were conditional on g's existence, necessary existence would discharge that condition. The theorem does not require g to produce the same token, perform every possible act, or fix a unique whole history.

### Discriminating assignments

**1. CND plus necessity and actual originality, without PRI.** Take W = {w0,w1}, with every pair accessible; rigid individuals g and h existing in both; and contributions e and f in one fixed productive respect. At w0, g entirely originally produces e, while h entirely originally produces disconnected f. At w1, h entirely originally produces e; g proximately performs its derived production. Set R(w0,g) false and R(w1,g) true, with h the distinct supplier of g in w1. There are no other O entries relevant to e or f. Each same-world, same-contribution, same-respect original production has at most one producer, so worldwise CND holds. Necessary existence and actual whole nonreceipt also hold, while essential nonreceipt fails. The g-role loses originality at w1, so PRI fails. No unchanged necessary token is presupposed.

**2. PRI plus weak mediate attribution, without SUP.** In a purely relational interpretation, instead put O(w0,g,e,k) and O(w1,g,e,k); put C(w1,h,e,k), but not O(w1,h,e,k); retain R(w1,g). CND and PRI hold, and the supplier is included in the original account, but essential nonreceipt fails. This assignment separates the weak C conclusion from the strong O conclusion. It is not asserted to satisfy the reports' substantive meaning of original production. That meaning is precisely the next test.

Both assignments only separate formulas. They certify no metaphysically possible reality, no equal credibility, and no defect in T17's adopted constitutive judgement. The first violates constitutive mode invariance; the second conflicts with the strong existential-originality reading when joined to T17's resource-instantiation transport. Their limited task is to identify the additional premises doing work.

### Does PRI already carry essential independence?

There are two readings, and the difference matters.

**Strong provenance reading.** If O means production from an originally instantiated, wholly unreceived productive resource of g, whole receipt of g makes that instantiation received under T17 P00050–P00051. It cannot retain the stipulated original provenance. Apply that transport in each accessible alternative: PRI already rules out R everywhere, without CND or recovery of a second producer. Essential nonreceipt need not be literally written into the definition of O, since the separate bearer-to-resource premise supplies the implication. Nonetheless, with that accepted premise, PRI is already a modal commitment strong enough to yield the desired conclusion. It is not an independent result of CND.

This does not make every proof from an independently warranted PRI circular. An independent reason that this very g must exercise such an original role in every alternative could furnish another route to essential nonreceipt. The present reports do not newly supply that reason through CND. T19's governing source-mode norm begins with an **essentially independent** qualified original (P00060); using it to establish independence would reuse the starting qualification. Its genus/token discussion (P00067–P00068) does not derive unreceived provenance in all alternatives merely from necessary generic activity. T17 P00120 likewise leaves necessary generic activity distinct from particular acts. The warrant and modality of PRI therefore require their own identification.

**Local-agency reading.** If O only means that g originates or sufficiently performs its own proximate act, g may do so while its actual existence and powers are received. T17 P00049–P00050 and T19 P00108 expressly allow that structure. Faithful mediate attribution then gives the supplier a role in the original productive account, while g owns or performs the proximate act. These are not two entire original productions in the same respect. Applying T19 CND to exclude them would erase the genuine secondary agency that P00051 and P00081–P00085 protect. Keeping the weaker reading of g's role while silently using the stronger O predicate for the supplier is a change of relation, not a proof.

### Additional scope needed by SUP

Recovering an actual original basis does not by itself recover one individual h that entirely originally supplies the selected e. T17 P00045–P00054 separates an actual original witness from individually complete common coverage. T19 P00082 explicitly warrants mediate participation; P00083 adds further premises for complete original provision. Restricting e to g's own contribution may help match scope, but does not automatically establish that one supplier covers its complete productive basis in the exact respect used by CND. That alignment must be stated and warranted.

T19's common-source conclusion concerns the actual warranted connected field (P00146–P00150). Applying that whole result in every alternative needs the corresponding field, qualification and coverage premises there. It is not supplied by actual connectedness alone, and importing its essentially independent source qualification cannot provide an independent proof of that same qualification.

Finally, if R includes all ontological reception, SUP must connect each such mode to an original **productive** supplier. T17 P00044 withholds a generic noncausal-ground-to-efficient-Creator transition. Without that additional typing premise, the production-only theorem excludes whole **productive** receipt, not every possible kind of existential derivation. Token-state reception supplies still less: acquired knowledge, an occasion, a received material condition or a particular state does not entail R(w,g), and the reverse inference is expressly rejected by T19 P00077 and controlled at P00090–P00091.

### Supplement verdict

The proposed production-only inference is valid under worldwise CND, PRI and the exact strong SUP. It is a useful positive conditional because it cleanly identifies how a genuinely invariant productive role could support essential nonreceipt without self-production or a fixed act token. At the reports' accepted strong sense of originality, however, PRI together with their bearer-resource transport already yields essential nonreceipt; at the weaker proximate sense, SUP and CND no longer have the same target. Thus the formal bridge is earned as a conditional, while no new independently warranted modal premise has been acquired. T17's affirmative essential-independence conclusion and T19's separately warranted CND remain unchanged.
