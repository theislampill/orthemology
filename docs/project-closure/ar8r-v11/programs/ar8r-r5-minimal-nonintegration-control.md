# AR8R-R5-MINIMAL-NONINTEGRATION control

The collision-free identifier `AR8R-R5-MINIMAL-NONINTEGRATION` distinguishes
this research control from repository revision labels.

## Control definition

The control allows the mathematical/operational, transcendental, and source
tracks to retain their separately scoped results and to interact only through
explicit typed interfaces. It withholds positive relation identity, one bearer,
one ground, and an integrated ontology unless an additional bridge produces a
stronger consequence than the separate realization.

## Current status

```text
AR8R-R5-MINIMAL-NONINTEGRATION: UNDEFEATED
overall: NO_INTEGRATED_CHAMPION
```

This is not a universal separation theorem. It is a live equal-strength control
over the declared checked architecture. A future result defeats it only by
supplying target-faithful identity, world truth, warrant, and a consequence not
realized by the separated tracks.

At the declared joint, defeat additionally requires all of the following: a
target relation central to the original transcendental argument; a sufficiently
complete target-faithful representation; a theorem excluding a live target
alternative; bridge premises that do not already entail the conclusion;
independent truth and warrant for those premises; a changed exact world-directed
conclusion; and survival against plural-resource, external-resource, and
representation-incompleteness rivals. Where T299/T300 supply an interface, the
target and causal semantics must themselves be independently fixed by the
operative domain rather than optional parameterization.

## Non-defeats

Campaign size, shared vocabulary, pair counts, one abstraction, source order,
role coherence, copied provenance, interoperable software, or common output do
not defeat the control and do not establish one metaphysical bearer.

No integrated-champion claim is admissible without a named, audited defeat
artifact reconciled against every retained control condition.
