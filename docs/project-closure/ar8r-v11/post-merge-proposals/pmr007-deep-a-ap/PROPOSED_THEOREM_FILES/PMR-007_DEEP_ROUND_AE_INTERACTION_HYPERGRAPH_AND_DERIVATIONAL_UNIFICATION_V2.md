## 2. IHDU-1A — exact family block-separability criterion

For a partition

\[
\Pi=\{B_1,\ldots,B_k\}
\]

of `V`, the following are equivalent:

```text
1. every f_j can be written as
   f_j(x)=c_j+sum_l f_{j,l}(x_{B_l});

2. every nonzero Möbius support of every f_j is contained in one block B_l.
```

### Proof

If the block-local decompositions exist, uniqueness of each multilinear
expansion forbids any monomial spanning two blocks.  Conversely, when every
support is block-contained, group each expansion's nonconstant terms by the
unique block containing them and absorb the constant once. ∎

## 7. Causal, empirical, and implementation firewall

not identify causal direction.  Observational OSM/PRH alignment, model stitching,

formal correspondence design.

coordinate semantics, intervention interfaces, target key, source/version
custody, and function-family completeness are independently validated.
