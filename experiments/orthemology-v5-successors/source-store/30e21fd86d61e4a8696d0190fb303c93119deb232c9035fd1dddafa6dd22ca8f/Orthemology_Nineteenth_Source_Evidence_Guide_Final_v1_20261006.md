## How to read the locators

“Printed” means that the listed page image was visually inspected in the specified edition. “Electronic” means a page label displayed by a transcription, without a new scan collation. “Hosted lines” identify the bounded retrieved passage, not permanent lines in a printed edition. Numbered website subsections such as 003 or 016 are not volume numbers. PDF pages below are one-based unless expressly stated otherwise.

A local rereading can verify a retained passage without adding a new source or independent witness. Different hosts may reproduce the same edition. The read ranges describe the underlying research inspections; preparing this guide did not repeat every primary-source reading. No complete author-corpus reading, manuscript collation or outside human-specialist review is claimed.

The broader traversal does not turn every source assertion into a verified fact, every report into authenticated testimony or every interpretative judgement into a theorem. A complete traversal of the selected printed witness also does not recover its author’s complete lost original. These qualifications limit what the source reading establishes.
