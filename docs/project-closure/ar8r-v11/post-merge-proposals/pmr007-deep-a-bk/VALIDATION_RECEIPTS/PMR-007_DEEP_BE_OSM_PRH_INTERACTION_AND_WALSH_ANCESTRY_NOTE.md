## OSM empirical method comparison

The supplied Sun et al. paper reports longitudinal CA1 recordings while mice
learned a task with visually ambiguous states.  Neural representations became
progressively decorrelated and orthogonalized in task-relevant locations.  The
paper compares several computational models and reports that CSCG reproduced
both the selected final orthogonalized state structure and the learning
trajectory among the tested models, while other architectures could reproduce
parts of the endpoint under selected designs or objectives.
