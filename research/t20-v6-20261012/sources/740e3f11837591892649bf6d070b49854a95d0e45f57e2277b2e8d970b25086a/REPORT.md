# Evidence withdrawal and continued epistemic warrant

9 October 2026 UTC. Source-critical T20 contribution to the live revocation and learning investigations. Research conclusions are separated from source attributions and protocol mathematics. No protected repository edit, new theological verdict, or programme closure is proposed.

## Result

Withdrawal of one argument preserves a warranted conclusion when a suitably truth-connected ground for the same content remains genuinely operative, or is faithfully retained and appropriately reactivated, independently of the respect in which the withdrawn route is defeated. An alternative proof merely available to an observer is insufficient. Conversely, a missing replayable certificate is insufficient to show that the subject has lost knowledge.

The positive discriminator is **the acquisition chronology and present operation of the surviving route, together with the exact scope of the defeater**. This is more demanding than saying that truth differs from proof, but less demanding than requiring every knower to articulate an epistemological theory. A later defective explanation cannot have originated antecedently possessed knowledge. It can nevertheless occasion a fresh insight; that insight needs its own warranting basis. These are substantially inherited findings, now directly reassessed in their Arabic context.

The new application result is a precise limit on the current mathematical abstractions: certificate-eligibility and accepted-evidence posterior states do not determine actual human warrant unless the abstraction also preserves the relevant uptake, basing and retention information. Nothing in this result makes the retained positive fiṭrah or attestation appraisal provisional by default.

## Five events with different consequences

1. **Loss of access to a proof.** The artifact becomes unavailable. This affects replay, public demonstration or a protocol's present certificate eligibility. It does not itself invalidate the inference, falsify its premises, or destroy an already acquired capacity. Continued knowledge requires actual retained grounds or a present alternative; the absent file alone settles neither direction.
2. **Discovery that the proof is invalid.** The defective inference ceases to supply adequate support. The conclusion can still be known through another route, including one antecedent to the invalid proof. The defect must be located: conclusion, premise, inference rule, semantic bridge and source attribution have different dependency scopes. If the only alleged route was actually invalid, the earlier conclusion was not knowledge *by that route*.
3. **Withdrawal of a speaker's authority or permission.** A present prohibition on publishing or using an artifact changes permitted action, not historical truth. A retraction of endorsement, evidence that the earlier attribution was false, or loss of relevant competence may instead undercut testimony. Content, recipient, reporting domain and effective time determine which event occurred. A prospective licence expiry is not retroactive falsification.
4. **Loss of warrant for a premise.** An undercutter can remove entitlement to use that premise through the affected route without establishing its negation. A different route may establish the very same premise; sharing propositional content is not the same as sharing the defeated evidential dependence. If the defeater attacks a shared reliability or interpretation bridge, apparently different proofs may both fail.
5. **Learning that the conclusion is false.** This requires positive warrant for the negation or an appropriate decisive refutation. For one fixed, time-indexed content, genuine knowledge of its falsity excludes genuine knowledge of it. The earlier state was then apparent knowledge, not a truth retrospectively erased. A world change between Q at t0 and Q at t1 concerns different indexed claims and must not invalidate knowledge of the earlier fact by equivocation.

These distinctions are the present analytical reconstruction. They are not offered as a five-part quotation or complete defeater theory attributed to either classical author.

### A compact typed defeater contract

Record a proposed defeater as `(target, kind, content scope, effective time, grounds for applying it)`. The following application rules sharpen its permitted propagation:

- **Proof-local:** a named derivation's step or application fails. Disable support through that step. Retain an actually operative B that avoids the failed inferential dependence. Do not clear the conclusion's truth or every proof of it.
- **Ground-local:** a particular acquisition or support for premise P is undercut. Suspend uses dependent on that ground. B may still use P if it independently warrants P; the shared proposition does not by itself create a shared defective route.
- **Source-wide within a specified domain:** source identity, authenticity or the universal veracity premise is defeated for domain D. Reassess every path relying on that source premise within D, including different documents and copies. Independent observation or inference may still warrant particular contents. A lie outside D is not automatically a counterexample inside D; a genuine false report inside D cannot coexist with the unchanged universal factive premise.
- **Direct-claim:** a sound refutation establishes not-Q for the same target. No genuinely factive warrant for Q can survive unchanged. Apparent simultaneous certificates require rechecking at least one premise, interpretation or derivation; do not average the contradiction into continued knowledge of both.
- **Access or permission only:** update retrieval, issue or use eligibility. Do not propagate an epistemic defeater unless the event also supplies independently warranted evidence bearing on a cognitive ground. Preserve the historical acquisition record.

The **retention guard** in the first three cases is not “another proof exists.” It is an actually acquired/operative same-content B, appropriately retained where necessary, whose truth connection and basing survive the justified scope of d. Where application of this guard is specifically disputed and the evidence is insufficient, identify the unresolved application bridge. Do not convert that modelling limitation into a verdict that the person lacks knowledge. A previously supported positive appraisal remains in force unless a relevant defeater is actually established; an incomplete reductive explanation or an imagined possibility of error supplies no such defeater.

## A positive retention witness and its limits

For a particular subject S, content Q and withdrawal d of route A, a substantive surviving-route case has the following separately supported components:

- **Same target:** route B concerns the same content and temporal/semantic scope as Q.
- **Grounded acquisition or present exercise:** S genuinely acquired Q through B, or now exercises a competent truth-connected B. The existence of a written proof somewhere is not this fact.
- **Operative continuity:** B presently bases the belief, or the known content has been faithfully retained and is appropriately reactivated. A current demonstration can establish a new route without pretending it restores every detail of the old one.
- **Defeater separation:** d's justified scope does not defeat B's premises, interpretation, source entitlement, competence or retention. An unknown alternative or an invented independent-source flag does not establish this separation. Equally, merely imagining that the previously supported B might be defective does not defeat B or require exhaustive faculty certification.

This is a sufficient-case architecture, not an exhaustive definition of knowledge or a demand for reflective self-certification by every knower. “Independent” here means independent of the **defeated dependence**, not probabilistically independent and not necessarily disjoint in propositional premises.

The source's mass-transmission example gives a concrete chronology: an earlier cognitive route can warrant Q before A exists; later criticism of A cannot show that all warrant originated in A. In a current arithmetic application, the retained CP19 content-using derivation offers a different constructive route: independently known 7+4=11, equality preservation under adding one, and the relevant arithmetic identities can ground 7+5=12 when actually used. Discrediting an unrelated teacher's explanation need not affect that competent calculation. This is a conditional illustrative application, not an observation of an actual learner or a proof that source trust never matters.

If all operative routes within a genuinely established scope are defeated and no warranted retention route remains, the affirmative epistemic appraisal must be withdrawn or suspended to the extent required by the adopted account. That is not permission to infer Q is false. A finite inventory may establish only that its own catalogue has no eligible certificate; it cannot silently certify exhaustion of all human grounds. Darʾ 5's criticism of unsupported exclusivity directly bears on this step.

## Consequence for the two live mathematical models

For the reset theorem, the output h is the declared certificate-eligibility/cost function. Clearing a coordinate is a status transition inside that contract. A theorem about the exact memory for those outputs remains sound without becoming a theorem about actual cognition. If a source-wide undercutter is admitted, its dependencies must include the shared authenticity or interpretation conditions, not just individual artifact leaves. Permissions should be represented separately when they govern issue/use rather than epistemic support.

For the accepted-evidence likelihood model, removing a factor from a retractable store is not automatically conditioning on newly learned falsity, undoing historical learning, or erasing an earlier recognition. An observed retraction or reliability failure can itself carry information and requires its own justified channel. Earlier uptake may have produced retained content or a capacity that survives deletion of the original source item.

A concrete obstruction arises if two histories have the same current artifacts and posterior, while only one includes competent uptake through an independent route that remains operative. An inventory-only q maps them together. It cannot then decode an appraisal that distinguishes actual basing. This is the inherited fibre obstruction applied to the source's acquisition/retention distinction, **not a new general theorem** and not a stipulative demonstration of actual human knowledge. The important missing observation is the independently supported episode, not an extra coordinate named “warranted.”

A bounded protocol can retain a justified summary of those episodes rather than an unlimited transcript. Nothing here proves that full historical storage is necessary. The abstraction needs an adequacy argument for its selected observations and future actions; the Boolean reset theorem does not supply the epistemological adequacy premises by itself.
