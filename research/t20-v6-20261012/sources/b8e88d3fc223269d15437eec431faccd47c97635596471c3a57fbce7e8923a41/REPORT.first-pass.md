# Original completion, generated provision, and a realised infinite account

7 October 2026. Scientific continuation beyond the prior bounded audit. This report preserves the affirmative T17 appraisal, the original-source countermodels, both sealed archives, and the difference between source interpretation, philosophical warrant, and formal entailment. It makes no closure claim.

## Result

There is a useful further discrimination at the first front. A locally satisfied, acyclic, all-received account can be globally determinate once an actual occurrence is specified. Its failure to yield an original resource is therefore not merely a failure to specify enough of its operating profile. The contested step is whether such a realised dependent system supplies the required existential explanation, rather than merely a consistent account of how already-realised items maintain one another.

A proposed generated-availability semantics makes that difference exact. It is not a new proof of original completion: its least-solution selection is an additional substantive interpretation. Moreover, applying that selection to every necessary condition is source-unfaithful, and requiring the whole target to be generated is stronger than the minimal original-witness conclusion. The checked controls distinguish both mistakes.

One genuinely generated original contribution relevant to an actual target can support the existing resource-to-bearer route while another target branch remains ungrounded. The same minimal witness does not thereby supply T19's complete common provider. Beginningless acts and infinitely many jointly relevant prerequisites are compatible with the positive generated-resource interpretation. Neither a sequence nor its invariant is reified into an original individual.

The new work improves the diagnosis of the warrant burden. It does not independently upgrade the current confidence in metaphysical original completion, identify a newly established actual original bearer, or turn noncausal existential grounding into efficient provision.

## 1. Exact evidence and question

T17 P40–43 distinguishes real local supply from a complete existential account that merely presupposes received actuality throughout. P44–52 separates noncausal grounding, productive-resource realisation, and the original bearer. P53–61 applies contingency-as-need and constitutive reception to that same bearer. P65–68 gives a separate positive reason for standing power, without inferring its actual exercise.

The prior ground-extension inquiry already reached the stronger affirmative position that an actually individuated, independent necessary ground can receive the pure-perfection argument. Its remaining issue is actual productive attribution. That result is not reopened as though it had been overlooked.

For the upstream completion question, the held primary source provides an unusually useful internal contrast. Ṣafadiyya electronic S002 L725–740 distinguishes successive particular effects from the origin of efficacy. Fresh visual inspection checked the corresponding argument against the held Adwa scan, printed396–400, PDF403–407, within nine complete pages inspected at PDF400–408. Printed397–399 says that an agent cannot acquire the capacity to act at all by an exercise that already presupposes that capacity. Yet individual acts can presuppose earlier acts, and a child can presuppose its parent. Printed400 makes the difference between the genus of efficacy and particular exercises explicit.

The supplied Oneness translation, printed17–22/PDF24–29, gives the same distinction between efficient-priority dependency and permitted interconditionality. Its fatherhood/childhood example is especially clear. The footnotes are separately attributed translation/editorial material; their blanket anti-regress summaries are not independent authorial demonstrations. Ṣafadiyya S003 L207 independently permits mutually conditioned created powers receiving their power from one cause.

These are source statements about explanatory roles. The source does not offer a theory of least fixed points. That vocabulary below is our analytical reconstruction, with a specific fidelity test.

## 2. What the source's strongest positive reason supplies

The affirmative claim is about the actuality of the resources by which production occurs. It is not the claim that a beneficiary becomes original after receiving supply. It is also not a demand for a temporally first act. A standing source can be always actual while its token acts succeed one another without a first token.

The rationale has three stages:

1. A real received efficacy is not merely a formula relating descriptions. Its concrete availability is what allows an agent to act at all.
2. Explaining that availability by another already available but received efficacy gives a genuine local explanation. It does not by itself explain availability in a nonreceived mode.
3. An account advertised as exhaustive must account for the actuality used by all those local explanations. Merely repeating the received mode, including infinitely, does not display a different mode of existential adequacy. The proposed original actuality performs that different role.

This is a positive explanatory distinction. It does not rely on denying ordinary derivative efficacy, denying infinity, or requiring a nonexistent bearer to produce itself. The source's anti-bootstrap argument is strongest where a proposed agent is meant to acquire *its capacity for any production* through that very capacity's own exercise. Such a proposal spends the capacity it is supposed to supply.

Extending that judgement to an infinite chain of distinct received bearers requires the whole-account principle in stage3. The finite same-agent bootstrap contradiction does not by itself establish that no infinite all-received system can be existentially adequate. The primary text asserts the broader prohibition with great confidence, but its declared evidentness is not a separate checked entailment.

## 3. Strongest opposing interpretation

The serious opposing interpretation need not say that receivers are powerless, that their receipt vanishes after supply, or that the chain has a concealed last link. It can say:

- Every member actually exists and possesses real efficacy supplied by another member.
- No member efficiently produces itself and no directed dependency cycle occurs.
- The indefinitely extended relation is not a temporal process of constructing reality from nothing. It is the realised account of continuing provision.
- Its actuality is not required to be generated by a least-solution procedure. A coinductive or global account may be adequate without an independent original stock.

Our infinite chain gives this interpretation more precision. For each natural n, resource n is supplied by n+1. Availability satisfies the full local equation D(n) iff D(n+1). Once the observed target D(0) is imposed, every D(n) is determined as true. There is no original resource. Lean verifies both the global consequence and acyclicity.

The observation is evidence that something exists, not a supplying original resource. Treating D(0) as a generative seed would change the explanatory question. Conversely, calling the globally realised solution an original whole, a necessary law, or the genus of efficacy would require a new ontological argument. The interpretation does not acquire an individuated original bearer merely because the mathematics describes one invariant pattern.

The affirmative reply is that a model constrained by the occurrence to be explained does not thereby explain that occurrence. A globally definite network can still presuppose the actuality whose original adequacy was at issue. The critic's strongest reply is that the exhaustive network of fulfilled dependencies *is* the adequate account, and no independent underwriting is necessary. This is the precise disagreement. Mathematical determinacy does not adjudicate it, but neither does the model establish metaphysical possibility or evidential parity with T17's affirmative rationale.

A proposed fundamental law requiring a nonempty solution sharpens rather than removes this issue. If the law is only a true description, one must explain why its truth supplies existential actuality. If it is a genuinely autonomous individuated reality, the inherited original-particular and pure-perfection arguments become available at their stated scope. If it is a genuinely nonobjectual ground, bearer realisation remains an additional ontological commitment. The earlier ground-extension work already distinguishes these alternatives.

## 4. Executed semantic test

`ExplanatoryGeneration.lean` has resource and rule types. Each rule has an output and an arbitrary, potentially infinite set of prerequisites. Original-resource availability is separately represented. The availability equation says that an available resource is either original or supplied by a rule whose prerequisites are available.

Generated availability is the smallest set closed under original resources and those rules. The explicit selection condition says actual availability is included in that generated set. Given forward closure, the condition is exactly equality with the least closed account. This equivalence is disclosed as a warning against awarding new independent warrant to a rephrased completion assumption.

The rules do not themselves smuggle a seed into every derivation: a separate nonempty-prerequisite guard is needed. A checked empty-rule control derives availability with no original input when the guard is removed. For a received-resource interpretation, the guard says there is genuinely something received rather than a production rule that silently originates availability without any represented source.

The executed controls establish:

- Full local equations, nonempty receipt, an infinite domain, acyclicity, and complete determination by an observed occurrence still allow an all-received account.
- Adding a disconnected original seed does not generate the unsupported branch. Mere existence of an original somewhere is insufficient for target relevance.
- A mixed target can have a generated original contributor and an additional ungenerated infinite prerequisite branch. Its original contribution is genuinely relevant to the target, yet the target is not fully generated.
- A theorem using only one generated contribution, rather than full target or field generation, obtains the same actual original resource with a supply path to the target. Applying the existing typed bearer bridge would still require its separate realisation and support-transport premises.
- Arbitrary prerequisite sets matter. The positive infinite-family example has a generated output beyond every finite iteration stage. Thus induction over finitary ancestry depth or a finite proof-tree bound would wrongly exclude an admitted positive case.
- Beginningless token acts coexist with an original standing resource. The act sequence alone does not supply that resource; it is explicitly present as an independent resource input.
- Mutually conditioned created powers from one common giver can be represented with original provision and a separate co-presence constraint. If the co-presence constraint is misclassified as generative priority, the same active assignment becomes ungenerated. The source itself permits the former type of interdependence.

The last control is decisive for interpretation. A generic algorithm which applies least closure to every necessary condition is not a faithful formalisation of the source. Before using generation as an explanatory test, one must justify which relations are origination/provision and which are co-presence, occasion, temporal order, or other conditions.

Fresh Lean4.19.0 checks with `--trust=0` succeeded using only `Init`. Every theorem's axiom readback is empty. Exact source/readback hashes and retained development diagnostics are in `VERIFICATION.json`. The checks certify these relative semantic statements, not the truth of original completion or the possibility of the rival account.

## 5. What changes for the original four-front obligation

The first-front result now has a more precise hierarchy:

1. An occurrence and a well-specified realised pattern of received efficacy do not logically force an original stock, even if that pattern is globally determinate.
2. One warranted, relevant original resource is enough for the existing minimal same-bearer argument; exhaustive generation of every target branch is unnecessary for that conclusion.
3. Full common-source coverage requires additional account-completeness and contribution-sensitive premises. The mixed target makes the gap explicit without withdrawing the minimal witness.
4. Generated *existential* provision is not automatically efficient agency. The ground-extension negative remains intact. Classifying a rule as productive must be warranted before using T19's mediate productive attribution.

This has cross-programme use without conflating domains. A grounded attestation or institutional authorisation procedure may intentionally require an independently supported starting point. An ontological account cannot inherit that rule merely because the same least-closure mathematics is useful for evidence systems. Likewise, genuine mutual constraints must not be mistaken for circular sourcehood. The shared formal pattern helps find the point at which a domain-specific warrant is needed; it does not supply that warrant.

## 6. Remaining questions, concrete next tests, and limits

Highest leverage remains the exact bridge from original existential provision of instantiated productive resources to original productive participation in the observed field. A useful next test would compare a *necessary preserving input* with a merely enabling or covarying condition under T19 P81–91, using the already held actual-causation and source-individuation cases. Its success condition is a source-supported relation criterion, or an explicit counterinterpretation preserving that criterion while efficient attribution fails. Repeating the capable-but-idle ground model alone would add no result. This connects directly to the parallel productive-provision inquiry.

For original completion itself, the next available philosophical discrimination is whether a proposed global nonempty-solution law is explanatory, merely descriptive, or an autonomous actual basis. The evidence required is the rival's positive account of how that law supplies actuality, and a source-grounded criticism of that role. Merely adding a nonempty-solution axiom, or automatically reifying it as a particular, would be ceremonial. The held source argument and present semantic test do not decide this ontology-neutral question. T17's adopted productive/bearer account remains affirmatively appraised; no contrary world has been established.

For essential independence, the current test adds no new reason that the same bearer must retain its whole-existence mode across alternatives. That remains T17's separately defended constitutive judgement and its preserved identity objection. A new proof obtained by assuming invariant existential constitution would not deepen its warrant. Relevant further work would need a particular rival identity account or additional source argument, rather than another role-switch table already covered by the existing controls.
