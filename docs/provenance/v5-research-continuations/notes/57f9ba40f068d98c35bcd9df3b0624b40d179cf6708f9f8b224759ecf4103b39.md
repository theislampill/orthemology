## Hypotheses and limitations

Navigation receives a candidate theta in the fixed live-support label B; its
source graph/closed-class/path progress is derived. Operation receives the real
MarkovQualifying predicate, sigma in B, and a genuine row mismatch. The tail
is quantitative only when p > 0; p=0 is intentionally a valid trivial boundary.

an artificial conditional law guarantee.

The supplied frozen augmented PMF is an analysis object, not a replacement
controller. The final probability statement is about the actual generated
policy's continuing-history event, not merely survival of a distinct rotor.
