# Primary sources and attribution controls

## Method and limits

The report reads primary works through electronic transcriptions and public-domain translations, with exact work/section locators and displayed page markers where available. It is not a manuscript or print-facsimile collation. Independent mirrors can share one transcription; their agreement is not counted as independent textual tradition. The search-return date is not the original publication date. Raw retrieved web/search text is omitted from this delivery derivative. The source register and omission inventory preserve exact locators and hashes for the consulted captures. Search-only snippets were not the sole evidence for a load-bearing attribution.

The source arguments are presented as arguments. An author's claim that something is rationally necessary, evident or universally agreed is not a fresh proof of consensus. Historical biological, cosmological and social examples are not adopted as independently established modern evidence. In particular, al-Nubuwwāt's anatomical and salinity illustrations, and its surrounding historically situated social rankings, do not supply premises of our philosophical reconstruction. Its asserted superiority of truthful, knowledgeable and just action is separately examined rather than insulated by the surrounding rhetoric.

The accepted sixth Knowledge and source packets remain unchanged. Their noninferential clarification governs every inference about source cognition in this continuation.

## Ibn Taymiyya sources

### IT1 Al-Risāla al-Akmaliyya in Majmūʿ al-fatāwā 6

Verified through the retained primary transcription and freshly reopened hosts:

- [QuranIslam digital transcription](https://quranislam.github.io/fatwa/ibn-taymiyyah/fatwa81.html)
- [Islamweb, book 22, item 591](https://islamweb.net/ar/library/content/22/591/مسألة-تفصيل-الإجمال-فيما-يجب-لله-من-صفات-الكمال)

Exact loci:

1. **6:76–79:** the already independently established necessary, self-sufficient, creative source; eligible perfection; original giver of power, knowledge and life; rejection of externally supplied perfection. This passage does not establish the entire upstream source argument in the act of applying the perfection route. The received-perfection and eligibility warrants remain explicit.
2. **6:79–80:** creativity and just, upright address in the comparison of the speaking and powerless mute agent. The passage distinguishes laudable speech/action from speech/action without qualification. Our communicative-perfection application is therefore about meaningful fitting address, not sheer noise production.
3. **6:85–87:** possible perfection, logically impossible tasks, successively occurring acts, and why not every particular act is beginningless. Absence of a particular act before its fitting occasion is not automatically deficiency.
4. **6:127–130:** replies concerning injustice, source independence and wisdom; at 129–130 the stronger positive contrast between bare unrestricted willing and action guided by Knowledge, Wisdom, mercy and beneficence. On the Islamweb capture these are lines 1558–1574. The text reports opposed approaches and then gives its endorsed synthesis; the report does not treat all reported definitions of injustice as equivalent.
5. **6:131:** response to the objection that ordinary or weak recipients are unworthy of a divine messenger. Powerful mercy, teaching and guidance support the fittingness of communication. This is a philosophical/school argument about eligibility and beneficence, not independent historical authentication of a mission. The continuation is at Islamweb lines 1578 onward.

Primary retained input: `tranche6/necessary-ground/inputs/mf6.txt`. The leading volume/page markers in this input are explicit. Relevant excerpts are copied with custody metadata; no predecessor file is edited. Fresh inspection reached the actual 131 continuation near Islamweb line1578; the source register binds the omitted capture hashes.

### IT2 Al-Ṣafadiyya 2:96–110

- [IslamicBook hosted subdivision 002](https://www.islamicbook.ws/amma/alsfdit-002.html)
- [IslamHouse paginated transcription](https://islamhouse.com/read/ar/الصفدية-272837)

The retained IslamHouse header identifies Muḥammad Rashād Sālim as editor, Maktabat Ibn Taymiyya in Egypt, second edition 1406 AH, two parts in one volume, and says pagination follows the print edition. These are host-provided bibliographic claims, not fresh print collation. The host number 002 is not itself a volume/page reference.

**Page-marker control:** the `(2/...)` markers in the IslamHouse transcription are trailing markers. A passage between `(2/104)` and `(2/105)` belongs to the text ending with the `(2/105)` marker. Earlier exploratory shorthand identifying a passage by the preceding marker is not retained as a final citation.

Exact locators:

- Hosted lines 630–635, around the text ending at **2:96–98:** type of productive activity versus one particular act/effect; the source may always act while no particular created item is beginningless. The exact phrase locating the first movement is “ذاته مستلزمة لنوع التأثير”.
- Hosted lines 658–665, text ending at **2:103–104:** bare attribute-stripped essence versus essence characterized by Power, Will and action; a completed exercise can necessitate its effect without being an unintelligent cause. The central bare/qualified-essence paragraph ends at **2:103**.
- Hosted lines 673–675, text ending at **2:105–106:** rejection of one wholly equal, undifferentiated Will being invoked as the sufficient differentiator without a differentiating ground. The first direct rejection is before **(2/105)**. The discussion of distinct willing of one object and another continues before **(2/106)**.
- Hosted lines around 690–700, text ending at **2:109–110:** complete Power and settled Will; genuine possibility rather than actual performance as the domain of Power; not willing versus inability; alternatives versus contradictory conjunction; an act requires a complete determination unlike the state in which it is absent.

The source's “reason/differentiator” and “complete determinant” must not be collapsed. A merely motivating reason is not thereby a complete efficient determinant. The text's account of completed efficacy is stronger, while its standing-attribute/particular-act distinction blocks the inference that every particular effect must be beginningless. This does not on its own settle modal collapse across all metaphysically possible alternatives.

The public-domain primary passage remains in `sources/safadiyya_excerpt.txt`; omitted online captures are hash-bound in the source register. The attempted generated PDF opening failed; it is recorded but never described as inspected. Final pagination is electronic-transcription pagination.

### IT3 Al-Nubuwwāt on Wisdom and prophetic authentication

- [Hosted subdivision 003](https://www.islamicbook.ws/amma/alnbwat-003.html)
- [Wikisource chapter subdivision 9](https://ar.wikisource.org/wiki/النبوات/9)

The two chapter/subdivision numbers are host divisions, not printed volume numbers. No exact print-edition page is claimed for these new Nubuwwāt loci. A secondary search hit associated a phrase with page376, but that print locator was not verified and is not used.

Exact source-text locators in hosted003:

- **Lines7–10:** if a wise source actually sends a messenger and requires reception, a recognizable indication must accompany the mission; original-perfection comparison supports source capability. This is conditional on a mission, not proof that a particular mission occurred.
- **Lines20–21:** coherent purposive arrangement and the linked Knowledge, Will and Wisdom inference. The historical anatomy/salinity examples are not research evidence of current biological explanation.
- **Lines21–22:** the positive cognitive/practical argument; the source knows what is fitting, its willing is differentiated by preference and it cannot act foolishly under the defended perfection account. The text's claim that vice arises from ignorance or appetite is the substantive motivational premise, not an analytic truth of Knowledge.
- **Lines23–24:** why miraculous efficacy without wise, truthful purposive certification fails to supply the intended prophetic-authentication inference. A mere unusual effect does not do the work of a source-authored identifying sign.
- **Wikisource9, lines93–94:** an indicator must distinguish the truthful prophetic claim from false claims; extraordinary and surprising effects alone are too coarse. The text discusses signs before and after a prophet's life and through followers, so mere simultaneity with a verbal claim is not the whole criterion.

The register binds the consulted paragraph captures; their raw current-site text is not distributed here. The transcription has a likely typographic “عام” where the surrounding argument concerns “عالم”; our paraphrase follows the unmistakable Knowledge argument rather than claiming a silently corrected critical text. No historical factual claim about a specific miracle is established by our argument.

### IT4 Majmūʿ al-fatāwā 8:145–153

[Islamweb, book22 item806](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/22/806/مسألة-حسن-إرادة-الله-لخلق-الخلق-وهل-يخلق-لعلة-أو-لغير-علة), especially web lines1434–1464.

The source distinguishes satisfaction in an act originated by the source from externally supplied existential completion; rejects treating absence before a fitting time as deficiency; and discusses different accounts of renewed willing and wisdom. The report uses the positive architecture and contrast, not a claim that every reported school view is the author's final doctrine. It also preserves the analogy's limit: the mere occurrence of satisfaction cannot exclude malicious ends while automatically allowing good ends; a normative distinction is still required.

### IT5 Jāmiʿ al-masāʾil 6:207–209

[Islamweb, book416 item688](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/416/688/فصل-قاعدة-في-إرادة-العدم-والإعدام-واستطاعته-وفعله), web lines1081–1086 and the duplicated vocalized text below.

The distinction is between a complete sufficient cause and a partial cause needing conditions, and between effective willing and the loved/approved final end. It supports separating occurrence, selected actuality and moral approval. It does not establish that every proposed actual harm is justified or that any desired creaturely end is guaranteed.

## Rival primary sources

### R1 Bird on intrinsic powers

[Alexander Bird, “Monistic Dispositional Essentialism,” author-hosted PDF](https://www.alexanderbird.org/Research/Papers/Monistic_Dispositional_Essentialism.pdf), sections1–2, PDF pp.1–2. See the bounded original summary in ATTRIBUTE_ASCENT.md §2. No PDF or retrieved article text is distributed.

### R2 Aristotle on immanent finality

[Physics II, Hardie and Gaye translation](https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/physics.2.ii.html), II.1 (192b) and II.8 (198b–199b), especially hosted lines138–159. Nature is an intrinsic principle and may act for an end without observed deliberation. This defeats an exhaustive thought-versus-accident dichotomy; it does not by itself prove a wholly impersonal ultimate ontology. The modern selected-function rival is also distinct from this Aristotelian finality.

### R3 Spinoza on Thought and nonvoluntarist causation

[Ethics, Elwes translation at Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/3800/pg3800-images.html), I.31–34 and II.1–3. Thought is an attribute; actual intellect and will are modes; divine production is not free willing in the targeted voluntarist sense. This is a thinking-source rival, not an example of complete absence of cognition. Our use does not assert the truth of Spinoza's necessitarianism, or reduce all distinctions between him and Ibn Taymiyya to one modal formula.

### R4 Hume on cognitive motivation and plural design

- [Treatise2.3.3](https://davidhume.org/texts/t/2/3/3), especially §§1–7, SBN413–416: cognitive relations alone do not supply the motivating end. This challenges a bare Knowledge→good-Will inference; ordinary akrasia does not by itself refute perfect original practical agency.
- [DialoguesV.5–9](https://davidhume.org/texts/d/5): finite design analogy does not automatically establish unlimited attributes, perfection or their union in one bearer; cooperative production is a live alternative in the dialectic. These are arguments voiced by Philo, not a declaration that Hume believes several deities actually created the universe.

### R5 Plotinus on the source beyond Intellect

[EnneadV.2.1, MacKenna and Page translation](https://www.ellopos.net/Elpenor/greek-texts/ancient-greece/plotinus/enneads-5.asp?pg=18), hosted lines22–28. The One's productive fullness yields Intellect while the One is not simply an item among its products. This is a positive source proposal which directly contests the relevant eligible-perfection comparison. It is not represented as inert matter, mere nothingness or an ordinary ignorant mind. The report does not adjudicate the entire Neoplatonic system.
