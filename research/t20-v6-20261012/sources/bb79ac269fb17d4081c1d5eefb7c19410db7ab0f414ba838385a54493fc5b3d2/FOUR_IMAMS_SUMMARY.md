# Four imams source ecology addition

The source is useful for shared orientation and fallible scholarship, with important attribution limits. Al-Khumayyis explicitly excepts disagreement about faith from his unity claim. The English edition combines his work with translator/editor additions. Its note155 loses an adverse qualification preserved in his Arabic text concerning one creed attributed to al-Shafi‘i. That passage remains unsuitable as authenticated personal epistemology. Fiṭrah and divine-action passages are leads, not proof of identical formal systems or the disputed complete-efficacy premise. The complete translated main work and selected supplementary units were read; the whole expanded volume was not.
