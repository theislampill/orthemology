# Numerical plurality and qualitative symmetry

## Result and scope

This audit tests a supplementary unity argument: numerical plurality → differing attributes → composition → deficient dependence. [Argument tested, p.320](https://almobadarah.com/uploads/books/5f6ae0298582cce21002cbe4.pdf#page=320)

The mathematical result is limited but precise. Two distinct bearer indices can have different own power and act tokens while their declared qualitative relational structures admit an automorphism exchanging them. This demonstrates a missing logical implication at that signature. It does not establish the metaphysical possibility of two perfect necessary originals.

Eight executable checks pass. They include both a transparent finite fixture and qualitative relational exports from the current joined reactive model. The latter retain its acknowledged lack of individual entire original provision in joint production. No stronger causal-unity argument is refuted by this symmetry calculation.

## 1. The finite structure

The minimal fixture contains:

- bearer indices A and B;
- distinct power tokens PA and PB;
- distinct act tokens AA and AB;
- a common created-object index q.

Its relations record which tokens inhere in which bearers, which acts are directed to q, an own-attribute prerequisite, and a symmetric irreflexive relation between the two bearers. Separable proper parts and foreign efficient receipt are represented by different relation symbols.

The tested permutation exchanges A with B, PA with PB, and AA with AB, while fixing q. The programme verifies that it preserves every relation in the declared signature. It therefore preserves more than a list of matching unary labels: the complete represented ownership and action pattern is preserved.

The fixture's labels “necessary” and “original” are explicit placeholders in a reduced model. They do not establish existence through oneself, unrestricted power or complete perfection. No predicate named “perfect” is inserted and then cited as evidence.

## 2. Test on the existing joined model

A second operation imports the current reactive candidate without editing it. For each of its three ordinary concordant reason-contexts, the export retains:

- bearer and standing power/knowledge tokens;
- actual act handles and inherence;
- available settings, selected settings and goals;
- represented intention and own-end satisfaction;
- forecast settings, goals and created-event contents;
- efficient-edge records and entire-provider attribution.

Exchanging the two bearers and their associated tokens is an automorphism in every exported structure. Goal, setting and created-event labels stay fixed. Individual entire-provider attribution remains empty in the joint cases.

This is a qualitative relational reduct. It is not a declaration that every feature of the implementation, much less every real fact about original agents, has been exhausted. Act handles also remain representation handles; their use does not establish numerical act identity across changed exercises.

## 3. Why the invariance consequence follows

The relevant logical result is standard. An automorphism preserves truth of first-order formulas without individual parameters:

1. Primitive relations are preserved by the definition of automorphism.
2. Equality is preserved by bijectivity.
3. Boolean combinations preserve the induction hypothesis.
4. Existential witnesses and universal ranges transport through the same bijection.

Consequently, a one-free-variable formula in this language has the same truth at A and B when the automorphism exchanges them.

The code checks the finite relation-preservation premise and representative formulas. It does not enumerate all formulas. The usual structural induction supplies the general first-order result; no new theorem or kernel certification is claimed.

## 4. Numerical distinction still permits property differences

The result must not be exaggerated into “A and B have every property in common.” With A available as an individual parameter, “x is identical to A” distinguishes them. With PA fixed as a parameter, “PA inheres in x” also distinguishes them. Both differences are explicitly checked.

These facts answer a different question from intrinsic qualitative inequality. A has its own PA and B has its own PB; each may have the same qualitative power-kind. The claim that B lacks A's particular attribute token does not yet show that B lacks an eligible perfection.

Weak relational discernibility is also present. A and B stand in an irreflexive relation to one another that neither bears to itself. Thus numerical plurality need not be represented as an absence of all structure or all discernibility.

If an argument requires that distinct originals possess different intrinsic qualitative characters, it needs an individuation principle of that strength. An anti-haecceitist or sufficient-reason defence might provide one. The present finite structures do not refute such a defence; they identify what must be supplied beyond ordinary identity logic.

## 5. Further semantic implications remain separate

Adding a primitive distinguishing feature to A breaks the swap symmetry. It does not, without an additional axiom, create a separable proper part or a foreign efficient donor. This controlled variant exposes the distinction between predication and constitution. It is not a theory of what any real divine differentiator could be.

An argument from a shared nature and an individuating feature to composition must establish that both correspond to real constituents in the relevant sense. Merely writing two predicates does not do that. If “parts” instead means conceptually distinguishable aspects, another argument is needed to obtain dependence on independently explanatory components.

Likewise, an own-attribute prerequisite and foreign efficient receipt are different relations. The source distinction between one's own intrinsic attributes and creatures/products supports keeping those meanings separate. It does not license cooperation between independently original peers. [Bayan, own attributes and self-sufficiency](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/415/419/)

A valid conditional perfection argument could require:

- every perfect original possesses every intrinsic pure perfection eligible for it;
- any two distinct proposed originals must differ in at least one such eligible perfection.

The second condition is stronger than the existence of some parameterized distinguishing predicate. Its independent justification is the remaining task. Alternatively, a composition argument needs an independently supported connection between individuation, real constitution and the relevant deficient dependence.

## 6. Ancestry and limitations

The inherited Deep-I research candidate already contained symmetric plural actualizers, primitive coupling, individual-tag controls and automorphism-based nonselection. Its own ancestry note assigns zero general mathematical novelty. That candidate status is not promoted by this audit.

The current contribution is a specific relational export and a clarified set of semantic obligations. It establishes neither that every real intrinsic feature is represented nor that primitive numerical plurality satisfies all explanatory principles. The models may be rejected on stronger grounds, including complete original efficacy, wisdom, indivisible self-explanation or a justified individuation principle.

The verification package contains the code, finite model output and eight passing checks. These establish the stated structural facts only. They do not certify a full plural-original metaphysics or close the larger unity inquiry.
