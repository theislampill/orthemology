# Inherited infinite ancestry scope

The retained formal control combines complete ancestral coverage with failure of global well-foundedness. It does not establish the actuality or metaphysical possibility of infinitely many universes, or the existence of a complete metaphysical explanation. Here “received” refers only to having a supplying predecessor in the declared relation.

This is an inherited-source clarification. Source hashes and existing definitions/reviews were checked; no new model, theorem, Lean replay or Seventh proof credit is claimed. ## Limits and rival distinction

Bare roothood is weaker than coverage. In `projections/connected-author/models/lean/source/InfiniteRootlessBranch.lean`, lines 18–23 and 58–91, the `Fork` has one root and a connected field, but its root does not cover the infinite branch feeding the target. It therefore lacks complete local coverage. This differs from `Rooted`, where the source precedes every subordinate member. The wholly rootless `Chain` at `UpstreamGround.lean` lines 48–66 has no covering member at all.

None of these definitions has a temporal order, universe predicate, event-commencement predicate, or an identification of efficient ancestry with temporal priority. Hence they supply no temporal-first-event theorem. A dedicated positive claim about temporal realization would require an additional interpretation and warrant; no such extension was made in this check.

The graph also has no productive-mode predicates. `ReviewerProbes.lean` lines 197–210 explicitly prevents promoting least ancestry alone to the strengthened act-accounting obligations. Consequently this clarification must not be presented as a new common model of every retained source premise, a necessity proof, or a complete explanation of an actual infinite cosmology.
