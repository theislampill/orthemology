   match narrows a tested computational comparison. It does not identify the
   biological learning mechanism, establish causal equivalence, or exclude
   untested models.

   `trajectory_matches_reported_order` directly inside the certifying profile.
   A substantive refinement must use an independently specified trajectory
   signature and derive the match relation from it.

3. **U-F03 — source extraction ceiling.** The seven-instance table is a bounded
   project extraction from article text and the prior source-verification
   record. It is not a reanalysis of raw neural/model data and must not be
   described as the complete experimental comparison.

4. **U-F04 — uniqueness scope.** “Only CSCG” is restricted to the paper's tested
   comparisons and reported evaluation. It is not universal algorithmic or
   biological uniqueness.

5. **U-F05 — encoding dependence.** The source reports that sensory-sequence
   serialization changes the decorrelation order while preserving selected final
   transition structure. Algorithm-family language alone is too coarse.

6. **U-F06 — metaphysical and normative nontransfer.** Endpoint/trajectory
   evidence does not establish truth linkage, proper function, one subject,
   mentality, personality, Necessary Being, or divine Speech.

7. **U-F07 — soundness overreach.** Global orthogonalization is neither necessary
   nor sufficient for strict soundness or pathway adequacy.

8. **U-F08 — neural/model object conflation.** Biological population activity,
   CSCG latent clones, model transition graphs, and orthemic profiles remain
   distinct objects; no neural transition matrix is estimated in the access
   copy.

9. **U-F09 — theorem-family ceiling.** The refinement characterization is the
   existing fibre/factorization mechanism. General mathematical novelty is zero.

10. **U-F10 — review independence.** The present checker is same-session and
    source-derived; it does not reproduce the experiment, official code, or an
    external review.
