# Primitive original cognition: a fairer joined interpretation

The earlier statements that cognition remained unrepresented were too broad once the interpretation is allowed the same primitive status for original knowledge that it allows for original agency and power. A created cognitive mechanism is not required by the source's ontology and is not demanded here.

## Enrichment executed on the same candidate

`src/intrinsic_knower.py` uses the SAME full-Q choice rule and provision interpreter as the joint candidate. Each rigid bearer has a standing knowledge attribute K_A or K_B, fixed as its intrinsic attribute across the represented reason-contexts. Inherence, truth-correspondence, intrinsic reason-determination and efficient productive edges are different relations.

The ordinary-case forecast is calculated from the assumed known intrinsic policies and fixed production law before actual execution is passed to the comparison. It does not inspect the actual peer execution or read a created output as a donor. Each productive choice is independently computed from that bearer's proposed fitting own-activity end. Knowledge of the peer's determined act and the output is thus reason-derived at the displayed signature rather than an arbitrary correct label.

In every represented reason-context, predicted exact acts, complete created set, production rules, positive supports and intentional satisfaction match the actual run. Knowing one's intrinsic reason-context yields accessibility to precisely its matching resolved state, and every true proposition over the represented contexts is known. The previous generic Lean identity-knowledge lemma also supplies a complete factive operator for an arbitrary world type. These definitions are semantic representations of primitive cognition, not efficient manufacture of knowledge by its object.

There is no efficient path from a foreign bearer or its act into either standing knowledge attribute. Adding an explicit B-act→K_A efficient-donation edge is detected by the negative control. The non-efficient truth relation is never inserted into that efficient graph. The model therefore DOES interpret nonreceipt in this declared structural respect; it is inaccurate to say that nonreceipt is wholly absent merely because there is no implemented cognitive mechanism.

The represented contexts vary the specified local end Q. They are not asserted to be alternative worlds under one fully fixed complete necessary intrinsic nature. Within a fixed reason-context, choice and forecast are fixed. This prevents an unnoticed modal-liberty conclusion.

## Factivity under intervention

The normal intrinsic-policy forecast does not remain true when an intervention suppresses B in defiance of that very known policy. This is expected and recorded. It prevents presenting that intervention as an ordinary admissible world satisfying every fixed premise. A true content can be assigned in the changed diagnostic context without implying efficient receipt, but that updated content is not deduced from the unchanged ordinary-policy forecast. The earlier distinction between knowledge used in planning and accompanying relational knowledge remains intact.

This does not refute relevance-restricted solo efficacy comparisons. The same remaining productive command can still be held fixed in the provision engine. It says exactly which additional epistemic premise is altered or counterfactually bracketed in that comparison.

## Premise status after enrichment

- Comprehensive truth and introspection: interpreted and checked over the represented world/proposition domain; a generic factive complete operator is available.
- Knowledge as a standing attribute of the same bearer: explicitly interpreted, with a fixed intrinsic attribute token.
- Absence of foreign efficient donation to that attribute: explicitly interpreted and mutation-tested at the typed graph signature.
- Foreknowledge of the peer's choice and result: computed from the stated intrinsic policies and law, then checked against execution; no temporal-learning claim.
- Source's whole necessary-bearer ontology and unrestricted actual-world application: not established by these constructions. This is a philosophical/metaphysical warrant limitation shared by the primitive original power and agency interpretation, not a special demand for a hidden cognitive machine.
- The source's claim that necessary concord itself makes each will part of what makes the other willing: remains the disputed application. It is not imposed by treating every truth relation, shared reason or mathematical definition as an efficient cause.

The fair reduced-signature credit is consequently stronger: this joined candidate is consistent with explicitly represented original standing knowledge, non-efficient truth-directed content and peer-independent productive choice. Its defects or remaining source-warrant questions lie chiefly in the particular own-activity norm, the coalescent production law, the complete-effect ontology and original-provision interpretation. It is not disqualified by a blanket claim that 'knowledge has not been modelled'. It remains neither a metaphysical possibility certificate nor a proof of actual plural originals.

At this typed signature, necessary agreement plus explicit unreceived primitive cognition and peer-independent policies is a counterinterpretation to the bare inference from correlation to reciprocal efficient donation or a third efficient donor. It does not refute the source's additional classification of the actual productive exercise as requiring complete original efficacy. That bridge must still be assessed at the appropriate source scope.
