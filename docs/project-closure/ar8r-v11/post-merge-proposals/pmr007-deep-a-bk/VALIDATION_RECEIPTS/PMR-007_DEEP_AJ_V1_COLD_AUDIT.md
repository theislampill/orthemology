## Blocking findings

### AJ-F01 — a fixed point was treated as a first-person subject

Being fixed by every automorphism of the neutral structure is necessary for a
neutral invariant singleton, but it is not sufficient for de se ownership,
intentionality, or personality.  A unique structural controller can remain an
impersonal role.

### AJ-F02 — invariance, implicit determination, and formula-definability were conflated

The exact finite result should concern invariant target sets and automorphism
orbits.  First-order definability and theory-level implicit definability need
separate scope guards.

### AJ-F03 — the neutral signature was underspecified

The theorem is only as strong as the complete declared reduct.  Every
load-bearing observation, intervention, episode, history, and source coordinate
must either be included or explicitly excluded.

### AJ-F04 — de se indexicality was collapsed into a self-model token

A token representing a body, controller, or system as `self` can be manipulated
by an impersonal reflexive controller.  Third-person self-representation does
not establish first-person ownership.

### AJ-F05 — one episode center was overread as stable personality

Even a formally centered episode does not identify one numerically stable
subject across episodes without lineage, memory, transition, and ownership
stability premises.

### AJ-F06 — common carrier and intentional owner were conflated

Co-location of uptake, selection, and execution in one structural bearer is not
intentional ownership.  Deep M, AA, AB, AD, AF, and AG already preserve this
firewall.

### AJ-F07 — source co-predication was not authority-typed

The Asfahani translation directly co-predicates knowing, choosing, willing, and
speaking of one source referent.  This may restrict a Track-N source-expanded
class; it is not a neutral H8 derivation.

### AJ-F08 — metaphysical possibility was overread from formal expansions

Personal and impersonal expansions establish logical nonentailment in the
declared class.  They do not establish equal metaphysical possibility,
probability, or realizability.

### AJ-F09 — consciousness, phenomenality, and Wisdom needed explicit firewalls

A formal center/owner relation does not by itself establish phenomenal
consciousness, moral personhood, stable Wisdom, or Speech.

### AJ-F10 — theorem-family and novelty ceilings were missing

The orbit obstruction is a standard automorphism-invariance fact and a central
application of the existing reduct/fibre family.  General mathematical novelty
is zero.

### AJ-F11 — implementation and empirical nontransfer were incomplete

OSM trajectories, daee control flow, agent self-models, and communication
protocols can instantiate neutral roles without establishing first-person
subjecthood.

### AJ-F12 — the positive bridge needed to be a typed contract, not a proof

The repaired packet may state the missing H8 premises and their formal
sufficiency only as a transparent classification contract.  It must not claim
to derive those premises from intellect alone.
