## Begin with the question, not the archive

Orthemology's [main exposition][main] distinguishes a concrete case, the repeatable state-type used to describe it, and the governing standard bound to that case. Its question is not only whether a conclusion is correct, but whether the reasoning and handling that produced it were adequate under the declared analysis. The [formal reference](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#formal-reference) develops the framework and a separate multi-actor component.

The wider research cannot be reduced to an audit checklist. The research includes an affirmative account of recognition and correction, ordinary tensor-moment theorems, differential geometry, quantitative coordination limits and a connection between linguistic interpretation and empirical feasibility. These have different hypotheses and source roles. Reading one does not confer acceptance on all the others.

## Recognition, sound cognition and correction

**Question:** How can a person recognise something correctly without first proving that every cognitive faculty is reliable, and what can reasoning contribute when recognition is obstructed?

The [Sound Reason substrate][sound] offers a positive answer. Its [basic-cognition account][sound-basic] and [proper-function account][sound-function] ground knowledge in truth-directed faculties operating in an appropriate environment, without requiring first-person access to an endlessly prior proof of their reliability. Its [corrective-reasoning account][sound-correction] gives argument a constructive role: clarify, remind and remove obstructions to recognition.

In its [broader use of **tawātur**][sound-tawatur], diverse, widespread and non-collusive concordance of rational and fiṭrī deliverances vindicates cognitive functioning itself. That is a substantive epistemological thesis, not merely a count of copied reports. The source argues it within its own account; this guide does not convert that account’s claims about all cultures or unimpaired cognisers into a newly verified empirical survey.

The [concrete/sound distinction note][concrete] contributes a different precision: actual instantiation is not the same predicate as epistemic soundness, and a correct output is not sufficient for an adequate truth-linked pathway. The [later retained-passage qualification][retained] keeps affirmative recognition and foundational appraisals while separating them from infallibility in every exercise and from additional productive-source premises.

**What the connection contributes:** recognition, cognitive vindication and correction form a substantive epistemic account. A source-dependence graph alone does not supply that whole account. These texts' philosophical assertions, source qualifications and stated premises remain their own; this reader layer neither authenticates all underlying primary passages nor diagnoses individuals as cognitively impaired.

### A positive foundational route

The [retained-passage report, section 2][retained-productive], explains a productive-adequacy argument: if the existence and productive actuality of an entire field are received, merely collecting its members—even without a first temporal member—does not supply an unreceived productive basis. The target is the wholly received field, not a fallacious inference that a cooperating whole must be as ineffective as each participant. The same report expressly allows real contributions and gains from cooperation.

This is the report’s affirmative source-bound route, not a new proof issued by this guide. Its premises concern actual receipt and sufficient production; their warranted application matters. The report also distinguishes a route whose bare conclusion is a Necessary Being from the later identification of the actual common Creator. Its later positive appraisals remain at their recorded, defeasible scopes; this distinction does not reset them to a blanket unresolved status. Nor does recognition have to wait for every person to traverse this particular proof.

Read the [three related works](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#recognition-and-correction), then the [remaining interpretation questions](CURRENT-FRONTIERS.md#recognition-and-foundational-application).

## Tensor histories and information geometry

**Question:** What information must a representation retain for a declared dynamical or geometric operation to remain correct?

The [tensor-moment work][tensor] studies full symmetric polynomial-moment records and their evolution under polynomial damping. Its [coercive-polynomial successor][coercive] gives a stronger written representability and continuation result under the stated all-polynomial square-positivity and regularity assumptions. This is actual conditional mathematics, not a proposal to give a metaphorical tensor shape to a mind. The complete path can identify an initial law even where the initial moment record alone does not identify a path.

The [complete Fisher development](../../mathematics/information-and-inquiry.md#6-the-categorical-fisher-dynamics-of-the-full-source) supplies an actual metric and gradient system on a positive joint probability law. Retaining conditional distributions and their mixture can discard source weights needed to preserve full ambient geometric observables. The [finite-sidecar corollary][sidecar] makes that loss precise on a fixed continuous audit-weight fibre. The [original finite-audit edition][fisher] remains available at its exact published revision.

**What these works contribute:** they specify different operation-preservation contracts, show when a retained representation is sufficient, and identify information that an exact supplement must retain. They do not assert that all interpretations need the strongest contract. A posterior-only task is weaker than preserving ambient Fisher norms.

The [selected results](RESULT-INDEX.md#tensor-moments) state the source hypotheses and distinguish the full moment path, finite records, ambient geometry and proposed noetic transfer.

## Coordination and information requirements

**Question:** How much information must a finite coordination record carry to explain sustained capture against increasingly dispersed noise?

For a fixed horizon, the [bounded coordination paper][coordination] gives an exact finite-alphabet Gaussian capture bound and a matching construction. Under its complete-mediation assumption, maintaining a fixed positive capture probability needs a mediator alphabet growing at least on the order of the square root of the horizon.

The point is not to count the bytes in a controller program. A program description or instantaneous readout need not include the entire mediator: side information and transcripts can matter. See the [finite-mediator result](RESULT-INDEX.md#finite-mediator) and its [application boundary](APPLICATIONS.md#coordination-records).

## A connection across research avenues

**Question:** Why is combining separately admissible answers sometimes an invalid way to construct a whole answer?

The [occurrence-feasibility join][join] connects **avenue 11, occurrence-sensitive language**, with **avenue 12, empirical and specialist validation**. In the selected linguistic records, complete source-relative interpretations form a restricted relation; multiplying the coordinate marginals creates unattested complete readings. In the missing-outcome problem, individually possible coordinate values need not form a jointly feasible endpoint.

**The common contribution is to retain the admissible joint relation before projection or optimisation.** A Cartesian relaxation may be useful as an explicitly labelled outer bound, but its extrema need not be attainable. The linguistic and statistical applications still need their own meanings, witnesses and justified feasible sets. One does not establish the other's empirical dependence or interpretative truth.

Follow the [worked comparison](APPLICATIONS.md#joint-feasibility-across-avenues) or the [underlying works](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#occurrence-and-feasible-completion).

## From general proof limits to a complete restricted service

**Question:** Which exact representation makes identity verification both semantically adequate and executable?

The [Fifteenth sequence](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#six-papers-in-the-fifteenth-mathematical-compendium) connects the earlier identity distinctions to exact classification and constructive algorithms. Certified Boolean endpoints have finite negative witnesses but no universal computable distinguishing-input bound; bare syntax adds a separate typability problem. Yet a declared finite grammar of natural arithmetic and zero tests admits a complete mask/coefficient checker and finite certificates, with an actual recorded Lean semantic-completeness theorem. A general obstruction and a restricted positive construction can therefore both hold.

This connects **avenue 8's semantic fidelity, avenue 9's exact calculus and avenue 10's effective procedures**. The contribution is to specify the service: endpoint syntax, certificate format, semantic relation and admitted expression grammar must match. Extending zero tests to equality tests changes the general problem again, while a one-variable subcase remains decidable. A finite Python/Lean comparison helps qualify an implementation but does not identify two wire formats or prove a parser. The [scoped result sequence](RESULT-INDEX.md#fifteenth-identity-classifications-and-constructive-subcases) keeps those distinctions and the positive algorithms visible.

## Collective competence and discriminating observations

**Question:** What additional observation could distinguish genuine use of two evidence inputs from a successful answer that used only one?

The [collective-observation paper](PAPER-CATALOGUE.md#what-final-answers-can-identify) supplies a concrete connection between **avenue 6's warranted collective diagnosis and avenue 12's empirical validation**. Two constructed mechanisms can have the same whole final-answer law and different intervention behaviour. A linked passive observation can separate their laws when the required conditioning, freshness and error model hold; final answers alone cannot supply that distinction for the matched pair.

The positive contribution is an observation contract and statistical decision services, not just a warning about underdetermination. Known separation supports finite error bounds; without it, a one-sided sequential detector has a different lawful stopping promise. The [worked applications](APPLICATIONS.md#designing-a-collective-observation-without-claiming-attribution) explain the role of the extra observation and the no-null-answer boundary. Applying the mathematics to actual groups still requires a warranted model and observation design; that bridge is not supplied by the group's success rate.

## Restoration when the correct standard is itself uncertain

AR8R supplies a useful connection between source interpretation, norm selection and temporal control. The [PMR-001 source finding][c03-ar8r-pmr-fitrah] therefore distinguishes disposition, recognition, articulated belief and assent. The [Specialist B analysis][c03-ar8r-spb4] adds rival causal explanations such as memorisation, compliance and temporary suppression of a defeater. Those distinctions determine what an application must represent and measure.

[Deep CQ's robust-viability proposal][c03-ar8r-cq] gives a finite mathematical form to another difficulty: the operative standard may be uncertain. Let `S` be a finite state set and `H` a nonempty finite family of candidate standards. Each `h` specifies a target region `T_h`, eligible actions `A_h(s)` and deterministic transitions `F_h(s,a)`. Define the common target and common actions by intersection. A common action is usable only if every currently retained standard permits it. For a region `X`, write

\[
\operatorname{Pre}_H(X)=\{s:\exists a\in\bigcap_{h\in H}A_h(s)\;\forall h\in H,\;F_h(s,a)\in X\}.
\]

The greatest fixed point

\[
V_H=\nu X\left[\left(\bigcap_{h\in H}T_h\right)\cap\operatorname{Pre}_H(X)\right]
\]

is the region from which one memoryless common policy can remain in every target indefinitely. The least fixed point

\[
W_H=\mu Z\,[V_H\cup\operatorname{Pre}_H(Z)]
\]

is the region from which such a policy can force finite entry into that viable region. A finite rank supplies the entry argument. These are claims about arbitrary switching between the retained standards, with the current state observable; they are stronger requirements than choosing a separate successful policy for each fixed standard. If two standards permit disjoint action sets, each can be individually workable while no common action exists.

[Deep CR's source-restriction proposal][c03-ar8r-cr] supplies the connecting result. Suppose independently justified source constraints reduce `H` to a **nonempty** `H′`, and every retained standard keeps its original targets, actions and transitions. Fewer standards weaken the universal requirements: common actions and common target regions can expand, and `V_H ⊆ V_H′` and `W_H ⊆ W_H′`. This makes explicit one way better source discrimination could increase the available restoration policy. The theorem does not prove that the source restriction is true or that a particular human being instantiates the finite model.
