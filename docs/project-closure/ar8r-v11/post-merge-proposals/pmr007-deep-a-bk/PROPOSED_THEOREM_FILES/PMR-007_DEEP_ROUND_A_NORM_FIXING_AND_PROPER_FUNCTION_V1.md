## 2. Typed norm-fixer data

For a fixed binary target proposition, let:

- `W` be a finite declared world class;
- `T:W->{0,1}` be its independently fixed truth-value map;
- `D_h:W->P_h` be the complete profile admitted by horn/account `h` as relevant
  to fixing the standard;
- `F_h:P_h->{0,1}` be a deterministic standard selected only from that profile.

Call `h` **truth-complete for T** exactly when some `F_h` satisfies

```text
F_h(D_h(w)) = T(w) for every w in W.
```

This is a target-relative semantic condition. It is not warrant, access,
reliability, proper operation, or praiseworthy teleology.

## 3. NFG-1 — truth-link factorization theorem

### Theorem

For the declared finite deterministic setting, a horn/account profile `D_h` is
truth-complete for `T` iff `T` is constant on every fibre of `D_h`:

```text
D_h(w)=D_h(w')  implies  T(w)=T(w').
```

### Proof

If `F_h o D_h = T`, equal profiles receive the same `F_h` value and therefore
have equal truth values. Conversely, if `T` is constant on each attained fibre,
define `F_h(p)` to be that common value for every attained profile `p`; extend
arbitrarily off the attained image. Then `F_h o D_h=T`. QED.

### Proper-function consequence

If two worlds agree on every fact admitted by a proposed function-fixing account
but require opposite truth-directed corrections, that account alone cannot fix
the truth-linked epistemic target across those worlds. A function account may
still fix a biological, artifact, organizational, practice, or contract-relative
function. What fails is the stronger transfer to objective epistemic proper
function.

This is a direct application of the established profile/fibre mechanism. No new
general factorization theorem or novelty credit is claimed.

## 4. Four noninterchangeable levels

```text
L0 descriptive regularity:
  frequent or stable behavior;

L1 function fixation:
  a declared causal-role, organizational, selected-effect, design, practice,
  objective, or source relation fixes what counts as performance/malfunction;

L2 truth-linked epistemic function:
  the function-fixing relation is independently connected to truth across the
  relevant alternatives and environment;

L3 warrant or proper noetic restoration:
  the mechanism operates appropriately in a suitable environment with the
  relevant reliability/probability, defeater, provenance, and uptake conditions.
```

No lower level entails a higher one without an explicit bridge. Candidate E
establishes pressure against token-autonomous authority; it does not by itself
establish L1, L2, or L3.

## 5. Horn-by-horn discrimination

### 5.1 Retained meta-standard

A retained amendment or corrigibility rule fixes which revisions are authorized.
It can explain self-amendment while remaining arbitrary or harmful. Two worlds
may share the same rule and revision history while the installed target is true
in one and false in the other.

```text
fixes: procedural authorization
truth link: not entailed
proper function: contract-relative unless a further function-fixer is supplied
strongest conclusion: SELF_AMENDMENT_OR_META_RULE_RELATIVE_CORRECTION
```

### 5.2 Trans-state or type-level standard

This horn must be subdivided.

- **Constitutive truth norm.** Truth itself fixes semantic correctness. This
  supplies L2 for the target by premise, but not a mechanism's access,
  reliability, proper operation, environment, or warrant.
- **Selected effect.** Selection history fixes a function and malfunction, but
  selection for deception or nontruth-directed success blocks L2.
- **Causal role/organization.** A role in a containing capacity or self-maintaining
  organization fixes L1 relative to that system. A stable harmful organization
  blocks objective fittingness.
- **Design/intention.** A design plan can fix artifact function. A malicious or
  incompetent designer blocks normative propriety; truth direction and good end
  remain additional.
- **Practice-relative standard.** A practice can correct tokens and revise rules.
  It remains practice-relative unless a truth/fittingness bridge is defended.
- **Fiṭrah-oriented standard.** Within the declared Atharī/Taymiyyan source frame,
  fiṭrah, soundness/corruption, reason, and authentic revelation can fix a
  school-internal proper-function account. The source premises do not migrate
  into a neutral world-directed theorem without independent source and bridge
  support.

### 5.3 Holistic normative fixed point

A unique global equilibrium may fix a trans-token standard. Coherence alone is
not truth linkage: two internally coherent equilibria can share every local
success and stability measure while assigning opposite target values. Uniqueness
also does not make the unique equilibrium true without an independent
correspondence or fittingness premise.

```text
fixes: global coherence/equilibrium when specified
truth link: requires more than coherence and uniqueness
proper function: holistic-relative unless truth/fittingness bridge is supplied
```

### 5.4 Primitive normative fact

A primitive fact may directly state the correct standard. If it includes the
truth-linked target, it can satisfy L2 by stipulation. It does not explain why
the norm obtains, supply teleology, locate a bearer, or establish Wisdom.

```text
logical viability: survives
explanatory stopping point: primitive
personal/teleological consequence: none
```

### 5.5 Error theory or relativization

This horn denies the objective target or reduces correction to contract,
preference, practice, or description. It is incompatible with the existence
premise of globally objective radical correction but is not refuted by the
conditional Candidate E theorem.
