## 2. Typed ladder

Use different sorts for content, bearer, episode, token, context, and source receipt. Define:

```text
ART(c): c is determinate/articulable;
MENT(g,c): g understands c;
CAP(g,c): g can intentionally express c;
ACT(g,c,e): g performs a speech act with c in episode e;
OWN(g,c): c is attributed to or originates from g in the relevant source sense;
EXPR(t,c,k): created token t expresses c under context k;
PROV(e,t): t descends from episode e through the declared provenance chain;
FID(t,c,k): t preserves the declared content target under k;
AUTH(g,e,t,k): source identity, episode, token, version, and authority are authenticated;
UNCR_N(c): Track-N source predicate that c is uncreated speech;
REV_N(g,c,t,k): Track-N revelational identification.
```

No identity between a token and a content is licensed; they occupy different sorts.

## 3. Ladder nonentailments

```text
ART -> MENT:
not entailed; abstract/brute semantic order survives.

MENT -> CAP:
not entailed; an understanding spectator may lack communicative capacity.

CAP -> ACT:
not entailed; a capable bearer may remain silent.

ACT -> OWN:
not entailed; quotation, relay, compelled utterance, or replay can produce a speech act without source authorship.

ACT + EXPR -> AUTH:
not entailed; forged or misattributed tokens can express the same selected content.

EXPR -> token/content identity:
ill-typed and not entailed.

FID for one target -> total content identity:
not entailed; translation and projection preserve only declared coordinates.

AUTH + FID -> UNCR_N:
not entailed neutrally; uncreated status is a source-theological predicate.

ACT -> REV_N:
not entailed; ordinary or nonrevelatory speech is possible.
```
