# Knowing production and voluntary determination

9 October 2026. Bounded source-semantic return. No protected edits, integration, closure, numerical recalibration, or historical research-duration claim.

## Result

**The involuntary-bodily-process example is inadequate as a counterexample to the source's strongest original-agent argument.** Awareness that something happens in one's body does not establish that the same knower originally produces that occurrence through the relevant cognition. The source explicitly distinguishes productive/operative knowledge from knowledge merely conforming to its object. That distinction was not invented to rescue the implication.

**Nevertheless, the operative-to-voluntary transition remains substantive.** Complete knowledge of one's own productive reality, knowledge causally relevant to an effect, and practically determining that effect are distinguishable claims. Darʾ does not establish their identity merely by its contraposition discussion. The source itself records the stronger intellectual-production rival, so replacing bodily awareness with operative cognition does not end the dispute.

The prior report already presented the bodily example as a semantic caution, not an actual countermodel. Preserve that limited warning; do not use it to defeat the full argument. The earlier `particular-agency-bridge-20261009/REPORT.md` already owns the substantive basing distinction, the operative/object-conforming source control through Bayān, and the positive practical-perfection appraisal. The present increment is principally **direct Darʾ 10:112 recovery, print confirmation of the 144–145 wording, and resolution of the 147 sun negation**, together with their application to the proposed bodily objection. It is not another independent agency proof. **The earned affirmative appraisal of informed, effective, intrinsically wise agency survives.** A rival's describability does not establish possibility, actuality, equal credibility, or defeat of cumulative perfection reasoning.

## 1. What the Arabic actually distinguishes

### A. The operative distinction is explicit before the disputed inference

Darʾ **10:112–113**, retained electronic `dar017.txt` L627–644, begins with al-Ṭūsī's exposition and then Ibn Taymiyya's reply. The reply distinguishes:

- Knowledge of the source's own self, which does not produce that self.
- Knowledge relevant to creating effects, which has an operative role and is a condition of their existence.
- Knowledge of something whose existence does not require the knower's cognition, illustrated by our knowledge of God, angels, prophets, heaven and earth.

The operative category is introduced as **knowledge of what the knower intends to do**. Mere cognition is then expressly denied to be creation without power and will. The distinction has substantive examples and a stated causal role, so treating every occurrence of *ʿilm* as detached propositional awareness loses context. But inserting the intention already present in this category into the antecedent of a proof of intention would be circular. An independently stated causal-condition version of operative knowledge is available; whether it invariably entails voluntary determination is the issue.

Fresh print scan inspection confirms these paragraphs at 10:112–113. [Electronic 10:112](https://shamela.ws/book/21506/3824), [volume scan](https://archive.org/download/WAQ44998/dtan10.pdf).

### B. The Asfahani corroboration is real, but its textual layer matters

The Dār al-Minhāj Asfahani scan, main **721–722 / PDF 821–822**, supplies the same distinction: knowledge of an independently existing object does not produce it; knowledge involved in our activity conditions its production; God's knowledge of creation does not replace power and will; self-knowledge does not produce the self.

This is a **long supplementary passage in the apparatus**, reported by the editor as unique to witness **ṭ**, whose own pages are 294–296. It is not the uninterrupted main body on 721–722. The editor records lacunae and proposes corrections. The central distinction is legible, but the damaged closing clauses are not silently reconstructed. PDF 820–823 were viewed to establish the layer and context. This corroborates already indexed A21; it is not a new independent doctrine or first reading of that work. [Identified scan](https://d1.islamhouse.com/data/ar/ih_books/single_01/ar_Explaining_Alasbahaneia.pdf).

### C. The self-knowledge route does not already identify cognition with production

Darʾ L643–644 explicitly continues the self-knowledge-to-effects argument even on an opponent's denial of choice, while rejecting that denial separately. L672–700 distinguish complete cognition from a person's partial apprehension: knowing the actual productive self fully includes its intrinsic qualifications and the effects entailed by its complete productive reality. L704–705 separately invoke self-knowledge and willing as routes.

L707–741 then expressly deny that divine knowledge of one item must efficiently produce another cognition, or that God must infer an initially unknown result. The argument concerns necessary accompaniment and our inference between the truths. It therefore offers a substantial account of complete self-cognition, **not** a manufacture of world-events from our inference that they are known.

The implication from complete self-knowledge to knowledge of effects can be granted without granting that cognition alone is their productive cause. At 10:146 the established self-knowledge premise remains explicit. The subsequent perfection discussion, L825–868, supplies positive reasons to ascribe knowledge; that support must retain its own credit rather than be relabelled as the result of the will argument.

## 2. The local logical transition is verified in print

At **10:144**, Ibn Taymiyya first approves the claim that in a nonvoluntary natural action the agent's knowledge **does not follow**. The next paragraph uses the stronger claim that nonvoluntary action **entails the absence** of knowledge. Printed **10:145** repeats the stronger exclusion and derives mutual implication. Fresh scan pages 144–147 confirm the wording; it is not merely an OCR or web-extraction artifact.

The two short printed formulations are:

- “لم يلزم كون الفاعل عالما”
- “غير الإرادي مستلزم لنفيه”

The first is non-entailment of knowledge; the second is entailment of its negation. The latter does not follow from the former by contraposition. If the stronger exclusion is independently true of the relevant kind of original productive action, the ensuing contraposition is valid. What the passage cannot do is establish that exclusion solely by repeating the weaker non-entailment.

This does **not** justify declaring the whole argument a converse fallacy. The text asserts the stronger premise, refers back to its wider account, and operates with a natural/voluntary distinction informed by knowledge's productive role. The correct criticism is local: the stronger premise needs that substantive warrant. Its domain cannot silently expand to every act or bodily process of every knowing creature. [144](https://shamela.ws/book/21506/3856), [145](https://shamela.ws/book/21506/3857), [146](https://shamela.ws/book/21506/3858).

There is also a newly resolved transcription defect. Printed **10:147** says the sun **is not** a complete productive cause of light. The retained electronic L808 omits the negation. The source's intended contrast is a dependent contributing condition versus the complete original producer, followed by the conditional claim that a genuinely self-knowing producer would know its product. This is a meaningful answer to transferring the sun analogy wholesale. Its old physical explanation is not independently validated here.

## 3. The strongest knowing rival is already present in the source

### Reported intellectual production, not an invented arbitrary predicate

At printed **10:138**, within **al-Ghazālī's quoted discussion**, an interlocutor proposes that the total order proceeds because it is intellectually represented, and would not proceed without that knowledge. This is a genuinely operative-intellect proposal, not awareness added to an independently operating natural mechanism. It is followed by the objection that other philosophers posit natural necessity without knowledge.

This is a verified **reported rival in Darʾ**, not a fresh direct verification of Ibn Sīnā's entire doctrine. The subsequent criticism of knowledge restricted to universals and of an unchanged eternally complete cause should not be suppressed: those are liabilities for that historically reported package. A strengthened hypothetical rival can grant exact particular knowledge and suitable intrinsic successive activity, but that is our diagnostic strengthening, not a claim that the quoted philosopher already held it.

### The strongest local reply

At **10:141–143**, **Ibn Rushd is being quoted**. He rejects identifying divine willing with needy human acquisition or being passively completed by an external desired object. He argues that cognition can concern both opposed alternatives whereas only one is produced; something determining beyond cognition merely as cognition must therefore be acknowledged. He calls that attribute will. He can also speak of willing necessarily through knowledge, so necessity by itself is not the opposed category.

Ibn Taymiyya's own response begins at **10:143**. He corrects Ibn Rushd's blanket attribution to philosophers, credits those who defend knowledge and will, and then offers his own 144–147 reasoning. Neither the claim of strict homonymy between human and divine will nor every detail of Ibn Rushd's characterization should automatically be attributed to Ibn Taymiyya.

The source's best positive point is **the difference between cognitive disclosure and practical specification**. Knowing mutually incompatible possibilities does not by itself explain the exercise producing this one. Corresponding truly to an already actual outcome is likewise not an independent production of it. That is real explanatory content, not merely renaming ignorance as nature.

### Where the strengthened rival must engage

A serious rival must explain why a particular representational or evaluative aspect of its complete intellect is productively determinative while its cognition of alternatives is not. It cannot merely append the fact that the actual output is known, or say that the intrinsic structure is whatever produces that output. It must provide a warranted productive interpretation of the relevant differentiation.

Still, an additional differentiating ground is not **automatically** a demonstrated volitional attribute. A rival may propose that intrinsic intellectual structure itself makes one content operative without an additional practical determination. The source challenges the adequacy of that proposal; the bare two-opposites inference does not separately exclude every such intellectual architecture. Knowledge of which possibility occurs and cognition's specifying its occurrence also must not be conflated.

Two cases must be kept apart:

1. If the rival denies only uncertainty, needy desire, deliberation, or a contrary outcome under the identical fully completed determination, it has not yet denied the source's intended voluntary agency.
2. If it denies genuinely source-owned practical determination altogether, while conceding causally operative cognition, it contests a substantive knowledge/power/will and practical-perfection account. It is not disposed of by defining operative knowledge as already chosen production.

There is a genuine convergence case between those descriptions: **if the proposed intrinsic cognitive orientation actually directs production for the source's apprehended fitting end, it may already concede the source's non-needy will**, despite denying a further separate executive intermediary. That is a reason to ask what work the orientation performs, rather than insist on the preferred word. Conversely, a law whose output an observer describes as directed has not thereby been shown to involve apprehension or practical ownership. A causal cognitive condition, an actual practical determinant, its particular exercise, and external compulsion are four different roles. None should be substituted for another.

The already completed particular-agency bridge and the O'Connor comparisons preserve these boundaries. The simplicity comparison in particular acknowledges purposive original production while testing the stronger need for a distinct intrinsic executive intention. This return does not reclassify that expressly purposive rival as nonvoluntary, or treat its previously discussed intermediary dispute as a new result.

## 4. What supplies positive warrant beyond the terminology

The cumulative source answer is richer than the narrow 144–145 exchange:

- The complete self-knowledge account rules out treating the original as unaware of its own real productive actuality.
- The operative/object-conforming distinction prevents factive awareness from being mistaken for complete efficacy.
- Particular-production arguments (Darʾ L923–937, L1023–1028) require knowledge adequate to actual particulars, not merely a generic representation that fits indefinitely many realizations.
- The criticism of an eternally unchanged complete cause (L1041–1053) addresses a particular necessitarian package's account of changing effects. It does not prohibit beginningless successive original activity.
- Akmaliyya's already studied comparison, canonical L035–046, L081–090, L098–109 and L235–240, argues for eligible intrinsic cognitive and practical perfections. It ranks informed discrimination and effective self-directed agency above incapacity or practical indifference. This is a separately supported comparative judgement, not a definition of *original* that excludes the rival by fiat.

The prior Bayān return is especially relevant: its L0076–0077 distinguishes cognitive correspondence, the intended end, desire, means and willing the means; L0091 refuses to infer a disputed attribute merely from an honorific; L0096–0103 compares purposive beneficence with merely beneficial output while separating independent reward-seeking from intrinsically valuing the good. The retained selections were reread here. This is inherited substantive support for the practical bridge, with its original limits intact, rather than an argument missing until this operation.

Fresh targeted Asfahani scan checks at main **403–405 and 408 / PDF 503–505 and 508** corroborate the inherited modal safeguard. Full power and decisive will entail the effect; necessity through the qualified source's own will and power is compatible with choice. An alternative can be within power and possible in itself while incompatible with the wisdom of the actual willing. These pages do not establish one uniquely necessary total history or libertarian alternatives under every same condition.

**Appraisal:** these distinctions give substantive, defeasible support to the affirmative practical-agency account. A source defender need not deduce all substantive metaphysics from school-neutral predicates before it can warrant belief. The narrowed local gap does not erase the already earned knowledge, pure-perfection and practical-wisdom results. Conversely, the bridge is not made deductively universal simply by putting *operative* before *knowledge*. The unresolved challenge concerns whether fully original intellectual productivity without genuine practical self-determination is an eligible, adequately explained actuality. This operation supplies neither an actual instance nor an independent possibility proof for it.

## 5. Typing controls and stopping point

- Keep the same bearer, act and product fixed. The person's cognition of a bodily event is not already that event's complete original production.
- Keep the cognition-token, the voluntary-act token and the external product numerically distinct unless an independently supported identity is given. Shared content, simultaneity or necessary accompaniment supplies none of those identities.
- The standing perfection of knowledge or will is not one eternally fixed particular cognition or act. Exact knowledge of a particular effect does not settle every theory of cognitive succession.
- No motion from an epistemic condition to an efficient cause is licensed merely by the word *condition*. Nor does a genuine operative role become mere accompaniment simply because divine attributes are inseparable.
- The original/creature contrast must concern independently established productive completeness and nonreceipt, not an ad hoc exemption from an inconvenient counterexample.

The bounded discriminator is answered. The bodily example should remain only a warning against an unrestricted awareness-to-will generalization. The source explicitly recognizes stronger knowing production, and its strongest reply is a substantive account of practical determination joined to pure perfection. No new general attributes campaign is warranted by this result. Sources, exact scopes, scan controls, input hashes and preserved defects are recorded separately; earlier work remains unmodified.
