# External termination and complete actual origination

## Refined result

The strongest source-unity argument does not require a separate object called a passive reception mode. It can concern **B actually exercising productive efficacy in x's origination**. If A's claim is complete ultimate provision of that whole actual origination, B's real exercised efficacy is within A's promised scope. If the efficacy is unborrowed from every other source, A cannot provide it as a distinct source. The retained unborrowed-source argument applies without identifying B's intrinsic act with a created external entity.

The remaining premise is therefore more precise than “modes must be real.” It is **closure of the complete-source target under the actual productive occurrences by which its dependents receive being**. Denying a separate mode-object does not by itself defeat that premise. Conversely, an act's having an external terminus does not by itself establish it. The passage from individually sufficient production of every dependent endpoint to complete provision of their whole actual origination is still substantive.

This is a representation-neutral refinement of the third-tranche productive-mode route, not a new mathematical uniqueness theorem. It identifies what a primitive-relation rival must really contest and why an intrinsic-act/external-effect distinction need not end the positive inquiry.

## 1. The exact surviving overdetermination proposal

Let x be the very same numerical dependent endpoint. Let A and B be distinct alleged original sources, each with actual, intrinsically possessed productive power. Let a_A and a_B be distinct actual exercises. The rival says that each exercise sufficiently produces x, while neither source supplies the other's exercise. It need not claim that the two exercises are numerically one. It can also distinguish actual causal production from counterfactual indispensability.

This proposal is not yet shown metaphysically possible. But neither has it been shown contradictory simply by observing two incoming arrows, or by comparing it with ordinary partial cooperation. It is stronger than two sources who each supply half a missing prerequisite. It grants that neither source's standing power is conferred by the other.

Now ask exactly what each has promised to explain:

- x's existence and intrinsic qualitative condition
- each dependent endpoint in a wider field, considered in that same way
- the whole actual origination of those endpoints, including which real exercises do the productive work

The third is an enlarged explanatory target even if it introduces no enlarged inventory of substances. The enlargement is legitimate when the question is complete actual sourcehood. It cannot be obtained merely by using the same word “complete” for the first two targets.

## 2. What external termination actually establishes

An actual transitive productive exercise by B must have a real effect. In the hypothesis, its external terminus is x. This establishes that B really produces x; it excludes a merely imagined act, an inert disposition, or a label for an uninstantiated response law.

It also makes B indispensable to **this being a case of B's actual production**. There cannot be actual receiving-from-B without B's productive exercise in that role. This is a meaningful fact about the admitted productive relation, not a demand that B's name appear in a sentence.

It does not yet establish that B is indispensable to x's numerical existence simpliciter. The overdetermination proposal is precisely a denial of that further step. Nor does external termination establish that a_B is an externally received entity. In an Athari interpretation, creating and the creature are different, and a source's intrinsic exercise can terminate in a creature without becoming one. The primary distinction in MF 16:364–368 blocks that shortcut.

A useful test is to vary the proposed action while holding the endpoint fixed *within the rival's own hypothesis*. A says x could remain when B ceases its exercise. Even on that hypothesis, the actual receiving-from-B could not remain. Thus the endpoint and its B-indexed actual reception are not interchangeable inside a necessity or explanatory-dependence claim. Whether the hypothesized variation is metaphysically possible remains a separate question; the test shows what the rival means to distinguish.

## 3. The mistaken substitution that can make the proof look easier

Consider the following attempted inference:

1. B's actual production of x is required for x-as-received-from-B
2. x-as-received-from-B is just x, not a second object
3. Therefore B's actual production is required for x

The inference fails without a premise that dependence is invariant under this change of explanatory respect. The fact that there is only one concrete endpoint does not make every characterization of that endpoint interchangeable in an origin-sensitive explanation. “The circle” and “the circle's being drawn by B” do not ask the same explanatory question. No second circle is needed for that difference.

The positive argument should consequently avoid a dilemma that says either a new mode-object exists or B is indispensable to x simpliciter. There is a third option: one endpoint with a real primitive productive relation, whose enactment has a determinate source. That option preserves both actual production and the distinction of explanatory targets.

A comprehensive origins-essentialist doctrine could block the rival's endpoint variation. But it would need to establish that all the actual origination at issue belongs to x's numerical identity, rather than only to a narrower product-as-originated description. Ordinary claims that some material or parental origin is essential do not automatically establish that every redundant productive occurrence is essential. Source-origin essentialism is an additional metaphysical proposal here, not a theorem supplied by the word “token.”

## 4. A positive argument without reifying another mode

The original mode proof can be recast around actual predication rather than an added substance. The subject matter is a concrete productive occurrence, not an abstract proposition that itself acts.

1. B really acts in originating x. Its contribution is actual efficacy, rather than a merely possible route
2. B's exercise is properly unborrowed: its ultimate efficacy is B's own original exercise, not supplied by another source
3. A is alleged to be independently complete for **the whole actual productive origination of x**
4. The occurrence described in step 1 belongs to that origination. A cannot omit it while keeping the full scope of step 3
5. Complete original provision of that occurrence's productive efficacy by a distinct A would make B's exercise derivative in the tested respect
6. Therefore A and B cannot both retain the alleged independent complete-source role as distinct sources

This is the same core source-exclusion reasoning as the retained HistoryComplete/UnborrowedActs argument. The refinement is that step 4 does not need to turn the receiving relation into a separately created object. The truth that B actually produces x must be supported by B's real exercise; a nominalist about relational objects can grant that without quantifying over a third substance.

There is no transfer of a material quantity called “efficacy” from source to effect in this argument. Creation need not be modeled as pouring a common substance into x. “Provision” identifies the ultimate productive basis of the actual exercise. If A merely creates a recipient capable of receiving B's independently supplied activity, A has supplied the recipient, not the whole productive actuality of its receiving-from-B.

There is also no claim that a source needs another act to make its first act. B's exercise can be the original enactment of B's own power. The contradiction concerns a second allegedly independent source's complete provision of that same original efficacy, not self-production of an action-object.

### What a primitive-relation rival must reject

It can deny that the actual relation requires a further mode-object. That objection alone leaves steps 1–6 intact. To escape, it must additionally do at least one of the following:

- Restrict A's completeness to the endpoint, or to endpoint existence across D, rather than whole actual origination
- Deny that B's exercise is genuinely original and unborrowed in the relevant respect
- Deny that ultimate productive accounting of an occurrence includes its actual efficacy, allowing an account to leave that efficacy independently furnished
- Deny the complete-source claim itself and offer irreducibly plural origination

Those are substantive rival positions. The first is a scope restriction; the last challenges individual completeness; neither is settled by mere realism or nominalism about modes.

## 5. The exact bridge from a dependent field

Suppose an earlier argument establishes that A independently produces every externally received endpoint in D. That claim alone does not imply step 3 above. The added bridge is:

**Origin closure:** where an actual member of D receives being through some real productive occurrence, the claimed complete original source of D is complete for that occurrence's productive actuality, not only for the endpoint considered apart from its actual origin.

The third-tranche m_B argument defends origin closure when the rival grants a real dependent effect-side mode. A must cover that mode, and B's efficacy is indispensable to its actualization. The present formulation shows how the same demand can survive omission of a mode-object if **whole actual origination** is independently fixed as the target.

But it does not make origin closure follow from every interpretation of “A produces all D.” A competing account can say that A and B each fully produce the same dependent objects while their actual source relations are primitive and are not themselves products of the other source. That description contests origin closure. It is not a counterexample after whole-actual-origination completeness has been granted; it is a rival to the claim that endpoint production earns that stronger role.

This is the exactly located unsupported inference. We should neither conceal it nor conclude that it has no rationale. A complete account of **actual sourcehood** ought to explain what actually supplies the origin, not only why an endpoint of the given kind exists. This is a strong positive reason for choosing origin closure in the intended argument. The further task is to establish that the source arrived at from actual dependence has this complete actual-origin role, rather than infer its satisfaction from the desirability of an explanation.

## 6. Why the stronger target is source-faithful

The freshly reopened Darʾ 9:338–341 passage distinguishes actual independent whole production from cooperation, then broadens the target to receptive material, enabling powers, and impediments. Its concluding account of unrestricted lordship concerns the thing in all relevant respects. This supports treating the intended source as more than an efficient input against a free background. It does not independently prove that every adequate endpoint explanation already has universal origin closure. [Darʾ 9:338–341, hosted lines 1613–1637](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/413/1768/عود-إلى-كلام-ابن-رشد-في-«مناهج-الأدلة»-وتعليق-ابن-تيمية-عليه).

The source's initial assertion of exclusivity should not be silently substituted for its proof against a rival who contests it. Nor should the modern reconstruction weaken the claim into merely having enough power under fixed conditions. The appropriate comparative question is whether the rival's actual origination is complete in the same all-relevant-productive-respects sense, and whether that strong role is independently instantiated.

This also preserves the act/attribute distinction. Real standing attributes and real intrinsic exercises need not be manufactured by an external source. The all-respects requirement here concerns actual productive origination of the dependent target, not a rule that every attribute, relational truth, or abstract implication has to be another created effect.

## 7. The overdetermination challenge after this repair

The argument now gives a clean dilemma to the strongest plural source proposal.

If it grants individual completeness for whole actual origination, the second genuinely unborrowed actual producer is incompatible with that completeness. This does not depend on whether the rival uses separate passive modes, primitive real relations, or source-intrinsic actions with external termini.

If it grants only endpoint sufficiency, even for every dependent endpoint, it has not granted the strong source role. It may remain a serious alternative to the existence of such a role. Defeating it requires origin closure and an argument that a bearer satisfying that closure exists. Describing it as an incomplete *ultimate* account may be justified by the defended explanatory target; that is different from proving its underlying ontology inconsistent.

If it denies actual source contribution by B and leaves only unused sufficient capacity, it is no longer a case of two actual whole producers. Necessary standing capacity is not itself actual origination. If it lets B's activity be supplied by A, it is no longer independent overdetermination at the original-source level. These exits are genuine changes of the hypothesized rival, rather than victories achieved by changing the meaning of “necessary.”

The result does not eliminate a single necessary impersonal source. Such a source can grant origin closure and still deny that its intrinsic adequate nature is cognitive. The B7/Knowledge obligation therefore remains even if this unity branch succeeds. Architectural R5 is not defeated by the conditional exclusion alone.

## 8. Credit and exact novelty

The third tranche already introduced the whole-token/endpoint distinction, the Suárez comparator, real dependent reception modes, productive-prerequisite closure, and the possibility of a primitive-relation reply. Those are explicitly retained.

This continuation contributes a more exact analysis of the remaining reply: external termination does not imply external receivedness of the source act; avoiding a mode-object does not by itself avoid actual-origin completeness; and substituting an endpoint for its source-indexed receivedness in an explanatory context is an unsupported change of respect. The representation-neutral reconstruction isolates **origin closure** as the precise remaining passage, while retaining the strong positive reason for the intended complete-source target.

No new modal satisfiability result, metaphysical-possibility certificate, source-critical edition claim, or world-directed proof of universal complete sourcehood is reported.
