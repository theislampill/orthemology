## Mathematical content and exact quantifiers

Let Cantor = ℕ → Bool. The input law is the infinite product of the Bernoulli(1/2) probability on Bool, constructed by Mathlib's Measure.infinitePi. For every finite coordinate set s and target sequence y, its cylinder mass is (1/2)^|s|.

For a switch flag h : ℕ → Bool, switched h x n copies x n when h n is true and otherwise outputs false. It is measurable. If every h n is false, its pushforward is Dirac at the all-false sequence and has singleton defect zero. If there exists N such that every n ≥ N has h n = true, each output singleton has mass bounded by (1/2)^k for every k, hence mass zero, and singleton defect is one. This eventual-tail condition is not replaced by the weaker condition that some flag is true.

A finite typed two-counter machine has n > 0 instructions, counters selected by Fin 2 and jumps in Fin n. Its instructions are halt, increment-and-jump, and zero-test/decrement with two jumps. Every bounded run is a concrete recursive computation. Halting is ∃k, the run after k inspected instructions has its halt flag true. The source compiler's stage-n flag is the run after n+1 inspected instructions, so an immediate halt is detected at stage zero. The proof establishes persistence of that flag and derives the two measure cases from the concrete machine, not from an assumed measure/halting correspondence.

The generated LOOP observer has an actual pushforward law. For every such machine M:

- defect(compiledLaw M) = 0 iff M never halts
- defect(compiledLaw M) = 1 iff M halts
- nonhalting gives the exact all-false Dirac law
- halting gives zero mass for every output singleton

## Genuine negative controls

Q8Witnesses contains concrete machines that immediately halt, increment forever, or increment then halt. The first two prove that both defect branches are inhabited.

Two deliberately incorrect compiler variants are independently tested:

1. Dropping the +1 bound misses immediate halt at stage zero
2. Testing cases against the moving pc rather than the frozen oldpc can execute an increment and the following halt within one source step

The common baseline compiles. WrongBoundMustFail.lean and WrongSnapshotMustFail.lean each exit 1 because decide +kernel proves the asserted equality false. The logs are actual false-proposition failures, not import/discovery/syntax failures. Their hashes and expected exits are in receipts/q8-negative-controls/RESULT.json. They give control evidence for the sensitive invariants, not universal proof credit beyond the already-checked theorems.

## Boundaries still open

Therefore the two measure equivalences alone are not being relabeled as a fully formal undecidability reduction. The general soft-mass formula, Π03 membership, Π03 hardness and threshold classifications remain separate CV-C developments. No canonical source adoption or programme closure is implied.
