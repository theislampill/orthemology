# Mixed single-exception source: adversarial report

Date: 2026-10-02 UTC. Fresh Seventh Orthemology v5 restart research. This report takes no credit from any previous Seventh work. It reads the two specified Sixth files as the inherited argument to be tested, not as proofs of their open premises. It changes neither file and promotes no claim into a canonical result.

## Result first

A necessary, unreceived, fully informed intelligent source can be described as genuinely excellent in every ordinary practical exercise, while knowingly issuing one harmful false safety attestation. The description can have an intelligible intrinsic motive and preserve actual derivative knowledge. It is therefore a substantially developed challenge to the inference from **original possession somewhere** to **essential practical impeccability everywhere**. It is not a demonstrated metaphysically possible necessary being.

The strongest newly checked primary passage is Hume, *Treatise* 3.1.1.22: “'Tis one thing to know virtue, and another to conform the will to it.” The surrounding argument explicitly asks for a necessary connection between the relevant cognition and will, including in the divine case. This precisely locates an inferential obligation. It does not establish that objective reasons, necessary sourcehood, and the proposed mixed motivational architecture are jointly metaphysically realizable. [RIV.H1](https://davidhume.org/texts/t/3/1/1)

Conversely, **perfect original possession**, if independently established as essential, comprehensive, nondefective responsiveness to decisive practical reasons, excludes the exception. The rival cannot grant that premise in that meaning and remain a counterexample. The dispute concerns the warrant for that stronger premise, not a contradiction concealed inside it.

A local bridge is available without settling every question of universal benevolence: an essentially nondefective truth-directed attester, correctly understanding the exact context-bound content, cannot knowingly issue an undefeatedly wrongful false certificate in that role. The remaining issue is why this source necessarily has that local integrity. Neither omniscience, original practical excellence in other cases, nor mere acceptance of an assertion's correctness norm supplies it.

## 1. The one proposition and a concrete bad event

Fix one proposition throughout:

**p: action root i remains safe for repair R in context/version c from certification through intervention.**

Let certification occur at t0 and intervention at t1. Specify c independently of p: it identifies the repair target, root identity, version, permitted input and environmental envelope, certificate meaning, intervention semantics, and the interval [t0,t1]. It must not define “valid context” as a context in which p is already true. “Safe” means that use of i for this R has the stipulated faithful-execution or independently authorized fail-silent behavior, rather than the harmful substituted act. A certificate for a different root, snapshot, version, repair, or interval does not assert this p.

The counterstory uses a **fixed already-compromised root**, not a fault that appears by changing the contract after certification. At t0, i contains a concealed deterministic branch: invocation of this exact R in c redirects the requested correction into destruction of the sound copy and its recovery log. Its ordinary diagnostic behavior remains accurate. The source knows this implementation, the recipient's response, the relevant future intervention, and the harm. It issues an authenticated, unambiguous certificate asserting p and intending reliance. At t1 the recipient performs R through i and the harmful branch executes.

Thus p was false at certification; the root was already unsafe for this repair even while benign tests passed. No equivocation between “currently passing checks” and “safe through intervention” is used. No unforeseen state change, stolen identity, accidental transmission error, alternative interpretation, quotation, or nonassertoric test explains away the attestation. The source could repair i, certify a genuinely safe root j, state not-p, or decline to certify, without comparable injury. For the normative test, none of the relevant facts supplies an undefeated justification for lying here.

This is a philosophical interpretation of the local actuation obligation, not an import of any channel theorem or a claim that its fixed-fault assumptions have been proved. The inherited dependency map distinguishes source cognition, practical integrity, truthful attestation, warranted uptake, and safe restoration precisely at V1, T0, D2, and R0. [RIV.B2, especially T7 and T9]

## 2. A motive with explanatory work, rather than a badness predicate

Call the proposed source M. Grant it necessary unreceived existence, intrinsic intelligent efficacy, complete relevant information, and no externally conferred power, information, or need for existential completion. Grant objective normative reasons, including the decisive reason not to perpetrate this betrayal. These concessions deliberately strengthen the challenge beyond denying moral reality.

M has two enduring practical orientations:

1. **Genuine concern.** It values derivative subjects' understanding, successful repair, and flourishing for their own sake. In ordinary cases, their goods supply the reasons for which it acts. It would sustain such cases even if no betrayal ever occurred. This counterfactual separates genuine concern from merely preparing useful victims.
2. **A particular malicious final aim.** M values, for its own sake, one actual episode in which a knowledgeable dependent agent's first irreversible reliance on M's freely given safety assurance becomes the means of destroying that agent's successful work. The special object is *betrayal of accomplished agency through entrusted assurance*. M's conative ordering places this episode above its otherwise effective concern when that unique relational situation arises. After its realization, no further case meets the aim's object.

The motive is not “M is malicious, so it lies.” The outcome specifies why genuine cognitive achievement, a reliable ordinary world, this certification, this root, and this timing are means to the selected end. Fooling a permanently incompetent puppet would not realize that end. Universally corrupting cognition would frustrate it. Gratuitous repetitions would not be required by the one-episode aim. The source has both a reason why this is a distinctive opportunity and a causal-explanatory account of its choice. That is explanatory differentiation, not normative vindication.

M neither believes that betrayal benefits the victim nor mistakes its motivational priority for all-things-considered objective fittingness. It understands that this is a defective exercise. The rival posits lucid disregard of a decisive reason, not ignorance of the reason. Calling this practically irrational on an objective standard is correct; the contested inference is that this defect cannot occur in an unreceived being which is genuinely excellent elsewhere.

The final aim need not be fear of losing control, frustrated appetite, a physiological reward deficit, or a demand that creatures recognize M's status. M can intend the episode while remaining existentially independent of its recipient. Its satisfaction is in an effect of its own agency. MF 8:145–147 distinguishes such originated satisfaction from completion supplied by an independent other. That text does not endorse malice; it blocks a symmetrical need objection unless a further moral asymmetry is defended. [RIV.T2](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/22/806/مسألة-حسن-إرادة-الله-لخلق-الخلق-وهل-يخلق-لعلة-أو-لغير-علة)

There is an explanatory cost. A uniquely selected betrayal orientation is a substantial intrinsic feature, not a free consequence of sourcehood. Its special object may look ad hoc against an otherwise generous character. A constitutive account of rational agency might also rule it out. Providing an intelligible final aim reduces the weakness of a bare predicate assignment; it does not erase those costs or prove this ultimate motivation possible.

## 3. Actual derivative knowledge is preserved

The ordinary world contains actual truths, cognitive subjects, truth-linked perceptual access, valid inference, and successful interventions. For example:

- A technician knows a component's actual state through a functioning state-sensitive sensor and valid independent cross-checks.
- The technician knows that a particular correction changes a demonstrated faulty state into a demonstrated sound state, having correctly understood and performed it.
- Another agent recognizes the first agent's avoidable loss as a reason for help and genuinely responds for that reason.

These are the inherited actual cognition and practical-excellence explananda, retained rather than relabeled. Their causal realization is the same kind of stable, content-sensitive organization granted to the affirmative source account. In ordinary nearby variations, the sensor and inference update with their objects; M does not arrange a chance procession of true-looking outputs. The technician's mathematical and local observational knowledge need not depend on a universal testimony guarantee from M.

At the exceptional act, the recipient's belief in p is false and hence is not knowledge. That one failure neither retroactively falsifies earlier truths nor shows that every derivative belief was unwarranted. A familiar epistemology may permit justified but false testimony-based belief; a stricter one may judge the certificate uptake unwarranted. Neither verdict by itself removes the genuine independently grounded knowledge just granted elsewhere.

A demanding global anti-skeptical theory could contest knowledge under this world description. The burden would be to show that *this local source policy*, rather than the bare imaginative availability of a bad source, defeats those actual truth-sensitive episodes. Since actual derivative knowledge is a premise of the test, the challenger cannot obtain a victory by quietly replacing it with mere behavioral success. Equally, this report does not claim to supply a complete metaphysics of cognition. It retains the genuine realization conceded to both sides and adds an explanatory exception in source testimony.

## 4. Hume: independent support and exact limits

### 4.1 Cognition and motivation are different explanatory jobs

In *Treatise* 2.3.3, Hume argues that factual and demonstrative reasoning informs an agent about objects and means, while practical impulse requires an end-directed orientation. He treats the errors relevant to his restricted use of “reason” as false suppositions and mistaken means. A destructive preference can escape those errors while remaining morally condemnable. This supports separating accurate world-modeling from the orientation that chooses what to produce. It does not make all efficient acts objectively reasonable under a richer account of practical reason. [RIV.H2](https://davidhume.org/texts/t/2/3/3)

In *Treatise* 3.1.1.22, Hume distinguishes the proposed invariance of moral relations from a necessary influence of those relations upon every will. He expressly notes the extension to deity. The dialectical relevance is unusually direct: showing a norm to be objectively cognizable does not yet demonstrate its indispensable governance of source action. His own wider denial of rationalist moral relations is **not** adopted here. The constructed rival borrows this separation while conceding the realist's reasons. It is thus not a claim that Hume himself defended the present necessary mixed source. [RIV.H1](https://davidhume.org/texts/t/3/1/1)

*Enquiry concerning the Principles of Morals*, Appendix I.11–12, contrasts factual ignorance with Nero's wrongdoing despite knowledge of relevant relations; I.18–21 distinguishes ultimate ends from reasoning about means. The Nero example supplies a human model of relevant knowledge failing to secure humane action. It does not supply omniscience, divine eligibility, freedom from all defect, or an independently existing source. Generalizing that human explanation to M requires the very metaphysical assessment still outstanding. [RIV.H3](https://davidhume.org/texts/m/app1)

### 4.2 “Fully informed all-things-considered reasons” has two readings

**Epistemic reading:** M knows every relevant fact and correctly understands the decisive balance of objective reasons. It nevertheless chooses the bad act because the malicious final orientation governs this exercise. This is logically describable without M believing both p and not-p or both that the act is fitting and that it is unfitting. Its practical failure is not a false proposition in its knowledge set.

**Normative-performance reading:** M is constituted so that every exercise appropriately answers the correctly known decisive balance. Malicious action is incompatible with that grant. No amount of appeal to Hume can preserve the exception while leaving the grant unchanged.

Intermediate motivational internalism also needs scope control. If apprehending a decisive reason necessarily gives M *some* motivation to comply, the mixed rival can retain that motivation and posit contrary dominance. To defeat it, one needs sufficient or indefeasible governance, not merely a necessary motivational contribution. If fully rational appreciation constitutively includes such governance, the affirmative position must justify why this source is fully rational in that strong practical sense; complete information alone does not secure it.

Accordingly, lucid malice is a coherent *externalist reconstruction* and a live denied bridge, not a neutral metaphysical discovery. Hume's argument gives it independent philosophical ancestry and an intelligible mechanism. It cannot certify its realization under an independently defended essence of perfect agency.

## 5. Weak and strong original possession

Let E(g,a) mean that g's exercise a responds fittingly to the decisive practical reasons relevant to it. Let O be the ordinary exercises, a* the exceptional certification, and D the relevant full domain including a*. Original possession concerns how g has a capacity or excellence; it is not by itself a quantifier over exercises.

| Grant | What it actually says | Does the single-exception rival survive? |
|---|---|---|
| Some genuine original excellence | g has E originally in at least one actual exercise | Yes. Its concern and fitting acts are genuinely its own. |
| Excellence in every ordinary case | For every actual a in O, E(g,a) | Yes, if a* is outside O. It is no rebuttal if “ordinary” is silently widened to D afterward. |
| Perfect capacity in eligible cases | g fully understands and can execute fitting responses throughout D | Yes, unless the capacity is defined or independently shown to govern every choice. Ability is not exercise. |
| All actual practical exercises impeccable | For every actual a in D, E(g,a) | No actual exception survives. This does not alone establish essential truthfulness in unactualized circumstances. |
| Essentially impeccable local domain | Necessarily, every relevant exercise in D is fitting | No exception in D survives. Exercises outside D remain unassessed. |
| Essentially comprehensive practical perfection | Necessarily, every eligible practical exercise is fitting | No mixed malicious exercise survives anywhere within the proved eligibility domain. |

A further ambiguity matters: original possession of the **perfection itself** may already mean the last two rows, whereas possession of an originally grounded **capacity for excellent exercises** means an earlier row. The rival should not be advertised as refuting the strong premise by satisfying only the weak one.

MF 6:76–79 argues from eligible creaturely perfection and original giving to source possession, then to intrinsic necessary possession. MF 6:129–130 expressly ranks discriminating wise and merciful willing above unrestricted efficacy lacking wisdom or mercy. These are substantive proposed bridges, not merely labels for omniscience or power. The rival contests their comprehensive application to source practical governance; it does not show the text intended only occasional excellence. [RIV.T1](https://quranislam.github.io/fatwa/ibn-taymiyyah/fatwa81.html)

The precise question is whether the actual derivative excellence being explained requires an original **incorruptible integrating character**, or only original capacities plus some genuinely fitting orientation and exercises. Showing that the giver cannot be wholly devoid of E is insufficient for the former. Showing that all currently actual exercises are excellent is also insufficient for the essential modality. Conversely, if the eligible pure excellence is independently identified as whole-domain indefeasible responsiveness and the original-giver completion warrant applies to that very excellence, the mixed source is excluded without an ad hoc anti-liar axiom.

### A stronger noninferiority grant and its scope

Let D_rec collect the ordinary context-types in which actual finite recipients exhibit the relevant truthful, reason-responsive excellence. The strengthened rival grants that M originally understands, can perform, and sincerely exemplifies that excellence throughout D_rec, with greater truthful competence and a stronger truth-directed orientation than any actual finite recipient. This is not mere possession of an inert capacity. M knowingly does good epistemic work for the fitting reasons. Its exceptional source-authorized safety attestation a* is an additional context outside the matched manifestation domain. The same source can have a stable policy whose unique trigger is its own first entrusted certification of that form.

The comparison should be charitable but exact. Greater information, expressive power, number of true utterances, or beneficial results cannot compensate for a lie **in the respect of practical integrity**. In a*, M really is worse than an otherwise eligible agent who honors the undefeated truth-directed reason. The rival grants that defect rather than changing the subject to its superior intelligence. The strengthened preservation grant means superiority in actual ordinary manifestations and the unmasked truth-directed disposition. It does not mean superiority in the total all-context response profile, which the rival manifestly lacks.

An original-giver principle blocks the rival if “same respect” already identifies E as an integrated character that is nondefective in every eligible attestation, or if its independent argument establishes that range. It does not block the rival merely by requiring original possession of the positive content instantiated in the finite actual truthful tokens. Those tokens instantiate fitting response here; they do not themselves instantiate essential any-context incorruptibility. A finite agent could itself later fail while its earlier excellence remains real.

This is a scope objection, not the claim that finite instances can never warrant a stronger pure-excellence principle. The proponent may argue that the limitation to D_rec belongs only to creatures, while the positive content, properly purified, is indefeasible integrated truthfulness. That would directly engage the rival. The work then lies in the purification, eligibility, and original-completion inference. It must not be supplied by silently replacing a comparison of actual recipient-given excellence with a universally quantified source manifestation guarantee.

Qāʿida fī al-maḥabba distinguishes theoretical and action-involving cognition and asserts that divine knowledge of good and bad actions entails corresponding love and hate. This makes the intended perfected cognition–orientation relation explicit. It does not provide a source-neutral proof that any intelligent necessary source must instantiate it. Further, love/hate understood as some positive/negative orientation is weaker than universally overriding effective governance; an essential connection strong enough for impeccability needs that stronger scope. [RIV.T3](https://ar.wikisource.org/wiki/جامع_الرسائل/قاعدة_في_المحبة)

### Essential disposition with an endogenous mask

Suppose truthfulness is an essentially held disposition: when M undertakes ordinary truth-directed assurance and no defeating concern intervenes, it accurately conveys what it knows. M can retain that stable disposition while an intrinsic competing final concern controls a*. Its truthful knowledge-producing activity elsewhere is genuine, and the same truthful response remains available at a*. The obstruction is an endogenous motivational mask, not an external supplier or a lost power. Necessary possession of a disposition conditional on unmasked circumstances is not necessary manifestation in masked circumstances.

This analysis depends on the disposition's actual content. If “essential truthfulness” instead means that no knowing false attestation can ever issue from M, the mask is excluded by that stronger essential property. Calling the weaker disposition a *perfect* virtue does not settle which content has been established. Nor must every theory count a person with one calculated betrayal as possessing the complete virtue of truthfulness. The rival needs only the genuinely excellent ordinary orientation granted in the weak premise; it must not claim the complete virtue under a definition it violates.

Unreceivedness rules out dependence on an external giver for the disposition. It does not, by its meaning, rule out two intrinsic practical orientations with an asymmetric priority. To exclude the proposed internal mask, the source argument must establish that intrinsic practical perfection is jointly integrated and undefectible. The present inherited framework permits real intrinsic attributes and renewed particular acts; this report does not introduce an ungranted pure-act or composition prohibition to disqualify M. An independently defended indivisible-excellence or comprehensive-governance warrant might exclude the mask. It is an additional source-nature claim, not the mere denial of borrowed existence.

## 6. The narrow truthfulness bridge

One can isolate a smaller claim than comprehensive goodness:

**L1.** A knowingly false safety attestation, undertaken as reliable truth-directed assurance and defeating the addressee's repair without an undefeated justification, is a practical defect in that communicative exercise.

**L2.** The source is essentially free of such defects in the identified role and domain.

**L3.** The source fully apprehends the exact proposition p, its truth value, its intended uptake, and its action-relevant temporal/contextual scope.

**L4.** The actual event is the source's authorized attestation of this p in that role, with the requisite reception and interpretation.

L1–L4 exclude the false-certificate event described above. If the source is fully informed and the actual attestation is genuine, p cannot be false without violating local integrity. An actual source that preserves integrity in this bad-root world would withhold p, attest not-p, or make the root safe before correctly certifying the requisite interval. It need not disclose everything, maximize welfare, eliminate all pain, or accept every finite agent's duty.

L1 has a strong independent rationale: the undertaken epistemic act purports to provide a truth-linked basis for reliance, and the source deliberately subverts that function to injure the recipient. The recipient's successful agency supplies a further recognized reason. This rationale identifies a defect; it does not render lying metaphysically impossible. A rule can govern the correctness of an act which someone nevertheless performs incorrectly. Defining “attestation” factively would conceal the work L2 must do.

For unrestricted source truthfulness, L1's “without an undefeated justification” qualification must be addressed. Sometimes a benevolent liar claims protection or another competing reason. The present counterexample deliberately removes such reasons. A proof of essential prohibition of **all** false attestations requires a stronger argument than exclusion of this gratuitous harmful lie, or a tightly defined communicative role in which deception is never justified. The exact case is therefore easier to condemn than every possible deception; it is already sufficient to test this repair warrant.

Authentication does not fill L2: it can establish that M really spoke while leaving M a liar. Knowledge of the repair target does not fill L3's action-root scope. A truthful snapshot does not fill the interval. A historical record of ordinary truthful acts can improve practical confidence in a local certificate, but does not logically convert L2 into an essential property. The inherited map already keeps these requirements apart. [RIV.B2, T0/D1/D2/R0]

### A different local route: independently known epistemic purpose

A second local bridge considers an independently identified sincere/effective epistemic purpose to make r know p through this attestation, plus full knowledge, instrumental coherence, and a settled capable exercise. This is a real alternative to deriving comprehensive moral perfection, but its formulations differ sharply.

1. **Actual achieved purpose:** If “effective” means that r has actually come to know p, then p follows from knowledge's factivity. This is sound, but it cannot establish p before that knowledge is independently established. Calling the recipient's belief knowledge solely because of this certificate would be circular.
2. **Settled governing purpose plus adequate means:** If M genuinely aims to make r know this exact p, fully understands what knowing p requires, has settled this exercise as adequate for that purpose, and is instrumentally coherent, then a known-false p cannot be the object realized through knowingly false testimony. Under these strong grants, the rival fails locally. A separate doctrine of universal benevolence is unnecessary.
3. **A benevolent standing purpose or subordinate concern:** A general aim to foster knowledge, or a genuine concern for r's knowledge outweighed here by another final aim, is compatible with the exception. Instrumental coherence of the governing betrayal plan does not guarantee successful service of every subordinate concern.
4. **An appearance or invitation:** A statement that the source intends knowledge, authentication of that statement, and ordinary knowledge-promoting history do not establish the token governing purpose. A liar can intentionally present exactly that appearance. The particular source could lie about its purpose as well as about safety.

The second bridge is not circular **as an implication**. It can become circular in its **application** if the only evidence for the factive epistemic aim is p's presumed truth, the source's assumed sincerity, or the same disputed certificate. If “sincere attestation of p” itself already means that M believes p, then full accurate information and sincerity directly exclude false p; the further purpose and capacity premises add no essential work to that inference.

One must also distinguish making r know p from making r believe p, giving r evidence concerning p, enabling r to know whether p, or sincerely wanting r eventually to learn the truth. The latter descriptions do not entail this affirmative p. Exact temporal and contextual scope remains indispensable: an aim to teach the present state of i is not yet an aim to give knowledge of safety through t1.

Independent evidence of token epistemic purpose is conceivable: demonstrable diagnostic acts, source behavior constrained by independent verification, transparent causal implementation, and coherent selection of means could support it. Such evidence need not first assume universal goodness. Whether it decisively identifies this particular intention, rather than making it more likely, depends on the evidence and available alternatives. No such evidence is supplied in the present test. Universal power would make mere ability to stage the appearance a particularly weak discriminator, although the mere possibility of staging does not erase all defeasible evidential weight.

The counterstory therefore does not refute the strong purpose bridge. It shows exactly which new grant defeats it: at a*, genuine knowledge-promotion is not M's settled governing end. Ordinary derivative knowledge and original practical excellence do not prove that it is. A warranted token-purpose bridge might establish the present certificate without establishing essential truthfulness across every other world and role; that is a legitimate narrower result if its evidence is truly independent.

## 7. What has and has not been established

- **Conceivability:** A detailed positive story is available, including standing motives, a discriminatory occasion, genuine ordinary concern, reliable derivative knowledge, and an exactly false attestation. This is stronger than an uninterpreted assignment of virtue predicates. It remains revisable if a constitutive contradiction is exposed.
- **Logical consistency:** The weak grants and the exception do not display a propositional contradiction. A formal interpretation can separate true cognition, conative priority, objective fittingness, and original capacity. This is relative consistency under those meanings, not a complete consistency proof of a theory of necessary minds. The strong essential-governance grant and the exception are inconsistent.
- **Metaphysical possibility:** Not established. Neither intelligibility, a finite model, nor Hume's account of finite motivation proves an intrinsically necessary mixed source can exist. The rival needs positive eligibility for its motivational structure and answers to original-perfection, essential-integration, and source-nature objections. The affirmative view likewise needs the disputed comprehensive/essential completion bridge rather than a definition of its preferred source.
- **Actual plausibility:** Underdetermined by the granted data. Ordinary cognitive success and concern fit both pictures; the contrived one-exception aim is an explanatory cost and has no supplied independent positive evidence. No likelihood ratio, actual source identification, or evil-god symmetry result follows. A possible hidden exception is not by itself a defeater of all ordinary knowledge.
- **Strict local conclusion:** Fully informed cognition plus original excellent exercises does not by those contents alone entail essential truthful attestation. Independently warranted essential local integrity does exclude the constructed false p. Whether sourcehood entails that integrity is the substantive remaining warrant.

## 8. Discriminating next evidence and stopping point

The strongest affirmative advance would explain why the **same pure excellence actually given to recipients** is indivisible at the source into excellent ordinary governance and one recognized defect. It must show that total essential practical integration is eligible and originally required, rather than simply assert that any defect is incompatible with “perfect.” A locally narrower advance could independently establish essential integrity in this authentic assurance role and avoid settling the entire moral cosmos.

The strongest adversarial advance would be a positive account showing the proposed intrinsic motivational architecture compatible with necessary underived intelligent sourcehood, rather than relying on our inability to find a contradiction. Hume supplies a distinction and a challenge to inference, not that account.

For actual repair, the actionable missing evidence remains exact: why this authenticated source is trustworthy about **root i, repair R, version/context c, throughout the certification-to-intervention interval**. Until such a bridge or independent certificate evidence is supplied, source knowledge and ordinary excellence leave the targeted repair warrant incomplete. No blanket skepticism, no promotion to metaphysical possibility, and no prior Seventh credit is needed for this bounded result.
