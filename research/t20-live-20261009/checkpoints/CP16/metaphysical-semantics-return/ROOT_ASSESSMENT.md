# Root assessment: source-faithful metaphysical semantics

9 October 2026. Focused T20 continuation; no final closure or integration.

## Present result

I have not found the broad collapse alleged as a risk in the inspected definitions. The original-bearer interface, nonabsorption diagnostics, qualitative-grounding reports, and recent PSR controls largely maintain their stated interpretation boundaries. This gives positive credit to work already completed without certifying all imaginable applications of those results.

The Arabic return is especially valuable because it blocks errors in both directions. Dar1:293–294 identifies the concrete thing's essence/existence without identifying knowledge, power and will. Dar1:281–282 distinguishes reciprocal concomitance from productive circles. Majmu6:97–101 treats inseparable intrinsic qualification as perfection through oneself, not external supplementation. These are substantive interpretive constraints. They do not license calling a genuinely external contribution mere concomitance.

## Consequential formal application restriction

The unrestricted total-explainer package proves that its explanatory fact has no distinct proper fact-parts. It cannot be used as a proof of divine attributelessness. That would require mapping intrinsic-attribute facts into proper parts of the explaining fact, a premise not established by attribute distinctness or inseparability. Conversely, the source doctrine alone is not a refutation of the mathematical package without that mapping. The restricted PSR repairs and the shorter self-explanation theorem have different assumption footprints and must remain separate.

This is a newly explicit transport restriction on an existing proved consequence, not a new kernel theorem and not a discovery that the earlier code secretly made attributes external agents.

## Modal evidence: a correction to the attempted correction

The initial semantic audit criticised the Schaffer appraisal too strongly. Independent comparison with the actual source and historical qualifications showed that the old report explicitly assigned only defeasible modal weight and denied a warranted two-original instance. The new audit has preserved its initial version and withdrawn the stronger charge. I agree with that revision.

The defensible wording is an apparently coherent modal candidate, rather than a causal possibility treated as already established. Intuitions and causal arguments can provide contestable prima facie evidence without amounting to independently established extramental possibility. Neither formal consistency alone nor a vivid imagined case authenticates a whole-original countermodel. Equally, lack of a conclusive proof does not make all defeasible evidence disappear.

No automatic reversal of the current common-source assessment follows. I continue provisionally to favour one actual complete common original source while withholding the stronger claim that the examined arguments exclude the strongest redundant-original alternative. This remains an assessment of disputed source/perfection/nonduplication bridges, not a certified plural-original metaphysical possibility.

## Domain and action safeguards

Different formal sorts for a bearer and intrinsic resource are not declarations of independent subsistence. If the bearer domain is widened to act-tokens, the reception and contingency premises require fresh interpretation. Uncreated does not automatically mean existentially independent, and contingent or temporally successive does not automatically mean created or externally perfected. A same-bearer attribute model does not by analogy authenticate two independently subsisting original agents.
