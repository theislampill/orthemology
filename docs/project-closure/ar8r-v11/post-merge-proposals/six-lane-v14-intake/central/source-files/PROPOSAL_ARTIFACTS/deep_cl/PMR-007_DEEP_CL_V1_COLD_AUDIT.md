## Blocking findings

### CL-F01 — registry-relative minimality was stated too generally

The two-anchor minimum holds only in the frozen registry that omits any independently eligible direct three-way anchor. A later anchor whose partition is already discrete would reduce the registry-relative cardinality to one. The theorem must state its registry and never present `2` as an architecture-independent metaphysical bridge count.

### CL-F02 — anchor cardinality is not an epistemic or evidential cost

The V1 phrase “residual bridge burden” risks treating one listed coordinate as one equally costly premise. Track-N intensional predication packages nineteen guards; common-bearer and uptake operationalizations may have different evidence costs. The formal output is a conflict-cover cardinality, not a warranted scalar burden.

### CL-F03 — target-coordinate sufficiency was conflated with measurement eligibility

`common_bearer` and `intentional_uptake` are frozen target coordinates. Their joint profile identifies U/I/P by construction, but no neutral target-blind operationalization of uptake has been supplied. The candidate must separate:

```text
formal coordinate sufficiency;
eligible neutral observation;
source-relative conditional classification;
and actual-world evidence.
```

### CL-F04 — Track-N predication cannot be treated as one unconditional anchor

The source-relative cross-cut exists only under the full Deep-CK source/world guard package. It is not an atomic neutral measurement and does not by itself establish actual-world U.

### CL-F05 — common bearer requires type discipline

The finite coordinate used here is one-world co-instantiation. It is not numerical transworld identity, unique bearerhood, derivational unity, or the intensional source bearer without additional premises.

### CL-F06 — architecture universe completeness was not established

The U/I/P class is a declared comparison surface, not an exhaustive ontology. Identifying a member of this finite class would not prove that one of the three exhausts the actual world.

### CL-F07 — theorem-family ancestry was incomplete

The general conflict-cover statement is the finite fibre-constancy/set-cover mechanism already represented by Deep BX, Deep BV/AR8R-T294, and standard partition refinement. It receives no new general theorem-origin credit.

### CL-F08 — source-compatible rivals were overcompressed

`I` and `P` are neutral or predicate-weakened controls. They are not fully source-preserving Track-N models. The repaired packet must state exactly where predicate weakening or common-bearer deletion enters.
