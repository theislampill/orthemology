Status: primary-source screen supporting a project-level contribution claim. It is not an exhaustive literature review, independent proof audit of the cited papers, or global mathematical priority verdict. No direct quotations are used.

## Bottom line

The proposed result is not a new general effect calculus, attribution semantics, query-determinacy theorem, evidence-cover principle or NP-hardness technique. The bounded increment is a source-role-specific derivation from open-fragment transport to its selected live original contexts, followed by an exact successor-access calculation and a correction about which access representation preserves interval structure. An ordinary shared-ID scope parser and conventional covering algorithms attain the same result.

The complete source-specific effect/access-geometry chain is not stated in the predecessor material inspected here; its authority-indexed cover subproblem already has a direct internal owner. That is a claim about the reviewed programme boundary, not proof that no external paper has ever combined the components. If the final source-bound case adds no application value beyond the abstract reduction, the result should be presented as a modest conditional application rather than a general research breakthrough.

## B. Open fragments and nested effects

Corin Pitcher's 2005 primary paper provides visibly pushdown expression effects for stream programs, expressly including unmatched call/return tokens and compositional input/output descriptions with lookahead. Its effect language and subject-reduction/progression treatment are substantially richer than the three-event context machine proposed here. Our finite symbolic normal form is an application-level restricted construction using this established style of reasoning. Sequential scope effects and associativity cannot serve as the stand-alone novelty claim.

## D. Paid observations, certificates and query determinacy

Charikar, Fagin, Guruswami, Kleinberg, Raghavan and Sahai's priced-information framework treats sequentially queried inputs and compares a query algorithm with sufficient information proving a function value. Its definition of a sufficient partial setting directly anticipates the information-certificate reasoning used here. Our all-positive covering argument is an elementary grouped-read special case in that established tradition. We claim neither a new decision-tree framework nor a new competitive/expected-cost result.

Inspected: primary extended-abstract PDF, introduction and sufficient-set/proof definition. Source: https://www.cs.princeton.edu/courses/archive/spr04/cos598B/bib/CharikarFGKRS.pdf. The JCSS article metadata is separately verified at https://doi.org/10.1006/jcss.2002.1828.

Koutris, Upadhyaya, Balazinska, Howe and Suciu's 2015 Query-Based Data Pricing is the closest cost comparator. It formalizes instance-based determinacy, minimizes the cost of determining view bundles, treats shared views, and proves tractable/hard pricing results. Its section 5.5 contains an explicit Set Cover reduction for a projection query. Thus minimum-cost sufficient views and arbitrary cover incidence are existing machinery, not new here. Our cost units can be reads/rounds/bytes rather than monetary prices, but that reinterpretation alone is not novelty.

## E. The exact surviving increment

A source reader's parse is not silently assumed to be the recipient's holdings. Open cuts yield explicit incoming context references; composing the supplied source-bound maps determines which original role keys the selected post-return outputs need. Shared original keys are collapsed for reacquisition cost, while same-content different-origin occurrences remain separate. A source-ordered adequate role-witness service retains convex coverage regardless of output reorderings; a genuinely different whole-card service can lose it. The read-cost theorem needs a total-decision requirement, whole-response locality and an actually realizable all-positive source family.

Those interface details are not cosmetic. Dropping them causes specific false conclusions: fictitious copying-induced hardness, an invalid lower bound defeated by a correlated outside witness, role/read queries to a still-unidentified parent, or treating a raw text interval as adequate global-force evidence. The independent design review found and required those corrections.

The claimed result should therefore be titled and scoped as **a task-relative source-context continuation and reacquisition calculation**. It supports a bounded source-attribution workflow. It is not general source authentication, translation correctness, independent testimony, new permission, numerical identity, or repeatable physical apoautosis.
