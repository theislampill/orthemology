## Current verdict

My sufficient-warrant verdict remains withholding for complete unborrowed provision by the qualified original throughout its connected field. This is a comparative philosophical judgement at a defeasible standard, not a deduction-only standard. The expanded reading gives the affirmative position more content and stronger explanatory support. The unresolved issue is still why voluntarily differentiated original provision must be an intrinsic deficiency, and why strict peer-excluding dominion is an eligible pure perfection already sufficiently warranted for the actual bearer.

I distinguish the claim that one complete common source is an attractive or preferred explanatory hypothesis from the stronger claim under review that the qualified actual original necessarily has the full source mode and therefore actually covers its whole connected field. I do not withhold merely because another arrangement can be drawn, or because a formal conditional cannot prove its premises. The counterpressure concerns the precise respect of perfection, ownership and sufficiency used by the positive argument.

## The strongest additional affirmative argument

E219–221 makes the perfection argument materially stronger than an appeal to unused capacity. Complete attributes have fitting manifestations. Real kingship requires life, power, will, knowledge, command, judgement, voluntary activity and differentiated effects. Limiting a ruler to one uniform kind of outcome can hide inability or ignorance. E221 further argues that wise purposeful agency is more praiseworthy than mere power devoid of good purpose. This is substantive evaluation, not lexical decoration.

The strongest affirmative use is that original divinity must be actually efficacious, wisely expressive and unrestricted in its rightful sourcehood. It cannot be rescued by positing a causally idle necessary object, nor by assigning it an arbitrary narrow ability while permitting derivative beings to possess relevant pure excellences that it lacks. I credit this as additional reason for the rich original-agency profile.

At e220 the text explicitly says that all conditions needed for the manifestation of kingship originate from God; consequently an independent supplier does not complete his kingship. That is a powerful coherence claim and the intended all-source thesis. It is not, at this point, an independently established reason that every relevant condition must originate from the same bearer. A reconstruction must preserve both the affirmative rationale for the richer profile and this direction of support.

Likewise, manifestation of a complete nature does not immediately assign every requisite of every product to each original contributor. A need for real and varied wise effects differs from an all-token, all-incoming provision rule. The source’s timing, purposes and relation distinctions make that difference substantive.

## Integration as positive comparative evidence

E231–234 argues from ordered diversity, reciprocal need, replacement through time, coordinated local functions and agents serving wider ends they do not individually intend. E233 explicitly says God organises many agents’ actions and makes some causes and ends for others. Thus its unity thesis does not erase local causal work.

This evidence genuinely favours a common wise ordering explanation over a collection of unrelated accidental powers. It also fits a comprehensive original source better than sources assigned permanently isolated domains. This is real non-deductive support. It should not be discounted simply because it is expressed through analogy, natural signs or cumulative insight rather than a formal syllogism.

The live comparison is with concordant original agency, not disconnected accidental agency. To show that the former is deficient, the account needs its source-mode or strict-supremacy qualification. E232 invokes the deficiency of one ruler’s subjects needing another ruler’s subjects. That comparison gives intuitive force to the norm; it does not by itself settle whether perfect original agents’ fitting voluntary roles must reproduce the competitive and dependence-sensitive structure of finite kingship. Consent preserves provenance, but provenance alone still does not fix the relevant intrinsic defect.

The same distinction limits the inference from one ordering source to one complete existential source. Ordering and sustaining conditions are real contributions, but the one coordinating reality must also be shown to originate every independently relevant requisite. The reading’s wider original-completion arguments are relevant here; simply counting coordinated functions cannot replace them.

The electronic e232 sentence following the hypothesis of other gods has a missing consequent. I retain that gap and do not supply a conjectured sentence. The intact surrounding integration and kingship arguments remain strong enough to assess; a textual lacuna is neither licence to dismiss them nor permission to complete them in the favoured direction. Historical physiology and cosmology in these illustrations are not upgraded to contemporary empirical verification.

## A stronger causal nonduplication attribution check

E145 begins with the Jabri’s objection that two independent causes of one effect would make it both dependent and independent. However, the Sunni reply at e146 explicitly adopts the impossibility of one numerical effect produced by two independent producers. It would be false to report that this is only an opponent’s view.

The reply also allows divine creation and creaturely causation of the occurrence in different respects: the creature’s capacity is itself a produced partial cause. E143 gives concrete receptive and environmental requisites beyond creaturely control; e146 preserves the creature’s effective contribution rather than making it idle.

Read with e50, this supports a same-respect restriction on actual independent whole production. It still does not establish that every notion of nonexclusive causal sufficiency is identical to exclusive original production. An interpretation that makes the latter contain no foreign contribution gets the narrow contradiction; a stronger anti-overdetermination interpretation must defend the needed causal dependence principle. The actual source-mode norm remains separate from either interpretation’s formal consequences.

E278 reinforces real contribution by rejecting acquisition understood as merely simultaneous occurrence without any influence on the act’s production. A common-source account cannot evade the problem by relabelling an actual positive contribution as causally inert.

E150 expressly says the valid mutual-hindrance argument excludes coequal agents whose powers belong to their own intrinsic realities, rather than being received from the other. Thus the original-versus-created distinction is an explicit source commitment, not a qualification invented to rescue its argument. The substantial extended defence in Darʾ and Minhāj must be assessed; e50’s created carrying example does not refute that fuller defence by itself. E151 also permits calling the creature a bringer of its act from nonbeing into being while denying independent production, confirming that the classification concerns scope and mode rather than the word “creating” alone.

E178 supplies another limit: a general divine willing that establishes a creature’s naturally active disposition need not be analysed into a separately individuated special divine volition for every breath, droplet, letter or particular will. The text immediately preserves comprehensive divine willing, power and creation through causes and wisdom. Complete original provision therefore need not mean an atomistic one-divine-act-per-token account; no such individuation thesis was used in this appraisal.

## Mediation and matching endpoints

E213–214 supplies two different defences of means. Some ends essentially require a condition, such as a relation requiring its relata; removing the condition changes the very target. In other cases direct production may be possible, but the mediated history realises additional goods for the intermediaries or the broader order. A coarse matching endpoint therefore need not preserve the whole value of the act.

These reasons support wise derivative mediation within universal original sourcehood. They do not establish that an independently original peer is possible or necessary for any good. They do block the shortcut that unlimited power, by itself, makes every more extensive unilateral undertaking an equally fitting substitute for every mediated undertaking. That shortcut would leave the source-mode classification undefended precisely where it needs support.

## Why this is a warrant judgement rather than a logical-gap report

The strongest reasons converge on an ultimate reality whose existence and intrinsic perfections are unborrowed, whose agency is real, whose acts express wisdom, and whose power is not a donated fragment. The complete-source account has genuine explanatory unity and economy. The wider study repeatedly confirms these positive considerations.

My remaining hesitation is not a refusal to believe without logical certainty. It is that the added universal step changes the property being assessed: from unborrowed intrinsic efficacy and fitting manifestation to every external requisite’s being actually provided by that same original. The source’s own distinctions between power and exercise, complete determination and product conditions, intrinsic wisdom and externally supplied perfection, and created effect and intrinsic act give a reason to resist that transfer unless its particular norm is independently justified. Strict supremacy presents another serious route, but its universal domination/peer-exclusion qualification faces an analogous eligibility question.

The alternatives are not equally credited, and no plural-world possibility has been demonstrated. Conversely, the affirmative does not earn every stronger universal just because the alternative has further explanatory work to do. I therefore retain the independent withholding judgement while recording the stronger affirmative case and the exact conditions under which I would change it.

The T17 ownership result is unchanged: complete adequacy of the existential account and real whole-original bearers are owned; singular all-incoming ownership of that adequacy is a new proposed strengthening. The precise new burden may be met by an independently warranted bearer-unity or singular-completion argument. Neither additional usage of “complete” nor the selected Shifāʾ passages supplies it merely by unpacking the earlier premise.
