## The new positive result

**A specified stochastic response law makes anonymous direct productive-route multiplicities identifiable from endpoint observations, without observing hidden route labels.** For two root indices, three exact probability probes recover the five possible positive-root/absence-guard types. Extending the endpoint readout to joint effect occurrences recovers which effects belong to the same route. Both results require explicit intervention and observation assumptions.

At fixed issued AB, independently retain each A-to-route incidence with probability 1/2 and each B-to-route incidence with probability 1/3. Keep absence guards evaluated on the issued profile. If the actual active route counts are a for A-only, b for B-only and c for AB, the probability of no effect is

q = (1/2)^a (2/3)^b (5/6)^c.

The exponents of primes 2,3,5 uniquely recover a,b,c. Solo-profile probabilities then recover the counts of A-requiring-B-absent and B-requiring-A-absent routes. These are unknown counts; the readout does not supply them. Exact inversion uses ordinary unique factorisation, not an additive law of physical efficacy.

The profile requirement is sharp for this query family. If A-only is never issued, a route enabled only there is invisible; likewise B-only. If AB is omitted, an AB route is invisible. Varying attenuation rates at the other profiles cannot expose a route whose guard never enables it. All three profiles are therefore necessary in the unrestricted guarded class, and one exact probability per profile suffices for one effect.

This is more than the earlier generic fibre criterion: it specifies a separating instrument, constructs its inverse over an unbounded finite-multiplicity class, characterises a weaker instrument's complete fibres, and proves a profile-query lower bound.

## What fails under weaker interventions

The distinction between an issued act and a productive incidence matters.

- Uniform independent attenuation of whole routes sees only their enabled counts. Its complete ambiguity is parametrised by a<=N_A, b<=N_B and a+b<=N_AB, with the remaining three counts determined by subtraction. The inherited coalescent and priority programmes have the same counts at every issued profile and remain indistinguishable under every uniform route-retention probability.
- One shared efficacy gate per bearer, with guards frozen, can distinguish coalescence from priority. It still cannot reveal duplicate A routes or AB routes absorbed by an active A route. Their shared-gate success condition remains the same for all rate settings.
- If the shared gate instead removes the issued act and causes guard reevaluation, the inherited coalescent, redundant and priority programmes all have the same OR response. This is another intervention semantics.
- Unknown rate calibration also destroys the stated inverse. One A route at survival 3/4 and two independent A routes at survival 1/2 have the same entire three-profile endpoint law.

Thus observing the desired response requires more than the availability of an ordinary switch for each original bearer. The specified support-dependent route success law, independence across distinct occurrences, calibration and guard semantics are substantive premises. Independent incidence gates are one implementation; a single gate per route with the same product-dependent success probability yields the same endpoint distributions. The data therefore do not identify the physical gate architecture. A source label does not establish that every occurrence involving that source can be thinned independently.

Aliases are handled separately. Repeating a representation with the same occurrence ID leaves its stochastic gates unchanged. Giving an occurrence a second independent gate would change the model, not merely its description. The tests distinguish repeated records, distinct stipulated occurrences, and conflicting records sharing an ID. Those identifiers remain interpretation-level data, not inferred metaphysical individuation.

## Exact probabilities are not finite experimental frequencies

For fixed rates, k and k+1 identical A-only routes have absence probabilities 2^(-k) and 2^(-k-1). With n independent trials, their total variation distance is at most n 2^(-k-1). A uniformly reliable finite-sample discriminator therefore cannot cover unbounded route counts with a fixed sample budget. Even a bounded class containing two full-support Bernoulli response laws cannot be discriminated with zero error from any fixed finite number of samples.

A known total count bound K gives a constructive, conservative confidence guarantee: every probability lies on a grid with denominator dividing 6^K, and distinct panels differ by at least 6^(-K) in some coordinate. Nearest-panel classification plus a variance and union bound yields an explicit sufficient sample size in `THEOREMS.md`. The bound is deliberately loose. Approximate frequencies must not be passed directly to an exact prime-valuation decoder.

No physical intervention, empirical dataset, or actual frequency measurement was performed. These are conditional identification and sampling results for the declared finite generative law.
