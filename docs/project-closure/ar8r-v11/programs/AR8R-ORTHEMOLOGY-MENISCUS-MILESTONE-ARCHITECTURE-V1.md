This document states the strongest coherent milestone architecture presently within reach of the orthemology program. It consolidates the prior milestone analysis, the corrected account of meta-noetic memetics, the complete accessible Codex-thread history, the current public `orthemology` and `daee-epistemics` repositories, the post-merge AR8R program, and the live V11 omission-reconciliation effort.

1. preserve the actual breadth of the research program without reducing it to a list of unrelated topics;

# I. The research thesis

Orthemology is not merely a vocabulary for “correct classification.” Its strongest research object is **result-plus-pathway correctness under typed conditions of source, reason, warrant, identity, reachability, transport, adoption, restoration, closure, and world relation**.

The unifying question is:

> Under what exact conditions can a truth-directed state, judgment, reason, agent, or collective become reachable, diagnosable, warranted, individuated, transported, synchronized, adopted, restored, and stably maintained without losing the source, proof, reason, provenance, burden, identity, or world relation that made it properly orthemic?

That question occurs at several levels.

```text
First-order orthemology:
What state or property does this concrete case instantiate?

Structural orthemology:
Was the result reached through adequate evidence, reason, policy,
authorization, routing, execution, and closure?

Meta-noetic orthemology:
What governing noetic structure made those categories, sources,
burdens, routes, and closure conditions operative, and how can that
structure itself be identified, transported, deformed, or restored?

Collective and agentic orthemology:
When can several agents jointly reach, know, warrant, adopt, execute,
or preserve a result that no one agent can safely carry alone?

Metaphysical orthemology:
What objective structures, grounds, or necessary conditions make
orthability, intelligibility, truth, proper function, and warranted
distinction possible at all?
```

The project therefore encompasses, without collapsing:

```text
metaphysics;
mathematics;
formal epistemology;
distributed epistemic logic;
diagnosability and active learning;
agentic epistemics;
identity and lineage;
provenance and evidential independence;
language, translation, and source custody;
noetic and meta-noetic structure;
restorative control and temporal games;
formal proof and specification fidelity;
runtime implementation;
and empirical validation.
```

# II. What “meniscus” must mean

## 1. Meniscus is not volume

None of the following is sufficient:

```text
a long-running campaign;
many theorem labels;
many repository files;
many finite countermodels;
many repaired audit findings;
a successful archive;
a passing validator suite;
a Lean file that parses;
an architecture that looks useful;
or a large crosswalk.
```

These can be necessary infrastructure. They are not a scientific break.

## 2. Meniscus entry gate

A result may become a `MENISCUS_CANDIDATE` only when it has all of the following:

```text
a precise central claim;
a proof, validated impossibility result, or substantial empirical evidence;
nontrivial novelty plausibility after real prior-art comparison;
importance beyond one local application;
integration across at least three major program milestones;
survival against the strongest relevant rival models;
theorem-family and false-multiplicity control;
source and specification custody;
no unresolved candidate-specific blocking defect;
a frozen packet;
a cold audit;
repair of every blocking finding;
and a distinct fresh rereview.
```

## 3. Meniscus break gate

`MENISCUS_REACHED` requires more than candidate eligibility. It requires that the result materially changes the research frontier. At least one of these must be true:

```text
it establishes a new integrated characterization theorem;

it introduces a useful invariant that yields nontrivial bounds,
classifications, or impossibility results across several domains;

it proves a sharp impossibility boundary that rules out a serious
architecture or family of architectures;

it supplies a genuinely new convergence, stabilization, or restoration theorem;

it establishes a non-question-begging positive bridge in the
transcendental orthability program;

it creates a formally and empirically supported agentic protocol or
specification standard unavailable from the predecessor frameworks;

or it yields a new formal unification from which several existing
results become strict corollaries rather than merely analogies.
```

Independent specialist scrutiny remains required where the theorem touches operator algebras, distributed synthesis, formal epistemology, metaphysics, Arabic primary sources, Islamic theology, causal inference, or machine-checked mathematics.

This roadmap assigns no overall integrated champion. The controlling disposition remains:

```text
overall integrated champion:
NONE
```

until one candidate survives the full meniscus entry and break gates. A milestone, architecture, repository packet, or source reconstruction may be locally valuable without becoming the integrated champion.

## 4. Anti-false-meniscus firewall

The earlier false-meniscus incident is a permanent control:

```text
a summary-level MENISCUS_REACHED field;
a zero-burden count;
a champion label;
or a campaign-close certificate

cannot override contradictory theorem, burden, source, rival,
audit, repair, prior-art, dependency, or candidate ledgers.
```

Closure must be recomputed from lower-level owners. Negative results, failed candidates, and preserved defects are evidence, not debris to be hidden.

Episodes carry actor, warrant, analysis, evidence, metaorthemes, policy, candidate families, inferred profile, route, claim ledger, residuals, action, lineage, and handoff structure. This makes “correct output by defective process” and “adequate process with a mistaken result” independently representable.

\[

It can alter availability, classification, weighting, source order, burden placement, affective salience, inferential permission, or closure. It may regenerate visible errors after one proposition is corrected.

It may fail because the intervention is unsuitable, misunderstood, refused, premature, source-inadequate, causally ineffective, or misses the load-bearing node.

# IX. Research method and anti-microresult controls

## 1. Depth requirement

For each meniscus candidate, use at least four genuinely different mechanisms:

```text
source-critical reconstruction;
model-theoretic or Kripke analysis;
graph/fixed-point/game formulation;
information or query-complexity argument;
order or optimization theory;
Lean-ready formalization;
executable countermodel search;
causal/empirical design;
rival metaphysics;
or theorem-family/prior-art analysis.
```

## 2. Anti-microresult firewall

The following can be useful subordinate results but cannot close a central wave by themselves:

```text
X does not imply Y;
one profile fails to determine one target;
one guard is necessary;
same type does not imply numerical identity;
receipt does not imply adoption;
one translation loses information;
one standard fibre criterion;
one standard fixed-point theorem;
one finite Boolean deletion model;
one procedural audit PASS;
or one zero-novelty application.
```

A subordinate result changes the frontier only if it:

```text
closes a load-bearing premise;
eliminates a serious rival;
forces a major architecture repair;
supplies a novel invariant or construction;
changes several downstream burdens;
or participates essentially in a larger integrated theorem.
```

## 3. Theorem-family control

Every result must be classified as:

```text
IDENTICAL_UP_TO_RENAMING;
DEFINITIONALLY_EQUIVALENT;
MUTUALLY_DERIVABLE;
STRICT_SPECIALIZATION;
STRICT_STRENGTHENING;
COROLLARY;
SAME_MECHANISM_NEW_CONSEQUENCE;
SAME_MECHANISM_NEW_COUNTERMODEL;
APPLICATION_ONLY;
INCOMPARABLE;
or UNRESOLVED.
```

New labels and new domains do not create new theorem origins.

## 4. Rival discipline

Every positive candidate must face the strongest relevant control:

```text

impersonal actualizer;
plural-resource model;
powers/dispositional model;
abstract-plus-concrete model;
brute or grounding-irrealist model;
common-source copying;
stale-version transport;
hidden-burden restoration;
self-sealing metaortheme;
or wrong-object formalization.
```

## 5. Negative-result value

A sharp impossibility frontier, exact minimal-premise map, or validated negative experiment is scientific progress when it closes live routes and prevents false integration.
