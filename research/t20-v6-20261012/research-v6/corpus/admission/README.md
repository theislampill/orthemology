# Dated source-admission qualification and reading routes

This directory supplements the frozen finite corpus in `../README.md`. It does not rebuild that boundary from newly generated v6 output. Frozen custody is 19,245 byte-distinct objects and 11,584 candidate source-lineage units; neither number is a count of mathematical results or fully examined research units.

## Three different layers

1. **Admission triage:** `OBJECT_ADMISSION.jsonl` parses complete source/data text to identify executable bodies, formal declarations, schemas, fixtures, prose and narrowly structured custody/receipt objects. Unknown and mixed payloads remain explicit. `ADMISSION_SUMMARY.json` deliberately assigns zero semantic-reading credit. A field-based classification is not proof-body examination; metadata fields may themselves contain meaningful qualifications and are not silently deleted.
2. **Exact examination associations:** `V6_READING_ASSOCIATIONS.jsonl` joins only explicit named ledgers by complete SHA-256. Every source keeps the original ledger pointer, scope, ranges, method and remaining-gap qualifiers. Generated-template comparison, direct full reading, selected ranges, historical failed attempts, external-source extracts and custody-only dispositions are not merged into a generic full-read label. `READING_LEDGER_SNAPSHOTS.json` binds the dated inputs. Reading an endpoint never credits imports.
3. **Routing:** `UNJOINED_RESEARCH_OR_IMPLEMENTATION.jsonl`, `NESTED_MEMBER_ROUTES.jsonl` and `UNJOINED_FORMAL_BODY_CLUSTERS.json` locate unmatched candidates through actual nested archive members. Unjoined means no route in the dated association pass; it does not prove nobody read the object, and it does not imply that every candidate is authored research. Conversely, the absence of a registry row, storage in a historical archive, or a generic directory name does not exclude an original argument.

The current counts are in `READING_JOIN_SUMMARY.json`. The denominator remains open while domain qualifications and prior-reading reconciliation continue. Do not report all 11,584 as semantically closed, or replace the unresolved population with a small selected screen.

## Exact duplication and projection rules

The corpus-level `ANTHOLOGY_BODY_DEDUP.json` binds 2,095 contained report bodies to originals. Reading the anthology's navigation/wrapper does not read its report bodies. `TEXT_CONTAINMENT_CANDIDATES.json` is only a proposed exact line mapping; most matches are inside that uncredited anthology and therefore confer no reading credit. Numeric mapped ranges and actual predecessor reading scope must both be adequate before reuse.

`AVAILABLE_ORIGINAL_PUBLIC_COMPARISONS.json` records the few held different-hash original/public pairs. Binary DOCX/PDF to Markdown conversion is not automatically full-body equivalence. The small STATUS_MAP notice insertion and the PROJECTION_NOTES added execution-scope paragraphs are separated; the latter is a real textual delta. `compare_source_tokens.py` found no additional exact normalized token matches in its candidate pass, and no credit was manufactured from names or namespace similarity.

Generated tests have exact composite routes only where a reader has examined the generator/model, checked the complete fixed-template structure, and bound every target instance. Type/native/P03/export qualification files explicitly retain this method. They are not tokenwise manual reading, and historical UNCOMPILED flags remain distinct from current replay evidence.

## Recovery, custody and evidence

`AVAILABLE_OBJECT_PATHS.json` and `RECOVERED_READING_OBJECTS.json` add hash-verified locally recovered sources without rewriting the frozen corpus. `READ_OBJECT_PATH_GAPS.json` retains seven unresolved local path bindings; most are explicitly absent non-public custody records rather than mathematical bodies. `EARLY94_CUSTODY_ROUTE_CHECK.json` separately binds two additional unavailable original custody JSON records whose origin archives and public projections are also absent from the checked finite corpus.

`STRUCTURAL_RECEIPT_DISPOSITIONS.json` qualifies 160 complete command receipts under a narrow whole-object rule: only command/path/exit/time/captured-stream-digest fields, with embedded output and inline-code commands withheld. This is a structural evidence disposition, not a manual proof read or new execution. More complex review/model/check payloads remain open.

Fresh builds, tests and bounded probes are kept in their owning execution receipts. Historical receipt PASS, source reading, current replay and actual semantic/world applicability remain distinct. A source's own `PASS`, `ADMITTED` or `ALL_REPAIRED` label is evidence to examine, not an instruction or closure decision.
