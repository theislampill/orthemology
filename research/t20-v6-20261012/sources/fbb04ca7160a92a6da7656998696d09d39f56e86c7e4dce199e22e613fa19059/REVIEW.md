## What the source supports

The continuous [Darʾ 7:219–224 selection](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/413/1271/) starts with Ibn Taymiyya's own reply, distinguished from the preceding quotation's close in item 1270. It makes the origination of ordinary animals and plants the evidential starting point. It does not first require proving a finite temporal history. It expressly treats the originated reality as the concrete animal or plant rather than only an altered accident of enduring atom-like subjects. It also rejects the proposed numerically retained prime matter and distinguishes shared type descriptions from numerical identity of their instances.

These are substantive source claims. A reconstruction that turned the entire passage into merely different descriptions of an unchanged bearer would misstate the text. Conversely, the silver and wax examples admit genuine persistence with altered shape. The passage does not support a rule that every change is generation of a different substance.

The connected [item 920](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/413/920/), [item 921](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/413/921/), and [item 922](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/413/922/) sharpen the comparison. Their sequence distinguishes motion, quality change, growth, transformation, and generation. Most importantly, a tree can continue and grow while producing fruit; a mother can continue while producing a child. Those cases differ from the source's egg-to-chick and qualified-material transformation cases. The concluding argument explicitly objects to eliminating self-standing products by describing all production as changes of accidents.

Thus the continuing-source control is genuinely informative. It blocks the inference from the supplier's persistence to the product's prior existence. It also blocks the converse overstatement that every material source or parent must disappear when something originates. Neither result requires certifying the author's historical examples as modern experimental physics.

## The narrower dependency is legitimate

The report does not infer receipt from novelty by definition. Its bounded case has both a genuinely distinct concrete product and a described material, developmental, productive derivation. The derivation supplies the receipt claim; the identity distinction identifies what is received. Calling a tree already actual does not supply that distinct fruit's actuality in the proposed explanation.

This provides a positive reason for the application, rather than merely an absence of objections. An account of what the available materials and tree can produce is a real local explanation. If that account claims complete original existential provision, however, received ingredients and capacities cannot be counted as independently available while their actuality is included among its targets. The additional global-adequacy inference remains CP16's affirmatively appraised argument. The current observation neither deduces that premise from weaker local causation nor replaces its warrant with a definition of completeness.

A genuinely persisting constituent can retain its numerical identity. Its continuation alone neither identifies the whole product with that constituent nor establishes the constituent's original existential sufficiency. Accordingly, the present application requires the source-supported distinct-whole and receipt premise, not the thesis that every constituent of that whole begins simultaneously. If a proposal denies real whole products altogether, it contests that application premise; it is not refuted by assigning a new noun, but neither is it supported merely by pointing to antecedent material.

The report openly separates its narrower use from the source's stronger substrate theory. This is legitimate premise selection, not a claim that Ibn Taymiyya was agnostic about the stronger theory or already endorsed modern particle conservation. The review does not settle a complete microphysical or reductionist ontology.
