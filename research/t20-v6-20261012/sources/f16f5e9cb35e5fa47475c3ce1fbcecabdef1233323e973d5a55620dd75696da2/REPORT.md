# Particular determination: intrinsic exercise, explanatory independence, and antecedence

10 October 2026 UTC. Targeted substantive T20 source-and-inference investigation. No integration, protected edit, closure, new formal result, or reopening of the earned Knowledge, Will, Wisdom, practical-perfection or common-source warrants.

## Result

**The proposed alternatives are not exhaustive. The complete efficient basis includes actually efficacious voluntary willing; an adequate explanation of that willing cannot consist only of reasserting its occurrence. But explanatory independence does not mean a separately subsisting, externally imposed, or temporally prior sufficient condition that determines the will without including its exercise.** The retained detailed reports already make much of this distinction. No actual inference from complete determination to an external or will-excluding antecedent was found in the controlling reports.

A targeted primary return supplies a more definite original-specific account than the frontier note currently states. In *Majmūʿ* 16:300–305, Ibn Taymiyya directly distinguishes future-directed resolve from present executive intent, locates particular differentiation in the subject's own cognitively apprehended reasons, and denies that the not-yet-existing intended object supplies an extramental cause. At 310–312, differentiation of knowledge and willing expressly does not imply separated countable components. This is a genuine specification of intrinsic volition's explanatory organization and a constraint on its individuation. It is not a calculation of the particular divine history.

**The precise remaining obligation is application-specific:** to claim that we have explained this particular determination, identify a relevant intrinsic practical/cognitive difference independently of describing it as whatever was chosen, and establish its actual determining role at the scope claimed. The sources support that demand. They do not require an independently stated unique normative maximum, a mechanically separate prior choice, or a complete humanly disclosed history before the existing wise-agency warrant can stand.

## 2. What the terminology does and does not establish

*Tarjīḥ* can express the active preferring or determining; *tarajjuḥ* can express the resulting preponderance or determination. That grammatical difference does not itself establish a source-endorsed exception permitting an agent to specify without adequate differentiation.

At *Majmūʿ* 8:135–136, Ibn Taymiyya first criticizes the claim that a voluntary agent, or an unchanged eternal will equally related to alternatives and occasions, actively specifies without a determiner. He then states the general principle using the possible thing's existence becoming determinate over its nonexistence. The author uses both sides of the distinction in one argument. It would therefore misread this locus to attribute to him the unqualified rule “uncaused determination is impossible, but voluntary determination needs no differentiating basis.” This is a source-position finding, not a proof that every competing account of explanation is incoherent.

Conversely, *murajjih* must not invariably be translated as a separate antecedent event. In *Miftāḥ* 2:921–923, Ibn al-Qayyim identifies choice itself as the determinant, and distinguishes the agent who determines through choice from absence of an agent. His reply targets the inference from an act's necessity through choice to its being involuntary. The determinant's necessitating role need not bypass willing.

The complementary *Shifāʾ* e140–144 and e175–177 passages preserve both claims: actual will is not replaced by bare capacity plus knowledge of benefit, but calling a subject naturally willing does not explain every new specification. E175's finite-subject regress argument does not transfer unchanged to an everlasting original. No new general-versus-micro-volition argument is offered here; that distinction remains inherited.

## 3. Actual intrinsic willing is part of production

The strongest anti-over-demand control is *Bayān* 5:253–255. The relevant positive clauses distinguish God's will, which is not received from another, from created decisive willing. The concluding account makes occurrence necessary relative to divine willing and nonoccurrence relative to its absence. It does not introduce an independent source that first forces God to will.

This supplies a real explanatory role: **the volition can explain the product.** The volition is not an idle name for a prior nonvoluntary mechanism. Nor is it a mere factive record of a product. But these claims do not entail that asserting “this volition occurred” explains why that volition took this particular content and occasion.

There are accordingly three distinct relations:

1. The voluntary determination actually produces its intended effect.
2. Its content, occasion and directedness have an adequate intrinsic basis.
3. We have independently warranted that basis and its relevance in the account offered.

The first does not automatically discharge the second or third. The third is an epistemic and argumentative independence requirement, not a claim that every explanatory item must be temporally before, ontologically separate from, or efficacious without the voluntary determination.

## 4. The discriminating original-specific return: Majmūʿ 16:297–312

The selected locus is the continuous explanation of *al-Akram* and particular willing. It was chosen because it addresses original willing directly, rather than supplying another created-context or achieved-end illustration.

### Knowledge alone is not the full explanation

At 297–299, wisdom is expressly more than possessing power, will or knowledge: the discussion concerns acting knowingly for fitting purposes. Thus 304's claim that particular willing follows knowledge is not a reduction of will to generic omniscience or to factive awareness of what was selected.

At 300 the human two-loaf/two-path examples are explained through practical or cognitive differentiation. They show that apparently equal objects need not present a completely indifferent practical situation. These human examples do not establish that God has limited availability, ignorance, or a human sequence of thoughts. No such transfer is used.

### The critique reaches new particular willing itself

At 301–303 the author distinguishes the genus of activity from a particular volition. He rejects not only a wholly unchanging generic will with merely relational updates, but also the account that adds new particular wills while giving no new ground of their specification. This is more direct evidence for the remaining demand than relying exclusively on the reported objection at 8:148.

The positive account at 303–304 distinguishes willing now that an act occur at a future time from executive willing at that time. The earlier resolve does not have “produce the act now” as its content. Their difference does not require a failure of earlier knowledge, a change in the good pursued, or an externally supplied capacity.

**This is a bounded individuation result:** future-directed resolve and present execution are discriminated by their intentional-temporal roles. It does not supply a count of independently existing volition-atoms or one divine micro-volition for each created event. It also does not explain why this act and this time were originally selected. A future plan naming them cannot be offered as a noncircular ultimate explanation of that selection.

### Intrinsic grounds and intended content

At 304–305, the particular volition is related to knowledge of a differentiating feature of the intended object. The object, before production, is present in cognition; it is not an already actual external thing causally completing the original. The specification of quality and measure is then expressly grounded in features within the agent. Bare capacity, common to many alternatives, is rejected as the complete differentiator; so is will treated as generic self-specification without a ground.

This produces a positive explanatory distinction rather than an encoded choice: **an object's being relevant as intentional content is not its supplying an extramental efficient cause.** Therefore explanation through apprehended object-features can be intrinsic to the agent even when directed toward an external product. One need not choose between an externally donated reason and an unexplained voluntary token.

Nevertheless, the expression “differentiating feature” is not a certificate identifying any actual feature in a proposed example. If it means only “whatever feature belongs to the outcome chosen,” nothing has been added. This return does not instantiate that placeholder for a particular whole divine undertaking. It establishes the source's relation and location of explanation, not every actual argument of that relation.

### Differentiation without separable components

The continuation at 310–312 explicitly separates discrimination among knowings and willings from physical separation or individually counted external items. Its argument preserves differentiation by object; denying separated components does not make all knowings or willings numerically identical, erase distinguishable occurrences, or prohibit finite indices used to represent them. A representation may index differing contents and occasions without claiming distinct substances, mutually separable parts, or a complete cardinal inventory of intrinsic acts. The positive result is therefore best stated as distinguishable intentional-temporal determination, not atomization of attributes. The electronic wording at 310 has repeated demonstratives; this paraphrase rests on the clear subsequent distinction between differentiation and separation, rather than silently repairing that string.

## 5. Is there a conflation in the retained argument?

**No demonstrated conflation in its controlling detailed formulations.** The productive-sufficiency report expressly rejects the will-excluding antecedent interpretation. CP20 likewise distinguishes sufficient occurrence from a unique antecedent normative ranking and preserves intrinsic willing as productive.

One phrase needs controlled interpretation: CP20 says an ultimate particular exercise without a “further adequate differentiating basis” disputes the source's demand. “Further” is warranted if it means more explanatory content than the bare assertion that the exercise happened. It is not warranted if it means a separately actual prior volition must always necessitate this one, independently of its intrinsic cognitive-practical activity. The newly read locus requires differentiating grounds within the agent and a nonempty account of new willing, but does not prove that stronger architectural requirement.

Nor should “unchanged indifferent basis” be confused with an earlier *partial description* of the same wise agent. Standing power and a generic good can remain unchanged while actual intentional content, occasion, and exercise differ. The sources reject sufficiency of the wholly indifferent complete basis; they do not establish that every description supplied by us was complete.

The words “complete particular determining basis” should accordingly be read as:

> The actual intrinsic, reason-sensitive determination adequate for the particular occurrence, including its willing and operative conditions, whose claimed explanatory contribution must be warranted rather than stipulated from the resulting choice.

This is an interpretive refinement and anti-overreading control, not a report that the retained analysis secretly assumed external determinism.

## 6. The exact residual and the strengthened positive result

The frontier can now be narrowed: this locus explicitly differentiates prospective and executive intent, locates their grounds intrinsically, and distinguishes intentional content without separated attribute-atoms. These are real explanatory constraints at original scope. The earlier note already affirmed intrinsic willing and operative conditions; it said that no unexecuted discriminator had been identified in its three inspected records, not that no account could exist. The present operation is an additive application, not a correction of an asserted universal absence.

What remains is not the mere demand to name an agent or to assert that God knows and wills. It is **which independently specified feature or relation actually warrants the particular determination under discussion**, and how its practical relevance and productive role are established without deriving them solely from the fact that it was chosen.

A proposed local explanation should therefore identify:

- Its exact target: wise kind of undertaking, particular means, executive occasion, or full initial undertaking.
- Content or conditions describable without the phrase “the option actually chosen.”
- Why those features matter to the apprehended end and the agent's actual exercise.
- The extent of discrimination they achieve, including what remains underdetermined in the account.

These are standards for the strength of a proposed explanatory claim. They do not impose unique numerical maximization or require replacing voluntary production with antecedent determinism. Some relevant differentiators can concern means, occasion or opportunity rather than a superior intrinsic value of an object. Conversely, no source-grounded original case of completely tied full reasons has been established here, and human availability or salience cannot silently supply one.

The surviving positive argument has three steps. Real voluntary exercise can be productively sufficient in the relevant respect; apprehended features can direct that exercise intrinsically without an external donor; intentional-temporal distinction need not fragment the source into independent components. Those claims answer specific objections to intrinsic particular agency. They do not insert the selected outcome into a premise and then pretend to derive its ultimate choice.

Accordingly, the general Will/Wisdom and practical-self-governance warrants remain earned even when the complete particular history is undisclosed. The precise open task is an independently warranted *application* of the account, rather than proof that every adequate explanation must be a prior deterministic mechanism or another global declaration that all individuation is missing.
