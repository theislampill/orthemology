# Bounded release for a source attribution task

## Decision and contribution

A knowingly organised attribution workflow is about to lose its original role-witness service. Its replacement still exposes every needed source fact, with unchanged permission, but allows only two overlapping whole-card reads. The recipient has a legitimate one-request budget for the declared next decision. In the controlled case below, retaining the middle context witness does not make the release adequate. Retaining either edge witness does. A valid task-specific verdict, when permitted and adequately warranted, is another sufficient route and can remove the need for later reads altogether.

This is a concrete precaution-selection result. It joins the independently defended due-care premise to the independently derived source-context/read interface, rather than inferring a duty from computational expense. It does not add a moral theorem, a new covering principle or a general continuation theorem. Ninth already established the need to carry future-relevant evidence or preserve an actually usable and permitted observation route. What this application specifies beyond that example is which of two equally sized, correctly source-bound packets is adequate when access is retained but its organisation changes.

## 1 A deliberately organised bounded reliance

G is an intelligent organiser, and R is a subject conducting a permissible source-attribution task for a real epistemic purpose. G has actively installed the witness service as a step in R's workflow, induced R to continue on that arrangement, and accepted responsibility for the impending handoff. These are descriptive relation facts, not an inference from G's ability to help. No claim that the abstract parser itself is a conscious subject is needed.

The workflow fixes the source snapshot, selected output occurrences, original-document role predicate, recipient and decision period. R has one permitted witness-service request in its final decision stage. This is an independently specified workflow allowance, not a penalty manufactured by the proof. R has a legitimate reason to complete the task within that stage; it has not validly accepted negligent withdrawal of the provision around which G organised it. A genuine change to the budget, deadline or task is possible only if actually arranged, rather than assumed after the fact.

G knows the impending service replacement and the recipient's holdings. Before retirement, G possesses adequately bound source-role witnesses and can permissibly transfer them or arrange a replacement. In this controlled comparison, the modest preparations described below are available, proportionate to the task's stakes, and unopposed by any undefeated competing reason. These are independent application premises. The read arithmetic does not establish them, and no practical-perfection premise is supplied by G's source position.

The normative premise is the accepted research-v3 principle: active organisation of another subject's legitimate bounded reliance, understood exposure and relevant control give a special pro-tanto reason for proportionate care, including fitting transition arrangements. The case supplies an available fitting response and stipulates that no competing reason excuses knowingly omitting every adequate response. The conclusion concerns G's choice before withdrawal. It does not predict G's compliance or require infallible success after reasonable precautions.

## 2 The attribution dependency is derived before the budget calculation

Use the continuation lane's generated sibling-context source family, not the actual historical page's access conditions. There are three original contexts a, b and c in that source order. Each has a fixed proposition occurrence and a role label x_i indicating whether its original-document occurrence belongs to the stipulated unquoted authorial role. The family includes an all-positive source and the three one-negative alternatives. The roles are fixed within each source snapshot; no role bit is flipped inside one immutable real object.

Each selected occurrence is emitted after returning from an unselected inner report. The suffix of each split fragment is `leave; emit(t_i)`. Its entry stack is `[inner_i, i, root]`, so symbolic evaluation makes the selected output depend on incoming reference I_1. The already verified boundary map substitutes I_1 with the original key i. Acquisition and justification of that map are preparation costs already paid and separately retained. They are not secretly reconstructed by the later role reads.

Transport and reconvergence produce selected output order c, a_1, b, a_2. The two a outputs have different carrier occurrence identities but retain the same original context a. Source-bound substitution therefore yields the distinct live set L = {a,b,c}, rather than four source roots. The next question Q is whether all four selected outputs have the specified role in the original witnessed document. Accordingly Q = x_a AND x_b AND x_c. The present carrier may quote every output; that current-carrier force is separately retained and is not converted into a fresh assertion by Q.

A previously completed task concerns a separate context d. Its witness may be retained or discarded according to that old task's needs. Completing d is not represented as completing Q while somehow forgetting Q's already adequate answer. The receiver initially has the source/snapshot bindings, boundary-origin maps, selected-output list, source order, card coverage index and the old result for d. It initially holds no a/b/c role witness, no adequate verdict for Q, and no informative source-recognition side channel.

The oracle assumptions are exactly the corrected continuation contract. Handles are opaque apart from equality and declared lookup operations. Role tokens, lengths, offsets, statuses and observable responses do not leak an omitted role. On the all-positive source, every read not covering a particular origin has the same complete observable response as on the alternative with just that origin negative. The ordinary shared-ID table or parser receives identical evidence and has all the same permitted alternatives. These are synthetic protocol premises, not claims that a knowledgeable real reader must be unable to recognise a historical passage.

## 3 Complete surviving access and an inadequate replacement

Before retirement, service S offers one source-ordered adequate role-witness window covering {a,b,c}. It costs one request. Adequacy means that the response contains enough bound opening/path evidence or equivalent warranted role records to establish the original roles. Raw inclusion of three proposition strings is insufficient.

After the proposed replacement, service C offers exactly two whole-card operations:

| Operation | Adequate original-role coverage | Request cost |
|---|---|---:|
| C_left | {a,b} | 1 |
| C_right | {b,c} | 1 |

Both cards remain available and permitted. Each is readable in the allowed final stage, but the stage permits only one request. No cross-card read, retained full-source read or free singleton lookup belongs to this proposed service. Such a route must be included if actually retained or supplied; it cannot be excluded simply to make this example work. The two service contracts are stipulated alternatives in a constructed workflow. They do not allege revocation of any public site's actual access.

G's live answering service also ends at the handoff under this proposed plan. Its prior knowledge does not constitute an accessible later oracle unless G or another accepted reader actually remains available to answer. Keeping such an answer route is a genuine additional preventive option. An actually available post-handoff request for a valid Q verdict would defeat the stated C-only bound and must be counted rather than ignored.

Costs here count requests only. The joint S response may contain more bytes than a C response. There is no constant-time, equal-bandwidth or equal-authentication-work assertion. Preparation, transfer and evidence verification have their own costs; the controlled case makes the small stated precautions feasible and proportionate without pretending their cost is zero. The exact bound is for the final role-read phase conditional on its documented holdings.

On the all-positive source, either one C card leaves an edge origin unobserved. The corresponding one-negative source gives the same complete observation but changes Q. Thus no correct deterministic total policy can answer Q after one C request in every admitted case. Two reads suffice. Finding a negative role can terminate earlier on particular inputs, but it does not support a total one-request undertaking. Returning unresolved is safe withholding, but does not complete the declared task.

This is a direct small instance of the already admitted exact cover/read result, not a fresh lower-bound method. It shows that every fact can remain accessible, and every permission can remain valid, while the replacement fails the particular recipient's bounded task.

## 4 Which preventive packet changes the decision

Let K be the set of valid role witnesses actually transferred before retirement. A known negative in K settles Q immediately. For the all-positive worst case, the remaining demand is L minus K. The exact later cost is the minimum cost of C cards covering that remaining set.

The three one-witness rows compare separately predeclared policies: always retain a, always retain b, or always retain c, independently of the source-role labels. Which origin is sent therefore supplies no additional label-dependent signal. This restriction defines the compared packet plans, not every permissible precaution. If G instead uses an agreed trustworthy value-dependent selector, the selected origin's identity can itself encode Q. That is an additional informative channel and must be credited, just like an adequate task-specific verdict. The middle-only lower bound cannot be applied to such a different protocol without reassessing its full observations.

| Actual handoff evidence or service | Unresolved all-positive demand | Minimum later requests | Meets budget 1 |
|---|---|---:|---|
| C with no relevant evidence | {a,b,c} | 2 | No |
| C plus a bound witness for b | {a,c} | 2 | No |
| C plus a bound witness for a | {b,c} | 1, using C_right | Yes |
| C plus a bound witness for c | {a,b} | 1, using C_left | Yes |
| C plus witnesses for all three | Empty | 0 | Yes |
| An adequate bound verdict for Q | Q already settled | 0 | Yes |
| S retained, or an adequate replacement joint window | {a,b,c} | 1 | Yes |

The witness for b can be completely authentic, correctly interpreted and current. Its inadequacy is neither forgery nor loss of identity: it fails to remove the source dependency that prevents a one-card completion. The edge and middle packets can use the same fixed witness-record envelope and binding dictionary, so the difference is which original role is carried, rather than an unexplained unequal payload budget. No total-storage optimality theorem is claimed.

This selection is useful precisely because maximal retention is unnecessary. G need not retain the full source, every ancestor's role, both copies of a as independent evidence, or even all three demanded roles. Either edge witness plus the real replacement service is sufficient. The ordinary shared-ID table computes the same result; nothing depends on using the programme's vocabulary or implementation.

An adequate task-specific verdict is a genuine alternative. G or an accepted competent reader may establish Q before retirement and transfer the supported result under a protocol that permits R to use it. The record must bind the fixed snapshot, selected output list and original-role predicate, and it must be epistemically adequate for R under that protocol. A signed guess or a mere source name is not enough. The truth bit is only the variable decision payload, not the whole evidential or authentication cost. The accepted due-care principle does not itself establish the reader's reliability, R's authority to receive the record, or its actual warrant.

If this verdict is permitted and available, the argument cannot demand edge-witness retention as the unique fitting response. A genuine whole-source copy, adequate role table, maintained joint read, appropriately accepted budget extension or informed task revision may also suffice. Their actual feasibility and terms must be assessed. A purely verbal promise to reread later supplies none of these operational premises. If the promise is backed by a real service, allotted request and valid evidence route, it can instead describe an adequate arrangement.

In particular, the case does not make information inaccessible simply to create a moral duty. It openly supplies G with transferable witnesses and a permitted verdict route. It evaluates the inadequacy of a specified fixed middle-only plan against those genuinely available precautions. It does not claim that G or a better-informed reader is unable to solve Q, or that R must remain ignorant despite receiving another valid signal.

## 5 The joined normative decision

The operational calculation establishes that the proposed C-only and C-plus-b handoffs do not provide the stipulated one-request completion capacity. It establishes that C-plus-a, C-plus-c and the other sufficient alternatives do provide it under their own evidence and access premises. It supplies no moral conclusion in isolation.

The due-care argument supplies G's reason to address the known exposure created by its actively organised reliance. The controlled application adds that G knows this exact difference and can take a fitting adequate precaution without an undefeated contrary reason. Together they support a concrete decision: G should not knowingly implement the inadequate middle-only plan while omitting all of the available adequate arrangements, then treat unchanged permissions and eventual accessibility as sufficient release.

The requirement is disjunctive and proportionate. It is to arrange an adequate response to this bounded exposure, not to preserve S forever, guarantee every later success, force R to accept help or solve every future attribution question. If an accepted certificate is the least burdensome fitting response, the principle permits it. If R competently declines an adequate offered arrangement, the further responsibility changes. If all adequate measures become overwhelmingly costly or impossible, the normative case must be reassessed; the two-read calculation alone does not forbid withdrawal.

This judgment can apply without any false assurance. It can also survive G's change of preference about supporting R. Neither point is newly established here: both belong to research-v3. Nor does the result show that any actual ultimate source violates its responsibility, that sourcehood confers jurisdiction over R, or that an original source essentially cannot breach this reason.

## 6 Exact comparison with Ninth and stopping boundary

Ninth's two-handoff calibration example already required acquiring the future-relevant binding before retirement or preserving an actual authorised query route. It already allowed task-relative small evidence and rejected copied addresses as sufficient access. Repeating those propositions would earn nothing.

The present application adds a specific decision under retained complete access: card geometry and an independent one-request allowance distinguish middle-witness from edge-witness release. The selected live contexts are derived through actual fragment return and shared-origin reconvergence, and the fixed-Q verdict control prevents overclaiming a necessity to preserve raw roles. That is a narrow project application with a falsifiable adequacy calculation. Its normative force is an application of the accepted care principle, not an independently new normative result.

No further positive bridge to source-wide practical priority, predictive fidelity, essential integrity, necessary existence or identity follows. If the programme requires a new foundation-level theorem rather than a concrete cross-programme decision, this example does not meet that demand. Its useful endpoint is the exact choice above; adding more card configurations would merely repeat it.
