# Orthemology through T20: a field guide

**Proposed consolidated research baseline for owner review.** Research cutoff: 11 October 2026, 11:32:55 UTC. This guide is a new synthesis of inherited research. Its publication, owner acceptance, and the start of T21 remain separate decisions.

## The programme in plain language

Orthemology investigates how a thing, judgement, process or organisation can be assessed and corrected without losing the distinctions that make the assessment meaningful. An answer can be correct while its route is inadequate. Two copies can say the same thing while having different sources. Two compressed descriptions can agree now while needing different responses to tomorrow's evidence. A coherent mathematical model can describe no actual instance of the intended philosophical object.

The programme addresses those problems through several mutually informative kinds of work:

- **Mathematics:** exact representations, information preservation, tensor relations, continuation, inquiry, proofs, probability and geometry.
- **Foundations:** objective evaluability, received and original actuality, necessity, complete sourcehood, Knowledge, Will, Wisdom and epistemic warrant.
- **Source research:** what arguments actually say, how their terms work, and whether a formal reconstruction preserves their intended propositions.
- **Operational applications:** restoration, provenance, collective diagnosis and noetic processes, with implementation and empirical adequacy assessed separately.

A conditional theorem is an achievement. A warranted philosophical premise is another achievement. An implemented algorithm, fresh kernel check and external empirical validation are further achievements. The consolidated record identifies which of these each result has.

## What the mathematical work now makes possible

### 1. Represent relations rather than only scores

The inherited graded tensor algebra treats role, order and higher-order interaction as genuine mathematical structure. Lower-order marginals can lose a relation that later becomes decisive. This is more than an analogy: later corroboration models exhibit equal proper marginals with different evidential consequences. The meaning of the represented relation still requires a justified application.

### 2. Preserve what future correction needs

The continuation programme asks which states may safely share a representation given specified future operations. T20 develops positive answers for source-root stores, guarded certificates, finite stochastic contracts, predictive states and historical revisions. In several finite classes it constructs the coarsest adequate quotient or an exact minimum additional record.

The result is not “store everything.” It is “state which operations matter, then preserve the distinctions they require.” A current posterior can be sufficient for one task and insufficient for a later source audit. Source identity alone does not prove source reliability or a person's actual epistemic basing.

### 3. Compute proofs and inquiry without changing their costs or semantics

Guarded proof construction can attain a minimum under its explicit finiteness and price conditions. Sharing changes the optimisation problem: separately cheapest branches need not yield the cheapest common proof graph. Type-theoretic results distinguish unchanged calculi from extensions, and proof equality from history or provenance equality. Later literal compiler and probability work strengthens source-level formal correspondence while keeping host execution and external randomness separately accountable.

The mathematical identification and sampling results remain genuine scoped research. Their preservation does not require inventing a theological application or keeping that branch the next research priority.

### 4. Use actual geometry and probability laws

The geometry programme contains genuine Hessian/Bregman and Riemannian constructions, not merely geometric language. It distinguishes metric contraction, projected gradient dynamics, invariant families, constrained correction, evidence reweighting and positive numerical evolution.

A sequence of purported moments must first come from a probability law. Realisability, determinacy, computable recovery and preservation by a flow are different questions. T20 has both constructive restricted-family/full-moment results and precise failures of unrestricted summaries. Neither side cancels the other.

### 5. Preserve coupling, timing and model identity

Two transition systems can agree on possible outcomes but disagree about their probabilities, timing or costs. Two uncertainty descriptions can have the same intervals while pairing their priors and likelihoods differently. Fixed-model inquiry cannot silently become a problem in which a new worst-case model is selected at each branch.

The constructive repairs retain the appropriate joint quantities: outcome and successor, holding time and event, model-indexed loss, or historical source version. These contracts compose only when their meanings and assumptions match.

These are inherited research judgements with articulated reasons and limits. This consolidation does not convert them into kernel-proved metaphysical facts or independently authenticated historical conclusions. Nor does a general theory's incompleteness erase particular arguments already earned.

Important achieved distinctions include:

- Recognition can be warranted without a prior explicit proof certificate.
- The actual-field common-source conclusion has a narrower scope than excluding every possible idle or disconnected original.

## The major cross-front gain

The joint programme can now distinguish three questions that are easily conflated:

1. Does the proposed object or epistemic relation actually obtain?
2. Does the representation preserve what the declared operations require?
3. Does the implementation realise that representation and those operations?

For example, a source-specific challenge to a necessity premise and a challenge to a constitutive-independence premise have different formal revalidation scopes. That is an actual application of dependency mathematics to the foundational arguments. The scope calculation does not itself establish either premise, manufacture a defeater, or determine a knower's actual grounds.

Likewise, a later audit can remove a report's testimonial standing while leaving the observed report relevant to what a verifier checked. Deleting all traces of the report can then be mathematically wrong. This shows why “correct the evidence” requires an explicit account of the evidence's changed role.
