# Foundational arguments and their mathematical interfaces

**Proposed T20 baseline for owner review.** This crosswalk preserves different kinds of support. A philosophical argument, source interpretation, formal entailment, software refinement and empirical finding are not interchangeable evidence. The current source-bound appraisal is inherited; consolidation supplies reconciliation and traceability.

## 1. Objective correction and actual received reality

**Current result:** Objective evaluability is not another entity alongside concrete reality. A warranted actual received occurrence can supply an entry to the productive argument; correctness of a proposition alone does not instantiate its object. Material generation provides a concrete source-guided example without requiring observed creation of matter from nothing. See FND-001–003.

**Mathematical interface:** A representation's domain and target must be supplied before testing whether its fibres preserve that target. Moment realisability is an instructive mathematical instance of checking whether the represented object exists, but it is not a proof of the metaphysical productive premise.

**Preserved boundary:** Formal consistency, true evaluation, actual occurrence and warranted cognition are distinct. A successful correction in code does not independently establish a conscious or factive cognitive episode.

## 2. Original productive actuality and an actual bearer

**Current result:** The positive totality argument requires provision of the received actuality of any derivative supplier it invokes. Transition equations or a collection of derivative suppliers do not automatically supply their received actuality. The source-bound appraisal treats this as a substantive adequacy principle. See FND-004.

**Formal interface:** OriginalBearerBridge retains the same target, resource and bearer through Completion, Realisation and same-respect SupportTransport. This establishes an actual original witness under those premises. It does not infer that one individual provides every member's entire production. See FND-005.

**Discriminating use:** ET-18 and FND-033 identify which changed premises require revalidation of which formal conclusions. The support calculation does not authenticate Completion or Realisation, and it does not itself introduce a reason to doubt them.

## 3. Necessity through oneself and essential independence

**Current result:** The later CP23 appraisal concerns the same actual bearer, not a possibly different necessary object in each stipulated world. Intrinsic existential adequacy must concern its actual being, not merely its behaviour conditional on instantiation. Essential whole-existence independence has a separately articulated constitutive route. See FND-006 and FND-008.

**Mathematical interface:** Necessity and constitutive nonreceipt have different support dependencies. A need-specific challenge and a constitution-specific challenge can therefore have different consequences. Shared occurrence/resource/bearer challenges can affect both routes.

**Preserved boundary:** Essence/existence conceptual distinction does not imply separate concrete components. Necessary co-obtaining does not imply productive receipt. Modal persistence under a chosen semantics is weaker than a defended claim of necessity through oneself.

## 4. Complete common original sourcehood

**Current result:** The inherited strongest appraisal is warranted provisional belief in one complete common original source of the relevant actual connected created order. It credits developed arguments about actual productive adequacy, standing perfection, complete power, harmonious cooperation and local nonduplication. It does not derive exclusivity merely by defining complete production as exclusive. See FND-007, FND-009–011.

**Formal interface:** The local at-most-one production judgement and the connected singleton gluing theorem have distinct roles. If every target already has one eligible complete source and adjacent targets share the relevant source, connectedness gives one common source. Local existence, eligibility and faithful productive overlap remain explicit premises. See FND-012.

**Preserved boundary:** The minimal original-bearer theorem supplies a relevant original resource; it is not automatically the stronger per-target entire-provider theorem. This limitation of the formal interface does not reset the independently appraised actual-field cumulative argument. The universal exclusion of every idle or disconnected original remains a stronger question.

## 5. Knowledge, Will and Wisdom

**Current result:** Knowledge of intentional production and comprehensive Knowledge have distinct positive routes. Informed practical determination, source power and fitting ends support genuine Will. Wisdom has achieved local applications, including the exclusion of transferred guilt and responsive demonstration after prior knowledge. Particular acts remain distinct from essential power and created effects. See FND-013–018.

**Mathematical interface:** Typed domains, causal roles and temporal dependency graphs can expose equivocations or indefinitely deferred completion of one occurrence. They do not show that beginningless successive completed acts form one unfinished act. Geometric descent can model a declared corrective process; it does not independently establish divine Wisdom or explain the chosen content of a voluntary act.

**Preserved boundary:** A proposed objective function does not become a warranted account of practical fittingness merely by being optimised. Conversely, absence of a universal optimisation theory does not erase the particular philosophical applications.

## 6. Owed response, authoritative specification and veracity

**Current result:** The positive owed-response argument uses original provision and practical perfection, not power alone. A warranted in-scope normative disclosure can establish a particular duty without a second independent derivation of constitutive selection title. Genuinely source-owned assertions receive a defeasible exceptionless veracity appraisal. See FND-019–022.

**Mathematical interface:** Permission, revocable allowance, retained control, binding undertaking and constitutive surrender require different state predicates and transition rules. A truthful claim's content, source and reporting scope must survive a representation if they control later obligations.

**Preserved boundary:** Generic causation of speech is not adoption of its assertion. Practical optimisation is not authority. An open local explanation of selection entitlement is not automatically a defeater of an already authenticated command.

## 7. Recognition, cumulative grounds and testimony

**Current result:** Basic recognition need not await a complete reflective proof of every faculty. Diverse jointly supporting grounds can warrant knowledge; copying and intellectual inheritance are not independent witnesses. Source-owned commission, reporting veracity and actual content attribution have separate roles. See FND-023–032.

**Mathematical interface:** ET-01–07 preserve root identities, audit context, historical versions and predictive probabilities where later correction needs them. ET-20 shows precisely how lower-order calibrated summaries can fail to determine corroborative force in a declared probabilistic family. Internal typed proof identity does not identify epistemic histories or actual basing.

**Preserved boundary:** These models provide no measured reliability for actual religious traditions and no automatic psychological classification. They can prevent mistaken double counting or loss of a surviving ground once the relevant interpretation is independently warranted.

## 8. A concrete cross-front dependency pattern

The already worked CP16/CP23/T17 revalidation is a useful starting case:

1. Retain actual occurrence, productive completion, resource realisation and support as separately identified premises.
2. Retain the additional necessity and constitutive premises with their different consumers.
3. Preserve which grounds a justified challenge actually reaches.
4. Re-evaluate only the dependent formal consequences while keeping genuinely unaffected routes available.
5. Separately assess whether the challenged premise is in fact weakened, and whether the subject's judgement was based on the surviving route.

Steps 1–4 have a bounded implemented support test. Step 5 is not settled by passing that test. This is substantive cooperation between mathematics and foundations without identifying the two.

## 9. The remaining high-value philosophical boundary

The historical request-context attestation appraisal remains positive and defeasible. Later source work strengthens the lower transmission link and distinguishes request, display, testimony and communicative adoption. The strongest expected-publicity objection asks about wider surviving request testimony. It remains incompletely resolved and must not be silently replaced by an easier claim about everyone directly seeing the event. See FND-028–029.

Other retained continuations include particular wise determination, concrete source-content access, stronger original-level universal exclusion, and a justified application of cumulative-ground representations. Their existence does not retrospectively downgrade completed arguments, and their pursuit belongs to the owner-approved continuation rather than an endless condition on delivering this consolidation.
