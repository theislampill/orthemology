## 1. Source appraisal, rather than premise naming

The source's positive reason is real and must not be discarded by calling least generation or original completion an assumption. T17 P40–43 distinguishes actual local received efficacy from a complete existential account which perpetually presupposes that efficacy in the received mode. The positive role for original actuality is existential adequacy through a different manner of possession. It is not the demand that received beneficiaries become original, a denial of secondary efficacy, a requirement of a first temporal act, or an objection to infinity as such. T17 P42 accepts that reason at its stated defeasible standard; the formal controls below do not reverse that judgement.

The strongest held primary argument was independently checked in electronic Ṣafadiyya S002 L725–740 and visually on four complete Adwa scan pages, PDF404–407 / printed397–400. Printed397 distinguishes the origin/genus of efficacy from complete efficacy for a particular act. An agent's ability to do anything cannot be acquired by an exercise already requiring that very ability. Printed398–399 makes the anti-bootstrap reasoning explicit. This is a positive explanation of why a purported source cannot supply the prerequisite of any production through its own not-yet-available production.

The same printed397 passage expressly allows complete particular efficacy to depend on the issue of a prior effect, with successive motion as the example. Printed398 permits a particular created child to depend on a particular created parent. Printed400 says the agent's being an agent in the original respect can belong to its essence while its particular production depends on another particular production. The contrast is therefore stronger and more precise than 'all conditions are harmless' or 'all causation is well-founded.'

The supplied Oneness text, printed17–22/PDF24–29, independently distinguishes efficient-priority dependence from fatherhood/childhood co-presence, which arises from a separate productive event. Ṣafadiyya S003 L207 similarly allows mutually conditioned created powers supplied by a third cause. Editorial footnotes in the Oneness translation are not silently promoted to separate authorial demonstrations. The electronic Ṣafadiyya passage contains a repeated clause at the particular-efficacy locus; the printed scan was used to check the substantive distinction, not counted as another independent witness or manuscript collation.

These sources warrant rejecting two conflations:

- A relation of necessary co-presence need not be generative priority.
- A genuinely productive prerequisite of a particular token need not be the origination of the standing power by which an agent can act at all.

The second point is the additional discrimination supplied by the new beginningless control. Merely fixing the first does not license a least-generated interpretation over all concrete token inputs.

Extending the anti-bootstrap insight to every infinite account of distinct received sources requires the exhaustive existential rationale of T17 P41–43. The source asserts the broader impossibility confidently. A mathematically consistent realised all-received account is not thereby an adequate explanation, a possible universe, or evidentially equal to that affirmative rationale. Conversely, a proof that a least-generated account has a seed does not add independent support to the selection of that account. The original report is right to preserve both directions of that distinction.

## 3. Nonidle same-witness obstruction

`ActualProvisionReview.lean` uses five resources and distinct bearer identifiers. G's original stock is intrinsic to G; K's original stock is intrinsic to K. H bears its productive power. Two other bearer identifiers bear the target and side-effect resources. Every resource and bearer is actual.

Three rules are actually used:

- G's stock existentially grounds H's power.
- H's power and K's stock productively supply the target.
- G's stock productively supplies the side effect.

The control verifies actual local equations, nonempty prerequisites, generated availability of all five resources, acyclicity, and no incoming broad support into either original. Actual bearer realisation and the received-bearer-to-received-resource transport are proved separately. G remains the bearer of the fixed original resource throughout.

There is a strict live support route from G's stock through H's power to the target. G is not merely capable or idle: it actually produces the side effect. There is also an actual original productive contributor to the target, K. Nevertheless productive paths out of G's stock stay at that stock or the side effect. G is not a productive contributor to the selected target.

The control therefore refutes the reduced structural inference even after several plausible repairs have been granted. It does not refute the stronger T19 premise that the fixed original resource is *productively* employed in producing this target. The grounded productive power of H and productive supply by G are precisely the relations that must not be identified without argument.

This is not a complete model of necessary perfected agents, unrestricted power, wise willing, or the actual cosmos. Its extra target contributor does not establish a metaphysically possible plural-original universe. The purpose is narrower: actual exercise elsewhere and a separately existing target producer cannot repair a missing target-sensitive relation for the same original witness.

## 4. A constructive local bridge and its limits

`same_resource_same_bearer_transfer` fixes resource r and target t before choosing a bearer. It assumes:

1. r is actual and nonreceived in the relevant existential respect.
2. Real resources have actual intrinsic bearers.
3. A bearer's received actuality transports to receipt of its intrinsic concrete resource in that respect.
4. Each edge of one selected, nonempty support route from r to t has an independently supplied nonempty productive realisation path.

The result keeps the same r and the bearer of that r, proves that bearer whole-original, and preserves the productive path to t. It does not select another original who happens to act. The finite K-to-target case supplies a positive nonvacuity control; the G-to-target case fails precisely at the productive certificate.

The fourth condition is a compositional sufficient criterion, not a claim that every support edge everywhere is an efficient primitive. It allows mediation and needs no intentional ownership by G of H's act. It is not presented as necessary for every possible attribution theory.

T17 P48–51 gives the resource/bearer steps positive content and explicitly warns that support transport is not an efficient-production arrow from bearer to attribute. T19 P81–84 gives genuine mediate productive attribution positive content while preserving the proximate agent's ownership. Those passages do not turn broad existential grounding into productive realisation by a theorem. Under a broad reading of 'supplies productive reality,' that precise application remains to be warranted; under an already productive reading, it remains an antecedent to be established. T17 P65–68's pure-perfection route supplies standing power and still does not prove the missing target exercise, even when actual exercise elsewhere is granted.

## 5. Beginningless provision is not merely co-presence

`BeginninglessProvision.lean` is a genuinely infinite control. Its resource type is `Option Nat`: `none` is the original standing stock, and `some n` is token n. Rule n requires both `none` and `some (n+1)` and produces `some n`. All rules are actually used. Both inputs count as productive under this declared interpretation.

The control establishes:

- All tokens occur and the full actual local equations hold.
- Every rule is sound and has actual nonempty inputs.
- The original standing stock has no incoming support.
- Productive ancestry is acyclic: successor-indexed tokens are earlier, and no edge returns to the original.
- The very same original stock directly contributes productively to every token.
- The very same original bearer is recovered at each target, with a valid intrinsic/receipt interface.
- Every token has an actual prior-token productive input.
- No token is generated in the least inductive account. Only the standing stock is generated.

This result sharpens the source-fidelity boundary. It does not show the original standing resource to be ungrounded. It shows that demanding an inductively generated proof for every token's entire antecedent structure is an additional restriction. That restriction cannot be defended merely by identifying all edges as genuine productive relations: the source permits earlier particular effects to condition later particular production without thereby origination-conditioning the genus of agency.

The model is source-motivated, not certified as a possible universe. 'The same original contributes to every token' is a proved coverage predicate; calling that predicate the source's complete adequate original account still requires interpretation. However, it supplies a direct adverse test to any proposal claiming that the source's permitted beginningless case automatically satisfies whole-token LFP selection. It does not.

A two-level reconstruction is therefore preferable for further tests: reserve original-availability generation for the specified standing-resource respect, and track actual token production and its historical prerequisites separately. Such a reconstruction must justify the map between those levels rather than discard a troublesome productive predecessor as merely temporal. The old minimal generated-anchor theorem can remain useful on the resource level; it should not become a proof that every actual token history is inductively generated.

## 6. No first joint token without the depth premise

At the author's request, `BeginninglessJointProvision.lean` adds a second original standing input to each token, while retaining the genuinely productive predecessor input. Every token has a strict contribution from both originals and an earlier jointly supported token. Neither original receives incoming support; the graph remains acyclic. Every jointly supported resource has an earlier jointly supported resource, and no finite natural-number depth can strictly decrease along all predecessor dependencies.

The proof is genuinely infinite. It does not replace an infinite sequence by a finite cyclic sample, conceal a terminal member, or identify either original with the sequence. It also proves that no token has an inductive generated derivation.

This is a targeted depth-premise test. The provision lane's `no_well_founded_joint_mechanism_regress` actually quantifies over `Joint : Nat → Prop` and demands an earlier joint at a smaller natural index. Its conclusion is correct under that hypothesis. Acyclicity alone does not supply the hypothesis. The new variant therefore blocks an unrestricted claim that every actual joint history has a first primitive combination merely because its productive graph is acyclic.

The one-original history has direct source motivation in the permitted beginningless particular-effect sequence. The two-original variant does **not** inherit source permission for two perfected originals from that permission. It is only a structural counterinterpretation to dropping the depth premise. Source-mode perfection, complete original efficacy, CND at its proper scope, and plural-original admissibility remain to be appraised. No conclusion here vindicates a plural-original universe.

## 8. Concrete continuation and stopping boundary for this review

- The executed integrated consumer now supplies a regression boundary for subsequent account changes: retain G's original bearer without inventing target productivity, and retain same-original productive contribution to beginningless tokens without demanding their least generation. The result needed is evidence for the particular productive certificate or a counterinterpretation retaining all independently warranted semantic conditions.
