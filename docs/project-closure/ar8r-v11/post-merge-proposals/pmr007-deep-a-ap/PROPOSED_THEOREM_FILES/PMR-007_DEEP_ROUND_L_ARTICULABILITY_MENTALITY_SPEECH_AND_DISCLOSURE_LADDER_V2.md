## 1. Typed levels

The following coordinates are nonidentical:

```text
ART(c):       content c is determinate/articulable;
MENT(g,c):    bearer g understands c;
CAP(g,c):     g has agentive capacity to express c;
UTTER(g,c,e): g performs a speech act with c in episode e;
AUTHOR(g,c):  c originates from g in the declared authorship sense;
ATTR_N(g,s):  Track-N source predicate that speech attribute s belongs to/subsists in g;
EXPR(t,c,k):  token t expresses c under context k;
RELAY(e,t):   t is a relay/manifestation downstream of episode e;
CREATED_TOK(t): t is a created expression token;
FID_T(t,c,k): t preserves the declared target content under k;
SID(g,e):     speaker/source identity for e is authenticated;
PROV(e,t):    the episode-to-token provenance chain is authenticated;
VER(t,k):     token/version relation is current and compatible;
AUTHZ(t,k):   disclosure/use is authorized in context k;
APP(t,k):     the token/content is applicable in context k;
UNCR_N(s):    Track-N source predicate that speech attribute/content s is uncreated;
REV_N(g,s,t,k): source-conditioned revelational identification.
```

Token, content, bearer, speech attribute, episode, and context occupy different sorts. Expression is a relation, not an identity.

## 3. Source-conditioned disclosure contract

For an accepted Track-N source architecture, define the **guarded disclosure eligibility record**:

\[
GD_N(g,s,c,e,t,k)
:=
MENT\land CAP\land UTTER\land ATTR_N\land EXPR\land RELAY\land CREATED\_TOK
\land FID_T\land SID\land PROV\land VER\land AUTHZ\land APP\land UNCR_N.
\]

When the source architecture also licenses the content/speech-attribute relation and revelational identification, `GD_N` warrants a **source-conditioned classification** that the created token expresses or relays source-attributed uncreated speech. It does not prove the Track-N premises neutrally, identify token and speech attribute, or show that every created expression has uncreated content.

`AUTHOR` is not substituted for `ATTR_N`. The latter is the theological source relation frozen by the supplied text; ordinary authorship and episode-level utterance remain separate.

## 5. UG-T7 and UG-T8 exact bridge status

Deep D's impersonal models still refute the bare entailment from total articulability to mentality. To derive mentality, one must independently defend a premise such as:

```text
all determinate semantic content is intrinsically content-for-understanding;
or
ultimate semantic normativity requires an intrinsic intentional bearer.
```

Those conceptualist premises are contested by abstract-structure, modal-primitive, powers, teleosemantic, distributed, and brute-semantic rivals.

Even if mentality is granted, speech requires an agentive expression capacity and an occurrence. Actual Speech still does not identify a specific revelation without source, provenance, fidelity, authority, version, and applicability guards.

## 6. UG-T9 created expression and claimed uncreated speech

The source-relative claim is not:

```text
created token = uncreated speech.
```

It is a guarded relation of expression, relay, or disclosure across different sorts. Multiple created tokens can express one selected content; one token can receive different interpretations under contexts; and copied bytes can fail source or applicability custody. The admitted PMR transport and language results therefore constrain, rather than replace, the source-theological bridge.

## 7. Source authority

The supplied Asfahani English translation states that informative and compositional speech are distinguished; that Divine speech is uncreated; that it initiates from Allah rather than being created in another; and that speech cannot be separated from the one to whom it is attributed. These propositions supply `ATTR_N` and `UNCR_N` only at translated-primary-access authority.

They do not establish Arabic-primary verification, neutral truth, or the exact modern formal relation `GD_N`. That relation is a reconstruction for custody and nonmigration analysis.
