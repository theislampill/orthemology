# Claims and novelty against Eighth

## Research question

Whether an independently defensible account of real possibility can carry the transition from a particular original actual bearer to its intrinsically sufficient, unconditional existence, without simply reasserting the inherited determination argument.

## New conditional results

N9N1. Object-level actualisability bridge. Actual unreceived g, reverse possibility coverage, productive actualisability of absent-but-possible g, causal-content fidelity, and unreceivability over the relevant manifestations entail necessary existence of this g. If unreceivability is only a-local, an appropriate two-step reach closure is also required. The proof does not assume necessary existence, but its modal and identity premises require independent warrant.

N9N2. Exact-origin-fact bridge. Actual U(g), possibility coverage at putatively g-less alternatives, exact-fact productive completeness, and causal-content fidelity entail necessary existence of g. This route needs neither a separate origin-essentialism premise nor two-step frame closure. The argument does not ask for a producer of absence.

N9N3. Pointwise relocation equivalence. Under causal-content fidelity, exact-fact completeness at v is equivalent to the implication from possibility of U(g) at v to E(g,v). With the required possibility coverage across actuality's alternatives, the restricted completeness principle is equivalent to g's necessary existence. A general law could justify that instance independently; a bare targeted assertion does not supply weaker conclusion-relevant content.

N9N4. Separation controls. Explicit finite interpretations distinguish object-level from exact-fact completeness, symmetry from the composition condition, modal grounding from productive realisation, and a necessarily occupied unique role from its current bearer. All actual productive premises can remain fixed while the alternative assignments vary. These are logical controls and do not establish that any interpretation is metaphysically possible.

N9N5. One-possibility indispensable-ground lemma. An invariant modal content q, an existing ultimate ground wherever q holds, and fixed same-particular ground identity imply the necessary existence of that ground. It requires neither a PSR for all actual facts nor one being grounding all possibility. Its same-ground premise is not supplied by actual productive origin alone. A further distributed-ground interpretation shows that invariance does not itself concentrate all grounds in one bearer.

The analytical result concerns the exact modal coverage and bearer-retention needed by N9N1–N9N5. It does not claim a new source quotation establishes its own premise's metaphysical truth. N9N7. The exact-content recognition discriminator tests whether the offered warrant actually distinguishes intrinsic unconditional existence from primitive actuality with the same admitted actual powers. It preserves basic rational recognition as eligible and does not require measurement, universal agreement or formalisation. This is a normative test of supplied content, not an empirical procedure certifying private acquaintance.

## What remains unestablished

Actual P1/P2 warrant for the admitted correction remains outside the conditional grants. No independently defended unrestricted productive law with the required exact-origin coverage has been established here. Nor has this bearer's unique indispensable identity as a modal ground, or unconditional intrinsic instantiation of its nature, acquired the needed actual warrant. A modal necessity conclusion alone does not specify a nonbrute intrinsic explanation. N2 and N3 remain open.

No verdict is issued that a brute cosmos, an empty world, a different original source, a received version of g or distributed grounds are actual or metaphysically possible. No canonical revision, operational authority, general truthfulness or deployment conclusion follows.
