1. **Information-preserving transport must include policy and cost.** A prior pushforward or matching one-step distribution is insufficient. Mixing priors changes the hidden-information problem. Supplying the model tag changes the experiment rather than solving the original common-policy problem.

Conversely the later actual paired-process and nonconvexity statements are stronger than mere analytic series bounds because the process, count and policy interfaces are explicitly assembled. Probe/replay identities and theorem body scopes should remain distinct.
