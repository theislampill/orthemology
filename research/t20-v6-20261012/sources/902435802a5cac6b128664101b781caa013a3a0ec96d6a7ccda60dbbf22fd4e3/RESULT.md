# One original source, many indispensable mediation identities

11 October 2026. Additive T20 research. No repository/projection edit, philosophical reset, publication, T21 commencement, or closure claim.

## Result and precise increment

The affirmative actual common-source result has a useful **positive representation consequence**: an original-provider label cannot in general serve as the identity key for composing explanations of secondary production. In the relevant connected field the provider label is the same, while the actual intermediaries, their uses, and their effects can remain different. Gluing two explanatory fragments on that constant label can invent a route that neither fragment witnesses. Preserving all endpoint answers is itself weaker than preserving compatibility of the particular explanatory witnesses being composed.

This report proves the exact distinction, supplies the smallest repair for each of those two contracts, and derives an unbounded separation: **one common original provider is compatible, at this represented secondary-routing signature, with arbitrarily large transfer rank and arbitrarily many indispensable routing identities**. Transfer rank measures the chosen derivative relation; it does not count original bearers. No independence of those derivative identities as original sources follows.

The general fibre, relational-join and continuation-preservation mathematics is inherited. The existing actual-use/provision adapter also already distinguishes bearer labels from identical actual input tokens. Those are not new discoveries or missing implementations. The increment here is the exact source-bound composition operation, its two different preservation contracts, and the resulting rank/memory consequence. It supplies a reusable semantic test for a proposed common-source-based compression, not a claim that a deployed T20 representation already makes this error.

## 2. What is being composed

Fix one actual scope, productive respect, and adequately interpreted record. Let:

- X be upstream episode/fragment identifiers;
- M be intermediary identities at the chosen semantic grain;
- Y be downstream episode/fragment identifiers;
- L ⊆ X × M and R ⊆ M × Y be the two represented actual-use relations.

An element of M need not be a substance, a part of an original bearer, an attribute-atom or an ontological primitive. It can be a concrete secondary event or the identity of an actual input whose reuse the evidence tracks. The application has to state which. L and R are **not every available or merely candidate causal route**. Their actual-use interpretation is supplied independently, just as in S03–S05.

For conjunctive multi-input production, a single pair in R means that this input was actually used, not that it alone sufficed for the effect. This report concerns composition of those actual-use incidences or of already warranted fragments with their other conditions retained. It never upgrades a path to a complete sufficient cause or entire original provider.

Two fragments compose along M when they concern the same intermediary. Their endpoint relation is

    C = {(x,y) : there is m with L(x,m) and R(m,y)}.

For actual explanatory witnesses we additionally retain the shared m and the particular fragment records; mere membership of (x,y) in C is a weaker output contract.

This is the middle-identity component of composition, not a theorem that matching one key suffices for every real explanation. Other jointly relevant assumptions, temporal scopes, causal respects and guards must already be compatible, or must be included in the interface and checked as well. Equal token names do not authenticate numerical identity, and a hash certifies bytes rather than their productive interpretation.

Let q:M→Z be a proposed retained identity summary. Its pushed-forward fragments are

    Lq(x,z) iff some m satisfies L(x,m) and q(m)=z;
    Rq(z,y) iff some n satisfies q(n)=z and R(n,y).

Composing the summaries gives

    Cq = {(x,y) : some m,n satisfy L(x,m), q(m)=q(n), R(n,y)}.

When Z is the set of original providers, the accepted common-source result makes q constant on the selected field. That is a legitimate answer to the original-provider question. The question here is whether it is also a sufficient join key for secondary explanation.

No new premise that provision is exclusive by definition appears here. No application of CP16 or CP23 is being reconstructed. The source-level common provider is inherited independently; the relations L and R say which secondary input a fragment actually uses.

## 3. Exact endpoint composition theorem

**Theorem 1.** Always C ⊆ Cq. For a fixed L,R,q, equality holds precisely when every summarized endpoint connection has at least one genuine intermediary lift:

    L(x,m) ∧ q(m)=q(n) ∧ R(n,y)
       ⇒ ∃k, L(x,k) ∧ R(k,y).

**Proof.** A genuine composite uses n=m, proving inclusion. The displayed implication puts each pair in Cq back in C. Conversely, Cq=C supplies that k for any displayed antecedent. ∎

The k in this theorem need not be either retained witness m or n. Thus equality of endpoint relations does **not** prove compatibility of the very two fragments submitted to the composition operation.

**Corollary 1a, common-provider collapse.** If q is constant and M is nonempty, then

    Cq = dom(L) × ran(R).

Here dom(L) contains x having some L-successor and ran(R) contains y having some R-predecessor. This also handles empty relations: both sides are empty when an appropriate factor is empty.

**Proof.** The equal-summary condition imposes no restriction on m,n. Their existential choices are independent, so the criterion is exactly membership of the Cartesian product. ∎

Consequently, source identity alone is endpoint-exact precisely when the actual two-stage relation already contains this entire rectangle. Original common provision does not itself assert that every secondary input feeds every downstream effect. S01 expressly preserves secondary differentiation, and S02 gives independently distinct products and roles.

**Separating control.** Let M={m0,m1}, X={x0,x1}, Y={y0,y1}, with L(xi,mj) and R(mj,yi) iff i=j. Give both intermediaries the same original provider g. Both local relations and both provider attributions are retained exactly. Genuine composition has only (x0,y0),(x1,y1). Composition through g additionally yields (x0,y1),(x1,y0). No source has been deleted or replaced and no product has been counted as an original. The failure is the join, not the common-source attribution.

This is a mathematical control at an interpreted secondary-routing signature, not a newly observed two-fruit history or a full metaphysical model. It shows that the preservation claim is invalid without the additional lifting condition. It does not show that any affirmative metaphysical premise is false.

## 4. Minimal endpoint repair and its exact scope

Fix R and permit arbitrary upstream relations L over a nonempty X. Write

    N_R(m) = {y : R(m,y)}.

**Theorem 2.** The q-summary preserves endpoint composition for every L iff N_R is constant on every q-fibre.

**Proof, sufficiency.** If L(x,m), q(m)=q(n), and R(n,y), equality of the two neighbourhoods gives R(m,y). The original m is a genuine lift.

**Proof, necessity.** Suppose q(m)=q(n) but the neighbourhoods differ. Exchange m,n if needed to choose y∈N_R(n)\N_R(m). Select one x and L={(x,m)}. Then (x,y) is summarized-composable through n but not genuinely composable. ∎

For a declared family of downstream continuation relations (Ra), replace N_R(m) by the entire profile (N_Ra(m))a. The same proof, one relation at a time, gives the exact criterion. This is a concrete specialization of inherited continuation-sensitive factorization, not a new universal minimal-memory theorem.

For finite M and a fixed pre-existing summary q, the coarsest endpoint-exact refinement groups two intermediaries iff they have both equal q-values and equal profiles. If a q-fibre has d distinct profiles, any additional discrete label must distinguish those d classes. Conversely they can be enumerated separately inside each fibre, reusing labels across fibres. Thus a fixed finite supplement alphabet needs precisely max_z d_z labels, or ceiling(log2(max_z d_z)) fixed-length bits when nonempty. This count excludes storage of the profile decoder, actual relation, exact identities required by other contracts, and retrieval machinery.

Under one common original provider, the minimal routing supplement is the number of distinct continuation profiles within that single source-fibre. It can be one when all intermediaries are interchangeable **for these endpoint queries**, or as large as the number of intermediaries. Original uniqueness settles neither value.

This statement is not safe for arbitrary later additions to the continuation family: a previously equal pair of profiles can then split. Nor does present equivalence authenticate a source, license an intervention, preserve subjective basing, or establish that an actual-use edge is productive.

## 5. Composing the actual witnesses is a stronger contract

Suppose the user of the representation asks, "Do these particular two local explanatory records compose?" Let the left record identify m and the right identify n. For equality-based interface composition, the answer is m=n, not "some other k gives the same endpoint answer."

The summary predicate q(m)=q(n) is sound for all admissible submitted pairs exactly when

    q(m)=q(n) ⇒ m=n

on the pairs that can occur as left outputs and right inputs. For unrestricted pairs it is exactly injectivity of q. For a fixed pair of relation domains it only constrains the cross-pairs between active left and right interfaces. Unused or inaccessible identities need not be retained solely for this bounded contract.

**Proof.** Equality implies equal summaries for every q. Soundness is the displayed converse. Noninjectivity supplies two distinct equal-summary identities and the corresponding left/right singleton records. Conversely injectivity turns any accepted equal-summary pair into an actual identity match. ∎

This does not require equality of the local proof records themselves. Two different derivations, stored at different locations or written in different words, may refer to the same intermediary. S03 explicitly preserves this distinction for event-specific derivation records versus reused actual original inputs. Conversely one source's distinct intermediaries do not become identical because the witness predicates look the same.

**Endpoint-safe but witness-unsafe control.** Let m0≠m1 have identical downstream neighbourhood {y}, and let a left record end at m0 while a right record specifically starts at m1. The quotient merging m0,m1 is endpoint-exact by Theorem 2: a route from the left to y genuinely exists. But those two records have different middle identities. Declaring their particular witnesses compatible changes the explanation, even though the endpoint assertion remains true.

**Permitted positive repair.** Retain the intermediary identity (or a faithful key for its equivalence class under independently warranted identity), semantic sort, scope/respect and record version; require a warranted bridge when two encodings use different keys for the same item. Distinct derivation identifiers can remain side by side. When only endpoint existence is requested, the smaller profile repair can suffice. The task decides which contract is required.

Typed proof uniqueness offers no shortcut around this distinction. S08's theorem compares evidence at an already fixed identity type, and its labelled data remain distinct. S09's transport consumes an identity premise. Neither warrants an identity of two productive intermediaries from equality of their common original provider. This report adds no rule to HasE/HasJ, no extraction function, and no metaphysical interpretation of proof-polynomial identity.

## 6. Unbounded derivative rank under one original source

For every positive integer n, take n distinct intermediary identities and the diagonal relations from §3. The represented transfer matrix over the rationals is I_n, with ordinary rank n. Over Boolean relational multiplication its Boolean factorization rank is also n.

**Ordinary-rank proof.** The columns of I_n are linearly independent: the i-th coordinate of a linear combination is its i-th coefficient. Any exact linear factorization through a d-dimensional intermediate space has rank at most d, so d≥n.

**Boolean-rank proof.** A Boolean factorization of I_n by d intermediate labels covers its 1-entries by d rectangles contained in the diagonal. A rectangle containing (i,i) and (j,j) for i≠j also contains (i,j), an excluded off-diagonal entry. Thus each rectangle covers at most one diagonal entry and d≥n. The identity factorization attains n.

The one-original ownership map still sends every intermediary to g. Its one-label relational composition yields the all-ones matrix, whose ordinary and Boolean ranks are one; it invents all n(n−1) off-diagonal routes. This calculation is about retaining the routing relation, not about production by independent original roots.

**Consequences.**

- A common original does not force a rank-one derivative relation, a one-dimensional explanatory interface, or a single actual secondary act.
- Large interaction/routing rank does not demonstrate several original bearers. At this signature there is no bound on that rank supplied solely by the number one of original providers.
- A small provider catalogue can coexist with a large indispensable derivative provenance record. The minimum profile count is n for the diagonal endpoint contract; the witness-identity contract also needs n distinguishable middle identities.
- The converse numerical reading is equally unsupported: low rank does not establish a common original. Rank is computed from a declared relation or array, not from the independent philosophical warrant for its original source.

Only the first three claims are accompanied here by the displayed unbounded family. The final point is a typing/warrant restriction, not a newly constructed many-original metaphysical rival. No scalar likelihoods, cognitive geometry, fundamental-particle ontology or fact-mereology enters the proof.

## 8. What changes and what stays inherited

**New bounded usable result:** a compositional application that distinguishes original-provider identity, derivative routing-profile identity, and actual intermediary identity; necessary-and-sufficient preservation tests; endpoint versus witness repair; the unbounded rank/memory consequence; an executable application to the exact inherited provision objects.

**Inherited:** original actuality, same-bearer necessity, essential independence, actual common-source warrant, source-native productive distinctions, the actual-use/provision join, the same-token overlap rule, mathematical fibre/continuation/join ideas, and proof-identity/provenance separation. Defeasible acceptance of the common-source conclusion is not downgraded because its mathematical application is conditional on a faithful representation.

The practical payoff is an exact rule for later synthesis: common original coverage may be carried across the whole field while secondary explanatory witnesses are composed only on the identity relation their own subject matter requires. Reducing one kind of plurality does not erase the other, and retaining the latter does not multiply originals.

### Root-counting safeguard for the joined programme

The same safeguard applies when these records meet the likelihood-provenance front. If two acquisition events have the same original productive provider, it does not follow that one is a copy of the other. If their calibrated likelihood vectors coincide, that also does not identify the acquisition events. S10 already proves that named withdrawal and activation can distinguish such otherwise equal posterior states, while S11 supplies the copy-versus-acquisition distinction. The present report therefore recommends three separately typed keys when those three questions are asked: original provider; secondary actual intermediary; authenticated evidence acquisition. A relation between them must be represented explicitly instead of inferred from the shared word "root". This recommendation is a semantic boundary, not an instruction to retain unnecessary duplicate storage or a claim to have identified actual independent historical witnesses.
