# De se knowledge, original perfection, and non-shareable sovereignty

8 October 2026 UTC. Focused T20 continuation. Earlier assessments and artifacts are unchanged. This is a written proof and premise audit, not a new action-choice simulation, full metaphysical possibility certificate, integration, closure, or research-duration certification.

## Result first

**Complete truthful first-person self-knowledge does not, by itself, turn the strongest retained necessarily concordant two-original interpretation into a contradiction.** A precise centered enrichment preserves its bearers, intrinsic resources, policies, actual acts, joint productive support, and all old non-epistemic facts. Each correctly knows its own identity and agency, the distinct peer's agency, and the absence of individual whole provision in the actual joint product. Neither must falsely think it is the other or falsely claim exclusive whole production.

That is a semantic enrichment. The retained code supplies owned acts, subject-indexed ends, a reason-to-choice policy and factive forecasts; it does not verify that recognized first-person content is the agent's action-guiding basis. A stronger practical de se interpretation is an additional, explicitly conditional bridge below, not a consequence of singleton epistemic accessibility.

This is a positive reduced-signature nonentailment result. It is not a verdict that two fully perfected original sovereigns are metaphysically possible. The retained account still lacks independent warrant for its original-peer co-manifestation, complete created inventory, and classification as wholly appropriate original efficacy. Appending a predicate called `Perfect` would not repair those omissions.

**Unrestricted sovereignty can yield uniqueness when its independently justified scope is strong enough.** The relevant extra premises can be stated without placing “there is only one” in a definition:

1. Universal mastery over every distinct bearer, together with immunity to another bearer's ultimate mastery; or
2. Actual whole-origin adequacy for one shared productive history, together with actual proper unborrowed exercises and the inherited subject/accounting exclusion; or
3. Uniform effective control of incompatible eligible targets, together with compossibility of the particular guaranteeing choices.

The third is already an inherited agency-logic result and does not defeat necessary concord unless its choice-compossibility and eligible-target premises are independently warranted. The first two do not require possible unwise conflict, but their authority or whole-origin scope is substantive. De se knowledge neither supplies that scope nor makes it dispensable.

The strongest new role for self-knowledge is **recognition followed by perfect practical conformance**: if joint original completion really is a constitutive pure defect, each agent knows that defect as its own and cannot knowingly enact it under an independently warranted conformance norm. The live question remains the defect classification and applicability of that norm. This is a more serious proposal than a false-self-description argument, but it is not a new derivation of the classification.

## 2. The centered distinction: whose truth is being known?

Let M be an interpreted modal structure with worlds W, the inherited rigid outer-domain bearer set B, an existence predicate E(w,b), and a fixed metaphysical accessibility relation. Its agency, productive-support, intrinsic-inherence, reasons, and receipt relations are already interpreted. For this modal enrichment take G to be the designated agents A and B, both present throughout the represented modal range: E(w,A) and E(w,B) hold at every represented world. This stipulation represents their persistence; it does not establish their eligibility as necessarily perfected originals. The necessary-center restriction avoids silently discarding modal alternatives in which an arbitrary contingent subject would not exist.

Use centers C = {(w,b): E(w,b) and b∈G}. At center (w,b), the term `I` denotes b. Bearer variables and ordinary quantifiers still range over the original B, with E used where required. They do not range only over the current center. Thus centering does not delete a foreign supplier, peer, or relevant non-agent resource.

For a rigid agent a∈G, the operator `@a φ` evaluates φ at (w,a). This makes perspective shifts explicit. Claims about other contingent subjects can still use ordinary bearer variables; shifts to their centers would require an additional existence-sensitive clause not used here. At (w,A):

- `I=A` is true and `I=B` is false.
- `@B(I=B)` is true.
- `@B(I=A)` is false.

B knows A's self-ascription by knowing `@A(I=A)`, not by falsely self-ascribing `I=A` at B's own center. This distinction is not weaker truthfulness. There is no truth at A's center that B must regard as a truth at B's center. Omniscience about every center and literal identity with every center are different requirements.

K below always denotes the current center's own knowledge. Another agent's de se cognition is expressed by an explicit center switch, such as `@B K(I=B)`. Its factive consequence is `@B(I=B)`, not `I=B` at A's center. No untyped cross-agent axiom `K_B φ → φ` with an unchanged observer-centered indexical is assumed.

The proper-act distinction is preserved similarly. A knows “this is my intrinsically issued exercise” of A's act; B knows the corresponding truth of B's act and knows that A's act is A's. Both may know the same complete external event and the same joint support relation. That does not make their intrinsic acts one token, convert B's first-person awareness into A's, or establish shared original ownership of one proper exercise.

No claim here concerns human phenomenology, embodiment, or the assistant's experience. If a theory demands that one subject numerically possess another's very first-person experience, that is an additional constitutive/identity theory. It is not contained in semantic knowledge of all true centered propositions.

## 3. A conservative-enrichment theorem, with its limits exposed

### Theorem 1: full factive centered knowledge need not change the old ontology

Start with an epistemic-free base language L0 interpreted in M, or a base whose already asserted epistemic facts are compatible with the expansion described below. Extend the language by `I`, center switches `@a` for a∈G, and a self-knowledge operator K. At each center c let the epistemic possibility set be exactly {c}. Lift metaphysical accessibility by `(w,b) R* (v,b)` iff `w R v`, preserving the same bearer through the modal step. Metaphysical accessibility is retained separately; it is not replaced by epistemic identity or an unmarked switch of subject.

Then the expansion M* has these properties:

1. Every old L0 sentence has exactly its old truth value at w, at every existing center over w.
2. For every formula φ of the extended, ordinary compositional language, M*,c satisfies Kφ iff it satisfies φ. Hence factivity, completeness, and positive/negative introspection hold at every center, including truths about one's identity, own agency, other centers, and the metaphysical modalities.
3. E, the same global bearers, original-resource relations, proper acts, selected policies, output, original support, and all original modal quantifiers are unchanged.

**Proof.** The reduct of M* to L0 is M. Induction on L0 formulas proves item 1; the modal step uses the unchanged metaphysical relation and bearer assignment. The semantic clause for K universally checks φ at epistemically accessible centers. Since the sole such center is c, Kφ is equivalent to φ, proving item 2, including iterated formulas. The newly added semantic clauses do not modify any base-domain element or base relation, proving item 3. Center switching only changes the distinguished subject of evaluation. It never changes the underlying world's bearer identities. □

This is a standard model-theoretic construction, used here to isolate the new proposed bridge. The inherited cognition work already has a generic identity-knowledge lemma; the present increment is its explicitly centered, shift-aware application and agency-scope audit, not another general factivity theorem. It is not a new theory of omniscience. It also does not override an old false-belief claim, an old assertion of ignorance, or an already fixed de se accessibility relation. If the old theory contains such incompatible claims, it may have no expansion of this kind. The retained rival's ordinary complete factivity and primitive-knower interpretation contain no such contrary self-ignorance requirement.

Theorem 1 also does not establish the actual intrinsic realization or metaphysical unreceivedness of knowing. The retained primitive-cognition interpretation supplies the separate structural account of those attributes. Its warrant obligations remain. Treating cognition primitively is not a special defect when the source permits primitive original knowledge, but semantic truth-completeness is not itself a proof that the primitive is really instantiated.

**Corollary.** At this semantic signature, a non-epistemic uniqueness conclusion cannot become entailed solely by adding the displayed full centered knowledge conditions: any reduced counterinterpretation has an expanded counterinterpretation. This does not establish the additional practical-basing relation between recognized first-person reasons and acts. Conversely, an inconsistent old original-perfection theory is not repaired by centering. In particular, no conclusion follows here about the consistency of a full perfection norm not actually satisfied by the retained base account.

### Epistemically loaded sovereignty is not a new shortcut

Suppose S(I) says “I possess whole original adequacy for this undertaking.” Complete truthful knowledge gives KS(I) iff S(I). Adding KS(I) as a premise therefore adds S(I)'s content; it does not derive it from generic self-knowledge. If the old actual support is {A,B} with no individual whole provider, both agents know the corresponding negative fact. They cannot truthfully know S(I) under that interpretation.

Thus a proposed contradiction based on “each knows I alone am sovereign” must justify the exclusivity-bearing content independently. The contradiction cannot be attributed to the first-person mode of knowledge itself.

## 5. Exact contradiction routes and where their extra premises enter

### Route A: universal mastery plus unassailability

Let M(a,b) mean ultimate mastery in a fixed respect: a has overriding authority over b's original determination in that respect. This must be an independently interpreted control/authority relation, not merely “a is greater” or “a is the unique sovereign.” Let P(a) mean that a qualifies for the proposed original-perfection profile.

The premises are:

1. A≠B, P(A), and P(B).
2. Universal-other scope: for any qualified a and any existing b≠a, M(a,b).
3. Unassailability: for any qualified b and any existing a≠b, not M(a,b).

Taking a=A and b=B in 2 gives M(A,B); taking b=B and a=A in 3 gives its negation. No possible quarrel, contrary choice, chronological priority, epistemic ignorance, or arbitrary power to do contradictions is used. This is an at-most-one conclusion; existence is separate.

The source gives a substantive motivation for this strong package. Akmaliyya canonical L264 puts omniscience and omnipotence together with being unassailable and overpowering everything other than Him; L265–266 then state the exclusive attribution. The displayed M-relation is a proposed fixed-respect regimentation, not a claim that the Arabic itself defines a formal overriding-choice relation. However, truthful self-recognition at L260 is not an independent derivation of universal-other scope. The relevant philosophical burden is why each proposed underived perfect bearer must have this all-distinct-bearers authority, rather than only unreceived efficacy throughout the created order. Efficient nonreceipt alone does not establish either the universal authority premise or immunity to every merely possible domination.

This route answers the broad sovereignty question honestly: **if “unrestricted” really includes ultimate authority over every distinct bearer, and “independent” includes immunity to another's ultimate authority, plurality fails even under necessary concord.** The theorem itself does not vindicate that normative/metaphysical interpretation. Calling any limitation “essential to this individual's kind” also does not automatically answer the source's comparative purity argument; the bearer-eligibility report already explains why that evasion is insufficient.

### Route B: actual complete origin and proper unborrowed exercise

The inherited act-accounting theorem uses sources S, proper modes M, and targets X. Its relevant independent premises are:

- Actual productive witness: each complete original undertaking for x includes a proper unborrowed exercise of that alleged source.
- History closure: a bearer claiming complete original accounting for x accounts for every mode genuinely belonging to x's actual full productive origin.
- Unborrowed-exercise exclusion: if m is properly t's unborrowed exercise and s supplies its ultimate productive account in that same respect, then s=t.

If A and B both have complete original accounting for the same x, choose B's actual proper witness m. History closure puts m in A's accounting, and the exclusion gives A=B. This is the already retained `uniqueness_of_history_complete`, not a new theorem of this report. Self-knowledge is unnecessary to its deduction.

Its application must remain properly typed. B's intrinsic original act is not automatically an additional created effect. A complete created-output inventory cannot include it merely because one wants uniqueness. The affirmative whole-origin rationale instead says that when x's actual receiving mode genuinely consists in reception through B's exercise, that efficacy is an indispensable productive prerequisite of the claimed full origin. The Eleventh article defends this stronger target-relative coverage while distinguishing it from endpoint sufficiency and from identity of the receiving mode with B's intrinsic act. That is the legitimate route to assess. The rival denies that each participant claims, or is required to have, that individually exhaustive account.

The exclusion also remains a substantive interpretation of proper original exercise. Shared event or power is insufficient to refute it; a genuine constitutive original co-ownership proposal must be assessed rather than defined away. The current rival need not claim co-ownership of one act: it retains two proper acts and a joint external manifestation. Its main disputed bridge is from perfected participation to individual full-origin completeness.

## 6. The strongest specifically de se proposal: recognition and conformance

The inherited anchored-source interface already separates `Classification` from `Conformance`, and its controls omit each independently. A genuinely epistemic refinement can weaken direct conformance to the following own-agency norm:

N: A perfectly practically ordered original never enacts an undertaking that it correctly recognizes de se as a constitutive pure defect of its own original agency.

The other premises are:

C: This irreducibly joint original undertaking is a constitutive pure defect of each relevant participant's appropriate original agency.

K: Each participant has complete truthful de se knowledge, including the correct normative status of its actual undertaking.

A: Such a participant actually enacts that undertaking.

From C and K, the participant knows “this undertaking is a constitutive pure defect of my original agency.” N excludes enacting it, contradicting A. This is a valid recognition–conformance argument. If the conformance norm is only about knowing enactment, self-knowledge is a genuine inferential link; it is not idle ornament.

But K does not prove C or N. C is precisely the narrowed original-efficacy classification that the strongest source and strongest rival dispute. N must concern an independently established pure defect, not merely a smaller product, unexercised standing power, a newly attained fitting end, or a freely chosen created means. The source's timely nonexercise, successive wise acts, and object-conditioned knowledge controls reject that overbroad reading. A relative cooperative good does not by itself vindicate the undertaking; equally, merely calling it relative does not automatically forbid choosing it. The claim that its mode violates constitutive self-sufficiency must do the work.

This formulation also avoids attributing vanity, rivalry, or a human need for exclusive credit to a perfect original. A reason to reject foreign original completion would have to concern genuine adequacy and the purity of agency, not hurt pride at another's presence. Conversely, if actual individual whole efficacy is independently shown to be an unqualified original perfection, claiming perfect knowledge while failing to recognize one's joint shortfall cannot protect the rival.

The direct source return supports the exact distinction. Akmaliyya L258–274 distinguishes pure perfection from mixed relative advantage, treats truthful disclosure of one's real attributes as perfection, and evaluates the falsity of others' claims. It supplies a positive universal-sovereignty conception, not a theorem that the mere occurrence of the first-person word “I” entails that conception. `wise-determination-continuation` further establishes the source's recognition of actual object-conditioned knowing as perfection without treating the object as its efficient donor. Transporting that epistemic relation into joint productive completion still needs the separate agency argument.

## 7. Earned conclusion and stopping boundary for this operation

The exact new discrimination is between three possibilities:

1. **Truthful awareness of the retained reduced original-agent facts:** semantic centered knowledge can be added without contradiction and without false claims of sole provenance. An action-guiding first-person role is a further conditional interpretation, rather than something supplied by the evaluation index alone. Neither interpretation duplicates subjects or erases their acts.
2. **Truthful awareness of an independently justified universal sovereignty or whole-origin norm:** each knows that stronger content. The impossibility then follows from the authority/adequacy premises at their defended scope; self-knowledge supplies transparency, and can mediate practical conformance.
3. **“I alone am sovereign” inserted as the required self-knowledge content without that independent justification:** factivity simply imports the contested exclusivity-bearing claim. That is not a new uniqueness argument.

The source's positive case for one comprehensive original source retains its saved defeasible strength, including its comparative-disqualification answer. This report neither reduces it to an arbitrary definition nor upgrades it to a new universal deduction from thinner premises. The rival retains reduced-signature consistency and the conditional means explanation; it does not receive a full original-perfection or unrestricted-universal-sovereignty certificate.

What would move the remaining philosophical question is an independently defended all-distinct-bearers authority scope or constitutive pure-efficacy classification, or a warranted original-peer ontology that addresses those exact norms. Another local factivity checker, another action table, a perfection flag, or a claimed ability to choose unwise contradictions would not do so. No Lean execution was needed or performed for this written discriminator, and no source warrant is inferred from a kernel or from finite consistency.
