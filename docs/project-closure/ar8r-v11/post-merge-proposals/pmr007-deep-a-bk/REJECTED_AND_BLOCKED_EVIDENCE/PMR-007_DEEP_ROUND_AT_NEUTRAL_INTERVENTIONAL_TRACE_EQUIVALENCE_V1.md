## 2. Finite controlled weighted systems

For each candidate `h∈{A,R}`, let:

- `S_h` be a finite hidden-state set of size `n_h`;
- `U` be a common finite action/intervention alphabet;
- `O` be a common finite neutral observation alphabet;
- `alpha_h` be the initial row distribution;
- `M^h_{u,o}` be a nonnegative `n_h×n_h` matrix;
- `sum_o M^h_{u,o}` be row-stochastic for each action `u`;
- `1_h` be the all-ones column.

The probability of an action-observation word

\[
w=(u_1,o_1)\cdots(u_k,o_k)
\]

is

\[
p_h(w)=\alpha_hM^h_{u_1,o_1}\cdots M^h_{u_k,o_k}\mathbf 1_h.
\]

Actions may be chosen adaptively from previous observations by one common policy that does not read the candidate label.

## 3. NIBE-1A — open-loop trace equivalence iff adaptive nonidentifiability

The following are equivalent:

1. `p_A(w)=p_R(w)` for every finite action-observation word `w`;
2. every finite-horizon deterministic adaptive policy induces the same transcript distribution under `A` and `R`;
3. every finite-horizon randomized adaptive policy whose private randomness is candidate-independent induces the same transcript distribution under `A` and `R`.

### Proof

`1→2`: under a deterministic policy, each transcript fixes its action sequence as a function of earlier observations. Its probability is exactly one of the word probabilities, so equality transfers transcript by transcript.

`2→1`: if a word has unequal probability, use the open-loop policy that issues its action sequence. The specified observation sequence has unequal probability.

`1→3`: condition on the policy's private random seed. Each deterministic component has equal transcript law; averaging preserves equality. `3→2` is immediate.

Thus no amount of adaptive neutral querying can distinguish models that agree on the full registered trace language.

## 4. NIBE-1B — finite linear-algebra witness bound

Form the combined difference system:

\[
\alpha=(\alpha_A,-\alpha_R),\qquad
M_{u,o}=\operatorname{diag}(M^A_{u,o},M^R_{u,o}),\qquad
\beta=(\mathbf1_A,\mathbf1_R)^T.
\]

Then

\[
p_A(w)-p_R(w)=\alpha M_w\beta.
\]

Let

\[
V=\operatorname{span}\{\alpha M_w:w\in(U\times O)^*\}
\]

and let `r=dim(V)≤n_A+n_R`. The reachable-row chain generated by words of length at most `k` either grows in dimension or is already invariant under every `M_{u,o}`. Therefore `V` is spanned by rows generated by words of length at most `r-1`, and in particular at most `n_A+n_R-1`.

Hence:

\[
p_A(w)=p_R(w)\text{ for all }|w|\le r-1
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
p_A(w)=p_R(w)\text{ for all finite }w.
\]

### Proof

Let `V_k` be the span of rows `alpha M_w` with `|w|≤k`. If `V_k=V_{k+1}`, then `V_k` is closed under right multiplication by every alphabet matrix, so `V_j=V_k` for all later `j`. Starting from dimension at most one, the chain can strictly increase at most `r-1` times. If every generator row through that bound annihilates `beta`, then every row in `V` annihilates `beta`, so all word differences vanish.

## 5. NIBE-1C — exact discriminator and burden relocation

A neutral adaptive discriminator exists iff some finite registered action-observation word has unequal probability. When it exists, a witness occurs by horizon at most `r-1`.

If no witness occurs, Deep AQ `GATE E` is blocked for the entire registered neutral intervention algebra. Breaking parity then requires at least one of:

## 6. Countermodels and positive controls

### AT-CM-HIDDEN-PERSONAL-TAG

Personal and impersonal expansions share identical `alpha` and `M_{u,o}` on every neutral variable but differ on an unobserved `Personal` predicate. No adaptive neutral protocol identifies the tag.

### AT-CM-ENDPOINT-SAME-TRAJECTORY-DIFFERENT

Two systems have equal endpoint success but differ on one intermediate observation word. The finite witness distinguishes learning mechanism or trajectory without establishing proper function or personality.

### AT-CM-REPRESENTATION-ERASURE

A common channel merges the only two distinguishing observation symbols. The represented systems become trace-equivalent. This is information loss, not evidence that the raw models were equal.

### AT-CM-CANDIDATE-DEPENDENT-POLICY

The policy reads the candidate label and selects a revealing action. This is a changed experiment with target leakage, not a neutral adaptive protocol.

### AT-CM-VERSION-DRIFT

A source or runtime version change alters which actions and matrices are admissible. Equality at version `v` does not transfer automatically to `v'`.

### AT-CM-INCOMPLETE-INTERVENTION-ALGEBRA

The models agree on all registered actions but differ under an unregistered causal intervention. Current nonidentifiability is experiment-relative, not absolute metaphysical identity.
