## What is inherited and what is proved here

Theorem V supplies the established unnormalization, Nussbaum–Carleman, fixed-frequency Fourier approximation, compact-test evolution, inverse-weight identification, initial recovery, and law-fibre classification route. This packet writes those steps out to verify that cumulative damping supplies every required bound, instead of assuming the weaker hypothesis fits that route automatically.

The new algebraic mechanism is a finite ordered backward Volterra expansion whose remainder is nonnegative on polynomial squares. A cumulative radial SOS certificate controls its full partial sums. Monotonicity of each unnormalized radial moment supplies uniform interior Gaussian bounds without regularity of the certificate constants or an SOS factorization. The rank-one polynomial and rotating examples separate cumulative from instantaneous radial certificates.

The polynomial-automorphism chain rule and pushback determinacy argument are inherited from the transport result. Replacing that result's instantaneous pulled-back hypothesis with the proved cumulative hypothesis is the extension; the old coordinate transfer is not recounted as new.
