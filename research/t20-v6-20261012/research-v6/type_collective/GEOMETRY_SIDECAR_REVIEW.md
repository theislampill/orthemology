# Independent review of the finite-sidecar / continuous-fibre corollary

Reviewed 2026-10-11. Target: `../root_math/FINITE_SIDECAR_GEOMETRY_BOUNDARY.md`, 37-line version read in full.

The argument is valid conditional on the cited ambient Fisher norm formula. Fixing the original signature makes a sufficient supplement injective on W: each positive known numerator divided by the retained norm recovers w_j, and normalization gives the remaining coordinate. Finite-valued exact sidecars cannot be injective when the positive-dimensional affine-open fibre is uncountable. A continuous injection into fewer than d real coordinates contradicts invariance of domain after embedding in R^d. Coordinates on ker Q attain d. The d=0 case is handled correctly.

Minor clarification requested: explicitly assume q is a normalized probability vector; then column normalization and Qw=q imply sum w=1. The source note currently leaves this implicit in “target”.

The distinction between the ambient gradients and gradients restricted to the fixed-P fibre is indispensable and correctly stated. So are the continuity, exact-preservation, fixed-rank/fixed-fibre, and optional-contract limitations. Arbitrary discontinuous encodings into one real number are outside the asserted lower bound. No inference from numerical source weights to empirical warrant follows.

This review examines the displayed corollary proof. It does not newly examine either controlling source body, and does not claim a fresh kernel proof or a general global dimension theorem.
