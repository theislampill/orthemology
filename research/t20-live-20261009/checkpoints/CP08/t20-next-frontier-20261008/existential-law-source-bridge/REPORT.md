# The autonomous essential-fact rival and the bearer bridge

8 October 2026. Targeted successor to `global-explanation-rival/REPORT.md` and the inherited original-capacity/explanatory-warrant inquiries. T20 remains active. No new tranche, integration, formal countermodel, metaphysical-world certificate, or closure is claimed. Earlier files are unchanged.

## Finding

The held Arabic source gives a substantive **particular-quality nonpriority argument**: a concrete subject is not assembled from antecedently existing universal predicates or its own already-inhering attributes. It also gives explicit distinctions between a conceived essence and an actual thing, between inferential order and real constitution, and between constitutive concomitance and efficient production. These are stronger and more precise than the slogan that laws cannot cause.

Those arguments do not yet refute the strongest rival now under examination: an actually obtaining, autonomous, nonobjectual qualitative essential fact which genuinely grounds concrete existence. The extra premise required is an **exhaustion and dependence claim**: every such candidate explanatory fact must either be a mental representation or have its reality in determinate subject–attribute instances, with a dependence on those instances that disqualifies it as their autonomous existential ground. Ibn Taymiyyah's stated ontology rejects a free-standing universal or quality; the read arguments do not independently establish that the modern predicate-functor fact proposal falls under that exhaustive classification.

This is chiefly an exact application and duplicate diagnosis. The universal/particular distinction, resource realisation, noncausal-ground/productive-source distinction, and necessary-reception control were already present. What becomes sharper is the collision between **essence as antecedent domain specification** and **essential predication as a feature of determinate reality**. No new Arabic premise closing that collision was found in the bounded rereading. Exact duplicate credit: the inherited `CONTINUOUS_NOTES.md`, range4 Window009 (S003116–144), already records the particular-quality argument; range5 Windows011–012 already record the epistemic-middle and antecedent-accident objections at645–653. Their comparison with this sharpened autonomous-essential-fact proposal is the present operation, not rediscovery of those arguments.

## 1. Preserve the rival being tested

Dasgupta's *Metaphysical Rationalism* distinguishes a brute fact, which is apt for grounding but has none, from an autonomous fact, which is not apt for grounding. The analogy between nominal definitions and real definitions is a positive motivation for that category, not permission to call any preferred stopping point autonomous. In §4, core essence is supposed to specify what the domain is in the first place; it is expressly not merely a special way of predicating a property of an independently given domain. Essentialist autonomy remains a working hypothesis, and neither ground nor essence receives an exhaustive defence in this paper. [D, journal383–389.]

Section9 separately assumes that at least some grounds of an existence fact are existence facts before deriving an essentially existing particular. Footnote44 offers a way to resist that premise: existence facts of particulars could be grounded in qualitative predicate-functor facts, with essence statements about being F whose content need not itself concern any individual's existence. The note says this could satisfy the PSR without any thing whose existence is part of or grounded in its own essence. [D, journal415, note44; §9 context credited to the main source worker and parent.]

The present target is consequently not an imaginary general concept called Being, a mathematical solution, or a disguised individual deity. Nor is Dasgupta being credited with Billon's entire law-relative infinitist programme. The main source worker's completed whole-paper reading reports that Dasgupta rejects nonterminating grounding chains as PSR-sufficient. His proposed autonomous base and Billon's explanatory-law treatment must therefore be assessed for compatible roles rather than combined into a doctrine neither author states.

For this Arabic test, the live proposal is conditional: suppose an autonomous qualitative basis actually obtains, has genuine existential explanatory force, and grounds dependent bearers and capacities without itself being an unreceived concrete bearer. Can the held arguments defeat that basis without assuming the bearer bridge?

## 2. What the universal passages actually establish

### A. Common predication is not a numerically common concrete item

Ṣafadiyya S001153–155 defends real resemblance between things and a common intelligible meaning while denying one numerically shared external humanity in Zayd and another person. Its formulations include:

> بل كل موجود في الخارج فإنه مختص بذاته وصفاته القائمة به لا يشاركه غيره فيها ألبتة

Every external existent, on the author's account, has its own particular self and inhering attributes. The passage opposes both reified universal objects and reducing genuine similarity to a mere shared word. It therefore must not be reported as the rejection of all common intelligibility or general truths. [SF1,153–155.]

S001183–191 distinguishes the natural, logical and intellectual universal and argues that externally existing instances are determinate. Even provisionally granting separated Platonic or immanent Aristotelian universals does not make a universal identical with the particular necessary existent. Its broadest explicit claim is:

> فلا بد على كل تقدير من إثبات موجود واجب بنفسه مغاير لهذا المطلق

But the argument here concerns identifying the necessary existent with unrestricted common existence. A rich qualitative existential law is not automatically that same unrestricted universal. Its explanatory content could be highly specific while not naming a particular bearer. The criticism of indiscriminate Being does not by itself settle that proposal. [SF1,181–191.]

S003556–590 confirms the positive distinction. A natural universal may correctly be said to exist externally insofar as what is conceived corresponds to determinate things. It is not universal externally. The text explicitly differentiates reality, cognition, speech and writing, and distinguishes conceptual division into species from dividing an actual whole into parts. The truth of a representation's correspondence does not identify the representation with its object. [SF3,556–590.]

**Application:** A mentally entertained law-token requires a thinker; that does not establish that the law's truth or explanatory force depends on being entertained. An external universal/type, a particular obtaining fact, and a cognitive representation are different proposals. A proof targeting only the first two as construed by the source cannot silently classify every modern nonobjectual fact as one of them.

### B. A quality-instance cannot be an antecedent constituent of the same bearer

The most useful additional precision comes from S003132–139, continuously read with111–149. The source argues that 'rational animal' and 'laughing animal' alter what is internal to a conception without rearranging the actual human's constitution. The particular human is not several substances corresponding to body, growth, sensation, voluntary motion and reason, nor a substance assembled from a bundle of prior accidents. The exact nonpriority formulation is:

> ومعلوم أن صفة الموصوف لا تكون متقدمة عليه لا في العقل ولا في الوجود

The follow-through preserves the reality of the attributes and rejects their alleged priority as constituent parts. S001198–215 gives the same dilemma more fully: if the proposed antecedent constituents are accidents, they already inhere in and require the subject; if separate substances, the human plainly is not the asserted multiplicity of subjects. [SF3,132–139; SF1,198–215.]

This supplies a real conditional objection. If a candidate existential ground is **the already-instantiated F-ness of this very bearer**, its role as an inhering property-instance cannot simply be promoted into prior autonomous constitution of that same bearer's whole existence. The direction of the proposed explanatory relation requires justification.

It does not settle the alternative where the proposed ground is a qualitative fact whose ontology deliberately does not presuppose that bearer. Calling that fact an 'attribute' in the source's sense would already make the disputed reduction. The argument has force against a wrongly typed property-instance; it is not yet a general demonstration that nonobjectual facts are impossible.

## 3. Real definition, core essence, and the exact new comparison

S003645–649 accepts a definition's role in distinguishing its object and mentions both definition according to a name and definition according to reality. It rejects automatically treating genus and difference as external constituents. It also distinguishes an inferential middle from an intervening constituent or cause in the thing:

> وهذا فرق يرجع إلى علم العالم بها لا إلى حقيقة هي علمها في نفس الأمر

The received wording is retained, not silently repaired. The inherited S36 scan-control record reports modern printed544/PDF551 as «لا إلى حقيقة هي (على ما) في نفس الأمر», with an apparatus discussion of cramped manuscript letters and the old «علمها». No new visual collation was performed here; the awkward wording is not made to bear the conclusion by itself. The surrounding contrast is clear: a difference in how the observer knows necessary predicates is not by itself an external priority relation. S003652–653 returns to the objection that inhering accidents cannot be their subject's antecedent constituent parts. [SF3,645–658.]

This is the strongest source-faithful challenge to Dasgupta's positive analogy:

1. A nominal definition can settle a question about a term without being proved from axioms.
2. The absence of such a proof obligation is not yet an account of an external reality's constitution.
3. Likewise, how a description packages essential and derivative predicates does not determine the direction of real existential grounding.
4. Therefore the move from definition-like autonomy to **existence-grounding authority** needs an independently credible metaphysics of core essence.

This is an application argument, not an attribution of a discussion of Dasgupta to Ibn Taymiyyah. It limits the analogy; it does not refute the modern account. Dasgupta expressly says autonomy is not defined epistemically, and §4 denies the model of essence as a special property-possession mode in an already given domain. His proposal is thus not answered merely by pointing out that two nominal descriptions differ. The hard question is whether an objective, domain-specifying qualitative essence has the alleged autonomous reality and existential role.

The Arabic source accepts necessary real attributes, so its criticism cannot fairly become 'there are no real necessities or essences'. Conversely, accepting real necessary attributes does not amount to accepting a nonobjectual autonomous fact as an antecedent ground of concrete existence. The two positions differ at this specific direction and category of constitution.

## 4. Necessary constitution does not supply efficient production

Akmaliyya120–134 distinguishes an independently existent subject's necessary attributes from external supplementation. It states that neither a wholly attributeless subject nor a quality with no subject exists externally. Its positive formulation is:

> فما هو قائم بنفسه فلا بد له من صفة وما كان صفة فلا بد له من قائم بنفسه متصف به

That is an explicit bearer principle for **attributes**. It is not a demonstrated theorem about every conceivable category of fact. Nor does the source collapse inherence into production:

> فإن الرب ليس بفاعل لصفاته اللازمة له

The same passage treats necessary concomitance as genuine without making either side efficiently manufacture the other. Ṣafadiyya S001175–180 and202–203 likewise distinguish the productive cause from the receptive/substrate relation and from a subject's not existing without itself or its indispensable attributes. [A,120–134; SF1,166–180,198–215.]

Consequently, granting a real bearer for an explanatory item would still not prove that this bearer efficiently produces the dependent field. An autonomous necessary ground, a necessarily constituted concrete being and an actual efficient producer are separate conclusions. Calling a law 'productive' would require evidence of the productive relation, rather than merely its explanatory adequacy or necessary holding.

The Akmaliyya's perfection argument remains available at its independently established scope. L035–046 supplies an a-fortiori argument for the perfected necessary being, a donor argument, and a rejection of circular or endlessly borrowed perfection. But L045's rejection of an all-received series is the inherited completion commitment; repeating it as a fresh answer to the autonomous-law case adds no warrant. And the donor argument applies once a giving/source relation is secured. It cannot obtain that relation merely by renaming a nonobjectual ground a giver. The earlier bearer-eligibility report already handles the positive comparative argument and its limits; nothing here withdraws that appraisal. [A,35–46.]

## 5. Why the knowledge/production argument is not a shortcut

S003374–390 quotes the attributed Avicennian identification of intellect, life, power and will, then disputes it. Ibn Taymiyyah distinguishes knower, knowing and known; argues that practical knowledge by itself is only a condition of activity; and says the truth of knowledge must fit its object. His succinct formulation is:

> فالعلم شرط في العمل لا أنه موجب للعمل بنفسه

The argument is stronger than simply declaring that an abstraction cannot cause. It asks what differentiates this realised order, and what makes the knowing subject able and willing to produce it. [SF3,374–390.]

But the present rival does not identify essential fact with anybody's act of knowing. No inference from 'conceiving does not produce' to 'an autonomous noncognitive fact cannot ground' is licensed. Nor does the source's temporal argument against an eternally complete efficient cause automatically refute an atemporal grounding explanation of a temporal history. Those are different relations and explanatory targets. This chapter can test a rival that equates intellectual content with productive power; it does not eliminate all existential-law ontology.

## 6. Modal and source-faithfulness guards

- 'Necessarily some actuality exists' is not 'this same particular exists necessarily'. Even a genuinely existence-entailing essence need not yet select a fixed original bearer.
- Necessary possibility is not actual instantiation. A law's necessarily permitting or licensing a nonempty history is weaker than its actually holding and determining one.
- A law-relative or near-world determination result is not an all-metaphysical-world result. Neither source correspondence nor the word 'essential' supplies that transport automatically.
- Necessary existence is not whole-existence nonreceipt. The inherited necessary-reception rival and same-witness requirements remain in force.
- Constitutive necessity of attributes is not efficient making. This is an explicit source distinction, not a concession forced only by the modern rival.
- An occurring divine act is not simply an externally created creature. S003111–149 distinguishes action from a purported eternally coeval product; its terminology must not be used to classify every emergent act as a created independent object.
- No first temporal act or full least-generated token history is inferred. The inherited beginningless originally supplied case remains a control.

## 7. Exact disposition and what would change it

The source supplies a substantial ontology and several reasoned objections to projecting conceptual composition into external constitution. It has not been reduced here to a bare traditional assertion. At the same time, citing its universal ontology is not an independent verification that the proposed autonomous qualitative fact has no admissible form.

The remaining bridge is exact:

**Does every actually obtaining, existentially explanatory qualitative fact have its ontological basis in determinate bearer–attribute reality, in a way that prevents it from autonomously grounding the existence of all those bearers?**

A positive answer would need a defence of the exhaustion and direction-of-dependence claims, not just a quotation that attributes have subjects. A negative answer would need a genuinely worked qualitative essential basis with existential explanatory authority, not just a new sentence labelled autonomous. The source worker's report that Dasgupta's note44 proposes a route without supplying that full concrete account is consistent with both burdens.

No new independently verified premise completing the bridge has emerged from these held loci. The record therefore remains an unresolved, now better specified source/ontology interface. The inherited T17 arguments and their typed conditional entailments are preserved; this is not a claim that confidence is automatically invariant. The main source worker's retained T17/T18 comparison confirms that autonomous noncausal ground and the withheld exhaustive bearer application were already acknowledged, so this is not a newly discovered logical gap. The stronger positive qualitative motivation nevertheless makes universal productive-bearer exhaustiveness less secure than it would be against a merely descriptive-law rival. Since these Arabic arguments do not answer that motivation at its strongest scope, confidence in unconditional conclusions resting on such exhaustiveness should be reduced accordingly. The conditional same-bearer theorem and the common-source comparison restricted to eligible agents remain intact. This report neither certifies the rival's metaphysical possibility nor upgrades the source's rejection into a new universal impossibility proof.
