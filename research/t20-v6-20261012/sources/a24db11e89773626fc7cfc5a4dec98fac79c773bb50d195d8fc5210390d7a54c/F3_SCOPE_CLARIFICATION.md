# Clarification of baseline finding F3

The 13:25:12 UTC baseline's F3 is **an unresolved reading issue, not a demonstrated corruption or mistranslation**. Its wording “Arabic ... itself problematic” and “variant/garbled strings” was too categorical if read as establishing textual damage. The actual evidence licenses a narrower caution: the supplied English sentence and electronic Arabic string require interpretation/edition checking before they can bear a new inference.

The literal supplied English at printed p.44/PDF51 is retained: “specification with the essence of volition is inconceivable”. The supplied PDF identity is `bed5565280dcb71b96fc3437cf2f9c07cdc75e6fe6e3798e24fe6bf886cad6ad`. The inspected image confirms that wording; it is not only an extraction artifact.

The current [Arabic hosted001](https://www.islamicbook.ws/ageda/alaqidt-alasfhanit-001.html), line115, reads `لأن التخصيص بذات الإرادة لها ممتنع بصريح العقل`. The immediate preceding argument affirms specification through volition; the wider source distinguishes generic willing from a determinate volition. Those facts do not by themselves fix whether the sentence concerns bare/generic will, volition's own specification, the object's specification, an antecedent for `لها`, or a damaged transmission. No omitted negation is supplied, and no candidate emendation is treated as established.

Follow-up located printed-edition records and a parallel electronic edition, but did not obtain a sound page-image collation of this exact sentence. Waqfeya's Dār al-Minhāj/Saʿawī scan link returned HTTP403; its Rushd scan link and older public Archive links were unavailable through the research tool. No denied scan access was bypassed. The linked [Shamela edition record](https://shamela.ws/book/11248) names a different editor/edition, and its preceding [p.62](https://shamela.ws/book/11248/58) was readable, but the following exact page did not return. The Shamela.org generated PDF is an electronic conversion, not an independently checked printed scan.

**Disposition:** withhold the exact disputed sentence as a load-bearing premise; preserve the literal wording plus interpretive uncertainty. Do not call it an established mistake. The baseline's uniqueness, necessity and local-agency conclusions do not depend on this sentence. F6's actual-versus-possible-nonexistence distinction and F2's spatial-versus-productive scope warning are separate findings and remain unchanged.
