# Essence, existence, and real inseparable attributes: focused source return

## Result and exact limits

The source-faithful reading is **one concrete qualified existent, with genuinely differentiated attributes that do not subsist separately**, not an attribute-free self, and not a collection of independently subsisting agents. Identity of a thing's actual existence with its concrete essence does not imply identity of its knowledge with its power, or identity of an attribute with its bearer. The requested Darʾ passage expressly rejects those identifications immediately after affirming essence/existence identity.

Necessary concomitance is not productive receipt. A condition or implication becomes a productive-dependence premise only after its role is established, not because it is written as a necessary conditional. Conversely, intrinsic qualification is not a license for a second independent original that supplies the first's being or efficacy. These distinctions preserve the project's already explicit safeguards; they do not establish that all current model applications satisfy them, which is the separate semantic auditor's task.

## 1. Majmuʿ al-Fatawa 6:95–101: what is denied and what is affirmed

Source: retained `t20-next-frontier-20261008/bearer-eligibility/sources/majmu-item595.txt`, unvocalized layer L371–395; [public witness](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/22/595/). The prior Akmaliyya reading covered the complete unique epistle6:68–140. This is a rereading, not a new first reading or independent witness. Items591/595/597/607 are not four distinct sources.

### The preceding answer is essential

At6:95–96 (L371–376) he responds to an opponent who alleges that if attributes perfect God, God would be deficient in himself. His reply does not accept the possibility of an actually bare divine essence. Supposing such deprivation is supposing something impossible. The sense of completion by an external entity is distinguished from the sense of possessing intrinsic necessary qualifications.

At6:97 (L381), authorial answer:

«أيريد به بشيء منفصل عنه أم يريد بصفة لوازم ذاته؟ أما الأول فممتنع. وأما الثاني فهو حق ولوازم ذاته لا يمكن وجود ذاته بدونها؛ كما لا يمكن وجودها بدونه وهذا كمال بنفسه لا بشيء مباين لنفسه.»

Close translation: Does he mean something separate from Him, or an attribute among the concomitants of His self? The first is impossible. The second is true: His self cannot exist without its concomitants, nor they without Him; this is perfection through Himself, not through something separate from Himself.

At6:97 (L382):

«إن أريد بالذات المجردة التي يقر بها نفاة الصفات فالصفات زائدة عليها وإن أريد بالذات الذات الموجودة في الخارج فتلك لا تكون موجودة إلا بصفاتها اللازمة. والصفات ليست زائدة على الذات المتصفة بالصفات وإن كانت زائدة على الذات التي يقدر تجردها عن الصفات.»

Here “added to essence” is relative to a denier's mentally stripped conception. It is not an ontological assembly of a bare existing subject plus independently supplied attributes.

### The objection must not become the author's commitment

The6:98 opening (L383) states an objection: if positive/existent attributes subsisted in Him, He would depend on them and they on Him, so He would depend on another. The reply begins atL384. At6:98–100 he denies that an attribute-free self-standing existent has external possibility merely because it can be mentally posited. Mental supposition can concern what cannot exist. At6:99–100 (L388–390), anything standing in itself must be qualified, and an attribute must belong to a qualified bearer. Neither assertion makes an attribute an independent substance.

### The decisive disambiguation

At6:100 (L394), in his own reply:

«ما تعني بالافتقار؟ أتعني أن الذات تكون فاعلة للصفات مبدعة لها أو بالعكس؟ أم تعني التلازم وهو أن لا يكون أحدهما إلا بالآخر؟ فإن عنيت افتقار المفعول إلى الفاعل فهذا باطل فإن الرب ليس بفاعل لصفاته اللازمة له بل لا يلزمه شيء معين من أفعاله ومفعولاته. فكيف تجعل صفاته مفعولة له وصفاته لازمة لذاته ليست من مفعولاته؟ وإن عنيت التلازم فهو حق.»

This rejects productive dependence between God and His **necessary attributes**, while accepting their necessary concomitance. It also distinguishes these from any **particular** divine act or product. Do not turn this into a claim that every particular act-token is eternally essential, or that divine acts are the same category as created effects. The local phrase does not by itself settle a complete theory of successive divine acts.

At6:101 (L395), concomitance is positively ranked as perfection: if the attributes of perfection could be absent, they would not be necessary to Him, and their acquisition would require another. The source reasons from God's intrinsic reality and perfection; it does not license an unrestricted rule that logical necessity supplies actuality or productive independence.

## 2. Darʾ 1:292–295: essence/existence identity explicitly coexists with multiple attributes

New retained electronic witness: `sources/dar1-item178.txt`, [item178](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/413/178/). Read with preceding items176–177 and following179–180. Item176 supplies the printed292 boundary. Item177 reports the disputed alternatives; item178 opens the author's reply.

### Argument before the famous identity sentence

At1:291–292 (item176), the issue is sharing in universals versus dividing a whole into parts. A universal human is not a literally shared part resident in each concrete individual. At1:292 (item177), he reports the opponent's dilemma: either existence is merely homonymous, is added to a quiddity, or God is pure existence stripped of positive quiddity. The reply says these are not exhaustive acceptable options.

At1:292–293 (item178 L002), common predication of existence does not mean numerically the same extramental constituent is located in each existent. A common meaning can subsume distinct existing things without becoming a component physically or ontologically shared between them.

### Essence/existence identity

At1:293, item178 L004:

«وإذا كان المخلوق المعين وجوده الذي في الخارج هو نفس ذاته وحقيقته وماهيته التي في الخارج، ليس في الخارج شيئان، فالخالق تعالى أولى أن تكون حقيقته هي وجوده الثابت الذي لا يشركه فيه أحد، وهو نفس ماهيته التي هي حقيقته الثابتة في نفس الأمر.»

Close translation: If a particular creature's external existence is its very self, reality, and quiddity externally, rather than two external things, the Creator has still greater claim that His reality is His established existence, shared by none, and the very quiddity which is His established reality.

The point concerns the **concrete existent**, not a proof that an arbitrary mentally specified essence is instantiated. It applies to creatures as well, so it does not confer necessity or self-subsistence on every existent. The attachment's `(1979,1:29)` cannot be adopted as a verified locator; the requested1:293 is supported by this electronic witness and El-Tobgui's explicit reference.

### The decisive immediately following control

At1:294, item178 L006:

«وهؤلاء الضالون يجعلون الواحد اثنين، والاثنين واحدا، فيجعلون هذه الصفة هي هذه الصفة، ويجعلون الصفة هي الموصوف، فيجعلون الاثنين واحدا كما قالوا: إن العلم هو القدرة وهو الإرادة، والعلم هو العالم ... ومعلوم أنه جوهر واحد له صفات متعددة».

He criticizes **both** reductions: identifying distinct attributes/attribute and bearer, and multiplying one qualified individual into several separate substances such as human, animal, rational, and sensitive. The authorial counterstatement is one substance with multiple attributes. Thus “all beings are simple” is unsafe unless narrowly and explicitly defined to mean the absence of a separately instantiated essence-plus-existence composition. It cannot mean that all real attributes are identical or fictitious.

The rest of1:294–295 rejects confusing mental abstractions with external entities. The continuation295–297 attacks equivocation in imported terminology. Item180 contains a likely omitted negative in a sentence about agreeing to ambiguous terms; no result here depends on repairing it.

Text flag: item178 L003 has «حقيقية» where El-Tobgui's transliteration and other electronic witnesses give «حقيقته». The clean full identity passage atL004 is quoted above instead; no unmarked emendation is made.

## 3. Darʾ 1:280–282: the exact primary anchor behind “purely mental distinction”

`sources/dar1-p281.txt` is [item169](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/413/169/), redirected from the printed-page281 link. Complete seven paragraphs read, with incoming context. This is not the same passage as292–295.

The source disambiguates composition. Assembly by another, prior separation followed by combination, and susceptibility to disassembly are denied of God. A technical use of composition for distinguishable aspects, such as knowledge versus power, is then treated separately.

At1:281 (L004–005), the subject qualified by necessary attributes cannot part from them or they from it. “Other” can indicate being knowable distinctly, or separability in time/place/existence. Those are not the same. The source then states:

«ومثل هذا التلازم بين الشيئين يقتضي كون وجود أحدهما مشروطا بالآخر، وهذا ليس بممتنع، وإنما الممتنع أن يكون كل من الشيئين موجبا للآخر، فالدور في العلل ممتنع، والدور في الشروط جائز.»

At1:282 (L006):

«ولفظ الافتقار هنا: إن أريد به افتقار المعلول إلى علته كان باطلا، وإن أريد به افتقار المشروط إلى شرطه ـ فهذا هو تلازم من الجانبين، وليس ذلك ممتنعا، والواجب بنفسه يمتنع أن يكون مفتقرا إلى ما هو خارج عن نفسه، فأما ما كان صفة لازمة لذاته وهو داخل في مسمى اسمه، فقول القائل: إنه مفتقر إليها كقوله: إنه مفتقر إلى نفسه.»

### What follows for foreign completion, and what does not

The wording permits reciprocal conditions in general form, but the worked case is the real inseparable self/attribute relation. It does not supply a constructive metaphysical possibility proof for two harmonious original agents. It immediately restricts the necessary-through-itself from needing something outside itself.

For a statement such as “A's act succeeds only if B does not interfere,” at least three readings must be separated:

1. **Logical concordance:** the conjunction of descriptions entails no conflict. No supplier or completing factor has been identified.
2. **An external enabling or completing requirement:** B provides something that completes A's being, power, or efficacy, or A cannot actualize its relevant act without an externally supplied condition. This needs its own metaphysical warrant; it cannot be inferred merely from a conditional formula.
3. **A hypothesis of one fully efficacious agent being opposed by another:** whether the combination is possible, and which conception of complete power it violates, requires the relevant power argument. The attribute case does not settle it.

Accordingly, a `foreign_completion` bridge cannot be introduced solely because a variable is called a condition or because two facts necessarily co-occur. It requires an evidenced external relation with the relevant completing role. Conversely, calling a genuine foreign completion “mere concomitance” would not protect a claimant to independence. Source1:282 concerns necessary being and intrinsic attributes; extending it from existence to every particular act must preserve the act/kind distinction and be separately argued.

## 4. Majmuʿ17:157–164 directly blocks “different attribute, therefore created entity”

[Item1726](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/22/1726/) returns a large chapter with repeated electronic layers. Relevant unvocalized selection is `sources/majmu17-all-electronic-layers.txt`, L488–493. Only this selection was continuously read; acquisition of the whole chapter is not a whole-chapter reading. Printed161–162 occurs inL490–491.

At17:158 the author affirms multiplicity of names, attributes, and divine words; denies that mercy, anger, and will are simply one and the same; and rejects an attribute-free external God. At17:159, L489 **reports the opponent's dilemma**: God's speech is either God or other-than-God; if the latter, it is created. He recounts Ahmad's reply through the parallel of divine knowledge and then rejects the ambiguity.

At17:160–161 (L490), he differentiates otherness as separability from otherness as being intelligibly distinct. Necessary attributes do not part from Him, but one may know an attribute without knowing another, or know the self without knowing the attribute. At17:161–162 (L491), the attributed self is not externally separable from its attributes, and the “addition” affirmed against deniers is:

«فهي زيادة في العلم والاعتقاد والخبر لا زيادة على نفس الله جل جلاله وتقدست أسماؤه. بل نفسه المقدسة متصفة بهذه الصفات لا يمكن أن تفارقها فلا توجد الصفات بدون الذات ولا الذات بدون الصفات.»

At17:162–163 (L492), knowledge is not the same kind of attribute as power, nor power as will. This strongly controls the attachment's “purely mental” wording: cognitive distinction does not imply invented attributes, and non-separability does not imply identity of the attributes.

## 6. Necessary through oneself is not merely modal quantification

A corroborative **reread** of retained Safadiyya002 L349–360 (no new printed-page mapping asserted) explicitly defines the relevant necessary-through-itself as lacking a producer or efficient cause; distinguishes that from standing without a subject; and says divine attributes can lack a producer while subsisting in God. Calling the self a cause in a sense of necessitating its attributes is distinguished from efficient production (L359). This is not an endorsement of God producing His own attributes.

The previously retained Darʾ4:259–260, item793 L001–005, likewise distinguishes a producer from the bearer in which necessity is instantiated. Both reinforce rather than erase the difference among:

- truth at every admissible world;
- being unproduced/unreceived;
- being self-standing rather than a quality in a subject;
- necessarily accompanying a concrete bearer.

These sources neither establish an S5 model nor refute all possible-world talk. The narrow point is that unreceived productive actuality and self-subsistence cannot be inferred from modal necessity alone, nor can real attribute distinction be converted automatically into independent-agent plurality.

## Consequences for the current semantic audit

The independent audit's conditional limitation on `part_of_total_explainer_is_itself` is supported: a theorem about a total explanatory **fact** having no distinct proper explanatory fact-part does not establish that God has no differentiated attributes. Mapping an attribute to such a fact-part would require extra premises and could conflict with this primary material. Real distinction by itself entails neither that particular fact-parthood relation nor a second independent agent.

Preserve any report/model that already types intrinsic qualification, productive receipt, modality, and bearer individuation separately. Correct a promotion only where actually found. Neither a formal countermodel nor an identity theorem supplies a metaphysical actuality/possibility witness without a justified interpretation.

### Additional narrow consequence: shared predicate is not a shared token

Darʾ1:292–293 (item178L002–003), read with the universal-versus-whole distinction in item176, also blocks an unwarranted numerical-identity bridge. Two claimants' falling under the same concept of existence or perfection does not by itself make their intrinsic qualifications one numerically shared external constituent, one attribute-token, or one bearer. Likewise, one cannot infer external composition from a common conceptual essence plus an individuating description without justifying that ontology. This constrains only an inference **from conceptual sharing alone**. It neither decides a substantive complete-power argument nor defeats an independently warranted nonduplication or de-se argument. No new formal theorem is claimed here.
