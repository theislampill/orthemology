## Exact argument rather than witness counting

The [identified Arabic edition](https://d1.islamhouse.com/data/ar/ih_books/single_01/ar_Explaining_Alasbahaneia.pdf) is Muhammad b. ʿAwdah al-Saʿwī's Dar al-Minhāj edition, the same 938-position scan identified by the inherited index. The inherited index and separate reviewer distinguish its first-edition notice 1430 AH from its 1428 AH catalogue line; the present worker did not newly inspect the title leaves. Main pp. 549–562 / PDF 649–662 were visually reread continuously. The Heraclius unit begins on p. 550 after the preceding Waraqah account, and ends with the cumulative paragraph on p. 561 before the next argument from historical traces. Received first-traversal credit stays with the earlier record.

The reported inquiry asks about ancestry, earlier claimants, previous lying, followers, defections, war, fidelity to agreements and teaching. The author does not simply repeat the questions and pronounce a threshold reached. On pp. 551–552 he explains their bearing on alternative causes. Royal ancestry would make recovery of ancestral rule an available motivation; an earlier local claimant would make imitation an available explanation. Their absence removes those specific supporting circumstances, but the author expressly says unusual departures occur. Likewise, someone may depart from his own habit toward a practice common among people. Stable truthfulness therefore adds information to the absence of those other motives; neither is an exceptionless entailment by itself.

The teaching and conduct questions contribute different content: consistency with the prophetic profile already known to the questioner, observance of agreements, and the character and persistence of adherence. On pp. 553–559 Ibn Taymiyyah connects these to his account of prophetic history and providential trials. That theological and historical background is a premise of his appraisal, not a neutral consequence of counting answers. Mere popularity, uninterrupted military success, and honesty alone are not interchangeable with the whole profile.

The philosophical gain is specific. A candidate explanation that fits one sign may fit the joint profile much worse, because the added fact bears on a different motive, practice or background expectation. For example, absence of inherited kingship leaves unusual ambition and imitation live; lack of a prior local model bears on imitation; a sustained record of truthfulness bears on habitual fabrication. Their combination is thus capable of reducing the force of rival explanations without any premise being a complete proof of prophecy in isolation. This is an analytical reconstruction of the author's stated reasons, not a probability calculation or an assertion that his considered alternatives exhaust every possibility.

The author concludes on p. 560 that the inquiry gave Heraclius certain knowledge. He reports al-Māzirī's objection that this cannot establish prophecy, which requires a miracle. Ibn Taymiyyah rejects that objection on p. 561 and invokes the familiar cumulative structure of reports, experience and recognition of a person's condition. One report can initially produce a degree of supposition and a further report strengthen it until knowledge results. This explicitly concerns acquisition from initially insufficient grounds, not just strengthening knowledge already possessed.

This establishes his positive source position and a substantive reason why combination matters. It does not independently authenticate the narrated conversation, validate every historical generalization, or demonstrate that its strongest certainty verdict transfers to a present observer. The absence of such a new authentication is not an automatic defeater of the retained affirmative assessment.

## Dependence and shared origins

Main pp. 683–688 / PDF 783–788 were visually read as a separate continuous supporting unit. At pp. 685–687 the author gives detailed agreement between two accounts where collusion and matching deliberate falsehood or error are excluded by the circumstances. The illustrations include two recitations of a poem and two messengers communicating the same detailed royal commission without mutual knowledge of their dispatch.

The epistemically relevant relation is not the metaphysical absence of a common source: a true poem or a real royal instruction is precisely the common origin being established. The contrast concerns whether agreement is explained by a common truthful object or instruction, rather than coordinated invention, copied error or other relevant common contamination. The text supplies a premise about circumstances making matching error or fabrication untenable in ordinary practice. Merely observing two matching strings does not establish that premise. Nor does absence of a direct meeting by itself exclude an intermediary or common erroneous source. This last point is an application limit, not a separate counterexample asserted by the author. At p. 688 he also distinguishes broadly transmitted general teachings from fine details whose transmission may have broken off or be known only to specialists. Agreement on a general core does not authenticate every detailed report.

The inherited qualitative controls are also directly confirmed in [*Majmūʿ al-Fatāwā* 18:48–51](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/22/1787/فصل-في-لفظ-المتواتر): report number, reporter qualities, accompanying indications and recipient knowledge can vary; a fixed count is rejected. Detailed agreement is considered alongside known absence of collusion and ordinary impossibility of such accidental agreement. The source's separate appeal to communal infallibility is theological, not an independently verified statistical rule. These controls were already owned by the July qualitative assessment preserved in the cumulative-attribution receipts.

## Knowing and reconstructed reasons

Main pp. 635–640 / PDF 735–740 were visually reread; the target unit runs from the end of p. 637 through p. 639's second paragraph. The author first rejects exclusivity claims on both sides of the miracle-versus-experience dispute. At p. 639 he distinguishes the grounds through which a person knows from the argument presented to an interlocutor: the person may be unable to recall, enumerate or express all the grounds. Others may lack the same experiential access.

Thus cumulative acquisition need not be a consciously rehearsed list of independent premises. A later reconstruction can clarify a genuine basis without being the historical cause of the original knowledge. Conversely, the reconstruction's availability does not establish that it operated in a particular initial recognition. Inarticulacy is neither disqualification nor authentication. These are A32's inherited distinctions, now pinned to the exact page.

## Appraisal and stopping boundary

Retain the positive proposition: qualitatively different, genuinely grounded indications can jointly discriminate where isolated indications remain insufficient. Do not demand that every indication be conclusive first. This does not license all agreeing collections, stipulated numerical independence, an unexplained confidence threshold, or duplicated support. The appraisal concerns what each ground actually adds, what warrants it, and why relevant rival explanations fit the combination less well. These are conditions of an adequate application, not a universal duty for ordinary knowers to reconstruct or certify their own cognition.

Keep fraud and sincere error distinct. The character strand has direct force against intentional fabrication; it does not alone establish accurate interpretation, unrestricted veracity or every later transmission. The source adds teaching, experience, circumstances and corroboration, but transferring its conclusion requires their actual relevant grounds. Generic conceivable alternatives do not thereby become established defeaters or equally credible accounts.

No new actual-human episode or independent metaphysical proof has been established. The exact inherited owners already contain the affirmative combination premise. This bounded work narrows the later residual through direct contextual reconciliation; it neither resets nor automatically increases the earned first-order warrant. Further broad source expansion would duplicate the task rather than supply its missing application evidence.
