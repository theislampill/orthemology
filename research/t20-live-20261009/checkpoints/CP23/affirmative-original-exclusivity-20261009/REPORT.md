# Affirmative original-production exclusivity: local manifestation and complete efficacy

9 October 2026 UTC. A bounded affirmative reconstruction and exact-source assessment. This report preserves the governing provisional common-Creator judgement. It neither integrates earlier work nor alters protected state, confidence numbers, programme status, or closure. No new rival campaign or mathematical model is undertaken.

## Result

**The strongest affirmative argument should be credited as a developed causal and perfection argument, including a substantive foundational judgement about actual production. It is not merely an exclusive definition of “independent,” and its rational warrant does not depend on deriving every premise from a weaker, causally neutral vocabulary.**

The cleanest reconstruction is local and actual: two distinct original exercises cannot each retain their entire positive productive manifestation in precisely the same numerical created actuality and productive respect. If the peer really contributes within that actuality, the contribution is real differentiated productive work; if that work completes or limits the first undertaking, its claimed unreceived entire efficacy fails. If it concerns another effect or respect, it is outside the alleged duplicate production. Jāmiʿ VI:95 gives the most direct local contribution premise; Majmūʿ 20:175–181 and Minhāj 2:180–183 explain its nonabsorption and perfection rationale. Ibn al-Qayyim expressly rejects a rescue by merely distinguishing the two producer-relations, while positively explaining how one effect can nevertheless have real hierarchical agency.

Three refinements matter:

1. **Actual-state nonidentity is enough for the whole-production exclusion.** The proof does not require a disjoint measurable token for every causal participant, a quantitative measure of work, a comparison in which a necessary original is absent, or cross-world origin-essentialism. Those are stronger obligations of particular trace/augmentation implementations.
2. **Intrinsic act identity alone is not enough.** The source distinguishes acts inhering in different bearers and then separately affirms different effects in the produced object. The latter is the substantive productive premise. One must give it warrant rather than present the former as its proof.
3. **The nonabsorption must concern the disputed effect and respect.** A larger total created inventory, owing to additional work elsewhere, would not by itself exclude duplicate whole production of this particular effect. This explains why Jāmiʿ’s local effect-part argument matters alongside Majmūʿ’s broader augmentation argument.

My assessment is affirmative but bounded: **the source’s local noncoincidence principle has positive prima facie warrant, reinforced by its account of original self-sufficient efficacy. The inspected material supplies no independently warranted original-level defeater. It remains reasonable to exclude the proposed duplicate whole attribution in the provisionally preferred account of actual original production.** This is not a new deductive theorem from intrinsic nonreceipt and occurrence-entailment alone. Nor does it independently establish every universal application of the local principle. The distinction preserves philosophical warrant rather than replacing it with a demand for deductive certainty.

## 1. The target and the predicates

Fix a numerically particular created effect **e**, its relevant state, and productive respect **r**. Suppose A and B are distinct original bearers. The challenged proposal says that each actually supplies the entire original productive work of e in r, with unreceived adequate power and actual effective determination. It does not merely say that either could produce something qualitatively similar.

Keep separate:

- **Current intrinsic power:** A’s power really belongs to A and is not furnished by B. This is not yet a completed productive exercise.
- **Alternative exercise:** A could accomplish the target under another admissible condition. That does not establish its actual whole accomplishment here.
- **Simultaneous actual efficacy:** A and B are genuinely operative now. Their simultaneity does not establish reciprocal efficient donation, and does not make either idle.
- **A shared intrinsic act:** one numerically identical act purportedly inheres as the act of both distinct bearers. The source rejects this identification.
- **A joint episode or common effect:** different intrinsic acts terminate in one episode or produced object. Rejecting a shared intrinsic act does not alone reject this.
- **Whole original production:** actual adequate provision of the entire productive actuality of e in r, including the relevant means and receptive conditions at their original level. This is stronger than a complete operating route through an already supplied environment.
- **Exclusive original attribution:** no distinct original also supplies that same entire production. This is the conclusion, not the initial meaning assigned to intrinsic nonreceipt.

Original production need not mean absence of materials, absence of means, a first moment of creation, or denial of creaturely agency. The source’s affirmative alternative expressly accommodates means whose relevant being and efficacy are received from the original source.

## 2. Exact source warrant

### 2.1 Jāmiʿ: the local claim is about external productive actuality

Printed VI:94–95 first distinguishes the two bearers’ acts: an act inhering in one does not inhere in the other. It then changes its grammatical and ontological subject to the **produced effect in the carried object**. The operative wording on 95 is:

> فأثر فعل أحدهما الموجود في المحمول ليس هو أثر فعل الآخر، بل هو غيره

Each act’s effect existing in the carried object differs from the other’s effect. The text then treats the carried result as from the aggregate and each participant as part of its productive agent. Later on the same page, it argues that a real helper either has no efficacy, in which case it is no helper, or has efficacy and has made part of the product. The continuation on 96 contrasts producing part in cooperation with producing the whole independently.

This is **a positive local effect-part claim**, not just two different descriptions of one relation. It is also not a statement that every contribution must be a separate body or independently observable object. Different positive productive contributions may exist in one bearer.

Printed 95 expressly allows that a cooperator could act alone under another condition. Printed 96 distinguishes that standing capacity from the actual unrestricted exercise of it: a participant who can do the whole but actually does only part has not manifested the whole power. Printed 98 explicitly returns necessary accord to this earlier argument. Thus neither solo capability nor necessary agreement is a case the source forgot.

The minimal local premise is nevertheless **separately asserted and defended**. The source does not first prove a general theorem that distinct intrinsic acts invariably yield distinct created effects. Its ordinary cooperation examples and its part/whole conception of real productive sharing are the grounds offered here. The scan supports the stronger local assertion; it does not turn act individuation into a proof of it.

Source: [received collection VI scan](https://csiislam.org/img/library_dine/c1473365dbab.pdf), printed 94–98. This lane directly inspected the retained image of printed 95; the adjacent scan/page controls are inherited from the verified return, not newly claimed visual readings.

### 2.2 Majmūʿ: why a genuine addition cannot simply vanish

At 20:175 the comparison holds the productive agent’s relevant condition fixed, including will, motion, instruments and effective operation. When another genuinely efficacious contributor is added, the source argues that the complete work must be more or greater. Ordinary solitary and cooperative performances with the same outward result are explained by changed effort or operation. At 176–178 the source connects this with complete power and decisive willing, expressly including mixed effects whose components cannot be separately assigned as entire objects.

This supplies an intelligible **retention rationale**: actual efficacy should not be counted as fully present and then have its completed manifestation quietly disappear from the productive accounting. Merely conserving an intrinsic command while changing what actually makes it effective does not preserve complete original operation.

At 180–181, productive dependence is connected to the bearer’s act and attribute from which the effect proceeds. This does not require the peer to deposit a new intrinsic power-token into the first agent. It concerns the actual adequacy of that agent’s exercise.

The source’s augmentation claim is substantial. It should not be weakened into mere nonzero input sensitivity. Equally, global additional work is not, by itself, a proof of the local claim about e. A new effect f elsewhere would enlarge the total inventory without settling whether e has two whole original attributions. The local contribution claim must be retained when the intended conclusion is local. This is a scope control on the reconstruction, not a new counterexample to the source.

Source: [Majmūʿ 20:174–184](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/22/2032/), retained item2032 L002–008 and L013. The electronic page markers are inherited edition correspondences.

### 2.3 Minhāj: adequate achievement is a real perfection

Minhāj 2:180 argues that efficacy belongs to the agent’s perfection: an efficacious agent is more complete in that respect than an inefficacious one. Its concrete example identifies receptive bodies, connections and movements that an alleged original has not provided. It is not the bare observation that another object exists.

The affirmative principle is: **the relevant original perfection includes the adequate efficacy actually exercised, rather than only an unbestowed inventory of powers and intentions.** If another original supplies a requisite without which that actual undertaking is incomplete, the achievement has foreign completion. Freely welcoming it does not change its source.

Necessary intrinsic power supplies the further constraint that a peer cannot simply remove or supply what belongs to the original’s constitutive completeness. In a completed chosen undertaking, the efficacy must therefore remain genuinely its own. This does not require every possible act to occur, or every particular created end to be an eternally necessary constituent of the agent. The source’s conditional claim concerns the adequacy of the undertaken act.

This explains why the argument is about excellence of agency rather than sole credit. The excellence at issue is self-sufficient achieved production. The remaining application question is whether the alleged redundant peer really contributes any missing completion or limits any of that efficacy; its mere presence cannot substitute for that finding.

Sources: [Minhāj 2:166–180](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/108/156/), especially retained L034–041; [2:180–182](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/108/157/); [the perfection continuation](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/108/158/), first six retained paragraphs.

### 2.4 Ibn al-Qayyim: a developed answer to different relations with one terminus

The received Shifāʾ e143 identifies real productive requisites: the agent’s power and will, the instrument, recipient and its receptivity, surrounding conditions, and removal of impediments. A creature’s genuinely effective act does not originally furnish all of these. E144 adds the receipt of the creature’s own power and will.

E145 refuses a purported power whose effect is sheer nonbeing. Positive actual efficacy is therefore required; idle attribution will not do.

Most importantly, at **e146** Abū al-Ḥusayn’s proposed reply distinguishes the two producer-relations, comparing them with one object known by two knowers. Ibn al-Qayyim’s Sunni respondent rejects that repair. He lists together the real efficacy of the creature, comprehensive divine power, the impossibility of one individual effect between two independently complete producers, and the need for an originator. He treats these as rational causal principles that must be reconciled, rather than abandoning one because a familiar explanatory vocabulary does not reconcile them.

His positive reconciliation is **asymmetric productive inclusion**: the creature’s power is itself an effect of the original power and a partial cause. The motion is really effected through the creature’s power and will, while that power, its productive operation, and its result belong within original creative provision. E150 explicitly distinguishes the rejected coequal intrinsically powered pair from the accepted received-power structure.

This is much stronger than an unexplained preference for one creator or a mere dictionary definition. It directly confronts the proposed different-relations/same-terminus repair. At the same time, the passage regards same-respect original nonduplication as a rationally evident principle; it does not newly derive it from two weaker occurrence implications. Giving this principle foundational warrant is legitimate philosophical assessment. Presenting it as an additional derived theorem would be inaccurate.

The knowledge comparison is controlled: the point concerns why different knowledge-attributions as such do not authenticate two complete original productions. It is not a denial that operative knowledge can have a productive role within intelligent agency. Nothing here alters the separate Darʾ operative-knowledge findings.

Source: [Shifāʾ received electronic body, section 002](https://www.islamicbook.ws/amma/shfa-alalil-002.html), e143–147 and e150. These are electronic, not scan-certified print pages. No technical inference uses the potentially defective phrase about the power that is a cause in the e146 continuation.

## 3. A minimal affirmative argument

The following is an analytical reconstruction, not a verbatim argument newly discovered in one passage.

**P1. Actuality.** Each alleged original is genuinely effective in producing e in r. Possessing power, intending e, knowing e, or being correctly named as a possible producer does not alone satisfy this premise.

**P2. Entire unreceived adequacy.** Each alleged original’s actual undertaking is adequate for the whole productive actuality of e in r through its own original resources. Created means are permitted when their relevant original adequacy is included; a foreign original does not complete the exercise.

**P3. Preservation of original perfection.** A peer cannot supply, remove, or restrict the completeness essential to such an original undertaking while the same undertaking is still called entirely adequate and unreceived. Realized efficacy belongs to the relevant perfection. This is the Minhāj/Majmūʿ perfection principle at its conditional scope.

**P4. Local noncoincidence of original productive manifestation.** Distinct genuinely original effective contributions in one numerical effect and respect have real differentiated productive content there; they are not two wholly coincident complete bestowals of that very same produced actuality. Real cooperative contribution either has such differentiated positive content or changes/completes the productive operation whose manifestation is being counted. Jāmiʿ’s external-effect distinction and proper-part argument directly support this premise; Majmūʿ supplies the retention rationale.

**P5. Whole means whole at the fixed target.** If A supplies the entire productive actuality of e in r, that entire actuality is what A’s completed manifestation covers in that respect. No genuinely distinct portion of that same work remains supplied by a coequal original while still being wholly A’s unreceived provision. This is ordinary whole/part reasoning applied after P4 has ruled out completely coincident productive contents; it must not be used to establish P4 by itself.

**Inference.** Suppose A and B both satisfy P1–P3 for precisely e in r. If their contributions have different positive content within e, their actuality is cooperative/differentiated rather than each’s whole unreceived provision. If one completes or restricts the other’s undertaking, P2 or P3 fails. If they supply different numerical effects or different productive respects, they do not instantiate the target hypothesis. The only remaining description makes their complete local productive manifestations wholly coincident. P4 excludes it. Therefore the pair cannot both be actual independent entire original producers of this e in r.

P4 is the indispensable productive judgement. The argument is not offered as a derivation of P4 from P1–P3. Nevertheless P4 is not smuggled into the definition of intrinsic independence: it is openly stated as a broader claim about real productive sharing, supported by the source’s actuality accounting and original-efficacy rationale. A foundational causal principle can have defeasible rational warrant without being analytically true or derived from causally neutral premises.

The conclusion is appropriately stronger than “perhaps the two agents disagree.” It also avoids claiming that unexercised capacity is defective simply because it is not exercised. Both putative agents in this argument are stipulated to exercise entire actual original efficacy in the target case.

### 3.1 Exact implication relation and interpretation burden

The identity reasoning can be displayed without a numerical work function. Let **q** denote the positive produced actuality constituting e in r. Let **cA** and **cB** denote what each exercise actually realizes **externally within that q**. These symbols do not mean the intrinsic acts, the propositions “A produces e” and “B produces e,” two causal arrows, or the source-labelled pairs (A,e) and (B,e). Nor do they assert separately existing substances or newly created obtaining-tokens. They are placeholders for actual external productive content, at the source’s intended effect/part-of-effect level.

The content interpretation is an explicit metaphysical commitment. A mere declaration that B is productive does not mechanically manufacture a fresh cB. Conversely, the two content terms are not made numerically different by their subscripts: without the local premise, both are allowed to denote q.

Because the contents are restricted to the fixed target and respect, each is contained within q. If A’s exercise is entire in the stipulated actual productive sense, it realizes all q; thus cA = q. Likewise B’s entirety gives cB = q. The substantive source premise is **N: genuinely distinct independent original contributions have cA ≠ cB at that external productive-content interpretation**. N and the two entirety identities are incompatible.

N is not deduced merely from a ≠ b for the intrinsic acts. It is the external-effect judgement expressed at Jāmiʿ 95 and Minhāj 3:321. Nor is N inferred from the proposition that each cause has a real effect: effect-reality establishes positivity, not by itself difference. The short identity inference only locates exactly what the wider actuality/perfection argument supports.

A **strong trace package** that, at this same content granularity, independently supplies cA and cB as nonzero actual external manifestations and makes those contents pairwise disjoint entails N: nonzero disjoint contents cannot be identical. Further faithful-retention and coverage clauses strengthen that package again. Merely finding different small traces somewhere inside two otherwise identical total contents would not establish their nonidentity. N does not require the strong package in return. Nonidentical contents can overlap; N says nothing by itself about their size, observability, separability into individual bodies, or persistence after changing the causal setting. Thus disjointness, quantitative enlargement and cross-setting retention are genuinely stronger demands than this actual-state nonidentity test.

This implication relation does **not** establish that N is metaphysically true. Near the special case of two entire productions, N is precisely the remaining nonduplication content. Calling it “manifestation nonidentity” earns no fresh independent proof. The improvement is to avoid treating stronger trace-engineering obligations as necessary conditions of philosophical warrant for the source’s narrower local judgement. P1–P3 provide the original-agent profile and positive reasons for N; they are not falsely reported as a completed independent derivation of it.

## 4. Why the warrant is positive

Four considerations jointly favor the affirmative principle.

1. **It discriminates actual production from mere successful prediction or attribution.** A genuine extra producer has to supply real productive actuality. This demand is independently supported by Ibn al-Qayyim’s rejection of a power with no positive effect.
2. **It accounts for actual cooperative work.** The source’s examples identify different acts and different effects within one product, while explaining equal-looking solo and joint outcomes through changed operation. The claimed differences are real productive differences, not only differences in causal labels.
3. **It explains the original/received boundary.** Ordinary agents act within supplied recipients and means; the proposed original must furnish the relevant productive adequacy itself. Importing a familiar redundant operating route does not automatically preserve that stronger condition.
4. **It connects completeness with a defensible excellence.** An actual achievement whose original productive adequacy comes from its own source is more self-sufficient in that respect than one awaiting external completion. The source does not need to declare every valuable relationship a defect.

These considerations do not constitute four independent proofs of local nonduplication. Their significance is cumulative explanation of why the principle is rationally attractive and belongs to a coherent account of real agency. Their force survives the observation that someone can write down a nonexclusive pair of causal relations. Syntax or lack of an efficient cycle does not supply an alternative account of actual original productive adequacy.

There is also a modest economy consideration, downstream of those substantive grounds: duplicating alleged entire original relations adds no demonstrated productive resource needed to explain this actuality. That alone would not establish impossibility, and it is not the main argument here.

## 5. What the act-token and origin-individuation route earns

### What it does establish

On the source’s bearer/act ontology, the act inhering in A is not numerically the same intrinsic act as the act inhering in B. A proposed rescue that literally fuses them into one intrinsic act while retaining distinct bearers conflicts with that ontology. A common act-type is not a common act-token.

The source then **independently** affirms local differentiated effects. Taking that judgement as warranted gives the short actual-state argument in §3. No cross-world comparison is needed; no necessary original must be deleted; the agents need not have independently recombinable choices.

### What it does not establish

Two distinct intrinsic acts do not, solely by their number, yield two created objects. Nor does a common productive episode have to be a third agent or a shared intrinsic property. Calling the episode “shared” leaves its productive adequacy to be assessed.

Generic origin-essentialism would not independently repair the proof. A theory that individuates e by its origin could assign e an essentially **plural** origin. Requiring a single original source in its identity would insert the desired uniqueness. Even distinguishing a hypothetical solo product from a joint product would not settle whether the actual joint product receives two whole attributions. The needed argument concerns actual productive content, not only how possible-world objects are named.

No examined passage provides an additional general injection theorem from distinct entire intrinsic act-tokens to distinct created effect-tokens. Minhāj 3:321 states the distinct intrinsic-act claim and the distinct external-effect claim in successive steps. Jāmiʿ 95 does the same. The source is stronger than a mere identity convention, because it gives substantive contribution/perfection reasons for the external-effect claim; it is not stronger by possessing an unreported origin-essentialist proof.

Source: [Minhāj 3:321 onward](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/108/366/), retained L002–003.

## 6. The strongest remaining challenge, at its warranted scope

The independently warranted challenge is a **limit on the inference**, not an authenticated two-original world: a difference between productive relations need not, by logic alone, be a difference between their external termini. Ibn al-Qayyim’s own positive account has one motion genuinely related to original creation and received direct performance. Thus an unrestricted claim that different effective acts always demand different effects is false to the source’s own distinctions.

The affirmative reply is substantial: those two relations have different productive modes and asymmetric receipt. They therefore do not satisfy the same-respect coequal-original antecedent of P4. The hierarchy provides a positive explanation of their compatibility; merely removing the hierarchy’s receipt relation does not warrant an equally adequate cooriginal case.

The remaining possible challenge must do more than restate that two relations could share e. It must give positive reason to think that **both relations really retain whole original productive adequacy** with no foreign completion, no suppression of entire manifestation, no change of target, and no change of productive mode. The inherited ordinary-cause material supplies limited pressure against careless universal analogies, but it does not independently establish that original-specific claim. This report undertakes no additional rival reading.

Equally, one cannot defeat the challenge simply by classifying every additional named cause as a missing requisite of A. An actual redundant relation, if warranted, would be precisely a challenge to that classification. The source’s local noncoincidence and original-efficacy principles must carry the argument openly.

**Present balance:** retain the affirmative local production judgement as prima facie warranted within the developed source account; retain the governing provisional one-common-original appraisal; do not promote the relation-only proposal into a defeater, and do not announce an unrestricted deduction from thin intrinsic nonreceipt. A newly supported original-level case or a better account of coincident productive actuality could require reassessment. No such new evidence appears in this return.

## 7. Earned addition and scope

This return’s main addition is the **minimal actual-state reconstruction**: target-local differentiated productive manifestation is enough for the whole-production inference. Full disjoint trace machinery, global quantitative augmentation, alternate solo worlds, and origin-essentialism are unnecessary to that narrower argument. At the same time, distinct intrinsic acts are insufficient without the local productive principle.

It also makes the warrant assessment explicit. The source’s rationally evident nonduplication claim is assessed as a substantive, defeasibly supported foundational judgement, rather than repeatedly described only as an unproved bridge. This does not enlarge its demonstrated scope or certify a new theorem. The e146 different-relations reply is an inherited source finding now directly read and integrated, not new source discovery.

The D7 common-output graph remains a conditional informational result. Factive knowledge can yield common-output agreement without entire productive adequacy. Original nonreceipt also does not establish Cartesian recombination of intrinsic choices. Neither supplies or defeats P4.
