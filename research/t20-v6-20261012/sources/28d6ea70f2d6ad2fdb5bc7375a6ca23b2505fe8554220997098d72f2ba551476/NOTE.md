Actual reading: abstract, introduction and section2; section5.1–5.3 continuously. Adjacent returned sections4.2/5.4 were inspected selectively. Appendix proofs were not read or replayed. Search-engine apparent freshness is not publication date.

Section5.3 presents the same classical total-space iterator for dependent induction; its detailed proof is deferred. Section5.2 obtains projective dependent pairs semantically from a Church encoding. This supplies additional ancestry for the construction, not novelty credit.

Direct transport remains unestablished: section2 includes equality reflection and uniqueness of identity proofs, while HasE’s reviewed construction uses explicit erased J without either assumption. The paper’s encoded Sigma and quasi-PER interpretation also differ from the source primitive Sigma and the countermodels just checked. Its model-valid equations are not automatically HasE derivations.

Disposition: retain as primary ancestry and a mapping control. No new Orthemology theorem, replay, extension adoption, or full-paper-review credit.
