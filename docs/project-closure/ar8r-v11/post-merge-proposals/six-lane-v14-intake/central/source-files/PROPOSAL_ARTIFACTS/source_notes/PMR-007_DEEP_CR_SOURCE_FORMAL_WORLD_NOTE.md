## What the sources supply

The corpus supports a source-relative picture in which fiṭrah is treated as an
original normative disposition, its health conditions cognition, corruption can
impair judgment, and rehabilitation/reminder can restore recognition.

## What the sources do not supply here

- an Arabic-primary verification of every quoted locus;
- one jointly stated eleven-coordinate source/world contract;
- a neutral theorem selecting the fiṭrah standard;
- an observed proxy for fiṭrah health;
- the Deep-CQ finite robust-controller theorem;
- a world-level proof of a personal designer, Wisdom, or Necessary Being.
