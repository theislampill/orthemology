## Executive disposition

The sustained lane does **not** obtain a positive transcendental or revelational
ascent. It does obtain a sharper dependency map and ten bounded results.

The central findings are:

1. **The Wisdom residual is bearer- and episode-indexed.** Objective fittingness,
   apprehension, intentional uptake, selection because fitting, reliable
   realization, and bearer applicability must align; global availability or
   successful output does not provide that alignment.
2. **A declared-frame root becomes nonborrowed only under incoming-grounding
   completeness.** Frame persistence still does not yield metaphysical
   necessity, self-subsistence, originator status, Creatorhood, or personality.
3. **Qiyās al-awlā, Speech, Names, wording, grammar, recitation, authority,
   recipient warrant, and execution form separate guarded stages.** The six
   AR8R qiyās guards remain project synthesis unless a source states them
   jointly.
4. **Noetic restoration is not identified by improved output.** Restoration,
   memorisation, social compliance, expectancy, defeater suppression, and
   silent latent change can share or invert observed profiles.
5. **Property ontology remains underdetermined by bearer-role incidence.**
   Nominalism, tropes, conceptualism, immanent realism, powers, real patterns,
   and plural realization can share the same formal reduct. Mind-body property
   dualism is a separate axis.
6. **Personality is nonidentifiable in a twin-closed profile class.** Finite or
   adaptive profile accumulation cannot help while each personal model retains
   an equally profiled impersonal twin.
7. **One role section is not yet one bearer in every relevant sense.**
   World-local singleton, coherent lineage, numerical identity, granularity,
   simplicity, integrated agency, Creator scope, and revealed referent remain
   distinct.
8. **Source/world readiness is recipient-indexed.** Textual custody, truth,
   referent, authority, access, competence, route reliability, defeaters,
   warrant, assent, and execution cannot be compressed into one source bit.
9. **Formal-root, Avicennian, and Taymiyyan vocabularies are not automatically
   one theorem family.** A predicate-preserving and rule-preserving
   interpretation map is required before ancestry labels stronger than analogue
   or unresolved are assigned.
10. **The repeated “correction term” is vector-valued.** Exactness, norm
    provenance, semantic fidelity, truth connection, function-fixing, bearer
    applicability, reason uptake, authority, recipient warrant, and execution
    are separate coordinates. An exact potential or stable score does not
    establish a personal or wise source.

The strongest impersonal, one-intellect nonpersonal, powers, abstract-order plus
concrete-actualizer, and plural/R5 rivals remain live.
