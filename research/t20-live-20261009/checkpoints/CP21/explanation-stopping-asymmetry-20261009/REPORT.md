# Nonnecessitating explanation and the reason for stopping

9 October 2026 UTC. Bounded substantive T20 comparison. Additive result only; no integration, closure, new formal model, or outreach.

## Result

**A warranted productive nature can explain a real difference between a caused choice and an uncaused occurrence. It does not, by that same argument, establish either the exhaustive adequacy of stopping at that choice or the necessary existence of its producer.** The two promotions require different further warrants.

The new comparative test applies O'Connor's meta-explanation to the already inherited strongest brute original, preserving that rival's downstream production instead of comparing indeterministic production with an uncaused downstream event. On that comparison, the proposed explanation of why exhaustive selection is unavailable survives on both sides. The remaining difference concerns the original's own existential adequacy. O'Connor's meta-explanation therefore does not independently establish the anti-brute asymmetry at original scope. The inherited positive intrinsic-sufficiency argument may establish it on its own philosophical warrant; the productive comparison neither replaces nor defeats that argument.

A second obstruction fixes what the stopping rule must establish. Genuine indeterminism excludes an exhaustive **necessitating** selection basis. If completeness is allowed to be nonnecessitating, exclusion of every fully adequate answer to the actual contrast needs a further explanatory principle. Informative probabilistic or reason-weighting answers are not automatically exhaustive answers. Equally, the impossibility of an exhaustive necessitating answer does not by itself establish that stopping there fulfills the appropriate standard. This is a dispute over the standard's warrant, not a discovered inconsistency in O'Connor's position.

## The strongest case for a principled limit

The proposal is not that an unexplained choice becomes explanatory because it is called free. Its strongest form supplies an actual agent, an intrinsic power, suitable reasons, and the actual productive exercise with purposive content. Necessary possession of a reason can figure in the explanation of a contingent action. Its explanatory relevance on this occasion need not make possession of the reason contingent. Note 14 correctly separates those propositions.

The inherited 2009 power report adds substance to this claim: reasons are supposed to structure exercise, and the agent produces an intention whose content embodies its end. This is more than an outcome inscription. Causal production need not consist in separately producing a second object called the causal relation. Nor must every explanatory fact be deducible from the description of standing capacity. Those are legitimate protections of the account.

If this power and its actual exercise are independently warranted, identifying them provides positive information about why the actual effect exists. Establishing the power's genuinely nonnecessitating operation would also explain why no exhaustive necessitating selection basis is available. The absence of such a basis would follow from the nature of a positively characterized producer, rather than from ignorance of an undiscovered determinant. This is the strongest prospective asymmetry against a wholly uncaused token occurrence.

The restriction to **genuine** indeterminism matters. A nonzero probability in an incomplete description establishes neither metaphysical openness nor the absence of further intrinsic conditions. Conversely, demanding a hidden determinant simply because complete production must be determining would assume the contested principle. The chapter's scientific analogy supports the intelligibility and explanatory usefulness of nonnecessitating causation within its adopted interpretation; it does not independently settle all-condition indeterminism or original agency. Printed 36 explicitly leaves more defence of the purposive model's coherence outstanding.

## The selection limit needs a fixed meaning of completeness

O'Connor's target at 34 is necessarily and fully contrastive explanation: an answer to why the actual course obtains rather than every admissible alternative. Note 13's discussion of mutually exclusive outcomes and nonzero chances must be read at that scope. Showing that a reason or propensity explains why one option was attractive, likely, or chosen under a particular description does not refute the absence of an exhaustive answer to the whole actual contrast.

Nevertheless, two claims remain distinct:

1. No complete basis necessitates this choice rather than another.
2. No further account could adequately explain the entire actual contrast.

If an exhaustive contrastive account means precisely a necessitating selector, genuine indeterminism warrants claim 1 and, under that meaning, claim 2. This is not necessarily a vacuous explanation: independently established features of a real power can explain why an independently specified demand cannot be met. But the conclusion answers why that demand is unavailable, not why meeting the weaker productive standard constitutes ultimate adequacy. The latter is a substantive stopping rule.

If a fully adequate contrastive account need not necessitate its explanandum, claim 1 no longer suffices for claim 2. A further argument must exclude an adequate nonnecessitating account of the whole actual contrast, not merely exclude some local reason-weighting information. The chapter supplies a conception on which the remaining exhaustive selection question has no such answer; its appeal to indeterminism is not a neutral derivation of that conception from the existence of real powers alone.

The Arabic comparison makes this exact. Majmūʿ L311–312 does not exempt divine specification while demanding a complete particular determiner elsewhere. Bayān's rejection of a foreign source of divine willing does not establish the absence of intrinsic intelligible determination. Majmūʿ L339–356 permits relevant intrinsic acts and conditions without reducing them to externally received perfections or to a single eternally fixed act. Thus resistance to foreign compulsion cannot independently warrant O'Connor's stronger stopping rule. Conversely, the complete-determiner defender must justify that rule's universal reach rather than insert necessitation into the definition of any genuine production.

This leaves a real philosophical contest. It does not require proving that every choice has a unique numerical optimum, that standing essence fixes the entire history, or that an intrinsic act is a separately created bearer.

## The stronger brute comparison isolates a different premise

The direct anti-brute report's rival is an intrinsically qualified, unreceived, possibly beginningless original that genuinely produces the received effects. It denies sufficient explanation of its own contingent obtaining. It does not deny production of its effects or propose that nothing produces them.

Compare the arguments while provisionally granting that rival the same relevant **downstream productive and motivational account**. This is an inference comparison, not a claim that a single complete essence can be both contingent and necessary, nor a certification that the rival is metaphysically possible.

The following consequence is important:

- If an indeterministic productive profile explains the actual effect and the absence of exhaustive necessitating selection, those explanations do not cease to work merely because the proposed producer's own obtaining is left brute.
- What fails to be explained in that rival is the original's existence, not the effect's productive provenance.
- Consequently, the contrast between caused and uncaused **effects** cannot by itself discriminate the necessary original from this brute original. The discriminator must concern the original's own existential mode.

The source defender has a positive answer: complete existential independence is intrinsic sufficiency, and such sufficiency excludes possible nonbeing. Darʾ 287–289 rejects the third alternative directly; Iṣfahāniyya L184 makes intrinsic sufficiency explicit. This is a reasoned foundational position, not a synonym for self-production or a necessity obtained merely by naming the source. Its warrant need not be a deduction from premises every rival already grants.

The sophisticated rival denies precisely the inference from lack of an external requisite to sufficient intrinsic necessary being. Its lack of a further productive episode does not itself answer that objection. Neither does its having powers that explain effects explain its own obtaining. The affirmative account therefore retains an intelligible explanatory advantage if its intrinsic-sufficiency judgment is credible. What the comparison establishes is the **location** of that advantage: it does not come from the nonnecessitating character of downstream explanation.

This also prevents a false parity claim. A produced contingent choice is not a fully unreceived contingent bearer. Giving the former a producer and reason does not exempt the latter from an existential question. But differentiating these subjects establishes distinct questions; it does not settle the permissibility of the second stopping point without the intrinsic-adequacy judgment.

## PCE II leaves this burden open

The two explanatory principles must not be conflated. PCE I ranges over contingent basic individuals, their collections, and events in or among them. It does not expressly quantify over every intrinsic act of a necessary bearer. The surrounding account, including the stronger dependency language at 35 and note 14's reason explanation, nevertheless aspires to more than that restricted formula. The limited quantifier is not evidence that the author deliberately leaves divine choice wholly unexplained.

PCE II permits either an explanation of a contingent event or truth, or a nonvacuous explanation of why it has none. **Its form alone therefore cannot require a first-order causal explanation of every concrete contingent original.** If a rival genuinely established a nonvacuous reason why such an original cannot have an explanation, it would be invoking the second alternative rather than violating the principle merely by lacking a first-order explanation. Requiring causal explanation in that case adds the force of PCE I or a further existential-adequacy thesis. No possible instance of the rival has been supplied here.

The simplest brute reply fails: stipulating that the original is fundamental, independent, or unexplained does not account for why its obtaining cannot have an explanation. Essential absence of an external cause, even if granted, would also leave open an intrinsic existential account. A serious rival would need independently supported grounds excluding the relevant explanations, while maintaining genuine contingency. Its possibility cannot be obtained from a consistent description or a table of worlds.

This criticism must not absorb the distinct autonomous-grounding alternative. The inherited Dasgupta assessment gives a substantive rationale for explanatory nonaptitude through real definitions, rather than a brute flag. Its base need not be a contingent bearer or an efficient producer; it therefore is not automatically another instance of the proposed B or of PCE II's contingent domain. It requires its own ontology and applicability assessment. O'Connor's expressed dissatisfaction with principle-based explanation at 29 is explicitly not a conclusive rebuttal. This pass does not newly settle that alternative's existential force, complete productive coverage, or inherited comparative appraisal.

Still, rejecting the stipulated reply does not establish that every stronger reply must fail. Printed 39 itself recognizes, conditionally, that successful arguments against necessary existence or against explaining contingency without collapse could supply a principled reason for unavoidable brute contingency. That concession is not an endorsement of those arguments and does not establish a particular PCE II instance. It confirms that a substantive limit argument, rather than the mere presence of an unexplained contingent item, is relevant to the assessment. The proposed global asymmetry remains dependent on the success and costs of competing metaphysical accounts.

O'Connor can thus oppose arbitrary termination without covertly retaining the strong PSR he explicitly rejects. He cannot obtain an exceptionless ban on every principled brute original solely from PCE II's wording. Conversely, the brute rival gains no positive modal warrant simply because the principle leaves this argumentative route conceptually open.

## Bounded disposition

The independently meaningful difference between real production and a wholly uncaused occurrence survives. It should not be dismissed as a definition of freedom. The proposed final limit on exhaustive selection remains conditional on the genuine power, its actual or possible instantiation, and a defended explanatory standard. The necessary source's superiority over the strongest brute original additionally turns on intrinsic existential adequacy. These are separate warrants, not three restatements of one gap.

No new defeater of actual original-resource Completion follows. The unchanged interface supplies an actual relevant unreceived resource; it does not require exhaustive selection or make every act a bearer. The brute-original dispute instead targets the further ContingencyNeed premise under its intended meanings. This pass neither grants a brute counterexample nor converts logical consistency into metaphysical possibility.

The substantive gain over the inherited reports is the isolated inference: **hold the downstream productive explanation fixed, and the meta-explanation of nonselection loses its discriminatory force between the two originals; apply PCE II, and excluding a principled inexplicability claim still requires the independent existential premise.** On the choice side, the exact remaining obstruction is the warrant for treating an independently grounded limit on necessitation as the right limit of complete explanation. No additional model or broad source campaign is needed to identify either issue.
