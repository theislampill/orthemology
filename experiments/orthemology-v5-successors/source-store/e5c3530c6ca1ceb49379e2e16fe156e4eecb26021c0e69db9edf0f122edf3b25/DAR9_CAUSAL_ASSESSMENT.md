# Direct reread of the extended independent power argument

Lead reader’s analytical note, 7 October 2026. Continuous electronic source016 lines700–779, with the exact range bound in [causal reread coverage](DAR9_CAUSAL_REREAD.json). This is a selected second inspection after the whole electronic body was studied, not a full second volume traversal.

## What must receive full credit

The author explicitly defends the mutual-prevention argument and directly addresses agreeing wills, necessary harmony, partial production, partition, turn-taking and acting only when the other refrains. An assessment saying that he simply overlooked those replies would be false.

He distinguishes causal circularity from permitted necessary concomitance. Intrinsic attributes coexisting with their bearer are his positive example. His later criticism of two intrinsically powerful originals is therefore a further argument, not an indiscriminate rule that every concomitance is received power.

The source moves from intrinsically possessed sufficient capacity to independent efficacy: each original must be able to perform without needing the other. At the same concrete production, independent whole production by one excludes independent whole production by another. He then interprets the other's action or control as a restriction on unilateral efficacy and argues that this contradicts the required power. Product-interdependence is also carried to the agents whose works supply the composite.

The reply to the opposing-actions analogy matters. A single agent refraining from one of two incompatible actions by its own choice differs, for the author, from an agent whose proposed exercise is blocked by another's actual action. He does not classify every conjunction of incompatible requested histories as an eligible object of power. His critique concerns external restriction of genuine power, rather than a failure to realise intrinsic contradiction.

At the end he separately condemns partial partnership as a deficiency of full divinity. This is the evaluative source-mode route and should not be represented as automatically proved by the whole-alone contradiction.

## Exact questions for further assessment

The unilateral efficacy claim is stronger than absence of an external donor of standing capacity. Its positive rationale is that original agency should be effective through its own intrinsic power rather than conditional on another original's permission or contribution. The argument deserves assessment at that stronger scope.

Three modal statements must nevertheless remain distinct: a matching outcome is within a source's capacity; the source can independently choose a fitting exercise under its fixed essential perfections; and that exercise succeeds across all independently admissible co-actions of another alleged source. A game-theoretic or modal formalisation cannot replace one with another without a warranted correspondence. Opposed independently enforceable outcomes are incompatible under a product space of choices, but that product-space or robust-control reading is itself an input. Wisdom-constrained joint admissibility is not established merely by separate ability predicates.

Numerical target identity is equally substantive. An ordinary source-neutral outcome, this actual history, an intrinsically original agent's own act, and the combination of two alleged whole independent productions are different objects. The author explicitly uses the same numerical production. The proposed modern interpretation must preserve that respect while avoiding definitionally assigning a unique provider before CND is separately tested.

This reread reinforces the breadth and seriousness of the primary argument. It does not presently remove the recorded scope question by itself, nor establish a possible plurality. The next assessment must compare these steps with the broader intrinsic-act and perfection account in Minhaj, rather than repeat a weaker objection already answered in Darʾ. The lead reader’s withholding at this checkpoint is a provisional philosophical appraisal, not a textual claim that no rationale exists.
