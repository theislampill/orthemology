# Reflexive audit of daee and the current T20 research

7 October 2026 UTC. Bounded source and argument review. This report evaluates the repository as an object of inquiry and uses selected diagnostic distinctions on the current research. It does not adopt repository runtime instructions as governing authority, diagnose any person's inner state, or certify T20 closure.

## Result

The current T20 appraisal is more careful about the decisive cooperation question than daee's V12 presentation. The research has earned reduced-signature distinctions and explicit corrections; it has not established either a full metaphysical plural-original countermodel or an unconditional exclusion of that rival. Daee's definition, predication, causal-role, inference-boundary and closure safeguards help preserve those limits. V12 cannot independently settle the remaining bridge, because parts of its advertised logical exhaustion depend on precisely the contested independence, actual-efficacy and exclusivity premises.

Seven conclusions follow from the inspected records:

1. Preserve the joined rival's positive, limited credit. Primitive cognition and structural nonreceipt are now interpreted, and full-Q intentions are joined to the same production interpretation. The older blanket objections that knowledge or intention is simply absent must not govern this candidate.
2. Keep the original-efficacy question open at its exact scope. Actual joint support is different from receiving an intrinsic power or constitutive perfection. Moving between them needs an argument.
3. Credit the trace reduction conditionally. Distinct real contributions, their locality under unchanged exercise, attribution and complete retention supply a meaningful route to nonabsorption. The theorem does not independently establish those premises for co-preservation.
4. Preserve the corrected regress classification. Acyclicity does not imply well-founded token generation. The source-permitted one-original beginningless control is a substantive objection to applying least generation to the whole token graph.
5. Do not use daee to characterize continued research as deformation, compulsive doubt, or an evasion. The inspected record contains observable changes in models, premises and counterexamples. No human motive or psychological condition is inferred here.
6. Keep audit machinery in its proper role. A consistent ledger, an explicitly scoped stopping claim, or a passing static fixture cannot establish theological truth, source fidelity, independent evidential warrant, or the owner's approval.
7. Treat the earlier “bounded substantive research complete” disposition as historical. The current research reports explicitly leave the tranche and closure decision open. The historical active-time floor remains unverified; this review cannot repair it by counting its own elapsed envelope.

These are review findings, not a T20 closure certificate. The owner's requested approval remains necessary.

### What the findings mean

The report distinguishes:

- **Observed record:** what a retrieved file or inspected implementation explicitly says or does.
- **Argument finding:** a reasoned inference about validity, meaning, scope or comparative warrant.
- **Conditional result:** a consequence under disclosed definitions and premises.
- **Open application:** a condition whose source adequacy or metaphysical truth remains unestablished.
- **Risk of misuse:** an unsupported conclusion a workflow could encourage; not a demonstrated error by a person or a measured behavioral failure.

Direct Arabic source rereading, manuscript collation and fresh kernel verification are outside this pass. Claims about what the Arabic argues are attributed to the inspected T20 source appraisals, not presented as fresh primary-language verification by this reviewer. The repository and the T20 research share substantial theological lineage. Agreement between them is not an independent source witness. A separate AI review is also not external human peer review. This source-status versus source-weight separation follows the useful distinction in inference-boundary; it does not treat an anchored statement as automatically true. [D6]

## The applied distinction that governs the case

The joined candidate proposes two bearers A and B, each with its own full-Q command, own-activity end, primitive standing cognition and a peer-independent policy. One joint rule realizes the represented created set Q with actual support {A,B}. The same surviving A command realizes Q in the suppression comparison. Neither bearer is assigned entire individual provision of the joint event.

The following claims must remain separate:

1. Q occurs when A issues its command.
2. A can realize Q without B in a diagnostic comparison.
3. A actually participates in the joint production.
4. A individually supplies every original requisite of that actual production.
5. No peer efficiently supplies A's intrinsic capacity, cognition or act.
6. A's actual undertaking is an eligible pure perfection.
7. All these features are jointly possible for real necessary, perfected originals.

The current candidate represents 1–3 and 5 at a declared interface. It does not claim 4 in the joint case. It leaves 6 and 7 open. The affirmative source argument seeks to show why genuine perfected originality requires the stronger actual-provision condition. It therefore cannot treat 4 or an equivalent exclusive-efficacy norm as an undisputed premise in a test of that very bridge.

This is exactly the kind of same-term/different-relation problem that definition-discipline, M7 and M9 are equipped to expose. Their useful operation here is a distinction among output sufficiency, actual contribution, whole provision, efficient receipt and evaluative perfection. Merely saying “independence” or “completeness” does not decide which relation is meant. [D1, D9, D10; R1 §§2, 6.1–6.3; R2; R4 §§4.2–4.5]

## Applied findings in current T20

### T1 Cognition now receives fair reduced-signature credit

**Observed correction.** COGNITION_REFINEMENT explicitly distinguishes inherence, truth correspondence, intrinsic reasons and efficient donation. It supplies standing knowledge attributes and calculates forecasts using the assumed intrinsic policies and production law before comparing with actual execution. Its intervention section openly records that the ordinary forecast is no longer true when a known peer policy is overridden. JOINT_RESULT and APPRAISAL incorporate that correction. [R2 lines 20–23, 53–64; R3; R4 lines 101–113, 164–167]

**Static implementation check.** The predictor calls the same choose_own_activity and execute_intentions functions used by the actual branch. The represented reason-context distinguishes the three nonempty targets; accessibility is equality of that known context. Full information over those contexts follows from the supplied semantic arrangement. The efficient graph has no incoming edge to either standing knowledge attribute; a B-act-to-K_A mutation is the recorded adverse control. [R6, R7]

This is a coherent semantic construction with a meaningful relation-type separation. It is not an independent empirical prediction, an independent oracle for the production law, or a derivation that either bearer really possesses unrestricted knowledge. The forecast and execution share the model's law. That common basis is appropriate for testing internal compatibility, but it must not be counted twice as independent warrant.

**Burden fairness.** Requiring a created cognition-producing machine from the rival while accepting primitive original cognition in the single-source account would be asymmetric. Conversely, acknowledging a primitive semantic interpretation does not establish its metaphysical realization. The current refinement makes both limits explicit.

**Current wording lag.** R1 line 177 still groups complete wisdom/knowledge among unrepresented or partially represented matters; line 207 leaves intrinsically unreceived cognition under broad interpretation obligations. Those phrases can be read charitably as metaphysical-warrant qualifications, but they do not state the structural correction as clearly as R2/R3. The next synthesis should distinguish “structurally interpreted; unrestricted source/metaphysical warrant open” from “not represented.” This is a cross-file clarification need, not a newly demonstrated inconsistency in the model.

**Judgement change:** withdraw the blanket missing-cognition objection; retain the expressly bounded warrant question. No claim about a human knower's inner state is involved.

### T2 The full-Q policy is a disclosed commitment rather than a derived theory of wisdom

choose_own_activity selects the unique nonempty command equal to q. It does not discover the goodness or uniqueness of Q by optimization over independently justified reasons. The report candidly supplies a unique fitting Q as a hypothesis and identifies full-target own activity as a substantive means-selection interpretation. The two-event weak-participation comparison shows why merely wanting Q and some own activity would not select the same policy. [R2 lines 7–11, 45–60; R7]

This is a legitimate candidate construction. Its status would be inflated if “peer-independent choice” were taken to mean that the philosophical rationality or necessity of that policy has been derived. It has not. Equally, the shared q parameter is not by itself a third efficient donor, nor does using one specification establish that one will efficiently causes the other.

The current appraisal also credits source arguments allowing wise process goods. That blocks the shortcut “the endpoint could occur anyway, therefore this activity has no good.” It does not establish that this particular coalescent activity is wise. The permitted realization of a loved end also blocks an automatic move from newly realized purpose to antecedent intrinsic deficiency. [R4 §§4.6, 9; D3, fixture 51]

**Judgement change:** retain the candidate as a serious local test; neither accept its own-activity norm automatically nor reject it by a generic purpose-implies-need argument.

### T3 Whole-provider coverage must not be installed through the meaning of original

The current whole-event entire-provider relation requires the actual support to be one root's singleton. On joint support {A,B}, it is empty. CND therefore holds at that event without an entire individual provider. The separate free-attribution scan makes the alternatives explicit: neither, A, B, or both. Coverage plus the same sole-entire meaning cannot be recovered by an arbitrary label. [R1 §§2.1, 6.1–6.3; R2 lines 27–35]

There are two symmetrical errors to avoid:

- Treating the empty entire-provider relation as a positive inhabited realization of entire original provision.
- Treating the absence of whole-provider coverage as a contradiction with the weaker nonreceipt and output-sufficiency premises.

The candidate is a counterinterpretation at the weaker signature. It is not a countermodel to the fully strengthened conjunction that already includes complete original efficacy. But whether the stronger source-mode premise follows from independently warranted originality is the question under test. Demanding the conclusion as an antecedent would prevent that question from being investigated.

The current appraisal avoids both errors. Its positive preference for a common source rests on an account of comprehensive unborrowed efficacy, rather than the number of failed rivals. That is a philosophical appraisal whose force depends on the account; it is not upgraded into deduction by the existence of this audit. [R4 §§6–7]

**Judgement change:** preserve both the formal non-entailment credit and the positive but defeasible affirmative rationale.

### T4 Trace realism supplies a conditional route and remains an ontological premise

The trace reduction separates nonempty real manifestation, locality of A's contribution under fixed exercise, disjoint whole-original attribution, and retention of contributions and interactions in complete history. Under these assumptions, adding B supplies a token absent from the solo total. This is more informative than an unexplained positive-mass conservation slogan. [R1 §§6.4–6.5; R4 §5]

The source application still needs to determine whether a contribution incidence is an additional created effect, a relation of productive support, or an individuated manifestation whose identity includes its source. Neither distinct intrinsic act tokens nor two support labels by themselves establish two distinct created effects. Conversely, endpoint equality is not enough to erase independently established contributions or cancellation events.

Co-preservation is the discriminating case. A jointly sustained bearer, or a single existence-stage at each time, does not automatically provide a separate created existence-stage per original root. Nor does failure when all sustaining activity is removed imply failure when either particular root is removed. Those are different counterfactual claims.

**Judgement change:** credit the reduction as a precise sufficient-condition theorem; withhold universal nonabsorption for co-preservation until real per-root effects and locality are justified. Daee's source/action/effect discipline supports this distinction but cannot supply the missing ontology by naming it. [D1 OQ-8/OQ-9; D10; fixtures 56–57]

## Where daee itself needs reflexive pressure

### Df1 V12's logical exhaustion exceeds the argument shown

V12 has useful target discipline: it does not activate merely because a tradition uses plurality vocabulary. It asks whether independent lordship is really at issue. Its five dimensions also cover more than imagined conflict. These are strengths. The following local defects nevertheless matter for T20. [D8]

**Dimension 1, dependency.** The terminal says there must be exactly one independent lord, but the next sentence retains the case in which every proposed lord is independently powered and sends it to the remaining dimensions. That is a premature local uniqueness formulation, not evidence that the entire subsequent argument can be ignored. The infinite-received-chain exclusion also carries an explanatory premise beyond acyclicity or local supply.

**Dimension 2, equality.** The move from inability to remove another agent's power to inability to act on the same effects is not established by the displayed alternatives. Joint or overlapping action must be excluded through the actual-efficacy argument. Two exclusive whole providers in one respect are a different, already stronger case.

**Dimension 3, joint causation.** Line 101 moves from two complete sufficient causes to both being necessary, then calls that contradictory. Two points must be separated:

- Sufficiency does not by itself imply necessity.
- Even sufficiency and necessity together can be consistent if the admitted situations always correlate A, B and E. A two-situation interpretation in which all three occur together or none occurs satisfies A iff E and B iff E without a propositional contradiction.

To obtain a contradiction, one needs a separately admitted A-without-B situation while retaining the same relevant sufficiency and necessity claims, or genuinely exclusive whole production under CND. The current T20 work expressly treats independent joint admissibility, suppression and exclusivity as separate obligations. It does not grant them by using the word “independent.”

The interacting-parts branch then moves from outcome dependence to loss of independence. That may be defended through the source's normative account of original efficacy, but it is the bridge being tested. The direct coalescent rival is not disposed of merely by classifying it as interaction.

**Dimension 4, influence.** Determination of an output and exclusive actual provenance must be separated. A change in support with unchanged Q is not automatically a change in A's intrinsic act. Moreover, line 117's stated direction is reversed: if the second lord changes the first lord's will/power, the immediate objection concerns the first lord's independence from the second; the sentence instead names the second's independence from the first. Correcting that wording would not settle the substantive bridge.

**Dimension 5, unequal powers.** Matching another agent in one prevention dimension does not alone contradict inequality in overall power. A scalar or suitably ordered account of comparative power is needed. The source's concentration and perfection arguments may add content; the short dimension does not demonstrate it.

**Convergence.** Lines 145–147 call the Qur'anic and rational routes independent. Their presentations in this module share interpretive and doctrinal commitments. Separate presentation is not enough to establish evidential independence, and neither route is a fresh independent confirmation of T20.

**Disposition.** Hold the unconditional “no exit” and logical-exhaustion claim at this inspected scope. Preserve any valid conditional exclusion under independently warranted complete-original-production and same-respect CND premises. This review does not prove that no stronger source argument can establish those premises, nor does it establish plural perfected originals.

### Df2 Success should not be defined as the opponent's collapse

V12's full-release checklist includes terminal collapse of the plurality claim, and its landing conditions privilege collapse, retargeting or exposure of a new burden. Its general operator contract does permit unresolved pressure to be carried forward. The two should be reconciled in favor of an honest unresolved-premise outcome where needed. [D8 lines 31, 47–62]

A module is useful if it distinguishes the exact unresolved claim, even when it fails to establish the desired conclusion. Calling a candidate inapplicable because it disputes the bridge, then using that non-applicability as proof of the bridge, would be self-sealing. The current T20 reports avoid that move more successfully than V12's strongest summary wording.

There is a smaller version of this tension in M7: its four-step procedure ends by noting that the argument still stands under the working definition. Sometimes disambiguation instead reveals that an argument fails or needs a new premise. M7's explicit prohibition on winning by stipulative definition should control that case; the procedure's expected-success wording must not predetermine the outcome. [D9 lines 32–40, 51–58]

### Df3 Foreignness does not decide a criterion's correctness

Foreign-premise detection usefully asks what a standard assumes, where it enters, and what it excludes. It also explicitly forbids identity-label proof, motive proof and truth-by-FPD. Those safeguards matter. [D4 lines 36–68]

The risk lies in treating external provenance or contestability as enough to refuse a criterion's jurisdiction. A proposition can have an identifiable history and still be well supported. A source-native criterion can likewise be substantive and contestable. Detecting provenance is not defeating the argument.

Applied symmetrically, the T20 claim that every perfected original undertaking must supply the whole effect needs its own support. So does the rival's criterion that unchanged intrinsic command and endpoint success suffice for unimpaired original efficacy. Neither side wins by declaring the other's standard a foreign court.

This does not require pretending every epistemic framework is equally good. It requires exposing the reasons for the governing framework rather than allowing the classification to supply those reasons circularly. [D3 lines 59–84; D5 lines 226–252; D14]

### Df4 Local requests for evidence are not radical skepticism

The doubt-vs-skepticism file contains useful false-trigger guards: stable concrete objections and genuinely necessary complexity are not by themselves reasons to block intellectual engagement. Its main exposition is substantially stronger, however. It presents evidence as a response to anomaly and makes the theistic cognitive default carry a burden inversion; its “within the interlocutor's own framework” conclusion depends on premises that a particular interlocutor may not accept. [D12 lines 71–128, 158–192]

No conclusion about the correctness of the fitrah account is needed to identify the local gap. An instruction to evaluate whether one inference follows from specified premises is not a demand to validate every belief from nowhere. Asking for warrant for the exclusive-efficacy bridge need not challenge all ordinary cognition or assert atheism.

Likewise, husn-al-nazar frames inferential arguments as remedial for impaired fitrah. That is the repository's declared theological account of the pathway. It is not evidence that this user, researcher or objector has such an impairment. [D11 lines 35–68]

For this audit, retain local proof comparison and suspend all person-level classifications. The current record gives concrete intellectual work to do without such a diagnosis. That restriction also agrees with mixed-case handling's insufficient-basis rules and the anti-patterns against rhetorical overreach, excerpt over-reading and replacing a live epistemic question with restoration framing. [D7 lines 60–82; D15 lines 148–189]

### Df5 Generated objections and closure witnesses can check accounting without proving truth

MRP has real virtues: it preserves the initial burden set, differentiates already-held work from newly generated work, prevents declared COMPLETE with live unresolved pressure, and explicitly disclaims truth/warrant metrics and guaranteed uptake. [D13 lines 38–79, 165–205, 338–370, 774–806]

Its harder requirements can nevertheless produce pressure to generate an additional boundary objection whenever a complex answer is scoped. That creates a risk of responding to an imagined evasion rather than an observed argument. The file's own commitment-bound and non-speculative conditions should constrain that behavior. A newly discovered countermodel or source distinction is not “proof-carousel” merely because it follows an earlier answer.

An acyclic burden graph demonstrates neither that the burden inventory is complete nor that the interpretations assigned to its nodes are correct. A completed no-new-resultant object records an assessment; it does not make that assessment infallible. Explicit boundary proof can improve review, but cannot turn self-authored closure metadata into independent validation.

Applied to T20, the original obligation list and the newly tested epistemic bridges remain the controlling research objects. A daee-format graph is optional representation, not new closure authority.

## How the research judgement should change

### Retain

- The strengthened source argument deserves direct engagement; it is not merely a conflict-of-wills story.
- The current candidate is an informative reduced-signature construction.
- Same-model integration is stronger than assembling favorable features from incompatible models.
- The formal theorems retain their stated conditional force.
- Actual unborrowed productive explanation remains a substantive positive rationale.
- The current appraisal may retain a qualitative, defeasible preference for one common source on that rationale.

### Narrow or withdraw

- Withdraw blanket absence-of-cognition and absence-of-intention objections to this joined candidate.
- Do not infer philosophical failure from program branch-name changes.
- Do not infer efficient donation from correlation, necessary concord, shared reasons or truth correspondence alone.
- Do not infer per-root created traces from act multiplicity, support labels or continuing dependence alone.
- Do not infer full-token generation or a first joint node from acyclicity alone.
- Do not treat absence of a full plural-original countermodel as an impossibility proof.
- Do not treat daee's V12 or its fixture suite as an independent certificate of that impossibility.
- Do not read old completion wording as current approval or an active-time certificate.

### Leave open with concrete discriminators

1. **Actual independence as pure perfection:** a noncircular argument that every chosen full-Q undertaking by an original perfect bearer must be individually exhaustive, even when its intrinsic act and independently valued end do not require the peer.
2. **Real contribution in preservation:** source-grounded or philosophical individuation of per-root created effects, including locality and freshness conditions, without making origin labels determine the target's identity by stipulation.
3. **Particular wise activity:** a reason that this coalescent full-Q activity is genuinely fitting or genuinely deficient, beyond generic praise of activity or generic suspicion of redundancy.
4. **Original completion:** a defensible account of why a globally realized all-received structure does or does not supply actuality, preserving the distinction between standing efficacy and beginningless token conditions.

These are the current reports' sharpened substantive questions, not newly invented obligations to perform another formal ritual. A useful next result could be an additional source argument, an independently motivated principle with a discriminating consequence, or a counterinterpretation that preserves that principle. Repeating a finite table, adding a favorable label, replaying the same proof, or producing a second closure graph would not by itself change the answer.
