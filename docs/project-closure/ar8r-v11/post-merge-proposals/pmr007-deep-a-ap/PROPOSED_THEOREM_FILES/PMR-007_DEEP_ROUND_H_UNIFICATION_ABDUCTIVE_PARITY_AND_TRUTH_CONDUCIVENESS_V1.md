## 3. UAP-1A — no Bayesian update under likelihood parity

For hypotheses `h_i,h_j` with positive priors and evidence of positive probability:

\[
\frac{Pr(h_i\mid E)}{Pr(h_j\mid E)}
=
\frac{Pr(E\mid h_i)}{Pr(E\mid h_j)}
\frac{Pr(h_i)}{Pr(h_j)}.
\]

If

\[
Pr(E\mid h_i)=Pr(E\mid h_j),
\]

then the Bayes factor is `1` and:

\[
\frac{Pr(h_i\mid E)}{Pr(h_j\mid E)}
=
\frac{Pr(h_i)}{Pr(h_j)}.
\]

Thus shared derivational structure provides no posterior discrimination among realization labels when the likelihoods are equal. Any posterior preference is prior-driven.

This is a standard Bayesian identity, not new mathematics.

## 4. UAP-1B — no score advantage under matched fit and cost

For a declared score

\[
Score(h;E)=Fit(h;E)-\lambda C(h),
\]

if rival realizers have equal fit and equal declared complexity cost, they tie. If `C(P)<C(I)`, the preference comes from the complexity measure; if `C(I)<C(P)`, the preference reverses. The evidence does not choose the coding or prior by itself.

A bare count of bearers, predicates, or words is not invariant. A “personal ground” may be encoded as one primitive while an impersonal architecture is expanded into many primitives, or vice versa. Candidate G therefore needs an independently justified complexity or explanatory metric rather than a vocabulary-sensitive primitive count.


## 6. Countermodels to overstrong unification claims

### Idle personal tag

Add a primitive predicate `Personal` to the impersonal integrated field without changing any dependency, output, or intervention. The label adds no explanatory work. One-bearer personal vocabulary is not itself unification.

### Impersonal same-structure model

The Deep-B two-primitive map realizes all cross-profile restrictions impersonally. Hence genuine derivational unity does not entail personal realization.

### Distributed compilation

A distributed architecture can compile to the same input-output and intervention profile without one subject possessing the whole evidence. Functional unity is not epistemic subject unity.

### Coding reversal

Choose one language in which the personal architecture has a short primitive name and another in which the impersonal field does. Raw description length reverses. An invariant cost theorem is required before simplicity can carry truth-conducive weight.

### Prior-driven posterior

Equal likelihoods with prior odds `9:1` yield posterior odds `9:1`; with priors reversed, the posterior reverses. Neither preference was generated by `E`.
