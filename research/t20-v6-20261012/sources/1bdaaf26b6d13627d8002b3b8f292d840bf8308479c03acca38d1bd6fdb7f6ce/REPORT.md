# Audit revision: the source-faithful rival to erasing a factor

10 October 2026 UTC. Targeted T20 contribution, scratch-only. No protected edits, source campaign, actual-person diagnosis, repository adoption, or programme closure.

## Discriminating result

**The strongest retained primary case is a provenance correction, not evidence deconditioning.** At *Darʾ* 8:534–535, mass-transmission knowledge precedes the later argument which the subject mistakenly credits with producing it. If that later argument is defective, removing its alleged warranting role corrects the acquisition account. It does not show that a real earlier probabilistic observation should be forgotten, nor that an independently acquired ground has disappeared. The new probabilistic audit operation applies only after an observation, its channel, and the audit process have actually been identified.

Two other retained passages make that application nontrivial. *Darʾ* 5:270 expressly treats genuine nontransmission under an established expectation of transmission as evidence; 5:281–285 makes several reports depend on one scoped authentication premise. Thus **audit outcome/selection and shared-source recalibration** are substantive rivals to one-root inverse multiplication. The earlier accepted-store report already warns about these possibilities. The proposed theorem can now give their exact probabilistic criterion; these source distinctions themselves are inherited.

No independently authenticated real-world audit episode in these inspected records establishes the premises for pure factor deletion. The result is an exact failure of automatic transport, plus concrete source-owned arguments explaining why the missing premises matter. It is not a refutation of conditional Bayes or the positive retractable-store theorem.

## 1. The retained concrete case changes which operation is appropriate

[Darʾ 8:534–535, item 1594](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/413/1594/) distinguishes knowledge, knowing that one knows, and knowing how it arose. Its mass-transmission example says that later reflection may confirm the knowledge or bring it back to attention; it cannot have originated knowledge already present before that reflection. The governing CP03 clarification correctly allows a defective argument to be a causal occasion for a different, independently warranted insight.

This provides a discriminating audit question:

- Was the disputed item an actual observation through a specified channel?
- Was it only the subject's retrospective explanation of already acquired knowledge?
- Was it an occasion that elicited a distinct truth-connected recognition?

Only the first directly instantiates the disputed observation variable as specified in a Bayesian deletion test. The other events can also be represented in a richer probabilistic model, but the second first requires correcting the provenance/basing attribution, and the third requires retaining the difference between occasion and warranting ground. The fact that a calculus assigned all three a likelihood vector would not establish their identity or preserve the same target.

In the second case, treating the bad argument as a second independent source could have been wrong even before the audit. “Remove the factor” can describe repair of that mistaken bookkeeping, but its correctness then comes from a corrected acquisition/dependence model, not from a general right to erase historical evidence after a negative appraisal. If the earlier operative route remains, the source's positive retention verdict is preserved.

## 2. The strongest source-owned rival keeps the audit as evidence

### Genuine absence is not a failed retrieval

[Darʾ 5:269–271, item 960](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/413/960/) first denies that absence of a particular proof implies absence of its conclusion. It then gives an exception: where the expected transmission really would accompany the occurrence, genuine nontransmission counts against the occurrence. The author does not supply a modern search-sensitivity model; the strong expected-transmission premise is substantive.

Consequently a source-faithful probabilistic reconstruction cannot equate these audit records:

1. One document could not be retrieved.
2. A sufficiently informative investigation established nontransmission under conditions in which transmission was expected.

Their likelihoods can differ. The second may supply evidence even when a questioned positive report is discounted. Audit results must therefore be included or shown irrelevant; erasing the original factor and discarding the audit's information is not licensed by the source's rule about alternative proofs. A discrepancy-triggered audit is a particularly clear modern test of this issue, but it is the math worker's constructed example, not a historical example in the passage.

### A shared factive premise is not many autonomous report factors

[Darʾ 5:281–282, item 967](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/413/967/) argues that the specified proof of a messenger's veracity covers the reporting domain rather than selecting some authenticated reports. [5:285–286, item 970](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/413/970/) restricts this claim to reporting from God, and distinguishes other acts.

A faithful conditional reconstruction is: U, together with authentic attribution and in-domain interpreted content, entails the report's truth. If an independently established counterexample really lies inside that conjunction, the conjunction cannot remain unchanged. This is stronger than lowering one report's generic reliability score. A mere alleged contradiction, disputed attribution, mistranslation, or act outside the reporting domain does not supply that counterexample. The source's own position is that the authenticating route remains sound; this report discovers no actual contrary prophetic report or theological defeater.

For a fallible statistical source, an audit of a shared reliability variable may recalibrate several surviving reports. Distinct root IDs do not establish independence from that shared variable. For the source's factive limiting case, exact inconsistency may require support exclusion or reconsidering a premise; strictly positive inverse factors cannot implement support exclusion. This limitation belongs to the later positive-store model. Pinned Deep AX already permits zero likelihoods and rejects outcomes impossible under both candidates. If the full declared model assigns the alleged audit event probability zero, ordinary conditioning is undefined. The root's `audit-aware-correction-root-20261010/ZERO_AUDIT_BOUNDARY.md` shows that arbitrarily small positive perturbations with the same prior can yield any prescribed positive audit posterior. Thus adding an unspecified small fallibility parameter is not a canonical repair. A warranted audit and a substantively supported replacement model are still needed. A merely hypothetical fallible-source example earns no defeater credit against the source's exceptionless scoped claim, and the retained positive source appraisal remains in force.

## 3. Five event types have different transport contracts

These are analytical event types, not a new five-part doctrine attributed to a classical author.

- **Source unreliability learned:** revise the report-generation or shared-calibration model using the actual audit evidence. “Unreliable” does not mean uninformative: a systematically inverted source can be informative, and selection can reconnect a random source's report to the target. Neither the report nor the audit is automatically erasable.
- **Epistemic undercutting:** the affected route loses entitlement or support without thereby establishing the conclusion's negation. Scope matters: a shared authenticity premise differs from one derivation step. The retained modern fact-tracking account also allows generic knowledge despite some misleading defeaters. Thus an apparent undercutter, reduced confidence, and destruction of objective factive warrant are not interchangeable. This last point is inherited from CP19, not a fresh primary rereading of that article.
- **Permission lost:** the allowed use, issue, or publication of an item changes. No evidential revision follows solely from that normative transition. An associated admission of fabrication would be an additional informational event. *Miftāḥ* 2:943's actual distinction between a previously addressed rule and default nonprohibition is a temporal normative control, not a theorem about Bayesian beliefs. Learning a rule changed at t1 need not falsify the claim that it applied at t0.
- **Forgetting:** the subject's available information or retained competence changes. *Darʾ* 7:425–426 allows slight recollection to reactivate knowledge; *Miftāḥ* 1:271–278 also allows weakening and genuine loss. A present-memory posterior can be a legitimate target, but it is then different from the ideal posterior on the full observation history. It requires a memory/retention model. Loss of one file does not establish forgetting; a known fact about what was forgotten may itself be informative.
- **Newly learned invalidity:** identify what failed. An invalid inference was never adequate in that respect, even if its invalidity was learned only later. A later discovery can correct one's appraisal without changing the historical truth of the conclusion. False attribution, a false premise, an invalid inference, and changed applicability have different dependencies. Pure deletion is justified only by the relevant repaired model, not by the word “invalid.”

None of these source positions supplies a numerical likelihood by itself. Conversely, a correct numerical posterior does not settle the retained subject's actual basing or truth-connected acquisition.

## 4. What the new mathematical result can earn

The appropriate audit target keeps retained evidence B, disputed observation Y, and audit A. The strongest deletion comparison already incorporates the audit: P(theta | B,Y,A) versus P(theta | B,A). Equality requires that the realized Y supply no residual target discrimination conditional on B and A, on the surviving support. Returning all the way to P(theta | B) additionally needs audit irrelevance or the corresponding exact combined-likelihood cancellation. The math worker owns the proof and executable examples; this report supplies the source-to-operation interpretation.

This separates three claims that “withdrawal” previously risked merging:

1. The accepted-factor store implements its declared deletion exactly.
2. That deletion matches full-history Bayesian revision under a justified observation/audit model.
3. That revision represents an actual person's retained epistemic warrant.

The first is already established. The new audit criterion can establish the second conditionally. The source cases above explain why it does not establish the third by itself. Noetic-correction's runtime/interlocutor type distinction is preserved, and its unrelated recognition-guard gap supplies no evidence for a human audit channel.

The useful increment is the exact operation choice and application boundary, not another knowing-versus-willing overview. The retained primary mass-transmission case is not a clean empirical instantiation of factor deletion. Its positive payoff is to preserve the earlier operative ground while correcting the later attribution. The retained expected-transmission and shared-authentication arguments identify exactly which information a full audit model must not discard.
