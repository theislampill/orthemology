## 1. Candidate setting

Let `S` be a finite neutral structural system with carrier `X`.  Let
`C=(C,Λ)` be a finite content algebra whose signature `Λ` contains exactly the
content relations and operations independently admitted into the neutral
comparison.  An interpretation is a map

\[
\mu:X\to C.
\]

Let `Γ=Aut(C,Λ)`.  For `γ in Γ`, define the recoded interpretation

\[
\gamma\mu=\gamma\circ\mu.
\]

The **neutral semantic profile** of `mu` consists of all structural facts of
`S` and all `Λ`-atomic relations among the interpreted contents of finite tuples
of `X`.

## 2. Candidate semantic-gauge claim

Every `γ in Γ` preserves the neutral semantic profile.  Therefore, if
`γ∘μ != μ`, literal content labels and referents are not identified by that
profile.  The profile identifies at most the orbit

\[
[\mu]_\Gamma.
\]

A literal interpretation is structurally identified only if its orbit is a
singleton or an independently warranted extra anchor removes the content
symmetry.

## 3. H7a relation

Deep AM characterized isomorphism-natural selection against automorphisms of
the **structural carrier**.  The present candidate concerns automorphisms of the
**content algebra**.  An asymmetric structure may select one natural mapping at
the structural level while a nontrivial semantic recoding remains.

The candidate therefore proposes:

```text
H7a1 structural canonizability:
  may hold;

H7a2 literal/constitutive semanticity:
  remains unestablished when the content gauge orbit is nontrivial;

H7a3 mental realization:
  remains separately open even when the content algebra is rigid.
```

## 4. Controls

```text

  a content algebra with only equality permits T/F exchange;
  a literal truth label is not identified.

AY-CM2 SOURCE-ROLE RELABELING:

AY-CM3 STRUCTURAL RIGIDITY WITH CONTENT GAUGE:
  S has no nontrivial automorphism but C does;
  Deep-AM H7a1 can hold while literal semantics remains open.

AY-CM4 DESIGNATED ANCHOR:
  adding an independently warranted truth or referent constant can trivialize
  the relevant content automorphism group.

AY-CM5 HIDDEN ANCHOR:
  adding the preferred label merely to force rigidity imports the conclusion.

AY-CM6 RIGID IMPERSONAL SEMANTICS:
  a content algebra and interpretation are rigid, but the implementing law is
  impersonal and no mental subject or Speech follows.
```

## 5. Intended central effect

The candidate seeks an exact nonidentifiability boundary for intrinsic
notionality.  It does not claim that all semantics is conventional, that no
literal reference exists, that an impersonal account is true, or that a source
cannot supply a semantic anchor.
