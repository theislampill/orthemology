# New primary source findings

## Dretske manuscript

[From Experience to Belief: Modes of Mental Representation](https://www.uh.edu/~garson/DretskeExperience.PDF), university-hosted typescript, manuscript pp. 1–21. All pages and eight endnotes were read, including visual inspection of every page. The last page names Fred Dretske and Stanford University; its publication venue and date remain unverified. Handwritten annotations are not attributed to Dretske.

The argument separates sensory representation from learned recognitional content. Its account permits an acquired classification to exceed what current experience represents. The relevant learning recruits information-bearing states; later occurrences exercise their established function. The argument does not supply a complete warrant theory. Its strong learning-versus-inheritance thesis is a philosophical commitment, not a developmental finding independently established here. Pages 4–6, 13–18 and note 6 carry the important distinctions; pages 19–20 preserve the historical explanation of efficacy. This is new complete reading of a previously first-page-only source, not acquisition of Explaining Behavior chapter 6.

## Adams Barker and Clarke

[Knowledge as Fact-Tracking True Belief](https://www.scielo.br/j/man/a/JS5dxNB79fZvM9pJxRk3HzF/), Manuscrito 40(4), 2017, 1–30, DOI10.1590/0100-6045.2017.V40N4.FA. Complete official HTML argument, references and 33 argumentative notes read. Note 34 is article metadata. No PDF-page reading claimed.

The authors combine sensitive reasons with a separately articulated basing account, permit factive inferential grounds, and distinguish generic knowledge from justifiable and tenable knowledge. Their account explicitly allows generic knowledge despite misleading defeating evidence. The final section adopts relevant conditionals to avoid vacuity for necessary truths. Its application still invokes substantive judgments about the actual intellectual or testimonial basis. The tax-lawyer section considers a selected proposition separately from accompanying falsehoods. The precise coherence claim tested in REPORT.md is theirs; Dretske is not its author.

## Ameri control

[براهين وجود الله في النفس والعقل والعلم](https://almobadarah.com/uploads/books/5f6ae0298582cce21002cbe4.pdf), retained first 1445/2024 edition, pp. 174–175, both freshly visually read. This is a targeted reread within the predecessor's completed chapter.

Page 174 rejects a direct logical inference from need to existence. Page 175 presents the storm-and-appeal episode as disclosure of recognition of the one divine power, then reports an exchange attributed to Jaʿfar. That historical attribution remains unauthenticated here. The narrative supplies a philosophical case, not an independently observed participant or a measured cognitive mechanism. The surrounding experimental citation receives no new empirical credit.
